About us Traditional banking has never really given people control over their financial life. Satispay exists to change that: to empower people through finance. That means making financial services easy and accessible for millions of users. We started with payments, then benefits, investing, cards... and we’re not done. Not even close. Same logic every time: find a real problem, solve it properly, then look for the next one. The destination? To build the most loved financial platform in the world. Getting there takes leaders who own outcomes across teams, not just tasks, and set the tone for refusing the “someone else will fix this” culture. If that's how you lead, you'll fit right in. What you'll be doing As our Chief Technology Officer (CTO), you will lead Satispay’s technology strategy and execution to empower people through finance and build the most loved financial platform in the world. Driven by our Ownership Culture, you will take full accountability for scaling a regulated, high-availability payments platform, modernizing legacy services, and building an engineering organization that uses AI as a daily multiplier. You will work closely with the CIO, CISO, and the CPO, and you will sit on the executive team. Here's what that looks like in practice: - Set the technical vision - Define the multi-year architecture and platform roadmap, balancing reliability, speed and cost as we add new products and markets. - Own platform reliability and security - Guarantee uptime, resilience and security for a payments system at scale, in line with PSD2/PSD3, DORA, PCI-DSS and Bank of Italy / ECB expectations. - Modernize the stack - Retire or rebuild legacy services, reduce technical debt and move towards a cleaner, more scalable service architecture. - Make engineering AI-native - Drive adoption of AI coding agents across development, code review, testing and incident response. Redefine engineering roles, ways of working and quality gates, and measure the impact on velocity and quality. - Build and grow the team - Hire, mentor and retain senior leaders and engineers, and develop early-career talent. Create a culture of ownership, fast shipping and high standards. - Partner across the business - Work with Product, Compliance, Risk, Data and Operations to deliver features quickly without compromising regulatory requirements. - Manage technology investment - Own the engineering budget, vendor and cloud strategy, and build-vs-buy decisions. - Represent technology externally - Act as a credible voice with regulators, partners, investors and the engineering community. You will lead and mentor high-performing teams, collaborate closely with peers with structured communication and transparency, demonstrating a commitment to integrity and ownership. Success in the first 12 months In your first 12 months, you will establish yourself as the undisputed technical leader, earning the trust and respect of the entire engineering organization. You will inherit a highly engaged team, and your goal is to sustain this culture by empowering them with true ownership while simultaneously ensuring that critical technical decisions are consistently executed correctly. As the department continues to increase in size, you will design and implement a strategic reorganization to scale our operations effectively. On the technical front, you will drive our transition to a 100% AI software factory model, building on our current foundation where most of our code is already AI-generated. This transformation must directly translate to accelerated development speeds, measurably reducing the cycle time from PRD to production release. Alongside this rapid innovation, you will partner closely with the CISO to fortify our security posture, specifically building robust defenses against new and emerging AI-driven attacks.
