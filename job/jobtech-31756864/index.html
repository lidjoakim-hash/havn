If you’re looking to make the best co-op games in the world, you’ve come to the right place. We're proud to have some of the best game makers and collaborators in the industry working with us. What's more, we're a fun bunch of game developers, who love to indulge in our passion for video games together. Our games are not for everyone. Are they for you? We are now looking for a Senior Game Designer Join the Helldivers 2 team and expand our mission toolbox with more systemic variables, more dynamic objectives and more room for emergent shenanigans. This is a hands-on role. You’ll come up with ideas for missions and game modes, then build, script, balance and debug them yourself in the engine. What you'll do: From Ideation to Game: Create missions. You’ll draft the core loop, flow and win/fail conditions, then script the working prototype yourself Design for Modular Worlds: Create mission structures and objectives that work with our semi-procedural map generation Design for Sandbox Emergence: Create mechanics that cut across mission objectives to spark unexpected encounters Engine Scripting: Script objective flows, encounters, interactable objects and success/fail states in our in-house engine Stress-Test & Debug: Test and debug your own setups, chasing down edge cases, logic breaks and performance issues so your missions hold up in live multiplayer Cross-Discipline Collaboration: Work closely with Level Design, Tech Design, Code, UI, Art and Audio Iterate with Live Data: Use telemetry, completion rates and player feedback to balance and improve missions after release Mentor & Lead: Share best practices in encounter flow, system design, and design documentation with other designers. Who you are: You’re as passionate about co-op shooters as we are. You’re friendly and helpful, but also efficient, hard-working and dedicated to the game design craft. You also… Have about 5+ years of game design experience in AA/AAA, with at least one shipped title where you personally implemented missions, encounters or systems Are comfortable scripting in the engine (Lua, Python, C# or similar). You can build mission logic and state machines without relying on engineers for the basics Are a Systems Thinker who understands player psychology, systemic design, pacing and core loops Have Autonomy & Problem-Solving skills that take a high-level pitch like “a multi-stage sabotage operation against an Automaton strongpoint”, break it down, greybox it and see it through to release Are able to write Clear design docs and explain your decisions to other disciplines Have Strong Co-op Sensibilities, to know what makes shared victory feel earned and team failure feel hilarious rather than cheap Are a Master of Emergent Sandbox Chaos, to balance objectives around unpredictable systems like roaming AI, physics and destructible environments Have Great English skills, both written and spoken How to apply If you think that this position matches your background and skills, we would love to hear from you. Please submit your CV in English as soon as possible. We ask that your application always include relevant work samples or a link to your portfolio. We interview candidates continuously and hire when we find a good fit. Practical Information Scope: Full-time: 40 hours per week with initial 6 month probation period Location: Office first, hybrid solution possible with minimum 3 days per week in the office Eligibility: You need to be eligible to work in Sweden. We are a pet friendly office! You can expect to find dogs and occasionally some cats around. What we offer Arrowhead Game Studios is a growing team of about 140+ people, best known for our co-op titles. Despite our size, we remain a friendly and down to earth bunch, who love to indulge in our passion for video games together.
