Revenue recognition experience is required. • Experience with high-growth companies, ideally in the Software as a Service (SaaS) and/or Platform as a Service (PaaS) industry. • Exceptional organizational skills with the ability to prioritize tasks effectively and work as part of a team. • Ability to deliver results and meet expectations in a fast-paced environment. • Intermediate knowledge of general accounting concepts and ASC 606. • Excellent written and verbal communication skills. • A data-driven mindset with a proactive approach. • Knowledge of SOX compliance protocols.
