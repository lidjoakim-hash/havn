THE ROLE Location: While this position is posted in a specific location, all of Oyster's positions are fully remote and you can work from home. Forever. To create the best experience for our new hire, this role requires you to be based within -5 / +3 UTC. As Senior Director of Product for our Employer of Record offering, you will own the product vision, strategy and execution across the full EOR employment lifecycle, from onboarding and documentation through time off, benefits, pay and offboarding. That spans the workflows our customers use, the internal tooling our operations teams rely on, and the experience of the employees we employ on our customers' behalf. This is the core of Oyster's business and it is operationally and legally complex. A payroll delay, a contract issue or a compliance gap becomes a customer escalation, a legal question and an operations fire on the same day. We want someone who treats the whole journey as one experience, for our customers and for the people who have to run it. As a part of the senior leadership team at Oyster, you will be shaping strategy well beyond your own teams, setting direction across multiple product development teams and trusted to make the calls that define where this part of the business goes. You will be leading through real change: we are raising the bar on performance, moving toward direct feedback and radical candour, and reshaping how we build with AI. WHAT YOU WILL OWN - Own the product vision, strategy and roadmap for EOR, and connect team-level work to company outcomes. - Be accountable for the end-to-end performance of the platform, including the downstream operational reality, not only what ships. - Turn highly localised, complex regulation into scalable, automated workflows rather than manual workarounds held together by our operations teams. - Prioritise across new capability, regulatory requirements, technical debt and operational improvement, across several teams at once. - Coach and grow a team of product managers and group product managers, moving them from delivering features to owning problems and outcomes. - Partner with Engineering and Design leadership as a real counterpart, holding proper tension on trade-offs without dominating or giving in too easily. - Partner with Operations, Legal and Finance to shape where this business goes, including the margin and risk picture. - Build an AI-first way of working in the product organisation
