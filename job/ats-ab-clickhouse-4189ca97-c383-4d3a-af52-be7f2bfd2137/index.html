The Learning Team at ClickHouse is growing fast as more users discover the power of ClickHouse and seek to understand how to use it effectively. As a Training Operations Specialist, you will be responsible for coordinating, managing, and supporting the logistics and administration of global training programs, with a focus on public and private instructor-led training events. You will collaborate with internal stakeholders to ensure seamless event execution and a consistent, high-quality learner experience. WHAT YOU WILL BE DOING: Here is a list of the typical tasks you would be performing as a Training Operations Specialist: - Schedule and organize virtual and in-person training events, including registration page setup, Zoom configuration, and email communications. - Coordinate training event logistics, including sourcing and booking venues, managing event requirements, coordinating instructor availability, and communicating with instructors and attendees. - Manage event registration, monitor enrollment, send reminders, follow-up emails, and track attendance to ensure smooth event execution. - Track and report training metrics, registration trends, attendance, and program outcomes to key stakeholders. - Partner with Field Marketing teams to coordinate public training events, including event scheduling, registration monitoring, and promotional support. - Collaborate with Sales teams to coordinate private training requests, manage scheduling and logistics, and support customer training needs. - Work closely with the broader Training team to coordinate resources, streamline processes, and ensure consistent execution across training programs. REQUIREMENTS: - 2+ years of experience in training coordination, operations, event management, or a related role. - Proficiency with marketing automation platforms, particularly Marketo, including registration management and email communications. - Proficiency with online communication and collaboration tools, including Zoom, Google Workspace, and Notion. - Ability to work independently and manage multiple priorities across various time zones simultaneously. - Detail-oriented, with excellent organizational, time management, communication, and process management skills. - Ability to learn new technologies and systems quickly and effectively. - A self-motivated attitude with the desire and determination to complete tasks in a fast-paced startup environment. BONUS POINTS: - Experience working with Learning Management Systems (LMS). - Experience with Salesforce, particularly reporting, dashboards, and tracking training-related data. - Background in project management, curriculum design, or learning and development. - Experience coordinating instructor-led training programs across multiple regions or global audiences. PERKS - Flexible work environment - ClickHouse is a globally distributed company and remote-friendly. We currently operate in over 25 countries. - Healthcare - Employer contributions towards your healthcare. - Equity in the company - Every new eligible team member who joins our company receives equity. - Time off - Flexible time off in the US, generous entitlement in other countries. - A USD$500 Home office setup if you’re a remote employee. - Global Gatherings – We believe in the power of in-person connection and offer opportunities to engage with colleagues at company-wide offsites.
