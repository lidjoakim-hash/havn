About the role Search is changing faster than at any point in the last 20 years. Buyers now find answers in Google and in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and AI Overviews, and we want ZoomInfo to always be the answer they get. As Senior SEO & AI Search Manager, you'll own projects across ZoomInfo's organic and AI search strategy, spanning all ZoomInfo domains, and the wider SEO and AI search roadmap. You'll write and ship content yourself, help manage our agency and vendor partners, and work closely with Sales, Product and Demand Generation. Success means connecting visibility to pipeline and revenue, not traffic alone. You'll report to the Director, Content Engineering. What you'll do • Own specific SEO and AI search projects end to end, each tied to a defined outcome such as visibility, AI mention share or pipeline • Write and own SEO and AI search content yourself, from research through to publishing • Deliver AI search projects that grow citation share, brand mentions and share of voice, and shape how AI answers describe ZoomInfo's key launches and capabilities • Set direction for an agent-ready site, optimized for AI agents and agentic workflows from content through to product, as part of our product-led growth push • Apply our Content Engineering frameworks and hold all search content to their quality standards • Help own day-to-day management of our SEO, link-building and AI-visibility partners • Own aspects of SEO and AI search measurement from visibility to revenue, and report it to marketing leadership • Support key projects such as product launches, aligning with Sales, Product and Demand Generation so search is in the plan from the start What You Bring • 5+ years in SEO, with experience at a B2B SaaS company • You're an effective writer in your own right, with experience owning content end to end • A proven record of connecting organic and AI search visibility to pipeline and revenue • Practical experience with AI search and a point of view on where it is heading • Strong technical SEO literacy, including agentic crawlability: you can diagnose issues and write requirements engineers can act on • Familiarity with content engineering: structured content, programmatic pages, entity optimization and AI-assisted workflows • Fluency with Ahrefs or Semrush, Google Search Console, GA4, crawlers and AI-visibility tools • Experience working with agencies and influencing senior stakeholders • Curiosity about how AI can make search and content teams faster and smarter Bonus points: experience running programmatic SEO plays; a background in GTM, data or MarTech products.
