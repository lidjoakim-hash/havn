LIFE AT UIPATH The people at UiPath believe in the transformative power of automation to change how the world works. We’re committed to creating category-leading enterprise software that unleashes that power. To make that happen, we need people who are curious, self-propelled, generous, and genuine. People who love being part of a fast-moving, fast-thinking growth company. And people who care—about each other, about UiPath, and about our larger purpose. Could that be you? YOUR MISSION The Global Account Manager – Financial Services will own UiPath’s relationship with a small number of high revenue strategic banking customers and lead our engagements with them at a global level. Sitting within our UK Banking go-to-market team, you'll act as the CEO for a portfolio of top-tier global banking accounts, orchestrating sales, delivery, and executive stakeholders across regions to unlock enterprise-wide value. You will work every day across the C-Suite, Digital Transformation, AI/Automation COE's, Operations, Risk, Compliance, Finance, and Technology teams to deliver value through UiPath’s automation and AI platform. We provide technology and solutions that help financial institutions streamline operations, improve accuracy, reduce risk, enhance compliance, and free employees to focus on higher-value work. You will be challenged and supported by the best in the industry as you build trusted relationships and pave your own path to success.
