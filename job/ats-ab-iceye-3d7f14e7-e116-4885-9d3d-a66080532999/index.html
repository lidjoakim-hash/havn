Role highlights: - Location: Espoo, Finland - Department: Global Manufacturing Organization - Reports to: Manager, Logistics Management - Employment type: Permanent - Workplace model: On-site (Cleanroom) - Employment is subject to applicable security screening (incl. SUPO, where required) Why this role matters: As an Electronics Verification Technician, you are a critical part of the highly international manufacturing team specifically responsible for ensuring every PCBA functions flawlessly before assembly. Reporting to the Electronics Verification Team Lead, embedded within the Logistics and QC Department, you will work in a clean area with ESD sensitive devices and classified information, operating test stations, high precision equipment, verifying electronic board assemblies, performing visual inspections, troubleshooting failed tests and providing immediate feedback to prevent defects from moving down the assembly line. Who We Are ICEYE is the world leader in sovereign intelligence from space. We deliver persistent monitoring capabilities to detect and respond to changes in any location on Earth. ICEYE owns the world's largest and most advanced SAR (synthetic aperture radar) satellite constellation. To our customers we provide intelligence with unmatched quality, latency and revisit times, in any weather, day or night. To governments who choose to operate their own constellation we provide this proven capability as a sovereign system. ICEYE-built constellations serve customers in defence and intelligence, environmental monitoring, insurance and emergency management. We enable fast decisions that contribute to a safer future. Founded and headquartered in Finland, ICEYE operates globally with over 1,000 employees across Poland, Spain, Germany, the UK, Australia, Japan, the UAE, Greece, and the US. Your day-to-day responsibilities - Conduct visual inspections, electrical verifications, and basic mechanical tests as well as quality checks during incoming operations of electronic parts, mechanical parts, commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) items, and subsystems to ensure compliance with specifications; - Update material status in the ERP and MES system, Record inspection results and maintain accurate, audit-ready, documentation; - Report non-conformances, support root cause analysis activities, contribute to the implementation of corrective and preventive actions across production and the supply chain, communicate persistent assembly errors to the production lead; - Perform real-time visual and mechanical inspection of SMT and Through-Hole assemblies to IPC-A-610 and J-STD-001 standards. - Flash firmware and bootloaders onto microcontrollers, troubleshoot test failures in real-time, identifying whether the issue is a component failure, soldering defect, or test fixture error. - Occasionally perform minor rework on non-conforming boards to keep production flowing. - Ensure inspection tools and equipment are properly calibrated and maintained; - Monitor inspection, logistics, production, storage, and laboratory environments to ensure compliance with safety and quality standards to support consistent manufacturing operations. What we’re looking for Must haves: - Strong background in Electronics, Mechatronics, Testing, or related fields, with computer literacy in Jira, Confluence, MES Systems, Linux, and with programming knowledge in Python, CSV, Bash, etc. - Direct hands-on experience in the use of complex electronic measurement tools, oscilloscopes, multimeters, bench power supplies, VNA, test jigs, etc. - Conversational level of spoken and written English. - Degree, diploma or applicable certifications: Master of Science
