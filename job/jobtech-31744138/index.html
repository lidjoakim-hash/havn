About the Job Intertalents Consulting AB is recruiting a Senior Plasma Experimentalist on behalf of a client working with advanced experimental physics and energy technology. We are looking for an experienced physicist who can combine practical laboratory expertise with a strong understanding of plasma behaviour and experimental data. The position offers an opportunity to influence how our client plans experiments, evaluates results, and develops its scientific capabilities. About the Role: In this role, you will join our client’s multidisciplinary research and development team. Your work will connect experimental design, plasma diagnostics, and data analysis, helping the team turn scientific questions into practical experiments and meaningful conclusions. You will provide senior scientific input throughout experimental campaigns and work closely with specialists in physics, instrumentation, data analysis, theory, and simulation. You will also contribute to technical decisions, support less experienced colleagues, and bring the experimental perspective into discussions about future research priorities. Your Key Responsibilities: Translate scientific objectives into experimental approaches and measurement plans. Provide technical guidance during the preparation, execution, and evaluation of experimental campaigns. Develop and apply analysis methods that help explain observed plasma behaviour. Assess experimental results using relevant physical models, detector principles, and simulation tools. Recommend improvements to diagnostic capabilities, measurement methods, and experimental procedures. Take ownership of scientific and technical challenges, working independently and together with other specialists. Support junior colleagues through practical guidance and knowledge sharing. Contribute to discussions about the direction and priorities of our client’s experimental research. Who You Are: We are looking for a senior physicist who enjoys working close to experiments and investigating complex scientific problems. You combine analytical depth with a practical approach and can move between detailed technical work and broader research questions. You are proactive, collaborative, and willing to support colleagues across different disciplines. You are comfortable making independent technical assessments and adapting your approach as new experimental findings emerge. We believe you have: Several years of relevant experience in experimental physics, with the ability to independently address complex experimental challenges. A strong practical background in designing experiments and developing, implementing, and applying physics data analysis methods. Experience interpreting experimental observations through an understanding of the underlying physics, detector behaviour, and relevant simulations. Hands-on experience with linear plasma devices. The ability to take technical responsibility and contribute scientific expertise within a multidisciplinary team. It is considered a plus if you also have: A PhD in plasma physics, experimental physics, or a related discipline, supported by scientific publications. Experience developing software for plasma research or scientific data analysis. Experience working in collaborative laboratory settings where priorities and experimental needs evolve. Experience supporting the development of junior researchers or engineers. Your expertise may cover one or more of the following areas: Plasma instabilities, transport, heating, and fueling. Operation and diagnostics of linear plasma systems, including mirror and cusp configurations. Optical and particle diagnostics, such as spectroscopy, interferometry, tomography, imaging, probes, and particle detection. Diagnostic calibration and the effects of plasma exposure on measurement equipment. Plasma-surface interactions, including wall conditioning, erosion, and deposition.
