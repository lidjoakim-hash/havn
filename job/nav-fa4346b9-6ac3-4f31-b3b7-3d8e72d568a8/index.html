Commercial Strategy Manager

Fossefall · Oslo, Norway

Lønn ikke oppgittHeltidvia NAVFrist 9.1.2027

The Opportunity Want to be the person who turns big ambitions into a plan that actually moves? Fossefall is strengthening its Revenue Generation team with a Strategy Manager who will own go-to-market planning and commercial execution. Reporting to the CRO, you will connect strategic priorities with disciplined commercial activity and help turn complex customer demands into clear plans, structured decisions and measurable progress. If you enjoy owning the plan and the follow-through, read on. What you'll do • Build and align a GTM plan covering priority customer segments, value propositions, routes to market, commercial priorities and ownership. • Translate priority opportunities into structured account and execution plans with clear owners, next steps, expected value and target dates. • Establish a repeatable operating rhythm for pipeline reviews, opportunity prioritisation, forecasting and commercial follow-up. • Identify where opportunities stall and implement actions that improve pipeline progression, stage conversion and sales-cycle time against an agreed baseline. • Develop commercial models and business cases for complex infrastructure opportunities. • Support contract and deal structuring, working directly with the Revenue Generation lead and senior executives to move opportunities toward agreement. About you • Experience from strategy consulting and infrastructure investment or development, ideally from AI, data centers, energy or infrastructure environments. • A Master's degree in Industrial Economics, Business Administration or Economics, or a comparable quantitative field. • Strong capability in commercial modelling and business-case development. You build cases that hold up for complex, capital-intensive opportunities. • Hands-on experience shaping and executing enterprise go-to-market strategies. You have taken a strategy from slide to results. • Experience supporting contract and deal structuring in complex commercial settings. • Confidence working directly with senior executives and translating strategic priorities into practical action. • Commercial strategy experience from complex infrastructure projects is a strong plus. What success looks like Within the first few months, you have built and aligned a clear GTM plan, translated priority opportunities into structured execution plans and established a consistent commercial operating rhythm. Priority opportunities have clear ownership, next steps, expected value and target dates. Pipeline baselines are established, improvement targets are agreed and the team has better visibility into progression, conversion and commercial priorities. Why Fossefall? Fossefall is building for long-term growth, which means many important ways of working are still being shaped. Joining now means having the opportunity to influence how the organisation grows, how teams collaborate and how high-quality work gets done. You will work close to capable colleagues, contribute to meaningful priorities and help build something that can last. About Fossefall Fossefall is a Nordic AI infrastructure company founded in 2025. We develop and operate AI factories powered by renewable energy, turning clean Nordic power into scalable, sovereign computing capacity for Europe, built in partnership with local communities, energy producers and technology leaders across the world. As a team, we are still small enough to know each other as people, and our culture is shaped by ownership, curiosity, warmth and collaboration. Interested?
