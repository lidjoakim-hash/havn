Did you know that Sweden's largest shipyard for ship repair and conversion projects is in Landskrona? At Oresund DryDocks, vessels from across Northern Europe and beyond come to us for complex repair, maintenance and conversion projects. With a strong reputation for quality, safety and delivery performance, we are a leading player in the maritime industry. Our success is built on the expertise and commitment of approximately 110 employees, working across project management, engineering, production and support functions. We are now looking for a Head of Production to lead and further develop our production organization. The Role as Head of Production As Head of Production, you will have overall responsibility for leading, developing and optimizing the production organization through your department managers within Steel, Piping, Mechanical, and Dock & Transport. You will ensure that the organization has the right resources, competencies, processes and working methods in place to successfully deliver projects according to established targets for safety, quality, cost efficiency and delivery performance. As a member of the management team, you will play a key role in driving operational excellence and contributing to the company's long-term strategic development. The position requires a visible and engaged leadership style, with a strong presence in the business and close collaboration with employees, customers and stakeholders. Experience You have several years of experience in a senior production leadership role within the maritime, shipyard, manufacturing or other project-driven industrial sectors. You have a proven track record of: Leading managers and larger production organizations through delegated responsibility Driving operational performance and achieving demanding business objectives Developing organizations, processes and working methods to improve efficiency, productivity and delivery precision Building and strengthening a proactive safety culture with a strong focus on HSEQ Managing operations in an international business environment Experience from project-driven production environments where flexibility, coordination and customer focus are critical is highly valued. Leadership Profile You are a confident and inspiring leader who leads by example. Through clear communication and a structured approach, you create engagement, accountability and commitment throughout the organization. You are results-oriented and combine a strong business mindset with a genuine interest in people development. You can balance strategic thinking with operational execution and are comfortable making decisions in a dynamic environment. To succeed in the role, you should have: Strong leadership and people management skills Excellent planning and organizational abilities A strong focus on quality, safety and customer satisfaction Good technical understanding and the ability to translate business objectives into operational activities Excellent communication skills in both Swedish and English, written and spoken Meritorious Qualifications Experience from the shipbuilding, ship repair or maritime industry Experience in Lean Manufacturing and Continuous Improvement What We Offer At Oresund DryDocks, you will become part of a highly skilled and dedicated team operating within an exciting international industry. We offer a challenging and varied leadership role with significant opportunities to influence both operational performance and the future development of the company. Our organization is characterized by short decision-making paths, a collaborative culture and a strong commitment to continuous improvement. You will work alongside talented colleagues from diverse backgrounds who share a passion for delivering high-quality solutions to our customers.
