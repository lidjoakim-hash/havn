Resend is growing fast, and there are now over 4 million developers using it. We’re building tools that make email better for developers. We care about dependable infrastructure, thoughtful design, and the experience of everyone building with our products. The Platform Squad builds and maintains the infrastructure, tools, and systems that keep Resend running reliably, securely, and at scale, empowering our teams to build, deploy, and operate services with confidence. This role is fully remote based in Americas or European timezones, with some expected travel for team offsites, conferences, meetups, and more. Read more about how we work, how we hire, and what we value here https://resend.com/handbook. IN THIS ROLE, YOU WILL… - Build and improve the platform that powers Resend, providing reliable infrastructure and tooling that enables engineers to move quickly and safely - Design and operate scalable cloud infrastructure, primarily on AWS, with a focus on reliability, performance and security - Automate operational work and build self-service workflows that make common infrastructure and engineering tasks easier - Establish platform standards and patterns that help teams build, deploy, and operate services consistently - Improve reliability and observability across Resend, including participating in incident response and turning operational issues into lasting improvements - Partner with engineers across Resend to solve infrastructure and platform problems and build reusable solutions YOU WILL BE A GREAT FIT IF YOU... - Bring 8+ years of experience across infrastructure engineering, platform, or SRE - Have strong experience managing production infrastructure as code, using tools like AWS CDK, Terraform, or Pulumi - Understand distributed systems and cloud infrastructure, including queues, workers, databases, caching, networking, and load balancing - Have solid working knowledge of AWS - Have strong experience with observability and monitoring, using tools like Datadog, Grafana, or OpenTelemetry - Are comfortable working across the stack, from load balancers and networking to delivery pipelines - Take ownership, communicate trade-offs clearly, and are comfortable working independently while knowing when to ask for help - Communicate clearly in written and spoken English and can consistently overlap with Americas working hours YOU WILL BE AN EXCEPTIONAL FIT IF YOU ALSO... -
