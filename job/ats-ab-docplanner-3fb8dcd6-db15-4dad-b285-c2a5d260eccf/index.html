MyDr is a team of new technology enthusiasts who have built from scratch and continue to develop advanced programs for Electronic Medical Documentation and comprehensive clinic management. The company has brought the Polish healthcare system into the 21st century and made it user-friendly for both doctors and patients. The flagship product - MyDr EDM - is used in over 10,000 clinics operating commercially as well as those contracted with the National Health Fund (NFZ). The program offers a complete package of functionalities essential for daily work in any medical facility: from advanced scheduling, through tools needed during appointments (modules for e-prescriptions, e-referrals, and e-sick leaves), to telemedicine solutions, and full integration with the government P1 Platform. MyDr was founded in 2017 and has since successfully grown and maintained its status as a leader in fully cloud-based EDM solutions in Poland's medical software market.
