The opportunity: The Enterprise Account Executive (ENT) is responsible for driving complex, high-value enterprise deals and establishing long-term strategic partnerships with large customers. This role owns advanced, multi-stakeholder sales cycles that often span regions, business units, and decision layers. The ENT AE operates with a high degree of autonomy, shaping deal strategy, influencing executive stakeholders, and orchestrating cross-functional teams to deliver differentiated, value-driven solutions. Strategic Discovery & Solution Architecture • Lead executive-level discovery to understand enterprise supply chain strategy, risk posture, and long-term business objectives. • Navigate complex buying groups across procurement, ops, finance, IT, and senior leadership. • Design integrated, multi-product Flexport solutions aligned to customer priorities and business outcomes. • Position Flexport as a strategic partner through compelling value narratives, business cases, and solution roadmaps.
