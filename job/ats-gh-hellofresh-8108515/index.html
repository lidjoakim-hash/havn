About The Team: This role oversees the smooth operation of the automated equipment during production; from setting up the equipment to analysing data to provide improvement areas. This is done through effective communication and using bespoke software tools. The Flow Controller mainly interacts with Operations Managers, Shift Manager, Engineering and Automation Tech teams. They monitor production lines, report issues, and help solve problems. They also assist with testing new systems and work on improving box production efficiency by addressing bottlenecks, stock issues, and quality problems, coordinating teams as needed. What You Will Be Doing: • Use Warehouse Control Systems (WCS) and Production Control Systems (PCS) to ensure that machine statuses are active and correctly set up. • Identify and, when possible, anticipate conveyor jams. Notify engineering if needed and ensure balanced box flow. • Conduct regular checks on line throughput, analysing choke points, and taking remedial action to address any issues. • Effectively coordinate service and support functions to ensure the smooth operation of the facility. • Provide reports and analysis on machine performance Who You Are: • Proven experience in a manufacturing or distribution centre environment. • Proficiency in using computer software and systems. • Problem-solving skills and the agility to make decisions in a fast-paced production environment. • Excellent organisational and time management skills, with the ability to prioritise tasks and meet deadlines. • Strong communications skills and ability to positively collaborate across teams and departments. • Lead by example and ensure that HelloFresh DNA is at the forefront of everything we do. What you will get in return: ● 70% off HelloFresh or Green Chef boxes ● Company pension scheme ● Gym membership ● Bupa private medical insurance (including dental & family cover options) ● Mental health first aiders and an employee assistance programme ● Nursery support scheme ● Cycle to work scheme ● Group Life Assurance Location: The Windmill, Plot 8, Unit 2 Smart Parc, Spondon, Derby, DE217HW Shifts: Wednesday - Saturday rotating 3 months days to 3 months nights on 7-7 shift To be considered for this role, you’ll need to have the independent right to work in the UK, as we're unfortunately unable to provide sponsorship at this time.
