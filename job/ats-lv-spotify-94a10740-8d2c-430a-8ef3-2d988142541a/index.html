Senior Product Manager — Subscriptions (Freemium)

Spotify · London, Sweden

💰 ca. 500 000–610 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)HeltidDirekte fra arbeidsgiverFrist 10.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

What You'll Do • Own the product strategy and roadmap for Spotify's Freemium experience in India, identifying opportunities to grow Free and Premium audiences. • Shape how listeners discover, understand, and experience Premium, creating compelling reasons to consider and choose a subscription. • Lead the discovery, development, and launch of consumer-facing features, from early ideas and experimentation through rollout and iteration. • Design locally relevant Premium experiences and propositions that reflect consumer needs, market dynamics, and different paths to subscription. • Develop and run experiments that deepen our understanding of listener behavior, improve conversion, and create lasting value for subscribers. • Use funnel, cohort, behavioral, and market insights to identify opportunities, set measurable goals, and prioritize initiatives with the greatest potential impact. • Partner with engineering, design, research, marketing, partnerships, and regional teams to bring new experiences to life and adapt successful approaches across markets. • Communicate product direction, results, and trade-offs to senior stakeholders, connecting listener experiences with subscriber growth and business outcomes. Who You Are • You have a strong track record in product management, bringing consumer-facing products and features to market at scale. • You understand what makes a product valuable to consumers and how product experiences, positioning, and perceived value influence willingness to pay. • You're experienced in using experimentation, A/B testing, and data analysis to inform product decisions and improve customer experiences. • You can translate complex behavioral and market insights into clear product opportunities, priorities, and measurable outcomes. • You understand the challenges of building products for diverse audiences and markets, balancing global product consistency with local relevance. • You're a thoughtful collaborator who builds strong relationships across product, engineering, design, marketing, research, and regional teams. • You're comfortable navigating ambiguity, making informed trade-offs, and adjusting direction as you learn. • You communicate clearly, bring different perspectives into decision-making, and help teams align around shared goals. Where You'll Be • This role is based in London, UK or Stockholm, Sweden • We offer you the flexibility to work where you work best! There will be some in person meetings, but still allows for flexibility to work from home.
