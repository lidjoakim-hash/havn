You will ensure that team members have the knowledge, practical skills, and confidence required to operate safely, consistently, and effectively.   Working closely with Fleet Operations, Safety, Engineering, People, and other teams across the business, you will identify capability gaps and translate complex operational and technical information into engaging, practical learning experiences. You will establish consistent training standards across both regions while adapting programs to reflect local operational, cultural, language, and regulatory requirements.   We’re looking for someone who is organised, collaborative, and passionate about developing others: a pragmatic leader who is comfortable working in a fast-moving, safety-focused environment and can balance immediate training needs with a longer-term strategy. As our Fleet Operations function grows, you will develop scalable training programs and processes that can support additional geographies in the future.   🧠 Your day-to-day   - Develop and maintain a comprehensive training strategy and roadmap for Fleet Operations in the UK, aligned with organisational priorities, operational requirements, and future geographical expansion - Design, implement, and regularly update onboarding, technical, safety, compliance, and professional-development programs for Fleet Operations teams - Partner with Fleet Operations leaders and subject-matter experts to assess skills, identify capability gaps, and prioritise training requirements -
