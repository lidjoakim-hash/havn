Role Summary This role is a part of TRATON Group R&D, located in Södertälje, Sweden As Unit Head, you will lead and develop the team while securing high-quality deliveries across our development initiatives. You will empower your employees through clear priorities, effective planning, follow-up and continuous improvement. Together with your team, you will solve technical challenges from a customer-centric perspective. The unit contributes throughout the entire development process, from concept development and implementation to product follow-up. You will also play an important role in strengthening collaboration across functions, ensuring that the Service Market perspective is considered throughout the product development process. Job Responsibilities Lead and develop the unit, including recruitment, competence planning and securing the right capabilities for current and future needs. Secure high-quality deliveries through effective planning, prioritization, follow-up and quality assurance. Set clear expectations and performance targets, conduct performance reviews and provide continuous feedback and coaching. Drive competence development, creating individual development plans that support both employee growth and the unit's long-term capability needs. Actively contribute to the Section Management Team, collaborating across functions and helping develop the overall organization. Who You Are We welcome applicants from all backgrounds – your unique experience and perspectives is valuable to us. Engaging leader who coaches, develops and inspires employees, with a present, responsive and supportive leadership style. Strong communicator and collaborator, comfortable working cross-functionally with many stakeholders and building effective relationships. Analytical and self-motivated, with sound judgement and a big-picture mindset that creates clarity and direction for the team. Experience from the Service Market and/or automotive industry is an advantage, but not a requirement. This Is Us Within TRATON Group R&D, you are an important part of something bigger. Joining us means gaining access to the ins and outs of the entire transportation industry. You will lead a team of nine highly committed employees, including Development Engineers, Technical Writers and a Spare Parts Preparator. We develop the aftermarket support needed to troubleshoot, maintain and repair drivetrain systems and complete vehicles. Our work supports Service Technicians across the global TRATON service networks, where fast access to accurate service information is essential for customer uptime. Through close collaboration with development and service functions, we help make our products service-friendly, efficient and safe to work with. You will be part of a strong team environment that encourages knowledge sharing and close cooperation across functions. With a structured development plan and a wide range of training opportunities, TRATON Group R&D supports your professional growth both locally and internationally.
