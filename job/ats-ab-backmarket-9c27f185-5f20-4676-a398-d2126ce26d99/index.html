JOB DESCRIPTION: The APAC Marketing Enablement Intern will help enable global marketing to be deployed for Japan and Australia. You will bring greater visibility from You will build practical ways of working between central marketing teams and APAC, helping teams understand what is happening in each market, what needs to be adapted, and who is responsible for the next step. The role combines process design, planning, documentation, and cross-functional coordination. This is not primarily a translation or task-execution role. Your goal will be to create repeatable systems that make collaboration with APAC easier and more effective. YOUR MISSION (IF YOU ACCEPT IT): 🥇 - Create and maintain shared calendars and documentation that give central and regional teams visibility on APAC activity, milestones, market adaptations, and open decisions. - Define a repeatable model for Australia to assess, adapt, and reuse relevant UK marketing initiatives. - Clarify what information, timelines, responsibilities, and decisions are needed before global initiatives can be rolled out in APAC. - Improve recurring request and handoff processes between central marketing teams and APAC. - Turn ambiguous information into clear workflows, templates, action plans, and operating documentation. - Work with Marketing Operations, the APAC marketing lead, and stakeholders across global marketing to ensure the agreed ways of working are adopted. YOU ARE IN THE RIGHT PLACE IF: ⭐ - You have native-level Japanese and are fluent in English. - You have created or improved a process, workflow, tracker, calendar, template, or documentation system. - You write clearly and can turn complex or incomplete information into structured next steps. - You can recognize when a global idea may need adaptation for Japan or Australia, and explain why. - You are organized, detail-oriented, and comfortable following through across multiple stakeholders. - You ask useful clarifying questions, identify missing context, and raise risks constructively. - You are interested in marketing operations, international collaboration, or improving how teams work together. Previous marketing experience is welcome but not essential. We are interested in transferable experience from studies, student organizations, internships, projects, or other environments where you have built processes and coordinated work.
