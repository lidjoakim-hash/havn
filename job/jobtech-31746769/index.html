Hotel F&B Manager

Förvaltningsaktiebolaget Tegelbacken · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 450 000–550 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 31.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

A great breakfast to start the day. A seamless conference lunch or a thoughtful room service experience. At Sheraton Stockholm, these moments matter, and we’re looking for a Hotel F&B Manager who can bring out the best in the people who create them. This is a temporary position for at least 1,5 years covering a parental leave.   About Sheraton Stockholm Hotel Sheraton Stockholm has been bringing people together in the heart of the city since 1971. Following an extensive renovation, we’re entering a new chapter with renewed spaces and fresh energy. With Archer Hotel Management as our operator and Marriott International as our brand family, we combine an international outlook with a strong sense of place. Your Opportunity We are looking for an experienced and engaged Hotel F&B Manager for a temporary cover position lasting at least one year. You will lead the food and beverage operations connected to the hotel experience: breakfast, minibar, room service, club lounge, our smoothie bar Juicy Jane’s and conference & banqueting service. This is a role for someone who enjoys being where things happen. From a busy breakfast service to the final details of a conference dinner, you will work closely with your teams to deliver consistent quality, smooth operations and a warm, professional welcome. Your scope covers the hotel’s F&B services, with our restaurant and bar managed separately by our Head of Restaurant & Bar. Your Role You will take responsibility for daily operations, team leadership and financial performance within your areas. By planning staffing around business needs, following up on revenue, costs and productivity, and maintaining clear service routines, you will create the conditions for both excellent guest experiences and a well-run operation. As a visible and approachable leader, you will support your teams during service, set clear expectations and help people develop. Recruitment, onboarding, scheduling, training and performance reviews are part of your responsibility, alongside building a working environment where people feel involved and take pride in what they do. You will work closely with the Kitchen, Front Office, Housekeeping and the meetings and events teams to ensure that every detail comes together. Guest feedback will guide improvements, while food safety, hygiene, sustainability and a safe working environment will be part of your everyday priorities.   Who You Are You have experience leading food and beverage operations, preferably within a hotel, and understand what it takes to deliver across breakfast, in-room dining and conference service. You combine sound financial judgement with a practical understanding of daily operations. Above all, you know how to lead people. You give clear direction, offer constructive feedback and build trust through your actions. You are comfortable stepping into service when needed, making decisions when the pace picks up and helping your team stay focused and motivated. You bring structure, attention to detail and a genuine interest in hospitality. You understand that strong results come from thoughtful planning, close collaboration and a team that feels supported. What We Offer You will join an iconic Stockholm hotel at an exciting stage in its development, with the opportunity to make a direct impact on our hotel F&B operations and guest experience. We offer a supportive team environment, opportunities for learning and development, and the chance to work within an international hospitality network - all in the heart of Stockholm. Join Us Ready to lead from the floor, develop your team and make every guest experience count? We would love to hear from you. Apply to become our Hotel F&B Manager at Sheraton Stockholm Hotel. This is a temporary cover position for a minimum of 1,5 years.
