About the role Are you interested in combining your technical expertise in the automotive industry with a role where you can take ownership, coordinate development activities, and drive technical functions forward? We are looking for a Senior Sensing Function Owner to join our client in Gothenburg, an international technology company developing advanced active safety systems for the automotive industry. In this role, you will take ownership of a specific function within camera-based sensing systems and ADAS (Advanced Driver Assistance Systems). You will collaborate closely with software development teams, testing and validation engineers, and other technical stakeholders to ensure that the systems meet defined requirements and deliver the expected performance. This position is ideal for someone who enjoys working at the intersection of technology, coordination, and leadership, taking responsibility for technical deliveries without necessarily having formal personnel management responsibilities. The position starts as a consultancy assignment through Maxitech, with the intention of transitioning into direct employment with the client after an initial period. You are offered • A technically challenging position where you contribute to the development of next-generation active safety systems in the automotive industry. • An international working environment involving collaboration with development teams and customers across different regions. • Opportunities to develop your skills in technical leadership, systems engineering, and advanced automotive technology. Your responsibilities You will be responsible for driving and coordinating the development of a specific function within camera-based sensing systems. Acting as a technical function owner, you will play a central role in ensuring that requirements, development, testing, and delivery are aligned throughout the development process. You will coordinate multiple teams and technical disciplines, both locally and internationally. The role requires you to plan activities, prioritize resources, and manage technical challenges throughout the development lifecycle. An important part of your responsibility is to ensure that the function's performance is verified through testing, data collection, and analysis. You will work in a dynamic environment where several projects and product generations may run in parallel. This requires clear communication, strong coordination skills, and the ability to drive progress despite changing priorities. Your main responsibilities will include: • Take ownership of a specific technical function throughout the development lifecycle, from requirements definition and planning to verification, validation, and delivery. • Coordinate and lead activities involving software developers, testing and validation teams, data collection teams, and other technical stakeholders. • Analyze, define, and follow up on technical requirements, ensuring traceability between requirements, development activities, and test results. • Plan and follow up on testing activities, data collection, and performance verification, while driving troubleshooting and continuous improvements. • Manage technical issues, prioritize deviations, and ensure that the function meets agreed requirements, performance targets, and delivery timelines. Your profile We are looking for someone with a strong technical interest and experience in systems engineering, function development, or technical coordination. You understand how complex technical systems are developed, tested, and validated, and you enjoy taking responsibility for aligning different teams toward shared objectives.
