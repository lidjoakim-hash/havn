Veritaz is a leading IT staffing solutions provider in Sweden, committed to advancing individual careers and aiding employers in ensuring the perfect talent fit. With a proven track record of successful partnerships with top companies, we have rapidly grown our presence in the USA, Europe, and Sweden as a dependable and trusted resource within the IT industry. Job Overview Assignment Description We are looking for an experienced Project Manager to lead cross-functional business development initiatives involving IT development, process improvement, digitalisation, and change management. You will work within a Business Excellence team focused on operational development, Lean methodologies, process management, digital projects, and business system improvements. The role requires strong project management skills and the ability to drive initiatives across complex, global organisations while ensuring successful implementation and adoption. What You Will Work On Lead business development projects focused on digitalisation and process improvement. Manage cross-functional projects involving business and IT stakeholders. Report project progress to sponsors, steering committees, PMO, and management teams. Lead feasibility studies with relevant business stakeholders. Develop project plans and coordinate project execution. Prepare requirements specifications for IT tool development. Drive change management throughout the project lifecycle. Support implementation and adoption in the organisation together with project sponsors. Contribute to sharing best practices and improving working methods. What You Bring At least 5 years of relevant project management experience. Experience leading projects within large, global organisations. Strong experience in IT projects and process development initiatives. Experience driving change management in complex organisations. Excellent workshop facilitation skills and the ability to consolidate and present findings clearly. Excellent presentation and communication skills in both Swedish and English. Ability to engage and coordinate cross-functional stakeholders. Meritorious Knowledge of business systems such as Teamcenter, SAP, and Salesforce (SFDC). Experience with Lean methodologies and business excellence initiatives. Experience with digital transformation and organisational development.
