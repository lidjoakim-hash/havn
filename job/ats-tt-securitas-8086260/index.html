HR Business Partner – Denmark • Location: Copenhagen, Denmark • Travel: Occasional travel within Denmark • Reporting to: Managing Director, Denmark • Salary Range: 41,000 - 47,000 DKK/month Gross Purpose Securitas Technology Denmark is entering an exciting period of growth, integration and organisational development. Following the acquisition of CamVision and continued expansion of the Danish business, we are seeking an experienced HR Business Partner to build, strengthen and deliver our people agenda locally. This is a unique opportunity to shape a growing organisation whilst remaining close to the day-to-day reality of the business. Reporting directly to the Managing Director and working as part of the leadership team, you will act as the organisation's trusted HR partner, supporting approximately 100 employees and 12 leaders. This role combines strategic partnership with hands-on delivery. You will help leaders navigate employee relations matters, support organisational change, improve HR processes, strengthen engagement, develop talent and establish scalable ways of working that support future growth. You will play a key role in the continued integration of CamVision whilst helping to build and strengthen the HR foundations needed to support future growth. Alongside partnering leaders on day-to-day people matters, you will help establish a Shared Services model for transactional HR activities, ensuring managers and employees receive a consistent and high-quality HR experience. Integration & Organisational Development • Lead the people aspects of the CamVision integration, supporting leaders and employees through change. • Help align structures, processes, policies and ways of working across the combined organisation. • Drive cultural integration initiatives that support collaboration, engagement and business performance. • Identify opportunities to improve organisational effectiveness and employee experience.
