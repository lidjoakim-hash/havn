Before the detail, here's the challenge you'd help us solve. We build the embodied intelligence that moves real vehicles safely, and the ecosystem a billion machines will run on in the future. Very few people in AI can say this. Every role here, whatever the team, plugs into that. Here’s what this particular role covers. 🛠️ About our People Operations Team Help shape how we support and grow our teams in Germany. As our People Partner, Germany, you’ll combine deep local expertise with the opportunity to build and improve the People foundations of a fast-changing business. Reporting to Josh Gladwin, you’ll strategically partner with Fleet Operations Germany and be a visible, trusted point of contact for employees and managers across the country. You’ll work closely with our global People Partner team and local leaders to turn compliance requirements into practical processes while building a strong employee experience.   🧠 Your day-to-day Audit and strengthen German People policies, processes and regulatory compliance, owning improvements through implementation. Manage employee relations cases end to end, including probations, grievances and exits. Partner with Fleet Operations leaders and provide responsive local guidance to employees and managers across Germany. Collaborate with departmental People Partners to deliver consistent global practices that meet local requirements. Build trusted relationships with local leadership, communicate clearly and contribute to a visible, people-oriented culture.   🧩 What you’ll be working on Auditing German People processes, identifying compliance gaps and project-managing improvements from recommendation through rollout, including policies, regulatory reporting, health checks and exit processes. Owning employee relations cases end to end, including probations, probation exits, grievances and other exits, working with works councils. Acting as the visible first point of contact for employees and managers across Germany, including local benefits, time off and day-to-day guidance. Strategically partnering with Fleet Operations Germany and operational stakeholders. Working with departmental People Partners on employee relations and process rollouts, and influencing leaders through clear individual and group communications.   🙌 You should apply if Deep practical expertise in Germany’s employment, regulatory and People compliance landscape. End-to-end employee relations experience, including exits, probations and grievances, plus experience working with a works council. A track record of independently building or improving People processes in a fast-paced, changing organisation. Experience partnering with operational stakeholders and influencing leaders. Fluent written and spoken German, with English sufficient for effective collaboration with global colleagues. An approachable, visible partnering style and the ability to attend the office 2–3 days per week, ideally 3.   🌱 Not ticking every box? That’s totally okay! If you’re passionate about autonomy and keen to learn, we encourage you to apply even if you don’t meet every requirement.   More about Wayve: 🚀 Wayve is building the leading AI platform for autonomous driving. We are pioneering an end to end AI approach that enables vehicles to learn directly from real world experience, developing the ability to adapt, generalise and improve at scale. Instead of relying on hand coded rules or pre mapped environments, our AI Driver learns to drive by understanding the world around it. The result is technology that navigates complex urban environments with intelligence, precision and natural flow, unlocking meaningful advances in both safety and efficiency. We believe autonomy represents a once in a generation transformation in how people and goods move, comparable to the shift from horses to cars, and from human driven vehicles to intelligent machines. Our ambition is to make autonomy universal. Wayve’s mapless
