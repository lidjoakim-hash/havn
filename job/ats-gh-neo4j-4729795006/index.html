Our Vision: At Neo4j, we have always strived to help the world make sense of data. The Role: Neo4j Customer Support Operations is a highly effective part of Neo4j’s Field Engineering organisation and provides a key foundation for the long-term success and satisfaction of our growing customer base. Neo4j Customer Support Operations serves as an interface between traditional Customer Support and Product Engineering teams to develop subject matter expertise within Customer Support, as well as developing highly-customised tools and processes to accelerate and enhance our overall service delivery. For a number of years, we have partnered with our Product Engineering Team to deliver a series of bespoke applications that are now used across the company on a daily basis as part of our collective work in supporting the global Neo4j customer base. The Customer Support Operations Team is responsible for the development and maintenance of many support and engineering applications, including our flagship Neo4j CODEX application. (Not related to OpenAI CODEX!) CODEX started as a Customer Support project over 4 years ago, with a lot of development through subsequent summer engineering internships over the last 4 years; and has since grown in functionality and scope to be a suite of applications forming the technical diagnostics hub for all of our Support, Field Engineering, and Product Engineering Teams. Throughout the history of Customer Support Operations and Product Engineering collaborating on this summer engineering internship programme, we have always delivered systems that have been transformative and admired across the company. We are immensely proud of welcoming some of our past interns back as full time, permanent employees who have gone on to be extremely successful and valuable engineers within Product Engineering developing our core graph analytics and database platform used and trusted around the world.
