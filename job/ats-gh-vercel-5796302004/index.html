About Vercel: Vercel is the agentic infrastructure company, freeing people and agents to ship what's next. For more than a decade we've helped builders move from idea to production with speed, security, and exceptional developer experience. Now we're scaling our products for both agents and people to ship and run software, built in the open and trusted by OpenAI, PayPal, Ramp, Supreme, and millions of developers worldwide. About the role As a Solutions Architect on the Field Engineering team, you'll be the trusted technical advisor to enterprise customers across pre-sales and post-sales, for teams running AI and web workloads on Vercel. You'll qualify technical fit, design future-state architectures, and run evaluations and proofs of concept before the deal, then guide adoption as lead architect after it, bringing in Professional Services, Developer Success, Product, and Engineering when customers need them. This is a hybrid role based in London, with three days a week in the office. What you'll do • Lead technical discovery with Sales, qualify use cases, and design target architectures for AI agents and web applications on Vercel • Own proofs of concept (POCs) and pilots end to end: set success criteria, guide implementation, and document outcomes for executive buyers • Build and deliver tailored demos, technical presentations, and workshops for developer and executive audiences • Act as lead architect post-sale for strategic accounts, guiding onboarding, reference implementations, and migrations, and tuning performance with Core Web Vitals and observability data • Run customer security, compliance, and architecture reviews, and feed recurring field gaps back to Product and Engineering What you need • 5+ years of experience in a customer-facing technical role such as solutions architect, sales engineer, or platform architect, working with web or cloud platforms • Hands-on JavaScript and TypeScript experience, including reviewing production application code built with Next.js, React, or another modern framework • Designed enterprise architectures covering web performance, caching, networking, and security on AWS, Microsoft Azure, or Google Cloud Platform (GCP) • Ran technical evaluations or POCs on enterprise deals with both executive and developer stakeholder Bonus if you • Built and shipped an AI-powered application or agent, for example with the AI SDK • Led a large-scale migration to a cloud-native or composable architecture (headless CMS, commerce, identity) • Supported customer security reviews against SOC 2, PCI DSS, or HIPAA Benefits: • Competitive compensation package, including equity. • Inclusive Healthcare Package. • Learn and Grow - we provide mentorship and send you to events that help you build your network and skills. • Flexible Time Off. • We will provide you the gear you need to do your role, and a WFH budget for you to outfit your space as needed.
