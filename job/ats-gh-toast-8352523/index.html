A day in the life The API Integrations Expert (Bilingual English/French) is an Associate level, salaried position that will contribute to the success of the Toast Customer Care Integrations team. As an API Integrations Expert (Bilingual English/French), you will assess customer needs, manage expectations, and respond effectively to resolve escalations quickly. The ideal candidate will be responsible for managing relationships with new and existing partners and customers. You will manage escalated technical and application support requests for customers who are using Toast Integrations and Toast APIs.
