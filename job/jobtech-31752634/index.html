Digital Design Lead - Digital Journey

H & M Hennes & Mauritz GBC AB · Stockholm, Sweden

Job Description WHAT YOU’LL DO As Design Lead within Digital Journey, you will shape and manage the end-to-end customer journeys across H&M’s digital channels. You will turn customer insights, user and commercial data into a clear view of the holistic experience, shared priorities across areas and product teams, and measurable outcomes. You will connect the product, design, tech, marketing, retail tech and customer service teams that shape every step of the journey, and manage journeys as living systems. Establish and drive the vision, goals, roadmap and priorities for customer journeys in line with H&M’s customer and business strategy. Prioritise improvements to the customer journey and be accountable for journey maps and service blueprints developed together with relevant roles. Combine customer feedback, research, behavioural data and commercial performance to identify pain points and opportunity spaces, and prioritise improvements with evidence. Support the definition of journey metrics and KPIs with relevant stakeholders, linking customer outcomes to business value. Run effective journey governance with clear roles, regular check-ins and early resolution of overlapping or conflicting initiatives. Coordinate product, design, tech, marketing, retail tech and customer service teams so that changes across channels fit together. Work with data and tech teams to connect data and tools, enabling continuous monitoring of journey performance. Partner with design and research to test and iterate solutions, and with orchestration teams to deliver more personalised customer interactions. Build journey-management capability through shared practices and clear communication. Demonstrate the business value of journey improvements by connecting them to outcomes such as revenue, churn and repeat purchase. Align each product team’s contribution with the holistic customer journey and support personalisation of the H&M digital experience. Work closely with Product Managers and Area Business Managers on this. WHO YOU’LL WORK WITH You will be part of Digital Product Design and work broadly work across product, digital design, technology, marketing, retail tech, customer service, data, analytics and research. The role requires close partnership with senior stakeholders and teams across H&M to create shared direction, resolve dependencies and ensure that improvements across channels deliver one coherent customer experience. You will report to the Journey Manager and, while your organisational home is Design, your scope and collaboration will span the functions that shape the end-to-end customer journey. WHO YOU ARE We are looking for people with… A background within in customer experience, service design or product management. Experience from other relevant disciplines, such as marketing, technology or data and analytics, is a plus, ideally within a large international organisation. Proven experience managing end-to-end customer journeys or services across multiple channels, touchpoints and teams. Experience building journey-management capability in organisations through coaching, training, playbooks, frameworks, practices or communities. Strong stakeholder management skills and the ability to influence and align product, technology, marketing and other teams without direct authority. Hands-on experience in journey mapping and service blueprinting, ideally across both digital and physical touchpoints. Experience combining qualitative research, journey analytics, behavioural data, commercial data and voice-of-customer insight. Experience defining metrics that connect customer experience with business results such as revenue and churn. Experience with journey-management tools, data integration, governance and ways of working across siloed teams in agile organisations.
