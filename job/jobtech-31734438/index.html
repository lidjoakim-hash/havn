Are you a driven and commercially minded sales professional with experience in developing customer relationships and identifying new business opportunities? We are now looking for an Account Manager to drive the growth of our clients business across Europe. In this role, you will combine strategic sales development with customer relationship management, market analysis and close collaboration with internal teams. About the position We at Perido are now looking for an Account Manager for our client. The position is based in Eskilstuna. Job responsibilities Develop and execute sales strategies for separator products in the European region, and achieve annual sales targets and collection tasks Maintain existing customer relationships, deeply explore customer needs, and promote long-term strategic cooperation Track trends in the industry, the competitive landscape, and customer dynamics, and regularly produce market analysis reports Coordinate internal resources (product, technical, supply chain, customer service, etc.) to ensure timely response to and delivery of customer requirements Participate in business negotiations, contract signing, and price management to ensure project profitability and risk control Maintain close collaboration with headquarters and the global sales team to promote cross-regional business synergy You will also be responsible for managing customer quotations, pricing and discounts within the authorized framework, as well as negotiating and signing sales contracts within your delegated authority. Your characteristics We are looking for someone who is proactive, results-oriented and commercially driven, with strong communication and negotiation skills. You are confident in building and maintaining long-term customer relationships and have the ability to understand customer needs and identify business opportunities. You are also analytical, structured and able to work independently while coordinating effectively with different teams across the organization. Does this sound like an exciting opportunity? We look forward to reading your application! Qualifications: Education/qualification: Bachelor's degree or above, with preference for majors such as materials science, chemistry, electrical engineering, marketing, and related fields Relevant experience: More than 3 years of sales experience, including at least 1 year of sales experience in the lithium battery industry (separator, cathode/anode materials, electrolyte, cells, or equipment are all acceptable) Technical/functional competence: Familiarity with lithium battery separator products, technology trends, and the European battery market is preferred Leadership/behavioral competence: Excellent business negotiation, customer development, and project management skills Language requirements: Fluent in English/German/Spanish/French, which can be used as working languages Contract type and hours Full-time, two-year consulting assignment.
