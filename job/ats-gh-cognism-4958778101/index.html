About Cognism Cognism is one of Europe's leading B2B Sales Intelligence platforms. Our mission is to help revenue teams find, enrich and engage the right customers through trusted, compliant and high-quality data. The Opportunity This is not a traditional Staff Engineer position. You will become one of the founding senior technical leaders responsible for shaping the architecture, engineering standards and technical culture of the Warsaw Hub.
