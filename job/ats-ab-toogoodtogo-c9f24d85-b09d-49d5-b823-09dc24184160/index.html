At Too Good To Go, we have an ambitious mission: to inspire and empower everyone to fight food waste together.   More than 1/3 of all food produced in the world is wasted. And that has a huge impact on the health of our planet. 10% of greenhouse gas emissions come from food waste and loss.   Through our marketplace app, we connect businesses that have unsold, surplus food, with consumers who can buy and enjoy it at ⅓ of retail value. We are a certified B Corporation with a mission to empower everyone to take action against food waste. Alongside our marketplace app, we create educational tools, explore new business solutions, and influence legislation to help reduce food waste.   We’re growing fast: Our community of 120 million registered users and 180,000 active partners across 20 countries, have together already saved over 500 million meals from going to waste   In 2023, Too Good To Go was recognised on Fortune's acclaimed 'Change the World' list, named as one of Deloitte's 'Best Managed Companies' and won a coveted 'Cultural Impact Award' at the annual Apple App Store Awards.   Your mission As Head of Group Financial Controlling, reporting directly to the Director Group Accounting, you will serve as the operational anchor and strategic engine behind the Group’s financial health, compliance, and systems transformation. In this role, you will lead a team covering both group and entity-level controlling, overseeing end-to-end month-end closing, IFRS compliance, local annual reporting and tax accounting in close cooperation with Group Tax and Legal teams.   Your core performance objectives center on maintaining robust group policies, streamlining Chart of Account and cash management processes, ensuring flawless group audit readiness, and enabling seamless international expansion.   Beyond day-to-day governance, you will actively champion innovation, contributing to global system implementations and our overall Finance AI journey. By providing vision, removing operational roadblocks, and empowering your team, you will establish best-in-class financial controlling processes that drive cross functional alignment and sustainable business growth.   As Head of Group Financial Controlling, reporting directly to the Director Group Accounting you will serve as a key contributor in securing the Group’s financial health, compliance and systems transformation.
