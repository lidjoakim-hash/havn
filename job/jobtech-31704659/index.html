About the Role As a Senior System Verification Engineer for Connectivity, you will be part of a collaborative and enthusiastic team working cross-functionally to verify the complete connected system, from onboard electronics and communication networks to cloud services, remote diagnostics, and software updates. Working in an agile environment, you will drive end-to-end connectivity verification and take technical ownership of verification strategies, system and integration testing, test automation, and complex troubleshooting. Through close collaboration across Volvo CE and Volvo Group, you will influence technical solutions, strengthen verification practices, and help ensure robust, secure, and reliable connectivity for our customers. If you are excited about solving complex technical challenges and seeing the real-world impact of your engineering, this could be the role for you. Who are you? You enjoy solving complex, real-world engineering problems and are curious about how connected technologies can make machines smarter, safer, and more productive. You combine a system-level mindset with hands-on technical capability and enjoy understanding how distinct parts of a connected system work together. You value collaboration, share ideas openly and take ownership of your work. You enjoy working in cross-functional and international teams, learning from others, and contributing your own expertise. You communicate effectively in English and are comfortable taking initiative and exploring innovative approaches to technical challenges. What will make you successful in this role: Strong Experience in connectivity, embedded systems, telematics, or system verification, preferably within automotive, construction equipment or other complex embedded systems. Good understanding of communication and connectivity technologies such as CAN, Ethernet, TCP/IP, cellular networks, Wi-Fi & machine-to-cloud communication. Hands-on experience with system and integration testing, troubleshooting and root-cause analysis across hardware and software. Knowledge of OTA/Remote Software Download, remote diagnostics and connected services is highly valued. Experience with test automation, scripting, and CI/CD-based verification, with the ability to identify opportunities for increased automation. Understanding of cybersecurity principles, diagnostics, and communication protocols for connected systems Ability to work across ECUs, machines, connectivity infrastructure, and cloud/back-office systems with an end-to-end system perspective. A collaborative mindset, strong technical curiosity, and the ability to take ownership and solve complex system-level problems. What’s in it for you? The role is based on-site at our Technology Center in Eskilstuna, where you will have direct access to our machines, test rigs, and modern verification facilities. This gives you the opportunity to test solutions in real environments and collaborate closely with development teams and other stakeholders.
