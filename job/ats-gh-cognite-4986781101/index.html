How you’ll demonstrate Ownership We're looking for a Senior AI native designer for our Cognite Oslo team to invent, prototype, and scale next-generation product experiences on top of Cognite Data Fusion (CDF). As a Senior Product Designer, you’ll use advanced AI tools and methods to move from insight → concept → working experience with high speed and clarity. You’ll partner closely with product, engineering, strategy, and customers to uncover unmet needs, validate hypotheses, and turn ambiguous problems into elegant, testable, data-aware solutions. You’re hands-on, experimental, and fluent in AI— converting user research to compelling prototypes, and shaping trustworthy, human-in-the-loop experiences that deliver business impact. The Impact you bring to Cognite Product Design Excellence • Prototype with real data and real models: connect to mock data or internal APIs; validate flows under realistic latency, streaming, and cost constraints. • Design for probabilistic systems: author patterns for imperfect answers, disagreement handling, correction, and escalation to humans-in-the-loop. • Express ideas at multiple fidelities: from prompt-generated concept boards to interactive agents, micro-workflows, and production-ready UI. • Partner with business designers and product strategists to evaluate desirability, feasibility, and viability; shape experience KPIs and success metrics. • Tell the story through compelling narratives, visuals, and prototypes that make the future feel obvious—and shippable. • Lead design sessions and facilitate cross-functional workshops Design Execution & Ownership • Own outcomes end-to-end from discovery to shipped experience: research plans, flows, systems, content, and component-level detail. • Operationalize AI in the UX: define tool/agent contracts, error states, monitoring hooks, and feedback loops to continuously improve quality and cost. • Collaborate tightly with engineering to scope increments, derisk hard problems early, and land details with high craft. • Continuously validate via user research, experiment design, and rapid iteration; make evidence-based tradeoffs across user value, reliability, and cost. Required Qualifications • 7+ years of product design experience • Fluency with AI-native workflows: prompt design, evaluation, and chaining; agents/tools; RAG; model/runtime constraints (latency, token/cost budgets). • Hands-on prototyping with real data (e.g., connecting prototypes to APIs/SDKs, lightweight code, or no/low-code tools) to validate feasibility and UX. • Strong user research skills: plan and run studies, synthesize qualitative & quantitative signals into clear insights and direction. • Portfolio demonstrating end-to-end ownership of complex, data-heavy or AI-powered experiences, including trust/safety and human-in-the-loop patterns. • Excellent collaboration & communication: proactive stakeholder management, clear storytelling, and comfort presenting to executives and customers.
