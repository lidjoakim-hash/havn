At Volvo Group, you will have the opportunity to turn complex engineering challenges into technology that makes a real difference on the road. You will join the Driver Support Functions team within Volvo Trucks Technology & Industrial Division in Gothenburg. We develop Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) throughout the entire product life cycle, from early concepts and requirements to software development, verification, vehicle integration and product maintenance. Our work includes functions such as Intelligent Speed Assist, glare-free lighting, driver distraction, drowsiness warnings, and pilot assistance. We collaborate closely across software, systems and verification, combining technical expertise with an agile and supportive team environment. We are now looking for a Senior ADAS Function Developer who brings deep software expertise, strong technical judgement and a passion for solving complex engineering challenges. This is an opportunity to take technical ownership, influence software solutions and contribute to the next generation of driver support functions across Volvo Group's global truck portfolio.   What you will do In this role, you will take ownership of end-to-end ADAS function deliveries, working across system development, software implementation, integration and verification. With a strong focus on embedded software development, you will play an important role in turning functional requirements into reliable, high-quality solutions. Your responsibilities will include: Develop ADAS functions and algorithms for advanced driver support applications. Translate customer, regulatory and system requirements into use cases, software designs and testable solutions. Design, implement and integrate embedded software using C++ in an Adaptive AUTOSAR environment. Contribute to software architecture, interfaces and integration on vehicle compute platforms. Define signals, interfaces, KPIs and evaluation methods to assess and improve function performance. Plan and support verification through simulation, SIL, HIL, test rigs and vehicle testing, analyzing results and resolving issues. Investigate complex technical challenges across software and system boundaries, contributing to robust integration and product maintenance. Collaborate with cross-functional engineering teams and contribute to continuous improvement, technical discussions and knowledge sharing.   Who are you? You are an experienced software engineer who enjoys solving complex technical problems and understanding how individual components contribute to a complete vehicle function. You combine hands-on technical expertise with a structured, analytical approach and are comfortable taking ownership of challenging engineering tasks. You thrive when tackling technically demanding software challenges, whether that means investigating complex system behavior, identifying root causes or developing robust solutions at code level.
