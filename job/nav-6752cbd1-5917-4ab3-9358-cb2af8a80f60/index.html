You can find more information about working at NTNU and the application process here.       Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v&#61;_KHQjc4ndas&t&#61;41sAbout the position As AI systems become increasingly available, we encounter more and more content that has been generated. Examples include text, images, audio, videos, as well as software. In some instances, it is crucial to be able to identify content that had been generated. Financial institutions need a solid basis to assess loan applications. Universities need to assure that submitted essays are the work of the student. Recruiters need to be able to tell whether an application is authentic. The position aims to develop methods to help identify generated content. Initially, the candidate will need to get a firm grip of the state of the art already available to identify AI-generated content. Later, the candidate conducts experiments with different models and methodologies to indicate the presence or absence of generated content. We plan to publish the results in peer reviewed conferences or journals. Your immediate leader will be the head of the DART unit. Are you ready to take your research career to the next level? We offer an exciting 3-year postdoctoral position that gives you the opportunity to develop a strong profile as researcher. You will also gain valuable experience that might be relevant for your further career. The purpose of a postdoctoral position is to build up a researcher profile that qualifies for a position as associate professor. The position may also offer additional skills that are relevant to the postdoctoral fellow's future career. Apply for this position to become part of our research environment and take the next step in your career with us. About the project  The position is associated with the AURA project. The project aims to advance the understanding in, and the development of AI to improve the region. The position is part of the work package on AI and safety. AI-generated can be used to mislead organizations. For instance, banks need to verify documents in the process of approving loans, companies need to verify the authenticity of applications, and municipalities need to authenticate communication with citizens. Duties of the position • Carry out high-quality research within the area of the position. • Deploy your work in collaboration with industrial partners of the region (if relevant). • Produce academic publications and popular science dissemination. • Participate in the work associated with the AURA project . • Mentoring MSc students, helping them with their research projects, and advising on methodology and academic writing. • Unless proficient in a Scandinavian language, the candidate will attend Norwegian courses.
