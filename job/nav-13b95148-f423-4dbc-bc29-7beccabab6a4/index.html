Do you have a passion for Italian gelato? Vito’s Gelateria in Heggedal Asker is looking for a creative, quality-minded gelato maker who enjoys working with great ingredients Hva vi ser etter We're looking for someone who is creative, reliable and takes pride in their work. Vi tilbyr A friendly workplace, varied tasks and the opportunity to be creative and develop new flavours.
