The Product Management team drives Asana's product strategy and execution, translating customer needs and opportunities into a compelling roadmap and working cross-functionally to deliver impactful solutions for team productivity and collaboration. We're helping to define the Work Management category and inventing new ways for teams to work more efficiently together and see the larger purpose behind their work. Our team works directly with Engineering, Design, Experience Research, and Data Science, as well as with Sales, Support, and Marketing to ship great products. We're looking for a Staff Product Manager to join the team that keeps complex, interdependent work accurate as it changes. This team owns tasks and how they connect and stay in sync — including subtasks, dependencies, milestones, and the approvals that keep changes safe — across the views people plan from like the Task Pane, Board, Timeline, Gantt, and Project Overview. These mechanics are what let enterprise teams model their most complex initiatives directly in Asana, so decisions here carry real revenue and competitive weight. With Asana's recent launch of Agentic Work Management, where AI Teammates work alongside people on the same plan, this PM will help pioneer this new category — owning the strategy for keeping scheduling and task relationships trustworthy as agents, not just people, propose and make changes. This role is based in our Warsaw office with an office-centric hybrid schedule. The standard in-office days are Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday. Most Asanas have the option to work from home on Wednesdays and Fridays. If you're interviewing for this role, your recruiter will share more about the in-office requirements.
