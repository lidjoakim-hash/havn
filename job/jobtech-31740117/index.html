Shape smarter, more efficient procurement processes at Seco. As Procurement Excellence Lead, you connect business needs, digital tools and continuous improvement to strengthen our procurement value flow and support reliable, sustainable operations. About the job You’re part of the Procurement department, working with specialists to review, improve, and automate procurement processes. By aligning Seco’s needs with Sandvik Group guidelines, you help develop processes, KPIs, and tools that support effective operations and sustainability goals. Job responsibilities Collaborate with IT and cross-functional teams to develop efficient procurement solutions. Ensure processes comply with relevant policies, guidelines, and procedures. Maintain clear, up-to-date process design, and documentation. Identify necessary process changes and manage exceptions that affect workflows. Establish measurements, support stakeholders, and oversee relevant training. Location & flexibility The location for this position is flexible, preferably close to one of our Seco sites. Your profile We’re looking for someone with business acumen and a degree in Business, Economy, engineering, or similar line of education. You have previous experience working with tools such as ARIS, SharePoint, and Power BI. It’s beneficial to have experience in Lean principles along with knowledge and understanding of ERP systems, especially M3. Operating in a global environment requires proficiency in English, both verbally and in writing. With an analytical mindset and structured ways of working, you have an eye for detail and enjoy solving complex problems. You communicate clearly, build productive relationships, adapt to change, and continue working toward meaningful results. The Team You join a team of specialists within Procurement and collaborate closely with IT and cross-functional stakeholders. Together, the team improves the procurement value flow and supports consistent ways of working. Our Seco culture   At Seco, we're united by a spirit of collaboration and a shared curiosity to learn and grow. We take responsibility for our actions, stay focused on our customers, and believe in winning together. For us, it’s also clear that our diversities form an amazing foundation for achieving great results. Curious about our workplace and benefits? Read more on our website.
