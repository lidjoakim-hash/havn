Fast Stream Growth Marketing Programme About the role: What's in the Box As AI is rapidly changing the the growth marketing landscape, HelloFresh is opening an exclusive rotational program, hiring highly talented and high potential individuals, and growing them into AI-native growth marketing professionals. Over 18 months, you will complete three rotations: owning and running a live paid channel and also being involved in wider strategic marketing topics: In this role, you will have autonomy and budget ownership; take on work with visible commercial impact, and help shape the AI-enabled ways of working, that the wider marketing organisation will adopt. You will be based in Berlin, but will also have the opportunity to gain experience from our team in New York City, on short-term assignments. What you'll do: The Recipe • Deliver priority projects: Take on scoped projects for host teams, such as a Performance marketing blueprint definition, a GEO pilot or an incrementality measurement project, from problem definition through design and to roll-out. • Run a live channel: Own campaigns, channel budgets and performance during your performance-management rotation, and make decisions that move the numbers and impact the P&L. • Apply and expand the AI growth playbook: Use AI to draft, analyse and automate real marketing work, and progress from everyday tools to custom agents, automated workflows and AI-powered dashboards. • Build a wide network: Work across Global Marketing and its partner teams, learn from expert teams, and build relationships with mentors and leaders across the business. • Contribute to group priorities: Support top-priority strategic projects at group level and share what you learn with host teams and the wider marketing organisation. What you'll bring: The Ingredients • Professional experience: Experience in a fast-paced environment, with a track record of owning work from problem definition through to delivery. • Graduate qualification: An MBA or Master's degree in business, engineering or data from top tier school. • Working AI fluency: Confident use of AI tools to draft, summarise and analyse, and the motivation to build custom agents, automated workflows and AI-powered dashboards. • Initiative and fast learning: An entrepreneurial mindset, a willingness to take initiative, and the adaptability to learn quickly as technology and ways of working change. You take ownership early and are comfortable challenging the status quo. • Structured problem-solving: The ability to break down complex, ambiguous problems and execute against a clear plan. • Analytical rigour: Comfort with data and numbers, and experience in turning analysis into decisions. • Stakeholder communication: Clear communication and a track record of influencing senior stakeholders, with a genuine interest in marketing and top-line strategy. Above all, we are looking for people who will make HelloFresh better. There are many different ways of developing skills, and we love diverse experiences! So even if you don't “tick all the boxes” but think you'd thrive in this role, we would really like to learn more about you.
