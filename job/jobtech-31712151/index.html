Job Description We are looking for an experienced Scrum Master to join a new development project within Interior Development, focusing on seats. The Scrum Master will lead a cross-functional development team, ensuring alignment towards project goals while driving planning, execution, and delivery according to agile principles. The role includes responsibility for coordinating team activities, supporting collaboration across stakeholders, and monitoring project finances. This includes following up and reporting on investments, material costs, and development costs to project management. Responsibilities Lead and support the team towards successful delivery of project objectives. Facilitate and promote agile ways of working. Plan, coordinate, and follow up on team activities and milestones. Ensure effective collaboration between cross-functional stakeholders. Monitor and report project financial status. Follow up investments, material costs, and development costs. Provide regular status updates and financial reporting to project management. Identify and manage risks, dependencies, and obstacles impacting project progress. Drive stakeholder alignment through effective communication and negotiation. Support continuous improvement within the team and development process. Education Must Have Bachelor's or Master's degree in Mechanical Engineering or equivalent technical field. Experience Must Have Minimum 4 years of experience as Project Leader, Project Manager, Scrum Master, or equivalent leadership role. Proven experience working in agile development environments. Experience leading and coordinating cross-functional teams. Experience with project planning, execution, and follow-up. Experience managing and reporting project costs and financials. Professional proficiency in spoken and written English and Swedish Nice to Have Experience from automotive product development. Experience within interior systems and/or seat development. Experience in mechanical engineering and product development. Experience working in large international organizations.
