Senior Software Engineer - Vitec BidTheatre

Vitec BidTheatre builds software and solutions for data-driven online marketing. Our platform enables media agencies, brands and other marketing professionals to manage and deliver programmatic advertising campaigns. We are looking for a Senior Software Engineer to join our small engineering team in Stockholm. The position offers a high degree of independence, technical ownership and the opportunity to influence the continued development of our platform. About the role As a member of our engineering team, you will work across our technology stack, developing and maintaining the applications and services that power our advertising platform. A key part of your role will be taking the lead in the continued development of BidTheatre Studio, our web application used by clients and internal teams to manage digital advertising campaigns. You will have significant influence over frontend architecture, technical decisions and development practices. Beyond the frontend, you will also be actively involved in developing and maintaining our backend systems, including APIs, business logic and data processing. We are looking for someone with solid experience in both frontend and backend development who is comfortable taking responsibility across the entire application stack. Our frontend uses React, TypeScript and Redux. Our backend is primarily Java-based, with technologies such as SQL, Redis, Aerospike, Kafka and Spark supporting our data-intensive platform. We embrace modern development practices, including AI-assisted coding, and encourage experimentation with tools that improve productivity and code quality. We believe you are an experienced and versatile developer who enjoys understanding how systems work as a whole. You are comfortable taking ownership of complex tasks, making technical decisions and working independently, while collaborating closely with your colleagues. Requirements 5+ years of professional software development experience Strong experience with React, TypeScript and modern frontend development Solid professional experience in backend development using Java or comparable technologies such as C#/.NET or Kotlin Ability and willingness to work with Java-based backend systems Good understanding of SQL and relational databases Experience developing and maintaining larger web applications Good understanding of software architecture, performance and maintainability Ability to independently drive technical decisions and development Experience with Git, testing and modern development practices Good written and spoken English skills Nice to have Experience with Redux Toolkit, RTK Query or Redux Saga Experience with distributed or high-volume systems Experience with AI-assisted development tools such as Codex, Claude Code or similar Interest in UX/UI design Experience with ad tech or programmatic advertising Degree in Computer Science Fluency in Swedish What we offer You will be part of a small, experienced engineering team where your contributions make a real difference. You will have the freedom to take ownership of your work, influence technical decisions and contribute across different parts of a complex platform. Our systems handle large volumes of data and real-time transactions, offering a broad range of technical challenges in a profitable and established software business. Application: The application deadline is 13th of December 2026. We encourage you to submit your application as soon as possible, as applications are reviewed and interviews are conducted on an ongoing basis. If you have any questions about the position, please feel free to contact Andreas Green at Andreas.Green@vitecsoftware.com. Location: The position is based at our office in Stockholm.
