Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team Our EMEA Marketing team is a dynamic, fast-growing group that loves turning customers into enthusiastic Stripe advocates. We work hand-in-hand with our Sales team to build demand, help users get the most out of our products, and craft marketing plans that truly resonate with local audiences. We have a lot still to build, and we’re tackling it as a diverse, international team that thrives on collaboration, bold innovation, and genuine curiosity. If you love to learn and want to make a meaningful impact, we’d love to have you join us. What you’ll do As a senior individual contributor, you’ll align regional and global marketing teams around shared priorities for EMEA Velocity, secure investment to deliver them, and balance global strategy with regional needs. You’ll directly lead high-priority initiatives across the region. You’ll shape how Stripe grows its Velocity segment across EMEA, with a particular focus on winning and expanding relationships with priority commercial accounts. You’ll combine regional strategy with hands-on field marketing, building programs that generate demand and accelerate pipeline across different markets. As the primary marketing partner on the EMEA Velocity leadership team, you’ll own the regional marketing strategy and investment plan. You’ll work closely with Sales leadership to identify the biggest opportunities across markets, decide where marketing can make the greatest difference, and turn those priorities into measurable business results. You’ll lead account-based programs, executive engagement, and market-relevant campaigns for priority commercial accounts, bringing together regional and global teams to deliver them. Your programs will engage senior decision makers and operational leaders on reducing developer complexity, improving conversion, and increasing the return on their financial infrastructure. You’ll also partner with Demand Generation on scaled digital demand campaigns and programs, connecting those efforts with regional business priorities. As a senior individual contributor, you’ll lead through influence across markets and teams, aligning Sales, Demand Generation, Product Marketing, and Global Campaigns around a shared plan. You’ll balance regional priorities with local customer needs, use results to guide investment, and build approaches that can scale across EMEA. You’ll help shape the marketing approach and directly lead the initiatives where regional context and ownership matter most.
