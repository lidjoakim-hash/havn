At Tetra Pak we commit to making food safe and available, everywhere; and we protect what's good – protecting food, protecting people, and protecting the planet. By doing so we touch millions of people's lives every day. And we need people like you to make it happen. We empower you to reach your potential with opportunities to make an impact to be proud of – for food, people and the planet. Job Summary Start your career development and become Tetra Pak’s next Future Talent! We are looking for highly motivated newly graduates to join us in 2027! Future Talent Programme is a Trainee Programme within Tetra Pak. We offer a permanent position from start in September 2027 at our site in Lund. You will onboard an individually designed development programme for 18 months connected to a specific position and the Tetra Pak organization. During the programme you will participate in trainings and job rotations throughout various parts of Tetra Pak to explore your potential and grow as a person. As a Trainee here with us, you will get to know our entire business and collaborate with colleagues all over the world. And this is just the beginning of your Tetra Pak career! In Lamination & Web Handling, we develop and support converting equipment that enables the production of today’s and tomorrow’s packaging materials. Our work focuses on coating processes, including extrusion coating, with a strong emphasis on sustainability. You will be part of this organisation throughout the programme and, on completion, work as a Development Engineer in one of our agile teams. Visit https://www.tetrapak.com/about-tetra-pak/careers/future-talent and read more about Tetra Pak Future Talent Programme! This position belongs to the Leadership Track. You will be based in Lund, Sweden but you will work in a global arena. What you will do As our new Future Talent – Lamination & Web handling you will: Collaborate with fellow engineers in an agile team to develop and maintain converting equipment and processes. Help ensure production solutions meet the requirements of the new packaging materials we develop. Share your knowledge to support internal and external stakeholders. Partner with suppliers and colleagues in other organisations to exchange knowledge and apply it where it makes a difference. Support our converting factories by applying engineering knowledge to achieve efficient, high-quality production. We believe you have A Master’s Degree in Mechanical Engineering or Engineering Physics (Graduated 2026-2027) Experience in the processing industry or equipment development is welcome, but not required. An interest in materials and material processing. An analytical approach and good problem-solving skills. An ability to understand and apply engineering solutions. You are fluent in English, written and spoken. Good written and spoken communication skills. International study or experience is an advantage, but not a requirement.
