Mechanical Shift Technician

Nordscale · Bjerkvik, Norway

Lønn ikke oppgittHeltidvia NAVFrist 5.1.2027

About the Role (Job Purpose) Join our operations team at the Bjerkvik facility, where you will help ensure that the cooling and mechanical systems supporting our data centres operate safely and reliably. The facility operates 24/7, and you will be part of a team working continuous shifts to ensure reliable operations around the clock.  We are building a larger operations team and will be hiring several mechanical shift technicians across different levels of experience. The role is suited both to those who are practically minded, early in their careers and looking to learn alongside experienced colleagues, and to those with broader experience from demanding mechanical environments who can work more independently with maintenance, testing and troubleshooting. No prior data centre experience is required.   What You Will Do  If you are early in your career, you will:  • Assist with the maintenance of mechanical systems, including chillers, pumps and ventilation systems  • Carry out routine rounds and inspections and report any issues or abnormalities  • Perform basic maintenance tasks, such as replacing filters and other wear parts  • Keep clear and accurate records of your work  • Learn from and support colleagues with troubleshooting and testing  • Work safely, follow our safety procedures and report hazards and incidents    If you have more experience, the role will also involve:  • Maintain and repair mechanical systems, including chillers, pumps, cooling towers, air handling units and rear door heat exchangers (RDHx)  • Carry out preventive and corrective maintenance, including replacing bearings, seals, filters and gaskets  • Troubleshoot cooling systems, pumps, motors and pipework, escalating the most complex issues to our technical specialists  • Perform functional testing, system flushing and quality checks on pipework and cooling systems  • Work with engineers and colleagues to fine-tune systems for energy efficiency  • Collaborate with colleagues, contractors and suppliers on parts and upgrades  • Follow lockout/tagout (LOTO) procedures, carry out risk assessments and ensure the safe handling of refrigerants and pressurised systems  We think you'll bring  If you are early in your career:  • A practical interest in mechanical systems and the motivation to learn about operations and maintenance  • Good English skills, as English is our working language    If you have more experience:  • A relevant mechanical background, such as a trade qualification or equivalent practical experience  • Operational experience from industrial or process environments, pipework and piping systems, refrigeration and cooling, the maritime sector, or other mechanical environments  • Hands-on experience with maintenance, repairs and troubleshooting  • Good English skills, as English is our working language  It would be an advantage, but is not required, if you have:  • A relevant trade qualification or practical experience in a mechanical trade  • Experience from industrial or process environments, refrigeration and cooling, the maritime sector, or other 24/7 operational environments  • Data centre experience  • Experience with refrigerants, pressurised systems or LOTO procedures  • Knowledge of energy efficiency in mechanical systems  • Experience working with and following up contractors  • Knowledge of a Scandinavian language  About you  You are practically minded, enjoy hands-on work and always put HSE first. You are eager to learn, take accountability for your tasks and enjoy being part of a team where everyone contributes.    For more experienced candidates, we also expect you to work independently, prioritise effectively in a demanding operational environment, and communicate clearly with colleagues and other technical disciplines. You are comfortable using digital tools for documentation, logging and reporting.    What we can offer  • Competitive salary and benefits  • A secure, long-term position in a growing industry  • Training and development opportunities  • A safe, inclusive and supportive working environment    If you are early in your career, you will have the opportunity to develop your skills and take on more responsibility over time. As you build your experience and demonstrate ownership, you can progress into a more independent role.  Equal Opportunities Statement  We are committed to fostering an inclusive, diverse, and equitable workplace. We believe that a variety of perspectives enriches our work environment, and we encourage applications from candidates of all backgrounds, experiences, and abilities.  We strongly encourage applications from people of colour, the LGBTQ&#43; community, people with disabilities, neurodivergent people, parents, carers, and people from lower socio-economic backgrounds.   If there’s anything we can do to accommodate your specific situation, please let us know.
