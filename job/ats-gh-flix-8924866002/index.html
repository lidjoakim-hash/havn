At Flix, we offer a dynamic work environment with competitive pay, strong growth opportunities, and a tech-driven approach to making travel more accessible, sustainable, and affordable. You will join Flix's People & Organization Digital Transformation team as a technically hands-on Business Analyst, bridging HR operations, Workday and emerging AI and automation technologies. You will understand how HR processes work end to end, identify opportunities to simplify and automate them, and turn business challenges into practical technology solutions. Beyond gathering requirements, you will help build and deploy workflows, integrations and AI-enabled tools, while supporting Workday tech operations and process optimization in close collaboration with our internal Workday technology team and FlixTech. The role reports to the Head of Digital Transformation and collaborates with HR process owners, People Operations, Workday Tech team, FlixTech, IT Security and Data Privacy teams globally. This role is based in Berlin (hybrid model).
