Electronics Lab Technician Husqvarna Group

Husqvarna AB · Jönköping, Sweden

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Do you enjoy hands-on electronics work and turning ideas into working prototypes? You will join our Technology team, a group of around 20 colleagues working with electronics for our robotic lawn mowers. The team brings together a mix of employees and consultants with expertise across hardware development, detailed electronics design, test development, lab activities, and supporting engineering systems. It is a collaborative and practical environment where you will work closely with hardware engineers and other specialists throughout the development process. Role Description: In this role, you will be part of our Lab & Test team, supporting our electronics development with practical lab work. You will work closely with hardware engineers and other colleagues across the team, helping them modify circuit boards, build prototypes and prepare electronics for development and testing. Your main tasks will include: Responsible for maintaining and supporting the soldering lab. Advanced soldering, including manual SMD rework on PCBAs Modifying and troubleshooting electronic assemblies Building electronic and electromechanical prototypes Supporting engineers with practical electronics and lab activities Working according to ESD and EPA requirements Your profile We believe you have a practical background in electronics, for example from electronics manufacturing, EMS, electronics production or a similar environment. You are comfortable working hands-on with circuit boards and electronic components and have a good general understanding of electronics and electromechanical circuits. You have: Around 3-4 years of relevant experience, particularly if you have worked extensively with soldering and electronics service Strong skills in manual soldering and SMD rework Good knowledge of ESD and EPA practices Good communication skills in Swedish and English Experience with 3D modelling and 3D printing and knowledge of IPC-A-610, IPC-7711, or similar soldering standards is a plus. This is an on-site role, where access to the electronics lab and close collaboration with the team are an important part of the everyday work. Location Huskvarna. We believe the best ideas happen when we are connected. We spend most of our time together in the office, creating space for collaboration, creativity, and fast decision-making. This is consistent across all our global offices and helps us maintain a strong culture, support learning and development, and ensure everyone has access to the people and resources they need to thrive.
