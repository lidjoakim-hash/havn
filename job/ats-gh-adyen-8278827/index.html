CDD Direct Customer Onboarding team Adyen is looking for a hands-on CDD Risk Analyst to join our Direct Customer Onboarding team and contribute to the growth and scaling of our customer base in EMEA. In this role, you will onboarding of merchants, including enhanced due diligence, KYC checks, and assessing the anti-money laundering risks, to protect Adyen and our customers. You will be working with a wide range of teams within Adyen, including commercial, compliance, and product to ensure that our customers and understanding of them remain up-to-date and compliant. You are expected to be very detail-oriented, take a risk-based approach, and have the ability to think critically with scalability always in mind, all while maintaining a high caliber work product. What you will do • End-to-End Onboarding: Manage end to end onboarding flows for enterprise accounts, conducting in-depth due diligence, analyzing complex ownership structures, and ensuring full compliance with regulatory obligations and internal policies. • Quality & Accuracy: Maintain "first-time-right" quality accuracy across all risk assessments and regional compliance standards as well as regulatory expectations. • Merchant Communication & Guidance: Act as the primary point of contact for merchants during onboarding, providing clear guidance and internal advice. • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Collaborate cross-functionally with commercial, compliance, and product teams to balance risk mitigation with business scalability growth, as well as have a critical eye on our own internal processes. • Risk Escalations: Handle complex risk escalations, providing expert analysis and decision-making on high-risk onboarding scenarios. • Policy & Standards Alignment: Align local operational procedures with global policy requirements and second-line compliance standards. Who you are • 3-6 years of experience in Customer Due Diligence (CDD), Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD), or KYC. • High sense of responsibility, integrity, and ability to prioritize tasks based on business needs. • Curious, analytical, and critical mindset with a strong problem-solving orientation and risk-based approach. • Clear communicator and team player, comfortable navigating ambiguity and tailoring messages for diverse audiences. • Experience collaborating with commercial stakeholders to navigate discussions while maintaining robust risk standards. • Experience with policies, procedures, and product teams to identify gaps and enhance processes.
