WHAT YOU’LL DO: • Manage a Dual-Domain Team: Mentor and oversee the team, setting technical standards across both product analytics and business intelligence. Foster continuous professional growth and establish best practices for data modeling and analysis; • Product Analytics: Support product development, track feature adoption and identify key user behavior and value drivers; • Ad-hoc Analysis: Serve as the strategic analytics partner to executive and business leaders. Run deep-dive diagnostic analyses to answer high-stakes operational questions (e.g., investigating sudden sales drops, identifying returns issues, etc.); • Business Case & Decision Support: Partner with business areas to model scenarios, evaluate growth opportunities, and provide rigorous, data-backed quantitative foundation for strategic business cases and new initiatives; • Business Intelligence & Enterprise Reporting: Manage company-wide core KPIs and maintain unified executive dashboards that provide single-source-of-truth visibility into the business; • Drive Self-Service Analytics: Manage and evolve our self-service analytics platform, ensuring a robust environment that empowers business teams to make data-driven decisions independently; • Collaborate with Data Engineering: Work closely with the Data Engineering team to evolve the company’s data infrastructure. You will be the voice of the analytics team, ensuring data quality and availability requirements are met to support advanced reporting; • Evolve with AI: Explore and adopt AI-powered tools to enhance analytical efficiency. You will have the opportunity to grow significantly in this area, applying AI to automate insights and solve business challenges. WHO YOU ARE: • Hybrid Analytics Leader: You bring a proven track record as Analytics Manager or Lead Analyst with expertise spanning both Business Intelligence and Product Analytics; • Strategic Connector: You excel at bridging the gap between technical data and business strategy. You have excellent communication skills and can influence stakeholders at all levels; • Collaborative Team Player: You strongly believe in teamwork. You are eager to work side-by-side with Data Engineers to build a scalable data ecosystem, understanding that your success depends on the quality of the data pipeline; • Tech Savvy & Analytical: You have strong skills in SQL and Python, with a data-driven mindset to test theories and measure success; • AI Growth Mindset: You are curious about the future of analytics.
