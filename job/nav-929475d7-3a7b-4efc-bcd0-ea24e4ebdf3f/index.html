FOOD TRUCK OPERATIONS MANAGER

Grp As · Aurland, Norway

Lønn ikke oppgittSesongvia NAVFrist 30.1.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Your next season starts in Flåm! FOOD TRUCK OPERATIONS MANAGER Flåm, Norway Bring your experience, your ideas and your love of working with people. Let's make the 2027 season a great one together. Good food brings people together - and a good manager helps a team bring its best to every shift. Join us in Flåm to lead our food trucks, with the fjords as the setting for your next season. You'll lead around 12 colleagues across food trucks based in one place. There's room to make the role your own: organise the day, help people grow and put your ideas into practice. You'll report directly to the CEO and work closely with HR. We'd love to meet a leader who enjoys being part of the action. When service gets busy, you'll be alongside your team - preparing food, serving guests and finding solutions together. Your part in the team • Help the team start each day with a clear plan, shared tasks and well-organised shifts. • Prepare Flåm staff schedules and arrange days off around operational needs. • Show colleagues how things work, train them and help everyone follow our standards and procedures. • Keep orders and supplies organised so the team is ready for service. • Be the person who listens, finds solutions and follows through on everyday problems. • Coordinate employee arrivals, departures and the practical arrangements around them. • Step into any operational position and support the team through the busiest parts of the day. • Keep in touch with HR on employee matters, share a weekly update with the CEO and flag important issues promptly. • Keep authorised spending organised and make sure expenses are documented. You'll feel at home in this role if... • You've managed food service operations and led a team before. • You enjoy bringing people together and lead by example, with clear communication and support. • You're practical, organised and calm when the pace picks up. • You take initiative, make sound decisions and look for workable solutions. • You can help across every operational position and travel when the role requires it. • You speak English at B2-C1 level and hold a valid category B driving licence. • You have the basic computer skills needed for schedules, reports and everyday documents. • You can be flexible and respond when unexpected situations arise. What's waiting for you • A full-time, hourly-paid role from mid-March to mid-October, with the possibility of extending your contract. • A team to lead, space for your ideas and the chance to see your contribution in action. • Close cooperation with the CEO and support from HR. • Help finding accommodation for your season in Norway. • A staff meal on each working day. Join our team and spend the season surrounded by Norway's breathtaking fjords! With food trucks in Flåm and other beautiful locations, we're all about great food, friendly faces, and making people feel welcome. We'd love to have you on board and share an unforgettable season together! Send your CV and recomendation: job&#64;grpnorway.com
