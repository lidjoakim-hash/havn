About the Role Rubrik is building a new Specialist Organization going into 2027: an overlay team of Account Executives and Sales Engineers focused on key product pillars, including cloud, unstructured data, SaaS, and identity protection. Specialists need to be more than product-aware. They need to be differentiated, technical, and credible enough to move complex deals forward in their workload area. The Sales Transformation Specialist is the enablement engine behind that goal. You will be the tight link between Product Management and the field, turning what is being built into training that AEs and SEs can use in front of customers. You will teach the team what customers care about, the challenges they face, how the underlying technologies work, how Rubrik protects them, and the value Rubrik delivers. About the Specialist Organization The Specialist Organization is Rubrik's overlay sales model, aligned to product pillars rather than geography. Specialists partner with core account teams to accelerate and shape opportunities in their pillar. The org will onboard a significant cohort of new AEs in early 2027 and will keep growing, so enablement must serve both new hires and tenured team members as products evolve.
