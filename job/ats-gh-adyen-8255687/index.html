Partnerships at Adyen The Adyen Alliances team drive global go to market partnerships with GSIs, Consultancies & Private Equity/Venture Capital. We are on a growth trajectory and are seeking a Senior Alliance Manager, GSIs to mature, scale, and lead our global partnerships alongside a select group of global system integrators inc.
