Job Title: Director, Operations IT - Manufacturing Execution Systems, Product Management Location: Södertälje, Sweden Location note: Suitable applicants from other AZ hub/Operations sites may be considered We are looking for a visionary and experienced IT Director to lead the global strategy, implementation and optimization of Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES) across our pharmaceutical operations. In this high-impact role, you will drive digital transformation, ensure alignment between technology and business priorities, and help advance manufacturing efficiency, quality and compliance. This is an exciting opportunity to combine strategic leadership, technology and manufacturing expertise while making an impact across a global organization. The role is based in Södertälje, Sweden. The Manufacturing IT capability is a global capability within Operations IT, responsible for delivering strategic digital technologies that enable safe, compliant and efficient manufacturing across AstraZeneca's Operations network. The team spans Manufacturing Execution Systems, Manufacturing Optimisation, Plant Maintenance, Warehouse Management, and Autonomous Manufacturing, working closely with manufacturing sites, enterprise IT teams and strategic technology partners to develop scalable global solutions through platform and product management. What you'll do: Strategic Leadership: Develop and execute the MES strategy to align with AstraZeneca's 2030 bold ambition. Develop an MES technology roadmap that considers and makes use of technology advancements that will benefit Operations through Manufacturing Execution processes that contribute to AstraZeneca's goals and objectives. Proactively plan MES projects and programs globally with relevant AstraZeneca sites/regions. IT Product Management: Define the MES product vision and roadmap in partnership with technology vendors and manufacturing stakeholders. Govern product backlog prioritisation, balancing business value, regulatory requirements, technical dependencies and available capacity. Oversee the full product lifecycle, from discovery and delivery through adoption, continuous improvement and retirement. Establish product success measures and use stakeholder feedback and performance data to guide investment and improvement. System Implementation and Optimization: Govern the design, configuration, integration, and deployment of MES solutions globally to ensure standardization and enterprise efficiencies. Ensure MES solutions align with industry best practices, regulatory requirements (e.g., FDA, GMP), and company standards. Continuously evaluate system performance and identify opportunities for optimization. Stakeholder Collaboration: Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including Manufacturing BPO's, Site teams, IT, ERP teams and external vendors to develop, deliver and maintain the MES strategy and MES systems globally. Collaborate with IT Business Analysts, Architects and Service Delivery teams to plan, deliver and operate MES solutions globally. Compliance and Risk Management: Ensure all MES implementations comply with regulatory requirements and cybersecurity standards. Develop and maintain SOPs related to MES usage, data integrity, and system validation. Budget and Vendor Management: Manage the MES program budget and ensure cost-effective solutions. Evaluate, select, and manage relationships with MES vendors and third-party service providers.
