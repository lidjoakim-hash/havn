Who we are:  At Rover, pets and their people are at the heart of everything we do. We connect pet parents with trusted pet care across the U.S., Canada, Europe, and Australia. Headquartered in Seattle and Barcelona, we’re a values driven, fast-growing tech company focused on building safe and personalized experiences tailored to the needs of each unique pet. We’re investing in AI as a business accelerator and provide every team member access to AI tools in service of creating better experiences for our community. We are proud to be recognized as a great place to work, having been named among the 100 Best Companies to Work For in Seattle Business Magazine and Washington’s Best Workplaces in the Puget Sound Business Journal. At Rover we’re committed to creating an accessible, inclusive, and welcoming community, which starts with our employees. Want to make an impact? Join our pack and come work (and play!) with us. We are the Client Platform team. We help other developers at Rover build high quality experiences quickly by focusing on underlying tools and frameworks.  We manage and build out core tooling and platforms for frontend development across the web and mobile apps at Rover. We on rare occasion will work in our Django backend, but it’s not a focus for our team. We are looking for this role to be primarily focused on web and React Native frontend.  Some of our key investments : Continuing to expand our cross-platform Design System. We maintain the shared library of re-usable components that make it easy to build cross-platform apps quickly. Moving the website to be served from Next.js. We are migrating the fundamental tech used to serve Rover.com to a community supported SSR platform.  Making it easier to build with AI. Building workflows, skills, techniques, and approaches for AI-based development and migrations across all teams.  Moving Rover to a React Native-first app + development cycle. We are completing our React Native migration (now serving 90+% mobile views) and fully migrating to a React Native development cycle.  Managing Rover’s adoption of modern tech - notably Next.js, React Query, Expo, and others.  As a Platform team, we build out the fundamental tech that powers the internals of Rover.com and the mobile apps. We empower developers to be able to focus more on building great experiences than fighting their tech. Technologies Rover.com is primarily built in React, served either from a SSR or Django backend, and compiled with Webpack. Our client API is written in Python in Django, which serves over 2 billion requests a month. Our mobile apps are a majority React Native. Our development environment centers around ready-to-use Codespaces. At Rover, we deploy to the web 40-60 times a day, with a median review -> merge time for PRs of 3 hours. So all that to say - we prioritize shipping small, incremental work often and quickly! The role We are looking for someone with a primarily web development background who is excited to work both on Web React and React Native. As a senior engineer on our team, you will: • Solve complex technical problems at the fundamentals of our web and React Native clients. • Work to set technical standards across Rover; then evangelize and educate on those standards. • Build cross-platform code that works across both web and React Native. • Help to set a technical roadmap and vision for the team. We don’t have a partner Product Manager, and so “set our own destiny” in a sense. • Work with other engineers in Barcelona to help mentor them and answer questions on Client Platform topics. • Represent Client Platform in broad forums and collect feedback that helps inform the team’s roadmap and vision. • Help mentor and coach more junior engineers. We are expanding our team’s presence in Barcelona.
