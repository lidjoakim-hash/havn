As a Fraud and Risk Officer Intern, you will be responsible for the ongoing assessment of Fraud risks, organizing the framework for action to monitor and prevent the risk of fraud on the Back Market platform. In this role, you will work closely with the finance team to ensure fraud risks are managed effectively.
