Who We Are: We’ve come a long way from our origins as a small startup. Today, we’re a truly global fintech company, with over 3000+ people from 80 nationalities, spread across 22 offices on 4 continents. And we’re growing and diversifying faster than ever before. SumUp enables businesses to get paid easily, process orders quickly, sell online instantly and manage their money more efficiently. We create the tools businesses need to make their business and their customer experience thrive. The Team: Our robust payments processing platform, consisting of various sub-platforms, has powered SumUp’s global business for over a decade. A critical component of this platform is our Payouts team, which owns the components responsible for payout profiles and payout scheduling. These systems play a critical role in the wider payout flow: they manage how and when merchants receive their funds and support the configuration needed for reliable payouts across markets. About the Role: Payouts are one of the most important parts of the merchant experience. When a merchant expects to receive their money, the process needs to be reliable, transparent and predictable. In this role, you’ll work on systems that directly support the movement of funds to SumUp merchants. You’ll help modernise existing components, build new capabilities and contribute to a platform that needs to operate reliably across markets and at scale. This is a role for an engineer who enjoys solving complex backend problems, working with distributed systems and balancing the needs of a legacy platform with the opportunity to build something better. Our Stack: Java (minimal setup, Spring Boot), Kafka Streams, PostgreSQL/RDBMS, RestEasy, VueJS.
