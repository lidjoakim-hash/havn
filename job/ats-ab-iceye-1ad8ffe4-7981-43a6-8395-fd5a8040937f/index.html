ROLE HIGHLIGHTS: - HR Generalist - Location: Warsaw, Poland - Department: Mission & Data - Reports to: Senior HR Generalist - Employment type: Permanent, full-time - Workplace model: Hybrid - Employment is subject to applicable security screening (incl. SUPO, where required) WHY THIS ROLE MATTERS: As an HR Generalist, you are a key member of ICEYE’s local subsidiary in Poland and part of our globally distributed HR team, responsible for ensuring a seamless and positive employee experience across the entire employee lifecycle for ICEYErs in Warsaw. You are the face of HR for our local office and the "1st Line of Defence" for all country-specific people matters. While our Global HR Operations team (the "2nd Line of Defence") designs global frameworks, tools, and data standards, you are responsible for the local tailoring and practical execution of these standards. You will handle high-touch, individual cases that require solid knowledge of Polish labor law and office culture. You bridge the gap between global ICEYE standards and local needs, collaborating closely with departments like IT, Finance, and Office Management to drive operational excellence and employee satisfaction. In short, your mission is to ensure that ICEYErs in Poland are happy and engaged so that they can deliver better business results! WHO WE ARE ICEYE is the world leader in sovereign intelligence from space. We deliver persistent monitoring capabilities to detect and respond to changes in any location on Earth.   ICEYE owns the world's largest and most advanced SAR (synthetic aperture radar) satellite constellation. To our customers we provide intelligence with unmatched quality, latency and revisit times, in any weather, day or night. To governments who choose to operate their own constellation we provide this proven capability as a sovereign system.   ICEYE-built constellations serve customers in defence and intelligence, environmental monitoring, insurance and emergency management. We enable fast decisions that contribute to a safer future.   Founded and headquartered in Finland, ICEYE operates globally with over 1000 employees across Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. YOUR DAY-TO-DAY RESPONSIBILITIES - Serve as a point of contact for local employees regarding standard HR-related inquiries, including questions related to employee benefits, internal policies, and employee lifecycle events. - Support the onboarding and relocation process by preparing standard employment documentation and country-specific paperwork. - Cooperate with external service providers on day-to-day operational tasks, escalating issues as needed. - Support local offboarding activities in line with established internal procedures. - Enter and verify payroll-related data within HRIS systems and cooperate with internal teams and local payroll vendors to support accurate payroll processing. - Maintain and update employee records and HR documentation within HRIS and other relevant systems, ensuring data accuracy, confidentiality, and compliance with applicable data protection regulations. - Update existing documentation related to local HR processes, procedures, and internal policies. - Identify and suggest opportunities for automation and improvement of local HR processes. - Provide local administrative support for global HR initiatives. - Support the communication and implementation of local HR programs and policies. WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR - Bachelor’s degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, or a related field (or equivalent experience), with 2-4 years of experience in a generalist HR or people role. - Strong working knowledge of Polish labor law, local benefits, and market HR best practices, with a track record of creating compliant HR policy documentation and internal knowledge base pages. - A “hands-on” approach with high attention to detail in contract management, local onboarding
