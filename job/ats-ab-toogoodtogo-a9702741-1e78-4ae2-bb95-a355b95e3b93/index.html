Head of PR - Cluster Centre

Too Good To Go · London, UK, United Kingdom

About Too Good To Go Too Good To Go is the world’s largest marketplace for surplus food. More than 150 million registered users and 200,000 retail and hospitality partners across 20 countries use Too Good To Go, and together they have saved more than 650 million meals from going to waste. We’re now entering an exciting next chapter, broadening what we offer and how we tell our story, and PR has a central role in making this land. About the role As Head of PR for Cluster Center, you’ll own our communications across six markets: the UK, Germany, Poland, Austria, Switzerland and Ireland. Each has its own media landscape and level of maturity, and the role brings them together under one clear direction while allowing each market to succeed on its own terms. This is a hands-on, highly collaborative role that combines leading local teams with close involvement in the work itself, with a focus on strategic PR that delivers real impact for the business. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES Strategy and storytelling - Define and deliver the cluster PR strategy, with support from Global PR, prioritising impactful coverage that builds reputation and supports business goals with consumers and partners. - Support local teams to drive proactive campaigns and storytelling moments as our brand and offering evolve, from consumer launches to impact data and food waste policy. - Adapt and activate global PR campaigns locally, and feed market insight back to Global PR so central campaigns work for the cluster. Team, budget and agencies - Lead, coach and develop a team of three PR managers across markets, raising the bar on impact, creativity, quality and consistency while giving each market room to adapt to local context. - Oversee agency relationships across the cluster, holding agencies accountable to business goals, and getting maximum value from every partnership. - Own and manage the cluster PR budget, allocating spend across markets, agencies and campaigns based on business priorities and measurable impact. External and internal stakeholder management - Act as a trusted partner to senior stakeholders across the cluster. Align competing priorities, influence decisions without formal authority, and keep communications rooted in business reality. - Lead on issues and reputation management in the cluster, in partnership with Global Communications. - Build and maintain relationships with top-tier consumer, business and trade media and key opinion leaders, with particular focus on the UK and Germany. Measurement and impact - Own PR performance measurement for the cluster within the global framework, setting and reporting on relevant KPIs. - Connect PR to both business and brand outcomes, working with other teams to track contribution to awareness, reputation, consumer growth and partner value. - Report regularly to cluster leadership with clear insights on what to scale, adapt or stop. What you’ll bring - Extensive experience in PR and communications, ideally in high-growth consumer brands, purpose-driven organisations or agencies. Marketplace or two-sided platform experience is a plus. - A proven track record leading communications across multiple countries, ideally within a matrixed global organisation. - A strong existing network across top-tier consumer, corporate and business media, especially in the UK and/or Germany. - A portfolio of high-impact, proactive campaigns that cut through and delivered measurable results. - Demonstrable experience measuring PR impact and linking it to business metrics, beyond coverage volume and reach. How you work - You’re strategic and hands-on in equal measure - You take ownership of outcomes and foster high accountability across teams - Excellent people leadership and coaching skills with an ability to empower teams across languages, geographies, and disciplines - Strong collaborator and communicator - you’re comfortable influencing and aligning with senior stakeholde
