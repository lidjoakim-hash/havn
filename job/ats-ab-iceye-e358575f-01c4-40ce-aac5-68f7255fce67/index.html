ROLE HIGHLIGHTS: - Senior GRC Engineer (Security) - Location: Espoo, Finland - Department: Product Security - Reports to: VP Product Security - Employment type: Permanent - Workplace model: Hybrid - Employment is subject to applicable security screening (incl. SUPO, where required) WHY THIS ROLE MATTERS:   ICEYE operates at the intersection of commercial space technology and sovereign defence, across a multi-country, multi-regulator footprint that includes Finland, Germany, Spain, Poland, the UK and Greece. We have an ISO 27001 management system, and multiple regulatory frameworks affect us: CRA, NIS2, indirectly DORA, their national implementations and national laws. Our customers include governments and defence agencies, and each brings its own national security requirements. Our compliance posture has to be credible to all of them: we have to turn requirements into things our products and teams do, and we have to be able to prove it. We are enhancing our GRC capability in the ICEYE Security team that is responsible for this as a whole, across four areas: ISO 27001 and other standards compliance, regulatory compliance, interpreting product compliance requirements against engineering facts, and building compliance and governance as code. We are hiring Senior or Staff GRC engineers into this team. Our candidates are not expected to cover all areas below, but instead will cover these areas as a part of the larger Security team with GRC as their main responsibility. Successful candidates bring depth in a combination of them and work as individual contributors, closely with the other Security team focus areas (Security Engineering, Security Operations, Physical Security), IT, Legal, Engineering, Product Management and Lines of Business.   WHO WE ARE ICEYE is the world leader in sovereign intelligence from space. We deliver persistent monitoring capabilities to detect and respond to changes in any location on Earth.   ICEYE owns the world's largest and most advanced SAR (synthetic aperture radar) satellite constellation. To our customers we provide intelligence with unmatched quality, latency and revisit times, in any weather, day or night. To governments who choose to operate their own constellation we provide this proven capability as a sovereign system.   ICEYE-built constellations serve customers in defence and intelligence, environmental monitoring, insurance and emergency management. We enable fast decisions that contribute to a safer future.   Founded and headquartered in Finland, ICEYE operates globally with over 1000 employees across Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. YOUR DAY-TO-DAY RESPONSIBILITIES The position will evolve according to the needs of the company and the growing team. Depending on your profile, it is likely to include a combination of the following areas. COMPLIANCE - Run the ISMS.
