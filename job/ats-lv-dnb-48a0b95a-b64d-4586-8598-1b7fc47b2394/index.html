The Senior Network Engineer serves as a technical leader in the design, implementation, and optimization of our enterprise and cloud network infrastructure. This role partners closely with Technology leadership, engineers, and end users to deliver secure, scalable, and high-performing network solutions with a strong focus on AWS and Google Cloud Platform (GCP). provide recommendations, build consensus, and execute solutions. • Recommend appropriate courses of action to network infrastructure engineers, NOC, MSPs, and IT management on technical and operational issues Essential skills and/or Certifications: • Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or other related field • Minimum 8 years of experience in network engineering • Expert knowledge of enterprise-level cloud architecture with a focus on network design and implementation (AWS and GCP, with a focus on GCP). • Networking certifications such as CCNA, CCNP, or equivalent is preferred • Expert knowledge of network infrastructure, protocols, and standards;
