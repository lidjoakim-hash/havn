Winning their trust takes sound legal judgement, strong relationships and work that holds up when tested. As Director of Legal Engineering, Strategic Accounts, you will report directly to our Head of Legal Engineering, EMEA, and lead a group of our most talented Legal Engineers. Your customers will be global and international law firms and multinational corporates. Your remit has two connected priorities: lead Legal Engineering on the biggest and most impactful cross-regional strategic accounts in EMEA, and establish and implement a forward-deployed Legal Engineering approach in post-sales. On strategic accounts, you will be the Legal Engineering counterpart to our Strategic Accounts leadership. In post-sales, you will build a model that puts Legal Engineers close to the customer's actual work, helping teams redesign workflows, implement working solutions and realise measurable value after the contract is signed. This is a leadership role with substantial personal delivery. Alongside leading the team, you will personally own demanding client work: lead critical conversations, build and demonstrate substantive workflows, diagnose difficult account situations and take responsibility for the result. You will set the standard through your own work and make the people around you better through clear direction, direct feedback, coaching and meaningful delegation. We are looking for someone with legal judgement, the problem-solving discipline of a strong consultant and the instinct to build. Someone competitive, ambitious and genuinely motivated by results, who earns the respect of excellent practitioners through the quality of their decisions and delivery.
