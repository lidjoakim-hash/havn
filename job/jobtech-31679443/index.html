TD Svensk AB is looking for a Senior Exterior Designer. Job Description / Key Responsibilities Develop exterior component and lighting concepts for production and advanced vehicle programs. Create distinctive front and rear lighting signatures, illuminated features, and integrated exterior details. Translate 2D design concepts into high-quality 3D solutions. Collaborate with Exterior Design, Studio Engineering, Vehicle Architecture, suppliers, and China-based stakeholders. Ensure design proposals balance creativity, technical feasibility, manufacturing requirements, and brand identity. Support design reviews, presentations, prototype evaluations, and production development activities. Required qualification Minimum 5 years' experience in automotive exterior design. Proven experience in exterior components and/or automotive lighting design. Strong portfolio demonstrating design development from concept to production. Experience working within OEMs, automotive design studios, or consultancy environments. Excellent understanding of vehicle exterior design, lighting graphics, materials, and production constraints. Ability to work independently and contribute immediately to active vehicle programs. Fluent English communication skills
