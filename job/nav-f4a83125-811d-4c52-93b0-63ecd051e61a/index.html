We are seeking a Sub-Contract Manager!

Folk AS · Lysaker, Norway

We are seeking an Sub-Contract Manager for a long-term assignment with one of our clients!   Accountabilities: • Lead the procurement and subcontracting strategy for compressor, liquefaction, rotating equipment and process-package scopes, ensuring alignment with project objectives, engineering maturity and execution plans. • Develop and maintain a strong network of OEMs, package suppliers and specialist subcontractors, driving supplier engagement, prequalification and market intelligence activities. • Define technical requisitions, scope, interfaces, assumptions and exclusions in cooperation with engineering, commissioning, project management and supply chain teams. • Lead the tendering process, including enquiries, clarifications, negotiations, bid evaluations, award recommendations and contract finalization. • Manage commercial and contractual activities during execution, including variations, claims, supplier performance, payment milestones, guarantees, risk mitigation and contractual compliance. • Follow up suppliers throughout execution, ensuring timely delivery while managing technical changes, schedule impacts and commercial consequences. • Build long-term supplier relationships within compressor, liquefaction and process-package markets to support project execution and future sourcing opportunities. Qualifications: • A relevant degree in Engineering, Supply Chain, Business, Law or a related discipline, with preferably 7&#43; years of experience in procurement, subcontracting or contract management. • An analytical mindset combined with strong commercial judgement and proven negotiation skills. • Experience managing high-value and technically complex procurement packages throughout both pre-award and post-award phases. • Experience with compressor packages, liquefaction systems, rotating equipment, refrigeration systems, process skids, modular process units or similar process-industry equipment. • Strong commercial and contractual experience, including negotiations, claims, variations, supplier performance management and risk mitigation. • A solid understanding of the interface between engineering and procurement, including technical requirements, package integration, change management and supplier deliverables. • Experience working with multidisciplinary stakeholders across engineering, project management, commissioning, legal, quality, finance and supply chain functions. • Strong risk awareness and the ability to manage uncertainty, supplier exposure and evolving project requirements. • Experience from international projects and supplier environments, with the ability to work effectively across cultures and organizational boundaries. • Experience working in SAP or other ERP/procurement systems is considered an advantage. Personal skills: • Commercially strong and technically curious, with the confidence to lead complex procurement and subcontracting activities in dynamic project environments. • Structured, proactive and resilient, with the ability to maintain control of scope, interfaces, risks, assumptions and supplier commitments in a dynamic EPC environment. • A confident decision-maker who creates clarity, drives progress and keeps stakeholders aligned when technical, commercial and schedule priorities compete. • Collaborative and people-oriented, with strong influencing skills and the ability to build trusted relationships across engineering, project management, suppliers, legal, quality, finance and senior leadership. • Assertive and constructive when challenging suppliers and internal stakeholders, while maintaining a professional and solution-oriented approach. • Calm under pressure and capable of managing ambiguity, changing priorities and contractual challenges without losing focus on cost, quality, schedule and project objectives. • An effective communicator who can translate technical changes and engineering developments into clear commercial impacts, business decisions and management-level recommendations. • Passionate about continuous improvement, supplier development and delivering successful project outcomes in a safe, sustainable and commercially sound manner.
