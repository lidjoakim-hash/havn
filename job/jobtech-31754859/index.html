Weights Engineer

Eccoci AB · Linköping, Sweden

💰 ca. 510 000–620 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Kontraktvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 17.2.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

We are looking for a Weights Engineer to join an engineering team. You'll own the mass properties of the product through design, build and certification, making sure weight, centre of gravity and inertia stay within limits and that the figures the programme relies on are accurate. This is a hands-on role where you'll work closely with design, stress, systems and manufacturing teams, and you'll be the go-to person whenever a design change affects weight. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES Building and maintaining mass properties models (weight, centre of gravity and moments of inertia) at component, system and whole aircraft level. Producing weight estimates, budgets and status reports, tracking actual against target and flagging risk early. Keeping the weights database current as designs change, working with design, stress, systems and manufacturing engineers. Running weight and balance analysis across loading conditions, including fuel, payload and configuration variants. Supporting weight reduction work, finding opportunities and assessing the trade-offs. Planning and supporting physical weighing of parts, assemblies and aircraft, then reconciling the results against predicted data. Preparing mass properties documentation for design reviews, customers and certification authorities. Maintaining weight control procedures and data in line with programme and quality requirements. REQUIREMENTS A degree in aeronautical or mechanical engineering, or a related discipline, or equivalent industry experience. Proven experience as a Weights Engineer or Mass Properties Engineer in aerospace or defence. A strong grasp of centre of gravity, inertia, and weight and balance principles. Experience extracting mass data from CAD, ideally CATIA V5 or 3DEXPERIENCE (NX or similar also considered). Advanced Excel skills and experience with weights databases or mass properties tools. Experience working to aerospace certification and weight control standards. Fluent English, written and spoken.
