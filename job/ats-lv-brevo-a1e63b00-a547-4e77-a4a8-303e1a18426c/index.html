At Brevo, we’re not just building a CRM. With our technology, we’re helping millions of organizations build lasting relationships with their customers. From emails and SMS to WhatsApp, Chat, and Marketing Automation, our tools are intuitive, powerful, and built to scale with every ambition. We give businesses a clear view of the customer journey, so they can focus on what matters: connection. As a certified B Corp, we’re proud to grow with purpose, committed to high standards of social and environmental impact, not just performance. Today, more than 500,000 businesses across 180 countries—from NGOs like Amnesty International to global brands like Carrefour, eBay, Louis Vuitton, and Michelin—trust Brevo to engage their audiences, cut through complexity, and deliver results. Our reliable technology and 75+ integrations help them create unparalleled customer experiences, without the usual tech headaches. We recently exceeded 200m ARR and reached a major milestone by becoming a Unicorn, backed by strong growth and global expansion - and we’re just getting started! Your Impact at Brevo: • Keep the office running smoothly, making sure supplies, equipment and services are always ready when people need them. • Build and maintain strong relationships with vendors and partners, from sourcing quotes to negotiating contracts. • Take care of invoices and administrative follow-up, so the office budget stays clear and under control. • Be the friendly face of the office: welcome guests, manage the front desk and access badges. • Spot issues early and fix them quickly, whether it’s maintenance, cleanliness or safety. • Shape the workspace with furniture, layout and space changes that make it more comfortable and functional. • Plan team lunches, celebrations and company events, from venues and catering to RSVPs and logistics, in close collaboration with the Workplace Experience Manager. • Suggest and test ideas that make the office more enjoyable, and share what works with the team. Who you are: • You have at least 3 years of experience in an office management or workplace role, and you thrive in a fast-paced, international environment. • You enjoy being the person people turn to, with a natural service mindset and genuine warmth. • You are fluent in French and English, both written and spoken. • You are highly organized and able to juggle multiple priorities without losing track of details. • You are positive, curious and creative, always looking for ways to make the workplace better. • You are proactive and hands-on, happy to roll up your sleeves when a problem needs solving. • You thrive on autonomy and take real ownership of the spaces and projects you manage. If you don't meet all the requirements above but believe this opportunity matches your expectations and experience, we still encourage you to apply! Why people love working at Brevo: • A place to grow, together: Join an international team in a bright, collaborative office located in a vibrant neighbourhood. • Practical perks for everyday balance: RTT days off, 50% public transport reimbursement, Sustainable Mobility Package (whether you ride a classic bike or go electric, we support your choice to commute greener) and daily Swile meal vouchers (€12.50/day). • Learning, every step of the way: Access to English classes and 155,000+ courses on Udemy, plus a strong internal culture of knowledge-sharing and support. • Wellbeing that works: Top-notch private healthcare (70% covered), 6-week second-parent leave, extra time off if your child is sick and needs you, and several prevention, health and wellbeing services on the Welii platform. • A culture that cares: From inter-office trips to regular team events, there are plenty of ways to connect beyond your day-to-day. You'll also find active social, green, and LGBTQIA+ communities, plus Work Council benefits via Leeto, all here to support what matters to you, inside and outside of work.
