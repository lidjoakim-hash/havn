Who we are About Stripe and Bridge Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career. Bridge, acquired by Stripe in 2025 and operating as a Stripe subsidiary, is a stablecoin-powered payments platform built to simplify global money movement. About the team The Financial Crime, Compliance and Risk Oversight (FinCRO) Investigations Management team provides second-line-of-defence (2LoD) financial crime risk oversight and coordinates casework across Stripe’s EMEA region. The team manages user risk and synthesises investigative information into decision-ready analysis and intelligence for MLROs, RCOs, and other key stakeholders. It also identifies control weaknesses, coordinates complex investigations, and supports local examinations and inquiries from financial partners, law enforcement, and other external stakeholders. What you’ll do We are looking for a financial crime professional with Luxembourg- and EU-specific experience to join the EMEA Investigations Management team. Based in Luxembourg, you will focus primarily on Bridge Building S.A. while also supporting Stripe’s broader investigations work.
