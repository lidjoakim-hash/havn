About Flexport: At Flexport, we believe global trade can move the human race forward. That’s why it’s our mission to make global commerce so easy there will be more of it. We’re shaping the future of a $10T industry with solutions powered by innovative technology and exceptional people. Today, companies of all sizes—from emerging brands to Fortune 500s—use Flexport technology to move more than $19B of merchandise across 112 countries a year. The recent global supply chain crisis has put Flexport center stage as we continue to play a pivotal role in how goods move around the world. We are proud to have the support of the best investors in the game who believe in our mission, solutions and people. Ready to tackle global challenges that impact business, society, and the environment? Come join us. As an SMB Account Executive you will be responsible for owning the full sales cycle for small and medium-sized customers, from opportunity qualification through close and initial onboarding. This role focuses on velocity, disciplined execution. You will generate your own pipeline and will manage the inbound (marketing generated) opportunities. Your responsibilities: Pipeline & Opportunity Ownership • Own the full sales cycle for SMB customers, from qualified opportunity through close. • Build and manage a healthy pipeline through self-prospecting. • Prioritize opportunities based on fit, urgency, and likelihood to close within defined sales cycles. • Maintain accurate opportunity management and forecasting in Salesforce (SFDC). Customer Discovery & Solution Selling • Consult with SMB customers to understand their business objectives, supply chain needs, and operational constraints. • Translate customer needs into clear, value-driven Flexport solutions. • Demonstrate Flexport’s platform and services through structured discovery calls, remote meetings, and tailored demos. • Clearly articulate pricing, value, and next steps to move opportunities forward efficiently. Closing & Onboarding • Lead commercial negotiations and close new business in line with revenue and volume targets. • Own customer onboarding and handoff by coordinating with Operations/Account Management. • Set clear expectations with customers on scope, timelines, and success criteria. • Ensure early customer success by staying engaged during initial execution phases. Prerequisites: • 6+ months experience as an SDR or 12+ months of sales experience in a previous role. • Demonstrated ability to manage multiple opportunities concurrently in a structured sales process. • Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with confidence engaging customers by phone and video. • A customer-first mindset with a bias toward execution and accountability. • Intellectual curiosity and willingness to learn a complex industry. • Fluent in English; additional local language skills preferred depending on region.
