Team Manager Infrastructure Office

Atlas Copco Industrial Technique Aktiebolag · Nacka, Sweden

💰 ca. 450 000–550 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 29.11.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Your role We are looking for a leader who is passionate about developing people, creating structure, and driving continuous improvement in a complex technical environment. As Team Manager Infrastructure Office, you will lead a team of highly skilled specialists organized in two Scrum teams. The team plays a critical role in enabling and supporting one of Atlas Copco's strategic software platforms, providing the infrastructure, tools, and expertise needed to develop the next generation of connected products. This is a leadership role where your success will be measured by your ability to develop people, create structure, drive continuous improvement, and ensure delivery in a business-critical environment. Your responsibilities will include: Leading, coaching, and developing the team and its individuals Driving process development, quality assurance, and operational excellence Managing recruitment, competence development, and resource planning Overseeing the infrastructure, tools, and platform capabilities that support software development across R&D Ensuring efficient operation of server infrastructure used for testing and software compilation Supporting production testing and helping resolve business-critical customer issues where hardware and software intersect Securing and developing key competencies within the team, including specialized Wi-Fi expertise Collaborating with stakeholders across R&D to support current and future platform needs You will be part of the department management team and contribute to the continued development of our software platforms, engineering capabilities, and ways of working. The Infrastructure Office is part of a newly formed department within R&D. Your skills and experience We believe you are an experienced leader who enjoys building high-performing teams and creating clarity in complex environments. You bring: Experience leading teams as a manager, project leader, or similar leadership role A strong ability to drive change, improve processes, and establish efficient ways of working Experience working in software development organizations Understanding of embedded software development, Devops and Linux-based environments Experience working with agile teams and Scrum methodologies Strong communication, planning, and stakeholder management skills Excellent English communication skills, both written and spoken As a person, you are structured, collaborative, and able to balance operational priorities with long-term development. You empower others, build trust, and create the conditions for your team to succeed. You are comfortable leading experienced specialists and are able to navigate both strategic discussions and urgent operational challenges. Your job location This position is based in Nacka, Stockholm, Sweden. Extensive collaboration takes place across our global R&D organization, providing opportunities to work with colleagues around the world. Application process Our selection process includes interviews, skills assessments and a background check. We look forward to getting to know you, learning about your strengths, and discussing how you can grow with us.
