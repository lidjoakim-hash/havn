ABOUT WATERSHED Watershed https://watershed.com/ is the enterprise sustainability platform. Companies like Airbnb, Carlyle Group, FedEx, Visa, and Dr. Martens use Watershed to manage climate and ESG data, produce audit-ready metrics for voluntary and regulatory reporting including CSRD, and drive real decarbonization. We are looking for team members who love product-building, want to work hard at a mission-oriented startup, and will collaborate with us in shaping the culture of a growing team. We have offices in San Francisco, New York, Denver, London, Paris, Berlin, Sydney, Mexico City, and remote team members across the US and Europe. We hope that you'll be interested in joining us! THE ROLE Forward Deployed Energy Engineers are a new role at Watershed, combining energy and advisory expertise with product-building skills and commercial instinct. You’ll operate at the cutting edge with Watershed customers to build and deploy the next generation of enterprise energy management. Energy is one of the largest costs on a big company’s P&L and one of its largest sources of emissions. Yet most enterprises have no one who owns how energy is purchased, consumed and reduced across all their sites. Watershed is building the product that changes that, and it’s the company’s biggest bet for its next chapter. We already help many of the world’s largest companies measure and cut their emissions, and our clean energy business has executed more than $100M in purchases. Now we’re building energy management from zero to one, with engagements underway with a small group of customers today and scaling in 2027. You’ll work with enterprises that spend tens of millions a year on electricity, embedded in their energy programmes. Starting from the energy data already in Watershed, you’ll find where they’re overspending, deliver the savings, prototype with AI what should become product, and help customers implement solutions, including buying clean power. The goal is an energy programme that wins backing from their CFO and facilities teams. This is a high-ownership role on a small, high-output team, with a direct line to commercial outcomes and real say in what we build. It reports directly to Watershed’s head of energy and decarbonization. What you'll do - Deliver energy engagements with measurable results: Run deep engagements with a handful of customers at a time: change tariffs, upgrade equipment, find and cut waste, and show the savings. - Win customers who vouch for us: Earn the trust of facilities and finance leads so they would tell a peer to work with Watershed. - Turn what works into product: Spot the problems that repeat across customers, prototype with AI, and work with our engineers to build an AI-led product for many. - Close clean energy deals: Help customers sign deals with existing clean power products (eg EACs, fixed-price VPPAs), yourself or alongside our account teams. - Make the team smarter on energy: Be the person colleagues go to on tariffs, loads and what actually cuts a bill. You might be a fit if you - Know how commercial and industrial sites use and buy energy (tariffs, loads, HVAC and controls, procurement) from time at an ESCO, an energy consultancy, an engineering firm or an energy-tech company. - Have built things: scripts, models, prototypes or AI-assisted workflows, and can show us one. Prototype-quality code is enough; moving fast matters most. - Are fluent with data tools (Excel, SQL or Python) and LLM tools, and can turn messy meter and bill data into something a customer acts on. - Have run client work from scoping to outcome, and are as credible with a facilities manager as with a CFO. - Can tell a compelling story with data, from raw numbers to insight to a recommendation someone signs off.
