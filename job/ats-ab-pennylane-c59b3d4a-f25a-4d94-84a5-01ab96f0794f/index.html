We are currently operating approximately 30k business accounts under a BaaS model with Swan. The role of Financial Services in our strategy is set to grow, both in terms of revenue and product contribution, to serve our 800k current customer base. As such, we are initiating a process to get licensed as Etablissement de paiement et Société de financement. Our ambition is to start onboarding new customers by mid 2027 before starting migrating existing customers. To meet this ambition, we are starting to hire our Tech, Product, Compliance, Legal and Ops leaders. We are looking for a Lead Legal Counsel to drive the legal function of our Financial Services activity and build our regulatory legal team. From the day you join until we are fully licensed, you will structure and execute the legal path to authorization: interpret requirements, translate them into an internal plan, and ensure deliverables are complete, consistent, and aligned across teams. You will help define the target operating model (intra-group and outsourced) and ensure it is legally defensible and well documented for the application. After licensing, you will shift from “building the file” to running and scaling the legal function of the regulated entity: ensuring ongoing compliance with ACPR expectations, enabling product growth safely, and keeping the operating model (including outsourcing) continuously audit-ready. You will own the legal oversight of day-to-day legal and corporate matters (policies, governance, reporting support, inspections), lead the legal work for launching new regulated products and entering new partnerships, and manage the legal aspects of onboarding and migrating customers to the licensed subsidiary (terms, communications, complaints/conduct risk).
