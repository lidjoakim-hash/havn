About the Role As Fivetran scales its Enterprise business, we're looking for a senior individual contributor – with a strong bias towards AI automation – to own the International Demand Generation across EMEA and APAC. You’ll build and run the always-on 1-to-many ABM engine for Enterprise accounts in EMEA and APAC: agent-driven workflows that turn account signals (intent, tech stack, CRM stage) into campaigns, creative, and landing pages.
