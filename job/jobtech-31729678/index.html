Pizza Chef

Restaurang Basta Falun AB · Falun, Sweden

Dream Big - Join Basta team in Falun! 🍕 About Basta Basta is more than a restaurant—it's a place where people connect. Inspired by the warmth and spirit of Italy, we bring together great food, genuine hospitality and passionate teams to create memorable experiences for every guest. Whether you're in the kitchen or on the floor, you'll be part of a team that brings energy, personality and pride to everything it does. Part of Urban Italian Group Urban Italian Group is one of Europe's fastest-growing hospitality groups, bringing together distinctive restaurant brands, passionate people and memorable guest experiences. As we continue to grow, so do the opportunities for those who want to build their careers with us. The Role As a Pizza Chef, you’ll prepare and cook pizzas to Basta standards while keeping quality, consistency and speed at the centre of every service. You’ll work closely with the kitchen team in a high-volume environment where guests can see the passion and skill behind every dish. What you'll be doing Preparing, stretching, topping and baking pizzas according to our recipes and standards. Delivering consistent quality and speed during busy services. Completing daily preparation and keeping your section organised and ready. Handling ingredients correctly, following allergen procedures and reducing waste. Maintaining excellent hygiene, food-safety and cleanliness standards. Communicating clearly and supporting the wider kitchen team throughout service. You'll thrive in this role if you Has experience working with pizza, dough or in a busy professional kitchen. Can stay focused and consistent during high-volume service. Is reliable, organised and willing to learn. Takes feedback positively and applies it quickly. Works well with others and communicates calmly under pressure. Cares about quality, cleanliness and the guest experience. Our culture and values We believe we work with people, not food. Guided by our values of Trust, Inclusion, Passion and Excellence, we’re committed to creating a workplace where everyone feels welcome, valued and empowered to grow. What do we offer? Be part of one of Europe’s fastest-growing hospitality groups. Build your career across multiple brands and countries. Work alongside talented people who genuinely love hospitality. International career opportunities. Referral bonus scheme. Shared accommodation (where applicable). Friends for life. Sound like your kind of place? If you are excited by joining us, we would love to hear from you. Bring your energy, personality and passion for Italian food to Basta. Urban Italian Group is committed to fair and inclusive recruitment. We welcome applications from people of all backgrounds. If you need a reasonable adjustment at any stage of the selection process, please tell our Talent Acquisition Team.
