ABOUT THE ROLE In this position you'll own performance analytics for Nordic market and help its Country Director grow the business. That covers every function: marketing, CRM, product and operations, and how together they drive revenue. You'll work with the Country Director and their functional leaders every week. You'll run the monthly and quarterly business reviews and explain to the Executive Committee how the market has performed. You'll help shape the annual strategy, and you'll challenge the Country Director when the numbers point somewhere else. The Nordics is a priority for the Group, so your work will be seen. You' will be a part of the senior performance analytics team, and the role gives you plenty of room to grow.
