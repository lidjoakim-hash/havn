At Toyota Material Handling Europe, we create the technology that keeps the world moving. With over 13,500 colleagues, we are passionate about supporting companies of all sizes in meeting today's and tomorrow's material handling challenges. We recognize that our industry is essential-and often critical-to everyday life and society as a whole. From our headquarters in Mjölby (Sweden) and Willebroek (Belgium), to our sales companies across Europe and our factories in France, Italy, and Sweden, you can be part of an extraordinary journey. Together, we are moving the world towards easier, more sustainable solutions. The Position As an Industrial Designer at Toyota Material Handling Europe's Design Center, you will shape the future of our products by developing market-leading designs across our portfolio while contributing to advanced concepts and future logistic solutions. You will transform insights, ideas, and technical requirements into compelling products that reflect the Toyota brand and deliver strong commercial value. Working closely with engineering teams, project managers, suppliers, and other key stakeholders, you will balance design excellence with functionality, manufacturability, and business objectives. Your responsibility Product Design & Development: Create and develop design concepts into feasible product solutions and detailed designs. Ensure products are functional and intuitive based on user needs and requirements. Design Implementation: Evaluate concepts through digital models and mock-ups, present proposals, and support engineering implementation. Brand & Design Identity: Maintain a consistent design language and contribute to naming and graphics. Project Coordination & Collaboration: Lead design activities and align stakeholders across functions and departments. Design Research: Monitor trends, competitors, materials, and technologies while supporting production, cost, and commercial considerations. We are looking for a creative, driven, and passionate Designer with a genuine love for design and innovation. You are excited by the opportunity to shape future products, create compelling design solutions, and bring ideas to life through strong form, detail, and craftsmanship. A Bachelor of Fine Arts (BFA), Master of Fine Arts (MFA), or equivalent degree in Industrial Design, Transportation Design, Product Design, or a related discipline is required. In addition, you bring at least 3 years of professional design experience, with a proven ability to develop products from concept to production. Experience in automotive interior design or a similar field is considered a strong advantage. We believe you bring: Strong skills in Alias, Adobe Creative Suite, and both hand and digital sketching. Able to translate ideas into compelling design concepts through sketching, rendering, and 3D visualization. Good knowledge of engineering principles, materials, manufacturing processes, and product development. Analytical and innovative, with the ability to transform user needs and market insights into relevant design solutions while balancing aesthetics, functionality, feasibility, and cost. Strong presentation and communication skills, with the ability to drive design projects and collaborate effectively in an international, cross-functional environment. Fluent English is required. Our Offer At Toyota Material Handling Europe, we're more than a global leader in a fast-paced industry-we're a collaborative team driven by innovation, continuous growth, and creating real value for our customers. Our diverse and international environment offers a workplace where your ideas are valued, and your contributions can make a meaningful impact. This position is based at our Design Studio in Mjölby, Sweden, with the opportunity to work in a hybrid arrangement, combining on-site and remote work.
