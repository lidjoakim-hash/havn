We're Tibber - the energy company that wants you to use less energy. When you join Tibber, you'll be part of building the tech that creates a real shift in how people consume electricity - good for our users, their wallets and the planet. We believe AI is how we accelerate that shift, and it's our people who lead it. The more we lean into AI, the more human judgment, curiosity and creativity matter. We're looking for a Senior Product Lead to join us – maybe that's you? Job Mission 🚀 As a Senior Product Lead, you'll play a key role in making our entry offers & plans, the first choice for homeowners switching energy supplier. You'll own the offer across Sweden, Norway, Germany and the Netherlands: what it is, who it's for, why it wins and how it makes money. Leading work across product managers in several capabilities, you'll turn our entry offering into plans that win in the market, and grow customers into premium plans, directly or through electrification. What you'll do ✅ • Build a clear view of which homeowners choose our entry offers and why they switch, by market and channel. Make it the basis for segmenting, targeting and pricing. • Set strategy and roadmap for entry offers & plans across four markets and every distribution channel. • Shape the winning offer for each channel: its components, target customer, value proposition and how we win. • Own the pricing guardrails, so Sales has clear guardrails for setting final market prices. • Set go-to-market direction, and build and coordinate product launches together with Commercial. • Track performance against clear success metrics, then evolve, reprice or retire offers based on evidence. • Anchor the roadmap with the commercial teams, our other offerings product leads & capability owners and lead the work across the product managers who build it. What we're looking for⚡ You are driven by impact and know how to turn strategy into execution. You're used to fast-paced environments and thrive when given clear goals and high accountability. We believe you'll thrive here if you: • Have owned a product line or a multi-team product area, and have led and grown product managers along the way. • Think commercially. You're fluent in unit economics, value pools, pricing logic and switching economics, and can defend an investment case to Finance and leadership. • Know energy markets, tariffs and home electrification. Or you can master them quickly, coming from a switching-driven market like price comparison, telco or insurance. Experience of the Nordic, German or Dutch markets and their languages is a strong plus. • Have taken products to market in a B2C subscription business, hand in hand with Commercial across comparison sites, performance, organic and partner channels. • Anchor every decision in real customer need, data and measurable outcomes, never in feature lists. • Bring engineers, Commercial and executives together behind one clear direction, in plain language and without formal authority. Tibber becomes more AI-enabled and we embrace new tools and ways of working. You don't need to be an expert, but you're excited to shape how AI shows up in our offers and to grow with it. The Tibber Mindset 💆🏻‍♀️💆🏽‍♂️ Tibber exists to drive energy independence – away from fossil dependency. Our tech helps hundreds of thousands of households shift their electricity consumption to more sustainable and affordable hours, strengthening the resilience of the grid. Not in theory. In everyday life. Being on a mission to change an industry means being prepared for the unexpected. We do what others say can't be done – and we do it together. We win, fail and grow together, staying curious and pushing for better - every day. AI is shaping how we work - and we believe it's how we accelerate the energy transition.
