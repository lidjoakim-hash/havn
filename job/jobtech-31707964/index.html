BAT is evolving at pace into a global multi-category business. Our purpose is to create A Better Tomorrow™ by Building a Smokeless World. To achieve our ambition, we are looking for colleagues who are ready to join us on this journey! Tomorrow can’t wait, let’s shape it together! British American Tobacco Sweden has an exciting opportunity for an Operations Finance Controller in Malmö. We're looking for a driven finance professional to join our Operations Finance team at BAT. You'll be at the centre of our month-end and budget cycles. You'll make sure our books are accurate, our numbers are explained, and our stakeholders have the insight they need to make decisions. If you like owning results, challenging the way things are done and working in a fast-moving FMCG environment, this role is for you. Your key responsibilities will include: Act as the Finance Business Partner for Manufacturing, QA, Logistics and other factory functions, providing financial insights and supporting operational decision-making. Lead monthly performance reporting and analysis, translating P&L results, manufacturing costs and key business drivers into actionable recommendations for site leadership. Coordinate budgeting, forecasting and planning processes, including OPEX, CAPEX, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow activities, while supporting the WOFF process and cost-saving initiatives. Ensure effective management of fixed assets, intercompany recharges, cost allocations, master data and other core controlling activities to support accurate financial reporting. Deliver timely and accurate month-end and year-end closing activities, including accruals, reconciliations, compliance controls and support for internal, external and tax audits. What are we looking for? Degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics or a related field, together with a relevant professional finance qualification. 1-3 years of experience in Finance within FMCG, manufacturing or another fast-paced operational environment, with exposure to financial planning, analysis and business partnering. Strong knowledge of financial systems and reporting tools, including advanced Excel skills; experience with SAP, BPC and BI tools is highly desirable. Excellent analytical, problem-solving and organisational skills, with the ability to manage large volumes of information, prioritise effectively and deliver accurate results under tight deadlines. Strong stakeholder management and communication skills, with the ability to influence business partners, present financial insights clearly and communicate fluently in English. What we offer you?
