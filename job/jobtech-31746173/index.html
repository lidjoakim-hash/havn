Scientist (temp cover) Analytical Chemistry and HPLC Nordic BioAnalysis AB | Södertälje, Sweden Are you an experienced analytical scientist with strong hands-on HPLC expertise? Join NBAB and take a key role in developing robust analytical methods and delivering reliable results for pharmaceutical, biotechnology and medical device clients. About NBAB Nordic BioAnalysis AB (NBAB) is a GMP-certified analytical laboratory with ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation for a defined scope of methods. Established in 2010 and based in Södertälje, we provide chemical, biochemical, microbiological and molecular biological analysis of materials and products, from development through to commercialisation. Our international team brings together experienced scientists, technicians and project managers committed to quality, scientific integrity and responsive customer service. About the role We are looking for a Senior Scientist with at least five years of hands-on analytical laboratory experience in a GMP and/or ISO-regulated environment, with a strong focus on HPLC. This is a practical laboratory role with substantial responsibility for HPLC method development, transfer, validation, troubleshooting and routine analysis. You will work independently, contribute scientific expertise to customer projects and help colleagues resolve analytical challenges. You will join a small, collaborative company where your expertise and ideas can influence laboratory practices and the company’s development. Your responsibilities · Develop and optimise HPLC methods, including sample preparation, separation conditions, detection and system suitability. · Plan, execute and document analytical method transfers, validation and verification studies. · Perform routine HPLC analysis of raw materials, active substances and finished products to support quality control, release testing and stability studies. · Investigate chromatographic issues, including poor separation, retention-time shifts, peak shape, carryover and inconsistent results. · Perform routine analysis such as TOC, Water content with KF and other wet chemistry analysis. · Evaluate analytical data, interpret results and prepare clear protocols, reports and laboratory records with full traceability. · Contribute to laboratory investigations, root-cause analysis and corrective actions in collaboration with quality colleagues. · Take responsibility for assigned instruments, including maintenance, calibration and qualification activities. · Provide technical guidance and share knowledge with colleagues. · Collaborate with project and quality teams to deliver accurate results within agreed timelines. Your experience and qualifications Essential · At least five years of hands-on experience in an analytical laboratory operating under GMP and/or an ISO laboratory quality system, particularly ISO/IEC 17025. · Extensive practical HPLC and GC experience, with demonstrated ability to develop, transfer, validate and troubleshoot methods, as well as perform routine analysis. · A university degree in chemistry, analytical chemistry, biochemistry, pharmaceutical sciences or a closely related discipline. · Strong understanding of laboratory documentation, data integrity, traceability and working to approved procedures. · The ability to plan and perform laboratory work independently, assess results critically and solve analytical problems. · Full professional proficiency in English. An advantage · Experience with HPLC-UV and HPLC-RI. · Experience with reversed-phase, size-exclusion or ion-exchange chromatography. · Experience with pharmacopoeial methods and pharmaceutical or medical device testing. · Additional skills in Karl Fischer titration, TOC, IR, UV/Vis or wet chemistry. · Experience mentoring colleagues or coordinating analytical projects. · Knowledge of Swedish.
