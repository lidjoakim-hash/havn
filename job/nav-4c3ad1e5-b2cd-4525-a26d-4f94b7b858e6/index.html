Site Supervisor – Data Centre Projects

Fortica · Bjerkvik, Norway

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Lead the work where it happens. Fortica builds and finishes data centres across Norway and the Nordics. As Site Supervisor you lead the daily work on site and make sure it is done safely, on time and to the right quality. What you will do • Supervise and coordinate daily work on site: our own crews and subcontractors • Make sure the work follows the drawings, the plan and the quality requirements • Do site inspections and find quality, safety or schedule problems early • Follow the safety rules together with the Site Manager and HSE Advisor • Keep site records and progress reports up to date • Take part in coordination meetings with the client and other contractors Our scope on site: ceilings, sandwich panels (insulated wall and roof panels), flashing, penetration sealing, finishing works and site services. Who you are • 2&#43; years' experience as a supervisor or foreman in construction • Experience from data centres, industrial or commercial buildings is a plus • You can read construction drawings and follow specifications • You know safety and quality on a construction site • Good English, spoken and written • Driving licence class B • Flexible and mobile: willing to travel and work on projects in Norway and abroad What makes you a good fit You are hands-on and organised. You give clear instructions, follow up your team and speak up when something is not right. Safety and quality always come first for you. What we offer • 3:1 rotation (3 weeks on, 1 week off) with flights, accommodation and meals covered • 4 % pension and health insurance • We help you get your HMS card (the Norwegian ID card for everyone working on a construction site) if you do not have one • Real responsibility on large, modern projects • A safe workplace with fair terms and a written employment contract • Part of CTS Group, one of Europe's leading data centre design and build groups You report to: Site Manager Locations: Narvik, Kristiansand and Fyresdal, Norway
