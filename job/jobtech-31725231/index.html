Description of workplaceThe research group is led by Associated professor Sandra Lindstedt and has a long-standing interest in research related to lung transplantation, post-transplant complications, and lung regeneration. The group focuses on developing novel therapeutic strategies to treat lung injury, improve donor organ quality, and reduce complications following lung transplantation. A particular focus is on acute respiratory distress syndrome (ARDS), primary graft dysfunction (PGD), and regenerative approaches to lung repair. We offerLund University is a governmental authority, which means you get special benefits, generous holidays, and a favorable occupational pension. Read more on the university's website about being employed at Lund University, Work at Lund University Work descriptionA associate researcher position is available within the Lindstedt research group, focusing on translational research in lung transplantation, lung injury, and regenerative medicine. The duties at the research unit primarily consist of experimental and laboratory-based research, with a particular focus on the development and evaluation of novel therapeutic strategies to improve lung repair and transplantation outcomes. The main responsibilities include: independently designing, planning, coordinating, and conducting research projects, as well as collecting, analyzing, interpreting, and managing experimental data. The work involves the development and optimization of experimental protocols, evaluation of therapeutic interventions, and application of cellular, molecular, ultra-high-plexed immunofluorescence and histological techniques to investigate mechanisms of lung injury, repair, and regeneration. The position also includes responsibility for preparing high-quality scientific manuscripts for publication in international peer-reviewed journals, contributing to research grant applications, and presenting research findings at national and international scientific conferences. The majority of the work will be conducted independently, while also involving close collaboration with internal and external research partners and clinical collaborators. In addition, the position includes mentorship and supervision of doctoral students, Bachelor’s and Master’s students, and other junior researchers, as well as contributing to the coordination and development of ongoing research activities within the group.
