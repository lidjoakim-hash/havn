This role, in a nutshell: Our international business has moved past the proving stage. The teams, the sales motion and the early wins are in place across EMEA, but still very much being built out in META (Middle East and Africa) and APJ. The operational foundation now needs to scale with them. We are hiring a Senior Revenue Operations Manager to be the hands-on operator behind planning, forecasting, territory design, reporting and process quality for these regions. This is an individual contributor role with a broad remit. You will work directly with regional sales leadership, report into the Senior Director of Revenue Operations EMEA, and apply global standards and systems to the realities of selling across multiple countries, segments and time zones. You will also be the person who raises it when a global standard does not fit a region, with a proposal for what to do about it.
