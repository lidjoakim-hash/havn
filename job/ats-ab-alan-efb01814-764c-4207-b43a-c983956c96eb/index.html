The Role As an Insurance Compliance Counsel at Alan, you will help Alan meet its insurance compliance and consumer protection obligations. You will work with the teammates who build our tools, processes and products to turn regulatory requirements into how they actually work. Working closely with senior members of the compliance team, you will run key parts of our compliance work day to day, flag risks early, and prepare the material we share with regulators on the topics you cover. You will also help connect the compliance function with internal control and Alan’s audit processes. That means tracking audit findings, following remediation through to completion, and making sure the fixes become part of how we operate. As Alan expands into new markets, you will help our compliance standards travel with us by adapting Group frameworks to local requirements, working with local counsel. The role sits withinthe Corporate Unit. You will work closely every day with the Legal, Insurance, Operations, Sales, Care and Product teammates across all of Alan’s markets. It’s a great opportunity to build broad compliance expertise and take on more ownership over time.
