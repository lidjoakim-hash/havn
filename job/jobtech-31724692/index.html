Client Service Associate Build a Long-Term Career in Finance (London)

Are you a Dutch speaker looking for an opportunity to develop professionally, learn new skills and build a long-term career within the financial services industry? Our client, a leading international investment management company, is looking for a motivated and detail-oriented Dutch Client Service Associate to join their growing team. This is an excellent opportunity for someone who enjoys working behind the scenes, supporting clients and business operations, and is eager to grow within an international organisation. No finance background is required – what matters most is your motivation, willingness to learn and ambition to develop. The Role As a Client Service Associate, you will play an important role in supporting the company's daily operations and ensuring a high level of service for its Dutch-speaking clients. This is primarily a back-office and administrative role, involving collaboration with different teams and financial institutions across Europe. Your responsibilities will include: Handling administrative and operational tasks in collaboration with leading banks and financial institutions. Supporting internal teams with client-related inquiries and daily operations. Preparing reports and maintaining accurate client information. Working closely with colleagues across different departments and European markets. Identifying opportunities to improve processes and contribute new ideas. Gradually taking on additional responsibilities as you develop within the role. Career Development What makes this opportunity particularly interesting is the potential for long-term career growth. Our client believes in developing talent from within and offers comprehensive training, ongoing support and opportunities to explore different career paths across the business. You will be encouraged to take initiative, develop new skills and take on greater responsibilities as you progress. Whether your future interests lie in operations, management, analytics or other areas of the business, there are opportunities to shape your own career path. Our client wants you to grow with the company and chart a course for a rewarding, long-term career. Your Profile We are looking for someone who is motivated, organised and genuinely interested in the position and the opportunity it offers. To be successful in this role, you should have: Native or fluent Dutch and good English skills, both written and spoken. A university degree or equivalent combination of education and experience. Strong communication and organisational skills. A structured and detail-oriented approach to your work. A collaborative mindset and the ability to work well within a team. A proactive attitude and willingness to learn and develop. An interest in building a long-term career within an international business environment. A background in finance, economics or business is an advantage but not a requirement. The Offer Full-time, permanent position. Competitive salary and benefits package. Comprehensive training and ongoing professional development. A collaborative and international working environment. Excellent opportunities for internal career progression. The chance to build a rewarding, long-term career within a growing international organisation. Location: London, United Kingdom (office-based). Interested? If you are a Dutch speaker looking for more than just your next job, and are excited about the opportunity to learn, develop and grow within an international company, we would love to hear from you! Apply today and take the next step towards building your future career.
