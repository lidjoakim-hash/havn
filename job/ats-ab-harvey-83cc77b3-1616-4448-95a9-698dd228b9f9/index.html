WHY HARVEY At Harvey, we’re transforming how legal and professional services operate. By combining frontier agentic AI, an enterprise-grade platform, and deep domain expertise, we’re reshaping how critical knowledge work gets done for decades to come. This is a rare chance to help build a generational company at a true inflection point. We have strong product-market fit and world-class investor support. We’re scaling fast and defining a new category in real time. The work is ambitious, the bar is high, and the opportunity for growth — personal, professional, and financial — is unmatched. Our team moves fast, takes ownership, and is deeply committed to the mission — operating with intensity, staying close to our customers, and pushing each other for excellence. We live by three values: Decisiveness, Simplicity, and Job's Not Finished. We act quickly on clear judgment over perfect information, we believe simplicity is what scales, and we're never satisfied with where we are. If you want to do the best work of your career alongside people who share that drive, we'd love to build with you. At Harvey, the future of professional services is being written today — and we’re just getting started. ROLE OVERVIEW Harvey is looking for a Compensation Business Partner to ensure our compensation philosophy, principles, and practices extend to all of our markets outside of the US, initially focused on EMEA and APAC. This will be our first Compensation Business Partner dedicated to our international business sitting within the EMEA region, and an opportunity to help shape how Harvey approaches compensation as we continue to scale globally. You’ll partner closely with our People Business Partners, Recruiting team, business leaders, and the broader Compensation team to ensure our programs remain competitive, thoughtful, and scalable across markets. We’re looking for someone who combines deep compensation expertise with strong business judgment. You should be equally comfortable analyzing market data, advising a recruiter on a complex offer, partnering with a PBP and developing a point of view on how Harvey should approach compensation in a new or rapidly evolving market. This is a highly collaborative role that will have a great degree of partnership across many functions. WHAT YOU'LL DO - Serve as the primary Compensation Business Partner for EMEA and APAC, developing deep context on our teams, talent markets, and hiring needs across the regions. - Partner closely with People Business Partners and business leaders on compensation decisions including new hires, promotions, internal mobility, and other complex compensation questions. - Build a particularly strong partnership with Recruiting, advising on offers, market competitiveness, and candidate-specific situations. - Help evolve Harvey's international compensation strategy as we scale, including our approach to geographic differentiation, market positioning, salary structures, and equity. - Bring deep equity expertise and help translate Harvey's equity philosophy across markets where candidate expectations and familiarity with US-style equity compensation can vary significantly. - Research emerging compensation practices and talent trends, particularly across the rapidly evolving AI market, and turn external data into clear recommendations for Harvey. - Conduct market analyses using compensation survey data and other sources, while applying judgment. - Partner with the broader Compensation team on compensation cycles, benchmarking, band development, and company-wide programs, ensuring international considerations are represented from the beginning. - Identify where our US practices translate internationally—and where they don't—and proactively recommend changes as Harvey enters and scales in new markets. - Help build the infrastructure, principles, and enablement that will allow Harvey's international compensation approach to scale. WHAT YOU HAVE - 5-7+
