Electronics Engineer, R&D Stockholm Want to start your engineering career by designing the electronics inside network cameras used around the world? We're looking for an Electronics Engineer to join our growing R&D site in central Stockholm. You'll work with electronics from the first concept all the way to a finished product that's ready to sell. Location: Stockholm, Sweden (on-site) | Employment: Permanent, full-time Who is your future team? Our Stockholm R&D site has grown to more than 50 colleagues. We're still early in our journey but we are growing fast. That means you can help shape how we work while being part of the world leader in network video. We develop network-based surveillance cameras. We work in projects together with engineers and designers from other departments such as mechanics, firmware and imaging engineers. We keep processes and control to a minimum, and ideas are welcome from everyone, not just from the top. Here, your voice is heard. What you'll do as an Electronics Engineer As an Electronics Engineer at Axis, you play a central role in designing, testing and certifying the electronics in products that help make the world safer and more secure. You'll be part of the project from concept to production, until the product you and your team built is ready to be sold. Projects vary a lot. They range from 3-month concept studies, for example in sensor technology, to 2-year projects developing advanced cameras. Your work will include: Designing electronics, including system design, schematics and component selection Testing and verifying your designs hands-on in the lab, including soldering Certifying the electronics so our products meet the required standards Working across disciplines with engineers and designers from other departments Following the product from concept to production You'll get a lot of responsibility and freedom from the start. Your mentor and experienced colleagues will support you as you grow into the role. Who are you? You're curious with an interest in technology, and electronics is what you're most passionate about. You like to take initiative and enjoy working independently. You find it easy to reach out to people and build contacts. You're thorough and take the time to understand the problem you're solving, both the big picture and the details. You enjoy sharing what you know, you stay humble, and you're motivated by working in an environment that keeps changing. What you need: A university degree in a technical field, preferably electronics A genuine interest in electronics development Professional proficiency in English Bonus points for experience with any of these areas: Soldering and modifications at PCB (printed circuit board) level Electronics measuring on PCB using oscilloscope and multimeter Testing and certification related to EMC (electromagnetic compatibility), ESD (electrostatic discharge), safety, etc. Knowledge of production What Axis has to offer We're a world leader in network video, where cutting-edge technology meets global impact. You'll work on meaningful projects that shape the future of security and surveillance, developing solutions used worldwide. As a fast-growing company, we offer exciting career opportunities. You'll grow professionally through continuous learning, supported by a team that values creativity, innovation and work-life balance. This is a unique opportunity to help build our R&D office in central Stockholm. You'll get the advantages of a small, agile site while also being part of a successful global organization. Your well-being matters to us. Our benefits include a company bonus, Friday cake, a wellness allowance, health insurance, and even your own Axis bicycle. Curious to discover more?
