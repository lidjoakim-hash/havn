Scania is undergoing a transformation from being a leading supplier of trucks, buses, and engines to providing complete and sustainable transport solutions. Together with TRATON and our sister brands MAN, Volkswagen Truck & Bus, and International, we are working to shape the future of mobility with innovative and environmentally conscious solutions. Our values – customer first, respect, team spirit, responsibility, and elimination of waste – are at the heart of everything we do. Together, we are at the forefront of creating a sustainable future. Role Summary As a Project Coordinator, you will play a key role in supporting a large-scale People & Culture transformation project at Scania. You will work closely with the Program Manager to coordinate project activities, monitor progress, prepare management reporting, and ensure alignment across multiple workstreams. The role combines hands-on project coordination with exposure to program management, governance activities, and strategic decision-making. This is an opportunity to contribute to a major HR transformation while developing your project management expertise in a dynamic and cross-functional environment. Job Responsibilities Support project planning, coordination, and follow-up activities across multiple workstreams. Collect, consolidate, and analyze project data, progress updates, and KPIs. Prepare reports, presentations, and decision-making materials for management and governance forums. Maintain project documentation, including project plans, risk registers, issue logs, and decision logs. Coordinate project meetings, workshops, and stakeholder forums. Monitor milestones, dependencies, risks, and deliverables together with business and IT stakeholders. Support HR systems implementation, enhancement, and decommissioning activities. Follow up on actions and help drive project activities forward. Contribute to continuous improvements in project governance, reporting, and ways of working. Who You Are Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Human Resources, Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related field. Minimum 3 years of experience in project coordination, project management, or a similar role. Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint. Strong analytical skills with the ability to structure, analyze, and present data. Experience preparing management presentations and project reporting materials. Strong communication skills and experience presenting to senior management. Ability to manage multiple priorities in a dynamic environment. Excellent stakeholder management and collaboration skills. Fluent in English, both written and spoken. This Is Us You will join a team driving the modernization of Scania's HR system landscape and People & Culture processes. Together with colleagues from HR, IT, Analytics, Process, Architecture, and Payroll, you will contribute to delivering sustainable improvements that simplify the employee experience and support Scania's strategic transformation journey. We value collaboration, continuous improvement, knowledge sharing, and a proactive approach to solving challenges. Scania Offers We offer a dynamic and engaging workplace where collaboration, innovation, and continuous improvement are part of everyday life. You will be part of a strong team environment that encourages knowledge sharing and close cooperation across functions. With a structured development plan and a wide range of training opportunities, Scania supports your professional growth both locally and internationally. Benefits include access to our health center in Gröndal or a wellness allowance, result bonus, flexible working hours, and company car leasing. Scania also arranges events for employees and their families, and for those living in Stockholm, convenient commuting is supported through direct Scania Job express buses to Södertälje.
