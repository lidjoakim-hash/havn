From your first day at Roku, you'll make a valuable - and valued - contribution. We're a fast-growing public company where no one is a bystander. About the team As the primary operational relationship layer between Roku and its Tier 1 content and development partners, we launch, grow, and sustain best-in-class streaming apps across the app lifecycle, from onboarding to EOL. We equally protect Roku’s platform integrity and monetization objectives and advocate for the best viewer experience, while helping partners reach and retain Roku viewers globally. Partner Engagement collaborates closely with cross-functional teams within Roku, including Content Distribution, Business Development, Commerce, Advertising, Product, and Engineering. About the role You will own a portfolio of partners across the UK, Ireland, France, Germany, Austria and other EU markets: public service and commercial broadcasters, subscription streaming services, pay-TV operators, and the third-party development agencies that build their apps. In Europe, you will work alongside Partner Engineering, Roku’s technical team for partners, including a Senior Partner Engineer based in London, who owns technical diagnosis and sign-off while you own the relationship, launch plan and incident communication. This external-facing role needs strong technical fluency, product sense, alongside excellent communication and stakeholder management skills. Level: Senior individual contributor, with no direct reports. Reports to: Director, Partner Engagement, based in New York. Location and travel: Based in London, in the office Monday to Thursday, with travel of up to 40% across the UK and Europe. Expected Impact: Partners’ apps in the UK and Europe launch on Roku on schedule and stay healthy after launch, Tier 1 tentpole events run without partner-visible incidents, and partners adopt new Roku platform features and follow Roku’s roadmap recommendations.
