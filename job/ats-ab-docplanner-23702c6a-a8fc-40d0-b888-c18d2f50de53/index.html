Job Description This role is remote within Brazil Your main project is the listing-based pricing rollout across markets. You'll diagnose what's working, propose fixes, get local and global teams on board, and roll out to new countries with clear targets and a steady weekly cadence. You'll also shape how the model evolves for next year. You'll own the ARPU (average revenue per user) target for the segment. Alongside the main project, you'll: - Move the ARPU target through specific pricing moves: tighter discount discipline, package mix shifts, price resets by market. Dig into anomalies fast. - Spot pricing opportunities across the marketplace that no one's looking at, and make the case for them. - Own the segment's pricing recommendations to GTM leadership: propose the moves, defend the trade-offs with data, turn decisions into rollout plans. - Drive alignment across sales, local commercial, and global teams on contested pricing calls, without formal authority. - Generate optimization hypotheses grounded in data. - Design and run behavioral experiments to validate pricing moves before scaling them. - Adjust and follow up on budget assumptions as we learn from the market. - Maintain country price lists in our internal systems. Qualifications What will help you thrive? - 5+ years in pricing, ideally SaaS or marketplace - Direct experience working with GTM or sales leaders on pricing decisions - Thorough with data.
