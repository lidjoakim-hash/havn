ROLE SUMMARY We're looking for a Staff Product Manager to own the AI platform every Photoroom product is built on. Our mission is that any team ships best-in-class GenAI visuals without needing a backend engineer: the primitives, the gateway and the no-code tooling turn adding an AI capability into a configuration step rather than an engineering project. On top of that sits the agentic layer — multi-step visual workflows that generate, check, reason, route and publish on their own. Working closely with engineers, ML researchers, and the product teams who ship on your platform, you'll decide what gets built, why, and how it ships.
