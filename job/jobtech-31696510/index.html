We are looking for a researcher in Psychology for a fixed-term position at the School of Behavioral, Social and Legal Sciences (BSR). The research is within the area of cognitive neuroscience of aging. Subject area The subject area for this position is Psychology. Background The Division of Psychology at Örebro University is part of the School of Behavioural, Social and Legal Sciences (BSR), together with the Divisions of Legal Science, Criminology, and Social Work. Örebro university is now hiring a researcher within the area of cognitive neuroscience of aging, with a particular focus on early life predictors of brain structural and functional connectivity in older age. The research will be conducted within the research environment LEADER (Center for Lifespan Developmental Research), which is headed by Jonas Persson, Professor in Psychology. Duties and responsibilities The research duties include preprocessing and analyses of structural and functional brain imaging data obtained by magnetic resonance imaging. A particular focus will be on analysing diffusion-weighted imaging (DWI) data to assess structural connectivity and brain network properties in older age and how these related to health, cognition, lifestyle and other factors in childhood and adolescence. Analyses of functional connectivity data from resting-state fMRI images and the relationship with early life predictors is also expected. The applicant will also have a leading role in dissemination of the results through international peer-reviewed publication and presentations.   Qualifications Those qualified for the appointment as a researcher are applicants who have obtained a doctoral degree or have a degree from abroad deemed to correspond to a doctoral degree. Assessment criteria A general basis for assessment is that the applicant is able to demonstrate the personal qualities required to successfully perform the duties and responsibilities at hand; that they are able to cooperate with other members of staff; and contribute to the development of university operations. Specific personal abilities that are of great importance for the position is documented expertise in the fields of cognitive aging and brain imaging. Of particular importance is proficiency in analysing structural brain connectivity data, connectome-based modelling and tractography of diffusion-tensor imaging (DTI)/DWI data combined with a documented ability in analysing functional MRI data. Experience of working with longitudinal MRI data is also highly desirable, along with experience in working with software such as Matlab, R and SPM. Information This is a fixed-term full-time position for 12 months. At Örebro University, salary depends on the successful candidate’s qualifications and experience. For more information about the position, contact Jonas Persson +46 19 301428, email: jonas.persson@oru.se, or Head of Unit Terese Glatz, +46 19 301205, email: terese.glatz@oru.se.
