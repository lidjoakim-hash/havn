We are looking for a Product Advisor to join our Navigation team, with a particular focus on K-Bridge and the Navigation portfolio. In this role, you will work at the intersection of product, technology and market, collaborating closely with Engineering, Sales, Delivery and Service. You will help ensure competitive, robust and future-ready navigation solutions throughout the entire product lifecycle.   Key responsibilities As Product Advisor, you will: • Manage and further develop K-Bridge and the Navigation portfolio • Develop and maintain product strategy and roadmaps • Manage products throughout their lifecycle, including upgrades, obsolescence and phase-out • Identify and prioritize product improvements based on customer, market and technology needs • Follow up product requirements, certification, type approval and relevant maritime standards • Support Sales and Delivery with product selection, technical clarifications and customer solutions • Collaborate with Engineering on the prioritization and development of new features and technologies • Contribute to business cases and decision-making related to product investments and technology choices  We are continuously reviewing candidates, apply today!  Qualifications and experience We are looking for someone with: • Relevant technical or maritime education, or equivalent experience • Experience with maritime systems, navigation or technical products • Good understanding of product development and lifecycle management • Ability to understand the relationship between customer needs, technology and commercial priorities • Strong collaboration and communication skills in both Norwegian and Englishx Experience considered an advantage Experience with Integrated Bridge Systems, ECDIS, Radar, INS, Autopilot, maritime sensors, classification or product certification is an advantage. Candidates with Seafaring experience as a Navigation officer, with a certificate of competency will be preferred. You are able to • Take ownership and drive topics towards clarification and decision • Work in a structured and solution-oriented way • Maintain a holistic product and system perspective • Work effectively across disciplines and organizational boundaries • Be curious about technology and constructively challenge established solutions This employment may require a pre-employment check procedure in accordance with company security standards, role or site requirements. Please note that this might affect the offer process or employment. What can we offer? At Kongsberg Maritime, you will have the opportunity to work with advanced maritime navigation systems and contribute to shaping the future direction of the Navigation portfolio. The role offers broad exposure across the organization and the opportunity to influence the entire product journey, from strategy and roadmap to development, certification, delivery and lifecycle management.  Join us in developing navigation solutions for the maritime operations of tomorrow.   Practical information: Work location: Kongsberg or other KM location in Norway For further information about the position, please contact the hiring manager: Thor Olsen, Product Manager. thor.olsen&#64;km.kongsberg.com   Kongsberg Maritime is a global leader in maritime technology. We deliver advanced solutions to the merchant fleet, offshore, fishing, and naval vessels, setting new standards for safe, efficient, and sustainable operations - both for new builds and existing fleets. In close collaboration with customers and research environments, we develop technology that solves real challenges at sea. Today, more than 30,000 vessels operate with our systems on board.
