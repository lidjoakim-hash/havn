About Starburst Starburst delivers enterprise intelligence at scale by giving organizations secure, governed access to all their data, wherever it lives. Built for distributed data environments, Starburst helps enterprises power AI and analytics without the cost and complexity of traditional data consolidation. With open standards including Trino and Apache Iceberg, Starburst enables trusted access to complete enterprise context while helping organizations avoid vendor lock-in. Leading global enterprises trust Starburst to fuel AI, analytics, and enterprise intelligence. Learn more at starburst.ai. About the role We're looking for an HR Manager to be a local culture and talent partner to our EMEA R&D organization. This is a hands-on, locally-embedded role where you’ll build trust with leaders and engineers face-to-face, own the full hire-to-retain lifecycle for the region, provide backup support for ongoing operations in region, and bring a local lens to programs designed centrally. This role will have direct influence on shaping the employee experience and culture in our R&D organization. As an HR Manager, EMEA R&D at Starburst you will drive: Culture & Team Health • Maintain an active, visible presence in the Warsaw office (minimum 3 days/week) and attend key meetings in person, not remotely. • Run regular 1:1s with first-line managers to stay close to team health and emerging feedback themes. • Conduct exit interviews for EMEA & APJ Engineering, analyzing turnover trends to partner with leaders on action plans. • Turn Feedback Survey results and other cultural indicators into specific, tracked action items for EMEA Engineering. • Surface emerging issues to key leadership and maintain a consistent pulse on employee sentiment. Talent & Hiring • Design and run a local hiring and onboarding program tailored to the region, complementary to our global onboarding practices. • Own sourcing and screening for regional engineering roles, building and maintaining a local candidate pipeline. • Design the interview loop in partnership with the Hiring Manager and set clear expectations for interviewer goals and responsibilities. • Manage new hire logistics from start to finish: offers, day 1 readiness, and compliance. • Build local market presence and influence to strengthen our employer brand in the region. • Champion internal mobility opportunities for engineers across the region, while ensuring we maintain fairness and equity across the process. Additional Scope While we build out longer-term operational coverage, this role will also provide secondary support across the following areas to ensure continuity and no gaps in coverage: Office Management • Serve as a secondary point of contact for day-to-day Warsaw office operations, providing coverage during absences, peak periods, or as workload requires. • Partner closely with the current Office Management & People Operations Specialist to stay looped in on ongoing priorities and vendor/facilities matters. Ensure feedback is mapped into local cultural connection points to keep teammates engaged. EMEA Operations Support • Provide backup support for EMEA People Ops activities including employee lifecycle transactions, stepping in during PTO, surge periods, or as capacity needs dictate. • Work alongside the current Office Management & People Operations Specialist to maintain consistent employee experience.
