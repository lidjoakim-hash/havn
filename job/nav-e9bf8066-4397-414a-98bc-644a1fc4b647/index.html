About the Role The Norwegian Meteorological Institute has an open position as Researcher. The position is affiliated with the Ocean and Ice Division in the Ocean Department, and is based in Tromsø. The Ocean and Ice Division develops and operates numerical models for forecasting of ocean currents, hydrography, sea level, waves and sea-ice. These models provide input to downstream services such as oil spill drift models and search-and-rescue support. The work is carried out in collaboration with national and international partners. The work involves the management of operational forecast systems, including coordination of research staff, project administration, and supervision of research activities. The candidate is expected to contribute in the development of the division's research portfolio according to the institute strategy and actively seeks external project funding. Furthermore, the successful candidate will be expected to coordinate and manage inter-institutional activities that MET Norway is partnering in. It is essential that you can work efficiently in a team environment both within MET Norway and in an inter-institutional framework. Among the tasks that the successful candidate will become involved in, is management of MET Norway's responsibilities in the Arctic Monitoring and Forecasting Centre (ARCMFC) of the Copernicus Marine Service. ARCMFC is coordinated by the Nansen Environmental and Remote Sensing Center, and also includes the Institute of Marine Research, Norway and the Danish Meteorological Institute. Met Norway's main responsibilities in ARCMFC are wave modelling, near real time forecasts for ocean circulation and sea ice, management and monitoring of communications with the service central dissemination unit at Copernicus, and monitoring of product quality. The successful candidate will act as a link between the Ocean and Ice Division, the Fram Centre in Tromsø, and ARCMFC and become involved in regional high resolution ocean and sea ice modelling for our national service, notably the application of ARCMFC results for this service. Probabilistic forecasting is of particular interest in this context.  Qualifications • PhD in geophysical/physical sciences, with emphasis on remote sensing techniques and/or physical oceanography. • Minimum 10 years of experience after PhD as an independent scientist with a strong record of peer-review publications. International experience is a requirement • Experience with management and/or technical implementations of operational forecast systems in either operational oceanography or weather prediction is expected • Strong track record with success in obtaining competitive research funding is beneficial (e.g. Research Council of Norway, EU Horizon  ESA ) • Experience in scientific programming within a Linux environment (Fortran, Python, Git) • Experience with numerical ocean and/or sea-ice models. • The working language at MET Norway is Norwegian. Foreign candidates must be willing to learn basic Norwegian. • Candidates must be able to work effectively in English and demonstrate fluency, written and oral We offer • Salary: NOK 840.000 - 940.000 per year, depending on qualifications and experience.
