Packaging Development Engineer

Incluso AB · Lund, Sweden

💰 ca. 490 000–640 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 31.1.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

We are looking for a Packaging Development Engineer for a global company in Lund. Start is January 11th, 8,5 months limited contract to begin with. This role is 100% onsite in Lund. In this role you will work with packaging material development. Main Responsibilities: Member of scrum team, translating requirements into sub-system requirements and technical specifications for area of competence Support the translation of requirements into packaging material design Define V&V strategy related to packaging material area for technology and product development projects Write test specifications, analyze and document results in development reports Coordinate & execute all packaging material activities according to existing procedures, standards and methods Collaborate with suppliers, specialists, laboratories, and converting factories Drive root cause investigations on packaging material performance and functionality Drive risk assessment for packaging material Required skills: You have analytical skills and you are able to manage complexity and ambiguity You have a natural drive for problem solving, able to coordinate, and deliver desirable results You have an academic degree, preferable Master of Science in Materials or similar. You are fluent in English and Swedish You have good communication and presentation skills You have strong collaboration skills Preferred skills: Experience in the product development process in the packaging area Experience in technical test methodologies in the area of packaging. Knowledge of Packaging Material, Food Package Interaction and Statistics Well acquainted and self-sufficient in Physical Testing Knowledge in Requirement Management procedure. Knowledge working in a Scrum team This role requires fluency in English and Swedish. This is a full-time consultant position in Lund through Incluso. Start is January 11th, 8,5 months limited contract to begin with. This role is 100% onsite in Lund. Please submit your CV as soon as possible since we will review the applications on an on-going basis. For more information about this role, please contact Marianne Nilsson recruiter at Incluso.
