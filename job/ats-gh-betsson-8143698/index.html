About the role We are looking for a proactive and highly organized Marketing Coordinator to support the execution of marketing activities across multiple channels. This is an excellent opportunity for a recent graduate who wants to build a career in marketing operations, campaign coordination and project management. You will help the marketing team coordinate day-to-day marketing activities, communicate with internal departments and external partners, and ensure campaigns are delivered on time. Responsibilities: • Coordinate the execution of marketing campaigns across multiple channels. • Support the daily tracking of active marketing campaigns and follow up on ongoing activities. • Create and manage tickets for internal teams (Design, CRM, Development, Compliance and other stakeholders). • Coordinate the delivery of marketing assets with external partners and agencies. • Assist in campaign planning, execution and internal communication. • Maintain marketing documentation, including presentations, reports, offers and campaign materials. • Organize and archive marketing documentation and ensure all files are accurate and up to date. • Prepare and maintain Excel trackers for campaigns, budgets, timelines and project status. • Support budget tracking, purchase orders and invoice administration. • Monitor deadlines and proactively follow up with stakeholders to ensure timely delivery. • Provide administrative and operational support to the marketing team. Requirements: • Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Economics or Business • Recent graduate or up to 1 year of relevant work experience. • Excellent knowledge of Microsoft Excel (mandatory). • Strong organizational and time-management skills. • High level of ownership, responsibility and attention to detail. • Proactive mindset with the ability to manage multiple tasks simultaneously. • Strong communication skills and ability to coordinate with different teams. • Ability to work under pressure and meet deadlines. • Native Croatian (written and spoken). • Very good English (written and spoken). Nice to have • Experience with project management tools (Jira, Asana, Monday.com or similar). • Basic understanding of digital marketing channels. • Experience working with presentations (PowerPoint/Google Slides).
