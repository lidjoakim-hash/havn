At Trustpilot, we're on an incredible journey. We're a profitable, high-growth FTSE-250 company with a big vision: to become the universal symbol of trust. We run the world's largest open customer review platform, and while we've come a long way, there's still so much exciting work to do. Come join us at the heart of trust! AI is changing the way consumers discover, evaluate and choose businesses online and Trustpilot is at the centre of that shift. Our reviews are increasingly becoming a trusted source for AI, with Trustpilot ranking as the fifth most cited page on the internet by ChatGPT in January 2026. As an Account Executive at Trustpilot, you’ll play a key role in helping businesses build trust and turn it into growth. You’ll own new business development across the Dutch market, working with companies to understand their challenges, uncover opportunities, and show them how Trustpilot can help them build credibility, strengthen their reputation, and win more customers.
