Operations Manager

Aktiebolaget Tetra Pak · Lund, Sweden

At Tetra Pak we commit to making food safe and available, everywhere; and we protect what's good – protecting food, protecting people, and protecting the planet. By doing so we touch millions of people's lives every day. And we need people like you to make it happen. We empower you to reach your potential with opportunities to make an impact to be proud of – for food, people and the planet. Job Summary Food, people and the planet are at the heart of everything we do. Through our technologies and services, we help customers produce safe food in a sustainable and efficient way. Within Tetra Pak Key Components, we are now looking for an Operations Manager – Separation to lead our operational organization and drive performance across Application Engineering, Order Engineering, Product Support, Technical Issue Management and operational excellence activities. Reporting to the Manager Separation, you will lead a team that ensure efficient execution and high-quality support throughout the product lifecycle. You will be instrumental in creating a responsive, efficient and customer-focused organization. The position is permanent and preferably based in Lund, Sweden. Some international travel may be required. What you will do As Operations Manager, you will lead the organization responsible for operational performance within the separation business. Your focus will be on execution, continuous improvement and customer satisfaction. Driving lead-time, forecasting and sales goals. Main responsibilities: Lead, coach and develop teams within Application Engineering, Order Engineering, Product Support and Technical Issue Management. Ensure efficient execution of customer order fulfillment and support activities. Secure that application know-how is effectively translated into customer solutions, operational excellence and business results. Drive continuous improvement initiatives to increase productivity, quality and responsiveness. Lead technical issue management and support escalation handling. Collaborate closely with customers, Sales, Services, Product Management, Supply Chain and Manufacturing. Drive operational planning, capacity management and resource allocation. Develop and continuously improve processes, tools and ways of working. Monitor, analyse and improve operational performance and key business metrics. Foster a culture focused on customer success, accountability and teamwork. Manage departmental objectives, resources and budgets. As a member of the Separation leadership team, you will contribute to both operational and strategic decisions affecting the business. We believe you have Proven leadership experience in operations, engineering, application support, technical support, service or manufacturing environments. Experience leading customer-facing technical organizations. Strong business understanding and operational mindset. Experience driving performance improvements, change initiatives and organizational development. Strong stakeholder management skills with the ability to build trust and drive alignment across functions, regions and organizational levels. Experience interacting with customers and translating customer needs into operational priorities. Strong analytical skills. Decisive decision making. Excellent communication and collaboration skills. Fluency in English, both written and spoken. You are a leader who creates structure, alignment and results. You enjoy solving complex challenges and building high-performing teams that consistently deliver to customers and stakeholders. You thrive in an international and dynamic environment where operational excellence and people leadership go hand in hand. Through strong stakeholder management, engagement and collaboration, you create the conditions for sustainable performance and long-term success. We Offer You A variety of exciting challenges with ample opportunities for development and training in a truly global landscape A culture that pioneers a spirit of innovation
