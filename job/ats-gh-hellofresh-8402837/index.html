The role As the Associate Director of Global Partnerships, you will own the new business development agenda for the HelloFresh Group, driving high-value, scalable partnerships across our core growth brands: Factor US, HelloFresh US, and global brand opportunities. Based in our Berlin office and reporting to the Sr. Director Partnerships & Sales, this is a senior individual contributor role where your impact is measured by the quality and scale of the deals you bring in generating net new activations. You will lead the global BD steerco, setting the agenda, building the process, and holding the function accountable for pipeline quality and progress across all markets and brands. Working with dotted-line relationships across local BD stakeholders globally, you will be the senior commercial voice on all net-new partnership opportunities, reporting progress directly to CMO-level stakeholders across the HelloFresh Group. Alongside core affiliate, Closed-User-Groups and brand-to-brand partnerships, you will play a founding role in establishing Card-Linked Offers (CLO) as a stable, scalable new channel for the HelloFresh Group, building the commercial model, identifying strategic partners, and working cross-functionally to bring it to market. This role is not about managing the affiliate channel, it is about landing the deals that open new growth frontiers. If you are a commercially driven BD operator who thrives on identifying opportunity at scale, building relationships at senior level, and turning a pipeline into revenue, this is the role for you. Above all, we are looking for people who will make HelloFresh better. We believe there are many different ways of developing skills and we love diverse experiences! So even if you don’t “tick all the boxes” but think you’d thrive in this role, we would really like to learn more about you. What you’ll do Business development & deal-making • Own and deliver quarterly and annual BD net activations targets across the HelloFresh Group brand portfolio, Factor US, HelloFresh US, and global partnerships • Build and manage a structured, qualified global partnership pipeline from prospecting through to signed agreement and launch, with a focus on high-value, multi-market opportunities • Develop commercial partnerships with tech-integration platforms (employee benefits, fintech, telecom) where the primary driver is new customer acquisition — own the commercial deal and partner relationship; technical integration is coordinated with Ops/Engineering, not built by this role. • Lead outreach strategy end-to-end: sequencing, messaging, qualification frameworks, and tooling (HubSpot, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, ZoomInfo) • Identify, evaluate, and prioritize high-potential brand partnerships across target verticals, B2B brands, fitness & wellness, fintech, lifestyle, and media • Negotiate and finalize complex commercial partnership agreements, ensuring terms meet HelloFresh Group's strategic and financial objectives • Develop and present compelling pitch materials, business cases, and commercial proposals for new partnership categories and global brand opportunities CLO channel development • Lead the commercial build-out of Card-Linked Offers (CLO) as a net-new, scalable growth channel for the HelloFresh Group • Develop the CLO partnership strategy, commercial model, and go-to-market framework in collaboration with Operations, Analytics, and local market teams • Manage and grow senior-level commercial relationships with CLO partners, acting as the primary point of contact at HelloFresh Group level • Track channel performance and report on CLO pipeline health and revenue contribution alongside broader BD metrics Global BD steerco & stakeholder management • Own and lead the Global BD Steerco, setting agenda, running cadence, building process, and ensuring pipeline coordination across Factor, HelloFresh US, and INTL markets • Work with dotted-line local BD stakeholders globally to align
