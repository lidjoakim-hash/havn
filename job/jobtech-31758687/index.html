Area Manager - Digital Discovery & Engagement (Interim - 12 months)

H & M Hennes & Mauritz GBC AB · Stockholm, Sweden

Job Description Join us as the Area Business Manager for Digital Discovery & Engagement where you will play a key role in shaping the future of Digital Discovery & Engagement and driving strategic initiatives across one of H&M's most important customer experience areas. This is a 12-month position covering a parental leave. You will have a forward-looking role with the opportunity to help evolve our digital experience into an inspiring and adaptive fashion world that feels unmistakably H&M. You and your team will help create experiences that feel relevant, personal and inspirational, while balancing strategic development with continuous improvement of the core customer experience. The area focuses on attracting customers to our digital store, inspiring discovery and engagement, and helping customers move confidently towards purchase. Its scope includes Search & AI Discovery, Navigate & Browse, Inspire & Discover, Fashion Trends & Curation, Locate & Relevance, Ranking and Recommendations, Size & Fit, Evaluate & Decide Experience and Omni Product Discovery. WHO YOU’LL WORK WITH You will work in close co-leadership with the Area Tech Manager, collaborating with the wider Digital team to connect business needs with technology direction, delivery and resource priorities. Together, you will be accountable for creating clarity across budget, people and delivery, enabling faster decision-making and one joint plan for the area. A key part of the role is partnering with Product Managers, Engineering Managers, Product Design, Commercial teams and other Digital areas to ensure that our solutions are shaped by customer needs, business priorities and insights. WHAT YOU’LL DO As Area Business Manager for Digital Discovery & Engagement, you will help shape how customers discover, explore and evaluate fashion across our digital touchpoints. Our mission is to make discovery inspiring, personal and effortless, helping every customer find their style and feel confident in every purchase, wherever they are. You will: Lead our Digital Discovery area consisting of 70-100 people. Set a clear business direction and vision for Digital Discovery & Engagement, aligned with the wider Digital and Commercial strategy. Translate customer and business needs into clear priorities, plans and measurable outcomes across the area. Own the area roadmap and prioritisation together with the Area Tech Manager, ensuring that investments and resources focus on the opportunities that create the greatest customer and business value. Lead improvements across the digital discovery journey, making it easier and more inspiring for customers to enter, search, browse, evaluate and confidently choose relevant products. Advance dynamic and relevant customer journeys, conversational discovery and experiences that strengthen fashion inspiration and engagement. Work closely with Product, Tech, Design, Commercial and market teams to connect customer insights and business priorities with scalable digital solutions. Drive customer-centric value creation through discovery, personalisation, search, recommendations, insights and analytics. Create alignment between teams and stakeholders, managing dependencies, sequencing and capacity across products and initiatives. Ensure the area is run responsibly, with clear ownership of resources, budgets, risks, performance and continuous improvement. Lead, coach and develop talent within the area, creating a collaborative, inclusive and forward-looking environment where people can grow and do their best work. WHO YOU ARE You are comfortable working in a fast-moving environment where customer behaviours, technology and priorities continue to evolve. You can create clarity in complex situations, make informed choices and help teams move forward even when the full path is not yet defined.
