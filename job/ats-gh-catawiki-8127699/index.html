At Catawiki, every day brings the extraordinary! Whether it’s Daniel Ricciardo’s Formula 1 Car, a Woolly Mammoth’s Skeleton, Lady Gaga's Jumpsuit or Usain Bolt’s running shoe, we encounter exceptional objects every day. We’re a one-of-a-kind marketplace for buying and selling special objects. Each week, more than 100,000 unique items are auctioned, all carefully curated by our passionate in-house experts. Having sold over 25 million unique objects, our mission is to become the world’s most popular destination for special objects. As a growing, diverse and sustainable scale-up, we proudly live by three core values. If these values resonate with you, we’d love to explore how you can join us. • Taking ownership and driving impact • Being open to change and feedback • Being passionate about our mission and our customers. Your mission Every Product Manager at Catawiki owns a meaningful part of the customer experience. In Demand, your mandate is straightforward: help more people find objects they care about, become active buyers and keep returning to Catawiki. Demand is not one feature or surface. It covers how buyers first understand Catawiki, how they browse and search, what we recommend, how confidently they participate in auctions and what brings them back. Your job is to identify the greatest opportunities across that journey, decide what matters most and lead a cross functional team from insight to measurable customer and business impact.The solution is not prescribed. You may improve ranking and recommendations, remove barriers to a first purchase, create stronger reasons to return or develop new AI enabled experiences. What matters is that you solve an important customer problem, prove the impact and scale what works. You will own the strategy, outcomes and execution for a cross functional team, working closely with Engineering, Design, Data Science, Marketing and Commercial teams. What you will do • Set and communicate a clear product vision, strategy and roadmap for a meaningful part of the buyer journey • Own measurable outcomes across discovery, engagement, conversion and retention • Use behavioural data, customer research and commercial insight to identify and prioritise the biggest opportunities • Turn ambiguous customer problems into clear hypotheses, prototypes and executable plans • Lead initiatives from early discovery through experimentation, launch and scaling • Define success metrics, analyse funnel and cohort performance and build a rigorous experimentation agenda • Design, run and evaluate A/B tests, turning positive, negative and inconclusive results into clear decisions • Work with data scientists and machine learning engineers to improve recommendations, ranking and personalisation • Partner closely with design to create simple, coherent and distinctive customer experiences • Use AI and rapid prototyping to test ideas early, reduce development risk and accelerate learning • Identify new AI enabled buyer experiences where the technology solves a meaningful customer problem • Create clarity, momentum and accountability across teams, including when priorities or incentives differ • Communicate strategy, performance, tradeoffs and recommendations clearly to senior and C level stakeholders Who you are Three qualities matter most in this role. Outcome ownership You take responsibility for customer and business results, not only for delivering features. You stay with the problem, remove obstacles and change direction when the evidence demands it. Systems thinking You understand that onboarding, discovery, recommendations, auction dynamics, CRM, commercial priorities and marketplace health influence one another. You can make thoughtful tradeoffs across the full system. Experimental rigour You use data, research, prototypes and controlled experiments to make decisions.
