About Mixpanel Mixpanel is the leading product intelligence and analytics platform, trusted by more than 29,000 companies to help understand how people use the products they build. By combining powerful analytics with AI that knows your business, Mixpanel helps teams see what’s working, diagnose what’s not, and decide what to build next. Learn more at mixpanel.com. About the People and Culture Team The Mixpanel People team exists to help every employee do their best work. We build the structure and support that enables a high-performing organization to move fast without losing what makes us Mixpanel - connection, candor, and the values we hold each other to. A core driver of Mixpanel's success is investing in our people and clearing the way for individuals and teams of every background to reach their full potential. The People function leads that work - executing our people strategy, advising senior leadership, and running HR operations with the rigor a high-performing company demands. Within that team, this role owns the EMEA go-to-market population specifically — three offices (London, Barcelona, and Tel Aviv) growing fast enough that the programs supporting them are still being built, not just maintained. About the Role This is a hybrid role based in London, supporting Mixpanel's EMEA business across our London, Barcelona, and Israel offices, embedded with our Go-To-Market leadership team. You'll report to our Global Head of HR Business Partners and operate as a strategic, trusted advisor to EMEA GTM leadership - shaping programs and policy, not just executing them. This is a multi-country role by design. Your GTM population sits across the UK, Spain, and Israel, so working knowledge of employment law and practice in all three is essential - the right call in one market is often the wrong call in another. Experience supporting teams in APAC is a strong plus as our footprint there grows, but it isn't required.
