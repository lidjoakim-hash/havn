As a Demand Planning Manager at Westwing, your key focus will be creating accurate demand forecasts and aligning them across the business. This will make you a crucial part of boosting availability and efficiency along the entire Supply Chain. You will be responsible for a subset of our beautiful Westwing portfolio, owning its forecast performance and continuously improving methods and processes. Additionally, you will closely collaborate with our Marketing and Commercial teams to integrate trends, promotions, campaigns, seasonality, and new products. Your insights will not only guide our operational supply planning decisions (e.g., when and how much to order), but also the strategic positioning of Westwing in the international market.
