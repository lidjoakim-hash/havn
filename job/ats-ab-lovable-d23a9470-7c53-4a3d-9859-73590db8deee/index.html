Senior Partner Marketing Manager

Lovable · London, United Kingdom

TL;DR The Partner Marketing team extends Lovable’s reach by educating, engaging, and enabling partners globally to build market awareness and generate demand for their Lovable-powered solutions and services. We recruit prospective partners and support the onboarding and growth of existing ones as they build, launch, market, and scale their businesses through the Lovable Partner Ecosystem. We work closely with GTM partnerships, product partnerships, sales, and product marketing, and serve partner types from major technology and platform partners to the connectors, agencies, and independent experts who build with Lovable every day. Why Lovable? For decades, the ability to build software belonged to a tiny fraction of the population: the people with the credentials, the technical training, the access. Everyone else had to wait, or pay, or give up. Lovable is the counterargument: We exist for the people with ideas who were locked out of software creation until now. Over 70 million projects have been built on the platform. Hundreds of thousands of people pay for it. The product handles real infrastructure, not only prototypes: authentication, databases, payments, and deployment. People run real businesses on Lovable, which means the stakes are real too. The comms challenge: when anyone can build, the platform has to be safe for everyone who does, and they need to know it. That takes someone who can tell the story of how we think about topics like safety, security, and reliability before anyone asks, and who can be honest, precise, and fast when the questions come up anyway. What you’ll do We’re looking for an experienced, creative, results-oriented marketer to develop and execute partner marketing initiatives across our full partner ecosystem. Connectors and integration partners are the immediate priority: Lovable’s rapidly growing connector roster (spanning search, communications, CRM, and data) needs launches, positioning, and demand generation. You’ll also work with the Lovable Partner Program team to run co-marketing campaigns and programs at scale that drive customer acquisition and activation, helping partners grow their business with and through Lovable. You’ll set and own Lovable’s marketing with, to, and through partners, and roll up your sleeves to execute across a high-volume, fast-moving roster. - Work with GTM Partnerships, Product Partnerships, Sales, and (Product) Marketing to build go-to-market initiatives across connector and integration partners, hyperscalers, and services partners. - Define priorities, investment model, scaling mechanisms, and success metrics across partner types, with connectors as the top priority given the roster’s size and growth rate. - Own and evolve the tiering framework that governs marketing investment per partner, and keep it workable as the partner count grows. - Administer and optimize market development funds (MDF), and build the joint GTM plans needed to fully activate committed co-marketing budgets. - Build and run an intake and prioritization system for inbound co-marketing requests from partners. - Drive adoption of scalable marketing and enablement programs across the partner base, and represent partner marketing’s needs in the broader GTM and marketing roadmap. - Collaborate with GTM teams (including marketing) to ensure partners are represented in initiatives across all regions. Who you are Meet the minimum requirements below to be considered. The preferred qualifications are a bonus. - 8-10+ years of relevant B2B marketing experience. - 3+ years managing partner marketing programs or teams. - Experience working with technology, platform, or services partners to build joint marketing plans and run programs that drive new customer acquisition and pipeline. - Experience running partner marketing across a large, growing roster (an ISV-style ecosystem with dozens to hundreds of partners). - Proven ability to influence partner marketing leaders
