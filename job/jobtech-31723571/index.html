Senior Digital Hardware Designer for PCB development

Wrknest AB · Göteborg, Sweden

💰 ca. 470 000–570 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 10.2.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

About the role Do you want to work with advanced electronics where quality and precision really matter? We are now looking for a senior digital hardware designer for our client, one of Sweden’s leading defence companies, for an assignment within PCB development for missile electronics. You will take responsibility for circuit boards all the way from concept to verified hardware. You work in a technically challenging environment, together with experienced colleagues from several engineering disciplines. This is a consulting assignment. That means you will be employed by Wrknest and work on assignment at our client. Your future tasks As a digital hardware designer, you follow the boards through the entire development process: from the first concept studies and requirements, through schematic design and simulation, to lab verification and integration into the final product. Examples of tasks: Carry out concept studies of future solutions, design approaches and architectures Take part in breaking down requirements and specifying designs and systems Realise detailed designs through simulation, component selection and schematic design Produce and review production documentation Test and verify performance in the lab Troubleshoot and support the integration of the product Keep to schedules and contribute to high quality and cost-effective solutions We are looking for you who have Around 10 years or more of experience in hardware and electronics design Solid experience with Cadence, HyperLynx and Matlab The ability to pass a security clearance, which in practice requires Swedish citizenship We see it as a merit if you have Experience from defence, aerospace or another industry with high demands on quality Experience of digital design with high-speed signals and FPGA Experience of signal and power integrity analysis As a person, you are Thorough and quality-minded, with a genuine interest in technology. You enjoy taking ownership of your work from start to finish and are comfortable working in teams with several engineering disciplines. Other information Start: Preferably 11 January 2027 Location: Gothenburg (Lindholmen), on site Scope: Full-time Employment type: Consulting assignment via Wrknest until 30 June 2027, with good chances of extension or permanent employment with the client Selection is ongoing, so feel free to apply today! About Wrknest Engineering Wrknest Engineering specializes in helping engineers and technicians take the next step in their careers. We work with the companies of the future, offering both recruitment and consulting assignments within technology and industry - always with people at the center. We believe in building long-term relationships and strive to be an engaged and attentive partner. Through us, you get the chance to be part of technical advances that shape tomorrow's society - in roles where your knowledge makes a real difference. Our ambition is for you to thrive, develop and feel proud of your work. Read more at www.wrknestengineering.se.
