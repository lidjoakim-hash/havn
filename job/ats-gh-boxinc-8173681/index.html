Why Box needs you: You will join the newly established Data Foundations team within Core Data. These services support a wide range of products and internal teams across Box. The team will take ownership of several existing services, improving their reliability, observability, scalability, and architecture. This is a great opportunity to work on distributed systems at scale and directly shape the team's technical direction.
