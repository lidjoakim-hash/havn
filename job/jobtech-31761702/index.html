Marketing, Communications & Business Development Manager Location: Gothenburg, Sweden Company: Aeronis AB Employment: Full-time About Aeronis Aeronis AB is a Swedish deep-tech company developing Edge AI and intelligent computing solutions for space, airborne and ground-based systems. Our technologies support applications across space, defence, critical infrastructure, energy and autonomous systems. We collaborate with industry partners, research institutions and innovation programmes in Sweden and internationally. As we expand our activities and move towards commercialisation, we are looking for an energetic, creative and hands-on Marketing, Communications & Business Development Manager to strengthen our brand, increase market visibility and support our commercial growth. About the Role This is primarily a marketing and communications position, with additional responsibilities in business development, grants and innovation programmes. It is not a traditional sales or account management role. We are looking for someone who can independently plan, create and execute marketing activities, rather than simply develop strategies or coordinate external agencies. The successful candidate will take ownership of Aeronis' marketing and communications activities, combining digital and traditional marketing with events, stakeholder engagement and commercialisation support. Key Responsibilities 1. Marketing & Brand Management Develop and implement Aeronis' marketing strategy and brand positioning. Personally create marketing materials, presentations, brochures, promotional content and campaigns. Maintain consistent branding and messaging across all company communications. Support product marketing, market positioning and commercialisation activities. 2. Digital & Traditional Marketing Manage and update the company website and LinkedIn presence. Create and publish engaging digital content, articles, newsletters and announcements. Plan and execute digital marketing campaigns and monitor their effectiveness. Coordinate traditional marketing activities, including printed materials, exhibitions, industry publications and promotional campaigns. Develop visual and written marketing content using appropriate design and marketing tools. 3. Communications, Events & Public Relations Represent Aeronis at Swedish and international conferences, exhibitions, networking events and industry meetings. Plan and coordinate event participation, marketing materials and follow-up communications. Manage company announcements, external communications and public relations activities. Build relationships with industry stakeholders, innovation organisations and potential partners. 4. Grants & Innovation Programme Support Identify relevant Swedish and European funding opportunities and innovation programmes. Support grant applications, particularly marketing, commercialisation, market analysis and impact sections. Assist with programme coordination, reporting, deadlines and project documentation. Support communication and dissemination activities for funded projects. 5. Business Development & Commercial Support Conduct market research, competitor analysis and customer segmentation. Identify potential customers, partners and commercial opportunities. Support lead generation, customer outreach and follow-up communications. Prepare commercial presentations, customer-facing materials and investor communications. Work closely with the CEO to support commercialisation and business growth. Required Qualifications & Experience Degree or relevant professional qualification in Marketing, Communications, Business or a related discipline. Demonstrated hands-on experience in marketing, branding and communications. Proven ability to personally develop and deliver marketing campaigns and promotional materials.
