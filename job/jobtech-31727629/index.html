Group Treasury Analyst

AFRY AB · Solna, Sweden

Company Description AFRY is a European leader in engineering, design, and advisory services, with a global reach. We accelerate the transition towards a sustainable society. We are 17,000 devoted experts in infrastructure, industry, energy and digitalisation, creating sustainable solutions for generations to come.  Continuous learning of all our employees is critical for our ability to deliver on our business strategy. We need to develop the right skills and foster a culture of continuous learning to win in a complex and competitive market. We want to offer dynamic and proactive learning opportunities for our people to develop themselves. Job Description Group Treasury is responsible for managing and coordinating the AFRY Group’s financial activities and financial risk exposure. The team consists of five dedicated professionals and plays a central role in supporting the company’s financial stability and growth. In this position, you will join a close-knit and collaborative team in a broad and dynamic role encompassing cash management, cash flow forecasting, bank guarantees, KYC processes, treasury operations, and related back-office activities. You will gain exposure to a wide range of treasury disciplines while working closely with stakeholders across the organization. Group Treasury is also driving several development initiatives, including the enhancement of cash management processes, banking relationships, and the treasury system landscape. As a key member of the team, you will play an integral role in shaping and implementing these initiatives, contributing to the continued development and efficiency of the treasury function. Qualifications We are looking for a candidate with a post-secondary education in finance, economics, or equivalent. Previous experience from a corporate treasury function, particularly within treasury operations, is considered an advantage. You are able to work both independently and collaboratively in a team-oriented environment. As the role involves active participation in and leadership of development projects, we are looking for someone with strong coordination skills and a proven ability to drive initiatives, manage stakeholders, and contribute to successful project delivery. Main responsibilities Support in preparation of monthly financial statements and reporting Manage bank and corporate guarantees Financial payments and transactions and related administration Responsible for banking authorizations and bank administration together with our Treasury Specialist Management of cashpools, group netting, and liquidity forecasting Main KYC contact person towards banks Maintaining key processes and static data in the Treasury Management System Actively participate in improvement projects within the Group Treasury You thrive in a collaborative environment and enjoy building strong relationships with colleagues and stakeholders. While being an effective team player, you are also self-driven and comfortable taking ownership of your areas of responsibility. You are an excellent communicator, with a structured yet flexible approach, enabling you to adapt to changing priorities and business needs. You are analytical, detail-oriented, and service-minded, with a genuine curiosity and a passion for continuous improvement. You enjoy learning new things and contributing to the development of processes, systems, and ways of working. As AFRY is a global organization, you communicate fluently in English, both verbally and in writing. We highly value personal qualities and look for individuals who embody AFRY's values: Brave, Devoted, and Team Player. Additional Information The position is a full-time, located at the headquarter in Solna, Sweden or in the office in Vantaa, Finland. You will report to the Group's Head of Treasury.
