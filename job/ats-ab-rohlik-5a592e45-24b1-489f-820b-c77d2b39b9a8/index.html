At Rohlik, we own our end-to-end operations—including all of our in-house technology—to deliver an exceptional customer experience and the freshest food from local artisans and farmers. As we continue to scale across Europe, our fulfillment centers rely on smooth, standardized execution to maintain high performance and quality. The Change Management team sits at the heart of site operations. We bridge strategy and execution by leading the rollout of new tools, systems, and standardized processes across our fulfillment centers. Your Role You've always been the person who fixes things on your shift. Now imagine doing that for our entire Berlin fulfillment center: full-time, with a clear mandate, a team, and central Operational Excellence support. As Change Manager for our Berlin FC, you will report to the Head of Change Management. You will own the site's change and project pipeline, ensuring successful implementation of initiatives, adoption of new processes, and standardization of practices. You will partner with stakeholders across local and central teams to align resources, monitor progress, and deliver sustainable improvements. This is not a step away from operations.
