Quality Assurance Engineer, Linköping

If you answer yes to the following questions, EVP QA could be a perfect fit for you! Do you have a great interest in quality, software testing and programming? Close collaboration with product management, developers and other test engineers - does that sound great? Would you enjoy working with a mix of manual, semi-automatic and automated testing methods? Are you passionate about staying up to date with new testing tools and trends? At Axis QA we're now looking for Test Engineers to our office in Linköping, Sweden. What you'll do here as Test Engineer As a test engineer in our team, you will perform testing in close collaboration with developers and other test engineers. You will work with implementation of automatic tests as your main focus but also be involved in exploratory tests for our products. We also want our test engineers to continuously develop and improve test environments, tools, methods and processes. Knowledge of Linux-based systems, network technology and programming in Python is required. Who are we looking for? We are seeking for someone like you - an individual with a wealth of knowledge and passion for quality and software testing. You have a bachelor's or master's degree in engineering, or equivalent knowledge and experience. As a person, you are creative, detail-oriented, responsible, unpretentious and appreciate collaborating with different people. We'd love to here that you have following Experience with Power over Ethernet (PoE). Experience with SNMP, LLDP and network management protocols. Sound understanding of TCP/IP, VLANs, MAC addressing and Ethernet interfaces. You should be ready to travel down to our HQ in Lund occasionally. What Axis has to offer This position is based in Linköping, primarily on-site at our office in Ebbepark. Our work environment fosters teamwork and innovation, with plenty of room for fun! Together, we enjoy activities like table tennis, Mario Kart, or a game of pickleball at Campushallen. We are looking forward to welcoming a new teammate to our growing team. Axis is a company that realizes the benefits of a diverse workforce. We know that diversity in groups creates a better working environment and promotes creativity, something that is fundamental for our success. To learn more about Axis, our innovative products, solutions, and vibrant company culture, explore: Life at Axis blog Engineering at Axis blog Innovation at Axis Ready to act? Come and interview us! Our hiring process is as much for you as it is for us - it is important that you get to form an idea of new colleagues, manager and culture at the company. At the end of the day, we should enjoy working together. A tip before an interview is therefore that you think about what you want to know more about, so that we can help you get answers.
