THE ROLE We are looking for a Value Engineer to join Legora in the UK, sitting at the intersection of Sales, Product, and Customer Success. You will partner directly with prospects and customers to quantify, articulate, and demonstrate the business impact of Legora. Working alongside Account Executives and Sales/Legal Engineers, you will translate complex legal workflows and quantitative findings into clear, executive-ready value narratives. As the first Value Engineer in the region, you will play a key role in establishing how we lead value-based customer conversations and enable our go-to-market teams. You will work directly with General Counsel, CFOs, and other C-level stakeholders. WHAT YOU’LL DO - Accelerate strategic deals by partnering with Account Executives and Sales/Legal Engineers to build compelling, executive-ready value narratives. - Translate customer workflows, operational challenges, and quantitative results into clear financial and business impact. - Lead value-focused conversations with General Counsel, CFOs, and other senior executives. - Develop and deliver value enablement training for sales and go-to-market teams across the region. - Create practical frameworks, tools, and assets that help teams communicate Legora’s value consistently. - Collaborate across Sales, Product, and Customer Success to share customer feedback, feature requests, and industry trends relating to value and ROI. - Support customer adoption and expansion by helping customers understand and measure the impact of Legora. WHAT YOU’LL BRING - Experience translating quantitative results and complex workflows into clear, actionable business insights. - The ability to build credible and compelling value narratives for customers. - Experience engaging directly with senior executives in a clear, confident, and concise way. - Strong communication skills, with the ability to simplify complex ideas for different audiences. - The ability to prioritise effectively and deliver high-quality outputs quickly without over-engineering. - A proactive, hands-on approach and enthusiasm for being the first person in this role within the region. PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS - Fluency in French or German. - An interest in teaching, coaching, and enabling sales teams. LOCATION - London preferred, with Stockholm also considered.
