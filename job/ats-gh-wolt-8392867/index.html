About Wolt At Wolt, we create technology that brings joy, simplicity and earnings to the neighborhoods of the world. In 2014 we started with delivery of restaurant food. Now we’re building the delivery of (almost) everything and you’ll find us in over 500 cities in 30 countries around the world. In 2022 we joined forces with DoorDash and together we keep on dreaming big and expanding across the globe. Working at Wolt isn’t always easy, but it’s definitely exciting. Here you’ll learn more, build more, and ship more than in most other companies. You’ll be challenged a lot, but also have a lot of fun on the way. So, if you’re a self-starter with drive and entrepreneurial spirit, this could be the ride of your life. Wolt is part of DoorDash, alongside Deliveroo. Together, we’re improving how people and businesses connect through local commerce. While this role is based in Zagreb, you’ll be part of an organisation that learns and grows across countries and brands. We’re looking for an Operations Associate to join our Courier Onboarding team in Zagreb. This is a hybrid role. In this role, you’ll help us turn more courier applicants into active couriers in Croatia, and make sure our cities have the couriers they need. Our Courier Onboarding team shapes a courier’s first experience with Wolt. We improve how applicants move from application to first delivery, and make sure couriers are ready to succeed from day one. What you’ll be doing You’ll be joining the Courier Onboarding team, which works across Croatia and neighbouring countries. Your focus will be Croatia, and you’ll work closely with colleagues in the region. Here’s what your day-to-day might look like: • Own the onboarding funnel in Croatia, from application to first delivery, finding where applicants drop off and driving the changes that improve conversion • Monitor courier supply across Croatian cities, spot gaps early and lead local actions that keep deliveries running smoothly • Turn data into clear recommendations, backing your decisions with evidence rather than assumptions • Improve processes and workflows, and help roll out new onboarding tools, so that onboarding can scale without extra manual work • Keep onboarding guidelines, training materials and FAQs clear and up to date, so couriers are ready for their first delivery • Work with CRM, Supply, Fleet and regional Operations teams to align onboarding work with wider business goals and campaigns What you’ll need to thrive • Relevant experience in operations, process management or a similar field, with demonstrated ownership of a process or set of numbers that you improved in a measurable way • Strong analytical skills: you use data, for example in Excel, Google Sheets or BI tools, to understand how a process performs and to support your decisions • Proven ability to work with colleagues and teams you do not manage, and to get things moving without formal authority • Clear written and spoken communication, including turning complex steps into simple instructions • A solution-oriented approach: you take initiative and make decisions with little oversight • Native-level Croatian and fluent English, as you’ll work with colleagues across the region Our humble expectations You’ll have ownership of an important part of how Wolt grows in Croatia, with room to use your judgement and see what your decisions change. The work is varied: one day a funnel analysis, the next a conversation with colleagues about a city that is short of couriers. You’ll learn from teams across the region and see the results of your work quickly. As part of DoorDash, you’ll have the backing of a global business, with the scale and resources to help you do your best work. What we offer • Competitive salary • Hybrid working, with flexibility to work from home • A laptop and monitor • Free access to Woltwide professional training programmes • Paid holiday, plus sick and personal leave days • Staff discounts on Wolt orders N
