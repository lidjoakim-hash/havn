At Rohlik, Integrated Business Planning and S&OP are not just planning routines—they are the backbone of how we run efficient operations, optimize resources, and scale sustainably. We're looking for a Senior Sales and Operations Planning Manager who will work closely with our Supply Chain leadership and help build our end-to-end forecasting function from the ground up. This is a highly hands-on, builder role for someone who thrives on business partnering, deep-dive analytics, challenging manual processes, and turning raw data into decision-ready insights across all our European markets. To be clear on scope: this role owns the order- and revenue-level demand forecast that feeds the S&OP cycle — not SKU-level demand planning, which sits with the Supply Chain team. WHY THIS ROLE IS EXCITING - Build from the Ground Up: You won't just execute a process someone else built—you are one of the founding members of a brand-new department, giving you a direct hand in shaping the future of Supply Chain at Rohlik. - Direct Business Impact: Your forecasts directly dictate inventory levels, labor scheduling, and delivery capacity across 5 countries and 12 fulfillment centers. - Automation & Ownership: You have the autonomy and mandate to kill manual spreadsheets. If a process is repetitive, you get to spearhead its automation alongside our BI and Engineering teams. - High-Growth E-Commerce Environment: Work in a fast-paced, high-velocity e-commerce and grocery ecosystem where data-driven decisions directly affect daily operations and customer satisfaction. WHAT YOU'LL DO - Demand Forecasting: Own the daily and weekly production of the group demand forecast (orders, SKU/order, and intraday shapes) across all markets, fulfillment centers, and planning horizons. - Accuracy & Bias Ownership: Drive forecast accuracy and bias performance by market and horizon (target: 98% accuracy at order/revenue level); conduct deep-dive root-cause analyses on deviations and implement corrective actions. - S&OP Core Analysis: Build and own the demand review pack and scenario models for the S&OP cycle, including quantified upside and downside cases. - Operational Alignment: Translate agreed demand into actionable inventory, warehouse workload, labor, throughput, and delivery requirements for operating functions. - Automation & Optimization: Proactively drive the automation of manual routines (e.g., mass-upload/locking for SKU edits, demand-chaser redesigns, automated AOV forecasting) instead of working around system gaps. - Executive Decision Support: Prepare daily and weekly data, analysis, and options that feed into executive updates and S&OP governance rituals. - Peaks & Campaigns: Lead forecasting prep for campaigns, seasonal transitions, and peak events, ensuring every manual adjustment is documented with clear assumptions and rationale. - Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner with market planners, Category, Marketing, Fulfillment, Last Mile, and BI teams to collect quantified inputs on time and keep planning data trustworthy. - Governance & Resilience: Actively identify and flag process gaps (single points of failure, missing notifications, undocumented calls) to make the department increasingly resilient over time.
