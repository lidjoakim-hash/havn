At Tetra Pak we commit to making food safe and available, everywhere; and we protect what's good – protecting food, protecting people, and protecting the planet. By doing so we touch millions of people's lives every day. And we need people like you to make it happen. We empower you to reach your potential with opportunities to make an impact to be proud of – for food, people and the planet. Job Summary Do you want to start your engineering career close to real equipment, practical testing and experienced colleagues? We are looking for a Test Engineer to join our development organization in Lund. This is an opportunity for a recent graduate or someone at the beginning of their engineering career who wants to learn through practical participation in real test activities. You will be part of and execute testing across equipment, functions, materials and new concepts. Working alongside experienced Test Engineers and specialists, you will learn how tests are prepared, performed, documented and followed up. You are not expected to work independently or know our technology from day one. Your main focus will be to participate, learn and gradually build the practical and technical skills needed for the role. This is a permanent position based in Lund, Sweden. Limited travel to suppliers or customer sites may be required depending on project needs. What you will do • Participate in the planning, preparation and execution of tests together with colleagues. • Help prepare test equipment, instrumentation and test conditions, and learn how a safe and reliable setup is created. • Learn and follow established safety procedures, test methods and documentation practices. • Learn to use measurement equipment and data logging tools under guidance. • Record observations, summarize results and learn how clear test specifications and reports are prepared. • Participate in troubleshooting with designers, automation engineers and other specialists. • Help maintain a safe, tidy and effective working environment in our test facilities. • Ask questions, share observations and contribute ideas as your experience grows. We believe you have • A relevant technical education in mechanical engineering, mechatronics, automation, electrical engineering or a comparable area. A higher vocational education or bachelor’s degree is suitable. • Interest in hands-on engineering and willingness to work practically with machines, components and test equipment. • A basic understanding of technical systems and measurements, together with an interest in learning more. • The ability to work carefully, safely and methodically. • Curiosity, willingness to learn and confidence to ask for guidance. • Good collaboration and communication skills. • Good written and spoken English. It would be beneficial if you have experience in one or more of the following areas, but we do not expect you to have everything: • Tetra Pak equipment or similar equipment used in food or packaging production. • Industrial automation or PLC-based systems. • Measurement technology, test instrumentation and data logging. • Electrical drawings, cabling or practical electrical troubleshooting. • Structured test methods such as Design of Experiments, FMEA or similar engineering methods. • Data analysis and statistical methods. • Automating parts of test execution, data collection or result handling. As a person you enjoy participating in practical work, observing experienced colleagues and learning by doing. You like working as part of a team, are comfortable asking questions and are open to feedback. You take safety and quality seriously and understand that development comes through practice, guidance and continuous learning.
