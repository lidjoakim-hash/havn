Product Owner, Seller Finance & Commissions The Product Owner, Seller Finance & Commissions will own the money flows between eMAG Marketplace and its sellers across RO, BG, HU and PL. This covers seller invoicing, payouts and the payment provider integration, rolling reserve and financial risk mechanisms, the financial data sellers see in the Seller Panel, API and AI assistant, and the marketplace commission model. The role has a planned evolution. In the first period, the Product Owner will take full ownership of the Seller Finance domain (invoicing, payouts, rolling reserve, seller financial data) and keep the existing roadmap and operations running. After this period, the role transitions to a dedicated focus on Commissions: commission grids and categories, commission discounts and incentive programs, commission calculation and invoicing, and the transparency sellers have over what they pay and why. This is a business critical role. Every change touches seller cash flow, accounting, and regulatory obligations (payment services rules, VAT, e-invoicing). The Product Owner will work closely with Engineering, Finance, Accounting, Legal, Commercial and Seller Support to translate financial and regulatory requirements into clear, scalable product solutions that reduce seller friction and support volume. Ideal candidates are product professionals with a solid understanding of financial flows, comfortable with both technical integration work and detailed business rules, who thrive in structured, data informed and multi stakeholder environments.
