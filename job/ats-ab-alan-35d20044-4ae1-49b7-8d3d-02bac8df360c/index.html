ABOUT THE GROWTH TEAM Growth Marketing at Alan covers a broad range of opportunities, including acquisition, segment and market growth, partnerships, content, experimentation, and international expansion. We are looking for adaptable marketers who can take ownership of a defined growth problem, learn quickly, and evolve as our priorities change.   YOUR MISSION As a Growth Marketer, you will independently own an ambiguous but defined Growth problem from diagnosis through execution. You will prioritise the highest-impact actions, coordinate the relevant stakeholders, and connect your work to measurable business outcomes. Concretely, our team works on missions such as: - Developing our channels: scaling paid acquisition, growing visibility in AI search and LLMs like ChatGPT, or growing our strategic partnerships - Crafting the go-to-market strategy for our different segments (e.g. retirees, freelancers, companies,...) - Producing performance creatives with AI workflows to create assets at scale Missions you could work on include: - Scale Alan's referral program - Develop and optimise our B2B and B2C growth through LinkedIn Ads - Own search acquisition across markets   WHAT YOU WILL DO - Independently own an ambiguous but defined Growth problem from diagnosis through execution. - Prioritise high-impact opportunities and make clear trade-offs. - Design and run experiments, campaigns, or programmes end to end. - Coordinate the relevant stakeholders and move initiatives forward with limited direction. - Use business and performance data to make decisions and establish a clear learning loop. - Adapt your approach based on results, customer insight, and feedback. - Connect your work to measurable business outcomes.
