You will play a key role in a new business initiative, bringing an established digital solution to a new customer segment and helping validate its potential in the European market. This is a role for someone who knows how to open doors, build trust with senior decision-makers and take a sales dialogue from first contact all the way to closing. You will work with an identified target market while also taking ownership of prospecting and building a structured sales pipeline. Your responsibilities will include: Drive sales and new business development within a new business initiative Prospect and identify relevant potential customers Initiate contact through cold calls, emails and other relevant channels Book and lead meetings with senior decision-makers within procurement and category management in HORECA and Foodservice Present and demonstrate the solution based on the customer's needs and business challenges Handle objections and move opportunities forward through the sales process Work towards proposals, agreements and closed business Build and maintain your own structured sales pipeline Work closely with senior management throughout the sales process YOUR PROFILE // YOU BRING THE EDGE We are looking for someone with: Several years of experience in a similar senior B2B sales role, with a strong track record of new business development and complex customer dialogues. Proven experience of new business development and outbound sales, managing the full sales cycle from prospecting and first contact to meetings, proposals and closing Experience owning and developing relationships with larger or strategically important customers The ability to build credibility and communicate effectively with senior stakeholders, particularly within procurement or category management Strong presentation and communication skills Fluency in English Comfortable working with digital tools and systems It is an advantage if you have: Experience in FMCG, HORECA, foodservice, convenience, food & beverage or the coffee industry Experience selling standardised IT or digital solutions to larger organisations Experience working with CRM systems, procurement systems or supplier management systems Swedish or additional European language skills As a person, you are driven, commercially minded and entrepreneurial, with a strong ability to build trust and long-term relationships. You listen to the customer, understand their needs and translate them into clear business value. You are results-oriented and comfortable driving a dialogue towards a decision, while maintaining a consultative and long-term approach. You thrive in a small, fast-moving organisation where you are expected to take initiative and contribute hands-on, regardless of seniority.
