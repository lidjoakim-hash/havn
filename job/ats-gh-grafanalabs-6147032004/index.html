Grafana Labs is the company behind Grafana Cloud, the fully managed observability platform trusted by more than 10,000 organizations to ensure reliability, resolve incidents faster, and optimize telemetry at scale. Built on open source and open standards and designed for interoperability across any stack, Grafana Cloud brings AI to observability and observability to AI, giving teams (and their agents) unified visibility so they can see, understand, and act on all their disparate data, wherever it lives, and move at the speed of their ambitions. Customers, including Anthropic, Bloomberg, NVIDIA, Microsoft, and Salesforce, rely on Grafana Labs. We are a 100% remote company with team members across 40+ countries, backed by leading investors including Lightspeed Venture Partners, Sequoia Capital, GIC, Coatue, J.P. Morgan, CapitalG, and Lead Edge Capital. Learn more at grafana.com and follow us on LinkedIn and X. We’re scaling fast and staying true to what makes us different: an open-source legacy, a global collaborative culture, and a passion for meaningful work. Our team thrives in an innovation-driven environment where transparency, autonomy, and trust fuel everything we do. You may not meet every requirement, and that’s okay. The Opportunity: Grafana Labs is seeking to grow our Business Value Engineer team and support defining, articulating, and quantifying business value across our products and services. As a member of the business value engineering team, you'll play a critical role in shaping the direction of this high-impact function. What You’ll Be Doing: • Customer Insights: Collaborate with customers to uncover new opportunities for growth and profitability through personalized storytelling. • Value Quantification: Develop compelling ROI/TCO models that identify value areas driven by new products, emerging trends, and market challenges. • GTM Enablement: Enable pre/post sales teams through training, content development and support; ensuring our sales organization is equipped to showcase Grafana's value with customers. • Value Storytelling: Partner with cross-functional teams to create a unified narrative that showcases Grafana's value proposition, aligning internal stakeholders and external messaging. • Strategic Partnerships: Collaborate closely with Account Executives, Solutions Engineers, Observability Architects to define and articulate the business value of our products and services. What Makes you a Great Fit: • Innovative Mindset: Possess a growth mindset, willing to learn new technologies and adapt quickly • Value Engineering Expertise: 3-5+ years of experience in value management, consulting, or engineering; deep understanding of building quantitative and qualitative analyses. • Data Storytelling Skills: Ability to analyze complex data sets, create compelling customer-facing stories, and communicate findings effectively from technical to executive level audiences. • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Experience partnering with GTM leaders (Sales, Customer Success, Marketing & Finance) to define and align on Grafana's value propositions. Bonus Points For: • 3+ years of Observability experience, preferably with Open Source tools like Grafana, Prometheus, or Loki • Familiarity with the Open Source landscape and community If you're ready for a high-impact role that combines data analysis, storytelling, technology enablement, and partnership-building with cross-functional teams – we'd love to hear from you! Compensation & Rewards: In the UK, the OTE compensation range for this role is £130,000 - £155,000. Actual compensation may vary based on level, experience, and skillset as assessed throughout the interview process.
