Concept Manager, Energy Production Solutions

Adven Sverige AB · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 450 000–550 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 3.1.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Adven is a dynamic energy group, driving the energy transition by developing sustainable and competitive solutions in partnership with customers, municipalities, and suppliers. Together with our 600 employees, we provide industries with steam, heating, cooling and infrastructure services that improve resource efficiency, as well as district heating for households and businesses and geoenergy solutions for large commercial properties. We have a long and solid track record with more than 50 years of experience within the field. Today we operate over 350 sites in Europe with a total energy volume of 5.1 TWh. Our annual turnover is €424 million, and our total balance sheet €1480 million. We are now looking for a Senior Concept Manager or Concept Manager to join our Energy Production Solutions team As a Senior Concept Manager or Concept Manager, your primary responsibility will be to develop viable energy production solutions, transforming early-stage concepts into project-ready designs that are: Aligned with customer needs Sustainable Executable within defined schedules, scopes, and operational performance In this role, you will identify solution opportunities for customers and shape the initial vision of each project. You will collaborate with industrial clients to create sustainable, value-driven technical solutions. This involves defining optimized energy production concepts including energy balances and preparing CAPEX and OPEX calculations. You will also initiate procurement of key technologies and pre-engineering works while coordinating with our project management and O&M teams during the project development and pre-engineering phase. Close collaboration with Energy as a Service sales function and other internal stakeholders is essential part of your daily work. Location The position is based in Stockholm, Sweden. This is a Group-level role that requires some travel between Adven’s operating countries across Northern Europe. We work flexibly in a hybrid model, combining office and remote work, with approximately three days per week in the office and two days working remotely. During the onboarding period, we expect a high level of office presence to support learning and integration into the team. To succeed in this role we expect you to have Relevant university level education A minimum of 5 years of relevant experience, with hands-on expertise in industrial scale energy production, preferably from planning to project execution Strong knowledge of relevant technologies like combustion boiler plants and/or electric boilers and energy efficiency Excellent problem-solving skills and a hands-on approach Proactive, results-oriented mindset with strong teamwork capabilities Strong analytical skills and attention to detail Excellent communication and writing skills in both Swedish and English Proficiency in Microsoft Office (Word, PowerPoint, Excel) Willingness to travel within Sweden and other locations where Adven operates. Experience from of Energy as a service model, solution selling or energy production projects is an asset. At Adven You will have the opportunity to work with highly skilled colleagues who are passionate about building sustainable energy solutions and supporting the industrial green transition. Together we deliver projects that create real environmental and business value for our customers. When we grow, you grow and develop with us. We offer you An international and fast-growing company where you will work on technically challenging and strategically important projects. The opportunity to develop technically and commercially viable concepts for industrial customers and turn early-stage ideas into executable projects. A supportive and collaborative work environment, clear targets, modern working tools and a comprehensive package of employee benefits.
