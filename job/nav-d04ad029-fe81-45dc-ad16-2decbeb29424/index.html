Petrolink Support is looking for a Completion Engineer to ensure a structured, traceable, and efficient completion and handover process for our client in Stavanger. Contract location: Stavanger Tasks Typical work tasks will be • Responsible for establishing fit-for-purpose completion structures, maintaining high-quality documentation and data, and coordinating Mechanical Completion, Preservation, Commissioning, and handover activities in accordance with project requirements and company standards. • Predictable and efficient project execution by reducing the risk of delays, ensuring compliance, and supporting the timely handover of systems, facilities, and documentation to operations. • Establish and maintain a fit-for-purpose completion and handover structure in PIMS in line with project requirements and company standards • Secure correct scoping and traceable documentation of Mechanical Completion, Preservation, and Commissioning activities in PIMS CMS • Maintain data quality and alignment between engineering, construction, and commissioning disciplines to enable efficient project execution and handover • Drive proper scope management • Ensure handover certificates (RFCC, RFOC) are properly coordinated, documented, and approved according to project requirements • Facilitate handover reviews. • Promote collaboration between project teams, contractors, and operations to achieve transparent, safe, and efficient completion processes • Safeguard compliance with client’s HSE principles, management system, and technical standards throughout all completion phases • Contribute to continuous improvement of work processes, system functionality, and best practices for project completion and commissioning management Qualifications We are looking for the right candidate with the following qualifications: • Minimum of three years’ relevant experience in oil and gas projects within engineering, fabrication, construction, and commissioning. • Subsea experience is preferred • Strong knowledge of PIMS CMS • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience • Fluent in written and spoken English • Proactive mindset with the ability to manage multiple tasks simultaneously • Strong competence within LCI processes and documentation requirements • Strong interpersonal, managerial, and communication skills • Team-oriented with a positive attitude • Focused on HSSE and quality in all project phases • Experience from the Norwegian oil & gas industry. Personal qualities We are looking for people with the following qualities: • Team player, strong collaboration and communication skills. • Ability to deliver results. • Structured and systematic. • High focus on Sustainability, Ethics and Company values.
