MERCOR AI RESEARCH FUND GRANTS: ECONOMICS OF AI One of the biggest challenges facing the industry today is determining whether frontier AI is capable enough to deliver value in real-world settings. A model can perform well on benchmarks and still struggle in production. That’s why investing in research, realistic evaluations, and robust verification is critical. Mercor is seeking to fund AI research on the economic impacts of AI. This is separate from the Mercor Research Fellowship, which funds individual experts (apply HERE https://www.mercor.com/careers/a0a98be0-d856-4129-b500-c0a3e412ef01/), our $5m of safety funding awards (apply HERE https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/mercor/1d59ce50-4207-4d95-b7fd-5a01e90b0897), and our funding of benchmarking research. The grant supports: - The time of experts from Mercor's platform - Researcher hours - API costs - Stipends for event and conference attendance BUDGET CATEGORIES Applications are grouped into three budget tiers. Please state your tier clearly in your application and ensure your overall budget ask falls within it: - Tier 1: under $50,000: Suited to focused, well-scoped projects such as pilot studies, evaluations, replications, or extensions of existing work. These applications undergo a streamlined review. - Tier 2: under $200,000: Suited to substantial projects requiring dedicated personnel time, meaningful compute, or data collection. Reviewed with proportionally greater attention to the budget breakdown and implementation plan. - Tier 3: under $500,000: Reserved for ambitious, multi-component research programs. These applications receive the highest level of scrutiny: reviewers will expect a detailed justification of each spend category, a clear account of team capacity to execute at this scale, and milestones against which progress can be assessed. The level of review scrutiny scales with the size of the ask, so applicants should request the smallest budget that credibly supports the proposed work.   RESEARCH AGENDA   We are interested in submissions in the following area: 1.
