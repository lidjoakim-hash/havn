Junior Structural Analysis Engineer – Marine Technology

Work with large-scale moving structures where structural analysis, advanced mechanics and material engineering come together. Help develop innovative wind-powered technology for more energy-efficient shipping, taking your calculations and design solutions all the way to full-scale applications. As part of a small engineering team, you'll have the freedom to influence the product and take ownership from day one. Whether you're a recent graduate or have a few years of experience, you'll get to tackle complex engineering challenges while developing your technical expertise. About the Role We are looking for a Junior Structural Analysis Engineer to join an innovative technology company in Stockholm, developing large-scale mechanical systems for more sustainable shipping. The company combines advanced engineering, mechanical design and material technology to create solutions that harness wind power and reduce fuel consumption in the maritime industry. In this role, you will focus on developing and improving existing products, making complex mechanical structures lighter, more efficient and easier to manufacture. Your work will range from detailed structural calculations and smaller design improvements to evaluating major design changes and new technical concepts. You will work closely with mechanical engineers and other specialists in a small, collaborative development team. The products involve large moving structures exposed to demanding environmental conditions, where loads, movement, material properties and mechanical functionality must all be considered. What makes this opportunity particularly exciting is the ability to influence the actual product. Your calculations and technical assessments will directly contribute to design decisions, and you will have the opportunity to follow your work through development, manufacturing and testing. With a high level of responsibility and freedom, you will play an active role in shaping both the technical solutions and the final product. Key Responsibilities Perform structural calculations and analyses of large-scale mechanical structures and components. Analyze and optimize designs with regard to strength, weight, functionality and manufacturability. Evaluate how different loads, movements and material properties affect structural performance. Prepare calculation reports and technical documentation to support engineering decisions. Work with calculation and simulation tools, including opportunities to develop and improve analysis methods. Evaluate design alternatives and contribute to technical solutions in close collaboration with mechanical engineers. Participate in product development, from early concepts and design iterations to final solutions and testing. About You A Master's degree in Mechanical Engineering, Naval Architecture, Engineering Mechanics, Marine Engineering or a related field. A solid understanding of structural mechanics, strength of materials and mechanical behavior under different loads. Experience with structural calculations and analysis, gained through academic projects or professional work. An interest in mechanical design and understanding how individual components contribute to the performance of a complete system. Good communication skills in English. It is considered an advantage if you have experience with finite element analysis (FEA), ANSYS, Python or similar engineering calculation tools. Experience with large mechanical structures, moving systems, hydraulics, steel or composite materials is also valuable. A background in marine engineering, offshore, heavy industry or working with DNV standards would be particularly relevant. As a person, you are self-driven, curious and comfortable taking responsibility for your own work. You enjoy getting into the technical details of calculations while maintaining an understanding of the bigger picture.
