About Cognism Cognism is building Europe's leading B2B Sales Intelligence platform. Our engineering organisation is transforming around customer outcomes, data quality and AI-first engineering. About the Role As a Senior Software Engineer, you will build the services that power Cognism's strategic Data-as-a-Service (DaaS), APIs and Model Context Protocol (MCP) capabilities. This role is ideal for engineers who enjoy building high-quality backend systems while owning software throughout its lifecycle.
