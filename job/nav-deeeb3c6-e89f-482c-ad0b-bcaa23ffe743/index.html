Automation Technicians – Upcoming Projects in Norway We are currently seeking skilled Automation Technicians to join upcoming projects in Norway on behalf of the customer. This is an excellent opportunity for professionals with hands-on experience in industrial automation who are looking to contribute to challenging and rewarding projects in a dynamic industrial environment. About the Role As an Automation Technician, you will play a key role in the installation, commissioning, and troubleshooting of industrial automation systems. You will work closely with engineering teams to ensure systems are implemented efficiently and operate reliably, supporting the customer's operational goals. Key Responsibilities • Install, configure, and commission industrial automation and control systems • Perform troubleshooting and diagnostics on PLC-based systems • Collaborate with engineers and project teams to ensure smooth project execution • Conduct testing and validation of automation equipment and systems • Ensure compliance with safety standards and project specifications • Provide technical support during commissioning phases Requirements • Hands-on experience with industrial automation systems • Solid understanding of PLC programming and troubleshooting • Experience with commissioning of automation equipment • Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail • Ability to work on-site and adapt to project timelines • Good communication skills and ability to work within a team What We Offer The customer offers the opportunity to work on exciting industrial automation projects in Norway, alongside experienced professionals in a collaborative and safety-focused work environment. How to Apply If you have the relevant experience and are interested in this opportunity, we encourage you to submit your application for consideration.
