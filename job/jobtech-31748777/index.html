Are you an experienced Prosthetist & Orthotist with expertise in lower-limb prosthetics We are currently recruiting, on behalf of a client, a Senior Prosthetist & Orthotist for a leading university hospital in Western Sweden. This is an excellent opportunity to join a highly specialized clinical environment where patient care, innovation, and professional development go hand in hand. You will work alongside skilled multidisciplinary teams and contribute to improving the quality of life for patients requiring advanced prosthetic solutions. About the role As a Prosthetist & Orthotist, you will be responsible for the assessment, fitting, and follow-up of patients requiring orthopaedic technical devices, with a particular focus on lower-limb prosthetics. You will work closely with technicians, physicians, physiotherapists, and other healthcare professionals to provide individualized solutions tailored to each patient's needs. The position offers exposure to a broad range of patient cases, including complex and highly specialized prosthetic care, while also providing opportunities to contribute to clinical development, innovation, and continuous improvement initiatives. Requirements Holds an EU-recognized Bachelor's Degree in Prosthetics & Orthotics (BSc). Has at least 2 years of clinical experience as a Prosthetist & Orthotist. Has solid experience in the assessment, fitting, and follow-up of lower-limb prostheses. Demonstrates strong clinical skills and a patient-centred approach. Is comfortable working both independently and as part of a multidisciplinary team. Has excellent communication and collaboration skills. Is interested in contributing to the ongoing development of clinical services, innovation, and patient care. Experience in a university hospital or highly specialized clinical setting is considered an advantage. What the employer offers A permanent position at a leading university hospital in Western Sweden. A modern and highly specialized clinical setting. Opportunities to work with advanced prosthetic solutions and complex patient cases. A collaborative and supportive multidisciplinary team. Professional development opportunities and long-term career growth. Competitive employment conditions and a high quality of life in Sweden. What Dignus Medical offers you Ongoing support from a dedicated recruiter before, during, and after your employment. Assistance with obtaining Swedish authorization and specialist approvals, if required. Help with accommodation and relocation, including support for your family. Access to a free Swedish language course with a private tutor via Teams or Skype. Interested? We would be happy to tell you more about this opportunity in a confidential conversation.
