And beyond. ⭐️ THE HEAD OF SALES BELGIUM ROLE ⭐️ Our ambition is to become the market leader in Belgium by 2030. As Head of Sales Belgium, you will own Alan’s commercial growth in the country. You will define and execute our sales strategy, lead and scale the sales organisation, and establish the operating model needed to deliver sustainable, ambitious growth and achieve this ambition. This is a hands-on leadership role. You will develop the team and its managers while remaining personally involved in the most strategic and complex opportunities. Your responsibilities will include: - Owning the Belgian sales strategy, revenue plan, and performance against growth targets. - Leading and developing a sales organisation of approximately 40 people, including managers and individual contributors. - Recruiting, onboarding, coaching, and retaining a high-performing team, while building clear career paths for its members. - Establishing the right sales methodology, processes, metrics, and operating rhythm to make performance predictable and scalable. - Personally supporting the most strategic and complex deals, particularly across enterprise and public-sector accounts. - Partnering with Marketing, Product, Sales Operations, and other teams to improve lead generation, sales efficiency, customer feedback loops, and market positioning. - Ensuring strong alignment between the Belgian go-to-market strategy and Alan’s global commercial approach. - Working closely with the Belgian GM and other senior stakeholders to turn market opportunities into sustainable growth. ⭐️ IS IT WHAT YOU ARE LOOKING FOR? ⭐️ You could be a strong fit for this role if you: - Have significant experience leading, coaching, and scaling B2B sales teams, including through managers or team leads. - Have delivered growth in complex and competitive sales environments, ideally involving enterprise, public-sector, or other long sales cycles. - Are comfortable combining strategic leadership with hands-on involvement in important customer opportunities. - Know how to build a high-performance sales culture through clear goals, strong coaching, effective processes, and data-driven management. - Are fluent in Dutch and English. French-speaking or French-understanding skills are a plus. - Are willing to work from our Brussels office several days per week to build strong relationships with the team and stay close to the business.
