About Wolt At Wolt, we create technology that brings joy, simplicity and earnings to the neighborhoods of the world. In 2014 we started with delivery of restaurant food. Now we’re building the delivery of (almost) everything and you’ll find us in over 500 cities in 30 countries around the world. In 2022 we joined forces with DoorDash and together we keep on dreaming big and expanding across the globe. Working at Wolt isn’t always easy, but it’s definitely exciting. Here you’ll learn more, build more, and ship more than in most other companies. You’ll be challenged a lot, but also have a lot of fun on the way. So, if you’re a self-starter with drive and entrepreneurial spirit, this could be the ride of your life. About the Team The Platform Hiring Team (PHT) is building a centralized, technology-enabled hiring engine for repeatable, high-volume roles across supported DoorDash, Deliveroo and Wolt businesses. You'll join Platform Hiring Operations, PHT's business-facing team. The team acts as a strategic partner to the business, owning demand, market context, and pipeline health. We translate business priorities into effective hiring plans, advise stakeholders on hiring decisions, anticipate risks, and remove blockers to ensure hiring supports business needs. About the Role As a Platform Business Partner, you'll be the primary business-facing partner for Wolt Market across the North region, translating business priorities and workforce needs into effective hiring plans. You'll own business readiness, attraction, and pipeline health, ensuring hiring demand is aligned with business strategy, timing, and cost expectations. You'll act as a trusted advisor to business leaders, using hiring data and talent-market insights to guide decisions on hiring strategy, attraction, location, timing, quality, and delivery trade-offs. You'll proactively identify risks, recommend interventions, and coordinate cross-functional partners to resolve blockers and keep hiring plans on track. You'll also identify opportunities to improve how we work, partnering with the Platform Hiring Manager and PM Team on scalable improvements, automation, and AI adoption. You'll balance local business needs with the centralised platform model, ensuring stakeholders have clear visibility and the right support to achieve their hiring goals.
