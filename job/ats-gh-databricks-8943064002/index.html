MKTQ128R10 Mission Databricks is at the forefront of innovation in the data and AI industry, empowering organisations to harness the power of data and artificial intelligence. As Sr. Field Marketing Manager for UKI, you will play a pivotal role in driving demand generation and business growth across the region. You will be responsible for crafting and executing innovative regional marketing strategies that generate high-quality leads, convert them into opportunities, and build a robust sales pipeline. This cross-functional role requires close collaboration with teams across Marketing, Sales Development, Sales, Partner Management, and other field teams. You will report to the Director of UKI Marketing. The impact you’ll have • Build the territory strategy with sales stakeholders and execute regional marketing programmes. • Lead end-to-end execution of regional field marketing programmes and events, both physical and virtual, ensuring seamless delivery and high engagement. • Build data-driven and integrated marketing plans in close alignment with corporate and EMEA marketing to increase awareness, drive engagement, generate leads, and support conversion rates. • Evaluate and execute the most effective partner and third party event co-marketing opportunities. • Effectively manage budgets and evaluate the ROI of all campaigns. • Collaborate closely with SDR and sales teams to enhance lead-to-opportunity conversion rates. • Generate qualified leads through innovative and cost-effective marketing strategies, driving pipeline growth, consumption and revenue. • Leverage data to continuously refine and optimise campaign strategies, ensuring sustained business growth and programme optimisation. • Establish strong communication channels with sales and regional teams to ensure the delivery of impactful marketing campaigns tailored to the region. What we look for • Proven experience in managing field marketing events, including proprietary, third-party and virtual events, with a track record of successful execution. • Demonstrated ability to deliver tangible business results through strategic marketing programmes. • Ability to lead and collaborate with cross-functional teams in a dynamic and fast-paced enterprise software environment. • Strong ability to develop and optimise data-driven marketing plans, consistently meeting KPIs within budget and tight deadlines. • Proven capability to manage multiple projects and priorities simultaneously, with a focus on execution and delivery. • Proven experience leading with AI, using AI tools from planning to execution to build, run and optimise marketing strategies that deliver measurable results.
