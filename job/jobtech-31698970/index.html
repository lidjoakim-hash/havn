About this opportunity Join Ericsson’s Business Network Engineering Unit as a Baseband System Developer. We are looking for newly graduated engineers to join our team in Lund! You will work in a team focused on developing and contributing to systemization for next-generation Radio Access Network products, with emphasis on baseband algorithms, signal processing, and multi-antenna systems. In this role, you will contribute to technical studies, requirements, simulations, and feature development for advanced wireless technologies. Our culture values inclusivity, collaboration, and innovation, with empathy and diversity as cornerstones. Team members are encouraged to express ideas and contribute to co-creation in a speak-up environment. You will collaborate with engineering, product, and standards-related teams, contributing technical input and analysis that support product development and future technology evolution. Success in this role will be demonstrated by delivering high-quality technical contributions, supporting robust system solutions, and helping improve network performance and efficiency toward product goals. What you will do Contribute to systemization for next-generation Radio Access Network products by supporting system requirements, technical studies, and feature development Develop, analyze, optimize radio resource management (Link level/L2) algorithms, with focus on Energy performance, MIMO, and beamforming strategies Perform link-level and network-level system studies to evaluate wireless network performance and efficiency for 5G/6G technologies Develop and refine software simulations and tools to assess design alternatives and support technical decision-making Collaborate with cross-functional and global teams to support system integration, alignment across technical areas, and successful feature delivery Support standardization-related investigations and contribute technical input in relevant internal and external forums Create and maintain systemization artifacts such as requirements, analysis reports, and architectural input that support product development and feature integration The skills you bring Relevant experience in wireless systems, preferably including 5G and emerging 6G technologies Good foundation in communications theory, digital signal processing, MIMO, and beamforming Analytical and structured problem-solving mindset, with curiosity and willingness to learn Experience with simulation tools and environments for performance evaluation Ability to collaborate across cross-functional and geographically distributed teams Education: BS/MS degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Telecommunications – recently graduated. AI and ML experience for both product development and AI in product solutions
