Role We are looking for a Senior Benefits Operations Specialist to join our People & Culture team. Reporting to the Senior Manager, People Operations EMEA, you will serve as a subject matter expert in the day-to-day execution and administration of our EMEA benefit programs in a remote role based in Ireland. In this position, you will act as the operational backbone of the benefits function, ensuring that the complete employee lifecycle from onboarding to off-boarding is managed with precision, empathy, and regulatory compliance, focusing on high-touch administration of complex leave cases and seamless benefits concierge delivery.
