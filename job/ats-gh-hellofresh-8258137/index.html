The role We are looking for a Senior CRM Manager - Transformation to make CRM Self-Service at HelloFresh a reality. Our central Growth function has a clear vision: Enabling our local marketing and product teams to reach our customers directly, via push notifications, in-app messaging and emails. For this to work in an efficient and customer-centric way, the right guardrails, tools and knowledge need to be in place. Your first mission is to design, launch and scale our CRM self-service model. You’ll do this hand in hand with our CreativeTech squad (Product and Engineering), who are building the future CRM environment in an AI-native way. You’ll work closely with the squad, and potentially be embedded in it. You’ll be the voice of CRM and of our internal users, helping define what this environment needs to look like so teams can send communications safely and independently. This is 0-to-1 work in a highly ambiguous space where no one has been before: no playbook, no rules. AI and LLMs change fast and often, so you’ll think about what we need in the future, even if today’s models aren’t ready yet, and be prepared for when they catch up. The learning curve will be steep, but you’ll climb it on the go, together with the team. Our goal: protect our environment from the agents, and protect the agents from our environment. From there, your focus will follow where CRM needs it most. You’ll take on further transformation projects, so this role is for someone who enjoys building something new and then moving on to the next challenge. Above all, we are looking for people who will make HelloFresh better. We believe there are many different ways of developing skills and we love diverse experiences! So even if you don’t “tick all the boxes” but think you’d thrive in this role, we would really like to learn more about you. What you’ll do • Lead the self-service rollout: Take end-to-end ownership of the programme, from framing the scope and aligning stakeholders to launching and tracking adoption • Shape the future CRM platform: Collaborate with Product and Engineering to define how our agentic CRM environment should work, bringing in the needs of users and helping prioritise what gets built next • Steer CRM Agents: the CreativeTech squad and CRM Operations to adapt AI use cases within our CRM workflows • Influence the CRM product roadmap: Collaborate with Product and Engineering to shape the future CRM platform, bringing in the needs of users and helping prioritise what gets built next What you’ll bring • Strong project or programme management skills, with experience driving complex initiatives across many
