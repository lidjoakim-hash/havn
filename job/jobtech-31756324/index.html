Veritaz is a leading IT staffing solutions provider in Sweden, committed to advancing individual careers and aiding employers in ensuring the perfect talent fit. With a proven track record of successful partnerships with top companies, we have rapidly grown our presence in the USA, Europe, and Sweden as a dependable and trusted resource within the IT industry. Job Overview Role Summary The role focuses on developing, maintaining and supporting mainframe-based applications within the banking and financial services sector. You will contribute to the continuous improvement and modernisation of existing solutions while ensuring reliable data delivery and operational stability. Working closely with colleagues and stakeholders, you will help drive the transformation journey towards more modern and scalable application environments. Key Responsibilities Collect, transform and distribute data products from multiple sources. Ensure timely and quality-assured data delivery to stakeholders across the bank. Manage, maintain and develop existing mainframe solutions. Support the onboarding of Sub Ledgers. Contribute to application modernisation and transformation initiatives. Develop, maintain and support applications throughout their lifecycle. Ensure operational stability and reliable application performance. Collaborate with cross-functional teams in an agile environment. Identify opportunities for improvement and contribute to technical development. Technical Stack & Requirements Mainframe development and environments. SQL and JCL. DB2. OPC (Workload Automation). IBM Developer for z/OS (IDz). COBOL development. Cloud environments, particularly Google Cloud Platform (GCP). Application maintenance, support and operations. Experience incorporating Large Language Models (LLMs) into development workflows. Qualifications & Experience Solid experience working in mainframe environments. Strong COBOL development skills, preferably within the financial services industry. Good knowledge of SQL, JCL, DB2, OPC and IDz. Experience with application development, maintenance and support. Understanding of agile methodologies and collaborative ways of working. Strong communication, analytical and problem-solving skills. Fluency in English. Meritorious Experience working with Google Cloud Platform (GCP). Experience using LLMs in professional development workflows or personal projects. Experience with banking, financial data products or Sub Ledger solutions.
