The role As the Lead Engineer for Low Observability & RF Interoperability, you will direct the effort to minimise the system's electromagnetic signature whilst ensuring all RF systems co-exist without degradation. Your core responsibilities involve developing low-signature operational concepts, managing RF deconfliction, and guiding system-level integration and verification. This role is pivotal in guaranteeing mission-critical performance and resilience, particularly within contested electronic warfare environments, ensuring our platforms maintain operational advantages when it matters most.
