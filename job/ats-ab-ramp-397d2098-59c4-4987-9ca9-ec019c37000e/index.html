We automate how over $275B in annualized spend flows in and out of 85,000+ companies: authorizing payments, flagging risk, categorizing spend, and closing books. The problems are high-stakes, data-dense, and unforgiving. We hire people with high agency and high urgency. We look for slope over intercept. We care less about where you trained and more about what you’ve built. At Ramp, everyone is a builder who owns problems end to end and makes consequential decisions that shape the outcome. The median Ramp customer saves 7.5% and grows revenue 16% in their first year – far in excess of businesses operating without Ramp. We believe every ambitious company deserves the same. If you want to build systems that directly shape how companies move and manage billions, Ramp is the place to do it. ABOUT THE ROLE Ramp is looking for a Senior Underwriter to join our Underwriting team in Europe. You’ll evaluate businesses applying to Ramp, make credit and risk decisions, and help improve how we assess customers across our products. This is an early regional role supporting EU and US applicants. We’re looking for someone with strong financial judgment who can work independently, make clear decisions with incomplete information, and help us build scalable underwriting processes. WHAT YOU’LL DO - Review applications for Ramp’s corporate card and bill pay products across the EU and US. - Analyze financial statements, bank data, bureau information, and other risk signals to assess business health and repayment ability. - Recommend the appropriate outcome, including approval, decline, a lower limit, or a request for additional information. - Communicate clearly with customers when additional information or context is needed. - Manage an underwriting queue while meeting quality and SLA expectations. - Partner with teams across Risk, Product, Engineering, Sales, and Customer Support to improve policies, tools, and workflows. - Identify emerging risks and use AI and automation to improve decision quality and efficiency. WHAT YOU NEED - 4+ years of experience in underwriting, credit risk management, commercial lending, credit operations, or a related risk role. - Experience in corporate cards, payments, fintech, banking, or related financial services. - Strong financial analysis skills and experience reviewing financial statements, bank data, and transaction activity. - Sound judgment and the ability to make defensible decisions with incomplete or conflicting information. - Clear written and verbal communication skills. - Experience operating independently and building or improving underwriting processes in a fast-moving environment. - Experience underwriting European businesses or working with EU-based financial services customers. - Experience using AI or automation to improve workflows. NICE TO HAVE - Experience at a high-growth startup or on a scaling operations team. WORK SCHEDULE This role will follow a schedule aligned with European customer needs, extending Ramp’s underwriting coverage beyond standard US business hours.
