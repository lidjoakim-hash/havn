Product Owner - Air Content

Etraveli Group AB · Stockholm, Sweden

Etraveli Group is a leading global flight technology provider, specializing in flight sales and offering flight content delivery and fintech products. We are here to solve complexity, by connecting millions of flights and travelers across the globe, from search and selection to trip and beyond. We partner with major global platforms such as Booking.com, Google Flights, Skyscanner, and Kayak, providing seamless flight booking and related services. Our B2B portfolio includes Precision, our specialist in travel fintech; Tripstack, our B2B Flights as a Service Provider and world leader in virtual interlining, serving as the gateway to Etraveli Group’s flight content and fulfillment services; Wenrix, the AI Infrastructure for profitable growth in air; and Flygresor.se, Sweden’s leading flight comparison site. We also operate our own online travel agency brands including Gotogate, Mytrip, and Flightnetwork. Every day we strive to make the world smaller for our customers and bigger for our people. Our diverse team of more than 3200 passionate professionals is what makes us the industry’s tech wonder and the best in the world at what we do. Major offices in Sweden (HQ), Greece, India, Canada, Israel, Poland, UK, and Uruguay. The Role We are looking for a highly technical and strategic Product Owner to lead our search experiences. Your mission is to build an independent, ML-driven distribution architecture that tackles complex, industry-wide flight distribution challenges. Success in this role requires balancing deep technical execution with high-level strategic thinking. You will own the development of modern search experiences designed to deliver exceptional speed, quality, and accuracy in flight content for our major enterprise partners. As the central driver of this initiative, you will expertly manage communication and collaboration across internal teams and external stakeholders and partners applying a strong product and commercial mindset to align technical deliverables with broader business goals using product and commercial mindset. Responsibilities With an entrepreneurial mindset you will be responsible for: Maximizing the value of the product resulting from work of the Development Team. Forming the long term product/domain plan and requirement assessment process. Inspiring and motivating the team with vision, long term direction, ambition and making the dev team accountable for the delivery . Continuously prioritizing and following up activities in ongoing product development. Facilitating in order to create a stimulating and creative work environment and accountable teams by focusing on the needs rather than providing solutions. Coordinating, steering and interacting with multiple stakeholders and facilitating a joint understanding by tailoring the language to the audience. Providing post-release and day-to-day support by collecting, filtering, organizing, prioritizing and following up bug reports and questions. Requirements At least 3 years of experience within the Airline Travel Industry. Proven experience as a Product Manager or Product Owner, successfully driving product development from ideation to launch. Ability to break down a complex concept/process into simpler parts and present it to someone with less insights. You are used to working with people and have excellent communication skills (verbal and written communication), being able to tailor your language to the audience you refer to. You thrive when taking on new responsibilities and feel comfortable making decisions. Ability to deliver a data driven approach with a customer-first mindset. Meritorious: Technical knowledge of how GDS and/or LCC booking processes work. Previous experience around Flight search will be a big plus. Experience of working in documentation programs such as Atlassian or Figma. Previous experience working in an Agile world with Scrum and/or Kanban.
