Mission Manager

SSC Space AB · Solna, Sweden

💰 ca. 450 000–550 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 20.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

At SSC Space, you are part of something bigger. Every day, our people contribute to missions that expand knowledge, support critical services, and help society benefit from space. We believe the best results come from passionate experts who collaborate, take ownership, and turn ambitious ideas into reality. As Mission Manager, you will oversee customer payload missions from initial planning through launch and post-mission review. Working in an international environment, you will bring together customers, brokers, satellite manufacturers, launch vehicle providers and operators, and internal teams to define mission requirements and coordinate delivery against agreed objectives. You will serve as the customer's primary point of contact throughout the mission lifecycle, combining technical coordination, project management, and customer relationship management. The role is part of Sales & Customer Operations within Orbital Launch & Rocket Test and reports to the Head of Sales & Customer Operations. JOB REQUIREMENTS To succeed in this role, we believe you have: A master's degree in aerospace engineering, mechanical engineering, or a related field, or equivalent work experience. A proactive, solution-focused mindset and strong analytical skills. The ability to read, understand, and interpret complex technical documentation. Effective communication skills and the ability to build strong relationships with customers, partners, and colleagues. A collaborative approach and the ability to work effectively across teams and organizational boundaries. A high level of spoken and written English. Experience with satellite systems, launch vehicle operations, mission planning, satellite qualification and integration, CAD and space industry practices, as well as familiarity with launch regulations is considered an advantage. A high level of spoken and written Swedish is also desirable. JOB RESPONSIBILITIES In this role, you will: Lead mission planning with customers and partners, defining objectives, requirements, interface control documentation, constraints, launch timelines, orbital parameters, payload integration plans, and mission success criteria. Act as the primary customer contact throughout the mission lifecycle, providing progress updates, responding to inquiries, and managing expectations to support customer satisfaction. Coordinate payload integration, testing, and verification with satellite engineers, launch engineers, payload specialists, and launch vehicle providers and operators. Conduct launch readiness reviews covering technical readiness, safety compliance, regulatory requirements, and risk management, and ensure necessary approvals and certifications are obtained. Oversee and coordinate launch campaign activities, including launch vehicle transportation, pre-launch preparations, and final readiness activities, together with launch site personnel, ground support teams, and external stakeholders. Manage mission projects, identify and assess risks, develop mitigation strategies and contingency plans, and address issues throughout the mission lifecycle. Conduct post-mission reviews, capture lessons learned, and implement improvements to processes and working practices for future missions. WE OFFER YOU Meaningful work connected to science, society and the future of space. The opportunity to collaborate with talented and passionate colleagues across disciplines, countries, and missions. Real responsibility, trust, and the freedom to influence both your work and your development. A unique chance to contribute directly to projects where you can see the impact of your ideas and efforts. Additional Benefits In addition to vacation and traditional public holidays, we offer extra paid leave throughout the year, such as time off between Christmas and New Year and Ascension Day, amounting to 6-11 extra days depending on the calendar.
