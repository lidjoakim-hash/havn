BM SHOWJUMPING AB Experienced Yard Manager – Professional Showjumping Stable Location: Skåne, Sweden Employment: Full-time, permanent position Salary: SEK 34,470–38,000 gross per month Accommodation: May be available by agreement Start date: By agreement About BM Showjumping AB BM Showjumping AB is a professional equestrian business based in Skåne, Sweden, specialising in showjumping horses. We are seeking an experienced, responsible and highly motivated Yard Manager to oversee the daily running of our equestrian facilities and the management, care and welfare of approximately 10–20 horses. This is a key position within our business, combining practical horse management, stable operations, staff supervision and the organisation of our competition activities. We are looking for someone who has substantial experience with competition horses, understands the demands of a professional showjumping environment and can take responsibility for maintaining the highest standards throughout the yard. Main duties and responsibilities 1. Horse management and welfare Taking overall responsibility for the daily care and management of 10–20 horses. Organising feeding, watering, bedding, grooming and general horse care. Monitoring the health, fitness, condition and welfare of each horse. Arranging and coordinating veterinary, farrier and other specialist appointments. Identifying potential health problems and ensuring appropriate action is taken. Managing horse turnout, stable routines and individual care requirements. 2. Stable and yard management Managing the day-to-day operation of the stable and equestrian facilities. Maintaining clean, safe and well-organised stables, paddocks and working areas. Managing feed, bedding, equipment and other supplies. Organising daily work schedules and stable routines. Overseeing routine maintenance and ensuring facilities are maintained to a high standard. 3. Staff supervision and organisation Supervising and coordinating stable staff. Allocating daily duties and monitoring the quality of work. Ensuring staff follow established procedures for horse care and welfare. Organising workloads around training, competitions and other operational requirements. Maintaining a positive, professional and productive working environment. 4. Riding and competition preparation Riding and exercising horses in accordance with their individual training requirements and ability. Assisting with the preparation of horses for showjumping competitions. Coordinating competition schedules and transport arrangements. Ensuring horses are appropriately prepared and cared for before and after competitions. Understanding the particular needs of competition horses and maintaining their fitness and wellbeing. 5. Administration and business operations Maintaining daily records relating to horse care, health and stable activities. Coordinating veterinary and farrier bookings. Managing stock levels and ordering feed, bedding and supplies. Assisting with scheduling, planning and general business administration. Reporting operational issues and contributing to the efficient running of the business. Experience and qualifications Applicants must demonstrate: Significant professional experience working with horses. Previous experience managing or supervising an equestrian yard or stable. Strong knowledge of horse health, welfare, feeding and stable management. Experience with competition horses, preferably showjumping horses. Practical riding experience and the ability to exercise horses appropriately. Experience organising staff and managing daily operational responsibilities. Good organisational, administrative and communication skills. The ability to work independently and take responsibility for decisions. A reliable, flexible and hardworking attitude.
