Business unit: Global Workplace Solutions Role: Facilities Coordinator and Team Lead Location: Central Stockholm, Sweden Reporting line: Senior Facilities Manager, Nordics & Lithuania CBRE Global Workplace Solutions (GWS) produces comprehensive property management services and solutions for its customers. We specialize in managing integrated real estate services, technical maintenance of real estate,project and project management services. At CBRE Global Workplace Solutions we believe our working spaces should allow everyone to feel comfortable,productive and inspired in their working environment. Because when people feel inspired, great things happen. We are a workplace team providing Facility Services across EMEA for a prestigious global technology client. Are you passionate about delivering world-class workspaces, building high-performing teams, and driving operational excellence? We are looking for an energetic and proactive Facilities Coordinator & Team Lead to champion our safety culture, elevate customer experiences, and lead the team of 3 facilities assistants. In this role, you will be the driving force behind our site's daily success, blending exceptional service delivery, innovative problem-solving, and inspiring people management. Core Responsibilities Safety and culture champion Actively foster an environment where safety comes first by logging quality hazards, closing out actions, and embedding compliance and RISE behaviors. Maintain full adherence to CBRE and client health and safety policies, ensuring a secure environment for staff, contractors, and visitors while assisting with evacuation team training and drills. Operational & Customer Excellence Deliver the Ultimate Experience: Provide high-class, professional customer service that keeps our spaces running seamlessly. Own the Environment: Take accountability for all facilities issues, proactively resolving remedial actions and maintaining immaculate space standards. Comprehensive Support: Lead your team in delivering exceptional soft services KPI & SLA Driven: Strive for excellence by consistently hitting and exceeding performance targets while building strong, collaborative relationships with client and supply partners. Innovation & Impact Take ownership of First Day of Business preparations and execution. Drive Continuous Improvement: Champion fresh ideas, share best practices, and constantly look for smart innovations to improve contract performance. Increase efficiency with the use of AI. People Leadership & Development Motivate & Recognize: Build a high-energy, engaged team culture through regular recognition and the performance management cycle. Grow Talent: Understand team strengths, champion professional growth, and support succession planning with consistent, constructive feedback and one-on-ones. Supplier & Financial Stewardship Supervise & Support: Coordinate supplier personnel and subcontractors to ensure flawless service delivery and strict adherence to safety protocols (RAMS reviews, permits, and inductions). Budget Management: Oversee operational budgets, handle invoicing and purchase orders, ensure compliance, and actively identify cost-saving opportunities for our client. Finance You oversee operational budget and accruals within contract guidelines and financial procedures ensuring spend compliance. You are responsible for your budget and you manage all costs to ensure it is respected and address any related queries, including invoicing/ PO's that might arise. You are continually looking for saving opportunities to reduce client costs. Why Join Us? This is a high-visibility, rewarding opportunity where your leadership will directly shape the daily experience of everyone on site. If you are a collaborative problem-solver who thrives in a fast-paced environment and loves bringing out the best in people, we want to hear from you!
