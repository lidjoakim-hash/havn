Did you know that Sweden's largest shipyard for ship repairs and rebuilding is located in Landskrona? Ships come here from all over the world, mainly northern Europe, to take part in our first-class work, and you can become an important part of our project-based delivery. We offer a dynamic work environment with a focus on problem-solving and a high level of service. With us, you will be part of a dedicated team of approximately 110 employees, ranging from project management to production.   We are now looking for a Welder for our Pipe Department - a job for you who want to carry out repairs and maintenance work in the shipbuilding industry! What does a Welder do with us? In the role of welder, you belong to the Pipe department and are responsible for all manufacturing, repairs and maintenance of pipes and fittings, hydraulic equipment and much more! We are looking for someone who has... relevant upper secondary education (industrial programme with a specialisation in mechanics). Experience with TIG/MIG/MAG Experience from heavy industry Experience in the shipbuilding and/or shipping industry is seen as very positive. Knowledge of hydraulics is an advantage. Good knowledge of English B driving licence As a person, you are positive and find it easy to collaborate with colleagues. You are driven, responsible and make sure that the job is done on time. For those who want to develop and grow with us, there are great opportunities to take on greater responsibility in the long term, such as in the Pipe Department or in one of our other departments. We offer At Oresund DryDocks, you will quickly become part of the team. We offer a varied job, with short decision-making paths, in an internationally exciting industry and in an easy-going and inspiring environment. The company consists of a wonderful mix of people from all over the world who are very competent in their areas of expertise. With us, everyone is equally important and highly valued. We help each other and contribute to Oresund DryDocks being an exciting and developing workplace. With us, you will have the opportunity to develop both as a person and in your professional role. You will be able to influence your work in both large and small. Every day we are faced with new opportunities and challenges. You need to be on site at the shipyard and we expect you to support and build trust with customers, subcontractors and among your colleagues.
