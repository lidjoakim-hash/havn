Role highlights - Role: Product Intern, ICEYE Intern Lab - Location: Espoo, Finland (onsite), potentially with some travel - Department: Product Management - Employment type: Fixed-term, fully paid internship (3-6 months, flexible) - Start: April / early May 2027 - Employment is subject to applicable security screening (incl. SUPO, where required) WHY THIS ROLE IS DIFFERENT Few people get to start their product career in space. You will. ICEYE Intern Lab is a fully paid, onsite internship for high-potential students who want to see what great product work looks like on real problems. You don't need product management experience to apply. We're looking for curious, fast-learning students with a strong academic record, ideally in a technical field, and a hands-on builder mindset. You'll join one of our Product teams as a contributing team member, working on a live project (not a simulation) with a dedicated manager and a mentor. You'll work closely with engineers, scientists and senior leaders, and your work will shape how customers use satellite data to make decisions that matter. The internship is fast and demanding, and it will stretch you. After 90 days, your manager and HRBP review your progress and discuss potential full-time opportunities where there is an approved opening. Spots are limited and we review applications on a rolling basis, so apply early. WHO WE ARE ICEYE is the world leader in sovereign intelligence from space. We deliver persistent monitoring capabilities to detect and respond to changes in any location on Earth. ICEYE owns the world's largest and most advanced SAR (synthetic aperture radar) satellite constellation, giving customers intelligence with unmatched quality, latency and revisit times, in any weather, day or night. ICEYE-built constellations serve customers in defence and intelligence, environmental monitoring, insurance and emergency management. Founded and headquartered in Finland, ICEYE operates globally with over 1000 employees across Europe, North America, the Middle East and Asia-Pacific.
