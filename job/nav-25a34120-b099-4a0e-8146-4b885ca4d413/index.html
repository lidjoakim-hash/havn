Lead a small team, make a big difference! FOOD TRUCK SITE COORDINATORS Eidfjord & Norheimsund, Norway Your next season could be here: good food, a small team and Norway's fjords all around you. We're inviting two experienced shift leaders to join us - one in Eidfjord and one in Norheimsund. If you enjoy welcoming guests, working alongside your colleagues and helping a busy day run well, we'd love to meet you. You'll be a working member of the team as well as its coordinator. One location has two employees plus the coordinator, and the other has three plus the coordinator. You'll know your team, share the work and help everyone pull together. You'll report directly to the CEO and work with HR on schedules. Bring your initiative and practical experience - there's a small team here that you can help make a real difference to. Your part in the team • Prepare food, welcome guests and work alongside your team throughout the shift. • Give everyone a clear plan and help the team keep orders moving smoothly. • Take pride in food quality, friendly service and a clean, well-organised workplace. • Keep track of stock and prepare orders so you have what you need for service. • Find practical solutions to everyday challenges and ask for support with important issues. • Stay in touch with the CEO about groups and related arrangements. • Work with HR on schedules, days off and the team's staffing needs. • Share a weekly update with the CEO and raise important matters as they happen. • Keep receipts for all authorised product purchases. You'll feel at home in this role if... • You've led shifts in food service and have practical hospitality experience. • You enjoy helping a small team work well together, especially when service gets busy. • You're comfortable working across all operational positions. • You take initiative, look for solutions and follow through. • Your colleagues can count on you to communicate clearly and lend a hand. • You speak English at B2-C1 level and hold a valid category B driving licence. • You have basic computer skills for reporting and everyday administrative tasks. What's waiting for you • Two full-time, hourly-paid roles, one at each location, from mid-April to the end of September. • The possibility of joining us again for the following season. • A small team to lead, direct contact with the CEO and support from HR. • Help finding accommodation for your season in Norway. • A staff meal on each working day. Join our team and spend the season surrounded by Norway's breathtaking fjords! With food trucks in Flåm and other beautiful locations, we're all about great food, friendly faces, and making people feel welcome. We'd love to have you on board and share an unforgettable season together! Send your CV and recomendation: job&#64;grpnorway.com
