SUPO, where required) WHY THIS ROLE MATTERS: ICEYE is looking for a Mission Management Team Lead to lead the team that takes every satellite from build start to commercial acceptance in orbit. Every ICEYE satellite has a named Mission Manager who owns its schedule, risks and status at tail-number level, working across assembly, integration and testing (AIT), Engineering, Reliability & Mission Assurance, Configuration Management, Launch Management and Satellite Operations. As Team Lead, you set the standard for how that work is done, grow the people who do it, and act as the escalation point when a satellite's schedule, risk or customer commitment is on the line. WHO WE ARE ICEYE is the world leader in sovereign intelligence from space. We deliver persistent monitoring capabilities to detect and respond to changes in any location on Earth. ICEYE owns the world's largest and most advanced SAR (synthetic aperture radar) satellite constellation. To our customers we provide intelligence with unmatched quality, latency and revisit times, in any weather, day or night. To governments who choose to operate their own constellation we provide this proven capability as a sovereign system. ICEYE-built constellations serve customers in defence and intelligence, environmental monitoring, insurance and emergency management. We enable fast decisions that contribute to a safer future. Founded and headquartered in Finland, ICEYE operates globally with over 1000 employees across Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. YOUR DAY-TO-DAY RESPONSIBILITIES   Lead and grow the team - Lead, mentor and develop a team of Mission Managers, setting clear priorities, roles and performance standards, and building a culture of ownership and continuous improvement. - Plan team capacity and satellite assignments as build cadence grows; hire and onboard new Mission Managers through a structured ramp-up. - Scale the team's proactive risk management as satellite cadence grows, through clear processes, good tooling and reliable data. Own the Build-to-Launch process - Own and evolve the Build-to-Launch process and its review framework: design, test and readiness checkpoints throughout the build, and decision gates for flight acceptance, launch readiness and commercial acceptance, applied consistently across products, sites and customers. - Ensure every gate is held with complete inputs, and that residual risks are clearly presented for acceptance by the gate approver. - Keep a clear, data-driven view of how every satellite is tracking, from review timeliness to total risk exposure, and share it with manufacturing leadership. Nonconformance and mission risk - Own the highest-priority issues, driving root cause analysis and corrective actions together with AIT Engineering and Reliability & Mission Assurance, and making sure lessons learned reach future builds. - Own the per-satellite risk register and assess the mission risk of every disposition that leaves a deviation on a flight satellite (use as is, repair, limited use): impact on schedule, launch slot, customer commitments and operations in space. - Act as the escalation path for mission critical risks that need a waiver decision. LEOP and commissioning - Make sure the team runs launch and early orbit phase preparation, execution and commissioning to one repeatable standard across missions and customers, and act as the escalation point for critical in-orbit events. - Set the standard for commissioning plans and reporting, and ensure in-orbit anomalies and their risks are handed over cleanly to Satellite Operations.
