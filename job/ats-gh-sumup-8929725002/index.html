Team description SumUp Spain is growing across four office locations, and we're building our own internal prevention service so that health and safety is looked after by an expert who knows our people and our offices. You'll be the first specialist in this function, starting from a blank page and setting up the service, the prevention plan and the way safety works at every site. You'll work with Workplace, People, Legal and managers, with real influence on how safety becomes part of everyday life at SumUp. Please note this is a part-time contract of 20 hours per week. 🎥 Take a look inside our Barcelona office What you'll do • Set up and run SumUp Spain's internal prevention service across all offices • Review existing risk assessments, close gaps and build the first internal prevention plan • Agree in writing which activities stay with the external prevention provider and which move to the internal service • Deliver and track mandatory safety training, and keep documentation audit ready • Set up contractor coordination (CAE) and report safety KPIs regularly to leadership What you'll bring • Qualification as a Técnico Superior en Prevención de Riesgos Laborales, with a Master's in Occupational Risk Prevention and at least two specialisations from Safety at Work, Industrial Hygiene, or Ergonomics and Applied Psychosociology. • Hands on experience running a prevention function independently, including risk assessments, prevention plans and contractor coordination (CAE) documentation under Spanish law. • Strong knowledge of Ley 31/1995 and RD 39/1997, and experience working alongside an external prevention provider. • Track record of building a prevention function from scratch or near scratch, with accurate, audit ready documentation. • Fluency in business level Spanish, working knowledge of English and the ability to explain regulations in plain language. Why you should join SumUp • 🌎 Opportunity to work with SumUppers globally on large-scale fintech products used by millions of businesses worldwide, from our Barcelona office.
