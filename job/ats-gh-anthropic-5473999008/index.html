About Anthropic Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems. About the role Anthropic's cloud partners are some of the biggest routes for Claude into enterprises across Europe, the Middle East and Africa. Each hyper scaler plans its marketing globally, and requires a dedicated regional counterpart who knows the EMEA market and can turn global programmes into activity that lands with customers regionally. As Partner Marketing Manager, Cloud Partners, EMEA, you'll be that counterpart. You'll own the EMEA end of our AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft relationships. You'll work closely with our global cloud partner marketing team and with EMEA partnerships, sales and marketing, to ensure that our joint GTM investments land with the customers and industries that matter most in EMEA. Your job is to make sure every major global cloud moment has a clear EMEA plan behind it, that regional co-marketing funding goes to regional business priorities, and that our cloud partners in the region always know how best to work with Anthropic. This is a chance to help build and shape EMEA partner marketing from the ground up, including how the function works. campaigns for named enterprise accounts • Track what each programme delivers in pipeline, influenced revenue and customer adoption, and report it back to partners and to Anthropic leadership • Turn
