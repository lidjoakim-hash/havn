Mycronic is a global high-tech company whose innovative solutions have been advancing electronics technology for over 50 years. Today we continue to grow and serve customers in an expanding variety of industries. What we do impacts the future of technology, and in turn, the way we live our lives tomorrow. We’re looking for people to join our talented, motivated and friendly team on the cutting-edge. Here, in an open, collaborative and flexible environment, you’ll work alongside leading industry experts and take on challenging projects that bring tomorrow’s electronics to life. Welcome to Mycronic! About the role Mycronic has experienced strong growth in recent years, both organically and through acquisitions. With headquarters in Sweden, Mycronic is a global organization with subsidiaries, agents, and distributors in over 50 countries. In this role, you will be part of Mycronic's PCB Assembly Solutions division (PA), a leading provider of equipment and solutions for surface mount technology (SMT). Our solutions enable electronics manufacturers to adapt production quickly while maintaining high efficiency, ranging from individual systems for specific process steps to complete production lines and advanced software for assembly process management and connected factories.  In this role, you will be part of a multidisciplinary engineering group comprising mechanical, electronics, and mechatronics engineers. You will also collaborate closely with colleagues across different functions, including architects, service, project management, product management, production, and management. With the majority of Mycronic’s R&D operations and production facilities located on-site in Kista, you will have the opportunity to work in close proximity to both the products you develop and the people involved in bringing them to life. Work tasks You will take part in the entire development chain, from requirements specification to design and implementation. This involves working with a diverse range of materials and technologies, such as welded and machined steel structures, aluminum and plastic components of various sizes, high-precision parts and units for highly accurate motion control.  You will for example: Work with the mechanical design and development of a range of products within the PA Division, including Mycronic Jet Printers, MYTower systems and Pick-and-Place machines.  Handle multiple parallel projects and work with both new development, maintenance and improvement of existing solutions and redesigning of current products.  Challenge and improve existing ways of working and current solutions by identifying opportunities for product development, driving innovation and initiating new ideas for solutions, features and accessories. Prepare and deliver presentations to senior management, highlighting technical developments, product improvement opportunities, new technologies and how mechanical engineering can contribute to the company's long-term growth and competitiveness. We are looking for This role is ideal for an experienced and driven Mechanical Design Engineer with a genuine passion for technology and engineering. You enjoy challenging established ways of working, questioning existing solutions and exploring new possibilities, while valuing the knowledge and perspectives of others. You take a hands-on approach to mechanical design, enjoy driving improvements and innovation, and have a strong ability to turn ideas into practical solutions. You are confident in your technical expertise, take initiative in driving technical development and naturally inspire and engage colleagues around you. You enjoy sharing your knowledge, exchanging ideas and getting others on board to move things forward.
