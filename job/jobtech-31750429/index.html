IT Support Technician (IT Supporttekniker) – Gothenburg, Sweden Location: Gothenburg, Sweden Work Arrangement: 100% Onsite Employment Type: Full-time Visa Sponsorship: Not Available Language: English required; Chinese language skills are an advantage. About the Role We are looking for a motivated and experienced IT Support Technician to join our team in Gothenburg, Sweden. The successful candidate will provide technical support to end users, troubleshoot IT-related issues, and ensure smooth daily IT operations. This is a 100% onsite position based in Gothenburg. Candidates must already have the legal right to work in Sweden, as visa sponsorship is not available. Key Responsibilities Provide first-line and second-line IT support to end users. Troubleshoot hardware, software, and network-related issues. Install, configure, and maintain laptops, desktops, printers, and other IT equipment. Support Windows operating systems and Microsoft 365 applications. Manage user accounts and access permissions using Active Directory. Provide onsite technical assistance and resolve IT incidents. Support basic networking issues, including LAN, Wi-Fi, VPN, DNS, and DHCP. Document technical issues and solutions in the IT ticketing system. Coordinate with internal IT teams to resolve complex technical problems. Ensure IT equipment and systems operate efficiently. Requirements Previous experience in IT support, helpdesk, or desktop support. Good knowledge of Windows 10/11 and Microsoft 365. Experience with Active Directory and user account management. Basic knowledge of networking and IT infrastructure. Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills. Good English communication skills. Ability to work independently and collaborate with a team. Must be able to work onsite in Gothenburg, five days per week. Must already have the legal right to work in Sweden. Preferred Qualifications Chinese language skills (Mandarin) are a strong advantage. Swedish language skills are an advantage. Experience with Microsoft Intune and Microsoft Entra ID. Knowledge of ITIL processes. Experience with ServiceNow, Jira, or similar ticketing systems. Relevant IT certifications. What We Offer An opportunity to work in an international IT environment. Exposure to modern IT technologies. Professional development opportunities. A collaborative and supportive team environment. How to Apply Interested candidates are invited to submit their updated CV. Please include "IT Support Technician – Gothenburg" in your application. Important: This position requires full-time onsite attendance in Gothenburg. Visa sponsorship is not available. Applicants must already have the legal right to work in Sweden. We look forward to receiving your application!
