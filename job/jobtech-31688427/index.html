Professionals Nord grundades med ambitionen att skapa en plats för människor att växa, på riktigt. En plats för människor att utvecklas, utmanas och nå sin fulla potential. Det är fortsatt drivkraften bakom allt vi gör – kompetensförsörjning inom rekrytering, personaluthyrning och Accelerated Learning. Vi utvecklar företag, individer och bidrar till att förändra branschen. Our client is an international company operating within the healthcare and life sciences industry, dedicated to developing and delivering innovative medical solutions that make a difference in people's lives. With a strong global presence, the company works to improve access to important treatments and healthcare solutions for patients worldwide. You will become part of the Global Supply Chain Operations department, playing an important role in ensuring the efficient flow and availability of products across the organization. Your work will contribute to making essential treatments accessible to patients around the world. Does this sound interesting? Send in your application today! Information about the position Professionals Nord is, on behalf of our client, looking for a Global Distribution Coordinator. You will work as a consultant through our staffing business.  Work tasks Customer Order administration, order taking, batch reservation, pick and pack ordering, shipment booking and monitoring, invoicing, crediting, returns and customer qualification Month-end financial reporting in terms of Stock and Sales Responsible for monotoring interface transactions between HQ and affiliates Serial Number alerts management (if applicable) Process Continues Improvement (if applicable) We are looking for You who has a education from university with focus on supply chain, logistics, export administration and/ or economics You who has experience in the medical or/and logistics field.  Fluent in Swedish and English, both orally and in writing Very good computer skills It is meritorious if you Have knowledge in Swedish, both orally and in writing Experience from a similar role in the pharmaceutical Industry   In this recruitment process we put big focus on your personal characteristics. To succeed in the role we see that you are: Structured Analytical A fast learner Have a business mindset START: As soon as possible COVERAGE: Full-time, until January 2026 CITY: Stockholm  SELECTION: Ongoing Apply for the position by clicking on "apply for the job/sök jobbet" below.
