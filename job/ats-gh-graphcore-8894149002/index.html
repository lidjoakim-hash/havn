Location: Bristol, UK Salary: £45,000 per annum Start date: September 2027 Applications deadline: Midnight, 29th November 2026 We encourage you to apply early, as we may close applications before the advertised deadline. The Graduate Programme at Graphcore Join Graphcore in a permanent role and start your career with two years of hands-on learning. From day one, you’ll be trusted to take ownership, share your ideas and help shape what we’re creating. You’ll tackle challenging problems alongside a team who support your development and are as excited about your success as you are. What you’ll work on Put your engineering foundations to work on the electronic systems behind Graphcore’s AI compute platforms. This role spans Hardware Design and Signal Integrity. Depending on your interests, project needs and developing strengths, you could focus on either area or work across both. You’ll build broad board-level engineering skills while developing deeper expertise. In Hardware Design, you could develop circuits, select components, create schematics and work with PCB layout through review, bring-up and validation. Projects may involve digital and analogue circuitry, power sequencing, monitoring, board management or interfaces such as I2C, SPI and GPIO. As you grow, you’ll take ownership of scoped circuits or subsystems through to controlled design release. In Signal Integrity, you could analyse PCIe and Ethernet links, DDR memory interfaces, clocks and other timing-critical signals. Work may span feasibility studies, PCB stack-up, materials, controlled impedance, routing and layout reviews, alongside pre-layout and post-layout simulation. You’ll compare simulations with laboratory measurements, investigate issues and take increasing ownership of defined analyses and interface reviews. Your team and support Hardware Design and Signal Integrity engineers work closely with PCB layout, silicon, validation, firmware, mechanical and thermal engineering, manufacturing and system teams. Together, they turn requirements into buildable hardware and investigate how it behaves in practice. Staff Hardware Engineers and Team Leads will support you through design reviews, laboratory work and day-to-day collaboration. You’ll learn how the team identifies risks before boards are built and resolves issues during development. Bring your questions and ideas, explain your reasoning and use evidence to challenge assumptions constructively. Take responsibility for your work and speak up when you spot a problem or need support. How you’ll learn and grow During the two-year programme, you’ll develop the judgement to connect circuit design and interconnect behaviour with the performance of a complete board. You’ll gain experience with tools such as Mentor Expedition for hardware design, and Ansys HFSS and Keysight ADS for electromagnetic and circuit simulation. Comparing models with measurements will help you test hypotheses, understand trade-offs and investigate faults methodically. Following work through review, implementation, validation and release will help you build confidence in your decisions, document your reasoning and see where you’d like to deepen your expertise.
