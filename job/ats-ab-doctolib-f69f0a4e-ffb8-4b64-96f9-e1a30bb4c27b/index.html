THE OPPORTUNITY Founded in 2013, Doctolib is Europe's leading AI and digital health company, serving 580,000 health professionals and 100 million people. We are building a new way to build software: agents doing the grunt work of writing, testing, reviewing and merging code, with engineers moving into a control-tower role to monitor and intervene. Your mission: build the tooling and workflows that let AI agents and the engineers who work alongside them  test, review, ship and monitor code reliably, so Doctolib can multiply its engineering output while keeping quality and control. THE ROLE We're looking for a Director of AI Developer Experience to lead our new AI Developer Experience team, reporting to the VP Platform Engineering. You will also own the existing CI and Developer Experience scope. Doctolib is moving AI adoption from a temporary SWAT team to a permanent capability. The current team includes two Product Managers and two engineers, with more people joining through internal transfers and hiring. Your team will own: - The agent-native testing and feedback infrastructure that lets agents know, within their own workflow, whether what they built is correct. - CI/CD and build systems for the monolith and satellite services, elevated from a CI-run team into an efficient agent-testing capability. - Developer productivity measurement in the AI era DORA, autonomous change rate and complementary indicators. - The inner-loop experience: local and remote environments, onboarding, editor integration and sandboxing. YOUR IMPACT Your responsibilities include but are not limited to: - Build the tools and workflows that allow agents to write, test, review and merge code, including evaluation, security checks and controls giving agents a fast, reliable feedback loop on whether what they build is correct. - Own testing and feedback for the monolith and satellite services, elevating the current CI setup from a CI-run team into an efficient agent-testing capability. - Own developer productivity measurement in the context of AI DORA, autonomous change rate and complementary indicators  and make it visible and actionable to feature teams. - Lead the inner-loop experience: local and remote environments, onboarding, editor integration and sandboxing. - Build and organise the team, developing agentic skills internally where needed, as AI adoption shifts from a temporary SWAT team to a permanent one. - Partner with Product Engineering and Product Management to understand internal users and drive adoption. YOUR TEAM Reporting to the VP Platform Engineering, Mike Aidane https://www.linkedin.com/in/maidane/, you'll join the Platform Engineering leadership team alongside directors and senior EMs for Application Platform, Data Services and Platform Services. You will lead the new AI Developer Experience team, which includes Automation Platform (CI team), ILOT (Internal LLMs Ops & Training team) and TestingOps. You'll partner closely with Product Engineering, Product Management, Release Engineering, Quality Engineering, Infrastructure Platform, SRE, FinOps, Security, Privacy and Compliance, and engineering teams across France and Germany. YOUR PROFILE You have led engineering organisations through large-scale Developer Experience transformations and know what it takes to ship great tooling fast, durably, and without sacrificing quality. You think in systems technical and organisational and are as comfortable setting architecture direction as you are making a hard technical call. Core qualifications: - You have led Developer Experience, Developer Productivity, Developer Infrastructure or Build/CI organisations serving 300+ engineers.
