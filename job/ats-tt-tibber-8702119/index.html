We’re Tibber - the energy company that wants you to use less energy. When you join Tibber, you’ll be part of building the tech that creates a real shift in how people consume electricity - good for our users, their wallets and the planet. We believe AI is how we accelerate that shift, and it’s our people who lead it. The more we lean into AI, the more human judgment, curiosity and creativity matter. We’re looking for a Staff Engineer to join us – maybe that’s you? Job Mission 🚀 As a Staff Engineer, you’ll play a key role in turning EVs, chargers and batteries into one reliable Virtual Power Plant. You’ll join a domain with three sub teams with a total of 15-20 engineers, reporting to the Engineering Lead for the domain. Together, we connect these devices, decide when they run and own the experience from gateway to app. The direction you set feeds straight into what customers save per kWh, and into a greener, more resilient grid. What you'll do ✅ • Set the medium-term technical direction for the domain with fellow engineers and Engineering Leads • Work across domains with fellow Staff Engineers to jointly define Tibber’s overall technical architecture. • Stay hands-on with code, architecture and technical design, finding the right balance between building and technical leadership. • Our domain is moving towards fully agentic development flows, and you’ll help lead the way. • Help engineers make decisions that lower the future cost of change and reduce dependencies, through principles, design reviews and RFCs. • Align work across teams, bridge product and technical vision, escalate early and step into the ownership gaps others walk past. • Spot opportunities and risks, propose and drive solutions, and frame tech debt in terms of business value. • Build reliability, observability and scalability into how our systems are made, improve how we work and set a high bar for facilitation. What we're looking for⚡ You are driven by impact and know how to turn strategy into execution. You’re used to fast-paced environments and we believe you’ll thrive here if you: • Bring Staff-level experience. You’ve worked as a Staff or senior engineer, where your technical calls shaped more than one team. • Build agentic-first. You have proven, hands-on experience with agentic software development, using tools like Claude Code and MCP servers. • Feel at home in our stack. You’re strong in C# / .NET, our main language, and comfortable with TypeScript/Node.js. You build confidently on AWS, Kubernetes and Terraform. • Bring deep expertise. You’re a recognised expert in at least one technical or business-critical domain and the systems around it. • Multiply others. You make engineers and teams around you more effective through mentoring and coaching. People seek you out, and you seek out those who could use a hand. • Steer through ambiguity. You take initiative where there’s no task list, guide conflicting views towards decisions that fit our strategy • Skilled at stakeholder management. You keep EMs, PMs and senior leaders aligned .
