As a Support Experience Guide, you're the human expert who steps in when our AI agent Finn needs a helping hand. You don't just fix problems; you make our users feel heard and build trust that lasts. Every interaction isn't just a closed ticket, it's a lesson that makes Finn smarter for next time. Finn is smart, but you're the genius behind the curtain 🤖✨ Take Ownership: • Make sure every user feels supported, understood, and never left behind: you'll take over escalated conversations that Finn (our AI assistant) hands off to a human. • Get to the root cause of why users needed to reach out at all, and drive fixes that remove the underlying problem: not just close the ticket. • Make Finn smarter after every interaction: spot where it fell short, and feed that back so the next Eva gets a better answer. • You’re curious by nature: you look for ways to improve how things work, not just how to get through the queue. • You’re calm under pressure, with a positive, solution-driven mindset when things get hectic. • You're comfortable being measured on KPIs and performance metrics. • You’re a teamplayer: steady, thoughtful, and proactive: the person others know they can count on. • You’re fully and perfectly comfortable speaking and writing in English. • You’re tech-savy and comfortable with new tools. • You see AI as an opportunity to actively look for ways to use it to work faster and better. • You’re available to cover morning, evening, and night shifts on weekdays, and on weekends.
