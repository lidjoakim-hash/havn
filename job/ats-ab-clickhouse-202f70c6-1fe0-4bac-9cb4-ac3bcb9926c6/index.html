Engagement Manager - EMEA

ClickHouse · United Kingdom, United Kingdom

ClickHouse is the open-source, real-time analytical database behind a lot of today's AI. More than 5,000 companies build on ClickHouse, including Anthropic, OpenAI, Tesla and Meta. As an Engagement Manager on our team, you run the operational side of our consulting projects. You make sure the right people are on the right work at the right time, that every project is scoped, scheduled, tracked and billed properly, and that customers, partners and our own engineers always know where things stand. Our Support Services team has always worked hands-on with customers. As more large enterprises move onto ClickHouse, that work is becoming a formal professional services practice, with scoped projects, partners delivering alongside us and hundreds of projects in flight each year. You'll be one of our first Engagement Managers, so you'll help build how this works. WHAT YOU'LL DO - Perform as an Engagement Manager in your region to manage operations and oversee delivery and reporting of consulting projects - Staff each project with Consulting Engineers, TAM Architects, Forward-Deployed Engineers, and partner consultants, working closely with Consulting Leadership - Collaborate with Customers, Technical Support, Product Management, and Engineering to ensure user and customer success with ClickHouse Cloud and products - Work with Sales, Finance, partners, and subcontractors on statements of work, agreements, quotes, order forms, and billing with a high degree of organization and accuracy - Develop a broad understanding of ClickHouse use cases and technology in order to identify and communicate planning and value in customer and partner meetings - Keep a clear plan and schedule for every project, and flag risks to scope, timeline, or budget including tracking time, utilisation, and progress WHAT YOU'LL BRING - 5+ years in professional services engagement or management, consulting, or program or project delivery, ideally at a Cloud or software company - Experience staffing and scheduling technical consultants across many projects at once - A good grasp of the commercial side of services: statements of work, time and materials, order forms, and billing - Experience working with partners and subcontractors on customer projects - You're highly organised and keep lots of moving parts visible to everyone involved - You're confident with engineers and customers, and curious about the technology to understand the work you're staffing - Fluent English NICE TO HAVE - Experience with professional services automation (PSA) tools - Salesforce experience - A background at a database, data, or cloud company GOOD TO KNOW - Remote (Amsterdam, Netherlands or London, UK) - This role isn't billed to customers. Your job is to make delivery run smoothly, not to deliver the work yourself - Travel is occasional, for specific projects or internal events - You'll work closely with our Consulting Management, TAM Architects, and Consulting Engineers, and with many other ClickHouse teams PERKS - Flexible work environment - ClickHouse is a globally distributed company and remote-friendly. We currently operate in over 25 countries. - Healthcare - Employer contributions towards your healthcare. - Equity in the company - Every new eligible team member who joins our company receives equity. - Time off - Flexible time off in the US, generous entitlement in other countries. - A USD$500 Home office setup if you’re a remote employee. - Global Gatherings – We believe in the power of in-person connection and offer opportunities to engage with colleagues at company-wide offsites. CULTURE - WE ALL SHAPE IT As part of a rapidly scaling start-up, you will be instrumental in shaping our culture. Are you interested in finding out more about our culture?
