Experienced Indian Cuisine Cook (South Indian, North Indian, Bengali & Gujarati Specialties) Hva vi ser etter • Minimum 3-5 years of experience as an Indian Cuisine Cook or Chef. • Proven expertise in South Indian, North Indian, Bengali, and Gujarati cooking styles. • Strong knowledge of Indian spices, ingredients, and traditional cooking techniques. • Experience with vegetarian and non-vegetarian Indian dishes. • Good understanding of food safety and hygiene standards. • Team player with a positive attitude and strong work ethic. Arbeidsoppgaver We are seeking a skilled and passionate Indian Cuisine Cook to join our kitchen team. The ideal candidate should have strong experience preparing a wide variety of authentic Indian dishes, with expertise in South Indian, North Indian, Bengali, and Gujarati cuisine. The successful candidate will be responsible for maintaining high food quality standards, ensuring kitchen hygiene, and contributing to menu development. Key Responsibilities • Prepare and cook authentic Indian dishes from: • South Indian cuisine (dosa, idli, vada, sambhar, rasam, biryanis, Chettinad dishes, etc.) • North Indian cuisine (tandoori items, curries, kebabs, naan, parathas, Punjabi specialties, etc.) • Bengali cuisine (fish curries, kosha mangsho, shorshe dishes, mishti preparations, etc.) • Gujarati cuisine (dhokla, thepla, undhiyu, kadhi, farsan, vegetarian specialties, etc.) • Ensure consistency in taste, presentation, and portion sizes. • Prepare ingredients, sauces, marinades, and spice blends according to traditional recipes. • Assist in menu planning and development of regional Indian specialties. • Monitor food inventory and assist with stock ordering. • Follow food safety, hygiene, and sanitation regulations. • Maintain a clean and organized kitchen environment. • Train and guide junior kitchen staff when required. Vi tilbyr • Competitive salary based on experience and skills. • Health insurance coverage. • Employer pension contribution. • Opportunity to work in a professional and multicultural environment. • Career growth and development opportunities. • Staff meals during work shifts. • Stable employment with a growing business.
