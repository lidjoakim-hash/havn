We are looking for a Social Media & Creative Intern to join our B2C Marketing team in Milan. Doctolib operates in dynamic and competitive markets. We contribute to the digitalisation of healthcare systems by improving access to care and simplifying healthcare professionals’ working lives across France, Germany and Italy. As part of our Italian B2C Marketing team, you will support the development and execution of social media and creative content designed to engage our patient community and raise awareness of Doctolib’s mission. You will contribute to the ideation, production and publication of content across our social media channels, combining creativity, strong storytelling and a genuine interest in healthcare and medical topics. This is an opportunity to join a dynamic and international marketing team, experiment with different formats, and help make healthcare topics more accessible, useful and engaging for everyone. WHAT YOU’LL DO Your responsibilities will include: - Supporting the planning and execution of social media content across platforms such as Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and other relevant channels - Contributing to the creation and management of the social media content calendar, coordinating timelines, briefs, approvals and deliverables with internal and external stakeholders - Developing creative content ideas based on social media trends, audience insights, Doctolib’s communication priorities and relevant healthcare topics - Supporting the production of visual, video and editorial content, including short-form videos, Reels, Stories, carousels, static posts and other social media formats - Supporting community management by monitoring comments, questions and interactions across social media channels - Participating in creative brainstorming sessions and collaborating with internal teams, agencies and external partners when relevant - Ensuring that all content follows Doctolib’s brand guidelines, internal processes and applicable legal, privacy and healthcare communication requirements - Analysing performance data and external benchmarks to derive insights and propose actions WHO YOU ARE You could be our next teammate if you: - Have a background in communications, media, marketing or a related creative field, or equivalent practical experience, combined with a genuine passion for social media, digital culture and content creation - Are creative, curious and eager to experiment with new ideas, formats and storytelling approaches - Have a strong interest in healthcare, medical topics and Doctolib’s mission - Have strong copywriting skills, with the ability to adapt your tone of voice to different audiences and platforms, as well as a good eye for visual communication, design and storytelling - Are comfortable using graphic design and video editing tools such as Canva, Adobe Creative Cloud, CapCut, Lovable or similar platforms, and contributing to the production of video and photo content - Have excellent communication skills in Italian and English - Are comfortable using AI tools and adopting an AI-first approach It would be fantastic if you: - Have created content for personal projects, university initiatives, student organisations, social media channels or previous internships, ideally with experience in short-form video production, filming, editing, lighting or sound recording - Have a basic understanding of social media analytics and performance metrics - Have a portfolio or examples of creative work that you would be happy to share with us AT THE END OF YOUR INTERNSHIP, YOU WILL HAVE LEARNED TO - Plan and execute social media content across different platforms and formats - Develop creative concepts and transform them into engaging visual and editorial content - Produce, record and edit short-form video content for social media - Adapt copy, formats and storytelling techniques to different audiences and communication objectives -
