THE AGENTIC WORK ENVIRONMENT TEAM The Agentic Work Environment (AWE) owns the environment everyone at Multiverse works in, wherever they are in the world. That covers the devices, workplace tools and accounts colleagues use every day, the platform our product is built and run on, and the identity and access that decide who, or which agent, can do what across all of it. Product teams own what they build; we own the ground it stands on, the path it takes to production, and the baseline standards it meets on the way. Our mission is to make the safe way the fast way for every colleague and the agents they put to work. For a security-minded AI engineer, that remit runs in two directions. In the product platform, you build security into the path itself: review agents that check every change against our baseline standards, one identity for customers, staff and agents, and detection and response through our SIEM and SOAR, so nobody, person or agent, has to remember to be secure. In the workplace, you secure the devices, tools and accounts colleagues around the world rely on. As more of them put agents to work, whatever their job title, you make sure every agent has its own identity, a named owner, scoped access and evals, so autonomy is earned on evidence rather than granted on trust.
