We engage with Sales, Product, and Engineering to structure security initiatives that add business value and foster high levels of customer satisfaction and loyalty. Our customers are our true north, and we live this value by consulting directly with strategically important customers to provide resolution on significant, business-critical security issues. This is a hybrid role based in our Prague office. Our hybrid model means you’ll work from the office at least two days each week.
