Lead Stylist (m/f/x) The role As Lead Stylist at HelloFresh, you will play a key leadership role in shaping the visual identity of our food content across recipe photography, brand campaigns, and multimedia channels. You’ll lead a team of talented food and set stylists, fostering a collaborative, high-performing studio environment that balances creativity, quality, and efficiency. This is both a strategic and people-focused role. You'll be responsible for managing the day-to-day operations of the styling team, mentoring individuals in their growth, and driving forward the creative vision and standards of the studio in close partnership with Art Direction, Photography teams and Production. Your focus will be on empowering your team and ensuring our content remains visually compelling and consistently on-brand.
