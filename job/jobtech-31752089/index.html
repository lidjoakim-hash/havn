A snapshot of your day Become part of Siemens Energy, where your journey as a Senior Development Engineer begins! Imagine developing your technical skills while building both social and professional networks. You will take responsibility for the lubrication oil system and the technical solutions in assigned development projects, and work closely with project teams to ensure that the products meet specified requirements. Your role will be crucial for delivering development projects on time and within budget, while supporting cross-functional collaboration and mentoring junior team members. With opportunities for personal development and hands-on experience, you will be at the forefront of innovation in energy technology. How You Will Make an Impact * You will own the technical solutions in assigned development projects. * Collaborate closely with project teams and project managers to ensure that the products meet specified requirements in line with the technical roadmap. * Ensure delivery on time and within the agreed scope and budget, and communicate clearly with stakeholders on technical matters. * Support cross-functional collaboration to contribute to the Product Center of Competence. * Mentor and support junior colleagues through review and training, strengthening the team's capabilities. What You Bring * You have a solid base of experience to draw on, which gives you good intuition in your judgments. You are used to evaluation/correcting other engineers' solutions. * You have experience with fluid systems. * You hold a university or master's degree in mechanical engineering, mechatronics, energy technology or an equivalent education. * You have a good command of Swedish and English, both spoken and written. * You have a strong interest in working with mechanical systems and can help make working methods and processes more efficient. * You work carefully and methodically, with the ability to analyze and critically assess products and solutions. * You are collaborative, driven, have excellent problem-solving skills, and are responsible and flexible, with the patience to dig into technical details. About the Team Our team consists of two technology areas with a total of 12 members. In the lubrication oil technology area, we currently have two experienced employees, one of whom is the technology area lead, as well as two newly graduated consultants and two consultants based in India. Together we are committed to developing innovative solutions that meet market demands and customer wishes, while fostering a collaborative and supportive environment. Our Gas Services division offers Low-emission power generation through service and decarbonization. Zero or low emission power generation and all gas turbines under one roof, steam turbines and generators. Decarbonization opportunities through service offerings, modernization, and digitalization of the fleet. We can offer you employment benefits such as: reduction of working hours, advance vacation, health care allowance and an eventual possibility to a flexible working place. Who is Siemens Energy? At Siemens Energy, we are more than just an energy technology company. With ~100,000 dedicated employees in more than 90 countries, we develop the energy systems of the future, ensuring that the growing energy demand of the global community is met reliably and sustainably. The technologies created in our research departments and factories drive the energy transition and provide the base for one sixth of the world's electricity generation.  Our global team is committed to making sustainable, reliable, and affordable energy a reality by pushing the boundaries of what is possible.
