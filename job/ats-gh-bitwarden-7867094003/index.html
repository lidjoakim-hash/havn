Principal Implementation Engineer, Customer Support

Bitwarden · Remote, United Kingdom, United Kingdom

Bitwarden is the trusted identity security leader for millions of users worldwide, empowering enterprises, developers, and individuals to securely manage and share sensitive information anywhere. Bitwarden makes it easy for all users to extend robust security across their devices with password management, secrets management, and passwordless and passkey innovations. The company is headquartered in Santa Barbara, California with team members located across the globe. Learn more at bitwarden.com. Bitwarden truly cares and wants all Bitwarden users to have the best experience while accessing their sensitive information. The Customer Success team helps customers and all users meaningfully engage with Bitwarden products while proactively planning for user growth, service availability, and to ultimately achieve their desired outcome. Team members have excellent product knowledge, superb technical skills, stellar grammar, attention to detail, and the ability to explain complicated things simply. Bitwarden seeks a server and cloud enthusiast, with experience in both communicating about and deploying these technologies. You will become a Bitwarden product expert and partner with the Customer Engagement Managers to help customers with their initial production deployments post-sales, as well as implement new features as they are released and increase their adoption of the Bitwarden platform. This is an all-remote team and we’re looking for someone located in the UK. RESPONSIBILITIES • Functioning as a Subject Matter Expert (SME) for Bitwarden and its suite of services • Leading technical planning and execution of new deployments, both to establish and adopt best-practices with a focus on large Enterprise engagements • Advising customers on industry best practices for the full stack of Bitwarden • Working with customers to solve issues and/or look ahead to identify solutions to situations they may face in the future • Providing superb guidance and assistance for each customer’s implementation of Bitwarden and general security • Serve as a dedicated customer point of contact for named customers through alignment to Customer Engagement Manager resources through the Bitwarden ticketing and email systems • Participate in customer quarterly business reviews by assisting in environment audits, best-practices reviews, and positioning new product features relevant to further expansion opportunities and/or increased adoption • Collaborating with Engineering and Product associates to triage bug reports and feature requests based on customer need and scale with a focus on large Enterprise deployments • Assist training and culture sharing in the Implementation Engineering Team • Guide knowledge sharing among Implementation Engineers within the team and in collaboration with Technical Writing to share best-practices, troubleshooting information, and recommended configurations • Lead and/or assist in internal team projects including customer environment replication for bug investigations, integrations with new third-party tools, and development of automations both for customer use-cases as well as internal team processes • Occasionally represent Bitwarden at customer meetings, industry events, or team offsites (some travel may be required). WHAT YOU BRING TO BITWARDEN • Excellent English and grammar • Excellent problem-solving skills (you might not know all the answers but you know how to find and communicate the solutions) • A passion for supporting your team members, growing together (technical ability and emotional intelligence), and sharing your knowledge with all • A strong sense of empathy and the ability to advocate for others • A passion for helping Bitwarden users • Experience from a previous technical position (e.g. Systems Engineer, Implementation Engineer, DevOps Engineer, IT Infrastructure Engineer, etc). Equivalent education or demonstrable skills may be accepted • Ability and desire to work remotely Familiarity
