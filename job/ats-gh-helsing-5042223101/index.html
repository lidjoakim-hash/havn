Who we are Helsing is a defence AI company. Our mission is to protect our democracies. We aim to achieve technological leadership, so that open societies can continue to make sovereign decisions and control their ethical standards. As democracies, we believe we have a special responsibility to be thoughtful about the development and deployment of powerful technologies like AI. We take this responsibility seriously. We are an ambitious and committed team of engineers, AI specialists and customer-facing programme managers. We are looking for mission-driven people to join our European teams – and apply their skills to solve the most complex and impactful problems. We embrace an open and transparent culture that welcomes healthy debates on the use of technology in defence, its benefits, and its ethical implications. The role As a Senior Systems Engineer for Flight and Mission Planning, you will serve as the domain specialist translating operational needs into buildable system architectures and requirements. You will define how missions are planned on the ground and dynamically replanned in the air — covering store configuration, navigation and electronic warfare (EW) data, cryptographic material, route planning and sensor-specific parameters such as frequency management. Crucially, you will establish the operational boundaries for what on-board autonomous agents may adapt in flight, and how those changes reach ground-based operators. Collaborating across our ground control station and airborne mission system teams, you will shape mission planning software that aircrews can rely on in demanding operational environments, keeping a human accountable for every mission decision.
