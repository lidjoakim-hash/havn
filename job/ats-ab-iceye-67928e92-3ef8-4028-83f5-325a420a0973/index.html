ICEYE INTERN LAB: MISSIONS INTERN Role highlights - Role: Missions Intern, ICEYE Intern Lab - Location: Espoo, Finland (onsite) - Department: Missions - Employment type: Fixed-term, fully paid internship (3-6 months, flexible) - Start: April / early May 2027 - Employment is subject to applicable security screening (incl. SUPO, where required) WHY THIS ROLE IS DIFFERENT Few people get to start their career delivering satellite missions. You will. ICEYE Intern Lab is a fully paid, onsite internship for high-potential students who want to see how complex satellite missions are delivered on real programs. You don’t need space industry experience to apply. We’re looking for curious, fast-learning students with a strong academic record and a hands-on, ownership-driven mindset. You’ll join one of our Missions teams as a contributing team member, working on live work with real customers, with a dedicated manager and a mentor. You’ll work alongside program managers, engineers and senior leaders, and see how a mission moves from signed contract to an accepted operating system. The internship is fast and demanding, and it will stretch you. After 90 days, your manager and HRBP review your progress and discuss potential full-time opportunities where there is an approved opening. Spots are limited and we review applications on a rolling basis, so apply early. WHERE YOU COULD WORK Missions turns every signed contract into an accepted, operating mission. We are hiring interns into three teams. You tell us which ones interest you in the application, and we match you based on your background and what the teams need. - Delivery Engineering: Own a piece of the technical thread of a customer mission, from requirements through verification to acceptance. Work on processes or tools that help engineers deliver missions predictably. Best fit if you study aerospace, space, physics, engineering or a related technical field and have hands-on analysis experience from coursework, a thesis or projects. - Program Management: Join the program teams and the PMO that keep missions on track. Help build plans, track risks and dependencies, and support improvement initiatives across Missions Programs. Best fit if you study engineering, industrial engineering and management, business or a related field, and have led a student organization, run events or delivered a project with a deadline and several stakeholders. - Delivery and Support: Help deliver, update and support the ground systems our customers run. You may start in software testing and QA and grow towards DevOps as you learn our software platform. Best fit if you study computer science, software engineering or a related field. - Sales and commercial operations: Join the team behind our sales leaders and the sovereign satellite deals they pursue. Help coordinate strategic campaign reviews, keep pipeline and deal data accurate, build reports and dashboards, and research markets and customers. Best fit if you study business, economics, industrial engineering and management, geography, political sciences, or a related field, and excel at communicating with senior stakeholders, have analyzed data, supported a sales effort, or run a project with several stakeholders.
