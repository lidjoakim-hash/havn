Tight spreads, deep books, an API people actually build on. Scripts for organic and paid video, copy for organic posts, and the occasional longer piece when an idea needs room. - Own the idea, not just the words. Turn a brief into a concept traders would actually watch or read, then carry it through to the final cut or the send button. - Run campaign copy end to end. Headlines, ad copy, landing pages, and email campaigns, from product launches to trader lifecycle journeys. - Translate the Kraken Pro voice into words and dialogue. Sharp, credible, never salesy. Make order types, funding rates, and liquidity clear without dumbing them down. - Move at market speed. Turn around reactive content for volatility, listings, and launches, with clear sign-off so speed never costs accuracy. - Partner across the business. Work with Social, Community, Video, Design, Product, and Marketing, plus the traders and product experts who know the platform best. - Increase and improve output by actively integrating AI wherever it helps. Ideation, drafts, repurposing. Faster output, same voice. - Learn from the numbers. Track what gets watched, opened, clicked, and converted. Double down on what works and drop what doesn't. - Ship responsibly. Work with Legal and Compliance so everything we publish is accurate and compliant across regions.   WHAT YOU'LL BRING - Receipts.
