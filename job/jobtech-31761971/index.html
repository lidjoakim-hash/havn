Quality Impression Feature Engineer / Feature Leader Location: Gothenburg, Sweden Employment type: Contract / Full-time Language: English About the Role We are looking for an experienced and motivated Quality Impression Feature Engineer / Feature Leader to join our client's team in Gothenburg. In this assignment, you will play an important role in developing and improving the Quality Impression feature for Heavy Duty Vehicles. You will be responsible for defining requirements, evaluating feature status and supporting product development throughout different project phases. You will also contribute to the long-term development of the feature by improving test methods, supporting simulation activities and contributing to roadmap planning. This is an excellent opportunity for someone with an automotive or product development background who is interested in quality, design perception and vehicle engineering. Key Responsibilities Handle, define and coordinate requirements related to the Quality Impression feature. Perform verification, evaluation and testing of models and physical products for Heavy Duty Vehicles. Assess and monitor feature status throughout different project phases. Provide technical feedback and collaborate with cross-functional engineering and product development teams. Drive the development and continuous improvement of feature definitions, methods and processes. Contribute to the long-term feature strategy and roadmap planning. Support the development and improvement of test methods and simulation activities. Prepare and communicate feature status reports to project stakeholders. Represent the Quality Impression area in discussions and technical reviews. Required Qualifications Educational background in Automotive Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Industrial Design, Product Development or a related technical field. Experience in automotive engineering, industrial design or product development. Understanding of product quality evaluation and development processes. Experience in requirements handling, verification, evaluation or testing. Good communication and collaboration skills. Excellent verbal and written communication skills in English. Preferred Qualifications Previous experience in Quality Impression or Perceived Quality. Experience working with Heavy Duty Vehicles or the automotive industry. Knowledge of CAE (Computer-Aided Engineering). Experience with visualization techniques and 3D rendering. Experience with simulation activities and product evaluation. Experience in feature development, roadmap planning and cross-functional project work. Personal Attributes We are looking for someone who is: Curious, analytical and solution-oriented. Proactive and comfortable taking ownership of responsibilities. Confident in communicating technical topics and representing their area. Collaborative, supportive and open-minded. Able to work effectively with different teams and stakeholders. Focused on continuous improvement and delivering high-quality results. Interested in This Opportunity? If you have experience in automotive product development, quality evaluation or feature engineering and would like to contribute to the development of Heavy Duty Vehicles, we would be happy to hear from you. Please submit your updated CV along with a brief description of your relevant experience.
