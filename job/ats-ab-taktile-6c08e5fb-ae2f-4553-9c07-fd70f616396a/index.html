About the role Taktile empowers financial institutions to transform into truly AI-native organizations. We’re growing rapidly with enterprise customers across banks and insurance companies, and we’re looking for a Forward Deployed Engineer (Ops). You'll spend much of your time working directly with enterprise customers, acting as their trusted technical partner to investigate issues, guide best practices, and help them get the most value from Taktile's AI platform. You will work closely with Taktile’s Engineering, Product and Customer Success Teams, ensuring our customers derive maximum value from Taktile while enjoying a top-tier experience. About you - You have 1–2 years of experience in a customer-facing technical role, partnering directly with enterprise customers to troubleshoot technical issues, advise on best practices, and deliver successful outcomes. Experience as a Solutions Engineer, Technical Account Manager, Forward Deployed Engineer, Customer Success Engineer, or Technical Consultant is preferred. - You are familiar with AI concepts including prompt engineering, large language models, and AI Agents. - You are curious and customer-centric, caring deeply about the Taktile platform experience. - You enjoy solving technical problems and finding innovative solutions to customer issues. - You have strong knowledge of APIs and data formats such as JSON and XML. - You are proficient in Python and SQL. - You have strong analytical skills and a hands-on mentality. - You communicate clearly in English with a wide range of customer profiles (engineers, business analysts, risk managers). - You enjoy working directly with customers and are confident leading technical conversations, asking thoughtful questions, and explaining complex technical concepts to both technical and non-technical audiences. - You are open to a hybrid work model and can work from our Berlin office at least three days per week.
