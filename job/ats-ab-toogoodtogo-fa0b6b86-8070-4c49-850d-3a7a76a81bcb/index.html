About Too Good To Go At Too Good To Go, we have an ambitious mission: to inspire and empower everyone to fight waste together. More than 1/3 of all food produced in the world is wasted. And that has a huge impact on the health of our planet. 10% of greenhouse gas emissions come from food waste and loss. Through our marketplace app, we connect businesses that have unsold, surplus product, with consumers who can buy and enjoy them at ⅓ of retail value. Too Good To Go was named in FastCompany’s March 2022 list of the World's Most Innovative Companies, and was honoured to be included in TIME’s list of the 100 Most Influential Companies of 2022. We’re looking for an exceptional Key Account Manager to join the UK & Ireland team at Too Good To Go. Your Mission Our Key Account Team is responsible for the strategic management, performance optimization, and scalable development of our important partners in the Out Of Home sector. That means restaurants, cafes, and bakeries, etc. As a Key Account Manager, you will report directly to the Key Account Lead for your sector. What you will do - Account management: Own and grow a portfolio of key account partners across categories, with partner retention and revenue growth as your top priorities. - Commercial acumen: Identify and close upsell and cross-sell opportunities, such as more stores, new categories or additional Too Good To Go solutions. Build the commercial case for each partner: reduced waste, recovered revenue on surplus and increased footfall from customers collecting their Surprise Bags. - Negotiation: Negotiate and manage commercial agreements, including pricing and annual terms, that deliver ROI for the partner and profitable revenue for Too Good To Go. - Stakeholder management: Run regular quarterly business reviews (QBRs) that present impact metrics such as bags saved, waste reduction and revenue growth. - Project management: Run the onboarding of new major partners in Denmark as structured projects with clear plans, owners and milestones. - Internal collaboration: Occasionally drive the onboarding programme for global initiatives, including train-the-trainer sessions for colleagues in other countries. - Forecasting and business planning: Build an annual plan for each key partner with clear revenue and growth targets, and keep it current in Salesforce. Qualify and progress your pipeline with MEDDICC and Solution Selling, always separating what the partner has confirmed from what we assume. Own your numbers and deliver an accurate forecast that can be pulled directly from Salesforce at any time. What you bring - A strong commercial mindset and a track record of hitting or exceeding revenue targets. - Experience with upselling and cross-selling within existing key accounts. - Proven project management skills, ideally from partner onboarding or rollouts. - Experience in training others, including people you do not manage. - Confidence with everyone from store managers to VPs in large organisations. - Commercial experience from retail, out of home and/or convenience (a strong plus), including an understanding of drivers such as footfall, basket size and margin. - Solid data analysis skills and the ability to turn numbers into a compelling business case. - Tech savvy, with disciplined pipeline and CRM habits (Salesforce preferred). - Native-level English Our values: - We Win Together - We Raise the Bar - We Keep It Simple - We Build A Legacy - We Care What we have to offer: - A rare opportunity to work in a social impact company where you can see real and tangible impact in your role. - Working alongside an international community of users, partners and colleagues across 20 countries that are on the same important mission. - Personal and professional development opportunities in a fast-paced scale-up environment. - An inclusive company culture where you can bring your authentic self to work - A strong, values-driven tea
