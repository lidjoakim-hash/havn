Senior Procurement Operations Manager - Factor Europe (all genders)

The role Factor is our newest brand - a healthy, ready-to-eat meal delivery service, and one of the fastest-growing parts of the HelloFresh Group’s European business. We're looking for a Senior Procurement Operations Manager to lead our Procurement Operations function and ensure our supply chain is running efficiently, scalable, and commercially effective. You'll oversee the end-to-end procurement operations process, optimise inventory and supplier performance, and continuously improve the systems and processes that keep our production running smoothly. Working cross-functionally with Planning, Production, Finance, Culinary, and Operations, you'll play a key role in ensuring the right products are available at the right time, while driving operational excellence and supporting Factor's continued growth. What you’ll do • Lead and develop our Procurement Operations team, responsible for purchasing, supplier coordination, inventory management, and waste reduction. • Own the end-to-end procurement operations process, ensuring ingredients and packaging are delivered On Time, In Full, and In Quality (OTIFQ) to support uninterrupted production. • Drive inventory governance, ensuing product availability and operational efficiency while minimising waste and stock risk. • Champion our zero-waste strategy through continuous optimisation of purchasing parameters, inventory controls, and forecasting inputs. • Partner closely with Planning, Finance, Supply Chain, Culinary, and Production to optimize purchasing strategies and ensure smooth day-to-day operations. • Monitor procurement KPIs, purchasing data, and system accuracy, using insights to identify trends, resolve discrepancies, and drive operational improvements. • Identify opportunities to automate, simplify, and standardise procurement and inventory processes through data, technology, and continuous improvement initiatives. • Develop reporting and dashboards that provide clear visibility of procurement performance, inventory health, supplier performance, and key operational KPIs. • Foster a culture of continuous improvement, operational excellence, and data-driven decision making across Procurement Operations. What you’ll bring • At least 5-6 years of relevant work experience in a Supply Chain, Operations, Procurement or Production field • Stress resilience and a hands-on approach • A proactive, data-driven, and result-oriented mindset • Flexibility for occasional weekend work and travel • Excellent communication skills, able to work across different teams and cultures • Fluency in English (German knowledge is a plus) What we offer • Freedom to initiate, execute and implement your own ideas • Lots of learning and development opportunities • 24 holidays based on 40 hours per week per year, pro rata • A hybrid work setup - with roughly 2 days in our Amsterdam office • Reimbursement of travel expenses to the office • Discount on your ClassPass subscription, and a discount for your gym membership via Bedrijfsfitness Nederland • Discount on your additional health insurance through Zilveren Kruis • A discount on your weekly, Factor, HelloFresh and/or Green Chef box in the Netherlands • Healthy lunches, fruit and snacks in the office • Competitive salary and pension scheme • Social activities with our culturally diverse team!
