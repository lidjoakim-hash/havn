This is Adyen Adyen provides payments, data, and financial products in a single solution for customers like Meta, Uber, H&M, and Microsoft - making us the financial technology platform of choice. At Adyen, everything we do is engineered for ambition. For our teams, we create an environment with opportunities for our people to succeed, backed by the culture and support to ensure they are enabled to truly own their careers. We are motivated individuals who tackle unique technical challenges at scale and solve them as a team. Together, we deliver innovative and ethical solutions that help businesses achieve their ambitions faster. About the Role Adyen's growth depends on finding the next verticals where we can win. As (Sr.) Manager, Emerging Verticals, you will explore new industries and turn early hypotheses into clear recommendations on if, where and how we should enter. You will do this by getting close to the market: talking to prospects, partners and industry experts, and working hand in hand with Sales and the regional commercial teams. You sit at the intersection of Commercial, Product and Strategy. You will get up to speed on an unfamiliar industry quickly, test what you learn in real customer conversations, and work with Product to understand how well our offering fits. The outcome is a recommendation to leadership (go, no-go, or go later), and for verticals we pursue, an initial value proposition, target segments and pilot approach that the commercial teams can execute. providers. • Quickly get to grips with a new vertical: value chain, payment flows, key players, business models and regulatory landscape. • Validate or kill hypotheses early through real conversations rather than desk research alone. • Size the opportunity and assess Adyen's right to win, at the level of depth needed for a good decision. • Commercial Partnership • Work closely with Sales, Account Management and regional teams to identify target customers, open doors, run pilots and build early pipeline. • Join customer meetings and support early deals, bringing industry insight and a clear value story. • Define target segments, ideal customer profiles and priority use cases that commercial teams can act on. • Capture learnings systematically and share them back with the commercial teams to support enablement. • Product & Offering Fit • Work with Product and Offering teams to assess how well our capabilities fit the vertical and where the gaps are. • Be the voice of the customer for the new vertical: translate customer pain points into concrete input for product prioritization, pricing and packaging. • Define the minimum viable offering and "readiness" criteria needed before we scale. • External Consultancy Management • Act as the main stakeholder for external consultancies: define scope and hypotheses, manage the relationship and timelines, and quality-check deliverables. • Decide, together with the Head of Emerging Verticals, when to bring in outside support and when to do the work in-house. • Make sure outside insights are integrated into Adyen's strategy and passed on to the owning teams. • Recommendation & Handover • Give leadership clear, decision-ready recommendations on whether and how to enter a vertical, including risks and investment needed. • Hand validated verticals over to a long-term Vertical Head and the commercial teams with a clear strategy, segmentation, GTM approach and learnings. • Maintain a pipeline of future vertical candidates.
