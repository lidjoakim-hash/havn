This is a globally remote position. We have existing employment in Canada, Germany, the UK, and the US. We will consider other candidates on a case-by-case basis. Mozilla Foundation is the place to build good technology alternatives. We believe the future of technology should remain open, so more people, in more places, can shape what gets built and who benefits. To bring that ambition to life, we are evolving our current incubator into the Mozilla Studio. As our inaugural Vice President of Studio, you will lead Mozilla Foundation’s entrepreneurial engine: executing in our thesis areas, backing unconventional technologies early, and helping them become thriving alternatives to the technologies that currently dominate our lives. We are looking for a proven operator with a global perspective. Today, too many consequential decisions about technology are made by too few. Promising ideas struggle to find support because they are too early for markets, too unconventional for venture capital, too technically ambitious for traditional philanthropy, or just too different for commercial incentives. This is especially true for builders in the global majority. We think philanthropy can play a different role: We can take early bets on technologies that expand collective human agency, strengthen the commons, and create promising alternatives to dominant financing, ownership, and technology models. We want to help founders strengthen their interventions, deepen their relationships with the communities they serve – what we call product-community fit – and cultivate the operating and financial models that allow their work to thrive. In an era of tumult, we believe that defiant optimism and humble experimentation will see us through and help make many possible futures become real. The Studio turns those beliefs into bets on alternative tech products and organizations. The opportunity We are seeking an exceptional Vice President of Studio to lead the entrepreneurial engine of Mozilla Foundation. This is a role for a seasoned operator: someone who has grown organizations, worked shoulder to shoulder with technical founders, and knows what it takes to turn an ambitious idea into a functioning, sustainable organization or product. Our Studio will be known for providing high-quality support that helps exceptional founders across the world overcome their most consequential barriers to success. • You’ll inherit an active portfolio to manage in current thesis areas, including our Democracy x AI and soon-to-be-launched Human-Scale AI initiatives, while building the next stage of the Studio’s model. • You’ll serve as a trusted strategic partner to founders, providing hands-on support for organizational design, deployment, and growth. • You’ll connect founders with outstanding engineers, product leaders, designers, entrepreneurs, and other specialists, activating your existing networks and the broader Mozilla communities worldwide to do so. • You’ll design a scalable combination of cohort-based learning and tailored support. • You’ll bring in Entrepreneurs-in-Residence, technical advisors, and operating partners to solve specific challenges. • You’ll help founders develop credible and creative paths to sustainability, whether through earned revenue, institutional adoption, public funding, philanthropic support, community stewardship, or other mission-aligned models. • Over your first six to nine months, you’ll build a small team: a Growth and Sustainment Lead, administrative support, and a bench of part-time technical partners and Entrepreneurs in Residence. Experience in activating the open-source community will be key. This is an opportunity to build a distinctive entrepreneurial studio at the intersection of technology and philanthropy, and demonstrate what becomes possible when first-believer capital is deployed in service of better technology futures.
