Join our customer in Ludvika and play a key role in optimizing long-term production plans within a highly complex manufacturing environment. About the role As a Master Planner, you will support the planning organization by developing the long-term production plan. You will collaborate closely with operations, project management, engineering, and sales to align customer demand with capacity. We offer a dynamic role in a global and highly collaborative environment where you can directly influence large-scale industrial projects and optimize critical production processes. Work tasks The role entails aligning customer demand, project requirements, and production capacity to ensure a smooth and optimized manufacturing flow. Develop, maintain, and continuously evaluate the master production plan. Balance demand, capacity, and production constraints across the entire manufacturing facility. Identify long-term bottlenecks, risks, and planning conflicts, driving scenarios to support decision-making. Coordinate planning activities across departments to align priorities and production sequences. Monitor changes and deviations affecting the long-term plan and recommend corrective planning actions. We are looking for Advanced knowledge in production planning, master scheduling, or scheduling within a complex manufacturing environment Good understanding of capacity planning, production flows, and bottleneck management Advanced knowledge of Excel and strong analytical skills Fluent in English, both written and spoken It is meritorious if you have Good knowledge of Swedish Experience with engineer-to-order or project-based manufacturing To succeed in the role, your personal skills are: Stress tolerant Orderly Responsible Intellectually curious Respectful Our recruitment process This recruitment process is handled by Academic Work and it is our client’s wish that all questions regarding the position is directed to Academic Work. Our selection process is continuous and the advert may close before the recruitment process is completed if we have moved forward to the next phase. The process includes two tests: one personality test and one cognitive test. The tests are tools to find the right talent for the right position, to enable equality, diversity, and a fair process.
