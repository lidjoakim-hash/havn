Job description: Your future team   At Powertrain Technology Engineering Sweden, we are product owners for components, systems, and complete powertrains, accountable for their full lifecycle, from advanced engineering through product development into maintenance. Our components span combustion engines, aftertreatment systems, transmissions, and e-mobility.     We bring together around 700 employees across Gothenburg and Malmö, together we master new technologies, strengthen digitalization, improve quality, reduce lead time and cost, and drive innovation and engagement.    What you will do   As Head of Powertrain Technology Engineering Sweden, you hold engineering responsibility for Platform Center Heavy Duty Engines, Transmission, and Application Center Heavy Duty Europe, plus our Malmö office developing turbocharging systems. You take the product ownership role and optimize complete powertrain systems using components tailored to each application.     Your first task is to work with colleagues across the Heavy Duty Engineering Value Chain and Powertrain Technology leadership to define a way of working that supports the Powertrain Technology Industrial Value Chain transformation, then adjust the engineering organization accordingly.    Who are you?  We believe leadership is a key driver of our success in an increasingly competitive and fast-paced environment. Leading at Volvo means setting a clear direction through customer-centric decisions and embracing new technologies. It means inspiring and engaging people around a shared vision, building trust and psychological safety, and recognizing contributions that support growth and performance.   You build high-performing teams, empower accountability and fast decision-making, and foster an inclusive environment where diverse perspectives are valued. Through clear goals and the effective use of digitalization, simulation, and AI, you deliver sustainable results that drive speed, quality, cost, and customer success.    What you bring:  Proven leadership experience with a strong track record of delivering results and developing teams.  Extensive experience within the automotive industry and deep expertise in Powertrain technologies.  A strategic mindset with the ability to balance long-term vision and short-term execution.  Strong ownership, sound judgment, and the courage to make difficult decisions.  Master’s degree in Engineering.    What's in it for you?  This is an opportunity to lead a key engineering organization and shape the future of Powertrain Technology. You will play a central role in the transformation of the Heavy Duty Engines value chain, influence product strategy, and lead around 700 skilled colleagues across Gothenburg and Malmö. Working at the forefront of innovation, digitalization, and sustainable transport, you will make a lasting impact on both people and technology.    Ready for the next move?   If you have any questions, please feel free to reach out to Sandra Viklund, Talent Acquisition Partner (sandra.viklund@volvo.com).     The last application date is 29:th of November.
