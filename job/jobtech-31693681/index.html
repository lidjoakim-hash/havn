You will take ownership of critical platform capabilities covering software download and installation, firmware updates, logging, debugging, diagnostics, and analytics services within connected vehicle environments. Working closely with architects, developers, test engineers, release teams, and business stakeholders, you will drive product value, technical quality, and the continuous evolution of embedded software platforms. The role combines strategic product ownership, technical leadership, Agile delivery, and strong knowledge of Android Automotive platform development. Key Responsibilities 1. Product Vision & Roadmap Define and drive the product vision and long-term technical roadmap. Establish priorities based on business value, customer needs, and technical dependencies. Align product development with platform architecture and business objectives. Drive product strategy and technology evolution. 2. Agile Product Ownership Own and continuously prioritise the product backlog. Translate business and technical requirements into epics, features, and user stories. Drive scope and prioritisation decisions within Agile development teams. Balance feature development with technical debt, security, reliability, and maintainability. Coordinate implementation priorities, product branches, and release planning. 3. Embedded Software Platform Development Safeguard platform functionality, quality, and technical sustainability. Oversee software download, installation, and update management capabilities. Support firmware updates, safe installation, and rollback mechanisms. Drive improvements in logging, debugging, diagnostics, and data collection. Support analytics services while ensuring privacy and compliance. Ensure platforms meet functional and non-functional requirements. 4. Stakeholder & Team Collaboration Act as the interface between business stakeholders and engineering teams. Collaborate with architects, developers, verification engineers, and release managers. Coordinate priorities and dependencies across multiple teams. Facilitate technical discussions and resolve competing priorities. Communicate roadmap decisions, progress, and delivery expectations. 5. Performance & Continuous Improvement Define and monitor KPIs, product outcomes, and performance metrics. Track platform reliability, software quality, and delivery performance. Identify opportunities to improve product capabilities and development processes. Support technical governance and software lifecycle management. Mentor colleagues and promote continuous learning.
