BMC Remedy / Helix Specialist – ITSM Consultant

Maxitech AB · Stockholm, Sweden

About the role Are you an experienced BMC Remedy or BMC Helix Specialist with a passion for IT Service Management? We are currently looking for a skilled consultant to join an exciting assignment at one of our clients, an established company in the telecommunications industry, based in Stockholm. In this hands-on role, you will be responsible for the daily administration, operation, maintenance, and continuous improvement of a business-critical ITSM platform. You will ensure system stability, troubleshoot technical issues, and work closely with internal stakeholders and external support partners to translate business needs into effective solutions. You are offered At Maxitech, we offer permanent employment from day one, a competitive compensation model, and opportunities for professional development. You will work on challenging technical assignments while benefiting from a supportive environment and a network of experienced colleagues. If you prefer working through your own company, we also offer the opportunity to join as a subcontractor. Your responsibilities • Manage the daily administration, operation, maintenance, and support of BMC Remedy/Helix. • Troubleshoot technical issues, manage incidents, and follow up on system-related problems. • Configure and customize workflows, forms, fields, permissions, and notifications. • Implement platform changes and improvements. • Gather business requirements and translate them into effective system configurations. • Coordinate and collaborate with external support and operations partners. • Test and verify changes before deployment to production. • Document technical solutions, configurations, and operational procedures. Your profile We are looking for a proactive, structured, and solution-oriented professional with strong hands-on experience in BMC Remedy or BMC Helix. You enjoy troubleshooting, improving systems, and collaborating with stakeholders. Most importantly, you have practical experience operating and maintaining the platform and can take ownership of technical challenges. Required Qualifications: • At least two years of hands-on experience administering and configuring BMC Remedy or BMC Helix, preferably three years or more. • Good understanding of ITSM processes, including Incident, Problem, Change, and Request Management. • Experience troubleshooting and supporting business-critical systems. • Ability to analyze business requirements and translate them into system configurations. • Experience collaborating with external vendors and support organizations. • Good communication skills in English. Preferred Qualifications: • Experience with ITIL-based processes and practices. • Experience integrating BMC Remedy/Helix with other systems. • Experience working in large-scale ITSM environments with multiple users and stakeholders. • Good communication skills in Swedish. Other information Employment Type: Permanent employment with Maxitech from day one or a subcontractor arrangement. Location: Stockholm, Sweden – Hybrid, with up to 25% remote work. Assignment Duration: Long-term assignment. Start Date: As soon as possible, preferably immediately, subject to an approved background check. Salary: Attractive and competitive compensation model. Application: Applications are reviewed on an ongoing basis, and interviews may be scheduled at short notice. Apply today!
