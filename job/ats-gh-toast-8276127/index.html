Toast creates technology to help restaurants and local businesses succeed in a digital world, helping business owners operate, increase sales, engage customers, and keep employees happy. Toast is making it faster and easier for retailers to get started on Toast: the path from a store's existing product data to a correctly configured, actively selling location. We are seeking a highly skilled, AI-native Senior Product Designer to lead that experience: someone with a strong portfolio, a track record of leading design for complex product areas, a focus on exceptional UI design, and the hands-on ability to build working prototypes with AI tools like Replit, Magic Patterns, or Claude. This role involves designing for the unique needs of a diverse retail customer base, including convenience stores, bottle shops, grocery stores, hybrid models, and beyond. You will lead design for Toast Retail's Experience Platform team, working day to day with product and engineering partners in our Dublin office and the U.S., and reporting to Toast Retail's design leadership in the U.S. Together you will turn product strategy into the setup and onboarding experiences that make retailers feel Toast is built for them. Why This Role Matters A retailer's first 90 days on Toast decide whether they stay. Before a store can sell, thousands of items need clean names, categories, brands, pack sizes, taxes, deposits, age restrictions, and images, and most of that data arrives messy, from another system or a spreadsheet. The experiences you design will decide how quickly a new store goes live, how much of that work Toast can do for them, and whether the people setting up the store, often owners juggling a business that is already open, feel in control or buried in it. You will be Toast Retail's design presence in our Dublin office: the on-site design partner for its product and engineering teams, and part of Toast's wider design community. As a senior designer working across regions and time zones, you will set design direction for your area with a high degree of autonomy: keeping local teams unblocked, making well-informed design and scope tradeoffs within the broader product vision, shaping the roadmap alongside your product and engineering partners, and bringing US-based design, product, and business stakeholders in when direction, customer impact, or strategic choices need broader alignment. A day in the life (Responsibilities) • Lead design for your product area: Take your area from early discovery through shipped UI, set its design direction and quality bar, and connect each workflow to the broader Toast Retail vision. Help shape what the team builds, not only how it looks. • Prototype with AI: Use AI prototyping tools such as Replit, Magic Patterns, or Claude to build working, interactive prototypes with realistic data, so ideas can be tested with customers and engineers before the team commits to them. Use AI across the rest of your practice too, from synthesizing research to exploring concepts, validating outputs carefully and modeling responsible, secure use of AI for the team. • Design AI-first workflows: Identify where AI can materially reduce customer effort, such as importing and cleaning up a store's existing item data, enriching items with brands, images, and attributes, and guiding new retailers through setup.
