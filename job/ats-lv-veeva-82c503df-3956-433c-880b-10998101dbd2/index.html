The Role If you have the ambition to join an exciting, well-established, and modern tech company, and have been seeking the ideal opportunity to do something new and interesting, then Veeva is for you! At Veeva—a proud Public Benefit Corporation (PBC)—we offer excellent opportunities for our people to capitalize on their skills and grow in a dynamic, unique, and exceptional work environment.
