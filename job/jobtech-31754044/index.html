Game Copywriter

Hacksaw Studios AB · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 390 000–480 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 30.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

About us Hacksaw Studios is a Stockholm-based game studio focused on creating innovative, high-quality gaming experiences. With a focus on performance and precision, we bring ideas to life through technical expertise and creative collaboration. We have wide distribution, and our games are played by millions of people every month by players all over the world. We are now looking for a Copywriter to join our team in Stockholm. In this role, you will work closely with our game development teams to create clear and engaging content, from in-game texts and game rules to product sheets and documentation. The Role Your key responsibilities include: Writing clear, engaging in-game texts that explain game rules and features while capturing players’ interest. Writing and editing product sheets that communicate each game’s features and mechanics. Creating, maintaining, and updating documentation about our games, platforms, and features. Coming up with creative names and ideas for our games, characters, and features. Collaborating with game development teams to understand game mechanics and translate technical information into accessible content. Using AI tools to support translation and proofreading, reviewing and refining the output for accuracy, clarity, and natural language. Reviewing and updating content to reflect changes to games and features. Incorporating feedback and coordinating revisions to meet project deadlines. What We’re Looking For Strong written and verbal skills in English A passion for writing and a creative approach to producing engaging and informative content. An interest in gaming or iGaming. The ability to explain game rules, mechanics, and features in clear, accessible language. Strong attention to detail, with a focus on grammar, consistency, accuracy, and compliance with relevant requirements. A structured and proactive approach to managing assignments and deadlines. A collaborative mindset and the ability to gather information from different teams. Openness to feedback and a willingness to revise and refine your work. Nice to Have Experience writing for games or the iGaming industry. Experience creating game rules, product sheets, or technical documentation. Familiarity with AI tools such as Visual Studio and Claude for translation, proofreading, and editing. Experience working closely with creative and technical teams. Proficiency in Swedish. What We Offer A modern office in central Stockholm. The opportunity to work with experienced and passionate colleagues in the gaming industry. The chance to help create games played by a global audience. Real influence, ownership, and opportunities for personal and professional growth. Wellbeing benefits, including a wellness allowance and regular in-office massages. Practical Details This is an on-site position in Stockholm. Unfortunately, we cannot assist with relocation or visa sponsorship. Ready to Join the Game Makers? Apply now! We review applications on a rolling basis.
