Every service gets a critical look before it moves - retire it, replace it with something managed, or rebuild it the way it should have been built - and we’re not building a spaceship where a bucket would do. To get there we want one more team member to own how we do cloud and infrastructure-as-code: how we build on AWS, how our Terraform is structured and reviewed, how AI plays into that and what we take on next. Oh, and now that AI agents are users too, we need to reflect that in our identity and monitoring platforms too. We’re a small team that owns our domain together, and divides work by what makes sense. We share what we learn, failures included. Some of us are highly specialised, some are generalists. Your role would be to bring the depth on AWS, IaC and Linux. This is not a pure build role: keeping the lights on, helping other teams and pitching in on whatever is on fire this week is part of the job for all of us as well.
