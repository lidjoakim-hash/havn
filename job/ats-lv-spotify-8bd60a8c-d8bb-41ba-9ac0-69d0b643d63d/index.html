Senior Product Manager - Spotify Connect

Spotify · London, Sweden

💰 ca. 580 000–820 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)HeltidDirekte fra arbeidsgiverFrist 2.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

What You'll Do • Own the product strategy and roadmap for your scope within Spotify Connect, turning the broader Seamless direction into clear priorities. • Work with research and data colleagues to understand cross-device behavior, identify friction, and define measures of success. • Partner with engineering and design to deliver intuitive and reliable experiences for discovering devices, controlling playback, and moving between them. • Balance improvements to everyday usability and reliability with experiments that uncover new value. • Work across teams and partner-facing functions to manage dependencies and deliver a coherent end-to-end experience. • Explore opportunities created by richer device understanding and emerging ways of interacting with Spotify, keeping privacy, permissions, and user control central. • Clearly communicate product choices, progress, and results, and use what you learn to improve the roadmap. Who You Are • You have experience owning and delivering high-quality consumer products, from identifying a problem through launch and iteration. • You can translate user needs into clear priorities and sustain progress across multiple dependencies. • You are comfortable working with technically complex products and can connect system behavior to the user experience. • You can communicate and debate strategic and technical complexity with senior stakeholders. • You use quantitative and qualitative evidence to make decisions and have experience designing and learning from experiments. • You care about the details that make a product dependable and easy to use. • You communicate clearly, collaborate well across disciplines, and are willing to work hands-on. • You have experience with connected devices, playback, platform products, or cross-device experiences, or an interest in developing your expertise in these areas. Where You'll Be • This role is based in Stockholm or London. • We offer you the flexibility to work where you work best!
