We’re looking for a Product Manager to join our Core Product Team, helping shape how customers create, manage and execute payments and transfers.  This role will be focused on both improving and enhancing the customer experience and ensuring our operational processes remain efficient and secure. You’ll span design & execution of new features and services, whilst experimenting with journey improvements plus building the back-end functionality and systems that support our infrastructure. Reporting to the Head of Product for Core, you’ll work closely with Engineering, Design, Compliance, Operations, Marketing, Insight, and Customer Support to identify customer problems, shape product improvements, and deliver simple, effective, and compliant experiences. This role would suit someone who is customer-focused, structured, analytical, and comfortable working with complex financial products. You don’t need to be a payments expert from day one, but you should be motivated to build strong domain knowledge and help customers feel more confident about navigating their finances. What you'll do - - Own and maintain the Payments & Transfers roadmap, working with the Head of Core Product to ensure priorities are clearly linked to customer needs, business goals, and product strategy. - Identify, frame, and prioritise customer problems, using data, research, customer feedback, operational insight, and commercial context to decide where the team should focus. - Lead product discovery for new opportunities, exploring customer needs, possible solutions, risks, dependencies, and success measures before moving into delivery. - Define clear product requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria, customer outcomes, and success metrics so Engineering, Design, and wider stakeholders are aligned on what we are building and why. - Work closely with Engineering and Design throughout delivery, making trade-offs, unblocking decisions, managing scope, and ensuring the team delivers high-quality customer experiences. - Improve key parts of the Moneybox Payments & Transfers experience, including how customers set up payments, transfer, contribute, understand their progress, and stay engaged over time, plus the back-end operational systems that support these experiences.  - Partner with Compliance, Legal, Operations, Marketing, Insight, and Customer Support to ensure product changes are compliant, operationally robust, clearly communicated, and designed with good customer outcomes in mind. - Monitor product performance and customer behaviour, using insight to identify opportunities to iterate, optimise, and improve the experience over time. - Communicate clearly
