Work in Sweden 2027 – Great Pay, Affordable Housing & Career Opportunities Svedberga Plantskola AB is hiring 2–3 experienced and hardworking employees for the 2027 planting seasons in Sweden. We welcome applicants from all countries, particularly Poland and Romania. Svedberga is an established Swedish company specialising in the sale, delivery and planting of hedges, trees and other plants throughout Sweden. We are looking for reliable, motivated and physically fit individuals who enjoy outdoor work, take responsibility and can maintain a consistently high working pace. Employment Periods – 2027 Spring season: 15 March – 30 June 2027 Autumn season: 15 August – 31 October 2027 IMPORTANT: You MUST be available to work during BOTH periods. Applications from candidates who are only available for one season will NOT be considered. For the right candidates, there is also an opportunity for continued employment and year-round work. Work Location and Working Hours Work is mainly carried out in southern Sweden at various customer locations. Full-time employment during both seasonal periods. Opportunities for overtime when required. Travel and occasional overnight stays away from the main work location are part of the job. Main Responsibilities Planting hedges, trees and larger plants Digging, ground preparation and landscaping work Loading, unloading and handling plants Practical work in nurseries and landscaping projects Other duties related to the company's operations Who Are We Looking For? We are looking for candidates with previous experience in physically demanding work, such as forestry planting, agriculture, nurseries, groundworks or landscaping. Mandatory Requirements: Very good physical fitness and stamina Previous experience in physically demanding, practical work Ability to maintain a high and consistent working pace throughout the working day Ability to work efficiently both independently and as part of a team Reliability, punctuality and a strong work ethic Willingness to work outdoors in different weather conditions English language skills – you must be able to understand work instructions and make yourself understood in English Valid Category B driving licence Availability during BOTH employment periods in 2027 Additional Qualifications – Highly Valued Category BE driving licence (car with heavy trailer) – a major advantage Experience driving with trailers and operating machinery Knowledge of Polish or Romanian Previous experience in planting and landscaping Please note: This is physically demanding work. We are looking for people who are accustomed to hard physical labour and can maintain a high working pace throughout a full working day.
