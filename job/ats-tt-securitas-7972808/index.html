Mid & East – Germany, Austria, Switzerland, Croatia, Slovakia, Hungary, Romania, Czech Republic, Poland, Turkey, Serbia About the role We are looking for an experienced Data, AI and Privacy Legal Counsel to join our team and lead legal support for the Central Hub (France, Belgium, The Netherlands and Luxembourg).
