About Pockit and Monese Pockit and Monese are FCA-regulated financial technology institutions providing e-money accounts and financial management tools. Together, we serve more than three million customers across the UK and Europe. We're a diverse, international and energetic team, with colleagues based across London, Newcastle, Tallinn and remote teams across Central Europe. We value curiosity, ambition, accountability and resilience, and we want everyone to feel at home, have a voice and know that their contribution matters. We give people the trust and space to take ownership, share ideas, influence decisions, and make a real impact on our customers and our business. The role Following Pockit’s acquisition of Monese in October 2024, the VP of Legal will play a critical role in the integration of the two regulated entities, overseeing group-wide legal strategy, corporate governance, and multi-jurisdictional regulatory compliance. The VP of Legal is a senior leadership position and a member of the Executive Team, responsible for building and leading the group’s legal function and providing strategic counsel to the CEO and Board of Directors. The incumbent will oversee the legal aspects of the post-merger integration, manage complex commercial negotiations, and ensure the group’s operations remain compliant with UK and EU financial services regulations. This is a role where you can build. The in-house legal function is being established rather than inherited. The successful candidate will shape the function’s operating model, decide what is handled internally versus externally, and grow the team as the group scales. What you will do 1.
