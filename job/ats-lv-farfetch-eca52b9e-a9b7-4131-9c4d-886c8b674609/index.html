LEGAL, ETHICS AND COMPLIANCE Working in close partnership, the Legal and Ethics & Compliance (E&C) teams operate as a strategic business area helping us navigate the complexities of a global business. The Legal team acts as guardians of our legal integrity, providing expert advice to protect our interests and manage risks.  Alongside them, the E&C team drives our commitment to doing business the right way by developing robust compliance frameworks, monitoring regulatory adherence, and fostering a strong culture of ethical conduct. We also have a large outdoor terrace, which is the perfect spot to have a team lunch or host events. THE ROLE The Principal, Financial Crime and Regulatory Compliance is a senior role responsible for building, scaling, and overseeing global AML and Sanctions compliance programs that protect the company from regulatory, financial, and reputational risk. WHAT YOU'LL DO Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Program • Design and lead AML frameworks across multiple jurisdictions, including Due Diligence, and Suspicious Transaction Reporting. • Establish consistent governance standards, documentation requirements, and operational controls to ensure regulatory alignment across markets. • Identify emerging AML risk typologies and direct the development of analytic methods, and escalation criteria. • Conduct comprehensive enterprise-wide AML risk assessments and implement robust remediation plans to address identified vulnerabilities. • Serve as the principal liaison for internal audits, external regulatory examinations, and law enforcement inquiries related to money laundering. Transaction Monitoring, Fraud & Ecosystem Defense • Design and manage Transaction Monitoring frameworks to identify suspicious activities and financial crime typologies across the global platform. • Extend Transaction Monitoring capabilities to function as a holistic defense mechanism, integrating preventative controls over fraud, Trust and Safety, and payment anomalies. • Support the design and operation of monitoring mechanisms for fraud, seller abuse, or related risk indicators, coordinating with internal functions as needed. • Partner with engineering and data teams to deploy advanced monitoring rules and real-time detection solutions to protect the platform's ecosystem. Sanctions Compliance Management • Lead global sanctions screening for new and existing partners/customers and ensure compliance with U.S., U.K., E.U., and other applicable sanctions regimes. • Oversee sanctions system configuration, alert review procedures, and escalation protocols to maintain screening accuracy and defensibility. • Implement and manage comprehensive e-commerce sanctions controls beyond entity screening, including IP geolocation blocking, restricted delivery address logic, and cross-border shipping restrictions for high-risk regions. • Provide guidance to business teams on sanctions implications for customers, products, and market expansion. • Monitor real-time geopolitical developments and proactively update internal sanctions policies to mitigate emerging jurisdictional risks. • Establish ongoing testing and calibration routines for screening tools to minimize false positives and optimize tools.
