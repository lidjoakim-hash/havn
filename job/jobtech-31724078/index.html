Job Description We are looking for an experienced Test Lead to lead and coordinate testing activities within a large-scale Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations (D365 F&O) and industry-specific ERP implementation across multiple pilot markets. You will be responsible for establishing and maintaining a structured test approach, ensuring that the solution meets business requirements, technical specifications and quality expectations. The role involves close collaboration with business and IT stakeholders, developers, solution architects, project management teams, software vendors and implementation partners. The project follows an Agile, AI-first delivery model, requiring a structured yet flexible and collaborative approach. Key Responsibilities Develop and maintain the overall test strategy, test plans, scope, timelines and entry/exit criteria. Lead and coordinate functional, integration, end-to-end, SIT, UAT, data quality, performance and AI-related testing. Coordinate test case design, test data preparation, environment readiness and test execution. Establish and manage defect processes, including logging, prioritization, triage, resolution and follow-up. Ensure traceability between business requirements, test cases and defects. Identify opportunities for test automation and improved regression testing. Monitor and report test progress, coverage, quality metrics, risks and dependencies. Lead and support the test team while coordinating activities across business, IT and external partners. Support release readiness, cutover rehearsals, go-live decisions and the transition into Hypercare. Must-Have Skills & Experience Proven experience leading testing activities in complex IT implementation or transformation projects. Strong knowledge of test strategy, test planning, test execution and defect management. Experience coordinating functional, integration, end-to-end, SIT and UAT testing. Experience working in Agile delivery environments. Strong stakeholder management, communication and leadership skills. Experience managing testing teams, dependencies and multiple stakeholders. Understanding of release management, cutover, go-live readiness and operational handover. Ability to report on test progress, quality, risks and delivery readiness. Meriting Experience Experience with Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations or similar ERP platforms. Experience with industry-specific ERP solutions. Experience leading testing in large-scale ERP programmes and multi-market implementations. Knowledge of test automation and automated regression testing. Experience with data quality, performance and AI-related testing. Experience supporting Hypercare and operational stabilization. Familiarity with AI-first delivery approaches.
