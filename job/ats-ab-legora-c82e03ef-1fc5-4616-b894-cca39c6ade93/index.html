THE ROLE BUILD & SCALE INSIDE SALES PROGRAMS - Design and run certification-based programs spanning BDR onboarding, SMB sales methodology, product and competitive fluency, objection handling, discovery, and pipeline management. - Build repeatable systems that standardize how BDRs and SMB AEs execute across the full sales cycle—from prospecting through close. OWN THE BDR & SMB PLAYBOOK - Develop and maintain the core playbooks, talk tracks, sequencing strategies, and outbound frameworks that define how BDRs generate and qualify pipeline. - Ensure SMB reps are equipped to run efficient, high-velocity sales cycles with clear value messaging tailored to legal buyers. PARTNER WITH CROSS-FUNCTIONAL LEADERS - Work closely with sales leadership, Revenue Operations, and Product Marketing to diagnose performance gaps, align on priorities, and build programs that move the metrics that matter—conversion rates, pipeline coverage, ramp time, and win rates. - Serve as the connective tissue between Sales, Marketing, and Product to ensure BDRs and SMB AEs are always current on positioning, launches, and competitive dynamics. DRIVE HIGH-IMPACT MOMENTS - Lead enablement strategy and execution for product launches, sales kick-offs, competitive shifts, and campaign activations. - Ensure the inside sales team shows up prepared, consistent, and sharp during every high-stakes moment. What will help you thrive: - 5+ years of experience across inside sales, business development, or sales enablement roles in high-growth B2B SaaS—ideally with direct ownership of BDR or SMB programs. - A builder with strong systems-thinking: you take ambiguity and turn it into structured, scalable programs that reps actually use. - Deep fluency in outbound sales motions, pipeline generation, and the mechanics of a high-velocity SMB segment. - Quota-carrying or BDR leadership experience is a strong plus—you understand the job from the inside. - Exceptional communicator with the executive presence to partner with senior sales leadership and the operational rigor to hold programs accountable to outcomes.
