Chefs Wanted – Join the First Season at Skibotn Sky Lodge

Skibotn Sky Lodges · Skibotn, Norway

💰 ca. 400 000–480 000 kr/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia NAVFrist 21.11.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

December–April | Staff accommodation on site | Opportunity to continue in Lofoten Spend your winter in Northern Norway and help shape the dining experience at a brand-new Arctic destination. Skibotn Sky Lodge is looking for passionate, reliable chefs to join our opening season. Hva vi ser etter We Are Looking for Someone Who • Has experience working as a chef in a hotel, restaurant, or similar environment • Has experience with buffet service and larger groups • Is structured and able to plan production efficiently • Can work independently and as part of a team • Is flexible regarding working hours • Has a positive attitude and takes pride in delivering good food • Understands the importance of quality, efficiency, and cost control • Speaks English or a Scandinavian language Arbeidsoppgaver What you'll be cooking Our food concept ranges from generous breakfast buffets to evening buffets and set menus. We want every meal to be a welcoming part of the guest experience, with good flavours, thoughtful preparation and consistent quality. Your work will include: • Preparing and serving breakfast, dinner buffets and set menus. • Contributing ideas to menus and helping establish kitchen routines. • Maintaining high standards of food safety, cleanliness and organisation. • Working with colleagues to keep service smooth and guests happy. Vi tilbyr We Offer • An exciting workplace in beautiful Lofoten • A varied working day with international guests and groups • Competitive salary based on experience and qualifications • A professional and friendly working environment • Flexible working hours according to operational needs • We can assist with staff accommodation in Stamsund A winter in Skibotn --- and perhaps a summer in Lofoten We arrange staff accommodation on site, where our seasonal team will live throughout the winter. For the right candidates, there may also be an opportunity to continue into the summer season at our sister hotel, Live Lofoten in Stamsund. A winter in Skibotn could be the beginning of a longer journey with us.
