About the Role We are looking for a commercially driven OTC Crypto Trader to join our team in Zug. You will be entrusted with large ticket sizes and expected to operate with professionalism, discretion, and a sharp commercial instinct. Key Responsibilities • Manage risk effectively on large ticket transactions, ensuring best execution and competitive pricing • Monitor markets and maintain awareness of macro and crypto-specific developments affecting trading • Collaborate with internal teams including technology, compliance, and operations Requirements • Minimum 2 years of trading experience, ideally on a flow or block trading desk • Experience in electronic / systematic trading with a solid understanding of algorithmic execution and quantitative trading workflows • Commercially driven with a strong client-facing mindset • Crypto trading experience is a strong plus • Demonstrable track record of handling large notional trades with sound risk management • Proficiency in Python, comfortable with data analysis, scripting, and trade tooling • Trustworthy, reliable, and able to exercise independent judgment under pressure • Work nonstandard weeks i.e. cover weekends and evening shifts as required in exchange for off days on other weekdays What We Offer • Exposure to a fast-growing, high-conviction crypto trading operation • Access to deep liquidity and a broad institutional counterparty network • Collaborative team environment in Zug, one of Europe’s premier crypto hubs • Opportunity for significant career progression as the firm scales About Us IMC is a research-driven trading firm where quantitative modeling, machine learning, and engineering shape how modern markets are traded. Our researchers, traders, and engineers work as a collective, combining rapid experimentation, advanced infrastructure, and real-time feedback to turn insight into execution and execution into advantage.
