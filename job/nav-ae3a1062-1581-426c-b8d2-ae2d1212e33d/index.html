The Global HSE Manager – Operational Safety is responsible for developing, maintaining and improving KM’s global approach to operational safety, with emphasis on preventing serious injuries and fatalities, strengthening operational risk management and ensuring effective implementation of operational control requirements across divisions, business units and sites. The position supports the Global Director HSE in strengthening KM’s operational safety performance, ensuring consistent expectations for high-risk activities, incidents, investigations, learning and corrective actions, and enabling the organisation to manage operational safety risks through clear requirements, practical guidance and effective follow-up. What you will be doing • Risk Management: Own the HSE Risk Management process, maintain the Global HSE Risk Register, and facilitate risk reviews and assurance activities. • Operational Control: Own the operational control process and coordinate the development, implementation and continuous improvement of global HSE operational controls. • Learning and Improvement: Own the global lessons learned and HSE Subject Matter Expert frameworks and facilitate HSE forums and meetings to strengthen knowledge sharing and continuous improvement. • Safety Culture: Drive Human and Organisational Performance (HOP) initiatives to strengthen KM’s safety culture. • Systems and Tools: Act as system owner, where assigned, for HSE tools and applications, including governance, development, supplier management, budgets and improvement roadmaps. • HSE Assurance and Support: Conduct audits and inspections, lead or support incident investigations, provide subject matter expertise, and deliver HSE training, advice and professional support. We think you should have • Bachelor’s or master’s degree in a relevant field, or equivalent relevant experience. • Solid experience with HSE risk management, operational controls and high-risk work activities. • Experience with incident reporting, serious incident investigations, corrective actions and sharing of lessons learned • Good knowledge of ISO 45001 and practical application of HSE management system requirements. Knowledge of ISO 14001 is an advantage • Good business understanding and ability to translate operational safety requirements into practical and effective ways of working • Experience from an operational HSE role is an advantage This employment may require a pre-employment check procedure in accordance with company security standards, role or site requirements.
