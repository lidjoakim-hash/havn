Job Overview Assignment Description We are looking for 1–3 senior embedded software developers to strengthen development teams working on vehicle safety, monitoring and automated driving-related systems. The assignment involves further developing two modules. One handles personnel safety, vehicle-wide monitoring and field generation to detect and respond to obstacles. The other replaces the driver and is based on a third-party product, primarily programmed in Structured Text. Candidates with automotive development experience are preferred. You should be an experienced, self-driven developer who can learn quickly, work effectively in busy teams and adapt to development environments and testing processes that are still being established. What You Will Work On Develop and enhance embedded software using C/C++. Work on vehicle safety and monitoring functionality. Develop obstacle detection and response capabilities. Contribute to a driver-replacement module using Structured Text. Collaborate with busy development teams and adapt to evolving development and testing environments. What You Bring Strong experience in embedded software development. Good programming skills in C/C++. Ability to learn new technologies and systems quickly. Self-driven approach and ability to work independently and in a team. Flexibility to work in evolving development and testing environments. Meritorious Experience in automotive or vehicle development. Experience with Structured Text. Knowledge of software engineering, testing, electronics systems and communication technology. Living within reasonable daily commuting distance of the assignment location.
