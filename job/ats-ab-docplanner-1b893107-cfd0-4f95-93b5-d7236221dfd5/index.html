Job Description As our AI Native Marketer, you own acquisition for Noa Notes: everything from the first time a doctor sees Noa to the moment they sign up and start their first session. That means ads and creatives, landing pages and the pitch on them, emails to our own database of doctors, newsletters, partners and new channels. You are a 360 marketer, not a paid-ads specialist. Cheap sign-ups are not the goal. Doctors should arrive educated and motivated, knowing what Noa does and ready to try it. We judge acquisition quality by what happens in their first session. Onboarding, activation and conversion to paid stay with product, and you work closely with them. You own the number (quality sign-ups at a CAC we can scale); channels are your choice. We're looking for a hands-on growth marketer who has owned an acquisition number and built the machine behind it. Think of how the fastest-growing AI apps run growth: one person with agents doing what used to take a team. Specifically, someone who: - Builds, not just manages. You build your own AI workflows and agents to produce content, launch campaigns, coordinate suppliers, and buy media at a volume a traditional team can't match. - Treats SaaS ads like e-commerce. Lots of creatives, fast tests, creative as the targeting, hard CAC discipline, measured on quality, not only volume. - Sets up tracking before spending. No euro goes out until we can tie it to a sign-up and what that doctor did next - Has creative taste. You know what makes a hook stop the scroll and you care about craft. - Gets to new channels early. Creators, newsletters, and getting Noa cited by AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Perplexity. How will you make an impact? Yes, it’s a lot. We believe one person with the right AI setup can now do what used to take a team, and you’ll take it over step by step. Ads & Creative - Produce a high volume of creatives every week: UGC-style videos, doctor testimonials, demos of Noa writing a note live, static ads, carousels. - Use AI tools (Claude, image, video and voice generation, editing tools) to go from idea to live ad in hours, not weeks. - Launch and run paid campaigns on Meta, Google (Search, YouTube, PMax), TikTok and LinkedIn, e-commerce style: broad targeting, creative as the targeting. Landing Pages & Pitch - Own the Noa web pages and landing pages: the pitch, the copy and the sub-pages for each specialty, country and use case, so doctors arrive knowing what Noa does and why it’s worth trying today. - Build and A/B test landing pages and sign-up flows for every campaign, in the languages of our markets. Email & Our Own Database - Turn the ~500K doctors on Docplanner marketplaces into Noa users with email, in-product and WhatsApp campaigns, segmented by specialty and country. - Build nurture flows for doctors who visited or signed up but never started their first session.
