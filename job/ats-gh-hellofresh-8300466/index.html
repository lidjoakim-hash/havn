The role As an Lifecycle Manager for Reactivations, you will be an integral part of HelloFresh's strategy addressing millions of former customers across 17 of our markets and 4 of our brands. You will be responsible for supporting the implementation of customer-centric data-based customer journeys, to help us translate our vision into reality. You will make suggestions and challenge our initiatives looking for effective communications and the health of our channels, while ensuring timely delivery of our execution roadmap. Collaborating with Product, Marketing and CRM Operations, you will be responsible for translating our roadmap into campaigns, including creative delivery and experiment setups. Working together with Senior Lifecycle Managers, local markets and central teams, like Marketing Analytics and Pricing, you will help set performance targets for conversions, CAC and ROI, and come up with innovative ideas to use our CRM channels (email, app, SMS, Direct Mail and more) to engage with our former base. Above all, we are looking for people who will make HelloFresh better. We believe there are many different ways of developing skills and we love diverse experiences! So even if you don’t “tick all the boxes” but think you’d thrive in this role, we would really like to learn more about you. What you’ll do • Support the development of CRM initiatives to deliver the annual strategic plans for Reactivations • Work cross-functionally with local markets, Pricing & Discount, Marketing Analytics and Product teams to uncover opportunities, refine ideas and map dependencies • Work together with senior peers to make recommendations, set targets and prioritise initiatives to deliver the most impact • Define hypothesis and design experiments to move us in the right direction with confidence • Brief and support the creative development of assets for CRM initiatives • Execute and support the implementation of CRM campaigns, including Direct Mail, Email, Push, SMS and other channels • Work with AI send decisioning models to optimize channel and content selection for these campaigns • Leverage data analysis to evaluate campaign performance and to identify issues and trends • Develop an understanding of our customer base, sharing these insights with the wider Reactivations team and stakeholders What you’ll bring • You are passionate about customer-centricity and have experience working with Direct Mail, Email and other CRM channels.
