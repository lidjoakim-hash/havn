BAT is evolving at pace into a global multi-category business. Our purpose is to create A Better Tomorrow™ by Building a Smokeless World. To achieve our ambition, we are looking for colleagues who are ready to join us on this journey! Tomorrow can’t wait, let’s shape it together! British American Tobacco Sweden has an exciting opportunity for a Line Lead in Malmö As Line Lead, you have overall responsibility for the daily production operations. You lead, coach and develop your team of 23 employees, with a focus on safety, quality, delivery and cost. You have clear responsibility for ensuring that the team always works in accordance with safety and compliance requirements. The team works together with you on shifts. The role mainly involves a 3-shift schedule, but may vary up to a 5-shift schedule depending on business needs. You collaborate closely with functions such as Quality, Engineering, Planning and HR, and work in an international environment where English is a natural part of everyday work. The role involves clear overall responsibility for people, results and structure, where you are expected to take initiative, drive change and actively contribute to the development of the business. An important part of the role is developing the team, creating engagement and ensuring that everyone has the right conditions to succeed. Your key responsibilities will include Ensure that production is carried out safely and in accordance with applicable quality, safety and compliance requirements including RCA and CAPA analysis. Plan staffing and create the right conditions for an effective shift organization. Follow up production, deviations and KPIs, and translate data into improvement actions. Lead and participate in improvement activities according to BAT ways of working, for example Lean. Lead, coach and develop the team in their daily work through clear objectives, continuous follow-up and regular feedback. What are we looking for? Proven experience in leading, coaching and developing teams, with the ability to set clear expectations, provide feedback and manage challenging conversations. Strong and inclusive leadership skills, combining empathy, trust and clarity to engage people and deliver results through others. Strong focus on safety, quality and compliance, with a structured and consistent approach to daily operations. Data-driven and improvement-focused mindset, with experience using KPIs to follow up performance and drive continuous improvement; Lean experience is an advantage. Good communication skills in Swedish and English; experience in manufacturing or another operational environment is an advantage. What we offer you? We offer a market leading annual performance bonus (subject to eligibility) Our range of benefits varies by country and includes diverse health plans, initiatives for work-life balance, transportation support, and a flexible holiday plan with additional incentives Your journey with us isn't limited by boundaries; it's propelled by your aspirations. Join us at BAT and become a part of an environment that thrives on internal advancement, where your career progression isn't just a statement – it's a reality we're eager to build together. Seize the opportunity and own your development; your next chapter starts here. You'll have access to online learning platforms and personalized growth programs to nurture your leadership skills We prioritise continuous improvement within a transformative environment, preparing for ongoing changes WHY JOIN BAT?
