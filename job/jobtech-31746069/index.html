Job ID: 6886 We are looking for a Senior Software Engineer to join our Black Ops unit on a full-time, permanent basis. This is the right role for you if you enjoy understanding complex legacy applications and using AI-assisted engineering to modernize them for strategically supported platforms. You combine hands-on development skills with the analytical judgement to challenge AI-generated changes, verify that application behaviour is preserved and deliver maintainable, production-ready software. Ideally, you will also help develop our framework for grounding and constraining AI-generated changes to reduce hallucinations and unsupported assumptions. About our team Meet the Black Ops team. We support the internal customers and engineers working with Nordea’s Internal Developer Platform, helping teams resolve complex technical challenges when expectations meet reality. You will play a senior, hands-on role in using agentic AI to refactor legacy applications and help development teams adopt modern platforms and DevOps practices. You will also have the opportunity to contribute to the framework that makes our AI-assisted workflows more controlled, verifiable and reliable. In this role, your main responsibilities will be: Analyse legacy applications, including Java systems, to understand their behaviour, dependencies and constraints before refactoring Use AI-assisted and agentic development workflows to refactor legacy code for strategically supported platforms while preserving required functionality Independently review AI-generated changes, challenge unsupported assumptions and verify correctness through code analysis and automated testing Build and strengthen regression tests to validate application behaviour before and after refactoring Build and maintain end-to-end CI/CD pipelines that integrate AI-assisted development with automated builds, testing and deployment Support the rollout of our agentic AI solution across development teams and help them adopt effective, responsible engineering practices Provide feedback on AI-generated results and help improve the framework’s context grounding, constraints and validation checks to reduce hallucinations and unintended changes Collaborate with application developers, architects and platform teams to resolve complex modernisation challenges and reduce legacy dependencies Develop DevOps practices within Service Domains and help teams become independent in building, deploying and operating modernised applications Ensure refactored applications meet Nordea Quality Standards, including security, maintainability and operational readiness We follow hybrid model of working (up to 40 % remote). This role is open in Poland (Tri-city or Warsaw), Finland (Helsinki), Sweden (Stockholm), Denmark (Taastrup), or Norway (Oslo). We offer employment with various benefits based on your location, which may include private health and life insurance, learning opportunities, holiday subsidies, bonuses and more. Who you are You are an experienced engineer who combines strong software development skills with curiosity, initiative and sound technical judgement. You see AI as an engineering tool, not a substitute for understanding the system or taking responsibility for the result. You are confident but humble, solution-driven and comfortable questioning generated code. You understand that software quality goes beyond code quality to include correct behaviour, security, reliable delivery and operability. You enjoy helping others adopt new tools and ways of working. This is the right role for you if you: Have a solid system engineering and development background Are comfortable reading unfamiliar legacy code and validating AI-generated changes against application requirements Have practical experience moving legacy applications or code to modern cloud environments Stay one step ahead by identifying technical risks, dependencies and improvement opportunities Are comfortable switching between hands-on engineering
