At Bladins International School of Malmö (BISM), we are looking for an enthusiastic and committed Physical and Health Education (PHE) Teacher to join our Middle Years Programme (MYP) for a one-year parental leave cover, starting in January 2027. If you are a dedicated teacher who enjoys inspiring students, building positive relationships and working in an international environment, we would love to hear from you. About Bladins International School of Malmö Bladins International School of Malmö (BISM) is an IB Continuum School located in central Malmö. We offer the IB Primary Years Programme (PYP) and Middle Years Programme (MYP) to approximately 360 students representing a wide range of nationalities and cultures. We are proud to be part of a global network of IB World Schools. As part of Bladins Foundation, a non-profit educational organisation with a long tradition of academic excellence, we are committed to providing a supportive, internationally minded learning environment where students are encouraged to become knowledgeable, caring and responsible global citizens. The Role As a PHE Teacher in our MYP, you will plan, teach and assess students in Physical and Health Education and contribute actively to their academic, social and personal development. You will work closely with colleagues within the relevant subject teams and across the school to provide engaging, inclusive and high-quality learning experiences for our students. Key responsibilities include: Planning and delivering engaging MYP lessons in PHE Assessing and supporting students' learning and development Creating a safe, inclusive and motivating learning environment Adapting teaching to meet the different needs and abilities of students Collaborating closely with colleagues and contributing to subject and school development Building positive and professional relationships with students, colleagues and families Contributing actively to the wider school community Who We Are Looking For We are looking for a qualified and committed teacher who is passionate about teaching and student development. You are: A qualified teacher, preferably with teaching qualifications in PHE Passionate about creating engaging and meaningful learning experiences Skilled at building positive relationships with students Collaborative and comfortable working as part of an international team Committed to inclusive education and meeting the needs of different learners Organised, flexible and able to take responsibility for planning, teaching and assessment Committed to continuous professional growth Previous experience of teaching within the International Baccalaureate (IB), particularly the MYP, is an advantage. Since English is the working language of the school, you must be able to communicate professionally in English, both verbally and in writing. Why Join BISM? At BISM, you will join a welcoming international community where people genuinely care about students, colleagues and learning. We offer: An international and multicultural working environment Opportunities for professional development and growth Dedicated and supportive colleagues The opportunity to work within an IB learning environment The chance to make a positive impact on students' learning and development Employment Information Position: PHE Teacher, MYP Employment type: Fixed-term parental leave cover Working hours: Full-time (100%) Start date: January 2027 Duration: One year Application We review applications on an ongoing basis and encourage you to apply as soon as possible. For more information about the position, please contact: Daniel McClaer, Head of School, Daniel.McClaer@bladins.se We look forward to welcoming a new colleague who shares our commitment to high-quality education, collaboration and student success.
