About Mozilla Mozilla is a mission-driven portfolio of companies and organizations working together to shape an internet that serves people and the public good. Currently, this portfolio includes: the Mozilla Corporation, which makes Firefox; the Mozilla Foundation, which champions our mission through philanthropic risk capital; Thunderbird Technologies Corporation, which makes Thunderbird; Mozilla Ventures, which invests in responsible tech startups; Mozilla.ai, which creates open source AI developer tools; Mozilla Data Collective, a data sharing platform for human agency and fair value exchange. It also includes a handful of smaller strategic investments and Mozilla's investment reserve. Mozilla.org serves as a steward of this portfolio, managing Mozilla's assets and investments to ensure they are used to advance the vision outlined in the Mozilla Manifesto. Mozilla Corporation is the non-profit-backed technology company that has shaped the internet for the better over the last 25 years. We make pioneering brands like Firefox, the privacy-minded web browser. Now, with more than 225 million people around the world using our products each month, we’re shaping the next 25 years of technology and helping to reclaim an internet built for people, not companies. The Challenge Mozilla’s mission to champion the internet as a global public resource is measured through a double bottom line: advancing that mission while succeeding in the market. At the Mozilla Corporation, we live our mission thesis through building our and scaling trusted, user-first products, with a focus on the Firefox browser. The global public policy team enables this by shaping laws and regulatory frameworks that support our open source technologies, strengthen user choice and privacy, and ensure fair competition to create the conditions for responsible innovation and long-term growth. Do you like working on big-picture problems? Join us to influence the future of tech policy! Mozilla is looking for an experienced Policy Communications Europe Lead to help set the public narrative on the role our browser, Firefox, and browser engine, Gecko, play in order to keep the web open. We build products in the public interest to support the Mozilla project. The work will cover privacy, AI, open source, competition, and internet policy—translating the positions Mozilla’s policy team develops into stories that get ahead of the debate, not just respond to it. In this pivotal role, you’ll drive Mozilla’s proactive policy media voice across regions and audiences, build narratives, craft media relationships, and build credibility amongst key stakeholders. You’ll translate complex, high-stakes policy issues into stories that connect, inspire, and drive impact. You’ll help Mozilla—and the people and partners who believe in an open internet—cut through noise, frame critical debates, and spotlight what’s at stake for users everywhere.
