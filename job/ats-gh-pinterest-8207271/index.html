As a Senior Insights Partner, you will be responsible for understanding your sectors’ and its advertisers’ business priorities, developing insight-led sales collateral designed to further Pinterest’s revenue objectives. You will partner with the Sales team to identify the most significant needs of advertisers that, addressable through analysis and research. You will ideate and execute insights research projects to meet these needs, through first-party behavioral data analysis, qualitative/quantitative studies, and integration of syndicated research. You will distill findings into compelling insights stories with actionable recommendations, deepening advertiser partnerships and driving revenue.
