Join our customer in a global security initiative shaping the future of data protection. About the role As a Senior IT Security Specialist, you will act as the Subject Matter Expert for Microsoft Purview and DLP. You will bridge the gap between technical implementation and business productivity, collaborating with Group Security, IT, Legal, and business units to ensure a secure yet productive digital workplace. You are offered An exciting opportunity to drive a high-impact gobal security initiative in close collaboration with Microsoft A dynamic environment with significant professional influence Work tasks You will lead the technical configuration, testing, and deployment of Microsoft Purview DLP while managing the organizational change and communication required for a successful rollout.
