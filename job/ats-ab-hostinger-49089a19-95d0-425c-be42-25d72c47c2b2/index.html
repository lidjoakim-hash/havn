YOUR ROLE AT HOSTINGER Join Hostinger's Abuse & Fraud team and help us build smart, scalable compliance and fraud prevention operations. As a Compliance & Fraud Operations Specialist, you strengthen compliance across our services, and protect legitimate customers by stopping fraud and abuse. You redesign workflows, challenge inefficient processes, and shape better ways of working, leveraging AI and automation to support your decisions, strengthen our prevention capabilities, and scale them as Hostinger grows. YOUR DAY-TO-DAY - Ensure compliance across our services. Investigate regulatory requests, GDPR, domain disputes (UDRP), and account recovery escalations. Take action based on applicable regulations and internal policies, and close gaps in existing controls and procedures. - Investigate payment fraud and abuse. Uncover suspicious transactions, fraudulent payment activity, and misuse of our services. Determine the right course of action to protect our customers and business. - Build prevention into our systems. Connect patterns across compliance, payments, abuse, and account recovery to identify systemic risks and drive changes to detection logic, monitoring tools, and preventive controls that stop threats before they cause harm. - Use AI and automation to amplify your impact. Find opportunities to automate repetitive checks, streamline case reviews, and shape AI-assisted investigation tools that free up your time for complex cases and higher-impact decisions. WHAT WILL HELP YOU SUCCEED - 2+ years of legal/regulatory compliance in tech, online services or e-commerce; not only AML - Knowledge of compliance requirements and their practical application: ICANN policies, GDPR, data protection, domain ownership claims, and fraudulent transfers. - Strong analytical and investigative skills, with the ability to connect patterns, identify root causes, and solve complex problems. - High curiosity and a continuous learning mindset, actively seeking new perspectives, exploring industry best practices, and questioning existing ways of working. - Challenge assumptions, speak up when something doesn't make sense, and take action to make things better around you. - Already using AI to build or automate processes in your work. - Comfortable with uncertainty and evolving responsibilities, adapting quickly as priorities change. - Fluent in Lithuanian and English Nice to have: - Experience with payment fraud, online abuse, account recovery, or fraud detection systems. - Familiarity with UDRP, EU consumer protection regulations, or other frameworks relevant to digital services. - Knowledge of international regulatory requirements and compliance practices. MEET YOUR TEAM Fraud never stands still, and neither do we. We're a hands-on, curious team that combines expertise in abuse, compliance, payments, and fraud prevention to stay ahead of evolving threats. We move fast, share knowledge, and learn from each other as new challenges emerge. We work closely with Legal, Customer Success, Product, and technical teams, bringing different perspectives together to tackle complex challenges. Beyond our own team, we equip others with the knowledge, detection tools, and capabilities to recognize and prevent abuse at their level, embedding fraud prevention across every function at Hostinger.
