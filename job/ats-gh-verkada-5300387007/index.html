Who We Are Verkada is transforming how organizations protect their people and places with an integrated, privacy-sensitive AI-powered platform that includes solutions for video security, access control, air quality sensors, alarms, intercoms, and visitor management. We’ve got serious momentum in the market: more than 30,000 customers (including 100+ of the Fortune 500), a $5.8B valuation, more than $1 billion in annualized bookings, and backing from CapitalG, Sequoia Capital, General Catalyst, Felicis Ventures, Next47 and more. Physical AI is one of the most consequential technology shifts of our time, and Verkada is at the center of it. You can look at all kinds of communities to see our platform’s impact in the world. It's the retailer that uses our agentic AI to deter theft before it happens. The warehouse that uses AI-powered alerts to make sure its team is protected on the floor with proper PPE. The school that’s alerted to a threat in real-time and triggers a lockdown in seconds, not minutes. We’re rapidly scaling this impact: today, more than 2 million Verkada devices are deployed across 170+ countries. About the Role Verkada is seeking an accomplished Enterprise Account Executive to accelerate growth across public sector accounts in the South of the UK. You will own territory strategy, develop new logo enterprise relationships and expand strategic accounts, working closely with our Channel, EDR, Marketing and Sales Engineering teams. We are looking for a well-connected, value-led seller who understands public sector buying processes, can engage senior decision-makers and knows how to collaborate with channel partners to create and close complex opportunities. This is an opportunity to help shape a high-potential territory as Verkada continues its expansion across EMEA. What You’ll Do • Own and execute a territory plan to win new public sector customers, expand strategic accounts and exceed quarterly revenue goals. • Develop executive relationships and lead conversations that connect customer priorities to measurable operational and commercial outcomes. • Work alongside Verkada’s Channel team and your existing partner network to identify opportunities, coordinate joint account plans and accelerate partner-supported deals. • Navigate complex procurement frameworks, multi-stakeholder buying groups and commercial negotiations, maintaining ownership of the customer relationship and sales cycle. • Collaborate with Marketing and Sales Engineering on targeted campaigns, demonstrations, trials and compelling business cases. • Build a high-quality pipeline, maintain disciplined forecasting and share customer and market insights with the wider business. What You Bring • A consistent record of exceeding enterprise technology sales targets, within UK public sector accounts. • An established network of senior customer contacts and public sector resellers, integrators and strategic partners across Southern UK. • Proven success building greenfield territories, winning net-new logos and expanding complex enterprise accounts. • Experience collaborating effectively with dedicated Channel teams on partner-led and co-selling opportunities. • Strong understanding of public sector procurement, routes to market and multi-stakeholder sales processes. • A commercially astute, value-led and MEDDPICC approach: able to create business cases, influence executives and sell outcomes rather than features.
