About the position Do you want to take a leading role in turning a proven microbial concept into a scalable climate solution? At the Faculty of Chemistry, Biotechnology and Food Science, NMBU, we offer a three-year researcher/postdoc position in microbial ecology, starting in early 2027. Agricultural soils are the largest human-made source of nitrous oxide (N₂O), a greenhouse gas almost 300 times more potent than CO₂. In a recent Nature paper, the Nitrogen group at NMBU showed that N₂O-reducing bacteria grown in biogas digestate can lower N₂O emissions from soil by 50–95%. This project, funded by the Grantham Foundation and the Platform for Agriculture &#43; Climate Transformation, aims to make the approach cheap and widely applicable: we will design synthetic microbial communities (SynComs) that degrade crop residues such as straw and use them to fuel the growth of N₂O-reducing bacteria. You will work alongside leading experts in the field. The Nitrogen group and the Microbial Ecology and Physiology group at NMBU have decades of experience in denitrification and N₂O biology and operate a robotized incubation system for high-resolution gas kinetics.  The project leader brings expertise in the ecology of synthetic communities, and large-scale field trials and adaptation of the concept to US systems and substrates are run in close collaboration with Prof. Wendy Yang’s group at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. As the project’s lead experimentalist at NMBU, you will drive the lab work – from strain isolation to community assembly and N₂O measurements – with strong support from experienced colleagues. Large-scale production and field trials are carried out together with the wider team and parallel projects, giving your lab results a direct path to the field. You will also mentor students and have the opportunities to help shape new research directions and contribute to future funding applications. This is an excellent opportunity for an early-career researcher to grow within a high-impact project and an experienced team. We look forward to your application! Main tasks The position is primarily experimental, and your responsibilities will include: • Leading the isolation and characterization of bacterial strains that degrade complex plant polymers and enhance the growth yield of N₂O-reducing bacteria • Designing and assembling synthetic microbial communities and testing them in microcosms • Measuring N₂O reduction and assessing the performance of candidate communities • Validating the best-performing communities in scaled-up cultivations • Contributing lab results and expertise to field trials, including work with a field robot • Mentoring master’s students and supporting the training of other team members • Presenting results at international conferences and seminars • Developing new research ideas and contributing to funding applications • Leading and contributing to scientific publications Competence Required qualifications • A PhD in microbiology, microbial ecology, biotechnology or a related field • Documented wet-lab experience with bacterial isolation, characterization or cultivation • Experience with independently planning and carrying out experimental research • A strong publication record in international peer-reviewed journals, relative to career stage • Good presentation skills, written and oral, in English Qualifications considered an advantage Experience with: • Complex substrates such as straw or other plant biomass • Isolation and characterization of bacteria • DNA extraction from bacterial isolates • Cultivation of microbial communities • Denitrification, nitrogen cycling or anaerobic cultivation • N₂O or other gas measurements • Design and planning of wet-lab experiments • Field trials • Data analysis and bioinformatics  Personal qualities • Highly motivated and ambitious, with a high work capacity • Analytical, with a strong ability to work independently and take initiative • Ability to lead and coordinate practical research activities • Good communication and collaboration skills • Flexible and solution-oriented Emphasis will be placed on personal suitability and on how the candidate’s experience fits the needs of the project. The overall assessment of qualifications will determine the ranking of applicants.
