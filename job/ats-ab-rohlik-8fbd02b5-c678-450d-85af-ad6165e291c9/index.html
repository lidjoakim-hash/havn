The role is overseeing all accounting activities for the relevant process – PTP, related to the Rohlik Group and other related entities.   WHAT WE EXPECT FROM YOU - Processing of Vendor invoices in line with company policies and controls - Processing of T&E - Preparing accruals and other PTP-related journal vouchers - Open items resolution - Assuring the timelines and quality of the reconciliation process - Handle escalations, complex reconciliations, and non-standard cases. - Participate in the performance management and KPIs setting process - Drive continuous process improvements and efficiency initiatives across the PTP function   WHAT WE LOOK FOR - 1-2 years of accounting experience in a similar role - Demonstrate experience in driving continuous improvement - Advanced English language skills.
