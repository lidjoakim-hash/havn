Trade Compliance Officer

Sandvik Aktiebolag · Malmö, Sweden

💰 ca. 350 000–410 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 8.12.2026

Do you want to shape trade compliance for a global business while staying close to real deals and day-to-day operations? At Business Area Rock Processing, we’re looking for a Trade Compliance Officer to lead our work across sanctions, customs, global trade and export controls. You combine a strong understanding of international trade compliance with a practical mindset and the confidence to turn complex requirements into effective ways of working. About the job You own and develop our trade compliance framework in line with Sandvik policies, helping our Divisions translate requirements into practical processes and controls. Working across the business, you combine governance with hands-on support in deals, projects, and operational matters. You report to our Chief Compliance Officer. Job responsibilities Establish monitoring and controls, support authority audits and represent Rock Processing in Sandvik’s trade compliance network at all levels in the organization. Improve trade master data and integrate compliance controls into how we buy, ship and sell, including screening, review and approvals. Monitor regulatory developments across the EU, US, UK, China and other markets, and prepare the business for major changes such as the EU Customs Reform. Own restricted party screening, conduct trade compliance due diligence and advise on risks, controls, contract clauses, Incoterms and customs matters. Support third-party risk activities and M&A. Develop and deliver training to internal teams and key partners. Location and flexibility The position is based in Malmö, Stockholm or Sandviken, Sweden. Other Sandvik locations in Europe may also be considered. Occasional travel is included in the job. Your profile You bring extensive experience in trade compliance within an international environment, ideally from an industrial company, and you’re comfortable working across both governance and operational matters. Your expertise covers sanctions, customs and export controls, and you know how to apply complex requirements in a global business. Your background includes: Solid knowledge of EU and US trade regulations, including customs classification, sanctions management, origin, valuation and Incoterms. Experience working with trade processes and data in ERP systems. Experience covering sanctions, export controls and customs, from governance through to operational implementation. Fluency in English, verbally and in writing. Experience with authority audits, licensing and external advisors is an advantage, as is knowledge of anti-bribery and corruption compliance, or data privacy. You’re independent and hands-on, with the drive to learn and take on new challenges. With strong integrity and a pragmatic mindset, you know when to stand firm and communicate complex topics clearly across different audiences. You communicate confidently with people at every level of the organization and tailor your message to each audience. Our culture At Sandvik, we’re tech driven, innovative and entrepreneurial. We believe that success is a team effort so we value diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive culture where people can be themselves and reach their full potential. So, we invest in supporting each other, learning together and celebrating our differences. Visit our stories hub, LinkedIn or Facebook to get to know us further.
