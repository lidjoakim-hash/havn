Key Account Sales Executive – Paris (Office-first) Team description Our Paris sales team helps small and growing merchants across France get set up with SumUp's payment tools, often as their very first introduction to the brand. We're a close-knit, target-driven group that thrives on outbound conversations, quick thinking and celebrating wins together. This role gives you a real seat at the table from day one: you'll own conversations end to end, learn the fundamentals of a fast-paced sales cycle and see the direct impact of your work every time a merchant signs up. What you'll do • 💼 Reach out to small business prospects by phone, email and social media, starting the conversation and building the pipeline • 🤝 Get to know each business's needs and show how SumUp can genuinely help them • 🎤 Run product pitches and demos that turn curiosity into commitment • 🔥 Manage the full sales cycle, from first contact through to closing the deal • 📊 Track performance against targets and use feedback and data to keep improving • 🧠 Share ideas with the team and help build an energised, collaborative office culture What we're looking for… • Experience in outbound sales or business development, with a genuine drive to learn and grow quickly. • Comfort working with high volumes of phone-based outreach. • Curiosity and confidence when speaking with new people and solving their problems. • A results-driven mindset with a track record of working towards targets. • Fluency in French and English, given daily contact with local merchants and a global team. Why you should join SumUp • 🌎 Opportunity to work with a truly global, multicultural team from our Paris location.
