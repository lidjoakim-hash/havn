Our AI-native workspace lets legal professionals move faster, think more clearly, and operate with sharper precision. By analysing thousands of documents in minutes and powering end-to-end workflows, we cut through complexity, teams can focus on what matters: judgment, strategy, and outcomes. 2,100+ customers across 80+ countries trust us, including Cleary Gottlieb, Goodwin, Linklaters, White & Case, Dentons, and Barclays. The role As a Senior Backend Engineer at Legora, you’ll build and ship products that transform how legal professionals work. You’ll have ownership across the full tech stack, from shaping technical direction to delivering high-quality experiences used by legal teams around the world. With plenty of autonomy, you’ll tackle meaningful problems, collaborate closely across teams, and have a real impact on both the product and the engineering culture. What you will be doing: - Architect and implement features for our web platform - Take ownership of our codebase, balancing high-quality code, security and speed of development - Work with technologies such as Node.js, Postgres, Elasticsearch, AWS, Azure and Kubernetes - Review code and mentor junior colleagues to support them in their development, making the team even better! - Drive continuous improvement and challenge us, the industry and yourself - You’ll travel regularly to Stockholm for onboarding, collaboration, and major product milestones — working shoulder-to-shoulder with the team when it matters most.
