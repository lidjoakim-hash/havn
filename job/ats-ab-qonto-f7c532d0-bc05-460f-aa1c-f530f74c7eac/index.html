KYC/B (onboarding) Team Lead - German Market

Qonto · Belgrade, Serbia, Serbia

💰 25 500–32 500 EUR/årHeltidFjernarbeid · EuropeDirekte fra arbeidsgiverFrist 20.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Join us as Team Lead Onboarding for the German market to lead the team that runs KYC/B onboarding for our German customers, from first document check to account opening. You will guide 10 agents based in Belgrade, Serbia, and make sure every onboarding decision protects our clients and Qonto’s regulatory standing as a licensed bank, while turning new applicants into active customers. ➡️ What you’ll do - Lead your team of 10: Run 1:1s, give regular feedback, coach agents on complex cases and set up structured onboarding for every new joiner. - Keep compliance and SLAs on track: Make sure your team applies KYC/B rules consistently, with a risk-based “stop and check” reflex, and meets onboarding SLAs for German customers. - Convert new clients: Keep the onboarding conversion rate of new German customers at a high level. You will find where applicants drop off, remove friction in the process and agent communication, and never lower compliance standards to do it. - Raise quality, speed and capacity: Maintain CSAT and cut handling time, backed by up-to-date SOPs and root-cause analysis. Own the team’s automation roadmap (Dust, Claude, internal assistants) so the team handles more volume without adding headcount. - Align the stakeholders: Agree priorities and dependencies with Risk & Compliance, Product and Ops leadership, and bring German-market needs into those discussions. ➡️ What we’re looking for - Compliance and acquisition mindset (deal-breaker): You know the fundamentals of KYC/B and know when to stop and check a case. You also care about turning each applicant into a customer, and you know how to do both without trading one for the other. - Team leadership: You have managed or coached a team of operations or compliance agents, ideally in a regulated environment, and you know how to give clear feedback. - Process and continuous improvement: You use data, including conversion and drop-off figures, to find what slows the team down, keep SOPs current and fix root causes rather than symptoms. - AI mindset: You use AI to make your whole team more efficient, not just your own work. - Languages: Fluent German and English C1, for working with German customers and with our English-speaking teams. ➡️ What we can offer you - A team to shape: You will define how a 10-person onboarding team works day to day, from coaching routines to how new agents get up to speed. - High visibility at a licensed bank: Your KPIs, including compliance, CSAT, SLAs and conversion, land on Ops leadership dashboards, and your decisions shape how safely customers join Qonto. - Cross-functional exposure: You will work with Risk & Compliance, Product and Ops leadership, the rooms you need to grow toward Senior Team Lead or Head of a KYC/B sub-domain. - An AI-forward scope: You decide where Dust, Claude and internal assistants can take work off the team’s plate. - Based in Belgrad, Serbia: 3 days a week on site.
