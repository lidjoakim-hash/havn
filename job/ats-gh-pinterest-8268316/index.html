Pinterest's Consumer Brand Partnerships team sits within a tiny-but-mighty consumer marketing org, alongside social, influencer, creative, and brand teams. Reporting to the Brand Partnerships Lead, the Brand Partnerships Manager (Europe) will be the inaugural brand partnerships team member to work on projects outside of the UK in Europe. As Brand Partnerships Manager, Europe, you’ll lead the rollout of Pinterest’s co-marketing advertiser brand partnerships opportunities across France, Germany and the Netherlands. This role is part of Consumer Marketing and will work with Global Sales Organisation (GSO) to make it easier for teams to identify, assess and deliver relevant co-marketing opportunities with advertisers. This includes scaling the Advertiser Co-Marketing Brand Toolkit in those regions, giving top-tier advertisers governed access to Pinterest IP off-platform while driving commercial revenue. This role combines regional programme leadership, brand governance, creative supervision and partner collaboration. You’ll help ensure opportunities are grounded in Pinterest’s consumer marketing priorities, consistent with our brand standards and practical to deliver, while supporting commercial outcomes. This is a marketing and partnership role that works closely with Sales; it is not a quota-carrying Sales position.
