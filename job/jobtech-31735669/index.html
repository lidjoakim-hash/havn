Allocation Controller Welcome to a role where analysis meets business, customer focus, and commercial impact. As an Allocation Controller at Kappahl, you will play an important role in ensuring that the right products reach the right stores at the right time and in the right quantities. If you are analytical, business-oriented, and motivated by turning data into actions that drive sales and customer experience, we would love to hear from you. What we offer This is an opportunity to work in a central commercial role where your analysis directly influences product availability, customer experience and business results. You will join a collaborative environment with the opportunity to contribute both to daily performance and to the continued development of Allocation at Kappahl The role You will be part of our Allocation team and work closely with Buying, Planning, Retail, E-commerce and our market organizations. The role combines day-to-day allocation with forward-looking analysis and continuous development. You will have a clear mandate to identify commercial opportunities, recommend actions and follow up the results. • Plan and execute product allocation across stores and online channels based on demand, sales performance and stock position. • Analyze sales, availability, sell-through and stock health to identify risks and commercial opportunities. • Develop and maintain store clusters and allocation parameters that reflect local demand and customer needs. • Translate insights into clear recommendations and actions for relevant stakeholders. • Monitor allocation KPIs and evaluate the impact of your decisions. • Contribute to the development of allocation processes, tools and scalable, data-driven ways of working. Are you interested? This is a temporary position for one year due to leave of absence, based at our new head office in Mölndal, with a planned start date as soon as possible. At Kappahl Group, we believe that diverse perspectives make us stronger. We are committed to creating an inclusive workplace where everyone feels welcome and has the opportunity to grow and succeed. Ready to join us? Apply with your CV today – no personal letter is needed. We review applications continuously, so we encourage you to submit your application as soon as possible, but no later than November 30th 2026. We look forward to hearing from you. Your profile You have a relevant university degree, or equivalent professional experience, within Economics, Business Administration, Supply Chain or a related field. Previous experience from allocation, merchandise planning, demand planning, business analysis, supply chain or another analytical retail role is valuable. • Strong analytical skills and confidence working with large data sets in Excel. • The ability to connect detailed analysis with customer needs and commercial outcomes. • Fluency in Swedish and English, both written and spoken. • Experience with allocation or forecasting tools, clustering methods or BI solutions is an advantage. • Curiosity about retail, customer behavior, digital development, data analytics and AI. Who you are You are analytical, proactive and solution-oriented. You take ownership, communicate clearly and enjoy turning insights into measurable actions. You enjoy collaboration and build strong relationships across functions and markets, and you are comfortable moving between strategic discussions, analytical problem-solving and operational priorities. You have a strong end-to-end perspective and understand how decisions and activities in one part of the business impact the wider value chain, enabling you to identify dependencies and drive outcomes across the entire flow. Most importantly, you recognize yourself in Kappahl’s values: Inclusive, Courageous and Joyful.
