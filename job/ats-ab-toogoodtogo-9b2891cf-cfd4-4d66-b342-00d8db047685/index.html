Too Good To Go is on a mission to inspire and empower everyone to fight food waste together. Our app connects people with surplus food from stores, restaurants and brands, so good food gets eaten instead of wasted. ABOUT THE ROLE Our website is one of our biggest growth channels, helping millions of people discover Too Good To Go and thousands of businesses join us as partners. We are looking for an SEO & Content Specialist to help it grow even further. You will be the first person in our Global Web team fully dedicated to content and search. It is a hands on role with real ownership and impact across all our markets, working closely with our Global Web Lead and developers. WHAT YOU WILL DO - Plan, write and optimize content that ranks, for both consumers and business partners. - Make sure our content is localized and optimized for every country we operate in, working with local teams across many languages. - Refresh and improve our existing content library to get more out of what we already have. - Run technical SEO across a large, multi language website: indexation, hreflang, structured data, site health and migrations. - Use Semrush and similar tools for keyword research, competitor analysis, rank tracking and site audits, and turn the insights into improvements. - Build our presence in AI powered search such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. - Improve key pages and journeys to turn more visitors into app users and business signups. - Set up and run A/B tests on the website. - Track and report performance, and turn data into clear next steps. WHAT YOU BRING - Significant of hands on SEO experience, covering both content and technical SEO. - A track record of growing organic traffic. - Excellent writing and editing skills in English. - Hands on experience with Semrush (or similar SEO tools), Google Search Console and Amplitude or GA4. - Experience with multilingual, multi country SEO and content localization. - Experience working with developers and writing clear requirements. - Curiosity about AI search and a test and learn mindset. - A self starter who enjoys ownership in a small, fast moving team. NICE TO HAVE - Experience with marketplaces, B2B websites or web to app journeys. - Experience using AI tools to scale content.
