About the Role As a Technical Solutions Consultant you will join Pigment’s EMEA Technical Solutions team, with a particular focus on the DACH and Central EMEA markets. You will lead the technical conversation with prospect IT, InfoSec, and enterprise architecture stakeholders, act as Pigment’s technical representative in customer meetings, and own end to end responses for RFPs and InfoSec questionnaires. Your job is to make sure that technical validation never becomes the reason a deal slows down. As part of an EMEA team you will also support opportunities across the wider region where demand requires it. DACH is a market where SAP solutions are  the incumbent in almost every account you will walk into. The single most important thing you will do is make it easy for a German enterprise IT team to understand exactly how Pigment sits alongside and integrates with their existing SAP estate. This role is therefore well suited to someone who has spent time in enterprise IT integrating SaaS solutions into an SAP ecosystem, and who now wants to bring that experience to the other side of the table. You will work alongside Functional Solution Consultants, Account Executives, the Security and Legal teams, and our partner ecosystem. Beyond frontline deal support, you will help build the reusable German language and SAP specific technical content that the wider EMEA GTM team relies on. This role is based in Germany, with Frankfurt preferred.
