Tasks and responsibilities: • Work in collaboration with local and Icelandic colleagues • Improve, manage, support and create PowerBI reports for different business domains: IT, Finance, Sales, etc. • Administer, maintain and troubleshoot issues in PowerBI environment. Support end-users in PowerBI field. • Monitor and react on system alerts, improve overall sustainability • Resolve BI related support issues, root cause analysis, troubleshooting of any occurred issues with integrations, data cubes, data bases, servers, etc. according to SLA. • Propose and implement solutions’ changes, improve performance, create new data integrations • Automate and improve data quality checks • Enhancement of data processing • Monitoring of existing solutions and their effective work • Migration of existing BI solutions to Cloud Professional requirements: • At least 1 year of experience in development or support of BI solutions based on Microsoft stack. • Good knowledge of MS SQL Server (queries in T-SQL, stored functions & procedures, script performance optimization etc.) • Experience in Power BI reports development • Basic skills in SSIS, SSRS, SSAS (Multidimensional and Tabular) • Basic skills in Power BI administration • Basic understanding of Windows Server (server roles, performance, software compatibility etc.) Be a plus: • Understanding of business processes in Finance & Control, Sales, Manufacturing & Operation domains • Familiar with Change Management process by ITIL • Experience with DB performance analysis tools: Database Performance Analyzer from Solarwinds (solarwinds.com), SentryOne Client (sentryone.com) or similar • Experience with Maestro (maestro.com) and Expectus exMon (exmon.com) software • Basic knowledge of Powershell scripts • Knowing of TimeXtemder (timextender.com) or similar automated data management platform • Experience with VCS (git) • Knowledge of Azure Data Analytics tools. Personal requirements: • Communication skills • Responsible and pro-active • Self-organized • Team player • English: upper-intermediate
