Job Purpose The Senior RLWI Operations Engineer provides senior-level engineering support for RLWI operations as part of one common Operations Engineering department. The role combines operational planning, stack and equipment readiness, technical documentation, execution support, risk management and post-job learning. The position handles more complex, high-risk or non-standard scopes than an Operations Engineer, while supporting the Lead Operations Engineer and coaching less experienced engineers. The role helps ensure safe, practical and efficient execution in accordance with TechnipFMC standards, client requirements, procedures and HSE expectations. Job Description • Plan and support RLWI operations as part of a project taskforce, including operational planning, execution sequences, readiness activities and assigned engineering scopes. • Prepare, update, create and review procedures, technical instructions, checklists and other operational engineering documentation. • Support equipment readiness, project-specific parts and consumables, procurement, logistics, service center follow-up, suppliers, sub-suppliers, mobilization, demobilization, maintenance, yard activities and offshore execution when required. • Contribute to operational risk assessments, readiness reviews, deviation handling, troubleshooting and Management of Change evaluations. • Support client, supplier and internal technical discussions related to planning, readiness and execution of assigned scopes. • Track progress, performance, deviations, lessons learned and improvement actions, and escalate issues early to the Lead Operations Engineer or manager. • Coach and support less experienced Operations Engineers through practical guidance, peer review and sharing of lessons learned. You are meant for this job if: • REQUIRED KNOWLEDGE/FORMAL EDUCATION: • • BSc or MSc within a relevant engineering discipline, or equivalent relevant experience. • REQUIRED EXPERIENCE:• Typically 5–10 years of relevant experience from RLWI, subsea, offshore, marine, field execution or similar operational engineering environments. • REQUIRED CERTIFICATE(S):• Willingness to obtain and maintain required offshore certificates, such as health certificate and BOSIET, when needed. • REQUIRED INTERPERSONAL SKILLS:• Capable of working independently and in teams, with clear communication towards engineering, operations, offshore personnel, suppliers and clients. • REQUIRED TECHNICAL SKILLS AND/OR PROBLEM-SOLVING SKILLS:• Strong understanding of operational planning, procedures, equipment readiness and practical execution constraints. RLWI experience is beneficial, but not required. • Strong technical skills within RLWI systems is beneficial. Experience with similar mechanical, hydraulic, electro/control systems and schematics is an advantage. • Strong skills in creating and reviewing technical documentation, procedures, checklists and operational instructions. • Strong problem-solving skills, with good judgement in risk, deviation handling, troubleshooting and escalation. • Capable of seeking safe and practical solutions related to QHSE, assembly, handling, operation, manufacturing, testing, yard work and offshore execution. • Additional information: • The position is part of one common RLWI Operations Engineering role family. Senior level differs mainly by complexity, independence, judgement, support to Lead Operations Engineer and coaching of less experienced engineers. • Travel, yard stay or offshore support may be required depending on project and operational needs.
