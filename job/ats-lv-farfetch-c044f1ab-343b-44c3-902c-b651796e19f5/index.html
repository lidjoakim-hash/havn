It offers a dynamic and welcoming environment where our employees can connect and network with a large community of tech professionals.   THE ROLE Are you passionate about networking, cloud technologies, and automation? Do you approach infrastructure with a DevOps mindset? If so, we'd love to hear from you. We're looking for a Cloud Network Engineer to join our Platform team and help design, automate, and operate secure, scalable, and resilient cloud network infrastructure. This is an exciting opportunity to work with modern cloud technologies, Infrastructure as Code, and network automation while contributing to solutions that have a real business impact. WHAT YOU'LL DO • Implement, and manage cloud networking solutions across modern cloud platforms. • Automate network provisioning and operations using Infrastructure as Code (Terraform) and CI/CD practices. • Build, maintain, and optimize secure, highly available, and scalable network architectures. • Configure, troubleshoot, and optimize network infrastructure, including routers, firewalls, VPNs, and Cloud networking services. • Simplify and modernize our network architecture by reducing complexity and improving reliability. • Monitor network performance, proactively identify issues, and drive continuous improvements. • Collaborate with Platform, Security, and Engineering teams to deliver resilient and secure infrastructure. • Contribute to technical standards, documentation, and operational excellence initiatives. WHO YOU ARE • A graduate in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. • Experienced in cloud networking, preferably in Azure (AWS or GCP experience is also valued). • You have strong understanding of networking fundamentals, including routing, switching, VPNs, DNS, TCP/IP, load balancing, and firewalls. • You have experience with: Infrastructure as Code tools such as Terraform; and Automation using Python, Bash, or similar scripting languages. • Familiar with Git-based workflows and CI/CD pipelines. • Experienced in Kubernetes networking and container platforms is a plus. • Analytical with troubleshooting skills and proactive approach to problem-solving. • You have a collaborative mindset with excellent communication skills and a passion for continuous learning and improvement. • Proficient in English. Nice to have • Industry certifications such as CCNA, CKAD, JNCIA, AZ-900, NSE, or equivalent. • Experience with hybrid cloud networking, SD-WAN, or Zero Trust architectures. • Familiarity with network observability and monitoring tools.
