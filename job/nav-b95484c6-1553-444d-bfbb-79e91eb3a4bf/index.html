Join the Snowhotel Kirkenes Winter Team 2026/2027! Snowhotel Kirkenes is the only hotel in the world made entirely of snow and ice that is open 365 days a year. Located between the mountains and the Barents Sea, our hotel and activity center offer unforgettable Arctic adventures in every season. We are now hiring team members for the 2026/2027 winter season and are looking for positive and service-minded people to fill the following roles: • Receptionists • Guide About the roles: As part of our team, you will help create a welcoming atmosphere and ensure guests have a smooth and memorable stay. Whether you are serving guests in the restaurant, handling check-ins and bookings, or maintaining clean and cozy rooms, you will be an important part of making the Snowhotel stay memorable and exceptional. Working in the Arctic means facing long, cold winters with dark days and challenging weather. We are looking for reliable, flexible individuals who thrive in a fast-paced, international environment and enjoy working as part of a close team. In summary, we’re looking for someone who: • Is positive and responsible • Is flexible and available to work evenings, weekends, and during peak periods, including the Christmas season • Preferably has experience in hospitality, customer service, or cleaning (but not required) • Values excellent service and genuine human connections – we want you to truly care about our guests • Is fluent in English; additional languages are a strong advantage • Is over 20 years old • The Guide position requires fluency in German. Knowledge of additional languages beyond English is highly desirable. We offer: • Seasonal contract (Mainly during the period Oct/Nov 2026 – March/Apr 2027) • Training and development through our Academy and Campus • A chance to live and work in one of the world’s most magical winter destinations • Exciting work with guests and colleagues from around the world • Basic work clothing and equipment • Assistance in finding accommodation (may require private market application) • Salary and benefits according to collective agreement • Friendly colleagues who believe in having fun at work • Opportunities for professional growth within the Norwegian Travel group
