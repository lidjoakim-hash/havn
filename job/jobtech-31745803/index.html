Junior Controller - Nordic Markets

Husqvarna AB · Jönköping, Sweden

💰 ca. 450 000–550 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 8.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

At Husqvarna Group, innovation is in our DNA. With over 330 years of heritage and a passion for pioneering technology, we design and deliver world-class products and solutions for forest, park, garden and construction care. From robotic lawn mowers to cutting-edge chainsaws and sustainable battery systems, we're shaping the future, and we want you to be part of it. We are now looking for a Junior Controller to join Gardena Division, part of Husqvarna Group, and strengthen our Finance team's operational and analytical capabilities as we continue to grow. About the role In this role, you will be part of a stable and experienced Finance team that plays a key role in the operational success of our business. This role mixes hands-on controlling with financial analysis, process improvement, and management reporting. You will work closely with the Nordics Hub Business Controller, Sales, and other stakeholders across the organization. As you settle into the role, you will gradually take full responsibility for operational controlling across the Nordic markets. Your main responsibilities include: Controlling, month-end close, forecast and budget for the Nordics (Sweden, Norway, Denmark and Finland). Support accounts receivable (AR) control in close collaboration with Sales and internal stakeholders to keep AR healthy and help evaluate credit limits. Support the monthly cost allocation, calculate, and follow up on customer bonuses. Support sales teams with financial input and questionnaires. Identify inefficiencies and drive improvements across finance workflows, using modern tools, Excel add-ins, and AI applications. Help with various accounting and controlling tasks across the P&L and Balance Sheet. You will report to our Regional Controlling Manager. About you You're a structured and thorough finance professional with a good eye for detail. Planning your work comes naturally and staying flexible when priorities change does too. With clear communication, you feel comfortable sharing your thoughts, whether that's asking questions, bringing ideas or raising something that's caught your attention. Finding smarter ways of working motivates you, and working independently suits you just as well as being part of a team. In addition, you bring: A bachelor's degree in finance, accounting, economics, or business administration. Around 1–3 years of experience in a controlling, accounting or finance role, with a strong grasp of end-to-end accounting principles, P&L and Balance Sheet. Strong Excel skills and openness to using modern automation and AI tools. Experience with a passion for financial analysis, generating data-driven insights, monitoring data quality, and summarizing and presenting financial data. Strong communication and organizational skills. Fluency in English, both written and spoken. Location This role is based in Huskvarna, Sweden. We believe the best ideas happen when we're connected. We spend most of our time together in the office, creating space for collaboration, creativity, and fast decision-making. This is consistent across all our global offices and helps us maintain a strong culture, support learning and development, and ensure everyone has access to the people and resources they need to thrive.
