About the Role Fivetran is building data pipelines to power the modern data stack for thousands of companies. We’re looking for a Senior Systems Engineer, Customer Support Systems who will play a key role in driving our Customer Support technology. As part of our Systems Engineering team, you will build and maintain the integrations, automations, and custom applications that power our Customer Support Systems, anchored by Zendesk. You’ll write code to solve support workflow problems, administer and configure the platform when needed, and primarily work closely with Customer Support, but also on occasion with Product Operations, RevOps, Legal, and other partner teams. You’ll join a small team and work alongside a senior systems engineer to learn from and collaborate with, sharing ownership of our Zendesk environment. You’ll be a backup administrator, so support operations stay covered when either of you is out. Technologies You’ll Use Zendesk (including Knowledge and Community), BigQuery, Salesforce, Jira, Slack, and Okta are systems you’ll use day-to-day. You’ll write code in languages such as Python and TypeScript, work with REST APIs and webhooks, and use Git and code review as part of your normal workflow.
