Sustainability Manager Nordics to Omya

Committo AB · Malmö, Sweden

Are you an experienced QSHE professional who enjoys working closely with industrial operations? Are you motivated by taking ownership, driving improvements and making a real difference across the organisation? We are now looking for a Sustainability Manager Nordics to join Omya in Malmö. Here, you will take on a broad and varied role, coordinating QSHE activities across the Nordic region, with a particular focus on Sweden and Finland. You will work in an international environment, collaborating with colleagues across different functions and countries. Sounds interesting? We look forward to receiving your application! ABOUT THE ROLE As Sustainability Manager Nordics, you will work closely with local site managers to ensure regulatory compliance, manage operational risks and maintain high standards across Omya's production and distribution activities. You will be part of an international matrix organisation, working with regional management, local operations and Omya's global Sustainability team. Your main responsibilities will include: Maintain and develop integrated management systems in accordance with ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001 and ISO 50001. Coordinate internal and external audits, certifications and corrective actions. Monitor regulatory requirements and support compliance with relevant legislation. Identify and assess QSHE risks and implement preventive measures together with local teams. Follow up on performance, KPIs and targets, and provide guidance and training where needed. The position is based at Omya's office in Malmö and involves frequent travel within the Nordic region to visit operational sites. You will report to the Head of Sustainability Nordics, based in Norway. ABOUT YOU We are looking for someone with a solid technical background who understands the challenges of working in a production environment and knows how to translate regulatory requirements into practical solutions. We believe you have: Relevant experience in QSHE, quality management, sustainability or industrial compliance. Experience from manufacturing or industrial operations, including exposure to chemical warehousing. Good knowledge of relevant ISO standards, management systems and regulatory requirements. A university degree in Engineering, Chemistry, Environmental Science or a related technical field. Fluency in Swedish and English, both written and spoken. Experience from chemical distribution, tank farms or sea shipments is considered an advantage. Finnish language skills are also a plus. As a person, you are proactive, structured and analytical. You build trust, communicate clearly and have the confidence to challenge existing ways of working when needed. With a diplomatic and collaborative approach, you know how to engage others and drive change without formal authority. CONTACT In this recruitment process, Omya is partnering with Committo. For more information, please contact Linda Samuelsson at linda.samuelsson@committo.se or +46 70 216 37 52, or Ida Ingeström at ida.ingestrom@committo.se or +46 70 434 76 81 at Committo. Interviews and selection are conducted on an ongoing basis, so we encourage you to submit your application as soon as possible. ABOUT OMYA Omya is a leading global producer of industrial minerals and a worldwide distributor of specialty materials. Omya was founded 140 years ago and remains a privately-owned, independent company headquartered in Switzerland but with offices and plants in more than 50 countries. Thinking of tomorrow. Together When you join Omya, you become part of a global team that uses its creativity, expertise and experience to make a difference to people's lives. Thinking of Tomorrow means focusing on the challenges of the future and how we can develop mineral products to solve them.
