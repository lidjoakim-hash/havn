Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world's largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team Product Sales is a team of deep functional and technical specialists who both lead and advise on deals for the most strategically important products in the Stripe portfolio. Revenue and Finance Automation (RFA) Product Sales is tasked with attacking a $100B market opportunity for back-office finance automation across billing, tax, revenue reporting, and data. What you'll do As a Billing Account Executive (Grower) at Stripe, you'll use your sales and product expertise to help drive growth to existing Stripe customers. You'll collaborate with the core sales, marketing, product marketing, and product teams to develop deep product and industry knowledge and drive strategic deals. You'll be instrumental in leading in-depth product discussions with existing and prospective customers—and building and executing against our go-to-market strategy. Responsibilities • Build deep, resilient, and multi-threaded relationships across SMB client organizations, with the ability to articulate value across different functions and key business stakeholders. • The capability to develop and execute formal Strategic Account Plans that map out the client organization, identify short-term and long-term growth opportunities, and forecast renewal or expansion cycles • Proactive communication and transparency with consistent value-adding engagements, updates, and ability to manage expectations effectively.
