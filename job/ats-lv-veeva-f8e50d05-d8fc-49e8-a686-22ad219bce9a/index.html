Veeva Systems is a mission-driven organization and pioneer in industry cloud, helping life sciences companies bring therapies to patients faster. As one of the fastest-growing SaaS companies in history, we surpassed $3B in revenue in our last fiscal year with extensive growth potential ahead.   At the heart of Veeva are our values: Do the Right Thing, Customer Success, Employee Success, and Speed. We're not just any public company – we made history in 2021 by becoming a public benefit corporation (PBC), legally bound to balancing the interests of customers, employees, society, and investors.   As a Work Anywhere company, we support your flexibility to work from home or in the office, so you can thrive in your ideal environment.   Join us in transforming the life sciences industry, committed to making a positive impact on its customers, employees, and communities. The Role Our deeply experienced Veeva Commercial Architecture team helps our Life Sciences clients maximize their investment in the Veeva Commercial Cloud suite of applications. We act as advisors to key customer stakeholders and help them make good decisions on how to achieve strategic goals through the deployment of technology on a global scale. Veeva architects have overall responsibility for ensuring that Veeva applications are configured using best practices, and that they are integrated effectively into our customer’s wider commercial architecture.   As a Commercial Architect in the Veeva Systems Professional Services group, you will participate and lead in business process discussion, application design, and the creation of technical and end-user requirements for Veeva Commercial products. You will then collaborate with a variety of Customers, Vendors, and Veeva colleagues during projects to drive the delivery of high-quality solutions, and increase Customer Success (one of Veeva’s core values).   Opportunities are available throughout Europe for this role as this is a remote position.  There is no work location requirement if a candidate is in close proximity to an airport and able to meet travel requirements. Candidates within proximity to Veeva office will have the opportunity to work there if preferred.    Qualified candidates must be legally authorized to be employed in the EU. Veeva Systems does not anticipate providing sponsorship for employment visa status for this employment position. What You'll Do • This position is 20% project management, 20% strategic, 30% analysis, and 30% technical (drawing on technical, business and/or product knowledge in advanced configurations of Veeva applications) • Participate and lead meetings with senior management and executives as the subject matter expert - simplifying complex technical architectures into language that makes sense to non-technical stakeholders • Comfortable working with technical resources and translating high-level concepts and requirements into technical language so they can be built • As a thought-leader you will continue to extend your subject matter expertise on the Life Sciences industry and the Veeva solutions • Mentorship: Provide direction to junior resources and support recruitment efforts • Creativity: Create high-quality assessments, application health checks, and commercial architectures, in support of the solution design • Drive and Determination: Ability to “wear any hat” necessary to make the Customer successful and always get the job done Requirements • Motivated and effective self-starter that can lead colleagues and customers through complexity to get results • 5+ years of consulting/architecture/data experience in life sciences - you should have projects working with multiple life sciences organizations on your CV • You should already have a deep knowledge of Veeva CRM plus it is highly desirable to also have real-world exposure to other Veeva Commercial Cloud Application ( Vault Promomats, Vault MedComms, CRM Events, Network, Align, OpenData).
