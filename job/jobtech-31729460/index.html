Jobbeskrivning Company Story Sigma Connectivity is a global tech house with all the resources needed to bring a product to market under one roof. We believe in investing in knowledge and education, making a positive impact on society and shaping a brighter future for all. Through our core values, at Sigma Connectivity we create an environment that fosters growth, creativity, and collaboration. Our company culture promotes the freedom to explore new ideas and push the boundaries of what is possible. Working with us means you will be working in an international environment with a diverse and inclusive atmosphere. We operate globally from 12 sites and have expert teams in North America, Sweden, Germany, Denmark, United Kingdom and Poland. We are looking for a Senior System Architect! In this role, you’ll translate client visions into robust blueprints for connected products, bridging hardware and software to define the project skeleton. As a part of the Industrialization & System Design team, you’ll own the end-to-end strategy for manufacturability, testability, and product launch—driving DFX (Design for Excellence, including DFM and DFT) directly into early PCB designs and defining test architectures from initial prototype validation through to mass production. What we offer High-Impact Engineering: Own the technical vision for complex, market-defining products from concept to high-volume manufacturing. World-Class Team & Culture: Work alongside elite cross-functional engineers in a collaborative, innovation-driven environment. Cutting-Edge Projects: Direct access to diverse, challenging hardware and IoT projects for leading global brands. Autonomy & Influence: High visibility and direct influence over architectural decisions, client strategies, and design-for-test practices. Work tasks This role focuses on the early-stage design and technical leadership of industrialization projects, ensuring that hardware and software components are optimized for production and testing. Architecture & Strategy: Lead system architecture definitions and create comprehensive production test strategies that balance quality, yield, schedule, and cost. Requirements Breakdown: Convert product, hardware, and quality constraints into clear specifications, including System Requirement Specifications, Production Test Requirements, and Test Implementation Specs. DFX & Risk Management: Review hardware schematics and PCB concepts early to identify manufacturability, testability, and reliability risks, driving proactive mitigations with R&D teams. Test Architecture Definition: Define test coverage, interfaces, embedded test functionality, fixtures, measurement methods, and factory data traceability. Cross-Functional & Client Leadership: Drive technical discussions and balance trade-offs with customers, EMS partners, suppliers, R&D, quality, and sourcing teams. Mentorship: Guide engineering teams on best practices in systems engineering, hardware testability, and production deployment. We are looking for someone with A degree in Electrical Engineering, Electronics, Mechatronics, Computer Engineering, Engineering Physics, or equivalent practical background. Proven experience working as a System Architect guiding complex electronic or embedded products from concept to production. Proven background in production and manufacturing engineering, with strong practical experience bridging development and mass manufacturing environments. Solid hardware depth to evaluate schematics, drive DFT/DFM improvements, and establish production test architectures alongside EMS partners. Fluent English communication skills to present trade-offs, draft specifications, and align stakeholders It is meritorious if you have Experience with connected/IoT devices, wireless protocols, or embedded systems.
