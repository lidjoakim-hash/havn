Digital Business Developer

Toyota Material Handling Europe AB · Mjölby, Sweden

💰 ca. 540 000–650 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 9.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Are you passionate about digital business, e-commerce and technology? Do you enjoy understanding business needs, exploring technical solutions and turning ideas into digital capabilities? We are looking for a Digital Business Developer to play an important role in the continued development of T-Store, our digital e-commerce platform. In this role, you will work at the intersection of business and technology, translating business requirements into functional and technically sound solutions. You will work closely with business stakeholders, developers and solution architects, taking responsibility for the day-to-day product development of T-Store. This includes understanding business needs, managing requirements and priorities, shaping solutions and coordinating development activities from initial idea to implementation. A key part of the role is understanding how different systems and applications interact. You will participate in technical discussions, contribute to solution design and work closely with architects and developers to ensure that new functionality is scalable, integrated and aligned with the wider application landscape. You will also contribute to agile ways of working, testing and quality assurance, as well as identifying opportunities to improve our digital sales capabilities and customer experience. Your main responsibilities will include: Drive the day-to-day product development of T-Store, ensuring alignment with business needs and the overall product direction. Work closely with business stakeholders to understand, challenge and translate business requirements into functional specifications and development requirements. Own and maintain the product backlog, including requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria and application-level priorities. Participate in technical discussions and contribute to solution design, evaluating alternatives and identifying potential technical implications. Coordinate development activities, requirements clarification and release planning. Take part in agile team activities, including Scrum Master responsibilities, and contribute to continuous improvement of the team's ways of working. Coordinate testing and quality assurance as Test Leader, ensuring that delivered functionality meets agreed requirements. Identify opportunities for innovation and improvement within digital commerce, contributing to concept development and proof-of-concept initiatives. We believe you have a combination of business understanding, technical curiosity and a structured approach to problem-solving. You are comfortable moving between business discussions and technical details, and you enjoy connecting the two to create practical solutions. Experience in digital product development, e-commerce, technical business analysis, solution coordination or a similar role. Good understanding of web applications, system integrations and how different applications communicate. Ability to understand application architecture, technical dependencies and the implications of different solution approaches. Experience working with developers, solution architects or technical specialists. Ability to translate business requirements into clear functional requirements, user stories and acceptance criteria. Experience with agile development methods, preferably Scrum. A structured and analytical approach, combined with strong problem-solving skills. Strong communication skills and the ability to collaborate with both business and technical stakeholders. Excellent written and spoken English. Experience with e-commerce platforms, cloud services or modern web technologies is considered an advantage. Our Offer At Toyota Material Handling, you will become part of an international organisation with a strong focus on innovation, digitalization and continuous improvement.
