Now we’re building the delivery of (almost) everything and you’ll find us in over 500 cities in 30 countries around the world. In 2022 we joined forces with DoorDash and together we keep on dreaming big and expanding across the globe. Working at Wolt isn’t always easy, but it’s definitely exciting. Here you’ll learn more, build more, and ship more than in most other companies. You’ll be challenged a lot, but also have a lot of fun on the way. So, if you’re a self-starter with drive and entrepreneurial spirit, this could be the ride of your life. Wolt is the leading local commerce platform in several markets across Europe and Asia. Wolt makes it incredibly easy for you to discover and get what you want. Delivered to you – quickly, reliably and affordably. And by doing so, we make cities better places to live. About the Role We are seeking an experienced B2B Lifecycle Marketing Automation Manager to join our Wolt Ads team. The successful candidate will be responsible for managing the Wolt Ads Lead Lifecycle process and for our customer relationship management (CRM) activities to drive engagement, retention, and revenue growth. The Marketing Automation Manager will work closely with the marketing and sales teams to develop and execute effective communications campaigns that increase customer loyalty and lifetime value. About the team Our Wolt Ads Product team is responsible for building our Ads business across 23 countries. Together with our in-market sales teams, the Marketing Automation Manager is responsible for setting up automated marketing communication programs that create, grow and retain Wolt Ads customers for both restaurants, retail and brands, as well as integrating communications with our Sales and Account management processes. You will understand lead generation, including lead scoring and qualification, and partner with local Ad managers and Sales Ops. You'll provide guidance, reporting and lead-stage insights so Salesforce follow-up on leads is consistent across countries. You will need to be a strong communicator with the ability to create and analyze reports about the customer lifecycle and client re-engagement. SQL is a plus, as are BI skills such as Looker and strong Excel skills. In this role, you will be reporting to our B2B Lifecycle Marketing Lead, and working with key stakeholders across B2B Marketing, Global Ads and Wolt Ads Strategy teams.
