Mechanical Design Engineer - Production Machines, Lund

We are looking for a Mechanical Design Engineer to join the Machine Mechanics team within Production Systems Hardware, part of the Production Engineering & Technologies department at Axis in Lund. In this role, you will design robust, cost-efficient mechanical solutions for the unique test and production systems used to manufacture Axis products. Who is your future team? At Production Systems Hardware, we develop the hardware for the machines that manufacture Axis products. We take solutions from initial concept all the way to fully tested and verified production equipment. The Machine Mechanics team consists of four dedicated mechanical engineers who specialize in developing mechanical parts for the production systems used on Axis production lines. What you'll do here as Mechanical Design Engineer In the Machine Mechanics team, you will design, develop and deliver high-quality, robust and cost-efficient mechanical solutions for Axis' unique test and production systems. You will work from concept to verified product in cross-functional projects. The role involves a wide range of interfaces and gives you the opportunity to build knowledge across different technology areas. In this role, you will: Design, develop and improve mechanical solutions using the Creo 3D CAD system and the Windchill PDM system Take an active part in development projects, with a focus on simplicity, scalability, repeatability and quality, to create the best possible production setup for Axis products Work from concept to verified product in cross-functional projects The role may include travel, mainly to our contract manufacturers around the world, for approximately 15 days a year. Who are you? You are structured and driven, with a natural commitment to seeing your deliverables through to completion. You enjoy working cross-functionally, you have an open mindset, and you help build a creative environment around you. You are curious and innovative, and you enjoy communicating with and influencing others, especially when it comes to production engineering. If that sounds like you, we believe this is the place for you. We'd love to hear that you have: A bachelor's degree or higher in mechanical engineering At least 2 years' relevant experience of product development Experience of 3D CAD, ideally Creo Experience of 3D printing and of designing parts specifically for 3D printing Very good communication skills in English Communication skills in Swedish Bonus points if you have any combination of: Design experience from mechatronics and automation Experience of designing and producing moving mechanics, and of designing aluminum parts for CNC machining International production experience, or experience of developing production equipment Experience of optics or metrology What Axis has to offer We are a world leader in network video, where cutting-edge technology meets global impact. Here, you'll contribute to meaningful projects that shape the future of security and surveillance - developing solutions used worldwide. As a fast-growing company, we offer exciting career opportunities. You'll grow professionally through continuous learning, supported by a collaborative team that values creativity, innovation, and work-life balance. Our Lund HQ Campus, including the impressive Grenden building, offers a dynamic environment with spaces crafted to encourage collaboration, whether through informal "fika" chats, formal meetings, or after-hours activities.
