We’re looking for an FP&A Apprentice to join our team in Paris at the beginning of January. Reporting to the FP&A Director, you will be an integral part of the finance department. What you will do: Financial Planning & Analysis: Drive efficiency across our budgeting, forecasting, and month-end variance analysis. Month-End Analysis: Support the month-end close process by analyzing departmental financial performance against forecasts. Reporting & Dashboards: Help build and maintain financial reports and dashboards in Pigment to enhance executive decision-making. Business Case & Ad-Hoc Analysis: Conduct ad-hoc financial modeling (e.g., SaaS stack reviews, hosting cost analysis) to evaluate strategic initiatives. Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner with R&D, Product, Legal, and IT teams to gather data, insights, and improve overall financial oversight. Tooling & Process Optimization: Support the rollout of revenue analysis tools and help streamline planning workflows. Documentation & Knowledge Sharing: Assist in documenting FP&A processes What you’ll need to succeed: Education: Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree or certification in Finance or related field. Analytical Skills: Strong problem-solving ability and attention to detail. Technical Skills: Proficiency in Excel (pivot tables, VLOOKUP, etc.) and PowerPoint. Familiarity with financial software (e.g., SAP, Oracle, Anaplan) is a plus. Communication: Strong verbal and written communication skills. Ability to convey complex data in simple terms. Work Ethic: Eager to learn, proactive, and able to manage multiple tasks in a fast-paced environment. Team Player: Comfortable working collaboratively and building relationships with stakeholders.
