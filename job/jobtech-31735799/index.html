At ABB, we help industries run leaner and cleaner—and every person here makes that happen. You’ll be empowered to lead, supported to grow, and proud of the impact we create together. Join us and help run what runs the world. This position reports to: Cluster Manager Northern Europe - BL Mining and Materials. Your Role and Responsibilities As Operations Manager, you will lead the full spectrum of operational and project execution activities across one or more local units. This includes overseeing project management, engineering, manufacturing, logistics, procurement, and continuous improvement initiatives. You will ensure that operations and projects are executed in alignment with company procedures, cybersecurity standards, contract specifications, and health, safety, and environmental (HSE) requirements. Your leadership will drive strategic alignment between operational strategies and broader business goals, foster collaboration and innovation, and promote a culture of excellence. You will be responsible for team development, stakeholder engagement, and delivering measurable results across both operational and project portfolios. This role demands strategic oversight, hands-on problem-solving, and a commitment to safety, integrity, and performance. To succeed in this role, you bring a strong background in operations and project leadership, strategic thinking, and a collaborative management style. You are passionate about building high-performing teams, driving continuous improvement, and ensuring customer satisfaction through operational efficiency and project excellence. Key responsibilities include: Developing and executing business action plans aligned with strategic targets and global/regional guidance Preparing and managing annual budgets, addressing cost-related issues proactively Driving operational and project performance through programs, goals, and metrics Supervising project execution and performance reviews to ensure contract fulfillment and timely delivery Ensuring customer expectations are met across quality, service levels, and lead times Promoting continuous improvement methodologies and behaviors across teams and project execution Supporting Sales teams with operational insights to enhance customer solutions Upholding compliance with health, safety, environmental, cybersecurity, and integrity standards Ensuring consistent management of the project portfolio in line with company policies. Building and maintaining strong relationships with internal and external stakeholders Coaching and developing high-performing cross-functional teams aligned with business objectives Identifying and supporting recovery efforts for troubled projects to prevent performance deterioration Qualifications for the Role Proven experience in operations management and project leadership across manufacturing and engineering. Strong understanding of project planning, budgeting, financial tracking, and performance metrics Demonstrated ability to lead cross-functional teams and foster collaboration Solid knowledge of contract management, claims handling, and procurement processes Expertise in Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) standards and implementation Experience implementing continuous improvement initiatives.
