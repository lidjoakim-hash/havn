TL;DR You're a communications professional who can plan and execute the work that advances Lovable's objectives across European media and policy environments, making sure our voice is heard on the issues that matter most. You'll pair sharp corporate communications with real policy fluency — working closely with internal teams and external partners to help media, policymakers, and other stakeholders understand Lovable's story and perspective on the issues that shape the environment we operate in. This is a balanced role, equal parts corporate communicator and policy operator. You'll report to the European Communications and Public Affairs Lead. WHY LOVABLE Lovable is the software creation platform that gives people the power to act on the problems closest to them. For decades, turning an idea into software required so much capital, technical fluency, and time that many ideas never came to life. Lovable is the counterargument: a platform for all people with ideas, ambition, and problems worth solving. From solopreneurs to small business owners to teams at companies like Adidas and Zendesk, people have built over 60 million projects on Lovable since its launch in November 2024. And we’re just getting started. We’re building a generational company from Stockholm, with growing teams in London, Boston, New York, and San Francisco. Our team is small, talent-dense, and moving quickly, with a culture rooted in extreme ownership, high velocity, and low-ego collaboration. We look for people who care deeply, ship fast, and are eager to make a dent in the world. Lovable is one of TIME’s 100 Most Influential Companies and has been recognized on the Forbes AI 50 and CNBC Disruptor 50, reflecting our momentum as one of Europe’s fastest-growing AI companies and one of the most ambitious places to build in this next era of software. WHAT YOU'LL DO This role makes sure Lovable has a credible, constructive voice in the media and rooms that matter — building a presence worth listening to and developing clear positions that reflect what Lovable actually builds and believes. Strengthen Lovable’s corporate narrative in Europe. Company news, employer brand, partnerships, and the milestones that build credibility over time. You’ll help shape the plan, draft the materials, manage media relationships, and make sure each moment lands with European audiences. Shape Lovable’s voice in the European conversation. Statements, op-eds and media commentary that position Lovable as a credible voice on AI, competitiveness, and building a global company from Europe — including on the policy issues that shape our environment. Advocate Lovable's positions on key policy files. You'll build and run our engagement with policy representatives across EU and Swedish institutions. You'll monitor the files that affect us, develop positions and shape messaging that reflects the company's reality and ambitions. Build coalitions and work through industry. You'll represent Lovable in trade associations and industry groups, finding shared causes that move issues forward. Act as a comms and policy partner to our cross-functional team. You'll work closely with the communications and cross-functional teams in preparing for engagements with policymakers, organizations, and press, strengthening the company’s voice in the European AI debate. WHO YOU ARE You've worked in environments where media and policy meet. You have experience from both policy work and corporate communications, and you're equally comfortable speaking to policymakers and media alike. You understand policy and can communicate it. That might mean experience from an EU institution, a trade association, a think tank, or a public affairs firm. Wherever you've been, you know how a file moves from idea to law, and you can read the politics, formal and informal, that determine what actually happens.
