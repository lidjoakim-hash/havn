About us: Founded in Copenhagen, Denmark, in 2014, Templafy’s success is built by our 60+ employee nationalities found at offices around the world. We believe that when people feel valued, heard, and empowered, they perform at their best—creating a win for all. Our unique product and dedication to innovation and excellence have raised over $200 million in funding from top investors like Insight Venture Partners, Seed Capital, Dawn Capital, Damgaard Company and Golub Capital. Role Overview The Sales Executive, EMEA is responsible for developing new enterprise relationships and helping customers realise meaningful value from Templafy’s Agentic Solutions. Based in our Copenhagen office, you will work with large organisations across EMEA to understand how they create business-critical documents and identify opportunities to make those processes faster, more accurate and easier to govern. You will lead complex enterprise sales processes from initial outreach, engagement through to commercial agreement, working closely with customers and Templafy specialists to build a clear business case and demonstrate how Templafy can support document workflows, brand governance and enterprise AI adoption. Key Responsibilities • Build and develop a high-quality pipeline of new enterprise opportunities across EMEA. • Run new-business sales cycles from initial outreach, engagement and discovery through to negotiation and close. • Understand customers’ business priorities and identify opportunities to accelerate important document workflows, improve consistency and reduce unnecessary manual effort. • Help customers quantify the value of improving how documents are created, governed and maintained across their organisation. • Develop compelling business cases that connect Templafy’s capabilities to measurable customer outcomes. • Engage senior business and technology stakeholders on the opportunities and challenges created by AI, including adoption, governance, scalability and reliable enterprise outcomes. • Work closely with pre-Sales and other specialists to bring relevant customer use cases to life and demonstrate value using real-world workflows. • Build strong, trusted relationships across complex stakeholder groups and maintain disciplined pipeline management, qualification and forecasting. Experience • 5+ years of enterprise SaaS or software sales experience, with a strong focus on new business. • A track record of achieving sales targets and winning complex, enterprise opportunities. • Experience building pipeline as well as developing opportunities generated through Marketing, partners and other channels. • Experience working with senior business and technology stakeholders within large organisations. • Strong experience in consultative, value-based enterprise selling. • Experience selling across EMEA is advantageous.
