GL Accountant: Adyen is looking for a General Ledger Accountant to join our Group Finance team in Amsterdam. In this role, you aren’t just "processing numbers" , you are the engine behind our Record-to-Report (R2R) cycle for Operational Expenses. You will act as the critical link between business operations and financial data, ensuring that as Adyen scales, our financial integrity remains rock-solid. You will move beyond simple data entry to maintain a holistic "risk mindset," proactively identifying vulnerabilities and anomalies before they ever hit a report. Being part of a centralized Finance Operations team means you’ll be working in the engine room of a listed financial institution, where your work directly impacts our global trial balance. What you’ll do • R2R Ownership: Act as the detail layer for Operational Expenses, owning the accuracy of GL bookings and ensuring a clean Trial Balance. • Accounting Control: Apply rigorous controls to the P2P and R2R cycle. You’ll validate data flowing from FinOps, manage supplier accruals, and reconcile prepaid assets. • High-Value Oversight: Act as the financial gatekeeper for high-value transactions, performing due diligence to ensure accuracy and relieving Financial Controllers of transactional approvals. • Risk & Anomaly Detection: Regularly review the full ledger to detect trends or vulnerabilities, acting as the first line of defense for data integrity. • Asset & Lease Management: Manage the Fixed Asset Register, including capitalization, depreciation, and executing lease accounting (IFRS 16) entries. • Intercompany & Reporting: Manage intercompany bookings and reconciliations, perform month-end variance analysis to validate OpEx trends, and support internal/external audits. Who you are • Experienced: You have a solid background with at least three years of experience in GL accounting, specifically within P2P cycles and R2R processes. • Detail-Oriented & Analytical: You are passionate about balance sheet integrity and "getting it right the first time. • Collaborative: You enjoy working with multiple stakeholders, from FinOps to various stakeholders such as Controllers and Tax lawyers, to ensure smooth financial reporting. • Proactive: You question the status quo and look for ways to automate or improve processes as we grow. • Adaptable: You can manage multiple projects, from IFRS 16 lease entries to intercompany reconciliations, without losing focus.
