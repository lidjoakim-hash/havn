Platform Product Manager at Husqvarna Group

Husqvarna AB · Jönköping, Sweden

💰 ca. 450 000–550 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 3.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Do you want to run a platform as a product, with developers as your customers? Our mission is to enable our DevOps teams to focus on building great applications by providing a shared internal developer platform, tools, templates and ways of working that simplify the entire software development lifecycle. Today, we support more than 20 teams across all three divisions, combining hands-on engineering with coaching, training and technical enablement. Ultimately, our goal is simple: remove complexity, create great developer experiences and enable teams to deliver secure, high-quality software faster. Role Description CORE Platform gives our DevOps teams a paved road to build, test, deploy and observe software safely and efficiently. Our ambition is to create a platform that teams choose because it makes their work easier, not simply because they have to use it. As Platform Product Manager, you will treat the platform as a product, with developers as your customers. You will stay close to our DevOps teams, understand their needs and use feedback and data to shape priorities, set goals and build a roadmap focused on outcomes rather than output. Working closely with domain leads and platform engineers, you will help ensure CORE Platform feels like one coherent developer experience rather than a collection of tools. You will connect technical platform development with the wider needs of Husqvarna Group, helping us invest where we can create the greatest value for both developers and the business. Looking ahead, we are also preparing for AI agents to become platform users alongside developers. You will help shape what this means for the platform and how it influences our future priorities. What you will do: Shape the platform strategy and roadmap based on user needs, business priorities and measurable outcomes. Work closely with developers to understand their needs, test ideas early and create effective feedback loops. Translate developer and business needs into clear platform priorities. Define and follow up on goals and metrics around adoption, developer experience and platform value. Work with domain leads and platform engineers to create a consistent and coherent platform experience. Continuously evaluate whether the platform is solving the right problems and be ready to evolve, start or stop initiatives based on data and feedback. Connect platform development to the wider business context and ensure investments create meaningful value for Husqvarna Group. Your profile Beyond your experience, you are genuinely curious about your users and approach product development with an outcome-focused and adaptable mindset. You understand that a platform is never finished and are comfortable changing direction as user needs, business priorities and technology evolve. You see the bigger picture and can balance the needs of developers with the value we want to create for the business. You are confident in setting priorities and saying no when needed, while being able to clearly explain the reasoning behind your decisions, regardless of who you are speaking with. You are also curious about the opportunities AI brings to software development, while maintaining a pragmatic view of where it creates real value. Most importantly, you are a humble and collaborative team player who enjoys working with others, brings a sense of humour and cares about succeeding together. We believe you bring: 5+ years of experience working in or closely with software engineering. A good understanding of the software development lifecycle and DevOps, including how software is built, deployed and operated. Experience in product management, ideally within a technical or internal product environment. A strong product mindset, with the ability to translate user and business needs into priorities and measurable outcomes. Experience defining and using metrics to understand adoption, user experience and the value a product delivers. Professional proficien
