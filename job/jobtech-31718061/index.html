Are you a structured and detail-oriented Accounts Payable Specialist with a passion for order, structure, and efficiency? Do you have experience in accounts payable within production, warehouse, or logistics and thrive in a dynamic, fast-paced environment? Then this might be the right challenge for you! About the position Perido is looking for an Accounts Payable Specialist on behalf of our client, an industrial enterprise driving large-scale initiatives within green energy and manufacturing. The position is located in Boden, with the possibility of working remotely two days a week. Your daily tasks In the role of AP Specialist, you will work in a fast-paced environment where you will be responsible for all parts of the accounts payable process with high accuracy and compliance. These are the main focus tasks: Manage and drive the accounts payable (AP) process with a focus on accuracy and adherence to internal controls Work proactively to efficiently and accurately handle and correct historical transactions Maintain order and structure in AP processes and ensure clear documentation Adapt to a changing business environment and contribute with problem-solving skills and flexibility Create stability, focus, and a calm, structured work atmosphere even under high pressure Your characteristics We are looking for someone who is highly structured, detail-oriented, and passionate about order and efficiency. You are a flexible thinker who understands the importance of correcting historical transactions and easily adapts to changing business needs. You thrive in a high-tempo, energetic environment where you maintain your focus and contribute calm and stability even in high-pressure situations. Is this a challenge you would like to take on? We look forward to reading your application, welcome with your application today! Qualifications: Experience working with accounts payable within production, warehouse, or logistics Fluent in English (written and spoken) Meritorious: Experience in SAP or Medius is highly advantageous Contract type and hours Full-time, consulting assignment until 2027-11-02. About Perido Why is our company named Perido? The name comes from the Latin word Peridoneus, which means 'well-matched'. Perido is a consulting and recruitment company specializing in the white-collar sector, dedicated to helping you take the next step in your career. Our vision is to ensure that everyone thrives and feels engaged at work. We have grown steadily since our founding in 2003 and now have over 1,500 employees across the country, including around fifty internal staff at our headquarters in Stockholm and Gothenburg. As a Perido consultant, you’ll play a key role in our organization, growing alongside us while strengthening our clients' businesses with your skills.
