SUPO, where required) WHY THIS ROLE MATTERS: As Director of MAITE Finland, you’ll scale ICEYE’s production to 130+ satellites a year. MAITE Finland (Manufacturing and Assembly, Integration and Test) covers everything between incoming material and a finished satellite on the launch manifest. That means subsystem production for ICEYE’s sites around the world, SAR satellite integration and validation, and the engineering, stock management, planning and smart manufacturing teams that make both work. You will own it all as one integrated system. This is one of the most consequential leadership roles at ICEYE. You will be accountable for taking production from today’s rate to that goal while keeping the quality and discipline that space hardware demands. We are looking for a leader who can see the gap between where we are and world-class manufacturing, and has the vision and leadership depth to close it through other leaders. WHO WE ARE ICEYE is the world leader in sovereign intelligence from space. We deliver persistent monitoring capabilities to detect and respond to changes in any location on Earth. ICEYE owns the world's largest and most advanced SAR (synthetic aperture radar) satellite constellation. To our customers we provide intelligence with unmatched quality, latency and revisit times, in any weather, day or night. To governments who choose to operate their own constellation we provide this proven capability as a sovereign system. ICEYE-built constellations serve customers in defence and intelligence, environmental monitoring, insurance and emergency management. We enable fast decisions that contribute to a safer future. Founded and headquartered in Finland, ICEYE operates globally with over 1000 employees across Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.   YOUR DAY-TO-DAY RESPONSIBILITIES   - Own the full output of MAITE Finland, from subsystem production through SAR satellite integration and validation to delivery to the launch manifest, with accountability at executive level. - Lead a leadership team of managers and function heads across production, stock management, planning, quality control and the production facility, setting clear goals and developing them into strong leaders. - Design the organisation for the next stage of growth, including structure, roles, spans of control and succession, so that skills and capacity are in place ahead of demand. - Define and own the multi-year roadmap from 50 to 75 to 130+ satellites a year, covering capacity, footprint, automation, make or buy decisions, budget, headcount and capital investment, with accountability for cost per satellite as well as output. - Own the production schedule and performance system (throughput, cycle time, yield, first-pass quality, on-time delivery and cost), with an operating rhythm of reviews and escalation that your leaders run, and make the hard trade-off calls between rate, quality and cost. - Lead the move to a pull-based, one-piece-flow manufacturing system, embedding Lean principles, workplace organisation (6S), visual management and standard work as a daily operating discipline across all production lines. - Own quality, safety, security and compliance, from in-process quality gates to final acceptance, and sponsor root cause analysis and corrective action together with Engineering and Quality. - Align MAITE plans with Engineering, Supply Chain, Programs, the launch manifest and counterparts at other sites, and represent MAITE in senior leadership, customer and auditor discussions as a member of the Global Manufacturing Organisation leadership team.
