Here’s what this particular role covers. 🛠️ About our German Fleet Operations Team   German Fleet Operations is at the core of Wayve’s on-road testing and deployment. Our team ensures daily operations are conducted with the highest levels of safety and efficiency. We foster an inclusive working environment where Vehicle Safety Operators (VSOs) are encouraged to contribute their ideas and raise safety concerns freely. Shift Leads are a pivotal layer of management for this team, entrusted with maintaining operational excellence on every shift.   🧠 Your day-to-day   - Lead and supervise a team of Vehicle Safety Operators (VSOs) during your shift, providing guidance, support, and direction to ensure tasks are completed efficiently and effectively - Oversee the day-to-day operations within the shift, managing work schedules, assigning tasks, and monitoring performance to ensure company standards and KPIs are achieved - Line manage a group of VSOs, ensuring regular monthly face-to-face documented contact with each team member, fostering high performance and addressing any issues - Ensure all operations comply with company policies and legal regulations, promoting a safe work environment by enforcing safety protocols - Monitor the quality of on-road testing during your shift, addressing issues or discrepancies and escalating them to the Quality Assurance team - Serve as the initial ‘Operations Officer’ for on-road incidents, following company Major Incident Management Plan procedures - Prepare and submit shift reports, including performance metrics, incident reports, and any deviations from SOPs - Coordinate individual and collective training and development events with the Quality Assurance team - Identify operational problems and challenges, developing and implementing solutions to ensure minimal disruption - Serve as the primary point of contact for the operations team during your shift, ensuring effective communication between team members, management, and other departments - Drive continuous improvement by implementing process enhancements and best practices   🧩 What you’ll be working on   - Leading and developing a high-performing team of VSOs, ensuring operational success and safety - Driving adherence to safety and quality standards across all daily fleet activities - Managing incident responses and reporting accurately and efficiently - Supporting team development through coaching, regular feedback, and training coordination - Working collaboratively with Quality Assurance and other stakeholders to raise the bar on operational excellence   🙌 You should apply if   -
