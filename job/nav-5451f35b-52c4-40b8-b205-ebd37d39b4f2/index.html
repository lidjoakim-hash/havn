Terminalworkers for Oslo Airport (OSL)

Anonym arbeidsgiver · Gardermoen, Norway

Logent is currently looking for reliable and hardworking terminal workers to join our team at Oslo Airport Gardermoen, with valid background check. This is a hands-on role involving physical work, cargo handling and typical terminal operations. We are looking for people who enjoy staying active, work efficiently and enjoy being part of a social group. And who couid start up now or later in November What you will do in this job • Manual handling, loading and unloading of goods • Breaking down pallets and separating goods • Re-palletising and repacking shipments for road transport  • Keeping the work area organised and safe Working hours • Two shift rotation, Monday to Friday 08:00–16:00 / 12:00–20:00 • You must be available working some weekends (15:00-23:00) Who are we looking for? We believe you will succeed in this role if you: • Are in good physical shape and comfortable with lifting and manual goods handling • Work efficiently and have a good working pace • Are reliable and responsible • Have a positive attitude and help your teammates • Are available for start up at a short-notice Language requirements • Good English skills are required • Basic Norwegian is an advantage, but not a requirement. Important – Background Check Due to airport security regulations, all candidates starting now must have passed the required background check before starting work. We offer • An active and varied working day • A good working environment with a solid team • Valuable experience within airport logistics and terminal operations • Opportunity for an early start Interested? Does this sound like something for you? Apply today! We review applications continuously and are looking to fill the positions as soon as possible. We look forward to hearing from you!
