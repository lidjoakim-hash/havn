Okta is looking for an experienced, self-motivated team player who will be a vital component for Okta’s sustainability (1) analytics and reporting, and (2) automation. This person will drive accurate and timely reporting and insights to meet the expectations of external stakeholders (customers, investors, and regulators) and internal stakeholders (executives, program leads, etc). This person will identify opportunities to automate data collection, analytics, reporting, and dashboards across various programs to streamline and simplify this work, and to enable our teams to scale to meet the moment and the growing stakeholder requirements. The role will drive impact as reporting is a customer business requirement, and data insights are foundational to building sustainability programs that drive business value and reduce environmental impacts. The ideal candidate for this role has 4+ years of data analytics, visualization, reporting, and automation experience, as well as sustainability, GHG accounting and carbon analytics experience. We are looking for highly motivated team players who have strong analytical skills, excellent attention to detail, and the ability to work cross functionally, as well as independently.
