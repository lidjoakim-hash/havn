The Community You'll Join The Homes team plays a key role in shaping the Airbnb stays experience. As a multidisciplinary team, we partner with Hosts to build high-quality, repeatable, commercially viable offerings that reflect our brand values. We're building something new: a dedicated Business Development function across EMEA, focused entirely on winning new professional supply for Airbnb.
