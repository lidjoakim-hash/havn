Merch that moves business needs a platform that moves just as fast. From scattered purchases without brand control, to the magic that happens when it all clicks: merch that makes a real impact, on one global platform customized for each client, as if they built it themselves. That’s what we’re building at Exakt. The platform is up and running, but this is only the beginning. Now comes the most advanced part, and we’re looking for an engineer with the drive and the skills to build it on a global scale. What you’ll do You’ll build and own our merch platform, full stack: TypeScript and React on the frontend, Node APIs in the middle and a MedusaJS headless commerce backend, all running on GCP. It’s live with our first clients, and the codebase is still young enough that your decisions will stick. From here, you’ll: Own the platform end to end, from architecture to checkout Refine and develop the product logic. One t-shirt can come in 62 colors and seven sizes, from several suppliers, production partners and locations. Find new ways to make it all run even more smoothly Take our client dashboard further, with the advanced features our clients didn’t know they needed, and even more transparency around orders, statistics and the environmental impact of what they buy Automate the manual work, on the platform and behind the scenes, so the team can spend more time on the creative work Set the technical direction with our founder, and help shape the team as we grow Who you are You enjoy figuring out what’s needed and making it happen without waiting to be asked. And you’re curious about the business behind the code, whether you’re shaping a feature or talking ideas through with a client, in English. You’re resourceful, and happy to use a smart existing solution instead of reinventing it You bring experience and an open mind. You know how to move fast, and you’re always curious about new ways of working You know when speed matters more than perfection, and you make sure the quick fix doesn’t become permanent You work AI-first, and take security and client data just as seriously You like people, and you’d rather be at the office with the team than on mute You’re comfortable across backend and frontend, and our designers take care of the design. Experience with e-commerce, Medusa or multi-tenant platforms is a plus, but not a must. Tech you’ll work with We’re guessing you already have the essentials. The limited editions are a bonus, and the rest you’ll pick up along the way.
