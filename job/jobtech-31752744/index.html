Job Description Are you passionate about creator culture, social-first content and the role creators play in shaping how brands connect with audiences? We’re looking for a Creator Marketing Lead, focusing on social and creative Strategy to help shape and elevate how we approach creator marketing across markets. In this role, you’ll combine the full scope of a Creator Marketing Lead with particular depth in social culture, creator-led marketing and creative strategy. You’ll bring a strong point of view on what makes creator content relevant, distinctive and effective, translating audience insights, cultural shifts and social behaviors into creator strategies and campaigns. You’ll work in Creator Marketing, under Creative Studios, work with local market teams and strategic functions to strengthen our creator marketing approach, ensuring our work is culturally relevant, creatively strong and connected to broader brand and business priorities. WHAT YOU’LL DO As Creator Marketing Lead, you’ll lead creator marketing across assigned markets while championing social-first and creative strategic thinking within the wider Creator Marketing team. You’ll translate strategic priorities and audience insights into clear creator recommendations, campaign approaches and activation plans, while supporting teams in delivering high-quality creator work at scale. You will: Shape quarterly creator marketing strategies, priorities and budget allocation across assigned markets or areas. Work closely with markets to identify opportunities and translate them into relevant creator activation plans. Shape creator recommendations, briefs, campaign approaches and creative direction through a social-first lens. Bring audience insights, creator culture, platform behaviours and emerging content trends into strategic and creative decision-making. Lead and guide Creator Marketing Specialists and Coordinators in campaign execution and delivery. Oversee campaign execution, budget management, performance and stakeholder alignment across assigned initiatives. Partner closely with the Creative Strategist and other strategic functions across Creative Studios to ensure creator initiatives are rooted in audience insight, cultural relevance and strong creative direction. Ensure creator marketing initiatives connect with broader brand, creative and business objectives while remaining authentic to creators and their audiences. Identify emerging creator, platform and cultural opportunities and translate them into actionable recommendations for the team. Develop and evolve creator marketing best practices, frameworks and ways of working that strengthen quality and consistency across markets. Act as a subject matter expert for social-first creator marketing, continuously helping evolve and future-proof our approach. WHO YOU ARE We’re looking for an experienced creator marketing professional who combines strategic thinking with a strong creative instinct and a deep understanding of social culture. You understand the creator landscape beyond individual campaigns. You’re curious about how audiences behave, how creator and platform cultures evolve and why certain ideas resonate. You can turn those insights into clear strategies, creative recommendations and relevant creator activations. At the same time, you have the operational and leadership capabilities required of a Creator Marketing Lead. You’re comfortable setting direction, managing priorities and budgets, guiding teams and influencing stakeholders across functions and markets. We are looking for people with: Extensive experience within creator or influencer marketing, with a strong track record of developing and leading creator marketing initiatives at scale. Deep understanding of social platforms, creator culture, audience behaviours and evolving content trends.
