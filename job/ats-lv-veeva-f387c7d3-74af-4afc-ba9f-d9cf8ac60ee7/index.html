The Role At Veeva Link, we're building the intelligence layer for life sciences, creating connected data applications that accelerate drug development and significantly improve patient outcomes. Our core belief: combining the highest quality data with state-of-the-art software delivers immense value. In your role as the Product Manager for Link Key People data, you take ownership sourcing, managing and maintaining data from health care professionals. That ranges from what podcasts they host to what scientific publication they have made. You will use data pipelines, AI and human oversight to provide high quality and compliant data. This is key data that builds valuable connections between people in life science. Leading to better progress for all humanity. You will collaborate with our data engineering, data product, and data operations teams. You will make sure our data is high quality and the production tools enable efficient execution. Product excellence is your main goal.
