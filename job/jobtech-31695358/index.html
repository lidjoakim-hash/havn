We are looking for a new Lead Technician based at our site in Cloud, Overturingen! A Snapshot of Your Day As our Lead Technician, you will be responsible for the daily leadership and supervision of service technicians, ensuring efficient planning and execution of service, operations, and warranty activities for turbines within your service area. You will build a long-term perspective with the team to ensure that Siemens Gamesa consistently delivers high performance and value to our customers. How You’ll Make an Impact Perform tasks delegated by the responsible Operations Manager. Coordinate, plan, and execute service, operational, and support activities for the assigned site or travelling technician team, including resource planning, vacation scheduling, and work rosters in collaboration with central planning. Ensure that safety is always the top priority, and that all technicians have the required training, safety equipment, work instructions, and risk assessments before commencing any work. Continuously follow up on incidents and drive improvements by implementing corrective actions and preventive measures. Ensure that site personnel and subcontractors are informed of and comply with agreed EHS procedures and the site-specific EHS plan. Utilize local and regional technical support resources to resolve technical issues on site. Take corrective actions, address deficiencies, and drive continuous improvements within the service teams. Be responsible for the ongoing development and improvement of productivity, KPIs, and other site and contract objectives, supported by the Operations Manager. Provide monthly reporting to customers and lead monthly customer meetings with support from the Operations Manager. Build long-term relationships with customers as the primary site contact and collaborate closely with suppliers to establish lasting partnerships. Manage operations under varying conditions and adjust work and resource planning to meet contractual obligations, optimize wind farm performance, and maximize team productivity. Lead the team in maintaining accurate and up-to-date documentation and records. Handle site administration, including reporting, order management, record keeping, and related administrative responsibilities.  What You Bring A passion for renewable energy and a commitment to driving the energy transition. The ideal candidate has a technical or vocational education, preferably with an electrical and/or mechanical background and relevant experience. You have several years of experience in a leadership, supervisory, or team lead role. In addition, you will: Demonstrate excellent planning and organizational skills. Have a technical background in electrical, mechanical, or hydraulic systems. Possess experience as a supervisor, team leader, or similar. Thrive in a fast-paced environment and preferably have experience within the wind energy sector. Be able to work independently as well as collaboratively within a team. Have strong English communication skills, both written and spoken. Swedish is considered a significant advantage. Hold a valid Category B driving licence. Be physically fit and comfortable working at heights. A medical examination is required, including the Swedish “Mast and Pole Certificate” (Mast- och Stolpintyg). Please note that if offered the position, you will be required to successfully complete a medical examination and a drug and alcohol test before commencing employment.   Who is Siemens Energy? Siemens Gamesa is part of Siemens Energy, a global leader in energy technology with a rich legacy of innovation spanning more than 150 years. Together, we are committed to making sustainable, reliable, and affordable energy a reality by pushing the boundaries of what is possible.
