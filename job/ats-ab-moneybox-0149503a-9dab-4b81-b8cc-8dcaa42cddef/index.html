About Moneybox At Moneybox, our mission is to give everyone the means to get more out of life. We're guided by our belief that wealth isn't about the money, it's about the means to more - more freedom, opportunities, possibilities, and peace of mind. Moneybox is an award-winning wealth management platform, helping over one and a half million people build wealth throughout their lives, whether they’re saving and investing, buying their first home, or planning for retirement.   Job Brief   We’re looking for a Compliance Advisory Analyst to join our Risk & Compliance team and help provide effective second-line advice, oversight and constructive challenge across Moneybox. Working closely with Product, Marketing, Customer Care and other teams, you’ll assess regulatory risks, support new propositions and changes, and help ensure good customer outcomes are embedded in decisions. You’ll own a varied portfolio of day-to-day advisory work, escalating complex or material matters appropriately, and help develop a proportionate, well-documented compliance advisory function. Given Moneybox's range of products, we would particularly welcome candidates with experience in pensions, retirement or investment products. What you'll do - Working closely with colleagues across Risk & Compliance and the wider business, you will: - Provide clear, timely and practical second-line compliance advice on business queries, product changes, customer journeys and operational proposals, identifying regulatory risks and proportionate mitigations. - Review and challenge financial promotions and customer communications across channels, applying relevant FCA requirements and internal approval standards. - Support product governance and change approval processes, including reviewing fair value assessments, target market assessments, Consumer Duty considerations and associated governance papers. - Assess customer outcome risks, including foreseeable harm, customer understanding, support needs and the treatment of vulnerable customers, and challenge proposals where necessary. - Provide second-line input into sensitive or complex complaints and Financial Ombudsman Service matters, including regulatory interpretation, root-cause themes and potential wider customer impact. - Prepare concise written compliance considerations, risk assessments, recommendations and records of advice that evidence the rationale for decisions and escalation. - Build constructive relationships with Product, Marketing, Customer Care, Operations and other stakeholders, offering solutions-focused challenge while maintaining second-line independence. - Contribute to horizon scanning by assessing regulatory developments, explaining their potential business impact and supporting the tracking of actions and owners. - Support the effective operation of compliance governance forums, including preparing papers, recording challenges and following up on agreed actions. - Identify recurring advisory themes, emerging risks and opportunities to improve guidance, controls and ways of working; collaborate with Compliance Monitoring and other specialists where appropriate. - Provide second-line advisory support across Moneybox's savings, investment and retirement propositions, including relevant product changes, customer journeys and regulatory considerations.
