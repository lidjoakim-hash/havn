Department Manager for Land Use and Community Relations Boliden is a high-tech metals company with its own mines and smelters that works long-term to guarantee society's access to base and precious metals. For a century, our work has been part of the value chains that have shaped modern society. At Boliden, we know that the metals we mine and make available will continue to play a crucial role in the future – what matters is how we produce them. Become a part of us and drive change for generations to come. Your opportunity Lead the stewardship of Boliden Mines’ land and forest holdings and secure the land our mines need to operate and develop over the long term. Boliden’s holdings include approximately 17,000 hectares of forest land, providing a substantial platform for long-term forest management and biodiversity initiatives. As Head of Land & Forest, you will lead and develop a specialist team whose work spans forest management and harvesting, land acquisition and sales, biodiversity, community relations and geographic information systems (GIS). You will work with Boliden Mines business units and sites, landowners, public authorities, Sámi communities and other local stakeholders to build the relationships that underpin our social licence to operate. You will set priorities for how Boliden manages its forests and plans for future land needs. That means weighing the long-term value of our forest holdings alongside operational needs, biodiversity and competing uses of land. Who you will work with You will lead and support a geographically diverse team of seven specialists based in Boliden, Gällivare, and Stockholm, including three Forest & Land Coordinators, two Social Sustainability Coordinators, one GIS Strategist, and one Biodiversity Specialist. You will also collaborate with internal teams, business units, landowners, public authorities, consultants and community stakeholders. You will also coordinate work across sites and represent Boliden in external forums where land, biodiversity and community matters are discussed. The position is placed in Boliden, you report to the Director of Sustainability for Boliden Mines What you will do Your main responsibilities include: Lead Boliden Mines’ work to secure the land our mines need to operate and develop. Work with the sites to identify future land needs early and translate them into plans, priorities and agreements. Ensure staffing, competence development, and health, safety and environmental matters within the team. Set the direction for managing approximately 17,000 hectares of forest land and the wider land and property portfolio. Use financial and operational analysis to weigh the costs, risks and long-term value of different options, from land acquisition and use to forestry and divestment. Translate Boliden’s biodiversity commitments into practical priorities for forest management and land-use planning, working with your team and the mine sites. What you bring You have led and developed specialist teams and bring substantial experience in forest management. You have made decisions that balance operational, commercial and environmental priorities, and you know how to turn long-term plans into work your team can deliver. A degree in forestry, such as a Swedish Jägmästarexamen or Skogsmästarexamen, or equivalent expertise gained through substantial relevant experience. Experience in long-term forest planning and operational forestry, with the ability to assess the financial implications of land and forest management decisions. Experience negotiating agreements with landowners or other external parties and building trust where land-use interests differ. A leadership style that gives specialists clear direction, supports their development and helps them work across disciplines and sites.
