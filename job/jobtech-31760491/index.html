Experienced Embedded Software Team Lead

Want to influence the bigger picture? At Saab, you can realise amazing innovations that keep people and societies safe. Together, we can truly make an impact - and your part matters. Your role We are looking for two Team Leaders to join our embedded software organization. One role focuses on Embedded Software Development and the other on Test Development. Both positions combine team leadership with hands-on technical work. You will lead a team of developers, testers, and requirements analysts working on advanced, safety-critical systems. Alongside your leadership responsibilities, you will actively contribute to the development work, either through embedded software development in C/C++ or test development and automation in Python. Your responsibilities will include: Lead and support the team's daily work, planning, and delivery. Collaborate with internal and external stakeholders to drive progress and achieve team objectives. Break down requirements and project goals into manageable work packages. Work closely with hardware and software teams to ensure successful system integration. Contribute hands-on through embedded software development in C/C++ or test development and automation in Python. Your profile We are looking for a humble and supportive leader who leads by example, drawing on your experience and technical expertise to guide and develop the team. You have strong communication and collaboration skills, enjoy building relationships with stakeholders, and combine a proactive mindset with excellent problem-solving abilities and attention to detail. To succeed in this role, you have: Proven experience as a Team Lead, Scrum Master, or Project Lead within an embedded software environment. Hands-on experience in embedded software or test development, preferably for safety-critical systems. Strong skills in C and Python, as well as experience with embedded systems, microcontrollers, and RTOS. Experience with Git/Dimensions and Agile development methodologies such as Scrum. Relevant degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related field. It is considered a merit if you have experience from safety-critical software development within industries such as aerospace, med-tech, or defence. Experience with security-focused development practices, embedded Linux environments, and model-based development tools such as MATLAB/Simulink is also valuable. Knowledge of hardware description languages, including VHDL or Verilog, will be seen as an advantage. The position may require a security clearance. If so, you must undergo and pass security vetting, including a records check, in accordance with applicable protective security regulations before commencing employment. What you will be a part of Explore a wealth of possibilities. Take on challenges, create smart inventions, and grow beyond. This is a place for curious minds, brave pioneers, and everyone in between. Together, we achieve the extraordinary, each bringing our unique perspectives. Your part matters. Saab is a leading defense and security company with an enduring purpose, to help nations keep their people and society safe. Empowered by talented people, Saab constantly pushes the boundaries of technology to create a safer and more sustainable world. Saab designs, manufactures and maintains advanced systems in aeronautics, weapons, command and control, sensors and underwater systems. Saab is headquartered in Sweden. It has major operations all over the world and is part of the domestic defense capability of several nations. Read more about us here.
