Since landing in EMEA in 2022, Airtable has built a full go-to-market function in the region and is now accelerating its next phase of growth. Our expansion includes a move to our fabulous new EMEA HQ at Borough Yards in London, alongside launching on-the-ground teams in France and Germany. Already, we power some of the world's leading FTSE 100, CAC 40, and DAX 40 brands—and we’re just getting started. You will work cross-functionally with our EMEA-based team of BDRs, Solutions Consulting, Services, Customer Success, Partnerships and Marketing to generate pipeline, map out solutions and ensure long-term success for new and existing customers.
