This is Adyen Adyen provides payments, data, and financial products in a single solution for customers like Meta, Uber, H&M, and Microsoft - making us the financial technology platform of choice. At Adyen, everything we do is engineered for ambition. For our teams, we create an environment with opportunities for our people to succeed, backed by the culture and support to ensure they are enabled to truly own their careers. We are motivated individuals who tackle unique technical challenges at scale and solve them as a team. Together, we deliver innovative and ethical solutions that help businesses achieve their ambitions faster. Partnerships at Adyen Over 40% of the deals that go live at Adyen involve a partner. Our five-year plan is to push that number well north of 70%. The Scaled Partnerships team builds and activates the wider partner ecosystem behind that goal: the long tail of tech and service partners, net new partners, and the upcoming partners of tomorrow. The role As a Scaled Partner Manager, you own and grow a portfolio of partners across your assigned markets and partner segments. You help build high-impact, long-tail partner ecosystems that generate a steady flow of referrals and pipeline. You ensure every partner is confidently activated and supported throughout their journey, from qualification and onboarding to enablement, go-to-market, support and growth. You use repeatable approaches and data to focus your time where it matters most. You’re part of one global Scaled team, shaping the playbooks and tools used across regions. You’ll work closely with Partnerships, Marketing, Partner Engineering and local commercial teams to deliver a consistent partner experience across markets. What you’ll do • Own and grow a portfolio of partners, with France as your primary market focus, while supporting and developing other partner segments and opportunities across the wider EMEA ecosystem. • Qualify and recruit net new partners, including inbound requests from our partner sign up or from local Commercial teams • Run onboarding: partner agreement, portal and payouts set-up, and initial enablement • Drive adoption of the partner program: requirements, tiering, certifications, verified integrations and benefits • Drive partner referrals and partner-driven pipeline together with Sales and AMs • Run repeatable activation and reactivation campaigns with Marketing • Use dashboards and ecosystem reports to understand your portfolio, spot gaps and report on partner health • Test new models and ideas, and turn what works into playbooks other regions can reuse Who you are • You have 3+ years of experience in a commercial role, such as partnerships, account management or
