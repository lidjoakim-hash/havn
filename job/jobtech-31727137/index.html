Finance Manager for a growing manufacturing company - south of Malmö Are you a skilled finance executive? Would you like to take on a key role in a small and rapidly expanding manufacturing company? Do you also have an eye for details and do you like the idea of products being manufactured in Sweden and sold worldwide? Then this could be your next opportunity! Our client is an independent Swedish business unit (turn-over 150 MSEK) operating in an international business structure who develops and manufactures at their Swedish site in the Trelleborg area. The company is currently in an exciting development phase and is now looking for a Finance Manager who can take on responsibility for the finance function in Sweden. This is a broad and hands-on finance management role: you will run the month-end close and personally prepare project costing, over-time revenue recognition, inventory valuation, reconciliations and the Group reporting package, supervising one Finance Assistant. The role works day-to-day with the MD and Project Managers to keep project costs and margins accurate. You will play a key role in supporting the business with financial insight, investment analysis, and decision-making support that drives performance and long-term development. Key Responsibilities: Project Accounting and Costing Financial Reporting and Control Budgeting, Forecasting and Analysis Cash and Treasury Management (supervising, reviewing) Support improvement of financial systems and processes Ensure compliance with tax regulations and applicable financial standards Your Profile: You have a relevant degree in e.g. business administration, finance, or accounting and several years of experience in the field. You also have proven experience in a leadership role. You have strong communication skills in both Swedish and English, written and spoken. Advanced Excel skills and experience with ERP systems (preferably Microsoft Dynamics BC) is essential. You have a good understanding of US GAAP and Swedish statutory requirements. Experience from manufacturing or project-based industry is an advantage, as well as experience in a multinational group environment with cross-border reporting responsibilities. As a person, you are a self-starter with the ability to manage multiple priorities and meet demanding deadlines. You thrive in a dynamic environment where you can combine strategic thinking with practical implementation. You have a high level of integrity and are a good communicator with excellent leadership skills. About the Position: This is a permanent position starting as soon as possible. The role is based south of Malmö. We review applications on an ongoing basis, so we encourage you to submit your application in English as soon as possible via the application link. For more information about the role, please contact Karin Dahlin, Standby Workteam, +46 733 76 39 88. We look forward to your application!
