If Braze sounds like a place where you can thrive, we can’t wait to meet you. The best software companies don't grow through direct sales alone. They grow through ecosystems: networks of partners who extend their reach, deepen customer relationships, and deliver outcomes no single company can achieve alone. The Braze platinum-tier global delivery partners, Deloitte, WPP, Publicis, and Merkle, are each owned globally by a Global Partner Account Director who sets the strategy, holds the executive relationship, and is accountable for that partner's performance everywhere in the world. But no Global Partner Account Director can be in every market, every week - global strategy only works if someone with regional judgement is making it land locally. As Partner Development Director for EMEA, you own the regional execution of the global platinum partner strategy across every platinum-tier partner active in the region. You will take the global account plan each Global Partner Account Director (GPAD) has set for their partner and make it real across the markets in EMEA. You build the regional plan yourself and take it to the AVP of Delivery as a position to defend rather than a draft for steering, make most decisions in-region, and escalate only when something needs a global call. Your manager will be the AVP of Delivery. In this role, you will collaborate closely with the GPAD assigned to each platinum account, the AVP of Partner Sales for EMEA and regional Partner Sales Managers. What You'll Own • Regional platinum coverage – be the senior Braze relationship for every platinum-tier delivery partner operating in EMEA (Deloitte, WPP, Publicis, Merkle, and any future platinum partner). • Executing the global account plan – translate each GPAD's global strategy for their partner into what actually needs to happen across EMEA: which regional practice leads to engage, which markets to prioritise, where the partner doesn't have meaningful local delivery capability yet, and how the partnership shows up consistently in every market. • Certification and delivery quality in-region – work with Partner Services and the Global Learning and Enablement team to make sure EMEA-based consultants at each platinum partner are certified to the bar Braze sets, and that Partner-Led Delivery engagements in the region hold up. • Platinum activation on regional pipeline – partner with the AVP, Partner Sales and their Partner Sales Managers covering EMEA to get the right platinum partner into the right regional opportunity. • Signal back into the global strategy – surface what's happening in EMEA (market dynamics, delivery friction, a regional practice under-resourcing the partnership) to the relevant GPAD, so the global account plan is reflective of regional considerations. • First line of escalation in-region – when something goes wrong with a platinum partner in EMEA, you're the first call.
