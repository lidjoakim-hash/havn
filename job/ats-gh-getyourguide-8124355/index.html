Team mission The Business Operations & Strategy team at GetYourGuide is the engine behind our commercial growth — turning strategic ambition into operational reality. We work across the full supplier acquisition lifecycle, combining data-driven insight, process excellence, and cross-functional collaboration to ensure GetYourGuide offers the world’s most compelling inventory of travel experiences. From scaling our fastest-growing acquisition channels to optimising vendor partnerships, we move fast, think big, and hold ourselves accountable to measurable impact. Within Business Operations & Strategy, the Inside Sales mission team is responsible for the strategy and economics of our outsourced Inside Sales channel — our highest-velocity route to acquiring new suppliers and activities. As Senior Revenue Manager, you will own the strategic direction, financial performance, and scalability of this channel: building the forecasting and unit-economics framework that governs it, designing the systems and processes that drive its growth, and ensuring our vendor partnership operates as one well-governed lever within a much larger machine. Your mission You will define the strategy and economics behind GetYourGuide's fastest-growing supplier acquisition channel — building the models, forecasts, and processes that let us scale it predictably and profitably.
