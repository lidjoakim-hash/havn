Supply Chain & Sales Process Consultant – SAP ERP Transition

Sway Sourcing Sweden Aktiebolag · Göteborg, Sweden

💰 ca. 410 000–480 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 18.2.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

A client in Gothenburg is looking for a consultant to support its supply chain and sales teams during an ERP transition. You’ll translate business needs into clear requirements, recommend process improvements and help move priority requests forward while teams continue their day-to-day work. About the assignment You’ll work with business stakeholders, product owners and IT teams to understand what users need, assess how proposed changes would affect existing processes and systems, and agree on next steps. The assignment covers both the ERP transition and ongoing process development. What you’ll do Gather and document business requirements, and assess their impact on processes and systems. Recommend practical solutions in collaboration with supply chain, sales, planning and manufacturing teams. Refine IT requests by clarifying requirements, expected business value and stakeholder priorities. Help prioritise requests and coordinate delivery of high-priority changes. Train small groups of users on new processes, tools and system changes. Support project coordination, documentation and administration. What you’ll bring Familiarity with supply chain and sales processes and how SAP supports them. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with an organised approach and attention to detail. Strong collaboration skills and fluency in spoken and written English. Proficiency in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel and PowerPoint. Experience in supply chain support or data analysis, or hands-on SAP experience, is an advantage. Application & Start Date Start Date: 2026-11-30 End Date: 2027-04-30 Application Deadline: 2026-11-25 Location: Gothenburg. 4 days/week on-site. Selections and interviews are ongoing!
