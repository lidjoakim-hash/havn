Riot Games Communications team works across games, esports, and entertainment to tell compelling stories, build trust with our communities, and create meaningful moments for players around the world. In EMEA, the League of Legends Regional Communications team brings those stories to life across a diverse media landscape, working closely with global teams, regional stakeholders, local markets, and agency partners. As an EMEA Public Relations Strategist, you will work under the supervision of the EMEA Communications Strategists to support the coordination and execution of PR strategies and tactics for League of Legends across EMEA, with a particular focus on LoL Esports. Your scope will span both Tier 1 and Tier 2 ecosystems and their key moments throughout the year. Working closely with the EMEA League of Legends team, you will help translate communications strategies into high-quality, coordinated PR activations that resonate with media and players across the region. Beyond esports, you will also support the EMEA Communications team across the broader League of Legends ecosystem. This includes day-to-day press office operations, pan-EMEA outreach around major League of Legends IP beats, media targeting and pitching, press materials and copywriting, spokesperson preparation, and communications reporting. The role is designed for a communications professional who combines strong executional skills with excellent knowledge of the media landscape, and is comfortable operating at the center of a fast-moving regional press operation. This will be a 12 month contract role and is based in Berlin, Germany, and requires the candidate to be located in Berlin, this role is expected to commence in January 2027.
