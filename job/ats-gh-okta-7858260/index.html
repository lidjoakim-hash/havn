Okta is looking for a driven and observant Localization Project Coordinator to be a key engine for our team's efficiency and success. While you will manage projects from start to finish, this role is about more than just moving tasks along. We are seeking a curious and proactive individual who is passionate about not only executing localization workflows but actively improving them. You will be at the heart of our operations, with a unique opportunity to identify points of friction, propose meaningful changes, and grow into a process expert. What you'll be doing: • Manage the end-to-end lifecycle of localization projects, ensuring on-time delivery while actively identifying opportunities to streamline the process. • Actively identify, analyze, and troubleshoot recurring issues in our workflows, and collaborate with senior team members to propose and help implement effective solutions. • Master our Translation Management Systems (TMS), not only to manage projects but to leverage features that can increase efficiency and quality. • Serve as a primary point of contact for internal stakeholders from various departments, ensuring clear communication and a smooth collaboration process. • Prepare and process a variety of file types for translation, performing meticulous quality checks to ensure our standards are met before every delivery. • Help build our team's knowledge base by assisting with the creation and maintenance of process documentation, style guides, and glossaries. What you'll bring to the role: • Exceptional attention to detail and a highly organized, analytical mindset. You naturally notice when things are out of place and are driven to understand why. • A solid understanding of localization workflows and how they adapt for different content requirements. • Hands-on experience working with Translation Management Systems (TMS). • Outstanding communication skills and a collaborative spirit, with the ability to articulate problems and suggestions clearly. • A proactive, curious, and positive attitude. You have a strong desire to learn, a keen sense of when to ask questions, and the confidence to propose changes when you see a better way.
