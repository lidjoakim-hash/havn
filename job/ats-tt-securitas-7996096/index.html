AWS Infrastructure Engineer Location: United Kingdom Purpose Design, build, and operate AWS infrastructure that underpins key platforms and services as the organisation continues its transition to cloud. Work as a hands-on AWS engineer, focused on delivering secure, scalable, and highly available environments. Take ownership of components of the migration and operation of business-critical platforms and services within AWS. Major Responsibilities • Design, build, and maintain AWS infrastructure using infrastructure-as-code principles • Support production and non-production environments with a focus on availability, performance, and security • Deliver backend infrastructure operations including server management, updates, storage, failover, and logging • Build and maintain core AWS services across compute, networking, storage, and identity (e.g. EC2, S3, VPC, IAM, RDS, CloudWatch) • Collaborate with application, DevOps, and security teams to deliver resilient cloud solutions • Integrate infrastructure with CI/CD pipelines to enable reliable, repeatable deployments • Implement monitoring, logging, alerting, and observability practices • Investigate incidents, contribute to root cause analysis, and drive continuous improvement • Support security, compliance, and audit requirements within cloud environments, including regulated or security-sensitive contexts • Work with cloud partners and vendors, including AWS and third parties, on architecture reviews and optimisation • Automate infrastructure provisioning and operational processes to reduce manual effort • Document infrastructure, standards, and operational processes • Primary working hours aligned to European time zones. Participation in a global on-call rotation. What Success Looks Like • Deliver stable, secure, and scalable AWS environments that support business-critical platforms • Contribute to the successful migration and operation of core business platforms and services in AWS • Maintain strong system availability and performance across production environments • Identify and resolve infrastructure issues proactively, improving reliability over time • Collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams to deliver consistent, high-quality solutions • Establish reliable and repeatable infrastructure practices Education • Degree or equivalent experience in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field Experience & Knowledge • Hands-on experience with AWS in production environments • Strong knowledge of core AWS services such as VPC, IAM, EC2, containers, storage, and monitoring • Experience with infrastructure-as-code tools (e.g. Terraform, AWS CDK, or similar) • Understanding of cloud networking, security, and identity management • Experience supporting highly available, business-critical systems • Familiarity with cloud migration initiatives • Experience integrating infrastructure with CI/CD pipelines and automation tooling • Exposure to containerised workloads or orchestration platforms (e.g. ECS, EKS) • Experience working within regulated or security-focused environments • Experience working with distributed teams • AWS certifications (e.g. Solutions Architect, SysOps Administrator) are beneficial Skills & Competencies • Strong problem-solving and troubleshooting skills • Clear communication across technical and non-technical teams • Collaborative approach to working with engineering, security, and operations • Ability to prioritise and manage workload effectively • Adaptability in a changing environment • Commitment to continuous learning and improvement Why Join Us Work on meaningful cloud transformation initiatives, helping to move critical platforms into AWS. Contribute to real-world infrastructure challenges while collaborating with experienced teams across the organisation.
