BUILDING THE FUTURE OF OPEN FINANCE Payward - the parent company behind Kraken, NinjaTrader, Breakout, xStocks, Payward Services and CF Benchmarks - has spent the last 15 years building one of the most modern and globally accessible financial infrastructure platforms in the industry, built to advance an open, global financial system. THE TEAM Bitnomial is building the next generation US-based global derivatives exchange, starting with digital assets and progressively expanding to encompass a full spectrum of commodities worldwide. Bitnomial operates a US CFTC regulated exchange (DCM), clearinghouse (DCO), and brokerage (FCM). It offers a range of crypto-settled derivatives, including futures, options, and a suite of live perpetual futures, used by brokers, hedge funds, institutions, and trading firms around the world. Bitnomial's exchange, clearinghouse, and brokerage systems are written largely in Haskell. In this role you will build the services and data models that record every order, fill, and settlement at a CFTC-regulated exchange and clearinghouse, with the database as a first-class part of the design. You will build features across these systems and design the schema, queries, and migrations behind them.   THE OPPORTUNITY - Build, test, and ship well-typed, well-reviewed features and fixes in Haskell services across the exchange, clearinghouse, and brokerage - Understand the order, trade, clearing, and settlement flows end to end - Design schema, constraints, and indexes for new features, using exact numeric types and audit trails so financial values cannot drift - Write migrations that are lock-safe and reversible on large tables, and review the migrations of other engineers - Read execution plans, find slow queries from production metrics, and fix the cause in the query, the index, or the access pattern - Monitor systems, respond to alerts, and support daily operations such as settlement cycles - Fix the cause of production issues and write down what you learned   WHAT YOU BRING - 3+ years of professional software engineering experience with a functional or strongly typed programming language such as Haskell, Rust, or C++ - Strong SQL and PostgreSQL skills, including transactions and isolation levels - Solid computer science fundamentals, including data structures, concurrency, and distributed systems: replication, consistency, retries and idempotency, and how services keep shared data correct when parts fail - Willingness to take part in on-call and operational shifts - Care and rigor with code and data that move money.
