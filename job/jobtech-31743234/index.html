Business Area Chief Information Security Officer

Sandvik Aktiebolag · Malmö, Sweden

Information security is critical to achieving our strategic objectives and building resilient operations. As Business Area Chief Information Security Officer (BA CISO), you lead information security and resilience across Business Area Rock Processing, ensuring that security is embedded in business operations, IT and OT environments, and digital products. About the job You set the direction for information security across the Business Area and ensure that risks are effectively managed while legal, regulatory, customer and Sandvik requirements are met. Acting as principal advisor to Business Area Management, you connect strategic priorities with effective security governance and execution. You report to the Business Area VP IT & CIO. Job responsibilities Lead the Business Area security team and drive a strong security culture and awareness. Manage information security risks across IT, OT and digital products. Ensure and monitor compliance with Sandvik requirements, applicable laws and regulations, customer requirements and Business Area maturity targets. Advise Business Area Management on information security and cyber risk, and report on compliance to Business Area Management and the Sandvik CISO. Represent the Business Area in the Sandvik Information & IT Security Council and other relevant governance bodies. Location & flexibility This position is flexible within Europe, and regular international travel is expected. Your profile You’re an experienced information security leader with a strong track record from a large, international organization. Your background combines strategic security leadership with risk and compliance management across IT and OT environments, preferably including digital products. Your background includes: Extensive experience in information security leadership, risk management and compliance. Experience leading a team and advising and communicating with executive management. A university degree in information security, computer science, engineering or equivalent. Professional fluency in English. Strong capabilities in organizational leadership, change management, strategic planning and executive stakeholder management. Knowledge of information security laws, regulations and standards such as EU NIS2, the EU Cyber Resilience Act, the EU AI Act, ISO 27001 and IEC 62443 are an advantage, as is a recognized information security certification. Being strategic and confident in an executive setting, you combine strong communication skills with the ability to lead change and bring security perspectives into business decisions. You’re comfortable working across organizational boundaries and engaging stakeholders at multiple levels. The team You lead the Business Area security team and collaborate closely with Business Area Management, the Business Area CIO, the Sandvik CISO and owners of IT, OT and digital product environments. You also represent the Business Area in Sandvik-wide information security governance forums. Our culture At Sandvik, we’re tech driven, innovative and entrepreneurial. So, we invest in supporting each other, learning together and celebrating our differences.
