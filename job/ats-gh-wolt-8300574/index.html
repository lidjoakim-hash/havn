About Wolt At Wolt, we create technology that brings joy, simplicity and earnings to the neighborhoods of the world. In 2014 we started with delivery of restaurant food. Now we’re building the delivery of (almost) everything and you’ll find us in over 500 cities in 30 countries around the world. In 2022 we joined forces with DoorDash and together we keep on dreaming big and expanding across the globe. Working at Wolt isn’t always easy, but it’s definitely exciting. Here you’ll learn more, build more, and ship more than in most other companies. You’ll be challenged a lot, but also have a lot of fun on the way. So, if you’re a self-starter with drive and entrepreneurial spirit, this could be the ride of your life. Are you an experienced finance professional with a solid background in designing and managing finance processes in a complex, fast-moving environment? Does playing a key role in shaping financial processes in a global tech company sound like your ideal next opportunity? If so, we would love to hear from you! This role is a for 1 year Fixed Term Contract What you will be doing: • GL Accounting for defined entities. • Processing of primary accounting documents and data input in ERP system. • Ensuring that the primary accounting documents are accurate and complete. • Implementing and ensuring group accounting policies and principles are followed. • Balance sheet reconciliations. • Month end closing activities for defined entities. • Financial statements preparations. • Assisting different stakeholders with accounting related questions. • Assisting with audit requests. • Preparation of various regulatory reporting. Our humble expectations • 4+ years of previous relevant accounting experience. • Background working in Financial Institutions, banking area is a plus. • Previous knowledge of accounting and IFRS. • Previous knowledge of Finnish GAAP and knowledge of preparing Finnish Financial Statements • Understanding of accounting processes. • Prior knowledge of SOX Compliance matters is a plus. • Good analytical skills. • Degree in Finance, Accounting. • Prior experience in NetSuite is a plus. • Fluent in English, both spoken and written. Finnish language skills are strongly preferred Additional information If you are excited about working in a high-growth environment, taking ownership, and being part of an extremely ambitious team, then click below to apply and get the conversation going! What you’ll get by joining us • Opportunity to be part of building something exceptional, in an international environment • Lots of learning and growth in a globally scaling tech company • Competitive salary Please note that we do not accept applications sent by mail. You should submit your application through our careers website!
