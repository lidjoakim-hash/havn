Company Description Bosch Thermotechnology AB in Tranås develops, manufactures, and sells advanced heat pumps that harness energy from nature’s own resources – rock, water, and air. In Tranås, you’ll find both our production unit and our competence and development center, where we constantly strive to stay at the forefront. Here we create smart solutions that simplify everyday life, contribute to a greener future, and provide our customers with safety and comfort We believe in technology that makes a difference – technology that is Invented for life. Job Description Your contribution to something big: Heat pump technology is acknowledged as a key solution for residential heating and cooling, enabling significant reductions in CO2 emissions. You will join a growing, cross-functional product development team, working on the hydraulics of residential heat pump solutions. As a Design Engineer, you will contribute to the delivery of innovative Bosch products with class-leading performance, helping to reduce our carbon footprint. Analyze customer, product, and safety requirements and translate them into robust technical solutions; Responsible for developing, maintaining and releasing product engineering deliverables and specifications such as: 3D CAD Models: Hydraulic components and assemblies; Design metallic parts, thermoplastic parts, piping; Hydraulic connections/interfaces concepts; Tolerance Analysis (2D, 3D, Statistical): 2D CAD Component, Sub-Assembly and Module Assembly Drawings: Generate technical documentation, drawings (2D) for all product components; Create MOS (Material Order Specification) for applicable parts; Specify materials for product components and investigate alternatives for current parts; Apply geometric dimensioning and tolerancing (GD&T) taking into account manufacturing processes and part function/performance demands; Create and maintain BOM (Bill of Materials) for all assemblies. Make feasibility analysis of cost / quality effective updates for current products. Define rapid prototyping solutions, in accordance with application and product technical and quality requirements to be possible to validate the solutions. Contribute in the definition of standards for Mechanical CAD Design topics. Coordinate product market-introduction activities, including: Product testing Product certification Technical documentation Support for production ramp-up activities Promote quality awareness and contribute to the continuous optimization of the existing product portfolio Apply product-engineering and quality tools, including FMEA (Failure Mode and Effects Analysis), 8D, and DRBFM (Design Review Based on Failure Mode) Qualifications What distinguishes you: Education: M.SC – Mechanical Engineering or equivalent Technical Skills: Experience in the design of hydraulic components and circuits, including plastic and metallic parts, preferably using CAD 3D Siemens Unigraphics (NX). Experience in specification of new components, material selection, manufacturability, tolerance chain calculation. Knowledge of materials engineering. Understanding of manufacturing processes, construction and assembly methods. Knowledge in the design of hydraulic and structural components and systems is valued. Knowledge in the product lifecycle management (PLM) software - Teamcenter: Siemens PLM Software. Familiarity with Product Development tools (FMEA, DFMA, FAM) is valued. Proficient with Microsoft office tools (Excel, Word, PowerPoint, OneNote). Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English Personal Skills: Ownership, initiative, and a structured approach to developing solutions Results-oriented, proactive, and committed to delivering Strong analytical and problem-solving mindset Collaborative team player with the ability to work effectively in cross-functional environments Clear, precise, and structured communicator, able to explain complex topics effectively Willingness to travel and work in an international environmen
