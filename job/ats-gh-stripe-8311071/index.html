Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team The Corporate Security Systems and Infrastructure (CSI) team safeguards Stripe’s people, offices, and operations by designing, delivering, and maintaining secure infrastructure across our global workplace portfolio. We take an intelligence-led, risk-based approach and partner closely with Workplace, IT, Security Operations, Legal, and external vendors to build reliable, scalable physical security systems and transition them to our Global Security Operations Center (GSOC) for ongoing monitoring and support. What you’ll do As a Corporate Security Infrastructure Project Manager, you will lead the security infrastructure workstream for new offices, expansions, relocations, and major workplace upgrades, with an initial focus on Europe, the Middle East, and Africa (EMEA) and support for global projects as needed. You will manage projects from early site assessment and design through installation, testing, commissioning, and operational handover. You will be accountable for scope, schedule, budget, risk, quality, and stakeholder alignment across a portfolio of concurrent projects. In addition to delivering individual projects, you will strengthen the CSI program by improving standards, documentation, vendor performance, reporting, and repeatable project management processes. You will work through ambiguity, surface decisions early, and communicate clearly with technical teams, business partners, vendors, and senior leaders.
