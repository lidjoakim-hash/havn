Elastic, the Search AI Company, enables everyone to find the answers they need in real time, using all their data, at scale — unleashing the potential of businesses and people. The Elastic Search AI Platform, used by more than 50% of the Fortune 500, brings together the precision of search and the intelligence of AI to enable everyone to accelerate the results that matter. By taking advantage of all structured and unstructured data — securing and protecting private information more effectively — Elastic’s complete, cloud-based solutions for search, security, and observability help organizations deliver on the promise of AI. What is The Role As a Software Engineer on our Control Plane - Hosted team, you will be responsible for technical design and work in key functional areas of Elastic’s Cloud offerings. You will participate in coding, crafting solutions, debugging sophisticated failure scenarios, and prioritizing bug fixes and features. You’ll analyze the current system, its strength and limitations. Examples include helping Support engineers in diagnosing problems or advising the Elasticsearch engineers with designing new features What You Bring • 5+ years of experience as a software engineer, with a track record of delivering high-quality code • Previous experience providing pragmatic technical leadership for a group of engineers • Knowledge of back-end languages such as Scala, Java or Go • Technical depth in one or more technologies relevant for SaaS (orchestration, networking, containers, etc.) • Passion for automation and quality Compensation for this role is in the form of base salary.
