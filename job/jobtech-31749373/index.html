About the Role As a System Software Verification Leader, you will be part of a collaborative team working cross-functionally across Software platform, HMI platform, and other system-level projects. You will lead verification from requirements and architecture through system integration, test environments, and final product validation, creating clarity and enabling people with different perspectives and experiences to contribute. Who are you? You enjoy bringing people together to solve complex, real-world engineering problems and are curious about how software, electronics, interfaces, and complete machine systems work together. You combine a broad system-level perspective with hands-on technical capability and can guide teams through verification challenges across software platforms, HMI, and embedded-system projects. You value collaboration, listen actively, and create space for different perspectives. You take ownership of outcomes while enabling others to contribute and grow. You are comfortable operating in ambiguity, adapting as priorities and information evolve, and creating direction when the path is not fully defined. You communicate effectively in English and are confident making pragmatic decisions, constructively challenging solutions and exploring novel approaches together with cross-functional and international teams. What will make you successful in this role: Demonstrated experience in system verification for automotive, construction equipment or other complex embedded-systems domain, with the ability to define and drive verification strategies, test plans, and quality. Broad understanding of software platforms, HMI systems, ECUs, vehicle networks, and system integration, including how user interaction, embedded software and machine functions come together at system level. Familiarity with CAN, LIN, Automotive Ethernet, or SOME/IP is valuable. Hands-on experience with verification tools and test environments. Experience with CANoe, CANalyzer, CAPL or VT System is an advantage. Experience in test automation and software-driven verification. Knowledge of Python, object-oriented programming, Git or CI/CD pipelines such as Jenkins and GitLab CI is beneficial. Experience with HIL or simulation-based verification environments, such as dSPACE, and an interest in building efficient, repeatable, and scalable testing. Ability to lead complex technical investigations, log analysis, and root-cause analysis, translating findings into clear and actionable feedback for development teams. Proven ability to set technical direction, establish verification priorities and drive commitments to completion, while improving automation, coverage, traceability, and efficiency across the verification life
