Development Engineer, Waterjet Systems

Kongsberg Maritime Sweden AB · Kristinehamn, Sweden

💰 ca. 510 000–620 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 6.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Are you ready for an exciting challenge within the marine industry? Due to an internal move, an exciting opportunity has now arisen for a Development Engineer to join our Waterjet Systems product team at Kongsberg Maritime in Kristinehamn. We are looking for a motivated engineer to become a core member in our team of experts. You will be surrounded by competent colleagues that are focused on developing new innovative products, handling our customers concerns and working in a cross-functional matter. You will be based in Kristinehamn together with your colleagues but also have close collaboration with our engineering teams in Finland, Croatia and Poland. Our suppliers and customers are located all over the globe, so travelling can be a natural part of work for the interested. Your Role As a Development Engineer at Kongsberg Maritime, you will be a key player in our Waterjet Systems product team. Your main responsibility will be to work on the development and implementation of innovative product design solutions. You will coordinate and collaborate with colleagues across different functions, as well as with customers and suppliers, to ensure the successful design and product development. In this role you will be involved in a variety of tasks. You may work in areas such as mechanics, hydraulics, and electronics, depending on your expertise and experience. The focus will vary based on which tasks are initiated and prioritized. What we can offer We offer an inspiring and safe work environment and the possibility to work with sustainable and exciting solutions for the future. A position with a wide range of challenging work tasks, opportunities to make a real difference and develop on a technical and personal level. You will have the opportunity to work with unique products at a world leading supplier of maritime solutions. You will work in an international environment and in multidisciplinary team collaboration. We can offer flexible working hours and the option to work partly remote based on your work tasks. Qualifications and experience Bachelor's or Master's degree in Engineering or a related field, with a strong technical mindset. Previous experience in design, CAD, project management, or product development is desirable but not essential. Experience in the marine sector or within structural analysis, hydraulics, materials science, condition monitoring, systems engineering, mechanical design, technical documentation, or project management is considered an advantage. However, if you do not meet all the requirements and still are interested and think that you have other knowledge or skills to offer, do not hesitate to apply. We value your personal characteristics highly, with the right attitude we’re here to help you grow. We are all about hands-on challenges and support to help you become the best development engineer you can be. We are open to provide any relevant training and internal development to increase your competence level. Due to the nature of an international customer and supplier base, a high level of English, both written and spoken, is required. Communication skills in other languages are advantageous. We appreciate your ability to take initiative and make things happen in a systematic way. An innovative mindset is essential, as is the ability to analyze and come up with new ideas and solutions. Collaboration is key to our success, so you should work well with others, communicate effectively, share knowledge, experience and support your colleagues in achieving team goals. A strong customer focus and understanding of business impact will be important in this role. Kongsberg Maritime works with customers across both commercial and naval markets. As this role is subject to export control regulations and requires security clearance, citizenship-related questions will be included in the recruitment process.
