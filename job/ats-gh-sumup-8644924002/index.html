SumUp Limited is our Central Bank of Ireland regulated electronic money institution, and it's entering a defining period of its growth. As Chief Operating Officer, you'll take overall accountability for the firm's internal operations, working alongside the SumUp executive team to make sure our governance, resilience, and regulatory standards match the scale of what we're building. This is a rare chance to shape the operating backbone of a regulated fintech from a senior, board level seat, at a moment when the firm is actively strengthening its structure and controls to support future growth. What you'll do • Set and deliver SumUp Limited's operational strategy in partnership with the senior leadership team • Own the firm's outsourcing strategy, including governance, oversight, risk assessment, and the outsourcing register • Maintain regulatory accountability documents, including the Statement of Responsibilities and the Management Responsibility Map under SEAR • Act as a senior point of contact for the Central Bank of Ireland, supporting inspections and regulatory engagement • Own operational resilience, business continuity, and wind down planning, including testing and incident response • Provide regular operational reporting to the CEO, board, and Central Bank of Ireland on performance, risk, and compliance You'll be great for this role if… • Significant senior leadership experience in an operations role within a Central Bank of Ireland regulated financial services firm or e-money institution • Strong working knowledge of the Individual Accountability Framework, SEAR, and Fitness and Probity requirements, gained through holding or supporting a PCF role • Track record of managing large, complex operational organisations across multiple markets • Experience owning outsourcing governance, operational resilience frameworks, and regulatory compliance programmes • Ability to deliver operational transformation and efficiency at scale while managing regulator relationships credibly and transparently Why you should join SumUp 🌎 Opportunity to work with a truly global, multicultural team from our homely, Dublin hub just steps from Harcourt Street Luas and charming Iveagh Gardens.
