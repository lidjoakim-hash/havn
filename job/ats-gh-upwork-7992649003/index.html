As a Senior People Operations Specialist, you will play a key role in delivering thoughtful, scalable people programs and operational support across the employee lifecycle. As part of our People Operations team, you’ll combine strong operational expertise with the judgment and partnership skills needed to support HR Business Partners, managers, and employees through both day-to-day needs and more complex people matters. You’ll own and continuously improve key people programs and processes, with a particular focus on employee lifecycle operations, immigration and global mobility, performance management, and HRBP enablement. You’ll serve as a subject matter resource in areas of expertise while building breadth across the broader People Operations portfolio. This role is ideal for someone who brings strong operational instincts, thrives in complexity, and enjoys moving between detailed execution, problem-solving, process improvement, and partnership. Responsibilities • Partner on onboarding, offboarding, organizational changes, and other employee lifecycle moments, providing guidance to employees, managers, and HRBPs on more complex People Operations matters. • Own end-to-end coordination of immigration matters alongside our external vendor, serving as a trusted internal resource for employees and managers navigating immigration processes and requirements. • Maintain accurate immigration records, documentation, communications, and workflows while supporting employment eligibility and related compliance requirements. • Navigate ambiguous or sensitive situations with discretion, sound judgment, and an understanding of when to independently resolve an issue versus engage the appropriate partner. • Provide guidance and operational support across coaching and performance processes, helping managers navigate tools, documentation, expectations, and next steps. Act as a trusted partner for employees and managers, balancing advocacy for people with business needs. • Support People Operations processes across our growing global footprint, partnering with internal teams and external vendors to address local requirements and maintain consistent employee support. • Identify opportunities to simplify and improve People processes, using technology, automation, and AI where they can meaningfully reduce manual work or improve the employee and manager experience. What it takes to catch our eye • 5+ years of progressive experience across People Operations, HR Generalist, shared services, or related roles, ideally in a fast-moving or evolving organization.
