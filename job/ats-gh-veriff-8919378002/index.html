About the team Veriff's Solutions Engineering team works at the intersection of technology, customer experience, and commercial strategy. We partner with customers to understand their business and technical needs, navigate complex challenges, and demonstrate the value of Veriff's solutions. Working closely with Sales and Account Management, we help build strong customer relationships and support Veriff's growth. The opportunity We're looking for a Lead Solutions Engineer to lead our Solutions Engineering team in EMEA. You'll combine technical expertise, people leadership, and commercial thinking to support enterprise customers, strengthen collaboration across teams, and drive business results in the region. You'll provide structure and direction to the team, improve how we work with customers and internal stakeholders, and help translate our go-to-market strategy into commercial success. This is an opportunity to make a meaningful impact in a fast-paced, growing technology company. What you'll achieve in your first 3 months • Build a strong understanding of the Solutions Engineering team, its priorities, and Veriff's go-to-market strategy. • Establish relationships with key customers and internal stakeholders across the Americas. • Identify and implement initial improvements to team management, collaboration, and operational processes. • Identify opportunities to strengthen customer engagement and improve commercial traction. What you'll achieve by 6 months • Deliver against key team KPIs and contribute to the commercial objectives for the Americas. • Establish clear ways of working across Solutions Engineering, Sales, and Account Management. • Become a trusted leader within the Americas organisation, supporting team development and building strong stakeholder relationships. • Improve the team's ability to support complex enterprise customers and drive successful commercial outcomes. What you'll bring — essential • Leadership experience: Experience leading, coaching, or developing a technical, customer-facing team. You're comfortable setting direction, creating structure, and helping people perform at their best. • Technical acumen: The ability to understand complex technical concepts, engage credibly with technical stakeholders, and translate customer requirements into practical solutions. • Customer communication: Strong communication and presentation skills, with the ability to adapt your approach to different audiences and explain complex concepts clearly. • Commercial mindset: An understanding of how technical solutions support business outcomes, with experience partnering with commercial teams to support customer and revenue goals. • Stakeholder management: Experience building relationships across teams, influencing decisions, navigating pushback, and handling challenging situations constructively. • Operational excellence: A proactive, structured approach to managing priorities, improving processes, and working effectively in a fast-moving environment. • AI mindset: Curiosity about AI and its practical applications, ideally with examples of tools or solutions you've built or experimented with. Nice to have • Experience in enterprise B2B SaaS, ideally within a startup or scale-up environment. • Experience working with large, complex enterprise customers and managing technical and commercial relationships. • Experience building or scaling a Solutions Engineering team or improving its processes. • Familiarity with identity verification, fraud prevention, fintech, or related technology domains. Why Veriff? Identity is the core of the digital economy. In this role, you aren’t just selling software; you are building the infrastructure that prevents fraud and enables global trust. You will have a direct, measurable impact on our expansion into the world’s fastest-growing markets. We are the preferred identity verification platform partner for the world’s most innovative growth-driven organizations
