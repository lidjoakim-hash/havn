In this role, you will take technical responsibility for electronic and electrical component development, supplier collaboration, component evaluation and strategic technology planning. You will work closely with internal engineering teams, cross-functional stakeholders and external suppliers to ensure that components meet technical requirements, quality expectations and overall product development objectives. Key Responsibilities 1. Electrical & Electronics Component Development Develop and document electrical and electronic components. Define technical requirements and specifications for component development. Perform and coordinate engineering simulations and technical evaluations. Evaluate new technologies and electronic components for potential product applications. Identify suitable components and recommend appropriate solutions for integration into products. Ensure technical documentation, component specifications and development records are maintained. 2. Supplier Management & Technical Collaboration Define and communicate technical requirements to external component suppliers. Coordinate supplier activities and manage the distribution of technical work. Take operational responsibility for supplier relationships within the assigned technical area. Evaluate supplier capabilities and recommend suitable suppliers. Collaborate with suppliers to resolve technical challenges and ensure successful component development. Monitor supplier deliverables and ensure alignment with engineering requirements. 3. Component Strategy & Roadmap Development Contribute to and drive product strategy roadmaps for assigned electrical and electronic components. Manage complex components or portfolios containing multiple less-complex components. Identify opportunities for component optimization, standardization and technology development. Support long-term component strategies in alignment with product and business objectives. Ensure new component decisions are technically sound and commercially viable. 4. Cross-Functional Collaboration & Technical Leadership Secure alignment and support from relevant engineering, procurement and business stakeholders. Lead complex technical activities and engineering projects. Influence technical decisions across multidisciplinary engineering teams. Mentor and support colleagues by sharing specialist knowledge and engineering best practices. Contribute to continuous learning, technical innovation and capability development.
