At Axis Communications we are passionate about creating a smarter and safer world! Are you excited about designing scalable data pipelines and enabling analytics and AI applications? We're looking for an experienced, hands-on Data Engineer to build and improve production data pipelines and data products for Global Operations. You'll work closely with our analytics team, process owners, and integration experts to make supply-chain data reliable and useful for reporting and analysis. This is a specialist individual-contributor role. You'll own defined pipelines and data products from implementation through ongoing improvement, while collaborating with colleagues on platform and architectural decisions. Who is your future team? Operations Development is a cross-functional team of around 30 experts in Supply Chain, Project Management, Innovation, and System Management. You will specifically join the Operations Development Analytics team, a team of 8 highly skilled engineers focusing on BI and Analytics within Supply Chain. You will be reporting to the Data & Analytics Manager for Operations Development. While ClickHouse, Qlik Sense and Power BI are our key data & analytics platforms, the team also develops advanced analytics solutions using machine learning, generative AI, and a broad range of modern technologies. What you'll do as a Data Engineer? As a data engineer, you will create value and enable business growth by bringing data from across Axis together to gather actionable insights. Data & Analytics is passionate about making data, analytics and insights easily findable and explainable for the many to enable better and faster decision-making. Your focus will be to: Develop and maintain data models and data products in ClickHouse and dbt. Build and support ETL/ELT pipelines from internal and external sources. Improve data quality, testing, monitoring, and documentation for existing solutions. Investigate and resolve data and pipeline issues Work with analysts, process owners, and integration experts to turn defined business needs into reliable data solutions. Share knowledge and contribute to the team's engineering standards Ensure that data access, privacy, and governance requirements are built into our solutions. Contribute to technical decisions within the team, and help the team choose solutions that balance reliability, scalability, and business value. Stay current with new data engineering technologies and practices, applying them where they create clear business value. Who are you? You are an analytical thinker who thrives in an innovative, ever-changing environment. You enjoy the challenge of building relationships across organizational borders and can support stakeholders to establish the best possible solutions. You aren't just interested in the "how" of the data, but the "why" of the business and you are genuinely curious about global supply chains and operational excellence. We're looking for someone with several years of practical experience building and supporting production data pipelines. You've worked with data models and transformations in a team environment and are comfortable taking responsibility for defined solutions. What we are looking for: Strong hands-on experience with modern data engineering practices, including workflow orchestration, transformation frameworks, analytical databases, and containerized environments. Experience with Airflow, dbt, ClickHouse, and Kubernetes is valuable. Experience in designing, building, and operating production-grade data platforms and end-to-end pipelines. With a track record of taking technical ownership and improving existing systems. Typically, candidates have around 3-7 years of relevant experience. Strong skills in SQL and Python, data modelling, and data quality testing, including anomaly detection. Comfortable troubleshooting data issues and working with unclear or changing requirements.
