About Tailscale Tailscale is making safe connection effortless by delivering software that makes it easy to securely interconnect people and their devices, no matter where they are. From hobbyists to multinational corporations, teams of every size use Tailscale each day to protect their networks, share access to internal tools, and more. We're building a future for the Internet that's easy, sensible, and safe, like it used to be. Founded in 2019 and fully distributed, we're backed by Accel, CRV, Insight, Heavybit, and Uncork Capital. About the Role Join Tailscale’s growing Sales team and help existing customers get more value from our product. As a Customer Account Executive, you will own the full sales cycle for expansion and renewal opportunities across a portfolio of existing customers—from account planning, prospecting and discovery through evaluation, negotiation and close. You will identify new use cases, build relationships with key stakeholders and help customers expand their adoption of Tailscale. This is a quota-carrying closing role for someone with previous full-cycle B2B SaaS sales experience. You will partner closely with Solutions Engineering, Customer Success, Support, Marketing and Product to deliver results for customers. We’re a collaborative team with a wide variety of backgrounds. We coach each other, share feedback and support one another in achieving our goals while staying focused on our customers’ needs. Key Responsibilities • Develop a strong understanding of Tailscale, its use cases and the business value it delivers to customers. • Proactively build and qualify expansion pipeline within existing accounts by identifying new teams, stakeholders and use cases. • Own and deliver against an expansion and renewal quota across your customer portfolio. • Run the full sales cycle: lead discovery, coordinate technical evaluations with Solutions Engineering, communicate value, develop proposals, negotiate commercial terms and close agreements. • Balance near-term revenue delivery with consistent pipeline generation and timely renewal planning. • Maintain accurate opportunity records and provide reliable weekly forecasts, with clear next steps, close plans and visibility into risks. • Build mutual action plans with customers and partner with Customer Success and Solutions Engineering to support adoption, demonstrate value and drive expansion and renewals. What We Are Looking For • 1+ years of quota-carrying, full-cycle B2B SaaS sales experience. • Demonstrated ownership of opportunities from prospecting and qualification through discovery, evaluation, negotiation and close. • Experience selling developer or technical products, particularly in a product-led growth (PLG) environment. • Strong discovery, presentation and relationship-building skills, including experience engaging senior managers and executives. • A track record of meeting or exceeding sales quota and the ability to manage multiple opportunities at different stages of the sales cycle. • Proven pipeline management and forecasting discipline, with experience using a CRM and tools such as Gong to manage opportunities and collaborate internally. • Experience partnering with Solutions Engineering, Customer Success and Support to deliver positive customer outcomes. • A proactive, accountable approach to growing existing customer relationships, with the curiosity and initiative to uncover new opportunities in a high-growth software business. • This role is based in London and requires three days per week in the office.
