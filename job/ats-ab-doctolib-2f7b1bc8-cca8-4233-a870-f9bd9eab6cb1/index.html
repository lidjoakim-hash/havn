WHAT WE DO Join a team of passionate and hard working entrepreneurs to transform healthcare!  Working in the tech team at Doctolib involves building innovative products and features to improve the daily lives of care teams and patients. We work in feature teams in an agile environment, while collaborating with product, design, and business teams. WHAT YOU'LL DO Implementing our ambitious expansion plan in Italy, we joined forces with Vettore Medical https://www.vettoremedical.it/ and we are now looking for a Senior Software Engineer to join our tech team in Milano. Our mission is simple: every product we build improves the daily life of healthcare professionals and patients. Joining Doctolib, you will be part of a team of 400+ software engineers: you will be writing high-quality, secure, performant and well-tested code that helps thousands of healthcare professionals spend more time caring for their patients, deploying it to some of the largest medical facilities in Europe.  As a Software Engineer in the Vettore Medical team, you will be working on a cloud SaaS solution for clinics and medical offices. You will join a small feature team and be responsible for evolving the product and integrating it within our ecosystem. Your responsibilities include but are not limited to: - Designing and implementing new features using Java (Kotlin) and Microsoft (.NET, C#) technologies  - Identifying opportunities to improve engineering standards, tooling and processes; - Work closely with product and other tech teams (infrastructure, security, tooling...) to help define our roadmap. WHO YOU ARE Before you read on, if you don't have the exact profile described below, but you feel this job description matches your skill set, we still encourage you to apply! You could be our next team mate if you: - You have 5+ years of experience with Java and are eager to work with object-oriented coding languages (part of our stack is Microsoft); - You have experience running software on cloud infrastructure; - You have experience with frameworks and libraries such as Kotlin or Spring Boot; - You have experience working with database technologies; - You enjoy figuring out why things work, not just that they work; - You write test-driven pragmatically engineered code and are eager to learn.
