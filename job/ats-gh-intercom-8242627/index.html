About this role Our website is one of the most important ways people discover what we offer, understand our value, and decide whether to talk to Sales or start a trial. The Web Growth team owns its roadmap and continuously improves the journey from first impression through to lead capture, demo engagement, signup, and trial start. You’ll treat the website like a product: understand user intent, find confusion and friction, form strong hypotheses, test them with users and data, and turn what you learn into better experiences. You’ll be just as comfortable shaping the problem and influencing the roadmap as you are designing the final interaction, prototype, or page. What you’ll be doing • Experimentation: Designing and iterating on experiments across landing pages, pricing, signup, and conversion flows to improve engagement, conversion, qualified leads, and trial starts. • Collaboration: Working with Growth, Self-serve, Brand, Marketing, Product, Data, Research, and Engineering to turn opportunities into clear, buildable experiences. • Data and insight: Using user insights and funnel data to identify opportunities, define hypotheses, and measure impact. • Product design: Applying product design thinking to comprehension, navigation, forms, interactive demos, responsiveness, and accessibility. • Prototyping and systems: Prototyping in Figma and code, using AI tools where useful, and working within shared components and brand systems. • Roadmap ownership: Bringing ideas to the Web Growth roadmap, not just executing briefs.
