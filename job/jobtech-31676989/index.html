Job description Are you a visionary software architect looking for a challenge that goes beyond standard app development? At Teledyne FLIR in Täby, we build the sensor systems and thermal cameras used globally in fire detection, industrial applications, and blue-light equipment. Now, we need your expertise to shape the brain inside these life-saving products. We are on a journey to centralize and streamline our Linux-based product offerings into one robust, unified software platform. As our new Embedded Software Architect, you will own the long-term vision, architecture, and roadmap for our Android/AOSP-based platform. This is a high-impact, strategic, and hands-on role. You will be the technical anchor for our R&D, tasked with balancing urgent product needs with sustainable, scalable, and reusable strategies. You won’t just maintain an architecture; you will define it. What we offer you True Autonomy: The mandate to define architectural principles and shape the future of our R&D. A Collaborative Culture: You will work within a highly skilled, senior team of architects in an environment that values mentorship, continuous learning, and innovation. Global Impact: The code you architect will run in critical systems used worldwide, making a tangible difference in commercial and government sectors. Application: Are you ready to take command of our platform architecture and drive innovation? We look forward to your application. Please note that this role is security-classified, and a security vetting process will be conducted as part of the recruitment. Responsibilities Drive Platform Strategy: Own the long-term architectural vision for our software platform, balancing urgent product needs with sustainable, reusable strategies. Lead Integration: Facilitate cross-functional synchronization across multiple teams to ensure scale, performance, and security. Translate Requirements: Collaborate with product management to convert system and business needs into high-level platform technical requirements. Technical Leadership: Act as the primary spokesperson for the platform area, guiding engineering decisions and fostering a culture of technical excellence. Drive Modernization: Lead the effort to centralize and streamline our Linux-based product offerings into a robust, unified platform. Qualifications We believe you thrive at the intersection of complex software, system engineering, and product development. You are a strategic problem-solver with a proven track record of leading technical decisions and successfully managing multiple product launches. To succeed in this role, we see that you have: Deep, hands-on expertise in Android platform architecture, AOSP, and embedded Linux. A strong background in embedded software development and system integration. The capacity to influence stakeholders and teams through clear, technical communication. Fluency in English (professional proficiency in Swedish is a merit). A relevant academic degree (e.g., M.Sc. in Computer Science/Engineering) OR equivalent deep, practical experience in the field.
