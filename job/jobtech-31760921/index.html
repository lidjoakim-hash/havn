Performance & Mediation Engineer

Academic Work Sweden AB · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 510 000–620 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 18.2.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Join a supportive, forward-thinking team at our customer where you will drive end-to-end Performance Management and data mediation, turning raw network metrics into reliable KPIs for fixed networks. About the role In this role, you will own and evolve the PM data pipelines connecting fixed-network devices and OSS to the target platform. You will work closely with cross-functional teams to deliver reliable, explainable data for both operations and business decisions. This is initially a consulting assignment where you will be employed by Academic Work and work as a consultant for our client, with a good possibility of extension. Work tasks This role involves developing, operating, and integrating data pipelines to optimize network performance management and observability. Develop and operate robust data ingestion and transformation flows to OPTIC Data Lake and NNMi/NOM. Integrate with adjacent systems like inventory, ITSM, and analytics using APIs and message queues. Collaborate with Solution Architects, Product Owners, and Performance Analysts to prioritize value. Onboard new data sources and extend metric models to reduce vendor dependence. We are looking for Knowledge of Python and SQL Basic knowledge of ETL/ELT frameworks and job orchestration Basic knowledge of SNMP (MIBs, polling), traps, and streaming telemetry Experience in working with data pipelines in OSS, telecom PM, or network analytics Fluent in English It is meritorious if you have Experience with enterprise NMS/OSS platforms (e.g., OpenText/Micro Focus, IBM, or similar) and data lake architectures Good knowledge of DevOps practices Good knowledge of message buses like Kafka and observability stacks like Grafana, Prometheus, or ELK Knowledge of Swedish To succeed in the role, your personal skills are: Stable Orderly Responsible Our recruitment process This recruitment process is handled by Academic Work and it is our client’s wish that all questions regarding the position is directed to Academic Work. Our selection process is continuous and the advert may close before the recruitment process is completed if we have moved forward to the next phase. The process includes two tests: one personality test and one cognitive test. The tests are tools to find the right talent for the right position, to enable equality, diversity, and a fair process.
