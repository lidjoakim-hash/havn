The Insurance team focuses on innovating on our health and disability insurance products, over 5 geographies including France, Belgium, Spain and Canada. Through the Program, you will develop a very precious blend of technical, business, entrepreneurial and product skills. After a 2-months onboarding, you may be involved in multiple missions in different streams of the Insurance team: - The Enterprise and Public Sector stream structures the best offers for our largest clients, whether a ministry or a corporate.
