Company description: . Job description:  Transport is at the core of modern society. Imagine using your expertise to shape sustainable transport and infrastructure solutions for the future. If you seek to make a difference on a global scale, working with next-gen technologies and the sharpest collaborative teams, then we could be a perfect match.  We shape the world we want to live in! Volvo Energy is a young company in the Volvo Group which supports customers in the electrical energy transition. Volvo Energy develops and commercializes competitive Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) and provides customers with reliable solutions which optimize energy costs, operational resilience, and renewable energy integration.   We are now looking for a new colleague to join our team at Volvo Energy as Lead Power System and Grid Compliance.   What you will do In this role, we are looking for a power system engineer who can lead the grid connection and compliance process for BESS projects. You combine strong power system knowledge with hands-on experience in PowerFactory and can translate grid code and grid owner requirements into technical studies, testing activities, and approval documentation.   You are structured, analytical, and comfortable taking technical ownership. You are a team player who works effectively with DSOs, TSOs, customers, suppliers, aggregators, consultants, and internal project and product teams.   Responsibilities: Lead the RfG and grid connection process for BESS projects, from requirement clarification through ION, EON, FON, compliance testing, and final approval. Act as the primary technical interface towards DSOs, TSOs, and other grid stakeholders throughout the grid connection process. Perform and coordinate PowerFactory, EMT, harmonic, protection, and other power system studies required to demonstrate compliance with grid code and connection requirements. Prepare simulation reports, compliance documentation, and technical submissions required for grid owner approvals and operational notifications. Coordinate compliance testing, witness testing, and verification activities with grid owners, suppliers, and project teams. Support supplier selection, design reviews, and technical requirements to ensure BESS systems meet applicable grid code and connection requirements. Lead aggregator sourcing, technical integration, and prequalification activities where required. Who are you? Expected qualifications:•    Degree in Electrical Engineering, Power Systems Engineering, or equivalent knowledge gained through professional experience.•    Experience with Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS).•    Experience with power system studies, simulations, and grid connection activities using PowerFactory or similar tools.•    Experience with grid code compliance and utility-scale energy projects, such as BESS, solar, wind, or substations.•    Understanding power system behavior, including protection systems, harmonics, and dynamic performance, with experience preparing technical studies, simulation reports, and compliance documentation.•    Self-motivated, structured, and solution-oriented, with strong communication skills and the ability to collaborate effectively with customers, grid owners, suppliers, and internal teams.•    Fluent in Swedish and English, both written and spoken.
