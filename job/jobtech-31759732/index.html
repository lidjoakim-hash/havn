Firmware Engineer

ASSA ABLOY AB · Stockholm, Sweden

Are you looking for your next challenge in Firmware Development? We are looking for a curious, driven, and engaged Firmware Developer to help shape the future of our connected smart lock solutions. In this role, you will contribute to the design, development, testing, and maintenance of innovative products that deliver secure and seamless access experiences to customers around the world. Join ASSA ABLOY Opening Solutions in Malmö, Sweden, and become part of a talented team where knowledge transfer, innovation, and engagement are at the heart of everything we do. We offer an environment that empowers you to grow professionally, take ownership of your work, and build a career you can be proud of. At ASSA ABLOY, our success is built on the values of Empowerment, Innovation, and Integrity. We are committed to fostering an inclusive and forward-thinking culture where new ideas are encouraged, people are inspired to make an impact, and collectively develop market-leading solutions that help our customers live, work, and move through the world more safely and conveniently. What you would do as our Firmware Developer You will join a small team of experts and play a key role in developing innovative connected products from concept to market. Working in a team-oriented, cross-functional environment, you will help create secure, dependable, and high-performing embedded solutions that deliver exceptional user experiences. You would also: Analyse product and system requirements and translate them into effective firmware architectures and technical solutions. Design, develop, test, and maintain embedded firmware for both new product development and existing product enhancements. Collaborate closely with hardware, cloud, mobile, and product teams to ensure seamless integration and successful product delivery. Contribute to the development of secure, reliable, and scalable technologies for the next generation of connected access solutions. Participate in technical reviews, troubleshooting, and continuous improvement activities to strengthen product quality, performance, and engineering practices. The skills and experience you need We are looking for someone who: Holds a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Embedded Systems, or a related field, combined with 3-7 years of professional experience in embedded real-time software development. Demonstrates strong programming skills in C and a solid comprehension of RTOS concepts, including threading, synchronization, memory management, and interrupt handling, along with hands-on experience in embedded software development, debugging, and modern development practices using tools such as J-Link, GDB, Git, or similar. Possesses strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to engage effectively in cross-functional teams and contribute to the successful delivery of complex embedded software solutions. Is fluent in English, both written and spoken. Proficiency in Swedish is considered an advantage. Is passionate about developing high-quality embedded systems and has a proactive mindset toward learning new technologies, improving software quality, driving innovation, and taking ownership of their work. Ideally, you would also have experience with Zephyr RTOS; microcontroller platforms from vendors such as Nordic, STMicroelectronics, or NXP; secure firmware development including secure boot, cryptography, key management, and certificate handling; IoT protocols and connectivity technologies such as MQTT, Matter, Bluetooth® Low Energy (BLE), Wi-Fi, and Thread; low-power battery-operated IoT devices; peripheral driver development for interfaces such as SPI, I2C, and UART; as well as Python programming and test automation.
