Construction & Facilities Manager (12-Month Parental Cover)

Axel Arigato AB · Göteborg, Sweden

💰 ca. 420 000–580 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 1.2.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

We’re looking for a Construction & Facilities Manager to plan, coordinate, and manage how Axel Arigato physical spaces are built, refurbished, and maintained - from stores to showrooms and pop-ups. Do you like seeing a project through from first plan to opening day, on time and on budget? You’ll own the budget for these spaces, working closely with contractors, landlords and teams across the business to keep each one safe, functional and well maintained. It’s a year of growth: three new US projects are live, and European expansion into the UK is underway. Come join us. WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR This role suits someone who stays calm under pressure and is organized by default. You plan carefully, follow through, and keep contractors, landlords and colleagues aligned when timelines get tight. You’re comfortable managing external partners across several markets, you hold suppliers to a clear standard, and you look for ways to make each project run smoother than the last. WHAT YOU WILL DO Own time schedules, RACI, budgets, labor and risk mitigation plans for every construction and refurbishment project. Lead projects from start to close-out, coordinating subcontractors and keeping landlords, contractors and internal stakeholders updated on progress. Review, adapt and execute lease, supplier and maintenance agreements through a transparent procurement process that reduces business risk. Run snag, punch and QC reports and follow up on execution, so every store opens to standard. Manage preventive and corrective maintenance with our facilities partners in the US and EU, tracking both performance and cost. Bring commercial design feedback into each project to keep store quality and customer experience consistent. Set and follow up department goals each quarter, making responsibilities clear and ensuring tools and resources to deliver. Partner with other departments to share knowledge and improve how we work together. ARE YOU THE ONE? We seek people with most of the following: A background in building and construction, through PMP or similar training in construction or facilities management, or 5+ years of experience in retail construction or facilities management. Experience leading a team or working in a similar role. A solid understanding of construction and maintenance requirements for commercial spaces. Experience managing external service providers across multiple markets. Strong time management and clear, confident verbal communication. A problem-solving mindset and the initiative to act on it. Familiarity with lean and/or agile ways of working. Confidence in Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, with AutoCAD, SketchUp or Asana as a plus. HERE’S HOW TO APPLY If you want to shape how our physical spaces are built and cared for, we’d love to hear from you. Apply by submitting your CV in English. Please do not include a photo or date of birth with your application; our recruitment process is competency-based. We review applications continuously, and the position may be filled before the closing date. CONTRACT TYPE & START DATE Estimated starting date: January 2027. Contract type: Fixed-term contract for 12 months, covering parental leave. Full-time. Location: Gothenburg, Sweden. On-site at our central office. A BIT ABOUT AXEL ARIGATO Founded in Sweden in 2014 by Albin Johansson and Max Svärdh, Axel Arigato was born with a disruptive mindset, modernising and democratising the sneaker landscape through a blend of style, quality, community and a digital-first approach. Grounded in Scandinavian minimalism and elevated with playful, intentional details, every piece is designed with care: crafted to move globally, worn with individuality. What began online expanded into physical spaces built for energetic cultural exchange: stores and experiences where conversations, performances and unexpected activations dissolve the boundaries between brand and culture. Today, Axel Arigato continues to evolve with purpose
