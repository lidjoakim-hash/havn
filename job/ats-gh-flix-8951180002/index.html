Shift Leader - FlixBus Shop Kyiv (m/f/d)

At Flix, we offer a dynamic work environment with competitive pay, strong growth opportunities, and a tech-driven approach to making travel more accessible, sustainable, and affordable. To strengthen our team in Ukraine, we are looking for a motivated and driven Shift Leader (m/f/d) for our FlixBus shop at the Kyiv Bus Station. You will lead the day-to-day running of your shift, guide and support the shop team, and ensure high-quality service and smooth operations for our passengers. This is a full-time, permanent position, working on a shift basis across the full week, including weekdays and weekends. About the Role • Shift Coordination: organize tasks and objectives (quality, sales, team development) at the start of each shift and ensure follow-up throughout the day • Operational Oversight: ensure smooth shift operations, with a focus on team well-being, safety, customer satisfaction, and uniform compliance • Service Quality: maintain a high standard of customer service and step in to resolve complex passenger questions, cancellations, and rebookings • Closing Procedures: oversee cash handling and cash counts, and secure end-of-shift processes and daily reconciliation • Team Support: provide hands-on support to the team during busy periods, delays, and operational disruptions, in coordination with station teams • Coaching & Feedback: guide, motivate, and give regular feedback to team members • Onboarding & Training: support the integration and development of new team members About You • 2+ years of experience in customer service, retail, or operations, including sales and cash handling; leadership or shift coordination experience is a plus • A natural team leader who motivates others and creates a positive, supportive shift atmosphere • Calm and resilient under pressure, staying solution-oriented during busy periods and unexpected disruptions • Proactive and well-organized, taking ownership of the shift and working independently when needed • Strong communication and interpersonal skills • Comfortable with MS Office, smartphones, web applications, and reservation systems • Flexible to work shifts from Monday to Sunday • Very good command of Ukrainian and English We recognize that everyone carries a unique set of valuable skills and experiences. If you think you could have an impact even though you don't meet 100% of the requirements, we still encourage you to apply. We want to hear from you! What We Offer • Travel perks: 12 free Flix vouchers + 12 discount vouchers for friends & family • Wellbeing support: Access confidential 1:1 counselling, courses, and stress management for yourself and up to four family members • Learning & Development: Take advantage of language classes, training courses, and expert-led sessions to grow your skills • Mentoring Program: Connect with experienced colleagues to gain insights and accelerate your career Why Join Flix? At Flix, you’ll find teams that rally together to overcome challenges and spark creativity. We believe in ownership culture - giving you the freedom to take initiative, make an impact, and shape your own career path. As we continue to expand across the globe, you can make a real difference in how we work. If you’re ready to grow and lead your journey, Flix is the place for you!
