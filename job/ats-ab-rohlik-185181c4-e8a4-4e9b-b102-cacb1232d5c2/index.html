At Rohlik Group, we are changing how people eat and live — delivering fresh, high-quality groceries to millions of customers across Europe. To fuel this growth, we need sharp financial insights, strong planning, and smart decision support. We’re looking for a Senior FP&A Analyst who can turn complex data into clear stories for senior leadership and investors — helping us scale efficiently and sustainably. This is a hands-on role for someone who enjoys working with large data sets, building analytical models and taking ownership of key FP&A workstreams. WHY THIS ROLE IS EXCITING - You will support decision-making at the highest level — working closely with the Regional Head of FP&A, Group CFO, Executive Board and investors. - You will own key FP&A workstreams with a high degree of independence and visible business impact. - Your work will go far beyond reporting — you will help shape strategic decisions and long-term financial direction. - You will work across multiple markets and gain broad exposure to the financial performance of the Group. - Rohlik is an AI-first company, and you will have the opportunity to help modernise how FP&A works through automation and smarter tools. WHAT YOU WILL OWN AND DELIVER - Partner with the Regional Head of FP&A and Group CFO on forecasting, budgeting, long-term planning and Business Reviews (WBR/MBR/QBR) - Consolidate management accounts and support alignment with IFRS standards - Build and maintain dashboards, financial models and running P&L - Prepare Board and investor materials, including financial tables, commentary and key insights - Challenge and support country finance teams, ensuring plans and forecasts are realistic and aligned with Group priorities - Develop scenario analyses and business cases for strategic decisions and new initiatives - Own the FP&A timetable and ensure timely, high-quality submissions across markets - Identify opportunities to automate, simplify and improve FP&A processes Success in this role means bringing data to life — turning numbers into clear decisions that help Rohlik grow efficiently and sustainably. WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR - 5+ years of experience in finance, consulting or a similar analytical environment - Strong financial modelling and scenario planning skills - Ability to extract meaningful insights from large and complex data sets - Experience with Excel, Tableau, NetSuite, DataRails or similar tools - Excellent analytical, communication and presentation skills - High attention to detail, strong ownership and the ability to manage multiple priorities - Confidence working independently in a fast-changing environment - Fluent English, spoken and written - Experience with automation, AI-driven analytics or modern finance tools is a strong advantage WHAT’S IN IT FOR YOU - Fair reward - You’ll get a strong fixed salary — no bonus games, no politics.
