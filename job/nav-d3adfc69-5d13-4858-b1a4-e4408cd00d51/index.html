Janitor About the role As a Janitor, you will be an important part of our team in Bjerkvik. You will help ensure that our buildings, outdoor areas and shared spaces are safe, functional and well maintained, providing a clean and professional working environment for employees, contractors and visitors, while helping create the right conditions for our critical infrastructure. This is a daytime position with regular working hours.   You will support the day-to-day upkeep of the site, carry out practical maintenance tasks and provide hands-on support to the people working at and visiting the facility.   What you'll be doing ●      Inspect and help maintain buildings, outdoor areas and shared spaces ●      Carry out basic maintenance and minor repairs ●      Take care of snow clearing, gritting and general upkeep around the site ●      Manage waste and recycling ●      Welcome contractors and suppliers and help ensure that site rules and procedures are followed ●      Prepare meeting rooms and provide practical support to employees and visitors ●      Report hazards and maintenance needs and follow our HSE procedures   About you ●      Previous experience as a janitor, caretaker, facilities assistant, maintenance worker or in a similar role, particularly in an industrial, energy or technical environment, is an advantage ●      You are practically minded and comfortable using hand tools ●      You are reliable, take responsibility and have a practical, solution-oriented approach ●      You have a strong HSE mindset and always put safety first ●      You are comfortable working both indoors and outdoors in all weather conditions   What we can offer ●      Competitive salary and benefits ●      A secure, long-term position in a growing industry ●      Training and development opportunities ●      A safe, inclusive and supportive working environment   Equal Opportunities Statement We are committed to fostering an inclusive, diverse, and equitable workplace. We believe that a variety of perspectives enriches our work environment, and we encourage applications from candidates of all backgrounds, experiences, and abilities.  We strongly encourage applications from people of colour, the LGBTQ&#43; community, people with disabilities, neurodivergent people, parents, carers, and people from lower socio-economic backgrounds. If there’s anything we can do to accommodate your specific situation, please let us know. About Nordscale Nordscale is the specialist operator for mission-critical AI data centers. We manage the operations that keep AI compute sites running reliably, efficiently, and safely. Founded as a strategic partnership between the AI hyperscaler Nscale and Norwegian energy company Nordkraft, we harness local expertise and data center operations experience to deliver service excellence to our customers, while building skilled local jobs and partnering with Norwegian businesses to create lasting value in the regions where we work. Our culture combines local Northern Norwegian values, international expertise, and disciplined operational excellence. We’re a team built on trust, ownership, and collaboration: we communicate openly, take accountability for outcomes, and work as one team across sites, partners, and functions to deliver performance people can depend on.
