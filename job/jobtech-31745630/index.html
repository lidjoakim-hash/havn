IPR Coordinator

Valmet Aktiebolag · Karlstad, Sweden

Do you enjoy precise, well-organized work, and are you curious about how innovations are protected? Are you curious about technology, innovation, and how groundbreaking ideas are protected? Join our global IPR team as an IPR Coordinator and become a key part of the journey from invention disclosure to granted patents around the world. In this role, you will work at the intersection of technology, intellectual property, and international collaboration, connecting inventors, patent engineers, R&D, Legal teams, and external patent agents. You will help ensure that valuable innovations are captured, protected, and managed efficiently while contributing to process development and data quality within a global organization. This role offers flexibility in location and can be based at one of our main offices in Sweden or Finland, with a preference for Karlstad in Sweden. Your responsibilities You will coordinate invention disclosures, register new matters in our IP management system, and work closely with inventors, patent engineers, and R&D teams throughout the innovation process. You will manage patent, trademark, utility model, and design filings by collaborating with inventors, external patent agents, and international stakeholders to ensure deadlines are met. You will maintain and develop our patent portfolio, monitor deadlines, coordinate office actions, and help secure high-quality intellectual property protection worldwide. You will support competitor monitoring activities and perform searches in international patent databases to help provide valuable patent information. You will manage IPR-related documentation, agreements, invoices, reporting, and data quality while supporting continuous improvements within the IPR function. Expectations for you You have a relevant post-secondary education and/or experience in administration, coordination, or a similar role. You have experience from IPR, legal services, or technology-intensive environments, or a strong motivation to develop expertise within intellectual property. You are comfortable working with digital systems, data management, reporting, and process improvement activities. You have strong organizational skills and the ability to manage multiple priorities while maintaining accuracy and attention to detail. You have advanced written and spoken Swedish and English proficiency. To succeed in this role Success in this position requires organizational skills, a structured way of working, and a strong commitment to meeting deadlines. You are comfortable coordinating stakeholders simultaneously and enjoy working in an international environment where accuracy and collaboration are essential. Your curiosity about technology and intellectual property, combined with a service-minded approach and strong digital skills, will help you create value across the organization. We also expect you to align with Valmet’s Leadership Principles—practical behaviours that guide how we lead, collaborate, and succeed together: Take accountability – Act with ownership, set clear expectations, and follow through Lead with courage – Champion change, try bold ideas, and learn quickly Build on trust – Listen to understand, be open, and create psychological safety Collaborate for growth – Work across boundaries, value diverse perspectives, and focus on customer value We offer You will join a global IPR function that works closely with R&D, Legal, and business teams across multiple countries. You will gain hands-on insight into the world of patents, trademarks, and innovation protection while working alongside experienced colleagues who are eager to share their knowledge. The role provides opportunities to develop your IPR expertise, improve processes and systems, and contribute to how intellectual property is managed globally.
