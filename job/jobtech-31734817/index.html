AI is changing not only what software we build, but how we build it. As our HiveAI Engineering Manager, you will lead the team at the centre of that shift. You will combine people leadership, strong engineering judgement and AI-assisted development to turn ideas into production-ready products faster. This is an opportunity to help shape both a product and the way of working around it. You will build and lead a small, high-trust development team where AI accelerates engineering without compromising quality, security or maintainability. About the role As Engineering Manager for HiveAI, you lead the people and shape how the team works and delivers. You will lead an international team with developers based across several Fellowmind countries, so building trust, alignment and a strong team culture across locations will be an important part of your leadership. You will work as a peer alongside our Product Manager and Chief Architect, each with a clear but closely connected area of ownership. The Product Manager drives the why and what. The Chief Architect sets the technical direction and guardrails. You lead the team and create the conditions for great engineering. The way we build matters. The HiveAI team works from problems and hypotheses, using AI to move quickly from idea to experiment and prototype. What proves valuable is then hardened into secure, scalable and maintainable software. You will help define what this way of working looks like in practice as the team grows. You will lead a team that brings together platform developers, who build and maintain HiveAI itself, and Frontier Fellows (Forward Deployed Engineers), who work close to our implementation teams and customers and bring what they learn and build back into the product. Your job is to bring these two perspectives together as one engineering team. What you will do: Build and lead the team. Own hiring, 1:1s, development and performance conversations, and build a strong, high-trust engineering culture across a distributed international team. Design the way we deliver. Drive fast build-and-learn cycles, remove impediments and favour working demos and clear ship, change or stop decisions. Turn prototypes into products. Together with the Chief Architect, make sure promising experiments cross the line into production with the engineering quality they need. Make AI a real engineering capability. Coach developers in prompting, coding agents, AI-assisted testing and code review, and continuously evolve how the team works with AI. Protect engineering understanding. AI should make engineers faster and help them learn, not become a substitute for understanding the systems they build. Keep the focus on outcomes. Build a team that makes good decisions about what to build, how to build it and when AI is, and is not, the right tool. Who are you? You are a people leader who still thinks like an engineer. You probably spent a significant part of your career as a software developer before moving into technical and people leadership. Today, you might be an Engineering Manager, Software Development Manager or a Tech Lead with people responsibility, ideally in a SaaS or software product company. You are comfortable when the destination is clearer than the route to get there. You coach and enable your team, make good decisions with incomplete information and know when a fast prototype is good enough to learn from, but not good enough to ship. We believe that you bring: Extensive professional experience in software development, followed by at least 2–3 years of people leadership, either as an Engineering Manager or as a Tech Lead with direct people responsibility. Hands-on experience using AI coding tools and agents, such as GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Claude Code or similar, as part of real software development.
