About Outreach   Outreach, founded in 2014, is the only complete agentic AI platform for revenue teams. Outreach infuses agentic AI, conversation intelligence, and assistive AI to power hundreds of use cases across revenue motions. From new logo prospecting to expansions, deal acceleration, driving retention, and forecasting, Outreach AI automates workflows and frees sellers to focus on more strategic conversations and actions. Revenue leaders benefit from connected account visibility, performance insights, and higher forecasting accuracy across every GTM team. World leading enterprise organizations use Outreach to power their revenue teams, including Databricks, SAP, Siemens, and Verizon to name a few.  About the Team Our AI Advisors are where the promise of the platform becomes real for our customers. We act as trusted advisors — to our customers and to each other — partnering across Sales, Product, Marketing, and Professional Services as one team working towards the same outcome: customers who see measurable value and keep coming back for more. AI is part of how we get there — sharpening the insights we bring to every customer conversation. We lead with a customer-first mindset, take ownership of the results we commit to, and bring the curiosity to keep learning as the way our customers' work continues to evolve. The Role We're looking for a strategic, data-driven Senior GTM AI Advisor to partner with customers in maximising value from our platform — leveraging AI to drive efficiency, adoption, and business outcomes. This role blends traditional CSM responsibilities with a strong focus on AI enablement, insights, and automation. AI is transforming how our customers operate, and this role makes sure they adopt it while they realise its full value, moving from manual processes to intelligent, automated workflows that drive real business impact. Success in this role shows up in the numbers our customers and Outreach both care about; Net Revenue Retention, Gross Retention, and AI feature adoption; because when customers realise value, growth follows.
