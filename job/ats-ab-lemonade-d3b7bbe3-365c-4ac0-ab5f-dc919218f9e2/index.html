We're looking for an Actuary to help us reinvent how actuarial work gets done — with AI, automation, and a genuine drive to find smarter ways forward. Based in Amsterdam, you'll sit at the intersection of finance and insurance, owning core reserving and regulatory work while actively building the faster, leaner workflows that will carry Lemonade's European expansion. We believe three things matter for every role at Lemonade: drive to push through challenges, efficiency that keeps standards high while moving fast, and adaptability that lets you pivot with data and AI insights. These aren't buzzwords, they're how we actually work. Our AI-first approach isn't just a tagline either. We're building the future of insurance with AI at the center, and we need people who are genuinely excited to learn and grow alongside these tools. In this role you'll - Own reserving processes and Solvency II calculations, ensuring accuracy and timeliness across all regulatory submissions - Contribute to key financial reporting deliverables — including SFCR, RSR, ORSA, and QRTs — working closely with the wider team to meet regulatory deadlines - Build and refine AI-driven workflows that make traditional actuarial work faster, leaner, and more scalable - Identify trends in data, translate them into clear insights, and communicate findings that inform real business decisions - Continuously raise the bar on actuarial compliance and professional standards as Lemonade grows across Europe What you'll need - 4+ years of non-life actuarial experience, ideally with a focus on personal lines - A Master's degree in Actuarial Science, Econometrics, or a related field - Hands-on experience with reserving and Solvency II capital requirement calculations - Familiarity with data science tools like Python or R, and a genuine comfort working alongside AI and automation - Experience in financial reporting for regulated insurers - The ability to take complex results and communicate them clearly to management and stakeholders - Excellent written and verbal English — this is a must - Ready to work in an office environment most days of the week
