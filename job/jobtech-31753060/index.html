IT Support Technician for an International MedTech Company!

Academic Work Sweden AB · Stockholm, Sweden

Are you a service-oriented problem solver? On behalf of our client, we are currently looking for an IT Support Technician in Stockholm for a 1-year temporary position. Become a key player at a global MedTech company whose tools make a difference in healthcare. Apply today! About the role Our client is an established and innovative Swedish medical technology company that develops digital decision-support tools to facilitate and ensure quality in assessments and treatment follow-ups within neuropsychiatry. With headquarters in Stockholm and operations in the UK and the USA, among other countries, the company is characterized by an open, multicultural, and family-like atmosphere where technology and societal benefit go hand in hand. As one of the colleagues on the team is going on study leave, we are now looking for a replacement for a one-year temporary position to secure daily IT delivery, starting with a thorough handover in December. In the role of IT Support Technician, you will serve as the hub for daily technical support at the Stockholm office, while also supporting colleagues at the London office remotely. You will work broadly with 1st line support (with the opportunity to take on 2nd line tickets as you grow into the role) in a mixed Windows and macOS environment. At the Stockholm office, there is an open "digital workplace" area where employees can drop by for quick assistance, making you a visible and appreciated point of contact for the entire organization. In addition to your colleagues at the office, you will collaborate closely with your own international IT team—consisting of colleagues in Stockholm, the UK, and the USA—holding weekly check-ins to distribute workload and drive tickets forward. You are offered: An international and welcoming workplace where you contribute to products that make a real difference for both healthcare providers and patients. The opportunity to work broadly in a modern Microsoft environment with great prospects for technical development toward 2nd line support and automation. A solid introduction at our client's company with a structured and thorough handover from your current colleague. Work tasks Daily Support & Ticket Management: Provide 1st line IT support (and 2nd line, depending on experience) on-site in Stockholm and remotely for the UK, as well as manage incoming tickets in Jira Service Management. User & Device Management: Take responsibility for onboarding and offboarding, hardware and license management (asset management), and administration in Microsoft 365, Entra ID (SSO), and Intune. Troubleshooting & IT Security: Troubleshoot in Windows and macOS, trace phishing emails in Microsoft Defender, escalate incidents, and work with existing scripts in PowerShell and Microsoft Graph. We are looking for Has 3–5 years of experience working in IT support or a similar IT service role. Has a strong technical understanding and practical experience working in a Microsoft environment, including Microsoft 365, Entra ID, Intune, and Defender. Has strong troubleshooting skills in both Windows and macOS. Has a strong security mindset and understands the importance of regulatory compliance and data security. Has very good proficiency in English, both spoken and written, as it is the corporate language and you will communicate daily with colleagues in the UK and the USA. Knowledge of Swedish is a plus. It is considered meritorious if you have: Experience with ticket management in Jira Service Management. Basic knowledge of PowerShell, Microsoft Graph, or AI tools to streamline workflows. Previous experience from a regulated industry, such as MedTech or Life Science. To succeed in this role, you have the following personal qualities: We place great emphasis on your personal qualities and are looking for someone who is distinctly service-oriented, helpful, and unpretentious.
