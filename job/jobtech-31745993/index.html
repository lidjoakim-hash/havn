UX Designer

Your New Role We are looking for a UX Designer for an exciting two-month assignment within a major technology transformation. The focus is on modernising customer service by designing a unified web-based workspace for customer service agents. In this role, you will take ownership of the end-to-end UX design process, from user research and workflow analysis to prototyping, usability testing and final design handover. You will conduct interviews with customer service agents, identify pain points and translate insights into intuitive user journeys, wireframes and high-fidelity prototypes. Working independently as the primary designer, you will collaborate closely with developers, technical leads and stakeholders to ensure the final solution is both user-friendly and technically feasible. You will deliver an implementation-ready Figma design, including documented components, interactions and design decisions. Company Presentation Our client is a well-established international retail company undergoing an exciting technology transformation. With a strong focus on customer experience, innovation and digital development, the company continuously works to improve its systems, processes and ways of working. Good to Know Scope of employment: Full-time, 100% Type of contract: Fixed-term employment as a consultant via JobBusters Working model: Hybrid Salary: According to agreement Start date: Immediately End date: Two months from start, with the possibility of extension To succeed in this role, you will need: At least four years of professional experience in UX or Product Design. Experience designing web-based B2B applications, enterprise solutions or internal tools. Strong skills in user research, interviews, workflow analysis and usability testing. Experience creating user flows, wireframes and high-fidelity prototypes. Excellent knowledge of Figma and experience collaborating with developers on implementation-ready designs. Ability to work independently, manage priorities and communicate design decisions effectively. A pragmatic, structured and proactive approach. What we Offer Secure employment with a collective bargaining agreement, insurance coverage and occupational pension Wellness allowance and employee discounts and offers via Benifex (e.g., health, leisure, transport and healthcare) Flex pension and access to the Lifeplan pension advisory service Additional compensation during parental leave Occupational health services Long-term assignments and a dedicated Consultant Manager for personal support Opportunities to build valuable experience, expand your network and grow your future career We look forward to receiving your application  We review applications on an ongoing basis. As recruitment processes in the consulting industry can move quickly, the position may be filled before the advertised deadline — so we encourage you to apply as soon as possible. You do not need to submit a cover letter. Instead, please respond to the screening questions included in the application process.
