Job Overview Job Description Overview Assignment Description We are seeking an experienced AI Engineer to develop AI-powered solutions for a wiring harness engineering organization within the automotive industry. The assignment focuses on designing and implementing AI tools that support engineers working with technical drawings, 3D models, electrical schematics, and engineering documentation. The initial phase is a 3-month pre-study to understand the engineering environment, available data sources, and existing workflows. The objective is to identify valuable AI use cases, assess technical feasibility, and establish a roadmap for a potential full-scale AI engineering platform. What You Will Work On Design, build, and implement AI-powered engineering tools. Develop solutions for analyzing technical drawings, 3D models, electrical schematics, and engineering documentation. Support the review and validation of drawings and schematics using AI. Identify potential design issues, inconsistencies, and improvement opportunities. Assess available data sources, engineering workflows, and technical feasibility. Identify and validate high-value AI use cases. Define a roadmap for an AI-powered engineering platform. Collaborate with engineering and software teams to improve quality, efficiency, and development cycles. What You Bring Several years of experience building digital solutions using AI technologies. Strong background in software development and system integration. Hands-on experience with AI/ML, Generative AI, or related technologies. Degree in Mechanical Engineering, Mechatronics, or a related discipline, or equivalent experience. Experience with technical drawings, CAD systems, or product development environments. Strong communication skills and the ability to bridge engineering and software domains. Previous experience in the automotive industry. Meritorious Experience in wiring harness design and development. Knowledge of CAD tools such as CATIA, Creo, NX, or similar. Experience with schematic design tools such as E3, Capital, Zuken, or Saber. Familiarity with PLM systems and product data management. Experience with Computer Vision and document intelligence solutions. Experience developing AI solutions for engineering, manufacturing, or automotive environments.
