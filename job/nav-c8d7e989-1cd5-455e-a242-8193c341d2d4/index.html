Senior UX/Service Designer in Oda Systems

Oda · Oslo, Norway

Lønn ikke oppgittHeltidvia NAVFrist 28.11.2026

The opportunity You'll design the tools that get groceries onto millions of doorsteps: not what shoppers see, but the systems used by people working in logistics operations at our fulfillment centers. Oda’s mission is to build the world’s most efficient retail system, so people have space for life. A huge part of that is our sophisticated fulfillment and delivery system, designed and built entirely by us. It packs and delivers nearly half a million items every day to around 80.000 homes and businesses across Norway and Sweden. What we’ve built is second-to-none in the world when it comes to efficiency, and it’s held together by internal tools and processes that make the jobs of operators and drivers easy, fast, ergonomic, and smooth. That's where you come in. You'll work in close collaboration with our colleagues in both Norway and at our sister company Mathem in Stockholm, and you’ll help us on the path to launching Oda Systems in new markets as we find customers who want to work with us to bring the most efficient solution for online groceries to more parts of the world. The role Service/UX designers play a major part in the design of our processes and tooling. Great processes are as much about ergonomics, workflow, physical space, team structures, and incentives as they are about screens. You’ll get the chance to not only work on software solutions but also consider how digital experiences should best interact with hardware/machinery, the physical space they’re situated in, and last but not least the operator routines around them. That combination is what makes logistics design at Oda different from most UX roles: you're never just designing a screen, you're designing a multi-dimensional workflow. You’ll join the Delivery team within Oda’s Logistics platform, which is responsible for everything that happens out on the road. You’ll design the best possible experience for employees working with everything from capacity and route planning to traffic coordination, delivering groceries to customers, and leaders following up drivers and operations. While we’re happy with today’s processes, we’re not satisfied: there is a ton to be done to make things more efficient, more automated, and smarter, so that we have even better control over capacity, drivers have a better workday, and customers get their food on time and in full. You'll work embedded in a cross-functional team, closely collaborating with other disciplines – Product Management, Engineering, Data and Insight, and Logistics development. This is a hands-on, office-based role. You'll be at our Fulfillment Center in Lier regularly, and at our Tøyen office most of the week (most of the team is at Tøyen at least 3 days a week). You’ll use AI actively in your processes, and contribute to driving agentic UX forwards. What you'll do in Oda • Lead big projects from start to finish: own them from the initial idea through concept, design, prototyping, implementation, testing, and follow-up after launch, working closely with your product team, as well as stakeholders in Logistics. You should feel comfortable putting on the PM hat and driving projects. • Conduct UX research and data analysis: observe what’s going on in operations, run workshops with your team and stakeholders, look at the data, gather feedback in operations. • Communicate and present your work to peers as well as stakeholders, showing your process, and the impact of your designs. • Map and model complex, systemic workflows. • Design user interfaces and build prototypes. You don’t have to be a pixel perfectionist, but you will draw screens. Prototype in Figma, or directly in code – whatever’s the fastest way to test an idea, with room to ship and test changes in production alongside developers. • Work closely with teammates and stakeholders all the way from ideation to roll-out, rather than handing off at the end of a solo-design process. • Contribute to setting the direction of the Delivery team, which determines its own projects based on company strategy • Work with other designers to spar, give feedback, and build a stronger design community across Oda. What we're looking for &#x1f31f; • At least 8 years of experience in UX, interaction, or service design, in-house or agency, with a portfolio showing full ownership from insight to shipped product • A full-stack design attitude: insight, concept, structure, and visual design, without waiting for handovers. Being open to being hands-on both on the warehouse floor and in our codebase. • Very solid fundamentals in product sense and design: demonstrated insight-centric process, root cause analysis, work framed in desired outcomes, ability to distill complexity. • Speed over perfection: You understand the importance of shipping fast, learning, and iterating over polishing a &#34;perfect&#34; blueprint. You leverage the advantage we have with having short feedback loops with our end-users. • A track record of driving process change: not just shipping a solution, but bringing the operators who'll use it along with you. • You already use AI as part of your own design process: you're genuinely interested in an AI-enhanced product development workflow, not just AI features bolted onto Figma. • Fluent English. Intermediate Norwegian or Swedish is a strong plus, since you'll be working directly with traffic coordinators, drivers, and leaders on-site. • Previous logistics experience is a plus, not a requirement. What we offer &#x1f680; • A flexible schedule built around a healthy work-life balance. • Modern, empowered product teams: democratized data access, good tools, a sustainable pace. We ship, and you will immediately see the impact of your work. • A team that's actively working AI into both design and development, not just talking about it, and genuinely open to designers who want to prototype or write code. • A design community across all Oda teams: UX design, Service design, Digital design, UX research, Content
