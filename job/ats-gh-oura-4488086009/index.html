Oura is looking for a Senior Product Designer to help make our international DTC experience first-class. This is an e-commerce-focused product design role with a clear growth mandate. You will take global DTC product work and make it relevant and compelling for non-US markets. That means more than translating pages or swapping images. You will design the local experience across content, creative, merchandising, experiments, payments, shipping, and checkout.You will partner closely with Product, Engineering, Creative, Marketing, Analytics, Research, and market stakeholders. You will help us learn what should be consistent across markets, what should be adapted locally, and where local customer needs should change the product itself.This role reports to the Director of Product Design, Commerce. What You Will Do: • Adapt global DTC journeys for international markets, balancing shared foundations with local customer needs, behaviors, and cultural context. • Design market-specific experiences across discovery, merchandising, product evaluation, product detail, purchase, membership, and post-purchase. • Partner with local market, Creative, and Marketing teams to localize campaigns, imagery, content, offers, and brand expression. • Translate local customer insights, competitive patterns, and business needs into global product decisions and reusable market-launch patterns. • Identify opportunities to improve conversion, revenue, average order value, attach rate, and customer confidence across international funnels; seek to reduce time to purchase cycle • Define hypotheses, success metrics, and experiments across acquisition, landing pages, product pages, bundles, upsells, membership, and checkout. • Use qualitative research, quantitative data, funnel analysis, customer feedback, and experiment results to prioritize work and guide product decisions. • Design and evolve cart and checkout experiences that support local payment methods, currencies, taxes, duties, shipping, delivery estimates, returns, and regional requirements. • Create clear experiences for availability, inventory constraints, payment and authentication states, order confirmation, and other operational edge cases. • Ensure localization improves relevance without changing the meaning of the transaction, price, product, or order state. • Produce user flows, prototypes, high-fidelity designs, and implementation-ready specifications within Oura’s design system, responsive, and accessibility standards. • Partner with Product, Engineering, Analytics, Operations, Payments, and Customer Care through critique, implementation, QA, launch, and post-launch iteration.
