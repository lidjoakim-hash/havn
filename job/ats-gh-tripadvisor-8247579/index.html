We leverage our brands, technology, and capabilities to connect our global audience with partners through rich content, travel guidance, and two-sided marketplaces for experiences, accommodations, restaurants, and other travel categories. The subsidiaries of Tripadvisor, Inc. (Nasdaq: TRIP), include a portfolio of travel brands and businesses, including Tripadvisor, Viator, and TheFork. About Bokun: Bókun, a Tripadvisor company, is the market-leading booking and channel management platform for the tours and activities industry. Thousands of Experience Operators around the world run their business on Bókun – from independent tour guides to multi-location attraction groups. We combine the reach and stability of a global leader with the speed and focus of a team that’s still building. About The Role: We’re creating a new Business Development Executive role to close the gap between how Experience Operators discover Bókun and how they grow with us. Today, customers either self-serve their way in or land with a dedicated Business Development Manager once they’re a Key Account. This role owns everything in between. You’ll own, grow and retain a portfolio of accounts with real potential – nurturing them over time and helping them get more value from Bókun. Some will grow into Key Accounts. Others will make this tier their long-term home, and need consistent account management and retention through our touchpoint programme – both are equally part of the job. You’ll also go after new business beyond our self-serve ICP: the accounts that need a conversation, not just a sign-up form. And you’ll work alongside our Business Development Managers to help qualify new potential enterprise opportunities. It’s a role built for someone who’s equally comfortable prospecting cold leads and nurturing warm opportunities – patient enough to play the long game with an account, and driven enough to go and win the next one. Wherever each account is headed, they deserve someone who takes real ownership of getting them there. Job Location: Hybrid This role is a hybrid position that requires 2/3 days per week/month in our Iceland office.
