Saab is a place where teamwork makes things fly. Literally. Join us and experience our supportive and friendly workplace. We are looking for a leader to take responsibility for Infrastructure & Developer Solutions, a key capability area within Aeronautics IT organization. The area is responsible for the delivery and continued development of secure and resilient infrastructure, cloud capabilities and integrated developer solutions that enable teams to build, deploy and operate digital products efficiently. Our infrastructure spans modern cloud and platform environments as well as highly secure and restricted environments with specific requirements on security, access, architecture and operations. As Head of Infrastructure & Developer Solutions, you will set the direction and drive the development of these capabilities, ensuring close alignment between business needs, technology enablement and operational delivery. The role combines transformation with operational responsibility. You will drive modernization, automation, standardization and improved developer experience while ensuring stable, secure and reliable service delivery. An important part of the role is to balance the opportunities of modern technology with the requirements that come with operating business-critical and security-sensitive infrastructure. You will lead through other leaders and work closely with other areas within the IT organization, Group IT and stakeholders across the business to ensure cohesion between strategic direction and operational execution. In this role, you will Lead and develop the Infrastructure & Developer Solutions organization and its leadership team. Set direction and drive roadmaps for infrastructure, cloud and developer capabilities. Ensure secure, resilient and reliable infrastructure services across both standard and restricted environments. Drive modernization of infrastructure and platform capabilities while maintaining operational stability. Strengthen developer enablement through integrated tools, platforms and services. Drive automation, standardization, interoperability and self-service where appropriate. Develop the competencies and capabilities required to meet future business and technology demands. Your profile We are looking for an experienced leader with a strong background in complex IT or technology environments and experience from infrastructure, cloud, platform engineering, DevOps or developer services. You have a track record of driving transformation while maintaining stable and reliable operational delivery. You are comfortable leading through other leaders, setting direction across multiple technical domains and translating business needs into technology capabilities. You are a relationship-oriented leader who creates alignment and collaboration across organizational boundaries. Experience from regulated, security-sensitive or highly controlled environments is an advantage, but not a prerequisite. This position requires that you pass a security vetting based on the current regulations around security protection. For positions requiring security clearance, additional obligations regarding citizenship may apply. What you will be part of Explore a wealth of possibilities.
