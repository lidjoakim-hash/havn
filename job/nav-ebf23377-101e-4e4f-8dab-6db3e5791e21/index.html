This position offers the opportunity to join the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA) Doctoral Network GLASS-CARE, collaborate with academic, museums, and industry partners across Europe, and develop expertise within a multidisciplinary research project on cultural heritage science. About the project About the GLASS-CARE project  GLASS-CARE (Green Light for Advanced Solutions in Stained-glass Conservation and Application of Research and Education) is a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA) Doctoral Network that brings together leading European universities, research institutions, and industry partners to train the next generation of heritage scientists. The project focuses on understanding, documenting, and monitoring change in cultural heritage using advanced technologies, including imaging, materials analysis, artificial intelligence, and data science. Through an interdisciplinary and international research environment, GLASS-CARE equips doctoral candidates with the skills needed to address current and future challenges in cultural heritage conservation and preservation. For more details, visit http://www.glasscare.eu/ About the DC project Within the GLASS-CARE project, the individual research project associated with this position (DC13) is entitled Multi-sensor data fusion for digital representation. The PhD project will investigate computational approaches for integrating data acquired through hyperspectral imaging (HSI), multispectral imaging (MSI), X-ray fluorescence (XRF), and photoacoustic techniques. The resulting computational framework will contribute to improved material characterization, condition assessment, and conservation decision-making for historical stained glass. The PhD candidate will address the following objectives (1) Multimodal data integration (2) Multimodal registration, alignment and data fusion (3) Feature extraction and dimensionality reduction (4) Unified digital representations (6) Validation through case studies. Expected results: (1)  A computational framework for integrating heterogeneous imaging and analytical data into unified digital representations of stained glass. (2) Advanced data fusion methods to minimize redundancy, preserve complementary information, and reveal hidden patterns across different imaging modalities. (3) Deliver validated computational models and tools for improved material characterization, visualisation, condition assessment, and conservation decision-making.
