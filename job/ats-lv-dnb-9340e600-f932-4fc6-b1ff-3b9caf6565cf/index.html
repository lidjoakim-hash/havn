We are now looking for a Senior Accountant to be based in Soborg, Denmark, who will be responsible for leading key accounting operations, financial reporting, statutory compliance, audit coordination and control activities for assigned Danish entities and businesses. The role will support both US GAAP and local GAAP reporting requirements, partner closely with local, regional and global stakeholders, and help drive process standardisation, automation and continuous improvement as the Finance organisation evolves. Key Responsibilities: • Prepare and coordinate statutory financial statements, regulatory filings and the annual audit process for Danish entities, ensuring compliance with local GAAP, US GAAP and statutory requirements • Manage local compliance activities, including VAT, tax, payroll accounting and other statutory reporting requirements • Deliver accurate financial reporting and analysis to local, regional and global stakeholders, including commentary on key business results and variances • Complete US GAAP month-end close activities and maintain accurate US GAAP to local GAAP reconciliations • Maintain a strong control environment, including SOX controls, balance sheet reconciliations and follow-up of audit actions. • Support revenue accounting activities, including billings, dunning, system reconciliations and related account controls • Provide technical accounting guidance on transactions, regulatory developments, system changes and wider business initiatives • Drive finance transformation and continuous improvement, including process simplification, automation, system enhancements and standardisation • Partner with Finance, Operations, Sales, FP&A, Tax and other teams to provide accounting guidance and support business needs • Contribute to knowledge sharing and collaboration across the accounting team, supporting a high-performance and continuous-improvement culture About you: • Degree in Accounting, Finance, Auditing, or a related discipline • 5+ years’ experience in accounting, financial reporting, or statutory compliance within a complex business environment • Strong knowledge of local statutory accounting requirements, tax regulations, and compliance obligations • Experience supporting audits, financial reporting, and regulatory filings • Knowledge of taxation, transfer pricing, and intercompany accounting would be advantageous • Proficiency with ERP and financial systems such as SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, or Workday • Advanced Excel skills, with the ability to analyse large datasets and produce complex reports • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with excellent attention to detail • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to build effective relationships across the organisation • Ability to manage competing priorities and adapt effectively in a fast-paced environment • Proactive, accountable, and solutions-focused, with a strong sense of ownership and a continuous improvement mindset • Fluent in English and Danish, both written and spoken
