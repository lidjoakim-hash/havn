About the Role Hykmann Global Business Solutions AB is looking for motivated Embedded Firmware and Software Developers to contribute to the development of advanced electronic systems for defence applications, including radar and missile electronics. You will join a multidisciplinary engineering team working on high-performance embedded firmware and software where reliability, processing performance and functional correctness are essential. The role covers the full embedded development lifecycle, from early requirements analysis and technical design through implementation, hardware integration, testing and verification. You will collaborate closely with system engineers, subsystem engineers and technical specialists, helping translate system-level requirements into robust firmware and embedded software solutions. We welcome candidates at different career stages, from engineers developing their specialist skills to experienced professionals who can take ownership of complex technical activities. Key Responsibilities Develop robust and high-performance embedded firmware and software for advanced electronic systems. Implement FPGA-based solutions using VHDL, C++ or comparable hardware-oriented programming languages. Collaborate with system engineers and stakeholders to analyse requirements and define technical solutions. Design, develop and maintain embedded software and firmware components. Integrate firmware and software with hardware platforms and complex system environments. Perform testing, debugging and verification, including testing in laboratory hardware environments. Investigate technical issues and support troubleshooting across hardware and software interfaces. Apply modern software development practices to maintain quality, reliability and maintainability. Contribute to technical design decisions and continuous engineering improvements. Participate in agile development activities and collaborate across multidisciplinary teams. Required Qualifications & Technical Skills Education Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Engineering Physics, Computer Science or a related technical discipline. Embedded Software & Firmware Development Experience in embedded firmware or software development. Experience with FPGA development using VHDL, C++ or a comparable language. Understanding of embedded systems and hardware/software interaction. Ability to develop and debug software for complex electronic systems. System Integration & Verification Experience working with complex technical systems. Understanding of firmware and software integration with hardware. Ability to perform testing, debugging and verification in laboratory or hardware-based environments. Strong problem-solving skills and attention to technical detail. Development Practices Understanding of structured software development practices. Ability to collaborate effectively with engineering teams and technical stakeholders.
