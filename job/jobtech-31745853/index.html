Join our team as a Logistic Process Quality Operator and help us deliver exceptional quality across our distribution network. This hands-on role puts you at the heart of our operations, ensuring every product meets the standards our customers expect.   What will you do? Drive quality culture across distribution sites through hands-on process control and quality assurance Identify logistics and manufacturing issues and coordinate with stakeholders to implement corrective actions Improve process robustness to minimize quality risks and protect customer satisfaction Oversee quality standards for rework and adaptation lines using disciplined change management practices What are the key skills we are looking for? Quality Control & Inspection — developing level; conducting inspections to ensure product and process standards are met Continuous Improvement — developing level; identifying opportunities to enhance logistics processes and reduce defects Problem Solving — developing level; analyzing quality issues and recommending practical solutions Collaboration — developing level; coordinating with cross-functional teams to resolve quality concerns Customer Orientation — developing level; maintaining focus on how quality impacts customer satisfaction Quality Authority — developing level; applying quality standards and procedures in distribution environments Report Writing — developing level; documenting quality findings and improvement actions clearly Coaching — developing level; supporting team members in adopting quality best practices What qualifications will make you successful? Sharp attention to detail and a commitment to upholding high standards every day Proactive approach to identifying and addressing quality issues before they grow Strong communication skills to work effectively across teams and functions Flexibility and eagerness to learn new quality processes and improvement techniques What's in it for me? Impactful role where your efforts directly enhance customer satisfaction and brand reputation Clear pathways for skill development and career growth in quality and operations Supportive team culture that encourages collaboration and values your contributions Training opportunities to expand your expertise in quality management and continuous improvement Ready to build a career in quality?
