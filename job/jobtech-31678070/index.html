Do you want to shape how AI creates value for customers, colleagues and businesses? At Addovation, AI is already part of our everyday work in delivery, support, sales and in the solutions we build for our customers. We're now looking for a AI Director who wants to take our AI journey to the next level and play a key role in driving business impact and innovations. With a clear mandate from the leadership team and sponsorship across the business, you will have the opportunity to turn AI ambitions into measurable business outcomes. About the role AI is transforming the way businesses operate, and at Addovation we see tremendous opportunities to help both our customers and our own organization benefit from that change. As AI Director, you will lead the direction of AI across Addovation. You will be responsible for identifying opportunities, turning ideas into scalable solutions, and ensuring AI becomes a natural part of the way we work and deliver value. This is a strategic role with a strong operational element, where you will collaborate closely with business leaders, consultants, developers and customers across the organization. You will lead Addovation's AI Council, bringing together stakeholders from across the organization to align priorities, accelerate adoption and ensure business value. You will have the opportunity to influence both our initial AI adaption and the AI-powered services we bring to the market. What you'll do As AI Director, you will: - Define and drive Addovation’s AI strategy, roadmap and priorities. - Define the direction for AI platforms, data foundations and AI agent architectures, ensuring solutions can be deployed securely and at scale. - Drive an AI-first way of working across delivery, sales and operations, helping teams embed AI naturally into daily work while taking advantage of “old school” competencies and capabilities where suitable. - Work closely with business units to develop AI-powered offerings and services within areas such as IFS, NetSuite, Oracle and Microsoft technologies. - Lead and coordinate AI initiatives across functions, ensuring business goals and technology capabilities align. - Drive AI adoption internally by enabling teams with tools, knowledge and best practices. - Establish governance, security and compliance frameworks for responsible AI usage. - Collaborate with IT and security teams to ensure AI solutions meet regulatory and organizational requirements. - Build and lead a network of AI Ambassadors who support adoption, knowledge sharing and experimentation throughout the organisation. - Represent Addovation externally in customer dialogues, partnerships, industry forums and market activities. - Create value through new and enforced commercial models Is this you? We believe you are passionate about how technology can create meaningful business outcomes. You combine strategic thinking with a hands-on mindset and enjoy turning opportunities into practical solutions. You are a strong communicator who can engage with both technical specialists and business stakeholders. You are curious, forward-thinking and motivated by helping others succeed through technology. Most importantly, you are excited about the possibilities AI creates while remaining pragmatic about how to generate sustainable and long-term value. What experience you'll likely have You likely have several years of experience working with technology, business systems, digital transformation or consulting. You have experience leading initiatives and bringing together people, processes and technology to achieve business goals.
