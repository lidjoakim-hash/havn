BUILDING THE FUTURE OF OPEN FINANCE Payward - the parent company behind Kraken, NinjaTrader, Breakout, xStocks, Payward Services and CF Benchmarks - has spent the last 15 years building one of the most modern and globally accessible financial infrastructure platforms in the industry, built to advance an open, global financial system. THE TEAM Founded in 2011, Kraken is one of the world's longest-standing crypto platforms, trusted by over 10 million individuals and institutions across the globe. It offers spot trading, margin, futures, staking, and OTC services, with products built for both individual investors and institutional clients. The opportunity Consumer Growth helps customers get more value from the Kraken app. We improve how people discover relevant features, build confidence using them, and keep coming back. We work closely with Product, Design and Data to understand customer behavior, identify opportunities and test ideas. Our engineers help shape what we build. You’ll bring ideas to the table, challenge assumptions and own your work from the initial hypothesis through launch, measurement and iteration. Ship fast experiments, from a couple of days to a couple of weeks, to improve conversion and retention in the Kraken app. - Find funnel drop-offs and turn them into testable ideas, then build, measure and iterate. - Design, develop and maintain high-quality React Native features for our consumer mobile app. - Help customers discover and adopt relevant features through improvements to customer journeys, incentives and A/B tests. - Use AI to build quickly, then review and ship with a high quality bar. - Deliver polished, user-friendly UI that makes the product feel great to use. - Build for correctness, accessibility, performance and reliability, with testing appropriate to the change. - Work closely with Product, Design, Data and Backend Engineering to define hypotheses and measure results. - Instrument your work so the team can evaluate customer behavior and experiment results, and use what you learn to improve, expand or stop an experiment. - Turn successful experiments into reliable, maintainable product experiences. - Proactively spot gaps and opportunities, and take ownership of the outcome.     WHAT YOU BRING - Strong experience building production mobile apps with React Native and TypeScript. - A product and growth mindset, focused on moving metrics rather than just shipping code. - Evidence of product impact: you can explain what you built, why you built it and what you learned after launch. - Curiosity about data, and comfort using analytics tools such as Mixpanel and Segment. - Experience working in a scale-up or other high-growth, fast-moving environment. - Fluency in using AI tools to build, review and ship code, while retaining ownership of engineering decisions and the quality of the result. - A strong eye for UI quality and polish. - A self-directed approach, with the confidence to find problems and fix them without being asked. - Comfort working from an ambiguous customer problem, proposing ideas and finding a small, useful change to test. - Collaborative working style across Product, Design and Engineering. - Strong written and verbal communication skills in English.   NICE TO HAVES - Hands-on experience running A/B tests and experiments, ideally with Statsig or a similar tool. -
