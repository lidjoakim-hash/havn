Entry level: Verification Developer

Ericsson AB · Stockholm, Sweden

About this opportunity We’re looking for a recent graduate to join our team as a verification developer. You’ll be an important member of the team, helping verify the performance of next-generation radio products. The team works across hardware and software, carrying out radio performance, electromagnetic compatibility and environmental testing to help ensure products meet industry and regulatory requirements. You’ll gain hands-on experience in performance verification, with opportunities to contribute to software solutions for the test environment and, over time, explore how AI/ML could improve efficiency and quality. What you will do Carry out performance test activities, including updating the test environment and framework. Select test cases to verify requirements, analyze results and troubleshoot issues. Contribute to software solutions and improvements to the test environment, with potential to apply AI/ML. Collaborate across teams to support product quality and continuous improvement. The skills you bring A bachelor’s or master’s degree in data science, electrical engineering, computer science or an equivalent field. Programming skills in languages such as Java, Python, C or C++. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with an interest in testing, verification and wireless or mobile technologies. Good communication skills and the ability to work collaboratively. A proactive mindset, curiosity and willingness to learn new technologies, tools and processes. Enjoyment of hands-on testing, troubleshooting and solving technical problems in a dynamic environment. A focus on product quality and an interest in helping ensure products meet customer and regulatory requirements. The ability to work collaboratively across teams, communicate clearly and share knowledge. A drive for innovation and continuous improvement. Knowledge of radio frequency technology is beneficial; experience with test automation or verification methods is an advantage. Why join Ericsson?At Ericsson, you´ll have an outstanding opportunity. The chance to use your skills and imagination to push the boundaries of what´s possible. To build solutions never seen before to some of the world’s toughest problems. You´ll be challenged, but you won’t be alone. You´ll be joining a team of diverse innovators, all driven to go beyond the status quo to craft what comes next.
