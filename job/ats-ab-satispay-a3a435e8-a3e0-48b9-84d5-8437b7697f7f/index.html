About us Traditional banking has never really given you control over your financial life. Satispay exists to change that: to empower people through finance. That means making financial services easy and accessible for over 6.5M users. We started with payments, then benefits, investing, cards... and we’re not done. Not even close. We follow the same logic every time: find a real problem, solve it properly, then look for the next one. The destination? To build the most loved financial platform in the world. If you own outcomes rather than tasks, stay close to what users need, and refuse to wait for someone else to fix what's broken — you'll find people here who work exactly in the same way. And together, you'll build something worth being proud of. What you'll be doing As our Finance Regulatory Reporting Specialist in Finance Operations, you'll ensure compliance, accuracy, and timely submission of all mandatory regulatory reporting while transforming and strengthening reporting processes within our scalable fintech infrastructure. Here's what that looks like in practice: - Regulatory Project Management – Lead and support Finance Transformation projects driven by new or updated local and international regulatory reporting frameworks. - Mandatory Reporting Ownership – Take full responsibility for the preparation, review, and timely submission of all mandatory regulatory and statutory reports required by supervisory authorities (e.g., Central Bank of Luxembourg, CSSF, or other relevant regulators). - New Product Compliance & Advisory – Partner closely with Product, Compliance, and Legal teams during new product launches to analyze regulatory impacts, define reporting requirements, and ensure full alignment with local frameworks. - Process Automation & Transformation – Identify opportunities to optimize, automate, and scale reporting workflows, reducing manual intervention and leveraging modern data solutions. - Audit & Supervisory Support – Act as a key point of contact for regulatory authorities, delivering structured, high-quality documentation and methodology notes. Who we're looking for This role needs someone proactive, project-driven, and ready to take full ownership of regulatory reporting domains in an evolving, fast-paced environment. - Experience – 3 to 5 years in regulatory reporting, compliance operations, or financial auditing within the fintech, banking, or financial services sector. - Regulatory Knowledge – Deep understanding of financial regulations and supervisory reporting frameworks (e.g., CSSF, Central Bank of Luxembourg). - Project & Delivery Focus – Proven ability to manage complex projects independently, translate regulatory prose into technical finance requirements, and meet tight deadlines with precision. - Cross-functional Ownership – Strong accountability for assigned reporting streams and seamless collaboration across Product, Data, and Tech teams. - Technical Skills – Proficiency with spreadsheets and comfort working with structured data; experience with business intelligence tools or automation workflows is a strong plus. - Languages – Professional fluency in both English and Italian to collaborate across international teams and local regulatory bodies. Don't worry if you don't tick every box. We believe in the power of different viewpoints and strengths.
