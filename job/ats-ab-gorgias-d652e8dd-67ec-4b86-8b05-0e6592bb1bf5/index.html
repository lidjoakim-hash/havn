Our mission is to empower companies to launch swiftly and effectively on Gorgias, showcasing the exceptional value of our flagship products: Helpdesk and AI Agent. Our team plays a pivotal role in the overall success of our products, influencing customer adoption, usage, and retention. As AI becomes central to how merchants support their customers, we're at the forefront of helping businesses unlock automation, configure AI Agent, and drive measurable outcomes from day one.
