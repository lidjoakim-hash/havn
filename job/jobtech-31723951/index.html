Company Description Bosch Thermotechnology AB in Tranås develops, manufactures, and sells advanced heat pumps that harness energy from nature’s own resources – rock, water, and air. In Tranås, you’ll find both our production unit and our competence and development center, where we constantly strive to stay at the forefront. Here we create smart solutions that simplify everyday life, contribute to a greener future, and provide our customers with safety and comfort We believe in technology that makes a difference – technology that is Invented for life. Job Description Join the Electric Solutions group at Bosch Home Comfort, where software engineering and DevOps come together to support the development of heat pump applications. You will work closely with engineers using model-based development approaches, building a strong understanding of how their software is designed, implemented, tested, integrated, and maintained. You will use that understanding to shape reliable, practical tools and workflows that make continuous software development easier. From fast feedback during development to reproducible builds and releases, your work will help teams deliver high-quality software with confidence. Your contribution to something big: Enable continuous development: Design and improve CI/CD workflows that support the way software teams work, from early development and integration through testing and delivery. Enhance automation: Automate builds, tests, code analysis, packaging, and release activities, with useful feedback that helps engineers identify issues early. Put software quality first: Consider what matters throughout the development lifecycle, including testability, reproducible builds, version control, traceability, and maintainability. Partner with engineers: Collaborate with software teams and turn real needs into effective platform improvements. Improve developer experience: Reduce repetitive manual work and help teams integrate and validate changes frequently and reliably. Keep delivery systems dependable: Monitor pipeline health, troubleshoot build and integration issues, and address bottlenecks across teams and sites. Scale delivery infrastructure: Contribute to reliable and scalable build infrastructure, including cloud-based services. Qualifications What distinguishes you: Education: Degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or related fields. Professional Experience & Skills: Methodologies: Solid experience in software development, CI/CD, DevOps and agile practices at scale. Programming: Practical programming skills in Bash, Git, Python, Go, and C. Operating Systems: Practical knowledge with Linux and Windows systems. DevOps Tooling: Practical knowledge of Jenkins, SonarQube, jFrog Artifactory, Bitbucket, Jira, and GitHub. Infrastructure as Code (IaC): Experience with IaC tools (Ansible, Terraform) and server administration (Windows/Linux) is a plus. Additional Tech Stack: Experience with MATLAB/Simulink, NoSQL databases (such as MongoDB), Grafana, Conan, and Maven is beneficial. Interpersonal & Language Skills: Languages: Fluent in English Working Style: Highly organized, collaborative, proactive, and structured with strong planning capabilities. Personality: Responsible, self-driven, eager to innovate and continuously learn, with a strong willingness to collaborate within international teams (including willingness for occasional business travel). Additional Information Welcome to Tranås – the heart of Bosch Home Comfort in Sweden Tranås, beautifully located by the idyllic Lake Sommen and surrounded by forests and nature reserves, is home to Bosch Thermotechnology AB in Sweden. Here, more than 600 associates work in manufacturing and research & development of innovative heating solutions for a sustainable future.
