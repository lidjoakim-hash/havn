You decide what gets built and how, and you own what you ship. How you'll grow: You'll shape how a payments team works with agents, from the guardrails to the workflows other teams reuse. The path continues to staff and lead roles, where engineers set standards, build agent workflows, and coach the team. This is you • You've built and run backend services in production. We work in Kotlin and TypeScript, but your judgment matters more to us than any single language • You read code and architecture quickly and can judge what's right and what will break, whether a person or an agent wrote it • You know where distributed systems fail: retries, race conditions, idempotency, and partial failures • You work with AI agents daily: you write clear specs, review their output critically, and know when to take the keyboard back • You communicate like an owner, in clear English Big plus: payments experience, such as payment providers, app store billing, subscriptions.
