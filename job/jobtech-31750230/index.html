atNorth is a leading Nordic data center company providing cost-effective, scalable high-density colocation and built-to-suit services to industry-leading organizations across Iceland, Sweden, Finland, and Denmark. With sustainability at our core, our data centers run on renewable energy and are designed according to circular economy principles. Through innovative design, power efficiency, and intelligent operations, we deliver reliable, secure, and high-performance infrastructure. Headquartered in Reykjavik, Iceland, atNorth operates eight data centers across the Nordics, with additional sites under development in Finland, Denmark, Sweden and Norway. We combine world-class technology with a people-first culture built on collaboration, ownership, and trust, empowering our teams to grow, take responsibility, and make a real impact. By joining our international team, you will help shape the future of sustainable digital infrastructure. Project Director at atNorth Are you an experienced project leader who thrives on taking ownership of complex, large-scale construction projects? We are looking for a Project Director to lead the delivery of one of our major data center projects in Sweden. In this key role, you will have end-to-end accountability from contract award through construction, commissioning and handover to Operations, ensuring the project is delivered safely, on time, within budget and to the quality expected by atNorth and our customers. You will lead the atNorth project organization and act as our senior delivery representative towards the general contractor, customer and key stakeholders. This is an opportunity to combine leadership, commercial expertise and project delivery while contributing directly to atNorth’s continued growth across the Nordics. Key responsibilities Take end-to-end ownership of the assigned project, delivering against agreed scope, schedule, budget, quality, HSE and Ready for Service (RFS) commitments. Lead the atNorth project organization and build the right team and capabilities for each phase of delivery. Participate in general contractor tendering, evaluation and contract negotiations, and take full accountability from contract award. Manage the GC contract and protect atNorth’s commercial and contractual position, including variations, claims, costs and schedule impacts. Own project cost, schedule and risk forecasting, proactively identifying deviations and escalating risks early. Act as atNorth’s senior delivery interface towards the customer, managing technical requirements, design changes and acceptance. Lead commissioning and integrated systems testing through to successful handover to Operations. Drive a strong health, safety and quality culture, ensuring contracted standards are consistently met. Lead, coach and develop project managers, leads and specialists across the project organization. Ensure strong project governance, reporting, documentation and close-out, while capturing lessons learned to continuously improve future delivery. Your profile Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Construction Management, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering or a related discipline. 10+ years of experience leading large-scale construction, infrastructure or mission-critical projects. Proven experience of owner-side project delivery, ideally from contract award through construction, commissioning and operational handover. Strong commercial and contractual experience, including contract administration, variations, claims and cost and schedule management. Proven ability to lead multidisciplinary project teams, consultants and contractors in complex project environments. Strong stakeholder and customer management skills, with the confidence to make decisions and communicate clearly in challenging situations.
