CAD & PLM Application Specialist

CERN · Sweden

💰 ca. 520 000–640 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Kontraktvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 13.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

What you'll do Join the Information Management (IM) Group within the Engineering (EN) Department to implement and support mission-critical CAD and Product Lifecycle Management (PLM) solutions for the Organization's engineering community. You will be part of the team enabling CERN's digital engineering transformation, where your expertise, experience, and ability to learn will be key. Your responsibilities Provide advanced technical assistance for CERN's portfolio of CAD and PLM applications. Plan and execute system testing and performance validation for a broad portfolio of engineering applications. Serve as a point of contact for user communities, translating specialised needs into technical solutions. Mentor and coach junior members of the team, fostering technical skill development and digital engineering adoption. Perform business analysis to bridge engineering requirements and technical delivery, supporting the implementation of innovative workflows, including visualisation standards and system integrations. Your profile Hands-on expertise with large-scale 3D design ecosystems (e.g. CATIA, Siemens NX, Creo, Revit). Expertise in Product/CAD exchange standards and experience with automated 3D model conversion workflows. Proven business analysis skills covering problem assessment, requirements engineering, and process optimisation. Strong ability to align enterprise-wide stakeholders and resolve complex technical challenges. Demonstrated track record in delivering technical support and coaching within a collaborative engineering environment. Bachelor's Degree or equivalent relevant experience in the field of Mechanical Engineering, Systems Engineering, or equivalent technical field or a related field. Your skills Configuration and use of information management systems. Knowledge and application of software life-cycle tools and procedures. Testing, diagnosing and optimisation of software.
