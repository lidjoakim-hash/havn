Chalmers University of Technology is seeking a Director for ourhttps://www.chalmers.se/en/research/infrastructure/ecommons/ (e-commons) who combines professional expertise and sound judgement with strong leadership and a clear focus on delivery. We are looking for a leader with a collaborative mindset, who puts the mission and collective success first, takes a broad organisational perspective, works effectively across organisational boundaries, and creates an environment that enables individuals and teams to succeed. About us  Chalmers University of Technology is driving academic excellence in order to address the challenges and needs of society and industry. We offer a creative and dynamic environment for research, education, innovation and applied science.  Chalmers e-infrastructure Commons (e-commons) is our integrated digital infrastructure for research, providing advanced digital resources, services, and expertise to support research activities across all our 12 departments. The e-commons services cover the life cycle of research data by integrating functions embedded at different parts of the university with functions available in national and international e-infrastructure initiatives and collaborations. The mission of e-commons is defined by the central Research Committee of Chalmers with representatives from all departments.   The host department for e-commons is https://www.chalmers.se/en/departments/physics-and-astronomy/  The Role  As Director of e-commons, you will hold a strategically important leadership role at Chalmers, with overall responsibility for leading and developing e-commons and ensuring high-quality, reliable services for the research community. You will also serve as Head of Division for the computational infrastructure within the Department of Physics and Astronomy.   You will set direction, build a strong and collaborative organisation, and bring together people and expertise across organisational boundaries to turn complex research needs into effective solutions. You will ensure that e-commons evolves in line with Chalmers’ needs and the developments in national and international e-infrastructures.  Your key responsibilities include:  Leading and developing e-commons in accordance with its mission, budget, operational plans, strategic objectives, and established policies, while ensuring the infrastructure continues to meet emerging research needs and developments in national and international e-infrastructures.   Holding overall responsibility for personnel, work environment, and operational management of the division, which currently consists of approximately 20 employees.   Advising the Vice President for Research and the Research Committee on e-infrastructure matters.  Collaborating with other support functions and research infrastructures. The position may involve representing the university in national and international e-science contexts.  Qualifications and merits The successful candidate should have: A master’s degree in a relevant field.  Proven leadership and management experience.  Experience in leading and collaborating within complex organisations.  Extensive knowledge of the e-infrastructure domain, including its current developments, opportunities, and challenges.  Excellent communication skills in English and Swedish, both written and spoken.  The following qualifications are considered advantageous:  A doctoral degree in a field relevant to the operational activities of e-commons.  Broad understanding of e-infrastructure needs across different research disciplines.  An established national and international professional network within the field.  Leadership experience within an e-infrastructure organisation.  Personal Characteristics  You are an experienced, collaborative, and pragmatic leader with sound judgement, integrity, and a strong focus on quality.
