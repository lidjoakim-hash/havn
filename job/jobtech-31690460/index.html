We're looking for a Software Engineer to join Censhine. Censhine is a technology company within the Centiro Group, building for complex, business-critical work. We develop connected systems shaped around how organizations operate, with AI deeply integrated into the technology and human judgement firmly in the lead. Our first major implementation has been developed in close partnership with Cedra, one of Sweden's leading audit, law and advisory firms. Together, we're building technology around the realities of their business, with the platform already supporting thousands of users across the organization. "We have worked with quality management for a long time and have built our own tools for quality assurance, compliance, and information security. We saw real value in the idea of combining quality control and client engagements within the same platform. That mindset became an important point of alignment between us and Cedra," says Niklas Hedin, CEO of Centiro. The impact you'll make As Software Engineer, you'll play a central role in building and evolving the Censhine platform. This is a hands-on role for someone who loves creating great software. You'll spend most of your time designing, developing and improving the product, transforming ideas and customer needs into reliable, scalable solutions that make a real difference for users. At the same time, you'll help shape the technical direction of the platform. You'll influence architectural decisions, drive technical improvements and ensure that what we build today supports where we want to go tomorrow. You'll have the opportunity to influence not only what we build, but how we build it. The work you'll do Design, develop and continuously improve features and capabilities across the Censhine platform. Build scalable, maintainable and high-quality solutions that create real customer value. Translate customer and business needs into robust software and great user experiences. Contribute to architecture and technical decisions that support long-term growth, performance and reliability. Work across the full development lifecycle: design, code, test, deploy and improve. Explore new technologies and AI-driven capabilities that strengthen the product and accelerate development. Identify technical risks, opportunities and improvement areas and turn them into action. Help establish engineering practices that balance speed, quality and long-term sustainability. Your vibe You are driven by curiosity, craftsmanship and a desire to build things that matter. You enjoy solving complex problems, exploring new technologies and turning ideas into working solutions. You thrive when you can combine hands-on development with technical ownership and enjoy thinking beyond the next feature to the future of the product. You are proactive, collaborative and comfortable taking responsibility. You enjoy working closely with others, sharing knowledge and continuously improving both technology and ways of working. Competencies & skills You build modern, scalable solutions and feel at home in the Microsoft ecosystem, especially C#, .NET and Angular, supported by today's engineering toolbox. You are comfortable working with: Modern cloud platforms such as Azure and GCP Cloud-native engineering practices APIs and integrations CI/CD automation Containers and Kubernetes Clean architecture and distributed systems AI is an important part of how we build and evolve our products. Whether you're already integrating AI into solutions or exploring new ways of applying it, you're excited about the possibilities it creates for both customers and software engineering. You bring Several years of experience in software engineering and product development. Strong experience building and maintaining modern software solutions.
