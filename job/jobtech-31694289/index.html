The Company Who are we? CLEEVEN, an international engineering consulting firm, was built around 2 extremely strong pillars: Our MISSION: Increase the level of self-awareness and the awareness of those around us Our CONVICTION: The excellence of European engineers must enable Europe to strengthen its position as a leader in Technological Innovation and R&D. As engineers, we actively work alongside our clients to ensure their global competitiveness. Do you want to take part in major technology projects while growing in an environment that values your personal development? Join us and become who you are! Why choose Cleeven? Because here, we invest in your future as much as in your expertise: An innovation-driven environment: Work on cutting-edge projects where your creativity and your initiative are valued and encouraged. A distinctive company culture combining performance and openness to the social sciences: Join a community of passionate people through regular events, inspiring encounters and a collaborative, performance-driven atmosphere. A unique personal development program: Take advantage of the Cleeven Coaching Program, a program designed to develop your human skills, in line with your ambitions. Tailor-made career development: A dedicated mentor supports you as your career evolves and as you work towards your professional goals. Ready to unlock your full potential? If you want a career where technology meets people, where your talent will be valued and supported, then Cleeven is your next step. “With Cleeven, become who you are!” Position and Responsibilities Your responsibilities Lead and coordinate energy delivery projects from planning to execution and handover. Manage project schedules, budgets, resources, risks, and quality. Coordinate stakeholders, subcontractors, and technical teams. Monitor project progress and ensure delivery according to agreed requirements. Ensure compliance with relevant safety standards and project procedures. Qualifications Around 5 years of experience in project management, preferably within the energy or power industry. Experience coordinating technical projects and multidisciplinary teams. Strong planning, communication, and problem-solving skills. Fluency in English; Swedish is an advantage. Currently we are recruiting 2 people. This is a permanent employment. Due to the nature of the employment, relocation in Sweden might be considered during your tenure.
