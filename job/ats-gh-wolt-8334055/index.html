About Wolt At Wolt, we create technology that brings joy, simplicity and earnings to the neighborhoods of the world. In 2014 we started with delivery of restaurant food. Now we’re building the delivery of (almost) everything and you’ll find us in over 500 cities in 30 countries around the world. In 2022 we joined forces with DoorDash and together we keep on dreaming big and expanding across the globe. Working at Wolt isn’t always easy, but it’s definitely exciting. Here you’ll learn more, build more, and ship more than in most other companies. You’ll be challenged a lot, but also have a lot of fun on the way. So, if you’re a self-starter with drive and entrepreneurial spirit, this could be the ride of your life. At Wolt, you can grow your account management career with a fun and fast-scaling global business. Part of the DoorDash team, we’re building a global logistics platform that can deliver (almost) everything in your city, to your door in minutes. Today, our products are used by 55+ million registered users who can now order from 200,000+ restaurants and retail stores across 30+ countries around the world. Our Account Management teams create and build our relationships with merchants. Together, we’ve helped big brands, local stores and neighborhood restaurants do over €15 billion’s worth of business through our platform. We’re constantly launching new cities and products, so there are always new opportunities to expand your skills and progress your career. As our new Account Specialist you’ll play a key part in helping our retail partners succeed on the Wolt platform by building strong relationships, delivering top-tier client experience, and driving growth through data-driven insights and close collaboration with cross-functional teams. What you’ll be doing As an Account Specialist, you will drive the commercial growth and success of Wolt’s retail partners by delivering data-driven insights and strategic consultation. You will manage a portfolio of accounts, collaborate closely with cross-functional teams to build tailored promotional campaigns, and foster long-term merchant relationships. Day-to-day in this role you’ll: • Partner growth by driving the commercial growth of existing retail partners through strategic, data-driven performance insights and consultation • Oversee a portfolio of retail partners, ensuring value creation across all stakeholders: partners, customers, couriers, and Wolt • Use data insights to identify opportunities for partners, propose tailored campaigns, and provide high-level consultation to boost results • Work closely with marketing to design and implement promotional activities that drive demand and maximize partner sales • Act as the key contact for appointed accounts, maintaining strong communication, running regular business reviews, and supporting expansion opportunities • Foster a positive, supportive work culture where people feel valued, engaged, and motivated to grow • Ensure smooth collaboration across functions to align goals and optimize partner performance • Contribute to building new structures and processes in a growing market, embracing the start-up mindset of rolling up your sleeves when needed Our humble expectations • 2 to 4 years of success in account management or B2B sales with clear commercial impact. • Strong analytical judgment and hands-on use of dashboards such as Looker or Power BI to make decisions. • Excellent relationship building and communication across a wide range of stakeholders. • High ownership and reliability.
