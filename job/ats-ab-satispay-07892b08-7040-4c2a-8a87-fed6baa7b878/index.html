About us Traditional banking has never really given you control over your financial life. Satispay exists to change that: to empower people through finance. That means making financial services easy and accessible for over 6.5M users. We started with payments, then benefits, investing, cards... and we’re not done. Not even close. We follow the same logic every time: find a real problem, solve it properly, then look for the next one. The destination? To build the most loved financial platform in the world. If you own outcomes rather than tasks, stay close to what users need, and refuse to wait for someone else to fix what’s broken — you’ll find people here who work exactly in the same way. And together, you’ll build something worth being proud of. What you’ll be doing As our Senior Manager - Indirect Sales Channel, you’ll build and scale the network of partners that brings our solutions to as many merchants as possible across the territory. You’ll report directly to the Head of Business Development and own our distribution strategy through third-party partners. You’ll look after the full lifecycle of our external sales partners, with one clear goal: growing our market footprint. Here’s what that looks like in practice: - Network building - Scout, recruit and onboard a wide commercial network of independent Field Consultants, master agencies and authorized reseller shops. - Channel strategy - Design and run the go-to-market plan for the indirect channel: pick the best territories, set commission structures and define the partner profiles that help us scale. - Partner enablement - Be the main point of contact for our partners. Train them on our solutions and give them the commercial materials to sell with confidence. - Targets and performance - Set ambitious sales targets, track how each partner is performing and put incentive plans in place to keep agencies and Field Consultants engaged and productive. - Field presence - Travel across the territory with an Area Manager approach to meet key agencies and major retail resellers, strengthen relationships and help them sell more. Who we’re looking for This role needs someone who has built external sales networks before and knows how to keep independent partners motivated, selling and growing. - Experience - 7+ years in B2B sales, with a good part of it as Area Manager, Channel Manager or Indirect Sales Manager. - Network building - Proven track record of structuring, scaling and managing networks of independent Field Consultants and commercial agencies from the ground up. - Channel knowledge - Hands-on knowledge of retail, hotels, restaurants and cafés (HoReCa) and local shops, and of how to reach them through third-party partners. - Product sales - Experience selling B2B products, commercial equipment or business solutions through external networks, with a focus on getting products adopted. - Strategy and analytics - Able to negotiate commercial terms with agencies, build profitable commission models and read regional sales data to improve territory coverage. - Languages - Full professional fluency in Italian and English, written and spoken. Don’t worry if you don’t tick every box. We believe in the power of different viewpoints and strengths. Your unique perspective is important as we build something special: if you’re passionate and can make a difference, we truly encourage you to apply.
