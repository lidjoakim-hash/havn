MongoDB’s EMEA Marketing team is hiring a Demand Generation Manager to drive digital acquisition among AI-native startups and digital-native companies, while building scalable, centralized enterprise programs across the region. Reporting to the EMEA Demand Programs Leader, you’ll create and run repeatable, integrated programs that generate engagement with target people and accounts, qualified leads, and pipeline, while supporting broader EMEA demand generation campaigns. Your primary focus will be AI-native companies and digital natives: fast-growing, developer-led organizations where MongoDB’s platform and startup programs are a strong fit. You’ll reach them through digital channels, founder and VC/PE community groups, and relevant events. This role blends strategy with hands-on execution: you’ll design programs, run them, and demonstrate their impact. We are looking to speak to candidates who are based in Dublin for our hybrid working model. scale what works, stop what doesn’t, and explore AI-enabled tactics and tools • Support the broader EMEA demand-generation plan by sharing channel insights and performance data, and running digital and demand campaigns for target enterprise accounts • Build scalable, centralized EMEA programs by identifying duplicated or similar country-level activity and replacing it with repeatable programs, templates, and playbooks • Partner with regional and country Marketing, Sales, Ecosystem, Global Marketing, Content, Developer Relations, Strategic Events, and Marketing Operations to connect programs to wider campaigns and secure assets, landing pages, and tracking • Manage budgets, agencies, forecasting, and spend efficiency;
