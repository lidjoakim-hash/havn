Farfetch is a leading global marketplace for the luxury fashion industry, connecting customers in over 190 countries with items from more than 50 countries and over 1,400 of the world’s best brands and charming boutiques from around the world. Farfetch opens a world of luxury for endless expressions of style, delivering a truly unique shopping experience and access to the most extensive selection of luxury on a global marketplace.    CREATIVE OPERATIONS We're a creative family, building copy, data, and visual assets for each corner of the Farfetch E-commerce Platform. We are dedicated to providing gold-standard imagery, content, and digital production services for our partners and creating an engaging on-site experience for our customers.   THE ROLE Validate product imagery to ensure it strictly meets Farfetch quality standards and technical art direction guidelines prior to publication on the platform.  This highly operational role focuses on high-volume quality control and digital asset management, requiring basic editing skills to ensure visual consistency and efficient production workflows.   WHAT YOU'LL DO Image Quality Validation • Validate imagery quality according to Farfetch guidelines and art direction standards; • Assess whether photographed and edited images meet the required quality to be published; • Ensure imagery meets platform requirements before going live. Operational Execution & Asset Management • Edit product images in line with production guidelines, ensuring consistency and adherence to established quality standards; • Maintain strict digital asset organization and adhere to the established file structure; • Upload, route, and manage image assets efficiently within the platform's production pipeline; • Quality Assurance ReportingLog and report imagery errors based on technical guidelines to ensure the smooth completion of the production pipeline; • Adapt quickly to changing operational priorities to process high volumes of assets efficiently; • Contribute to maintaining a high-performance environment. WHO YOU ARE Technical skills: • Solid technical understanding of digital image workflows and asset management systems; • Basic Photoshop skills for minor image retouching and color correction. Operational capabilities: • Strong attention to detail; • Ability to work with high-volume image production; • Strong time management and ability to meet production targets. Collaboration And Communication: • Ability to give constructive feedback on image quality; • Strong team player mindset, with a collaborative approach to problem-solving and shared goals; • Ability to adapt quickly to changing priorities and contribute across multiple areas when needed; • Open to feedback and continuous improvement, with a focus on team success over individual output. Core Skills • Quality Control & Visual Judgment; • Attention to Detail & Visual Precision; • Operational Execution (high-volume production); • Image Editing & Photoshop Skills.
