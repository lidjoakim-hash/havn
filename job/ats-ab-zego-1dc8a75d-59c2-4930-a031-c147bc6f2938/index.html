Purpose of the role Complaints are where things get real - the customer is already frustrated or in distress, the stakes are higher, and poor handling has direct regulatory consequences. As a Complaints Handler, you'll own a caseload of customer complaints from acknowledgement through to final response. You'll investigate thoroughly, reach fair outcomes, and explain them clearly, all in line with DISP and Consumer Duty - and you'll take pride in getting it right for the customer first time. You'll report to the Complaints Team Leader and work closely with teams across Claims, Fraud, Customer Support, and Sales to get to the right answer and fix the root cause. Case Handling - Own and resolve a caseload of customer complaints following initial acknowledgement - Investigate each complaint thoroughly, analysing all the facts and related documentation - Gather the information you need from internal teams and external partners - Draft Final Response Letters that are clear, fair, and evidence a full investigation - Keep customers informed throughout and manage expectations along the way - Stay in control of your workload by actively monitoring and updating case management systems Quality & Compliance - Keep complaints logged, acknowledged, and progressed within DISP timescales - Apply Consumer Duty and vulnerable customer standards consistently across every case - Escalate complex, sensitive, or high-risk cases to your Team Leader promptly - Support complaints reporting to management, senior leaders, QA assessors, and Compliance Collaboration - Work with Claims, Fraud, Customer Support, and Sales to resolve complaints and address root causes - Contribute to feedback sessions, process improvement discussions, and complaint trend insight What you will need to be successful Essential - Proven experience handling complaints end to end within an FCA-regulated environment, ideally motor insurance. - Working knowledge of DISP and Consumer Duty as they apply to complaint handling. - Clear, concise written communication, and the emotional intelligence to handle difficult conversations well. - An analytical, organised approach - you can balance multiple priorities and deliver within SLAs. - You work AI-first. You will use AI daily here, and we mean daily. You do not need to be an expert, but you do need to arrive curious, experiment fast, and take ownership of getting good quickly. People who wait to be trained will find this uncomfortable. Desirable - Experience of ShareDo or equivalent case management systems.
