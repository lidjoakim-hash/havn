Director of Data & AI This role reports to the Chief Product Officer (CPO). Remote within Sweden Some travel, under about ten days a quarter Every business aviation trip starts with someone needing to be somewhere. Thousands of brokers and operators use Avinode to turn that need into a flight, and every request, quote, and booking they make is a signal nobody else holds. Data has been a key product and strategy differentiator in the software industry for years. In the AI era, the importance of clean, timely, and accurate data only accelerates. AI drives efficiency for our customers and our business, but unless it is powered by a solid data foundation, its convincing conclusions risk taking us in the wrong direction, faster. Data & AI is a company-wide concern, but to create focus and accountability on the foundations that serve the rest of the company and the product, we are bringing the necessary competencies – starting with data product management, engineering, and analytics – together as one team. This role is about leading that team, and we're looking for real depth in the space, not just general leadership. What you’ll do You’ll ensure the delivery of governed data products built around Databricks and its suite of tools. You’ll make sure entities and metrics have a single agreed-upon human- and agentreadable meaning regardless of their source. You’ll put models and governed, evaluated agents into production, with accuracy and cost you can prove. You’ll lead a team consisting of a product manager, analysts, and an engineering team with its own manager. You’ll grow the team – both in size and capabilities. We’re investing in this fast-evolving space, giving you the opportunity to help shape the team to meet upcoming competence needs. You’ll have strategic and operational responsibility for the new Data & AI function, where you’ll contribute with a strong voice in shaping the company-level direction. You’ll make sure your team and the company understand the strategy, priorities, decisions, and expected outcomes. Who you are You have 10+ years of experience in data product, engineering, or analytics. You have a 6+ year track record of building capable, high-performing teams and helping people grow through clear expectations, feedback, and coaching. You communicate clearly and confidently with technical and non-technical audiences, adapting your message to the audience and context. You can explain technical priorities, trade-offs, risks, and decisions in terms that support business decisions. You're still close enough to the work to hold technical direction. This matters more to us than the size of the team you last ran. You've done at least two of these three properly, and you're literate in the third: - Built a governed semantic or metric layer that people actually trusted, across systems that didn't agree. - Put ML models into production and kept them working. - Shipped LLM or agent systems into production, with evaluation, cost control, and latency as first-class concerns. Nice-to-haves Hands-on experience with Databricks in production. A marketplace, two-sided platform, or another business where the data is the moat. Experience being an early senior data or AI leader somewhere. What do we offer you? We are proud to offer you a workplace where we have high engagement and where creativity and curiosity is always much appreciated.
