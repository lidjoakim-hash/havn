Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years. About The Team The Voyager team at Anduril Industries is an engineering and design division with vast experience in developing innovative computing and communications solutions for the network edge. We specialize in integrating enterprise networking capabilities from global IT leaders with purpose-built hardware and software platforms designed to meet the most stringent environmental requirements. WHAT YOU’LL DO • Own production test, manufacturing, & quality for a specific product line — from incoming inspection through final test and field feedback. • Design, build, and maintain test fixtures and automated test procedures for electronic assemblies & full products. • Conduct data analysis and data mining to identify trends and patterns in product quality. • Develop and maintain KPIs & OKR metrics to track product quality and identify areas for improvement, driving improvement through cross-functional teams. • Write documentation required for quality management of hardware at scale, such as work instructions, quality plans, and inspection requirements. • Work with technicians and other engineers hands-on, building and measuring prototype hardware. • Possibly travel to suppliers or contract manufacturers to help solve production or quality issues. REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS • Bachelors or masters degree in electrical/electronic engineering, computer engineering, or a related technical field. • Competent working in a lab or production environment using power supplies, electronic loads, digital multimeters, and other bench test equipment. • Must be available to work full time in our Dublin office. • Must be available to start full time in July 2027. PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS • Demonstrated ability to creatively problem-solve issues related to electronic or mechanical hardware in a resource-constrained environment. • Experience with microcontrollers such as the RP2040 is beneficial but not required. • Knowledge of communication protocols such as I2C, RS232, RS245, Serial. • Comfortable with soldering PCBAs both with through hole and SMT components would be greatly beneficial but not required. • Experience with dealing with both prototype and high volume manufacturing solutions. • Experience with CAD software & 3D printers is beneficial but not required. • Software scripting experience in any language such as Python or C++ is beneficial but not required. • LabVIEW software experience is greatly beneficial but not required. • Familiarity with Linux environments, particularly Ubuntu is beneficial but not required. • Experience with or exposure to networking and computing concepts is beneficial but not required. • Personal experience with inspection systems, data analysis, numeric methods, MATLAB, or other software packages. • Previous internship experience within manufacturing, test, or quality production is preferable but not required. • Comfortable digging into test data to find the root cause of a yield issue, not just flagging it. • Demonstrated in-kind or direct team leadership skills.
