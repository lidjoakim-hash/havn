VP, Global Compliance Strategy and Policies

Crypto.com · London, United Kingdom

Key Responsibilities Global Compliance Strategy • Define and own the multi-year global compliance strategy, aligned to the firm's market expansion, product roadmap, and regulatory licensing plans. • Serve as a senior advisor to the CCO and executive leadership on regulatory risk appetite, new-market entry, and product launches (e.g., derivatives, payments, prediction markets, stablecoins, tokenized assets). • Conduct horizon scanning across major regulatory regimes (MiCA, EU AMLR/AMLA, UK FCA, US federal and state frameworks, MAS, HKMA/SFC, VARA) and translate emerging requirements into proactive strategy. • Lead regulatory change management: assess impact, prioritize workstreams, and drive cross-functional implementation. Policy Governance and Framework • Own the global compliance policy suite: AML/CFT, sanctions, market abuse, KYC/CDD, transaction monitoring, travel rule, advertising, data and recordkeeping, and code of conduct policies. • Establish policy architecture and governance standards — drafting conventions, tiering (global policy vs. local procedure), approval workflows, review cadence, and version control. • Ensure consistent global standards with legitimate local tailoring; arbitrate conflicts between group policy and jurisdictional requirements. • Design and track policy effectiveness metrics; report periodically to executive management and boards/committees as required. Leadership and Stakeholder Management • Build, lead, and develop a high-performing central team of policy and strategy professionals. • Partner with regional compliance heads, Legal, Product, Risk, Operations, and Government Affairs to embed policy requirements into systems, processes, and product design. • Represent the firm with regulators, industry bodies, and working groups; support license applications, examinations, and audits with policy evidence. • Own policy-related training strategy and compliance culture initiatives in partnership with HR/Learning. Requirements • 12+ years in compliance, financial crime, or regulatory affairs, with at least 5 years in a senior leadership role managing teams. • Demonstrated experience owning a global or multi-jurisdiction policy framework at a regulated financial institution (bank, payments, broker-dealer, e-money, or crypto exchange). • Deep working knowledge of AML/CFT and sanctions regimes; strong grasp of market conduct regulation. • Track record engaging directly with regulators and supporting licensing, examinations, or enforcement matters. • Experience operating in matrixed, global organizations across at least two of the US / EU-UK / APAC regions. • Excellent written communication — able to draft board-grade and regulator-grade documents. Nice to have • Crypto/digital assets experience, including travel rule implementation, VASP licensing (MiCA, VARA, NYDFS, MAS), or blockchain analytics tooling. • Experience standing up or restructuring a central policy function during hypergrowth or market expansion. • Legal qualification (JD/LLB) or compliance certifications (CAMS, ICA, CRCM). • Familiarity with GRC platforms and policy management tooling.
