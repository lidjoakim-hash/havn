Since we opened our doors in 2009, the world of commerce has evolved immensely, and so has Square. After enabling anyone to take payments and never miss a sale, we saw sellers stymied by disparate, outmoded products and tools that wouldn’t work together. So we expanded into software and started building integrated, omnichannel solutions – to help sellers sell online, manage inventory, offer buy now, pay later functionality, book appointments, engage loyal buyers, and hire and pay staff. Across it all, we’ve embedded financial services tools at the point of sale, so merchants can access a business loan and manage their cash flow in one place. Afterpay furthers our goal to provide omnichannel tools that unlock meaningful value and growth, enabling sellers to capture the next generation shopper, increase order sizes, and compete at a larger scale. Today, we are a partner to sellers of all sizes – large, enterprise-scale businesses with complex operations, sellers just starting, as well as merchants who began selling with Square and have grown larger over time. As our sellers grow, so do our solutions. There is a massive opportunity in front of us. We’re building a significant, meaningful, and lasting business, and we are helping sellers worldwide do the same. The Role Not every seller comes to Square directly. A growing number reach us through resellers who sell Square on our behalf, and through independent contractors who represent Square in their own communities. Together these two motions make up our channel sales function. As Senior Marketing Manager, Channel Sales, you will own marketing for both motions worldwide. You will be the primary marketing point of contact for the global channel sales team, turning what they need into programmes that scale. Most of your time will go into building scalable solutions that equip every reseller and contractor to sell Square well. The rest will be spent working shoulder to shoulder with our highest-performing resellers on activity that grows both their business and ours. You will partner closely with channel sales leadership, events, content and regional marketing teams.
