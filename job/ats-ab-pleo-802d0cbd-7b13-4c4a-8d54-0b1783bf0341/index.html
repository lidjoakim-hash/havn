About The Role Fraud doesn't sleep. And neither does our ambition to stop it. At Pleo, we've built one of Europe's leading spend management platforms. But with scale comes risk, and our Fraud Operations Team plays a critical role in investigating, responding to, and mitigating fraud, turning detection signals into action and ensuring our customers are protected. And now, we're growing the team. We're looking for a Fraud Operations Analyst to join us. You'll work closely with our Fraud Operations Specialists and Fraud Operations Manager to process fraud alerts, investigate cases, and make sure nothing slips through the cracks. This is a hands-on, operational role, perfect for someone who wants to build deep fraud expertise in a fast-moving fintech environment, with the structure and guidance to grow.
