Skilled Painters - Bergen Skuteviken Personell AS is seeking skilled and independent painters for projects in Bergen and the surrounding area. As a painter, you will work with both interior and exterior painting on renovation and new-build projects. Typical tasks include surface preparation such as filling, sanding, and smoothing of walls and ceilings, application of paint using roller, brush, and preferably spray equipment, as well as finishing work with a strong focus on quality and precision. We are looking for painters who take pride in high-quality workmanship and can work efficiently both independently and as part of a team. We offer: • Competitive salary: NOK 270 -290 per hour (depending on experience) • Permanent employment • Exciting and varied work tasks (both interior and exterior) • Free professional tools and work clothes • Housing in company apartments if needed • Supportive and service-oriented administration Requirements: • Minimum 3 years' professional painting experience • Strong skills in both exterior and interior paintwork • Knowledge of Norwegian painting methods, especially interior groundwork and plastering • Ability to follow and understand technical instructions and building methods • Scandinavian or English language skills (professional competence is most important) Personal qualities: • High work ethic • Reliable and punctual • Structured and able to plan the workday effectively Interested? Please send your CV and application as soon as possible. Applications are reviewed on an ongoing basis.
