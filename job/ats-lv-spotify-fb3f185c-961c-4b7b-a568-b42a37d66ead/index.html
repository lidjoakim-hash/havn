Senior Backend Engineer - Subscriptions

Spotify · London, United Kingdom

💰 ca. 56 000–68 000 GBP/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)HeltidDirekte fra arbeidsgiverFrist 19.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

What You'll Do • Design, build, and maintain scalable Java services that power Spotify's merchandising platform and help listeners discover and upgrade to Premium. • Work with your team to develop backend systems, APIs, and data models using Google Cloud Platform (GCP). • Partner with product managers, data scientists, and engineers to turn complex challenges into practical, scalable solutions. • Build and improve data pipelines that support experimentation, recommendation logic, and machine learning applications. • Shape technical decisions that improve system reliability, performance, and maintainability as our platform grows. • Take ownership of production services, using monitoring, observability, and continuous improvement to deliver reliable experiences. • Share knowledge through code reviews, technical discussions, and collaboration to help your team learn and grow. Who You Are • You have 5+ years of experience developing backend services with Java. • You have a solid understanding of computer science fundamentals and experience building distributed systems at scale. • You're experienced in designing, deploying, and maintaining cloud-based services using platforms such as GCP or AWS. • You know how to design APIs and work with partners to turn product needs into reliable technical solutions. • You understand what it takes to operate high-traffic services, including monitoring, performance optimization, and reliability. • You enjoy collaborating across disciplines, sharing ideas, and taking ownership of projects from design through delivery. • You value feedback, continuous learning, and finding better ways to build systems and work together. • You're familiar with technologies such as gRPC, BigQuery, Bigtable, content management system integrations, or machine learning applications. Where You'll Be • This role is based in London, UK. • We offer you the flexibility to work where you work best!
