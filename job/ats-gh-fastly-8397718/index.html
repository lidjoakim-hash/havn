Staff Talent Acquisition Partner, EMEA As a member of our Global Talent team, the Staff Talent Acquisition Partner in EMEA drives the recruiting process end to end across Fastly, hiring great people for teams across the Revenue and Client Services organizations in EMEA. You'll spend most of your time driving requisitions from kickoff to offer, and you'll also serve as a go-to resource for our regional leaders. We partner closely with hiring managers to identify and hire top-performing candidates. We deliver a best-in-class candidate experience that gives genuine insight into our culture and what it's like to work at Fastly. We're looking for a seasoned recruiter who brings a passion for technology, creativity, and a solutions mindset. What You'll Do Your primary focus • Own a full requisition load across all functions in EMEA, from intake to offer. • Partner with hiring managers up to VP level to build hiring plans and strategies for high-performing talent. • Source and engage passive candidates through multiple channels, including LinkedIn. • Build talent pipelines through creative sourcing campaigns, tactical research, referrals, and events. • Run the interview and hiring decision process with interviewers and hiring managers. • Drive offers in partnership with hiring managers and the Compensation team. • Recommend improvements to sourcing, process, and outreach. • Deliver a great candidate experience from start to finish. • Support Inclusion & Diversity efforts and partner with HRBPs, Benefits, Immigration, and People Operations. In addition • Act as a trusted point of contact and reliable resource for regional stakeholders and leaders. • Share market insights and hiring trends with the EMEA team, and advise on hiring processes and timelines, with a focus on candidate experience. • Deliver accurate hiring forecasts and reporting to support regional planning and decision-making. What We're Looking For • 10+ years of progressive Client Services or Revenue recruiting experience in a highly technical organization, including a track record of closing senior and hard-to-fill roles. • Proven ability to source and attract passive talent through multiple channels. • Ability to build strong relationships with People Leaders and senior leadership, and to drive consensus across functions. • Autonomy and a problem-solving mindset. • Excellent interviewing skills, screening for ability, experience, and culture. • Extensive tech industry knowledge, including the ability to identify target companies, conferences, and other talent sources. • Strong consultative and interpersonal skills, with the ability to communicate effectively with colleagues at every level of the organization and with external partners. • Knowledge of employment and HR policies in EMEA is highly advantageous. Work Hours: • This position will require you to be available during core business hours.
