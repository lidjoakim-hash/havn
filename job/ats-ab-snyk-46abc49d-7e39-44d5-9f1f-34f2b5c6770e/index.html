Job Summary We're looking for a Senior Solutions Engineer based in France to lead the technical side of the sales cycle for Snyk's French-speaking enterprise and mid-market customers. You'll run demos and proofs of concept in French, advise security and engineering leaders on how to secure code, dependencies, containers, and cloud infrastructure, and act as the voice of the French market back to our product teams. This is a high-impact role at a company building the security layer for an AI-native software world, in a market where native-language credibility makes a real difference. About the Team You'll join Snyk's Solutions Engineering team, the technical bridge between our Sales, Product, and Customer Success organizations. You'll partner daily with Account Executives covering France and with channel partners, and work alongside Solutions Engineers across EMEA who share playbooks, demos, and lessons from the field. What You’ll Do - Lead the technical aspects of the sales cycle for French-speaking prospects and customers, from discovery and demos through proof of concept and technical close - Deliver credible, tailored technical demonstrations and workshops in French and English to developers, DevOps teams, security practitioners, and senior technical leaders - Own and manage proofs of concept end to end, setting clear success criteria, keeping stakeholders aligned, and translating results into business value - Partner with Account Executives to build account strategy across the French market, including large enterprise organizations in regulated industries such as banking, insurance, public sector, and industry - Advise customers on secure development practices, open source and cloud native security, and adoption of AI-assisted coding tools, including data protection and compliance considerations that matter in France and the EU - Work with French and European channel partners and system integrators to build their proficiency in selling and supporting Snyk, and shape joint value propositions and integrations - Bring French customer and partner feedback to Product and Engineering, influencing roadmap decisions and local requirements - Adapt and localize demos, battlecards, and technical content for the French market, and share reusable assets with the wider EMEA team - Mentor Solutions Engineers on the team and help raise the bar on demo quality, discovery, and POC execution - Represent Snyk at customer meetings, local events, and conferences in France What you’ll bring - Native or bilingual-level French, written and spoken, with professional fluency in English. You'll present to executives and technical audiences in French and collaborate with global teams in English - 5+ years of experience in sales engineering, solutions architecture, or a related technical customer facing role in the developer tools, application security, or DevOps space - Experience working closely with highly technical customers at enterprise level in the French market - Familiarity with a wide range of programming languages and how they work, plus a solid understanding of developer and DevOps ecosystems, CI/CD, and cloud native practices
