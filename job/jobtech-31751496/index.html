Sales Operations Specialist

Antaros Medical AB · Uppsala, Sweden

Would you like to work for a growing company devoted to making a real difference in drug development through groundbreaking imaging? A place that is constantly moving forward, with great science, great people, and an informal atmosphere? Then keep reading. At Antaros Medical, we use advanced imaging methods such as MRI and PET to design and deliver clinical trials for decision-making and differentiation in drug development. We value competence and development as much as ideas and solutions. Relationships and trust are key to success, and we currently have around 160 employees across Sweden and the US. As part of our continued growth, we are now looking for a Sales Operations Specialist to join our commercial team in Sweden. Role overview As a Sales Operations Specialist, you will play a key role in supporting our commercial operations and ensuring a seamless customer journey. Working closely with Business Development, Project Management, Finance, Legal, and Operations, you will coordinate proposals, contracts, budgets, and commercial documentation while helping maintain efficient and scalable ways of working. The position combines proposal development, pricing and budget preparation, contract administration, commercial operations support, and process improvement activities. You will be reporting to our Director Sales Operations, based in Uppsala. This role offers an opportunity to contribute directly to the growth of an international life science company while supporting partnerships with global pharmaceutical and biotech companies. Key responsibilities Commercial & Proposal Coordination Prepare and coordinate proposals, budgets, and commercial documentation. Support Business Development throughout the customer lifecycle. Maintain and improve proposal templates, pricing tools, and supporting materials. Contract & Documentation Management Support administration of contracts, change orders, and related commercial documentation. Maintain document control, tracking, and compliance with internal processes. Sales Operations & Process Excellence Support CRM administration, reporting, and commercial data management. Help improve processes, increase efficiency, and drive operational excellence across functions. Who are we looking for? We are looking for someone who enjoys bringing structure to complex workflows and collaborating with a wide range of stakeholders. You are organized, proactive, and detail-oriented, with strong communication skills and a solution-oriented mindset. You enjoy working with numbers, budgets, and complex Excel-based calculations, and you are comfortable managing multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment. Most importantly, you are motivated by being part of a growing organization where your contribution can make a real difference. Requirements 2-4 years of experience in Sales Operations, Commercial Operations, Proposal Management or similar Experience from a clinical research organization (CRO), imaging services provider or another project-based service organization. Experience with pricing, budgeting, and proposal development. Experience managing and coordinating proposals, budgets, contracts, or other commercial documentation involving multiple stakeholders. Experience working with commercial agreements such as MSAs, SOWs, Change Orders, or similar contracts. Bachelor's degree in business administration, Life Sciences, Project Management, or related field. Strong Excel skills, including working with complex calculations and data, as well as proficiency in Microsoft 365. Professional fluency in English, both written and spoken. Advantageous Experience within Life Science, biotech, pharmaceutical, or healthcare environments. Experience with medical imaging or imaging-based clinical research.
