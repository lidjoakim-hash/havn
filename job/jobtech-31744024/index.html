At Tetra Pak we commit to making food safe and available, everywhere; and we protect what's good – protecting food, protecting people, and protecting the planet. By doing so we touch millions of people's lives every day. And we need people like you to make it happen. We empower you to reach your potential with opportunities to make an impact to be proud of – for food, people and the planet. Job Summary As Mechanical Design Owner, you will be responsible for defining mechanical design specifications and driving the development of innovative, reliable, and fit-for-purpose solutions for Separators. You will serve as a key technical expert, providing guidance and support across multiple functions, including Sales, Issue Resolution, PLC, and PC&L, to ensure engineering excellence throughout the project lifecycle. In this role, you will lead the resolution of complex technical challenges, facilitate design reviews, and represent the mechanical design function in technical discussions with customers and suppliers. You will play a pivotal role in promoting best practices, driving continuous improvement initiatives, and ensuring the highest standards of quality, performance, and compliance in mechanical design. The position is based in Lund, Sweden. To support the operational requirements of this role, priority will be given to candidates who are already based locally and have the legal right to work in the country. What you will do Deliver mechanical design templates, specifications and drawings on complex and customized separation solutions Take lead and provide expert support in mechanical design to other areas like Sales, Issue resolution, Product Lifecycle, and Product Creation. Proactively lead and coordinate communication with the order team, ensuring clear visibility of project status, risks, priorities, and order changes. Plan, structure, and execute projects by defining phases, milestones, and deliverables, ensuring completion on time, in full, and within budget. Drive productivity and efficiency through the effective utilization of standardized processes, reusable solutions, and approved component libraries. Identify opportunities for continuous improvement and contribute to the development and enhancement of engineering processes, templates, tools, and best practices. Act as a mentor and technical coach, supporting the development of colleagues within the organization and sharing expertise across functions. Foster collaboration among cross-functional teams to ensure successful project execution and customer satisfaction. Promote a culture of quality, continuous improvement, and engineering excellence in all project activities. We believe you have Master's degree (MSc) in Mechanical Engineering, Product Development, or a related engineering discipline. 10-15 years of experience in a similar Mechanical Design Engineering or Lead Design Engineering role within an industrial or manufacturing environment. Strong technical expertise with a proven understanding of equipment installation requirements and customer operations in on-site environments. Extensive experience in the design and development of manufacturing and processing equipment, with a solid understanding of design-for-manufacturing principles. Expert knowledge of mechanical design methodologies, including the development and implementation of engineering standards, guidelines, tools, and best practices. Excellent understanding of product portfolios, system functionality, and the interaction between mechanical components and overall equipment performance. Experience of working with safety solutions (CE marking/Machinery Regulation) for industrial equipment. Demonstrated ability to lead complex technical discussions, drive engineering decisions, and collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams, customers, and suppliers.
