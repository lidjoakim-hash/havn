Design & UX Lead

Sandvik Aktiebolag · Sweden

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

As Design & UX Lead, you shape how users experience our cloud products and wider product portfolio. You own the design strategy and lead hands-on design, user research and usability, ensuring real user needs guide how our products are designed and developed. Here, you get the opportunity to: Design intuitive software experiences hands-on, from early concepts and prototypes to detailed solutions. Work closely with customers to understand their needs, workflows and challenges, and turn those insights into better user experiences. Simplify complex workflows in the machining industry through practical, user-centered design. The Cloud Product Organization brings together the capabilities needed to build and grow our cloud products. With clear responsibilities and close connections across the wider TFS portfolio, we work together to create customer value. About the job You create a consistent and user-centered design direction across the product portfolio. Working closely with Product Managers, Product Owners and Product Specialists, you turn research and user needs into practical design decisions and bring the user perspective into product priorities and delivery. Job responsibilities Drive UX and design strategy across the product portfolio. Lead and contribute to user research, discovery, prototyping, and usability activities. Translate user needs and research insights into design direction and product improvements. Create consistent, coherent, and usable experiences across products. Collaborate with Product Managers and Product Owners as concepts move toward delivery, drawing on Product Specialists for domain expertise and validation. Advocate for the user perspective in product decisions and prioritization. Location and flexibility This position offers flexibility to work remotely or from one of our entities in Sweden, Germany, the US, or India. Your profile You bring strong experience in UX or product design, preferably within digital products, software or SaaS. What matters most is a strong portfolio demonstrating UX thinking, research and end-to-end design work, alongside your ability to combine strategic design ownership with hands-on practice. Your background includes: Experience owning or shaping design strategy, beyond executing individual designs. Hands-on experience in user research, discovery, prototyping and usability testing. Experience translating user insights into clear design decisions and product improvements. Close collaboration with Product Managers, Product Owners and technical teams. The ability to create consistent user experiences across multiple products or complex product areas. A degree in UX Design, Interaction Design, Human-Computer Interaction, Digital Design or a related field is relevant, but equivalent practical experience is equally valued. User empathy guides how you work, and you communicate design decisions and research insights clearly with both technical and non-technical stakeholders. You’re comfortable influencing without formal authority and collaborating across disciplines. Being curious and pragmatic, you balance user needs with technical and commercial realities while moving confidently between strategic thinking and hands-on work. The team You join a ten-person team that includes the Head of Cloud Product and covers the dedicated Cloud Product Organization as well as shared product portfolio capabilities. As one of the portfolio-wide roles, you collaborate particularly closely with Product Managers, Product Owners and Product Specialists, bringing design, research and usability perspectives across the broader TFS product portfolio. Our culture Our role is clear – through every action, every day, we make the shift and advance the world through engineering.
