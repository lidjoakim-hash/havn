Sales Associate Stockholm - On-Call

Axel Arigato AB · Stockholm, Sweden

Join our Stockholm team as an on-call Sales Associate! A flexible contract that fits perfectly around studies or other commitments. Shifts are based on business needs, especially during peak periods and for coverage. MORE ABOUT THE ROLE AND TEAM The new Sales Associate should be able to work as part of a team and provide an exceptional shopping experience while having a genuine interest in our Stockholm community and aiming to contribute to the teams overall performance and values. WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR Our Sales Associates are responsible for excellent customer service, increasing brand knowledge, executing visual standards and driving performance goals set for our store in cooperation with the Store Manager. Key Responsibilities will include: To develop and maintain good product knowledge and provide excellent customer service in line with the company’s expectations. An understanding of current products, trends and collections and ensuring that customers are informed and aware. To continuously build and maintain customer relationships-our customers are our community! To replenish stock and maintain high standards of merchandise and product presentation. To know your city and the social movements within it. To use this information as a tool during the customer service ceremony. To be aware of daily sales targets and KPI goals, actively driving personal performance to support the store's business growth. Work in and promote a team-oriented environment that can have fun whilst remaining professional. ARE YOU THE ONE? We seek people with most of the following: Great communication skills with a social personality; You enjoy meaningful conversations, listen actively and translate customer needs into memorable buying experiences. Engage customers with genuine enthusiasm and pick up on details that let you tailor your approach to each customer. Work well with others and are a strong team player. Open to new things and receptive to constant development. Self-motivated, can work productively and take initiative. Value creativity, diversity and progress, and you thrive in a modern, purpose-driven environment Passionate about Axel Arigato products and brand is a plus. Prior sales experience in retail is preferred. Sales experience with high-end retail brands is an advantage. Willing to work flexible hours and availability as per business needs HERE'S HOW TO APPLY Apply by submitting your CV in English ASAP. Our recruitment process is strictly based on competencies therefore, we kindly ask you not to include your picture or age with your application. We process applications continuously and the position may be filled before the last day of application. Contract type: On-Call Estimated starting date: ASAP A BIT ABOUT AXEL ARIGATO Founded in Sweden in 2014, Axel Arigato is intrinsically grounded in culture, community, and craft. Driven by curiosity, the brand has consistently connected its approach to the rhythm of what is happening in the wider world, looking to an ever-evolving contemporary society for both purpose and inspiration. It presents diverse collections of elevated and wearable footwear and ready-to- wear, fusing together Scandinavian minimalism with unexpected, contemporary design. The brand has nine physical stores in five countries, as well as a network of over 250 wholesale partners and retail concessions. Axel Arigato is committed to fostering connections beyond the conventions of commerce, and are a proud equal opportunity employer who values a diverse workforce. As such, its core mission remains true to this day: to build a positive platform through the power of community, inclusivity, and memorable experiences. OUR VALUES : DROP Diversity, Respect, Openness and Passion are our values that we live and breathe. They are the foundation for our current state and what we will always bring with us to the future.
