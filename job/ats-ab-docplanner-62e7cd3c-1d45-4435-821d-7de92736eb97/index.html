Company Description At Docplanner Group, we’re on a mission to help people live longer, healthier lives. As the world’s largest healthcare platform, each month, we connect 24 million patients with 280k doctors across 13 countries (through brands like ZnanyLekarz, Doctoralia, MioDottore, DoktorTakvimi, and jameda). Our marketplaces, SaaS and AI tools simplify daily tasks and help doctors, clinics and hospitals work more efficiently, so they can focus on what really matters: caring for their patients. Learn more about our products here: https://pro.doctoralia.es/pro.doctoralia.es http://pro.doctoralia.es Why join us? 📊 Real impact – We help doctors help patients. Your work truly makes a difference. 📈 At scale, yet agile – 3,000+ employees, but still fast, flexible, and hands-on. ✨ Shape the future, sustain growth – Make a difference now and build for long-term success. WHAT YOU'LL OWN - Define and maintain process and quality standards for CS execution across the marketplace business. - Establish and run the regular operating cadence (business reviews, escalation management, performance check-ins) with local ops leaders. - Own the CS operational readiness and validation process for new product releases across the business. - Own quality validation: verify that local execution aligns with the Quality Lead's rubric, proactively flagging and closing gaps. - Drive cross-market consistency so the same customer problem is solved with the same high standard in every country. - Partner with the Quality Lead and Strategic Ops data team to translate QA findings and core metrics into concrete operational improvements. - Support the rollout and alignment of strategic initiatives across all market operations. - Reshape inbound contact channels by optimizing AI-led deflection, routing, and self-service. - Identify operational efficiencies to sustainably reduce cost per ticket globally without sacrificing quality. REQUIREMENTS - 5+ years in customer service or operations management. - Project management experience - Comfortable reading and shaping data - Expeience building projects and leading from 0 to 1 - Experience operating across multiple markets. - Strong stakeholder management without formal authority (dotted-line experience). - Process design and documentation discipline. - Comfortable owning quality validation and process governance. - Fluent English. - Nice to have: multi-market marketplace or consumer business experience, an additional European language (ES, PL, IT, PT, CZ, DE) WHAT TO EXPECT FROM OUR HIRING PROCESS We like to keep things transparent and efficient!
