Instructor & Customer Success Manager (6200)

Shield AI · Kyiv, Ukraine

As the Instructor & Customer Success Manager, you will lead a team responsible for developing and delivering classroom, simulator, flight, maintenance, and scenario-based training for Shield AI products. You will ensure customers in Ukraine have the knowledge, skills, and confidence required to safely operate, maintain, and effectively employ our technology.  You will be responsible for instructor development, training quality, operational readiness, curriculum standards, and customer satisfaction. This is a hands-on leadership position that requires close collaboration with customers, engineering, product, flight operations, business development, and other internal teams. The ideal candidate is an experienced training leader, an effective communicator, and a credible UAS professional who is comfortable operating in dynamic domestic and international environments.  What you’ll do:  • Lead, develop, and mentor a team of customer training professionals and UAV instructors. • Establish team priorities, performance expectations, staffing plans, and professional-development pathways. • Oversee the development and delivery of classroom, simulator, flight, maintenance, and scenario-based training for Shield AI products. • Ensure training programs prepare customers to safely operate, maintain, deploy, and effectively employ Shield AI systems. • Maintain high instructional standards through instructor qualification, observation, coaching, certification, and continuous evaluation. • Lead the development and standardization of curricula, lesson plans, lecture materials, simulator scenarios, practical exercises, assessments, and supporting documentation. • Ensure student training records, instructor qualifications, course materials, and program metrics remain current and accurate. • Coordinate training schedules, aircraft, simulators, facilities, airspace, frequency management, personnel, and other operational resources. • Communicate directly with customers and partner-force leadership regarding training requirements, student progress, operational readiness, and program outcomes. • Build lasting customer relationships through continuous engagement, refresher training, and support for effective product utilization. • Adapt training for students with varying technical abilities, operational backgrounds, cultural contexts, and levels of experience. • Support domestic and international training events, including extended deployments when required. • Deliver instruction and provide hands-on training support when mission or staffing needs require it. • Partner with engineering, product, design, and flight-operations teams to incorporate customer and instructor feedback into product and training improvements. • Support new-product validation, testing, field evaluations, and the development of training capabilities for new product releases. • Collaborate with business-development and sales teams to demonstrate Shield AI products to prospective customers. • Establish and monitor training-program metrics, identify performance gaps, and implement improvements that increase safety, consistency, scalability, and customer success. • Ensure training activities comply with applicable company policies, safety requirements, security procedures, aviation regulations, and customer expectations. Required qualifications:  • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent combination of education and relevant experience. • Five or more years of experience in technical, aviation, military, or UAS training. • Two or more years of experience leading instructors, training teams, or comparable operational teams. • Demonstrated experience developing and delivering classroom, simulator, flight, maintenance, or scenario-based instruction. • Working knowledge of UAS operations, flight and mission planning, aviation safety, and training operations. • Ability and willingness to travel internationally up to 50%, including for extended periods when required. •
