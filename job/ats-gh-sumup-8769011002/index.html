As a KYC EDD Operations Agent, you'll clear transaction alerts, carry out enhanced due diligence checks, and speak directly with merchants and consumers to sort out issues quickly, helping prevent complaints before they happen and keeping SumUp's platform trustworthy for millions of businesses.
