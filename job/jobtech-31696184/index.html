Veritaz is a leading IT staffing solutions provider in Sweden, committed to advancing individual careers and aiding employers in ensuring the perfect talent fit. With a proven track record of successful partnerships with top companies, we have rapidly grown our presence in the USA, Europe, and Sweden as a dependable and trusted resource within the IT industry. About the service Assignment Description We are seeking a structured and customer-focused Technical Project Manager to manage projects involving control systems, substations, technical services, and product deliveries within the power and energy sector. You will coordinate projects throughout the entire lifecycle, from order intake and planning to execution, testing, commissioning, and final delivery. You will act as the main contact for customers, suppliers, sales teams, and internal project teams, ensuring delivery on time, within budget, and according to requirements. What You Will Work On Manage projects related to substation control systems, automation, and technical deliveries. Coordinate project activities from initiation and planning through execution, testing, commissioning, and final delivery. Communicate with customers regarding technical requirements, schedules, contracts, and delivery status. Coordinate engineering teams, suppliers, and subcontractors. Manage procurement, material planning, and supplier coordination. Handle project finances, cost follow-up, forecasting, reporting, invoicing, and payment tracking. Ensure project documentation and contractual requirements are fulfilled. Coordinate site activities, installation, testing, and commissioning. Identify project risks, manage changes, and take proactive measures to ensure successful delivery. What You Bring Degree or equivalent experience in electrical engineering, automation, power systems, or a related technical field. Technical knowledge of substation environments and control systems, preferably protection and control or substation automation. Proven project management or project coordination experience within the power, energy, or automation industry. Understanding of technical project deliveries, engineering, services, products, and site activities. Proficiency in SAP. Knowledge of project standards and contractual frameworks such as NLM09, ABT06, ESA, and BAS-U/BAS-P. Fluency in Swedish and English, both spoken and written. Strong communication, organizational, and customer relationship skills. Ability to manage multiple projects and take ownership of deliverables. Meritorious Experience with Salesforce. Experience managing complex technical deliveries in the power generation, transmission, distribution, or renewable energy sectors.
