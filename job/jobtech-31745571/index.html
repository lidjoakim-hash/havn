Northmill Bank is a challenger bank at the intersection of technology and finance, committed to revolutionizing the way people manage and protect their financial well-being. We are creating a different kind of banking experience, digital yet personal. Northmill Bank was founded in 2006 and have grown to over 240 employees in 3 countries and over 270 000 customers. We use the latest technology to develop safe, smart, and user-friendly products for our customers. They are the sole reason why we do what we do. We are a 100% cloud-based product company where technology is the driver to create smarter banking products. Grab this opportunity to be a part of us and our journey! About the role As Senior Fraud Specialist, you will play a central role in our efforts to prevent, detect and investigate fraudulent activity across our Swedish and Finnish markets. You will take ownership of complex and high-exposure fraud investigations, make independent decisions within your mandate and act as a senior point of expertise for the team. In addition to your operational responsibilities, you will provide quality assurance through four-eyes reviews, support and coach colleagues, and contribute to improving our fraud detection rules and processes. Working closely with the Head of AFC, Fraud Specialists, Operations, Customer Care and AML, you will help ensure that we maintain strong controls while continuously adapting to new and evolving fraud patterns. This is a senior specialist position with professional leadership responsibilities, but no formal line-management responsibility. Key responsibilities Lead complex fraud investigations, including account takeovers, APP scams, mule networks, organized fraud and cross-border transactions. Make independent decisions on fraud cases, including account blocks, contract terminations and police reports within your mandate. Perform four-eyes reviews and quality assurance of investigations and decisions made by other Fraud Specialists. Identify emerging fraud trends and typologies, translating insights into improvements in our fraud prevention capabilities. Lead the preparation, testing and documentation of changes to our Hawk AI detection rules and monitoring models. Analyze transaction data and detection performance to identify opportunities to improve our controls. Act as a subject matter expert, supporting and coaching colleagues in investigative methods, fraud typologies and documentation. Prepare fraud reporting and support regulatory reporting requirements. Collaborate with internal stakeholders, other financial institutions and law enforcement when needed. Who you are We believe you are an experienced fraud professional who combines strong investigative capabilities with sound judgement and a structured approach to decision-making. You are comfortable taking ownership of complex situations, making independent assessments and guiding others through challenging cases. You are analytical, detail-oriented and curious about how fraud patterns evolve. At the same time, you understand the importance of balancing risk management, customer experience and commercial considerations. You also enjoy sharing your knowledge, challenging existing ways of working and helping a team continuously improve. What we are looking for At least four years of experience in fraud prevention, fraud detection or financial crime, with substantial hands-on experience in operational fraud investigations. Strong knowledge of fraud typologies such as account takeover, APP scams, SIM swapping, bust-out fraud, mule networks and business email compromise. Experience working with automated fraud monitoring systems, ideally Hawk AI, including detection rules, testing and calibration. Understanding of transaction monitoring, device intelligence and session intelligence. Strong analytical skills and the ability to independently investigate and assess transaction data.
