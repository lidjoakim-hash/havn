About the position A PhD position is available in the Robotics Group at the Faculty of Science and Technology. The PhD position is for a period of 3 years, with a desired start date of 1st of January 2026. Recent advances in AI and Large Language Models (LLMs) bring major challenges regarding safety, uncertainty management, accountability, and runtime assurance in autonomous robotic systems. Operating in safety-critical, highly complex, dynamic, and hostile environments require systems to maintain formal safety guarantees while optimizing operational efficiency. As a selected candidate for the announced PhD position, you will research and develop novel, context-aware, AI-enabled Predictive Runtime Verification (PRV) frameworks and adaptive monitors. By anticipating anomalies or failures before they occur, your research will enable proactive corrective actions and real-time replanning in robotic autonomous systems. The PhD position is part of the research project PREVAIL - Predictive Runtime Verification of AI-enabled Systems, funded by the Research Council of Norway (RCN). The project builds upon scientific foundations established in the RoboFarmer project and is conducted in close strategic partnership with the Norwegian Defence University College (FHS) who will also serve as co-supervisor for the candidate and provide opportunity for research stay at FHS. In addition, the PhD work will provide ample opportunity to conduct research in collaboration with international academic partners, most notably at Académie Militaire de Saint-Cyr Coëtquidan, France, University of York, UK and Royal Institute of Technology (KTH) in Stockholm, Sweden. The PREVAIL project will encourage the selected PhD candidate and provide opportunities to benefit intellectually from interaction and extended visits with internationally recognized researchers and institutes. This PhD position is funded through the Research Council of Norway's call Supplementary Funding for Ongoing Projects to Enhance Knowledge and Results Transfer from ICT Research to the Defence Sector. The funding scheme requires candidates to be eligible for security clearance at a minimum level of &#34;Secret&#34;. As the project is expected to involve research subject to Norwegian export control regulations, appointment is contingent upon the successful candidate obtaining the required security clearance and any necessary export control licences or approvals. Main tasks • Survey state-of-the-art AI-enabled Predictive Runtime Verification (PRV) methodologies and analyze their suitability for defence-oriented, safety-critical, autonomous robotic applications.  • Formulate and develop novel algorithms, formal methodologies, and tools for context-aware PRV and AI-enhanced monitor synthesis. • Conduct a research stay at the Norwegian Defence University College (FHS) to align research with the domain-specific requirements and preferences. • Integrate PRV frameworks with adaptive risk-mitigation, failure avoidance, and real-time replanning mechanisms. • Perform early-phase prototyping, software implementation (e.g., ROS 2), and testing on robotic platforms in defence-relevant environments and scenarios. • Author scientific papers for presentation at international high-impact conferences and peer-reviewed journals. • Complete mandatory PhD coursework and seminars in accordance with NMBU doctoral regulations. Competence Required qualifications • A Master's degree or equivalent in Robotics, Computer Science, Cybernetics, Formal Methods, Applied Mathematics, or a closely related field. Foreign degrees must correspond with the admission criteria for the PhD program. Candidates submitting M.Sc. thesis within the application deadline may be considered. • In the Master’s degree, both the average grade point and the Master’s thesis, must be B or better in the Norwegian educational system. Applicants who have recently graduated with excellent results may be given preference. • Proficiency in both written and oral English in correspondence with the admission criteria for the PhD program. • Solid knowledge and background in at least two of the following fields: Formal Methods / Runtime Verification, AI / Machine Learning, Autonomous Systems / Control, or Software Development for Robotics (e.g., ROS/ROS 2).  • Strong programming skills (Python, C&#43;&#43;, etc.)  • The successful candidate must be eligible for security clearance at a minimum level of &#34;Secret&#34; and any licences or approvals required under Norwegian export control legislation • Personal suitability and motivation for the position. Qualifications considered an advantage • Experience with formal verification, model-checking, runtime monitoring, and/or temporal logic synthesis.  • Experience with AI models, Large Language Models (LLMs), or reinforcement learning integrated into robotic control loops. • Experience with safety assurance, risk analysis, adaptive systems, or research involving real-world autonomous applications. • Practical experience working with physical mobile robotic hardware, sensors, and simulation environments. • Previous research and peer-reviewed publications within these domains.
