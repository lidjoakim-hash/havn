Vehicle Test Engineer At Tata Technologies, we make product development dreams a reality by designing, engineering, and delivering the products of tomorrow for the world's leading manufacturers. Role Overview We are seeking an experienced Vehicle Test Engineer to execute end-to-end Validation & Verification (V&V) of Alarm, Tailgate, and Lid functions at vehicle and subsystem levels. The role focuses on ensuring customer function compliance, identifying issues early, and supporting successful integration and release in line with automotive development milestones. Key Responsibilities Vehicle Testing & Validation Perform vehicle-level testing (VIL) for: Tailgate / Boot Lid operations (switches, foot sensor, app control) Alarm functions (motion sensor, inclination, reduced guard) Charge lid functionality (where applicable) Validate functions against customer requirements across all test levels (vehicle, subsystem, component) Test Execution & Coverage Develop, maintain, and execute test cases Work with automation to understand the process, later stage you must maintain and update the automation scripts. Ensure requirement traceability and test coverage Support regression and integration testing for software releases Fault Detection & Analysis Identify, reproduce, and document issues observed during testing Perform log analysis (CAN/LIN) and basic debugging Raise and track defects in VIRA/Jira Collaborate with SW/HW teams for root cause analysis Integration & Release Support Support software flashing, vehicle integration, and release validation Participate in build verification, integration testing, and gate activities Required Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Electronics / Electrical / Automotive Engineering Swedish Valid driving license (mandatory for vehicle testing) 5–10 years of experience in: Vehicle / System / ECU Testing Technical Skills Strong knowledge of vehicle electrical systems and functions Experience with: Vector tools (CANoe, CANalyzer) Diagnostics protocols (UDS) ECU flashing and log analysis Familiarity with HIL testing and test automation using vteststudio / Automation Desk Key Competencies Strong analytical and debugging skills High attention to detail and quality Effective communication and teamwork Proactive and hands-on approach If you are passionate about bringing innovation to the projects you work on, then we would love to hear from you.
