With a proven track record of successful partnerships with top companies, we have rapidly grown our presence in the USA, Europe, and Sweden as a dependable and trusted resource within the IT industry. Job Overview Assignment Description We are looking for a Verification & Test Engineer to support the development of advanced process equipment within a technology-driven R&D organization operating in a highly regulated industry. The assignment focuses on verification and testing of components, modules, and complete systems to ensure performance, robustness, and quality throughout the product development lifecycle. You will work closely with design engineers, project teams, and system specialists, combining hands-on laboratory testing with data analysis, technical documentation, and continuous improvement activities. What You Will Work On Plan, execute, and document verification activities for components, modules, and complete systems. Perform functional, endurance, robustness, and destructive testing. Test mechanical, electromechanical, and fluid-handling components such as valves, pumps, sensors, and assemblies. Conduct environmental testing under controlled temperature and humidity conditions using climate chambers. Perform ingress protection (IP) testing and evaluate sealing performance and product integrity. Build, modify, and improve test fixtures, test rigs, and laboratory setups. Collect, analyse, and evaluate test data to support engineering and design decisions. Develop and maintain test methods, procedures, and verification documentation. Prepare technical reports and communicate test results to project stakeholders. Collaborate with engineering teams throughout the product development lifecycle. What You Bring Technical degree in Mechanical Engineering, Mechatronics, Biomedical Engineering, Industrial Engineering, Engineering Physics, or a related field. Experience in verification, validation, testing, or laboratory activities within product development. Experience planning and executing structured test activities according to requirements and protocols. Strong understanding of test methods, laboratory equipment, test rigs, and measurement systems. Ability to analyse test data and present technical conclusions clearly. Experience preparing technical documentation and test reports. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with attention to detail. Good communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work across engineering functions. Fluent Swedish and English, written and spoken. Meritorious Experience testing valves, pumps, sensors, fluid systems, or process equipment. Experience with environmental testing and climate chamber operation. Knowledge of ingress protection (IP) testing and sealing verification. Experience in regulated industries such as MedTech, Life Science, Pharma, Diagnostics, or Industrial Equipment. Familiarity with Design Verification (DV) and Verification & Validation (V&V) methodologies. Experience developing automated test methods. Programming and scripting experience using Python or AI-assisted tools. Experience with data analysis and visualization. Experience building or modifying test fixtures and laboratory equipment. Familiarity with Lean, 5S, and continuous improvement methodologies.
