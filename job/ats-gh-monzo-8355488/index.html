The Partner Risk Delivery Lead exists to turn our partner risk standards into consistent day to day practice, ensuring controls are understood, evidence is reliable and issues are followed through to closure. You will work directly with partner account teams and risk leads, as well as colleagues across Global Ops, Operations, TPRM and specialist teams. You will independently own defined areas of partner risk delivery, investigate concerns and help partners embed Monzo’s expectations across every location and country we serve. As Partner Risk Delivery Lead, you will independently operate the Partner Risk Management framework set by the Partner Risk Manager. You will own agreed delivery areas from planning through to completion, maintain accurate records and evidence, challenge where standards are not met and escalate material concerns. The Partner Risk Manager will remain accountable for the overall strategy, standards, governance and material risk decisions. 🔑 You’ll play a key role by...
