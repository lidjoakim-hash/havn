In this role, you will set the strategic direction for the section, translate strategy into execution and create the conditions for your organisation to deliver sustainable business value.   You will have overall accountability for the section and play an important role in strengthening our competitiveness, developing strategic supplier relationships and managing business-critical opportunities and risks.   Working in a highly international environment, you will collaborate closely with senior stakeholders, suppliers and counterparts across functions and regions. What you'll do As Head of Battery and Power & Electrics Procurement, you will:   Set the strategic direction for the Battery and Power & Electric Procurement section, translating company and Procurement priorities into clear objectives, plans and execution. Lead, develop and empower leaders and teams, building organisational capability, accountability and a strong performance culture. Drive business and procurement performance, balancing commercial value, cost, quality, delivery, risk and long-term competitiveness. Shape category and supplier strategies, developing strategic supplier relationships and ensuring external competitiveness while proactively managing business-critical opportunities and risks. Drive global and cross-regional alignment, collaborating closely with senior stakeholders and relevant counterparts across functions and geographies. Set direction for strategic negotiations and major commercial decisions, acting as a senior escalation point and enabling strong negotiation approaches across the organisation. Drive continuous improvement and transformation, challenging established ways of working and creating the conditions for simpler, faster and more effective execution. What you'll bring We believe you are a senior leader with a strong commercial mindset and the ability to combine strategic thinking with decisive execution.   You bring: Significant experience from Procurement, Supply Chain, commercial leadership or another relevant business environment, with responsibility for complex and business-critical scopes. Demonstrated experience leading leaders and professional organizations, building capability and creating sustainable performance. Strong business and commercial acumen especially on battery area, with the ability to translate strategy into clear priorities, decisions and measurable outcomes. Extensive experience of complex negotiations, strategic supplier relationships and senior stakeholder management. The ability to lead and make sound decisions in situations where information is incomplete, priorities compete and the way forward is not always obvious. Strong communication and influencing skills, with the ability to create clarity, alignment and engagement across functions, cultures and geographies. Fluency in Chinese and English, both written and spoken.
