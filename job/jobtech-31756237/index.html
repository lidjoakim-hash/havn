Certification Engineer

Allgon AB · Göteborg, Sweden

💰 ca. 510 000–620 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 5.1.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Who We Are Allgon is a leading manufacturer of wireless machinery control systems, delivering customized solutions to companies across the industrial sector. Our customers engage with us through our subsidiaries Tele Radio, Åkerströms and Sistematica. We are passionate about developing industrial remote control solutions that enhance safety and efficiency worldwide. You will join our Product Compliance & QESG team, which works with product compliance, quality, environment, sustainability and management systems. We are a small, close-knit team spread across Gothenburg, Björbo and Málaga. You report to the department manager at the head office in Gothenburg. The role can be based in Gothenburg, Björbo/Borlänge or Málaga, Spain. You will work closely with colleagues across Allgon’s global organization. About the Role As a Certification Engineer at Allgon, you make sure our products obtain and keep all required approvals before and after market release. This covers radio, RF exposure, EMC, electrical safety and functional safety. You will also support the work to meet new requirements under the EU Machinery Regulation and cybersecurity legislation. You will independently manage certification projects, from updates to existing approvals to new approvals in development projects. You will work closely with R&D, product management, quality, manufacturing, external test laboratories, certification bodies and our subsidiaries. Key Responsibilities: Interpret and apply EU and international regulations and standards (RED, EMC, Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230, FCC, ISED and others) Support R&D with functional safety: safety requirements, risk assessment, PL/SIL verification and safety documentation (ISO 13849, IEC 62061, IEC 61508) Drive product cybersecurity compliance (RED Article 3.3 d/e/f, EN 18031, the Cyber Resilience Act and the Machinery Regulation) Plan and run certification projects Coordinate testing with external laboratories, and work with TCB/FCB, Notified Bodies and authorities Review test reports, technical files, declarations of conformity and product labeling Manage updates, renewals and maintenance of existing approvals Monitor regulatory changes and assess their impact on our products Provide certification input during new product development and market expansion Support customer service and subsidiaries with certification expertise What We’re Looking For You are a detail-oriented and analytical engineer with a strong interest in product certification and regulatory requirements. You enjoy working independently, take ownership of your tasks and are comfortable coordinating many stakeholders. With a structured approach and clear communication, you turn complex requirements into practical actions that support product development and market access. Key qualifications include: Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Electrical Engineering or a related field Experience in product certification, regulatory affairs or a similar role Strong knowledge of RF and EMC standards and global wireless certification requirements Knowledge of and practical experience with functional safety (ISO 13849, IEC 62061/IEC 61508), e.g. risk assessment and PL/SIL determination and verification Experience working with EU and international regulations (e.g. RED, EMC, Machinery Regulation, FCC, ISED, MIC) Ability to review and interpret test reports and certification documentation Understanding of PLM systems and certification documentation processes Fluent English, spoken and written. Swedish, Spanish or other languages are also a plus. It is a plus if you have: A functional safety certification Experience with product cybersecurity (RED Article 3.3 d/e/f, EN 18031, CRA) Experience with RF exposure assessment and TCB/FCB processes Experience with marine/offshore type approvals from classification societies (e.g. DNV, Lloyd’s Register) Project management experience What We Offer Work with cutting-edge, high-quality products in a dynamic environmen
