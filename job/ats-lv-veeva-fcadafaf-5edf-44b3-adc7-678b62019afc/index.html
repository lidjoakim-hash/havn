The Role Join Veeva as a Team Manager and Country Lead in Poland, combining SaaS Managed Services delivery with local leadership. You will lead a high-performing team of consultants and act as the primary cultural and administrative representative for Veeva in Poland. This position is fully remote in Poland, requiring up to 15% of time for business travel across EMEA. What You’ll Do • Lead the hiring and people management strategy for our Managed Services team in Poland, aligning compensation, market knowledge, and local labor laws with the Veeva standards. • Directly manage a high-performing team of experts and managers by ensuring accurate resource planning, capacity management, and individual performance management. • Collaborate closely with other Professional Services and Managed Services teams to drive operational synergy. • Engage directly with customers and serve as the escalation and delivery lead on selected strategic accounts. Requirements • 10+ years of overall professional IT consulting experience, including recent employment at a global consulting or software company. • 5+ years of direct experience managing a billable consulting or services team of at least 30 people within the local market. • Strong, proven track record of hiring and scaling professional teams in Poland or Central Europe. • Clear and practical understanding of the Polish tech market and Polish labor law. • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English. • Based in or around Poznan, Lodz, Warsaw, Krakow, or Wroclaw.
