WHAT YOU’LL BE DOING As Head of IT Product Management you own the IT Product Management domain and are responsible for the strategy, portfolio, roadmap, and performance of the solutions supporting Product Lifecycle & Data, R&D & Engineering Applications, Product Configuration, and Compliance & Documentation. Leading a team of five, you will define the domain strategy, roadmap, and investment priorities, ensuring technology drives innovation, efficiency, and business value. Working closely with business leaders, engineers, architects, and external partners, the role ensures that product development, engineering, and product data capabilities are aligned and integrated to support efficient end-to-end business processes. Your key responsibilities: Domain Strategy & Leadership: Own the Product Management domain strategy, roadmap, investments, KPIs, and financial performance, ensuring alignment with business objectives and delivering measurable business value, innovation, and sustainable growth. Product Portfolio & Roadmap Ownership: Lead the Product Management portfolio and roadmap, ensuring strategic investments, sustainable technology decisions, and the transition from project-based delivery to long-term product and capability ownership. Business Partnership & Value Realization: Act as a strategic partner to business leaders and key stakeholders, driving value realization through aligned priorities, clear roadmaps, and technology-enabled business improvements. Product Lifecycle & Capability Development: Lead the development and continuous improvement of product lifecycle systems, product data, and engineering applications to support efficient, scalable, and compliant business operations. Delivery & Product Team Leadership: Lead and develop the Product Management organization by establishing clear accountability, aligned priorities, and effective ways of working that enable cross-functional teams to deliver business value, operational excellence, and continuous improvement. Architecture, Integration & Data Alignment: Ensure domain solutions align with enterprise architecture, integration and data standards, supporting scalable, simplified end-to-end processes while coordinating dependencies across connected business and technology domains. Operational Health & Lifecycle Management: Ensure the operational health, performance, security, and continuous evolution of the Product Management domain by balancing innovation, lifecycle management, technical sustainability, and service excellence while delivering measurable business value.
