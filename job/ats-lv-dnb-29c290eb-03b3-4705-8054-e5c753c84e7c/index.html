We are so excited to now be looking for an experienced public policy and regulatory affairs professional to help us anticipate and navigate a rapidly evolving global regulatory landscape. Reporting to our Senior Director, Public Policy & Regulatory Affairs in London, you'll play a key role in shaping our approach to regulatory change across Europe and globally. You'll lead important elements of our global regulatory readiness programme while supporting the development of policy positions, advocacy strategies and engagement with policymakers, regulators and industry bodies. You'll work closely with senior leaders and teams across the business to assess emerging regulatory developments, understand their potential impact and translate complex requirements into clear, practical recommendations. Your remit will span some of the most important issues affecting the data and technology sector, including AI, data protection, financial crime, consumer protection, ESG, competition and international trade. This is an opportunity to combine regulatory insight, public policy and business strategy in a highly visible global role, helping Dun & Bradstreet respond effectively to regulatory change while supporting responsible innovation and growth. Based in our beautiful offices in Paddington, London, this role works on a hybrid basis with at least 3 days on site and will require occasional travel.
