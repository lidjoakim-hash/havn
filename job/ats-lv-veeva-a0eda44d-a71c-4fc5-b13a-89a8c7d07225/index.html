The Role Join Veeva Systems as a Migration Architect and play a key role in shaping how leading Life Sciences organizations move their critical data and content to the cloud. As a trusted partner and customer advocate, you’ll collaborate across Services, Product, Sales, and Strategy to ensure long-term customer success and continuous improvement.   This is a remote position within the EU/UK. We encourage qualified, EU/UK-based candidates to apply.
