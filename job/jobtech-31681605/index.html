🚀 We’re Hiring: Experienced Computer Vision Engineer – C++ | Camera & LiDAR | Autonomous Driving Company: Hykmann Global Business Solutions AB Role: Experienced Computer Vision Engineer Specialisation: Computer Vision | C++ | Camera & LiDAR | Robotics | Autonomous Systems Experience: Several years of relevant industry experience Work Environment: Hybrid About the Role Hykmann Global Business Solutions AB is looking for an experienced Computer Vision Engineer to contribute to the development of advanced computer vision algorithms and intelligent perception systems for next-generation mobility and autonomous technologies. You will work on high-performance algorithms involving camera and LiDAR data, focusing on calibration, vehicle motion estimation, vision-based localization and geometric computer vision. The role offers an opportunity to work across the complete software development lifecycle, from research and proof of concept to production-ready implementation, simulation and real-world testing. Key Responsibilities Design, develop and optimize computer vision algorithms for camera- and LiDAR-based systems. Develop geometric computer vision solutions, including visual odometry, camera calibration, localization and 3D reconstruction. Develop algorithms for real-time calibration and vehicle positioning. Drive product development from initial concepts and requirements through implementation, code reviews, integration and testing. Develop high-performance, maintainable and production-grade C++ software for real-time systems running on Linux. Perform algorithm evaluation and verification using simulation environments, Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) setups and real-vehicle testing. Develop Python-based tools for monitoring, analysing and visualizing algorithm performance. Conduct experiments, evaluate research findings and transform theoretical concepts into practical engineering solutions. Analyse performance metrics and real-world datasets to improve algorithm accuracy, reliability and efficiency. Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams across computer vision, deep learning and platform engineering. Contribute to software architecture, technical discussions and continuous improvement initiatives. Required Skills & Experience Strong programming skills in C++, with experience developing high-performance and maintainable software. Several years of relevant professional experience in software development or algorithm engineering. Background in Computer Vision, Robotics, Computer Science, Software Engineering, Electrical Engineering or a related technical discipline. Strong understanding of mathematics relevant to geometric computer vision. Practical experience developing computer vision algorithms and working with sensor data. Experience integrating software into complex products or real-time systems. Ability to translate mathematical models, research findings and theoretical concepts into production-ready software. Experience working across software development, testing and integration activities. Strong analytical and problem-solving capabilities. Good communication and collaboration skills. Meritorious Experience Experience in any of the following areas will be a significant advantage: Industrial Computer Vision. Camera and LiDAR-based perception systems. Autonomous driving and Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS). Visual odometry, sensor calibration and localization. Deep Learning and its application in computer vision. Python programming for engineering tools, data analysis and visualization. GPU programming, CUDA and parallel computing. Computer graphics and 3D reconstruction. Embedded software development and Linux-based systems. Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) testing and simulation environments. Software architecture, system design and requirements management.
