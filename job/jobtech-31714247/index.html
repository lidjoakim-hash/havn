Senior Product Manager - Stockholm or Hudiksvall

Björn Lundén AB · Hudiksvall, Sweden

Want to tackle complex customer problems, shape product strategy, and turn insight into measurable impact? Come and join us as a Senior Product Manager. About the Role We’re looking for a Senior Product Manager who combines strategic thinking with hands-on product leadership. You’ll shape the vision and strategy for your product area and turn customer insights into measurable customer and business outcomes. You’ll lead your Product Trio and the work of your empowered cross-functional team through expertise, influence, and collaboration, without formal people-management responsibilities. What You’ll Do Product Leadership Set the vision and strategy for your product area. Lead discovery and delivery with your Product Trio and cross functional team. Use customer insights and data to define impactful solutions. Set success metrics and ensure measurable outcomes. Align stakeholders across commercial, technical, and customer-facing teams. Lead through influence and champion modern product practices. You’ll often lead your team remotely rather than work together in person day to day, making strong communication and trust-building essential. Who You Are To succeed in this role, you bring strategic thinking, strong collaboration skills, and a natural drive to move things forward. We place more weight on your personal qualities than on having the “perfect” resume. You are: A strong collaborator who builds trust and brings people together. Confident in making decisions, even in complex situations. Proactive and self‑driven, with a strong sense of ownership. Strategic, analytical, and able to prioritise what matters most. Goal- and results-oriented with the energy to drive progress. A supportive, inclusive leader who develops and coaches others. Guided by integrity and empathy, fostering a positive and safe environment. What You Bring We believe the right mindset and personal qualities matter most. But to thrive, you likely have: Experience as a Product Manager working with modern product development. Curiosity and drive. You make things happen rather than wait for direction. Strong communication skills in English; Swedish is an advantage. Experience with SaaS, the Product Operating Model, or Agile frameworks is a plus. Experience working with AI coding assistants and context repositories is a plus. Who We Are We are Bjorn Lunden, the business platform that simplifies everyday life for small and medium-sized businesses. Our belief is simple: every business deserves opportunities to grow, and we’re here to make that happen 🐻. We’re on an exciting journey. With bold ambitions, our vision is to become one of the leading providers of accounting and financial software solutions in Northwestern Europe. With our roots in Hälsingland, specifically in the small village of Näsviken, we’ve grown rapidly in recent years, transforming from a smaller national company into an international group. Today, we operate in Sweden, the Netherlands, and Denmark. Our goal is straightforward: to make life a little easier for entrepreneurs and business owners. We believe we can do that through great software, knowledge, innovation, and TACO. Not the kind you might be thinking of - but our version: Trust, Ambition, Collaboration, and Ownership. What We Offer We offer you a role in an international, growing company with a strong market position and the ambition to challenge industry giants. The wellbeing of our employees truly matters to us, and we want to be a fair and responsible employer where people feel good and enjoy their work. We strive for a healthy work-life balance and support this through competitive conditions and benefits and flexible work opportunities. We want to be the natural first choice for people who want to thrive and have fun at work. Application This is a full-time, permanent position based in Stockholm or Hudiksvall. Application deadline is 13th December 2026, but selection and interviews may take place on an ongoing basis. The position
