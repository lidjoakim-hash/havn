As a Production Planner, you will be in charge of our operational production planning and ensuring a smooth production flow. This role plays a key part in delivery performance, capacity utilisation, and process optimisation through efficient scheduling, material availability checks, and close coordination with the Production Manager. Location: Kassel, Hesse Reports to: General Manager of Kassel Operations What You’ll Do • Create and maintain production orders based on demand and capacity data. • Check material availability in the system before order release. • Book and track production orders in the ERP system. • Analyse and address discrepancies between system and physical stock. • Support operational purchasing based on predefined materials and suppliers. • Coordinate closely with the Production Manager to align capacity and optimise resource planning. • Identify and support resolution of material shortages. • Evaluate and report lead times, order statuses, and production KPIs (e.g. delay causes). Key Responsibilities • Production Planning: Manage and maintain production schedules aligned with demand and available capacity. • Order Execution: Create, release, and track production orders in the ERP system. • Material Coordination: Ensure material availability and resolve stock discrepancies in coordination with warehouse and purchasing. • Cross-Functional Alignment: Work closely with the Production Manager to optimise planning and capacity utilisation. • Reporting & Analysis: Monitor production KPIs, delays, and lead times to support performance improvement. What You Bring • Completed vocational training or degree in business/logistics. • Experience in production planning or control. • Proficient in ERP systems (system-agnostic) and MS Excel. • Structured, self-directed, and systematic working style. • Robust analytical skills with attention to detail but not over-focusing. • Solution-oriented and factual communication style. • Fluent in German and English (spoken and written). Collaboration with the Production Manager • The Production Planner handles system-side planning and control – the Production Manager is in charge of execution on the shop floor. • Daily coordination and capacity alignment to ensure implementation and adaptability in production. • Clear role separation ensures efficiency and avoids overlaps.
