Unit for Field-based Forest Research Would you like to take on a key leadership role, guiding a dedicated team and contributing to the future of field-based forest research? With us, you will have the opportunity to work outdoors in spectacular natural surroundings and help develop groundbreaking projects addressing forest ecosystem and climate-related issues. We are looking for a hands-on professional with research experience and leadership skills to lead operations at the Siljansfors and Jädraås Experimental Forests, as well as serve as a member of the management team for the entire Unit for Field-Based Forest Research. This position offers excellent opportunities for someone who enjoys a varied range of responsibilities, thrives on challenges, and wants to contribute to research on boreal forest production and forest ecosystems. In 2024, the Unit revised its strategic operational plan through to 2030, resulting in a partially new organisation structure that has recently been implemented. In line with the Faculty’s ambition to broaden its activities, the Unit’s remit is also being gradually expanded. This calls for a cohesive and functional organisation across the Unit, structured around its four main areas of activity: long-term experiments, major research infrastructure, commissioned projects, and environmental monitoring and analysis. About the position You will be a member of the Unit’s management team. As manager of the Siljansfors and Jädraås Experimental Forests, you will have overall responsibility for operations within your area of responsibility (Svealand and southern Norrland), including financial and personnel management, as well as occupational health and safety matters. Your role is to ensure that activities at the experimental forests develop in a way that best supports field research, environmental monitoring, and education, while remaining within the allocated budget. Cooperation within SLU and engagement with the surrounding community are also ongoing aspects of the station managers responsibilities. As the station manager, you will work together with your colleagues to ensure that ongoing forest field experiments are maintained and revised according to plan, using modern technology, and that new experiments are established today to address future research questions. You will be responsible for the content of metadata databases and the databases associated with them. You will also be responsible for ensuring that the Siljansfors team actively participates in the steering groups for the Unit’s various main areas of activity, and that the tasks agreed upon by these groups are implemented within your area of responsibility. An important part of the role involves receiving requests and, whenever possible, carrying them out. These may concern the establishment or revision of forest field experiments, environmental monitoring assignments, as well as teaching or outreach activities.
