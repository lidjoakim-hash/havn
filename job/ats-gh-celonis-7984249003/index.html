Celonis is the trusted platform to industrialize Enterprise AI. At our core is the Celonis Context Model — which combines process data, business knowledge, and intelligence into a living digital twin of the enterprise that AI can actually understand, turning AI's operational blind spots into operational clarity. World's leading companies trust Celonis and its global ecosystem of partners to make their AI agents, people, systems, and processes work together, achieving transformational outcomes. We believe there's a massive opportunity to unlock global productivity and sustainability by giving AI the context to understand how every business process really works. Join our mission to make processes work for people, companies, and the planet. The Team: The Celonis Business Apps Supply Chain team is an innovative group of product and technology enthusiasts focused on creating and deploying next-generation solutions in the Supply Chain domain. We are transforming how enterprise solutions are built driven by our customers' business critical problems. Our team strikes an ideal balance between deep technical execution (including advanced data engineering and AI tooling) and a sharp customer focus to create highly impactful products that accelerate value realization across our customer base. The Role: You will gain experience in the Supply Chain and related processes and act as the interface between customers, our Go-To-Market and our Product & Engineering teams, shaping the future of Celonis’ product offering in this domain. You get hands-on: prototyping on the platform, wiring up data, building AI-driven solutions, and iterating directly with the people who'll use them.
