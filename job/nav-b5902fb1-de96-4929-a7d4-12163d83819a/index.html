We are proud to contribute to the development of the region and to creating a brighter, greener future.About Nova IndustriNova Industri AB is now part of SMS group Nordic, with SMS group GmbH in Germany as our parent company. We are a leading service company specializing in industrial production and mechanical engineering, with operations in Boden and Kalix.For more than 20 years, we have delivered high-quality solutions in turning, milling, welding and steel construction. Our customers are primarily industrial companies in Norrbotten, and our strong local presence is an important part of our success.About the RoleWe are looking for someone with several years of experience in milling, as well as operating and programming 5-axis CNC machines. You are able to produce a finished component based on technical drawings and work instructions, and you have a good understanding of machine operation and programming. At Nova Industri, you will work with advanced machinery in a technology-driven environment.Your ProfileTo be considered for the position, we would like you to have:    Upper-secondary vocational training in an industrial field or equivalent experience.    Several years of experience in milling and working with 5-axis CNC machines.    Experience in offline programming.    A good ability to read and interpret technical drawings and work instructions.    The ability to understand technical documentation.    A strong commitment to maintaining a safe, clean and well-organized work environment.Experience programming and working with control systems such as Fanuc, Heidenhain, NUM, Siemens and Tebis is an advantage. Experience with CAD/CAM programming and Mastercam is also an advantage. Since our work environment is international, the ability to communicate in English is beneficial. A Category B driving licence and an overhead crane certificate are also advantageous.As a Person, You Are    Precise and strongly focused on safety and quality.    Self-motivated, able to take initiative and solve problems that arise in your work.    Cooperative and flexible in your approach.How to ApplyDoes this sound like an interesting opportunity?
