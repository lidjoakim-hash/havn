At Taskrabbit, we want to transform lives one task at a time. As a company we celebrate innovation, inclusion and hard work. Our culture is collaborative, pragmatic, and fast-paced. About The Role As the Lead Partner Manager, IKEA Global Partnership, you will drive strategic alignment and operational growth between Taskrabbit and IKEA across global markets. Reporting to the Director, Global IKEA Partnership, you will translate high-level vision into actionable market initiatives, establishing key governance frameworks and introducing innovative touchpoints for customers to discover and book home services. This hands-on role offers a direct, visible path to shaping partnership strategy, working directly with diverse team members, and building meaningful relationships across senior IKEA leadership and country partner managers. Often you visit IKEA stores and regional offices, traveling across international markets to engage directly with key stakeholders on the ground and position Taskrabbit as an integral part of the IKEA customer journey.
