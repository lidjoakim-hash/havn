Who we are Helsing is a defence AI company. Our mission is to protect our democracies. We aim to achieve technological leadership, so that open societies can continue to make sovereign decisions and control their ethical standards. As democracies, we believe we have a special responsibility to be thoughtful about the development and deployment of powerful technologies like AI. We take this responsibility seriously. We are an ambitious and committed team of engineers, AI specialists and customer-facing programme managers. We are looking for mission-driven people to join our European teams – and apply their skills to solve the most complex and impactful problems. We embrace an open and transparent culture that welcomes healthy debates on the use of technology in defence, its benefits, and its ethical implications. The role As a Physical Security Specialist at Helsing Germany GmbH in Munich, you will oversee and safeguard the comprehensive physical security of our locations. You will manage the entire supply chain of Official Secrets, ranging from rigorous facility inspections to the validation of implemented security systems and the secure handling of classified materials. This role is crucial to maintaining the operational integrity of our facilities, ensuring that our environments remain secure against evolving threats while enabling our teams to advance our core mission safely. updates and alerts across standard internal communication channels • Draft comprehensive incident reports, shift logs, and operational documentation to maintain accurate security records • Monitor and
