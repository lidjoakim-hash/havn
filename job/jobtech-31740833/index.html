ABOUT US Carotte currently consists of three companies: Housekeeping, Food & Event, and Staff. Together, we provide comprehensive solutions for everything related to food, meetings, and service for our clients. The group was founded in 1982 and has offices in Stockholm, Jönköping, and Gothenburg. Carotte Housekeeping is one of the fastest-growing companies within the Carotte Group and the cleaning industry. For us, Housekeeping means taking office cleaning to the next level. We provide high-quality cleaning services with a focus on the overall office environment. We currently work with several clients who require these types of services, while also recognizing a growing market demand for a high-quality cleaning company. Our vision is to raise standards within the industry and deliver first-class housekeeping services to corporate clients in Stockholm, Jönköping, and Gothenburg. ABOUT THE POSITION Main Responsibilities We are looking for Housekeepers/Cleaners to join Carotte Housekeeping in Gothenburg. In this role, you will work at our clients' premises, carrying out a variety of cleaning tasks. We are looking for individuals interested in part-time positions at our exciting client locations. Working hours vary and include mornings, daytime, and evenings. Full Job Description We are looking for someone who takes pride in creating clean, welcoming, and pleasant environments for our clients. You are motivated by quality and customer satisfaction. You are detail-oriented, responsible, efficient, service-minded, and have a keen eye for detail. You have a positive attitude and always strive to exceed customer expectations. You are comfortable working both independently and as part of a team. Previous experience in commercial cleaning or similar work is required, as well as knowledge of Swedish or English. ADDITIONAL INFORMATION We look forward to receiving your application! Please apply by clicking the "Apply Now" button. Applications are reviewed on an ongoing basis, and we encourage you to submit your application as soon as possible. Please note that we do not accept applications via email. However, if you have any questions or need further information, please feel free to contact us at mirey@carotte.se.
