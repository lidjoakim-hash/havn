ROLE HIGHLIGHTS Title: Stock and Logistics Specialist Department: Global Manufacturing Organisation Reports to: Logistics and Stock Manager Location: Valencia, Spain Type of Contract: Permanent Working model: On-site ABOUT THE ROLE ICEYE is looking for a Stock and Logistics Specialist to take ownership of stock and inventory control at our Valencia site and to get materials to production as fast as possible. You will combine hands-on warehouse operations with inventory accuracy, the receiving process, material planning, and rapid delivery, working closely with Production, AIT, Engineering, Quality, and Global Supply Chain. Your priority is simple: the right material, in the right quantity and condition, delivered to the right production area as soon as it is needed, so that our teams can build and test satellites without waiting. You will also provide clear inventory insights and KPI reporting that support high-impact initiatives across ICEYE's Global Manufacturing Organization. This is a hands-on role in a growing manufacturing environment where processes and systems are continuously being developed. You will be expected to work with accuracy and discipline, follow and improve established procedures, keep the warehouse organised, and raise issues when something is not correct. WHO WE ARE ICEYE is the world leader in sovereign intelligence from space. We deliver space-based intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance (ISR) capabilities to governments and allied nations, including sovereign and turnkey ISR missions built on our synthetic aperture radar (SAR) satellite technology, as well as access to data from the world's largest SAR satellite constellation. Our near real-time data works day or night and in any weather, with ultra high-resolution imagery and high-frequency revisits. It serves defence, intelligence, security, and maritime domain awareness customers, as well as civil protection and commercial users in natural-catastrophe intelligence, insurance, maritime monitoring, and finance. Founded and headquartered in Finland, ICEYE operates globally, with offices in Finland, Poland, Spain, Japan, the UAE, Greece, and the US, and more than 900 employees. WHAT YOU WILL DO YOUR DAY-TO-DAY RESPONSIBILITIES - Run inbound and outbound logistics for all satellite components, ensuring prompt receipt, verification, and documentation, with every receipt booked in ERP/WMS right away. Deliver materials, picked and kitted to requirements, to production and AIT areas in the correct sequence, quantity, and condition, prioritising urgent builds and tests. Prepare, pack, and support outbound shipments, coordinating with carriers when required. - Maintain accurate inventory records, including tracking, labeling, and barcoding of all parts and materials, and make sure every movement is booked correctly. Plan and execute periodic physical stock counts, audits, and cycle counts, and investigate and resolve discrepancies. Implement and manage material obsolescence plans, including identification, tracking, and replacement of obsolete components before production use. - Ensure proper storage, handling, and preservation of sensitive parts, including ESD-sensitive devices and RF/electronics components, following traceability, quarantine, and non-conforming material procedures. Keep storage areas clean and organised, including bin maintenance, material repackaging, and FIFO/FEFO procedures. - Coordinate with Global Supply Chain to monitor stock levels, manage safety stock targets, and prepare replenishment plans aligned with production schedules. Support quality and compliance by enforcing proper documentation practices, material identification standards, and handling procedures. - Support inbound inspection and the disposition of non-conforming materials with Quality and Supplier teams.
