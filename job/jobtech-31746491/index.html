Frontier Fellow - Forward Deployed AI Engineer

Fellowmind Sweden AB · Malmö, Sweden

The most valuable product development happens where the product meets reality. As a Forward Deployed Engineer, you work at that intersection: you turn AI agents into production-ready customer solutions and bring what you learn back into the product. We call this role a Frontier Fellow: part senior engineer, part trusted technical advisor and part bridge between our customers, delivery teams and the HiveAI product team. HiveAI is Fellowmind's agent platform. About the role You belong to the HiveAI product organisation, but you will do much of your work alongside our implementation teams and customers. Building one agent is easy now. Building agents a business can rely on every day is not. That's where you come in: you take on the hardest problems in our customer projects and solve them. The job doesn't end when the solution works. You turn what you learn into HiveAI: when relevant, functionality, patterns and components built for one customer become reusable capabilities that help the next customer and everyone else. What you will do Fix the hard parts. Step in where agents meet real business systems, identity and users, and make them work. When shared components fall short, build your own. Get AI agents into use, fast. Work with our delivery teams from the first customer conversation to go-live. Put a working first version in front of users within days. Be a front runner. Help customers and Fellowmind teams make sound architecture decisions, challenge approaches that won't scale and explain technical trade-offs clearly. Shape HiveAI. Share recurring requirements and architectural insights with the HiveAI development team. Raise the bar for everyone. Share knowledge, patterns and best practices so implementation teams can deliver HiveAI successfully on their own. What you might build An agent that takes over a whole task, like handling supplier invoices or preparing quotes, and asks a person when a decision is needed. An agent that works across ERP, CRM and the intranet on behalf of the person who asked. A team of agents that share one business process, handing work to each other. The tools, MCP servers and integrations they need. Who are you? You are an experienced engineer who enjoys being close to both the technology and the customer. You can't stop building agents, and you want people to actually use them. You'd rather show something that works than a design document. You make good decisions without all the information, and you're as comfortable in a customer workshop as in a code review. You bring: Coding agents such as GitHub Copilot as your everyday way of working, and the judgement to know which generated code will hold up in production. At least 4 years of professional software development experience, with strong skills in at least one programming language and solid engineering habits: branches, pull requests and tests Experience taking software into production, where security, reliability and performance matter. Practical knowledge of AI models and agents: how they behave, what they cost, how to measure them with evals. Working knowledge of Azure, Entra ID, On-Behalf-Of and least privilege. Direct customer-facing experience, from technical discovery and workshops to architecture discussions and communication with senior stakeholders. A good understanding of modern product development, including when customer-specific functionality should become a reusable product capability. Nobody ticks every box. If you're strong in several of these and know where to find help for the rest, we'd like to hear from you. What is HiveAI? At Fellowmind, we're building HiveAI, a platform for developing, deploying and operating AI solutions at scale. It gives enterprises a secure and scalable foundation to move beyond isolated experiments, so AI becomes part of how the business operates.
