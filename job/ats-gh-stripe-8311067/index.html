Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team The Seller Experience & Insights team is responsible for ensuring Stripe’s Go-to-Market teams have the tools, systems, processes and policies they need to sell effectively. We identify friction in how sellers get work done, translate seller needs into scalable solutions and partner across Product, Engineering, Data Science, Sales Operations and Enablement to improve productivity and the quality of Stripe’s customer engagement. What you’ll do Stripe is evolving how Sales Development Representatives identify, engage and qualify prospective users. Agentic workflows can remove repetitive work and help SDRs focus their time on judgement-rich customer conversations. Realising that value requires more than introducing new tools: it requires a coherent end-to-end experience across targeting, outreach, task execution, lead handoff, follow-up, qualification and reporting. You will own the business experience and improvement roadmap for Stripe’s SDR workflows. You will work closely with SDRs and their leaders to understand how work happens today, identify the highest-value sources of friction and shape tools, processes and policies that make the organisation more effective. Your initial coverage will be Stripe’s agentic outreach solution and Gong Engage, including how these capabilities interact with Salesforce, Orum, Gmail, LinkedIn and supporting routing and data services. Over time, the remit may expand to other parts of the SDR tooling and workflow ecosystem. You will act as the connective tissue between frontline sellers and the teams that build and operate these capabilities. You will turn ambiguous problems into clear requirements, prioritised roadmaps, measurable launches and continuously improving workflows.
