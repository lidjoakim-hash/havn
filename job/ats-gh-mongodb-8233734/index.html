MongoDB is seeking an exceptional Product Manager to own disaster recovery and business continuity for the world's leading developer data platform. In this role, you will own the product vision, requirements, and execution plan for Standby Clusters, MongoDB's disaster recovery offering for both Atlas and Enterprise Advanced. Standby Clusters continuously replicates a production cluster to an isolated standby in a separate region, datacenter, or cloud provider, delivering sub-minute recovery time and near-zero data loss. You will work closely with engineering, customers in regulated industries, and the field to take the product from preview to GA and beyond, and to shape MongoDB's broader strategy for replication, recovery, and resilience. We are looking to speak to candidates who are based in Dublin for our hybrid working model.
