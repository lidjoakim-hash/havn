Job ID: 7501 (Senior) Quantitative Analyst, Model Monitoring and Performance (permanent position) Are you a quantitative analyst with a knack for navigating complex databases and producing clear analysis? Do you have knowledge of credit risk processes and an interest in, or experience with, IRB, IFRS9 or similar models? Then this opportunity may be for you! Quantitative Analyst to join the Model Monitoring and Performance team (MMP) within Risk Models. Nordea are currently developing and implementing a suite of new, state-of-the-art IRB and IFRS9 models. MMP is responsible for ensuring that the new models’ performance is continuously scrutinized, and for identifying any issues arising within them. Further, we are responsible for assessing and overseeing how IRB models are used in credit processes, including whether model use is in line with regulations, internal requirements and the approved purpose of the models. Our key stakeholders are model developers, model users in Business and senior management. About our team Meet the Model Monitoring and Performance team. Our role is to contribute to assessments of how IRB models are used in credit processes, communicate identified issues and help develop clear internal guidelines for appropriate model use. Our team is an inspiring, competent and creative group of professionals in an international environment. In your daily job, you’ll be applying your quantitative and data analysis skills to produce clear and concise communication about model performance and performance issues to model developers and senior management. You’ll mainly be working with Python, SQL and Snowflake, but SAS and other tools may be used here and there. The role will be located in Helsinki or Stockholm and team colleagues are in Stockholm, Helsinki and Copenhagen. Collaboration. Ownership. Passion. Courage. These are the values that guide us in how we work and how we make decisions – and that we imagine you share with us. What you’ll be doing Main responsibilities in this role: Quantitative analysis of model performance for all components of IRB and/or IFRS9 models (PD, LGD, EAD). Communicating conclusions of your analysis to model developers and senior management – in reports and meetings. Developing, maintaining and operating the IRB and IFRS9 model performance monitoring frameworks. Collaborating with a variety of stakeholders including risk management, model development, model validation and model users across the bank. Assessing how IRB models are used in credit processes, identifying potential gaps against regulatory and internal requirements, and communicating conclusions and follow-up needs. Who you are This is the right role for you if you: Are driven by a desire to know more. Love to make sense of data. Are self-driven and take ownership. Find it easy to understand others, and make yourself understood by others. Love to think in terms of the big picture, but know that the devil is in the details. Enjoy learning, and are excited about bringing your ideas to the table. Are honest and reliable, and willing to speak up even when it’s difficult. Think it is important to get governance right. Your background and skills include: Master’s in a quantitative field such as mathematics, engineering, econometrics, data science, finance, or similar. Excellent communication skills (English is the working language). Sound knowledge of applied mathematical modelling and excellent quantitative skills. Proficiency in Python and SQL. Knowledge of credit risk processes and how risk models and parameters are used in credit assessment and decision-making. Knowledge of or interest in IRB, IFRS9 or similar models – practical experience with model use, monitoring or governance is a definitive plus.
