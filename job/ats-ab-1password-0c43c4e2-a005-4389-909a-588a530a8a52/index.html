The GTM Performance & Strategy team helps teams turn strategy into consistent execution through targeted coaching, practice, and performance improvement. As a Senior Field Performance Partner, you’ll work with frontline Sales and Channel leaders across EMEA to diagnose performance gaps, build focused development plans, and use data and AI to help teams improve. Your work will help 1Password’s go-to-market organization perform at its best while advancing our mission to enable a safe, productive digital future. This is not a traditional enablement role. We don’t believe more training is always the answer. We believe enablement should start with the performance problem, use data and AI to understand what’s happening, and apply the most targeted intervention possible. You’ll independently own the performance improvement cycle for the teams you support: diagnose → prescribe → activate → reinforce → measure → adjust. You’ll combine business results, AI-generated coaching insights, field observations, and manager input to identify where teams and individuals need support. You’ll turn those insights into focused enablement plans, use AI to scale individualized practice and coaching, help managers reinforce the right behaviors, and measure whether performance actually changed. As 1Password continues to scale our partner-led GTM motion, you’ll also help make channel execution part of how our field operates every day. Rather than treating channel as a separate motion or specialist skill, you’ll help managers and teams build the behaviors needed to effectively identify, engage, and sell with partners as part of their everyday work. Your job isn’t to keep people busy with enablement. It’s to help them perform better. How we’re using AI today Across GTM, we’re transforming the way we work with AI. We expect every seller, CSM, and team member to become a power user, using it daily to work smarter and move faster. AI is already embedded in how we operate – from real-time coaching that sharpens how we sell and interact with customers, to surfacing insights that help us anticipate needs – creating more time for meaningful customer engagement. If you’re excited to reshape how you think, make decisions, and drive growth with AI, you’ll feel right at home here. Expectations of this role This is a hybrid role. You will be expected to work from 1Password's London office 3 days per week, with the flexibility to work from the office up to 5 days based on business needs.
