Senior Software Engineer – Platform & Backend

SAVR AB · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 600 000–720 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 15.2.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

SAVR is a fast-growing Swedish fintech company on a mission to reinvent investing. We empower investors with a platform that offers lower fees, greater transparency, and a radically improved experience. We’re not just building another investment service — we’re reimagining how financial products should work for people, not just institutions. As part of our ambitious, innovative and curious team, you’ll help shape the future of investing in Sweden and beyond. The Role As a Senior Platform & Backend Engineer at SAVR, you’ll work across cloud infrastructure and backend development with security embedded in everything you build. You’ll help evolve the cloud platform our services run on while contributing directly to backend services and integrations. From improving deployment pipelines and strengthening access controls to building new functionality, you’ll work across the systems that power our investment platform. You’ll collaborate closely with our engineering teams to make our platform more reliable, secure and efficient and make it easier for developers to build, deploy and operate their services. Our infrastructure is built on AWS and managed through Terraform, with CI/CD powered by GitHub Actions. Our backend services are built in Kotlin, PHP/Laravel and Python and run on ECS, with data in Aurora, PostgreSQL and Snowflake. Your Mission Build and evolve our cloud platform. Improve our AWS infrastructure, networking, deployment pipelines and operational tooling through Terraform and automation. Develop backend services and integrations. Design, build and maintain reliable software, working closely with other engineers on functionality and architectural improvements. Strengthen security across infrastructure and applications. Improve access controls, secrets management, vulnerability scanning and secure CI/CD practices, making security a natural part of how we develop and operate our systems. Improve reliability and observability. Develop monitoring, logging and alerting, troubleshoot production issues and address their root causes. Make development smoother. Reduce manual work and improve the tools and workflows that help our engineering teams build, deploy and run services confidently. Balance performance, scalability and cost. Make thoughtful technical decisions that meet today’s needs while supporting future growth. Share knowledge and raise engineering standards. Contribute through code reviews, infrastructure reviews and constructive feedback across the team. You’ll Thrive in This Role If You… Have 5+ years of professional engineering experience, with hands-on experience across cloud infrastructure and backend development in production environments. Have strong experience with AWS and Terraform, alongside CI/CD, Docker and container orchestration such as ECS or EKS. Understand how applications, APIs, databases and cloud infrastructure fit together, and enjoy troubleshooting across those layers. Have practical experience securing cloud environments and services through approaches such as least-privilege IAM, secrets management, vulnerability management and secure API design. Care about automation, observability and building systems that are reliable and maintainable. Enjoy taking ownership, collaborating across disciplines and balancing delivery with long-term improvements. Communicate fluently in English and value sharing knowledge and giving and receiving feedback. You must also hold Swedish citizenship or a valid EU work permit, as visa sponsorship is unfortunately not available for this role. Our Cultural DNA At SAVR, we’re: 🧠 Open-minded – we embrace new ideas, diverse perspectives, and continuous learning 🚀 Bold – we take smart risks, move fast, and aren’t afraid to fail forward 🤝 Honest – we value transparency, feedback, and doing the right thing We’re a startup, so things can be a little messy — and that’s part of the thrill.
