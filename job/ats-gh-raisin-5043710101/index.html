Raisin is the world’s leading platform for savings and investment products. Founded in 2012, the FinTech connects consumers with banks in the EU, the UK and the US. This gives consumers better interest rates and banks a diversified form of refinancing. Our vision is to offer savings and investments without barriers and thus open up the global 160 trillion euro market. Raisin currently employs more than 800 people from over 75 countries worldwide. Today, the platform holds over 80 billion euros in assets from more than one million investors which have accrued over 5 billion euros in returns. Team We are looking for a dedicated and competent professional to strengthen our team in the area of Solution Management EU, Customer Service. In this role, you will take on complaint handling, primarily for our German-speaking markets. Do you have a knack for de-escalation, work in a structured manner, and keep an overview even in challenging situations? If you want to drive complex cases forward in a solution-oriented manner and enjoy a constructive error culture, we look forward to receiving your application! Your Responsibilities • End-to-End Case Management: Independently manage and resolve complex customer complaints and legal cases within deadlines, maintaining a professional, solution-oriented approach even in high-frequency environments. • Legal Triage & Case Preparation: Screen incoming customer inquiries for legal relevance, escalate sensitive or high-risk cases appropriately, and structure case documentation to support the Legal team’s decision-making. • GDPR & Regulatory Compliance: Handle data privacy (GDPR) requests end-to-end, ensuring strict compliance, accurate documentation, and timely follow-ups. • Compliant & Empathetic Communication: You craft clear, empathetic, and comprehensive responses to complaints and legal cases and ensure that every response is also regulatory compliant. • Risk Assessment & Root Cause Analysis: Identify potential legal or operational risks early, systematically analyze complaint drivers, and initiate sustainable process improvements. • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Act as a key interface between Legal, Product, and Operations to assess, mitigate, and resolve legally complex or operationally challenging customer cases. Your Profile • Completed training, ideally in banking and/or legal field (e.g., as a paralegal / legal assistant), or a comparable qualification - career changers with customer service experience are also welcome! • At least 1-2 years of experience handling customer inquiries with a legal and/or complaint focus, ideally in the financial services industry, customer service, or a similar role. • Basic knowledge of contract law, data protection law, or commercial law is an advantage – willingness to familiarize yourself with new legal issues is desired. • Strong understanding of de-escalation strategies as well as the ability to communicate complex issues in an understandable and solution-oriented manner. • A strong ability to prioritize correctly even with high ticket volumes and to comply with Service Level Agreements (SLAs). • A high sense of responsibility, teamwork skills, and a proactive approach to problem-solving. • Minimum C2 fluency (written and spoken) in German is required, along with good working proficiency in English (C1). Any additional language skills are a strong plus. Join our mission, join our team – and grow with us! At Raisin, we care about each other and it is one of our top priorities to foster an open and caring environment in which everyone feels welcome and comfortable. Our culture is strongly driven by our ambitious team, which connects more than 75 different nationalities.
