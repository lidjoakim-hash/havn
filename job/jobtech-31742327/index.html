Business Area Marketing Manager

Roxtec International Aktiebolag · Karlskrona, Sweden

💰 ca. 450 000–550 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 30.11.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Roxtec is the world-leading developer and manufacturer of modular-based sealing solutions for cable and pipe penetrations, serving customers in more than 80 markets worldwide. As Business Area Marketing Manager, you will take on a strategic role within our Global Marketing team, with the opportunity to influence Roxtec’s market presence and contribute to our continued growth. The role combines a global perspective with close collaboration across the business. Your role In this role, you will act as a strategic partner to the Business Area organization, turning business strategies and priorities into relevant marketing initiatives. You will identify market opportunities and communication needs and ensure that Roxtec’s value proposition, solutions, and brand are effectively communicated to relevant target audiences. You will report to the Brand & Corporate Communications Manager. Your responsibilities • Developing market communication strategies, content, and activities tailored to priority target audiences. • Creating, implementing, and monitoring global marketing plans aligned with business objectives. • Managing and coordinating marketing communication projects from concept to execution. • Collaborating with and supporting local marketing teams around the world. • Strengthening collaboration between Global Marketing and the Business Area organization. • Identifying communication priorities and recommending marketing initiatives that create measurable business value. Your qualifications • University degree in marketing or a related field. • Several years of experience in strategic and operational marketing. • Experience in an international B2B industrial or manufacturing environment is considered an advantage. • Strong communication and project management skills. • Experience working closely with business stakeholders to translate business needs into marketing activities. • Fluency in spoken and written English. Who you are You are business-oriented and proactive, with a strong understanding of how marketing and communication can support the business. You enjoy collaborating across functions and cultures and thrive in a dynamic environment. Speed, simplicity, and flexibility are guiding principles in how we work at Roxtec and should feel natural to you. Read more about our Core Values here (https://www.roxtec.com/en/about-us/about-roxtec/roxtec-core-values/). What we offer • A strategic marketing role with an international scope. • The opportunity to work closely with the business and influence our marketing direction. • Close collaboration with colleagues across marketing, sales, product management, and business areas worldwide. • A collaborative environment built on trust and continuous improvement. Application If you have any questions regarding the position, please contact Annette Birgerson, Brand & Corporate Communications Manager, Global Marketing, +46 733 31 32 22, or Elin Jurjaks, HR Manager Sweden, +46 733 31 31 89. We review applications and conduct interviews on an ongoing basis, so we encourage you to submit your application as soon as possible, no later than 2026-11-30.
