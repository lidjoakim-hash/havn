At Flix, we offer a tech-driven environment where innovation meets real-world impact, with competitive pay, strong growth opportunities, and a culture of collaboration and ownership.  As a Senior Product Owner (m/f/d) in Supply division you will make an impact by owning the problem space of supporting operational staff in the field, driving the full product lifecycle from discovery to outcome measurement, working in close partnership with a global landscape of stakeholders, and shaping the next generation of our products. At the centre of this problem space is an app supporting bus drivers, train attendants and station staff in their daily operations around the world. This Android application does not only provide key workflows to its users, but also allows Flix to better understand operational compliance in the field so we can guarantee the best possible passenger experience. We're currently building the next generation of this app, making this an exciting moment to join and shape its long-term direction. Your engineering counterparts are an Engineering Manager, three Backend engineers, two Mobile engineers and one Data Analyst. Your focus will be on strategy, discovery, and outcomes, while the engineering team owns delivery and ceremonies. If you're excited about working on a constantly evolving product used by thousands of operational staff around the world, send us your application! presenting a business case to a stakeholder feels as natural as running a discovery session with users • Proven ability to build strong working relationships with business stakeholders and drive joint decision-making without relying on formal authority • Experience owning product problems that cut across team boundaries, keeping multiple stakeholders aligned on direction and trade-offs • You're energised by strategy, discovery, and outcomes; you understand that delivery coordination sits with the engineering team • Strong analytical foundation; you can connect user research, usability signals, and business metrics to make confident product decisions • Nice-to-have: Experience with mobile app products, ideally in an operational or logistics context We recognize that everyone carries a unique set of valuable skills and experiences.
