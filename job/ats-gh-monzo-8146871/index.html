Our Payments team ⭐ At Monzo, payments are more than just moving money from A to B. They are a core part of how customers experience trust, speed, and financial control every day. The role also sits at the intersection of Monzo’s broader ambition to build truly borderless banking and expand into new markets. You’ll partner closely with Product, Engineering, and Design to help shape Monzo’s payments strategy to make Monzo's global ambitions a reality, enabling revenue and customer growth with a resilient, self-serve banking platform.
