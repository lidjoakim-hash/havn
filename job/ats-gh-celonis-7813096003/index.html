The Team: As part of the Legal and Trust team, you will collaborate closely with cross-functional departments to enhance legal operations, streamline contracting workflows, and uphold information security compliance. The Role: Do you want to get to know a unique end-to-end view into Celonis and take the opportunity to actively shape the operations of a hypergrowth SaaS company?
