SUPO, where required) WHY THIS ROLE MATTERS   The Senior FP&A Manager is a high-impact strategic finance role. This is not a reporting function. You will be the financial brain behind major investment decisions, business cases, and operational strategy — partnering with senior leaders across the business to shape how ICEYE allocates capital, scales operations, and plans for the future. We are looking for an exceptional candidate who is ready to own and transform the FP&A function, not maintain it.   WHO WE ARE ICEYE is the world leader in sovereign intelligence from space. We deliver persistent monitoring capabilities to detect and respond to changes in any location on Earth.   ICEYE owns the world's largest and most advanced SAR (synthetic aperture radar) satellite constellation. To our customers we provide intelligence with unmatched quality, latency and revisit times, in any weather, day or night. To governments who choose to operate their own constellation we provide this proven capability as a sovereign system.   ICEYE-built constellations serve customers in defence and intelligence, environmental monitoring, insurance and emergency management. We enable fast decisions that contribute to a safer future.   Founded and headquartered in Finland, ICEYE operates globally with over 1000 employees across Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. WHAT YOU WILL BE DOING Board & Executive Reporting - Contribute to board and investor materials, ensuring financial narrative is clear, accurate, and tells a coherent story. - Support the CFO in preparing for board meetings, investor updates, and external financial communications. Business Cases & Investment Decisions - Lead the development of rigorous business cases for major investment decisions — new programmes, capital expenditure, technology platforms, and strategic partnerships. - Challenge assumptions, stress-test financial models, and present clear go / no-go recommendations to the CFO and executive team. - Own post-investment reviews to close the loop on business case assumptions and improve future decision-making. Strategic Planning & Long-Range Forecasting - Own the long-range financial plan, working alongside the FP&A Director and executive team to translate strategic ambition into financially coherent multi-year models. - Identify the key value drivers across the business and build the analytical frameworks that connect operational activity to financial outcomes. - Provide forward-looking insight on revenue, margin, and cash — surfacing risks and opportunities before they appear in the numbers. FP&A Process Transformation - Redesign and simplify the budgeting, forecasting, and reporting processes to reduce cycle time and increase the quality of insight delivered to decision-makers. - Drive the implementation and adoption of planning and BI tooling, shifting the function away from manual spreadsheet dependency. - Establish clear FP&A standards, templates, and governance that scale with the business.
