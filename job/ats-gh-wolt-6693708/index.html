About Wolt At Wolt, we create technology that brings joy, simplicity and earnings to the neighborhoods of the world. In 2014 we started with delivery of restaurant food. Now we’re building the delivery of (almost) everything and you’ll find us in over 500 cities in 30 countries around the world. In 2022 we joined forces with DoorDash and together we keep on dreaming big and expanding across the globe. Working at Wolt isn’t always easy, but it’s definitely exciting. Here you’ll learn more, build more, and ship more than in most other companies. You’ll be challenged a lot, but also have a lot of fun on the way. So, if you’re a self-starter with drive and entrepreneurial spirit, this could be the ride of your life. Calling all team-players - are you looking for a flexible role that could shape the future of retail? If so, then let’s talk! We are looking Grocery Associate (24 hours weekly) to join our WM Pelgulinn in Tallinn! 🤩 What you’ll be doing • Taking in orders through our Merchant app • Picking and packing groceries, and handing them to customers and courier partners • Unloading food and supplies that we receive from vendors • Maintaining order and cleanliness of the store Our humble expectations • You are proactive and a fast learner with the ability to multitask • You're able to work in rotating shifts from Monday to Sunday • Experience from working in a grocery store is an advantage! • You have a professional proficiency in Estonian; English is a plus! • You have the right to work in Estonia What you’ll get by joining us • Wolt credits cashback benefit • Employee assistance program • Wolt-wide training programs • Frequent and systematic feedback cycles and systems around it, for the benefit of self-development • Variety of growth opportunities within Wolt • Two team events per year Next steps If you are excited about working in a high-growth environment, taking ownership, and being part of an extremely ambitious team, then click below to apply and get the conversation going!
