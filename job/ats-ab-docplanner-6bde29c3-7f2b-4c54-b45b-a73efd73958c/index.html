THE ROLE Docplanner's Customer Success organisation serves hundreds of thousands of healthcare professionals across 13 markets, through several business units: Individual Doctors, Clinics, and our Practice Management Software (PMS) products. Each of them relies on the CRM to onboard, retain and grow customers efficiently. This role leads the Global CS Ops team that designs those CRM processes. The team turns business goals into processes and tooling that improve churn, win rate and, above all, productivity, measured as more customers and MRR served per CS FTE. You'll lead a team of 5 analysts, each owning one business unit or enabler: Individual Doctors, Clinics, PMS, and the tools integrated into the CRM (e.g. meetings and quality). You'll be the main point of contact for the countries' requests: deciding what matters, what doesn't, and saying no when needed. We are close to finishing the migration of all CS teams from HubSpot to Salesforce with 100% of products in Salesforce expected by mid-2027. Your job is to land it, then make the most of Salesforce as our single CS platform, quantifying the gains and detecting the main opportunities to deliver more impact. This is a role reporting to the Global Director of Customer Success Operations & Analytics.
