As Director, Regulatory Compliance (UK), you will serve as the senior compliance authority and strategic partner for Airwallex’s rapidly expanding UK presence. Operating as a licensed Electronic Money Institution (EMI) supervised by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA), Airwallex requires compliance leadership that sees regulatory integrity not as a roadblock, but as a critical commercial enabler and competitive differentiator. In this role, you will lead our UK compliance framework, serve as the primary interface with local regulators and industry bodies, and work cross-functionally across Product, Strategy, Commercial, and Operations to safely launch cutting-edge payment infrastructure.
