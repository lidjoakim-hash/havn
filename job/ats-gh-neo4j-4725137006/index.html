Product Marketing Manager - GraphAware Solutions Job Location: UK, Czechia or Spain (Remote, with some travel) Reporting To: Marketing Director, Neo4j GraphAware Why apply? At Neo4j GraphAware, we help organisations make sense of complex data to uncover hidden relationships, investigate risk, and make better decisions. Our solutions support customers tackling some of the most demanding challenges in financial crime, intelligence analysis, and other mission-critical environments. Following GraphAware's acquisition by Neo4j, we're entering an exciting new phase of growth. Combining GraphAware's specialist technology and industry expertise with Neo4j's global reach creates a significant opportunity to bring our solutions to new customers and markets. We're looking for a Product Marketing Manager to help shape how we position, communicate, and bring these solutions to market. Your mission As Solutions Marketing Manager, you'll be responsible for understanding our customers, markets, and technology, and turning that knowledge into compelling positioning, messaging, and sales enablement assets that help us win business. This is a product marketing role focused on industry solutions rather than individual products. Your initial focus will be on our Financial Crime Intelligence solution, helping financial institutions detect complex risks and investigate financial crime. Over time, you'll have the opportunity to work across other solutions, industries, and customer segments. You'll combine strategic thinking with hands-on execution, whether that's developing a new value proposition, equipping sellers for customer conversations, or shaping the messaging for a go-to-market campaign. Key responsibilities: • Customer and market understanding: Develop an understanding of our target industries, customers, and use cases. Research customer challenges, industry trends, and buying behaviors to identify target segments, develop ideal customer profiles and buyer personas, and inform our go-to-market strategy. • Positioning and messaging: Own the positioning and messaging for our solutions, translating complex technical capabilities into clear customer value propositions.
