Turn customer and market insights into business opportunities as Nordic Value Proposition Analyst at PostNord. Do you enjoy turning customer, market and commercial data into clear insights and business opportunities? Perhaps you are currently working as a Commercial Analyst, Customer Insights Analyst, Customer & Market Insights Analyst or in a similar analytical role. Join PostNord in Stockholm, Helsinki, Oslo or Copenhagen as Nordic Value Proposition Analyst and help build the fact base behind stronger Nordic value propositions, sharper sales activation and measurable commercial impact. You will collaborate across markets and functions, help shape how customer and market insights are gathered and used, and build insight capabilities that directly influence commercial decisions. Purpose, Offer and Benefits Join a Nordic team where your insights help shape stronger customer value propositions and commercial decisions. You will work closely with colleagues across markets and functions, with broad exposure to sales activation, customer experience and product development. You will also have the opportunity to build insight capabilities, develop ways of working and challenge established assumptions with data. We offer you a flexible hybrid workplace with a prestige-free environment where we as a team help each other to reach our goals. We encourage self-sufficient leadership for development and career opportunities. In addition to the benefits mentioned above, we offer: Work in a Nordic and flexible environment Good insurance and occupational pension conditions Wellness allowance & access to office gym and swimming pool Employee benefits through PostNord´s own employee foundation PostNord Plus Employment according to a collective agreement. Your Responsibilities You will help ensure that value proposition development and commercial activation are grounded in evidence. By combining data and insight from multiple sources, you will identify customer pains, needs, buying triggers, value gaps and segment opportunities. Turn data into actionable insight: Identify and structure relevant data, tools and sources across customers, markets, products, CX, sales, brand and countries to build a strong fact base for commercial decisions. Deepen customer and market understanding: Identify customer pains, needs, buying triggers, value gaps and segment opportunities across markets and customer groups. Drive commercial priorities: Translate insights into clear recommendations for value propositions, Sales Plays and commercial activation, providing fact-based input to roadmaps, business cases and continuous improvement. Measure performance and impact: Define relevant KPIs and measurement frameworks, and follow up the performance and commercial impact of value propositions, sales materials and activation initiatives. Build effective feedback loops: Consolidate input from countries, sales teams, customers, CX and product teams, identifying recurring gaps and unmet needs and translating them into actionable feedback for Product Managers. Enable data-driven decision-making: Develop dashboards, analytical materials and decision support, collaborating with IT and other stakeholders to continuously improve the tools, data structures and reporting capabilities needed for high-quality analysis. The Bigger Picture PostNord is on a transformation journey where customer value, profitable growth and Nordic harmonisation are central. Nordic Parcel Product Management owns and develops the Nordic parcel portfolio across markets. As Nordic Value Proposition Analyst, you provide the fact base for Nordic value proposition development. You support and collaborate with Value Proposition Leads, CX Leads, Product Managers and commercial stakeholders by translating customer, market, product, sales, CX and country insights into clear analyses, opportunity areas, prioritisation input and actionable recommendations.
