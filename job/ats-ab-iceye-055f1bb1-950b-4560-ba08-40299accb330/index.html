ROLE HIGHLIGHTS: - Spacecraft Analysis Engineer - Location: Espoo, Finland - Department: Spacecraft Hardware - Reports to: Spacecraft Analysis Team lead - Employment type: Permanent - Workplace model: Hybrid / Office - Employment is subject to applicable security screening (incl. SUPO, where required) WHY THIS ROLE MATTERS: The Spacecraft Analysis team plays a key role in the development of the next generation of ICEYE’s SAR satellites and constellations, as well as the optimization and continuous improvement of the existing fleet. As a Spacecraft analysis engineer you’ll be involved in all stages of the satellite and constellation design, CONOPs definition, and fleet operation optimization, through rigorous, physics-grounded analysis, working closely with engineers across all disciplines to make sure the spacecraft performs as intended, from the drawing board through to operations. Coming up with creative ways to find answers to challenging engineering questions while working along a multidisciplinary team is part of daily life. As we control almost every aspect of our technology from hardware to software, you will work with world-renowned experts in a highly collaborative environment. WHO WE ARE ICEYE is the world leader in sovereign intelligence from space. We deliver persistent monitoring capabilities to detect and respond to changes in any location on Earth. ICEYE owns the world's largest and most advanced SAR (synthetic aperture radar) satellite constellation. To our customers we provide intelligence with unmatched quality, latency and revisit times, in any weather, day or night. To governments who choose to operate their own constellation we provide this proven capability as a sovereign system. ICEYE-built constellations serve customers in defence and intelligence, environmental monitoring, insurance and emergency management. We enable fast decisions that contribute to a safer future. Founded and headquartered in Finland, ICEYE operates globally with over 1000 employees across Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. YOUR DAY-TO-DAY RESPONSIBILITIES - Feasibility and performance analysis during the all stages of the spacecraft design, including budgets, CONOPs, space and ground segment interfaces. - Analyze, model, and optimize the performance of integrated SAR satellite systems. - Support launch, early operations and commissioning activities, ensuring successful deployment and operation of the spacecraft. - Perform mission analysis and develop flying rules to support system design. - Lead initiatives to enhance reliability and performance for on-orbit operations. - Support customers and program management with mission analysis, system documentation and on-orbit validation and verification. WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR Must haves: - Degree in Space Engineering, Physics, Electrical Engineering or other relevant discipline. - Understanding of fundamentals of physics in terms of space environment, attitude control, RF propagation, heat exchange, orbital dynamics etc. - Ability to work along with multidisciplinary teams including all sorts of engineering, operations, project management and sales engineering. - Great communication skills. - Experience in systems engineering processes, requirements management, and verification methodologies. - Ability to plan, perform and properly analyze results of an experiment, simulation, or test. - Critical and analytical thinking, - Coding and data analysis skills in Python and/or Matlab. - Positive and proactive attitude. - Regardless of the level of experience, the desire to learn new things and new ways to do engineering is a must.
