OUR VISION We aim to become the most beloved financial Operating System for French SMEs and Accounting Firms — and soon, European ones. We help entrepreneurs get rid of time-consuming accounting and financial tasks while giving them access to the information they need to make better decisions for their business. ABOUT US Pennylane is one of the fastest-growing Fintechs in France — and soon in Europe. In five years, we have: - 💻 Built a groundbreaking accounting and financial platform for SMEs and accounting firms - 💰 Raised €225 million from leading investors, including Sequoia - 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Grown from 7 co-founders to more than 850 Pennylaners - 🌍 Built an international and remote-friendly environment with more than 25 nationalities - 🤝 Earned the trust of thousands of customers and accounting firms - 🚀 Reached more than 700,000 SMEs and 4,500 accounting firms in France WHY THIS POSITION IS CRITICAL TO OUR MISSION At Pennylane, Product is at the heart of how we create value for our customers. We are now making a major bet on AI: not only to automate existing workflows, but also to fundamentally change how people interact with their financial software and extend what the product can do. Our AI ecosystem is being built across several complementary layers: - A shared AI platform, or “harness”, that provides the foundations required to run reliable agents: context, memory, tools, skills, loops, guardrails and observability - Studio Coworker, a conversational product experience that helps users analyse information and take action inside Pennylane - Autopilot, which automates accounting and financial workflows to reduce manual data entry - Studio Builder, the product you will own, enabling users to build and deploy personalised apps, automations and agents on top of Pennylane Studio Builder is our most ambitious AI product initiative. The idea is simple but powerful: give customers the ability to extend Pennylane according to their own needs, without requiring them to build everything from scratch. Users should be able to describe what they want to achieve and rely on AI to create, configure and deploy the right experience directly within Pennylane. This could unlock a new way to use financial software: more personalised, more extensible and increasingly capable of adapting to every company’s specific workflows. As Senior Product Manager, AI, you will shape this product from the ground up. You will define the vision, understand where AI can create genuine customer value and turn a highly complex technical ambition into a simple, useful and scalable product experience. You will join one of the key teams shaping the future of Pennylane. You can also read more about Pennylane’s position at the intersection of AI, accounting and financial software in The New York Times https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/23/business/dealbook/pennylane-ai-accounting-france.html. WHAT YOU WILL OWN THE VISION AND STRATEGY FOR STUDIO BUILDER You will define how Studio Builder should help customers create, personalise and extend Pennylane. You will identify the most valuable use cases, understand where customers struggle today and establish a clear product strategy that balances ambition, usability and technical feasibility. THE END-TO-END BUILDING EXPERIENCE You will own the experience through which customers: - Define what they want to build - Configure the relevant data, context, tools and actions - Test and iterate on their app, automation or agent - Deploy it within Pennylane - Monitor its usage and improve it over time THE FOUNDATIONS OF AN EXTENSIBLE AI PRODUCT You will work closely with the AI platform team to ensure that Studio Builder makes the underlying capabilities of Pennylane accessible and useful to customers.
