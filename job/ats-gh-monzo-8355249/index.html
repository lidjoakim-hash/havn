Turning our partner estate direction into successful outcomes requires clear programme design, coordinated delivery and strong ownership across teams and partners. The Partner Strategy and Delivery Manager exists to determine how agreed priorities will be tackled. You will turn a strategic focus area or business outcome into a clear programme approach, define what needs to happen and lead the work through delivery, stabilisation and handover. Delivery is expected at every level within the pillar. In this role, most of your time will be spent designing how an agreed priority will be tackled and leading the resulting project or programme. You will contribute evidence and recommendations that can influence future priorities, and you will remain sufficiently close to the detailed delivery to ensure the approach works in practice. 🔑 You’ll play a key role by...
