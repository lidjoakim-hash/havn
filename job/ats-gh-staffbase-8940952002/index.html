This is a hands-on technical role within Staffbase Build, responsible for turning specific enterprise customer needs into reliable, scalable extensions of the Staffbase platform, using its extensibility capabilities (widgets, plugins, integrations, and other platform objects). The role owns assigned customer solutions end-to-end, defining the use case with stakeholders, building and testing across the stack, deploying it, and validating it with users. The aim is to drive adoption and stickiness of the platform, not to create one-off, standalone customer solutions. The role acts as a bridge, helping customers do what they and the platform can't do alone yet, and feeds what's learned back into shared standards and the product roadmap. This is a hybrid role. Core in-office days in our Minneapolis office are Tuesdays-Thursdays, and it is expected that folks will be in the office a minimum of 2 day per week during core days. The role reports into the regional Build Leader.
