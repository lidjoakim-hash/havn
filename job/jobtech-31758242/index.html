Junior Test Engineer - Stoneridge

Oak Consultant Group AB · Örebro, Sweden

Since 1965, Stoneridge has designed and manufactured advanced, award-winning technologies including driveline and transmission actuation systems, vision systems, emissions control systems, safety systems, and security and monitoring systems for vehicle OEMs in the commercial vehicle, automotive, off-highway and agricultural vehicle markets. We’re focused on the areas of greatest market need – both today and tomorrow. Our core products and technologies are aligned with industry megatrends including safety and security, vehicle intelligence, fuel efficiency and emissions. Join the Stoneridge Team as we continue to build upon our strong history and reputation for quality products to deliver innovative products and systems that address a need, exceed our customers’ expectations, and motivate our team. Objective of the position At Stoneridge in Örebro, our Production Engineering team ensures that every product leaving our production lines meets the highest standards of quality and reliability. As a Junior Test Engineer, you will work closely with experienced engineers to develop, maintain and improve the test systems used in our manufacturing processes. This is an excellent opportunity for a recent graduate or early-career engineer who wants to gain hands-on experience in electronics, software, automation and production technology within the automotive industry. You'll report to the Production Engineering Manager and become part of a collaborative team where curiosity, problem-solving and continuous learning are highly valued. What You'll Do Support the development, maintenance and improvement of production test systems and equipment. Assist in the introduction and validation of test solutions for new products and production lines. Troubleshoot test equipment and support production teams in resolving technical issues. Participate in testing, verification and evaluation of prototypes and pre-production products. Help create and maintain test-related documentation, work instructions and training materials. Analyze test results and production data to identify opportunities for quality and process improvements. Collaborate with colleagues across Production, Quality, Manufacturing Engineering and R&D. What You Bring BSc or MSc in Electrical Engineering, Electronics, Mechatronics, Embedded Systems or a related engineering field. Up to 3 years of relevant experience through employment, internships, thesis projects or extracurricular activities. Basic understanding of electronics and the ability to read electrical schematics. Interest in testing, automation, manufacturing and problem-solving. Familiarity with programming or scripting is advantageous. Experience with LabVIEW, C#, Python or similar tools is a plus. Knowledge of measurement equipment, electronics testing or industrial automation is beneficial. Strong analytical skills and a willingness to learn in a technical environment. Professional working proficiency in Swedish and English. Meritorious qualifications Experience from automotive, manufacturing or industrial environments. Knowledge of vision systems, CAN communication or industrial networks. Familiarity with data analysis tools such as Minitab or similar software. What We Offer You will be welcomed to a fun, diverse and inclusive workplace where our culture is built on our core values: Integrity, Customer Orientation, Teamwork, Adaptability, Accountability and Social Responsibility. At Stoneridge, you'll have the opportunity to learn from experienced engineers while contributing to products that improve safety and efficiency in commercial vehicles worldwide. We offer a supportive environment where professional development, mentorship and continuous learning are encouraged. This role provides an excellent foundation for a long-term engineering career within a global automotive technology company focused on innovation, safety and sustainability. Interested? We collaborate with Oak Consulting
