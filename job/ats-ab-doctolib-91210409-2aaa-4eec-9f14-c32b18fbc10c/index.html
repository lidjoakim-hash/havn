We are looking for a Principal AI Engineer to join the Practice Efficiency team at Doctolib. Your mission will be to define and deliver how AI transforms the daily work of healthcare professionals — through intelligent scheduling, automated document processing, smart task management, and AI-assisted patient communication. As a Principal-level individual contributor, you will own the deepest technical challenges within the Practice Efficiency scope: setting the AI architecture bar, driving rigorous evaluation standards, and directly shaping how AI-powered features reach healthcare professionals and their patients at scale — starting with the Phone Assistant, currently one of Doctolib's most strategic product bets. Working in the tech team at Doctolib means building innovative products and features to improve the daily lives of care teams and patients. WHAT YOU'LL DO Your responsibilities include but are not limited to: - Own end-to-end AI architecture decisions for Practice Efficiency — from model selection and agentic system design to production deployment patterns,, and strategic build vs. buy calls - Architect and deliver agentic applications at scale, defining how Practice Efficiency AI features are structured, evaluated, and iterated on in production - Design robust, scalable AI infrastructure: model and prompt versioning, guardrails, uncertainty quantification, cost optimisation, and observability — enabling the team to ship reliably and fast - Lead the technical delivery of the Phone Assistant's AI capabilities, balancing ambitious product ambitions with engineering rigour and production reliability - Ensure responsible AI delivery in a regulated healthcare context, embedding explainability, GDPR/HDS compliance, and human-in-the-loop design across all Practice Efficiency AI features - Partner closely with Product Managers and cross-functional stakeholders to translate customer and business needs into sound technical decisions, acting as a bridge between engineering depth and product delivery WHO YOU ARE Before you read on: if you don't have the exact profile described below, but you feel this job description matches your skill set, we still encourage you to apply. You'll be a great fit if you: - Have 10+ years of software engineering experience, with at least 5 years focused on building AI products — including experience building AI systems before the rise of large language models, giving you strong fundamentals in evaluation, data pipelines, and system design under uncertainty - Have a strong background in building and operating large-scale distributed systems — we value deep systems engineering expertise and will consider profiles who bring exceptional scale experience alongside a solid grounding in AI - Have hands-on experience designing and shipping agentic applications, with a clear understanding of the challenges around reliability, observability, and evaluation in production agentic systems - Are product-centric by nature: you have worked closely with Product Managers and product organisations, bring genuine business sense and customer closeness, and see yourself as part of the product delivery chain — not just the technical one - Are fluent in English It would be fantastic if you: - Have experience with LLM fine-tuning, RAG systems, or automatic speech recognition in production environments - Have built AI products in healthcare, financial services, or another regulated domain where safety and auditability are non-negotiable - Have contributed to or built shared AI platforms used by multiple product teams LIFE AT DOCTOLIB TECH - Our solutions are built on a single fully cloud-native platform that supports web and mobile app interfaces, multiple languages, and is adapted to country and healthcare specialty requirements. - Our stack in this product area is Ruby on Rails and TypeScript/React in the monolith, Kotlin for new services, with Postgres, Redis and Kafka under
