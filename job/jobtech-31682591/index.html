Join us in our mission to electrify the world for a greener future! At Senior Material, we are currently seeking a dedicated and analytical Operations Analysis Engineer to join our growing team. If you are passionate about data-driven decision-making, operational excellence, and continuous improvement, this opportunity is for you! WHO ARE WE? We work every day to electrify the world for a greener future. Through a thin plastic film – more precisely, a separator film – we help make lithium-ion batteries durable and safe. It is one of the key components ensuring the optimal performance of batteries. Founded in 2003 in Shenzhen, China, Senior Material has been driven by innovation since the very beginning. Since 2021, we have established our European headquarters in Eskilstuna, Sweden. Here, we are developing one of Europe's most modern production facilities, contributing to the electrification of transportation. We have an exciting journey ahead of us, and now you have the opportunity to be part of it! ABOUT THE ROLE: As an Operations Analysis Engineer at Senior Material, you will play an important role in monitoring and analyzing operational performance, identifying improvement opportunities, and supporting data-driven decision-making across the organization. You will be responsible for analyzing production performance, maintaining operational KPIs, preparing management reports, and supporting capacity and cost optimization. Working closely with Production, Supply Chain, Quality, Finance, and other departments, you will contribute to improving operational efficiency, productivity, and overall business performance. In summary, you will play a key role in driving operational excellence and continuous improvement within our Operations department!
