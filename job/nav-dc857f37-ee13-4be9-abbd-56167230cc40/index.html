Electrical Shift Technician

Nordscale · Bjerkvik, Norway

About the Role (Job Purpose) Join our operations team at the Kvandal facility outside Narvik, where you will help keep the systems supporting our data centres running safely and reliably. The facility operates 24/7, and you will be part of a team working continuous shifts to ensure reliable operations around the clock. We are building a larger operations team and will be hiring several shift technicians across different levels of experience. Whether you are newly qualified and looking to build your experience alongside experienced colleagues, or already have operational experience and are ready to work more independently with maintenance and troubleshooting, we would like to hear from you. Previous operational experience is an advantage, but not a requirement.   What You Will Do If you are early in your career: • Assist in the operation and maintenance of electrical equipment such as switchgear, transformers, UPS systems, generators and batteries • Perform rounds and inspections, and report deviations • Respond to alarms using BMS systems and follow approved procedures • Keep clear logs and maintenance records • Learn from and support colleagues, including during major maintenance work • Work safely, follow our safety rules, and report hazards and incidents   If you have more experience, the role will additionally involve: • Operating and maintaining electrical equipment, including high-voltage installations and switchgear • Performing planned, preventative, predictive and corrective maintenance, inspections and condition monitoring • Troubleshooting electrical faults and escalating the most complex issues to our technical specialists • Participating in commissioning, energisation and testing as the facility grows • Collaborating with contractors and grid operators, including during major maintenance windows • Following electrical safety rules and permit to work routines Who We Think You Are If you are early in your career or have limited previous experience: • Trade certificate (Fagbrev) as an electrician, or equivalent relevant vocational qualification • Proficient in English, as it is our company language • Motivated to learn and develop within a 24/7 operational environment  If you have more experience: • Previous operations experience from industry, energy, maritime or other 24/7 environments • Strong electrical fault-finding skills and the ability to interpret single-line diagrams • Experience working independently with electrical operations and maintenance It is desirable, but not a requirement, that you have: • Data centre experience • Knowledge of transformers, switchgear, UPS systems or generators, or of Schneider, Eaton or ABB systems • Knowledge of NEK 400 • Knowledge of a Scandinavian language Personal Qualities You are practically minded, eager to learn, and have a strong HSE mindset. You take accountability for your tasks and collaborate well with others. If you have more experience, we also expect you to prioritise effectively in a demanding operational environment and communicate clearly with colleagues and other technical disciplines. You are comfortable using digital tools for documentation, logging and reporting.   What We Can Offer • Competitive salary and good terms • A secure, long-term position in a growing industry • Training and development opportunities • A safe, inclusive and supportive working environment At Nordscale, you get the opportunity to develop further. If you show that you take ownership and build your competence, you can over time move into a more independent role with greater responsibility.   Equal Opportunities Statement We are committed to fostering an inclusive, diverse, and equitable workplace. We believe that a variety of perspectives enriches our work environment, and we encourage applications from candidates of all backgrounds, experiences, and abilities.  We strongly encourage applications from people of colour, the LGBTQ&#43; community, people with disabilities, neurodivergent people, parents, carers, and people from lower socio-economic backgrounds. If there’s anything we can do to accommodate your specific situation, please let us know.
