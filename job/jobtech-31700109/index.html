🚀 We’re Hiring: Design Engineer – BEV Chassis Integration | CATIA V5 Company: Hykmann Global Business Solutions AB Role: Mechanical Design Engineer – BEV Chassis Integration Specialisation: Mechanical Design | Chassis Integration | Sheet Metal Design | Pneumatic Systems | CATIA V5 Experience: 3+ years of relevant experience Location: Södertälje, Sweden Work Model: On-site (100%) Assignment Period: 1 December 2026 – 29 December 2028 Language: English About the Role Hykmann Global Business Solutions AB is looking for an experienced Mechanical Design Engineer to contribute to the development and integration of components and systems for Battery Electric Vehicle (BEV) chassis solutions. You will work on mechanical design, component integration and installation solutions within heavy-duty vehicle platforms, with a particular focus on sheet metal components, brackets, pneumatic systems and chassis packaging. The role involves working with advanced CAD tools, developing detailed 3D and 2D designs, coordinating with suppliers and collaborating with cross-functional engineering teams to deliver technically robust and production-ready solutions. Key Responsibilities 1. Mechanical Design & Component Development Design and develop mechanical components and installation solutions for BEV chassis applications. Develop sheet metal components, brackets and mounting solutions. Design and integrate pneumatic components and installation systems. Create detailed 3D models and 2D engineering drawings using CATIA V5. Ensure designs comply with applicable engineering standards and technical requirements. Perform tolerance analysis and ensure dimensional accuracy. 2. Chassis Integration & Component Packaging Work with component packaging, interfaces and installation requirements. Ensure effective integration of mechanical and pneumatic components within vehicle chassis architectures. Evaluate installation space, clearances, interfaces and mounting requirements. Develop practical design solutions considering functionality, manufacturability and serviceability. Support the integration of components within electric vehicle chassis platforms. 3. Supplier & Cross-Functional Collaboration Collaborate with suppliers and multidisciplinary engineering teams throughout the design and development lifecycle. Participate in technical discussions, design reviews and engineering coordination activities. Support supplier interactions and resolve technical design-related challenges. Coordinate design requirements and ensure alignment across relevant stakeholders. 4. Verification, Documentation & Continuous Improvement Support design reviews, validation activities and technical investigations. Prepare and maintain engineering drawings, specifications and technical documentation. Support manufacturing and production-related activities when required. Contribute to design optimization, knowledge sharing and continuous improvement initiatives. Required Skills & Experience Bachelor's or Master's degree in Mechanical Engineering, Automotive Engineering or a related engineering discipline. Minimum 3 years of relevant mechanical design engineering experience. Previous R&D experience with Scania/Hero or the relevant TRATON organization is mandatory. Strong practical experience in one or more of the following areas: Sheet metal design and development. Brackets and mechanical installation solutions. Pneumatic components and installation systems. Component packaging and chassis integration. Proficiency in CATIA V5, including 3D modelling and 2D engineering drawings. Experience with tolerance analysis and dimensional engineering. Good understanding of component interfaces, packaging and installation requirements. Knowledge of design for manufacturing and manufacturability principles, particularly for sheet metal, plastic and/or pneumatic components. Ability to develop practical and technically sound mechanical design solutions. Good communication and collaboration skills. Flu
