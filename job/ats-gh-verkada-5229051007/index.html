About the Role The Director of Mid-Market Sales position is responsible for hiring, managing and mentoring a team of talent to meet and exceed their sales revenue goals. The Mid Market team focuses on accounts up to 2,000 employees in the Corporate space. This team has been tasked with maintaining high activity standards; daily prospecting, pipeline growth, prospect qualification, and delivering assigned monthly and quarterly sales revenue targets.
