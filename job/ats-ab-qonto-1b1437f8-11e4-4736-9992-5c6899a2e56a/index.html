Head of KYCB 🇩🇪

Join us as Head of KYCB - Central Europe and lead our KYCB operations for Germany and Austria, balancing efficiency, strict compliance and a great customer experience. You'll lead around 75 agents and 6 team leads, work with our BPO partner, and team up with senior financial-crime leaders, under the guidance of Tannia https://www.linkedin.com/in/tanniarojasquiroga/, our Head of KYCB. ➡️ What you'll do - Lead and scale: You'll run KYCB operations for the German and Austrian markets, adapting our global standards to local regulatory requirements. - Manage and develop your team leads: You'll grow your team leads as a manager of managers, driving high performance. - Drive operational performance: You'll deliver ambitious targets across conversion, compliance (initial and periodic due diligence) and customer satisfaction. - Partner with our MD Central Europe: You'll align operational execution with the growth goals of the Central Europe region. - Oversee BPO relationships: You'll manage our BPO partners to handle volume fluctuations and support growth in Germany and Austria. ➡️ What we're looking for - Management experience: You've managed people managers, not just individual contributors, for around three years. - Industry knowledge: You have a strong compliance background in fintech, banking, insurance or another regulated industry, with expertise in KYC, KYB and anti-financial-crime regulations. - BPO and scale: You've managed BPO (Business Process Outsourcing) partners and scaled operations in a high-growth environment. - Mindset: You're a structured problem-solver who takes ownership of complex topics and uses AI and automation to work smarter. - Languages: You're fluent in English and German. ➡️ What we can offer you - A role you shape: You'll be our first Head of KYCB - Central Europe and shape how the teams are organised. - Lean ways of working: You'll work in a fast-paced environment built on Lean and visual management, with tools like Forest admin, Zendesk and Notion. - A caring team: You'll join a transparent team that values problem-solving and operational excellence. - Senior partnership: You'll partner closely with our MD and financial-crime leaders within Central Europe. ➡️ Your future manager You'll report to Tannia Rojas Quiroga https://www.linkedin.com/in/tanniarojasquiroga/, our Head of KYC/KYB. She joined Qonto in 2019 as a Customer Success Officer and grew from Team Lead to Head of Customer Success before taking on KYC/KYB. Tannia is quality-driven, turns standard procedures into engines for continuous improvement, and loves developing her people.
