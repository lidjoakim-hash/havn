The Role As Senior Communications Manager, you will own the operational backbone of GetYourGuide's newsroom. You’ll build the infrastructure it runs on –the story pipeline, media CRM, distribution channels, and owned properties — and you ensure that our original storytelling moves through the system and lands at the right time and on the right channels. This role is an IC position and would be operational vs strategic.
