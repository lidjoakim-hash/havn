Are you a specialist in Rehabilitation Medicine looking for a role where you can truly make a difference while enjoying an excellent work-life balance? We are seeking an experienced rehabilitation medicine specialist to join a modern, multidisciplinary rehabilitation department in one of Sweden's most scenic and welcoming regions. Here, you will work alongside highly skilled colleagues, contribute to life-changing patient outcomes, and enjoy a lifestyle surrounded by nature, fresh air, and short commutes. About the Role As a rehabilitation medicine specialist, you will play a key role in coordinating and guiding the rehabilitation journey of patients with neurological conditions, spinal cord injuries, acquired brain injuries, and chronic pain disorders. You will work in close collaboration with physicians, physiotherapists, occupational therapists, psychologists, nurses, and social workers, ensuring each patient receives comprehensive and individualized care. The department combines inpatient rehabilitation with several specialized outpatient teams, creating a varied and stimulating work environment. In addition to clinical responsibilities, you will have the opportunity to contribute to service development, assess referrals, and support the education and supervision of junior doctors. Why Join This Team?
