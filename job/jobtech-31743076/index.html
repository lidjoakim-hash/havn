Environmental Coordinator OL31 Rail Project, Vagnhärad, Sweden Shape the future of Swedish infrastructure HOCHTIEF is one of the world's leading infrastructure and construction groups, employing approximately 38,000 people worldwide. We deliver complex projects across a wide range of sectors, including roads, bridges, tunnels, railways, airports, ports, buildings, and both conventional and renewable energy. With operations across Europe, North America, and the Asia-Pacific region, HOCHTIEF combines engineering expertise with innovative and sustainable solutions to shape the infrastructure of the future. We are currently looking for a collaborative Environmental Coordinator to join the OL31 railway project in Vagnhärad. This is a role within one of Sweden's most important infrastructure projects, where safety, coordination and collaboration are critical to success. About the Project The OL31 Project forms part of the Ostlänken programme, one of Sweden's largest and most important railway infrastructure investments. Ostlänken is a new approximately 160-kilometre double-track railway connecting Järna and Linköping, designed to increase capacity, improve regional connectivity and support sustainable transport across Sweden. The project involves complex civil engineering works, including tunnels, bridges, earthworks and rail infrastructure. Working on OL31 offers the opportunity to contribute to a landmark infrastructure programme while supporting the recruitment and development of a diverse, international project organization. About the Role As an Environmental Coordinator, you will support the project organization in the daily management of environmental matters and ensure compliance with project, client, and regulatory environmental requirements. You will act as the environmental team's operational representative within the production organization, providing hands-on support on site and helping to identify, manage, and mitigate environmental risks at an early stage. Key Responsibilities · Conduct regular environmental inspections, site walks, and environmental audits. · Provide day-to-day support and guidance to site management and production teams on environmental matters. · Monitor compliance with environmental requirements from Trafikverket, authorities, permits, and project procedures. · Support and participate in investigations of environmental incidents, deviations, and non-conformities, and follow up on corrective actions. · Support production teams with matters related to material and soil mass management. · Monitor water management activities, treatment solutions, and environmental protection measures. · Ensure compliance with project chemical management requirements and support chemical handling processes. · Prepare, maintain, and follow up environmental documentation, records, and reporting. · Act as the operational link between the environmental function and the production organization. · Promote environmental awareness and best practices across the project. Your Profile Mandatory Requirements · 2-4 years of experience in environmental coordination within construction, civil engineering, infrastructure, or similar projects. · Practical experience working closely with production teams on active construction sites. · Experience with environmental inspections, soil and material management, water management, and chemical management. · Ability to independently identify, manage, and resolve environmental-related issues. · Strong communication and collaboration skills. · Structured, proactive, and solution-oriented approach to work. · Good knowledge of Swedish and English, both written and spoken. · Valid Category B driving license.
