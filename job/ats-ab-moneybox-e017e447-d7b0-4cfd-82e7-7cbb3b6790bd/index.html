THIS IS NOT CURRENTLY A LIVE POSITION. WE ARE PREPARING FOR Q1 2027 NEEDS. ABOUT MONEYBOX At Moneybox, our mission is to give everyone the means to get more out of life. We're guided by our belief that wealth isn't about the money, it's about the means to more - more freedom, opportunities, possibilities, and peace of mind. Moneybox is an award-winning wealth management platform, helping over one and a half million people build wealth throughout their lives, whether they’re saving and investing, buying their first home, or planning for retirement. About the opportunity We're building a small pool of trusted freelance motion graphics designers we can call on as projects come up. To be clear from the start, this isn't an immediate project or a fixed contract. Joining the pool means you'll be one of the first people we get in touch with whenever we need extra motion design support, whether that's in-app animations for a product launch or assets for a brand campaign. When a project lands, you'll be briefed by and work alongside our in-house Brand and Product Design teams, helping bring the Moneybox app and brand to life through motion. Most of the work will be delivered as Lottie animations that ship straight into our iOS, Android and web products. Each piece of work will be scoped individually, with timelines and rates agreed before anything starts. Having people ready who already know how we work means we can move quickly when the need arises, and you get a direct line to interesting, varied briefs from an award-winning brand. What You'll Do - Design and animate motion assets for the Moneybox app, website and campaigns, to bring our product and brand experiences to life. - Build and export production-ready Lottie animations as JSON and dotLottie (.lottie) files, so they can be dropped straight into our apps by our engineering teams. - Optimise animation file size and performance, to keep the app fast and smooth across iOS, Android and web. - Work from Figma designs and Moneybox brand guidelines, to make sure motion feels consistent with our wider look and feel. - Collaborate with designers, product managers and engineers throughout each project, to make sure animations meet the brief and behave as intended once built. - Test animations in context and hand over clean, well-labelled files, so developers can implement them without back-and-forth. - Respond to feedback and iterate on concepts within agreed timelines, to deliver work that's ready to ship. - Edit and assemble short-form video content where a project calls for it, to support marketing and social campaigns. - Contribute reusable animation components and motion principles, to help build a consistent motion language over time. Who You Are - Comfortable picking up a brief at short notice and getting up to speed quickly, as projects will come up as and when they're needed. - Detail-focused, caring as much about how an animation performs on someone's phone as how it looks in After Effects. - A clear communicator who flags questions, risks or timing issues early rather than at hand-off. - Collaborative and open to feedback, happy to iterate with designers and engineers until it's right. - Self-managing and reliable, able to plan your own time and deliver to agreed deadlines while working remotely. Experience & Skills Essential - Proven experience creating Lottie animations and exporting them as JSON and dotLottie files for live apps or websites. - Strong working knowledge of Adobe Creative Suite, particularly After Effects (with Bodymovin or the LottieFiles plugin), Illustrator and Photoshop. - Confident using Figma to work from design files, components and prototypes. - A good understanding of what Lottie does and doesn't support, and how to optimise animations for mobile performance. - Experience working within brand guidelines or a design system. - A portfolio showing motion work for digital products, apps or brands.
