About Wolt At Wolt, we create technology that brings joy, simplicity and earnings to the neighborhoods of the world. In 2014 we started with delivery of restaurant food. Now we’re building the delivery of (almost) everything and you’ll find us in over 500 cities in 30 countries around the world. In 2022 we joined forces with DoorDash and together we keep on dreaming big and expanding across the globe. Working at Wolt isn’t always easy, but it’s definitely exciting. Here you’ll learn more, build more, and ship more than in most other companies. You’ll be challenged a lot, but also have a lot of fun on the way. So, if you’re a self-starter with drive and entrepreneurial spirit, this could be the ride of your life. About the Team The DoorDash Global Sourcing team supports DoorDash, Deliveroo, and Wolt across more than 40 markets within the Global Procurement, Sourcing, Travel and Supply Chain organization. We partner with the business to select the right suppliers, negotiate strong commercial agreements, and deliver value through savings, business enablement, and risk reduction. Our team covers Technology, Corporate Services, and Marketing. We are building more consistent sourcing practices across brands and improving our tools and ways of working, including the use of AI. This role offers the opportunity to develop your sourcing skills, learn a broad range of service categories, and make a measurable impact in a growing global business. About the Role As a Sourcing Specialist II, you will support sourcing across the full Corporate Services portfolio for Deliveroo and Wolt. You will partner with the business to understand requirements, evaluate suppliers, negotiate commercial terms, and move agreements through to execution, balancing cost, quality, risk, and speed. Your day to day will include managing fast moving, low risk sourcing engagements and supporting senior team members who serve as the Sourcing DRI on larger projects. Over time, you will take increasing ownership of sourcing projects of medium complexity, managing timelines, stakeholders, and agreed outcomes with growing independence. This is an individual contributor role with responsibility for delivering reliable analysis, practical recommendations, and results within a defined scope. You will support a broad range of Corporate Services categories, such as professional services, security, facilities and workplace, customer support, HR and benefits, recruitment, and contingent workforce.
