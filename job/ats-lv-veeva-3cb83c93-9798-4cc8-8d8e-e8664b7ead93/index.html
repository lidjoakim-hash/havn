Veeva Systems is a mission-driven organization and pioneer in industry cloud, helping life sciences companies bring therapies to patients faster. As one of the fastest-growing SaaS companies in history, we surpassed $3B in revenue in our last fiscal year with extensive growth potential ahead.   At the heart of Veeva are our values: Do the Right Thing, Customer Success, Employee Success, and Speed. We're not just any public company – we made history in 2021 by becoming a public benefit corporation (PBC), legally bound to balancing the interests of customers, employees, society, and investors.   As a Work Anywhere company, we support your flexibility to work from home or in the office, so you can thrive in your ideal environment.   Join us in transforming the life sciences industry, committed to making a positive impact on its customers, employees, and communities. The Role As Field Marketing Manager at Veeva, you have the opportunity to learn from a high-caliber team at a high-growth company. You can make a real impact across many areas, including demand generation, events, digital marketing, sales enablement, and communications. If you are results-driven, resourceful, and strive for excellence, this could be the perfect role for you. What You'll Do • Develop and execute field marketing plans, working with key stakeholders to define the goals, strategy, content, tactics, KPIs for the year • Plan and execute virtual and in-person events from planning, preparation to execution, and reporting • Build integrated marketing campaigns utilizing various channels, content types, and tactics, including email marketing (Marketo), social, digital advertising, sales enablement etc. • Work with internal teams such as product marketing, creative services, content, product, communications, and digital teams to deliver field marketing programs for both customers and prospects • Support the execution and optimization of outreach programs to engage current and prospective customers, leveraging LinkedIn, media publications, and other channels • Help organize the annual Veeva Summit, including logistics, customer communities, internal and external communications, event app management, and reporting. • Own the weekly and monthly marketing program reporting and metrics for your area, leveraging the data to drive decision-making Requirements • 4+ years of relevant B2B marketing experience (5+ years for Senior Manager) • Self-directed and able to manage multiple stakeholders, priorities, and projects • Exceptional communication, writing, and organizational skills • Well organized with very high attention to detail • Proficient in delivering content via social media tools such as LinkedIn • Growth-minded and a team player Nice to Have • Experience with salesforce.com and Pardot preferred • Life sciences or SaaS experience is a plus Interviewing with Veeva We value your time and believe in a transparent hiring process. Here is the process you can expect. • Follow the application process and submit your resume. • Within 3 days, you will receive a link to a personality assessment administered by a third party, unless you have already completed it with Veeva previously. • Once you complete the assessment, our team will review your full application package and follow up via email with our decision. • If moving to the interview stage, the process is as follows: • A conversation with the hiring manager • A practical case exercise • A final conversation with our group's Senior Leader. • Once all interviews are complete, the manager will be in touch with a final decision.
