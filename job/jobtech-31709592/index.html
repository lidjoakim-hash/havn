Lead Mechanical Engineer – Data Centre Project This is not a role for someone who wants to hide behind procedures or wait for instructions. We’re looking for a Lead Mechanical Engineer to take full ownership of mechanical engineering and commissioning delivery on a major data centre project. This role sits right at the centre of the project — technically, operationally and commercially. If you enjoy complexity, accountability and leading engineers in an environment where mistakes are expensive and success is visible, you’ll feel at home here. The Role As Lead Mechanical Engineer, you are responsible for planning, managing and coordinating all mechanical engineering and commissioning activities on the project. You lead the mechanical engineering function end to end — design, procurement, installation, testing, commissioning and handover — while mentoring engineers and maintaining standards under pressure. This is a hands‑on leadership role. You are expected to make decisions, solve problems and keep things moving. What You’ll Be Doing You will: Take full responsibility for mechanical engineering delivery on the project Lead and manage the mechanical project engineering team, assigning engineers to work packages Own the design management process, either directly or through appointed leads Own the commissioning process, ensuring systems are buildable, testable and ready when required Drive EHS compliance, promoting safe systems of work and leading by example on site Oversee procurement from a technical perspective, including RFQs, technical evaluations, equipment selection and delivery tracking Control quality from design through installation and commissioning, including ITPs, test packs, FATs, SATs, walkdowns and system handover Coordinate closely with Electrical and CSA counterparts to ensure clear system boundaries and responsibilities Manage system trackers, technical submittals, RFIs, deviations and change from a technical standpoint Lead commissioning activities, flushing, balancing, pressure testing and certification Identify risks early, own mitigation plans and escalate when required Engage in programme development, commissioning sequencing and look‑ahead planning Support business development through strong client, designer and contractor relationships Mentor project and graduate engineers, building capability across the team Provide clear, structured reporting to senior engineering and business leadership In short, you make sure the mechanical scope works — technically, safely and commercially. The Project This is a large‑scale, mission‑critical data centre build. It’s fast, complex and highly coordinated. The client expects certainty, not excuses.
