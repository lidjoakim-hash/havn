Senior Gameplay Animator Elsewhere Entertainment is looking for a Senior Gameplay Animator to bring an exciting new IP to life. You’ll create polished, responsive character animation with a strong focus on combat, working closely with talented developers to deliver gameplay that feels as good as it looks. You’ll combine excellent animation craft with a deep understanding of player experience. As an experienced member of the team, you’ll take ownership of complex animation work, share your expertise and help build a collaborative culture grounded in inclusivity, mutual respect, empathy and professionalism. You’ll help us by… Creating and refining high-quality player and enemy animations for combat, locomotion, traversal and moment-to-moment gameplay. Delivering combat animation that is readable, responsive and satisfying, with careful attention to timing, anticipation, impact and player control. Working with motion-capture data and keyframe animation to produce convincing performances that meet gameplay and creative needs. Collaborating with Design, Engineering, Art, Audio and Narrative to ensure animation supports gameplay mechanics, character and the wider player experience. Implementing and tuning animation in-engine, including state machines, blending and transitions, to achieve fluid and reliable results. Applying your understanding of the 3Cs (character, camera and controls), AI behaviours and camera animation to solve gameplay challenges. Taking ownership of animation tasks from exploration through implementation and polish, balancing quality, performance and production priorities. Sharing constructive feedback, supporting other animators and contributing ideas that improve team tools and workflows. You’re awesome because you have… Substantial gameplay animation experience and a strong contribution to at least one shipped game. A portfolio demonstrating high-quality player or enemy combat animation and a feel for responsive gameplay. Strong animation fundamentals across human and creature movement, including timing, weight, balance and physicality. A sound understanding of combat readability, hit reactions, attack cycles, locomotion and player control. Hands-on experience working in game engines and implementing animation within gameplay systems. Experience with motion-capture editing and cleanup, and blending mocap with keyframe animation. The ability to work effectively across disciplines, respond to feedback and solve creative and technical problems collaboratively. Proficiency in Maya and the flexibility to learn new tools and workflows. A thoughtful approach to sharing knowledge and helping teammates grow. Not sure you meet all the qualifications?
