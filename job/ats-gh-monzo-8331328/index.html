You’ll join our validation squad and provide expert independent validation and oversight across a broad suite of credit models. This is a generalist role rather than one focused on a particular model type. You’ll work across models used for impairment, capital, stress testing, forecasting and credit decisioning, including IFRS 9 PD, LGD, EAD and economic response models; decision science scorecards and origination PD models; and Net Present Value (NPV) / unit economics models. Strong data and analytical skills are a must, as is broad expertise in credit risk modelling and model risk management. You’ll have hands-on experience in model validation and/or development within financial services, and be comfortable getting into the data and code to form an independent view across different model types.
