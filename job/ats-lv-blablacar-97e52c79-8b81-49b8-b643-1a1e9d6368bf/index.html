About BlaBlaCar BlaBlaCar is the world’s leading community-based travel app enabling 40 million active members to share rides across 41 countries each year. Its technology matches drivers with empty seats with passengers heading the same way, so they can share the cost of the trip. BlaBlaCar’s mission is to be the go-to marketplace for shared travel, bringing carpooling, buses, and trains together in one place. Globally, the BlaBlaCar Group also includes Obilet, the leading online bus ticketing platform in Turkey. By partnering with over 20,000 operators, the Group offers a wide choice of affordable and sustainable transport solutions. In 2025, the BlaBlaCar community connected 2.7 million meeting points worldwide and enabled 138 million connections between travelers. Drivers saved 568 million euros by carpooling, and all of the BlaBlaCar Group’s mobility services contributed to avoiding 4 million tonnes of CO2 emissions. Your Mission We are looking for  our very first Procurement Lead to join BlaBlaCar on a Permanent contract (CDI).  Up until now, BlaBlaCar’s procurement grew fast and decentralized. Today, we manage ~€30m annual spend across 250+ passionate internal contract owners in 21 countries. To support our scale and ambitious roadmap, we are creating this dedicated role from scratch! Your mission is a true 0-to-1 challenge: build, structure, and scale our global procurement framework, drive major high-stakes negotiations (payments, strategic SaaS), and set up modern third-party compliance guardrails across our international hubs. Reporting directly to Lydie Convert (Head of Cash - department of 5 people), you will have the autonomy to shape BlaBlaCar’s procurement function, set priorities, and drive strategic negotiations. Sitting within the Finance (40 people), your role will span the business, working closely with teams across all departments, all entities. You will build this function with the active support of Finance, Legal, Security, Tech, and business owners, drawing on their expertise while owning the procurement roadmap and leading its implementation. This is an opportunity to combine independent ownership with strong cross-functional collaboration and deliver tangible business impact.
