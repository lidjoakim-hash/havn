Veritaz is a leading IT staffing solutions provider in Sweden, committed to advancing individual careers and aiding employers in ensuring the perfect talent fit. With a proven track record of successful partnerships with top companies, we have rapidly grown our presence in the USA, Europe, and Sweden as a dependable and trusted resource within the IT industry. Job Overview Assignment Description We are looking for an experienced Senior Business Analyst to join a large transformation programme focused on developing a next-generation Trade Surveillance platform. The platform is designed to identify and manage potential market abuse and ensure regulatory compliance across global financial markets. You will act as a key link between business and IT, translating complex business needs into clear functional requirements. You will work closely with international surveillance teams in Europe and the USA, as well as development, product and other stakeholder teams. What You Will Work On Gather, analyse and translate business requirements into functional specifications, user stories and acceptance criteria. Define, document, prioritize and test data-related and functional requirements. Ensure solutions balance business needs, technical feasibility and business value. Collaborate with international Surveillance teams, Commercial teams and System Engineering. Support project planning, requirements gathering and delivery follow-up. Identify and resolve delivery blockers. Work with Risk, Compliance and Audit teams to validate requirements and address issues. Manage and prioritize the project backlog. Establish and improve processes and governance routines. Lead the calibration and fine-tuning of surveillance models across asset classes, including equities and fixed-income instruments. Support business-as-usual activities, including reviewing business initiatives, market abuse risk assessments and user queries. What You Bring Master's degree in Economics, Finance, Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. Minimum 5 years of experience as a Business Analyst or Product Analyst within capital markets, financial technology or a regulated trading environment. Strong understanding of trading data structures, trading protocols, order management, execution flows and exchange rules. Knowledge of financial instruments and asset classes, including equities, fixed income, derivatives, futures, forwards and options. Proven ability to translate complex business and technical requirements into functional specifications, user stories and data flow diagrams. Experience working in agile delivery environments. Experience with JIRA and cross-functional development and product teams. Strong analytical skills and advanced SQL-based data analysis capabilities. Good English communication skills. Meritorious Experience with Trade Surveillance, compliance monitoring or regulatory frameworks such as MAR, MiFID II, FINRA, Nasdaq Market Model and Exchange Rules. Experience with large-scale data integrations and multi-asset market data flows. Knowledge of instrument reference data and differences between financial products from a surveillance perspective. Experience working with global teams and international stakeholders. Experience with surveillance platforms such as NMS, NTS, Actimize, Trading Hub, Scila or similar. Understanding of surveillance system calibration. Advanced JIRA experience, including backlog and sprint planning. Experience with Tableau, Power BI or similar data visualization tools.
