Company description: Ericsson AB Job description: About this opportunity Ericsson’s Early Career Program is an 18-month journey for you who are recently graduated or have up to five years of relevant experience. Through three rotations, including one international and market exposure, you will tackle challenges that matter, work alongside people who expect you to contribute, question, move things forward, and bring to closure.   The Excellerate track is designed to develop future leaders who are motivated by performance, pace, and commercial impact. Rotations are cross-functional, and may be across Sales, Commercial Management, Strategy, Supply, Finance and more. Working on priorities that directly influence results and learning how different parts of the business connect to create value.   This isn’t a programme where you sit on the sidelines. From day one, you’re hired into a role, trusted to contribute, supported to grow, and expected to make an impact. Find out more about the Early Career program here.   Your journey within the Early Career Program will start as a Strategy Manager. This role is designed for individuals with exceptional potential rather than extensive experience. It offers broad exposure to senior leaders, strategic choices and business priorities across Ericsson Networks, creating a unique foundation for future leadership roles within Ericsson. What you will do As a Strategy Manager in Ericsson’s Early Career Program, you will join BA Networks Strategy, a team responsible for identifying and shaping the strategic questions that matter most for Ericsson Networks. We bring together perspectives from markets and sales, technology and across Ericsson to clarify implications, develop recommendations and help shape and drive the direction of our Networks business.   Your work will be varied and project-based. You will take complex and sometimes ambiguous questions, bring structure to them, develop fact based insight, and help translate those insights into implications, options and recommendations. You will learn how major business decisions are framed, challenged and translated into action.   You will work in an environment across business strategy, market and technology outlooks, business planning, strategic analysis, risk management and executive decision support. The team works on topics that influence the future direction, competitiveness and performance of Ericsson’s largest business..   Take on complex and sometimes ambiguous business questions and bring structure to them. Conduct quantitative and qualitative analysis to uncover insights and support strategic decisions. Use data, digital tools and AI to strengthen the way strategic questions are explored and solved. Explore markets, business trends and strategic opportunities to understand what they mean for Ericsson Networks. Work across teams and functions to gather perspectives, challenge assumptions and move strategic topics forward. Turn analysis into clear, concise recommendations and decision material for senior stakeholders. Contribute to several strategic projects and priorities, building broad exposure across technology, markets and business topics. Help frame strategic questions, challenge assumptions and clarify trade-offs to support business decisions. You will bring Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to bring structure to unclear problems. Confidence working with data and turning information into meaningful conclusions. Clear and concise communication, with the ability to explain your thinking simply. Curiosity and confidence when approaching unfamiliar problems and topics. Strong collaboration skills, including listening, asking thoughtful questions and moving discussions forward. Ambition, ownership and a willingness to learn and develop quickly.
