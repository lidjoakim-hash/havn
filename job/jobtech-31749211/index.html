Validation Engineer

Scania CV Aktiebolag · Södertälje, Sweden

Scania is undergoing a transformation from being a leading supplier of trucks, buses, and engines to providing complete and sustainable transport solutions. Together with TRATON and our sister brands MAN, Volkswagen Truck & Bus, and International, we are working to shape the future of mobility with innovative and environmentally conscious solutions. Our values – customer first, respect, team spirit, responsibility, and elimination of waste – are at the heart of everything we do. Together, we are at the forefront of creating a sustainable future. Role Summary The EDVA Validation Analysis team plans and carries out complete-vehicle validation, developing global verification and validation plans from both project and COIN perspectives. The team defines and follows up on targets, assessing vehicle performance, reliability and operating costs against customer expectations. During validation, we coordinate internal and external tests and collect data from test vehicles, vehicle fleets and customers. By analysing how our solutions perform in real customer operations, we monitor vehicle characteristics and product maturity, evaluate whether customer expectations are being met, and turn our findings into clear feedback for SCANIA and TRATON R&D. This feedback supports product development and continuous improvement. We currently work extensively with the OTI, FT and LP test platforms, and aim to expand and develop our platforms, methods, analytical capabilities and ways of thinking. Achieving this also depends on strong collaboration with property owners, product planning and market companies across Group and BID. Job Responsibilities Your main responsibilities will include: Taking part in the planning stage to understand the deliverables for project and COIN milestones. Following up on complete vehicle testing, internally and externally. Asking relevant questions and analysing data collected from vehicles, vehicle users and fleet managers. Working with the section, attribute owners and other expert functions to analyse and present conclusions. Validating that attribute targets are met for new initiatives and existing products. Contributing to the continuous development of vehicle function validation, including its methods and processes. Who You Are To succeed in this role, you have a strong knowledge of Scania’s vehicles, product offering and component systems. You understand truck applications and use cases and have experience of complete vehicle testing and validation. Knowledge of Scania’s and TRATON’s OnePDP development process is also important. A data-driven mindset and knowledge of software development are important for analysing vehicle data anddeveloping our validation methods. A pragmatic approach is essential so you can make well-founded assumptions when information is incomplete. Travel is part of the role, and you should feel comfortable meeting customers in Sweden and across Europe. A C/CE driving licence is a strong advantage. This Is Us The group has 10 employees, most of whom are highly experienced and have a long history at Scania. We are loosely organised around Planning, Validation and Data Follow-up, while striving to work across these areas and learn from one another. Scania Offers We offer a dynamic and engaging workplace where collaboration, innovation, and continuous improvement are part of everyday life. You will be part of a strong team environment that encourages knowledge sharing and close cooperation across functions. With a structured development plan and a wide range of training opportunities, Scania supports your professional growth both locally and internationally. Benefits include access to our health center in Gröndal or a wellness allowance, result bonus, flexible working hours, and company car leasing. Scania also arranges events for employees and their families, and for those living in Stockholm, convenient commuting is supported through direct Scania Job express buses to Södertälje. Applicat
