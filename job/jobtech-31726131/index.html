AWS Cloud Architect at Husqvarna Group

Husqvarna AB · Jönköping, Sweden

💰 ca. 520 000–640 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 3.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Do you want to design cloud solutions that dozens of teams reuse, instead of building for just one? Our mission is to enable our DevOps teams to focus on building great applications by providing a shared internal developer platform, tools, templates and ways of working that simplify the entire software development lifecycle. Today, we support more than 20 teams across all three divisions, combining hands-on engineering with coaching, training and technical enablement. Ultimately, our goal is simple: remove complexity, create great developer experiences and enable teams to deliver secure, high-quality software faster. Role Description As a Cloud Architect in CORE Platform, you will help shape the AWS foundation that our DevOps teams build on. Instead of every team solving the same cloud challenges themselves, we create reusable patterns, guardrails and infrastructure-as-code that make it easier to build, deploy and operate software securely and efficiently. You will combine hands-on cloud engineering with architecture and enablement, designing and operating shared AWS capabilities while working closely with the teams that use them. Their challenges and feedback become input for better automation, self-service and reusable solutions across the organization. Together with colleagues across Platform Engineering, Product and Security, you will help develop a coherent platform where reliability and security are built in from the start. You will also run Game Days, including sessions together with AWS, and use those learnings to continuously strengthen the platform. Looking ahead, we are preparing for AI agents to become platform users alongside developers. You will have the opportunity to help shape the cloud patterns and secure environments that make that possible, while influencing how Husqvarna’s use of AWS evolves. What you will do: Design and build reusable AWS patterns, templates and guardrails as code. Ensure our cloud capabilities are reliable, secure and performant. Improve automation and self-service based on the needs of our DevOps teams. Run Game Days and turn learnings into concrete improvements. Evolve our cloud patterns as AWS, technology and user needs change. Coach and mentor engineers across teams. Your profile Beyond your technical expertise, you have a customer-focused mindset and genuinely care about creating solutions that teams find useful and want to adopt. You are curious, pragmatic and comfortable exploring new technologies and unfamiliar challenges. As a senior colleague, you enjoy sharing your knowledge, coaching others and helping people grow. You are also curious about how AI is changing software development, while taking a balanced and practical view of where it creates real value. Most importantly, you are a collaborative and humble team player who brings a positive attitude, a sense of humour and a willingness to succeed together. You have: 5+ years of professional experience working with cloud technologies, with deep knowledge of AWS. Strong experience with Infrastructure as Code and designing and running containerized applications in AWS. Experience building highly available, reliable cloud solutions and the ability to explain the trade-offs behind your architectural decisions. Experience with container services such as ECS and/or Kubernetes platforms such as EKS. Professional proficiency in English, both written and spoken. Experience with AWS CDK is a plus. Location Huskvarna. We believe the best ideas happen when we are connected. We spend most of our time together in the office, creating space for collaboration, creativity, and fast decision-making. This is consistent across all our global offices and helps us maintain a strong culture, support learning and development, and ensure everyone has access to the people and resources they need to thrive.
