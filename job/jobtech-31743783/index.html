Senior Backend Engineer (Node.js/TypeScript)

Pinetree AB · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 570 000–690 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 15.2.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Build the software that manages thousands of time nodes in 5G networks. Mobile networks depend on precise timing. Net Insight’s Zyntai platform keeps those networks synchronized, and Zyntai Director is the software used to manage the time nodes. Today, the platform supports around 2,000 nodes generating roughly 500,000 metric data points per second. Net Insight is looking for a senior engineer to join the five-person team behind Zyntai Director. You’ll develop backend services in Node.js and TypeScript, work with Kubernetes and AWS, and help the team keep Director reliable as customer networks grow. The role The Director team is part of Net Insight’s Sync R&D organization, which has around 15 engineers. Your work will span feature development, system design and troubleshooting in production. Upcoming work includes adding IPv6 support. A customer-reported issue might take you into the metrics to understand why a service behaves differently in a live network. Your responsibilities will include: Building and extending backend services and APIs in Node.js and TypeScript. Designing services that can handle larger networks and higher volumes of incoming metrics. Investigating performance bottlenecks and improving reliability. Adding networking capabilities, including IPv6 support. Tracking down production bugs, testing fixes and following up on customer-reported issues. Working with product owners on the scope and technical design of upcoming features. Supporting customer proofs of concept alongside Tech Sales. Tech stack and environment Backend: Node.js and TypeScript for Zyntai Director’s services and APIs. Containers and orchestration: Kubernetes. Cloud: AWS. Distributed systems: Services that manage time nodes across telecom networks. Metrics and observability: High-volume metrics processing, performance analysis and production troubleshooting. Networking: API design, IP networking and work on IPv6 support. Net Insight is also investing in AI-assisted development for its engineering teams and exploring how AI could support future product features. Technical ownership and ways of working There is no separate architecture department at Net Insight. The Director team makes its own design decisions. When you take on a feature, you’ll help decide how it should work, discuss the trade-offs with the product owner and stay involved through implementation and testing. If an issue shows up in a customer’s network, the same team investigates it. The team uses Scrum and Kanban, with daily stand-ups. The Sync group is small enough that engineers work directly with Product, Sales and Tech Sales. That includes joining customer discussions during a proof of concept, helping test a capability and hearing firsthand what the customer needs from Director. About you You have experience building and maintaining backend services in production. You’ve worked with distributed systems and investigated what happens when load increases or a service starts behaving unexpectedly. You’re comfortable contributing to design decisions and want to stay hands-on with code. For this role, you should bring: Experience developing backend services and APIs in a production environment. An understanding of scalability, reliability and the trade-offs involved in designing distributed systems. Experience working with a cloud platform such as AWS, Azure or GCP. Comfort working primarily in Node.js and TypeScript. Experience with Kubernetes, IP networking or systems that process large volumes of metrics is particularly relevant. You’ll also have opportunities to work directly with customers, especially during technical evaluations and troubleshooting. Location and employment This is a permanent, full-time position based at Net Insight’s headquarters in Solna, Sweden. You can combine office and remote work.
