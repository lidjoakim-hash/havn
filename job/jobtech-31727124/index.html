Program Manager Environmental Governance

Hunt Consulting is a reputable consultancy firm specialising in program and project management, business development and executive management. Our clients include international organisations and non-governmental organisations working to advance the sustainable use of natural resources worldwide. The work of the clients may include but is not limited to international collaboration, policy engagement, knowledge sharing, and capacity development. The opportunity We are seeking a highly motivated and experienced Program Manager to support the development and implementation of international programmes and policy initiatives related to sustainable resource governance. The successful candidate will contribute to policy analysis, programme development, stakeholder engagement, and international cooperation efforts aimed at strengthening governance frameworks and supporting evidence-based decision-making. This position offers an exciting opportunity to work in a dynamic international environment with governments, multilateral organisations, research institutions, NGOs, and private sector stakeholders. Key Responsibilities Support the design, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation of international programmes and projects related to sustainable resource management. Conduct policy analysis and research on environmental governance issues at local, national, and international levels. Engage with governments,international organisations, donors, and other stakeholders to advance programme objectives. Build and maintain relationships with senior government officials, ministries, public authorities, development agencies, and international partners. Contribute to the development of policy briefs, reports, technical papers, and knowledge products. Organise and facilitate workshops, consultations, conferences, and multi-stakeholder dialogues. Support fundraising efforts, including proposal development and donor reporting. Monitor developments in international environmental and water policy and identify implications for programme activities. Represent the organisation in external meetings, working groups, international forums, and professional networks as required. Qualifications and Experience Essential Requirements Master's degree or higher in Environmental Governance, Environmental Policy, International Development, Natural Resource Management, or a closely related field. Minimum eight (8) years of relevant professional experience in sustainable governance of natural resources or related fields. Demonstrated experience working in the Global South with international environmental and sustainability policy processes. Proven experience organising and hosting events at international conferences related to natural resource management. Experience engaging with senior government officials, ministries, public authorities, intergovernmental organisations, and other high-level stakeholders. Strong understanding of environmental governance, environmental policy, sustainable development, and international cooperation frameworks. Experience working in Sweden with Swedish stakeholders. Fluency in English, both written and spoken. Strong analytical, research, drafting, and report-writing skills. Excellent interpersonal, stakeholder engagement, and facilitation skills. Advanced computer proficiency, including Microsoft Office Suite and practical experience with SAP, Microsoft Project, or similar enterprise and project management systems. Demonstrated ability to work independently while effectively contributing to multinational and multidisciplinary teams. Willingness and ability to undertake extensive international travel. Desirable Qualifications Additional language skills, particularly Spanish, Swedish, German, or other internationally relevant languages. Experience working with international NGOs, UN organisations, development agencies, research institutions, or donor-funded programmes. Experience in natural resource management approaches. Kno
