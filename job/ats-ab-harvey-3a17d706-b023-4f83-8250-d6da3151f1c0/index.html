ROLE OVERVIEW As a Mid-Market Customer Success Manager at Harvey, you are the custodian of value for a portfolio of law firms and in-house legal teams across DACH — you own their mutual success plan and their renewal. You connect Harvey's technology and expertise to each customer's goals, moving them along the AI maturity curve so adoption keeps deepening, and you are directly accountable for retaining and growing that relationship. You will run your book as a portfolio, deliver a consistently high-quality customer journey at scale, and partner with Account Executives, Legal Engineers and Product to turn adoption into measurable value and expansion. WHAT YOU’LL DO - Deliver the customer journey: run kickoffs, implementation reviews and QBRs tailored to each customer, anchored on a mutual success plan that ties Harvey to their objectives, priorities and measures of success. - Drive adoption and value: engage end users, administrators and senior stakeholders — managing partners, general counsel, heads of innovation — to position Harvey as essential to how their legal work gets done, build on proven use cases, and quantify ROI in business terms. - Orchestrate cross-functionally: bring in Sales, Legal Engineering, Deal Desk, Legal and Finance at the right moment, and represent customer needs back to Product and Engineering. - Expand: identify and progress expansion opportunities within your accounts, partnering with Account Executives where appropriate. - Own the renewal end to end: run your book as a portfolio — knowing whether each account is in Defend, Maintain or Grow, with evidence — build renewal plans and timelines, forecast accurately, lead commercial conversations, negotiate terms and close renewals on or ahead of schedule against a gross and net revenue retention target. WHAT YOU HAVE Ideal candidates for the Mid-Market CSM role at Harvey can demonstrate comfort and experience with the following: - 3–4+ years in customer-owning roles at tech or SaaS platforms, including direct ownership of renewals, retention or a commercial / account-management target. - A track record of owning and delivering against a revenue, retention or net-revenue-retention number. - An AI-native way of working: you use AI and sophisticated tooling daily and can show how it has changed your own output. - Experience running the renewal motion end to end — forecasting, renewal planning and leading commercial negotiations. - Account management and portfolio planning across a high-volume book of business, including segmentation and revenue-based prioritisation. - Quantifying and communicating ROI and measurable business value to customers, grounded in concrete use cases. - Identifying at-risk accounts early and executing
