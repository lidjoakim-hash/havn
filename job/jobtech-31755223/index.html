VP AI Ventures

E-commerce Recruit Nordic AB · Göteborg, Sweden

Help Build the Next Generation of Mathematics Learning Albert Group is a Nordic EdTech group developing digital education services that help children learn and grow. Through Albert Junior and Sumdog, the group has established experience in digital learning for both home and school. Albert Group is now developing a new AI-powered mathematics platform designed to make learning more personalised, engaging and effective. The platform will offer two complementary services. For schools, the focus is on providing teachers with better tools for instruction, classroom activities and understanding students' learning progress. For home use, a standalone service is being developed where children can learn new mathematical concepts, practise their skills and receive personalised support through AI. Both services will work independently while also having the potential to complement each other and strengthen children's learning. For this initiative, E-commerce Recruit is looking for a VP AI Ventures to take overall responsibility for developing the product, building the business and bringing the offering to market. About the Role You will take full ownership of developing the new business, from product strategy and technical priorities to launch and commercial growth. The product is already under development. Your role will be to take it forward, establish clear priorities and ensure that technology is translated into valuable, commercially viable services. You will lead a small team with expertise in technology and education, while also working with external developers and specialists. The role involves close collaboration with other businesses within the group and reports directly to the CEO. Your key responsibilities will include: Own the product strategy, roadmap and priorities for the AI-powered platform. Lead internal developers, educational specialists and external consultants, while driving key technical decisions. Ensure the product is grounded in relevant educational research and that technology supports learning. Develop and execute go-to-market strategies targeting both schools (B2B) and parents (B2C). Take responsibility for the business model, positioning, pricing and commercial launch. Drive pilot programmes and measure learning outcomes, user engagement and business performance. Build the team and business step by step, with responsibility for budget and results. Develop the business with international expansion in mind, initially focusing on the Nordic markets and the UK. During your first months, you will familiarise yourself with the existing product development work and establish a clear product and go-to-market plan. The next priorities will be to launch pilots, measure results and create the conditions for acquiring paying customers. Who Are We Looking For? We are looking for someone who has experience building, developing and launching digital products or new business areas, and who understands what it takes to turn an early-stage idea into a viable business. You have a strong technical understanding and hands-on experience with AI-powered products. You do not need to write the code yourself, but you must be able to understand technical opportunities, challenge development teams and make well-informed decisions. You also bring a strong commercial mindset and experience turning product ideas into offerings that customers want to use and are willing to pay for. We believe you have: Experience building and launching digital products, preferably within SaaS, with responsibility for both product development and commercial results. Hands-on experience with AI products, such as LLM-based features, prompt engineering, evaluation or personalisation. Experience leading developers, product teams or external technology partners. Experience developing and executing go-to-market strategies. A data-driven approach based on testing hypotheses, measuring results and making informed decisions. Fluency in English, both written and spoke
