Veeva Systems is building the industry cloud for Life Sciences to help companies work in a more efficient and connected way. Learn more about our products, vision and values, and status as a public benefit corporation on our website.   The Role We are hiring students into our Consultant Development Program (CDP) as Technology Consulting Interns – a role specifically designed for Bachelor's and Master's students in their final year of studies. In this role, you’ll learn our industry-leading cloud platforms and work with cutting-edge AI solutions to transform how life sciences companies operate. Empowered from day one by a team-first culture, you will accelerate your career growth as a technical consultant who thrives in an environment where we work hard for our customers and each other. Make a direct impact and partner with pharmaceutical, biotech, and medical device companies to design, configure, and deliver tangible technology solutions that customers use every day. We also have partnerships with local universities which support internship requirements and academic credits.   We believe in empowering high - potential people to achieve excellence. No prior industry, technical, or consulting experience is required — you bring your drive, curiosity, and hunger to learn, and we will provide comprehensive training, structured education, and ongoing individual mentorship every step of the way. You will work in a supportive environment with experienced mentors and team leads dedicated to helping you succeed and grow.   This program will build the core consulting and technical expertise you need for long-term career success, with the potential for a seamless transition into a full-time Associate Consultant position upon graduation with no duplicate hiring needed. Start Dates: Continuously hiring Commitment: 6–12 months for at least 20 hours per week during core business hours What You'll Do As an intern on the Managed Services team, you will become an expert on Veeva’s technology. Under the continuous guidance and mentorship of experienced architects and project leads, you’ll work directly with customers to innovate and improve their business operations by: • Tailoring the software: You will configure our platform to fit exactly what the customer needs—whether that is turning on new features, organizing their information, or adapting the system to match how their business operates. • Testing & problem-solving: Before new features are released to a customer’s entire company, you will help them test those ideas safely to ensure everything works accurately and efficiently. What We Are Looking For | We don’t require a specific academic background, tech skills, or life sciences experience. We require a specific mindset: • Drive: You thrive in a challenging, intense environment that rewards initiative and rapid learning. • Curiosity: You have a genuine desire to be hands-on with software and AI and to master new technologies. • Communication: You are fluent in English with excellent verbal and written communication skills. • Analytical Mindset: You are a strong critical thinker with a proven track record of academic performance (in any degree/field of study). • Integrity: Veeva is a Public Benefit Corporation (PBC) and a “do the right thing” company and we expect that from all employees. • Work Ethic: You are committed to excellence and have a passion for working hard to achieve results. • Experience: You are an active student at a Hungarian university pursuing a degree (Bachelor's or Master's) in any field and in the final year of your studies. • Availability: You are able to commit to at least 20h/week during core business hours.
