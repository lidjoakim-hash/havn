About us Traditional banking has never really given you control over your financial life. Satispay exists to change that: to empower people through finance. That means making financial services easy and accessible for over 6.5M users. We started with payments, then benefits, investing, cards... and we’re not done. Not even close. We follow the same logic every time: find a real problem, solve it properly, then look for the next one. The destination? To build the most loved financial platform in the world. If you own outcomes rather than tasks, stay close to what users need, and refuse to wait for someone else to fix what's broken — you'll find people here who work exactly in the same way. And together, you'll build something worth being proud of. What you'll be doing As our Senior Strategic Project Manager within the Office of the CEO, you will be a primary execution driver behind our most critical cross-functional initiatives. You will serve as the strategic bridge connecting executive leadership with operational, regulatory, product, and business teams across our Luxembourg and Milan hubs. While you will work side-by-side with internal Legal, Compliance, and Risk experts who own the regulatory subject matter, your core strength is senior strategic leadership and project execution: acting as a key leader within the organization to orchestrate complex initiatives across different verticals, driving high-impact project timelines, and turning corporate obligations into seamless operational workflows. Here's what that looks like in practice: - Solve complex business challenges - Tackling intricate business problems by developing innovative, data-backed solutions with a consistent user-first approach. - Stakeholder management and strategic partnership - Managing relationships with C-level stakeholders and acting as a thought partner for leadership on specific strategic areas. - Strategic influence - Influencing major decisions through in-depth analysis and proven insights, integrating diverse perspectives into coherent strategies. - Champion methodology and best practices - Developing and introducing new methodologies, best practices, or frameworks within the team or company, ensuring a consistent user-first approach. - Champion Regulatory Requirements in Roadmap Design - Act as the project manager champion for what regulatory and supervisory requirements apply to a given initiative, ensuring they are proactively factored into project roadmaps at the design stage - partnering closely with Legal, Compliance, and Risk. - Govern & Monitor Multi-Hub Projects - Establish project governance, track milestones across offices, proactively remove cross-departmental bottlenecks, mitigate delivery risks, and provide crisp, data-driven reporting to executive leadership. - Anchor Luxembourg Operational Synergies - Serve as an authoritative on-the-ground project leader in Luxembourg, ensuring that financial institution governance requirements seamlessly harmonize with Satispay’s group-wide strategy and product velocity.
