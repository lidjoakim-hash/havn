Salary: SEK 28,000–30,000 gross per month, depending on skill level · Start immediately or March–May 2027 · Apply by 31 December 2026 Frontic AB is a company based in Gothenburg (Göteborg), Sweden, providing light carpentry and grounds maintenance for customers in the Gothenburg area. We are now looking for more skilled people who enjoy hands-on work, being outdoors and a varied working day. The job The work covers two areas – you can be strong in one or both: Light carpentry: building and repairing fences, decks and patios, small repairs and simple construction work. Grounds maintenance: lawn mowing, hedge trimming, planting, leaf clearing and other care of gardens, yards and green spaces – plus snow clearing in winter when needed. Who we are looking for Experience in carpentry and/or gardening You work independently and are careful and reliable Basic English or Swedish A category B driving licence is a plus What we offer Varied work, much of it outdoors A small company where decisions are made quickly Opportunities to develop in the job Employment in Sweden in accordance with Swedish law Key details Type of employment: seasonal to begin with, with the possibility of full-time employment Start date: immediately, or in March, April or May 2027 Salary: SEK 28,000–30,000 gross per month, depending on skill level Location: Gothenburg and surrounding area How to apply Send a short CV and a few lines about your experience to per.hoff@hotmail.com. Please apply in English or Swedish. Photos of previous work are welcome. If you have any questions, feel free to email Per Hoff at the same address. Application deadline: 31 December 2026
