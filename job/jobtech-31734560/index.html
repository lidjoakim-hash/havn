Sales Account Manager to Ex-Change Parts

EdZa AB · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 450 000–550 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 14.2.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Do you want to work in the global helicopter aftermarket and develop sales with customers across international markets? We are now looking for a Sales Account Manager to join Ex-Change Parts and contribute to the company's continued growth. About Ex Change Parts Ex-Change Parts AB operates within the global aviation industry and specializes in helicopter support, including the sale, exchange and related services for helicopter parts and components. The company has over 26 years of industry experience and serves more than 2,000 customers worldwide. Ex-Change Parts has a strong position within the international helicopter aftermarket and works with customers across both commercial and military aviation. The company works with several helicopter models, including the Super Puma family and smaller helicopter models. About the Role As a Sales Account Manager, you will be responsible for developing sales within your assigned markets and building strong, lasting relationships with both existing and prospective customers. You will have an international focus and work closely with customers to understand their needs, identify new business opportunities and provide the right solutions. The role includes a high volume of quotations, customer follow ups and orders, as well as close collaboration with internal teams. You will also prepare and conduct customer meetings and represent the company at trade shows and other industry events. Responsibilities and Tasks Develop and maintain relationships with existing and new customers. Identify customer needs, new business opportunities and customer needs. Manage quotations, follow up with customers and handle orders throughout the sales process. Prepare and present products and solutions to customers. Analyze customer and market data to support pricing and sales decisions. Work closely with internal teams to ensure the right products and solutions are offered. Travel internationally for customer meetings and trade shows. Your Experience and Skills We are looking for a driven salesperson with experience in B2B or technical sales. Experience from the aviation industry, preferably within helicopters, is a strong advantage but not a requirement. A strong background in technical sales can also be a great fit for the role. We see that you have: Experience in B2B or technical sales. Fluent Swedish and excellent English, both spoken and written. Experience working with international customers. A strong commercial mindset and a drive to achieve results. The ability to understand and sell technically complex products. Meritorious: Experience working with aircraft or helicopter component sales. Knowledge of the aviation industry. Understanding of FAA and EASA certification of parts. As a person, you are driven, positive and comfortable meeting and communicating with different types of people. You enjoy working towards clear goals and are motivated by achieving results together with your team. You are curious, flexible and comfortable taking responsibility for your work. We Offer This is an exciting opportunity to join a well established company operating in the international aviation market. You will become part of an international company where you get the opportunity to develop your skills, build a global network and have a direct impact on the business. You will work in an entrepreneurial environment with short paths to decision making, a high level of responsibility and a team that is driven, customer focused and enjoys working together. • Hybrid working and a high level of flexibility. • International travel and the opportunity to build a global network. • Generous wellness benefits. • Strong focus on work life balance. • Generous pension conditions and parental leave top up. • Social activities and team events. • A family oriented and transparent company culture.
