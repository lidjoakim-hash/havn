We are looking for construction cleaners in greater Oslo area

RENT Renholdstjenester AS - Construction cleaning · Norway

We are looking for you who enjoys physical work. As a service worker/cleaner in the Building Cleaning department. Building cleaning is performed in connection with the construction or renovation of buildings, ranging from large commercial buildings to apartments and single-family homes. Our clients are contractors in the Eastern Norway region. You will perform rough cleaning and final cleaning, cleaning according to the &#34;Rent Tørt Bygg&#34; (RTB) standard, removal of construction dust, building materials, waste, and dirt. Cleaning of walls, ceilings, floors, windows, technical rooms, and many other exciting tasks on construction sites in the Eastern Norway area. We have plenty of work. If you have no prior experience, we are happy to train you. The most important thing for us is that you have the right attitude and good health. We are looking for you who have: • Some work experience from physical work is desirable, preferably from construction work or cleaning • Sufficient Norwegian oral proficiency (A2 level) and/or speak good English • Driver's license class B • The job can be physically demanding, so you should be in normal good shape • You must be efficient, yet precise, and deliver according to our customers' quality requirements • You must be service-oriented and flexible regarding work tasks and workplace, and you must also be accommodating and helpful towards our customers. You must expect some overtime • Building cleaning is an important job, so it is important to us that you take pride in the work you do • You are positive and contribute to a good work environment We offer you: • Regular working hours (07:00-15:00) • Regular salary conditions • Regular working conditions • A good work environment Send us a few lines about yourself and a CV including references . Mark your application &#34;BYGGRENHOLD&#34;. We ask that you do NOT call us! If you happen not to hear from us, we still hope you will apply another time - because suddenly it might be you we are looking for.
