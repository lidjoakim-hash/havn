ABOUT US At LangChain, our mission is to make intelligent agents ubiquitous. We build the foundation for agent engineering in the real world, helping developers move from prototypes to production-ready AI agents that teams can rely on. We began as widely adopted open-source tools and have grown to also offer a platform for building, evaluating, deploying, and operating agents at scale. With $125M raised at Series B from IVP, Sequoia, Benchmark, CapitalG, and Sapphire Ventures, we’re at a stage where we’re continuing to develop new products, growth is accelerating, and all team members have meaningful impact on what we build and how we work together. LangChain is a place where your contributions can shape how this technology shows up in the real world. Today, our platform includes LangSmith (Observability, Evaluation, Deployment, Fleet, and Sandboxes), our open source frameworks (LangChain, LangGraph, and Deep Agents), and the newly launched LangSmith Engine for autonomous agent improvement. We have 100M+ monthly open source downloads, 6,000+ active LangSmith customers, and 5 of the Fortune 10 use LangSmith in production (+ 35% of the Fortune 500 overall), including teams at Klarna, Clay, Coinbase, Workday, Lyft, Cloudflare, Harvey, Rippling, Vanta, LinkedIn, Monday.com, Nvidia, and Bridgewater. ABOUT THE ROLE As the Deployed Engineering Manager for Northern Europe, you will lead a team of deployed engineers from our hub in Amsterdam, working with our customers across DACH and Benelux. You'll help build and shape the team, grow the people in it and make sure our customers get the technical partnership they need to build and run great agents in production. This is an opportunity to have outsized impact at a company shaping the future of AI-powered software. DEs work alongside our sales team to help our prospects and customers build reliable, scalable agents for their business using our suite of products: LangChain, LangGraph, Deep Agents and LangSmith. The team focuses on pre-sales efforts, but stays on accounts to not only close new business but also to make our customers wildly successful. Your first job is your people. You will support great deployed engineers, ramp them quickly, coach them through live customer work and build a team where they do the best work of their careers. You'll stay close enough to the technology and to our customers to be credible with both: unblocking your team on hard engagements and stepping in on the region's most important opportunities. You will report to the Director of Deployed Engineering, EMEA and partner closely with LangChain's sales leaders covering DACH and Benelux, with shared ownership of regional outcomes. This role is mostly people leadership, with real customer engagement and revenue accountability alongside it. As part of the EMEA deployed engineering leadership team, you'll also bring what you learn back into the playbooks, processes and standards we use globally.
