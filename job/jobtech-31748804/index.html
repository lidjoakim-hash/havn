Are you looking for a part-time job in Stockholm? Do you like to explore and keep active? Are you searching for a high-paced, active and rewarding job where you get to make a real impact in your city? Then read on! We are Velove, and we are looking for bicycle riders to join our team in Stockholm. We are a startup with a vision to change the world of urban mobility, fight against climate change, and create more people-friendly cities. We are here to "Beat Vans" with our innovative and energy-efficient solution to last-mile delivery. About the job: Delivering e-commerce parcels for different vendors Riding the coolest of four-wheeled electrical cargo bikes around Stockholm 3-6 hours evening shifts that starts around 5pm Shift based employment or Monthly Salary On average, new riders earn approximately 153,4kr to 194,7kr per hour, including supplements for evenings and weekends*. Requirements: Have the right to work in Sweden and be over 18 years of age Have Coordination/Personal Number issued by Skatteverket Speak English on a conversational level You are a natural on a bike and enjoy being active/working out! This is a physically demanding job so it's important that you enjoy this Available to work weekdays in the evening between 5pm - 11pm Available to work 2+ shifts per week Thrive in a high-paced and active workplace Perks: Employment under a collective agreement (kollektivavtal), with pay, supplements, working hours, pension and insurance all regulated by the agreement Great community of riders Free and enjoyable workout Opportunities to grow within the company Opportunity to do something meaningful while you work, eliminating vans and reducing people’s carbon footprint Apply today, we want you on board! One more thing... Make sure to answer the questions in your own words. We really want to get to know you, not your AI assistant 😏
