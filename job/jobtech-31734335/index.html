CNC Operator - Jordbro

Prowork Bemanning AB · Haninge, Sweden

💰 ca. 390 000–470 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 6.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Do you have experience working with CNC machines and enjoy the combination of technology, precision, and high-quality craftsmanship? We are now looking for a CNC Operator to become an important part of our joinery workshop in Jordbro. CNC is still relatively new to our workshop, which means you will play a key role and have the opportunity to help shape and develop how we work with CNC going forward. About the role In our in-house joinery workshop, we manufacture bespoke fitted interiors such as kitchens, wardrobes, bookcases, and furniture. You will work closely with our skilled joiners and project managers and be responsible for ensuring a high standard of programming, production, and quality. Your main responsibilities will include: Programming the CNC machine based on technical drawings. Operating and maintaining the machine, including setup, tooling, and basic maintenance. Manufacturing components for bespoke fitted interiors. Reading technical drawings and planning material usage to minimise waste. Checking dimensions, precision, and overall quality. Contributing to a clean, safe, and well-organised workshop. When required, visiting customers in the Stockholm area to take measurements. Who are we looking for? We are looking for someone with at least three years of experience programming and operating CNC machines within joinery, interior manufacturing, or furniture production. You are familiar with CAD/CAM software, confident reading technical drawings, and comfortable working independently. No specific formal education is required. What matters most is your hands-on experience and your understanding of what it takes to achieve a high-quality finished product. As a person, we believe you are detail-oriented, independent, and quality-conscious. You appreciate an organised workplace and work well with others. Experience in fine joinery, high-end bespoke interiors, or taking measurements at customer sites is a strong advantage. What we offer You will join a family-owned company with around 50 employees, including temporary staff, with broad expertise in areas such as joinery, fine woodworking, painting, and tiling. Decision-making is quick and straightforward, and you will have a role where your knowledge and experience can make a real difference. Most importantly, you will have the opportunity to take significant responsibility for our CNC operations and help develop this area from the ground up. Practical information Employment: Full-time Working hours: Monday–Friday, 07:00–16:00 Location: Jordbro Contract: Six-month probationary employment, with the intention of transitioning into permanent employment Start date: By agreement Travel: Occasional travel within the Stockholm area. No overnight stays. Does this sound like the right next step for you? We look forward to receiving your application!
