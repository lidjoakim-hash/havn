This is a genuine team-building role: You will build the team, not just carry a number. Recruit, develop, and retain sellers who become better sellers and leaders. You'll also be the sales half of a close partnership with your Customer Experience counterpart, working together so wins in the sales cycle turn into long-term customer success.
