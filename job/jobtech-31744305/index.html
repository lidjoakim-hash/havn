SALES/BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT AND MARKETING ASSOCIATE At Enable Banking, we build the Open Banking infrastructure behind the financial products. Our mission is simple; to turn Open Banking into a relevant foundtition of companies. We're now looking for a Sales & Marketing Associate to help us on that mission. This is a hybrid role with a 50/50 split: half of your time goes to sales and business development, and half goes to marketing, content, and brand. A key part of this role is helping us grow beyond the EU. As we expand into new non-EU markets (such as the UK, Moldova, Serbia, Georgia, Albania, and other countries with developing Open Banking ecosystems), you'll help research these markets, build our first customer and partner relationships there, and adapt our positioning to each local context. Role Description As a Sales & Marketing Associate at Enable Banking, you won't just book meetings or publish posts. You'll own the link between the two: turning market insight into stories and campaigns, and turning those campaigns into qualified conversations and pipeline. Because we're a small company, your impact will be immediate and visible. The content you create and the conversations you start will directly influence our pipeline, our positioning, and how we talk about our product. You'll work closely with sales, marketing, product, and leadership, helping bring financial products to market across industries such as accounting software, lending, and next-generation fintech. If you enjoy both the analytical and the creative side of growth, are motivated by results, and are curious about how digital financial infrastructure can create real-world impact, we'd love to hear from you. Who we are Enable Banking operates at the forefront of Open Banking. We’re an Open Banking provider operating across 29 EU countries, helping companies access high-quality financial data through one scalable API. Join us in a dynamic environment and help us to help our customers to commercialize our technology. Enable Banking is an AISP operating as a PSD2 API aggregator, integrating to all the major banks across the EU. Our customers integrate to Enable Banking’s API and gain access to all the major banks, instead of integrating to each bank individually. We're looking for a person who thrives on jumping into the action, tackling new challenges head on, and learning fast alongside our growing, ambitious team. Here, challenges aren’t obstacles, they’re opportunities to learn, grow, and make a real impact every day. Your daily mission includes: Sales & Business Development (50%) - Respond to and qualify all inbound leads, ensuring no opportunity slips through the cracks 📞 - Research target markets, segments, and use cases to build highly targeted prospect lists 🔍 - Support our expansion into non-EU markets (UK, Serbia, Georgia, Moldova, and similar), from market sizing and regulatory landscape mapping to first outreach and partner building - Build and nurture relationships with prospective customers and partners, and book meetings that drive growth - Keep HubSpot updated and actively follow up on prospects throughout the pipeline 💻 Marketing & Content (50%) - Write and publish content for LinkedIn and other digital channels, including company posts, thought leadership, and customer stories ✍️ - Plan and support marketing campaigns and events, and represent Enable Banking at industry events and conferences across the EU - Translate Open Banking use cases and market trends into clear, engaging messaging for different industries and new markets 💡 - Build go-to-market materials and campaigns for new non-EU markets, adapted to local audiences, regulation, and use cases - Track performance across web, social, and campaigns through dashboards and analytics, and use the insights to optimise content and conversion 📈 - Work closely with sales, product, and leadership to keep messaging, positioning, and sales materials aligned 🤝
