ABOUT THE TEAM OpenAI’s GTM Partnerships team builds a strategic, global partner ecosystem to accelerate customer success, support responsible enterprise AI adoption, and drive durable growth in support of OpenAI’s mission. We work across Sales, Solutions Engineering, Customer Success, Product, Marketing, Operations, and partner organisations to turn strategic partnerships into measurable customer and commercial outcomes. Cloud providers, global systems integrators, consulting firms, and technology partners play a critical role in helping customers adopt and scale OpenAI’s products. Our Partner Sales & Development team connects these partnerships to the field through joint account planning, opportunity development, coordinated sales execution, and delivery alignment that helps customers move from initial interest to production adoption. ABOUT THE ROLE We are hiring a Partnerships Lead to lead our partner sales and development team for Northern EMEA. Reporting to the Head of GTM Partnerships, Europe you will manage and develop a regional team, own the regional partner business, and work alongside OpenAI Sales leadership and strategic partners to generate pipeline, advance complex pursuits, and drive revenue and customer adoption. This is a people management role that combines team leadership, partner business development, co-sell execution, and operational discipline. You will translate broader partnership priorities into a focused regional plan, set team goals and account coverage, and coach your team to engage the right partners at the right point in the customer journey. You will build relationships with partner field leaders and remain hands-on in priority pursuits, working with technical and delivery teams to turn commercial commitments into sustained customer value. You will be accountable for both regional business performance and the success of your team. We are looking for an experienced people manager who can hire and develop talent, establish clear expectations, manage performance, and build a culture of accountability, collaboration, and customer focus. IN THIS ROLE, YOU WILL: - Own the partner sales and development plan for your assigned region, Northern Europe, aligning team coverage, target accounts, partner priorities, sales plays, and execution milestones with regional Sales leadership - Drive regional commercial outcomes across partner-sourced and partner-attached pipeline, bookings, production adoption, and expansion, with clear accountability for business performance. - Identify and develop new partner-led opportunities. Evaluate where partners can create differentiated customer value, establish executive sponsorship, and build a path from initial engagement to joint execution. - Lead account mapping and joint account planning with OpenAI sellers and partner teams to identify whitespace, qualify opportunities, and prioritize the accounts and use cases with the greatest potential. - Orchestrate complex co-sell pursuits, aligning account teams, partner sellers, technical stakeholders, and services organizations around customer needs, clear responsibilities, and mutual action plans. - Build trusted relationships with regional partner executives and field leaders, while coordinating with global partner owners to ensure regional execution supports the broader partnership strategy. - Partner with Solutions Engineering, Customer Success, Product, and partner delivery teams to address technical and implementation dependencies and help customers deploy, adopt, and expand AI workloads in production. - Create repeatable field engagement models, partner enablement, sales plays, and escalation paths that make it easier for OpenAI and partner teams to work together effectively. - Establish disciplined pipeline reviews, forecasting, business reviews, and performance reporting.
