Celonis is the trusted platform to industrialize Enterprise AI. At our core is the Celonis Context Model — which combines process data, business knowledge, and intelligence into a living digital twin of the enterprise that AI can actually understand, turning AI's operational blind spots into operational clarity. World's leading companies trust Celonis and its global ecosystem of partners to make their AI agents, people, systems, and processes work together, achieving transformational outcomes. We believe there's a massive opportunity to unlock global productivity and sustainability by giving AI the context to understand how every business process really works. Join our mission to make processes work for people, companies, and the planet. The Team The Product Incubation team is a highly specialized unit within Celonis Services that bridges the gap between Product & Engineering (P&E) and enterprise customer reality. Our mission is to guarantee the successful 0-to-1 deployment of Celonis's newest platform capabilities. We act as the forward scouts for the Celonis Customer organization (CCO). We collaborate closely with Product & Engineering during the alpha and private preview phases of new products, stress-test them in complex customer IT environments and build the foundational knowledge, reference architectures and playbooks that allow the rest of the Celonis Customer and Service organization to scale these innovations globally. The Role As a Lead Product Incubation Specialist, you will be the technical spearhead for our most strategic platform shifts, including the Celonis Context Model (CCM), Apollo AI agent orchestration and end of life migrations. You will lead the technical implementation of these early-stage products at selected early-adopter customers to drive outcomes, funnel critical insights back to P&E and establish the "Gold Standards" for future scale within Celonis Services. The work you'll do: • Pioneer Early-Adopter Deployments: Lead the technical architecture and hands-on deployment of pre-GA and newly released Celonis capabilities (e.g., CCM, AI Agents, Zero-Copy integrations) at strategic customers. • P&E Counterpart & Feedback Loop: Serve as the primary technical field interface for Product & Engineering. Stress-test prototypes, identify structural integration gaps or customer pain points and provide structured, prioritized feedback to shape the product roadmap prior to General Availability. • Create the Practice Capital: Translate your initial, hands-on implementation experiences into scalable playbooks, reference architectures and best practices. Collaborate to enable the broader technical field on how to deploy these products safely at scale. • Resolve High-Stakes Technical Escalations: Act as the technical escalation anchor when early-adopter implementations encounter platform-level roadblocks on immature technology. • Cross-Functional Alignment: Build strong relationships across Celonis Services, Business Apps and P&E to ensure that customer infrastructure realities and business needs are accurately reflected in the product lifecycle.
