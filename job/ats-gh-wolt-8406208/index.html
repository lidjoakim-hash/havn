About Wolt At Wolt, we create technology that brings joy, simplicity and earnings to the neighborhoods of the world. In 2014 we started with delivery of restaurant food. Now we’re building the delivery of (almost) everything and you’ll find us in over 500 cities in 30 countries around the world. In 2022 we joined forces with DoorDash and together we keep on dreaming big and expanding across the globe. Working at Wolt isn’t always easy, but it’s definitely exciting. Here you’ll learn more, build more, and ship more than in most other companies. You’ll be challenged a lot, but also have a lot of fun on the way. So, if you’re a self-starter with drive and entrepreneurial spirit, this could be the ride of your life. About the Team DashOS is DoorDash's merchant technology platform. It brings together the products restaurants and local businesses use to run their own business, sell on their own channels and keep their guests coming back, including online ordering, websites and apps, and loyalty. DashOS connects these tools with the demand and growth learnings of the DoorDash, Wolt and Deliveroo marketplaces, so merchants can grow both on our marketplaces and on their own channels. In EMEA, we are building DashOS market by market. The team is small and works like a startup within the larger DoorDash. When joining DashOS, you will help shape a new business from an early stage: what we offer merchants to fuel their growth, how we take it to market and how we support merchants once they are live. Our Strategy & Operations team builds the DashOS business end to end in EMEA: what we focus on, how we measure success, and the playbooks that make growth repeatable. We work in small cross-functional pods that find improvement opportunities, launch and iterate, together with Product, Engineering, Analytics, Finance, Legal, Marketing, our merchant and consumer teams. About the Role We are now looking for a Senior Manager, Revenue Operations to build the commercial engine behind DashOS. You will own the systems, numbers and operating rhythm that turn a multi-product strategy into predictable revenue, across all DoorDash, Wolt and Deliveroo markets in EMEA. What you'll be doing As the Senior Manager, Revenue Operations for DashOS EMEA, you will partner directly with DashOS commercial leadership to co-own the go-to-market plan and run the operating cadence for the sales and account management teams selling DashOS. This is a senior individual contributor role for a builder: you will set up the tracking and planning foundations, then use them to make the sales organisation measurably better. Day-to-day in this role you'll: • Build the revenue tracking infrastructure: own our CRM setup and the data flows into our BI stack, so pipeline, activities and revenue for every DashOS product live in one place • Run the commercial operating cadence, including pipeline tracking, pacing and weekly forecasting with sales and account managers, and own the DashOS commercial narrative to EMEA leadership • Own sales planning across markets: set targets and quotas through bottom-up and top-down allocation, and design books of business, territories and coverage models that match our growth goals • Turn sales performance data into action by building dashboards that act as the single source of truth for account-level performance and risk, and surfacing insights that managers and sellers can act on • Design and roll out incentive systems, including MBOs, SPIFFs and variable compensation, together with Finance, so sales and account managers own DashOS revenue alongside marketplace revenue • Make DashOS part of the core commercial motion, so it shows up in every account manager's book of business, account plans and business reviews rather than running on the side • Automate and scale with AI, removing manual work for sellers and managers (e.g. pre-populated account plans, automated pipeline views) so teams spend their time with merchants Our humble expect
