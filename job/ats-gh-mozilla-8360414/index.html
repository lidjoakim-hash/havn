Why Mozilla? Mozilla Corporation is the non-profit-backed technology company that has shaped the internet for the better over the last 25 years. We make pioneering brands like Firefox, the privacy-minded web browser. Now, with more than 225 million people around the world using our products each month, we’re shaping the next 25 years of technology and helping to reclaim an internet built for people, not companies. Our work focuses on diverse areas including AI, social media, security and more. And we’re doing this while never losing our focus on our core mission – to make the internet better for people. The Mozilla Corporation is wholly owned by the non-profit 501(c) Mozilla Foundation. This means we aren’t beholden to any shareholders — only to our mission. Along with thousands of volunteer contributors and collaborators all over the world, Mozillians design, build and distribute open-source software that enables people to enjoy the internet on their terms. About this team and role: We’re building a new B2B business for Firefox— bringing a privacy-first, data-safe browser and productivity bundle to European organizations. As we grow our EU-based demand generation team ahead of our Q1 2027 bundle launch, we’re looking for an experienced Field Marketing Manager for France based in France. You will independently own the regional field plan, budget and senior Sales partnership for the France region and broader EU enterprise market. You will turn named-account priorities into a focused portfolio of high-value executive and technical experiences, with particular attention to French-language delivery, local enterprise buying dynamics, and the needs of complex, multi-stakeholder organizations. You’ll work closely with Sales, Channel, Product Marketing, and Content teams to bring our go-to-market strategy to life on the ground, and you’ll help build the processes and playbooks this team relies on as it scales from a small pilot into a full regional function.
