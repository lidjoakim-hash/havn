Job Description We are looking for a Senior Sales Operations Growth Specialist to join our Global Sales team. This role is for someone who likes making outreach engines run smoothly. You’ll bring clarity, speed and own our sales engagement stack to grow reach rates across every market while taking the day-to-day tool complexity off Local Sales teams. If you enjoy building clean workflows, testing what converts and seeing your work move the needle every week, you’ll fit right in. How will you make an impact? - Reach rate: Builds its own dashboards from day one and owns the reach rate country meeting: clear agenda and follow-up of the numbers. - Talkdesk: Ring group and number-sharing strategy; watches for burned numbers; every new joiner has a licence in the right ring group from day one. Partner with the tools team to ensure delivery. - Call fevers: Builds the list, rotation, assignment and sequence for each call fever with Local Sales Ops, using the BL leaders’ preparation skill. - Sequences and WhatsApp: One sequence library, A/B tests and reply rates; explains the sequence strategy to the markets; makes the changes within SLA, call fevers included. - DP Meet: Reviews scheduling with Marketing and leads Dynamic Assignees: pool rules by tenure (for example, from 6 months a rep joins the DP Meet pool) and one-off exceptions. - One update process: Talkdesk, DP Meet and WhatsApp follow the HiBob people-change process that Macarena coordinates, the same way as Salesforce. - Sales no-show: Reduce sales no-show reminders based on AI tools and processes. . How success is measured: keep improving reach rate and keep testing new strategies and channels. - Reach rate per market and channel, month over month - Sequence changes live within SLA - New joiners ready in Talkdesk and DP Meet from day one - Less Local Sales Ops time spent on the tools Qualifications What will help you thrive? - 2–6 years of experience in consulting, operations, analytics, or business development. - Educational background in STEM or business-related fields. - Fluent in English; Spanish, Portuguese, Polish, or other languages are a plus. - Proven track record optimizing sequences, testing messaging and cadences across channels, with hands-on experience managing sales engagement tools (such as Talkdesk, email/call sequence platforms, WhatsApp Business and scheduling tools). - A track record of delivering projects end-to-end - from problem definition to implementation. - Strong analytical thinking and ability to translate data into actionable decisions. - Confident working with data — analyzing large datasets to spot trends, issues, and opportunities. - Curious and hands-on with AI tools and automated workflows - Comfortable collaborating across teams and working with multiple stakeholders. - Clear and concise communication skills, both written and verbal. - Familiarity with tools like Salesforce, HubSpot, Zapier, Looker Studio, Tableau, or similar. - Basic working knowledge of SQL or similar tools to extract and manipulate data.
