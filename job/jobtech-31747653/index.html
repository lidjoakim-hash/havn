Job description Business Performance Manager – BA Biomaterials, Stora Enso Are you ready to bridge the gap between commercial strategy and data-driven analytics in a global company leading the transition to a circular bioeconomy? We are now looking for a Business Performance Manager to join Stora Enso Biomaterials in Stockholm or Helsinki. About the Role As Business Performance Manager within our Sales, Marketing & Supply Chain organization, you will play a pivotal role in driving commercial excellence and profitability. This role blends the hands-on analytical depth of a business controller with the strategic problem-solving and advisory mindset of a management consultant. Acting as a trusted business partner to Sales, you will translate complex commercial and financial data into actionable insights. You will provide critical decision support on pricing, customer and product portfolios, and account planning—helping our commercial teams make sharper, data-backed decisions. The position is based in Stockholm or Helsinki, offering a hybrid way of working that combines office collaboration with remote flexibility. You will report to the Head of Marketing & Sales Excellence. Occasional travel may be required. We are looking for a proactive, commercially minded professional who effortlessly navigates complex data while keeping an eye on the bigger strategic picture. You thrive in a dynamic environment, enjoy challenging the status quo, and actively look for ways to elevate commercial processes through data, digitalization, and technology. You are structured, take end-to-end ownership from initial analysis to follow-up, and excel at building strong, trust-based relationships with stakeholders at all levels. Please submit your application through our recruitment tool as soon as possible, as we review applications and conduct interviews on an ongoing basis. For more information about the position or the recruitment process, please contact Recruitment Consultant Jeanette Walters at (jeanette.walters@randstad.se). Responsibilities Drive and develop structured commercial performance management, including defining relevant KPIs and conducting regular performance follow-ups. Build, maintain, and continuously improve Power BI dashboards and analytical models to empower business decision-making. Analyze profitability across customers, products, and markets to pinpoint key performance drivers and recommend concrete improvement actions. Provide analytical leadership and decision support on pricing, customer and product portfolio optimization, and account planning. Own and evolve pricing principles as well as contracting governance. Monitor and evaluate Sales Incentive Programs and sales overdue reporting. Collaborate cross-functionally with Sales, Supply Chain, Controlling, IT, and Legal to streamline and enhance commercial processes and ways of working. Qualifications Master’s degree in Business Administration, Finance, Industrial Engineering, or a related field (or equivalent work experience). Several years of experience in commercial performance management, business/sales controlling, management consulting, or a similar analytical role. Strong grasp of commercial drivers, pricing strategies, profitability analysis, and performance management. Proven track record of developing and maintaining Power BI reports and dashboards, as well as leveraging CRM and ERP systems for data-driven decision-making. Exceptional analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to turn data into clear recommendations and tangible business actions. Strong communication and presentation skills, paired with experience working across diverse functions and stakeholder groups. Fluency in English, both written and spoken. Proficiency in Swedish or Finnish is considered a strong advantage. About the company Stora Enso AB Stora Enso is a global leader in renewable materials with a strong focus on packaging.
