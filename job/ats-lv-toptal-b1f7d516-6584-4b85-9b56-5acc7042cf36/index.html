About Toptal Toptal is a global network of top talent in business, design, and technology that enables companies to scale their teams, on-demand. With $200+ million in annual revenue and team members based around the globe, Toptal is the world’s largest fully remote workforce. We take the best elements of virtual teams and combine them with a support structure that encourages innovation, social interaction, and fun. We see no borders, move at a fast pace, and are never afraid to break the mold. Job Summary: As an Enterprise Sales Executive at Toptal, you will be a key driver of growth within our AI Solutions business. In this role, you will identify, develop, and close opportunities with enterprise clients by helping them solve complex business challenges through forward-deployed AI solutions, AI software and platforms, AI services, and AI-enabled transformation. You will develop tailored solutions that help clients move from AI strategy and experimentation into production, combining Toptal’s capabilities across AI Strategy & Transformation, Agentic Solutions, Data Services, AI Platforms, Industry Solutions, Joint Partner Solutions, and Managed Services to achieve measurable business outcomes. This role is designed for true hunters who thrive in a high-performance, client-facing environment. It is ideal for individuals who have successfully sold enterprise AI solutions and are passionate about building a book of business from the ground up, forging executive relationships, and creating long-term client value. Success in this role is measured by your ability to convert new enterprise relationships into durable revenue and establish Toptal as a core AI services provider embedded in your clients’ most critical initiatives. Enterprise AI has shifted rapidly from experimentation to execution. Companies are investing in models, agents, AI platforms, data infrastructure, and AI-enabled applications, but many are struggling to translate those investments into production systems and measurable business outcomes. The challenge is no longer simply selecting an AI model or platform. Enterprises must identify the right workflows, connect AI to their proprietary data and knowledge, integrate existing systems, build and deploy agents and applications, establish evaluation and control frameworks, drive adoption, and continuously operate and improve AI in production. Toptal brings together world-class AI and domain talent, forward-deployed solution delivery, AI platforms and capabilities, strategic technology partners, expert-in-the-loop operations, and managed services to help enterprises move from AI promise to production. This role exists to convert that market opportunity into new enterprise relationships and position Toptal as a strategic partner for companies deploying AI across their organizations. This is a remote position with significant client-facing fieldwork across the US. Applicants who reside within commutable distance of a major metropolitan area are strongly preferred. We do not offer visa sponsorship or assistance. Resumes and communication must be submitted in English. Responsibilities: *The following information is intended to describe the general nature and level of work being performed. Once you have signed net new customers, you will partner with Client Partners to deepen and expand those strategic relationships over time. Net new accounts may begin with a specific AI initiative, workflow, platform implementation, or business problem requiring a disciplined approach to defining the opportunity and matching the right Toptal capabilities to the client’s needs.
