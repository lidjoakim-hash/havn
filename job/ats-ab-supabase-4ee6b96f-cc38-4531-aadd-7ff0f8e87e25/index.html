ABOUT SUPABASE Supabase is the Postgres development platform, built by developers for developers. We provide a complete backend solution including Database, Auth, Storage, Edge Functions, Realtime, and Vector Search. All services are deeply integrated and designed for growth. What we're looking for We're looking for a Red Team Lead to establish our Red Team and strengthen the security culture at Supabase. As the team lead, you'll build the team and work alongside them to plan and execute realistic attack simulations across the entire tech stack. You'll guide the team to turn findings into practical recommendations that drive meaningful security improvements. This role is ideal for someone who combines technical expertise with thoughtful, people-first leadership. Success in this role means our team brings security at Supabase to new heights. WHAT YOU'LL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR - Develop our Red Team from the ground up. - Lead and mentor a team that values technical depth, curiosity, collaboration, and accountability, where people can challenge ideas and learn. Align their efforts with their career growth and organizational security goals. - Stay technically connected to the planning and execution of operations. Guide the team to make sound judgment calls and communicate operational impact to various audiences. - Define the team's strategic roadmap and operational practices for our environment and threat landscape. Use metrics and impact narratives to communicate the team's success. - Build strategic relationships with relevant teams, including Legal, Engineering, IT, HR, and Finance, for cross-functional influence and amplified security impact. Foster a collaborative relationship with defensive teams. - Advise leadership on strategic growth beyond the Red Team. - Establish Supabase as a thought leader in the security space. Share appropriate research and lessons publicly through writing or talks. YOU MIGHT BE A GOOD FIT IF YOU - Have experience leading adversary emulation teams, especially balancing hands-on work and management as a player-coach. - Have experience with global asynchronous teams and can work independently. - Are familiar with cloud-native offensive operations, including cloud and CI/CD pipelines, and creating and securely deploying command-and-control (C2), reconnaissance tools, and infrastructure for adversary simulations. - Understand attack techniques targeting browsers, cloud infrastructure, databases, SaaS applications, supply chains, Linux and macOS devices, and identity and access systems. - Naturally build rapport, trust, and engagement within Security and across the organization. - Work collaboratively with kindness and respect. - Can clearly communicate complex security concepts to a wide range of audiences, including executive leadership. WHAT WE OFFER - Fully Remote We hire globally. We believe you can do your best work from anywhere.
