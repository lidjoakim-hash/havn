Drive the growth of our autonomous material flow solutions as a high-agency, collaborative, commercially oriented generalist. You will manage complex commercial projects, support partner relationships, and improve and automate our sales operations from lead to contract. What we're looking for Relevant experiences • Proficient in both Norwegian and English (written and spoken). • 2&#43; years of experience contributing to B2B sales, negotiations, or partner management (ideally for engineered/technical solutions). • Proven track record of managing meetings, workshops, and communication with external customers and diverse stakeholders. • Strong generalist mindset with high ownership, comfortable switching between sales, contractual, technical, and project management topics. • Willingness and availability to travel (approx. 5-10%), mainly to customer sites in Europe and partners in the Nordics. Nice to have • Technical understanding of robotics, automation, industrial hardware, or complex software systems. • Hands-on experience with CRM and Project Management systems (e.g., HubSpot, Attio, Notion, Airtable). • Familiarity with contract drafting, procurement, or basic commercial law. • Combined technical and business education (e.g., Industrial Economics) • Project management certification Drive commercial execution & scale our sales machine. As our Sales & Project Manager, you will tackle the commercial processes at OmniMod. Supporting our commercial pipeline, you will guide sales projects from early exploration to contract signing, ensuring clear customer communication, accurate project pricing, and seamless stakeholder alignment. • Commercial Execution Manage the day-to-day sales activities, calculate project pricing, produce customer deliverables, and track CRM deal data to keep our pipeline healthy. • Outreach & Growth Initiate lead generation campaigns, qualify incoming inquiries, and participate at industry trade shows to showcase OmniMod's modular tech. • Partner Enablement Run enablement initiatives to train and support system integrator partners in marketing and selling our products effectively. • Process Automation Streamline operations and automate manual administrative and sales reporting workflows to increase our capacity. • Delivery Coordination Coordinate cross-functionally with technical and operational teams to drive delivery schedules and maintain client alignment.
