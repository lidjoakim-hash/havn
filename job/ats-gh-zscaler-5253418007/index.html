Role We are looking for a Benefits Operations Specialist to join our People & Culture team. This is a remote role based in Ireland, reporting directly to the Senior Manager, People Operations EMEA. As a Benefits Operations Specialist, you will act as a subject matter expert in the day-to-day execution and administration of our EMEA benefit programs. You will serve as the operational backbone of the benefits function, ensuring the employee lifecycle—from onboarding to offboarding—is managed with precision, empathy, and full compliance, with an emphasis on high-touch administration of complex leave cases and the seamless delivery of our benefits concierge service.
