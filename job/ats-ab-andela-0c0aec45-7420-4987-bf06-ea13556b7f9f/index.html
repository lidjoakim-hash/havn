About Andela: At Andela, we know brilliance is evenly distributed around the world, but opportunity is not. For over 10 years, Andela has connected its customers with top global, remote technical talent from over 135 countries with the majority residing in emerging markets like Africa and Latin America. As one of the world’s largest talent marketplaces, Andela gives companies greater flexibility to quickly deploy qualified technologists. With talent highly skilled in advanced technologies to support Application Development, Artificial Intelligence, Cloud & DevOps, Data Engineering, and much more, customers experience 33% faster project delivery. The company’s exclusive AI-powered platform, Andela Talent Cloud, is the industry’s only unified platform managing the complete global talent lifecycle and enables customers to fill individual roles or engage fully managed teams up to 66% faster. Andela is on the precipice of two breakout industry transformations: one in staffing/hiring and the other in software development, both accelerated by generative AI. Are you exceptional and hungry to work in a scaling business through transformation and growth? Join us and change the world. JOB SUMMARY As the Client Marketing Manager, you will own the full scope of programs that deepen relationships with Andela’s clients, amplify the voice of the customer, and surface market intelligence that directly supports commercial growth. You will operate the Client Advisory Board, lead Voice of the Customer and NPS programs, produce a robust bank of case studies and testimonials, and serve as the connective tissue between Andela’s client base and its broader go-to-market strategy. You will operate as a strategic partner to Sales, Customer Success, and senior leadership — ensuring that the client perspective informs everything from product positioning to growth strategy. This role reports to the VP, Global Head of Marketing.
