About the position We are looking for SFO (after-school club) assistants who can contribute to the development of SFO at BISS Gausel and BISS Sentrum. The positions are 0%, to cover sickness and absence of SFO staff. SFO opening hours: Sentrum: Mornings during term-time - 07:15 to 08:45 Monday to Friday (7,5 hours) Afternoons during term-time -14:30 to 16:45 Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday (8 hours), and 14:00 to 16:45 on Wednesdays (2,45 hours) Holiday SFO - eight hours between 07:30 and 16:30, five days a week for 6 weeks (8 hours per day) during the school holiday periods in February, June, August, and October.  SFO is closed in the school holidays at Easter, Christmas and for the month of July. Gausel: Mornings during term-time - 07:15 to 08:45 Monday to Friday (7,5 hours) Afternoons during term-time -14:45 to 16:45 Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday (8 hours), and 13:45 to 16:45 on Wednesdays (2,45 hours) Holiday SFO - eight hours between 07:30 and 16:30, five days a week for 6 weeks (8 hours per day) during the school holiday periods in February, June, August, and October.  SFO is closed in the school holidays at Easter, Christmas and for the month of July. The positions are available immediately. Tasks and responsibilities • Implementing SFO activities as directed by the SFO leader • Contributing to the SFO activity programme • Working alongside SFO team members during the school term and some school holidays • Communicating effectively with the children, the school administration and parents Qualifications and experience • Experience with organising and running activities for children • An ability to take initiative and contribute to the development of SFO • Relevant experience in an educational setting • Excellent collaboration and communication skills • Able to create good relationships with pupils, parents, and colleagues • Good spoken English • Experience working in an international environment would be an advantage Personal qualities  • Passionate about inspiring the love of learning and personal development in students • Able to handle difficult child-centred situations and provide emotional support • Ability to act confidently and set clear boundaries • An energetic and dynamic approach to work • Ability to adapt to a fast-paced environment • Good work ethics - punctual, flexible, loyal • Team player and a role model for the children • Talent for creative work • Willingness to work outside in all kinds of weather  • ​​​​​​Patient, caring, and open minded  More information BISS Schools are committed to safeguarding and promoting the welfare of children and young people and expect all staff and volunteers to share this commitment. All offers of employment are subject to a criminal record check.
