At Tata Technologies, we make product development dreams a reality by designing, engineering, and delivering the products of tomorrow for the world's leading manufacturers. We are currently looking for an experienced Technical Lead to join our Chassis Engineering team, with a primary focus on Steering and Brake Systems. This is an exciting opportunity to lead vehicle-level testing activities, support validation programs, and collaborate with global engineering teams to develop next-generation mobility solutions. About the Assignment As a Technical Lead, you will be responsible for leading and coordinating vehicle-level validation activities for steering and brake systems. You will act as the primary onsite interface for testing activities in Sweden, working closely with customer teams, offshore engineering teams, and development organizations. The role combines hands-on vehicle testing, system validation, test strategy development, defect management, and cross-functional coordination throughout the complete testing lifecycle. Main Responsibilities As a Technical Lead, you will: Lead and drive vehicle-level testing activities for Steering and Brake Systems. Define, plan, and execute testing strategies for new development projects. Coordinate test activities between onsite and offshore engineering teams. Act as the primary point of contact for customer-facing testing activities in Sweden. Perform and support: Vehicle Testing System Testing HIL (Hardware-in-the-Loop) Testing SIL (Software-in-the-Loop) Testing Ensure proper test planning, execution, reporting, and defect tracking. Collaborate with development teams to resolve issues and improve system performance. Coordinate closely with Attribute and Calibration teams. Lead vehicle-level attribute verification and validation activities. Support continuous improvement of testing methodologies and validation processes. Required Qualifications Valid Swedish Category B Driving License. Minimum 8 years of experience in: System Testing Functional Testing Vehicle Testing Strong knowledge of: Steering Systems Brake Systems Vehicle-Level Validation Experience with HIL/SIL test environments, including dSPACE platforms. Proficiency with Vector tools: CANoe CANalyzer Experience with ECU debugging tools such as: INCA Embedded Debuggers Strong experience in end-to-end ECU and software validation activities. Experience with: UDS Diagnostics Automotive communication and validation workflows Technical Competencies Experience with: vTESTstudio dSPACE AutomationDesk Python for test automation and scripting ECU software testing and integration Diagnostic testing and troubleshooting Vehicle-level verification and validation Defect management and issue resolution Meritorious Qualifications T1 and T2 Test Driver Licenses Knowledge of CI/CD environments such as Jenkins. Experience with ISO 26262 Functional Safety Testing. Experience within chassis systems development. Exposure to vehicle dynamics and attribute verification activities. Why Join Tata Technologies?
