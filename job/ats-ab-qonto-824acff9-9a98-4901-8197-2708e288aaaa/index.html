Lead Sales Inbound - New Business - France

Qonto · Barcelona, Spain, Spain

💰 ca. 20 000–24 000 EUR/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)HeltidDirekte fra arbeidsgiverFrist 20.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Join us as Lead Sales Inbound - New Business - France to build, coach and scale the inbound sales team for the Core segment in France. You’ll report to the Head of New Business – Sales and are accountable for the team’s results. You’ll also work closely with Partnerships, Marketing and Growth Ops, and act as the sales acquisition contact for the Western Europe Managing Director. ➡️ WHAT YOU’LL DO - Coach your team: Run structured 1:1s, team meetings and regular feedback to build sales skills and keep activity and quality metrics consistent across the Core France segment. - Build the team: Recruit and onboard new salespeople as the team grows to support growth targets, with hiring validated by the Head of New Business – Sales. You also handle complex or sensitive HR conversations yourself. - Shape the sales process: Define and refine how the team runs discovery, demo and closing for the Core France inbound segment. Adapt established frameworks when segment dynamics or team performance shift. - Manage with data: Use Salesforce reporting to track team metrics, diagnose performance gaps in conversion and pipeline health, and decide what to coach on. - Work with the teams around you: Partner with Product, Onboarding and Sales Operations to improve sales materials and the customer journey. Flag local opportunities and bottlenecks to Partnerships, Marketing and the Western Europe Managing Director, and decide how to act on them. ➡️ WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR - B2B SaaS sales experience: You know the full sales cycle, you’ve sold on value and met or exceeded quota, and you can show others how. - Management, coaching and team development: You have at least a first experience managing people. You’ve coached salespeople, supported new hires and helped build a results-oriented culture through regular, structured feedback. - Salesforce and sales analytics: You’re advanced in Salesforce and comfortable with Outreach and Aircall. You read pipeline and conversion data to find the cause of a gap, then design the fix. - Process discipline: You turn strategy into repeatable processes that a team can execute every day. - Product knowledge: You learn the product, pricing and positioning for the Core France segment in depth and can teach them to your team. ➡️ WHAT WE CAN OFFER YOU - Real ownership of a team: You decide team composition, how the team executes its sales cycles and the performance standards it works to. - Influence beyond your own targets: Your input on lead quality and segment go-to-market helps shape how the region grows, not only whether it hits this quarter’s numbers. - A seat between sales and Growth: You work every day with Partnerships, Marketing and Growth Ops, and with Product, Onboarding and Sales Operations on materials and customer journey. - A direct line to the Western Europe Managing Director: You’re their point of contact for the sales acquisition channel. - Established frameworks to build on: Proven approaches for pipeline management, coaching cadences and conversion optimization, which you adapt to the Core France segment. The stack is Salesforce, Outreach and Aircall. [DIVERSITY] At Qonto, we understand that true diversity isn’t just about ticking boxes on a hiring checklist.
