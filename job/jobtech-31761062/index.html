Prototype Technician

Koenigsegg Automotive AB · Ängelholm, Sweden

We are looking for skilled and motivated Prototype Technicians to join our Prototype Department at Koenigsegg. Our Prototype Department is responsible for building prototypes, test vehicles, and pre-production cars using the latest technologies and designs. The team plays an important role in vehicle development, testing, and preparing new models for production. We are looking for individuals with a broad technical understanding, a hands-on mindset, and a strong willingness to learn and develop. Responsibilities: As a Prototype Technician, you will work hands-on with the assembly, modification, and development of prototype vehicles and components. You will support testing and validation activities, troubleshoot technical issues, and contribute to continuous improvements. Working closely with engineers and other technicians, you will play an important role in bringing new vehicle concepts to life Skills & Requirements: Experience in automotive mechanics, vehicle assembly, prototype development, or a similar technical field. Strong mechanical skills and a broad technical understanding of vehicle systems. Ability to read and interpret engineering drawings and technical documentation. Experience working with hand tools and workshop equipment. Experience with composite materials, vehicle bodywork, machining, or welding is an advantage. A structured and detail-oriented approach with a strong focus on quality and safety. Good problem-solving skills and the ability to work both independently and as part of a team. A willingness to learn, develop, and take on new technical challenges. You don't need to know everything. What matters most is your technical curiosity, hands-on approach, and willingness to learn. We review applications on an ongoing basis, so don't wait to submit yours! Koenigsegg is most known for building some of the fastest cars in the world; with almost all of our megacar technologies that are patented and made in-house by our almost 500 world-class technicians, engineers, sourcing experts, production craftsmen, and communicators. What we’ve created is a unique proving ground of engineering creativity that makes use of our breakthrough megacar development and legacy to push the boundaries of sustainable mobility technology. So, what’s next? We are on a universe-denting mission to develop the next generation of breakthrough tech and products. We are looking for outstanding, impactful, and authentic individuals to grow with us. Our ambitions are inspiring but will push the limits of what is possible – requiring one to work efficiently amid our cross-functional teams to execute at high-quality levels. The goal is clear: innovate and transform.
