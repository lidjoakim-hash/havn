Order Desk Manager

Sandvik Aktiebolag · Svedala, Sweden

💰 ca. 450 000–550 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 1.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Take the lead in developing our growing Order Desk in Svedala. We’re looking for an experienced supply chain and order management professional with a genuine interest in leadership and people development, and the ambition to grow as a leader. Here, you get the opportunity to: Shape processes, ways of working and service levels in a new position. Lead and develop a team with broad expertise across the order flow. Collaborate across a global network of sales areas, production units, suppliers and logistics partners. About your job You lead and develop our Order Desk, taking end-to-end responsibility for the flow from customer order through delivery to invoicing. By creating structure, strengthening performance and ensuring reliable execution, you help us provide excellent customer service and on-time delivery. Job responsibilities: Lead and coach your team to ensure timely and accurate processing of customer orders. Manage the order backlog, allocation and customer communication during supply disruption. Resolve escalated customer issues and coordinate corrective actions. Oversee inbound and outbound logistics, freight partner relationships and correct invoicing. Establish and monitor service levels and performance indicators related to delivery, lead time, customer satisfaction and employee engagement. Set objectives, and collaborate with sales areas, production units as well as regional and global stakeholders. Location and flexibility This position is in Svedala, just outside of Malmö, Sweden, with a hybrid working model. Your profile With several years of experience in order management, customer service or supply chain, you understand how to create and efficient and dependable order flow. You’ve also demonstrated a genuine interest in leadership and people development, ideally combined with previous leadership or team coordination experience. Your background includes A bachelor’s or master’s degree in supply chain, logistics, business, engineering or a related field. Experience working with internation stakeholders. Good knowledge of Microsoft Office. Fluency in English, while knowledge of Swedish or Finnish is a plus. Knowledge of sales and operations planning, inventory control, process improvement, trade compliance or ERP systems is also valued. A strong sense of ownership helps you move work forward and take responsibility for results. Structured and accurate, you remain calm when priorities shift and approach challenges with practical, solution-oriented mindset. Collaboration comes naturally, and you’re motivated by developing both people and processes. The team You lead a team of order coordinators, logistics coordinators and buyers based in Sweden and Finland. Together, the team manages customer orders, delivery coordination, logistics and invoicing within Screening Solutions. Our culture At Sandvik, we’re tech driven, innovative and entrepreneurial. We believe that success is a team effort so we value diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive culture where people can be themselves and reach their full potential. So, we invest in supporting each other, learning together and celebrating our differences.
