Product Designer, Growth Acquisition

Mentimeter · Stockholm, Sweden

Mentimeter is an engagement company with a simple goal: to make every leader better. Through interaction, insights and recommendations, its platform helps organizations turn participation into better understanding and stronger performance. Founded in Stockholm in 2012, Mentimeter is used by 20 million people each month across 1.3 million organizations. At Mentimeter, we believe better leadership starts with listening and that the strongest results are achieved together. That’s why we foster a culture built on curiosity, collaboration and shared ownership. By joining us, you’ll help leaders turn participation into deeper understanding and stronger performance, advancing our ambition to help more than one billion people listen, learn and work better together. The role We are looking for a Senior Product Designer to join our Acquisition team and help us evolve and rethink what a modern web experience can be. This is not a traditional web designer role. Our ambition is to build an agentic web experience: a website that is increasingly intelligent, adaptive and able to help each visitor find what they need. We want to move beyond static pages and traditional funnels towards experiences that respond to intent, context and behaviour. You will help turn that ambition into something real. We are early in this journey. Much of today's work is improving pages, journeys and experiments, and you will help define what the agentic experience becomes. Our website is often the first touchpoint with Mentimeter. You will work at the intersection of brand, product, UX and growth on mentimeter.com, our most visible surface, where millions of people first meet our brand and start their journey with us. A key part of the role is shaping how we introduce the product: making what Mentimeter does easy to understand, relevant and compelling, and creating clear paths from discovery to trying the product. You will create distinctive, on-brand experiences that combine strong visual craft with thoughtful UX and a growth mindset. The goal is to help the right people discover us, understand our value and take the next meaningful step. The team You will be part of a cross-functional acquisition team in our Growth area, bringing together design, engineering, content, SEO, paid acquisition, analytics and experimentation. You will partner closely with Growth Marketing on campaigns, landing pages and the journeys that connect them. The team owns the journey from relevant traffic to registration and qualified inbound demand. The website is our primary surface, but we think beyond individual pages: we look at the complete system connecting discovery, content, user intent, conversion and learning. We work continuously, ship frequently and improve the experience through evidence rather than opinion. You will also work closely with our design system- and content design teams, translating our design language and accessibility standards to the web so our external presence holds the same quality as our product. What you will do • Turn customer insight, behavioural data and business opportunities into hypotheses, then design and launch experiments, evaluate the results and use the learning to improve the wider system • Design thoughtful, high-quality web experiences that make complex ideas feel simple • Think in systems rather than isolated pages, creating reusable patterns, components and journeys, and help shape our design system for the web • Design for different audiences, intents and stages of the customer journey, making the experience more adaptive and personalized over time • Collaborate with content and SEO specialists to create content structures that work for people, search engines and AI discovery • Apply CRO principles to landing pages, navigation, messaging, forms and conversion journeys • Work closely with developers to take ideas from concept to live experience, using AI and agentic workflows to explore, create and ship faster • Balance
