As Manager, Sales and Business Development -- Inertial Sensors and Clocks, you will win new business and grow existing accounts in an assigned international territory. You will build long-term relationships with system integrators and end users and contribute directly to our growth targets. The role focuses on defense and New Space, two of the fastest-growing markets for our tactical-grade gyros, IMUs and clocks. Key Responsibilities: • Win new business with defense and space primes and system integrators across EU, NATO and partner countries. • Own the full sales cycle, from identifying opportunities to negotiating and closing contracts. • Build trusted, long-term relationships with customers' business and engineering teams. • Lead proposals, RFI responses and technical white papers. • Represent Safran Sensing Technologies at customer meetings, trade shows and industry conferences. Qualifications • Bachelor's degree or higher in engineering or another technical field. • Sales experience and knowledge of the defense and space markets. • A proven track record of closing deals across the full sales cycle, from prospecting and qualifying to negotiating and closing. • Excellent written and spoken English. • Strong customer focus, ownership and drive, with the ability to prioritize independently and learn new technologies quickly. • Willingness to travel internationally. What we offer • A key role in a growing technology company, backed by the global strength of Safran. • World-class products with a proven track record in some of the most demanding applications on Earth and in space. • Direct access to leading defense primes and space companies across Europe and NATO countries. • Real influence, with short decision paths and the freedom to shape how you work. • Room to grow, with more responsibility as you succeed. • Competitive pay and flexible working arrangements.
