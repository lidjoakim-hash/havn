Mentimeter is an engagement company with a simple goal: to make every leader better. Through interaction, insights and recommendations, its platform helps organizations turn participation into better understanding and stronger performance. Founded in Stockholm in 2012, Mentimeter is used by 20 million people each month across 1.3 million organizations. At Mentimeter, we believe better leadership starts with listening and that the strongest results are achieved together. That’s why we foster a culture built on curiosity, collaboration and shared ownership. By joining us, you’ll help leaders turn participation into deeper understanding and stronger performance, advancing our ambition to help more than one billion people listen, learn and work better together. Build Mentimeter's enterprise growth engine 40 million users have experienced the value of using a Menti! Building on that product-led foundation, Mentimeter is used daily by more than 84.000 organisations globally across all industries and we now serve more than 3,200 Enterprise customers globally. Our next challenge is to increase a systematic enterprise demand growth engine - and we are looking for a VP Enterprise Marketing to lead that journey. This is a global leadership role responsible for how Mentimeter creates, identifies and converts enterprise demand. You will lead our Enterprise Marketing organisation, report to our Chief Sales Officer and join Mentimeter's Global Enterprise Leadership Team. Your primary focus will be EMEA and North America, while building the foundations for APAC. The goal is to connect our product adoption, organic reach and customer base into an Enterprise qualified pipeline, ARR and customer growth. What you'll lead You will lead three existing teams, each with an experienced leader, across three connected areas: Digital demand Build and optimize enterprise demand generation and capture across web, paid media, account-based programs, content distribution, conversion and nurture. Field and customer marketing Develop a global strategy across events, partner programs, customer advocacy, regional activation and use-case storytelling - measured by account engagement, adoption, pipeline and expansion, not simply attendance. Executive Exchange Build Mentimeter's Executive Exchange into a distinctive platform for strategic customers, including our Enterprise Community, executive networks, education and certification programs.
