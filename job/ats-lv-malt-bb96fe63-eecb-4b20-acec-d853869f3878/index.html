Explore your future career  🔭   As our Bid & Proposal Manager, your mission is to partner with sales and account management teams to deliver high-impact, professional, and winning bids & proposals for complex international requirements.   As a core pillar of our global enablement strategy, you will own the market requirements, coordinate complex B2B deals across global boundaries, and build out a scalable, AI-driven proposal architecture to directly drive Francerevenue growth. Key responsibilities ✨ • End-to-End Bid Management: Lead and manage the end-to-end response process for international tenders (RFPs) from intake to submission, ensuring all technical, legal, and commercial questions are compelling, compliant, and accurately answered. • Win Strategy & Collaboration: Partner with commercial opportunity/contract owners, sales, and account management teams to identify client priorities, define "win themes," and coordinate cross-functional inputs from subject matter experts across the organization. • Deal Scoring & Governance: Structure, implement, and drive the adoption of a formal deal scoring methodology. Prioritize deals with the highest potential value, strategic alignment, and win likelihood, and actively facilitate critical "Bid/No-Bid" discussions to optimize resource allocation. • Global Content Architecture: Establish, curate, and maintain Malt’s international "Single Source of Truth" RFP content library (covering current GTM records, country-specific offers, rules of pursuit, legal, IT security, and data privacy practices). • AI Adoption & Scaling: Drive the usage metrics, optimization, and team-wide adoption of our internal MaltyAI RFP assistant to scale content delivery and maximize response efficiency. • Data-Driven Insights: Track, analyze, and report on RFP/proposal data, win/loss rates, core reasons for outcomes, and competitor movements to continuously improve international success rates. • Content Excellence: Draft, edit, and review clear, professional, and high-impact content that powerfully sells the commercial value of Malt to enterprise clients. About you 🧑‍🚀 • Commercial Acumen: Strong commercial awareness with the ability to connect cross-functionally, facilitate collaboration, encourage creative problem-solving, and confidently navigate conflict across teams. • Relevant experience (8-10 years): Proven track record in bid management, proposal development, or professional writing within a fast-paced complex B2B environment. • Project Management & Detail: Superb project management skills with a proven ability to track milestones, prioritize effectively, and deliver high-quality proposals under tight deadlines with meticulous attention to detail • Knowledge Management: Skilled in constructing, organizing, and maintaining comprehensive standardized content libraries to scale documentation globally. • Native level in French and fluent in English is mandatory. • Writing Excellence: Strong writing, editing, and reviewing skills with high attention to detail.
