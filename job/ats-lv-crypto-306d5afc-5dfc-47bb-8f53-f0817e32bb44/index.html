About the Role The Senior Manager, Global Compliance Strategy and Policies (UK) is a senior member of the central policy team, responsible for translating Crypto.com’s global compliance framework into operational policies, standards, and procedures with primary ownership of UK and EEA regulatory alignment. Working under the VP’s direction, you will draft, maintain, and drive adoption of policy to ensure that UK and EEA regulatory requirements are accurately reflected in the global framework. This is a high-ownership individual contributor / small-team lead role with significant exposure to the CCO, regional compliance heads, and UK/EEA regulators. You will be the central team’s primary point of accountability for UK and EEA-facing policy content. Key Responsibilities Policy Development and Maintenance • Draft, review, and maintain assigned domains of the global policy suite — AML/CFT, sanctions, KYC/CDD, transaction monitoring, travel rule, market abuse, financial promotions, and recordkeeping — ensuring consistency with group drafting standards and tiering. • Lead the UK overlay: own UK-specific annexes and local procedures that implement global policy in line with UK requirements (MLRs 2017, Proceeds of Crime Act 2002, OFSI sanctions regime, FCA Handbook including SYSC, and the UK cryptoasset financial promotions regime). • Conduct gap analyses of existing UK policies against evolving regulatory expectations; propose and execute remediation. • Coordinate policy approval workflows, version control, and periodic review cadence for assigned domains. Regulatory Change Management (UK / EEA) • Run the UK and EEA regulatory change pipeline for the central team: monitor FCA, HM Treasury, OFSI, JMLSG, and where relevant EEA supervisors; assess impact on policy; drive updates through the change process. • Translate UK and EEA rulemaking, FCA policy statements, enforcement actions, and supervisory letters into policy requirements and implementation tasks. • Support horizon scanning input to the VP for UK and EEA-related strategic planning, licensing, and product launches, including FCA cryptoasset registration, UK stablecoin regime, and MiCA adjacency for EEA operations.
