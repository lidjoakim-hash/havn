Since 1975 Klättermusen has been a pioneer in refined Scandinavian Mountaineering Equipment. Our designs combine utility and extreme durability, with consideration for the environment. Rigorously tested in real-world conditions, our equipment reflects Klättermusen’s commitment to creating the best and most sustainable equipment available. We are seeking an experienced Head of Strategic Procurement and Production to lead and develop Klättermusen’s global purchasing and production team. The role translates our product ambitions and growth plans into clear priorities, long-term sourcing strategies and reliable manufacturing capacity across apparel, footwear, equipment and accessories. You will set the framework, own the overall results and ensure operational work is properly resourced, delegated and followed up, enabling delivery on quality, capacity planning, cost and lead-time targets. You'll report to the CEO, located in Åre including frequent traveling to Asia.
