Editor, UK & Ireland

Spotify · London, United Kingdom

💰 ca. 34 000–41 000 GBP/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)HeltidDirekte fra arbeidsgiverFrist 18.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

The Music Mission team owns Spotify’s end to end proposition for music creators and the experiences they create for fans. The team is dedicated to building tools and services to enable creation, promotion, expression, and monetization at scale. Editorial UK & Ireland sits at the heart of the listener experience on Spotify. We reflect culture and drive artist discovery through playlists, storytelling formats, and tools, with a focus on new music. As our Pop Editor, you’ll bring deep cultural expertise and a data-informed approach to shape how millions of people discover music across playlists, video, and other parts of the app. What You'll Do • Spot music and pop culture trends across the UK and Ireland early, and turn them into programming and promotional opportunities for Spotify. • Curate, analyse, and maintain a large portfolio of playlists for a range of audiences, moods, and moments, identifying content gaps across genres. • Develop and produce editorial video series, from formats and scripts to storyboards, and appear on camera to interview artists. • Brief internal and external creative teams with clear direction, and manage feedback through to delivery. • Write titles, descriptions, social posts, and in-app copy with a strong, unbiased editorial voice. • Use data and analytics, including video performance, to understand listener behaviour and shape future content. • Lead cross-functional projects end to end with Artist & Label Partnerships, Marketing, Comms, product teams, and our global editorial team, and present results within the market. Who You Are • You have 5+ years of experience in the music industry, whether programming music for streaming, TV, radio, or other media, or working in music journalism or content. • You have deep knowledge of music and culture, with a focus on pop and pop culture in the UK and Ireland, and a wide understanding of genres and styles. • You have a track record of discovering emerging artists, songs, and trends ahead of the mainstream. • You’re numerate and comfortable analysing and interpreting data, and you use it to set goals. • You communicate ideas clearly, whether in a creative brief or a single line of copy, and you have strong organisational skills. • You’re comfortable working on camera and interviewing artists, or keen to learn. Presenting or video production experience is a bonus. • You know Spotify well as a user and fan, and you understand how it fits into the wider UK and Ireland music ecosystem. • You’re a great teammate who adapts well to change, cares about detail, and is open to new tools. Where You'll Be • You’ll be based in our London office. • We offer you the flexibility to work where you work best. There will be moments that require in-person collaboration, and the team will plan for these intentionally. Spotify is an equal opportunity employer.
