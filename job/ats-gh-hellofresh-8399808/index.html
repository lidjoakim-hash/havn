Work with HelloFresh in Warsaw and its HelloTech organisation, HelloFresh’s global technology backbone with more than 1000 people, building the digital products that power our end-to-end food experience. From meal kits and ready-to-eat meals to specialty offerings like pet food and premium meat & seafood, HelloTech creates the platforms that bring tailored food solutions to millions of customers every month. Our subscription-based, direct-to-consumer model relies on technology at every step, from customer-facing apps and personalization logic to pricing, forecasting, supply chain optimization, and initiatives that help reduce food waste. While our brands operate independently to serve distinct customer needs, they are united by shared platforms, data, and operational excellence built by HelloTech. HelloTech works in autonomous, cross-functional alliances, each owning a specific product or domain end to end. By working with our Warsaw office, you will help shape scalable, data-driven products used across our markets, working with a modern tech stack and international teams to continuously improve how people discover, order, and enjoy HelloFresh’s products, today and in the future. About the role: What's in the Box HelloFresh is moving toward an AI-first model, requiring Staff-level strategists to set the technical direction for that shift across the UX organization. As a Staff UX Strategist & Researcher, the service provider will tackle the most ambiguous, highest-stakes questions across the customer experience. The scope includes determining which problem spaces warrant technical focus, setting research and strategy standards across teams, and turning uncertainty into structured decisions that hold up at the executive level. The role involves stress-testing leadership assumptions before capital investment is committed and leaving the organization better equipped to make evidence-backed decisions. The provider will bring a distinct point of view on how AI transforms research and strategy, utilizing it as a functional partner across the product design lifecycle. Strategy and research form the primary area of deep expertise, supported by enough breadth to define quality standards across interface design, service design, and content. This role does not include people management responsibilities. At HelloTech, flexibility and cross-functional collaboration are core to how we work. While this role is aligned to a specific Alliance, strong candidates may also be considered for opportunities across different teams or projects.
