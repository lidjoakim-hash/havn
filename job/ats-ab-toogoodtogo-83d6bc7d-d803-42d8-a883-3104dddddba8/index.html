At Too Good To Go, we have an ambitious mission: to inspire and empower everyone to fight waste together. More than 1/3 of all food produced in the world is wasted. And that has a huge impact on the health of our planet. 10% of greenhouse gas emissions come from food waste and loss. Through our marketplace app, we connect businesses that have unsold, surplus product, with consumers who can buy and enjoy them at ⅓ of retail value. Too Good To Go was named in FastCompany’s March 2022 list of the World's Most Innovative Companies, and was honoured to be included in TIME’s list of the 100 Most Influential Companies of 2022. We’re looking for an exceptional Senior Enterprise Business Development Manager to join the UK & Ireland team at Too Good To Go. Your Mission As a Senior Enterprise Business Development Manager, your mission is to lead the acquisition of major multi-category retail partners and restaurants across the UK and Ireland through a targeted, consultative, and solutions-oriented approach. You will spearhead Too Good To Go’s strategic expansion into non-food retail verticals while continuing to scale our presence with enterprise grocery and restaurant partners. You will be responsible for designing and executing a repeatable, scalable model for new partnerships, working closely with internal and external stakeholders to accelerate our growth across both markets. Based in the UK, you will operate as a senior individual contributor, leading complex enterprise sales cycles end-to-end. Your Role - Drive enterprise acquisition: Lead the commercial go-to-market and partner acquisition across new non-food verticals (e.g., Health & Personal Care, Vitamins & Nutrition, Household, Petcare, Plants & Flowers), grocery retailers and restaurants. - Build the expansion playbook: Develop a scalable, repeatable enterprise sales model tailored to the UK and Irish markets to unlock new partnerships. - Full-cycle ownership: Own the end-to-end business development lifecycle—from territory planning and C-level prospecting to commercial pitch, pilot design, negotiation, and contract execution. - Executive engagement: Build trusted relationships with senior decision-makers (C-suite, Commercial, Operations, Merchandising, Supply Chain, and Sustainability) in tier-one retail organizations. - Stakeholder navigation: Demonstrate executive presence and influence across complex, matrixed enterprises to align diverse internal buyers and secure multi-level buy-in. - Industry representation: Act as the face of Too Good To Go within the UK retail community, representing our circular retail proposition at industry forums, trade events, and high-level partner meetings. - Strategic negotiation: Lead complex commercial negotiations that protect partner brand equity while driving scalable commercial impact for Too Good To Go. - Cross-functional leadership: Partner closely with Legal, Finance, Product, Operations, and Marketing to build tailored rollouts that meet retailer operational requirements. - Sales excellence: Maintain accurate pipeline forecasting and deal progression leveraging enterprise qualification frameworks (e.g., MEDDIC / MEDDPICC). Requirements - Extensive experience closing complex, high-value enterprise partnerships—ideally within enterprise retail, health & beauty, hospitality, consumer goods, digital marketplaces, or SaaS/technology platforms. - Proven track record leading complex, multi-stakeholder sales cycles with persistence, resilience, and strategic discipline. - Demonstrated executive presence, with the confidence and credibility to present to and negotiate with Board and C-level executives. - Strong commercial and strategic acumen, to identify win-win outcomes and articulate solutions around inventory recovery, operational efficiency, and brand protection. - Skilled in developing robust business cases, financial projections, and tailored commercial proposals to drive partner negotiation
