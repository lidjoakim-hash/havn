About Tailscale Tailscale is making safe connection effortless by delivering software that makes it easy to securely interconnect people and their devices, no matter where they are. From hobbyists to multinational corporations, teams of every size use Tailscale each day to protect their networks, share access to internal tools, and more. We're building a future for the Internet that's easy, sensible, and safe, like it used to be. Founded in 2019 and fully distributed, we're backed by Accel, CRV, Insight, Heavybit, and Uncork Capital. About the Role Tailscale is looking for a Developer Relations Engineer to own and improve the developer experience across Tailscale’s expanding product portfolio. You’ll help developers understand, adopt, extend, and build with our core VPN/connectivity product, while also shaping how that experience evolves as Tailscale expands into new product areas like secure AI access (Aperture), privileged access management (PAM), and whatever comes next. You’re a builder, investigator, and storyteller. You write code, chase technical curiosities all the way down, and turn what you learn into things other developers can use like demos, docs, talks, blog posts, sample apps, workshops, videos, tools, and open source contributions. You build things, break things, explain things, and keep going until the thing you’re working on has a name. You’re comfortable speaking at conferences and just as comfortable digging through an API, a GitHub issue, or a weird networking edge case until it makes sense. You’re probably the kind of person who runs a local AI model in your homelab and can explain exactly why. You ask “why does it work that way?” often, and your instinct is to make the answer clearer, simpler, and better for the next developer. Requirements • Strong software engineering fundamentals, with demonstrated experience building, debugging, and shipping real projects through professional work, open source, self-directed learning, or ambitious personal projects. • Ability to create clear, useful developer-facing materials, including code samples, demos, tutorials, docs, blog posts, videos, talks, and workshops. • Deep curiosity about how systems work, especially across networking, infrastructure, security, identity, cloud, open source, AI, or developer tooling. • A track record of building and explaining technical systems, such as production software, developer tools, infrastructure projects, homelabs, local AI setups, networking experiments, or open source contributions. • Excellent written, verbal, and visual communication skills, with the ability to explain complex technical ideas clearly, creatively, and practically. • Experience presenting to technical audiences, whether through conferences, meetups, livestreams, workshops, podcasts, videos, or internal engineering forums. • Comfortable working in public through GitHub issues, pull requests, community forums, social channels, open source projects, and developer feedback loops. • Strong developer empathy, emotional intelligence, and product sense; you notice friction, understand why it matters, and help translate it into better docs, APIs, examples, workflows, and product experiences. • Ability to work cross-functionally with engineering, product, marketing, support, community, and other technical stakeholders while operating independently and driving high-impact projects from idea to execution. • Comfortable managing multiple workstreams, meeting regular publishing, event, or launch deadlines, discussing technical tradeoffs in detail, and traveling for events, conferences, and community moments. Nice to Have • Experience with Go
