Market Ethics Manager (Sweden) – Nordic Marketing Company - 6 months consultant assignment - AstraZeneca Do you want to help AstraZeneca combine innovation with the highest ethical and regulatory standards? Are you an experienced professional within the pharmaceutical industry who can provide clear guidance, enable compliant creativity and make well-balanced scientific and ethical decisions? Then AstraZeneca might be the place for you! Our AstraZeneca Nordic Marketing Company employs around 550 people and has offices located in Copenhagen, Stockholm, Oslo, and Espoo. AstraZeneca Nordic is a recognized leader in its core therapy areas and aims to improve the lives of millions of people. We drive innovative science, deliver key launches, and build close collaboration and partnerships within healthcare. Introduction to role: We are seeking a Market Ethics Manager to join the our market ethics team. In this role, you will be responsible for the scientific and market ethical review and sign-off of marketing materials, activities and relevant non-promotional initiatves. You will ensure that materials and activities comply with national rules, regulations and practices, as well as AstraZeneca internal standards. By combining strong scientific judgement with a deep understanding of the pharmaceutical ethical code, you will help cross-functional teams develop high-quality, compliant and innovative communications and activities. The role operates within the country and collaborates closely with teams across the Nordic organisation, including Medical and Regulatory, Commercial, Market Access, Communications, Compliance and Legal. Accountabilities: Scientific and Market Ethical Review Perform timely scientific and market ethical review and sign-off of materials, assets and activities in accordance with applicable rules and processes. Ensure that marketing materials, activities and relevant non-promotional initiatives comply with national requirements, local practice and AstraZeneca internal standards. Engage relevant subject-matter experts when needed to ensure high-quality and well-informed sign-off decisions. Apply sound judgement to optimise innovation while interpreting ethical and regulatory requirements. Guidance, Training and Capability Building Provide practical advice, coaching and day-to-day guidance to colleagues on national market ethical rules, practices and AstraZeneca standards. Develop and deliver training for relevant employees to strengthen understanding and consistent application of requirements. Champion AstraZeneca ethical standards locally while supporting creativity and innovation through a deep understanding of applicable regulations. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to develop and implement company interpretations and guidance on challenging or complex compliance and ethics topics. Process, Compliance and Continuous Improvement Stay up to date with external trends, ethical code updates and internal developments related to market ethical rules and practices. Drive the development of market ethical practices relating to material development and activities. Execute efficient and timely review workflows and identify opportunities to improve operational processes. Support the preparation of technical documentation and evidence for complaint responses and appeals. Contribute actively to the development of the function, team effectiveness and continuous improvement. Ensure that all tasks are completed with the correct quality and in accordance with written procedures, current legal requirements and internal standards. Maintain and further develop personal expertise within the market ethics area. Diversity & Inclusion We are proud to have a unique workplace culture that inspires innovation and collaboration. Here, employees are empowered to express diverse perspectives and are made to feel valued, energized and rewarded for their ideas and creativity. Why AstraZeneca?
