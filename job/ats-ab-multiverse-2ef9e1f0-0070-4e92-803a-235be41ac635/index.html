Lead Product Manager

Multiverse · London, United Kingdom

Multiverse is the upskilling platform for AI and Tech adoption. We have partnered with 1,500+ companies to deliver a new kind of learning that's transforming today’s workforce. Our upskilling apprenticeships are designed for people of any age and career stage to build critical AI, data, and tech skills. Our learners have driven $2bn+ ROI for their employers, using the skills they’ve learned to improve productivity and measurable performance. In April 2026, we announced $70 million in strategic funding, led by Schroders Capital, with participation from StepStone Group, Lightspeed Venture Partners and General Catalyst. At an increased valuation of $2.1bn, the round makes us Europe’s first EdTech double unicorn. But we aren’t stopping there. With a strong operational footprint and 800+ employees, we have ambitious plans to continue scaling. We’re building a world where tech skills unlock people’s potential and output. Join Multiverse and power our mission to equip the workforce to win in the AI era. THE OPPORTUNITY We are at an inflection point in our journey. Our platform now serves tens of thousands of learners, demand for AI skills is accelerating, and we are investing seriously in the product and technology that will take us to the next stage of scale. This is a role for someone who wants to build the product that gets more people into transformative learning, and upskill people with critical data and AI skills faster. THE ROLE We are looking for a Lead Product Manager to own Prescribe: the product that helps a prospective learner find the right programme for their skills and their employer's goals, then takes them to a confident commitment and a successful start. You will be the strategic counterpart to a Senior Engineering Manager, working alongside design and data, and closely with our go-to-market and fulfilment teams. Growth at Multiverse means getting more people from first interest to a confident start, and it is a product problem, not a marketing problem. You will own the conversion metrics, set the strategy, run the experiments, and drive the outcomes. AI will drive much of the improvement, and you will decide where it helps. As a Lead, you will work regularly with the most senior leaders across the company. You will report to the Single Threaded Leader for Diagnose & Prescribe and will not manage other product managers. The role is based in London, hybrid, three days a week in the office. WHAT YOU'LL OWN AI-native product experiences - Shape new conversational, AI-first experiences that help learners and customers understand the best programme for their skills and career development. - Decide where AI genuinely improves a match and where it only adds noise, and build the evaluation and guardrails that keep it trustworthy in front of real users. - Turn learner and customer behaviour into product direction. Prescription and eligibility - Own the logic that connects a learner's skills to a programme and to a customer's levy funding, where mistakes carry a real financial and regulatory cost. - Work with Legal, Compliance and Fulfilment to make eligibility and compliance rules configurable and explainable, so business owners can change them without a rebuild. - Make every recommendation something a learner and a customer can understand and challenge. Recommendation, commitment and finalisation - Own the journey from recommendation to a learner committing and starting. - Identify high-leverage drop-off points and own the experimentation roadmap. - Design product interventions that adapt to learner needs and behaviour and shorten the time to a confident start. - Collaborate with GTM, Operations and Data teams to improve attribution and the quality of what enters the funnel. Metrics and experimentation - Own conversion from prospect to committed learner, with retention and programme fit as the guardrails. - Champion a culture of hypothesis-led, structured experimentation.
