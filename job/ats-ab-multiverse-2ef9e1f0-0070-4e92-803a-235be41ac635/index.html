Every team has AI. Few have mastered it. Humans were built to adapt, but AI is now moving faster than most workforces can. Multiverse is the AI adoption layer: the missing link between the AI tools that companies have invested in and the people expected to use them. Our ambition is to put AI capability in every worker’s hands. Apprenticeships got us started: we’ve trained over a third of the UK’s data and AI apprentices, worked with a third of the FTSE 100, and generated £2bn+ in verified ROI. Now Mastry, our new adaptive learning platform, takes that further, guiding a company’s AI workforce transformation from start to finish. Mae, our AI guide inside Mastry, is a real-time AI agent that thinks, speaks, listens and sees, working alongside the tools you already use, like Claude and Copilot. We closed our latest fundraising round in April 2026, with $500m+ raised in total from VC partners including Index Ventures, General Catalyst, Schroders Capital and Lightspeed. With a $2.1bn valuation, we are Europe’s first EdTech double unicorn, and we are just getting started. If you want to shape how the workforce wins in the AI era, come and join us. THE OPPORTUNITY We are at an inflection point in our journey. Our platform now serves tens of thousands of learners, demand for AI skills is accelerating, and we are investing seriously in the product and technology that will take us to the next stage of scale. This is a role for someone who wants to build the product that gets more people into transformative learning, and upskill people with critical data and AI skills faster. THE ROLE We are looking for a Lead Product Manager to own Prescribe: the product that helps a prospective learner find the right programme for their skills and their employer's goals, then takes them to a confident commitment and a successful start. You will be the strategic counterpart to a Senior Engineering Manager, working alongside design and data, and closely with our go-to-market and fulfilment teams. Growth at Multiverse means getting more people from first interest to a confident start, and it is a product problem, not a marketing problem. You will own the conversion metrics, set the strategy, run the experiments, and drive the outcomes. AI will drive much of the improvement, and you will decide where it helps. As a Lead, you will work regularly with the most senior leaders across the company. You will report to the Single Threaded Leader for Diagnose & Prescribe and will not manage other product managers. The role is based in London, hybrid, three days a week in the office. WHAT YOU'LL OWN AI-native product experiences - Shape new conversational, AI-first experiences that help learners and customers understand the best programme for their skills and career development. - Decide where AI genuinely improves a match and where it only adds noise, and build the evaluation and guardrails that keep it trustworthy in front of real users. - Turn learner and customer behaviour into product direction. Prescription and eligibility - Own the logic that connects a learner's skills to a programme and to a customer's levy funding, where mistakes carry a real financial and regulatory cost. - Work with Legal, Compliance and Fulfilment to make eligibility and compliance rules configurable and explainable, so business owners can change them without a rebuild. - Make every recommendation something a learner and a customer can understand and challenge. Recommendation, commitment and finalisation - Own the journey from recommendation to a learner committing and starting. - Identify high-leverage drop-off points and own the experimentation roadmap. - Design product interventions that adapt to learner needs and behaviour and shorten the time to a confident start. - Collaborate with GTM, Operations and Data teams to improve attribution and the quality of what enters the funnel.
