BSH Home Appliances is a global leader in the home appliance industry, committed to creating innovative solutions that enhance the quality of life at home. BSH Northern Europe oversees the Nordic countries as well as Latvia, Lithuania, and Estonia. We take pride in offering top-quality brands, including Bosch, Siemens, NEFF, and Gaggenau. If you are passionate about operational efficiency and eager to contribute your expertise in logistics, join us in shaping the future of home appliances at the BSH-operated warehouse in Nykvarn. In your role as Process Excellence Support, you will be responsible for addressing the bugs, gaps and enhancements of logistics systems and processes for BSH Northern Europe. You will play a vital role in ensuring seamless daily operations and fostering continuous improvements. YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES Your success will be defined by: Driving sustainable system and process solutions that enhance operational efficiency and reduce costs Timely and successful completion of projects within scope and targets You will achieve these by: Provide first line support for system bugs and process gaps Analyze data and carry out direct observations to identify root causes and opportunities Map processes, create flow charts and gap analyses to define improvement scope Lead and execute "system and process changes", "new implementations", and "optimization initiatives" Coordinate and communicate with internal and external stakeholders Identify and initiate AI and automation opportunities YOUR PROFILE Degree in industrial engineering or similar Foundation knowledge in logistics including warehouse and transport processes Analytical ability, agile and proactive mindset with excellent problem‑solving skills SAP-S4 Hana: transport management system - TMS/TM invoicing, last mile control - LMC, warehouse management - WM/EWM, forklift management – ITM, track and trace system – GTT, data tables, interfaces, EDI/API connection, carrier connector Experience leading continuous improvement projects and applying AI/advanced analytics Proficient in office suite (Excel, PowerPoint, Word, Visio, Power BI) Fluent in English, Nordic language is a plus Application For more information about the role, please feel free to contact Sercan Eminoglu, Head of Process Excellence, mailto:sercan.eminoglu@bshg.com. We welcome applications on an ongoing
