We are now recruiting a Director FCA Global Tax Process Owner   FCA is responsible for tax compliance for a majority of Volvo Group’s legal entities. In the global context FCA operates in, it is important to secure strong tax compliance and efficient tax compliance processes that support Volvo Group. Regulatory developments and major finance and technology transformation initiatives, including SAP S/4HANA and MF2.0, create significant opportunities for further process development, automation and standardization across countries and regions.   Therefore, we have established a new global process owner role with the responsibility to drive our tax compliance optimization agenda, coordinate standardization, digitalization, and efficiency of tax compliance processes within FCA.   You will also directly lead a team of 2 global tax sub-process owners for Pillar 2 and Indirect taxes.   In this role you will be working closely together with colleagues in the Group Tax department as well as with a network of colleagues working with tax compliance processes in FCA and other Volvo organizations. This role offers a unique opportunity to shape and drive our global tax process transformation agenda in a complex, fast-changing regulatory and technology environment.
