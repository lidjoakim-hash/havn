Join our customer's innovative AI Programme and drive the future of Regulatory Reporting. This role offers the unique opportunity to implement cutting-edge GenAI and Snowflake solutions within a leading financial environment. About the role As an AI & Regulatory Reporting Consultant, you will bridge the gap between regulatory expertise and AI engineering. You will be hands-on, rapidly prototyping solutions and driving them toward production readiness to deliver measurable value to the organization. You are offered Opportunity to work on high-impact AI initiatives, utilize modern data platforms like Snowflake, and shape the future of intelligent regulatory reporting in a collaborative, forward-thinking environment. Work tasks In this role, you will accelerate the implementation of the AI programme, focusing on automated testing, control intelligence, and reporting analytics using GenAI and Snowflake. You will work closely with the AI Programme Lead, AI Champions, Regulatory Reporting SMEs and technology teams across three priority areas: 1) AI-enabled UAT and testing, including automated validation, reconciliation, and exception analysis. 2) Control Intelligence, supporting development of intelligent data-quality and control capabilities leveraging technologies available across the organization. 3) Reporting analytics and insights, including period-on-period analysis, anomaly identification, root-cause analysis and "talk to your data" capabilities. Work tasks: Translate business requirements into scalable, governed, and production-ready technical solutions. Develop AI-enabled UAT and testing solutions, including automated validation and reconciliation. Build Control Intelligence capabilities leveraging modern data technologies. Create reporting analytics and insights such as anomaly identification and 'talk to your data' features. Rapidly prototype AI solutions and mature them into sustainable, maintainable systems. Collaborate closely with AI Champions, SMEs, and technology teams across the organization. We are looking for At least 4 years of experience as a Data Analyst/AI Engineer, with the following skills and experience: Strong experience with GenAI/AI technologies. Strong experience in Snowflake, SQL and data analytics. Hands-on experience developing AI agents, automated controls or intelligent analytics. Strong focus on governance, traceability, maintainability, and production readiness. Demonstrated ability to translate business requirements into working technical solutions. Comfortable working across business, data and technology teams. Fluent in English, both written and spoken. Swedish citizenship, citizenship of a Nordic or EU/EEA country, or alternatively a valid work permit in Sweden. This is required as the position starts in January 2027. It is meritorious if you have Prior experience within Banking, Finance, or Regulatory Reporting. Relevant Azure, Snowflake, or AI certifications. To succeed in the role, your personal skills are: Trustful Goal oriented Stable Responsible Intellectually curious Our recruitment process This recruitment process is handled by Academic Work and it is our client’s wish that all questions regarding the position is directed to Academic Work.
