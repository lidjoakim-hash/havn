Key Responsibilities Define and establish technical architecture and common software development patterns. Develop and maintain reusable infrastructure components and development frameworks. Lead DevSecOps activities and promote secure software development practices. Participate in hands-on system development, implementation and maintenance. Required Skills & Experience Competence Level 3, typically 4–8 years of relevant professional experience. Understanding of technical security frameworks, internal governance models and regulatory requirements. Experience managing structured technical changes and securing business alignment.
