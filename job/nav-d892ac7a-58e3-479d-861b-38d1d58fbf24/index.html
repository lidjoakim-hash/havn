On behalf of our customer, we are seeking Project QHSE Lead for immediate start!   The Project QHSE Lead is responsible for ensuring effective implementation of Quality, Health, Safety and Environmental management requirements throughout project execution. The role shall ensure compliance with contractual requirements, applicable standards, regulatory requirements, and the One Management System while driving a proactive quality and safety culture across project teams, suppliers and stakeholders.   Tasks/responsibilities: • Promote awareness and implementation of the One Management System and project QHSE requirements across the project team. • Drive a strong QHSE culture by integrating QHSE considerations into project activities, meetings, and decision-making processes. • Develop, implement, maintain, and communicate project QHSE plans and documentation in accordance with contractual, project-specific, and company requirements. • Coordinate and monitor quality inspection activities, third-party inspections, supplier ITP, and related reporting. • Coordinate and monitor Mechanical Completion readiness activities, including scope preparation, status reporting, and punch list management, ensuring project requirements are met prior to handover. • Facilitate audits, reviews, ensuring timely closure of findings and corrective actions. • Manage non-conformances, deviations, and concessions, ensuring effective resolution and close-out. • Prepare and deliver project QHSE performance reports to internal and external stakeholders. • Support, coach, and train project personnel in QHSE processes, tools, and systems Education and Experience: • Bachelor's degree in engineering, Quality Management, Health, Safety & Environment (HSE), • Industrial Management, or related technical discipline • Minimum 5–8 years of QHSE experience within Oil & Gas, Subsea, Offshore, EPC/EPCI or manufacturing industries • Experience supporting project execution, supplier quality, inspections, audits, non-conformance management and mechanical completion activities • Working knowledge of ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 management systems • Extensive experience in a QHSE position can compensate the lack of education You are: • Strong safety and quality leadership mindset • Good communication and stakeholder management skills • Team player with ability to influence multidisciplinary teams • Structured, proactive and results-oriented • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills • Ability to work independently and manage multiple priorities • Proficiency in Microsoft 365 and digital quality management systems • Fluent in English, both written and verbal Folk can offer: • Good long-term opportunities with our clients • Individual and adapted follow-up while on assignment • Good career development opportunities in an interesting and innovative sector • Competitive terms and conditions Social events and pleasant tokens of appreciation throughout the year We see possibilities in your competence! In Folk, we work in accordance with our values: ethical, personal, enthusiastic and flexible.
