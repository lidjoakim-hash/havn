Join us in making a difference The trivago B2B Independent Hotels team has an exciting opportunity for an intern who wants to build real digital marketing skills while learning how the business side of the metasearch hotel industry works. What you'll need to thrive: • Studying Marketing, Digital Business, Media Informatics, Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related field. • You write code confidently — solid HTML and CSS, and enough JavaScript to debug your way out of trouble — and you communicate confidently in English (our company language). • A genuine learning mindset — you approach challenges with curiosity, seek out feedback, and continuously look for ways to develop and grow. • A performance mindset — you set ambitious goals for yourself and have the determination and resilience to follow through and achieve them. • An open and curious mindset toward AI tools and automation — you actively explore how tools like Claude and Cursor can enhance your work, and you’re excited to grow alongside evolving technology. • A valid work permit authorizing employment in Germany (required for all internship applicants).
