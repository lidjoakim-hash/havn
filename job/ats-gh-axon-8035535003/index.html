Your Impact We are seeking an International Accounting Senior Manager to join our growing international finance team and take ownership of the accuracy, timeliness and control of financial reporting across Axon's 20+ international entities. This is a senior individual contributor role with no direct reports. You will lead through technical expertise, judgement and influence, directing the work of outsourced statutory preparers and partnering with colleagues in corporate (US) accounting, tax, treasury, revenue and internal audit. You will own the international period-end close and the overall plan for local audits and statutory filings, act as a technical authority on IFRS and US GAAP, serve as the lead contact for local auditors and filings, lead entity set-ups, and drive the improvement and automation of international accounting processes. The role suits an experienced, fully qualified accountant who is comfortable making and defending accounting judgements, working across time zones, and raising the standard of the team around them.
