We are seeking a Control System Engineer with immediate start!   As a Control Systems Engineer, you will play a key role in the delivery of control systems for subsea processing projects.   You will act as a technical focal point within project teams, coordinating interfaces between customers, suppliers, and internal disciplines. The role combines technical responsibility, stakeholder management, and project execution, giving you ownership of control system scope throughout the project lifecycle.   You will contribute to equipment deliveries, technical specifications, documentation reviews, supplier follow-up, system integration activities, and testing. The position offers significant exposure to multidisciplinary EPC projects where collaboration and communication are critical to success.   Tasks/Responsibilites: • Lead and support control system engineering activities in studies and delivery projects • Coordinate technical interfaces between clients, suppliers, and internal engineering disciplines • Prepare, review, and maintain technical specifications and documentation • Participate in and lead technical meetings with customers and suppliers • Support equipment deliveries and ensure compliance with project requirements • Collaborate with Supply Chain on procurement activities and supplier follow-up • Participate in FAT, EFAT, and system integration testing activities • Support testing and delivery activities at facilites when required • Ensure compliance with national regulations, international standards, project requirements, and certifying authorities • Contribute to safe and successful project execution with a strong focus on HSE • Support global project teams and stakeholders across multiple locations • Travel occasionally within Norway and internationally Qualifications: • BSc or MSc in Electronics, Automation, Electrical Engineering, Cybernetics, or a related discipline • Approximately 7&#43; years of relevant experience within automation, control systems, or subsea engineering • Good understanding of PLC-based control systems • Knowledge of industrial communication protocols • Experience with electrohydraulic control systems • Experience reviewing technical documentation and engineering deliverables • Professional English skills, written and verbal Personal skills: You are a proactive and solution-oriented engineer who enjoys working in complex, multidisciplinary project environments. Combining strong technical competence with excellent communication skills, you are comfortable engaging with customers, suppliers, and cross-functional teams to drive projects forward.   You take ownership of your scope, build trusted relationships with stakeholders, and contribute to successful project execution through collaboration, coordination, and technical expertise. Whether reviewing technical documentation, managing interfaces, supporting testing activities, or solving engineering challenges, you bring structure, accountability, and a practical mindset to your work.   Duration: ASAP - 30.06.2027, with the possibility of an extension Location: Bergen.
