ABOUT SUPABASE Supabase is the Postgres development platform, built by developers for developers. We provide a complete backend solution including Database, Auth, Storage, Edge Functions, Realtime, and Vector Search. All services are deeply integrated and designed for growth. ABOUT THE ROLE We’re hiring an Account Executive to ASEAN (singapore, malaysia, SEA) across our commercial and enterprise segments. You’ll sell to founders, engineering leaders, and technical buyers. We’re after someone who has done this before, enjoys the technical depth of the work, and wants to help define how Supabase sells in the region. WHAT YOU’LL DO - Own the full sales cycle from first conversation to close, including multi threaded enterprise deals - Run technical discovery and tie it to business outcomes the buyer cares about - Work with Sales Engineers and Product on demos, proofs of concept, and architecture reviews - Help customers see where Supabase fits in their stack and what it replaces - Build and run your own territory plan, including pipeline, forecasting, and account strategy - Work with AWS and regional partners and resellers to open and progress deals - Handle data residency, security, and procurement conversations with enterprise and public sector buyers - Hand off to Customer Success so customers onboard well and expand WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR - 5+ years in a technical sales role, ideally selling developer tools, databases, cloud infrastructure, or open source - A record of consistently beating quota, with numbers you can walk us through - Experience closing both fast moving commercial deals and longer enterprise cycles with procurement, security, and legal involved - Enough technical fluency to hold a credible conversation with an engineer without bringing a Sales Engineer to every call - Comfort selling to founders, CTOs, and platform teams - Strong judgment about where to spend your time across a territory spanning multiple countries and several time zones - Self-directed, at ease with ambiguity, and happy to build the playbook as you go NICE TO HAVE - Experience with Postgres, or a competing backend or database platform - An existing network across the ASEAN startup, scale-up, and developer ecosystem - Experience with cloud marketplace or co-sell motions, particularly AWS - Familiarity with ASEAN compliance expectations, such as Singapore PDPA, Malaysia PDPA, or financial services and government requirements WHY SUPABASE You’ll sell a product developers already love, with real inbound demand and a fast growing open source community behind it. The territory is yours, the ownership is real, and the team is small enough that your ideas get used rather than filed. WHAT WE OFFER - Fully Remote We hire globally. We believe you can do your best work from anywhere. There are no Supabase offices, but we provide a WeWork membership or co-working allowance you can use anywhere in the world. - ESOP Every team member receives ESOP (equity ownership) in the company. We want everyone to share in the upside of what we’re building together. - Tech Allowance Use this budget to set up your ideal work environment—laptop, monitor, headphones, or whatever helps you do your best work. - Health Benefits Supabase covers 100% of health insurance for employees and 80% for dependents, wherever you are. Your wellbeing and your family’s health are important to us. - Annual Off-Sites Once a year, the entire company gathers in a new city for a week of connection, collaboration, and fun. It’s a highlight of our year. - Flexible Work We operate asynchronously and trust you to manage your own time. You know what needs to be done and when. - Professional Development Every team member receives an annual education allowance to spend on learning—courses, books, conferences, or anything that supports your growth.
