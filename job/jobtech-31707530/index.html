Welding Engineer

ESAB Aktiebolag · Göteborg, Sweden

💰 ca. 510 000–620 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 20.11.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

We are looking for a Welding Engineer to join our Applications Engineering team within R&D Filler Metals. This position offering the opportunity to combine welding engineering expertise, hands-on technical work and project management. You will support internal and external customers by investigating technical challenges, coordinating welding trials and testing, and developing reliable and cost-effective solutions. You will work closely with R&D development engineers, product managers, welders and laboratory specialists, making this a highly collaborative and varied role. Lead and manage Applications Engineering projects from initial request through to final reporting. Provide technical welding expertise and support to internal and external customers. Plan and coordinate welding trials, testing and technical investigations. Work closely with chemical, metallographic, mechanical and non-destructive testing laboratories. Analyze test results and develop conclusions and recommendations. Develop welding processes, procedures and recommendations for customer applications. Support the application of ESAB filler metals in different customer applications. Perform competitor comparisons and evaluate new and existing product combinations. Read, interpret and apply relevant welding standards, regulations and test methods. Prepare technical reports and document project findings and recommendations. Collaborate with R&D and other internal stakeholders to deliver effective solutions. When required, support activities directly at customer sites. Degree in Welding Engineering, Materials Engineering or a related technical field. Minimum BSc; MSc or PhD is an advantage. Several years of relevant experience in welding technology and welding applications. Proven practical experience with welding processes, equipment, materials and process parameters. Proven experience in project management or a role involving significant ownership and coordination. Deep knowledge of welding standards and test methods is required. IWE qualification is required Fluent English, both written and spoken, is required. Swedish is highly desirable but not mandatory.
