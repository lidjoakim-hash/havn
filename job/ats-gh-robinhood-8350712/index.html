Bitstamp Europe S.A. is Robinhood’s EU-regulated crypto exchange and the company’s primary licensing hub for European operations. Under Robinhood, Bitstamp Europe is the cornerstone of the company’s ambition to become Europe’s leading regulated crypto platform - serving institutional, retail and B2B clients through spot, perpetual futures, and crypto-as-a-service offerings. As President, Bitstamp Europe, you will serve as the general manager and senior-most leader of Bitstamp Europe S.A., with full P&L accountability for Robinhood’s European crypto business. You will be the company’s most senior face to European regulators - primarily the CSSF in Luxembourg, with whom you are expected to qualify as an Authorized Manager and to institutional partners, counterparties, and key stakeholders across the region. This is a high-visibility, externally facing leadership role requiring equal mastery of financial regulation, business strategy, and team leadership. This role is based in our Luxembourg office, with in-person attendance expected 5 days per week. At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.
