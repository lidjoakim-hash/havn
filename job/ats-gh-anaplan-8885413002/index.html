About the Role Anaplan has an opportunity for a self-motivated Service Manager to join our cross-functional Platform Engineering group. In this role, you will support the systems used by hundreds of software engineers and thousands of customers. Working within the Platform Service Management (PSM) team, you will collaborate closely with colleagues across engineering, security, and operations to streamline delivery, optimize platform stability, and foster operational excellence. We have unparalleled access to our users; they are our colleagues! Your Impact As a Service Manager, you will drive operational excellence by commanding the end-to-end incident management lifecycle.
