ABOUT WATERSHED Watershed https://watershed.com/ is the enterprise sustainability platform. Companies like Airbnb, Carlyle Group, FedEx, Visa, and Dr. Martens use Watershed to manage climate and ESG data, produce audit-ready metrics for voluntary and regulatory reporting including CSRD, and drive real decarbonization. We are looking for team members who love product-building, want to work hard at a mission-oriented startup, and will collaborate with us in shaping the culture of a growing team. We have offices in San Francisco, New York, Denver, London, Paris, Berlin, Sydney, Mexico City, and remote team members across the US and Europe. We hope that you'll be interested in joining us! THE ROLE Watershed is on a mission to help companies measure, reduce, and report their emissions. Our go-to-market organization continues to grow, with an emphasis on scale and deepening our presence in EMEA. We're looking for a hands-on operator to run sales operations for the region and build the tools & processes our sellers rely on. You'll partner closely with EMEA sales leadership to drive the day-to-day operating rhythm of the team - forecasting, pipeline reviews, and planning - and dig into the data to identify risks and opportunities for growth. This is a role for an operator who builds what they design and has already rewired their work around AI. Alongside the operating rhythm, your project work will be building AI agents, tools, and workflows that help our AEs, SDRs, and Solutions teams move our revenue metrics. This position is based in our London office 4 days per week. You will: - Partner closely with EMEA sales leaders to shape and execute our growth strategy in the region - Support headcount, territory, and quota planning for the region, including account ownership and territory governance - Own the EMEA forecasting process: run regular forecast and pipeline reviews, and inspect deals to improve predictability - Define and track the metrics that show how our revenue teams are performing, and turn them into clear recommendations for leadership - Be the go-to resource for our London office on Salesforce and our revenue tech stack - Build AI-first workflows and internal tools, with tools like Claude Code, that change how our sales teams work day to day You have: - 6+ years of work experience, including 3+ years in Sales Operations, Revenue Operations, or GTM Operations at a high-growth B2B technology company - Direct experience supporting sales teams: you've run a forecast cadence, territory or quota planning, and the reporting behind a revenue number - Strong analytical skills, with hands-on experience building models and interpreting sales metrics - Hands-on experience with Salesforce: you build your own reports and dashboards. Bonus if you've owned administration, automation, or data model design - AI is already embedded in how you work. You've built AI workflows that other people use, not just prototypes - Strong communication skills: you turn data into clear recommendations for sales leadership and build trust across teams Must be willing to work from an office 4 days per week (except for remote roles) Watershed has hub offices in San Francisco, New York, London, and Mexico City and satellite offices in Denver, Sydney, Paris, and Berlin. Where we have offices, employees are expected to be in office for 4 days per week.
