Industrial Engineer Södertälje

Soros Consulting AB · Södertälje, Sweden

💰 ca. 510 000–620 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 15.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

We are looking for an Industrial Engineer for an assignment supporting product introductions within Final Assembly at a global manufacturing company. Om uppdraget In this role you represent Final Assembly in product development projects, working at the intersection of Product Development, Manufacturing, Suppliers and global production units, with a focus on assembly processes, production readiness and continuous improvement. You help develop and improve assembly processes and working methods, define requirements for new assembly methods and production equipment, optimise global assembly sequences, and support the introduction of new products into manufacturing environments while ensuring that production targets for safety, ergonomics, quality and productivity are met. Obligatoriska krav Samtliga krav behöver kunna styrkas tydligt med beskrivningar och hänvisningar till uppdrag i CV. University degree in Engineering, Industrial Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering or equivalent. Experience from engineering, manufacturing, production, process development or product development environments. Experience working with cross-functional stakeholders. Understanding of production processes and manufacturing operations. Knowledge of manufacturing and assembly processes. Understanding of process development and continuous improvement. Ability to work with technical requirements and production methods. Professional working proficiency in English and Swedish. Meriterande Master's degree or Civil Engineering degree. Specialisation in Production Engineering, Manufacturing Processes or Industrial Operations. Several years of experience in Production Engineering. Experience with product introductions, NPI or NPD. Experience from automotive, truck, heavy vehicle or industrial manufacturing. Experience working in global or international organisations. Experience with assembly process development and industrialisation projects. Experience with CATIA or ENOVIA. Knowledge of Lean Manufacturing tools. Experience with production system development. Experience with ergonomics and safety assessments. Experience with production equipment development. Knowledge of industrialisation methodologies. Personliga egenskaper You are communicative, collaborative and solution-oriented with a strong analytical mindset, take initiative, adapt well to changing conditions, and enjoy working with multiple stakeholders in a cross-functional, international environment.
