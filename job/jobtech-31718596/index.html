Who we're looking for • Part-time job seekers, ideally students with a genuine interest in childcare • Candidates who can dedicate at least one afternoon per week • Responsible and creative individuals with strong social skills • Candidates looking for a long-term commitment of at least 6 months • Native or fluent German speakers with good English or Swedish skills About the Position • The family lives in central Stockholm • One child 4 years old • Help is needed 2–3 afternoons per week with pickups and babysitting • Working hours: approximately 15:00–18:00 or 16:00–19:00 • Flexibility to choose the days that work best for your schedule • If this specific family does not match your availability, you can still apply, and we will try to find another suitable family for you What We Offer • A flexible part-time role, approximately 3–10 hours per week, with the possibility of working with multiple families • Competitive starting salary • Comprehensive written references upon completion of your employment • Close contact and ongoing support from our central office • A friendly community of fellow babysitters Commitment We prioritize building long-term relationships and are looking for candidates who can commit for 6–12 months. Apply Today! If you think you would be a good fit for this role, apply in either English or Swedish. We look forward to hearing from you!
