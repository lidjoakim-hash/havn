The Role Are you an experienced operations leader who thrives in a hands-on environment and enjoys being close to the operation and the team? As Senior Operations Manager you will be responsible for the safe, reliable, and efficient execution of Yubico’s day-to-day operations at our Stockholm site, covering production, programming, laser marking, product customisation, inventory management, warehouse operations, and order fulfilment and dispatch. This is a hands-on operational leadership role. You will set priorities, solve problems, develop people, and ensure the operation consistently delivers against customer and business commitments. You will have overall responsibility for operational performance across safety, quality, service, inventory accuracy, productivity, cost, and people development, while building the processes and capabilities required to support Yubico’s continued growth. You will lead a team of approximately 9 people today, with expected growth to around 12, and will work closely with colleagues across Supply Chain and the wider organisation. This is a full-time, on-site role based in Stockholm, five days per week. Success in the role means that the operation delivers reliably every day while continuously improving. Customer orders leave on time and at the expected quality, inventory is accurate, risks and problems are addressed early, and the team understands its priorities and takes ownership of performance.
