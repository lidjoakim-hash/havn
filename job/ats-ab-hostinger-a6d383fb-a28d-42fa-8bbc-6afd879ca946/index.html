YOUR ROLE AT HOSTINGER Content Team Lead (for tutorials) in the Content team. You'll own how Hostinger's tutorials get made - at speed, at quality, and at scale. From running the end-to-end production pipeline and setting the quality bar to developing a team of writers and editors, you'll be the person who turns SEO's planned topics into published tutorials that help millions of people succeed online. You'll work closely with the SEO team and report to the Head of Content. Curious to learn more?
