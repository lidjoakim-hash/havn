Remote- An overview of this role As a Professional Services Engineer at GitLab, you’ll work with customers to deliver installation, migration, training, and advisory services that help them adopt GitLab successfully. You’ll contribute to deployments ranging from single-node Omnibus installations to reference architectures built with infrastructure as code (IaC) and configuration as code (CaC), using established approaches and getting guidance on more complex designs. You’ll support migrations from other systems to GitLab SaaS or self-managed deployments, and help customers make practical decisions that improve reliability, security, and day-to-day workflows. You’ll work closely with customers and GitLab’s Product and Engineering teams to resolve delivery questions and share feedback that can improve GitLab and our services. You’ll own assigned work, communicate progress clearly, and collaborate with Engagement Managers, Project Managers, and other engineers to deliver customer outcomes. Some examples of our projects • Supporting GitLab installations and reference architecture deployments using tools like Terraform and Ansible across major cloud providers.. • Contributing to migrations to GitLab SaaS or self-managed GitLab, and sharing customer feedback with GitLab’s Product and Engineering teams..
