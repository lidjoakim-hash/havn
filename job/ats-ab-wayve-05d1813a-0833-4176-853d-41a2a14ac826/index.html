PRODUCT MARKETING MANAGER 📣 About our Marketing Team As a Product Marketing Manager at Wayve, you’ll lead the strategic positioning of our Embodied AI software products for automotive OEMs. You’ll develop and execute a global product marketing strategy that drives demand, awareness and positive perception of our automotive products while establishing Wayve as a leading AI developer for assisted and automated driving. You’ll work across Product & Strategy, Technical Business Development, Automotive and Marketing to develop compelling narratives and materials that communicate Wayve’s unique value proposition to automotive OEMs, Tier 1 suppliers, and SoC and systems architects. 🧠 Your day-to-day You’ll own product positioning, messaging and value propositions for Wayve’s Embodied AI products, translating complex AI and autonomous-driving technology into clear, differentiated and commercially relevant narratives. You’ll develop integrated marketing campaigns, content and sales enablement that build awareness and trust in Wayve’s technology and support pipeline growth. You’ll also monitor the competitive landscape and work closely with technical and commercial teams to ensure our positioning reflects both the capabilities of our products and the needs of the automotive market. This is a role for a strategic thinker and strong communicator who can connect technical innovation with customer needs and help elevate Wayve’s market presence as the automotive industry moves toward increasingly advanced driving automation. 🧩 What you’ll be working on: - Developing and implementing a global product marketing strategy that positions Wayve as a leading developer of Automotive AI and our Embodied AI software as a key enabler for OEMs transitioning to advanced assisted and automated driving. -
