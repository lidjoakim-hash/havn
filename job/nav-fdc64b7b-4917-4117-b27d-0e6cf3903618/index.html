Make an impact with us at Kongsberg Maritime.  We're seeking talented individuals to help us shape the maritime future. If you're ready to be part of this exciting journey, your adventure begins here! As an Electrical Engineer – Energy Storage Systems, you will play an important role in our journey towards greener shipping. The position requires a strong understanding of battery systems and how they interact with the vessel’s electrical system, from battery design and electrical integration to safe and reliable operation. No matter if you're just starting out or have a few years of experience, we're here to help you grow. We are all about hands-on challenges and support to help you become the best Electrical Engineer you can be, right from the start of your career. We're all about fostering a creative, collaborative vibe where everyone can bring their ideas to the table. What you will be doing • Development and engineering of energy storage systems with focus on electrical design • Preparation of product documentation according to regulations • Participate in verification activities, troubleshooting and lifecycle support on Energy Storage systems • Perform electrical verification and validation of battery systems, including short-circuit behaviour, grounding/insulation concepts, and operating voltage ranges • Technical support towards sales and aftermarket We think you should have • BSc or MSc in Power System Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Marine Engineering, or a related field • Relevant practical experience may compensate for formal education • Experience with electrical power systems, battery systems or energy storage system integration • Understanding of electrical protection, short-circuit calculations, grounding/insulation concepts and high-voltage systems • Knowledge of relevant industry standards, classification rules and flag states is an advantage • Fluent written and oral communication skills in English This employment may require a pre-employment check procedure in accordance with company security standards, role or site requirements. Please note that this might affect the offer process or employment. At Kongsberg Maritime, we believe that diversity fuels innovation. We encourage you to apply, even if you do not meet every requirement.  Your unique perspective is valuable to us. Your skills, attitude and perspective could be exactly what we are looking for! You are able to • act on own initiative, make things happen and accepts responsibility for the results • adhere to company rules and procedures and execute plans with commitment and determination • use a methodical and systematic approach, plan ahead, define clear priorities and allocates resources effectively • demonstrate specialist knowledge and expertise in own area We are continuously reviewing candidates, apply today! Why join us • An exciting and important position working for the world-leading supplier of maritime solutions • Be part of a diverse team spread across several countries, embracing collaboration and cultural diversity. • We offer competitive pay based on objective criteria to ensure fairness and internal equity along with pension contributions, health- and employee insurance. • Take part in the green shift for the ocean space and work with sustainable and exciting solutions for the future.   Where you will be working: Bergen, Norway or any other KM Location Interested in knowing more?
