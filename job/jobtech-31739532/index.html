Procurement Finance Director, Global Supply

Valmet Aktiebolag · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 670 000–810 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 17.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Are you ready to take on a role where your financial expertise directly influences global procurement strategy? Join us as a Procurement Finance Director and become a key business partner to our procurement organization. In this newly established position, you will help build the financial foundation for procurement excellence, strengthening data-driven decision making, and improving cost competitiveness across a global organization. You will work closely with senior stakeholders, contribute to strategic initiatives, and play a vital role in shaping how procurement performance is measured and developed in the years ahead. Your responsibilities You will partner with the global procurement leadership team and provide financial guidance to support strategic and operational decision-making. You will develop, monitor, and improve procurement performance measurements and KPIs, helping the organization turn insights into actions. You will lead forecasting and budgeting activities while preparing and enhancing monthly reporting related to procurement costs, purchase volumes, purchase price variance, capacity costs, and DPO. You will validate and support procurement savings initiatives, ensuring financial transparency and a strong link between strategy and execution. You will collaborate with procurement, production, business area finance teams, and GS Finance colleagues to develop data platforms and increase visibility into procurement performance across the business. Expectations for you You hold a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Finance, Business Administration, Industrial Management, or a related field. You bring 8-12 years of experience within financial management, supply chain controlling, operations controlling, procurement controlling, or related finance leadership roles. You have a strong understanding of accounting principles, excellent analytical capabilities, and the ability to translate analysis into business actions. You have experience working with reporting and analytics tools. Knowledge of Power BI, Microsoft 365 applications, and advanced Excel skills are highly valued. You have advanced English proficiency and enjoy collaborating with stakeholders across a global environment. To succeed in this role Success in this position comes from combining strong procurement finance expertise with a business partnering mindset. You are comfortable analyzing complex business cases, developing KPI-driven performance management, and identifying opportunities for improvement through data and insights. You thrive in dynamic environments, show curiosity when facing uncertainty, and enjoy building new ways of working while supporting strategic transformation initiatives. We also expect you to align with Valmet’s Leadership Principles, practical behaviours that guide how we lead, collaborate, and succeed together: Take accountability – Act with ownership, set clear expectations, and follow through Lead with courage – Champion change, try bold ideas, and learn quickly Build on trust – Listen to understand, be open, and create psychological safety Collaborate for growth – Work across boundaries, value diverse perspectives, and focus on customer value We offer This is a unique opportunity to join a growing global finance organization to support the future development of procurement finance capabilities. You will become part of the Global Supply Finance organization and work closely with procurement leadership, production organizations, local controllers, and business controllers across the company. The team is focused on strengthening procurement performance, driving savings initiatives, improving reporting, and building greater transparency through data and analytics. The role offers broad global exposure, close interaction with senior leaders, and the opportunity to make a visible impact on business performance.
