Research Engineer – Cell Culture | Consulting Assignment, Uppsala

SallyQ AB · Uppsala, Sweden

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Are you a hands-on Research Engineer with experience in mammalian cell culture? Would you like to contribute to exciting research and development projects within the Life Science industry? SallyQ is currently looking for a Research Engineer for a consulting assignment in Uppsala. You will join an international R&D environment, working alongside experienced scientists and engineers in a collaborative and innovative setting. About the assignment In this role, you will support research and development activities focused on mammalian cell culture, cell line development and molecular biology. You will work in a laboratory-based environment where you will plan, perform, analyze and document experiments. The position offers a combination of hands-on laboratory work, troubleshooting and collaboration with colleagues across different areas of expertise. Key responsibilities Plan, perform and evaluate laboratory experiments involving mammalian cell culture in different formats and scales. Perform transfection of mammalian cells. Carry out cell sorting and flow cytometry. Perform molecular analysis of cell pools and clones. Support expansion and scale-up of cell cultures for production purposes. Analyze experimental results and troubleshoot laboratory procedures. Maintain accurate laboratory records and documentation according to established guidelines. Present and communicate scientific and technical results within the team. Collaborate with colleagues to achieve project goals and timelines. Your background We are looking for a hands-on, collaborative and solution-oriented person who enjoys laboratory work and has a strong interest in cell culture and molecular biology. Qualifications: Master's degree in Biology, Biotechnology, Biomedicine or a related Life Science field. Hands-on experience in mammalian cell culture is essential. Practical experience with laboratory techniques and analytical methods. Good communication skills in English, both written and spoken. Experience in cell line development, transfection, flow cytometry, PCR/qPCR or molecular cloning is considered an advantage. Experience with cell culture scale-up, protein production or industrial R&D is a plus. You are structured, flexible and enjoy solving practical challenges, both independently and as part of a team. Apply We are reviewing applications on an ongoing basis, so don’t wait to send in yours. If you have any questions, you are very welcome to contact Hanna Peterson at SallyQ (hanna.peterson@sallyq.se)
