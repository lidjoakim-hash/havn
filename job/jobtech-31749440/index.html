Key Account Manager

A-Search AB · Hörby, Sweden

Nolato MediTech develops and manufactures polymer products and systems for global Medtech and Pharma customers. We offer extensive turnkey solutions within plastic & silicone molding as well as innovative assembly solutions. Do you want to work with sales in an industry where structure, quality and long-term partnerships matter more than quick deals? Do you enjoy being the hub between the customer and the organization, leading the commercial dialogue together with experienced specialists? Then this could be the right role for you! About the role As a Sales Account Manager at Nolato MediTech you work with sales within contract manufacturing of medical devices. You are responsible for driving customer dialogues and managing business processes from request for quotation to signed agreement, in close collaboration with internal functions such as engineering, quality, purchasing and controlling. Instead of selling a finished product, you lead and structure the commercial side of complex projects with long life cycles. You make sure the customers’ needs are clearly understood, that internal stakeholders are aligned and that we offer sus-tainable, high-quality solutions that meet regulatory requirements. You report directly to the Managing Director, who holds the overall sales responsibility, and you work together with a sen-ior Sales Account Manager who acts as your mentor and support. You become a key player in a structured, professional sales organization with clear processes and decision paths. We have two facilities that you will support, Hörby and Lomma. The position is placed at our Hörby facility Main responsibilities Contribute to new sales by identifying and developing business opportunities with new and existing customers, in line with Nolato MediTech’s strategy and established ways of working Drive, coordinate, and prepare quotations in accordance with the Quotation Review process Act as the customer’s primary point of contact (pricing and commercial contracts) Communicate and negotiate product prices and services with customers Lead and facilitate Business Review meetings with customers Work closely with the Medical Solutions business area to ensure alignment of customer strategies, pipeline, and business opportunities You always have access to strong expertise within engineering, quality and regulatory affairs, and you work together with these functions in your commercial decisions. Background and skills To succeed and thrive in this role, we believe you have 5–10 years of experience in sales, account management or commer-cial project management, preferably from: medical devices or life science contract manufacturing another technically advanced and regulated industry You bring: Solid technical understanding that enables you to hold qualified discussions and sell technical solutions and manu-facturing set-ups Experience from long sales cycles and complex customer relationships Strong ability to work in a structured way, document and follow processes Excellent communication skills, both internally and externally Fluency in Swedish and English, spoken and written Profile We believe you are someone who: Works in a structured and methodical way Uses the organization’s expertise naturally and knows when to involve the right people Builds long-term, trust-based relationships with customers and colleagues Feels comfortable not having all the answers yourself, and ensures the right answers are developed and communi-cated Has strong negotiation skills and a professional, confident manner We offer A key role in a growing company within medical technology A structured organization with clear mandates, processes and decision paths Close collaboration with the Managing Director and senior colleagues Opportunities for long-term development within Nolato MediTech and the Nolato Group Application We look forward to receiving your application.
