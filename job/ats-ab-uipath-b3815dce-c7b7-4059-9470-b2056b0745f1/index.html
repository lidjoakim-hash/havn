LIFE AT UIPATH The people at UiPath believe in the transformative power of automation to change how the world works. We’re committed to creating category-leading enterprise software that unleashes that power. To make that happen, we need people who are curious, self-propelled, generous, and genuine. People who love being part of a fast-moving, fast-thinking growth company. And people who care—about each other, about UiPath, and about our larger purpose. Could that be you? YOUR MISSION WorkFusion, a UiPath company, builds AI agents that take on the heavy lifting of financial crime operations. Banks and financial institutions use them to move faster, stay compliant, and scale without adding headcount. As a Senior Solutions Consultant based in London, you’ll turn product capability into buying decisions for financial institutions across the UK and Europe. You’ll work with Account Executives as a co-owner of deal outcomes. You’ll run discovery workshops, design solutions, build and deliver demos, lead proof-of-value engagements and own content creation across several strategic accounts. The role sits where Sales meets Product. You’ll bring field intelligence back to the team, influence the roadmap and help shape how WorkFusion goes to market in EMEA. The scope is broad because the bar is high: you move without being asked, figure things out quickly, and connect technical depth to commercial outcomes. WHAT YOU’LL DO AT UIPATH Deal Execution - Own the solution side of the sales cycle, from qualification through close and expansion - Build and deliver tailored demos and proof-of-concept engagements that make the value concrete - Lead discovery workshops to uncover real requirements, then turn them into solution designs and WorkFusion business cases - Work across multiple AEs and strategic accounts without losing depth or precision Internal Impact - Work with Product and Engineering to design new demos that solve real industry problems and fit our delivery approach - Bring field insight back into the organisation proactively and consistently - Mentor others on the solutions consulting team by sharing what you’ve learned from the field, from product updates, and from what worked and what didn’t - Partner with Marketing on industry events, analyst briefings and webinars, and act as the voice of the customer on solutions materials Ecosystem & Strategy - Define and influence go-to-market strategy for strategic partnerships, and enable partner sales and solutions teams to carry the message on their own - Stay current on industry trends, UK and European regulatory change, and competitor moves, and use that context directly in deals - Keep deep platform knowledge through close work with product owners, engineering and industry consultants WHAT YOU’LL BRING TO THE TEAM - A Bachelor’s degree in business administration, computer science or a related technical field.
