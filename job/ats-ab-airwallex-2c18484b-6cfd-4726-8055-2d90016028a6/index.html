ABOUT AIRWALLEX Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own. We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.   HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT As a founding member of the EMEA Customer Success function, you’ll help define how we support SME and mid-market customers as the business scales. You’ll own the customer journey through implementation, go-live and ongoing servicing, partnering closely with Sales, Account Management, Solutions Engineering, Support, Billing and Operations. - Build the operating model for Customer Success across EMEA, helping shape how we support SME and mid-market customers. - Own onboarding activities including pricing configuration, vendor onboarding, feature validation, invoicing and additional client reporting. - Project-manage feature implementation and coordinate stakeholders to move customers successfully from setup through go-live. - Monitor usage, service-level adherence and failure rates, turning customer insights into practical improvements. - Act as an escalation point for support and technical issues, using your product expertise to identify solutions and keep customers informed throughout resolution. - Build trusted relationships with customers and internal teams, creating a proactive and consistent customer experience as the business scales. You’ll work from our London office three days a week. WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR Essentials - At least five years’ experience in customer success, operations or account management, ideally supporting enterprise, SME or mid-market customers. - Experience managing customer onboarding, implementation or activation processes from initial setup through go-live. - Strong relationship-building and communication skills, with the confidence to work with people across different functions, backgrounds and levels of seniority. - A technically minded approach, with experience delivering, supporting or troubleshooting SaaS or other technical solutions for customers. - Strong attention to detail and a habit of checking work carefully to prevent downstream issues. - The ability to manage multiple stakeholders, prioritise operational detail and keep customers informed through complex or time-sensitive issues. Preferred - Experience in fintech, payments or another technology-led financial services environment. - Experience working with tools such as Google Workspace, Salesforce, SQL, Looker or Zendesk. - Experience monitoring customer usage, service levels, failure rates or other operational metrics. - Experience supporting SME or mid-market customers in a high-growth environment. YOU'LL THRIVE HERE IF - You own the outcome and you don't wait for permission to fix what's broken. - You're comfortable making decisions with incomplete information and moving quickly anyway. - You enjoy working closely with people across multiple countries and time zones as part of one connected, global team, including flexing your hours occasionally to make that connection work. - You say what you mean.
