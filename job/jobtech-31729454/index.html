Are you passionate about protein analytics and scientific problem-solving? Would you like to contribute to the development of innovative injectable products in a global, science-driven organization? We are looking for a Scientist to join our Analytical Development – Protein Chemistry team within Injectables Development in Uppsala. In this role, you will contribute to the development and validation of analytical methods that support the advancement of new products and technologies. Working in a collaborative and highly skilled environment, you will help generate the scientific insights that drive innovation and ensure product quality throughout development. This is an exciting opportunity to join a growing organization where your expertise will contribute directly to the future of injectable aesthetics. Make your mark. Grow with us. Your team Analytical Development – Protein Chemistry is part of Galderma's Injectables Development organization in Uppsala. The team provides analytical expertise that supports the development of innovative products and technologies, helping to strengthen and expand Galderma's portfolio. You will join a collaborative and highly engaged team where knowledge sharing, scientific curiosity, and mutual support are valued. Together, we work in a dynamic environment where quality, innovation, and patient focus guide everything we do. Your mission As a Scientist within Analytical Development – Protein Chemistry, you will plan, perform, and interpret analytical studies supporting product and process development. You will contribute to solving complex analytical challenges related to proteins and biologics while ensuring that all activities are conducted in accordance with applicable quality standards and regulatory requirements. Working closely with colleagues across Development, Quality, Regulatory Affairs, and other functions, you will play an important role in advancing development projects from early-stage investigations through method validation and implementation. This role offers a broad range of scientific and technical challenges, providing opportunities to deepen your expertise while contributing to projects with real impact on Galderma's future portfolio. Your profile You are a curious and collaborative scientist who enjoys combining scientific expertise with practical problem-solving. You are comfortable working in project-based environments, communicating complex information clearly, and collaborating with colleagues from different disciplines and backgrounds.
