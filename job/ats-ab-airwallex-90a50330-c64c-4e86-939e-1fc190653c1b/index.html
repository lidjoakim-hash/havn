ABOUT AIRWALLEX Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own. We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.   HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT You'll own the growth lifecycle across acquisition, activation, conversion, retention and expansion. You'll help turn customer signals into relevant journeys, next-best actions and measurable growth. - Build and improve lifecycle programmes that help businesses realise value from Airwallex's products at each stage of their journey. - Partner with Product, Sales, Marketing, Data and Engineering to turn customer signals into relevant journeys, next-best actions and measurable growth. - Use experimentation, automation and customer insight to identify opportunities, improve performance and scale what works. You'll work from our Amsterdam office three days a week. WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR Essentials - Experience owning lifecycle, CRM, growth marketing or customer engagement programmes end to end. - A track record of using customer data, segmentation and experimentation to improve activation, retention or expansion. - Experience working cross-functionally with Product, Marketing, Sales, Data and Engineering teams. - Strong commercial and analytical judgment, with the ability to connect customer behaviour to business outcomes. - Experience operating in a high-growth environment where priorities evolve and you're expected to create structure from ambiguity. Preferred - Experience in B2B SaaS, fintech, payments or another complex product environment. - Experience building automated, multi-channel customer journeys at scale. - Familiarity with marketing automation, customer data platforms, experimentation tools or product-led growth systems. YOU'LL THRIVE HERE IF - You're comfortable owning the roadmap yourself. - You own the outcome and you don't wait for permission to fix what's broken. - You're comfortable making decisions with incomplete information and moving quickly anyway. - You connect dots across functions and hold the big picture without losing grip on the details. - You come to the table with a point of view, not just a problem. LEARN MORE ABOUT YOUR TEAM Growth at Airwallex is building a unified, AI-native growth system across product, sales, and marketing. Rather than applying AI to isolated tasks, we use customer context, machine learning, automation, and continuous learning to transform how Airwallex acquires, activates, converts, retains, and expands customers. You’ll build high-traffic platforms, experimentation frameworks, and intelligent decision systems that turn signals—such as company profile, product usage, lifecycle stage, behavior, and intent—into personalized journeys, next-best actions, and measurable incremental value. The work spans both human + AI and autonomous operating models: AI-native tools that increase the leverage of growth teams, alongside agentic workflows that can research opportunities, launch and optimize campaigns, orchestrate customer journeys, and measure outcomes within clear strategies and guardrails.
