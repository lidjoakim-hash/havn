If you seek to make a difference on a global scale, working with next-gen technologies and the sharpest collaborative teams, then we could be a perfect match.   Your future team Are you passionate about developing advanced vehicle technology while bringing people, technical solutions and teams together? At Volvo Group, you will have the opportunity to combine hands-on involvement in ADAS function development with a key role in supporting agile collaboration and successful engineering deliveries. You will join the Driver Support Functions team within Volvo Trucks Technology & Industrial Division in Gothenburg. We develop Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) throughout the entire product life cycle, from early concepts and requirements to software development, verification, vehicle integration and product maintenance. Our work includes functions such as Intelligent Speed Assist, glare-free lighting, driver distraction, drowsiness warnings and pilot assistance. We work in agile teams, collaborating across engineering disciplines to develop reliable driver support functions for Volvo Group's global truck portfolio. We are now looking for an ADAS Function Developer & Scrum Master who combines a strong technical foundation with a holistic perspective and a genuine interest in enabling team collaboration. This is a role for someone who enjoys understanding the technology, connecting different parts of the development process and helping teams move forward together.   What you will do In this combined role, you will contribute to the development and delivery of ADAS functions while supporting the team's agile ways of working. You will connect technical development with coordination and collaboration, helping ensure that functions progress from requirements to verified solutions. Your responsibilities will include: Develop ADAS functions and contribute to end-to-end function development. Translate customer, regulatory and system requirements into use cases, software designs and testable solutions. Coordinate function development activities and support alignment between software, systems and verification teams. Take on Scrum Master responsibilities, facilitating agile ceremonies, supporting planning and helping the team identify and remove impediments. Identify and help resolve technical and organizational dependencies, bringing the right people together to overcome obstacles and keep function development moving forward. Maintain a holistic view of function deliveries, development roadmaps and dependencies across teams. Plan and support verification through simulation, SIL, HIL, test rigs and vehicle testing, analyzing results and resolving issues. Facilitate cross-functional collaboration, promote continuous improvement and contribute to knowledge sharing within the team.   Who are you? You are a technically grounded engineer who enjoys seeing the bigger picture. You understand how complex vehicle functions are developed and delivered, and you are motivated by both solving technical challenges and helping people work effectively together. You are someone who naturally connects the dots, sees dependencies before they become obstacles and takes initiative to bring clarity and alignment when development challenges arise.
