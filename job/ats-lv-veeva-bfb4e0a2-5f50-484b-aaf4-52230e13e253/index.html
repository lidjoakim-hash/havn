The Role Join Veeva as we lead the life sciences industry’s fundamental shift from digital documents to process digitalization. As Process Architect, you will be the hands-on architect of this transition, converting legacy, document-centric processes into a modern, data-centric ecosystem within Veeva Validation Management. We are seeking a technical lead to convert legacy documents into normalized, relational data environments. This individual understands the requirements for mapping Commissioning, Qualification, and Validation processes, turning templates and static records into fluid, searchable, and actionable information. In this role, you will act as a digitalization expert, guiding customers from paper-heavy or paper-on-glass solutions to data-centric workflows. You will also be responsible for owning the development and maintenance of specialized tooling and methodologies required to migrate and manage data across Vault environments. This is a work anywhere, full-time permanent role with Veeva. Our work processes are designed so that you can be productive in the environment that works best for you. This is a customer-facing role and we have no work location requirement if you are in close proximity to an airport and able to meet future travel requirements.
