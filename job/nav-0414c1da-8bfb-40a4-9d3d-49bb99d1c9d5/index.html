Join our food truck team in Flåm! 6 full-time seasonal positions · May through the end of September 2027 · Flåm, Norway Spend your summer by the fjord, preparing food, meeting guests from around the world and working with a team that helps each other during the lunch rush. We're looking for six friendly, reliable people to join our food truck team for the 2027 season. If you've worked in a kitchen, café or food truck before, we'd love to hear about it. If this is your first hospitality job, you're welcome to apply too. We'll show you how we prepare our food and help you get comfortable with each part of the job. What you'll do • Prepare and serve food using our recipes. • Welcome guests, take orders and make sure they get the right food. • Keep your workstation clean, stocked and ready for service. • Follow food hygiene procedures and help your teammates when it gets busy. What we're looking for • You're available from end of April / begining of May through the end of September 2027. • You speak English at B2 level or above. • You're dependable, willing to learn and comfortable working in a busy team. What we offer • Full-time seasonal work with hourly pay. • Training at the start of the season. • Help finding accommodation in the area. • A staff meal on every working day. How to apply Send your CV and a short introduction to office&#64;tellornorge.com. Tell us a little about yourself and confirm that you're available for the full season.
