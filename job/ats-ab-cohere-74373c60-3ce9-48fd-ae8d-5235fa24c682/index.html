We believe that our work is instrumental to the widespread adoption of AI and we are looking for folks that want to be part of that. We obsess over what we build. About the Role: Cohere seeks a high-performing Talent Coordinator, eager to take on a new challenge here in our Talent Operations team at Cohere. You will schedule a high volume of roles as a member of a small but mighty coordination team, who partner closely with key stakeholders to ensure interviews are arranged in a timely manner and that candidates have an excellent experience interviewing at Cohere. If you consider yourself highly organised, collaborative and someone who goes above and beyond to ensure excellence, we’d love to hear from you. In this role you will be a liaison between teams supporting hiring managers, the Talent Team, the People team, and many more. Our Talent Coordinators are a primary internal and external point of contact and are a core part of our TA function supporting the scaling of Cohere. The team is not a behind the scenes support function, they are the beating heart of the Cohere Talent team. You will schedule a high volume of interviews diligently and efficiently, manage a world class candidate experience, build strong and strategic relationships, manage multiple calendars and own the interview process with seamless communication and ownership from start to finish. Our ATS is Ashby and we utilise the Advanced Scheduling functionality! Candidates must be based in Germany to be considered for this opportunity.
