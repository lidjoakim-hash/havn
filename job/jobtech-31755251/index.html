Position description The doctoral student will work in the PyroMet project, where we develop a computational fluid dynamics (CFD)-based digital twin of an industrial slag fuming furnace together with Boliden, Linde and Luleå University of Technology. The research focuses on understanding and modelling reactive multiphase flows at high temperature, where gas and slag interact through chemical reactions. The student will develop physics-based models of the fluid flow, heat transfer and reactions in such systems, and will use machine learning to build fast simulation methods that keep the accuracy of high-fidelity CFD. The aim is to support both the experimental and numerical activities of the project, further develop the existing numerical modelling framework for submerged injection, and optimise the relevant system components to improve the efficiency and performance of the slag-fuming process.   The work combines theoretical and experimental fluid dynamics with modern computational tools, Optimisation methods and also data-driven methods. Part of it will be done together with the industrial and academic partners.   The doctoral student is responsible for his own doctoral education, following the individual study plan. This includes planning and carrying out research together with supervisors and colleagues, publishing the results in peer-reviewed journals, and completing 60 credits of courses.                                                        Entry requirements, General entry requirements, Specific entry requirements, Selection criteria   This advert is abridged.
