Securitas Risk Intelligence (SRI) Securitas Risk Intelligence delivers actionable intelligence on the global security and threat landscape, creating decision-making advantage and confidence to protect people, property, and assets. As part of Securitas' intelligence-led approach to security, we combine data- and tech-driven insights with expert human analysis to deliver risk intelligence that is accurate, timely, relevant, and actionable. About the role Reporting to the CFO of Security Risk Management (SRM) and being part of SRI Leadership Team - the Finance Business Partner owns business controlling and performance management for SRI and acts as the finance business partner to VP SRI. The role will make SRI’s economics more visible and actionable, while establishing a more rigorous, forward-looking and technology-enabled approach to commercial, financial and operational performance. The role will be collaborating with dedicated financial control and financial shared service. SRI needs stronger dedicated financial ownership and deeper performance insight. The role will improve the speed and quality of decisions, strengthen adherence to the financial model, raise the quality of business cases and create systematic follow-up on expected benefits, supporting sustainable and profitable growth. Responsibilities Business partnering and decision support: Act as the day-to-day finance partner to VP SRI and SRI leadership. Translate commercial and operational choices into financial implications, alternatives and recommendations. Challenge assumptions constructively and maintain an independent finance perspective. Performance management: Own the SRI performance view across commercial performance, financial results, operational drivers and forward-looking indicators. Explain underlying causes, identify risks and opportunities early, and convert analysis into clear actions and follow-up. Runs the recurring business reviews. Planning and forecasting: Own the SRI business-planning cycle, including budget, forecasts and longer-term outlook. Drive timelines, assumptions, quality and consistency with the agreed financial model. Ensure forecasts are robust, evidence-based and action-oriented. Business cases and benefits tracking: Strengthen the quality and consistency of investment and commercial business cases. Test assumptions, quantify scenarios and risks, and establish disciplined post-decision follow-up against expected outcomes. Reporting, data and automation: Improve data structures, definitions and controls. Use automation and AI where they materially improve speed, quality, scalability or insight. Finance team collaboration: Work closely with the SRI Financial Controller, setting priorities for reporting, analysis and finance support. Leverage the Controller's ownership of financial control, data integrity and recurring processes to enable stronger performance management and business decision support. Support to SRM: While the primary focus is on SRI, the role will provide targeted analytical and ad-hoc support to the CFO of SRM and contribute to broader SRM performance-management priorities and develop towards a wider remit over time. Requirements Candidate profile Analytical strength: analytical ability and data literacy. Able to structure ambiguous questions, reconcile conflicting information, identify root causes and form evidence-based conclusions. Finance foundation: Grounding in business controlling, performance management, financial planning, modelling and business cases. Connecting operational drivers to P&L impact. Commercial judgement: Understands that finance exists to improve decisions, not only to report results. Comfortable challenging senior stakeholders while remaining pragmatic. Learning agility and potential: Learns unfamiliar businesses quickly, actively seeks responsibility, forms independent views and demonstrates clear capacity to grow into a broader role.
