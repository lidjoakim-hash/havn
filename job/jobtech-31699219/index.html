Test Fleet Engineer needed!

Perido AB · Göteborg, Sweden

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Are you technically minded and enjoy combining coordination, problem-solving and collaboration? In this role, you will play an important part in ensuring the availability and efficient utilization of a test vehicle fleet. Working closely with project management, test departments and other stakeholders, you will help keep operations running smoothly and contribute to the development of future trucks. About the position Perido are looking for a Test Fleet Engineer for our client, a large and well-known company in the automotive industry. The position is located in Gothenburg, where the work takes place on-site in the office. Your daily tasks The purpose of the Test Fleet Engineer is to secure maximum utilization of test vehicles through close collaboration with project management, test departments and workshops. You will be responsible for the truck uptime for the complete vehicle fleet, from delivery to divestment. Your main responsibilities are: Act as the first line of support to ensure vehicle uptime, and second-line support for field test trucks Coordinate activities related to unplanned vehicle stops, including securing workshop resources and identifying, ordering and managing required materials Plan and coordinate towing and special transportation between test sites Coordinate resources and transport documentation required for importing and exporting vehicles to and from countries outside the EU Optimize fleet utilization by analysing vehicle usage, maintaining an appropriate fleet size and planning timely vehicle divestments to minimize tied-up capital Lead and follow up on actions related to unplanned stops and vehicle uptime in relevant project meetings Communicate requirements, provide input and share knowledge with stakeholders whose activities affect vehicle uptime Proactively ensure that projects meet Test Fleet Center requirements Order materials and services from external partners and process related invoices. Maintain test fleet vehicles between loan periods Identify, lead, document and implement continuous improvements to the role and its processes Your characteristics We are looking for someone who is proactive, structured and solution-oriented, with the ability to work independently and take ownership of tasks from start to finish. You have strong communication and collaboration skills and feel comfortable working with stakeholders across different functions and locations. You are able to connect strategic objectives with day-to-day activities, manage several parallel tasks and find effective solutions when challenges arise. You are also confident in your ability to manage projects and have a good understanding of vehicle technology and product development. Does this sound like an exciting opportunity? We look forward to reading your application! Please submit your application for this role in English. Qualifications: Product knowledge Ability to connect and apply strategic goals with daily activities Project managing Problem solving Good communicator Work well independently and in teams Effectively collaborate with stakeholders of diverse roles, expertise, perspectives, backgrounds, and geographic locations Fluent in English Well oriented in the most used systems, PROTOM, KOLA, GLORIA, IMPACT, BOSS Contract type and hours Full-time consulting assignment until 2027-06-01.
