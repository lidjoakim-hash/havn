Job Purpose: In Controls & Automation, we execute and support subsea Control System deliveries. Our proprietary technologies provide functionality for monitoring and optimization of subsea Production and Intervention control systems, enabling safe and efficient operations. With our expertise and comprehensive solutions, we challenge what's possible. Every day. We are seeking a skilled and motivated Lead Engineer to lead the engineering work in projects, being the link between Product Project Manager and the Product and Project Engineers. In projects, the Lead Engineers will take charge of the sub-system design and technical scope, including client relations, and follow-up project engineers supporting the project. Technical negotiations with clients, clarify interface details towards other disciplines and participate in interdisciplinary reviews and workshops. EFAT (extended factory acceptance test) is the final test for equipment delivered from our department and this test is owned by the Lead Engineer. The IWOCS & LoF department delivers the following products into projects: SACU/IWOCS, Reel & Umbilical, FlyWOCS and Umilical-less systems. Included in the IWOCS product family are IWOCS control system containers, SACU control system containers, SIL Safety systems, HVAC and Shutdown panels. Controlling Landing Sting Systems and X-mas trees hydraulicly and electronically. The IWOCS products are mostly built by 3rd party, so very little in-house production.  Job Description: • Ensure that the system delivered will cover the operational needs as well as being within the given regulations and requirements. • Build and maintain the Bill of Material for the project. • Own the Scope of Supply for the project deliveries. • Work closely with the customer's Lead Engineer, on operational needs and solutions. • Oversee the Project Engineer's work for the project. New solutions and reuse of existing equipment. • Final system testing (EFAT). Write the procedure and lead the test in collaboration with Project Engineers, Service Personnel and Test Manager. You are meant for this job if: • Bachelor degree in an appropriate Engineering discipline • Long experience in the hydraulic and electric engineering environment • Strong understanding of subsea engineering principles, production and workover products and systems. • Ability to collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams and stakeholders. • Fluent in English both oral and written Your future at TechnipFMC: • Your journey starts with our onboarding program • As an international company, we provide you with great opportunities for internal growth • You will have the opportunity to attend development activities • We have benefits like company cabins, in-house gym, and sports teams • You will be secured by good insurance and retirements plans • Collaborative and innovative atmosphere that promotes teamwork Practicalities: Application date: 17th of November 2026, candidates will be evaluated on a continuing basisCandidates must be able to legally work and reside in Norway, without sponsorship Place of work: Kongsberg For more information, please contact Manager Harald Aas, harald.aas&#64;technipfmc.com
