Airbnb was born in 2007 when two hosts welcomed three guests to their San Francisco home, and has since grown to over 5 million hosts who have welcomed over 2 billion guest arrivals in almost every country across the globe. Every day, hosts offer unique stays and experiences that make it possible for guests to connect with communities in a more authentic way. The community you will join: Airbnb is seeking a Communications Intern to join the Italy Communications team for a full-time, 12-month internship. The Communications team at Airbnb is responsible for telling our story around the world. Whether we are briefing press on inspiring travel trends, highlighting unique experiences led by local hosts, or explaining how travel on our platform benefits local families and communities, we are the voice of Airbnb on a broad range of topics. We're a fast growing and high energy team with a passion for people, places and great stories. If you'd like to be part of one of the world's greatest communications teams, come tell us your story. The difference you will make: You’ll support our consumer communications manager on Press relations and influencer campaigns. Press Office: • Assist in the creation of press releases and press kits, including storytelling ideation, coordinating with agencies to format materials into press kits, drafting press releases, and developing additional supporting creative assets. • Coordinate with agency partners on local communication initiatives, media plans, strategic announcements, and other media-related topics. • Overview internal reporting workflows such as populating weekly reports for Italy, and keeping the communication calendars up-to-date. • Generate reports by analyzing KPIs from internal monitoring tools. Project Management: • Project management planning: ability to support in creating and maintaining one-pagers, Google Slides, Excel sheets and reports to track activities for various communication initiatives. • Manage the publishing of newsroom posts for the Italian communications team. • Oversee calendar invitations for the Italian communications team. • Assist in organizing press conferences & media events. Influencers: • Assist in coordinating with agencies for organizing trips / events for influencers. • Learning about reporting on influencers campaigns. A typical day: As an intern, you’ll be integrated into a team where you’ll contribute to challenging projects. Your manager, team, and our incredible employee community will strive to be superhosts to you during the summer. We provide the support, mentorship, fun, and real world experience needed to accelerate your learning and make a real impact on our product, as Airbnb revolutionizes connection, belonging, and travel.
