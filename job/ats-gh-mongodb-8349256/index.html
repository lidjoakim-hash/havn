The Enterprise Advanced team is a diverse group of individuals across Europe and India, who develop software to run MongoDB on any type of infrastructure at global scale. Our software and services allow users to deploy fault-tolerant, globally distributed MongoDB clusters in minutes. The main focus of this team is to adapt our software to manage MongoDB clusters which are deployed in data centers or private cloud platforms, while offering cutting edge capabilities to our self-managing customers. You will work on the core functionality for all of our products, mainly on the Ops Manager, Cloud Manager, and Automation products. Our team's end users are some of the largest businesses in the world, deploying massive clusters and processing huge amounts of data. This role can be based out of our Dublin office or remotely in Ireland. This role will report to an Engineering Manager based in Poland.
