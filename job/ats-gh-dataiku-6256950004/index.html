About the role As a Talent Operations Specialist on our Global Talent Team, you’ll be the engine behind how we hire, combining sharp coordination, smart systems and a strategic mindset to help us hire smarter, faster and better. This role blends exceptional interview coordination with talent strategy support and ownership of our TA operations. You'll use automation and AI to free up recruiter capacity, keep our hiring systems and vendors running smoothly, and raise the bar on the experience we give candidates and hiring leaders. About Global Talent at Dataiku We're not just filling roles. We're building the strongest possible teams, aligned to real business needs and backed by a world-class approach to talent.
