Survey Technician / Survey Engineer – Precisiona AB Would you like to join a growing company where quality, responsibility, and teamwork are at the heart of everything we do? Precisiona AB is looking for a dedicated and detail-oriented Survey Technician / Survey Engineer to join our team. We work on a wide range of construction and civil engineering projects and are seeking a professional who can contribute technical expertise, commitment, and strong problem-solving skills. About the Position As a Survey Technician / Survey Engineer at Precisiona AB, you will be responsible for setting out, surveying, quantity calculations, verification measurements, and technical documentation within construction and civil engineering projects. You will work both independently and in close collaboration with project managers, contractors, and other professionals to ensure accuracy and quality throughout the various stages of each project. Our projects are primarily based in the Stockholm area, but assignments may also take place in other locations throughout Sweden. Therefore, candidates must be flexible and willing and able to work outside Stockholm when required. Requirements We are looking for candidates who have: Previous experience as a Survey Technician or Survey Engineer within the construction or civil engineering industry. Relevant technical education, such as a degree in Civil Engineering, Surveying, Geomatics, or a vocational qualification in surveying or geodesy. Good understanding of construction processes and the ability to interpret technical drawings. Experience with modern surveying instruments and related software. A valid Category B driving licence. Good communication skills in Swedish or English, both written and spoken. Preferred Qualifications The following qualifications are considered an advantage: Experience working on infrastructure and large-scale civil engineering projects. Knowledge of CAD, BIM, SBG Geo, MS Office, and AMA (Swedish construction specifications). Experience with GNSS, total stations, and laser scanning, preferably using Leica or Trimble equipment. Experience with quantity surveying, measurement-based quantity verification, and machine control systems. Personal Qualities We place great importance on personal qualities. To succeed in this role, we believe you are: Responsible and independent – able to manage your tasks effectively and take ownership of your work. Detail-oriented and organised – committed to accuracy, precision, and high-quality results. Solution-focused – capable of identifying challenges and developing practical solutions. Communicative and team-oriented – comfortable collaborating with professionals from different disciplines. Flexible and adaptable – willing to work across different projects, environments, and locations. What We Offer At Precisiona AB, you will have the opportunity to become part of a dedicated team within a growing company. We offer: Diverse and challenging construction and civil engineering projects. A collaborative working environment built on responsibility, professionalism, and technical expertise. Opportunities for professional growth and increased responsibility. Competitive employment terms and conditions. Opportunities to work on a variety of projects across Sweden. How to Apply Does this opportunity sound right for you? Send your CV and a brief introduction about yourself to info@precisiona.se, using "Survey Technician" as the email subject line. Applications will be reviewed on an ongoing basis, so we encourage you to apply as soon as possible. We look forward to receiving your application and welcoming you to Precisiona AB!
