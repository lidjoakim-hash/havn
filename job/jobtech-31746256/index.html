Location: Borlänge Employment type: 6-month project-based employment Start: As soon as possible On behalf of our client, we are looking for a Document Controller in Borlänge who is interested in developing their skills within document and information management while working on a large-scale technical project. In this role, you will be an important part of the project team, helping to ensure that drawings, technical documentation and other project information are up to date, correctly structured and available to the right people. You will work closely with Project Managers, Design Managers, technical specialists, subcontractors and other stakeholders involved in the project. This is an exciting opportunity for someone who is structured, detail-oriented and interested in developing their skills while working on a major technical data center project. About the role As a Document Controller, you will help create structure and maintain control of the project's documentation and information. Your responsibilities will include: Registering, organising and updating project documentation. Ensuring that documents and drawings are stored correctly and in accordance with project procedures. Managing document numbers, metadata, versions and revision status. Updating and maintaining document registers. Distributing documents and drawings to the relevant stakeholders. Following up on documents awaiting review or approval. Managing incoming and outgoing documentation. Supporting the project team with documentation throughout the different phases of the project. Assisting with documentation for commissioning and final handover. Ensuring that documentation is accurate, traceable and up to date. You will also interact with different parts of the project organisation and help ensure that the right information is available when needed. Systems and digital document management An important part of the role is managing documentation in various digital systems and project platforms. These may include: Autodesk Construction Cloud (ACC) BIM 360 ProjectWise SharePoint Aconex Microsoft Office You do not need to have experience with all of these systems from the start. What matters most is that you have good computer skills, are comfortable working with digital tools and can quickly learn new systems and ways of working. Who are we looking for? We are looking for someone with at least three years of relevant experience who is interested in continuing to develop within document and information management. You may have previous experience in roles such as: Document Controller Project Documentation Coordinator Project Administrator Technical Administrator Project Coordinator Administrator within construction, installation, industrial or technical projects Previous experience within construction, installation, data centers, infrastructure, industry or energy is an advantage, but not a requirement. To succeed in this role, we believe that you Are accurate, organised and structured. Enjoy creating order and have a strong eye for detail. Are comfortable handling large volumes of information and documentation. Have good computer and system skills. Have good knowledge of Microsoft Office. Are able to quickly learn new digital systems. Have good communication skills and enjoy working with others. Can work independently and take responsibility for your tasks. Are solution-oriented and comfortable asking questions when something is unclear. Can prioritise between different tasks in a fast-paced project environment. Knowledge of document management, technical drawings, quality management systems or ISO 19650 is an advantage, but not a requireme
