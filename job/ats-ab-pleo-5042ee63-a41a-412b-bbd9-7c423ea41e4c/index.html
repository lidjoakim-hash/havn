ABOUT THE ROLE We're looking for a Marketing Operations Manager to join our Marketing Operations, RevOps team at Pleo. In this role, you'll help build the operational infrastructure needed to support our upmarket expansion into Mid-Market and Enterprise segments and be part of our Marketing Ops team as we scale our spend management platform across growth companies in Europe. If you're excited about designing account-based go-to-market systems, establishing AI enablement across operational workflows, and solving complex cross-functional challenges, and are passionate about marketing operations, RevOps, and systems architecture, then this is the opportunity for you! WHO YOU’LL BE WORKING WITH AND REPORTING TO You’ll report to our Associate Director, Marketing Operations and work closely with teams in Marketing Operations, RevOps, and Sales Ops. Our team is highly collaborative and dedicated to powering how the marketing team runs—from campaign operations and reporting cadence through to AI adoption and process improvement. You’ll also have the chance to partner with teams across Data, BAT (Business Automation & Tools), CX Ops, and Engineering to ensure success and maintain a single account truth across systems.
