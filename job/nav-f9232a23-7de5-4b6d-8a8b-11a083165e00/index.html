Frequentis - Technical Solution Consultant – Digital Tower ATM

Cruit AS · Oslo, Norway

Help shape the technology behind safe and reliable air traffic operations. At Frequentis, we develop technology that supports some of the world's most critical operations. Now we're looking for an experienced Technical Solution Consultant to play a key role in ongoing Digital Tower programmes. In this position, you'll help turn the operational needs of air traffic controllers into technical solutions that work in practice. From defining requirements and shaping system design to integration, testing and final acceptance, you'll contribute to ensuring that complex systems perform as intended. You'll work in an international environment alongside air traffic professionals, systems engineers, developers, suppliers and project teams. This is an opportunity to take real technical ownership and make a visible contribution to the future of air traffic management. Your role As Technical Solution Consultant, you'll connect operational requirements with technical implementation. You'll help create clarity across complex systems, identify dependencies and ensure that solutions are properly verified before entering operational use. Your key responsibilities will include: • Translating operational and customer needs into clear, structured and testable requirements. • Contributing to solution architecture, system design and technical decisions. • Coordinating system interfaces, integrations and technical dependencies. • Developing test strategies, test cases and acceptance criteria. • Planning and supporting verification, validation, Factory Acceptance Tests (FAT) and Site Acceptance Tests (SAT). • Following up technical risks, deviations and documentation throughout the delivery process. • Working closely with customers, air traffic controllers and international technical teams. What we're looking for We believe you have solid experience with complex technical systems, within Air Traffic Management (ATM. You bring practical experience in several of the following areas: • Experience with Digital Tower or ATM technology including Remote Tower solutions, A-SMGCS, camera and video systems, surveillance solutions, control-room interfaces, simulators, SESAR projects or operational safety assessments. • Requirements engineering and systems engineering. • Solution architecture and system integration. • Verification, validation, testing and technical acceptance. • Translating operational workflows into technical requirements and system behaviour. • System Integration and Transition • Technical documentation, traceability and risk management. • Air Traffic Controller experience is a plus. You have a relevant bachelor's or master's degree in engineering, aviation, systems engineering, IT or a related field. Substantial relevant experience may compensate for formal education. Excellent written and spoken English is required. A Scandinavian language is an advantage. You don't need to have held a specific job title. What matters is your ability to take technical ownership and guide complex solutions from requirements through integration, verification and accepted delivery as well as experience in working in matrix organisations. Why Frequentis? At Frequentis, your work contributes directly to the safety and reliability of air traffic operations. You'll join an international technology environment where you can: • Make a meaningful impact: Work on safety-critical solutions used in real operational environments. • Take technical ownership: Influence system design, integration and how solutions are verified and accepted. • Collaborate internationally: Work with experienced specialists, customers and operational stakeholders across the ATM industry. • Develop your expertise: Build experience across ATM operations, solution architecture, systems engineering and verification. • Grow professionally: Explore longer-term opportunities towards roles such as Lead Systems Engineer, Solution Architect or Design Authority. The position is based at Frequentis' Nordic office in Oslo, with flexibility according to project needs. Regular international travel should be expected. Frequentis offers competitive compensation, pension and benefits, structured onboarding and opportunities for professional development. Interested? We would love to hear from you. Apply with your CV and application. Qualified candidates will be assessed on an ongoing basis.
