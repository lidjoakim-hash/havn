Learning & Development Lead

Axis Communications Aktiebolag · Lund, Sweden

💰 ca. 350 000–420 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 11.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Do you want to be part of shaping how a global tech company learns and develop our people? As our Learning & Development Lead, you'll drive Axis's global L&D framework, develop our learning platform and digital tools, and help keep a strong learning culture across Axis. The role is based at our headquarters in Lund. Who is your future team? You'll join our People Attraction and Development team in Group HR. The team has a mission to drive the development within the areas of employer branding, recruitment, social sustainability, and learning & development. Together with colleagues in Axis organizations around the world, L&D plays a central role in driving our learning culture, keeping our global programs, processes and systems running smoothly, and supporting a growth mindset. What you'll do as Learning & Development Lead This is an experienced individual contributor role. You'll lead and succeed in your role through your expertise, your influence, and the strong relationships you build with stakeholders around the world. You will: Drive, maintain and develop our global L&D framework and initiatives, working closely with L&D teams across Axis Own the day-to-day improvement of our learning management system (LMS), Workday Learning, drive the development of digital learning tools Lead L&D projects from start to finish, including the change management that helps new ways of working take hold Promote and facilitate our global learning initiatives, such as the Axis Mentorship program and learning on demand Drive initiatives that strengthen our learning culture and work with regional and local L&D teams to put Axis's learning strategy into practice. "I can offer you a role where you'll lead exciting projects, work with AI and digital tools, build relationships with many different stakeholders, and work with great professionals and lovely people. As a manager, I believe in trust-based relationships where we are partners, and my role is to support you in reaching the goals we set together." - Irina Belenkaya, Group People Attraction and Development Manager Who are you? You're curious, driven and enjoy working with both people and systems. You can connect our vision for the future of learning with technology and turn business needs into solutions that work for our teams and colleagues. You're comfortable navigating complexity and motivated by a fast-paced environment where you deliver results together with your colleagues and provide great service. You embrace feedback and are humble and self-aware. You have: A degree in Human Resources or equivalent At least 5 years' experience in a similar L&D role Experience managing and developing an LMS, ideally Workday Learning. Experience with project management and change management Hands-on experience using AI tools in your L&D work Experience with global or multicultural learning initiatives Strong presentation and facilitation skills Fluent English, written and spoken It's a plus if you speak Swedish, have experience with Confluence and Jira, and have a broader HR background. Practical details The role is full-time and on-site at our Lund headquarters, five days a week. It includes occasional travel. What Axis has to offer In addition to all the exciting things we've described above about this position and the fantastic team you'll be working with, we can offer you a workplace where we truly care about each other and our success. We are proud of the fantastic culture we create together, which is characterized by openness, collaboration, curiosity, trust and innovation. Ready to act? Axis is a company realizing the benefits of a diverse workforce. We know that diversity in groups creates a better working environment and promotes creativity, something that is fundamental for our success. We welcome all applications.
