IT Support & System Administrator

Antaros Medical AB · Mölndal, Sweden

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Do you enjoy helping colleagues, solving technical challenges, and improving systems and processes? Are you looking for a role where you can combine hands-on IT support with system administration, security, and improvement initiatives? If so, you might be the person we're looking for. At Antaros Medical, we are pioneering imaging methods such as Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) and Positron Emission Tomography (PET) to design and deliver clinical trials for decision-making and differentiation in drug development. We value competence and development as much as ideas and solutions. Relationships and trust are key to success and having fun while doing meaningful work is just as important to us. We currently have about 160 employees at our offices in Sweden in Gothenburg, Uppsala, and Lund, as well as a subsidiary in the US, and we are continuously growing and developing. As part of our continued growth, we are looking for an IT Support & System Administrator to join our team in Mölndal and help ensure a stable, secure, and efficient IT environment that supports Antaros Medical's employees, operations, and continued digitalization. Role overview In this broad and hands-on role, you will combine IT support, access management, system administration, and continuous improvement initiatives. Working closely with colleagues across the organization, you will help maintain day-to-day operations while contributing to the ongoing development of our IT environment and processes. Key responsibilities Providing IT support and troubleshooting technical issues Managing user accounts, permissions, and access requests Administering and maintaining Microsoft 365, Entra ID, Intune, and other internal systems Performing system maintenance, patching, upgrades, and implementations Monitoring systems and investigating incidents Supporting system lifecycle management activities Participating in and driving IT, digitalization, and security-related initiatives Identifying and implementing process and system improvements Training and supporting internal users when needed This role offers significant ownership and autonomy and is an excellent opportunity for someone who wants to broaden their experience within IT operations, infrastructure, security, and system administration. Who are we looking for? We are looking for someone who is service-oriented, collaborative, and enjoys helping others. You are analytical and solution-oriented, with a structured approach to troubleshooting and problem-solving. You are also proactive and self-driven, take ownership of your work, and actively look for ways to improve systems and processes. Finally, you are structured and detail-oriented, with an appreciation for quality, documentation, and well-defined processes. Requirements Post-secondary education in IT, computer science, systems administration, or a related field. Equivalent work experience will also be considered. At least 2 years of experience within IT support, helpdesk, system administration, or a similar role Experience working with Microsoft 365 administration, Entra ID, and Intune Good knowledge of Windows environments Professional proficiency in English, spoken and written Meritorious Experience from Life Science, pharmaceutical, healthcare, or other regulated industries Experience with Azure, AWS, or other public cloud platforms Experience with system validation, testing, and change control processes IT security experience PowerShell scripting Experience with Fortinet products or network administration Knowledge of ISO 27001 and/or GAMP Are you interested?
