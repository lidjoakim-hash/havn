Shape how we grow, compete, and protect the value we create. As General Counsel for Business Area Machining, you are a trusted leader and strategic partner to the Business Area President and Management Team. Bringing together legal, intellectual property, compliance and governance expertise, you help turn complex matters into confident decisions, secure growth and a strong culture of integrity. About the job You act as senior legal adviser to the Business Area President and Management Team and take an active part in the management and governance of Sandvik Machining. With overall functional responsibility for Legal Support, Compliance, Legal M&A, Patents and Intellectual Property, you lead an international function supporting the business area and its divisions across multiple jurisdictions. Through proactive risk management and clear, simple governance, you balance risk to enable a decentralized, global business where accountability sits close to the customer. You also hold responsibility for our policy portfolio, Machining Way, and the continued development of the Machining management system. Working closely with Sandvik Group Legal and the Legal Management Team, you contribute to Group-wide priorities, governance and functional development. You report to the President of Business Area Machining and are a key member of the Management Team. Your impact Your work makes a tangible difference to our performance. You help the business win and grow by securing strong commercial agreements and protecting our intellectual property and innovation. You enable acquisitions, divestments and partnerships to be executed with speed and confidence. You protect our license to operate in a changing regulatory landscape and build trust among customers, authorities and colleagues through a credible compliance and Speak Up culture. Job responsibilities Provide commercially oriented legal counsel to senior management and integrate legal, compliance and intellectual property perspectives into strategic decision-making. Lead the Legal, Compliance and IP function, setting priorities and developing its structure, capabilities, senior professionals and ways of working. Lead and inspire the global Legal function: set direction and priorities, build capabilities and succession, develop senior professionals and foster a high-performing, collaborative team culture. Oversee material legal and compliance risks, including commercial agreements, disputes, regulatory developments and the implementation of relevant Sandvik policies and procedures. Lead the business area's compliance framework and oversee Speak Up and Code of Conduct investigations, including escalation, remediation and follow-up. Provide strategic leadership of Legal M&A activities across acquisitions, divestments, joint ventures, investments and partnerships. Hold responsibility for patents and intellectual property, ensuring that IP considerations are integrated into strategy, innovation and commercial decision-making. Location This position is based at the World Trade Center in Stockholm, Sweden and requires global travel. Your profile To succeed in this executive position, you bring the legal and leadership capability to operate confidently at senior management level. You provide clear, pragmatic, and commercially grounded legal guidance under time pressure, enabling rapid decisions while protecting the company’s interests, reputation, and compliance obligations. You demonstrate sound judgment, calm leadership, and the ability to balance legal risk with business objectives. The position calls for someone who can lead across legal disciplines while building strong collaboration across a complex international organization. Your background includes: Broad expertise in commercial legal matters, risk management and complex transactions.
