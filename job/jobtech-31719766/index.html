Development Engineer

Randstad AB · Uppsala, Sweden

Job description Are you passionate about molecular biology, assay development, and shaping the future of precision medicine? We are currently looking for a hands-on and collaborative R&D Development Engineer for a 6-month consulting assignment at a world-leading, innovative life science company in Uppsala. Join a dynamic R&D team driving groundbreaking protein biomarker discovery! About the Role As part of an innovative R&D organization focusing on proteomic sciences, you will play a key role in building and evaluating novel protein biomarker panels for future translational products. This position combines practical, hands-on laboratory work with close cross-functional collaboration, translating scientific concepts into robust, scalable solutions that empower researchers worldwide. To succeed in this role, you are curious, structured, and a true team player. You enjoy hands-on bench work in a fast-paced R&D environment where open communication and knowledge sharing are valued. You are motivated by contributing to innovative technologies that advance biomedical research and improve human health. As a consultant at Randstad Life Sciences, you get the opportunity to work at a cutting-edge company in the life science sector while having the security of a collective agreement, competitive salary, pension, insurances, and wellness allowance. You will also have a dedicated Consultant Manager supporting you throughout your assignment. Selection and interviews are conducted continuously, and the position may be filled before the final application date. We look forward to receiving your application in English as soon as possible! Responsibilities Plan and execute laboratory experiments according to project plans and timelines. Evaluate assay and panel performance using established analysis tools and quality criteria. Analyze, document, and communicate experimental results following standard operating procedures. Collaborate across multidisciplinary R&D teams to achieve product development milestones. Participate in technical discussions, troubleshooting activities, and scientific reviews. Support the transfer of product specifications and knowledge to internal stakeholders. Contribute to the continuous improvement of laboratory methods, workflows, and development processes. Qualifications Required: M.Sc in Molecular Biology, Biotechnology, Biochemistry, or a related scientific discipline. 2–5 years of relevant hands-on laboratory experience in assay development, product development, or laboratory-based research. Practical experience with molecular biology techniques such as PCR, qPCR, NGS library preparation, and/or immunoassays. Strong ability to analyze and interpret experimental data and draw scientifically sound conclusions. Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English. Good working knowledge of Microsoft Office (Excel, Word, PowerPoint, Outlook). Proven ability to thrive and collaborate effectively in multidisciplinary teams. Nice to have: Experience with multiplex protein detection technologies or molecular diagnostics platforms. Experience with laboratory automation systems and high-throughput workflows. Familiarity with LIMS and structured experimental documentation. Knowledge of product development processes within regulated environments.
