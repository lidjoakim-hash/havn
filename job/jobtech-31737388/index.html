Description of the employer Helse Fonna has around 4,500 employees who work to provide patients with the best possible treatment and care. Our mission is to provide specialized healthcare services to the more than 180,000 inhabitants of Sunnhordland, Indre Hardanger and Nord-Rogaland. Helse Fonna is a local healthcare company owned by Helse Vest RHF. We have three somatic hospitals in Haugesund, Stord and Odda, a Valen psychiatric hospital in Kvinnherad, four district psychiatric centers (Stord, Karmøy, Haugesund and Folgefonn) and ten outpatient clinics. To get to know us better, you can visit https://www.helse-fonna.no/ Please also follow us on Facebook. Job description A permanent nursing position, a supportive team and a varied working day - all in one place. Do you enjoy busy days when your work makes a difference? No two days are quite the same on our ward. Some are fast-paced, while others allow time for professional reflection and conversations with colleagues. We also offer extended shifts, and you can help influence your own work schedule. Our combined surgical and medical ward at Odda Hospital has nine inpatient beds and two municipal emergency care beds for Ullensvang municipality. We work closely with doctors, physiotherapists and other healthcare professionals. We can assist you with finding accommodation. You may also be eligible for a NOK 25,000 student loan reduction after one year of residence, subject to the applicable scheme. You will have opportunities for professional development in a close-knit working environment, with nature right on your doorstep. Main responsibilities: . Deliver nursing care, perform clinical procedures and monitor patients . Provide preoperative and postoperative nursing care . Document care plans, targeted interventions and evaluations . Work with the multidisciplinary team to provide high-quality patient care . Coordinate discharges and collaborate closely with municipal healthcare services . Supervise and train students and new employees We offer: . Varied work and broad clinical experience . A supportive working environment . Training and induction for both newly qualified and experienced nurses . Opportunities for learning and professional development . Extended shifts and preference-based scheduling Applicants are encouraged to upload verified diplomas and transcripts through Vitnemålsportalen where possible. Applicants educated abroad should attach relevant recognition or approval documentation as indicated in the original vacancy notice. Skills requirements: Required qualifications: . Norwegian authorisation as a registered nurse . Good digital skills . Good Norwegian language skills, both spoken and written Personal qualities: . Take responsibility, work independently and know when to seek advice . Maintain an overview and prioritise effectively in busy situations . Collaborate well with colleagues and other healthcare professionals . Work well in a multidisciplinary team and manage a busy working day We place emphasis on personal suitability.
