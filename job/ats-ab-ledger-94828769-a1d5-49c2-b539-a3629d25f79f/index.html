CONTEXT & PURPOSE OF THE ROLE The Total Rewards team is entering a high-intensity period: a year-end compensation review cycle, active benchmarking work, custom bonus plan reviews, and a rising volume of analytical deliverables supporting executive and board-level reporting. To protect bandwidth and quality, we are bringing in a mid-level contractor to work closely with and under the supervision of our Paris-based Compensation & Benefits Expert, reporting directly to the Head of Total Rewards. This is a hands-on, execution-focused role for someone with genuine C&B grounding who can work with light direction once context is set, and who understands what makes compensation data credible. WHAT YOU'LL BE WORKING ON Year-End Compensation Review - Support data preparation and validation for the annual merit, performance, and bonus review cycles: building and maintaining population files, tracking timelines, and flagging data quality issues before they become problems. - Work closely with the C&B Expert on modelling and scenario analysis (budget impact, distribution curves, outlier identification), producing clean, auditable outputs. - Contribute to salary budget modelling and refresh of salary structures using latest market data, including updates to merit matrices. - Coordinate with HRBPs and People Ops on data collection and sign-off workflows to keep the cycle on track. Analytical Work for Board & Executive Reporting - On direction from the Head of Total Rewards, carry out the underlying data pulls, analysis, and cross-checks to populate compensation metrics and summaries used in board and executive slides. - Produce clean, presentation-ready tables and data panels with accurate footnotes and sourcing, so outputs can be directly incorporated into slide decks. Bonus Plan Design Reviews - This contractor may provide support to the C&B Expert and Head of Total Rewards during bonus plan reviews, including - Preparing and updating slide decks to support design discussions. - Running employee data analyses and refreshing payout range modelling for plan redesigns. - Using AI tools to update bonus plan rules documentation in Word as designs evolve, ensuring plan write-ups accurately reflect agreed changes ahead of final review. Ad Hoc C&B Support - Support the C&B Expert on operational tasks: Workday data uploads, equity grant list preparation and validation, and patent premium tracking. - Support the C&B Expert and Head of Total Rewards on ad hoc projects.
