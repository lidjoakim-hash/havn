Business Line Manager - Service (Sales Marketing & Operations)

Atlas Copco Industrial Technique Aktiebolag · Nacka, Sweden

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Your role Lead the future of industrial service in the Nordics We are looking for a Business Line Manager Service Nordic to lead and grow our service business across the Nordic region. In this role, you will have full profit and loss responsibility and play a key part in shaping the future of our aftermarket business, driving profitable growth, digital transformation, and operational excellence. At Atlas Copco Industrial Technique, we deliver advanced assembly and quality assurance solutions to some of the world's leading manufacturers. Our service business supports customers across industries such as automotive, electronics, aerospace, and general manufacturing, helping them maximize productivity, quality, and uptime throughout the lifecycle of their operations. As Business Line Manager Service Nordic, you will lead a diverse organization across sales, service operations, support functions, and business development. Reporting to the General Manager, Customer Center Nordic, you will be responsible for developing and executing the Nordic service strategy while ensuring strong business performance and customer satisfaction. You will: • Drive profitable growth and deliver on business goals. • Lead and develop a high-performing Nordic organization. • Strengthen our service offering and customer value proposition. • Accelerate digital and AI-enabled ways of working. • Be a key member of the Nordic management team. Your skills and experience You are an experienced leader with a strong commercial mindset and a track record of delivering results in a technology-oriented B2B environment. You know how to build engagement, lead through change, and balance strategic direction with operational execution. We believe you have: • Strong people leadership and stakeholder management skills. • Experience driving growth, transformation, and organizational development. • Senior leadership experience with business and commercial responsibility. • Experience working in complex, international or matrix organizations. • A genuine interest in digitalization, data, and emerging technologies. Your job location The preferred location is Gothenburg, Sweden. The role has a Nordic scope and includes regular travel across the region. Hybrid work is supported according to business needs and local policies. Why it is great to work with us This is an opportunity to make a real business impact in a global technology company with a strong culture of innovation, collaboration, and continuous development. You'll have the freedom to shape the future of the Service business while leading change across the Nordic region. Application process Our selection process includes interviews, skills assessments and a background check. We look forward to getting to know you, learning about your strengths, and discussing how you can grow with us.
