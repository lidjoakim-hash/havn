Join NordicNeuroLab in developing electronics for the iMRI environment!

NordicNeuroLab AS · Bergen, Norway

Lønn ikke oppgittHeltidvia NAVFrist 1.1.2027

NordicNeuroLab is expanding their work within interventional MRI (iMRI), and developing a communication system for these workflows.   As part of this initiative, we are building a dedicated engineering team, where we are looking for an experienced Hardware / Electronics Engineer to strengthen the iMRI team. In this role, you will take ownership of key technical challenges and help shape the solution, driving it from its current early stage towards a commercial product over the next two years.   What will you be working with? Your work may include: • Analog and mixed-signal circuit design • Schematic and PCB design • Embedded hardware and component selection • Prototyping and technical concept evaluation • Testing, measurement, troubleshooting and verification • EMC/EMI and noise mitigation, including filtering, grounding and shielding • Hardware and system integration • Development with manufacturability and cost in mind • Collaboration with suppliers, consultants and specialist resources when required Because the system will operate in the MRI environment, you will work with technical challenges where the solution may not always be obvious from the outset. A hands-on approach to testing, experimentation and problem-solving will therefore be important.Some testing will take place in real MRI environments, so some travel should be expected.   Who are we looking for? You have a relevant background in electronics, hardware engineering or a related discipline, combined with practical experience developing electronic products or systems.   Experience in analog and mixed-signal electronics is particularly interesting to us, ideally combined with schematic and PCB design. At the same time, the role requires technical breadth and an interest in working across disciplines.   You should also have strong English communication skills, both written and spoken, and work well in a collaborative, fast-paced development environment.   Most importantly, we are looking for someone with the drive to make things happen. Someone who takes ownership of a problem, digs into it and keeps moving when the first approach does not work.   We believe you will thrive in this role if you: • Have a hands-on engineering mindset • Take initiative and ownership of technical challenges • Enjoy creating and developing new solutions • Work independently while collaborating closely with others • Are comfortable challenging technical decisions and discussing different approaches • Are curious and willing to work outside a narrowly defined specialist area • Can balance technical quality with progress, manufacturability and cost   Additional experience that would be valuable Experience or familiarity with any of the following would be an advantage: • Wireless systems, such as Bluetooth LE and Wi-Fi • Audio electronics, including ANC and ENC • RF • Collaboration with suppliers, consultants and specialist resources • Design for manufacturability • Medical devices or regulated product development • Electronics for demanding electromagnetic environments • MRI-related technology • AI-assisted engineering tools and familiarity with agentic AI Experience with medical devices or electronics for the MRI environment would be highly relevant, but is not a prerequisite.   You do not need to tick every box. You may be particularly strong in one area and have broader experience or interest across others. We value strong engineering fundamentals, practical experience and the ability to learn and work across disciplines.   Why NordicNeuroLab? You will join the iMRI initiative at an early stage of the development process and have the opportunity to influence important technical decisions..   You will work with challenging technology, where real clinical workflows and the MRI environment shape the engineering challenges.   NordicNeuroLab offers a flexible and collaborative working environment, with the opportunity to take on significant technical responsibility and contribute your expertise across different areas of product development.   For questions regarding this position, please contact Michelle Kolskår, Head of Sales at Folk AS.
