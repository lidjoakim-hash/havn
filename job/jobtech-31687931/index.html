Business assistant

Hitachi Energy is seeking a proactive and organized Business Assistant to provide comprehensive administrative and operational support to various departments and leadership. This role is crucial for ensuring the smooth daily functioning of business operations and contributing to overall organizational efficiency. Job Responsibilities Coordinate and facilitate internal and external meetings, including preparing agendas, taking minutes, and distributing materials. Act as a liaison between departments, internal stakeholders, and external partners, maintaining clear and professional communication. Support project management activities, including tracking deadlines, preparing updates, and organizing resources. Handle confidential information with discretion and professionalism. Order and manage office supplies and equipment, ensuring adequate stock levels. Manage and maintain calendars, schedule meetings, and coordinate complex travel arrangements for executives and teams. Prepare and edit reports and documents, ensuring accuracy and professional presentation. Assist with budget tracking, expense reporting, and processing invoices. Perform general administrative duties as required to support business operations. Job Qualifications Associate's degree in Business Administration, Secretarial Science, or a related field; Bachelor's degree preferred. 2+ years of experience in an administrative or business support role, preferably within a large corporate environment. Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook) and experience with collaboration tools. Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English. Proven ability to handle sensitive and confidential information with integrity. Experience with enterprise resource planning (ERP) systems is a plus.
