Project Technical Engineer Auxiliary Automation

Hitachi Energy Sweden AB · Ludvika, Sweden

💰 ca. 510 000–620 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 10.2.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

The opportunity We are a team of 15+ professionals located across multiple sites, delivering Automation & Communication solutions for HVDC projects worldwide. Our scope includes Auxiliary Automation Systems, SCADA, RTU, Communication Systems, and Cybersecurity, supporting projects throughout their lifecycle. Our team brings diverse technical and project backgrounds, enabling us to adapt to the dynamic project environment while ensuring compliance with contractual requirements and customer expectations. We are responsible for defining work breakdown structures, planning deliverables, and driving successful project execution. We are looking for a motivated engineer who is eager to develop within Auxiliary Automation and contribute to innovative HVDC projects. In this role, you will work closely with experienced colleagues and gain hands-on experience with control and monitoring solutions for critical infrastructure. This is a great opportunity for someone early in their career who enjoys technology, problem-solving, and collaboration in an international environment. How you'll make an impact Be proactive and solution oriented. Support the design, implementation, and testing of Auxiliary Automation solutions for HVDC projects. Work with control and monitoring systems for auxiliary equipment and plant services. Assist in the integration of automation systems with communication networks and other project disciplines. Collaborate with customers, suppliers, and internal stakeholders throughout project execution. Contribute to technical documentation, system testing, and project deliverables. Learn and develop within a multidisciplinary engineering environment. Your background Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Automation Engineering, Control Systems, or a related field. Interest in industrial automation, control systems, or auxiliary plant systems. Basic understanding of PLCs, HMI systems, industrial communication, or automation technologies is considered an advantage. Knowledge of communication protocols and networking concepts is beneficial. Experience through internships, coursework, thesis projects, or early-career engineering roles is valuable. Strong communication skills and the ability to work collaboratively in a global environment. Fluent in English, both written and spoken.
