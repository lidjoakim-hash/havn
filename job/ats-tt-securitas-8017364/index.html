Securitas Group Securitas is a world-leading safety and security solutions partner that helps make your world a safer place. Do you want to help develop cutting-edge security solutions? Pacom is looking for a driven and engaged Full Stack Developer with experience in C# .NET to strengthen our development team in Stockholm. Join Pacom – A Leading Provider of Integrated Security Solutions Pacom is an innovative company specializing in integrated security solutions and centrally managed security management for businesses and organizations with high security requirements. As part of the Securitas Group, we combine more than 40 years of innovation with a clear vision: to protect critical assets and simplify security operations for customers worldwide through advanced and scalable security platforms that consolidate functions such as: • Access Control • Video Surveillance • Fire and Intrusion Alarms Our solutions create a unified operator environment for smooth and efficient security management. Your Role at Pacom As a Full Stack Developer at Pacom, you will play a key role in the development of our security solutions. Personal Attributes: • Strong problem-solving and analytical skills. • Excellent communication skills and a collaborative team-player mindset. • Structured and detail-oriented, with a strong focus on quality. • Curious, engaged, and eager to learn and develop new technologies.
