Highly qualified maternity nurse / paediatric nurse for a 2-month-old baby in Madrid Stockholm’s International Nanny Agency is looking for a highly qualified and experienced maternity nurse or paediatric nurse to care full time for a 2-month-old baby with a family in Madrid. This is an exceptional opportunity for a top professional in infant care, and the salary is extremely competitive and negotiable. About the role Location: Madrid, Spain Care for: a 2-month-old baby Hours: full time, with varied hours Salary: extremely competitive and negotiable, depending on qualifications and experience Start: as soon as possible Your responsibilities Full care of the baby, including feeding, bathing, changing, settling and sleep Establishing and maintaining healthy feeding and sleep routines Supporting the parents with breastfeeding or bottle feeding Monitoring the baby’s health, growth and development, and spotting any concerns early Supporting age-appropriate development through play, tummy time and sensory activities Keeping the nursery, baby clothes, bottles and equipment clean, sterilised and organised Keeping records of feeds, sleep and nappies, and giving the parents clear daily updates Advising and reassuring the parents as their baby grows Who we’re looking for Highly qualified: a recognised qualification in paediatric nursing, neonatal nursing or midwifery, or as a specialist maternity nurse Highly experienced: several years of professional experience caring for infants, with excellent references from previous families or employers Up-to-date infant first aid and CPR training Expert knowledge of infant feeding, sleep and development Calm, warm and reassuring, with excellent judgement Discreet, professional and trustworthy Flexible and able to work varied hours A strong communicator who works well with the family and other household staff Languages: some Spanish Animal-friendly: the family has cats and dogs, so you need to be comfortable with pets How to apply Send your CV, copies of your qualifications and your references to info@stockholm-nanny.se, with “Madrid – maternity nurse” in the subject line. We review applications as they come in, so we encourage you to apply soon.
