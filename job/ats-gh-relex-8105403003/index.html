Who we are We’re bold thinkers and kind teammates, growing fast but staying grounded. Our Nordic roots and global outlook shape a culture of friendliness, creativity, and collaboration, all powered by openness and shared wins. We care deeply about doing the right thing - and doing it together. We aim to improve the flow of goods, time, and energy - and we know we can’t shape the future by clinging to the past. So, we keep evolving, together. What we do RELEX runs the planning decisions behind 700+ retailers and manufacturers worldwide, deciding, at real scale, what gets forecast, stocked, priced, promoted, and produced across the entire value chain. Few problems combine this much data, this much complexity, and this much real-world weight: get it right and a shelf stays stocked, a factory line doesn’t stall, food doesn’t rot in a warehouse before it’s sold. Get it wrong, and it shows up in actual losses, for some of the largest retail and manufacturing brands in the world. We’ve grown for 76 consecutive quarters straight, and it’s accelerating: the first half of 2026 was the strongest in the company’s history, with new contract value up 83% year over year. We’re a team of 2,000+ people, headquartered in Helsinki, with offices across Europe, North America, and Asia-Pacific. At RELEX, Product Designers own the user experience of their product or domain – from problem definition to shipped quality by utilizing AI-assisted workflows. You will be working as part of R&D organization in RELEX Production team who owns their outcomes and ways of working end-to-end. You will also have the support of Design Excellence community of 15+ designers across RELEX to share your craft and learn from your peers. As a Product Designer, you will own the user experience across our Production planning and Scheduling solutions – setting the bar for quality, influencing what gets built, and driving design coherence. You’ll work closely with engineering and industry experts who configure and deliver these solutions to customers, and with other Product Designers to turn complex planning logic into experiences that help planners to understand, trust and act on their decisions. What we need you to do and be Bring your ideas. Challenge ours. Grow with us. • Own the UX vision and quality for RELEX Production: Act as the end-to-end owner and expert of user experience. Maintain the quality through craft and taste, and keep it grounded in the real work of planners and production sites of our manufacturing customers • Think in systems: Be curious to understand complex information architecture and planning domain to keep innovating on our platform tools. Shape the product structure so that new solutions ship fast while leaving room for growth • Stay close to users: Lead research and discovery (customer visits, observation, interviews, user testing) with planners and schedulers to keep solutions connected to the realities of production planning.
