The Product team builds features end-to-end. From designing our data models to implementing the subtle interaction behaviors that differentiate good software from great software. We work closely with UI designers and are supported by our infrastructure team. We aim to delight users with both large new features and smaller, daily product enhancements—thanks to our continuous deployment architecture. We want to create a superlative user experience, down to the smallest details. As a Staff Engineer on the Agency Experience team, you will focus on growing the newly released Asana for Client Management product. You will work full-stack in close collaboration with Product and Design to develop new functionality that improves product adoption and helps our customers succeed. We’re looking for full-stack engineers who care deeply about customers and are passionate about building products that improve the way millions of teams work together. As a member of the team developing the software of a product-led company, you won’t just code — you will be an integral part of the entire product development process, from conception to post-launch reflection. This role is based in our Warsaw office with an office-centric hybrid schedule. The standard in-office days are Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday. Most Asanas have the option to work from home on Wednesdays. Working from home on Fridays depends on the type of work you do, and your recruiter can share more about the in-office requirements. We offer a Contract of Employment (UoP) for our employees in Poland. What you’ll achieve • Drive product and prototype development to maximize our speed of learning. • Partner with cross-functional teams—including Product Managers, UX Designers, CSMs, Sales, Marketing, and customers—to find opportunities that maximize the combination of user value, business value, and feasibility. • Uncover the largest technical risks and build mitigation strategies around them. • Drive the technical design and implementation of each product iteration. • Provide technical leadership and mentorship to more junior engineers on the team. About you • 7+ years of experience in software engineering, with experience leading product engineering teams • Experience working on and leading projects that are full-stack in nature, touching both front-end web application code and their supporting back-end counterparts • Quick learner and comfortable transitioning between different contexts, codebases, and skill sets to jump in and rapidly unblock teammates facing thorny obstacles • Deep understanding of product risk • Comfort exploring ambiguity to uncover new paths forward • Demonstrates curiosity about AI tools and emerging technologies, with a willingness to learn and leverage them to enhance productivity, collaboration, or decision-making At Asana, we're committed to building teams that include a variety of backgrounds, perspectives, and skills, as this is critical to helping us achieve our mission. If you're interested in this role and don't meet every listed requirement, we still encourage you to apply.
