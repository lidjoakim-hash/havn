Head of Data & Analytics Do you want to build the foundation for how data is governed, managed and used across a global organization? Troax is strengthening its global IT organization and establishing Data & Analytics as a core enterprise capability. We are now looking for a Head of Data & Analytics who wants to take a key role in this transformation and help enable a more data-driven Troax. The position has a global scope and is primarily based in Jönköping, with the possibility of being based at another Troax office in Sweden. Linus, VP of IT, shares what it’s like to work at Troax. "What makes this an exciting time to join Troax is that we are building and developing several important capabilities within IT. There is a real opportunity to come in, take ownership and help shape both the direction and how we work going forward. At the same time, you are close to the business and can see how your work contributes to a global industrial company with a clear purpose and products that create real value for customers. For me, a good working environment is built on clarity, trust and open dialogue. I want people to feel involved, understand the bigger picture and have the freedom to take responsibility and influence how we work. If you enjoy building something new, collaborating across an international organization and seeing tangible progress from your work, Troax offers a very exciting environment.” About the role This is a newly established position with a Group-wide perspective and significant opportunity to influence. You will be given the mandate to build the Data & Analytics capability from the ground up, creating the structures, governance and common data foundation needed to make critical information trusted, accessible and useful across the organization. You will work across the organization, bringing together business, IT, data owners and external partners, while collaborating closely with specialists in areas such as Architecture, AI & Automation, Enterprise Integrations and Business Applications. Key responsibilities: Define and drive the Data & Analytics strategy and roadmap. Establish Data Governance, data ownership and data quality across the Group. Develop Troax’s approach to Master Data Management and establish and develop a common Data Foundation. Create a coherent global approach to BI, analytics and reporting. Ensure that critical data is trusted, accessible and useful to the business. Establish principles, standards and governance for data management. Build and coordinate a virtual data organization with relevant data owners and specialists. Coordinate internal capabilities and set requirements for external partners. Enable the business to develop relevant analytics and reporting based on a common and reliable data foundation. Meet Ellen, Global Item Master Lead. "What I really appreciate about working at Troax is the combination of being part of a global company while still having short decision-making paths and a lot of individual responsibility. There is a lot happening within the company, and with the digital transformation ahead there are great opportunities to influence how we work and how things are developed going forward. The culture is very open and helpful, and it is easy to connect with colleagues across departments and countries. You quickly gain a broad understanding of the business and how different parts of the organization are connected. For someone who enjoys taking initiative, building new ways of working and seeing the actual impact of what you do, Troax is a place where you are given the mandate to make a real difference." Your profile We are looking for a senior profile with experience in building or developing Data & Analytics, Data Governance or Data Enablement. Your background could be in data management, data governance, master data, BI and analytics, enterprise architecture or related areas where business and IT development meet.
