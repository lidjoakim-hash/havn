We are publicly traded on Nasdaq Stockholm; our global footprint includes headquarters in Stockholm, Sweden, Silicon Valley, California; and Singapore. Why Join Our Mission? At Yubico, we are guided by a shared belief that your work matters. We operate under a cultural philosophy of Speed with Integrity, pushing the boundaries of technology to secure the AI frontier—protecting both human and agentic AI interactions—and leading the transition to post-quantum cryptography. In everything we do, we live by our core values: • Battle Friction: We constantly look for new ways to simplify our products, our work, and the user experience. • Inspire Higher: We think big, champion the open internet, and strive to do the right thing even when it is difficult. • One Team: We are open, responsive, and collaborative, overcoming challenges and celebrating our successes together. The Role Yubico is looking for an energetic, results-driven Sales Manager to accelerate enterprise growth across the United Arab Emirates (UAE) and the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (KSA). This is a specialized Enterprise Cybersecurity & Identity sales role requiring direct experience selling to GCC enterprise, government, and banking accounts. Based on the ground in Dubai, you will serve as the primary door-opener and strategic deal owner in the region, operating with a strong "hunter" mindset to secure strategic enterprise accounts. Working in close alignment with our Dubai-based Channel Manager and Solutions Engineer, you will directly drive top-tier enterprise opportunities across Government, Banking & Financial Services, Defense, Energy, and Telecommunications. Task & Responsibilities: • Direct Revenue & Territory Growth: Drive end-to-end sales execution for major target accounts across the UAE and Saudi Arabia to expand Yubico’s regional footprint. • Enterprise Door-Opening & Prospecting: Actively build, qualify, and advance a robust sales pipeline, holding direct executive meetings with CISOs, CIOs, CTOs, and IT leadership. • Strategic Ecosystem Collaboration: Work in tandem with the regional Channel Manager to co-sell through top-tier regional distributors, elite resellers, and MSSPs. • High-Impact Deal Focus: Lead complex negotiations and commercial strategy for high-value strategic deployments ($500K+ deal focus), delegating mid-market run-rate business to partners. • Local Market Agility: Leverage your physical presence in Dubai to engage clients directly, adapt seamlessly to local business dynamics and spontaneous scheduling, and establish strong strategic relationships. • Cross-Functional Synergy: Partner closely with internal Solutions Engineering, Channel Leadership, and Legal to structure seamless proof-of-concept (POC) projects and enterprise agreements. Basic Qualifications: • Bilingual Proficiency (Preferred): Professional fluency in English is required; native or professional Arabic fluency is highly preferred. Exceptional candidates with a proven track record and strong enterprise relationships across the GCC will be strongly considered regardless of Arabic proficiency. • Experience & Track Record: 5+ years of proven enterprise sales success in Cybersecurity, Identity & Access Management (IAM), Public Key Infrastructure (PKI)
