About Staffbase We inspire people to achieve great things together. Our mission is to help organizations unlock the power of inspirational communication with the first AI-native Employee Experience Platform. Our industry-leading and award-winning agentic AI communications channels - intranet, employee app and email solutions - create engaging experiences that connect and empower employees. Headquartered in Chemnitz, Germany and New York City, with offices in Berlin, London, Sydney, Tokyo, Prague, and Minneapolis–St. Paul, our diverse team of 550+ employees supports 1,500+ customers—reaching over 14 million employees—in transforming their employee experience. We are proud to be a Unicorn company—privately valued at over $1 billion—demonstrating strong growth, innovation, and lasting impact in our industry. Together, we’re shaping the future of workplace communication. We are looking for a Senior Solutions Engineer to join our high-caliber Sales Org! As a Senior Solutions Engineer, you partner with our Account Executives, collaborate with Product and cross-functionally work with Engineering & Customer Success to drive sales cycles with existing & future customers. The ideal Solutions Engineering candidate brings a strong SaaS technical foundation paired with sharp sales competencies needed for revenue generation. Join a team that partners with the world’s most forward-thinking enterprises — from Toyota and Alaska Airlines to Adidas, Sephora, DHL, ALDI, and more. Important: This is a hybrid role. Core in-office days are Tuesday - Thursday, and it is expected that team members will be in the office (London Waterloo) at least 2 days per week. You will support UK and Middle East teams and must have the ability to travel as required to support events and on-site meetings.
