Household assistant / house manager for a family in Madrid The Waugh Room is looking for a sharp, proactive and well-organised household assistant to work full time for a family in Madrid. This role suits someone with a background in hotels, hospitality, management or administration who enjoys keeping a household running smoothly behind the scenes. About the role Location: Madrid, Spain Hours: full time, with a flexible schedule Duration: long term Your responsibilities Preparing the home before the family arrives, so everything is ready and welcoming Managing schedules, appointments and the household calendar Coordinating with the other household staff and service providers Handling day-to-day household administration Running errands and driving when needed Who we’re looking for Experience: a background in hotels, hospitality, management or administration Sharp and on the ball: you notice details and stay one step ahead Proactive and autonomous: you see what needs doing and handle it without being asked Communicative: you keep the family and staff informed and coordinate clearly Languages: some Spanish Driving licence: required Animal-friendly: the family has cats and dogs, so you need to be comfortable with pets How to apply Send your CV and a short cover letter about your experience in hospitality or household management to thewaughroom@proton.me, with “Madrid – household assistant” in the subject line.
