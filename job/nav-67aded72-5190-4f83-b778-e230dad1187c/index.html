Industrial Pipefitters  and Hydraulic Tubing Royal Industriservices AS is recruiting several experienced industrial pipefitters for an offshore project involving hydraulic tubing fabrication and installation. Project details • Location: Offshore and onshore  • Duration: November 2026, continuing into 2027. • First mobilization: 26 November 2026. • Positions available: Several .• Rotation 4/2 Responsibilities and qualifications Applicants should hold an industrial pipefitter trade certificate or have extensive relevant experience with: • Hydraulic tubing systems using Ermeto EO-2 fittings. • Fabrication and installation of stainless steel tubing, from 10 × 2 mm to 38 × 4 mm. • Mandrel tube bending machines, such as the Transfluid DB series or similar. • Tube bending and installation according to hydraulic drawings and schematics. • Walform or similar hydraulic tube connection systems. • Hydraulic cleanliness and contamination control requirements. We are looking for reliable, accurate and safety-conscious personnel who work well independently and as part of a team. Uploading a CV does not replace this requirement. This allows us to quality-assure your qualifications and assess your suitability for the project. Include your work history, education, relevant experience and availability. Upload supporting documentation, including your trade certificate, relevant training certificates and offshore certificates. Questions about the position?
