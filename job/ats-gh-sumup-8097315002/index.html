Account Executive EU GTM | London, United Kingdom About the team Join our high-energy team in London and help us bring smarter payment solutions to growing businesses across the UK and Europe. At SumUp, we're on a mission to empower merchants by making payments simple, accessible, and hassle-free. Today, we support over 4 million businesses in 30+ countries — and we're just getting started. As an Account Executive, you'll be part of a collaborative, fast-paced team driving growth and helping business owners with £50k+ in revenue access the tools they need to succeed. What you'll do • Proactively identify and reach out to prospects via calls, emails, and social — you'll be the one starting the conversation • Build strong relationships by understanding the unique needs of each business and showing how SumUp can make a real difference • Go on-site to meet prospects and clients in person — building trust face-to-face and delivering demos that convert • Deliver value-driven product pitches and demos that help convert curiosity into commitment • Own the full sales cycle — from first outreach to deal closed • Track your performance, meet your goals, and continuously level up through feedback and data • Share insights, collaborate with your team, and help build an energised outbound culture in the office You'll be great for this role if… • You have 1–2 years of outbound sales or business development experience — and a drive to learn and grow fast in the role. • You're confident, curious, and comfortable with high-volume outreach (mostly phone-based). • You love solving problems and helping people. • You're results-driven and thrive on smashing targets. • You bring a positive, team-first attitude and enjoy celebrating wins with your colleagues. • Bonus: Experience in payment tech, SaaS, or hospitality tools is a big plus.
