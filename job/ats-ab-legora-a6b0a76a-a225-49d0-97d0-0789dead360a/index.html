About the role Legora is building the AI-native workspace for legal work, with lawyers. Our Legal Engineers are lawyers who help the world's leading firms turn AI into real change in how they practise. France is one of our fastest-growing markets, and we have just opened our Paris office. We are looking for a Team Lead to lead our Legal Engineering team for law firms in France. This is a player-coach role: you will lead and develop a team of Legal Engineers while personally running our most strategic firm engagements. You will report to the Director of Legal Engineering, South EMEA. What you'll be doing - Leading the team day to day: priorities, 1:1s, coaching, quality review, capacity and escalations. - Hiring, onboarding and developing Legal Engineers, and setting the standard of excellence for the team. - Personally leading high-value engagements, from discovery and pilots to firm-wide rollouts, with managing partners, practice heads, innovation and IT teams. - Turning commercial priorities into execution with our Go-To-Market and Engagement teams: pilots that convert, rollouts that stick, renewals and expansions. - Building relationships at partner and management level, and designing sponsor and champion models inside firms. - Turning what works into reusable methods: playbooks, templates, French-language content and training. - Being the voice of French law firms inside Legora, bringing evidenced product feedback to our Product team. - Representing Legora in the French legal market, on AI and the future of legal practice. What you bring - A strong legal background, typically 8+ years of experience in sophisticated law-firm practice, ideally at counsel or senior associate level in a top-tier firm. - Proven people management: you have hired, coached, given hard feedback and managed performance. - Commercial acumen: you understand how firms make money, how partners decide and how firms buy, and you can tie your work to business outcomes. - Credibility with senior lawyers: you can run a difficult workshop with partners and lead an executive readout. - Real fluency with generative AI: you use it, you test it, you know where it fails and you can explain why. - Structure without bureaucracy, and the energy to build in a fast-growing team. - Native or bilingual French and fluent English. Strong pluses - Experience in legal innovation, knowledge management, legal tech or legal AI. - A track record of building something: a practice, a team, a function or a company. - A visible voice in the French legal community.
