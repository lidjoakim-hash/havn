The team As a Product Analytics Engineer Intern, you will join the Product Analytics team supporting Ledger Wallet across Product, Business, and Growth/CRM teams. Reporting directly to the Product Data Analytics Manager, you will play a key role in ensuring tracking data quality, and scaling our analytics infrastructure to support company-wide data-driven decision-making. What you’ll be doing - Cross-Functional Collaboration: Work closely with Product Managers, Data Engineers, and Data Scientists to deliver insights and support Growth/CRM personalization pipelines. - Experimentation & A/B Testing: Specify and support A/B test setups, funnel evaluation, and advanced statistical analysis for product features. - AI Governance & Automation: Help establish governance, guardrails, and custom skills for users querying analytics platforms (Mixpanel, Snowflake MCP) via AI tools, and contribute to building end-to-end AI-assisted tracking workflows. - Tracking Quality & Investigations: Triage, investigate, and follow up on tracking issues to maintain data sanity. - Documentation & Enablement: Develop comprehensive documentation and data models (dbt/data mart) to empower stakeholders - Privacy & Compliance: Maintain standard privacy-aware constraints across all data modeling and analytics workflows. What we're looking for - Final-year student in Engineering, Computer Science, Data Science, or a related technical field seeking an end-of-studies internship. - Strong proficiency in SQL, Python scripting, and data modeling concepts. - Analytics engineering mindset with a focus on data quality, reliability, reproducibility, and documentation. - Understanding of A/B testing methodologies, statistical analysis, and funnel evaluation. - Familiarity with Product Analytics tools (e.g., Mixpanel, Amplitude) and data visualization tools is a strong plus - Hands-on experience or deep curiosity regarding AI agents and workflow automation is a strong plus. - Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English. - Interest in Web3, digital assets, and crypto is a plus.
