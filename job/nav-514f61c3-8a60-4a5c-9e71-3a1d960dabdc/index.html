WE ARE HIRING GUIDES FOR SEASON 2027 who would like to be based in Voss/nearby areas!  Are you passionate about Norway’s stunning fjords, rich culture, and vibrant landscapes? Nordic Gateway is seeking enthusiastic German speakers to join our team for the Spring and Summer season. Lead tours through the breathtaking regions of Eidfjord, Flåm,and Vik, and make every journey an unforgettable experience for travelers from around the world! • Season starts in February all the way to December. Who We’re Looking For: • Language: GERMAN and ENGLISH  • Based already in Voss or nearby areas. If you’re not currently based here, we can assist in finding accommodation. • Friendly, approachable personality: Prior customer service experience is an asset, as we’re looking for guides who will like to make our guests feel welcomed • Passion for Norwegian nature, culture, and history: Whether you’re well-versed or just passionate, we value enthusiasm and dedication to sharing Norway’s wonders. • Flexible and adaptable: Willing to travel on short notice and take on varied assignments in different locations. • Reliable and trustworthy: Honesty and reliability are core values for our team. Why Join Nordic Gateway? • Highly Competitive wages • Varied assignments across multiple stunning locations, including Eidfjord, Flåm, Vik. • Future career opportunities: Continue as a guide in other seasons or explore the potential to work in other Norwegian ports. Application Details • Start with Your Language Skills: Please list your language proficiency at the beginning of your application.  • Highlight your previous job experience related to tourisim/guiding. Don’t miss this opportunity to spend your summer sharing Norway’s majestic fjords and vibrant culture with travelers from around the world. Join us for an adventure of a lifetime with Nordic Gateway!
