IT-support, Treasury

Sandvik Aktiebolag · Stockholm, Sweden

Are you interested in the intersection of IT and treasury/finance? We’re looking for a detailed and collaborative IT support professional who wants to develop their expertise in a global environment. Joining Sandvik Group Treasury, you help shape reliable and effective IT systems while building connections across an extensive international network. About your job At Treasury, we support our global business in managing financial exposures and transactions. As part of Treasury IT, you ensure high-quality support, maintenance and continuous development of systems used for payments, trade finance, internal invoicing, netting, foreign exchange, credit management and money market deals. Job responsibilities Provide operational IT support to business key users and other key stakeholders. Coordinate IT-related changes with relevant stakeholders. Gather business needs and translate them into requirements and directives for system development and maintenance. Drive system development in line with our strategy and targets, including opportunities within AI. Coordinate acceptance testing, user education and user documentation. Participate in IT projects and review, update and report on internal IT general controls. Location This position is based at our group headquarters in World Trade Center in central Stockholm. Your profile Your experience comes from IT, treasury/finance and payments, and you understand IT systems, treasury and finance processes. Perhaps you’ve worked with internal system responsibility in treasury/finance or banking sector or gained comparable experience from another relevant environment. Your background includes An understanding of IT systems, payments combined with treasury and finance knowledge. Experience supporting or taking responsibility for internal business systems. The ability to communicate fluently in English and Swedish, both verbally and in writing. Stakeholder management and networking skills in an international environment. Collaborative and curious, you listen carefully and turn business needs into practical solutions. You’re comfortable moving between the bigger picture and important details, while a hands-on and agile approach helps you respond to changing priorities and ad hoc situations. Our culture At Sandvik, we’re tech driven, innovative and entrepreneurial. We believe that success is a team effort so we value diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive culture where people can be themselves and reach their full potential. So, we invest in supporting each other, learning together and celebrating our differences.
