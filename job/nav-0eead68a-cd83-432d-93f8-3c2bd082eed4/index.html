We are a small company who is growing fast and steady based in Tromsø, doing northern-lights and fjord guided tours. We are looking for a person to work as a guide with driver's B license and &#34;kjøreseddel&#34;, speaking FRENCH or ITALIAN, experience with photography starting work as soon as possible. Hva vi ser etter FRENCH, ITALIAN languages necessary. European Driver's license B Norwegian Kjøreseddel Experience with photography and editing. Arbeidsoppgaver Northern-lights tours and fjord guided tours, by car, with small tourists groups, and some good quality photography. Workload may vary based on bookings, and any upper limits will be discussed and agreed together with the employee. The role involves driving on icy roads at night; however, with the training and guidance we provide, it is a routine, and straightforward task anybody can easily do. Vi tilbyr Pay: €1,500 - €6,000 before taxes per month (depending on bookings) -Free training -Free housing (housing is challenging to find and is the greatest expense in Tromsø) -Base salary of 10000 NOK before taxes, with opportunity to have a very high salary depending on the number of bookings we will have. -And, of course, the opportunity to see the northern-lights very often!! NB: we will not answer to applications from people speaking other languages other than French and Italian.
