A family living in Örgryte, Gothenburg, is looking for an independent and reliable Family Manager / Housekeeper. The family has two children, aged 9 and 10, and leads a busy lifestyle. They are looking for an organized person who can take overall responsibility for the household and help create a smooth and well-functioning everyday routine. This is a long-term position of approximately 30–35 hours per week, primarily during daytime hours on weekdays. Working hours may vary from week to week depending on the family’s needs. The main focus of the role is cleaning, laundry, cooking, and household organization. Some childcare responsibilities and practical assistance with other family members may also be required. Responsibilities: Regular cleaning, organizing, and general maintenance of the home. Laundry, ironing, folding, and garment care. Making beds, changing bed linens, and caring for household textiles. Keeping the kitchen, wardrobes, and storage areas clean and organized. Restocking household supplies, running errands, and grocery shopping. Meal planning and cooking. Assisting with preparations for dinners and guests when needed. Supporting the children with their daily routines and helping with homework when necessary. Providing basic care, supervision, and agreed-upon practical assistance to other family members when needed. We Are Looking for Someone Who: Has documented experience working in private households as a Housekeeper, Family Manager, or in a similar role. Has solid experience in cleaning, laundry, ironing, garment care, household organization, and cooking. Is independent, proactive, detail-oriented, and thorough. Is mature, caring, reliable, and comfortable around both children and elderly individuals. Is flexible and solution-oriented when the family’s needs change. Is discreet, professional, and respectful of the family’s privacy. Holds a valid Category B driver’s license. The family is looking for someone who would like to become a long-term and valued part of their everyday life. It is important that you feel comfortable working in a private home environment. Above all, the family values practical experience, good judgment, personal suitability, and a genuine passion for providing excellent service. Does this sound like the right opportunity for you?
