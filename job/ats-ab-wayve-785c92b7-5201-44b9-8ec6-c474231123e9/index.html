Here’s what this particular role covers. 🛠️ About our Vehicle Operations Teams   Our world-class, diverse Vehicle Operations team is dedicated to testing and advancing our autonomous driving technology across Germany. As a Vehicle Safety Operator, you will be at the heart of our real-world AI development, running on-road experiments, providing actionable feedback, and upholding the highest safety standards in collaboration with our research and development teams.   🧠 Your day-to-day   - Conduct on-road experiments and testing with our vehicles throughout Germany, ensuring all activities are safe and efficient. - Monitor artificial intelligence systems while driving, actively responding to any hazards to guarantee safe operation. - Evaluate vehicle and AI performance during tests, providing structured, expert feedback to engineering and research teams. - Support safety and engineering processes by identifying and reporting safety and vehicle maintenance issues. - Deliver real-world driving input to help train and refine our AI before public road testing.   🧩 What you’ll be working on   - Testing and evaluating autonomous vehicle technology in actual road scenarios. - Reporting system feedback and real-world driving insights to help improve our AI’s performance. - Maintaining safety standards, supporting incident reporting, and collaborating on issue resolution with engineering and safety teams. - Travelling nationally and internationally as required for tests and demonstrations.   🙌 You should apply if   - Hold a full, clean German driving licence. - Have at least 5 years' driving experience, primarily in Germany, with no blameworthy insurance claims in the past three years. - Fluent in English and German. - Strong communicator who shows empathy and teamwork. - Comfortable working independently and as part of a team. - Adaptable, flexible, and motivated to solve problems with innovative solutions. -
