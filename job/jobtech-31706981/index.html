Student Care Coordinator

Internationella Engelska Skolan i Sverige AB · Upplands Väsby, Sweden

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

We are now seeking a dedicated Student Care Coordinator (SCC) to help develop our work regarding student safety, a calm learning environment, attendance, and student well-being at IES Upplands Väsby. As an SCC, you serve as a visible and reliable adult presence in the daily life of the school. You will work in accordance with the IES ethos of "tough love" - combining genuine care with clear expectations and consistent follow-up. Working closely with the school leadership and teaching staff, you will help ensure that all students receive the right support and the best possible conditions for success. The role encompasses all grade levels at the school, from preschool class through to year 9, but will have a main focus on our students in years 4-9 Responsibilities: - Work with the school’s PMKB (anti-bullying/harassment policy). - Safety and a calm learning environment: Maintain a visible presence within the school and engage in proactive work regarding conflict prevention, the prevention of abusive treatment, and student safety.  - Student Support: Supporting students facing social, psychosocial, or behavioral challenges through conversations, relationship-building, and individual support interventions. - School Attendance: Monitoring and analyzing student attendance, identifying concerning absence patterns early, and collaborating with students, guardians, mentors, and the student health team to promote better attendance. - Active Presence at School: Contributing to a positive school environment by being present during breaks and in common areas, and supporting the school’s daily routines. - Collaboration and Communication: Maintaining a dialogue with guardians and working closely with school staff. We are looking for someone who is empathetic, organized, and solution-oriented, with a genuine commitment to the safety and development of children and young people. You are skilled at building trusting relationships, capable of handling challenging situations professionally, and able to combine a warm, welcoming approach with clear expectations. We look forward to receiving your application!
