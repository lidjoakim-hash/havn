As our Maintenance Manager, you'll lead the mechanical maintenance team within our Cold Mill organization and play a key role in building one of the world's most modern and sustainable steel plants. Working closely with Operations, Engineering, Projects, and Reliability teams, you'll ensure our mechanical assets perform safely and efficiently throughout commissioning, ramp-up, and full production. This is a unique opportunity to build and develop a high-performing maintenance organization from the ground up, shaping both the team's capabilities and the maintenance strategy that will support Stegra's long-term success. What you'll do As Maintenance Manager, you'll be responsible for ensuring the safe, reliable, and efficient operation of our mechanical equipment while developing a strong team and a culture of continuous improvement. Your responsibilities will include: Leading and developing the mechanical maintenance team, fostering a strong safety culture, high performance, and continuous learning. Developing and executing maintenance strategies, including preventive and predictive maintenance programs, to maximize equipment reliability and value creation. Planning and leading maintenance activities, upgrades, installations, commissioning, and improvement projects across the Cold Mill area. Driving equipment performance through data-driven maintenance, reliability improvements, effective use of CMMS, and close collaboration with Operations, Engineering, suppliers, and contractors. Managing the department's budget, maintenance resources, documentation, and compliance with safety, environmental, and quality standards. What you'll bring We're looking for an experienced maintenance leader with a strong technical background in heavy industry and a passion for building teams and improving reliability. A degree in Mechanical Engineering or equivalent industrial experience, together with several years of leadership experience within mechanical maintenance in a heavy industrial environment, preferably the steel industry. Strong technical expertise in Pickling Line Tandem Cold Mill, Acid Regeneration Plant (ARP), Batch Annealing, and Skin Pass Mill processes, including commissioning and production. . Experience from greenfield projects, plant start-ups, or commissioning, along with a solid understanding of preventive maintenance, reliability engineering, and asset management. Experience working with CMMS and other digital maintenance systems, as well as knowledge of ISO 9001, ISO 14001, and ISO 45001. Experience with SMS equipment is considered an advantage. A collaborative and hands-on leadership style, strong analytical skills, excellent communication abilities, and a genuine commitment to safety, continuous improvement, and developing people. Benefits at Stegra At Stegra, you’ll join a company where your work has real impact on the climate, the industry, and future generations. We’re building a culture rooted in safety, trust, and inclusion, where people feel supported to grow and thrive.
