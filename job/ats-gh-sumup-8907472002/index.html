Financial Products General Ledger Team Lead Sofia, Bulgaria | Permanent, office-first Take a look inside our Sofia office Team description The Financial Products General Ledger (FGL) team sits at the strategic core of SumUp's financial operations. We own the financial integrity of SumUp's financial products (payment services, issuance of electronic money), ensuring every euro of revenue and cost from our payments, merchant business accounts, banking and cash advance lending products is accurately captured, reconciled and reported. As SumUp scales, our work has become highly analytical and data-driven. We combine deep accounting knowledge with SQL, AI-assisted reconciliation tools and advanced data analytics to understand not just what was recorded, but why and how our business operates financially. You'll thrive here if you want to understand how the business works, not just record what happened. This leadership role is the owner of the European FGL team. You will lead a team of 6 people, own the quality and timeliness of the e-money and PSP close, and drive the automation and controls that will support our IPO-readiness. What you'll do • Lead the team of 6 accountants, setting clear expectations, priorities and workload distribution. • Coach team members on complex reconciliations, accounting judgment calls and month-end routines, supporting their professional growth and fostering a culture of precision, curiosity and continuous improvement. • Work on subject matter that covers the core activities of regulated business in the fintech industry: payment processing, issuance of electronic money and cash advances, characterized by complex financial transactions and big data sets. • Map the accounting flow for new products and ensure correct treatment before launch. • Lead the audit and year-end close readiness, with timely responses to auditors. • Own the documentation and continuous improvement of process governance (Desktop Procedures, Process Maps), internal control systems and key performance indicators. You'll be great for this role if... • You have a minimum of 6 years of relevant experience across multiple accounting domains, including reconciliations, general ledger management and IFRS financial reporting, ideally in an international company. • You have prior experience leading an accounting team. • You hold an ACCA certification (or are near completion). • You want to understand why discrepancies happen and how to prevent them. • You lead through influence, coaching and clear communication. • You are motivated to drive automation, controls and process improvement in a fast-growing fintech. • You have hands-on experience with SAP or other ERP systems.
