A Snapshot of Your Day As a ShareASpace Application Expert, you start your day by monitoring the health and performance of the ShareASpace platform that supports collaboration across expanding PLM environments. You work closely with engineering teams, business stakeholders, IT specialists, and external vendors to resolve complex application issues, coordinate releases, and maintain reliable integrations between ShareASpace, PLM systems, and SAP ERP. Throughout the day, you contribute to cloud operations in Microsoft Azure, support automated deployment processes through Azure DevOps, and help ensure high-quality product data and BOM structures. Your work directly supports the stable, secure, and scalable roll-out of Collab Nova across multiple business areas.
