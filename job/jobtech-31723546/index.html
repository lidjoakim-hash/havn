Job Overview Assignment Description We are looking for a Senior Solution Architect specializing in UiPath and Robotic Process Automation (RPA) to take overall architectural responsibility for enterprise automation services within a Digital Workplace environment. You will define the technical architecture and governance of the UiPath platform, ensuring automation solutions are scalable, secure, maintainable and aligned with the organization's long-term automation strategy. The role involves close collaboration with Product Owners, internal architects, technical specialists and external delivery partners. What You Will Work On Own the solution architecture, technical direction and governance of the UiPath/RPA platform. Ensure automation solutions are scalable, secure and maintainable. Guide solution design, platform usage, system integrations and lifecycle management. Define clear architectural boundaries between UiPath and other automation platforms. Review and quality-assure technical solutions and deliveries from external vendors. Collaborate with Product Owners, internal architects and external delivery partners. Ensure internal control and long-term ownership of automation solutions. Drive architectural decisions and continuous platform improvement. What You Bring Extensive experience as a Solution Architect with strong UiPath and RPA expertise. Proven experience in solution architecture and integrations within large IT environments. Practical experience with GitHub and CI/CD. Experience in architecture governance, technical standards and quality assurance. Proven ability to review and validate solutions delivered by external vendors. Ability to independently drive architectural matters. Strong collaboration skills with business stakeholders, Product Owners and technical specialists. Meritorious Microsoft Power Platform. ServiceNow. API Management (APIM). Azure Service Bus. Event-driven automation and integration architecture. Digital Workplace and enterprise automation experience.
