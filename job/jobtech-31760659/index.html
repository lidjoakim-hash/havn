What you’ll do Right now, we are looking for a Contract Manager to our Contracts team in the assigned project area. In this role you will report to our Project Supply Chain Manager for the assigned area, and work within our Project function. The team is responsible for managing contracts, commercial risk, and contractor performance across the project. As Contract Manager, you will support project and construction teams on site by ensuring contracts are applied consistently, risks are managed early, and commercial matters are handled in a structured and proactive way. Specific to this role, the day-to-day tasks will include the following but as we are a growing company with very little silos between teams, other tasks might be included as well. Manage site contractors from a contract and commercial perspective, ensuring compliance with agreed terms Support contracting strategies and contribute to contract negotiations and reviews Handle change requests, change orders, and claims, including processing and enforcement Identify contractual and commercial risks and drive mitigation actions Ensure consistent interpretation of contract terms across teams and stakeholders Act as a key interface between site teams and functions such as Legal, Engineering, and Finance Support project teams with contractual matters, correspondence, and dispute handling Drive alignment across contract managers and contribute to lessons learned and continuous improvement What you’ll bring You’re curious and believe that real progress happens when we work together. You’re not afraid to challenge old ways of thinking and you treat others with respect while doing it. At Stegra, we value people who take initiative, build trust, and help create a workplace where everyone can do their best. Specific to this role, we would like to see that you can tick off a fair amount of the boxes below 10+ years of experience as a Contract Manager on major construction or industrial projects Strong legal, commercial, or technical background, supported by a relevant degree or equivalent experience Solid experience managing contracts, claims, and change processes in complex projects Strong understanding of contractual risk management and mitigation strategies Clear and structured communication skills, both written and verbal Detail-oriented with a proactive and hands-on approach Experience working in cross-functional, site-based project environments Benefits at Stegra At Stegra, you’ll join a company where your work has real impact on the climate, the industry, and future generations.
