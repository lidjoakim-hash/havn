At Trustpilot, we're on an incredible journey. We're a profitable, high-growth FTSE-250 company with a big vision: to become the universal symbol of trust. We run the world's largest open customer review platform, and while we've come a long way, there's still so much exciting work to do. Come join us at the heart of trust! We are looking for an enthusiastic and hands-on Legal Counsel to join our fast-paced and fun Litigation & Disputes team. The team is responsible for handling all legal cases brought against Trustpilot, anywhere in the world. We also work with our Enforcement and Fraud Detection teams to take action against bad actors who threaten the integrity of our platform - this is a unique opportunity to work on both pro-active and defendant cases in-house. As part of the wider Trust team, your work will directly support our mission to make Trustpilot the universal symbol of trust. This is a high-impact role where you will have the opportunity to drive real change and protect the integrity of our platform. If you have an inventive spirit, a knack for solving tough problems, and a desire to make a real impact, we’d love to hear from you. As a Legal Counsel within our Litigation & Disputes team, you'll dive into a diverse range of responsibilities that shape Trustpilot's legal landscape. What you’ll be doing: • Defending Disputes: Manage significant pre-action disputes and live claims brought against the business globally, covering issues such as defamation and regulatory compliance. • Proactive Enforcement: Advise on enforcement strategies against businesses and bad actors engaging in harmful practices, to protect our platform's integrity. • Litigation Strategy: Help craft and execute litigation strategies tailored to key markets, including the UK, US, and Germany, working closely with external counsel when needed. • Strategic Feedback: Translate dispute trends into actionable advice for Product and operations teams, driving better business practices and mitigating future risks. • Policy & Compliance: Contribute to advice on regulatory requirements and help to develop, implement, and review robust content moderation policies. • Process Innovation: Identify and implement tech-driven solutions to make our legal operations more scalable and efficient. Who you are: • Your background: A UK-qualified solicitor with proven litigation experience, ready for your first or second in-house role. • Your work style: Comfortable balancing autonomous work with teamwork in a fast-paced, global environment. You thrive in a shifting regulatory landscape and enjoy tackling novel legal challenges where the answers aren't always obvious. • Your approach: Commercially astute. You understand what drives business value, are comfortable assessing risk in gray areas, and can proactively translate complex legal and regulatory concepts into straightforward, pragmatic advice.
