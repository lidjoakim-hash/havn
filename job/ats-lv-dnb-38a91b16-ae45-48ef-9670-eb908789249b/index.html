The Commercial Director is the senior sales leader for Norway, responsible for commercial strategy, revenue growth, market development, and leadership of the full sales organization and sales-supporting functions.  This role drives sales performance, customer and channel development, pipeline execution, and commercial excellence while building a high-performing sales organization. The position maybe required to fulfil specific Country Manger responsibilities where needed. Essential Key Responsibilities • Develop and communicate a compelling vision for Norway, aligned with our global strategy. • Lead sales, revenue, operating expenses, and profitability; collaborate with Finance to manage and analyze results. • Implement and refine sales strategies to achieve sustained growth, focusing on strategic customers and partnerships. • Champion innovation and new product introductions in collaboration with various business lines. • Drives a strong sales operational cadence across the business including account planning, business reviews, metrics and reporting. • Sets clear expectations of talent and performance management to measure. • Coach and develop leaders and team members to maximize engagement and performance. • Foster cross-functional collaboration to identify market opportunities and challenges. • Works closely with the marketing function to evaluate market trends and dynamics, competitor activities and customer requirements to keep D&B as a market leader • Advocate for customer needs in all internal and external interactions. • Act as the Country Manager where needed for local level communications, Townhalls and signing events. • Additional duties as assigned.
