Your impact At Mollie, we want to ensure all Mollies have a productive and comfortable workspace. In this fun and independent role, you will be responsible for the smooth operation of our Lisbon office, maintaining a clean and well-stocked environment. Your efforts will directly contribute to a positive and efficient working atmosphere. You'll also be prepared to occasionally travel to and work from our other sites to support seamless coverage of the Workplace team. Our ideal candidate has great administrative and hospitality skills, previous exposure to administrative/clerical work will be a benefit. The ability to work independently and proactively in an ever changing environment is important as well as a can-do attitude to thrive in a social, high paced environment.
