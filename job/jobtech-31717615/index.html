Shape the future of Tre's consumer business At Tre, we're looking for a Business Management Team Lead who wants to play a central role in shaping the future of our consumer business. This is a highly visible leadership role where you'll lead a team of Business Managers and specialists while working closely with stakeholders across Commercial, Product, Marketing, Sales and Finance. From commercial planning and business performance to go-to-market execution and prioritisation, you'll help drive the decisions that shape what we bring to market and how we create value for our customers. If you're motivated by combining commercial impact with people leadership and enjoy operating in a fast-paced environment where priorities shift and opportunities emerge, this could be your next challenge. About the roleAs Business Management Team Lead, you'll lead the Business Management function within Consumer Market and be responsible for both people and business performance. Together with your team, you'll drive planning, forecasting, performance management and prioritisation across the consumer business. You'll work closely with leaders throughout the organisation, helping turn strategic ambitions into actions and measurable results. The role also offers the opportunity to lead and develop a talented team by creating clarity, structure and an environment where people can grow and perform at their best. What you'll do In this role, you will: Lead and develop a team of Business Managers and specialists. Drive forecasting, planning and business performance across Consumer Market. Partner with stakeholders across Commercial, Product, Marketing, Sales and Finance. Enable successful go-to-market initiatives and cross-functional execution. Support prioritisation and decision-making in a dynamic business environment. Identify opportunities to strengthen customer value, growth and profitability. Establish effective ways of working that drive accountability and results. You can look forward toA role with significant influence on Tre's consumer business, customer offering and commercial direction. You'll work closely with experienced leaders across the organisation while leading a team that plays a key role in shaping business outcomes. At Tre, you'll join a fast-moving organisation with short decision paths, a strong challenger mindset and plenty of opportunities to grow both your leadership and commercial expertise.
