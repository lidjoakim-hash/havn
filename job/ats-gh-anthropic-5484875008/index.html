About Anthropic Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems. About the role As a Strategic Account Executive for quantitative firms at Anthropic, you'll join the foundational team introducing our frontier AI platform to the world's leading quantitative hedge funds, systematic asset managers, market makers, and proprietary trading firms. You'll drive adoption of safe, frontier AI by securing strategic, enterprise-scale deals with firms whose researchers, technologists, and traders are among the most technical users in financial services. You'll draw on your experience selling to quantitative firms to grow revenue while becoming a trusted advisor to your customers, helping them embed AI into quantitative research, software engineering, data, risk, and operations workflows, and uncovering its full range of capabilities. Working closely with Product and Marketing, you'll continuously sharpen our value proposition, sales motion, and market positioning for quantitative firms. This role suits someone energised by building a new category with customers who test every claim, often build their own technology, and set a high bar for performance, security, and protection of their intellectual property. You'll identify high-potential accounts and run the strategies to win and grow them. By driving deployment of Anthropic's emerging products in quantitative firms, you'll help these organisations unlock new capabilities while advancing the responsible development of AI.
