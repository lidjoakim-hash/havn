Our Mission ⚖️ At Doctrine, we're building Europe's leading legal AI platform. Our belief? Law shouldn't be slowed down by repetitive tasks. It should be augmented by a specialised AI, designed to understand, analyse and produce legal reasoning at the highest standard. Our ambition? To help legal professionals work with greater reliability, efficiency and peace of mind, through a single, comprehensive and secure platform. Today, 27,000 legal professionals across five countries (France, Italy, Germany, Spain and Luxembourg) trust us to do just that. Our growth is guided by a clear approach: a global vision, grounded in a deep understanding of local specificities. Behind this ambition, a team of around 250 people, united around one goal: supporting lawyers and legal professionals at every stage of their work, from research to drafting. And we're just getting started. We grow fast. Really fast. Organically (new products, new countries) and through acquisitions: 5 companies snapped up in 3 years, including a fresh one in Spain. The result in 2026: close to 30,000 customers across 5 countries. The German market? It just blew out its first birthday candle. Doctrine is the European leader in legal AI. But a German lawyer doesn't work like a French one, and in-house counsel in Berlin don't have quite the same needs as their Paris counterparts. Every market has its own playbook. That's where you come in: putting Doctrine's growth in Germany into overdrive.   The team you're joining A Growth team of 4: your future manager, one Growth on Italy, two on France. We're hunting for the 5th avenger to complete the squad and take full ownership of the German market. On the other side of the table, a team of full-funnel Account Executives ready to turn the leads you generate into wins. The starting point (pretty sweet, honestly 👀) A high-growth SaaS model, and a profitable one (yes, both) Rock-solid product-market fit with real momentum: close to 30,000 lawyers and legal professionals already on board A top-tier Go-to-Market team (Marketing, RevenueOps & Sales) to help you uncover new pockets of growth An established spot as the European leader of a category we helped invent: legal AI A cutting-edge tooling stack and a loaded content library to fire on the right levers and shut down objections Your missions Inside the Growth team, hand in hand with Sales, PMM and Product, you spend your days sharpening the German funnel, a mix of inbound and outbound. And the best part: everything is open to challenge. Got a better idea? We're all ears.
