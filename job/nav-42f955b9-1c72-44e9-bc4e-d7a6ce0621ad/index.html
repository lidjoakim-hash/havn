About the position Do you want to work as a researcher in the field of host-microbiome interactions in human liver disease funded by the ERC? One position is available as Researcher (SKO1109) - a temporary 3-year position - at Research Institute of Internal Medicine at Institute of Clinical Medicine, funded by an ERC/ European Research Council Consolidator grant. Research Institute of Internal Medicine at the Institute of Clinical Medicine, University of Oslo, is physically located at Rikshospitalet, Oslo University Hospital, and consists of 6 research groups, and a total of about 60 employees. The research profile of the Institute is to combine clinical medicine and molecular biology to establish a bridge between bench and bedside. This is of utmost importance to exploit in clinical medicine the recent development in immunology, biochemistry and molecular biology with the ambitious aim of developing novel treatment modalities and new diagnostic tools. Integrated in the institute is the Norwegian PSC Research Center (NoPSC), which is a world-leading center dedicated to research on the chronic liver disease primary sclerosing cholangitis (PSC). Several features make PSC an intriguing model disease; more than 80% of the patients have inflammatory bowel disease, for which the link with the biliary inflammation is poorly known, and the disease may recur after liver transplantation. The center comprises three research groups covering all aspects of research related to primary sclerosing cholangitis, from basic genomic and experimental studies to clinical research and clinical trials. More about the position The research group, led by Prof. Johannes Hov, has over the last decade established a strong clinical and translational microbiome research environment with access to a wide range of tools including metagenomics, mouse models and a germ-free animal facility. The group has recently successfully completed the ERC Starting Grant project “StopAutoimmunity”, which has led to several important discoveries. We are now seeking a skilled and ambitious co-worker to take on challenging projects in the context of the European Research Council (ERC) Consolidator Grant “FatVersusBile”, which focuses on how the gut drives liver health in humans and mice, in particular, in the setting of inflammatory bowel disease. The project has a major discovery agenda in both human material and experimental/gnotobiotic approaches. The person employed in this 3-year researcher position will together with the Principal Investigator (PI) take the lead in the omics part of the project, with a focus on project activities using human materials, but will contribute to the entire project portfolio, contributing to and/or overseeing all advanced bioinformatic and statistical analyses together with the PI. The work tasks will involve project planning and coordinating omics analyses relevant for gut-liver axis research, in vitro and in vivo experiments, potential role in lab work, data management, advanced bioinformatics and biostatistics, scientific writing, supervision. A collaborative attitude within the group and research institute to achieve common goals is expected. Importantly, we seek a candidate who is on a path towards independence, as signified by a significant publication track in this or in highly relevant fields, experience in applying for independent funding as PI and experience with scientific supervision at PhD level. Your main tasks will be • Coordinating biochemical and other omics analyses • Develop for and maintain data management strategies • Performing bioinformatic and biostatistics analyses using R and Python • Contribute to project planning  • Contribute to grant writing • Supervise PhD students and support and mentor postdocs and other scientific personnel  Qualifications In this role you must have: • Appointment to the position of researcher in position code 1109 requires a doctoral degree. Applicants must hold a relevant PhD in medical or health sciences • Significant work experience and publication record as postdoctoral researcher (>3-5 years) • Excellent written and oral proficiency in English is a requirement • Excellent skills and experience using R (and Python programming) as tool for statistics and visualization of relevant data (examples preferred) •  Extensive experience with statistical analysis of omics data and biomarker analyses including different methods for missing data handling and survival analysis. It is an advantage if you have the following skills and experience: • Previous research experience related to gut microbiome in human health is an advantage • Hands-on experience within molecular biology is an advantage • Formal training and previous experience with animal experiments is an advantage  We are looking for you who is • Well organized with high working capacity and productivity • Excellent team working skills • Strong motivation for a career in science, including project management and scientific writing Employment in the position is based on a comprehensive assessment of all qualification requirements applicable to the position, including personal qualifications.
