Senior Embedded Software Engineer

Perten Instruments Aktiebolag · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 600 000–720 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 20.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

As a Senior Embedded Software Engineer, you will play a key role in designing, developing, and optimizing firmware and embedded applications for Perten instruments. You will work closely with cross-functional teams, including hardware, software, and product development, to deliver reliable, high-performance solutions that meet stringent quality requirements. In the future, this role may also take ownership and responsibility for CDI spectrometer firmware development. The ideal candidate has a strong background in C and C++ and experience with embedded Linux application development and the Windows operating system, combined with strong collaboration skills and a problem-solving mindset. Responsibilities Design, develop, and debug firmware for embedded devices and instruments. Write efficient and optimized low-level code in C and C++. Develop and maintain hardware drivers for peripherals such as GPIO, UART, SPI, I2C, and ADC/DAC. Develop and maintain firmware for STM microcontrollers and other embedded platforms. Develop motor control firmware, including stepper motor drive and positioning. Develop and maintain applications for embedded Linux and Windows environments. Implement and debug communication protocols (e.g., SPI, I2C, UART, CAN, Ethernet). Perform system-level testing, debugging, and validation to ensure reliability and performance. Conduct root cause analysis of firmware-related issues and implement fixes. Create and execute test plans for firmware and hardware validation. Use tools such as oscilloscopes, logic analyzers, and JTAG debuggers for troubleshooting. Contribute to build automation, continuous integration, and release processes in line with a DevOps approach. Collaborate with hardware, software, and product development teams to define system architecture and requirements. Document system designs, specifications, and processes for internal and external stakeholders. Support manufacturing and field teams in resolving technical issues. Basic Qualifications Master's degree in electrical engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field. 5+ years of hands-on experience in embedded systems development. Strong background in C and C++. Python is an advantage. Experience with embedded Linux application development and the Windows operating system. Strong understanding of microcontrollers, microprocessors, and SoCs (e.g., ARM Cortex). Experience with stepper motor control. Knowledge of communication protocols (SPI, I2C, UART, CAN, USB). Experience in low-level driver development and board bring-up. Knowledge of power management, memory optimization, and real-time constraints. A DevOps mindset, with an interest in automation and continuous improvement. Strong collaboration skills and a problem-solving mindset. Preferred Characteristic Knowledge of spectrometers and spectroscopy instrumentation. Experience with the EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) or secure product development. Experience in creating a Windows master disk (system image) for instrument deployment. Experience in developing and building Windows device drivers. Knowledge of secure firmware development, including cryptographic protocols and secure boot. Experience with embedded application development on ARM microcontrollers. Familiarity with the software development lifecycle (SDLC) and agile methodologies. Hands-on experience with Raspberry Pi Compute Module development. Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
