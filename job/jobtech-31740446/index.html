Data Engineer

IKEA IT Aktiebolag · Malmö, Sweden

💰 ca. 510 000–620 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 1.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Who you are You see yourself helping us develop products with data. You work agile, within cross-functional teams to keep IKEA leading in an exciting and fast moving environment. Our diverse and global team within Data & Analytics is growing and we would love to talk with you if you recognize yourself in some of the following: · Excited about IKEA’s core values and vision of creating a better everyday life for the many people. · A team player who promotes informed decision-making based on data and builds cross-functional relationships with peers. · Considers data to be the starting point for solving (almost) any business problem. · Has a background in analytics, computer science, engineering, or equivalent (at least MS or equivalent) · Good knowledge of advanced data structures and distributed computing · Good knowledge of SQL for data processing and analysis · Good knowledge of Data products and offerings on Google Cloud Platform, and experience with Dataform on GCP would be good to have. · Broad knowledge of programming languages (e.g., Java, Go, Python, or Scala), including concepts from functional and object-oriented programming paradigms · Broad knowledge of new and emerging tools for extracting, ingesting, and processing of large datasets (Apache Spark, Beam, Kafka, or equivalent) · Good knowledge of machine learning or simulation/optimisation models and how they rely on data feeds for their implementation · Good knowledge of digital product development principles and the importance of rapid validated learning cycles to optimise performance long term · Passionate about the possibilities of Data & Analytics and the value it can generate for the business. · Curious world-class problem solver who wants to keep learning! This is our wish list! If you don’t recognize yourself in all these points, you might still be an excellent candidate for the role. We like to think long term and invest in people’s development together with us. The IKEA culture and values are very much a part of our business and day-to-day work life. For you to thrive and grow with IKEA it’s important for us that you share our values! You can read more about our values and life at IKEA on our website: www.ikea.com What you'll be doing day to day As a Data Engineer, you will be working in a cross-functional team. Here, we are building the global Ingka tool that shows the most important INGKA & Retail KPIs. Specifically, to give management a complete Group performance overview, reducing the need for alternative reports, and facilitating the process of going from facts to insights to actions with clear accountability. Our team works with around 40+ different source systems and hosts close to 70 KPIs in the tool. You will be a thought partner on questions like: · How should we extract, merge, transform and serve data to enable usage at a global scale? · What user needs do we prioritise first, making the optimal trade-off between quick business impact and building for the future? · How can we ensure the reliability and quality of the data, and how do we educate team members in cross-functional teams about its importance? Perhaps you have your own ideas on problems to solve? We are on a journey to become a data-driven company, and you will have the opportunity to help shape our path based on your knowledge and experience. What is our role within IKEA? The Data & Analytics function is a core pillar of Group Digital. We are responsible for shaping and driving digital objectives with deep internal competence along the data value chain, ranging from key enablers such as Data Management and Data Architecture to product success drivers like Data Engineering and Advanced Analytics. You will work in Data & Analytics together with other Data Analysts, Data Scientists, Data Engineers, Data Stewards, and Data Architects. IKEA has long been a global leader in home furnishing. We are proud of our vision to improve the everyday life of many people.
