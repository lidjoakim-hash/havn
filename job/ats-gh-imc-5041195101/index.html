IT Operations Engineer

IMC Trading · Zug, Switzerland, Switzerland

💰 ca. 84 000–102 000 CHF/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)HeltidDirekte fra arbeidsgiverFrist 18.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

At IMC, technology is not a department; it’s at the heart of everything we do. Developed in-house, our innovative software makes millions of trading decisions daily, and we win by making better and faster decisions than our competition. Our on-site IT Support team helps us move at a higher pace and quality each day, from hands-on support for end users to configuring and automating our systems to scale our support capabilities. In this fully on-site position, you are the first person people come to with any IT issue, whether it’s a MacBook, a SaaS application, identity and access management, or hardware and AV. You support our growing Zug office and act as first- to third-line support. Our environment is predominantly macOS and cloud-based, with a small Windows footprint. You’ll work across a wide range of SaaS and custom systems. To succeed and grow, you take ownership, work autonomously, and keep improving an IT environment that runs smoothly and is ready to scale. Your Responsibilities: • First-to-third line support for our users, mainly on macOS. • Managing and supporting our end-user platform, including Microsoft 365, Okta (SSO, MFA, lifecycle management), Slack and the Atlassian stack (Jira, Confluence, Jira Service Management). • Device management and patching: Jamf for macOS and Intune for phones, including enrollment, configuration profiles, app deployment and compliance. • Onboarding and offboarding, including account provisioning, access management and device setup. • Installing, setting up and servicing hardware and peripherals, including laptops, monitors, printers, and audio and video conferencing systems. • Troubleshooting, escalating and documenting network and connectivity issues. • Automating repetitive tasks and keeping knowledge base articles and runbooks in Confluence up to date. • Organizing and performing desk moves, room setups and physical equipment moves. • Everything else that keeps us moving. Your Skills and Experience • A degree in an ICT-related field (BSc level ideally), or an equivalent qualification such as Informatiker/in EFZ. • At least one year of experience in a similar role in a fast-paced environment. • Strong macOS knowledge and hands-on experience with JAMF (Pro). • Experience with Microsoft 365 administration and Intune; working knowledge of Windows. • Familiarity with Okta or a similar identity provider (SSO, SCIM, MFA). • Experience administering or supporting Slack and the Atlassian suite. • Scripting experience (Bash, Zsh, Python or PowerShell) is a plus, and so is Linux experience. • Excellent troubleshooting and diagnostic skills, with the ability to solve problems creatively and proactively. • Willingness to take on physical tasks such as desk moves and room setups. • A strong drive to grow (at IMC we are committed to helping with that). • You work autonomously, with a sense of urgency, and prioritize based on business needs. • Available and willing to work on-site full-time in Zug. • Proficient in written and spoken English; German is a plus. About Us IMC is a research-driven trading firm where quantitative modeling, machine learning, and engineering shape how modern markets are traded. A stabilizing force in markets since 1989, we provide liquidity across trading venues, delivering the best outcome in value and risk management to investors. Using our own technology and capital, we build proprietary systems and algorithms that operate across global markets. Our researchers, traders, and engineers work as a collective, combining rapid experimentation, advanced infrastructure, and real-time feedback to turn insight into execution and execution into advantage.
