Veeva Systems is a mission-driven organization and pioneer in industry cloud, helping life sciences companies bring therapies to patients faster. As one of the fastest-growing SaaS companies in history, we surpassed $3B in revenue in our last fiscal year with extensive growth potential ahead.   At the heart of Veeva are our values: Do the Right Thing, Customer Success, Employee Success, and Speed. We're not just any public company – we made history in 2021 by becoming a public benefit corporation (PBC), legally bound to balancing the interests of customers, employees, society, and investors.   As a Work Anywhere company, we support your flexibility to work from home or in the office, so you can thrive in your ideal environment.   Join us in transforming the life sciences industry, committed to making a positive impact on its customers, employees, and communities. The Role  Veeva is looking for a highly motivated individual with real-world life science industry experience in Regulatory Operations or Regulatory Affairs to drive customer success with our Veeva Regulatory Information Management (RIM) products in Europe. If you are a person who has a blend of experience building regulatory submissions and understanding of the key technical product features behind these processes, then this could be the role for you!   Product Experts play a key role in the product organization, supporting software product managers specialising in Submissions, Submissions Archive and Publishing applications. You will advocate the voice of the customer by creating problem statements, using a blend of your industry knowledge, experience with European/ ROW regulations, and an understanding of real need behind the ask, to help shape the product roadmap for Submissions, Submissions Archive, and Publishing. Product Experts build strong relationships within the Veeva RIM community, lead educational campaigns, create content to showcase Veeva RIM, and represent the product organization in online communities, customer meetings and at industry events.   This role is ideal for someone passionate about RIM processes, eager to collaborate with customers from end users to executives, and excited to drive their success. You should have the skills and personal characteristics to work with autonomy, proactiveness and drive – demonstrating the ability to succeed where there is ambiguity in a fast-moving environment. What You'll Do • Be the product evangelist and provide subject matter expertise for the Veeva RIM products, especially focused on the Veeva Submissions, Submissions Archive and Publishing use cases and associated features. • Stay up to date with the latest features and roadmap direction and be able to communicate the future roadmap vision. • Provide education for Veeva RIM customers in our Customer Communities and a range of forums. Product experts are key in communicating the value of our products, the customer business problems we are solving and presenting product functionality. • Engage with customer stakeholders to communicate product roadmap and vision. • Contribute to best practices and process recommendations within the Veeva RIM product suite. • Be the customer advocate for future product innovations. Gather, analyze, and prioritize customer feedback into problem statements, identifying the needs of the customer, feeding these insights into roadmap strategy and release planning. • Collaborate with Product teams to deliver valuable content and resources for product and release communication. • Provide cross-functional support to internal teams, including Services, Strategy, Sales, and Product Support. Requirements • 3+ years within the regulatory industry for life sciences in Regulatory Operations, Regulatory Affairs, or related areas.
