Customer Service Representative

Mouser Electronics Nordic AB · Malmö, Sweden

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Customer Service Representative (f/m/d) What to expect: The Customer Service Representative is responsible for providing the highest level of service to customers by processing orders, quotes and assisting customers with catalog information, always maintaining and exhibiting Mouser values to each and every customer. Generates new and repeat business through customer service initiatives. Accurately processes customer transactions such as orders, quotes or returns with a strong focus on service excellence. Follows up with customers to ensure ongoing customer satisfaction and to resolve any customer concerns that may arise. Provides information regarding availability of in-stock and out-of-stock items. Provides customers with cross-selling and up-selling opportunities, and offers associated products and promotional items. Researches and expedites customer orders. Monitors and ensures delivery date and product quality to meet customer requirements; verifies customer orders are processed correctly. Confirms special handling orders are expedited. Fills requests for catalogs, information or samples. Supports local Customer Service teams, and provides support for other teams when needed. Other duties as assigned. What we are looking for: 1-2 years’ experience in a customer service role, or equivalent combination of education and experience. Strong ability and desire to provide excellent customer service and to promote and maintain high standards of quality. Maintains a high level of commitment to achieve goals and has the desire and ability to deal directly with B-2-B and B-2-C customers and represent Mouser Electronics. Professional verbal and written communication skills in Swedish and English; additional knowledge of Danish and/or Norwegian language would be a plus. Proficient in MS Office; experience with other systems are a plus. Understands and able to correctly work with multiple currencies and other requirements with regard to processing international orders. Exhibits strong analytical skills, problem solving and negotiation skills, and analyzes the impact of decisions before execution. Demonstrates profound planning and organization skills and works very structured. Accountable and integer, energetic and pro-active personality. Self-motivated and result-oriented and a good team worker (cross-cultural). Takes initiative to expand knowledge for position utilizing all available sources What do we offer: A long-term perspective with responsible tasks and attractive development opportunities in a dynamic team. You can also expect: A modern, friendly, and inclusive workplace environment Permanent employment contract Competitive compensation 30 days of annual leave and paid special leave Pension program, TGL, and life insurance Health allowance of 5,000 SEK per year Regular company-paid health checks (annually or every second year) Appointment for assessment of health and work capacity when needed A wide range of training and development opportunities through our Mouser University Employee referral bonus program Complimentary fruit and beverages at the workplace Our office is located at the following address: Mouser Electronics Nordic AB Jungmansgatan 12, 211 11 Malmo / Sweden The hybrid setting requires to come 4 days / week to the office. Please submit your CV in English. The salary range for this position is SEK 29,000 - SEK 33,350.
