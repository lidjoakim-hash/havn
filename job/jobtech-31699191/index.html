Ability to collaborate with suppliers and manage technical discussions. Strong communication and stakeholder management skills. Structured, proactive, and solution-oriented approach. Meritorious Experience in cab development or vehicle interiors. Experience with ventilation, HVAC, or air management systems. Experience in large-scale automotive development organizations. Experience with supplier nomination and sourcing activities.
