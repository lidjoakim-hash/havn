Veritaz is a leading IT staffing solutions provider in Sweden, committed to advancing individual careers and aiding employers in ensuring the perfect talent fit. With a proven track record of successful partnerships with top companies, we have rapidly grown our presence in the USA, Europe, and Sweden as a dependable and trusted resource within the IT industry. Job Overview Role Summary Our client is seeking an experienced Cloud Security / DevSecOps Consultant .The assignment focuses on strengthening cloud, application, and platform security while supporting development and platform teams in implementing secure engineering practices. The role involves providing hands-on security expertise across the organization, helping teams integrate security into their development processes, cloud environments, and CI/CD pipelines. The consultant will work within a decentralized security governance model, collaborating closely with engineering teams while contributing to common security standards, guidelines, and practices. This is a practical security engineering role combining operational security support, cloud security, DevSecOps enablement, and technical advisory responsibilities. Key Responsibilities Provide hands-on security support to development and platform teams, addressing security findings, vulnerabilities, incidents, and risk assessments. Support the integration of automated security controls, vulnerability scanning, and security policies into CI/CD pipelines. Contribute to secure design, implementation, and configuration across cloud environments, applications, and container-based platforms. Support vulnerability management, third-party dependency governance, and software supply chain security. Work closely with engineering teams to introduce practical security measures and promote secure development practices. Technical Stack & Requirements Cloud Security: Hands-on experience with at least one major cloud platform, including core services such as compute, identity and access management, networking, logging, and monitoring. DevSecOps & CI/CD: Solid experience with modern software development practices and integrating security controls into CI/CD pipelines. Application & Infrastructure Security: Strong understanding of secure development practices, vulnerability management, and infrastructure security. Infrastructure as Code: Practical experience with Terraform, CloudFormation, or equivalent tools. Vulnerability & Dependency Management: Experience identifying, assessing, and managing vulnerabilities, third-party dependencies, and software supply chain risks. Container Security: Experience with container orchestration platforms and securing container-based environments is advantageous. Security Automation: Familiarity with automated security testing, code scanning, and artifact scanning solutions is beneficial. Security Frameworks: Knowledge of common security and compliance frameworks is considered an advantage. Qualifications & Experience Previous experience in DevSecOps, Security Engineering, or security roles embedded within development or platform teams is highly relevant.
