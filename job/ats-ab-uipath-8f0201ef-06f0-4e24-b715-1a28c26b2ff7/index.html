YOUR MISSION As a Partner Sales Executive focused on Financial Crime Compliance (FCC), you'll navigate partner ecosystems, building strong executive relationships, trust, and new revenue streams through value-based selling. You'll grow revenue and drive adoption of the UiPath platform for FCC use cases — such as anti-money laundering, KYC, and fraud — by partnering with world-class consulting and service organizations, and build the plans that help those partners deliver maximum success for their financial services customers. This is a unique overlay role: you'll drive partner sales for FCC across the entire company, and no two days will look the same. You'll build offerings and go-to-market strategies — and see them through to execution — with sophisticated, non-transactional partners such as global systems integrators and advisory firms.
