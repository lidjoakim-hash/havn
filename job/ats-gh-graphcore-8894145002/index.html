Location: Bristol, UK Salary: £45,000 per annum Start date: September 2027 Application deadline: Midnight, 29th November 2026 We encourage you to apply early, as we may close applications before the advertised deadline. The Graduate Programme at Graphcore Join Graphcore in a permanent role and start your career with two years of hands-on learning. From day one, you’ll be trusted to take ownership, share your ideas and help shape what we’re creating. You’ll tackle challenging problems alongside a team who support your development and are as excited about your success as you are. What you’ll work on Put your coding skills to work on tools that help developers understand and improve how software runs on Graphcore hardware. As a Graduate Software Engineer in the Analysis Tools Team, you’ll design, develop and test features across libraries, command-line tools and graphical applications. Your work will help users see where time, memory and hardware resources are being used, so they can optimise applications and resolve issues. You’ll fix bugs and support end-users with guidance on using the tools. It’s a chance to connect software development with the hardware beneath it and see how useful profiling information helps developers get more from Graphcore systems. Your team and support The Analysis Tools Team works close to Graphcore’s hardware and software stack. It collects profiling information, processes it and turns it into insight that developers can use. You’ll learn from experienced engineers through practical engineering, regular code reviews and open technical discussions. You’ll be supported to test ideas and build confidence as you develop features and solve problems. Decisions are driven by profiling data, customer needs and team experience. You’ll be encouraged to ask questions, challenge existing approaches and share ideas for making things better. Take responsibility for your work and speak up when you spot a problem or need support. How you’ll learn and grow During the two-year programme, you’ll build practical experience in software development, testing and performance profiling, working across libraries, command-line tools and graphical applications. Hands-on debugging and code reviews will help you develop your engineering judgement: how to investigate a problem, test an approach and use feedback to improve your work. Working on tools used by developers and supporting customers will help you understand their needs, explain technical findings clearly and see how your work connects to the wider hardware and software system. What we're looking for • Programming experience in one or more languages – preferably Rust, TypeScript - gained through study, projects, internships, placements or practical work • A logical approach to debugging, testing and solving unfamiliar problems • Exposure to CI/CD pipelines, build tools, version control or collaborative software development practices • Clear communication and a willingness to learn through feedback, ask questions and share ideas Bring an interest in TypeScript, React, Rust, C++ or other technologies used by the team, and curiosity about performance profiling, relational databases, memory management, multithreading or hardware utilisation. You don’t need experience with every technology we use. You can demonstrate these skills through coursework, a dissertation, personal or team projects, internships, placements or other relevant experience. Tell us what you built or investigated, how you approached it and what you learned.
