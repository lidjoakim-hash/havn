Cost Accounting Manager

Canonical · Home based - EMEA; Office Based - London, UK, United Kingdom

Canonical is a leading provider of open source software and operating systems to the global enterprise and technology markets. Our platform, Ubuntu, is very widely used in breakthrough enterprise initiatives such as public cloud, data science, AI, engineering innovation, and IoT. Our customers include the world's leading public cloud and silicon providers, and industry leaders in many sectors. The company is a pioneer of global distributed collaboration, with 1200+ colleagues in 75+ countries and very few office-based roles. Teams meet two to four times yearly in person, in interesting locations around the world, to align on strategy and execution. The company is founder-led, profitable, and growing. We are hiring a Cost Accounting Manager, ideally a candidate with a passion for systems accounting, to drive cost recognition process, decisions, systems and reporting. In our finance function we aspire to set the industry pace for speed and accuracy of reporting. We aim to achieve this by driving alignment across operational processes, cost structures and financial systems to drive consistency, transparency and efficiency. We are looking for a professionally qualified accounting leader with expertise in cost accounting and IFRS (including IFRS 16/IAS38) to build scalable cost review and cost recognition processes. This lead will work across teams including Information Systems, Legal, Business Services, Financial Planning & Analysis and Commercial Business Systems to align understanding and ensure that the accounting treatment of costs, vendor management and operational spend is accurate, consistent,and highly automated. Perhaps unusually we prefer a leader with deep personal interest in the software and technology industry, and ideally some data analytics and software engineering experience. The Cost Accounting Manager will ensure the proper application of cost accounting guidance for public market scrutiny. This is a management position which requires confidence in presentation, setting goals, and directing the work of others. We expect to grow a team focused on cost with a particular emphasis on systems and automation, that puts us at the top of the leaderboard for clarity, correctness, transparency and speed of reporting as a global tech company. Location: This role is home-based in EMEA time zones, with the option to be based in our London office. It will report to our Global Financial Controller. Key responsibilities • Collaborate with Procurement, Commercial Systems, Operations,Legal, and FP&A teams to design and maintain scalable cost management, allocation and reporting processes. Drive process and configuration improvements in NetSuite and related systems for end-to-end automation of the cost life cycle • Incorporate industry best practices, emerging technology and AI to raise the bar for reporting speed and accuracy • Review and evaluate the cost recognition impact of non-standard expenses • Lead, mentor and develop a team of cost accounting and systems accounting professionals • Assist in month-end cost close, including reconciliations, fluctuation analysis, and other reporting • Develop and maintain effective internal controls over cost transactions and reporting • Research and write accounting memos on significant cost recognition and other related policy matters • Act as the primary liaison with external auditors in matters related to purchase-to-pay. • Ensure compliance with internal policies and regulatory requirements while identifying and managing cost accounting risks.
