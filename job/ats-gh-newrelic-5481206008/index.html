Your opportunity New Relic is seeking an Infrastructure Engineer to join our IT Engineering team, with a focus on network infrastructure and enterprise AI enablement. You will build and operate the networks that keep our global workforce connected and secure, and you will help deploy AI tools that make the company faster. This is an engineering role built around automation, scalability, and reliability, with real ownership over the platforms you run. We are looking for a systems thinker who automates repetitive work, writes clear documentation, and makes sound tradeoffs between security, usability, and speed of adoption. You will partner with Security, Compliance, Facilities, IT Support, and Engineering teams, and you will have room to lead projects and shape the roadmap. What you'll do You will own and improve core network infrastructure across our global offices while driving the technical rollout and governance of AI platforms across the company.
