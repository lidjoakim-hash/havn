This role combines specialist knowledge of engine performance, hardware component specifications and embedded software calibration. You will lead a defined technical area, guide complex engineering activities, and ensure calibration datasets are correctly configured, synchronised and released. You will also influence technical methods, tools and product roadmaps while sharing knowledge and strengthening engineering capabilities across the organisation. Key Responsibilities Engine Performance & Calibration Drive engine performance and calibration development. Calibrate embedded software function datasets according to defined requirements and operating conditions. Support calibration activities involving engines, exhaust aftertreatment systems (EATS), drivelines and vehicle functions. Contribute to performance development in line with verification plans. Lead a defined technical area and provide expert guidance on complex engineering challenges. Hardware Component Specification Select feature-related hardware components and define detailed specifications. Work with specifications such as FIEspec, turbo specifications and camshaft profiles. Evaluate hardware features and their impact on engine performance and calibration. Ensure alignment between hardware characteristics and embedded software calibration requirements. Dataset Management & Delivery Coordinate calibration dataset synchronisation and configuration. Manage the release of complete calibration dataset deliveries. Ensure compliance with verification plans and established development processes. Maintain reliable and controlled dataset configurations throughout the development lifecycle. Technical Leadership & Improvement Recommend improvements to engineering methods, tools and working practices. Provide technical input to product and system roadmaps. Build and maintain technical networks across the organisation. Share specialist knowledge, mentor engineers and support capability development. Identify opportunities to improve calibration practices and engineering capabilities. Required Technical Expertise Extensive experience in engine performance development and calibration. Calibration of embedded software functions. Strong understanding of powertrain systems, including engines and drivelines. Knowledge of exhaust aftertreatment systems (EATS). Hardware component selection and detailed specification. Understanding of hardware and software calibration interactions. Caronisation and release management. Verification planning and calibration development processes. Engineering methods, tools and continuous improvement. Senior Expert Profile Typically, 12+ years of relevant professional experience with recognised expertise in the discipline. The successful candidate should demonstrate: Recognition as a technical expert, internally and externally. Ability to lead complex technical activities and influence engineering decisions. A strong track record of shaping technical practices and development approaches. Experience mentoring, educating and developing other engineers. Awareness of industry developments, emerging technologies and engineering trends. An established professional network.
