Your mission At Bitpanda, we are redefining how Europe invests. The Global Creative Studio is the creative engine behind all internal and external brand communications spanning 360° marketing campaigns, product launches, performance ads and brand storytelling. We are seeking a Senior Creative Copywriter (German) to join our cross-functional team of designers, 3D artists, and AI-savvy art directors. In this role, you will define the voice of Bitpanda across our different markets and translate complex financial concepts into bold, clear, and high-converting creative concepts. If you are an agency-seasoned wordsmith looking to trade client decks for true brand ownership, cutting-edge AI tools, and high-visibility campaigns, this is your next step.
