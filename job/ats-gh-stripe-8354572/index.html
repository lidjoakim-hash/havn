Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies - from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups - use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team The Government Relations & Public Affairs team represents Stripe to governments, regulators, industry partners, and broader stakeholder audiences. We help the company understand and adapt to a changing global policy environment, anticipate and respond to challenges, and advocate for the needs of our users and the online economy. The team works closely with Policy Strategy, Legal, Communications, Marketing, and regional product and go-to-market teams to ensure coordinated, consistent engagement worldwide. What you’ll do Stripe is looking for a Public Affairs Campaigns Lead to build our profile in the policy landscape across EMEA, with a particular focus on the EU, its Member States, and the UK. Working closely with colleagues in Government Relations, Communications and Marketing, you will help turn Stripe’s policy priorities into compelling campaigns, develop thought leadership, and deliver policy events that reach the right audiences and shape the conversation. This person will be a truly great campaigns operative, someone willing to roll up their sleeves and who prides themselves on executing with excellence. You will help make Stripe’s ideas and expertise, and our users’ experiences, more visible and impactful - building a distinctive, credible voice for Stripe among policymakers and the wider policy community. If you are up for the challenge, we would love to hear from you!
