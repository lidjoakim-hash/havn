Veritaz is a leading IT staffing solutions provider in Sweden, committed to advancing individual careers and aiding employers in ensuring the perfect talent fit. With a proven track record of successful partnerships with top companies, we have rapidly grown our presence in the USA, Europe, and Sweden as a dependable and trusted resource within the IT industry. Job Overview Role Summary Our client is looking for an experienced DevOps / AI Specialist to bridge the gap between business teams, developers and cloud platform engineering. In this role, you will build and maintain secure, scalable and efficient environments that enable employees to develop, test and deploy AI-powered applications and services. You will play a key role in driving AI adoption across the organization by developing cloud infrastructure, automating deployment processes, implementing AI Governance practices and enabling teams to transform innovative ideas into production-ready solutions. Key Responsibilities Design, build and maintain secure and scalable AI development and deployment environments. Develop and manage cloud infrastructure using GCP and AWS. Implement and maintain containerized applications using Docker and Kubernetes. Build and manage CI/CD pipelines using GitHub Actions. Automate infrastructure provisioning and configuration using Terraform. Develop automation tools, integrations and backend services using Node.js. Establish and implement AI Governance, security and compliance frameworks. Implement monitoring, logging, alerting and operational processes. Manage application, model and data versioning. Evaluate new AI initiatives for technical feasibility, security and scalability. Collaborate with developers, editorial teams and platform engineering teams. What You Bring Several years of experience as a DevOps Engineer, Cloud Engineer, Platform Engineer or in a similar role. Strong hands-on experience with Docker and Kubernetes. Practical experience implementing CI/CD pipelines using GitHub Actions or similar tools. Solid knowledge of Terraform and Infrastructure as Code. Good programming skills, preferably in Node.js. Experience working with public cloud platforms, primarily GCP and preferably AWS. Understanding of AI Governance, security and responsible AI practices. Experience building and maintaining secure, scalable cloud infrastructure. Strong troubleshooting, automation and problem-solving skills. Qualifications & Experience The successful consultant should have several years of experience in DevOps, Cloud Engineering or a similar role, with strong practical knowledge of Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform and CI/CD pipelines. The role requires experience with GCP and AWS, good programming skills (preferably Node.js), understanding of AI Governance and security practices, and the ability to build, automate and maintain secure, scalable cloud-based environments.
