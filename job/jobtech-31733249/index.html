Customer Experience Leader

Ingka Services AB · Malmö, Sweden

💰 ca. 310 000–360 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 27.11.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Who you are We are looking for a customer-focused leader who understands customer needs, behaviours and what creates exceptional customer experiences. You are passionate about creating value for customers and enjoy turning insights into improvements across markets and customer touchpoints. You thrive in collaborative environments, bringing people together around shared goals and translating customer insights into actions that drive meaningful business impact. You are comfortable navigating complexity, setting priorities and mobilising people and resources to deliver results. With a balanced approach, you can drive progress in short term while building capabilities and sustainable value for the future. To succeed in this role, we believe that you bring: Experience working with Voice of Customer programs, customer feedback management and customer journey development Strong analytical skills and the ability to translate qualitative and quantitative insights into actionable recommendations and measurable outcomes. Experience collaborating across functions, stakeholders and markets in a complex environment. Strong communication and relationship-building skills, with the ability to influence and create alignment A proactive and collaborative approach, with a passion for continuous improvement and customer value Key requirements: Extensive experience from roles within customer facing business Experience leading teams, projects or strategic initiatives focused on development and change A proven ability to drive improvements in organizations operation across both physical and digital customer channels Strong stakeholder management skills and experience building cross-functional collaboration Excellent communication skills in English Ability to set direction, navigate complexity and deliver results through others Your responsibilities As part of this role, you will help how IKEA understands, develops and improves customer experiences across the global omni ecosystem by: Drive the development and continuous improvement of customer experiences across the market omni ecosystem Translate customer feedback, behavioural data and market insights into priorities, recommendations and measurable business outcomes Identify and prioritise opportunities end-to-end customer journeys to improve customer satisfaction, loyalty and business performance Partner with markets and cross-functional teams to ensure customer-centric solutions are developed, implemented and scaled effectively Contribute to the development and adoption of the Omni Experience Framework and ways of working across markets Monitor customer experience performance, share best practices and support a culture of continuous learning and improvement Act as a trusted partner and SME in customer experience and Voice of Customer methodologies. Together as a Team Within Group Commercial, the Customer Experience team is responsible for understanding customer needs, measuring experiences and driving improvements across the omni ecosystem. We work closely with markets, digital, commercial and operational teams to ensure customer insights influence strategic decisions and business development. Together, we are committed to creating simpler, more relevant and more enjoyable experiences for customers while supporting profitable growth for IKEA. Apply now! This is a permanent role located in Malmö, Sweden. Ingka co-workers will be prioritized in this recruitment process. Last day for application is November 27th
