Group Strategy Specialist

Nobina Sverige AB · Stockholm, Sweden

Nobina Group, the largest public transport operator in the Nordics, is looking for a driven and ambitious Strategy & Business Development Lead who wants to help shape the future of our business. In this role, you will work closely with our executive management to drive strategic development, business growth and long-term value creation. It is an exciting opportunity for someone who enjoys complex challenges, turning insights into action and making a real impact. The Role & Your Impact As Group Strategy Specialist, you will drive the development of Nobina’s group strategy and business planning process, working closely with our CEO, CFO and executive management to translate strategic ambitions into concrete plans and initiatives. You will coordinate the executive management agenda, prepare strategic analyses and decision-making material for executive management and Board meetings, lead group-wide projects and support potential M&A opportunities. Key Responsibilities: Drive the development of Nobina’s group strategy and business planning process Coordinate the executive management agenda in collaboration with the CEO and CFO Prepare strategic analyses and decision-making material for executive management and Board meetings Lead strategic, group-wide and cross-functional projects Support the evaluation and development of M&A opportunities What You Bring We are looking for an ambitious and analytical person with a strategic mindset and the ability to turn ideas into action. You are comfortable navigating complex challenges, combine a hands-on approach with a strong business perspective and enjoy building relationships and engaging stakeholders across the organisation. You are also flexible, solution-oriented and motivated by driving change and achieving results. To succeed in this role, we believe you have: 4–5+ years of experience as a management consultant or within strategy, business development or project management in a relevant organisation Proven experience leading projects and collaborating across functions Experience presenting and engaging with senior management and board-level stakeholders A university degree in Business, Engineering or a related field Strong digital proficiency and experience using digital tools to support analysis and collaboration Fluency in both Swedish and English, verbally and in writing Why Nobina At Nobina, we are committed to creating an inclusive work environment where every employee feels valued. Our leaders take responsibility, make courageous decisions, and consistently embrace an outside-in perspective to meet the needs of our passengers. By being business-driven and focusing on the bigger picture, we strive to develop our operations and create sustainable solutions for the future. Our culture is built on respect, care, and responsibility, and we constantly seek new perspectives to drive innovation.
