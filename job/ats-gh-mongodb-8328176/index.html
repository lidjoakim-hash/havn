MongoDB is looking for a Senior Product Manager to define and execute the strategy for our Developer and Agentic Experience across MongoDB Atlas which is our best-in-class, fully managed, multi-cloud database service. In this role, you will shape how developers, platform teams, and AI agents build on and operate Atlas through programmatic interfaces such as APIs, CLI, Agent Skills, and Infrastructure as Code (IaC). You will turn complex infrastructure operations into intuitive, reliable, and increasingly automated experiences that accelerate customer adoption and drive Atlas growth. You will own the vision, strategy, and 12-month roadmap, including how Atlas evolves to support AI agents. AI agents are changing how teams build and operate cloud infrastructure, and this role will shape how Atlas leads that change. We are looking to speak to candidates who are based in Dublin for our hybrid working model.
