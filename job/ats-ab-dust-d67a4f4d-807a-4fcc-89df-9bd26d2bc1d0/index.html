THE ROLE Dust is growing fast, and we want every person who joins us to feel welcome, supported, connected and able to do their best work. As our People Experience Manager, you'll shape how people experience Dust, from their first day and the moments that bring us together to the way our offices in Paris, London, New York and San Francisco support the team. We're looking for someone who is operationally excellent and creatively ambitious. You'll handle the details carefully, and you'll also look for ways to make the employee experience much better. One day you might be redesigning part of onboarding, the next planning a company offsite, acting on survey feedback, or working with a site lead to improve one of our offices. This role works as a partner across the company. You'll work most closely with People Operations, who lead on the core people processes. Together, you'll make sure the employee experience is well run behind the scenes and thoughtful in how people actually experience it. In Paris, you'll also work closely with our Office Manager, who runs our HQ on site and leads much of our day-to-day office life and events. In London, New York and San Francisco, you'll coordinate with local site leads. You'll also work with IT, Finance, Legal, Marketing, leadership and outside partners. Your guiding principle: consistency where it matters, local adaptation where it creates a better experience. WHAT YOU WILL DO: OWN THE EMPLOYEE JOURNEY - Own the onboarding experience end to end, working closely with our People Ops Specialist, from pre-boarding and equipment to first-day logistics and the first few weeks, so new joiners can contribute from day one in every location. - Work with People Ops, IT, Finance, hiring managers, our Paris Office Manager and site leads so every handoff in the employee journey is smooth. - Partner with People Ops and IT on offboarding, so people leave with the same care they got when they joined. - Be a trusted point of contact for everyday employee questions and needs, and know when to bring in People Ops. BUILD CULTURE AND COMMUNITY - Run and improve our team rituals, including weekly lunches, team drinks, dinners and company gatherings. - Organise one or two company offsites or retreats each year. - Create moments that help people connect, celebrate progress and feel they belong, across offices and time zones. - Pay attention to the details that make experiences feel personal and inclusive, such as dietary needs, accessibility, preferences and team milestones. - Partner with People and Marketing on selected external and brand-building events.
