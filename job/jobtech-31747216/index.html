Finance & Compliance Operations Specialist Join Gigapay and help build the financial and compliance infrastructure behind the creator economy. This is a hands-on operations role with real ownership across payments, bookkeeping, support and compliance, with the opportunity to document and automate how the business scales. About the role Gigapay helps brands and agencies work with creators globally in a fast, compliant way and without operational chaos. As a Merchant of Record, we handle onboarding, compliance, reporting and payouts so companies can scale creator programmes across markets. As our Finance & Compliance Operations Specialist, you will run the daily operational work that keeps payments moving accurately and customers supported. You will match incoming client payments, provide final sign-off on creator payouts, manage balances across accounts and currencies, and resolve exceptions such as failed, reversed or duplicate payouts. You will also handle support cases within our four-hour response target, support agency onboarding, complete KYC and right-to-work checks, and manage correspondence with Swedish authorities. Alongside daily operations, you will keep bookkeeping and bank reconciliation accurate and turn recurring cases into clear procedures, templates or automations with Tech. This is not a back-office administration role. You will have direct impact on customer experience, financial accuracy and the operational foundations of an international fintech as it grows. What we are looking for Required: • Ability to work from our office in Gamla Stan, Stockholm, three days per week. • Hands-on bookkeeping experience, including supplier invoices, transfers between accounts, currency exchanges, bank charges and bank reconciliation. • Fluent written and spoken Swedish and English. • Confidence using AI tools, spreadsheets, admin interfaces and a bookkeeping system. Nice to have: • Bookkeeping experience in Sweden. • Experience from fintech, payments, KYC/AML or compliance. • Experience with multi-currency payments or international banking. • Professional contact with Swedish government agencies. • Basic legal or contract-administration experience. What we offer Competitive salary plus equity. You will work from our Gamla Stan office in a hybrid setup, three days per week, and gain broad exposure to the engine of a fintech scaling internationally. You will have meaningful ownership over how finance, compliance and operations are documented and improved. Practical information Stockholm, hybrid. Full-time, permanent position. Office presence three days per week in Gamla Stan. Gigapay is partnering with Assistor24 on this hire to keep the process fast and transparent. We review applications as they come in and may hire before the deadline, so apply early.
