About the position We are seeking a researcher in anaerobic microbiology with good technical and programming skills to work on a project related to mixotrophic gas fermentation. The aim of the project is to develop novel methods for enriching carbon-efficient microorganisms and characterising their metabolism. Your background We are looking for a candidate with practical experience in anaerobic microbiology and bioinformatics methods, such as the analysis of metagenomic sequencing data and metabolic modelling. Strong technical skills are also required, as the position involves the construction, further development and troubleshooting of bioreactors. Experience in developing AI-based systems using large language models, such as retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems, is a strong merit. Such experience would also enable the successful candidate to contribute to an additional project focused on developing an AI-based advisory tool for farm-scale biogas production. The candidate is expected to independently lead the experimental and analytical work within the project, collaborate with other researchers in the group, and supervise students. Qualifications PhD in biotechnology, microbiology or another relevant field Knowledge of anaerobic microbiology and metabolic modelling Practical experience in bioprocesses and associated technical skills Very good written and oral English skills Good programming skills in R or an equivalent programming language Experience in software development Knowledge of machine learning or other data-driven approaches to the analysis of biological datasets Experience in supervising PhD students Additional qualifications Experience in developing AI systems based on large language models Very good written and oral Swedish skills Knowledge of biogas production Assessment criteria The assessment will be based on the applicant’s scientific competence and experience relevant to the research area of the position. Considerable emphasis will also be placed on personal qualities, particularly the ability to collaborate and communicate effectively with other members of the research group and the wider scientific community.  About us The position is based at the Department of Molecular Sciences at the Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences (SLU) in Uppsala. The project will be carried out within the Microbial Biotechnology research group. Please visit our https://www.slu.se/en/departments/molecular-sciences/ for more information. Read more about our benefits and what it is like to work at SLU on our https://www.slu.se/om-slu/jobba-pa-slu/.
