Group Business Controller

Elekta AB (publ) · Stockholm, Sweden

We don’t just build technology. We build hope for everyone dealing with Cancer. Elekta is shaping the standard of care, together with healthcare providers, by developing precision radiation medicine that brings advanced radiotherapy into routine clinical practice. Our solutions support consistent, efficient and personalized care, addressing evolving patient needs. Through adaptive radiotherapy, precision treatment delivery and integrated workflows, Elekta supports care teams across a range of clinical settings. Each year more than 2 million patients are treated using Elekta solutions across over 130 countries. Elekta is headquartered in Stockholm, Sweden, with over 4,000 employees and offices in more than 40 countries. The company is listed on Nasdaq Stockholm. For more information, visit elekta.com. What You’ll Do At Elekta As a Group Business Controller, you will play a central role in our Group Business Control function, helping provide senior management with clear, reliable financial insights that support informed decision-making. You will lead and coordinate group-wide planning and forecasting, analyse financial performance and key business drivers, and work closely with regions, business lines and the wider finance community. Through strong financial analysis and business partnering, you will help strengthen performance, improve the quality and consistency of reporting, and drive greater cost awareness across the Group. Responsibilities Coordinate the Group budget and forecast cycles, including consolidation, challenging assumptions and explaining changes in outlook. Analyse and quality assure Group financial performance against budget, forecast and prior periods, identifying key revenue, margin, expense and cash drivers. Lead performance reviews with regions and business areas, agree follow-up actions and track progress. Prepare concise financial reports, insights and decision material for senior management and the Board of Directors. Develop Group performance measures, reporting definitions and processes to improve consistency and comparability across the business. Act as a finance business partner to senior stakeholders, providing analysis on costs, strategic initiatives, risks and opportunities. Support a reliable monthly close and contribute financial analysis and quality review to interim, annual and other external reporting. Partner with Finance and IT to improve financial BI applications, data quality and reporting processes. What You’ll Bring Bachelor's degree in finance, economics, industrial engineering or a similar field. At least 5+ years of experience in financial analysis, business planning, forecasting and performance management. Experience with financial reporting, consolidation, planning and BI systems. Ability to translate complex financial information into clear, concise insights and decision material for senior stakeholders. Strong stakeholder management and business partnering skills, with the ability to work effectively across regions, business lines and functions. High attention to detail and sound financial judgement. Ability to challenge assumptions constructively and turn financial analysis into practical recommendations. A collaborative approach and the ability to build effective relationships across a global finance organisation. Fluency in both English and Swedish is preferred. What You’ll Get 30 paid vacation days per year. 4 additional Elekta days off. Defined contribution pension scheme. Private Medical Insurance. Lunch Benefit. Contribution to wellness activities. A collaborative office environment four days a week, working alongside talented colleagues in a supportive and engaging workplace. Application process We look forward to receiving your application. Please submit your CV in English via the “Apply” button. We review applications on an ongoing basis, so we encourage you to apply as soon as possible.
