Operations Manager

CRS Laboratories AB · Skellefteå, Sweden

💰 ca. 420 000–580 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 22.11.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

CRS Laboratories is looking for an Operations Manager for Sweden to oversee the operations of our Swedish site and manage projects and customer relationships in Sweden and Norway. Key responsibilities: - Managing the PhotonAssay installation project - Managing the day-to-day operations of CRS’s Björkdal laboratory - Personnel management (approximately 6 employees) - Managing projects based in Sweden and Norway - Managing customer relationships and acquiring new customers Initially, the role will focus on delivering the PhotonAssay project, which is expected to take approximately six months. The other responsibilities can be arranged flexibly alongside this initial priority. We expect you to have: - A proven track record in project management - Experience in a supervisory role - Fluency in Swedish and English - Relevant industry experience or education (e.g. chemistry, geology) - Strong communication and networking skills This is a permanent, full-time position. The position is based at CRS’s site in Kåge. Flexible remote working arrangements are possible initially, but regular on-site presence will be required within a few months. For further information about the position, please contact our COO, Daniela Vesterbacka, at daniela.vesterbacka@crs.fi, +358 44 749 9711. Please send your application and CV in English by email to minttu.sipovaara@crs.fi. Be sure to include your salary expectations in your application. We use written aptitude assessments as part of our recruitment process. CRS Laboratories Oy is a laboratory company founded in 1994, with its main laboratory located in Kempele, Finland. The company specialises in sample preparation and analytical services for the mineral exploration and mining industries.
