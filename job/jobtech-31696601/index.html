At Tata Technologies, we make product development dreams a reality by designing, engineering, and validating the products of tomorrow for the world's leading manufacturers. Due to our continued growth, we are now recruiting for an Electronic Hardware Engineer to join our team in Gothenburg, Sweden. We are looking for an Electronic Hardware Engineer to join our team and contribute to the development of advanced electronic systems within the automotive domain. In this assignment, you will collaborate closely with development teams, test environments, and suppliers to ensure high quality and robustness in our ECU platforms and electronic hardware. Responsibilities Design, review, and develop advanced electronic hardware for automotive systems. Support requirement development, including requirement definition, analysis, review and follow-up with suppliers. Assist the team by breaking down complex hardware and electronics topics into clear, actionable information. Participate in technical discussions with stakeholders and external suppliers. Support test teams and rig environments when needed, including troubleshooting, verification, and technical analysis. Systemization of HW capabilities and interface alignments. Modularization of future component set-up. EMC expertise for the team. Process development around the DCU (Door Control Unit) component's system. Advanced Engineering, investigating emerging technologies. Critical Skills & Experience We are looking for someone with: Proven experience in advanced electronic hardware design and development. Proven experience in mechatronics design and ECU design for such loads would be a big plus. Strong capability in requirements engineering – creation, analysis and review. Ability to decompose complex technical topics into structured, useful data for decision-making. Experience driving technical discussions with suppliers and navigating topics across teams. Why You? You are a skilled hardware engineer with the right mindset and a strong technical foundation. You have demonstrated the ability to: Support cross-functional teams across different ECU projects. Manage both ongoing issues and new development topics simultaneously. Contribute actively to technical dialogues, troubleshooting, and analysis in collaboration with testers and rig engineers. Drive hardware-related discussions both with suppliers and internal stakeholders. Why Join Tata Technologies? If you are passionate about bringing innovation to the projects you work on and want to help shape the future of automotive electronic systems, we would love to hear from you. Tata Technologies: Engineering a better world. Tata Technologies would like to thank all applicants for their interest; each application will be reviewed against the set criteria for the role. We would like to advise that only candidates under consideration will be contacted. If you do not hear from us within 10 working days following the closing date, it will mean that unfortunately your application has not been successful. We will however retain your details for any suitable future opportunities.
