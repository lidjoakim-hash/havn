Who we are We simplify wealth creation. Founded in 2014 in Vienna, Austria by Eric Demuth, Paul Klanschek and Christian Trummer, we’re here to help people trust themselves enough to build their financial freedom — for now and the future. Our user-friendly, trade-everything platform empowers both first-time investors and seasoned experts to invest in the cryptocurrencies, crypto indices, stocks*, precious metals and commodities* they want — with any sized budget, 24/7. Our global team works across different cultures and time zones, bringing our products to more than 7 million customers, making us one of Europe’s safest and most secure platforms that powers modern investing. Headquartered in Austria but operating across Europe, our products are built by fast-moving, talented, “roll-up-your-sleeves-and-make-it-happen” kind of people. It’s these diverse perspectives and innovative minds operating as ONE TEAM that keep Bitpanda at the cutting edge of our industry. So if you’re someone who thinks big, moves fast and wants to make an impact right from day one, then get ready to join our industry-changing team. Let’s go! Your mission As the Manager, Growth Analytics at Bitpanda, you will build the core decision engine for our redesigned Growth organization. Your mandate is to establish trusted measurement, define clear customer economics, and deliver the high-impact analysis that dictates where the company invests. With direct access to decision-makers, you will build an analytics capability that materially shapes our customer acquisition, retention, and commercial outcomes. What you'll do • Set the Growth measurement strategy and operating model, establishing trusted, company-wide definitions for CAC, payback, LTV, cohort performance, and incrementality. • Lead forecasting, performance reviews, and decision-grade analysis to drive faster, more reliable, and economically grounded capital-allocation choices. • Build and mentor a high-performing team of analytics engineers and senior analysts. • Partner closely with Finance, Data, Product, and channel leaders to establish source-of-truth reporting and automate critical data workflows. • Elevate the quality of our experimentation and make insights widely accessible across the organization without compromising on strict analytical standards. Who you are • You have a proven track record of building or leading an analytics capability that goes beyond basic reporting to actively change commercial and product decisions. • You are technically credible in SQL, data modeling, experimentation, and measurement, with the confidence and expertise to challenge weak analysis. • You possess a deep understanding of acquisition economics, cohorts, incrementality, and forecasting to set the gold standard for the company. • You excel at creating clarity from fragmented data, prioritizing the few specific models and metrics that materially improve business outcomes. • You are a strong cross-functional leader capable of guiding analysts while fostering tight partnerships with Data, Finance, Product, and channel owners.
