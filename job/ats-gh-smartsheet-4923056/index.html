The GTM Enablement Team provides customer-facing leaders and reps with the learning materials, content, and resources to drive growth and success. Partnering with the sales team and broader field enablement team, the Senior Regional Enablement Manager - EMEA will develop and execute impactful, data-driven enablement and readiness solutions for transforming the customer engagement process, sales messaging, sales tools and other specialized content within the region. Success is measured by the overall business impact of these solutions in driving behavioral change and business outcomes through cross-functional collaboration. This role reports to the VP Revenue Enablement, and is based at Smartsheet in London, UK.
