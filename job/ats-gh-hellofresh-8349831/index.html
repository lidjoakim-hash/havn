The role: What's in the Box Are you passionate about food safety, supplier excellence, and driving quality standards across international supply chains? We’re looking for an experienced FSQA Manager for Produce Herbs and Mushrooms to join our international Food Safety & Quality Assurance team in Amsterdam. You’ll take full ownership of this category, working with suppliers across Europe and with partners in Procurement, Sourcing, Supply Chain, and Distribution Centres across multiple markets. What you’ll do: The Recipe • Lead food safety and quality management for the herbs & mushroom categories across international suppliers. • Conduct on-site supplier audits and approval visits (Europe) to ensure compliance with EU food safety legislation and HelloFresh standards. • Lead onboarding of new suppliers and products, ensuring full compliance with food safety, legal, traceability, and quality standards. • Drive strategic quality improvement initiatives to reduce complaints and improve customer satisfaction. • Own supplier performance metrics including complaints, out-of-spec deliveries, compliance KPIs, and corrective action follow-up. • Investigate and manage quality incidents using structured root cause analysis and ensure robust corrective and preventive actions (CAPA). What you’ll bring: The Ingredients • A science-based degree (e.g. Food Science, Biology, Microbiology, Chemistry, or equivalent) is required. • 3+ years of experience in Food Safety / Quality Management within the food industry. • Experience in production, processing, or supply chains is highly preferred. • Proven experience auditing food manufacturers (GFSI standards such as BRC, IFS, FSSC 22000). • Solid understanding of EU food safety legislation and international supply chains. • Excellent English skills (written and spoken). • Willingness to travel in Europa; approximately 20% travel expected. • Strong communication with the ability to influence suppliers across different cultures. • Pragmatic, solutions-oriented, and confident in decision-making, even under pressure. • Comfortable working in a fast-paced, international environment with multiple stakeholders. • Experience working across multi-market organizations is a plus. • Intermediate to advanced Google Suite / MS Office skills (Excel/Sheets, PowerPoint/Slides, Word/Docs). Above all, we are looking for people who will make HelloFresh better. There are many different ways of developing skills, and we love diverse experiences! So even if you don’t “tick all the boxes” but think you’d thrive in this role, we would really like to learn more about you. Are you the missing ingredient? If this sounds like a tasty opportunity, we’d be excited to hear from you. We aim to review your profile and respond within 5 business days.
