The Opportunity As Category Manager, you will lead the development and execution of category strategies for High Voltage Breakers supporting HVDC projects worldwide. The role offers a unique combination of strategic sourcing, market engagement, supplier relationship management, tender support, technology understanding, and business development. Working at the interface between Supply Chain, Sales, Engineering, Project Execution, and Technology Development, you will help ensure that category strategies support both immediate project needs and long-term business objectives. You will be the key driver of the category roadmap, translating market intelligence, supplier capabilities, and business requirements into actionable strategies that strengthen competitiveness, ensure supply security, and support business growth. The role requires strong communication, stakeholder management, and influencing skills to align diverse stakeholders, manage complex business situations, and drive decisions that create measurable business value and outcomes. How You'll Make an Impact Develop and implement category strategies aligned with business growth, technology, roadmaps, and market developments. Develop and maintain a long-term category roadmap, ensuring alignment with business priorities and future market needs. Build and manage supplier relationships to secure capacity, innovation, quality, and competitiveness. Support commercial and tendering activities through market intelligence, cost analysis, supplier insights, and sourcing strategies. Drive market intelligence activities, including supplier analysis, technology trends, capacity outlook, competitive dynamics, and cost drivers to support strategic decision making. Drive early supplier involvement and leverage supplier expertise to support technology and business objectives. Lead supplier negotiations, contract management activities, and business reviews to maximize value creation. Identify and execute cost optimization, localization, and supply chain improvement initiatives. Monitor market trends, emerging technologies, competitive developments, and supply chain risks to support informed business decisions. Translate market trends, supplier capabilities, and business needs into actionable category plans. Influence and align stakeholders across Sales, Engineering, Project Management, Operations, and SCM to support business objectives and successful project execution. Lead and navigate complex business challenges while driving accountability, progress, and measurable business outcomes. Drive supplier performance improvement initiatives related to quality, delivery reliability, sustainability, innovation, and operational excellence. Contribute to successful HVDC project execution through effective supply chain planning and supplier engagement. Live Hitachi Energy's core values of safety, integrity, collaboration, and continuous improvement. Your Background Bachelor's degree in Engineering (preferable), Supply Chain Management, Business Administration, or related technical field. Several years of experience in one or more of the following areas are required: Supply chain management (Strategic sourcing, Category management) High-voltage products and systems (key differentiator) Technical sales Product management Bid and proposal management Understanding project environments, industrial markets, customer requirements, technology trends, and commercial drivers. Ability to influence stakeholders across functions and organizational levels. Experience managing supplier relationships, commercial negotiations, contracts, and business performance. Proven experience developing category strategies, roadmaps, and long-term supplier strategies. Strong market intelligence and analytical capabilities with the ability to convert insights into business actions and sourcing strategies.
