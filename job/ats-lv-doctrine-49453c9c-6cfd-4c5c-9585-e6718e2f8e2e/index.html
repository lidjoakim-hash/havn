Our Mission ⚖️ At Doctrine, we're building Europe's leading legal AI platform. Our belief? Law shouldn't be slowed down by repetitive tasks. It should be augmented by a specialised AI, designed to understand, analyse and produce legal reasoning at the highest standard. Our ambition? To help legal professionals work with greater reliability, efficiency and peace of mind, through a single, comprehensive and secure platform. Today, 27,000 legal professionals across five countries (France, Italy, Germany, Spain and Luxembourg) trust us to do just that. Our growth is guided by a clear approach: a global vision, grounded in a deep understanding of local specificities. Behind this ambition, a team of around 250 people, united around one goal: supporting lawyers and legal professionals at every stage of their work, from research to drafting. And we're just getting started. The context Following rapid and profitable growth in France, Doctrine has expanded to Italy and Germany, then acquired Maïté, a Spanish legaltech company, at the start of 2026.  We are now looking for a Machine Learning Engineer to keep improving our legal AI for the Spanish market. You will work on models and pipelines that process Spanish legal documents, and your work will directly shape the product roadmap for Spain. Please note: the position is based in Paris, in our offices in the 18th arrondissement. You can find details about our full stack on GitHub. Good to know: prior professional experience in the legal field is not required. However, being eager to commit and build expertise in understanding legal documents is essential.
