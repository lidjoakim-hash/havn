Senior Application Engineer HVAC Offshore Wind

Hitachi Energy Sweden AB · Västerås, Sweden

💰 ca. 570 000–690 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 18.2.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

The opportunity As a Senior Application Engineer for HVAC Offshore Wind, you will join a global team shaping reliable, stable, and high-quality power systems. Working closely with customers, consultants, and cross-functional teams, you will understand challenges, develop optimized solutions, and support growth aligned with our Grid & Power Quality Solutions and Service (GPQSS) strategy. You will act as a trusted technical advisor, influencing technology direction, customer engagement, and business development activities across AC Offshore Wind applications. The role also includes building competence in offshore wind configuration optimization to support future market growth. This position is based in Västerås or Stockholm, Sweden, or Mannheim, Germany. How you’ll make an impact Engage early with customers to understand power systems needs, identify opportunities, and build long-term relationships with focus on technology and optimization of OSW configurations. Support Business Development and Sales teams in developing HVAC Offshore Wind solutions tailored to customer requirements. Develop optimized, cost-effective solutions across our Offshore Wind portfolio. Represent Application Engineering in cross-functional teams, ensuring stakeholder alignment and successful solution integration. Coordinate complex technical inputs, including new technologies, throughout the business development and sales cycle. Manage technical risks, issues, and changes while ensuring clear communication and mitigation plans. Drive strategy, innovation, and continuous improvement of Offshore Wind processes, tools, and methodologies. Promote GPQSS offerings and ensure compliance with safety, quality, and business processes. Your background Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Power Electronics, or a related field. Extensive experience in application engineering, business development, or lead engineering within HVAC Offshore Wind. Experience in Onshore or HVDC Offshore Wind is a plus. Experienced in guiding teams, coordinating complex projects, and fostering a high-performance culture, Good understanding of grid codes, compliance requirements, and power system studies. Knowledge of power system analysis, including steady-state, RMS, and EMT simulations using relevant software tools. Expertise in Offshore Wind project configurations, including WTGs, electrical infrastructure, structural design, control systems, and plant control strategies. Proven ability to engage customers, build long-term relationships, and balance technical and commercial perspectives in international environments. Fluent in English, additional languages are an advantage. Willing to travel internationally, circa 20%.
