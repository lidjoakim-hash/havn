About the Role Abnormal is growing fast, and the way we show up in the world has to keep pace. Our Strategic Events are where our story stops being a pitch and starts being an experience, where CISOs and practitioners walk in curious and walk out convinced. This role will support the strategic events programs across EMEA, including major third-party events, flagship first-party events, and high-profile executive events. As Strategic Events Specialist, EMEA, you'll bring new ideas and experiences to how we create impact across our events program. You're someone who thrives on a great project plan, keeping deadlines tracked and on target, and managing multiple stakeholders at once across different time zones. Your communication is second to none, and you're always thinking one step ahead when it comes to delivering an event, pre-event and on-site. You understand the importance of an event running smoothly, and love learning as you go to optimise for the future. This role is perfect for somebody looking for their next step up: a hands-on IC role where you’ll play a lead role in some of our largest flagship events across EMEA, and pitch in on general team tasks day to day. You'll run established event formats independently and apply good judgement within known processes, while leaning on your manager for guidance on new or first-of-their-kind activations. If that sounds like your kind of thing, we want to hear from you!
