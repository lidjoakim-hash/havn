Associated Project Electrician

Hitachi Energy Sweden AB · Ludvika, Sweden

Job Description Join our dynamic project team to handle electrical and optical connections in our test facilities at HVDC. You will be involved in testing the control and protection systems for our HVDC systems and contribute to our development efforts. This role offers opportunities to work abroad and requires responsibility for electrical works and ensuring electrical safety in assigned projects within our test facility. Do you want a challenging and exciting job in a varied environment? – Gun Norling, Team Manager FST Operations - HVDC Control & Protection Operations We are interested in learning more about you and what you can contribute to so don’t hesitate to apply even though you don’t fulfil all requirements. How you’ll make an impact Responsible for electrical work and electrical safety in assigned project in our test facility You will be part of a project team that makes all electrical and optical connections in our test facilities. You will be testing the control and protection system for our HVDC systems. You will be part of our development as a team but also your individual development. Since we are a global company, we offer the opportunity to work abroad. Your background You hold a high school education as electrician or similar. Previous work as an electrician are required You are interested in technical solutions and curious to learn new things. Learning by doing - you are a good team player with an open mindset. Fluent in English, knowing Swedish is a plus More about us We believe great work thrives in an environment where people feel genuinely supported and fairly rewarded.
