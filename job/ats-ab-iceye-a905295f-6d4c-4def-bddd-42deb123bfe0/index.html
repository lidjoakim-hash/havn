ROLE HIGHLIGHTS: - Role: Power Electronics Engineer - Department: HAPS - Location: Valencia, Spain - Reports to: HAPS Avionics Team Lead - Working type: Hybrid - Employment is subject to applicable security screening (incl. SUPO, where required) THE MISSION OF THE POWER ELECTRONICS ENGINEER As Power Electronics Engineer on the HAPS program, you will contribute to the design of the power subsystem of ICEYE's stratospheric electric aircraft. The role is hardware focused, spanning circuit design, lab bring-up, and test. The engineer will own defined subsystems end to end, from requirements definition through schematic design, hardware bring-up, verification testing, and documentation for readiness review. WHO ARE WE? ICEYE delivers space-based intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance (ISR) capabilities to governments and allied nations. This includes sovereign and turnkey ISR missions leveraging ICEYE's world-leading synthetic aperture radar (SAR) satellite technology, as well as access to data from the world's largest SAR satellite constellation. These capabilities enable partners to detect and respond to critical changes anywhere on Earth with unprecedented speed and accuracy, day or night and in any weather, supported by ultra high-resolution imagery and high-frequency revisits. As a trusted partner for defense, intelligence, security, and maritime domain awareness, ICEYE's near real-time data creates a tactical advantage for mission-critical operations. ICEYE operates internationally with offices in Finland, Poland, Spain, Japan, the UAE, Greece, Germany, and the US. The company has more than 900 employees, inspired by the shared vision of improving life on Earth by becoming the global source of truth in Earth Observation. DAY TO DAY RESPONSIBILITIES: - Battery testing and characterization: cell and pack level testing, including capacity, impedance, and cycling measurements, and support for safety qualification testing (e.g. short circuit and thermal runaway testing) - Solar array integration: defining and validating the electrical interface between the solar array and the power bus, including protection circuitry and bring-up testing - Power electronics design: designing and validating protection and power conversion circuits, including the bus tie unit (an electronically controlled high current protection device with current sensing and MCU level telemetry and control) and DC-DC converters - System verification: board level bring-up and system integration testing to confirm the power subsystem performs to specification under nominal and fault conditions - Documentation: producing and maintaining design documentation, test reports, and requirement compliance records REQUIREMENTS: MUST HAVE SKILLS - Demonstrated experience in power electronics design for battery powered and/or solar powered systems, including familiarity with DC-DC converter topologies - Practical experience with hardware bring-up, debugging, and verification testing of power electronics using standard laboratory equipment (oscilloscopes, electronic loads, power supplies, battery cyclers) - Experience with lithium-ion battery systems, including characterization, safety limits, and thermal behavior - Technical grounding in solar/photovoltaic array integration, including electrical interfacing of a variable voltage source to a power bus - Proficiency with schematic capture and PCB design tools, preferably Altium Designer - Experience with embedded systems as applied to power electronics, including telemetry acquisition and control interfaces between power hardware and a microcontroller - Strong technical writing ability, with experience producing design documentation, test reports, and requirement traceability records - Ability to work independently against defined requirements while coordinating effectively within a small technical team NICE TO HAVE SKILLS -
