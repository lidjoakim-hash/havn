Here’s what this particular role covers. 🛠️ About our Fleet Operations Engineering Teams   Our Fleet Operations Engineering team ensures the seamless operation, reliability, and safety of our autonomous vehicle fleet. As a vital part of the business, the team is responsible for rapid fault diagnosis, vehicle support, and process optimisation in high-impact environments. Field Engineers work closely with global teams, driving efficiency through hands-on technical problem-solving and collaboration.   🧠 Your day-to-day   - Diagnose and resolve vehicle software and hardware faults onsite to maintain fleet uptime - Perform rapid, structured fault triage, ensuring a 5-minute response time - Follow standardised diagnosis and repair protocols, maintaining efficiency and compliance - Collaborate with global field engineering colleagues to coordinate technical support and escalate issues - Communicate clearly with the Field Engineering Lead regarding repair progress and shift handovers - Participate in on-call rotations and provide technical coverage across multiple regions - Maintain strict adherence to safety and compliance standards for all repairs - Support ongoing improvements by identifying issues and providing actionable feedback   🧩 What you’ll be working on   - Performing onsite diagnostics and repairs to keep the autonomous fleet operational - Delivering rapid technical support and fault resolution under defined SLAs - Collaborating with engineers and cross-functional teams to troubleshoot challenging issues - Ensuring proper documentation and communication for smooth transitions between shifts and locations - Enhancing operational processes and contributing feedback for continuous improvement - Adhering to and promoting strict safety protocols and compliance standards - Using platforms like Datadog, Mender, and JIRA for monitoring, diagnostics, and reporting - Participating in a shift rota and on-call schedule, covering weekends and multiple time zones   🙌 You should apply if   - You hold a full German driving license - You have strong experience in vehicle diagnostics, fault triage, and repair—preferably in automotive, fleet operations, or ADAS technology - You excel at troubleshooting and resolving technical issues quickly (meeting strict SLAs) - You communicate clearly and collaborate well with global and cross-functional teams - You are rigorous about safety, incident management, and compliance to operational procedures - You thrive in fast-paced, evolving environments and are flexible for on-call/weekend shifts - You are familiar with diagnostic tools, software monitoring platforms (e.g., Datadog, Mender), and ticketing systems like JIRA - You are fluent in both English and German   Desirable - Bachelor’s degree (B.Sc. or equivalent) in Automotive, Mechanical, Electrical/Electronic Engineering, Computer Science, Robotics, or a related technical field - Knowledge of ADAS testing, safe vehicle-operation procedures, and high-voltage/EV certification - High-voltage/EV experience and practical diagnostic skills with electric vehicles - Experience identifying recurring issues and improving operational processes for safety and efficiency   Additional Requirements: -
