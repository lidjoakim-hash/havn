About the job As a Engineering Consultant focusing on Materials Engineering within Surface Materials at Condesign Engineering, you work on exciting assignments at some of the world’s leading technology companies. Together, we identify customer assignments with our many customers that interest, challenge and motivate you in an environment where you can both thrive and grow. As a consultant, you can work on different assignments over time, and you get valuable career development. We are growing and are now in need of additional talented employees. About the role, Role description: Condesign is seeking a Materials Engineer that will be working with surface materials within the automotive industry. You will be part of the product development and work with both internal stakeholder as well as with external suppliers to refine and develop different types of surface materials for various projects. Project management and coordination, supplier management and interaction, reading and reviewing test reports, colour measurement together with decision making is a big part of the work. Travels to national and international suppliers will most likely be part of the role. Your Profile and Required skills: Engineering Background Master of Science in Materials Engineering, preferably focusing on Surface Materials Automotive industry experience Surface materials and surface treatment experience (vinyl, leather, textile, plastic, paintwork/clear coat) Colour measurement experience Test reports creation and test reports interpretation for decision making and approvals Project management & coordination skills Supplier Management Familiar with Agile way of working Fluent in English, both spoken and written. Swedish is meritorious. Driver’s license B Willing to travel for supplier visits. Desired skills: Experience in supplier manufacturing environment Material lab environment experience Experience in Konica Minolta Spectra Magic KOLA and other AB Volvo related system tools Available within 1 month’s notice Personal attributes You have a positive and open attitude, are curious and have a drive to improve and deliver at the top. You are also a good team player who likes to achieve results both as a team, organization and individually. About Condesign Our team of approximately 150 dedicated consultants brings a broad range of experience from diverse industries to help our customers achieve success today and in the future. We are committed to innovation and growth and strive to provide the best possible service to our customers. Condesign is a privately owned engineering company that provides customers with high-quality services and innovative solutions throughout the entire product life cycle, from R&D and design to aftermarket.
