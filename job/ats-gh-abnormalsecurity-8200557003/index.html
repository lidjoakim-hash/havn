Regional Director, Enterprise Sales This is your moment Sweden. Norway. Denmark. Finland. Four markets, one region, and a team of elite sellers who are already winning. Abnormal is one of the fastest growing cybersecurity companies in the world, and we are looking for a Regional Director to take the Nordics to its next level. Every CISO in the region is facing the same problem: attackers are using AI to get past defences built for a different era. Abnormal exists to change that. You will be the leader who gets into the deals, runs to the fire, and builds on a team that is already formidable. This is not a role for someone who manages a forecast from behind a screen. It is for a leader who would rather be in the room with a CISO than in a meeting about the meeting. Your mission Overachieve the Nordics new annual recurring revenue (NARR) quota by winning enterprise accounts (2,000+ mailboxes) from first conversation to signature, and by growing them long after. Along the way, you will: • Take an elite team and make it even better, through coaching, inspiration and an unrelenting standard. • Be hands on in the deals that matter, side by side with your Account Executives. • Spend your time where the revenue is: with customers, prospects and partners. • Build a channel in the region from the ground up, with your own hands. • Make the whole region move together, leading marketing, SDR and channel through a matrix model. How you will lead Hands on, always. You love getting into the deals with your AEs. You shape the strategy, join the critical calls, open doors at CISO, CIO and CTO level, and help close complex, multi-stakeholder deals from $200K+ through to $1M+. Inspire the team. You lead with energy and example. You know what great looks like because you have done it, and you make the people around you want to do it too. Run to the fire. When a deal is wobbling, a customer needs you or a competitor shows up, you are the first one there. Thrive in velocity. We are low friction on process and high on pace. You enjoy agility, you make decisions quickly, and you are comfortable in a dynamic, Slack-based working culture where the best ideas move at the speed of a message. Be external first. You will spend more time out in the market than in internal meetings, because that is where the pipeline and the relationships are. What you will own • A programmatic sales playbook. Run a disciplined, repeatable approach to selling, embedded in the 3-Whys and MEDDPICC. Inspect deals with rigour, forecast monthly and quarterly with credibility, and lead Quarterly Business Reviews that drive action. • The team. Develop each Account Executive in the areas that matter most to their skills and NARR results. Recruit when the region needs it, and keep a bench of future talent warm so you are never caught short. • The matrix. Hold responsibility across channel, marketing and SDR in the region, bringing them together behind one plan to generate pipeline and accelerate new business. • The channel. Build strategic relationships with existing channel partners and open up new ones. This is a hands-on builder role, not a hand-off. • The customer. Maintain strong relationships with key customers in the region, focused on value realisation, timely renewals, rapid expansion and referrals. • The proof. Partner closely with Sales Engineering to deliver outstanding demonstrations and a repeatable proof-of-concept and technology validation programme. • The voice of the market. Be the voice of the customer and prospect inside Abnormal, with Sales Engineering, the POV team, Product and Marketing, so the right things get prioritised and more revenue gets closed. What you bring You are a proven winner who holds yourself accountable to consistent over-achievement, and who is hungry to build on something already strong. • 10+ years of enterprise sales experience, with a track record of exceeding quota selling security, networking and/or software.
