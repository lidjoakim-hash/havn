Social Media Content Creator — Urban OM Title: Social Media Content Creator Company: Urban OM AB, Wallingatan 20–22, Stockholm Scope: Part-time — ~8h/week Reports to: Studio Manager & Event Manager Start: as soon as possible Equipment: Own camera/phone WE'RE HIRING! SOCIAL MEDIA & CONTENT CREATOR AT URBAN OM Stockholm · One day a week Are you creative, passionate about storytelling, and love yoga? We might be looking for you! At Urban OM, we're looking for a creative and enthusiastic person to help us capture the energy, people and everyday magic of our yoga studio in the heart of Stockholm. We want someone who loves creating beautiful, engaging content, has an eye for photography and video, and knows how to bring stories to life on social media — both on our own channels and in the ads we run to reach new people. ABOUT URBAN OM We're possibly Stockholm's largest yoga studio — over 2,000 visits a month. Three rooms in the middle of town, a broad schedule of yoga, dance, meditation and breathwork, and a growing community. A place people come to move, breathe, land and meet each other — and where they get to be exactly as they are. WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR Someone who loves filming, photography and creating engaging Reels and social media content. A creative eye for aesthetics, storytelling and capturing authentic moments. Someone who understands Instagram and current social media trends. Someone who can also create ads for social media — the content we put money behind to reach new people in Stockholm. A genuine interest in yoga, wellness and community. Someone who enjoys being around people and wants to become part of our studio family. Someone who is independent, full of ideas and excited to help our community grow. WHAT WE OFFER A creative and inspiring environment surrounded by wonderful teachers, students and a community passionate about yoga and wellbeing. You'll get to be part of the everyday life at Urban OM, create content around our classes, workshops, events and teachers, and of course, practice yoga with us! We're looking for someone who wants to be more than just behind the camera. Someone who genuinely enjoys the yoga world and wants to grow with us. SOUNDS LIKE YOU? Send us a little introduction about yourself, your experience and some examples of content you've created. Write to us at jobs@urbanom.se. We'd love to hear from you! Urban OM | Stockholm WE BELIEVE That our purpose in life is to be exactly who we are and to be all that we can be. That we need to embrace not just the splendours of life but the short comings too. That our joy, our wellbeing, our peace is all within. We ought to then not ask what the world can do for us but what we can do for the world. And the answer is at the very least to spread the joy, to contaminate our surroundings with uncompromising truth, positive vibes and heart warming energy, to explore, look around with the curiosity of a child's eyes, run, laugh, dance, love, empathise, encourage, uplift, share. And say thanks!
