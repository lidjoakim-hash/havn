Warehouse Employee

Crisp · Amsterdam, Netherlands

💰 ca. 43 000–51 000 EUR/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)HeltidDirekte fra arbeidsgiverFrist 2.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

At Crisp, we are on a mission: making better-tasting food accessible to everyone, using the power of the shortest farm-to-table supply chain in a way you can be proud of. As a Warehouse Employee, you ensure that the ultra-fresh products from our suppliers are processed in the warehouse, and then pack them up so they can head out to the customer! WHAT YOU WILL BE DOING As a Warehouse Employee, you work alongside your colleagues to ensure our customers' products are packed to perfection. You will be active throughout the day, with a varied set of tasks: - Scanning and collecting products in the warehouse, ensuring all groceries are neatly packed and chilled before they go out to our customers. - Restocking products, accurately sorting orders, and efficiently preparing shipments. - Part of our grocery selection is kept cold; you don't mind working regularly in a chilled environment of 3–6°C or in the freezer at -26°C. ABOUT YOU - You are available at least 32 hours per week - You work with great attention to detail. - You are reliable. - You have good communication skills. - You enjoy physically active work. - You are currently living in (or close to) Amsterdam. WHAT WE OFFER - An hourly wage up to €17.99. - A solid pension plan for the future via BPFL. - A fixed percentage of your salary to spend freely via Alleo (e.g., Zalando, Swapfiets, gift cards, courses). - A referral bonus when you bring in new "Crispies." - And... a substantial discount on your own Crisp groceries, because you deserve to eat well at home too.
