At Toyota Material Handling Europe, we create the technology that keeps the world moving. With over 13,500 colleagues, we are passionate about supporting companies of all sizes in meeting today's and tomorrow's material handling challenges. We recognize that our industry is essential-and often critical-to everyday life and society as a whole. From our headquarters in Mjölby (Sweden) and Willebroek (Belgium), to our sales companies across Europe and our factories in France, Italy, and Sweden, you can be part of an extraordinary journey. Together, we are moving the world towards easier, more sustainable solutions. The Position At Toyota Material Handling Europe, we are now looking for a structured and proactive Project Coordinator Manager to join our strong team within Service & Logistics department. We have a very active role in all development projects around the R&D centers in Europe and in this role, you will manage the complete lifecycle of the products - from concept and development through industrialization and production launch until the usage of our products at our customer sites, all from an aftermarket perspective. Together with your colleagues you will provide valuable input to ensure that aftermarket requirements are captured, and you will follow up on key activities to secure successful project outcomes. You will work in cross-functional project teams where you will play a central role in ensuring that all spare parts data and information are accurate, complete, and available throughout the project lifecycle. You will report to the Manager of Parts Project Coordination Your responsibility Participate as a core team member in product development projects, representing the Service & Logistics function and ensuring requirements and activities are identified, coordinated, and followed up. Collaborate closely with multiple departments across Toyota Material Handling, including Project Management, Product Management, R&D, Purchasing, Production, Order Management, and Aftermarket. Manage spare parts related activities together with key suppliers, including external truck manufacturers producing products under Toyota brands (OEM). Keep the organization informed and engaged regarding project status, milestones, and ongoing activities. Follow the Engineering Change process in development projects, ensuring aftermarket requirements are validated and implemented in ERP systems. Ensure all spare parts are accurately documented in Spare Parts Catalogues and available for ordering through ERP systems (SAP). Work cross-functionally to continuously improve processes, ways of working, and collaboration across departments. Ensure compliance with TMHE policies, guidelines, and requirements. Your Profile Excellent communication and presentation skills, with the ability to influence, collaborate, and build strong relationships at all levels of the organization. Well organized and detail oriented, with the ability to manage multiple priorities in a structured and efficient manner. Proactive and driven, with a continuous improvement mindset and a passion for developing both processes and ways of working. Experience working in cross-functional teams and collaborating across larger organizations. Experience or knowledge of Toyota Material Handling products is considered an advantage. Fluent in Swedish and English, both written and spoken. Experience working with systems such as SAP, Enovia, and the Microsoft Office suite. A degree in Engineering, Logistics, Supply Chain Management, or equivalent professional experience. Our Offer At Toyota Material Handling Europe, we're more than a global leader in a fast-paced industry-we're a collaborative team driven by innovation, continuous growth, and creating real value for our customers. Our diverse and international environment offers a workplace where your ideas are valued, and your contributions can make a meaningful impact.
