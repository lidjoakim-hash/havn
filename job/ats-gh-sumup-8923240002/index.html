Account Executive Manchester, United Kingdom Team description SumUp is growing its sales presence in Manchester, helping local shops, cafés and businesses find payment solutions that fit their needs. As one of our first Account Executives in the region, you'll own your pipeline, win new business and build relationships with merchants across Greater Manchester. This role combines outbound sales with face-to-face customer meetings, giving you the independence to develop your territory while working closely with our London team. And through SumUp's partnership with Manchester United, you'll also have opportunities to use matchday experiences to connect with local merchants. Manchester and the surrounding area • Use our Manchester United partnership, including opportunities to invite merchants to matches, to strengthen commercial relationships • Manage your targets and
