Finance & Project Manager

OmniMod · Fornebu, Norway

💰 ca. 620 000–750 000 kr/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia NAVFrist 13.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

A structured all-rounder who will help scale our financial processes and lead deployment projects from contract to handover. Relevant experiences • Minimum 2 years in financial controlling, accounting, audit, or FP&A. • Proficient with P&L, balance sheets, cash flow, and advanced Excel. • Experience leading complex projects with external customers, suppliers, and stakeholders. • Professional fluency in both Norwegian and English (written and spoken). • Working as a high-agency, structured and detail-oriented team player. • Willingness and availability to travel to customer sites during project deployment and handover phases. Nice to have • Background from robotics, automation, or hardware-focused companies. • Experience with ERP/accounting tools (e.g. Tripletex, PowerOffice, Xledger). • Experience with contracts, basic commercial law, or supply chain procurement. • Combined technical and financial education (e.g. Industrial Economics) or PM certification. Build the financial & operational backbone of next-gen logistics As our Finance & Project Manager, you will be the ultimate organizer who bridges finance, procurement, and operations. You will support our strategic growth by streamlining processes and improving execution across finance, supply chain, and delivery to our customers. • Financial Control Own day-to-day cost follow-ups, reconciliations, budget vs. actuals, and support monthly P&L reporting. • Supply Chain Link Track purchasing costs, manage POs, and sync supply chain data directly with accounting. • Project Leadership Drive signed deployment projects from contract to handover, tracking milestones and managing budgets. • Process Automation Map, streamline, and automate manual finance and reporting workflows to help us scale. • Legal & Contracts Support contract management by coordinating supplier/customer contracts and maintaining the obligations register. A peek under the hood You will work with modern financial and operational software, including ERP and accounting systems (such as Tripletex, PowerOffice, or Xledger), advanced Excel modeling, and project management tools to build a centralized, automated reporting structure that connects finance, supply chain, and deployment tracking.
