About the Role Oyster’s Go-To-Market organisation is scaling fast, with an ambitious growth agenda that spans new markets, new products, and a step-change in brand and execution. Our Chief Revenue Officer needs a strong partner to help the GTM leadership team turn that agenda into disciplined, connected, day-to-day execution. As Business Manager for the GTM organisation, reporting to the CRO, you will work directly with the VPs of Sales, Marketing, Partnerships, and RevOps to keep the organisation running smoothly, moving quickly, and pulling in the same direction. You will be equally comfortable in the detail of an operating cadence and in the judgement calls of a cross-functional strategic project, and you’ll take real ownership of outcomes, not just process. What You’ll Do Translate strategy into a GTM plan - Partner with GTM leadership to turn company strategy into a GTM plan (OKRs and key initiatives), driving focus across Sales, Marketing, Partnerships, and RevOps. - Run a regular cadence to track progress, sharpen “what’s next”, and drive accountability. Design the initiatives GTM needs to hit the plan - Identify and help design the cross-functional initiatives that matter most. - Bring structure and rigour to ambiguous problems, and know when to escalate versus solve it yourself. - Draw on your knowledge of other GTM organisations to shortcut to what works. Drive execution of the highest-stakes initiatives - Own select initiatives end to end (scoping, resourcing, running, and reporting on results) while bringing the GTM teams around you along. - Push initiatives from decision to delivery, not just decision to plan. Run the operating rhythm - Build and run the GTM operating rhythm, including leadership meetings, business reviews, planning cycles, and QBRs, so GTM executes as one team. - Partner day-to-day with the VPs of Sales, Marketing, Partnerships, and RevOps as a trusted advisor, spotting and closing operational gaps early. - Diagnose friction between teams (broken handoffs, misaligned priorities, duplicated work) and broker fast, pragmatic fixes.
