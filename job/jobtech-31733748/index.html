Job description Junior Expert – Respiratory Viruses Surveillance (Consultant Assignment) Location: Stockholm, Sweden (European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control – ECDC) Start Date: Mid-January 2027 Duration: Initial 3-month contract (maternity leave cover, with strong potential for extension) Application Deadline: November 25th, 2026 Are you passionate about public health, epidemiology, and infectious disease surveillance? Randstad Life Sciences is currently looking for a Junior Expert to join the Respiratory Viruses Section at the European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control (ECDC) in Stockholm. This is an exciting opportunity to contribute to European health security and work at the forefront of zoonotic respiratory virus monitoring in an international, scientific environment. About the Assignment As a Junior Expert consultant, you will support the Respiratory Viruses Section in monitoring, assessing, and reporting on respiratory pathogens, with a specific focus on zoonotic influenza. The assignment covers a maternity leave starting in mid-January 2027, with an initial 3-month order and an expected extension thereafter. Key Responsibilities: Support event-based surveillance of respiratory viruses and the ongoing monitoring of zoonotic respiratory viruses. Assist in updating risk assessments regarding avian influenza for humans within the EU/EEA. Contribute to the drafting and compilation of avian influenza monitoring reports. Maintain and update records of zoonotic influenza cases. Provide input and support to colleagues regarding indicator-based surveillance of respiratory viruses, specifically related to potential zoonotic influenza events and data from individuals exposed to animals with avian or swine influenza. Qualifications and Experience To succeed in this role, you bring a solid scientific foundation combined with strong analytical and communication skills. Required Competences: Education/Background: A university degree or relevant professional experience in public health, epidemiology, disease surveillance, or respiratory viruses. Domain Knowledge: Good understanding of epidemiology, communicable disease prevention and control, zoonoses (particularly respiratory viruses), and the One Health approach. Surveillance & Data: Prior understanding and some hands-on experience with communicable disease surveillance, data collection, and scientific reporting. Technical Skills: Very good proficiency in Microsoft Office (Excel, Word, PowerPoint). Language & Communication: Very good command of English, alongside excellent written and spoken communication skills. What We Offer At Randstad Life Sciences, we specialize in matching scientific talent with leading organizations. As a consultant with us, you will have a dedicated Consultant Manager supporting you throughout your assignment, competitive employment terms, and the chance to gain invaluable experience within an EU agency. How to Apply If you are ready to take the next step in your public health career, please submit your application as soon as possible, as selection is ongoing. Important Application Instructions: Our application system only allows you to upload one single file. Therefore, you must merge the following two documents into one combined PDF or Word file before applying: Your CV in English (preferably using the Europass CV template). A Motivation Letter in English (maximum 1 page) clearly outlining your job-specific expertise and the personal characteristics you bring to this role. Applications missing either document or not merged into a single file cannot be processed for client submission. We look forward to receiving your application!
