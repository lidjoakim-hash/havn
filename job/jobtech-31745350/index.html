Technical Program Manager

Hitachi Energy Sweden AB · Västerås, Sweden

💰 ca. 450 000–550 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 16.2.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

The opportunity Are you an experienced program leader with a passion for governance, strategic execution, and software-driven innovation? At Hitachi Energy, we are developing advanced solutions that support the modernization and digitalization of the global energy system. We are now looking for a senior Technical Program Manager to lead complex technical programs, govern product and release deliveries, and ensure successful execution across multiple stakeholders and disciplines. In this role, you will be responsible for overseeing strategic programs and release initiatives, ensuring alignment with business objectives, technical requirements, governance frameworks, and financial targets. A key part of the position is to drive program governance through established gate models, monitor risks and dependencies, and enable informed decision-making throughout the program lifecycle. You will work closely with software development teams, engineering, product management, finance, and senior leadership to ensure successful program execution This role is ideal for a senior project or program management professional who combines attention to detail with the ability to see the bigger picture, navigate complexity, and drive strategic outcomes in a fast-paced and highly collaborative environment. How you’ll make an impact Lead large-scale technical programs and release initiatives from planning through execution and successful delivery. Own program governance and ensure compliance with established stage-gate and release management processes. Coordinate cross-functional teams across software development, engineering, operations, product management, and business functions. Facilitate program roadmaps, schedules, budgets, resource plans, and governance structures. Drive effective risk management by proactively identifying, assessing, and mitigating program risks and dependencies. Monitor program performance through key milestones, deliverables, financial forecasts, and quality objectives. Support strategic decision-making through data-driven analysis, reporting, and stakeholder alignment. Communicate program status, risks, and recommendations to senior leadership and executive stakeholders. Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives, including adoption of digital tools, AI-driven capabilities, and best practices in program management. Your background Bachelor's or Master's degree in Engineering, Computer Science, Information Technology, Business, or a related field. PMP certification is a requirement. Minimum 10 years of experience leading complex technical projects and programs, with a proven track record of delivering results in large and cross-functional organizations. Strong experience from software development environments and a solid understanding of software development lifecycles, release management, and software engineering concepts. Experience working with structured governance models, stage-gate processes, and program steering frameworks, with the ability to balance strategic thinking and risk management with attention to detail. Strong financial acumen, including experience with forecasting, budget management, Estimates at Completion (EACs), and financial analysis. Hands-on experience with project and program management tools such as Azure DevOps and Jira. Experience with Monday.com is considered an advantage, as are advanced Excel skills. Experience from the energy sector is highly valued. Knowledge of network control systems, grid automation solutions, or related technologies is considered a strong advantage. Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to influence senior leaders and drive alignment across teams. Experience applying AI-enabled solutions is considered a plus, and fluency in English is required.
