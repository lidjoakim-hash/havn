ROLE HIGHLIGHTS: • Manufacturing Technician, Unmanned Aircraft (Fabrication and Integration) • Location: Valencia, Spain • Department: HAPS • Reports to: Head of Manufacturing • Employment type: Permanent • Workplace model: Onsite • Employment is subject to applicable security screening (incl. SUPO, where required)   WHY THIS ROLE MATTERS: As Manufacturing Technician on the HAPS team, you will be hands-on at the heart of building something genuinely unprecedented — a stratospheric, unmanned electric aircraft that will set a new mark in aviation history. Your work spans the full breadth of assembly and integration: fabricating lightweight carbon fibre composite structures, building cable harnesses and high-density battery packs, integrating avionic subsystems, and running end-to-end ground tests to verify that every system is ready to fly. Beyond the build itself, you will play an active role in developing and refining the assembly and testing processes that will carry this programme from early prototypes into a mature, repeatable production flow — working closely with engineers to transition new designs from drawing board to reality.     WHO WE ARE ICEYE delivers space-based intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance (ISR) capabilities to governments and allied nations. This includes sovereign and turnkey ISR missions leveraging ICEYE’s world-leading synthetic aperture radar (SAR) satellite technology, as well as access to data from the world’s largest SAR satellite constellation. These capabilities enable partners to detect and respond to critical changes anywhere on Earth with unprecedented speed and accuracy, day or night and in any weather, supported by ultra high-resolution imagery and high-frequency revisits. As a trusted partner for defense, intelligence, security, and maritime domain awareness, ICEYE’s near real-time data creates a tactical advantage for mission-critical operations. Designed for dual use, the platform also serves civil protection and commercial users for natural-catastrophe intelligence, insurance, maritime monitoring including oil-spill detection, and finance, contributing to global security and community resilience. ICEYE is headquartered in Finland and operates globally across Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. We have more than 900 employees, united by a shared vision: improving life on Earth by becoming the global source of truth in Earth Observation.   YOUR DAY-TO-DAY RESPONSIBILITIES - Assembly of large, but very lightweight unmanned aircraft - Fabrication of lightweight aircraft structures/components and building CFRP composite parts - Development of new assembly, integration and testing processes, and tools for an ultra-light UAV - Manufacturing of electronics assemblies and sub-level components (such as cable harness manufacturing and battery manufacturing of state-of-the-art high power density batteries) - Transitioning new designs from engineering to manufacturing   WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR Must haves: - 2+ years of relevant experience as an electrical/electromechanical/electronic technician with a hands-on approach - Experience with mechanical prototyping (3D printing, using basic mechanical workshop equipment) - Basic knowledge of CAD tools - Good command of the English language, both oral and written Nice to haves: - Hands-on experience in aircraft, robot, race car or similar system prototyping & manufacturing - Good systems and physics understanding - Experience with composites - Experience working with electronic equipment (prototype boards, harness, test setups) - Development of process improvements and setups to improve cycle times, repeatability, and maturity - Proactive, can-do attitude - Curiosity and passion about technology   APPLICATION PROCESS • TA Partner Screen Call • Hiring Manager Interview • Technical Interview • Values & Fit Interview • SVP of Engineering Interview   WORKING AT ICEYE At
