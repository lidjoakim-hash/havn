You will find more information about working at NTNU and the application process here.    Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v&#61;_KHQjc4ndas&t&#61;41sAbout the position Are you motivated to take a step towards a doctorate and open up exciting career opportunities? As a PhD Candidate with us, you will work to achieve your doctorate, and at the same time gain valuable experience that qualifies you for a further career in higher education and research, in and outside academia. Candidates with strong interest in metallurgical processes holding MSc degree in materials science/process metallurgy are invited to apply. The PhD candidate will be a part of a research group of about 20 PhD candidates and Postdocs. The PhD candidate will also work closely with research institutions like SINTEF and the Norwegian Mn-ferroalloy industry. The candidate will work on optimizing the control and handling of off-gases from Mn-alloy production for increased energy efficiency and future carbon capture technologies. Your immediate leader will be the Head of Department, and your supervisor will be Prof. Gabriella Tranell. Duties of the position In the Norwegian PhD degree 4 courses must be passed in addition to the research work. The research work will be of experimental nature including literature review, writing of papers and the final PhD thesis. Required selection criteria • You must have a professionally relevant background in material science or process metallurgy and experience in high temperature experimental work. • Your education must correspond to a five-year Norwegian degree program, where 120 credits are obtained at master's level • You must have a strong academic background from your previous studies and an average grade from the master's degree program, or equivalent education, which is equal to B or better compared with NTNU's grading scale. If you do not have letter grades from previous studies, you must have an equally good academic basis. If you have a weaker grade background, you may be assessed if you can document that you are particularly suitable for a PhD education. • You must meet the requirements for admission to the faculty's doctoral program  • A fundamental part of the work will be the reporting of the results, and the candidate must be experienced in writing journal articles.  • Good written and oral English is compulsory. The appointment is to be made in accordance with NTNUs guidelines for recruitment positions for general criteria for the position. Preferred selection criteria • Working- or academic experience from Mn alloy production is preferred • Knowledge of Norwegian or a Scandinavian language is required for teaching duties Personal characteristics • The candidate must be able to work independently and progress in the research work by own achievements.  • He/she must be creative in solving experimental challenges.  • It is expected that the candidate has a strong research-ethical approach.  Emphasis will be placed on personal qualities. Salary and conditions In the position of PhD Candidate, code 1017, your gross salary will normally be NOK 580 000,-per annum. A 2% statutory contribution to the State Pension Fund is deducted from the salary.  The period of employment is 3 years, with the possibility of up to one-year extension with 25% teaching duties in agreement with the department. Appointment to a PhD position requires that you are admitted to the PhD programme in Material Science within three months of employment, and that you participate in an organized PhD programme during the employment period.  The position is conditional on external funding. As an employee at NTNU, it is important that you keep yourself up to date with academic and organizational changes and adapt to them. For the necessary professional and social interaction, it is a prerequisite that you are physically present and available to the institution on a daily basis. The appointment is carried out in accordance with the principles of the State Employees Act, and Export control (legislation that regulates the export of knowledge, technology and services). Candidates who, after assessment of the application and attachments, are considered to bein conflict with the criteria in the latter act, will not be able to be employed.
