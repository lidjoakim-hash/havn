For over 20 years, Smartsheet has empowered teams to manage work seamlessly and scale solutions smarter. Now, in our most ambitious chapter yet, we are uniting human teams with AI agents. As we continue building this operation, we’re looking for talented and motivated Software Engineers who are passionate about continuous learning and excited to grow in a fast-paced, innovative environment. Our core platform includes a legacy Java monolith alongside a growing ecosystem of microservices, primarily built in Java and Go (with a small number of Kotlin services). On the front end, product teams that deliver UI experiences use React. You will work remotely from Bulgaria and will be reporting to an engineering leader.
