The challenge at the centre of this role is the distance between a demo and a product. A model that works in a notebook is not a feature that works for hundreds of millions of players, or a tool a game team trusts with their daily work. Help us Close that gap: the evaluation that tells you it's actually good, the latency and cost envelope that makes it viable, the failure modes you find before players do, and the plumbing that makes it boring to operate. We're not looking for someone who wants to train a frontier model. We're looking for someone who gets unreasonable results out of the ones that exist. We are looking for someone to build and ship: AI features in live games, and the internal tools that give superpowers to Supercellians. Some of that work will take you inside a game or central team for a while, close enough to their craft to build the right thing with them. In our team, you'll work closely with our AI Platform Engineers, AI/ML engineers, Clients Programmers, Cloud Engineers. You’ll also collaborate with other teams including Data, QA, Engine and game teams.
