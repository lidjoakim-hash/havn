WHY THIS ROLE EXISTS   We are about to hire the people who will define the next phase of this company. Not backfill a plan someone else wrote — define it. The quality of who joins Dust over the next eighteen months is the single highest-leverage variable in whether we build the category or watch someone else do it.   That work needs a recruiter who treats hiring as a craft rather than a queue. Someone who can hold a real point of view on what "great" looks like for a role, defend it with a hiring manager who disagrees, and then go find that person themselves — from a cold start, without a lead gen team, a sourcing pod, or a playbook waiting for them.   You'll be an early member of a small Talent team. That means you own roles end to end, and you also help build the thing itself: the assessment design, the interview loops, the calibration, the data we use to know whether any of it is working.   We're looking for someone who cares about the candidate experience for all parties internal and external. And prioritizes protecting Dust's culture by being thoughtful, thorough and leading by example in being a true team player.     WHAT YOU'LL OWN   Full-cycle hiring, end to end. You run your own searches from role kickoff to close. Sourcing, outreach, screening, interviewing, offer strategy, negotiation. You are the owner of the outcome, not a coordinator of other people's opinions.   Assessment design, not just assessment delivery. For every role you open, you help define what evidence would actually predict success — then you build the loop that surfaces it. You'll push back on interview stages that exist out of habit and replace them with ones that test something real.   Hiring manager partnership with a spine. You'll spend as much time calibrating hiring managers as you do talking to candidates. When a manager says "not quite the right feel," you'll ask what they mean until there's a defensible answer, and you'll say so when there isn't one.   Market intelligence. You'll know your talent market better than anyone else in the building — who's good, where they are, what moves them, what compensation actually looks like right now — and you'll bring that back into how we scope roles and design offers.   The candidate experience. Our hiring process is the first honest sample of what working here feels like. You'll make it expedient, direct, well-communicated, and worth a candidate's time whether or not they get an offer.   Building the function. Process, tooling, structured scorecards, funnel data, quality-of-hire signal.
