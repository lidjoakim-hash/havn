Work with HelloFresh in Warsaw and its HelloTech organisation, HelloFresh’s global technology backbone with more than 1000 people, building the digital products that power our end-to-end food experience. From meal kits and ready-to-eat meals to specialty offerings like pet food and premium meat & seafood, HelloTech creates the platforms that bring tailored food solutions to millions of customers every month. Our subscription-based, direct-to-consumer model relies on technology at every step, from customer-facing apps and personalization logic to pricing, forecasting, supply chain optimization, and initiatives that help reduce food waste. While our brands operate independently to serve distinct customer needs, they are united by shared platforms, data, and operational excellence built by HelloTech. HelloTech works in autonomous, cross-functional alliances, each owning a specific product or domain end to end. By working with our Warsaw office, you will help shape scalable, data-driven products used across our markets, working with a modern tech stack and international teams to continuously improve how people discover, order, and enjoy HelloFresh’s products, today and in the future. About the Team: Behavioral Data & Experimentation is a cross-functional platform team with end-to-end ownership of digital tracking across all markets globally at HelloFresh: from tracking infrastructure all the way to curated data products and the hypothesis validation mechanisms (e.g., A/B testing). Our customers are the engineering and product teams across HelloFresh, so reliability, automation, and developer experience are central to what we build. The squad brings together software engineers, data engineers, and data scientists to build the tracking, event, and statistical infrastructure that drives product decisions. Our work enables customer experience improvements and business growth. About the Role: What's in the Box As a Senior Mobile Engineer, you'll own and evolve the mobile app tracking architecture that powers behavioral tracking and experimentation across HelloFresh's brand portfolio. Your core platforms are Android (Kotlin) and React Native. You will also contribute to iOS and Web codebases as the work requires, alongside teammates with deep expertise on those platforms. AI is part of our daily work: you'll use Claude, Zed, and Opencode across design, development, and testing, and help the team adopt new practices as they emerge. Above all, we want people who make the team stronger. Even if you do not meet every requirement, we would still like to hear from you. What you’ll do: The Recipe At HelloFresh we are moving away from a model where software developers just execute tickets toward one where product engineers are trusted to own customer problems.
