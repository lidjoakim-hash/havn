About Anthropic Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems. About the role The Anthropic Residency is our paid, fixed-term research program, with placements lasting between six weeks and six months. We are looking for people who have led ambitious work in a variety of technical fields and want to transition to full-time work focused on making sure AI systems are safe and beneficial for all of humanity. If you are a professor in a quantitative discipline, a technical founder or a staff-level engineer and want to transition to work on transformative AI, we want to hear from you. Background in machine learning is not required. What to expect • A concrete, scoped project matched to your background, with direct mentorship from Anthropic researchers • Access to Claude models and GPU compute • Connection to the broader AI safety and security research community Interview process The interview process will include an initial application, recruiter screen, technical assessment, research discussion and a reference check. Applications for this round close on November 22, 2026 (11:59 PM PT). We expect to make offers in January 2027. You may be a good fit if you • Are motivated by making sure AI is safe and beneficial for society as a whole • Are excited to transition into working on empirical AI research full-time • Have led ambitious work in a variety of technical fields (professor in a quantitative discipline, a technical founder or a staff-level engineer) • Thrive in fast-paced, collaborative environments • Can implement ideas quickly and communicate clearly Strong candidates may also • Have experience authoring research papers in machine learning, NLP, or AI safety • Have experience with LLMs, reinforcement learning or Kubernetes clusters and complex shared codebases Mentors and research areas Potential Anthropic mentors include: • Sara Price: leads the Alignment Training team; background in data science • Ethan Perez: leads the Alignment Science team; background in machine learning • Evan Hubinger: leads the Alignment Stress-Testing team; formerly at The Machine Intelligence Research Institute (MIRI) • Samuel Marks: leads the Scalable Oversight team; background in mathematics • Elana Simon: researches the interpretability of language models and biological models; background in biomedical informatics • Belinda Li: researches how language models represent the world, users, and themselves; background in natural language processing (PhD, MIT) • Jack Lindsey: leads the Model Psychology team; background in theoretical neuroscience • Nicholas Carlini: researches adversarial machine learning and AI security; previously at DeepMind and Google Brain • Jascha Sohl-Dickstein: works at the intersection of safety and pretraining; background in physics and neuroscience, formerly Principal Scientist at Google Brain Residents will work on empirical research questions in collaboration with Anthropic's research teams.
