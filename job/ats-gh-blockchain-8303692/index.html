Blockchain is connecting the world to the future of finance. As the most trusted and fastest-growing global crypto company, it helps millions of people worldwide safely access cryptocurrency. Since its inception in 2011, Blockchain has earned the trust of over 90 million wallet holders and more than 40 million verified users, facilitating over $1 trillion in crypto transactions. We’re looking for a bright, highly organised and proactive Junior Team Assistant to join the CEO Office at Blockchain.com. This is a fantastic opportunity for someone early in their career who is eager to learn, take ownership and gain exposure to the day-to-day operations of a fast-growing global business. Working closely with the Chief of Staff, you’ll help ensure the CEO's daily schedule, travel and logistics run smoothly. No two days will look the same, and you'll need to be comfortable switching between tasks, adapting to changing priorities and getting things done quickly and efficiently. We're not looking for someone with years of Executive Assistant experience. We're looking for someone with the right attitude: tech savvy, resourceful, detail-oriented, dependable and happy to roll up their sleeves and help with whatever needs to get done. This position is based in our London office and may involve occasional domestic or international travel, sometimes at short notice. The role requires a high level of integrity, professionalism and confidentiality. References will be checked as part of the recruitment process. Calendar & Scheduling • Support the Chief of Staff in managing the CEO's calendar, coordinating meetings and keeping schedules organised. • Schedule internal and external meetings across multiple time zones. • Prepare daily schedules, reminders and relevant briefing materials. • Take notes during regular meetings, track action items and follow up to ensure completion. • Help coordinate business and personal commitments, ensuring everything runs smoothly. Daily Support & Hospitality • Coordinate catering, refreshments, meals and meeting room arrangements. • Help anticipate and manage the CEO's day-to-day needs, from organising coffee and meals to preparing meeting spaces. • Make restaurant reservations and coordinate hospitality arrangements for meetings and events. • Support ad hoc requests and assist with whatever is needed to keep the day running efficiently. Expenses & Administration • Track and process expenses, receipts and supporting documentation. • Maintain accurate and organised records. • Provide general administrative support to the Chief of Staff and CEO Office. Personal Coordination • Assist with scheduling personal appointments, reservations and other logistical arrangements. • Handle ad hoc personal requests with professionalism, discretion and attention to detail.
