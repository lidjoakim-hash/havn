🚀 We’re Hiring: Electrical Engineer – Cable Harness Routing Designer | Automotive Company: Hykmann Global Business Solutions AB Role: Electrical Engineer – Cable Harness Routing Designer Specialisation: Cable Harness Routing | Electrical Installations | CATIA V5 | Vehicle Integration | Automotive Engineering Location: Södertälje, Sweden Work Model: 100% On-site Assignment Period: 1 December 2026 – 30 November 2028 Languages: Swedish and English About the Role Hykmann Global Business Solutions AB is looking for an experienced Electrical Engineer – Cable Harness Routing Designer to contribute to the development of advanced electrical installation solutions for next-generation commercial vehicles. You will be responsible for designing, developing, and releasing cable harness routing solutions that meet electrical, mechanical, manufacturing, quality, serviceability, and cost requirements. The role covers the complete product development lifecycle, from concept development and detailed CAD design to prototype builds, verification, industrialisation, and engineering changes. You will collaborate closely with electrical architects, mechanical designers, manufacturing engineers, purchasing teams, and external suppliers to ensure efficient, robust, and production-ready electrical installations. Key Responsibilities 1. Cable Harness Routing & Electrical Installation Design and develop cable harness routing solutions using industry-standard CAD tools. Create and maintain 3D routing models and technical documentation. Develop electrical installation solutions for vehicle platforms. Optimise routing based on packaging, robustness, manufacturability, serviceability, quality, and cost. Ensure compatibility with surrounding mechanical and electrical components. 2. CAD Design & Technical Development Develop detailed 3D models and technical documentation for electrical installations. Use CATIA V5 or CATIA 3DEXPERIENCE for routing and installation design. Perform tolerance analysis and installation feasibility assessments. Participate in concept development and engineering design reviews. Ensure compliance with technical requirements and product development standards. 3. Cross-Functional Engineering Collaboration Work closely with electrical architects, component owners, and mechanical design teams. Coordinate with manufacturing engineering, purchasing, and external suppliers. Participate in technical discussions and propose practical engineering solutions. Ensure alignment between electrical design, mechanical integration, and manufacturing requirements. Manage multiple activities and maintain effective stakeholder communication. 4. Prototype, Verification & Production Support Support prototype builds and electrical installation activities. Participate in verification and validation. Troubleshoot cable harness routing and installation issues. Support technical investigations and resolve design-related problems. Contribute to industrialisation and production engineering. Manage engineering changes and maintain accurate documentation. 5. Continuous Improvement Identify opportunities to improve routing methods and design practices. Contribute to engineering standards and technical best practices. Apply Design for Manufacturing (DfM) and Design for Assembly (DfA) principles. Support continuous improvement across electrical installation and vehicle integration. Required Skills & Experience Education Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Mechatronics, or an equivalent technical discipline. Relevant professional experience may also be considered. Cable Harness Routing & Design Practical experience in cable harness routing or mechanical installation design. Understanding of vehicle electrical installations, packaging, and component integration. Knowledge of manufacturing, assembly, and serviceability requirements. Understanding of DfM and DfA principles.
