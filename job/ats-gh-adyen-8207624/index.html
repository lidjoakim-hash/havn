Senior Partner Solutions Engineer, Cellular Connectivity As a Senior Partner Solutions Engineer, Cellular Connectivity within Hardware Partner Engineering, you will own the technical relationship with Adyen’s cellular connectivity partners throughout the service lifecycle. Cellular connectivity is a critical dependency for payment terminals operating globally. The environment is technically complex and highly distributed: an issue may involve the terminal, SIM/eSIM, connectivity provider, roaming partner, local mobile operator, network configuration or Adyen systems. You will act as Adyen’s primary technical counterpart to these partners, combining technical understanding with partner management. You will drive technical priorities, service improvements, complex investigations, incidents, RCA and corrective actions, while helping ensure that connectivity solutions remain reliable and suitable as Adyen’s terminal fleet and provider landscape evolve. You will work closely with Product, Engineering, Support, Supply Chain, Procurement and Commercial Partnership. You are not expected to gatekeep direct engineer-to-engineer collaboration. Your role is to create clarity around technical ownership, connect the right people, establish priorities and commitments, and ensure important actions are driven to closure.
