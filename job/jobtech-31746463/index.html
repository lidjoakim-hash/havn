Turn sound waves into better diagnostics and cell therapy At AcouSort, we use acoustofluidics, a combination of sound waves and microfluidics, to handle cells and particles gently and without labels. Our technology grew out of more than 20 years of research at the Department of Biomedical Engineering at Lund University. Today it’s used in research instruments such as AcouWash and AcouTrap and will revolutionize sample processing within diagnostics, biomarker research and cell therapy manufacturing. We’re now looking for an R&D Scientist who can support taking acoustofluidics devices from simulation through validated prototype to product lunch. What you’ll do Design and optimize acoustofluidic chips and systems for blood and cell processing Model acoustic fields, fluid flow and particle behavior (COMSOL, MATLAB) Build prototypes using micro milling, 3D printing and lithography Validate performance on blood and cell samples, including plasma quality, hemolysis and cell viability Work with clinical and industrial partners, and help with grant applications and scientific communication Who you are PhD in biomedical engineering At least 5 years of hands-on experience with acoustophoresis and acoustic particle manipulation in microfluidics Expertise with both acoustic field simulation and lab work, including CAD, imaging and flow cytometry Experienced with blood samples and relevant cytometry analyses Motivated by turning research into products that reach the market We offer A key role in an ambitious team, where your work goes straight into products used by researchers, clinics and the cell therapy industry. Interested? Please send your application to jobs@acousort.com. Applications are reviewed continuously. About AcouSort AcouSort is a medical technology company developing critical components for instrumentation used in the diagnostics, analytics, and cell therapy processing markets. AcouSort’s components allow for automated refinement of biological samples such as blood or cell preparations, providing instrumentation manufacturers with a state-of-the-art ability to integrate sample processing steps that traditionally must be performed manually. AcouSort’s strategy is to use our innovative technology to revolutionize today’s healthcare by providing a solution to automate and integrate sample processing steps, allowing for a new generation of medical devices to be developed. Through collaborations with leading Life Science companies our integrated technology will eliminate manual handling steps while saving time, money, and ultimately – lives. Our core technology builds on more than 20 years of acoustofluidics research and development headed by Thomas Laurell, professor at Lund University at the Department of Biomedical Engineering and co-founder and board member of AcouSort. The company is listed on Nasdaq First North Growth Market in Stockholm.
