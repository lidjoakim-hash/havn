Economic Officer at the Embassy of Israel to Norway and Iceland

Embassy Of Israel to Norway and Iceland · Oslo, Norway

About the Job The Embassy of Israel to Norway and Iceland is seeking a dedicated and proactive Economic Officer. The primary goal of this position is to promote bilateral trade and economic cooperation between Israel and Norway, as well as to handle, guide, and assist Israeli businesses looking to establish themselves or expand into the Norwegian market. Working closely with the embassy's diplomatic staff and relevant stakeholders, you will play a key role in fostering commercial ties, supporting trade delegations, and bridging the gap between Israeli innovation and Norwegian industry. As part of the Political Department at the embassy you will be expected to also contribute to other tasks that the department handles. Central Tasks Include, but are not limited to: ● Trade Promotion: Actively work to expand commercial, industrial, and technological collaboration between Norwegian and Israeli companies. ● Business Assistance: Provide guidance, logistical support, and strategic advice to Israeli businesses, startups, and entrepreneurs seeking to establish operations or partnerships in Norway. ● Network Building: Establish and maintain a strong professional network with Norwegian businesses, industry associations, investors, and governmental bodies. ● Analysis & Reporting: Monitor and analyze economic trends, market developments, and business opportunities within the Norwegian market to prepare decision-making briefs for the diplomatic team. ● Coordination & Events: Coordinate trade delegations, B2B meetings, seminars, and promotional events showcasing Israeli technology and commerce. ● Inquiries: Handle commercial inquiries from both Israeli and Norwegian companies seeking market entry or partnership opportunities. Qualifications and Experience ● Relevant higher education (Bachelor's or Master's degree) in economics, business administration, international trade, finance, or a related field. ● Relevant professional experience in business development, trade promotion, B2B sales, or project management. ● Excellent written and oral communication skills in both Norwegian and English. ● In-depth understanding of the Norwegian business landscape, regulatory framework, and economic environment. ● Strong interest in international trade and fostering bilateral economic relations. Personal Characteristics ● Proactive, independent, and self-driven with a strong sense of initiative. ● Excellent interpersonal and networking skills, with the ability to build and maintain professional relationships. ● Solution-minded and commercially oriented. ● Analytical mindset with the ability to manage multiple tasks in a fast-paced diplomatic environment. ● A passion for Israel and strengthening the Israel-Norway economic relationship. Application Details ● Salary: Determined based on educational background and experience. ● Application Process: Please submit your CV and a short cover letter to: ambassador-assistant&#64;oslo.mfa.gov.il ● Note: Candidates must be based in or willing to relocate to Oslo and cannot hold Israeli citizenship.
