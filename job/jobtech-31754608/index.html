Company description: Ericsson AB Job description: Join our Team About this opportunity  In Global Intelligent Business Services (GIBS), we are passionate about simplification, transforming business workflows to drive operational excellence and shift effort from transactional activities toward value creation and insights for the business.     We are now looking for an experienced finance leader to lead GIBS Financial Planning & Analysis delivery flow in a subset of Group Function scope and be part of the future FP&A operating model design and development. A person with high integrity, sound judgement and the ability to operate effectively in a complex global environment.  The role enables financially sound decision-making through integrated planning, forecasting, performance analysis and decision support and creates a standardized, data-driven and increasingly automated flow.  Role Overview  The Head of GIBS Financial Planning & Analysis owns operational delivery, performance and service quality across the defined FP&A scope.  The role provides forward-looking analysis, improves forecast accuracy and drives automation, self-service reporting and AI-enabled analysis.    What you will do: Own operational delivery, performance and service quality across the defined FP&A scope.  Translate strategy into financial targets, plans and KPIs and lead forecasting, scenario modelling and performance analysis.  Provide decision support for investments, cost actions and resource allocation by connecting financial and non-financial data.  Create a Data Insight powerhouse and standardize the use of data across the flow.  Drive standardization, simplification, automation and AI adoption to continuously improve self-service reporting and AI-enabled analysis, forecast accuracy, cycle times and quality of insights.  Contribute to the design and evolution of the future FP&A operating model, working closely with Process Owners, MA and BA teams to define design principles, scope, and target capabilities.   Be part of the development of a standardized, digital, and scalable FP&A delivery model, leveraging automation, data, and AI-enabled ways of working to enhance insight generation, operational excellence, innovation, and value creation in line with business priorities.  Develop FP&A leadership capability, workforce skills and an accountability-oriented culture.  Build and inspire a high-performing team, fostering a culture of accountability, collaboration, innovation, and continuous improvement while developing talent and exemplifying Ericsson values and behaviors.    What you will bring:  Extensive Finance leadership experience across FP&A or business control.  Strong expertise in planning, forecasting, performance analysis and decision support.  Strong analytical, financial-modelling and scenario-planning capabilities.  Proven stakeholder, people and change leadership.  Strong finance and business acumen, with the ability to translate business priorities into clear operational tactics, transformation roadmaps, and measurable business outcomes.  Proven experience in complex global transformations, operating model evolution, process standardization, simplification, automation, digitalization, and AI-enabled improvements at scale.  Continuous improvement-oriented mindset, coupled with high integrity, sound judgment, and the ability to operate effectively in a complex, global environment  Executive presence, communication, collaboration, and influencing skills, with the ability to work effectively with Finance stakeholders.  Inspiring people leader with a proven track record of building, developing, and empowering high-performing teams and fostering a culture of accountability, collaboration, and continuous improvement.
