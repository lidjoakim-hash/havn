Head of Property Operations & Technical Services

Sway Sourcing Sweden Aktiebolag · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 400 000–480 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 15.2.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

We are looking for an experienced Facility Management leader to drive the development of safe, reliable, compliant and cost-efficient facilities for a multi-site logistics organisation. The role combines strategic leadership with operational oversight. You will establish a shared FM strategy and governance framework, coordinate regional delivery and act as the main contact for the organisation’s central real estate function. Key responsibilities Develop and implement a common FM strategy, service standards, processes and improvement roadmap across regions and sites. Lead and develop regional FM teams and facility coordinators, establishing clear responsibilities, objectives and ways of working. Oversee facility services, building operations and technical maintenance to support reliable day-to-day logistics operations. Coordinate maintenance, inspections, contingency arrangements and facility-related safety and compliance activities with relevant stakeholders. Lead facility projects and support new site openings, customer implementations and operational changes. Manage supplier performance, contracts, budgets and cost follow-up in collaboration with Procurement and Finance. Drive energy efficiency, sustainability initiatives and continuous improvement while maintaining safety, compliance and service quality. Build effective collaboration with operations, landlords, real estate, security and health, safety, environment and quality teams. Your focus will be to create a coordinated FM function that strengthens local delivery, supports business continuity and meets the organisation’s evolving operational needs. Essential requirements Demonstrated leadership experience in Facility Management, property operations or technical services, ideally within logistics, industry, retail or a similar business operating across multiple sites. Solid knowledge of integrated facility services, technical maintenance, mandatory inspections, supplier management, contracts, budgeting and cost efficiency. Experience introducing consistent processes, developing teams and driving change across decentralised or geographically dispersed operations. Strong commercial awareness and analytical skills, with the ability to translate operational requirements, financial data, risks and service results into clear priorities and measurable improvements. Experience delivering facility projects and incorporating FM requirements into new sites, customer onboarding and operational changes. A good understanding of how FM responsibilities connect with workplace health and safety, fire protection, security and environmental requirements, including clear allocation of accountability. Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to build trust and collaborate with site teams, senior leaders and central corporate functions. A hands-on, organised and results-focused approach to leadership. Fluency in Swedish and English. A relevant academic degree or equivalent professional experience. Professional qualifications in Facility Management or property management are an advantage. A valid driving licence.
