Safety System Engineer 🛠️ About our Engineering Teams At Wayve, we are developing AV2.0: an end-to-end AI driving system designed to learn and adapt across a wide range of driving environments, with the ambition to scale autonomy globally. Our Safety team tackles one of the most important and complex challenges in that mission - assuring the safety of our Automated Driving System (ADS) as it evolves across vehicle platforms, applications and operating environments. You will help design and orchestrate the safety assurance framework that makes this possible. 🧠 Your day-to-day You will lead functional safety activities for Wayve's ADS and its integration into current and future vehicle platforms, as well as third-party safety-critical systems. Working closely with internal engineering teams, external partners and other safety experts, you will build a deep understanding of the system, assess risk, shape the safety concept and architecture, define verification and validation, and develop the evidence needed to support a robust safety case. 🧩 What you’ll be working on: • Build and maintain Item Definition work products for each vehicle platform and safety-critical integration. • Conduct HARAs, FMEAs and FTAs to identify risk and define safety concepts that reduce risk to an acceptable level. • Work with design teams on system architectures that satisfy the safety concept, including decomposition decisions and derived software and external requirements. • Support software teams in implementing safety requirements on target hardware. • Define test specifications across integration levels, including vehicle-level verification and validation of the safety concept. • Build structured safety cases using clear claims, arguments and evidence. • Contribute to production safety work products and evolve safety processes with process and compliance teams for certification activities. 🙌 You should apply if: • You have at least 5 years of hands-on automotive functional safety experience applying ISO 26262 across different systems, with strong working knowledge of Parts 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 and 9. • You have conducted vehicle-level testing at proving grounds to verify and validate safety concepts. • You are experienced in risk analysis and safety engineering techniques such as HARA, FMEA and FTA, and can translate findings into practical safety concepts, architectures and requirements. • You can work effectively across software, hardware, systems and external partners, and are comfortable debating safety-related trade-offs at different levels of the organisation. • You are pragmatic in balancing risk and benefit, especially in a fast-moving development environment, while maintaining high safety standards. • You are a self-starter who can move between long-term, high-level safety thinking and detailed subsystem or application-level problem solving, and you are confident challenging ideas while remaining open to constructive feedback. ✨ Nice to have • Practical experience with ISO 26262 Part 6, including production software development and software-tool qualification. • Experience managing suppliers of safety-related hardware or software, including DIA definition, supplier audits and assessments. • Experience developing safety cases for automated driving systems, including computer-vision-based systems, and enough knowledge of neural networks to discuss safety assurance approaches with scientists and engineers. • Experience with SOTIF, including identifying triggering
