Elastic, the Search AI Company, enables everyone to find the answers they need in real time, using all their data, at scale — unleashing the potential of businesses and people. The Elastic Search AI Platform, used by more than 50% of the Fortune 500, brings together the precision of search and the intelligence of AI to enable everyone to accelerate the results that matter. By taking advantage of all structured and unstructured data — securing and protecting private information more effectively — Elastic’s complete, cloud-based solutions for search, security, and observability help organizations deliver on the promise of AI. What Is The RoleElastic, the Search Analytics company, is seeking a Jnr Public Sector Account Exec. You’ll own a greenfield Dutch public sector territory: municipalities, healthcare, education and research. This is a hunter seat with loads of potential, Elastic has the momentum in this segment. You’ll open doors, build champions, and turn Search, Observability and Security use cases into new projects, with a main focus on applying AI. What You Will Be Doing • Build and run a territory plan that prioritizes where to hunt first, using account tiering and contact coverage. • Generate your own pipeline through outbound, events, partners and public sector networks. Many accounts have no contacts yet, so you’ll build coverage from scratch. • Run full-cycle deals with MEDDPICC discipline: economic buyer, champion, decision process, paper process. • Navigate Dutch public procurement: tenders, Europese aanbesteding, framework agreements, and partner and reseller routes. • Expand the existing customers, especially in universities and research. • Forecast accurately and keep Salesforce clean. • Work with Solutions Architects, partners and Elastic specialists to land technical wins. What You Bring • We want to hire a sales talent, with <5 years of B2B enterprise software sales, with a proven new logo track record or proven SDR capacity • A preference for Public sector experience in the Netherlands (government, healthcare or education). You understand procurement cycles and can build a deal around them. • Experience selling technical solutions in search, observability, security, data or cloud. • Fluent Dutch and English. You’ll write and sell in Dutch to Dutch-speaking buyers. • Strong qualification habits. You know when to push, when to pivot, and when to walk away.
