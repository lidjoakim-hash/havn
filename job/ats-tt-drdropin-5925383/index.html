We are looking for a skilled and personable gynaecologist to join our team in Fulham, London, on a flexible, self-employed basis. Dr.Dropin is a private healthcare provider originally established in Scandinavia. With over 30 clinics across Norway and more than 2 million patients treated, we are known for our modern clinics, excellent digital tools, and efficient, high-quality care. In just a few years, we have built one of Norway’s largest gynaecology services – and we are now bringing that expertise to the UK as we expand our specialist offering. We opened our first UK clinic in Fulham in 2024 and are making healthcare more accessible – offering same-day appointments, transparent pricing, and experienced doctors. As a gynaecologist with us, you’ll be part of a supportive team and enjoy working in a well-equipped, modern environment, with full flexibility over your schedule. We’re looking for a gynaecologist who: • Is GMC-registered and fully licensed to practise in the UK • Has experience in private healthcare or the NHS • Enjoys working independently and communicating clearly with patients • Wants to offer high-quality care in a modern, patient-focused clinic We offer: • A newly renovated clinic with top medical equipment • Flexible, part-time sessions – you choose when you work • Admin support, booking systems, and digital tools so you can focus on your patients • A friendly, professional environment with GPs, dermatologists, and physiotherapists already on site If you’re a passionate and experienced gynaecologist looking for a fresh opportunity in a growing, international healthcare company – we’d love to hear from you.
