ABOUT AIRWALLEX Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own. We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.   HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT You’ll help Airwallex manage and improve the economics of card payments, with a focus on scheme fees and interchange across Visa and Mastercard. You’ll turn complex network rules and payment data into accurate configurations, actionable analysis and decisions that improve cost visibility and margin performance. - Own payment-cost analysis across scheme fees, interchange and network rules, identifying changes and translating them into clear financial, product and operational requirements. - Maintain accurate fee tables, eligibility criteria and transaction-level logic with Product and Engineering, ensuring payment-cost calculations and customer billing reflect current network rules. - Build models and reporting across markets, products, payment methods and transaction types to identify margin drivers, anomalies, leakage and opportunities to improve payment economics. - Support cost and margin optimisation through transaction qualification, BIN routing, cost configuration and related levers, contributing to pricing, product design, market launches and commercial decisions. - Strengthen governance, testing, reconciliation and controls by comparing expected costs with actual network charges and helping drive root-cause resolution. - Partner with Finance, Product, Engineering, Data, Operations and external payment partners to resolve scheme pricing, interchange and rule-related questions. You’ll work from our London office three days a week. WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR Essentials - Experience in payments, acquiring, issuing, card networks, payment infrastructure or a related role, ideally within a global PSP, acquirer, scheme, issuer or fintech. - Strong knowledge of card payment economics, particularly Visa and Mastercard interchange, scheme fees, transaction qualification and network rules. - Experience translating complex payment rules, fee schedules or network requirements into system logic, configurations and controls. - Experience working with payment-cost systems or controls, including interchange tables, scheme fee logic, BIN-based rules, transaction attributes or similar payment configurations. - Strong analytical and quantitative capabilities, including experience working with transaction-level data, economic modelling and reconciliation of expected versus actual payment costs. - Commercial judgment and the ability to connect payment mechanics to pricing, product decisions and margin optimisation. - Exceptional attention to detail and ownership, with the discipline required to manage complex rules where small errors can create meaningful financial impact at scale. - Strong cross-functional execution, with the ability to work effectively across Product, Engineering, Finance, Data, Operations and external payment partners.
