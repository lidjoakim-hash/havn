The Team The Partner Strategy and Operations team drives the growth, productivity, and operational scale of Okta's Global Partner Sales organization. Reporting to the Senior Director of Partner Strategy & Operations, this team operates as a key cross-functional hub partnering directly with senior Partner ecosystem leaders to align operational performance metrics with long-term business goals. Role As a Partner Strategy and Operations Manager, you will serve as a key individual contributor and thought leader playing an essential role in setting the foundation to scale our partner operations. You will combine analytical insight, strong business acumen, and cross-functional partnerships to operationalize, measure, and optimize partner performance across global regions. Responsibilities • Manage the weekly cadence of the Partner reporting foundation, including forecasting, lead generation, ad-hoc reporting, and analytics. • Develop formal reporting packages for Partner sales effectiveness and conduct regular metrics reviews with Partner leaders and supporting teams. • Measure and evaluate Partner representative and manager ramp time and productivity across global roles and regions. • Structure and lead key strategic initiatives, establish and track KPIs, and refine best practices and Partner sales policies. • Deliver data-driven recommendations for Partner business planning, scorecards, and forecasting processes. • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define system requirements, optimize sales reporting frameworks, and scale operational infrastructure. • Identify, evaluate, and implement AI-driven tools and workflows to automate partner data analysis, streamline reporting, and enhance predictive forecasting accuracy.
