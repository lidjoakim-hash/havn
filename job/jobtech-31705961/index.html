We’re one of the world’s oldest start-ups - and we’re just getting started. At Husqvarna Group, innovation is in our DNA. With over 330 years of heritage and a passion for pioneering technology, we design and deliver world class products and solutions for forest, park, garden and construction care. From robotic lawn mowers to cutting-edge chainsaws and sustainable battery systems, we’re shaping the future - and we want you to be part of it. About the Team The Electromechanics Design Department within Global Robotics R&D Technology consists of 16 engineers specializing in verification, mechanics, mechatronics, and electronics. We are responsible for the electromechanical systems used in our robotic mower platforms and work across the complete product lifecycle, from technology investigations and feasibility studies to product development projects and maintenance activities. Our team defines system requirements, drives technical coordination across disciplines, and leads verification and validation activities in close collaboration with software, electronics, sourcing, quality, and manufacturing teams. We offer an international and collaborative working environment characterized by openness, professionalism, and a strong passion for technology and innovation. About the role We are looking for a Design Engineer with experience in electrical and/or electromechanical component development to strengthen our electromechanics team. In this role, you will play a key part in the development of future robotic platforms and act as a technical contributor and coordinator in cross-functional projects. Depending on experience, the role may also include technical leadership responsibilities. The primary focus of the role is the development and management of Power Supply Units (PSUs), including PSUs both with and without communication interfaces. Responsibility also includes other electrical components such as wiring harnesses, user interface hardware, connectors, and related electromechanical subsystems. Development activities are carried out in close collaboration with both internal stakeholders and external development partners and suppliers. Your responsibilities will include: Development, specification, and integration of power supply units and other electrical components Defining component requirements and securing technical compliance throughout the product lifecycle Coordinating development activities in tight cooperation with suppliers and external engineering partners Leading technical investigations, concept evaluations, and feasibility studies Cross-functional coordination in tight cooperation with mechanics, electronics, sourcing, quality, manufacturing, and others Planning and execution of verification and validation activities Driving continuous improvements in development methodologies, processes, and engineering tools Supporting technology roadmaps and future platform strategies within electrical systems and components Required Qualifications Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Mechatronics, Mechanical Engineering, or equivalent About 3 years of relevant industrial experience Experience in product development involving electrical or electromechanical systems Experience working with suppliers and external development partners Strong communication skills in Swedish and English, both written and spoken Meritorious Experience The following experience is considered highly valuable: Development and specification of Power Supply Units (PSUs) Electrical distribution systems, wiring harnesses, connectors, and related components Low-voltage electrical systems and electronics integration Product development within consumer products, robotics, automotive, or related industries Verification and validation of electrical and electromechanical products, especially round component certification Technical leadership or project coordination Experience working with global suppliers and development partners Expe
