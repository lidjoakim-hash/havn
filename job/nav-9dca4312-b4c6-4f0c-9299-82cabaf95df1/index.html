Do you like Italian food and working in a busy kitchen? Our restaurant in Stavanger is looking for Chefs and Dishwashers to join our team. About Olivia Olivia serves authentic Italian food like pizza, pasta, and antipasti. We use good ingredients from small, family-owned producers in Italy. Our restaurants are inspired by Italian cities and offer a warm, friendly atmosphere. Olivia has restaurants in Norway, Sweden, and Finland. About the job At Olivia Stavanger, you will work in a professional kitchen with good colleagues. The work can be busy, but we help each other and work as a team. We offer: • Competitive salary • Good working environment and supportive team • Uniforms provided and washed • Training and learning on the job • Opportunities to grow and develop • Stable workplace in a well-known restaurant group We are looking for people who: • Like to work in a kitchen • Have some experience in the kitchen • Can work well with others • Like to work in a busy environment   • Are reliable and motivated • Are willing to learn • Have a positive “can-do” attitude We are recruiting continuously.
