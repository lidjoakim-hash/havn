Contract: 9-month fixed-term contract (FTC) The Global Payroll Analyst is a 9-month fixed-term contract (FTC) role responsible for optimizing global payroll processes and analyzing payroll data to support accurate, timely, and compliant operations. As part of the Global Payroll team, this person will identify discrepancies and trends, support payroll processing and audits, improve controls and workflows, and translate complex data into actionable recommendations. The role works closely with external providers and partners across payroll operations, finance, people, and process excellence. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES: - Collect, analyze, and interpret multi-country payroll data to support accurate processing, reporting, and decision-making. - Validate payroll inputs and outputs; identify and resolve discrepancies involving employee records, benefits, taxes, deductions, and payments. - Support payroll processing and help ensure country-level payroll and tax compliance. - Conduct regular payroll audits and reconcile payroll records with general-ledger accounts. - Investigate trends and root causes, then recommend practical improvements to accuracy, efficiency, controls, and compliance. - Use payroll systems, HR systems, spreadsheets, and data-transformation tools to improve reporting and workflows. - Coordinate payroll activity and data exchange with external vendors and cross-functional partners. - Respond to payroll-related questions professionally and resolve issues with appropriate urgency. - Maintain current knowledge of payroll regulations and industry practices, translating changes into operational updates. - Strengthen documentation, controls, and team practices so improvements are repeatable and scalable. CORE REQUIREMENTS: - 3+ years of experience in payroll analysis or related payroll/finance roles. - Hands-on multi-country payroll experience and a strong understanding of payroll tax and compliance requirements. - Demonstrated experience analyzing large datasets, investigating discrepancies, and producing actionable insights. - Advanced Excel or Google Sheets skills, including lookups, pivot tables, and efficient data manipulation.
