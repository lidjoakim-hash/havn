Why Box Needs You This Senior Engineering Business Operations Manager will be a key partner for the Engineering Business Operations and Strategy (EBOS) organization, responsible for providing operational support and expertise to Box Engineering teams across the Build organization. This role will have a focus on our EMEA teams. You will guide and prepare teams through planning and reporting processes - including Annual Planning, Quarterly Planning, Quarterly Business Reviews, and monthly Operational Reviews - and drive critical processes for tracking performance metrics and helping leadership measure and report on key goals. We are looking for someone who can help us scale in a way that supports our continued growth. This position reports directly to the Senior Director of EBOS. What You’ll Do • Own optimization strategies and recognize opportunities to improve team functions; design and improve operational workflows to reduce friction and increase efficiency. • Guide and prepare Engineering teams across EMEA for all planning and reporting processes, including Annual Planning, Quarterly Planning, Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs), monthly Operational Reviews, and related cadences. • Drive and maintain OKR tracking and KPI measurement for engineering teams; build reporting and dashboards that give leadership clear visibility into performance and progress against goals. • Partner with Engineering leadership on production support and SLA management; monitor SLA performance, and support resolution and communication. • Compile and analyze data from engineering and cross-functional partners to assess performance, identify operational bottlenecks, and support strategic decisions. • Create and deliver presentations and materials for leadership and business reviews; ensure consistent, accurate reporting across EBOS and Build organization stakeholders. • Collaborate with Product, Engineering, and Finance on planning alignment, resource allocation, and operational initiatives. • Support ad-hoc EBOS projects and operational improvements to enable scale and continued growth. Who You Are • 5+ years of experience in business operations, engineering operations, or a related operational role. • Strong operational and process ownership: you guide teams through planning cycles and reporting with clarity and consistency. • Experience driving OKR tracking, KPI measurement, and performance reporting
