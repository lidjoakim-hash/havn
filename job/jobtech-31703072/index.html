Denna position rapporterar till: Digital Lead - Regional __ You’ll join ABB Energy Industries, part of ABB’s Automation division. We deliver electrical automation and digitalization solutions to industries including nuclear power, district heating, hydropower, water, pharmaceuticals and chemicals, using ABB’s industry-leading technologies. In this role, you’ll help deliver our cybersecurity portfolio and make a meaningful difference for our customers. Your responsibilities Design and implement secure OT solutions and interfaces with IT systems. Assess cybersecurity risks, identify security gaps and recommend effective remedies. Develop robust system and network architectures. Assess and document the cybersecurity posture of OT systems and network infrastructure. Implement disaster recovery solutions for OT systems. Deliver security updates and foundational security controls as part of service agreements. Provide services such as IT/OT system hardening, role-based access control and risk assessments, helping customers meet cybersecurity standards and requirements, including the NIS2 Directive. Your background You have a bachelor’s degree in computer science, information technology or a related field—or equivalent experience. Most importantly, you bring practical experience with cybersecurity solutions and services, as well as OT/IT architecture or infrastructure. Experience implementing cybersecurity solutions for control systems and/or SCADA is an advantage. You understand network maintenance and troubleshooting, firewall configuration, routing protocols and network traffic monitoring. Knowledge of Network Intrusion Detection (NID) and/or Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) is a plus. Certifications such as GISP or ISA/IEC 62443 (IC32 or IC33) are advantageous.
