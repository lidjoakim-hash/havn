Lead Solution Engineer - Mollie Connect Build with us Businesses deserve better from finance. Less friction, more freedom. Since 2004, Mollie has been on a mission to make payments and money management effortless for every business in Europe. Today, more than 250,000 companies trust our all-in-one platform to get paid, manage money and grow on their terms. Simple, scalable and built with real businesses in mind. We're one of Europe's fastest-growing fintechs, with 950+ Mollies across 14+ locations. Our people make this growth possible. We move fast, build with purpose and care deeply about our customers. Whether you're solving problems, building market-leading products or exploring how AI can make work smarter, you'll have the freedom to create and the trust to deliver. If you're ready to grow, shape the future of fintech and join an ambitious, high-performing team, this is the place for you. Your Impact You will lead our Solutions Engineering team for Mollie Connect, a team of six, spread across three offices, that bridges Product and our most demanding users: platforms that embed and monetise payments and financial services through Mollie. Your team operates at the intersection of technology, product, and commercial strategy. You translate complex business requirements into technical solutions and vice versa, from the first pre-sales conversation through implementation and beyond. Your counterparts are CTOs, solution architects, and engineering teams at Europe's most ambitious software platforms, marketplaces, and enterprise organisations. This is a player-coach role. You'll lead and develop the team while staying deeply hands-on with our most complex and strategic engagements. You'll shape how we sell, how we implement, and how we feed learnings back into the Connect product roadmap. What you'll ship Lead & develop the team - Mentor and grow a team of six Solutions Engineers. Own hiring, performance management, and career development in partnership with our HR Business Partner. - Define the team's way of working and set OKRs that align with Mollie Connect's strategic priorities. - Align closely with Partner Management team leads to ensure seamless collaboration across commercial and technical workstreams. Pre-sales & consultative selling - Lead upmarket pre-sales / upsell engagements alongside partner acquisition and partner growth managers, acting as a trusted technical advisor to prospects and designing solutions that unlock value. - Develop and refine the team's consultative selling approach as we move toward larger, more complex platform partners. Implementation consulting & technical delivery - Stay hands-on with the most complex integrations, particularly multi-entity setups with unique compliance, regulatory, or architectural challenges. - Guide the team in supporting partners from integration design through technical validation and go-live, keeping all stakeholders aligned throughout. Product & strategic influence - Represent the voice of platform partners in product discussions and contribute to shaping the Mollie Connect roadmap as part of the domain leadership team. - Collaborate with Engineering Managers, Partner Marketing, and Product Marketing to ensure our solutions and enablement materials reflect real-world partner needs. What you'll bring - 7+ years of experience in Solutions Engineering, Technical Consulting, Sales Engineering, or a comparable role in the software/SaaS industry. - At least 2 years of experience leading a team, whether as a formal team lead or in a senior player-coach capacity. You know how to develop people, set direction, and create the conditions for a team to do their best work. - Strong technical acumen. You're comfortable reading and discussing APIs, integration architectures, and technical documentation.
