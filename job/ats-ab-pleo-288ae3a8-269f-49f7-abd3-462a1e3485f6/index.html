ABOUT PLEO Messy spend management is tricky business. And tedious processes are a lose-lose situation for all involved, not just finance. At Pleo, we're changing that. We build spend solutions that make managing money seamless, empowering, and surprisingly effective for finance teams and employees alike - with a vision to help all businesses ‘go beyond’. The word ‘Pleo’ actually means ‘more than you’d expect’, and living by that mantra has been the secret to our success over the last 10 years. Now, we’re at a pivotal moment in our journey; every move we make has a direct impact on our 40,000+ customers, our business, and our collective success. We need people who take pride in uncovering customer needs, who turn complex problems into simple solutions, challenge the way things are done (respectfully), and always aim high. With great ambitions driving us forward, we can’t say we’ve got this whole thing figured out. And frankly, that’s half the fun! What we can say is that we’re a driven, progressive, and, importantly, a kind bunch of 850+ people from over 100 nationalities, all committed to delivering the future of business spending, together. ABOUT THE ROLE We're looking for a GTM Enablement Associate to join our GTM Enablement team at Pleo. In this role, you'll be the backbone of our enablement programmes - the glue between big-picture strategy and flawless execution. As we scale enablement across Sales, Customer Success, and Partnerships, you'll manage the operations, content creation, and platform infrastructure that makes programmes actually work. If you love variety, thrive on building structure out of ambiguity, and are energised by the thought of enabling thousands of Pleo colleagues to perform at their best, you'll fit right in. You'll work closely with our GTM Enablement Manager and Enablement Specialist and commercial teams across the business. WHO YOU’LL BE WORKING WITH AND REPORTING TO You'll report to the RevOps Director and work closely with the GTM Enablement Manager and Enablement Specialist and teams across Sales, Customer Success, Partnerships, and Product Marketing. Our GTM Enablement team is highly collaborative and dedicated to unlocking commercial performance across Pleo's go-to-market organisation. You'll be part of a function that's growing fast and shaping how Pleo enables its people to succeed.
