By analysing thousands of documents in minutes and powering end-to-end workflows, we cut through complexity, teams can focus on what matters: judgment, strategy, and outcomes. 2,100+ customers across 80+ countries trust us, including Cleary Gottlieb, Goodwin, Linklaters, White & Case, Dentons, and Barclays. THE ROLE: As an Engineering Manager, Foundations at Legora, you'll lead the team responsible for the core backend services and systems at the heart of our platform. You'll architect and deliver the high-scale services that power legal professionals' workflows every day, drive technical excellence at the foundation layer, and help shape a platform relied on by forward-thinking law firms around the world. This is a role for someone who thrives on owning complex, critical systems and loves to build, mentor, and scale both people and software. WHAT YOU WILL BE DOING: - Lead and grow a team of backend engineers, including hiring, mentoring, and developing engineers at all levels - Own team health: keep your team efficient, unblocked, and set up to do their best work - Set the technical bar: drive code quality, security, and system design standards across the team - Design and implement high-scale core backend services and APIs that power the entire Legora platform - Contribute directly to the codebase while supporting the growth of your team - Lead architecture discussions and technical decision-making on foundational systems - Set and uphold high standards in code quality, security, and system design - Collaborate closely with product engineering teams to provide robust, well-designed foundations they can build on confidently - Review code, give feedback, and mentor engineers at all levels - Champion best practices in backend engineering, service design, and continuous improvement - Work with Node.js, Azure, AWS and Kubernetes WHO YOU ARE: - Background as a seasoned full-stack / backend engineer with 1–3 years of leadership experience - Strong communicator who leads with empathy and clarity — passionate about mentoring others and helping teams grow - Deep experience building and operating high-scale distributed systems and core platform services, with end-to-end ownership from design through production reliability - Takes pride in building systems that others depend on, and holds a high bar for correctness and resilience - Proactively and independently solves the most pressing problems - Thrives in a fast-paced environment where the answers aren't always clear and processes are few - But most importantly: someone who gets stuff done, and understands that building a successful company takes real hard work and isn't always glamorous   This is a Stockholm-based, 5-day in-office role — we believe building together in person drives better outcomes.
