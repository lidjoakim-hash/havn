As an Accountant for Europe, you will support accounting and finance activities across multiple European entities, ensuring compliance with company policies, IFRS requirements, and local statutory regulations. What you will do: • Process accounting transactions in accordance with IFRS and local statutory requirements to ensure accurate and compliant financial records. • Perform day-to-day accounting activities, including processing vendor invoices, employee expense claims, customer invoices and prepare and execute payments through electronic banking platforms in accordance with internal controls and approval procedures. • Support Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, and General Ledger processes as required. • Participate in month-end and year-end closing activities, including posting journal entries such as payroll, accruals, prepayments, depreciation, reclassifications, and other accounting adjustments • Maintain fixed asset registers, calculate depreciation, and ensure accurate accounting treatment of capital expenditures • Prepare account reconciliations, including vendor, customer, bank, and general ledger accounts, and investigate any discrepancies. • Support the preparation of statutory financial statements and tax-related reporting in cooperation with external advisors. • Perform variance analysis and validate accounting balances to ensure accuracy and completeness of financial information. • Participate in finance transformation and accounting projects, including process improvements, implementation of group policies and procedures, and onboarding of new entities into the Warsaw Accounting Hub. What makes you a strong fit: • Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, Economics, Business Administration, or a related field.
