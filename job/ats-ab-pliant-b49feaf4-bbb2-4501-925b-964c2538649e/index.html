ABOUT US Pliant is a modular B2B payments platform combining commercial card issuing, payment accounts, FX and spend management for businesses and AI agents.   Businesses across travel, e-commerce, insurance and AI-native technology use Pliant to manage complex payment workflows, while fintechs, software platforms and banks use its infrastructure to build and operate their own commercial payment offerings.   Pliant's API-first platform also enables AI agents and applications to access regulated payment infrastructure with permissions, controls and auditability built in.   Founded in 2020 and headquartered in Berlin, Pliant serves businesses and partners across Europe and North America. A principal member of Visa and Mastercard, Pliant issues commercial cards in 13 currencies across 32 countries under its European e-money license and through regulated partners in the UK and US.   Learn more at www.getpliant.com http://www.getpliant.com. ABOUT THE ROLE We are seeking a motivated and proactive Business Development Manager (m/f/d) to prospect small to medium customers on our biggest market so far, the DACH market at Pliant. The ideal candidate will focus on establishing new strong relationships in the fintech space as well as understanding their needs, and collaborating with the Customer Success team to drive long-term growth. This role will be based in Berlin at our HQ or our office in Vienna where we have a hybrid work policy. WHAT YOU WILL DO - From Discovery to Deal: Conduct discovery calls, demos, and closing of inbound partner leads - Building Pipelines: Generate interest via outbound prospecting to fill your pipeline. - Customer advocacy: You take ownership and communicate skilfully to contact potential customers and build a strong customer pipeline - Understanding: It's important to you to know your contact's needs and problems, ask the right questions and listen to them in order to pick up your customers where they are at the moment - Advise: You put yourself in your customers' shoes and identify for them how we at Pliant can deliver the most value to them WHAT YOU WILL BRING - 2-4 years previous work experience in the banking or financial services industry in a customer facing role - Excellent communication and presentation skills both written and verbal, especially with our inbound partner leads and eager to do outbound outreach when ready - Ability to establish and maintain strong relationships with key customers and stakeholders - Strong willingness to learn and develop your career in a fast paced Fintech environment - You speak German fluently, along with English or any additional European languages would be an advantage, but not essential - Berlin or Vienna based ideally - hybrid work model in place   WHAT WE OFFER   - The opportunity to work in a growing team with big responsibilities that thrives on a strong exchange of knowledge and excellence - Attractive remuneration - Your choice of preferred OS, Windows or Mac - Flat hierarchy and transparent communication in a relaxed, professional atmosphere - Opportunity to develop your talent in a dynamic team with ambitious goals - Flexibility and possibility to work remotely - Monthly mobility benefit - Wellhub Membership - Company card with a monthly allowance for lunches, coffee, etc. with co-workers   Pliant is an equal opportunity employer.
