Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. ABOUT THE JOB: We are on the lookout for an exceptional Senior International Accounting Manager to lead Anduril’s accounting operations across Continental Europe and the Middle East from our Amsterdam office. This role owns the month-end close, statutory reporting, audit deliverables, and accounting operations for the Company’s legal entities throughout the region, and is the senior accounting leader on the ground as that footprint expands. You will stand up and scale the accounting function while partnering with Legal, Tax, Treasury, Payroll, and Operations as each new jurisdiction comes online. This is a leadership seat: you will hire, develop, and lead the accounting team in Amsterdam, and manage the outsourced providers supporting the countries where we do not yet have our own people. The ideal candidate has run a multi-country close, is fluent in the difference between a US GAAP reporting package and a local statutory filing, and would rather design the process than inherit it. Join us in our mission to disrupt the defense industry and elevate your career with a team dedicated to making a significant impact on a global scale.
