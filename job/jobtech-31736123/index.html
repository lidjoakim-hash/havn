Swedish & Finnish Speaking Customer Support

Incluso AB · Stockholm, Sweden

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

We are looking for a Swedish & Finnish Speaking Customer Support for a company in Solna. Start is ASAP, 1 year limited contract to begin with. This role is full-time and hybrid with at least 3 days per week onsite in Solna. Description: You will work independently to receive incoming calls and provide the highest quality customer service. Most of your tasks will be related to financial services and supporting the customer's end customers, including blocking bank cards, etc. Your work will be carried out in Finnish, Swedish and English. We are staffed 24/7, which means that shift work is required during daytime, evening, and night shifts, as well as weekends. Responsibilities •Receive incoming calls from customers, mainly individuals but also stores and bank personnel, and assist them with their inquiries •Register all incoming inquiries in various systems •Resolve incoming inquiries according to the situation •Provide top-level service and communication to our customers, finding the best solutions based on the situation •Work to establish and develop good relationships with the company's customers Experience and Qualifications •Proficiency in Finnish, Swedish (B2 and higher) and English is a must •Has previous experience in a customer service role where you have dealt with individuals, for example, via phone, store, restaurant, etc. •Is service minded. •Good knowledge of computer systems •Good qualifications in German language are advantage This is a full-time consultant position in Solna through Incluso. Start is ASAP, 1 year limited contract to begin with. This role is full-time and hybrid with at least 3 days per week onsite in Solna. Please submit your CV as soon as possible since we will review the applications on an on-going basis. For more information about this role, please contact Marianne Nilsson recruiter at Incluso.
