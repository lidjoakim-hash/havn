Founded in 2013, we're transforming how work gets done through our tech-powered, human-centered platform. What makes us different:   - A diverse team of 600 Malters across 6 European countries - A culture that champions equality (50% of our Comex are women) and inclusive growth - Backed by top investors including ISAI, Serena Capital, Eurazeo Growth, Goldman Sachs, and BPI - A mission to give everyone the freedom to work differently   Ready to help shape the future of work? Your next chapter starts here! 🪐   At Malt we believe that Ambition is the Way, so all lists of missions and responsibilities are non-exhaustive. Explore your future career 🔭 As our new HR Business Partner, you will be the key operational driver supporting our division managers in France.  You will join the Human Resources department, working in direct synergy with HR leadership and Centers of Excellence (CoE). You will help bridge strategic people initiatives with day-to-day team realities. Your purpose is to foster high performance, support organizational growth, and ensure an exemplary employee experience across the entire talent lifecycle. This role is a 6 month fixed term contract. Key responsibilities ✨ • Partner with the HRD and division managers to develop and implement HR policies that support business growth. • Advise managers on organizational changes, people implications, and the execution of transformations. • Coach and advise managers on high-performance management, talent pipelining, and career progression using our skills framework. • Ensure day-to-day compliance with local labor laws when managing employee relations, legal watch, and workflow formalization. • Deploy core HR processes, including performance calibrations, compensation cycles, and internal mobility. • Analyze employee data and reporting to provide actionable insights and proactive solutions to the business. About you 🧑‍🚀 • Relevant educational background in Human Resources, Employment Law or a related field. • Relevant experience (1+ years, internships or apprenticeships welcomed) in an HR generalist or HRBP support role. • French on a native level and strong English proficiency to operate smoothly within our international environment. • Solid foundation in local labor regulations and day-to-day employee relations compliance. • Strong appetite for HR data analysis, change management, and AI integration tools to automate workflows. How to join the mission? 🚀 • First call with Agathe, our Talent Acquisition Lead, to better understand your background, aspirations, and answer any questions you may have. (30-45 minutes) • Interview with your future manager to delve into your experience and the specifics of the role. (45-60 minutes) • Final conversation with a member of our people leadership team to exchange on team culture, Malt’s strategic direction, and mutual fit.
