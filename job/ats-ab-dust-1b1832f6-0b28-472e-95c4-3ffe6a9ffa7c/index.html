SUMMARY Dust is hiring a User Researcher to be the connective tissue between our users and the teams building for them. This is a high-leverage, embedded research role — your work will directly shape product decisions, inform GTM positioning, and surface the insights that help us serve customers like Datadog, 1Password, and Cursor at a higher level. You'll be the first dedicated researcher on the team, which means you'll build the function from the ground up, sitting close to the people shipping the product every day.
