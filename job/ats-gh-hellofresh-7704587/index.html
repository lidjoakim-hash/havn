Join one of France’s fastest-growing foodtech scale-ups At HelloFresh, we’re transforming the way people cook and eat. Through our meal-kit boxes, we help thousands of customers cook delicious, balanced meals at home while reducing food waste and simplifying everyday life. Operating in 15+ countries, HelloFresh is one of the world’s leading foodtech companies. The French market, launched in 2020, has experienced hyper growth and became market leader in 2021. Behind this growth is a simple formula: technology, data, and a team of ambitious people working together to reinvent food at home. If you want to work at the intersection of data, strategy, and growth, and have a tangible impact on a fast-scaling business, this internship is for you. Your Role As a Growth Strategy Data Analyst Intern, you will join the Analytics & Performance team and work closely with the Growth Marketing team to help drive HelloFresh’s growth in France. You will act as a data partner to business stakeholders, turning complex data into actionable insights that influence key strategic decisions, marketing investment decisions, and customer lifecycle optimization. Your work will directly impact critical growth levers such as customer acquisition, retention, pricing strategy, and website optimization. This role combines business analysis, experimentation, and data modeling, giving you end-to-end exposure to how data drives decisions in a growing company. This role is ideal for candidates who want to apply rigorous analytics to real business problems and see their insights translated into concrete growth decisions. Your Impact You will work with modern analytics tools including SQL, Tableau, and Google Sheets, as well as internal data pipelines to analyze large-scale datasets and support data-driven decision-making across the Growth team.
