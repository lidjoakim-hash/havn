Area Sales Manager - Metallurgy Products

På ABB hjälper vi industrier att bli mer resurseffektiva och renare – och varje person här bidrar till resultaten. Du kommer att få möjlighet att leda, få stöd att växa och kunna vara stolt över den påverkan vi skapar tillsammans. Gå med oss och hjälp till att driva det som driver världen. Denna position rapporterar till: Global Head of Sales & Marketing __ Help customers make steel and aluminum production more efficient. At ABB, you will work with technologies that support more productive and sustainable industry. In this international sales role, you will grow ABB Metallurgy Products in selected export markets and build lasting partnerships with customers around the world. Your role and responsibilities ABB Metallurgy is a global supplier of electromagnetic stirring and braking systems for the steel and aluminum industries. As Area Sales Manager, you will manage sales in your assigned markets, develop new opportunities and work with colleagues across ABB to shape strong customer solutions - from the first discussion through offer and delivery. The position is based in Västerås, Sweden. You will report to the Global Sales and Marketing Manager, Metallurgy Products, and travel regularly to your markets, up to approximately 50%. Develop market plans for your assigned territories and deliver against sales volume and margin targets; review performance and agree recovery actions where needed. Lead sales activities and build trusted, long-term relationships with customers by understanding their priorities and providing a responsive experience. Identify, qualify and pursue new business opportunities in existing and emerging markets. Work with global colleagues to communicate ABB’s value proposition and support effective marketing activities. Coordinate customer offers with the Global Sales Manager, ensuring proposals are technically sound and commercially accurate. Manage sales administration and support project activities when required. Track market developments and competitor activity, sharing insights that inform sales plans and help address risks. Your background A relevant degree in engineering, business or a related field. B2B sales experience is valued, and candidates with a strong technical background are encouraged to apply, even if they have only a few years of sales experience. Experience in the steel and/or aluminum industry is an advantage. Strong communication and relationship-building skills, with a customer-focused and professional approach. Fluent English, spoken and written. A proactive, adaptable working style and willingness to travel internationally, up to approximately 50%. More about us We develop and deliver electromagnetic stirrers and braking systems to steel and aluminum producers worldwide. Our solutions support important production processes such as melting and casting, helping customers improve process performance and use resources more efficiently. Our R&D and Simulations teams use advanced calculation tools and multiphysics models to assess and optimize electromagnetic actuation, including in customer projects.
