Integration Manager

Marshall Group AB · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 450 000–550 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 27.11.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Marshall Group is the audio, tech, and design powerhouse uniting musicians and music lovers through genre-breaking innovation. Our flagship brand, Marshall, is uniquely positioned with over 60 years of rock ‘n’ roll attitude on stage, at home, and on the go. Our iconic products are brought to life by a dedicated team of 800 passionate employees and sold in over 90 markets worldwide. What you’ll do: As Integration Manager, you will define how systems, data, and digital capabilities come together across Marshall Group. You will own the integration strategy and platform, while building and scaling a dedicated integration function that supports our ERP, eCommerce, PIM, and wider application landscape. This role sits at the centre of our digital ecosystem and plays a key part in enabling stable operations, efficient end-to-end processes, and future growth. Roles & Responsibilities: Define and drive the Group-wide integration strategy and roadmap Own and evolve the Azure integration platform with a focus on scalability, stability, and security Build, lead, and develop a high-performing integration team, including nearshore capabilities Ensure reliable data flows and strong ownership across key business systems Establish best practices for integration architecture, monitoring, governance, and compliance Who we’re looking for: You are a technically grounded leader who enjoys shaping direction while staying close to real systems and teams. You are comfortable working across business and IT, able to translate complex needs into clear and pragmatic integration solutions. You bring structure without overengineering and know how to balance long-term architectural thinking with day-to-day delivery. You probably have the following experiences & skills: Hands-on experience with Azure Integration Services such as Logic Apps, Service Bus, Event Grid, and API Management Previous experience working with integrations, ideally within a team or platform setting Good understanding of integration patterns, APIs, and data flows across enterprise systems It's a bonus if you also have experience collaborating with distributed or nearshore teams, some exposure to data platforms, BI, or AI initiatives, prior experience with direct reports, and familiarity with ERP, eCommerce, or PIM environments. Our pledge: We thrive to foster an inclusive workplace, and we do not discriminate on the basis of race, religion, disability, colour, national origin, gender, sexual orientation, age or marital status. We firmly believe that Marshall thrives when our employees do, leading to better experiences for our consumers. We encourage applications from all over the world. To enable transitioning from another country for a role at Marshall, we offer relocation support. This support is tailored for each role but always includes visa/work permit application, local authority registration, and a home-finding service.
