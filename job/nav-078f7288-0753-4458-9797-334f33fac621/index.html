Composite Team Lead

Krush Tech · Oslo, Norway

Lønn ikke oppgittHeltidvia NAVFrist 7.1.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

About the Role We are looking for a Team Lead to coordinate daily work in our Composite production team. You will ensure that activities are carried out safely, efficiently, and to quality requirements. Key Responsibilities • Coordinate daily team activities, distribute tasks, and balance workload according to production priorities. • Monitor progress and ensure production targets and deadlines are met. • Ensure work follows established instructions, procedures, and quality requirements. • Identify and report defects, deviations, and production issues. • Train and support team members, including new employees. • Ensure compliance with HSE requirements and maintain workplace organization. • Identify opportunities to improve production efficiency and workflow. Requirements • Experience in production or manufacturing. • Experience guiding or coordinating other employees. • Strong organizational, communication, and problem-solving skills. • Responsibility, attention to detail, and focus on quality. • Working knowledge of Ukrainian (to communicate with team members in Ukrainian). • Basic English communication skills (A1 or higher). Area-Specific Requirements Сomposite • Experience in composite manufacturing or processing of similar materials. • Understanding of composite materials, production processes, and related equipment. • Experience with resins, chemicals, or similar production materials is an advantage. What We Offer A hands-on leadership role in a growing production environment, with opportunities to develop your skills and contribute to team and process improvement. How to Apply Please submit your CV using the application form below. Applications will be reviewed on a rolling basis.
