An overview of this role You'll build and lead the regional team of product Subject Matter Experts who win GitLab's most complex opportunities. Your team covers four product groups: Security & Compliance, Platform, Core DevSecOps, and AI & Competitive. You'll partner with regional sales leadership to decide where SMEs go, coach your team to technical wins, and make sure every engagement leaves the Account team stronger. This is a player-coach role. You'll stay close to the technology and step into critical deals yourself. What you'll do • Hire, develop and retain a regional team of SMEs across four product groups, with a clear growth path for each person. • Run the regional engagement model with Account SA and Post-Sales leaders. Keep triage fast, coverage focused on the top opportunities, and the line between Account SA and SME ownership clear. • Partner with regional sales leaders and AVPs on deal strategy, forecast calls and product-category plans. • Review SME coverage weekly against the regional pipeline. Move capacity to where product-category commit and consumption are at risk. • Step into strategic deals as an executive technical sponsor when the opportunity calls for it. • Coach SMEs on PoV design, executive storytelling and competitive positioning. Celebrate wins openly and treat misses as data.
