Additional citizenship requirements may apply depending on the security classification. About the Role Hykmann Global Business Solutions AB is looking for talented Digital Design Engineers to contribute to the development of advanced digital electronics for fighter radar and defence-related electronic systems. You will join a growing digital design engineering environment, working across the product lifecycle – from initial requirements and conceptual studies to hardware implementation, integration, verification and product maintenance. The role involves developing high-performance digital circuit boards and electronic hardware where performance, reliability and system integration are critical. You will collaborate with system engineers, subsystem engineers and technical specialists to translate system-level requirements into robust hardware solutions. Depending on your experience, you may contribute to areas ranging from high-frequency function-related electronics to computer platforms, data links and advanced digital processing hardware. Key Responsibilities Develop robust, high-performance digital hardware solutions for advanced radar and defence electronics systems at PCB/PBA level. Translate system and subsystem requirements into practical digital hardware designs. Select suitable electronic components and evaluate their performance and integration requirements. Perform or support high-speed signal integrity simulations, including designs operating at 10 Gbps and above. Develop electronic schematics and provide support for PCB layout activities. Work with digital electronics architectures involving CPUs, FPGAs, SoCs and DDR memory. Participate in board bring-up activities in collaboration with relevant engineering stakeholders. Plan and perform hardware testing, integration and verification activities. Develop scripts and tools to support testing and integration. Conduct conceptual investigations and technical studies for future hardware solutions, architectures and design principles. Contribute to technical decisions, design reviews and continuous improvement initiatives. Provide production support and participate in ongoing product maintenance activities. Required Qualifications & Technical Skills Education Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Engineering Physics or a related technical discipline. Digital Electronics & Hardware Design Experience developing digital electronics at PCB/PBA level. Understanding of digital hardware architectures and component integration. Experience with CPU, FPGA and DDR memory implementations. Knowledge of SoC-based digital electronics is relevant. PCB/PBA Development Tools Experience with professional PCB/PBA development toolsets such as Cadence and/or Siemens. Ability to work with electronic schematics and hardware design documentation. Understanding of PCB design and layout considerations. Signal Integrity & Power Integrity Experience with Signal Integrity (SI) and Power Integrity (PI) simulations. Familiarity with tools such as Cadence Sigrity and/or Siemens HyperLynx SI/PI. Understanding of high-speed digital design considerations. Testing & Integration Experience with hardware testing and integration at PCB/PBA level. Ability to develop test scripts using Python and/or MATLAB. Structured approach to hardware verification and troubleshooting.
