Technical Sales and Service Representative Are you technically skilled, self-driven and motivated by combining hands-on work with long-term customer relationships? MAXI-PRESS is looking for a Technical Sales and Service Representative to support and develop its business across Sweden and the wider Scandinavian market. This is a varied and independent position for someone with experience from a technical industrial environment who would like to combine field service, installation and customer-focused technical sales. About the company MAXI-PRESS is a German company that has developed, manufactured and supplied spare parts, wear parts and consumables for industrial laundries since 2006. Its product portfolio supports machinery from a wide range of manufacturers and helps customers maintain reliable and efficient production. The range includes membranes, ironer padding, conveyor belts and numerous other specialist components, spare parts and consumables for industrial laundry equipment. MAXI-PRESS became part of the JENSEN-GROUP in 2024 while continuing to operate under its established brand. The business is now entering an exciting phase of growth and is looking to strengthen its presence across Sweden and Scandinavia. About the role As a Technical Sales and Service Representative, you will become an important customer-facing representative of MAXI-PRESS in Scandinavia. The role combines practical technical work with sales and the development of existing customer relationships. Initially, the position will have a strong technical focus. You will visit industrial laundries to carry out installations, light maintenance and the replacement of wear and spare parts. This may include installing ironer padding, replacing conveyor belts and identifying requirements for spare parts and consumables. Working directly at customer sites will give you a natural opportunity to understand each customer’s machinery, operations and future requirements. The commercial side of the role is therefore focused on developing existing relationships and recommending relevant technical solutions rather than traditional cold-call sales. Your main responsibilities will include: Carrying out light technical maintenance and installations at customer sites Installing and replacing ironer padding, conveyor belts and other wear parts Identifying customer requirements for spare parts and consumables Developing and maintaining long-term customer relationships Providing technical advice and recommending suitable products Preparing quotations and following up on sales opportunities Planning customer visits and independently organising your working week Working closely with technical specialists and colleagues within MAXI-PRESS and the JENSEN-GROUP Supporting the continued growth of MAXI-PRESS across Sweden and Scandinavia This is a home-based position involving extensive travel, primarily by car. You will mainly support customers in southern Sweden, Denmark and parts of Norway. Approximately 75 per cent of the role may involve travel, including regular overnight stays. We are therefore primarily looking for someone based in southern Sweden, preferably in Skåne or within the area extending from Gothenburg southwards. Your exact location will affect how frequently customer visits can be completed as day trips.
