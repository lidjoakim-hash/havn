Grafana Labs is the company behind Grafana Cloud, the fully managed observability platform trusted by more than 10,000 organizations to ensure reliability, resolve incidents faster, and optimize telemetry at scale. Built on open source and open standards and designed for interoperability across any stack, Grafana Cloud brings AI to observability and observability to AI, giving teams (and their agents) unified visibility so they can see, understand, and act on all their disparate data, wherever it lives, and move at the speed of their ambitions. Customers, including Anthropic, Bloomberg, NVIDIA, Microsoft, and Salesforce, rely on Grafana Labs. We are a 100% remote company with team members across 40+ countries, backed by leading investors including Lightspeed Venture Partners, Sequoia Capital, GIC, Coatue, J.P. Morgan, CapitalG, and Lead Edge Capital. Learn more at grafana.com and follow us on LinkedIn and X. We’re scaling fast and staying true to what makes us different: an open-source legacy, a global collaborative culture, and a passion for meaningful work. Our team thrives in an innovation-driven environment where transparency, autonomy, and trust fuel everything we do. You may not meet every requirement, and that’s okay. If this role excites you, we’d love you to raise your hand for what could be a truly career-defining opportunity. This is a remote opportunity and we would be interested in applications from the UK, Ireland, Sweden, Spain and Germany. The opportunity: Grafana Labs is building a native experimentation platform: a Grafana app that lets teams define A/B tests, connect them to feature flags and product telemetry, and get trustworthy, statistically sound answers about what their changes actually did. It started as a hackathon project and is now used by Grafana engineering and product teams in our internal environment to run real experiments. The next step is to make it a first-class product: one we rely on every day internally and, over time, one we offer to Grafana Cloud customers alongside Frontend Observability, Session Replay and our other user-experience capabilities. You'll join the Product Analytics team, a group of highly technical product analysts who help teams across Grafana understand how our products are used and run experiments on them. As the team's dedicated software engineer, you'll own the engineering of the experimentation app and have a lot of influence over where it goes. You'll partner closely with data scientists on the statistics, data engineers on event pipelines and the data warehouse, software engineers across Grafana on feature flags, Frontend Observability and the App Platform, and product stakeholders who rely on experiment results to make decisions. This is a hands-on engineering role that ships production code. It is not an analyst role. About the product: The experimentation app is built on the Grafana App Platform: • Frontend: a TypeScript and React app plugin using @grafana/ui, plus a custom datasource plugin that powers experiment statistics panels. • Backend: Go, built with the Grafana App SDK. Experiments are Kubernetes-style custom resources defined in CUE, with an operator, admission webhooks, and support for declarative, GitOps-style management. • Feature flags: integration with OpenFeature and the OpenFeature Remote Evaluation Protocol (OFREP), Grafana's multi-tenant feature toggle service, and an embedded GO Feature Flag evaluator. • Statistics engine: a Bayesian analysis engine (TypeScript, with a Go port) that produces a direction-aware verdict per metric, probability to be best, credible intervals, expected loss and sample-size recommendations. • Data: experiment exposure and outcome events flow from product instrumentation through RudderStack into BigQuery, or from Grafana Faro into Loki, and are queried live through Grafana datasources. • AI: experiment authoring assisted by Grafana Assistant, which discovers relevant data and drafts the experiment
