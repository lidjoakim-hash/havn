The role As a Lead Photographer in the HelloFresh Studio, you are part of our Photography team, working across food, product, lifestyle, and marketing productions. You are first and foremost an exceptional photographer with deep technical and visual expertise, setting the benchmark for photographic quality, craft, and production excellence across the Studio. You lead through expertise, influence, and example, helping elevate the capabilities of the photography team while maintaining high standards across large-scale asset production. You play a key role in developing photographic talent, evolving ways of working, and driving continuous improvement. You collaborate closely with Producers, Styling, Culinary, Post-production, and Creative teams to deliver high-quality and scalable visual production across multiple brands. You stay up to date with industry developments and actively support AI direction, experimentation, and content generation initiatives within the Studio and broader organization, helping translate new technologies into practical production workflows. What you’ll do • Lead by example and continuously raise the bar for photographic quality and execution across the Studio • Execute and oversee food, product, lifestyle, and marketing photography to the highest commercial standards • Coach, mentor, and support photographers through feedback, knowledge sharing, and hands-on guidance to elevate overall team capability • Define, maintain, and continuously improve photographic standards, shooting guidelines, and best practices • Work together with Studio leadership to improve production quality, studio efficiency, and scalable ways of working • Collaborate closely with Art Direction, Styling, Culinary, Post-production, and Creative teams to ensure strong and consistent visual execution • Support complex productions and solve creative and technical and production challenges on set • Drive consistency across large asset volumes while maintaining visual excellence and operational efficiency • Actively contribute to Studio and company AI initiatives by supporting experimentation, developing practical use cases, and helping integrate AI-supported and hybrid content creation workflows • Act as a trusted expert and escalation point for photographic quality, technical decision-making, and production excellence What you’ll bring • You are a seasoned photography professional with 7+ years of experience in commercial photography, ideally including food, lifestyle, product, and high-volume e-commerce content production • You demonstrate exceptional photography craft and technical excellence. Providing a portfolio is mandatory • You have deep expertise in lighting, composition, camera systems, color, production environments, and visual storytelling • You have experience operating in fast-paced production environments and collaborating across multiple disciplines and stakeholders • You naturally elevate others through coaching, feedback, mentorship, and sharing best practices • Experience leading photographers or creative production teams is a plus • You are highly influential, collaborative, and comfortable aligning stakeholders without relying on hierarchy • You have a strong production mindset and continuously look for opportunities to improve workflows, quality, and efficiency • You stay current with developments in photography, visual production, and AI-supported content creation, and understand how to apply new technologies in a practical production environment • You have expert knowledge of Capture One and strong understanding of color grading and image editing.
