Together with a colleague, you will be responsible for operating one of MELITEK’s extrusion lines, where polymer compounds for medical-device applications are manufactured.The length of the training period depends on your progress. When you and MELITEK assess that you are ready, you will complete an internal qualification. After passing, you will become a Machine Operator and may help train new colleagues. YOUR MAIN RESPONSIBILITIES • Operate and monitor an extrusion production line together with a colleague • Clean production equipment and prepare it for the next production run • Carry out changeovers between production batches • Perform ongoing checks of products and equipment • Package finished goods • Complete batch, production and quality records in accordance with MELITEK’s ISO 13485:2016 quality management system • Use production equipment, auxiliary equipment and the warehouse management system (WMS) after training YOUR QUALIFICATIONS • Experience from a manufacturing or production company • Practical interest in technology and mechanics • Experience using IT systems • Experience working with a quality management system (QMS) • Ability to read and write Danish - the role includes batch documentation in Danish • A valid Category B forklift certificate is preferred but not required YOUR PERSONAL PROFILE • You are quality-conscious and take pride in your work • You are reliable, committed and maintain good order and structure • You work well both in a team and independently • You approach different tasks with a positive and flexible mindset • You are physically fit for production work WORKING HOURS AND CONDITIONSThe production teams work three 12-hour shifts per week on either day shift from 06:00 to 18:00 or night shift from 18:00 to 06:00. Production currently runs six days per week.Allowances, pension and other employment conditions follow the Danish Industry Collective Agreement and local agreements. MELITEK also offers health insurance and a food and fruit scheme. Start date: By agreement. MELITEK OFFERS • Individual training with a clear development path from Production Operator to Machine Operator • A position in a modern production facility manufacturing materials for medical-device applications • Close cooperation with experienced colleagues • Employment conditions governed by the Danish Industry Collective Agreement and local agreements • A workplace that values service, flexibility, learning, continuous improvement and professional pride • A strong team spirit across the whole organization with social events throughout the year ABOUT MELITEKMELITEK is a privately owned Danish company with administration and production in Nørre Alslev on Falster.
