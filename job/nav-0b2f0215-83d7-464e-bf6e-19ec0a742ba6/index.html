About the position We invite applications for a full-time position as Associate Professor in cell biology at the Section for Physiology and Cell Biology, Department of Biosciences, Faculty of Mathematics and Natural Sciences, University of Oslo. The Department of Biosciences seeks an active researcher with a strong publication record within cell biology. The successful applicant should be on an upward trajectory and have strong potential to develop and execute research projects at a high international level. The research should address fundamental questions in cell biology using animal- or plant-based systems and create synergies with current research in the section and department.  Research in the Section for Physiology and Cell Biology includes physiological adaptations to extreme conditions, comparative immunology, muscle physiology, neural mechanisms of learning and memory in health and disease, plant signalling and development, cellular and molecular mechanisms of disease including stress signalling, hormone action and cancer biology, as well as intracellular membrane trafficking, organelle dynamics, and mechanisms regulating cell migration and organization. The research groups develop and apply advanced technologies including live imaging, molecular tools and gene engineering, omics technologies and bioinformatics. The Department of Biosciences is organized into four research sections; Section for Physiology and Cell biology (FYSCELL), Section for Environmental and marine biology (EMB), Section for, Ecology, Biodiversity, and Evolution (EBE), and Section for Molecular Biology, Biochemistry and Bioinformatics (MBB). The research at the department focuses on fundamental biological processes from the molecular and cellular level to the population and ecosystem level. The department has recently moved into the new Life Science Building, which brings together researchers and students from chemistry, pharmacy, biosciences and laboratory medicine and provides state-of-the-art research infrastructure. The department provides access to advanced research infrastructure, including state-of-the-art core facilities, field stations, and specialized platforms that support interdisciplinary life science research across multiple disciplines. Your main tasks will be • Develop and lead an internationally competitive research programme in cell biology, using animal- or plant-based systems and taking advantage of existing infrastructure and common research facilities. • Secure external research funding and establish or contribute to national and international research collaborations and initiatives. • Carry out research-based teaching in cell biology and molecular biology at bachelor´s, master´s and PhD levels, including laboratory-based courses and supervise master’s and PhD students. • Contribute to the academic development of the section and department through collegial activities, scientific leadership, and administrative duties. What skills are important in this role? Required qualifications: • Completed PhD or equivalent doctoral degree in cell biology or a related field, with a strong scientific track record within cell biology • Documented experience in securing external research funding. • Applicants must document academic qualifications in cell biology equivalent to an Associate Professor position. Scientific qualifications will be given significant weight, with particular emphasis on the quality and extent of scientific output within cell biology over the last five years, as well as experience with modern experimental research methods and techniques. • The successful applicant must be able to carry out research-based teaching in cell and molecular biology at bachelor, master and PhD levels including laboratory-based courses and supervise Master and PhD students. • Pedagogical qualifications and teaching and supervision experience at all levels will be an important factor in the evaluation process and should be well documented in a teaching portfolio. The applicant should describe their qualifications in view of the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning (SoTL) framework which includes:• Focus on student learning • A clear development over time • A researching approach • A collegial attitude and practice • Applicants who at the time of appointment cannot document sufficient teaching qualifications (minimum formal requirement is a 200 hours pedagogical programme) will be required to obtain such qualifications within a two-year period. • The successful candidate must demonstrate mastery of both English and one of the Scandinavian languages as working languages, in order to be able to teach and otherwise be able to participate in all functions the position may involve. Candidates who do not have sufficient mastery of a Scandinavian language (B2 level) at the time of appointment, will be required attain B2 level proficiency in Norwegian within three years of employment. Desired qualifications: • Candidates should be able to establish, lead and conduct research projects, also as a collaborator. Experience in scientific and administrative management is an advantage. • International research networks, outreach activities, collegiality, and the ability to foster a positive working environment will be considered in the evaluation. • The person appointed will be requested to take on some administrative duties. Hence such experience will be an advantage. • Experience in utilizing advanced research infrastructure such as those available at the Department of Biosciences and the University of Oslo, and the ability to contribute to their further development, is an advantage. What are we looking for in you?
