About the service Assignment Description We are seeking an experienced BI Developer / Data Analyst to join a Data & Analytics team working on next-generation analytical solutions using modern data platforms. Collaborate with developers, project managers, UX specialists, and analysts. What You Bring Strong SQL and DBT experience, with the ability to independently drive analytics from requirements to delivery. Experience with Looker, Tableau, or similar visualization tools. Experience in data modeling and working with large datasets. At least 5 years of relevant experience as a Data Analyst, Analytics Engineer, or similar. Proven experience translating business needs into analytical solutions. Fluent Swedish and English, both spoken and written. Meritorious Google Cloud Platform (GCP) and BigQuery. GitHub. LookML and semantic layers. AI-assisted development tools such as Copilot or Claude. Data and analytics product development. Self-service analytics and user-focused reporting. JavaScript/TypeScript and modern frontend frameworks such as Vue.js. Experience building interactive charts and data visualizations.
