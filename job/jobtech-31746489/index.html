Mechanical Development Engineer - Electrical Integration Focus

Siemens Energy AB · Finspång, Sweden

A Snapshot of Your Day Join our open-minded team where collaboration and support are at the heart of what we do! In this dynamic role, you will engage in designing structural and layout solutions for our gas turbine packages, producing manufacturing data in Siemens NX along with essential technical documentation. You will play a crucial role in strengthening our capability within electrical integration by working closely with the Electrical Engineering department. Your efforts will support the placement and routing of electrical installations, such as junction boxes, cable trays, conduits, and cable routing within our NX environment. This position offers a unique opportunity to blend traditional Structure & Layout work with a growing specialization in electrical integration, helping to shape future methodologies alongside our electrical experts. How You’ll Make an Impact Design structural and layout solutions for gas turbine packages while producing manufacturing data and technical documentation in Siemens NX. Develop and maintain 3D layouts, installation concepts, and design documentation for package structures and equipment arrangements. Drive the integration of electrical installations within the package layout, including junction boxes, cable routing, cable trays, conduits, and installation supports. Collaborate closely with Electrical Engineering to ensure electrical solutions are efficiently incorporated into the overall product design. Participate in the development and maintenance of product standards, design guidelines, and best practices within Structure & Layout and electrical integration. Work with procurement, suppliers, and cross-functional engineering teams to develop robust, cost-effective, and manufacturable technical solutions. Support development projects and customer orders by delivering high-quality designs that meet project requirements, schedules, and quality expectations. Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives, focusing on electrical routing methodologies, NX design processes, and installation concepts. What You Bring A degree in Mechanical Engineering or a related field. Proficiency in Siemens NX for design and documentation. Experience in structural design and layout, particularly in the context of gas turbine packages. Relevant knowledge of electrical systems, including familiarity with electrical integration concepts and installation practices. Strong collaboration skills to work effectively with cross-functional teams. A commitment to delivering high-quality designs that meet project requirements. Advanced English proficiency is required. About the Team The Structure & Layout team consists of 19 skilled engineers specializing in structural engineering and layout design for complex energy projects. Organized into two collaborative teams, we foster a close-knit and supportive work environment where teamwork, knowledge sharing, and mutual support are key to our success. We place a strong emphasis on personal and professional development, ensuring that everyone has the opportunity to grow. This commitment to quality and continuous improvement creates a culture where individuals thrive while delivering high-quality solutions. Our Gas Services division offers Low-emission power generation through service and decarbonization. Zero or low emission power generation and all gas turbines under one roof, steam turbines and generators. Decarbonization opportunities through service offerings, modernization, and digitalization of the fleet. We can offer you employment benefits such as: reduction of working hours, advance vacation, health care allowance and an eventual possibility to a flexible working place. Who is Siemens Energy? At Siemens Energy, we are more than just an energy technology company. With ~100,000 dedicated employees in more than 90 countries, we develop the energy systems of the future, ensuring that the growing energy demand of the global community is met reliably and sustainably.
