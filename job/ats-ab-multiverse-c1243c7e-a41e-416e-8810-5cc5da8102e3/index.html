Success in the short to mid-term will be defined by how well we execute against our strategy, how capable we are to continuously improve core operating metrics and process and how supported our 300+ coaches are to have a daily, positive impact to our apprentices and learners. As a Post-Gateway Success Coach, your primary focus will be on providing essential support and guidance to our apprentices as they prepare for their End Point Assessment (EPA) and resits. You will play a pivotal role in supporting our learners as they transition from on-programme learning to the successful completion of their EPA. You will be responsible for ensuring that learners are well-prepared, motivated, and engaged throughout this critical phase of their apprenticeship. Your role involves tracking engagement, facilitating EPA preparation, providing 1:1 support to Apprentices and, when needed, facilitating resubmissions. Additionally, you manage learner escalations and escalate when necessary. Your work contributes to learners succeeding in their EPA and continue their journey toward professional growth.
