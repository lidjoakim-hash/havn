Tour Guide – Voss & the Fjords of Western Norway - Summer 2027

Voss Og Fjordane Guideservice As · Voss, Norway

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Trade a life of hustle and bustle for a summer in the Norwegian fjords and mountains. Meet guests from around the world, join our friendly international team and gain valuable experience while exploring spectacular landscapes, small communities and some of Norway’s most beautiful destinations. Hva vi ser etter Who We Are Looking For If you are curious, service focussed, reliable and enjoy working closely amongst a team of individuals who enjoy connecting with people and working in a dynamic environment, then we are very interested in talking to you. Requirements: • Fluency in English and at least one, of the following languages: German, Spanish, Italian, French, Norwegian, Dutch or Chinese. Portuguese can also be an advantage. Proficiency in two or more of these languages in addition to English is considered a strong advantage. • If you are not a Norwegian citizen, you must be an EU/EEA citizen or already have a valid Norwegian work permit. • Interest in Norwegian history, culture, and nature. • Guide Certification (or willingness to obtain one). • Strong and demonstrable verbal communication skills are a huge advantage • An experienced team player Not Certified Yet? As a part of our quality guarantee to our clients, our guides must hold at minimum a Guide Certification ensuring high professional standards. Several certification options are available in Norway. We work together with and recommend the Guide Academy Certification Programme , which includes an engaging online course (completed remotely) and an intensive on-site training session in Voss starting April the 12th 2027. No prior guiding experience is required, only passion and enthusiasm! We sponsor the certification fee for candidates who commit to working a full season exclusively with Voss og Fjordane Guideservice. Arbeidsoppgaver What is working as a Guide in the Voss area like? As a Tour Guide, you'll lead guests through some of Western Norway's most spectacular fjord and mountain landscapes. Assignments range from scenic coach and fjord tours to ferry excursions, walking tours and hikes. You'll lead groups of up to 50 guests, share local stories and culture, and take responsibility for creating a safe, engaging and memorable experience. The job is dynamic: schedules can change, and some tours will be repeated throughout the season. But every group is different, and there are also opportunities to work from other ports and experience more of Western Norway. Vi tilbyr What We Offer • An international job in the heart of Fjord Norway - Experience a more authentic side of Norway, living and working among fjords, mountains, traditional villages and small local communities. Voss gives you a quieter base close to nature, while your assignments take you through some of Western Norway's most attractive destinations. • A seasonal job with variety - Most assignments take place between May and mid-October, with additional guiding opportunities from April and into the later part of the year. • Guaranteed minimum seasonal income - More predictability while still retaining the variety and flexibility that comes with guiding. • Professional training and certification sponsorship - We'll help you gain professional qualifications and can sponsor your Guide Certification, including visits to destinations and venues throughout the region when you commit to a full season with us . • Fair working conditions - We focus on creating a sustainable, inclusive and positive working environment. We provide full paid training on company specific procedures, tour logistics and guest service standards, • Explore more of Fjord Norway - Your workplace extends far beyond Voss, with opportunities to guide in Flåm, Eidfjord, Ulvik, Vik, Odda, Rosendal and other destinations across the region. Assignments outside our normal region come with higher pay and paid travel time. • Opportunities to grow - Guiding can be a first step into a wider career in tourism, hospitality and destination development. Support with finding accommodation Moving to Voss? We help make the move easier by arranging accommodation for most of our seasonal guides. This is subject to availability and at the guide's own expense. We can also provide guidance if you prefer to find your own place.
