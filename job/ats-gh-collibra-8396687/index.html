Joining Collibra Collibra helps organizations confidently use data and AI, anywhere, at any scale. As enterprises move AI from experimentation to production, governance becomes an operational challenge. Organizations need visibility into how AI systems are built, what data they rely on, who has access, and how decisions are made. They need trusted context, clear controls, and the ability to scale responsibly. We're looking for a Forward Deployed Engineer to work directly with strategic customers as they operationalize data and AI governance across complex enterprise environments. You'll sit at the intersection of engineering, product, and customer outcomes, helping customers solve challenging technical problems while identifying opportunities to improve and extend the Collibra platform. This role is ideal for someone who enjoys building, problem-solving, and working closely with customers to deliver measurable business impact. Forward Deployed Engineers at Collibra are responsible for • Partnering directly with strategic customers to understand technical requirements, architecture, and governance objectives. • Designing and implementing solutions that integrate Collibra with data platforms, AI systems, cloud environments, and enterprise applications. • Building prototypes, integrations, accelerators, and reusable workflows that help customers realize value faster. • Supporting complex customer deployments where standard implementation approaches are insufficient. • Working alongside Customer Engineering, Professional Services, Product, and Engineering teams to solve high-impact technical challenges. • Helping customers operationalize AI governance, data governance, data quality, and access governance capabilities. • Identifying patterns across customer engagements and translating those insights into scalable product improvements. • Acting as a trusted technical advisor to customer engineering, architecture, and governance teams. • Contributing technical documentation, implementation patterns, and best practices that improve future deployments. You have • 5+ years of experience in software engineering, solutions engineering, technical consulting, sales engineering, or a related technical role. • Experience building applications, integrations, or automation using Python, Java, JavaScript, or similar programming languages. • Experience working with APIs, cloud platforms, enterprise architectures, and distributed systems. • Strong understanding of data architectures, metadata management, governance, security, or AI systems. • Experience translating complex business requirements into technical solutions. • Excellent communication skills and the ability to work effectively with technical and executive stakeholders. • Strong problem-solving skills and comfort operating in ambiguous environments. • Demonstrated proficiency in leveraging AI tools (e.g., Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT, Copilot) to solve real-world business challenges, drive measurable outcomes, or streamline workflows. • A bachelor’s degree or equivalent related working experience is required • This position is eligible for visa sponsorship You are • Curious about how complex organizations operate and solve problems. • Comfortable moving between technical implementation and strategic discussions. • Energized by customer challenges and motivated by measurable outcomes. • Able to navigate ambiguity and create structure where it doesn't exist. • Passionate about helping organizations build trust in data and AI. Measures of success Within your first month, you will • Develop a strong understanding of Collibra's platform, customers, and strategic priorities. • Build relationships with Customer Engineering, Product, Engineering, and Customer Success teams. • Participate in customer engagements and begin contributing to technical solution design. Within your third month, you will • Lead technical workstreams for strategic customer initiatives. •
