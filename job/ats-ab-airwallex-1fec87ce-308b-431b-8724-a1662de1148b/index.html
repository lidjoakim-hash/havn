ABOUT AIRWALLEX Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own. We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.   HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT You’ll build and execute the Enterprise Banking sales motion across DACH, helping large enterprises manage complex financial operations through connected capabilities across corporate treasury, spend management, workforce financial controls and Agentic Banking. - Own the full sales cycle for complex, multi-stakeholder opportunities, typically involving deal sizes of $250K+ ARR, from prospecting and discovery through commercial negotiation and contract signature. - Engage senior finance and business leaders, including CFOs, treasurers, finance directors, heads of payments and operations, and connect Airwallex’s capabilities to their priorities and measurable business outcomes. - Shape the sales playbook and go-to-market strategy by bringing customer insight, competitive intelligence and market feedback to the Enterprise Banking product, marketing and implementation teams. - Build and manage a high-quality pipeline through outbound prospecting, partner referrals, field events, industry networks, account planning and whitespace analysis. - Lead consultative, value-led sales processes across discovery, stakeholder mapping, value proposition design, executive presentations, business cases, procurement, legal negotiation and close. - Partner with Solutions Engineering and Product on tailored demonstrations and proof-of-concepts, and coordinate with Implementation after close to support a seamless handover and strong early adoption. You’ll work from our Munich or Berlin office three days per week.
