Head to getyourguide.careers to take the first step. The Team Mission Strategic Partnerships is GetYourGuide's partner distribution channel. It generates a significant share of the company's revenue, with a team of 30 people across EMEA, the Americas and APAC. A dedicated partnership tech team sits in Product and Engineering; the Partnerships Director sets its product strategy. Partners include online travel platforms, banks and loyalty programs, affiliate networks, travel agents and more. The ambition is to triple the channel within three years. That means changing how the channel operates, not optimizing the current momentum. Your Mission We are hiring a director-level leader to run the Partnerships function. You will set the strategy for growing the partner channel, set the product direction for the partner platform, and directly lead teams across sales, account management, and strategy and operations. The function was brought together in 2026 from two teams and is being rebuilt around partner segments. You get to shape it from close to the ground up, with the COO as your sponsor, and you run it end to end from day one. This is a highly visible role based in Berlin, working in a hybrid model. We offer a competitive relocation package to support your move—successful candidates will be expected to be based in Berlin or happy to relocate.
