Legal & Compliance Intern (f/m/x)

HelloFresh · Amsterdam, North Holland, Netherlands, Netherlands

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

The role: What's in the Box As a Legal & Compliance Intern in our Benelux Legal team, you'll be the first point of contact for day-to-day legal questions across the business. You'll support our legal counsels by taking ownership of routine contract reviews and compliance tasks, so commercial projects keep moving without delay. Our team works closely with Marketing, HR, Operations and Procurement to help HelloFresh grow while managing risk in a fast-moving, regulated food and e-commerce environment. This is your chance to see how in-house legal work directly enables the business, and to build hands-on experience you won't get in a lecture hall. What you’ll do: The Recipe • Draft, review and process standard commercial agreements such as NDAs, Terms & Conditions and vendor contracts, in collaboration with our legal counsels • Act as the first filter for legal questions from teams like Marketing and HR, giving practical answers and escalating complex matters to the right counsel • Support the team in keeping our compliance and regulatory obligations up to date, including record-keeping and basic regulatory checks • Organize and maintain our legal databases and templates so colleagues can quickly find what they need • Research legal topics relevant to our industry and turn your findings into clear, concise summaries for the team • Help improve our legal processes by spotting recurring questions and suggesting templates, FAQs or smarter ways of working What you’ll bring: The Ingredients • Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Law at a university or hogeschool • A basic understanding of contract law and corporate compliance • Excellent written and spoken English and Dutch • A pragmatic, problem-solving mindset: you see legal as a partner that helps the business move forward • Strong organizational skills and the ability to work independently on high-volume tasks • A proactive attitude and eagerness to learn Above all, we are looking for people who will make HelloFresh better. There are many different ways of developing skills, and we love diverse experiences! So even if you don’t “tick all the boxes” but think you’d thrive in this role, we would really like to learn more about you. This is a paid internship designed to offer hands-on experience in a fast-paced, international environment. As an intern, you will have structured guidance to support your learning and development. We aim to create an environment where you can build practical skills, gain exposure to how HelloFresh operates, and learn through doing. The internship is suitable for students or recent graduates whose educational requirements allow them to complete an internship during the proposed period. During the application process, we will ask required questions regarding internship eligibility and university requirements (such as duration, documentation, or approval criteria). Please ensure you are able to provide this information as part of your application.
