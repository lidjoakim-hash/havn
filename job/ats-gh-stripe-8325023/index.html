Millions of companies - from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups - use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. About the team Stripe’s SMB and Commercial Sales organization helps ambitious businesses adopt Stripe’s financial infrastructure and accelerate their growth. The team is responsible for acquiring new users, expanding Stripe’s relationships with existing users, and building durable partnerships across a diverse and fast-moving market. We’re looking for an experienced second-line sales leader to lead our SMB and Commercial business across Southern Europe. You will manage a team of sales managers and Account Executives, set the regional strategy, and be accountable for delivering sustainable growth across multiple countries, customer segments, and sales motions. This is a highly visible leadership role requiring strong commercial judgment, operational discipline, and the ability to develop leaders. You will work closely with regional and global Sales leadership and cross-functional partners across Marketing, Partnerships, Product, Solutions Architecture, Customer Success, Deal Pricing, and Operations. You will combine strategic leadership with hands-on involvement in the business. You’ll help your managers raise the performance of their teams, engage directly in the region’s most important opportunities, and represent Southern Europe in regional and global planning.
