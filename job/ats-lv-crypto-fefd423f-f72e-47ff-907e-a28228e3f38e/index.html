Crypto.com’s Compliance function safeguards the business and its customers by ensuring the group operates within applicable laws, regulations and industry standards across every market we serve. The function is organised around three core pillars: Regional Compliance, Compliance Advisory: Policies, Product and Governance, and Compliance Support and Oversight. Together, these teams partner with the business to enable responsible growth, uphold our regulatory obligations, and protect the trust our customers place in us. The Regional Compliance team owns day-to-day compliance execution in-country: local licensing, regulator engagement, exams and audits, and acting as the first line of compliance partnership to the regional business. About the Role At Crypto.com , we are building the future of digital and capital markets finance at a global scale. We are looking for a sharp, commercially minded, motivated Senior Financial Crime Manager to join the UK Compliance team reporting to the UK Head of Compliance and MLRO. In this high impact role, you will hold end to end responsibility for the effectiveness of the UK financial crime framework across our Digital Assets, Capital Markets and EMI business in the UK. We are seeking an experienced and driven Senior Financial Crime & Regulatory Compliance Manager to support the management and oversight of regulatory compliance and financial crime risks across Crypto.com’s UK Cryptoasset Exchange and Electronic Money Institution (EMI) businesses. This is a senior role with end to end responsibility for effectiveness and operation of the firm's financial crime framework. You will provide strategic and operational support across all aspects of the financial crime framework including maintaining a robust controls program and overseeing the continuous evolution and improvements to the financial crime framework including but not limited to advising on risk assessments across clients and products, transaction monitoring and advising the business on financial crime risk matters. The role requires strong financial crime expertise, ideally gained across digital asset firms, fintech, or traditional financial services , together with a sound understanding of wider UK regulatory compliance requirements. The successful candidate must be comfortable working independently, providing practical advice and engaging with senior stakeholders and Group functions.
