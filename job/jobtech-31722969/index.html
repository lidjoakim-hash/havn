Company description: Ericsson AB Job description: Join our Team About this opportunity: Are you looking for a new challenge and ready to create the 6G future? This could be the opportunity for you!   We are looking for a System Architect position within the Ericsson Silicon FPGA organization in Lund. Ericsson is investing heavily in the future 6G technology where leading-edge ASIC and FPGA development is key towards our product development.     As part of our team, you will drive and contribute to the definition, systemization, and development of our FPGAs that are part of Ericsson´s mobile network infrastructure products for the 5G and upcoming 6G network. You will be actively involved from the early pre-study phase through definition and development of the FPGA project until the product is released to the market.   You will have the opportunity to work closely with other system architects across different sites and will interface with external FPGA suppliers and stakeholders.     What you will do: The FPGA System Architect has the following responsibilities:   Systemize FPGAs, their related blocks and subsystems, while supporting the Radio portfolios  Develop and set requirements on the architectural domain and support prototyping activities within the 6G technologies  Support the design team during development, verification and validation of the design.   Accountable for compliance with product requirements as well as area, power and performance KPI’s.   Conduct technical assessments of change requests or new requirements.   Responsible for technical decision making to secure the best technical solution and development effort.   Responsible for SoC product documentation (Requirement Specification, Implementation Specification, Pin List, etc.).   Main technical contact towards vendors or partners.     The skills you bring: Master's Degree or PhD in Electronic Engineering or equivalent education is preferred   Over 10 years experience in sophisticated FPGA (or silicon development) end-to-end development   Excellent knowledge of FPGA technology   Knowledge of the design environment and design methodology (ex: EDA tools)   Knowledge in VHDL and/or System Verilog   Knowledge of radio access system and their components   Successful cross-team and cross-site experience   Occasional International Travel will be required   Quality-consciousness
