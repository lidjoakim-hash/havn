Who we are We’re bold thinkers and kind teammates, growing fast but staying grounded. Our Nordic roots and global outlook shape a culture of friendliness, creativity, and collaboration, all powered by openness and shared wins. We care deeply about doing the right thing - and doing it together. We aim to improve the flow of goods, time, and energy - and we know we can’t shape the future by clinging to the past. So, we keep evolving, together. What we do RELEX runs the planning decisions behind 700+ retailers and manufacturers worldwide, deciding, at real scale, what gets forecast, stocked, priced, promoted, and produced across the entire value chain. Few problems combine this much data, this much complexity, and this much real-world weight: get it right and a shelf stays stocked, a factory line doesn’t stall, food doesn’t rot in a warehouse before it’s sold. Get it wrong, and it shows up in actual losses, for some of the largest retail and manufacturing brands in the world. We’ve grown for 76 consecutive quarters straight, and it’s accelerating: the first half of 2026 was the strongest in the company’s history, with new contract value up 83% year over year. We’re a team of 2,000+ people, headquartered in Helsinki, with offices across Europe, North America, and Asia-Pacific. In response to our fast growth and market expansion, we are looking for a Lead Product Designer to own the user experience of core platform capabilities end to end, embedded directly with the teams that build them. You are a Lead-level designer with excellent design judgement and the skills to understand platform users’ needs and product goals. You apply agentic engineering workflows to ship features into production and contribute directly to delivering customer value. This is a hands-on role where you shape the scope of the work and are accountable for high-quality design outcomes. The role requires understanding both the technical infrastructure behind applications and the use cases they aim to solve. Being part of the platform department calls for systemic thinking and solutions that scale to broader use cases and needs. This role reports into Platform Engineering and is part of the Design Excellence community of 15+ designers across RELEX. What we need you to do and be Bring your ideas. Challenge ours. Grow with us. • Own the user experience of Core Platform application's configuration end to end, embedded in the team that builds it • Work daily with engineers: talk an idea through, review the first build, and bring it up to production quality, and prototype new features with agentic tools • Contribute directly to code, combining design judgment with AI-assisted development to ship to production • Work with the internal builders who use the platform to shape the team’s direction and keep the UI consistent with the main product • Cover design quality for both UX and UI for the team, so craft and quality are yours to set and keep What you’ll bring to the table Our five values are our playbook. We stay curious, back each other up, and make space for both innovation and laughter. • Strong product design thinking and execution: have a clear approach on identifying what to build, with good quality • Systems thinking and information architecture: ability to learn a complex platform quickly • Research skills in technical domains: experience working with technical, business-first internal users • Hands-on contribution to shipped solutions: you contribute to working front-end code and are comfortable with agentic and AI-assisted development • Comfort with ambiguity and the ability to build structure in a fast-moving environment • Interest in building tools for the people who build, and in AI-assisted development • Strong collaboration with engineering, product and design, and the ability to challenge assumptions with evidence • Fast learning
