Are you an experienced software engineer looking to lead through technical expertise, mentorship, and collaboration – while continuing to develop hands-on software? For Alfa Laval's IoT Development Team, we are now looking for a Technical Lead to drive the development of scalable IoT solutions for Alfa Laval's global product portfolio! About the role In this role, you will be part of a collaborative IoT development team, serving as the technical anchor and guide for engineers while turning business requirements into scalable cloud solutions. Alfa Laval contributes to a more sustainable future through technical innovation. The company's solutions help customers improve efficiency across industries such as marine, energy, water treatment, food production, and pharmaceuticals. Alfa Laval's IoT solutions enable remote support, data visualization, and performance analytics for industrial machinery. By analyzing data in real time, the systems help global customers optimize operations and regulatory compliance, reduce unplanned downtime, and lower their environmental footprint. Alfa Laval offers a collaborative and inclusive environment where employees are encouraged to take initiative, share ideas, and turn innovations into reality. You are Offered Global Impact within IoT: The opportunity to build advanced IoT solutions that connect industrial machinery and are used worldwide. Your primary responsibilities: Lead technical design, solution definition, and estimation activities, translating business requirements into scalable and maintainable solutions.
