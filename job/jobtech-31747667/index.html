Job Overview Role Summary Our client is seeking a Subject Matter Expert within the Devices domain who can work independently across one or more endpoint technology areas and provide deep technical expertise. The assignment focuses on managing and continuously improving device platforms while maintaining stability, availability, security, and operational reliability across the assigned environment. Key Responsibilities Manage, maintain, and further develop technical platforms within the Devices domain. Ensure platform stability, availability, and security within the assigned area of responsibility. Plan, drive, and execute lifecycle management activities for endpoint platforms and related technologies. Analyze, troubleshoot, and resolve complex technical issues, including root-cause investigations. Test, validate, and implement new technical solutions and platform capabilities. Provide technical expertise and decision support to the Product Area Manager and the wider Devices team. Transfer technical knowledge to colleagues and the support organization through guidance and documentation. Contribute to incident management, problem management, and root-cause analysis activities. Support deployment, configuration, automation, and ongoing administration of modern client platforms. Technical Stack & Requirements Endpoint management: Microsoft Intune, modern endpoint management, and device administration. Client platforms: Windows client environments and Windows 365 / Cloud PC. Configuration management: Microsoft Configuration Manager / SCCM. Automation: PowerShell scripting and automation of device management activities. Operations: Deployment, configuration, troubleshooting, and lifecycle management. Security: Secure administration and management of modern endpoint platforms. Qualifications & Experience The successful consultant should have deep technical expertise across several of the listed device and endpoint technology areas. The role requires the ability to operate independently, investigate complex technical problems, implement sustainable solutions, and support both engineering and operational teams. Strong practical experience with incident handling, problem management, root-cause analysis, platform lifecycle activities, and knowledge transfer is expected.
