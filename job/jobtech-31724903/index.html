Company Description Vattenfall is one of Europe’s largest producers and retailers of electricity and heat. Our main markets are Sweden, Germany, the Netherlands, Denmark, and the UK. The Vattenfall Group has approximately 21 000 employees. We have been electrifying industries, powering homes and transforming life through innovation for more than 100 years. Job Description Where there’s you, there’s a way. At Vattenfall, we’re determined that there’s a way to leave fossil fuels behind -starting with fossil-free energy and electricity. Bring your curiosity, expertise and ability to turn complex insights into clear direction. Together, we can turn insight into impact. As our Director Group Brand Insight, you’ll lead our insight agenda, connect research with brand strategy and translate evidence into clear strategic direction and actionable recommendations - presented with analytical rigour and clarity. This is a senior role for an experienced insight professional who has built and led insight functions in a complex organisation. Reporting to the VP Brand and joining the Group Brand Management Team within the Communications team, you will shape the insight agenda across brand strategy, communications, media and performance. Your tasks and responsibilities Define how insights are built, structured and shared across the Group, collaborate across markets, functions and disciplines to create one trusted source of truth that guides strategy, priorities and investments holistically within Vattenfall (beyond brand). Lead and develop the Group Brand Insight function, including people leadership, while advising senior stakeholders and managing research partners and budgets. Lead brand health tracking, strategic research, communication testing and KPI reporting across markets. Turn audience, stakeholder and market insights into recommendations that guide investment and improve effectiveness. Create and deliver insights making complex evidence accessible and recommendations actionable, taking ownership from insight through to recommendation and following through on agreed priorities. Advise senior stakeholders, share accessible insights, coach team and manage research partners and budgets. Qualifications You combine analytical rigour with strategic judgement and a clear understanding of how insight contributes to business outcomes. You communicate with clarity, collaborate across markets and disciplines, and develop others through shared expertise. Relevant degree in marketing, business, behavioural or social sciences, statistics or a related field, or equivalent experience, with strong research and analytical grounding. At least 15 years of relevant experience in marketing, branding or insights within a large, complex organisation, with proven experience of translating insights into strategic recommendations and decision support. Expertise in quantitative and qualitative research, brand diagnosis and tracking, communication evaluation and media effectiveness. International team leadership, agency and budget management, fluent English and willingness to travel. Strong executive communication skills, with the ability to translate complex findings into clear, engaging and visually compelling presentations. Proven ability to shape insight strategy, building foundation for the long-term strategy, building the frameworks and governance that make insights shared, consistent and trusted across the organisation. Additional Information Why join us? Come and shape the questions, not just the answers. You’ll connect research with strategy and creativity in an international team. Bring your curiosity, perspective and sense of humour. Together, we want to explore ideas, learn from each other and celebrate progress. Different backgrounds and experiences are welcome.
