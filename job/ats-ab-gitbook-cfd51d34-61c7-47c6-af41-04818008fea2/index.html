👋 A LITTLE ABOUT US We’re building the best platform for authoring, publishing, and maintaining world-class docs. Close to 40,000 sign-ups per month, over 100k Monthly Active Users, used by 2M+ people and thousands of teams like Zoom, FedEx, Nvidia, Snyk, and Google. We’re profitable, backed by P9 Capital, Notion Capital, and Fly VC, and our amazing team is spread across 15 countries in North America, Europe, and Asia. We’re hiring an Enterprise Account Executive to help ambitious teams rethink how prospects, customers and partners learn about and use their products. The role - Build and own your pipeline through a mix of inbound leads and proactive outbound prospecting into target enterprise accounts. - Own the full enterprise sales cycle, from prospecting and discovery through evaluation, negotiation and close. - Build relationships with technical leaders, documentation teams and executive stakeholders. - Develop account strategies and compelling business cases around customer needs. - Partner with marketing, product and customer success to win customers and deliver a strong handoff. - Build a healthy pipeline and maintain clear, reliable forecasts. What you bring - A track record of closing complex enterprise SaaS deals. - Confidence selling to technical buyers and navigating multiple stakeholders. - Strong discovery, outbound prospecting and negotiation skills. - Curiosity about developer tools, documentation and how AI is changing software discovery and adoption. - An ownership mindset: you’re comfortable building a playbook, not just following one. - Fluency with AI tools (Claude, GPT, Grok etc), using them to work efficiently, reduce busywork and focus on the tasks that matter most. Interview process 1. People team intro - 30 minutes: Share your background, learn about GitBook and explore mutual fit. 2. Head of Sales conversation - 30 minutes: Discuss your experience, approach to enterprise sales and ambitions for the role. 3. Peer Account Executive conversation - 1 hour: Dive into how you sell, collaborate and build pipeline, and learn about the team’s day-to-day work. 4. Case study presentation - 1 hour: Present your approach to an enterprise sales scenario to the Head of Sales and Account Executives. 5. CEO culture fit interview - 30 minutes: Explore our values, company vision and how you’d contribute to GitBook’s next chapter. 👥 Join GitBook at a pivotal time as we evolve our product and team. This is a chance to shape our product, craft, and culture https://www.gitbook.com/about#open-roles.
