ABOUT THE ROLE We're looking for a bright, analytical problem-solver early in their career to join our Product Development team at incident.io. This is a junior, hands-on role for someone who enjoys getting stuck into complex problems, bringing structure to ambiguity and making things happen. You'll work closely with our Product Managers, helping unblock important work and taking ownership of projects that don't always have an obvious solution. The work is varied. You might be figuring out how to improve the flow of customer feedback between our go-to-market and Product teams, helping coordinate the legal and financial work needed to expand into new markets, or working through challenges with our telecom operations. Some projects will involve research, analysis and figuring out the best approach. Others will be much more execution-focused, where the challenge is coordinating different teams, navigating blockers and making sure things actually get done. You'll need to be comfortable doing both. We're particularly interested in people who've spent the first couple of years of their career in management consulting or a similarly analytical, project-based environment. You'll have developed a strong foundation in structured problem-solving and be looking for an opportunity to apply those skills within a growing technology company. You don't need previous experience in Product Management or Product Operations. In fact, this isn't a traditional role in either of those areas. We're much more interested in how you think, how quickly you learn and your ability to take a problem and run with it.
