System Tester – Pro Robotic R&D – Husqvarna Group Want to help shape the future of professional robotic lawn care? Join our Pro Robotic R&D team and work hands-on with robotic mowers used in demanding professional environments. Our team develops and maintains robotic mower solutions for customers such as football clubs, parks, commercial properties and golf courses. Reliability, efficiency and consistent performance are essential, and that is where our System Test team comes in. As a System Tester, you will be part of the System and Test team, verifying system behaviour, functionality and overall product quality. Role Description This is a hands-on role where you will test our robotic products in real field environments, simulated and lab environments, as well as through automated test pipelines. You will work closely with system engineers, software and product development teams, and other test functions to understand how the complete system performs. Your responsibilities will include: Verifying new functionality and bug fixes in field and lab environments Performing regression and acceptance testing Identifying, analysing and documenting deviations from requirements Tracking test progress and ensuring coverage against requirements Contributing to improved test methods, workflows and ways of working The exact focus of the role can be adapted based on your previous experience, technical strengths and areas where you want to continue developing. You will travel approximately four times per year, with each trip lasting around two weeks. Your profile You are curious, structured and quality-minded, with a genuine interest in understanding how technology works in practice. You enjoy combining hands-on testing with analysis and problem-solving. Furthermore, take ownership of your work, know how to prioritise and enjoy collaborating across different engineering disciplines in a dynamic R&D environment. We believe you have: At least 2 years of experience testing embedded products A B.Sc. or M.Sc. in Software Development, Embedded Systems, Mechatronics, Electrical Engineering or a related field Professional proficiency in English, both spoken and written, Swedish is a plus A Swedish B driving licence The possibility to travel It’s a plus if you also have experience in: System-level test automation Data analytics Linux and/or ROS Electrical load testing Jira or Azure DevOps ISTQB or a similar software testing certification Location The role is based on-site in Huskvarna, combined with travel related to testing at field locations. You will travel approximately four times per year, with each trip lasting around two weeks. We believe the best ideas happen when we are connected. We spend most of our time together in the office, creating space for collaboration, creativity, and fast decision-making. This is consistent across all our global offices and helps us maintain a strong culture, support learning and development, and ensure everyone has access to the people and resources they need to thrive.
