ROLE OVERVIEW Legora is looking for an Editorial Specialist to join our Communications team in London or Stockholm. This role will sit within the Editorial team and help shape how Legora shows up across social, editorial, and owned content channels. You’ll be responsible for producing sharp, high-quality content that brings Legora’s story to life — from day-to-day social media production to longer-form editorial pieces, customer stories, campaign assets, and thought leadership. The ideal candidate is a strong writer, fast editor, and detail-oriented operator who understands how to turn ideas, news, and company moments into clear, compelling content for a professional audience.
