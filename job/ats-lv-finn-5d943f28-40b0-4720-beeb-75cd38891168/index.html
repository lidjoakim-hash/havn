Your Role As a Senior Controller, you take a key role in steering FINN’s financial performance. You deep-dive into P&L, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow performance, explain variances against plan and identify the underlying drivers, with a particular focus on Gross Margin and unit economics. You translate data-driven insights into clear, actionable recommendations that help leadership steer the business. In addition, you contribute to our Group Management Reporting under HGB & IFRS and drive process and system improvements to scale with FINN’s growth. Your Mission • Contribute to Management Reporting: Translate monthly and year-end closing accounts (HGB & IFRS) into an insightful Group Management Reporting for P&L, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow Statement, targeting internal leadership, board, shareholders, and banks. Provide clear commentary on business performance and financial variance analysis against the plan, while continuously helping to develop the structure and content of the Management Reporting. • Analyze Performance & Business Drivers: Deep-dive into financial and operational data to analyze P&L, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow performance, and identify deviations against plan, the drivers of cost, revenue and cash flow, and their underlying root causes. • Steer Gross Margin & Unit Economics: Own the analysis of Gross Margin P&L line items and unit economics, providing transparency on planned and actual profitability per subscription. Partner with leadership and business owners to evaluate financial trade-offs based on data-driven insights, for example in demand and supply steering, and to implement and track initiatives that improve profitability. • Improve Processes & Systems: Drive company-wide process improvements, for example strengthening and aligning the monthly provisions process, and system improvements, such as setting up analyses in Lucanet or SAP-based cost center reporting, to improve the accuracy and transparency of our monthly financials. Your Profile Must have: • Proven Track Record & Education: Deep, proven track record in Controlling, FP&A, or Financial Analysis with + 4 years of professional experience —ideally gained in a fast-paced scale-up, tech company, top-tier consulting firm, or Big 4—backed by a strong university degree in Finance, Controlling, Business Administration, or a related field. • Financial & Analytical Expertise: Strong analytical acumen and high attention to detail, combined with solid working knowledge of accounting principles and financial statements. • Spreadsheet Mastery: Advanced proficiency in Excel or Google Sheets to model, structure, and steer complex financial data. • Mindset & Communication: Proactive, solution-oriented approach to complex financial topics, paired with fluent English skills (C1/C2 level) to convey financial insights clearly to stakeholders. Nice to have: • Enterprise Financial Systems: Hands-on experience working with Lucanet and/or SAP S/4HANA. • Additional Language Skills: Good working knowledge of German to support cross-functional collaboration.
