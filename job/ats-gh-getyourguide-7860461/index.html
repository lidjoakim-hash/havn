Millions look to us for unique activities they can trust, and it’s all powered by our commitment to make every single journey extraordinary - including yours. Ready to unlock your potential with a community of fellow explorers? Find your next role at our Berlin HQ or one of our local offices around the globe, from New York to Bangkok. Head to getyourguide.careers to take the first step. Team mission We’re growing the UX team and looking for several Senior Product Designers to join us in Berlin. You’ll work in one of two product areas, both central to how travelers find and choose what to do: Discovery Group: This team helps travelers find the right experience on GetYourGuide. It owns search, the homepage and core experiences like activity cards, maps and filters that millions of travelers use every day. Comparison Group: Owns the activity detail page (ADP) — the moment a traveler decides whether to book. The team’s mission is to make that decision easy and confident, through curated experience content, smarter comparison tools and reviews.
