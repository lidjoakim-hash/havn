Stories have the power to make the ordinary extraordinary. They help us think bigger, see beyond ourselves, and deepen our connection with others. Mofibo is part of Storytel Group, one of the world’s leading audiobook and e-book streaming platforms. Storytel brings unlimited listening to millions of users across 25 markets. Driven by our purpose “Leading the future of storytelling, we move the world through stories”, we inspire and entertain people around the world by blending innovation with tradition. We bring stories to life across various formats for everyone to discover. Anytime. Anywhere. Ready for your next chapter? We’re looking for a Partnerships Manager to join the team in Copenhagen! About the role As a Partnerships Manager, you play an important role in the success story of how Mofibo grows through intake & distribution partnerships. You’ll work closely with the Director of Global Partnerships and the Head of Partnerships Manager to ensure progress, address challenges, and follow up on performance metrics for a wide range of partnerships. Plenty of other collaborations will take place daily, both with Storytellers and external collaborators. Your main responsibilities will include: • Owning the Danish partnerships pipeline, across CPA-based intake and strategic distribution partners, end-to-end from prospecting and negotiation to launch and performance management. • Developing commercial business cases by assessing addressable audiences, subscriber potential, acquisition economics, revenue opportunity and ROI. • Leading cross-functional execution of distribution partnerships, working closely with Product & Tech, Marketing, Legal, Finance, Analytics and other relevant teams to ensure successful integrations and launches. • Building and maintaining strong relationships with partners, establishing effective governance and identifying opportunities to scale and expand existing partnerships. • Analyzing partner performance using tools such as Looker and MS Excel, tracking KPIs and taking action to improve acquisition, conversion, revenue and overall partnership performance. • Monitoring Danish market dynamics, industry trends and competitor activity to identify new partnership opportunities and inform commercial strategy. • Identifying, negotiating and closing partnerships across telcos, digital platforms, loyalty programs, financial services and OEMs, with commercial terms aligned to Storytel's growth and profitability goals. About the team The Partnerships team is a global team with local execution excellence, with the main mission to accelerate Storytel’s growth through high-impact distribution and intake partnerships. Your colleagues are based in various locations across the globe in local offices and at Storytel’s headquarters in Stockholm. This provides strong exposure to global strategy, senior stakeholders, and cross-market initiatives. Locally, your closest colleagues in Copenhagen are the Country Manager, the Marketing Manager and the CRM Manager - just to name a few. About you To thrive in this role, we believe that you have several years of experience working with profitable sales or partnerships in Denmark. Thanks to this, you bring best-in-class know-how, industry trends and successful sales tactics.
