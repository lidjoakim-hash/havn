We are seeking a Mechanical Engineer!

We are seeking an experienced Mechanical Engineer for an assignment with one of our clients within the subsea and energy industry.   The position combines mechanical design with technical project responsibility. You will be responsible for engineering activities in projects involving standard and customized products, from design and planning through fabrication, assembly and testing.   The assignment is based in Bergen, with office-based work five days per week. Some travel to the client’s production and testing facility should be expected.   Responsibilities: • Lead and coordinate engineering activities in projects involving standard and customized products. • Design metering systems and related mechanical equipment. • Take responsibility for the engineering scope throughout project execution. • Develop and maintain engineering plans and ensure progress against project schedules. • Coordinate engineering activities and resources in cooperation with the Project Manager. • Develop 3D CAD models of components and complete systems. • Prepare fabrication documentation, engineering drawings and procedures. • Perform internal and external design reviews. • Review and approve engineering drawings and technical documentation. • Follow up components, sub-suppliers, fabrication and production activities as required. • Support assembly and testing activities at the production facility. • Participate in equipment testing when required. • Support the Project Manager with engineering expertise and technical input. • Participate in client meetings where technical expertise is required. • Ensure designs comply with relevant industry standards, safety requirements, regulations and client specifications. • Identify opportunities for technical improvements and cost-efficient solutions. • Coordinate independently with relevant stakeholders, including suppliers, clients, project management and engineering disciplines. • Ensure high standards of HSE, quality and engineering practice throughout project execution.   Qualifications: • BSc or MSc within Mechanical Engineering, Mechanical Design or a related discipline. • Minimum 5 years of relevant experience within mechanical design and project execution. • Experience with Creo or an equivalent 3D CAD system is essential. • Experience with Teamcenter is highly preferred. • Experience from subsea, oil & gas, offshore or other technically complex industries is preferred. • Experience with metering and measurement systems is considered an advantage. • Experience working with mechanical systems, equipment integration, fabrication and testing. • Experience coordinating engineering activities within projects. • Good understanding of engineering documentation and manufacturing drawings. • Good written and spoken English.   Personal skills: • Proactive and self-driven, with the ability to take ownership of engineering activities from planning through delivery. • Structured and solution-oriented, with a strong focus on quality and deadlines. • Confident in making technical decisions based on engineering data, experience and risk assessment. • Able to balance technical requirements, cost and schedule considerations. • Strong communication skills and comfortable working with clients, suppliers and multidisciplinary project teams. • Collaborative and able to coordinate effectively across engineering, project management, production and testing. • Practical and hands-on in your approach, with an interest in following the product from engineering through assembly and testing. • Comfortable working independently and taking responsibility for progress within your area.
