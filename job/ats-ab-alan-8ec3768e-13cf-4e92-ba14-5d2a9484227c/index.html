MISSION Alan is scaling internationally (900+ employees, 100+ healthcare professionals, 5+ countries) and needs IT Operations Specialists to own device security, SaaS platforms, identity & access, and the daily IT experience of every Alaner, while contributing to build projects that remove operational toil. IT at Alan is more than just a helpdesk. Operations and support ('Run') take roughly 60% of your time initially, while the rest goes toward owning project-based initiatives ('Build') tailored to your experience level. As you ramp up alongside senior engineers, your focus will naturally shift toward more build-focused work with wider impact. The scope below is what the team owns, not a checklist for one person. We staff people according to their strengths and interests, and it evolves over time. SCOPE 🛟 SUPPORT & ONCALL - Own L1/L2 oncall for 900+ Alaners across 5+ countries, with a clear escalation path to engineers - Triage, communicate to users during incidents, and escalate appropriately - Document recurring issues and reduce ticket volume through self-service 💻 DEVICE LIFECYCLE & ENDPOINT COMPLIANCE - Own the full device lifecycle: procurement, zero-touch enrollment (Apple Business Manager, Kandji), repair, retrieval - Maintain role-based Kandji blueprints and least-privilege configurations - Contribute to device compliance across all countries (MDM, EDR, FileVault) and to audit evidence - Own the physical side: laptop locker, hardware inventory, office equipment - Take over day-to-day device operations as we bring them back in-house from our outsourced provider 🔑 IDENTITY, ACCESS & SAAS PLATFORMS - Own onboarding and offboarding end-to-end, across both automated and manually-managed tools - Administer Google Workspace, SSO/SAML, licences and access reviews - Manage day-to-day vendor relationships and procurement on your scope ⚙️ BUILD - Own a build project end-to-end, with a more experienced engineer to pair with and learn from - Examples from the current roadmap: rebuilding the onboarding intake, the meeting room audiovisual equipment and room-booking revamp, the IT playbook for new offices, ISO 27001 / ACPR evidence collection - Ship automation and self-service tooling that measurably reduces IT toil MILESTONES First 2 months - Join IT oncall rotation (helpdesk) with buddy/shadowing support - Discover Alan's processes, stack (Kandji, Apple Business Manager), SaaS ecosystem, and identity tools (Google Workspace, SSO, Alan Home) - Handle most typical IT operations issues autonomously (equipment, SaaS access, account provisioning) First 6 months - Handle IT oncall shifts autonomously, solving most problems that do not require engineer input - Own onboarding/offboarding workflows end-to-end - Have wide knowledge of Alan
