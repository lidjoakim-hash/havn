🚀 We’re Hiring: EE System Design Engineer – Thermal Management | Automotive & Embedded Systems Company: Hykmann Global Business Solutions AB Role: EE System Design Engineer – Thermal Management Specialisation: Electrical/Electronic Architecture | Embedded Systems | AUTOSAR | Functional Safety | Cybersecurity Location: Gothenburg, Sweden Position Type: Temporary Number of Openings: 1 About the Role Hykmann Global Business Solutions AB is looking for an experienced EE System Design Engineer to contribute to the development of advanced thermal management solutions for autonomous driving systems. In this role, you will work with vehicle electronics, electrical architecture and embedded software development. You will drive early-stage system design activities, investigate alternative electrical and electronic concepts, and help define optimal solutions across hardware, software, ECUs and communication networks. You will collaborate with engineering specialists and development teams to ensure that system concepts meet technical, functional safety and cybersecurity expectations throughout the development process. Key Responsibilities Lead the exploration and evaluation of alternative electrical and electronic concepts during early project phases and product maturity activities. Perform system-level design of embedded systems, including the logical allocation of functions across hardware and software components. Define and evaluate suitable ECU configurations, communication buses and system architectures, including AUTOSAR-based solutions. Identify functional safety and cybersecurity risks and support the definition of appropriate mitigation concepts in collaboration with domain experts. Document evaluated alternatives and selected system concepts in technical reports, including the main concept documentation (TECR). Define diagnostic strategies for thermal management systems supporting autonomous driving applications. Support system and diagnostic trouble code (DTC) documentation using relevant engineering tools. Provide technical expertise and system-level guidance to development teams during solution implementation. Support vehicle-level integration of hardware and software components. Present technical concepts, design decisions and recommendations to engineering stakeholders and management. Required Technical Competence We are looking for a system design professional with strong knowledge of embedded systems and automotive electrical/electronic architecture. Relevant experience should include: Embedded Systems & System Design Extensive experience in embedded system and function design. Understanding of how vehicle functions and services are implemented across application software, software platforms, electronic control units and electrical components. Experience with logical system design and hardware/software allocation. Understanding of ECU selection and system architecture considerations. Software Platforms & Electronics Knowledge of AUTOSAR architecture and platforms. Experience with Embedded Linux or comparable embedded software platforms. Understanding of ECUs and their integration with sensors, actuators and other vehicle components. Vehicle Communication Technologies Automotive onboard communication technologies and protocols, including: CAN LIN Automotive Ethernet Functional Safety & Cybersecurity Understanding of functional safety challenges in automotive systems. Knowledge of automotive cybersecurity principles and risk mitigation. Ability to identify system-level risks and contribute to safety and cybersecurity concepts. Vehicle Integration & Diagnostics Experience with vehicle-level integration of hardware and software components. Understanding of automotive diagnostic strategies and DTC documentation. Ability to support system documentation and technical design decisions.
