When travelers are searching for a hotel, we want the obvious choice to be trivago! Our leading metasearch engine is super fast and constantly optimized - enabling millions of travelers to compare hotel prices from hundreds of booking sites and find great deals in just a few clicks. We use cutting-edge technology, real-time auction, and machine learning techniques with petabytes of data to create an experience - time and money saved! In the lively city of Düsseldorf, we seize opportunities to learn everyday, innovate, and make an enduring mark on the travel industry. At trivago you will find those who aren’t afraid of change but rather embrace it, turning every challenge into a pathway for growth. Join trivago, work with a great team, and grow with us! Join us in making a difference Every day, travelers rely on trivago’s website to find their perfect stay at the best price, and you’ll make sure it works seamlessly for them. As part of our team of 10 Website Frontend QA Engineers, you’ll partner with Software Engineers and Product teams to ensure every feature, bug fix and technical change ships in top shape. We’re looking for someone with a strong quality mindset: motivated, proactive and detail-oriented. You’ll own quality for large initiatives with real autonomy, shape our test automation, and dig into metrics, logs and results to catch issues before our users do. We work AI-first, so you’ll use AI to rethink how we test, from smarter test generation to faster root-cause analysis. At trivago, ambition and support go hand in hand: we hold high standards and invest in each other’s growth. Ready to own quality for millions of travelers? Apply now! Get an inside look at trivago's engineering culture: How you’ll make an impact: • Verify changes to our core web application within an agile, multidisciplinary team, from user-facing frontend features to the backend changes that power them. • Find defects through manual, exploratory, automated and regression testing, and manage defect prioritisation, escalation and the backlog. • Build and maintain automated test cases, suites and frameworks with fellow QA and Test Automation Engineers to keep our website reliable. • Analyse A/B test data, product metrics, error logs and test results with business intelligence tools, and configure tools and alerts that monitor live quality to uncover root causes early. • Keep stakeholders informed on the progress of upcoming changes, and coordinate releases and rollbacks with confidence. • Make AI a core part of how you test, automate and analyse, mentor peers, shape our QA practices and drive improvements across the whole team. What you'll need to thrive: • Proven hands-on QA experience on web applications: you combine manual and exploratory testing with data analysis, and your eye for detail catches even the smallest errors or unintended changes. • Practical test automation experience with Selenium and Java (or similar), plus a solid understanding of web-based technologies such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Git and APIs. • Strong communication and coordination skills: you confidently own complex initiatives end to end, align diverse stakeholders and collaborate smoothly in large, dynamic teams. • A passion for learning and openness to change: you enjoy new challenges, welcome feedback and adapt quickly in our highly agile environment. • A performance mindset: you set ambitious goals and have the determination and resilience to reach them. • An open and curious mindset toward AI tools and automation: you actively explore how AI can improve testing and quality work, and you’re excited to grow alongside evolving technologies. Not ticking every box? We still want to hear from you. Apply, tell us what motivates you, and you could be a great match—now or in the future. Your application does not need to include a photo.
