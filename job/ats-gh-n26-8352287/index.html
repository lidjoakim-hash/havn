About the Opportunity We are seeking a Senior Site Reliability Engineer to join the Platform Engineering Segment in the Mesh Team. The mission of Platform Engineering is to provide trusted, performant, self-service platforms to empower product teams to build the bank the world loves to use. As one of the first banks completely hosted in the cloud - our security, resilience and productivity standards require not only the use of a modern technology stack, but the building of teams in line with our principles and in the service of our product teams, the company and our customers. In this role, you will: Be responsible for designing, implementing and maintaining our service mesh in order to ensure the reliability, performance, and security of our services. Working in this area involves a deep understanding of microservices, service mesh architecture, networking protocols and cloud computing, as well as the ability to diagnose and resolve complex technical issues. More specifically, you will: • Design, develop and implement systems, services and solutions, with the aim of improving the security, reliability, scalability and availability of systems in the following domains: • Service Mesh • Internal Networking • Partner Connectivity • Secrets Management • Service Discovery • Take ownership of our service mesh and it's underlying network stack and ensure its optimal performance and reliability • Ensure N26 availability by creating monitors and alerts, diagnose, and resolve issues related to the service mesh • Collaborate with other teams and support them in bringing critical new features to N26 • Stay up-to-date with industry trends and new technology developments in service mesh • Identify and automate repetitive tasks • Monitor the
