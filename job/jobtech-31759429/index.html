Brew & Packaging Operator

Omnipollo AB · Upplands-Bro, Sweden

💰 ca. 330 000–390 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 18.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Are you passionate about beer, quality and production processes? Do you want to be a key player in brewing and packaging our award-winning beers? Then you might be our next Brew & Packaging Operator! Omnipollo AB is a Swedish brewery founded in 2010 by brewer Henok Fentie and designer Karl Grandin. Over the years, the brewery has won numerous awards for its beer and exports its beer to over 40 countries. Omnipollo AB has a number of restaurants and bars including Omnipollos hatt, Omnipollos Flora, and Omnipollos kyrka in Stockholm as well as Omnipollos Tokyo in Japan. About the Role As a Brew & Packaging Operator, you will play a central role in our production facility in Kungsängen and in our brewery in Sundbyberg. Your main responsibility is to ensure that our beers are brewed and packaged with the highest precision and quality, whether in cans, kegs, or bottles. You will work across the entire process – from brewing, cellar work, dry hopping, transferring beer, and cleaning to packaging and logging. Together with your colleagues, you will take responsibility for creating a good working environment and developing our operations. The work is physical and requires precision for us to maintain our high routines and industry standards. Your Responsibilities: Brewhouse & Cellar work Milling Mashing Lautering Boiling Chilling Fermentation checks Dry hopping Tank transfers Packaging work Packaging beer in cans, kegs, and bottles Centrifugation and pasteurization Logging and documentation of work Performing cleaning and CIP (Clean in Place). Preparation of incoming and outgoing shipments of raw materials and finished products Contributing to a good working environment and improving our working methods Who Are You? We are looking for someone who has a strong interest in beer and production processes. You are a problem solver who does not hesitate to take on challenges and find creative solutions. You have a desire to learn and work well in a team as well as independently. To succeed in this role, you also need to be thorough and responsible, as well as physically fit and able to work efficiently in a dynamic environment. Qualifications: Brewery technician education or equivalent At least two years of experience working in food/beverage production, preferably in a brewery and/or packaging facility Valid B driver's license, forklift license is a plus Basic computer skills Technical skills Fluent in English (both spoken and written); proficiency in Swedish is a strong plus About the Employment The position is full-time with an initial six-month probationary period. You will work closely with the team in our packaging facility in Kungsängen, as well as our brewery in Sundbyberg. We believe in warmth, professionalism, and the idea that everyone should feel at home with us. Our reach is global, but our heart is rooted locally. It is our community and collaboration that make us so successful. Want to join us on our journey? Send your application and any questions to jobs@omnipollo.com. The application deadline is December 18 , 2026. Interviews are conducted on an ongoing basis, so the position may be filled before the closing date. Welcome with your application - and thank you very much for your interest! We try to respond to everyone who applies to us, but sometimes we must prioritize operational activities and cannot get to all administrative tasks. So if you haven't heard from us within two weeks, we have moved forward with other candidates.
