In 2022 we joined forces with DoorDash and together we keep on dreaming big and expanding across the globe. Working at Wolt isn’t always easy, but it’s definitely exciting. Role background Our Merchant Services and Compliance Operations teams ensure Wolt meets regulatory requirements across all markets. As part of this, we’re strengthening our KYC/KYB processes to support scalable and compliant operations. As a Merchant Compliance Administrator, you’ll review merchant information and make sure everything is accurate, complete, and compliant. This role is Greek-speaking, based in Budapest, Hungary, and works closely with our in-market teams. We offer flexibility - the position is available full time, part-time (20 or 30 hours per week) and is also suitable for students, with a minimum commitment of 20 working hours per week. What you’ll be doing • Review and validate merchant-submitted KYC/KYB documents (e.g. ID, business details) • Check for accuracy and completeness based on internal guidelines • Flag missing or incorrect information for follow-up • Call merchants directly in Greek to clarify details, resolve discrepancies, or request missing documentation • Use internal tools to manage and track tasks • Support quality checks and maintain high data accuracy • Collaborate with Operations, Compliance, and Product teams Our humble expectations • 1+ year of experience in operations, admin, compliance, data validation, or similar • Strong attention to detail and ability to work with structured data • Comfortable handling sensitive information • Able to follow processes and work accurately at scale • Experience with tools such as Google Workspace or similar • Good organizational and time management skills • Clear communication and collaboration skills • Fluent in English and Greek (additional languages are a plus) • You have the right to work in Hungary • Nice to have: basic understanding of legal or business documents What we offer • 🌍 Independent work in a dynamic, international team • 💳 SZÉP-card* • 🏥 Private health insurance* • 🏋️ All You Can Move card (self-financed)* • 💙 Employee Assistance Programme - therapy and wellbeing* • % Discount on your Wolt orders* • 👶 Extended parental leave for fathers: 16 weeks total* • 💻 Modern work equipment • 🫶 Volunteer day • 🍿 Snacks & drinks in the office 🎓 Please note: Student contracts work a bit differently, benefits marked with * are available only to non-student employees If you are excited about working in a high-growth environment, taking ownership, and being part of an extremely ambitious team, then click below to apply and get the conversation going!
