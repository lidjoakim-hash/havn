Global Operations is responsible for the health, governance and strategic value of Monzo’s partner estate. As a Partner Estate & Relationship Manager, you’ll be the main ongoing relationship owner for the partners and suppliers assigned to you. You’ll work within the strategy, goals and standards set by the Senior Partner Estate & Relationship Manager, using strong judgement and a high level of independence to drive issues, opportunities and commitments through to resolution. You’ll apply Monzo’s Partner Management Framework, lead effective partner governance and make sure our relationships, contracts and commercial arrangements support the outcomes Monzo, Operations and our customers need. You’ll build strong working relationships with partner client management teams and Operations leaders globally. Operations will own detailed day to day service performance. You’ll stay closely connected to understand what is working, where partners are struggling and where the relationship, contract, commercial arrangements or partner environment need to change. This is an individual contributor role with no direct reports.
