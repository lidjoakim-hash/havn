Production Operator - PCBA Manufacturing

HMS stands for Hardware Meets Software and we work to make the world more connected, productive, and sustainable. Our industrial communication products enable millions of machines, such as robots, drives, and air-conditioning systems, to connect with software and information systems – a necessity for meeting future demands for energy efficiency and sustainability. Our production site in Halmstad is one of our primary manufacturing hubs, delivering high-quality products to customers worldwide. We are now looking for a Production Operator to join our production team in PBCA manufacturing and help us manufacture electronic products with the right quality, quantity, and delivery performance. About the Role  As a Production Operator in our machine hall, you will be part of our PCBA manufacturing process. Depending on your training and assignment, you will work with areas such as material preparation, machine setup and operation, inspection, soldering, and basic troubleshooting. Experience from electronics manufacturing is an advantage, but not a requirement. We will provide the training needed for your role. Key Responsibilities: Produce PCBA according to the production plan, approved documentation, machine programs, and work instructions Operate and monitor equipment in our SMT and/or THM production Prepare materials, machines, and programs for production and product changeovers Monitor production quality through processes such as SPI, AOI, reflow, soldering, and visual inspection Identify deviations and perform basic troubleshooting within your training and authorization Follow procedures for safety, ESD, traceability, quality, and material handling Register production information and contribute to 5S and continuous improvement according to our Operational excellence program. About You  We are looking for a responsible and quality-conscious team member who enjoys practical, technical work in a production environment. You work carefully and methodically, follow instructions, and take action when you identify a risk or deviation.  You bring:  Previous experience from working within a manufacturing environment Technical understanding and good computer skills The ability to work accurately according to instructions and quality requirements A strong sense of responsibility and good collaboration skills The ability to work both independently and as part of a team Fluent in Swedish to follow work instructions and safety information Experience from electronics manufacturing, PCBA, SMT/THM, soldering, or inspection is an advantage Experience from continuous improvement or 5S is an advantage Why Join Us?  Work hands-on with advanced electronics manufacturing at a key production site  Be part of a collaborative team focused on safety, quality, and reliable delivery  Develop your competence across several areas of PCBA production  Contribute ideas and participate in continuous improvement through PEP  Help manufacture products that connect machines and systems around the world    About us HMS Networks make the World more connected, productive and sustainable. Our industrial communication products enable millions of machines such as robots, drives and air-conditioners to get connected to software and systems - a necessity to meet the future demands for energy efficiency and sustainability. We are an innovative growth company based in Halmstad, Sweden with 1100 employees in more than 20 countries. HMS is an equal opportunity employer. We celebrate diversity and are committed to creating an environment where everyone feels included, respected, and empowered. Want to help us make the world more connected and sustainable?
