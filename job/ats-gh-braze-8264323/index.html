WHAT YOU’LL DO As a part of the Braze People team, the People Solutions Generalist will play a pivotal role in supporting the overall employee experience and success of the People team by driving change across the department to meet business goals. This role will oversee our EMEA region and provide valuable support across various People team functions that impact our employees globally. We’re looking for someone excited about having a hand in redefining how the People team operates and how we support our employees. This role will report to the Senior Director, People - EMEA and work closely with different divisions across the People team. This is a unique opportunity to collaborate cross-functionally and build general knowledge and experience in People Operations. RESPONSIBILITIES As the Generalist, you will: • Support the implementation of company-wide initiatives, such as the engagement survey and performance reviews, and collaborate with stakeholders in planning, execution, and communication. • Assist the development of internal processes and programs and revamp current employee programs owned by People Solutions to ensure continued effectiveness. • Oversee and manage complex global mobility and immigration cases. Act as a consultant for the business and internal People teams, handling intricate cases and ensuring every employee's relocation or visa experience is smooth and legally compliant. • Work on the introduction of new policies for the region, as well as reviews of existing policies. • Take the lead on key workstreams such as the EU Pay Transparency Directive, in partnership with Legal and the Senior Director, People - EMEA. • Support Talent Development team with employee-facing training when needed. • Support the company’s expansion plans by collaborating with teammates to work with our Employer of Record. • Continuously identify opportunities to improve our operations and service delivery within the Shared Services model to deliver efficient and exceptional support to employees and the business. • Collaborate with specialized functional People teams, People Business Partners, and other stakeholders to drive People team initiatives and ad hoc projects to support the team toward business goals. • Support employment-related inquiries from candidates, employees, and managers on complex and/or sensitive matters through the ticketing system and/or meetings. These include, but are not limited to, benefit questions, career development, leave of absence, policies, and relocation requests.
