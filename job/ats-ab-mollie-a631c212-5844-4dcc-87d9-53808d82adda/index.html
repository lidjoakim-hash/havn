Build with us Since 2004, we've had one clear goal: to make payments and money management effortless for every business in Europe. Today, more than 250,000 companies use Mollie to get paid, manage money and grow – with products designed to be simple, scalable, and dependable. With 950+ Mollies across 14+ locations, we care deeply about autonomy and craft. So we work in small teams, with real ownership, and we trust you to make the right decisions. We're building for the long term, so we provide the tools you need, processes you can rely on, and a balanced work environment to help you do work you're proud of. Sound like your kind of place? Let's build together. Your impact You'll make sure our customers get the most value from our products. As a trusted advisor, you'll build strong, lasting relationships with some of our largest Belgian customers. You'll anticipate their needs, offer insights and deliver tailored solutions that keep them with us. You'll also spot chances to grow accounts by upselling relevant payment methods and expanding product adoption. Regular business reviews, check ins and proactive support will be central to your daily work. Belgium is leading with in person payments (IPP) customers, which makes this a key market with a big impact on the payments industry. Our customers are highly engaged with the Customer Success team. This role suits someone who wants to be proactive and enjoys working closely with customers and peers. You'll be the key link between the customer and our success, working with decision makers and external partners and gaining experience managing complex relationships and business reviews. You'll work closely with the CSM team and our internal Product teams to resolve issues, share feedback and keep improving the customer journey. You'll use dashboards and CRM tools to guide your decisions, turn challenges into opportunities and help build loyal customers who actively promote Mollie. Small upsells, strong retention and good relationships add up to real growth for Mollie. You'll join two other CSMs in Belgium, with the team lead based in Amsterdam. Belgium is part of an international team of 8 across FR, IT, UK, SE and PL. From here, you can grow into Customer Success Manager. What you'll ship - Strong relationships across a portfolio of around 60 to 80 customers. You'll build trust with stakeholders through regular check ins and business reviews, and act as their main point of contact. - Proactive customer management, including monthly face to face meetings and events. - Renewals managed, churn reduced and upsell opportunities identified. - Customer needs advocated for internally, and escalations handled as they come up. - Customer insights collected to support product enhancements and make sure our offerings align with client needs. - Health scores, CSAT/NPS and account KPIs tracked, with actionable insights shared with leadership. What you'll bring - Experience building and maintaining customer relationships, handling customer questions, identifying needs and ensuring a positive customer experience. - Experience managing customer account activities, including calls, callbacks, email communication and follow up meetings. - Experience with negotiation, such as contract renewals, as well as upselling and cross-selling to expand customer value. - A data driven mindset, with working knowledge of CRM systems and proficiency in Excel. - Ideally native Flemish, though native Dutch speakers will also be considered, and fluent English, both written and spoken. Grow your way At Mollie, growth is personal. We believe everyone should have the chance to develop their skills, explore new challenges and shape their career on their own terms. You'll get regular feedback and performance reviews to support your development, with fair and transparent salary reviews along the way.
