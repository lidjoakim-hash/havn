Are you passionate about crisis management, emergency preparedness and building organizational resilience in a global organization?

Kongsberg Gruppen Kongsberg Maritime - Business Support · Kongsberg, Norway

We are looking for an experienced and motivated Global Security Manager – Crisis Management to strengthen our global security and resilience capability. In this role, your main responsibility will be crisis management and business continuity, you will work close with our team, and also be involved in a variety of security areas such as personnel security, physical security, information security and travel security.  You will have a key responsibility for further developing our global approach to crisis management and emergency preparedness. You will work closely with management, security professionals, business units and functions across the organization to ensure that we are prepared to respond effectively when serious incidents occur. The position combines strategic development, governance and hands-on operational crisis management, and requires someone who is comfortable working both proactively and in situations where time, information and predictability may be limited. Your key responsibilities: • Develop, maintain and continuously improve the company’s global Crisis Management framework, including governance, processes, plans and supporting tools. • Support and further develop the organization’s Emergency Response and Crisis Management structure globally. • Support the establishment and development of local and regional emergency response capabilities, crisis management exercises and training for management teams and relevant functions. • Contribute to the broader development of Business Continuity Management and organizational resilience. • Together with the Security team you will monitor relevant global developments and emerging risks that may affect our people and operations. • Participate in operational crisis response when required, as a important coordinating role in the global crisis management team. • Collaborate with the Travel Security Board team. We are continuously reviewing applications, apply today! At Kongsberg Maritime, we believe that diversity fuels innovation. We encourage you to apply, even if you do not meet every requirement. Your unique perspective is valuable to us. Your skills, attitude and perspective could be exactly what we are looking for!   What we are looking for: We believe you have solid experience within crisis management, emergency preparedness, security, resilience or related disciplines, combined with the ability to translate frameworks and requirements into practical solutions. Relevant experience may come from international industry, corporate security, emergency preparedness, defence, police, public authorities or consulting. We are particularly interested in candidates who have: • Experience with crisis management and emergency response in a complex organization. • Experience developing crisis management plans, frameworks and response structures. • Experience planning and facilitating exercises, training and scenario-based crisis management activities. • Experience supporting management during serious incidents or crises. • Strong understanding of crisis leadership, decision-making and coordination under pressure. • Ability to work across organizational, geographical and cultural boundaries. • Strong stakeholder management and communication skills. • Excellent written and spoken English. Experience from an international organization with global operations will be considered an advantage. Knowledge of recognized standards and good practices within crisis management and business continuity, including ISO 22301 and ISO 27001, will also be beneficial.  This employment may require a pre-employment check procedure in accordance with company security standards, role or site requirements. Please note that this might affect the offer process or employment. This employment requires security clearance by the Norwegian National Security Authority. The successful candidate may start work with limited accesses whilst the clearance is being processed. Please note that this might affect the offer process or employment. Personal qualities To succeed in this role, you are structured and analytical, while also being pragmatic and operational when the situation requires it. You: • remain calm and focused in demanding situations; • are comfortable making progress when information is incomplete; • communicate clearly with both management and operational teams; • build trust and strong professional relationships; • take initiative and drive improvement; • are curious and continuously look for ways to strengthen preparedness; • understand that effective crisis management is ultimately about people, leadership, preparation and the ability to act. You should also be comfortable working in an international environment and travelling when required. Why join us? Serious incidents rarely follow the plan. - Good crisis management therefore depends not only on having procedures in place, but on having an organization that is prepared, trained and capable of making good decisions when it matters most. As Global Security Manager – Crisis Management, you will have the opportunity to influence how a global organization prepares for and responds to critical events, working across countries, functions and management levels. You will be part of a professional security environment where preparedness, learning and continuous improvement are important elements in protecting our people, operations and business.  Work location: Any of Kongsberg Maritimes locations in Norway, though Kongsberg, Horten or Lysaker is preferred.
