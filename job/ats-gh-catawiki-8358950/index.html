About the job *This is a 6-month paid internship We are looking for an intern to support Catawiki’s Communications team on a 6 month contract. As our Communications Intern, you will assist on Communications initiatives as well as on the general day-to-day running of the busy Catawiki press office. Reporting to our Communications Manager, this role calls for a versatile communicator who can confidently shift between researching timely topics, drafting compelling press materials—including pitches and press releases—and supporting proactive media outreach. You’ll play a key role across a broad range of projects, from company announcements and data-driven stories to object-focused communications, helping craft narratives that resonate with media and diverse audiences. Using data in storytelling, reporting on daily coverage and supporting the day-to-day management of the company's corporate social channels are also part of the role. What you’ll do • Managing the press monitoring routine, tracking the coverage generated on a daily, monthly and project based basis • Managing the press newsrooms, uploading all relevant content in a timely manner • Managing our corporate social media channel (LinkedIn) • Support the Comms team with writing and distributing news releases and media pitches, as well as drafting interview briefings and FAQs, helping to secure top tier media coverage for Catawiki • Support in the handling of media enquiries, including involving relevant team members and supporting on any information required • Support with research on various topics, ideation / brainstorming sessions and proactive communications campaigns across consumer and corporate • General support to the team on day-to-day logistics, such as meeting arrangements, general operations, meeting minutes, reporting, etc. • Support with the daily management of our PR agencies across 8 European markets • Assisting on reactive media issues management if needed, including the support on developing statements Who you are • In order to be considered for this internship, you need to be registered as a student in a Dutch university during the entire internship period. A tri-party agreement will be requested. • Studying communications, journalism or PR - previous comms work experience at a media outlet, PR agency or brand is a plus • Excellent writing skills (English) and strong verbal communication skills - essential • Knowledge of a wide range of new and traditional media and social media outlets – essential • Competent on Microsoft Office - Word, Excel and PowerPoint - G Suite - essential • Incredibly passionate about our mission and goals • Creativity, an innovator and an ability to think big • Hard working, hands-on and pressure resistant. Can-do attitude and willingness to roll up your sleeves, dive deep and get the job done in a fast changing environment • Bilingual in French, Italian, German, Dutch or Spanish is a plus Where you’ll be This role is based in Amsterdam, the Netherlands, with remote working 3 days a week.
