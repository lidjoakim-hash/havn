Transport is at the core of modern society. Imagine using your expertise to shape sustainable transport and infrastructure solutions for the future. If you seek to make a difference on a global scale, working with next-gen technologies and the sharpest collaborative teams, then we could be a perfect match. Design Engineer, Volvo Construction Equipment Would you like to help develop the construction equipment of the future? Together, we create world leading products in the premium segment, with a focus on quality, safety and care for the environment. We are looking for Design Engineers to join the Exterior department at Volvo Construction Equipment in Braås. This position is located on-site in Braås outside Växjö. About us Volvo CE in Braås, near Växjö, developes articulated haulers and wheel loaders and manufactures articulated haulers. Our development departments are constantly working on innovative solutions and designs for new products. All to ensure that we remain at the forefront of development and pushing limits, regarding new product and feature development, product improvements and aftermarket quality. You will join a global team of skilled professionals who work closely with colleagues in production, purchasing, aftermarket and also with our chosen suppliers. With production located at the same site, you will have the opportunity to follow your design solutions from development through to the finished product. About the role As a Design Engineer, you will work with the mechanical design of components and solutions in plastics, sheet metal and tubular structures for wheel loaders and articulated haulers. You will contribute to product development and improvement work in collaboration with colleagues and other functions, taking responsibility for the quality of the team’s deliveries.You will work closely with Product Design in Gothenburg to bring new design languages to our machines. This calls for a strong understanding of plastics, sheet metal and tubular structures, and how to translate design concepts into functional, manufacturable solutions. You will also take the lead on design-related topics and act as a point of contact for other departments. By driving collaboration, coordinating discussions and working across functions, you will help ensure that the team’s deliverables are completed successfully. Your work will focus on exterior components for wheel loaders and articulated haulers. Who are you? You enjoy combining technical expertise with an open and solution-oriented approach. You are positive, flexible and a team player with an interest in innovation and product development. You are keen to learn and develop, both individually and as part of a team, and are willing to take the lead when needed. You are comfortable taking initiative and stepping forward when the situation calls for it. You communicate clearly and enjoy working across departmental boundaries, while taking responsibility for the team’s shared deliverables. You have: B.Sc. or M.Sc. degree in Mechanical Engineering or an equivalent qualification Experience in mechanical design Strong communication skills in Swedish and English, both spoken and written Experience with Creo or another 3D modelling tool Experience or knowledge in any of the following areas is an advantage: Construction equipment or heavy vehicles Design of sheet metal, plastics or tubular components FEM calculations Lean and Agile ways of working Ready for the next move? As part of the recruitment process, a background check as well as alcohol and drug testing may be conducted. You will be informed if applicable. Last application day November 29.
