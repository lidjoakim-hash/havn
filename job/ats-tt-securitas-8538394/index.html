About the role Global HR Business Partner role works as part of the newly established Global HR Business Partnering team, created to build and embed a new way of working for HR Business Partner support for geographically distributed teams. It supports a globally distributed workforce in the business-critical global functions Global Clients and Security Risk Management (SRM), supporting senior leaders in a matrixed environment to drive consistent execution of core people processes for employees based in all countries. The first priority is to, within the global HRBP model, establish excellent operational basics and ensure execution discipline across the most critical people processes. Success in the first phase will be measured by consistent execution, reliability, and improved visibility across core people processes. As we continue to scale globally, there is a need for more consistent, coordinated HR support to stay aligned across countries and units and to build truly high performing teams. This role will support two critical global functions to ensure strong execution of core people processes.
