Veterinarian in Europe? Ready for your next career move in Sweden? Are you an experienced veterinarian looking for new professional opportunities in Sweden? Join one of Europe's leading veterinary healthcare providers and become part of a strong professional community with access to modern facilities, advanced veterinary medicine and excellent opportunities for further development. Why Sweden? At AniCura Sweden, we are continuously looking for experienced and talented veterinarians from across Europe who want to take the next step in their careers and contribute to the development of high-quality veterinary care. Sweden offers a unique combination of: Excellent work-life balance. Modern, well-equipped hospitals and clinics. High medical standards and advanced veterinary care. Strong focus on professional development and continuing education. A safe, family-friendly society with stunning nature and vibrant cities. Competitive compensation and attractive employment conditions. Opportunities for experienced veterinarians At AniCura Sweden, we believe that exceptional veterinary care is built by skilled professionals with different backgrounds, clinical experience and areas of expertise. Whether you are an experienced small animal veterinarian looking to broaden your clinical experience, a specialist seeking a new professional challenge, or a resident looking to further develop within your field, we offer opportunities to grow and make a meaningful contribution. Experienced small animal veterinarians For veterinarians with solid clinical experience, AniCura offers the opportunity to work alongside highly skilled colleagues in modern hospitals and clinics. You will have access to a wide range of cases, strong professional networks and opportunities to further develop your clinical expertise. We value veterinarians who are confident in their clinical work, enjoy collaboration and are motivated to contribute to high-quality patient care. Specialists, residents and advanced practitioners We are continuously looking for board-certified specialists, residents and veterinarians with advanced clinical competencies who want to contribute to and shape the future of veterinary medicine in Sweden. We welcome expertise in areas including, but not limited to: Orthopedic Surgery Soft Tissue Surgery Internal Medicine Emergency and Critical Care Diagnostic Imaging Neurology Anesthesia and Analgesia Oncology Cardiology Ophthalmology Dermatology Dentistry Exotic Animal Medicine Rehabilitation and Sports Medicine Clinical Pathology And many other veterinary disciplines. Where in Sweden? With AniCura, your career opportunities extend across Sweden - from vibrant metropolitan areas and university cities to beautiful coastal regions and scenic countryside communities. Whether you are looking for the pace and opportunities of a major referral hospital or the close-knit atmosphere of a regional clinic, we can help you find the right professional and personal fit.
