Diagnostics Engineer

Integro Consulting AB · Göteborg, Sweden

💰 ca. 510 000–620 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 17.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

We are looking for a Diagnostics Engineer for our client of commercial vehicles. Job Description: You will be part of the development team for off-board diagnostics solutions . Off-board solutions provide ways to fault trace, test and calibrate vehicles on aftermarket. This work requires a wide understanding of the complete platform, such as dependencies between functions and how end-user will interact with the tool and vehicle on aftermarket. Problem solving, adaptability, and creative solutions on a case to case basis are strengths that are considered very important in this position. Key Responsibilities: • Develop Diagnostic Service Tool (both test and calibration) Operations • Develop fault tracing steps • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to create optimal solutions for aftermarket • Verify operations at test rig & complete vehicle • Develop troubleshooting strategy for DTCs and symptoms that are considered relevant for machine/components within area of responsibility • Contribute actively in an Agile development environment Skills & Experiences required: • Minimum 4–5 years of hands-on experience in Embedded Software, within the automotive industry • Work Experience with Grade-X or aftermarket diagnostics tools and aftermarket technology is a must • Solid understanding of electrical systems architecture • Strong background in complete vehicle level • Ability to read, analyze, and implement engineering specifications • Previous experience working in an Agile team setup • Proficiency in English – both written and spoken Personal attributes As a person you are self-motivated and a fast learner. You enjoy sharing knowledge and is helpful to others in the team. You are a real team player with a positive attitude. You work effectively and enjoy problem solving. You communicate well with others and have to have excellent organizational skills and work in a structured way in order to be successful. Additional info: We schedule interviews on a rolling basis so do not wait until end date. You will work 100% onsite in Gothenburg
