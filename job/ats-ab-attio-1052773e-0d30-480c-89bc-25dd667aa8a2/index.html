ABOUT THE ROLE Attio is an AI-native platform trusted with our customers' most sensitive data - and keeping that data secure is foundational to everything we build. As we scale, so does the challenge: a large multi-tenant SaaS platform, AI-driven product features that introduce entirely new attack surfaces, and an engineering team shipping fast with AI-assisted development. We're looking for a Security Engineering Lead to build and lead Attio's Product Security function, spanning application security and security operations. You'll work alongside our Engineering Leadership to make Attio secure by design, lead the response when things go wrong, and recruit a world-class team of Security Engineers and SecOps practitioners. This is a rare opportunity to define how security works at a company operating at the frontier of AI. This is a hands on engineering role with team leadership responsibility over our growing product security function. You will not be responsible for GRC, we want you focussed on building.   WHAT YOU’LL DO - Build and lead Attio’s Product Security function. Secure Attio’s large-scale, AI-native SaaS platform, and the sensitive customer data on which it operates. - Work with Engineering Leadership peers to ensure that Attio is secure by design. - Understand and mitigate the unique risks and attack vectors associated with AI-driven product features, and AI-assisted software development. - Ensure that Attio is at the forefront of secure software development and operational practices. - Lead production security incident response; deeply understand security issues and their associated root causes and risks, create remediation plans, and drive to resolution. - Recruit and retain a world-class team of Security Engineers and SecOps practitioners.   WHAT YOU’LL BRING - Full understanding of the Application Security landscape in a high scale, multi-tenant SaaS context. - Appreciation of the tools and techniques used to secure SaaS applications, through prevention, detection and remediation of security issues. Understanding of how to integrate those tools into an effective, scalable, cost-efficient program that delivers a secure service. - First-class incident response skills, through the entire incident lifecycle of detection, remediation, clean-up, and post-incident corrective action. - Experience leading a team with line management. You should be a mentor who can recruit, retain and develop a high-quality Product Security team that continues to deliver for the business as Attio scales. DESIRABLE - Background in software engineering - Experience of running an Application Security program in a high scale, multi-tenant SaaS context. - Experience of hiring and managing a team of Security Engineers, and working with cross-functional partners. - Experience of security certification and re-certification programs. - Project or program management experience.   WHAT WE OFFER - Competitive salary of €120,000 to €145,000 - Equity in an early-stage tech company on an incredible trajectory - Apple hardware - Team off-site in fun places!
