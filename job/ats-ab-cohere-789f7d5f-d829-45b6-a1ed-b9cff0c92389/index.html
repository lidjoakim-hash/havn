WHO ARE WE? Cohere is the leading security-first enterprise AI company. We build cutting-edge foundation AI models and end-to-end products that are designed to solve real-world business problems. We’re training and deploying frontier models for enterprises who are building AI systems. We believe that our work is instrumental to the widespread adoption of AI and we are looking for folks that want to be part of that. We obsess over what we build. Each one of us is responsible for contributing to increasing the capabilities of our models and the value they drive for our customers. Cohere is a team of researchers, engineers, designers, and more, who are all passionate about their craft. We are a global technology company headquartered in Toronto with key offices in London, New York City, San Francisco, Montreal, Paris, Berlin and Seoul. Join us! ABOUT THE ROLE? Cohere’s Procurement team buys everything from pens to compute. We’re looking for a Lead, Direct Strategic Sourcing to own the categories closest to how we build: the technology, software, engineering talent and, over time, the compute that power our research and engineering teams. Reporting to the Head of Procurement, you’ll build this pillar from the ground up. You’ll set category strategy, run our most complex and highest-stakes technology negotiations, and become a trusted commercial partner to engineering, research and infrastructure leaders. You’ll put the structure in place so these buys scale with both speed and control, and as the function grows, you’ll hire and lead a small team of specialists and analysts. This is an opportunity for a hands-on, technically fluent sourcing leader who thrives in a high-growth environment, is comfortable navigating ambiguity, and wants to shape how a frontier AI company buys the technology it runs on.
