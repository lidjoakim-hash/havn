Introduction Aker QRILL Company is a world leader in the sustainable production of krill-based products. We are dedicated to enhancing the nutritional value of feed for aquaculture and livestock and contributing to a more sustainable global food system. Our business has experienced consistent growth within the Aker BioMarine organization. In 2024 we were spun out as a stand-alone entity, and we remain a reputable company in the maritime industry known for a focus on operational excellence. Working with us means you appreciate diversity, are highly skilled within your field and can cope with unexpected situations. You enjoy a fast pace, tolerate quick decisions and continuously ongoing changes. You are a true team player who “chips in” when the situation requires so. We are seeking two highly skilled and motivated Chief Engineers to join our dynamic team on our krill fishing vessels. What you will do As the Chief Engineer, you will oversee all technical operations within our organization. You will be responsible for leading the engine department, managing projects, and ensuring the efficient operation and maintenance of our equipment and facilities. The ideal candidate will have a strong background as Chief Engineer, exceptional leadership skills, and a proven track record of success in similar roles. Chief Engineer is overall responsible and in charge of all technical installations onboard the vessel, and all R&M and ordering of spare parts for these components as well as responsible for, and being sure costs are held at or below budget. Other responsibilities will be: • Lead and manage the engine department, providing guidance, mentoring, and support to achieve project goals and objectives. • Oversee the design, development, and implementation of engineering projects, ensuring compliance with industry standards and regulations. • Manage the maintenance and repair of equipment and infrastructure, optimizing efficiency and minimizing downtime. • Collaborate with other departments to support company objectives and initiatives. • Develop and implement strategies to improve operational performance and reduce costs. • Ensure compliance with safety regulations and promote a culture of safety within the engine department. Your profile Are you the Chief Engineer we’re looking for? The right candidate has experience from, preferably, other shipping and fishing companies. You are structured and systematic yet flexible and responsive. You have a positive, can-do attitude and manage to roll up your sleeves with the motivation to respond in a 24/7/365 operation. We think you have: • Engineer Officer Class 1, CoC M1. • Medical Certificate • Safety Course for officer • At least 5 years of experience in role as Chief Engineer. • Strong leadership and management skills, with the ability to inspire and motivate a team. • Excellent problem-solving abilities and analytical skills. • Proficient in project management software and tools. • Knowledge of regulatory requirements and industry best practices. • Effective communication skills, both verbal and written. Location Vessel/Antarctica. Embarkation date approx. May 2027. Benefits: • We believe that diversity drives success. Everyone at TQC should feel involved, included, and respected. • Competitive compensation and pension scheme • Comprehensive insurance program • Unique location in Antarctica Travel and rotation Crew changes are normally arranged in Montevideo, Punta Arenas and Puerto Williams. 3 shift rotation with approximately 1 period on board (approx. 3 months) and 2 periods home (approx. 4-5 months) but may deviate slightly due to operation. Our value chain stretches from our vessels and sustainable krill harvesting operations in Antarctica, through our logistics hub in Montevideo, and all the way to customers around the world. The Company operates a fleet of five vessels, including two cargo vessel and three fishing vessels, leading krill fishing within the Antarctic region. The three fishing vessels operate on the fishing grounds year-round, except for one month in October, which is dedicated to maintenance and repairs while the cargo vessel transports products and crew to and from the fishing grounds throughout the year. Our offices are located in Fornebu and Aalesund, with the logistics base and product warehouse in Montevideo, Uruguay. We are about 500 employees globally, of which about 430 work on board our krill vessels in Antarctica. We are a diverse company with more than 25 nationalities, spread across all time zones and most continents. QRILL™ Aqua is a functional feed ingredient for shrimp, marine fish, and salmonids, increasing productivity in aquaculture - through improved growth, health and quality. QRILL™ Pet is a functional ingredient for pet nutrition with documented health benefits for companion animals.
