Calibration Engineer

Integro Consulting AB · Göteborg, Sweden

💰 ca. 510 000–620 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 17.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

We are looking to expand our EATS´ team with a Calibration Engineer to work within Engine Controls & Diagnostics. Job description: You will work in a team responsible for securing that the engine fulfils the emission legislation, by developing and calibrating its own inhouse software models. The team consists of calibration engineers and function developers. You & the team will calibrate start and catalyst heating, emission control and purge control. The team delivers engine software to combustion engines in both mild hybrid and hybrid applications. From an early stage you will be involved in the development of new powertrain concepts and its software, where the team has the responsibility for vehicle emissions, and develop solutions that meet both legal requirements and give the customer an outstanding product. Early in the projects you will be collecting data from the engine test rigs, in order to calibrate the software-models. Validation and later, final verification are always performed in cars or what the application is suited for. The job variety is huge and the need for further development is always there, which provides an interesting research atmosphere. What you will do: You will work in a team with experienced engineers but have a clear area of responsibility. Your main tasks will be to calibrate emission-related functions. The team is doing a large amount of our work in different engine test cells located in the laboratory, but also in vehicles on site and during expeditions to find extreme climates and altitudes. You will use advanced programs for modelling and data processing for best results. ﻿Main tasks: Plan and develop methods of the calibration process. Define and handle requirements within the system and from other areas. Deliver calibration and verification of emission related functions. Verify calibration in rigs and cars in different environments. Evaluation and problem analysis. Is this you? To enjoy and succeed in the role, we see that you have a great interest in the latest technology, and you have an interest in powertrain controls and calibration. You also like to dig into a huge amount of data. You are a positive and self-driven person, who strives to see "the big picture" to be able to contribute in the best way. You appreciate being a part of a team and are a good communicator. Further, you are always curious, and love to solve problems in a pragmatic way! Skill requirements Qualifications: • We believe you have a Bachelor’s or Master’s in the field of Mechanical, Physics, or Electrical Engineering, or equivalent You have a passion for cars and internal combustion engines. • Fluent in Matlab/Python/Simulink • Fluent in English (written and spoken) • Driver’s license (Swedish B-level, passenger car) • Fluent in Swedish (written and spoken) is a plus • Good knowledge of internal combustion engines • Passion for vehicles and powertrains • Theoretical courses in combustion engines, software programming and electronics • Experience in ETAS INCA software ﻿Personal qualities • Self-driven with a positive attitude • Strong analytical skills as well as being able to execute tests “hands-on” • You plan, conduct, summarize and report your work in a structured and thorough manner • A curious and problemsolving mindset • You can easily communicate and collaborate • A true teamplayer open and supportive This is a fantastic opportunity to contribute to advanced engine development while being part of a collaborative and supportive team. You need to be living in Sweden and be able to work 100% onsite in Gothenburg. Looking forward to meeting you!
