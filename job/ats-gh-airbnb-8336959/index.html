Every day, hosts offer unique stays and experiences that make it possible for guests to connect with communities in a more authentic way. The Community You Will Join: The Homes Supply team plays a critical role in ensuring Airbnb secures a diverse and high-quality supply of stays listings. The Difference You will Make: In this role, you will lead a team of Market Associates responsible for a high-velocity, input-driven account management motion supporting our long tail of Small/medium sized Hosts. You will own execution excellence across rapid optimisation workflows, holding the team accountable to activity-based KPIs while driving consistent, scalable impact across the region. You will also serve as a key stakeholder and operator for all scaled supply acquisition and retention initiatives within your assigned territory. This includes organising and executing regional roadshows and events, developing strategies to grow and retain supply, and rapidly scaling best practices across the team. The role partners closely with Host Services and Community teams to ensure seamless delivery, strong host engagement, and measurable outcomes. This role plays a critical role in driving host quality, supply performance, and campaign effectiveness at scale.
