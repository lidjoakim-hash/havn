About the job You will join the IT System Engineering team within the IT Department, alongside the IT Support and IT Project Management teams. Based in Paris, you will report to the IT System Engineering Manager and contribute to the design, deployment, maintenance, and continuous improvement of Contentsquare’s core IT platforms. This is a full-time, permanent position with no direct reports. You will work across identity and access management, endpoint and mobile device management, SaaS applications, automation, and AI-enabled IT initiatives. The role is primarily on-site in Paris, with some remote work permitted according to company policy. What you’ll do Design, build, deploy, maintain, and continuously improve the core IT platform across identity and access management, endpoint management, enterprise mobility, and cross-functional SaaS applications. Manage and improve Okta, Jamf, Google Workspace, Slack, Microsoft Intune, macOS, and Windows environments, ensuring secure, reliable, and effective services for employees. Build and maintain automated workflows using Okta Workflows, Zapier, Workato, or similar platforms to simplify IT processes and improve the employee experience. Develop, maintain, and improve Bash scripts and other scripts used to automate IT operations, system administration tasks, monitoring, configuration, and user or device lifecycle processes. Collaborate with the IT Support team on escalated cases and cross-team projects, providing technical expertise across identity, endpoints, access, SaaS applications, and device management. Lead assigned initiatives autonomously, contribute to project management, provide clear progress updates, and use tools such as Jira to track projects and deliverables. Use AI tools and set up MCP servers to support automation, IT operations, and the AI at Work initiative. Outcomes you will work toward Maintain secure, reliable, and well-managed identity, endpoint, and SaaS environments for Contentsquare employees. Improve the efficiency, scalability, and consistency of IT operations through automation and scripting. Resolve escalated cases effectively in collaboration with the IT Support team. Deliver owned projects and initiatives with clear planning, documentation, stakeholder communication, and follow-through. Identify and implement practical opportunities to improve security, performance, reliability, and employee experience across the IT platform.
