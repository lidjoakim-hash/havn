Finance Graduate Programme 2027

Arla Foods AB · Solna, Sweden

Are you interested in shaping your career in Finance within a global company? Join Arla Foods as a Finance Graduate in Denmark, Sweden, or Germany, where you will develop your skills, gain international experience, and drive business impact. As part of a dynamic Finance organisation, you'll contribute to sustainability efforts and help shape the future of Finance using cutting-edge digital technologies and data. About our Finance Graduate Programme The Finance Graduate Programme is a 2-year programme consisting of three rotations across our global Finance organisation. You'll enhance your core finance skills and develop essential competencies in Business & Performance, Data & Digital, and Strategy & Execution. Throughout the programme, you will be exposed to key finance disciplines while developing your strategic, personal, and professional skills to ensure you have the capabilities needed to succeed in the future of Finance. We are looking for graduates to join in Denmark, Sweden, and Germany, and as part of the programme, you will have a 6-month rotation abroad in one of our European offices. Your Finance Graduate journey begins on 1st September 2027, where you will be hired on a permanent employment contract from day one and will continue your career within Finance in Arla Foods after finishing the 2-year graduate programme. How will you make an impact as a Finance Graduate? As a Finance Graduate, you will be a part of a highly competent Finance organisation contributing to both daily operations and cross-functional projects to drive the business forward. You will get your own areas of responsibility, enabling on-the-job learning, and you will get to explore the following areas within Finance Business & Performance (12 months – home country) You will apply analytical skills to provide guidance for decision-makers and develop a strong understanding of financial performance through analysis and modeling, while enhancing your ability to influence others Data & Digital (6 months – home country or abroad) You will leverage data to generate insights, grow advanced analytics and visualization skills, and apply digital technology to automate and optimize tasks for efficiency Strategy & Execution (6 months – home country or abroad) You will build and communicate business cases for investments, engage in problem solving and stakeholder management, and use finance to drive business growth and create value What will make you a successful Finance Graduate? To apply for the Finance Graduate Programme, please ensure that you can tick off the following boxes Hold a bachelor's or master's degree from a university by September 2027 or have graduated within the last 2 years. We are open to various educational backgrounds, so what truly matters is your interest in Finance and curious mindset Minimum 1 year of relevant experience from work and/or extracurricular activities Being professionally fluent in English Currently residing in, or willing to relocate to, either Denmark, Sweden or Germany, and being internationally mobile for at least 6 months as part of the programme In addition, we are looking for graduates who Lead with confidence, make independent decisions, are results-oriented while valuing the journey to achieve those outcomes Build positive relationships with a collaborative mindset and are open to feedback Communicate complex information clearly and effectively Demonstrate curiosity and a willingness to learn and explore Use an analytical and structured approach to problem-solving with a proactive attitude Why join Arla’s Finance Graduate Programme? As part of our Finance Graduate Programme, you will receive a variety of development opportunities every step of the way. To ensure your professional and personal development, you will benefit from Responsibilities from day one enabling on-the-job learning in three different areas Graduate development camps, taking place in different markets with your graduate colleagues
