Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states. Waymo's cars may be self-driving, but its construction projects are steered by experts like you. Safety is our foundation and is at the heart of everything we do. As Waymo's Regional PreConstruction Manager you will be responsible for the due diligence and preconstruction of a variety of facilities including Service Centers, EV Charging Facilities, Office, and Lab/ R&D typologies across the globe for Waymo in an extremely fast paced environment with a high degree of ambiguity. At every turn, process excellence, collaboration, and detailed cross-org communications are critical. This role follows a hybrid work schedule and reports to The Head of Project Delivery You will: • Lead Regional Technical Due Diligence: Own and manage the technical due diligence process for all candidate properties within your region, reviewing power capacity, zoning, land usage, environment risks and ODD alignment • Oversee Feasibility, Test-Fits & Cost Estimates: Direct and review the spatial test-fit layouts, conceptual cost estimates, and initial project schedules in your region • Track Regional Milestones & RAID Governance: Actively track critical-path milestones, manage dependencies, and maintain regional Risks, Assumptions, Issues, and Dependencies (RAID) logs across all active pre-construction projects to proactively identify and resolve bottlenecks • Drive Regional Artifact Standardization: Standardize and enforce the use of global pre-construction artifacts (e.g., scope templates, due diligence checklists, and handoff deliverables), ensuring every regional project starts from a consistent, reusable baseline and that all variations are intentional and controlled • Manage Regional Vendor Sourcing & Accountability: Oversee the regional pre-construction vendors (e.g., architects, civil engineers, cost consultants, etc.), holding them accountable to high performance and rigorous deliverable quality • Build & Lead the Regional Pre-Con Team: Recruit, mentor, and develop a high-performing regional pre-construction team, growing individual capabilities and scaling pre-con coverage to match the expanding market pipeline.
