Who we are Helsing is a defence AI company. Our mission is to protect our democracies. We aim to achieve technological leadership, so that open societies can continue to make sovereign decisions and control their ethical standards. As democracies, we believe we have a special responsibility to be thoughtful about the development and deployment of powerful technologies like AI. We take this responsibility seriously. We are an ambitious and committed team of engineers, AI specialists and customer-facing programme managers. We are looking for mission-driven people to join our European teams – and apply their skills to solve the most complex and impactful problems. We embrace an open and transparent culture that welcomes healthy debates on the use of technology in defence, its benefits, and its ethical implications. The role Helsing's CA-1 programme is building the software-defined intelligence layer for one of Europe's most advanced combat aircraft. As a Senior Systems Engineer specialising in Product Security, Cryptography and Cyber, you will be responsible for defining and governing the security architecture of the entire platform — from the requirements that shape hardware and software design, through to the compliance artefacts that satisfy national and NATO authority expectations. This is not a role confined to policy or paperwork. You will develop the aircraft security concept from first principles, own the design of intra- and inter-vehicle communication security, and lead Helsing's approach to platform-level cryptographic management, including functions such as erase and zeroise. You will work directly with customers — requirements workshops, technical reviews, solution showcases — and with German national security authorities, making professional fluency in German an operational necessity. The candidate who will thrive here brings a rare combination of systems engineering rigour, deep cryptographic and cyber knowledge, and the ability to translate complex security requirements into engineering reality on a programme that genuinely matters.
