ABOUT US At OakNorth, we're on a mission to empower ambitious businesses and the communities they serve. Since 2015, we've lent over $21 billion across the UK and US, helped create more than 58,000 new homes and 36,000 new jobs, and supported hundreds of thousands of personal savers — all while driving economic growth in the markets we serve. ABOUT THE TEAM The Portfolio Monitoring team is responsible for the ongoing management and surveillance of a complex, high-value lending book. We track performance, identify emerging risk and make sure the Bank has a clear, current view of its exposure. It's a hands-on team — close to the data and the clients, and expected to surface issues early and act on them. We work across leveraged finance, real estate and corporate lending. Our approach combines rigorous credit analysis with commercial judgement and direct engagement with borrowers and other stakeholders. We work closely with Credit, Workout & Restructuring, Legal and Operations, and increasingly use technology and AI to sharpen how we monitor and report. ABOUT THE ROLE We are looking for a commercially minded credit professional with experience in corporate or leveraged lending to join as a Director. You will take ownership of a portfolio of complex trading and leveraged finance credits, maintaining a forward-looking view of performance and acting early when risk emerges. You will understand the borrower, its business plan, capital structure and key stakeholders; challenge financial performance and forecasts; and determine what changes mean for the Bank's position. You will lead borrower discussions, assess requests for waivers and amendments, and present clear, evidence-based recommendations to senior management and Credit Committee. This is not a reporting role. It calls for hands-on ownership, sound credit judgement and the confidence to challenge assumptions and stand behind recommendations. You will work closely with Credit, Workout & Restructuring, Legal and Operations, including on the timely escalation and handover of deteriorating credits where appropriate. WHAT YOU'LL DO Own and actively manage the portfolio - Take ownership of a portfolio of complex trading and leveraged finance credits, maintaining a clear view of each borrower's performance, financial position and emerging risks - Lead annual credit reviews, assessing business performance, management forecasts, risk grade and appetite, and recommending any action required - Challenge performance against the original underwriting assumptions and understand the borrower, its management team, sponsor, capital structure and potential exit or refinancing routes - Manage watchlist and early warning processes, identify deterioration promptly and agree appropriate monitoring, actions and escalation - Work with Workout & Restructuring and other colleagues when a credit deteriorates, ensuring a clear and timely handover where appropriate Lead borrower engagement and credit decisions - Lead direct discussions with borrowers, management teams and sponsors, building relationships while confidently challenging performance and protecting the Bank's position - Own waiver, consent and amendment requests, assess their credit implications, negotiate terms and present clear recommendations for approval - Engage with co-lenders, agent banks, advisers and Legal as needed, understanding stakeholder positions and the Bank's rights under the lending documentation - Prepare and present well-structured credit papers and portfolio updates to senior management and Credit Committee, setting out the key facts, risks, options, recommendation and rationale - Follow through on agreed actions, decisions and escalation, ensuring issues are addressed rather than simply reported Apply rigorous financial and portfolio analysis - Analyse and challenge financial statements, trading performance, cash flow and liquidity forecasts, leverage, debt service and covenant headroom
