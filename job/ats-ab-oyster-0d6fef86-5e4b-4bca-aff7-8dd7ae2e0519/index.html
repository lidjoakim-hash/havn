Role Summary Oyster is looking for a Partner Marketing Manager to build a scalable engine that creates demand and pipeline through our partner ecosystem. You will inherit an active portfolio, keep important commitments moving, and decide what to continue, improve, or retire using performance and cost data. Working across Channel and Marketing, you will design and execute programs that market to partners, with partners, and through partners—turning partner strategy into repeatable go-to-market motions with clear measurement. This is a high-autonomy individual-contributor role with global remit, budget ownership, and direct partnership with the Director of Demand and Growth. Key Responsibilities - Design, orchestrate, execute, and measure partner marketing programs across the ecosystem. - Build programs that market to partners, with partners, and through partners. - Protect existing partner commitments during handover while reviewing the portfolio for performance, cost, and strategic fit. - Own and allocate the partner marketing budget, including co-marketing and MDF, and defend investment decisions with return data. - Develop partner segmentation, positioning, messaging, and repeatable operating models. - Create joint programs across partner types, including technology partners. - Enable partners to take Oyster’s message and value proposition to their audiences. - Align Channel, Demand Generation, Product Marketing, Content, Sales, and Revenue Operations around integrated plans. - Establish credible reporting for partner-sourced pipeline, partner-influenced pipeline, engagement, and program ROI. - Apply AI and automation processes to improve segmentation, personalization, program development, execution, and insight generation.
