About the position Be part of the future with NMBU! The Department of Plant Sciences (IPV), Faculty of Biosciences, at the Norwegian University of Life Sciences (NMBU) has a vacancy for a permanent 100% position as Associate Professor in Population Genetics and Plant Breeding. We are looking for an ambitious researcher and dedicated educator to strengthen NMBU's internationally recognized research and education in plant genetics, population genetics, and plant breeding with a focus on forage crops. This position represents a strategic investment in one of the department's core academic disciplines and will play a key role in educating the next generation of plant breeders and geneticists while advancing research that contributes to sustainable agriculture and future food security. The successful candidate is expected to establish an internationally competitive research group within population genetics and plant breeding, with particular emphasis on quantitative genetics, breeding methodologies, genomics, genetic diversity, and the breeding of outcrossing crops. As part of our academic team, you will teach and supervise Bachelor, Master, and PhD students, contribute to attracting competitive national and international research funding, and strengthen collaborations with breeding companies, research institutes, and universities in Norway and abroad.  You will play a leading role in further developing our educational portfolio within genetics and plant breeding and contribute to innovative teaching methods and student-active learning. The successful candidate will become part of a highly interdisciplinary research environment with close collaboration across the Department of Plant Sciences, the Faculty of Biosciences, and other faculties at NMBU. The position also offers opportunities to contribute to interdisciplinary initiatives in population genetics, genomics, breeding methodologies, and climate-resilient agriculture, supporting both plant and animal science communities. We are proud to educate highly skilled graduates and to conduct research with a strong societal impact. We are looking for candidates who combine scientific excellence with a passion for teaching, collaboration, and innovation. We look forward to welcoming a new colleague who will help shape the future of genetics and plant breeding at NMBU. Main tasks The successful candidate is expected to • Conduct research of the highest international standard and strengthen research in population genetics, genomics, genomic selection, breeding methodologies, and climate-resilient crop development. • Publish regularly in peer-reviewed international journals. • Contribute to both basic and applied research. • Secure external funding from national and international funding agencies, including Horizon Europe and the Research Council of Norway. • Supervise PhD, MSc and BSc students. • Contribute to innovation and collaboration with industry and breeding organization.  • Network and collaborate within and outside NMBU and actively participate in interdisciplinary research initiatives across NMBU. • Contribute to the strategic development of plant genetics and breeding activities at the department. • Develop and deliver high-quality teaching in population genetics, plant breeding, quantitative genetics, genomics, and bioinformatics for plant breeding across Bachelor's, Master's, and PhD programmes. • Contribute to educational excellence through student supervision, curriculum development, pedagogical innovation, and the implementation of research-based teaching and modern digital learning approaches. • Conduct administrative tasks and other joint tasks at the faculty in collaboration with colleagues and students.
