Solution Consultant (Oslo)

What if your next tech role didn't make you choose between people and technology? At ConnectMyApps, Solutions Consultants work with both. We build integrations for client across Europe that make their business applications work together, across HR, finance, ERP, CRM and more. We're growing our Customer Solutions team and are looking for a Solutions Consultant to join us in Oslo. What you'll do You'll work across the whole customer journey, from first conversations and onboarding to solution design, implementation, testing and ongoing support. That includes: → Working out what the customer is trying to achieve, even before the requirements are technical → Exploring what customer and third-party systems can (and can't) do, and how their APIs let them talk to each other → Turning business needs into clear technical specifications → Using AI to help turn those specifications into reliable integrations, then reviewing, validating and refining → Leading test sessions with customers and stakeholders → Working closely with customers, partners and our team to make it all work in the real world This isn't a traditional developer role. We want someone who thinks first and builds second: someone who can look at a problem, understand what's going on, ask the right questions, find a solution and explain it clearly to everyone involved. You don't need to be an experienced coder, but you should be curious about how technology works and willing to find your way around solution components and code. What we're looking for Someone who moves comfortably between people, business and technology. You: → Communicate and present well, and are comfortable in front of customers → Can turn business conversations into technical requirements, and explain technical ideas in plain language → Understand APIs and how systems work together → Think logically and enjoy solving problems → Pick up unfamiliar systems quickly → Have used or experimented with AI agents for coding and/or document generation → Enjoy variety: different systems, industries and problems → Are comfortable with fast pace and having to switch context between projects → Are a real team player New graduates are welcome. We care more about potential, curiosity and mindset than years of experience. Fluency in English and a Scandinavian language is a must. Other languages are a bonus. Why ConnectMyApps? A part of The Amesto Group, ConnectMyApps was founded in 2014 with the purpose of simplifying integration and setting business free from manual data entry. We're a small team that changes the way companies work. Here you'll work directly with customers and see the impact of what you build. Many of the tools and improvements we use today came from the team's own ideas. So, when you see a better way of doing something, we want to hear it, and we want you to explore it. You won't just follow an established process. You'll help shape what our way of working looks like. We foster a strong team spirit. We have regular team events, spend time together outside work and, importantly, enjoy each other's company. You won't be one small piece of a big puzzle. You might actually leave a mark. Benefits We offer competitive salary, pension, health insurance, career development, travel and hotel discounts, Ruter discounts, mobile phone packages, discounted lunch at an excellent canteen, and discounts at selected Oslo shops and services. • Location: Oslo, Norway • Position type: On-site • Level: Junior level • Requirements: Fluency in English and a Scandinavian language required • Education: University degree or similar • Work permit: Must have full residency/work permit for Norway.
