Here at HelloFresh, we are a global meal kit delivery service that aims to make cooking at home easy, fun, and convenient. Convenience for customers is the backbone of our business, therefore it's imperative that our customers receive their deliveries on time, in full, to the right location and in perfect condition. We are seeking an experienced B2C Logistics Team Lead for the Nordic region (Norway, Sweden, and Denmark) to lead our Logistics Performance Team. In this local senior leadership role, you will be responsible for driving operational excellence, optimizing logistics performance, and ensuring a high level of customer delivery satisfaction across the region. The successful candidate will provide strategic direction, manage team performance, collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders, and implement initiatives that enhance delivery reliability, efficiency, and the overall customer experience. Above all, we are looking for people who will make HelloFresh better. There are many different ways of developing skills, and we love diverse experiences! So even if you don’t “tick all the boxes” but think you’d thrive in this role, we would really like to learn more about you. What You’ll Do (The Recipe) Performance Management: • Senior point of contact owning relationships with our Middle & Final mile 3PL’s with a focus on improving performance, overall customer experience and reducing costs. • Track and analyse carrier metrics, such as OTD, service quality & reliability, cost efficiency. Identify areas for improvement and develop actionable insights. • Crisis management e.g. mitigating delivery risks (eg snow) to ensure minimal disruption to service. Team Leadership • Lead and motivate your team, leading by example at all times and exemplifying HelloFresh DNA. • Ownership and continuous improvement of our best in class engagement scores, making HelloFresh and specifically the Logistics department a great place to work. • Relentless prioritisation and open communication to ensure everyone is working towards the same goals. Budget Management • Full P&L ownership. • Contract management, reducing delivery costs through supplier negotiations and other creative initiatives. Innovation • Create long term solutions to reduce delivery errors in conjunction with our key suppliers and internal teams, with a strong focus on using data to measure the effectiveness of initiatives. • Evaluate new partner opportunities, deciding the selection and onboarding of new carriers based on performance, service capabilities, and cost-effectiveness. • Workcross functionally, representing the logistics department whilst also supporting wider business initiatives that improve customer experience and retention. • Evaluate customer NPS and Trustpilot feedback to identify key opportunity areas and set mid / long term initiatives to improve customer sentiment. • Set the long term strategy alongside the business objectives, drilling these down into quarterly OKR’s and KPI’s that cascade through all members of your team.
