The platform enables the development of configurable AI applications, primarily focused on improving software development efficiency and automating engineering activities. In this role, you will design and develop intelligent automation pipelines that combine conventional software engineering techniques with the capabilities of Large Language Models. You will be part of a team driving the adoption of AI across engineering systems, working closely with development teams to identify opportunities, develop practical solutions and configure AI/ML capabilities for different applications. This position is suited to a technically strong and curious engineer who enjoys experimenting with emerging technologies, translating ideas into working solutions and collaborating across different engineering domains. Translate technical concepts and use cases into working implementations. Collaborate with other development teams to identify suitable AI application areas. Support teams in configuring and adapting the ML platform for their specific requirements.
