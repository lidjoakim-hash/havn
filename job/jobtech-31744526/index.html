ERP Programme Planning Specialist – Global ERP Transformation Are you an experienced ERP Programme Planning Specialist who enjoys bringing structure, clarity and control to complex international transformation programmes? Do you thrive in an environment where planning, resource management and collaboration are essential to successful delivery? We are currently looking for an experienced ERP Programme Planning Specialist for an exciting consulting assignment with one of our clients, a leading international company within the industrial sector, based in Lund, Sweden. This is an opportunity to play an important role in a large-scale global ERP transformation programme, working closely with international teams, project managers and senior stakeholders. About the role As an ERP Programme Planning Specialist, you will be part of a Transformation Management Office (TMO), supporting the planning and coordination of multiple ERP rollout projects. Your main focus will be to create and maintain a clear, integrated overview of programme timelines, milestones, resources and risks, ensuring that programme leadership has the information needed to make informed decisions. The role combines strategic programme planning with hands-on coordination, analysis and continuous improvement. Your main responsibilities Develop and maintain an integrated programme plan across multiple ERP rollout projects. Coordinate milestones, schedules and dependencies across international teams and workstreams. Manage resource allocation, capacity planning and forward-looking resource forecasts. Identify resource bottlenecks, scheduling conflicts and potential delivery risks. Maintain programme-wide risk registers with clear ownership and escalation procedures. Monitor programme progress and provide structured reporting to senior stakeholders. Analyse planning, resource and risk data to support informed decision-making. Support scenario planning, prioritisation and replanning when programme requirements change. Collaborate closely with project managers, business stakeholders and technical teams to ensure alignment across the programme. Who are we looking for? We are looking for a structured, analytical and proactive professional with solid experience in large-scale ERP implementations, international system rollouts or complex business transformation programmes. You understand the challenges of coordinating multiple projects, managing dependencies and ensuring that resources are effectively planned and utilised. You are comfortable working in international environments, communicating with senior stakeholders and translating complex information into clear, actionable insights. Key skills and experience Proven experience in programme planning, PMO/TMO or large-scale ERP transformation programmes. Experience coordinating international ERP implementations or system rollouts. Strong understanding of integrated programme planning and milestone management. Practical experience in resource management, capacity planning and forecasting. Experience identifying and managing risks and dependencies across multiple projects. Strong analytical skills and experience working with planning and resource data. Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills. Ability to work independently while maintaining a programme-wide perspective. Excellent communication skills in English. Nice to have Experience with SAP or other enterprise ERP platforms. Background in global transformation programmes within industrial or manufacturing organisations. Experience with programme planning, reporting or resource management tools. Experience supporting senior leadership with scenario analysis and decision-making.
