Packaging Team Leader

Omnipollo AB · Upplands-Bro, Sweden

💰 ca. 290 000–350 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 18.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Are you passionate about beer, quality, and production processes? Do you want to be a key player in packaging our award-winning beers? Then you might be our next Team Leader! Omnipollo AB is a Swedish brewery founded in 2010 by brewer Henok Fentie and designer Karl Grandin. Over the years, the brewery has won numerous awards for its beer and has several bestsellers such as Levon, Nebuchadnezzar, and Noa Pecan Mud Cake. Omnipollo AB exports its beer to 45 countries. Omnipollo AB has a number of restaurants and bars including Omnipollos hatt and Omnipollos kyrka in Stockholm as well as Omnipollos Tokyo in Japan. About the Role As a Team Leader at our packaging facility in Kungsängen, you will play a central role in leading and distributing the daily work. Your main responsibility is to ensure that the daily operations including tanking, centrifugation, pasteurization, packaging, and ongoing equipment maintenance run efficiently and correctly. You will work closely with the Head of Production, the production team at the brewery, and the Warehouse Manager. You will coach, lead, and coordinate the Packaging Operators to ensure a smooth and efficient workflow. Together with your colleagues, you will take responsibility for creating a good working environment and developing our operations. The work requires precision to maintain our routines, industry standards, and to produce products of high quality that meet all statutory requirements. Your Responsibilities: Leading and overseeing the daily operational work at the packaging facility to ensure efficient and smooth processes. Coaching and leading Packaging Operators, ensuring their work follows company guidelines and industry standards. Performing ongoing floor maintenance and planning to minimize production downtime, as well as acting as the main contact for technical repairs (welders, plumbers, electricians, etc.). Assisting in staff schedule planning in collaboration with the Head of Production. Acting as a supervisor and mentor for new staff and interns. Ensuring routines and SOPs are followed in daily work and actively contributing to their development. As well as being a part of the daily operations: Operating centrifugation and pasteurization. Packaging our products. Logging and documenting work. Transferring beer out of the brewery and into the packaging facility. Performing cleaning and CIP (Clean in Place). Who Are You? We are looking for someone who has a strong interest in production processes and a natural aptitude for leadership. You are a problem solver who can quickly identify and resolve practical or technical issues that may arise during the packaging and brewing process. You have a desire to motivate a team of brewery workers, delegate tasks effectively, and ensure excellent communication within the group. To succeed in this role, you also need to be competent in performing taste and quality tests to ensure our products meet company standards, and be committed to ensuring the team complies with all food, safety and statutory guidelines. Qualifications: Brewery technician education or equivalent. Minimum 4 years of experience working in packaging at a brewery. Experience in a leading or coordinating role with an emphasis on technical aspects is highly meriting. Solid expertise regarding packaging equipment, centrifugal separators, pasteurizers, the brewing process, and the finished product. Technical skills to handle and maintain brewery equipment. Valid B driver’s license and forklift license (Truckkort). Fluent in English both spoken and written; Swedish is a huge plus. About the Employment The position is full-time with an initial six-month probationary period. You will work closely with the team, as well as our brewery and bars. We believe in warmth, professionalism, and the idea that everyone should feel at home with us. Our reach is global, but our heart is rooted locally. It is our community and collaboration that make us so successful. Want to joi
