About the position  A PhD research fellowship is available in the Oral Physiology and Cancer Research Group (OPCG) at the Institute of Oral biology, Faculty of Dentistry, the University of Oslo. One of our group’s major interests is the study of how extracellular vesicles from cancer cells affect various normal cells, both in the immediate vicinity of the tumor and at a distance, changing their phenotype to support the growing tumor mass. In the proposed project, we aim to investigate the effect of extracellular vesicles from oral squamous cell carcinoma (OSCC) on the function of normal cells, including endothelial cells.  The candidate will apply various experimental methods to assess whether the normal phenotype changes towards a tumor-supporting one upon OSCC-derived extracellular vesicle exposure. Such alterations may include increased proliferation, modifications of intracellular Ca2&#43; signalling pathways, shifts in the metabolic profile and more.      More about the position The main purpose of the fellowship is research training leading to the successful completion of a PhD degree.  Project title: “Tumor-host interaction in oral squamous cell carcinoma (OSCC)” This is a 100% full-time, three-year PhD Research Fellowship position. The successful candidate is expected to produce three scientific papers and a doctoral thesis. The place of work is Oslo, Norway. More about the fellowship Admission to the PhD programme at the Faculty of Dentistry is a prerequisite for taking up the position. The candidate is expected to complete the project within the fellowship period. A final plan for the research training must be approved and formalised no later than three months after the start of the position.
