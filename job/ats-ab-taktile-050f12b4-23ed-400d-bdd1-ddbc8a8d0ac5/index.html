Taktile empowers financial institutions to transform into truly AI-native organizations. We’re growing rapidly with enterprise customers across banks and insurance companies, and we’re looking for a Senior Full-Stack Engineer to join our Platform team. About the role Our Engineering and Product Design teams are building multiple verticals on our platform at high speed, leveraging AI and building agentic functionality into the platform itself. As we scale into the world's largest regulated financial institutions, identity and access management (IAM), authorization, and fine-grained permissions have become foundational to our enterprise readiness. We are hiring a Full-Stack Engineer to join the team building Taktile's IAM and permissions platform: the authorization backbone that powers enterprise-grade access control across our product. Joining us, you'll design and build permission models and access controls end-to-end, from the React/TypeScript access-management and admin UIs down to the Python (FastAPI) authorization services. You'll take on the problems that make enterprise authorization genuinely hard: multi-tenant isolation, fine-grained permission checks in the hot path of millions of decisions a day, audit and access trails, and the constraints of regulated financial services. What You'll Do - Design, build, and operate core authorization primitives such as roles, policies, and fine-grained permission checks (RBAC/ReBAC), along with the authentication and SSO/OAuth/OIDC flows that sit on top of them. - Guarantee strict multi-tenant isolation so every customer's data and decisions stay fully segregated. - Keep fine-grained permission checks fast in the hot path of millions of automated decisions per day, where both latency and correctness are non-negotiable. - Build audit and access-trail capabilities that give customers and auditors a verifiable record of who accessed what, when, and why. - Design for regulated-industry constraints (financial services, SOC 2 / ISO 27001, data residency), where access control is a compliance requirement, not just a feature. - Leverage leading edge AI tools (e.g. Claude) on a daily basis to build industry-shaping AI-native capabilities. Requirement: - 5+ years of professional full-stack experience building backend services in Python (FastAPI or equivalent), including REST APIs and databases - Strong proficiency with React and TypeScript. - Experience building access control systems, with exposure to authentication and authorization concepts (RBAC, SSO, OAuth/OIDC, multi-tenant permissions). - Strong engineering fundamentals with a passion for simplicity and precision. - Experience integrating into AWS or similar cloud providers. - Fluency in English, both written and spoken, is essential as we operate in a remote environment requiring clear and effective communication. Nice, but not required - Deeper backend specialization (async patterns, performance tuning, data modeling). - DynamoDB or Postgres, SQLAlchemy. - Hands-on experience designing authorization systems at scale: fine-grained / ReBAC models, policy engines (OpenFGA or Zanzibar-style systems, OPA), or multi-tenant RBAC for enterprise SaaS. - Prior ops or on-call experience. Our Offer - Work with colleagues that lift you up, challenge you, celebrate you and help you grow. We come from many different backgrounds, but what we have in common is the desire to operate at the very top of our fields. If you are similarly capable, caring, and driven, you'll find yourself at home here. - Experience a truly flat hierarchy and communicate directly with founding team members. Having an opinion and voicing your ideas is not only welcome but encouraged, especially when they challenge the status quo. - Learn from experienced mentors and achieve tremendous personal and professional growth. Get to know and leverage our network of leading tech investors and advisors around the globe. - Receiv
