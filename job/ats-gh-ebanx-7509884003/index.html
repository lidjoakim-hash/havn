We are building a Talent Pool for a future Business Development Specialist role based in the UK, aiming to keep strong candidates mapped for upcoming opportunities. In this position, you would support EBANX’s global expansion by acquiring new business and forming strategic partnerships in key regions. As a front-runner of our expansion, you would onboard new customers, strengthen relationships, and reinforce our presence among digital clients.
