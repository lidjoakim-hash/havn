Your New Role  We are looking for an experienced and driven Interim Senior Commercial Manager to join our client, a leading international organisation, during an important transition period. In this role, you will lead an international Marketing & Product team, providing clear direction, setting priorities and ensuring business continuity. You will play a key role in driving the commercial agenda, strengthening customer value and identifying opportunities for sustainable growth and profitability. You will work closely with senior management and cross-functional stakeholders across different markets, translating business insights into actionable strategies and leading critical initiatives from planning through implementation. Key responsibilities Lead and develop an international Marketing & Product team, establishing clear goals, priorities and expectations. Drive commercial strategies and initiatives focused on customer value, business growth and profitability. Take ownership of strategic, cross-functional projects, ensuring successful execution and measurable results. Analyze customer needs, market trends and business performance to identify opportunities and support commercial decisions. Collaborate with senior management and key stakeholders across functions and international markets. Provide strong leadership during organizational change, ensuring team engagement, stability and continuity. Balance long-term strategic objectives with operational priorities to maintain momentum and deliver business results. Company Presentation Our client is committed to a fossil-free future, bringing together diverse talents to drive real change. Employees apply their expertise, grow, and contribute to solutions that truly matter – a workplace where curiosity, creativity, and passion are highly valued. Good to Know Scope of employment: Full-time, 100% Type of contract: Fixed-term employment as a consultant via JobBusters. Working model: Hybrid, 3 days onsite and 2 days remote Start date: As soon as possible End date: 2027-06-30, with the possibility of extension In your application: Please ensure that your CV clearly demonstrates how you meet the qualifications required by the client. To succeed in this role, you will need:  A bachelor’s or master’s degree in business, Economics, Marketing, Strategy or a related field, or equivalent senior commercial experience. Extensive experience in senior commercial leadership roles within complex, international organizations. A proven track record in commercial strategy, marketing and customer-driven business growth. Solid people management experience, including leading and motivating teams through change or transition. Strong project and programme management skills, with experience successfully delivering complex, cross-functional initiatives. The ability to navigate confidently between executive-level strategic discussions and hands-on implementation. Excellent written and spoken English. Meritorious: Experience working across European markets.
