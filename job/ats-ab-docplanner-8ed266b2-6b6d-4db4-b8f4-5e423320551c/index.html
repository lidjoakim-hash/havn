Job Description Docplanner's Paid Marketing team - a B2B function focused on acquiring doctors and clinics - is looking for a Senior Specialist in Creative Strategy & Performance to own the full creative lifecycle for paid media: from strategy and briefing, through asset production management, to performance intelligence and testing. This is a hybrid strategist/operator role - half creative thinking, half process and data discipline. You'll be the bridge between performance data and creative output - turning what's working (and what isn't) across our nine markets into sharper briefs, faster production cycles, and a continuously improving testing roadmap. We are looking for a person with a Performance Marketing background that has hands-on experience in campaigns management and targets delivery. If you are a performance marketer who loves testing and can turn insights into repeatable creative performance, read on! How will you make an impact? Creative Strategy - Define the creative strategy and testing roadmap for paid campaigns across all markets and ad platforms, aligned with performance goals - Translate campaign objectives and audience insights into clear, actionable briefs - Partner with local market teams to adapt global creative direction to local nuances - language, culture, and regulatory requirements Asset Preparation & Coordination - Own the end-to-end production workflow: brief - content and design coordination - review - localization - trafficking - Coordinate with in-house designers and freelancers to keep timelines predictable - Build and maintain a repeatable process - briefing templates, naming conventions, approval workflows - to reduce bottlenecks and rework Creative Intelligence & Testing - Run structured A/B and multivariate tests across Meta and other paid channels, defining hypotheses, success metrics, and test cadence - Build creative performance reporting (Meta Ads, Tableau, Amplitude) that identifies which elements - format, hook, messaging, visual style - drive results - Maintain a creative intelligence repository documenting what's worked, by market and format, to inform future briefs Tracking & Measurement - Ensure creative-level tracking is implemented and tagged correctly, in partnership with the tracking/tech team, so performance can be attributed accurately - Monitor creative fatigue and flag refresh cycles proactively, before performance declines Team Flexibility - Step into hands-on campaign management when needed - covering colleagues' absences or supporting new market initiatives. What We're Looking For - 4+ years in performance marketing with a proven track record in creative strategy, or a media buying background with hands-on experience in paid social creative (Meta Ads required; TikTok, Google, LinkedIn, or Bing a plus) - Hands-on experience in paid media performance, including campaign set-up and management - able to step into hands-on campaign management when team capacity is stretched - Demonstrated experience managing creative production across multiple stakeholders -
