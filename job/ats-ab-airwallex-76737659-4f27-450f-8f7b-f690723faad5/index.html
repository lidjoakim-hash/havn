ABOUT AIRWALLEX Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own. We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Working across Legal, Product, Operations, Sales, Marketing and other teams, you’ll lead strategic commercial and product integrations that support user adoption and revenue growth. - Build and maintain trusted relationships with external executives and decision-makers, as well as internal cross-functional teams. - Lead product integration scoping, and structure and negotiate partnership agreements and value exchanges that work for both sides. - Manage risk and secure cross-functional buy-in for partnership agreements and product integrations. - Shape a range of deal types and own partner relationships across verticals, structures and deal stages, from pre-deal strategy through implementation and ongoing partner management. - Measure partnership return on investment, understand performance metrics and identify optimisation levers.
