Continuous Improvement Manager

Yara Norge As Glomfjord Fabrikker · Glomfjord, Norway

Lønn ikke oppgittHeltidvia NAVFrist 16.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Are you passionate about continuous improvement and creating lasting results? As our Continuous Improvement Manager, you will play a key role in strengthening our improvement culture, driving change and creating measurable impact across Yara Glomfjord. Hva vi ser etter • Master's or bachelor's degree, preferably in process engineering, another relevant engineering discipline, economics or finance. Relevant experience can compensate for formal education. • Minimum five years of operational experience from a production environment. Experience from the process or chemical industry is an advantage. • Leadership experience and/or documented experience leading cross-functional improvement and change processes. • Good understanding of Lean, root cause solving tools, continuous improvement, structured problem solving and benefits realisation. • Strong analytical skills and the ability to turn data and improvement opportunities into practical action. • Excellent communication and facilitation skills, with the ability to train and develop groups and individuals. • Good oral and written communication skills in Norwegian or another Scandinavian language, as well as English Arbeidsoppgaver • Develop a shared understanding of the need for change and continuous improvement across the organisation. • Facilitation of Root cause problem solving (RCPS), execution of A3 methodology and ensure that the local organisation has the capability to execute investigations with high quality. • Facilitate improvement initiatives that deliver measurable impact on safety, quality, delivery reliability, costs and productivity • Ensure that high-quality actions are identified, action tracking and follow-up • Build competence and change capacity through training, coaching and practical support to improvement teams. • Contribute to the prioritisation and execution of the plant's overall improvement portfolio in close cooperation with the leadership team. Develop local strategy for implementation. • Coordinate the plant's energy management work and contribute to systematic follow-up of energy performance and improvement actions. • Contribute to experience sharing and collaboration with relevant expert communities in Yara. Vi tilbyr • An open and inclusive working environment based on our four values: Ambitious, Curious, Collaborative, and Accountable • Competitive salary and employment conditions • Opportunities for both professional and personal development, and the chance to live and work in one of Norway's most scenic regions!
