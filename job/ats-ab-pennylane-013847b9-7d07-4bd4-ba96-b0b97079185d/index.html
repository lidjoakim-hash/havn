WHY THIS ROLE MATTERS TO OUR MISSION Pennylane is scaling fast, and so is our ambition for Talent Acquisition. We're opening a new permanent Tech Recruiter role to sustain our growth in 2026 & beyond. Our vision is clear: be recognized as strategic partners of the Engineering team, not just a delivery function. We hire the best Engineering profiles, at the right time, all across Europe, with a candidate and manager experience that's second to none, powered by data and augmented by AI. How you'll contribute as a Tech Recruiter You'll join Pennylane's Tech, Data and Security Talent Acquisition team (a growing team of 5 recruiters, led by Rémi Gourmel) and own recruitment across the Engineering scope all across Europe (the roles include Backend, Frontend, Fullstack, Devops engineers, Engineering Managers, Senior Engineering Managers). In 2025, this team recruited 191 talent, and we’re on track for 2026 as well. This is an individual contributor role with real ownership of your perimeter, and room to lead structuring projects for the team. You won't just fill roles: you'll act as a trusted advisor to your hiring managers, bring market intelligence and data to influence decisions, and help shape how we recruit tomorrow. MISSIONS - Own full-cycle recruitment on your Engineering scope, with the support of your manager and team. - Be a strategic partner & advisor to hiring managers: challenge, anticipate, alert, and recommend beyond simple pipeline reporting. - Produce data & predictive reporting (forecasts, early warnings, KPIs) that influence hiring decisions (comp, location, drop-out risk…). - Leverage AI in your recruitment process (sourcing, screening, automation of Ashby stages) and contribute to our shared AI/agent library. - Contribute to team projects & rituals: referral program, employer branding, Process, Candidate experience, D&I, AI.. - Build and nurture talent pools proactively on your strategic roles. - Deliver an outstanding candidate experience end to end. - Challenge and improve our processes, tools and best practices in a continuous-improvement mindset. Our Recruiting stack: Ashby, HireSweet, Dust, Notion AI, LinkedIn Recruiter, Google Suite WHAT TO EXPECT FROM YOUR LIFE AT PENNYLANE Within 1 month - Get onboarded. - Learn your scope, meet your team, hiring managers and the wider Pennylane. - Start recruiting on key positions. - AI : Discover our AI stack, shared agent library and how the team uses AI day to day. - Projects : Understand our ongoing team projects and rituals, and identify where you could contribute. Within 3 months - Build and run pipelines on your roles, and deliver. - Design effective sourcing strategies for strategic positions. - Start bringing data points and recommendations to your hiring managers. - AI : Start using existing agents/automations in your daily workflow and automate the first stages of your Ashby process. - Projects : Contribute to an existing team project and bring ideas to improve our processes. Within 6 months - Recruit top talent, above market standards. - Act as a full advisor to your Business unit: market reality, business stakes, org topics. - AI : Build or adapt your own agent/automation, measure its impact on a key KPI (TTH, conversion, time saved…) and share it back to the team. - Projects — Actively contribute to a structuring project (process, candidate experience, data, D&I, employer branding…) end to end.
