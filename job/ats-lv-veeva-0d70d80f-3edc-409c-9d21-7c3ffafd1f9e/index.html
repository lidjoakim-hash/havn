Veeva Systems is a mission-driven organization and pioneer in industry cloud, helping life sciences companies bring therapies to patients faster. As one of the fastest-growing SaaS companies in history, we surpassed $3B in revenue in our last fiscal year with extensive growth potential ahead.   At the heart of Veeva are our values: Do the Right Thing, Customer Success, Employee Success, and Speed. We're not just any public company – we made history in 2021 by becoming a public benefit corporation (PBC), legally bound to balancing the interests of customers, employees, society, and investors.   As a Work Anywhere company, we support your flexibility to work from home or in the office, so you can thrive in your ideal environment.   Join us in transforming the life sciences industry, committed to making a positive impact on its customers, employees, and communities. The Role The Link and OpenData vision is to become the global leader in deep and reference data for life sciences. The core of our products is manually curated data captured using rule-based web research. We create high-quality data through efficient processes.   As a Process Expert in Data Operations, you will improve processes across all operations teams. You will support the teams in simplifying and increasing the resilience of processes. You will be hands-on in improving our tools. You will improve interfaces between teams. You will ensure your improvements are carried out throughout the organization. You will work closely with the Director of Data Operations.   This role will enable you to gain a broad and deep understanding of our data operations and, by that, learn about the different Link and OpenData products. You will work on a broad range of projects with many different stakeholders.   To thrive in your role, you need to be strong in conceptual thinking while being hands-on. You are a fast learner, detail-oriented, have a growth mindset, and make decisions based on facts and KPIs. You communicate effectively with stakeholders and can deliver high-quality results within a tough timeline. You are comfortable working remotely in an international environment.
