ABOUT SUPABASE Supabase is the Postgres development platform, built by developers for developers. We provide a complete backend solution including Database, Auth, Storage, Edge Functions, Realtime, and Vector Search. All services are deeply integrated and designed for growth. About the Role We're hiring a Product Manager for Supabase Realtime https://supabase.com/docs/guides/realtime. You'll own the surface area developers use to build live, collaborative and event-driven apps on Supabase: Broadcast https://supabase.com/docs/guides/realtime/broadcast, Presence https://supabase.com/docs/guides/realtime/presence, Postgres Changes https://supabase.com/docs/guides/realtime/postgres-changes, and the capabilities we build next. You'll work closely with the Realtime Engineering team and with partners across Product Management, Design and GTM. This role is ideal for someone who's excited about building tools for millions of developers and who has a passion for the types of user experiences unlocked by simple, scalable real-time channels. You should have a nuanced understanding of full-stack application development, including existing and emerging real-time and sync patterns. WHAT YOU’LL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR - You'll collaborate with customers of all sizes and expertise levels: from an indie developer adding live updates to a side project, to startups building collaborative SaaS, to enterprise teams streaming live data to dashboards. - You'll own the problem statements and solution rationale behind the Realtime roadmap. This means working with Engineering and Design to shape how Supabase crafts the developer experience for the problems you’ve prioritized solving. - You'll shape how Realtime evolves to enable future application experiences, including its role in AI apps and alongside other Supabase products. - You'll own Realtime packaging and pricing, working with Pricing, Finance and Billing. - You'll use data to understand how Supabase developers are using Realtime features: where they succeed, where they get stuck, and what to tackle first. - You'll keep Engineering, Design and GTM aligned. You'll own the plan of record for what's coming, why, and in what order. YOU MIGHT BE A GOOD FIT IF YOU MUST HAVE - 5+ years of Product Management experience on developer tools, or are an ex-founder with equivalent depth. You're comfortable commenting on an architecture doc, following a design discussion, and advocating for the user when trade-offs are required. - Have owned an app development platform, feature or framework where real-time was core to the developer experience. - Get your own answers from data. You know what you care about in terms of product instrumentation and are comfortable querying and cleaning your own findings. - Are comfortable with Postgres and familiar with logical replication. - Work async by default. Supabase is fully remote. You make decisions in writing and move work forward without meetings. STRONG SIGNAL - You have an existing audience among developers, through social media, open source or developer advocacy. - Worked on a sync engine, local-first or live-query product. - Worked with gaming, AR or collaborative-tool customers. - Shipped something using Supabase. WHAT WE OFFER - Fully Remote We hire globally. We believe you can do your best work from anywhere. There are no Supabase offices, but we provide a WeWork membership or co-working allowance you can use anywhere in the world. - ESOP Every team member receives ESOP (equity ownership) in the company. We want everyone to share in the upside of what we’re building together. - Tech Allowance Use this budget to set up your ideal work environment—laptop, monitor, headphones, or whatever helps you do your best work. - Health Benefits Supabase covers 100% of health insurance for employees and 80% for dependents, wherever you are.
