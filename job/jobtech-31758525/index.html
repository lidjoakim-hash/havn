Senior Project & Change Lead AI Implementation

VX Fiber Service Delivery AB · Umeå, Sweden

💰 ca. 520 000–640 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 20.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Have you led an AI initiative from idea to implementation and helped teams adopt it in their day-to-day work? VX Fiber is looking for a senior, hands-on lead who combines experience implementing AI with strong project and change management skills. VX Fiber is the operating system for operators. For more than 20 years, we have built and run the platform behind open access fiber networks. In this assignment, you will work with our teams to assess and implement selected AI initiatives and help shape how they are adopted across the business. This is a temporary assignment offered as fixed-term employment or on a consultancy basis, with the possibility of extension. You will work on site in Umeå at least four days per week, alongside the teams involved. The assignment You will lead the project and change work for selected AI initiatives, from assessing their potential and making recommendations to leading delivery and adoption for initiatives approved by leadership. You will work closely with developers, operational teams and leaders to test assumptions, involve the people whose work will change and maintain business continuity through implementation. What you will do Assess initiatives and prepare recommendations. Bring together technical and business specialists to evaluate feasibility, expected benefits, costs and risks. Develop business cases and recommend which initiatives to pursue and how to phase implementation. Lead delivery. Turn approved initiatives into practical plans with clear milestones, owners and dependencies. Coordinate work across teams, resolve issues and flag decisions that need leadership input. Involve teams and support adoption. Give the people affected a role in testing solutions and shaping how they will be used. Work with managers and teams to identify changes to skills, ways of working and responsibilities. Follow up on outcomes. Agree how to measure success, track adoption and benefits, and adjust implementation based on what teams learn. What you bring Experience leading AI initiatives through implementation and supporting their adoption in day-to-day work. Senior project and change leadership experience in a software development or technology environment, with a track record of leading across teams through influence. The technical fluency to engage credibly with developers, challenge assumptions and bring in the right specialists. Experience building business cases and turning technical and operational findings into clear recommendations. A hands-on approach and the ability to build trust and lead constructive discussions when teams have different perspectives. ﻿Interested? Tell us about an AI initiative you helped implement, what you personally led, how you involved the people affected and what changed as a result. Apply between 19 November 2026 and 6 December 2026 23:59 (Europe/Stockholm) VX Fiber is a Swedish technology company with over 20 years of global experience in fiber-to-the-home (FTTH) solutions and open-access networks. Our mission is to deliver pioneering fiber network solutions that empower individuals, businesses, and communities to achieve digital freedom. Embracing open access and innovation, we are strengthening our position as an industry leader through collaboration, world-class performance, and digital transformation.
