If you seek to make a difference on a global scale, working with next-gen technologies and the sharpest collaborative teams, then we could be a perfect match.  We shape the world we want to live in! Volvo Energy is a young company in the Volvo Group which supports customers in the electrical energy transition. Volvo Energy develops and commercializes competitive Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) and provides customers with reliable solutions which optimize energy costs, operational resilience, and renewable energy integration. We are now looking for a new colleague to join our team at Volvo Energy as Lead BESS Electrical Design Engineer. What are we looking for in this specific role? In this role, we are looking for an experienced electrical design engineer passionate about electrification and energy transition. You will play a key role in developing electrical design solutions for BESS turnkey projects and bringing our future products and offers to market. You are a structured and solution-oriented team player who works effectively with customers, grid owners, suppliers, consultants, contractors, and cross-functional project teams to deliver safe, compliant, and buildable solutions.   Responsibilities include:•    Produce and maintain the complete BESS site design package in AutoCAD, including single-line diagrams, site layouts, cable sizing and routing, cable schedules, and equipment lists.•    Define the protection philosophy and settings and coordinate protection interfaces across the BESS, site, and grid connection.•    Design earthing and lightning protection systems.•    Define the metering strategy, including metering points, equipment, and grid owner and revenue-metering requirements.•    Support in the plant control and communication strategy covering interfaces between PCS, EMS/PPC, SCADA, grid operator, and aggregator.•    Define the civil works requirements and subcontractor scope.•    Prepare the technical content of procurement packages and review supplier documentation and designs for compliance.•    Act as BAS-P for projects where assigned.•    Support site activities and commissioning when required and approve or produce the final as-built documentation.•    Develop reusable design standards and validated solutions to improve quality and efficiency across future projects. Who are you? Expected qualifications: •    Degree in Electrical Engineering, or equivalent knowledge gained through professional experience.•    Experience with Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS).•    Experience in electrical design for power, energy, industrial, or utility-scale projects.•    Experience with protection systems, grid connection, controls, metering, and electrical safety.•    Experience producing electrical design documentation in AutoCAD or similar.•    Understanding of HV, MV, and LV systems, earthing, lightning protection, and applicable compliance requirements.•    Self-motivated, structured, and solution-oriented, with strong communication skills and the ability to collaborate effectively with customers, suppliers, contractors, consultants, and internal teams.   Meriting:•    Experience acting as BAS-P.
