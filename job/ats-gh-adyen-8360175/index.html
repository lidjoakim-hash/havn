The Role We’re looking for a Strategy & Program Manager to help shape the future of Adyen’s Global Operations. You’ll sit within the Strategy & Execution team and work across our core operational domains: Support, Professional Services, and Merchant Operations. This role is for a strategic operator who connects commercial ambition, technical innovation, and operational delivery. You won’t be tracking tasks. You’ll be taking on the hardest, least-defined problems in the organization and turning them into structures that work at scale. Your focus will shift as priorities shift, so expect a broad scope and an environment that keeps changing. Your mission is to make sure that as Adyen grows, our operational engine becomes more powerful, effective, and agile. What you’ll do • Solve high-complexity, systemic challenges: Take on high-stakes, ambiguous projects where no playbook exists. You’ll create the structure, define the “why,” and drive the “how,” taking undefined concepts from zero to scalable reality. • Design for the end-to-end customer lifecycle: Make sure a commercial idea translates into seamless Merchant Ops onboarding, a smooth Professional Services implementation, and world-class Support. You design for the customer experience and for the effectiveness of our internal teams. • Unblock the organization: Identify process debt and technical roadblocks that slow us down, and replace them with scalable, automated, or simplified workflows. • Connect global pillars: Act as the link between Adyen’s global product pillars and our operational domains, so our operations are ready before new products launch. • Steer with data: Use performance metrics as a roadmap for action. Spot friction early and use data-led diagnostics to guide the organization toward better ways of working. • Build AI into how we work: Go beyond adopting third-party tools. Re-engineer our processes, tooling, and ways of working so we scale through intelligence rather than complexity. Who you are • A strategic operator with 5+ years of experience in strategy consulting, tech strategy, business operations, program management, or operations in a high-growth environment.
