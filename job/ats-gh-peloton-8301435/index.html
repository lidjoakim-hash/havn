ABOUT THE ROLE The Associate Producers, Studio cover and support every modality and every instructor working at PSL. Associate Producers, Studio primarily work in the studio at the point of production. They work in teams in a fast moving environment. The key skills they need to demonstrate include exemplary teamwork, clear communication and the ability to take decisions based upon fast changing, limited or sometimes conflicting information. Associate Producers, Studio ensure Instructors are supported in Studio, making sure there is a culture of trust, collaboration and transparency in the studios. Associate Producers - Studio ensure any key development feedback is shared with the Senior Producer - Studio and Instructor Strategy so it can be fed back to help Instructors. Associate Producers, Studio ensure key protocols are understood and followed, collaborating with the Senior Coordinator, Studio to streamline protocols for Senior PAs and PAs where necessary.
