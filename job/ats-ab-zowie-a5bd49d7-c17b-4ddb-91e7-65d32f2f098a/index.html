About Zowie At Zowie, we build AI agents that handle millions of real conversations every month, for brands like InPost, Empik, Decathlon, and Allianz. They track packages, sort out claims, and actually resolve the issues people bring them. We’re changing how people interact with brands. At scale. About the role Enterprise brands are starting to let AI agents talk to their customers. Making those agents work well (understand their problems, solve them, sound natural and offer a seamless experience) is becoming a discipline of its own. At Zowie, AI Deployment Strategists own it - they deliver the agents our clients rely on. If you love playing around with AI, this is where you get to build AI agents for real. Every agent you ship ends up talking to thousands of real people: someone chasing a refund, moving a delivery, checking their insurance cover late at night. Each client comes with a goal and a puzzle made of their systems, processes and customers. You work out the approach, build the agent, test it with the client, and keep improving it once it’s live. Our past tutorials http://docs.zowie.ai/docs/onboarding-tutorials-to-ai-agent can give you a general idea of what you can build on the platform. Nowadays most of that is done by your own AI agents, connected to our platform through MCP. You build AI agents (with AI agents), help shape the tooling that makes it possible, and become an expert in real-world AI adoption. Intrigued? Reach out and let’s chat! What you’ll do - Bridge business needs and technology by diving into customer processes, identifying improvements, and translating them into technical solutions. - Handle advanced configurations, integrations with customer systems (e.g., ERPs, e-commerce platforms, CRMs), and custom implementations. - Work hand-in-hand with AI - both on production agents, and in your own workflow where you automate repeatable processes, and set up tooling. - Act as a trusted advisor and ambassador, building strong relationships with champions and stakeholders. - Collaborate with internal teams as a customer advocate and influence product roadmaps. Ideally, you’ll bring with you - 5+ years of experience working with B2B customers, including 3+ years of experience within solutions consulting, technical account management, or complex product delivery (ideally with enterprise clients). - Ability to own engagements end-to-end - run workshops, turn requirements into solutions, and drive them to delivery. - Solid technical grounding - you understand how APIs and integrations (REST, GraphQL) work, and you get up to speed on complex software fast. - AI-native way of working - LLMs are already built into your daily workflow. - Business acumen - you understand the client’s business well enough to challenge what they ask for. - Track record of building relationships that clients value. - Strong communication skills in Polish and English, with the ability to explain complex technical concepts simply. It’ll be awesome if you have - Hands-on experience in building AI solutions, including prompt engineering. - A coaching mindset - you enjoy teaching and enabling people to use technology on their own. AI-native culture Our team embraces AI as a fundamental part of how we work - not just for speed, but for creativity, documentation, and scale. We’re looking for people who are excited to develop within this space and contribute to such culture by bringing ideas to the table, and continuously evolving their approach to leverage emerging tools.
