ABOUT ELEVENLABS ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology. We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $22B - multiples of 11, always. We have expanded from voice into three main platforms: - ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale. - ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages. - ElevenAPI gives developers access to our leading AI audio foundational models. Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you. WHAT WE OFFER - Innovative culture: You’ll be part of a generational opportunity to define the trajectory of AI, surrounded by a team pushing the boundaries of what’s possible. - Growth paths: Joining ElevenLabs means joining a dynamic team with countless opportunities to drive impact - beyond your immediate role and responsibilities. - Learning & development: ElevenLabs proactively supports professional development through an annual discretionary stipend. - Social travel: We also provide an annual discretionary stipend to meet up with colleagues each year, however you choose. - Annual team offsite: Each year, we bring your entire team together in a new location - past offsites have included Croatia, Italy and Cancún - Co-working: If you’re not located near one of our main hubs, we offer a monthly co-working stipend. ABOUT THE ROLE - Maintaining accounting records in accordance with the Polish Accounting Act and the company’s accounting policies (local statutory accounting alongside primary reporting under US GAAP). - Working with an external partner to implement and launch a new NetSuite module tailored to Polish regulatory requirements. - Reporting issues and errors during and after implementation. - Recording and reviewing financial documents (invoices, bank statements and journal entry supporting documents) for accuracy, completeness and compliance with formal requirements. - Reconciling account balances, maintaining the fixed asset register, and preparing balance sheets and financial statements. - Preparing and reviewing VAT registers and tax returns (JPK_V7, CIT and other mandatory reports). - Participating in monthly and annual closes and preparing the company’s financial statements. - Liaising with external authorities and preparing documentation, including contact with tax offices, the Social Insurance Institution (ZUS) and Statistics Poland (GUS), as well as supporting audits. - Monitoring changes in tax and accounting regulations and implementing the necessary updates to processes and the system. - Contributing to improvements in accounting processes.
