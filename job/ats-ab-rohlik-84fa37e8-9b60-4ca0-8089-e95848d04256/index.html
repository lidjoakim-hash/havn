Founded in 2014 in the Czech Republic, Rohlik is the European leader of e-grocery in Central Europe. Already active in the Czech Republic (Rohlik.cz http://Rohlik.cz), Hungary (Kifli.hu http://Kifli.hu), Austria (Gurkerl.at http://Gurkerl.at) and Germany (Knuspr.de http://Knuspr.de). By owning its end-to-end operations, including all technology in-house, Rohlik provides a superior customer experience and the freshest food from local farmers and artisans, as well as a broad supermarket selection.   The Operations team ensures every order is received, picked, packed and dispatched flawlessly. We operate high-throughput fulfillment centers, setting the standard for efficiency, workplace safety and customer satisfaction. Our mission is to ensure smooth operations, continuous improvement, and a high-performance culture in every site.   The Area Manager - Lift position is an elite, high-visibility entry point into our Talent Leadership Accelerator. This role is explicitly reserved for top-tier graduates who already demonstrate advanced level of analytical maturity and leadership drive today. As a "Lift" participant, you will spend your first 12 months mastering core operational excellence at a single site, eliminating systemic inefficiencies, and preparing to scale into country-wide, cross-functional leadership. The ultimate goal of this position is a fast-track promotion to Operations Manager within 12 months, tracking toward Senior Leadership within 24–36 months.     WHY IS THIS ROLE EXCITING - Unparalleled career velocity: A compressed leadership path designed to turn a traditional 6 to 8 year progression into Senior Operations Management into 24-36 months. - Direct senior mentorship: You will be formally paired with a Senior Leader (Fulfilment Centre Manager or Director) for monthly strategic mentoring and shadow senior leaders to see how high-velocity sites are run. - Cross-border peer network: Collaborate with a select cohort of high-potential operational leaders across the Czech Republic and the DACH region. - Real ownership from day one: You won't just maintain existing workflows, you will lead a site-level step-change project to eliminate systemic defects and see your ideas implemented without waiting for slow corporate approvals. - Direct business impact: Your decisions on the floor directly influence cost-per-order, delivery reliability, and the experience of thousands of customers every day.     WHAT YOU’LL DO - Own daily shift execution: Drive operational performance across shifts, ensuring strict compliance with safety, quality, delivery windows, and cost-per-order targets. - Lead and inspire large teams: Manage and motivate front-line operations associates and team leaders across different shifts, building a culture of high engagement and accountability. - Deliver a site-level step-change project: Identify root causes of complex fulfilment centre inefficiencies and execute data-driven improvements to eliminate them. - Optimise warehouse workflows: Use rigorous metrics tracking to contribute to labour planning, workflow design, and throughput initiatives. - Partner across functions: Work closely with Safety, Operational Excellence, HR, IT, Maintenance, and Finance to solve operational bottlenecks. - Champion workplace safety: Role-model safety standards on the floor, conduct audits, and lead proactive interventions. - Contribute to business reviews: Partner with site leadership in preparing weekly business reviews while learning how comprehensive site performance is managed.     WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR -
