Company description: Epiroc is a leading productivity partner for the mining, infrastructure and natural resources industries. Withcutting-edge technology, Epiroc develops and produces innovative drill rigs, rock excavation and constructionequipment, and provides world-class service and consumables. The company was founded in Stockholm,Sweden, and has passionate people supporting and collaborating with customers in more than 150 countries.Learn more at www.epirocgroup.com. Job description: Are you an experienced finance professional who wants to work close to senior management, gain broad exposure across a global organization and take the next step in your development? It all starts with people. People like you. Join our team We are now looking for a Divisional Controller to join the Drilling tools division (RGD) Finance team. This is a key role supporting the VP Controlling & Finance and the divisional finance organization, combining financial control, consolidation, performance management and business analysis. The role provides broad exposure across RGD’s global operations, with the opportunity to work with Customer Centers, Product Companies, Distribution Centers and other parts of Epiroc. You will join an experienced finance team and collaborate closely with Business Lines, controllers and finance colleagues across regions. Your mission As Divisional Controller, you will have a central role in ensuring high-quality financial reporting and supporting divisional performance. The role combines financial controlling with business controlling, from closing and reporting to performance analysis and forecasting. The role requires you to move between financial details and the bigger picture, and to help management understand what is behind the numbers. Your main responsibilities will include: Coordinate and participate in divisional monthly, quarterly and annual closing and review processes, including adjustments and eliminations, and ensure consistency across P&L, balance sheet and KPIs. Analyze reporting from operational units and ensure high-quality, timely financial information. Analyze divisional performance and identify key drivers behind deviations from plan, forecast and prior years. Coordinate forecasting and planning across the division, collaborating with controllers across regions and operational units to improve forecast quality. Partner closely with the VP Controlling & Finance and support management with analysis and financial insights. Support strategic initiatives, business cases, investments and profitability improvement programs, and participate in cross-functional projects. Contribute to developing reporting, dashboards and analytical tools and identify opportunities to simplify and automate ways of working.     Profile description:Your profile You are curious and analytical, with an eye for detail and the ability to step back and see the bigger picture. You enjoy collaborating across functions and cultures, are comfortable sharing your perspective and listening to others, and take responsibility for following up on what has been agreed. We believe that you have a genuine interest in how automation and AI can help us work smarter in finance. You have a relevant university degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics or a related field, and you will likely bring around 5–10 years of experience from financial controlling, business controlling, FP&A, business finance or a similar area. We believe that you bring: Experience working in a global or matrix organization. Strong Excel skills and experience with ERP and reporting systems. Experience using Power BI or similar data visualization and reporting tools, with the ability to interpret and analyze the information they provide. Good English communication skills, both written and spoken. Experience from controlling within production, supply chain or distribution and from consolidation systems such as Tagetik or similar is an advantag
