QUALITY ASSURANCE What We Do The Quality Assurance team owns the testing process, risk management, and quality evaluation of all products and functions across Epic. Our team works closely with developers and production to ensure the best possible user experience for our community and customers. What You'll Do Epic Games is looking for a full-time Senior Tester II for our testing group in Brazil. The person in this role will own the testing approach for a specialized domain within a major Epic product, and will be expected to make that domain measurably faster and more reliable to test over time through better methods, tooling, and use of data. You will work embedded with the development team, developing and executing tests with or without QA Analyst guidance, and proactively identifying problems beyond the day-to-day in order to solve them at the root rather than one case at a time. The Senior Tester II is also a technical reference point for the wider test team, mentoring testers and raising the quality bar through example. If you are interested in a fast-paced environment, capable of navigating interpersonal quandaries, and desire to work on a world-class QA team, then we would love to talk to you. In this role, you will • Own the test approach for your area, developing and executing tests independently, with or without QA Analyst guidance • Design and introduce sustainable testing methods,
