Place of work: Boden What you’ll do Right now, we are looking for a Field Engineering Manager, BoP & WTP for our Engineering team. In this role you will report to our Head of Engineering and sit within our Project Delivery function. The role is based full-time at our construction site in Boden. As Field Engineering Manager, you will be the key technical link between design engineering and construction for our Balance of Plant and Water Treatment Plant areas. You will work closely with EPCM partners, contractors, OEMs, and internal teams to resolve technical and constructability issues quickly, ensure field changes are properly managed, and keep construction moving without compromising safety, quality, cost, or schedule. Specific to this role, the day-to-day tasks will include the following but as we are a growing company with very little silos between teams, other tasks might be included as well. Act as the primary on-site engineering contact for BoP and WTP, connecting design teams with construction and contractors Review drawings, specifications, vendor documentation, and technical information to identify and resolve constructability issues Manage STQs, RFIs, Design Change Requests, and Field Change Requests, ensuring timely resolution and clear communication Lead constructability reviews together with site supervision, contractors, and design engineers Coordinate technical matters with EPCM partners, OEMs, Procurement, QA/QC, HSE, and Project Controls Manage technical deviations, field changes, Site Instructions, redlines, and as-built documentation Support construction planning and look-ahead activities by identifying engineering-related risks to cost and schedule Lead and coordinate site-based field and discipline engineers across civil, structural, mechanical, piping, electrical, and I&C disciplines What you’ll bring You’re curious and believe that real progress happens when we work together. You’re not afraid to challenge old ways of thinking and you treat others with respect while doing it. At Stegra, we value people who take initiative, build trust, and help create a workplace where everyone can do their best. For this role, we are looking for someone who combines strong design engineering experience with a practical understanding of how construction works in the field. You should be comfortable challenging drawings and specifications, solving technical problems on site, and working across organizational boundaries to keep execution moving.
