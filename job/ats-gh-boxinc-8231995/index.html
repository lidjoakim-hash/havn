Box is headquartered in Redwood City, CA, with offices across the United States, Europe, and Asia. By joining Box, you will have the unique opportunity to continue driving our platform forward. WHY BOX NEEDS YOU Box IT End User Services' primary role is to provide technical services and support to all employees world-wide.
