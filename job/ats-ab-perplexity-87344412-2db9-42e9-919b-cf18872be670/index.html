Research & Development Site Lead (London)

Perplexity · London, United Kingdom

Perplexity builds frontier intelligence systems for the world's most discerning users. We're rapidly scaling our technical team to meet this ambition, including with Perplexity London, our UK-based research & development hub. We're seeking an exceptional R&D leader to serve as Site Lead for Perplexity London. As our senior leader in the United Kingdom, you'll shape Perplexity's presence in one of the world's leading AI & technology hubs. You'll exercise functional responsibility over a major technical portfolio, along with organizational responsibility for Perplexity London at large. Joining Perplexity London as Site Lead represents an opportunity to build and scale a world-class R&D organization in a region known for technical excellence in AI. You'll enjoy autonomy and support from our cofounders (who each possess extensive research backgrounds) to make Perplexity London a leader in the UK AI ecosystem. You'll also help shape the growth of Perplexity Frontier Research https://research.perplexity.ai in London. This comes with the opportunity to define major parts of our technical agenda across multi-agent orchestration, reinforcement learning, continual learning, systems, and frontier evaluations. Strong research and/or engineering chops are essential, along with the ability to build strong culture, inspire exceptional team members, and collaborate closely with leaders and colleagues around the world.   RESPONSIBILITIES - Head up Perplexity London, taking responsibility for what the site delivers, how it scales and its standing in the UK. - Work directly with Perplexity's cofounders & leaders in other offices so London's work connects to company priorities. - Shape how the London office runs day to day including its culture and quality bar. - Keep a working grasp of the detail weighing in on designs and research plans - Be the face of Perplexity in the UK AI community building relationships with universities, labs and at events. - Define our R&D agenda in London across numerous areas of AI and other technical disciplines.   QUALIFICATIONS - Extensive background in research and engineering, with evidence of real-world impact via shipped systems, published papers, and/or open source. - Has built, scaled, and led technical teams accountable for ambitious roadmaps. - Well connected in the UK or European AI community across industry labs and universities. - Knows at least one of our research areas in depth and can judge the quality of work in the others. - Used to collaborating across time zones with teams in the US.
