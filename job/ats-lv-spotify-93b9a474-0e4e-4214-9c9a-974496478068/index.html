We have a great opportunity for an Artist & Label Partnerships Manager to come and join the band in Berlin. The Artist & Label Partnerships team is the face of Spotify to artists, managers, labels & distributors. As an Artist & Label Partnerships Manager your mission is to ensure that key artists, managers, and labels see Spotify as the #1 partner for artist development and monetization. In this role you will work with a set of core label partners and artists. This role has a strong focus on launching innovative partnerships and initiatives and you will drive activations that enable, educate, and unlock new opportunities for our partners and Spotify.  We seek a highly motivated partner manager based in Berlin with a strong partnerships, music marketing and music / entertainment / media background. This individual must be able to prioritize, multi-task, and influence in a fast paced environment.  This role is on a 15-month fixed-term contract. What You'll Do • Build and foster relations with key partners (artists, managers, labels, industry organizations) across Germany, Austria and Switzerland • Act as a representative of Spotify externally in the music industry and empower artists and labels with knowledge, insights, and tools they need to succeed on Spotify, acting as their advocate and champion. • Music Marketing and culture: Stay ahead of music trends and key moments to proactively identify and activate new music and content opportunities. • IRL events and music marketing: Proactively identify and pursue new growth opportunities in areas such as superfan engagement and live events together with cross-functional teams, work together to bring these initiatives to life. • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Work closely with Consumer Marketing, PR and other teams, acting as a representative of the artists & labels you work with. Who You Are • You have 2+ years of music industry experience, preferably in an artist & partner-facing role. • You have experience and knowledge working in a broad spectrum of culture with a focus on music and content. • You have expertise in the electronic music space (preferred) • You have a strong understanding of the digital music landscape and streaming platforms. • You are an excellent project manager with a track record in leading projects from end to end, including marketing campaigns, live events and cultural activations • You have experience in project managing offline to online activations, incl. Working on live events, content capture and digital extensions of those • You possess excellent negotiating, presentation and people skills • You are comfortable in very fast paced, driven work environments and can do well in a team that moves quickly • You are well organized, with the ability to plan and deliver on tight deadlines • You're a great teammate, adapt well to change, and have a positive attitude • You are fluent in German and English Where You'll Be • This role is based in Berlin, Germany • We offer you the flexibility to work where you work best!
