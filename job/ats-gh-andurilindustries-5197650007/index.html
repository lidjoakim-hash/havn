Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years. ABOUT THE TEAM The Hardware Platform Organization is Anduril's central hardware engineering org. We build the shared technologies, tools, and infrastructure that power hardware development across every Anduril division, from actuators and propulsion to batteries, avionics, rocket motors, and test operations. Our teams set engineering standards, run development manufacturing and test sites, and develop core technology products that ship across multiple product lines. ABOUT THE JOB We are looking for a Configuration Manager to join our team. This role owns configuration management and change control across critical Anduril hardware programs. You will define and maintain product baselines, drive engineering change processes, and serve as the focal point for configuration identification, status accounting, and audits in a fast-paced design, engineering, and manufacturing environment. WHAT YOU’LL DO • Own end-to-end configuration management for assigned programs, including configuration identification, change control, status accounting, and audit activities. • Define and maintain product baselines (requirements baseline, EBOM, MBOM, SBOM) in Teamcenter, ensuring compliance with industry and organizational standards. • Manage the engineering change process from initiation through implementation: document change requests, compile impact analyses, drive reviews through CCB/CRB, and track execution. • Facilitate configuration control forums including Change Control Boards and Technical Review Boards, setting agendas and ensuring decisions are recorded and communicated. • Lead and coordinate configuration audits (FCA/PCA), providing direction before, during, and after audit events to prevent and resolve issues. • Partner with Configuration Management Analysts to build and maintain status accounting dashboards that track ECRs, ECNs, and implementation status. • Provide guidance to engineering teams on Teamcenter workflows for engineering release, part revisions, BOM updates, and documentation modifications. • Perform periodic audits of as-built records against product definition data to ensure configuration accuracy across the product lifecycle.
