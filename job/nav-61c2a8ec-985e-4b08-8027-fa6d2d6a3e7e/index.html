We are looking for a Head of Engineering to lead the engineering vertical (Process Engineers, Maintenance, Automation) and drive technical efficiency, operational stability, and continuous development of engineering solutions. The ideal candidate is a strategic engineering leader with experience managing teams and delivering complex industrial projects, capable of combining technical expertise with operational performance. Responsibilities • Plan and coordinate engineering activities in production • Ensure technical support for manufacturing processes and equipment reliability • Lead engineering projects (modernization, new line launches, CAPEX initiatives) • Cooperate with contractors and equipment suppliers • Analyze production losses, downtime, and defects; implement optimization and automation solutions • Build and develop the engineering team (KPIs, feedback, performance management) • Ensure compliance with technical, industrial, and safety standards Requirements • Degree in Engineering (Mechanical, Automation, Electrical or related) • Minimum 5 years of industrial manufacturing experience • At least 3 years of engineering team leadership • Experience delivering CAPEX projects is an advantage • Strong knowledge of manufacturing processes and project management • Lean / TPM / Six Sigma knowledge • Experience with CAD / CMMS / ERP systems • English — B2 What We Offer • An opportunity to join a growing technology company at an exciting stage of development • Strategic role with high impact • Meaningful responsibility and direct influence on products and processes • A collaborative and international working environment • Professional development and career growth opportunities • Competitive compensation and benefits package We review applications on an ongoing basis and encourage interested candidates to apply as soon as possible.
