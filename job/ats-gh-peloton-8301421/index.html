ABOUT THE ROLE The Talent Producer is responsible for end-to-end management and editorial preparation and execution for their assigned Peloton Instructors. This role is a creative and executional partnership, working closely with Instructors to shape and produce high-quality, engaging content for Peloton’s platforms. This role is inherently "on the ground support" with Talent requiring presence in the studio and control room to facilitate seamless production. This candidate is required to be fluent in the German language.
