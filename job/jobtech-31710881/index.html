About the job Are you a technically interested person with hard skills within heat exchangers AND have a passion for sharing knowledge and educating others? Then we believe this might be your dream role. As a Competence Development Specialist, you will be part of a global competence development team and play a key role in shaping the skills and expertise of colleagues and partners worldwide. Your job is to deliver comprehensive product and application training sessions focused on Hygienic Heat Exchangers for the food and pharmaceutical industries. You will facilitate both on-site and hands-on training at global product training hubs, with sessions typically lasting two to five days. Key Responsibilities: Support the development and delivery of training, engaging with process and project engineers as well as field service professionals. Collaborate with regional product and application teams, acting as a "train the trainer" to ensure consistent and effective knowledge transfer across regions. Contribute ideas and feedback to enhance learning materials and methods. Assess training needs and adapt programs to meet the requirements of diverse audiences, including field sales, technical support, and service teams. Provide ongoing mentorship and support to trainees, fostering knowledge retention and practical application. Maintain and update training documentation to ensure accuracy and relevance. Global travel is part of the job, approximately 50-70 days per year with 12-15 training sessions per year. Trips often combining multiple sessions to deliver impactful learning experiences and you will be away for one week at a time. We have a solid understanding for our employees need to be able to balance work and family life and travels are mostly planned. You will be part of a diverse, experienced, and supportive team of ten and report to Ole Andersen, a trust-based and empowering leader who leads with clear expectations. We offer you a role with diverse, multicultural environments where collaboration and inclusion are highly valued. With Alfa Laval you will get to grow a global network of professionals. Who are you We are looking for you who truly enjoy sharing knowledge and helping others grow their technical skills. You are curious and open-minded and have the drive to inspire others and continuously seek new ways to make learning engaging. You thrive in diverse, multicultural environments, are approachable, eager to grow and enjoy working as part of a team. What you know Requirements: Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Technical Education, or a related field. Solid technical background in Heat exchanger industry, ideally within the food and pharmaceutical industries. Experience in technical training, product knowledge transfer, or hands-on service/engineering roles. Strong communication and presentation skills, with the ability to make complex technical information accessible and engaging. Experience with "train the trainer" programs or similar knowledge-sharing initiatives is an advantage. Familiarity with tools such as PowerPoint, experience with usage of case studies, and hands-on assignments.
