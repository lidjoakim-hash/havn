Who we are Helsing is a defence AI company. Our mission is to protect our democracies. We aim to achieve technological leadership, so that open societies can continue to make sovereign decisions and control their ethical standards. As democracies, we believe we have a special responsibility to be thoughtful about the development and deployment of powerful technologies like AI. We take this responsibility seriously. We are an ambitious and committed team of engineers, AI specialists and customer-facing programme managers. We are looking for mission-driven people to join our European teams – and apply their skills to solve the most complex and impactful problems. We embrace an open and transparent culture that welcomes healthy debates on the use of technology in defence, its benefits, and its ethical implications. The role As an Specialist Export Control / ITAR, you are the central point of contact for all questions regarding export control, customs, and sanctions. You bridge regulatory compliance with operational speed, ensuring technology, software, hardware, and technical data cross borders legally without unnecessarily slowing down Engineering, Procurement, Sales, or Logistics. Acting with high agency, you take full ownership of daily operations—from licensing and screening to the customs interface—collaborating closely with HR, Legal, Supply Chain, and our international entities (especially in the US). You drive automation and scalable licensing architectures to enable our growth, designing the critical processes relied upon by our Executive Export Control Officer (Ausfuhrverantwortlicher) while formal liability remains at the management level.
