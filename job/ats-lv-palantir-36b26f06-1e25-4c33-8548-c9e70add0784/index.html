A World-Changing Company   Palantir builds the world’s leading software for data-driven decisions and operations. By bringing the right data to the people who need it, our platforms empower our partners to develop lifesaving drugs, forecast supply chain disruptions, locate missing children, and more. The Role We're looking for an Information Security Engineer who can think broadly about how infrastructure is built, attacked, defended, and improved. You may have deep experience in one area—such as endpoint security, Active Directory, Data Loss Prevention, cloud security, identity, or detection and response—but you’re motivated by solving security problems across the environment. As an Information Security Engineer focused on infrastructure security, you'll help protect Palantir's global endpoints, servers, identity systems, applications, networks, and data flows. You'll work across teams to reduce attack surface, improve defensive controls, investigate security events, and turn security findings into durable architectural and operational improvements. This is not just a corporate security role; the adversaries we face are sophisticated. We need someone who understands both the details and the broader system: how a weakness in one part of the environment can create risk somewhere else, how controls interact, and how to build security improvements that hold up in practice. Core Responsibilities • Design, implement, and improve security controls across endpoints, servers, identity systems, cloud services, SaaS applications, networks, and data flows. • Drive security posture improvements through hardening standards, configuration management, vulnerability remediation, access controls, monitoring, and ongoing validation. • Assess security risks in new platforms, products, architectures, vendors, and services, and turn findings into practical recommendations and engineering work. • Build and maintain automation for security operations, including configuration validation, drift monitoring, patching, access reviews, policy enforcement, alert triage, and security hygiene. • Partner with Identity, Infrastructure, Engineering, Legal, and other Information Security teams to strengthen security architecture and address recurring risks. • Turn insights from assessments, red team exercises, incident investigations, and operational reviews into durable fixes, such as configuration changes, architectural improvements, policy updates, and automation. • Build expertise in one or more security domains while contributing across the broader infrastructure security lifecycle. What We're Looking For • Experience securing one or more major infrastructure domains, such as Windows, macOS, Linux, Active Directory, cloud platforms, SaaS applications, networking, identity systems, or enterprise endpoints. • A broad infrastructure security mindset and curiosity about systems beyond your primary area of expertise. • Familiarity with common infrastructure attack paths, adversary tactics, techniques, and procedures, and the controls used to prevent, detect, and contain them. • The ability to assess complex environments and recognize how weaknesses across identity, endpoints, applications, networks, cloud services, or data flows can combine to create risk. • Experience evaluating security tools and validating that controls are deployed effectively and continue to work as intended. • Familiarity with infrastructure-as-code, configuration management, CI/CD, or other approaches to making security controls repeatable and scalable. What We Value • The ability to own projects, make progress through ambiguity, and balance competing priorities while working well with colleagues. • Experience in complex, high-assurance, regulated, or rapidly changing environments. • Comfort moving between strategic problem-solving and hands-on technical work. • Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to explain technical risks and work
