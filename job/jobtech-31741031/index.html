Company description: Ericsson AB Job description: Join our Team About this opportunity: Are you an experienced IC Validation Engineer ready to make an impact? Join our dedicated IC validation team within the PEU Silicon. You will play a key role based in Lund Sweden in validating and verifying state-of-art ASIC’s that are shaping the future. This opportunity involves the entire process: from developing IC validation frameworks and executing electrical measurements to support product integration. We’re looking for a collaborative, solution-oriented team player who’s also self-motivated and ready to mentor others. You approach challenges with an innovative mindset, always open to new ideas and improvements. If you’re passionate about pushing the boundaries of IC validation and thrive in a collaborative, forward-thinking environment, this role is for you!   What you will do: Validate high-quality and high-performance IC products to meet rigorous standards, as well as customer expectations. Customize test scripts and test programs, specify test boards requirements, and create validation plans for new devices. Contribute to bring-up activities for new ICs, including initial manual debugging and investigations. Perform core electrical and functional validation measurements over PVT, perform power correlation exercises, and performance testing. Troubleshoot technical issues and provide expert customer support when needed. Drive continuous improvements in processes, product quality, and validation methods. Coordinate the collaborations within global ASIC validation team The skills you bring: Extensive experience with at least 6+ years in electrical validation and component-level functional verification, with strong hands-on expertise in lab setups and equipment. Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer or Electrical Engineering, or an equivalent technical field. Proficiency in scripting languages like Python, Matlab, and knowledge of programming languages like C and C++. Experience in code repository management Git. Deep knowledge of ASICs, FPGAs, and hardware test setups, with proven ability to design and troubleshoot complex validation processes. Technical expertise in the technical areas, like SERDES, Ethernet, PCIe, CPRI, DDR, Power, Clock, I3C, etc. Strong analytical skills, capable of interpreting validation data, diagnosing issues, and deriving actionable insights to enhance product quality and performance. Clear communication skills in both written and spoken English, with an ability to document technical findings effectively and collaborate across teams.   Strong plus Knowledge of Ericsson ASIC architecture, functionalities Experience of design, verification and validation of Ericsson ASIC Experience with Ericsson Radio or RAN-Compute HW/SW product development and validation
