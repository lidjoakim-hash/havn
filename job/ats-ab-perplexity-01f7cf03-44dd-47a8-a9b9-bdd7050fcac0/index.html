Perplexity is hiring our founding Account Executive in EMEA. As our first seller in the region, you'll build the business from the ground up, landing our first EMEA enterprise customers, shaping how we go to market, and helping lay the foundation for the team that follows. This role is ideal for a tenured seller who loves being in the field, wants to build something new, and is excited to shape the future of enterprise AI. RESPONSIBILITIES - Drive new business by prospecting, pitching, and closing Perplexity Enterprise across EMEA. We expect early traction to mirror our strongest customer industries in the US, including legal, financial services, healthcare, and professional services, but this person will help shape the initial territory strategy, prioritize opportunities, and lay the foundation for the EMEA team that follows - Build pipeline from scratch through outbound, events and your own network - Lead the full sales cycle, from first meeting through technical evaluation, security and procurement, negotiation, close and onboarding - Build senior relationships with C-suite and technology leaders, championing Perplexity's value and customer outcomes - Run high-impact demos and respond to RFPs and technical inquiries - Shape our EMEA go-to-market: messaging, pricing feedback, partnerships and the playbook future hires will run - Partner closely with US-based Sales, Product, Marketing and Customer Success teams across time zones - Meet or exceed individual revenue targets on a monthly and quarterly basis - Keep accurate pipeline and account records in Salesforce QUALIFICATIONS - 7+ years of full-cycle SaaS sales experience selling to UK enterprise customers - Proven record of meeting or exceeding annual revenue targets in a high-growth environment - Experience opening a new market, territory or segment, ideally as an early or founding seller - Skilled at complex sales cycles with multiple stakeholders and decision makers - Proven success self-sourcing pipeline through outbound prospecting - Comfortable operating independently with ambiguity and limited local support - Exceptional written and verbal communication, comfortable presenting to C-suite executives - Experience with CRM systems (e.g. Salesforce) - Right to work in the UK NICE TO HAVE - Experience as a sales leader who wants to return to carrying a bag - An existing network across UK enterprise buyers - Experience selling AI or data products
