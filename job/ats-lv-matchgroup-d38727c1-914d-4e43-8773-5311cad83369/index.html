About the role: Tinder is investing in bringing people together beyond the screen. Our Events program curates in-person experiences that give members new ways to connect and we’re scaling fast. The Business Development Operations Specialist is a contract role that provides critical and direct support, knowledge, curation, and management for the Events initiative. Source and curate events • Knowledge and understanding of Gen Z trends and values • Curation of a variety of unique events for all users, genders, and orientations Manage partner events • Working with our direct partners on day to day comms, issues, questions, event curation, maintenance, in-app insertion and edits when needed Maintain event inventory by city or MG brand • You will be the main owner and directly responsible individual for a subset of cities or MG brands and their ongoing event inventory • This means consistently, on a day to day basis, owning the selection, curation, copy, image, insertion, and editing of each event in your city Apply judgement • Be able to quickly distinguish and evaluate all qualities and factors of an event that will or won’t appeal to Tinder users Where You’ll Work • Willingness to work some hours that overlap with US PST. • Because most of the team is based in the US, you will need to be comfortable with working autonomously You’ll Need: • Bachelors Degree • Experience planning small-to-mid-size events, especially for Gen Z • Experience with high volume metadata and asset management • Fluent in TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube, including formats, trends, and what actually performs with Gen Z • A pulse on culture, creators, and what makes a partnership feel authentic rather than transactional • Knowledge and experience in Google Suite and familiarity with AI tools • Proactive with a bias for speed and problem-solving directly when able • Understanding that events work is around the clock; and open to occasional late night and weekend work • Priority for a candidate who speaks fluent German, regardless of location Nice to Have • Conversational Spanish, Portuguese, or French As part of our team, you'll enjoy: • The hustle of a startup with the impact of a global business • Tremendous opportunity to solve some of the industry’s most exciting problems • Working with an extraordinary team of smart, creative, fun and highly motivated people • Comprehensive health coverage, competitive pay, team bonding events, yoga classes and social events Commitment to Inclusion At Tinder, we don’t just accept difference, we celebrate it.
