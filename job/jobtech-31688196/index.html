Service & Warranty Project Manager

Hitachi Energy Sweden AB · Västerås, Sweden

The opportunity With a pioneering technology-leading offering, Hitachi Energy’s Power Quality Solutions support the green energy transition with solutions that bring resilience, stability and increased capacity to the power grid. As a result, more renewable energy sources can be integrated, environmental impacts minimized and lower the risk of disruption in the grid.  At Hitachi Energy, we value our people as our greatest asset, as we leverage diverse thoughts, backgrounds, and experiences to foster a culture of innovation and collaboration. With our skilled and global project teams, we are there to support our customers worldwide to meet their sustainable goals, paving the way for a more sustainable, flexible, and secure energy system. Are you at the beginning of your career and interested in combining technology, customer interaction, and project management? As a Service & Warranty Project Manager in our Global Centre of Competence for Power Quality, you will join a collaborative and international team where you will develop your skills while helping customers around the world solve technical challenges and ensure a reliable power systems. As a Service & Warranty Project Manager, you will play a key role in ensuring customer satisfaction and operational excellence within Hitachi Energy's Power Quality service portfolio. You will manage a broad range of warranty and service projects, working closely with customers, engineering teams and service organizations worldwide to resolve technical challenges, drive continuous improvements and deliver successful project outcomes.  “Join our dynamic team, where you will have the chance to work on exciting projects and tackle different challenges every day, supporting our customers in ensuring reliable operation of their installations throughout the lifecycle.” - Josefine Rysjö, Hiring Manager. How you’ll make an impact Managing ongoing Warranty and Service projects for the Power Quality portfolio, acting as a point of contact for internal and external customers. Handling a variety of tasks in the projects such as fault investigations, coordination of site activities, and customer relationship management. Supporting the resolution of quality issues for your assigned projects together with the engineering team, suppliers, and customers. Operational and financial reporting for projects while driving continuous improvements to enhance efficiency and quality. Collaborating globally with responsible parties in the regions and local operation units to support our customers around the world. Delivering according to customer expectations within budget and expected quality. Your background Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Industrial Engineering or other equivalent work experience. Recent graduate or early-career professional with 1-3 years of experience, experience from the energy sector is beneficial but not required. Interest in project management, customer relations, and technical problem solving. Strong HSE, quality, and integrity mindset, combined with an organized, structured, and collaborative approach to work, with the ability to prioritize tasks and take responsibility for your deliverables. Fluent in Swedish and English, both written and verbal; other languages such as Spanish or German are considered an added value.
