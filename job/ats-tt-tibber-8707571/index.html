We’re Tibber - the energy company that wants you to use less energy. When you join Tibber, you’ll be part of building the tech that creates a real shift in how people consume electricity - good for our users, their wallets and the planet. We believe AI is how we accelerate that shift, and it’s our people who lead it. The more we lean into AI, the more human judgment, curiosity and creativity matter. We’re looking for a Senior Backend Engineer, Energy Tariffs to join us – maybe that’s you? Job Mission 🚀 As a Backend Engineer, you’ll play a key role in making new energy tariffs easy to launch. Your work decides how quickly customers get a tariff that fits their life. The result: more customers saving money and shifting their usage to better hours. What you'll do ✅ • Build the platform that makes launching a new tariff as simple as setting it up, instead of building it from scratch every time. • Turn what customers want into a clear model of their energy contract, from signing up to switching and moving. • Evolve the services behind it, which handle each customer’s plan, contract and pricing. • Get new tariffs live in Sweden, Norway, Germany and the Netherlands, together with our Billing and Market Integration teams. • Shape our tariff offerings with the product manager, using data and customer insight, and bring the engineering view on feasibility and priorities. • Work with a platform team to pull shared, reusable capabilities out of the code base. • Work agent-first: use agentic development tools daily, build agents on Tibber’s internal agent platform and set up guardrails that let agents move fast. What we're looking for⚡ You are driven by impact and know how to turn strategy into execution. You’re used to fast-paced environments and thrive when given clear goals and high accountability. We believe you’ll thrive here if you: • Have owned the design and operation of production backend services, and are strong in TypeScript/Node.js or C#/.NET, with a willingness to work in both. • Deliver independently and take ownership, within a technical direction set with the team. You break down work, ship to production, spot problems and flag blockers early. • Are comfortable in complex business domains such as contracts, pricing, subscriptions or billing. Experience with energy, utilities or other regulated subscription businesses, or with config-driven systems and pricing engines, is a plus. • Have evolved live systems safely, through migrations and refactoring, not only built greenfield. • Connect the dots between customer needs, market rules and code. You care why a tariff exists and enjoy building a system, not just a feature. • Communicate clearly with engineers and product or business stakeholders, collaborate across team boundaries, and are comfortable with ambiguity and change. • Already work with AI in daily development: you direct agents, review their output critically and can reason about code you didn’t write. Tibber becomes more AI-enabled each day and you’re excited to grow with it and to help the team do the same. The list is long, and nobody ticks every box. If you already do the core of this and want to keep growing, apply! At Tibber you get room to power up.
