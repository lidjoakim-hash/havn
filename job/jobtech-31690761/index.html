Are you a qualified nurse looking for a permanent position in Norway, with the opportunity to work in a nursing home or home care services? On behalf of a municipality in Agder, Southern Norway, we are recruiting registered nurses for permanent positions within municipal healthcare services. These roles offer varied clinical responsibilities, independent nursing practice and close collaboration with other healthcare professionals. You will work with patients who have different medical and care needs, contributing to safe, high-quality healthcare in a supportive professional environment. The municipality is located approximately 30 minutes from the coastal town of Risør, surrounded by forests, lakes and beautiful countryside. Here, you can combine a rewarding nursing career with a quieter lifestyle and easy access to Norway’s southern coastline. About the positions The municipality provides a range of healthcare and care services, including nursing home care, short-term care, dementia care and home-based nursing services. In the nursing home, you will care for patients who require round-the-clock nursing, treatment and medical follow-up. Your responsibilities may include general nursing care, dementia care and supporting patients with complex or long-term health conditions. The nursing home has long-term care units, short-term care beds and a dedicated unit for people living with dementia. In home care services, you will provide nursing care and follow-up to patients living in their own homes or supported housing. You will carry out independent nursing assessments, provide medical treatment and help patients maintain their independence and quality of life. In both settings, nurses work closely with healthcare assistants, doctors and other healthcare professionals to ensure safe and coordinated patient care. Responsibilities Your responsibilities may include: Assessing, monitoring and evaluating patients’ health conditions Administering medication and performing clinical procedures Providing nursing care, treatment and follow-up for patients with acute and chronic conditions Caring for elderly patients and people with dementia or complex healthcare needs Collaborating with doctors, healthcare assistants and other healthcare professionals Providing guidance and support to patients, relatives and colleagues Maintaining accurate documentation in electronic patient record systems Contributing to quality improvement and the development of healthcare services Specific responsibilities will vary depending on the department and service. Further information about the workplace, duties, working hours and shift arrangements will be provided during the recruitment process. Qualifications EU/EEA citizenship and a valid nursing qualification and licence from an EU/EEA country Norwegian nursing authorisation is required before starting work. We will guide you through the application process Good English communication skills and motivation to learn Norwegian. Norwegian language training is provided Good communication and teamwork skills Ability to make independent nursing assessments and take clinical responsibility Experience in nursing homes, elderly care or home care services is an advantage A category B driving licence may be required, depending on the workplace A satisfactory police certificate is required before starting work, in accordance with Norwegian regulations. Who we are looking for We are looking for dedicated and compassionate nurses who are confident in their clinical skills and comfortable taking responsibility for patient care. You should enjoy working with patients who have different healthcare needs, communicate well with colleagues and contribute to a positive working environment. We are particularly interested in candidates who are motivated to build a long-term nursing career in Norway and are open to relocating and settling in the municipality.
