About the Role You will work as part of a multidisciplinary Data & Analytics team, collaborating with Data Scientists, Machine Learning Engineers, Platform Engineers, and Data Engineers to accelerate data-driven business development. You will support multiple business domains by developing analytical solutions, robust data pipelines, dashboards, and data models. Dashboard Development & Data Visualisation Design and develop intuitive, professional, and user-friendly dashboards and reports. Select appropriate visualisation tools based on business requirements and analytical objectives. Develop advanced dashboards using Power BI and other visualisation platforms. Help business users and superusers create and maintain their own dashboards. Translate complex data into clear insights and actionable recommendations. Collaboration & Continuous Improvement Collaborate with Data Scientists, ML Engineers, Data Engineers, and other technical specialists.
