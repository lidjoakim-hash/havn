Supply chain and Procurement Manager

EnginZyme AB · Solna, Sweden

💰 ca. 450 000–550 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 10.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

The Supply chain and Procurement Manager supports the entire lifecycle of our enzyme-based solutions—from procuring raw materials and components to delivering the final product to the customer. This expanded role consolidates the entire end-to-end supply chain, giving you single-point accountability for spend management, inventory health, distribution efficiency, and daily business operations. The role We are seeking an agile and strategic Supply chain and Procurement Manager to lead our supply chain from start to finish, from the suppliers we buy from, through our contract manufacturers, to the customers who receive our products. In this multifaceted role, supporting our product line director and the overall company activity, you will be responsible for procuring highly specific materials related to enzymes and biomanufacturing, managing day-to-day activities with existing raw material suppliers and production facility, supporting cost-saving procurement strategies, and optimizing logistics networks. You will also oversee daily warehouse, fulfilment, international shipping and business workflows to maximise productivity and efficiency. The ideal candidate is a master of cross-functional execution who balances hands-on operational ownership and a higher-level picture of supply chain and process efficiency oversight. You will choose and negotiate with suppliers, plan materials for each CMO campaign, keep inventory accurate across our sites and arrange shipments that arrive on time and comply with regulations. When costs rise, stock runs low or a delivery slips, you will spot it early, bring the right people together and drive it to a solution. This is a hands-on role with plenty of room to build the function your way as we grow. Key Responsibilities Manage the purchasing of raw materials, from request to delivery. Identify and qualify new suppliers and CMOs in collaboration with the Product Line Director Develop and maintain the supply base, focusing on quality, delivery and cost efficiency. Establish and update agreements with key suppliers and CMOs, including pricing and purchase terms. Plan and coordinate material supply for production campaigns at our CMOs, and follow up on timelines. Monitor inventory levels to secure material availability while avoiding excess stock. Coordinate inbound and outbound shipments, and ensure compliance with import/export regulations. Follow up on spend against budget and drive cost-saving initiatives as needed. Streamline daily operations and improve processes for ordering, receiving and shipping. Who you are Required A university degree in supply chain management, logistics, industrial engineering or similar. At least 5 years of experience in production planning, supply chain or procurement within the manufacturing industry, specialty chemicals, cosmetics, food or a similar industry. Experience from driving cross-functional collaboration, improvement work and process development. Excellent English skills, both spoken and written Nice to have Experience navigating global trade tariffs, customs clearance protocols, and international sourcing networks. Proven ability to operate as subject matter expert in a company building its operations function. Experience in a scale-up moving from R&D to pilot or commercial production, including technology transfer to CMOs. What we offer A people-driven, forward-looking company of 30+ people from more than 15 nationalities; A fast-moving, collaborative and multidisciplinary team shaping the future of industrial biotech; A start-up environment with minimal red tape, real responsibility and ownership, and room to learn and grow; A competitive salary and wellness grant; 25 days of paid annual leave plus bridge days, occupational health and injury insurance, and an occupational pension plan mirroring ITP-1.
