ABOUT PLEO Messy spend management is tricky business. And tedious processes are a lose-lose situation for all involved, not just finance. At Pleo, we're changing that. We build spend solutions that make managing money seamless, empowering, and surprisingly effective for finance teams and employees alike - with a vision to help all businesses ‘go beyond’. The word ‘Pleo’ actually means ‘more than you’d expect’, and living by that mantra has been the secret to our success over the last 10 years. Now, we’re at a pivotal moment in our journey; every move we make has a direct impact on our 40,000+ customers, our business, and our collective success. We need people who take pride in uncovering customer needs, who turn complex problems into simple solutions, challenge the way things are done (respectfully), and always aim high. With great ambitions driving us forward, we can’t say we’ve got this whole thing figured out. And frankly, that’s half the fun! What we can say is that we’re a driven, progressive, and, importantly, a kind bunch of 850+ people from over 100 nationalities, all committed to delivering the future of business spending, together. ABOUT THE ROLE We're looking for a Commercial Legal Counsel to join our Legal team at Pleo ideally based in Copenhagen, Denmark, although we are flexible on location for the right candidate. In this role, you'll serve as a trusted legal advisor across the business, protecting our interests and enabling growth as we scale our fintech platform. You will have a central role in our deal desk, procurement, contract review and negotiation, support vendor management, marketing and intellectual property work. You will help drive business growth by making commercial deals move quickly while keeping legal risk under control. Maternity cover: this is a fixed-term maternity cover position with a start date as soon as possible. Contract length is at least 1 year. WHO YOU’LL BE WORKING WITH AND REPORTING TO You'll be part of the Product and Commercial Law team and work closely with GTM (go-to-market) teams, Product, Security and Compliance. Our legal team is highly collaborative and dedicated to managing legal risk while enabling business growth and maturity. WHAT YOU’LL BE DOING As a Commercial Legal Counsel, you will: - Serve as the point of contact for business queries across all departments, providing timely, practical legal guidance on day-to-day commercial matters. - Support the deal desk, triaging incoming commercial requests and driving them to closure within agreed timelines. - Contract review and negotiation, covering customer, vendor and partner agreements, and drafting and redlining against our standard positions and playbook. Responsible for ensuring our customer facing terms and conditions are updated as needed. - Procurement and vendor management, reviewing and negotiating supplier agreements, including data processing agreements, and managing the vendor lifecycle from onboarding to renewal. - Track contractual obligations, making sure key dates, renewals, notice periods and ongoing commitments are monitored and acted on. - Support marketing and intellectual property, reviewing marketing claims, campaigns and promotions, and protecting our trademarks, copyright and brand. - Drive legal operations, improving contract templates, workflows and tools so the team works more efficiently and consistently. - Advise on regulation, including GDPR, DORA and payments regulation where they affect commercial agreements and products.
