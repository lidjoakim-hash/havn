Aimpoint AB is the company that invented and developed the red dot sight, for which we are today the world-leading provider. Aimpoint sights are used in the civilian hunting and shooting markets, as well as by police and military forces. The company has over 570 employees and a turnover of approximately 2,3 billion SEK. Sales in the United States are managed through our American subsidiary, Aimpoint Inc. Our headquarters are located in Malmö, with production facilities in Malmö and Gällivare. Aimpoint is part of the Sandberg Development group. For more information about the company, please visit www.aimpoint.com/se Production Line Manager About the role At Aimpoint, quality and safety always come first. We are now looking for Production Line Managers to strengthen our production organization in Malmö. This is a shift-based leadership role operating on a rotating two-shift schedule, where you will be responsible for leading a team of approximately 50-60 operators and Shift Leaders in a manufacturing environment that combines manual assembly with automated production processes. You will spend most of your time on the production floor, close to both employees and products, ensuring that daily production runs according to plan while maintaining the highest standards of quality, safety, and performance. As a Production Line Manager, you will be responsible for daily operations during your shift, leading and developing your team, resolving operational challenges, and driving improvements together with employees and key stakeholders across the organization. You will play an important role in creating a safe, engaging, and high-performing work environment while contributing to Aimpoint's continued growth and operational excellence. In this role, you will: Lead, coach, and develop operators and Shift Leaders in daily production activities. Ensure production targets, quality standards, and safety requirements are met. Allocate resources, prioritize work, and manage operational deviations. Drive continuous improvements and support Lean-based ways of working. Promote a safe, engaged, and high-performing work environment. Collaborate closely with Production Engineering, Quality, Planning, Supply Chain, and other key functions. Support employee development through feedback, onboarding, and performance follow-up. Have the authority to stop production whenever quality or safety standards are at risk. Required Qualifications Experience leading or coordinating work in a manufacturing environment. Experience from assembly-based manufacturing with an understanding of manual and automated production processes. Experience working with quality, standardized work methods, and deviation management. Experience with health and safety activities and continuous improvement initiatives. Willingness and ability to work shifts. Fluent in Swedish and English, both written and spoken. Good computer skills and experience with business systems and Microsoft Office. Meriting Qualifications Experience with direct people management in production environments. Experience leading through Shift Leaders or Coordinators. Experience from high-volume manufacturing with high quality requirements. Knowledge of Lean Manufacturing and continuous improvement methodologies. Experience from automated production environments. Familiarity with ISO 9001 and ISO 45001 standards. Technical education and/or formal leadership training. To succeed in this role You are an engaged and energetic leader who thrives in a fast-paced manufacturing environment. You enjoy being present where the action happens and are comfortable making decisions when quality, safety, or delivery performance is at stake. As a leader, you are clear, structured, and approachable.
