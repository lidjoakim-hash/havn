Would you like to use data analytics, automation and visualization to create clear insights in audit and assurance engagements? The opportunity As a Senior Associate in Audit Analytics & Data Insights, you will deliver analytics that strengthen audit quality and risk identification. You will work with complex datasets and translate requirements from audit teams and stakeholders into clear, well-documented analytical outputs. Your key responsibilities Design and deliver analytics across financial, operational and compliance engagements. Extract, transform and analyze large datasets using SQL, Databricks and Alteryx. Build reusable workflows and automated routines that improve efficiency and consistency. Create dashboards and visualizations in Power BI or comparable tools. Perform data-quality assessments, reconciliations, trend analysis and exception reporting. Document data sources, transformation logic, testing, assumptions and findings in a clear, auditable manner. Skills and attributes for success Qualifications Approximately 2–4 years of relevant experience in audit analytics, data analytics, business intelligence or a related field. Experience from audit or from preparing analytics for audit or assurance, with an understanding of audit methodology, risks, controls and how analytics can generate audit evidence and insights. Strong hands-on SQL skills, including queries, data transformation and validation of large datasets. Experience with data visualization, preferably Power BI, and the ability to communicate findings to both technical and non-technical stakeholders. Strong attention to detail and documentation discipline. Meritorious Experience with Alteryx, Databricks, Python, SAP or Oracle. Knowledge of Purchase-to-Pay, Order-to-Cash or continuous monitoring. Experience with audit automation, process mining and AI-enabled tools to enhance audit quality, efficiency and risk identification. A relevant qualification or certification in audit, accounting, information systems or analytics. As a person you are Structured, curious and solution-focused. Comfortable working with others and clear in your communication. Committed to delivering well-documented, high-quality results.
