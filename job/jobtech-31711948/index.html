Job Description We are looking for an experienced Business Analyst with strong solution understanding and the ability to bridge business needs with technical implementation. Key Responsibilities Develop a deep understanding of the solution, its functionality, and how it is used across the organization. Collaborate with Business Owners and stakeholders to gather, clarify, and challenge new requirements by asking the right questions and identifying underlying business needs. Assess the end-to-end impact of proposed changes and understand dependencies across related systems, processes, and solution components. Translate business requirements into clear technical work items, functional specifications, user stories, and high-level solution designs or pseudocode. Facilitate discussions and workshops with business stakeholders, SMEs, and technical teams to ensure alignment on requirements and proposed solutions. Engage with Business SMEs and dealer representatives to collect feedback and incorporate insights into solution improvements. Create wireframes and prototypes in Figma to support requirement elicitation and solution validation. Work closely with Tech Leads, Solution Architects, SMEs, and DPOs throughout the delivery lifecycle. Support testing activities by validating business logic, solution behavior, and requirement fulfillment. Required Experience & Competencies Proven experience working as a Business Analyst, Solution Analyst, or similar role in a complex business and technology environment. Strong stakeholder management and communication skills, with the ability to interact effectively with both business and technical audiences. Understanding of Power BI and Power Pages. Familiarity with Databricks and UDP (Unified Data Platform) concepts. Experience working with queries, Excel, data entities, and data analysis. Strong understanding of large datasets, multiple data sources, and data ingestion/integration flows. Ability to analyze business processes and understand how changes affect upstream and downstream systems. Experience supporting testing, validation, and quality assurance activities related to business requirements and solution logic. Comfortable working in cross-functional teams involving business stakeholders, architects, technical leads, and subject matter experts. Preferred Qualifications Experience working in data-driven environments with complex integrations and reporting solutions. Hands-on experience creating business process flows, prototypes, and requirement documentation. Knowledge of end-to-end data flows, system integrations, and solution dependencies.
