Cyber Security Engineer – Automotive About the Role We are looking for an experienced Cyber Security Engineer to join our clients project within the automotive industry. In this role, you will work in a fast-paced, agile environment, supporting software development teams in implementing cybersecurity requirements and ensuring compliance with automotive security standards. You will play a key role in performing cybersecurity analyses, conducting reviews, and delivering work products according to ISO/SAE 21434. Key Responsibilities Support software development teams with automotive cybersecurity expertise and guidance. Perform cybersecurity risk assessments, including Threat Analysis and Risk Assessment (TARA). Develop and maintain cybersecurity work products according to ISO/SAE 21434. Conduct cybersecurity analyses at concept and technical levels. Collaborate with internal development teams and suppliers to review cybersecurity requirements and solutions. Ensure alignment with automotive cybersecurity regulations, including UNECE R155 and R156. Contribute to continuous improvements in cybersecurity processes and development practices. Required Skills & Experience Degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or equivalent. Minimum 3 years of professional experience in cybersecurity. Strong knowledge of ISO/SAE 21434, UNECE R155, and R156. Experience performing TARA and automotive cybersecurity risk assessments. Good understanding of software development and engineering processes. Knowledge of information security standards such as ISO 27001 / ISO 27000. Experience working in Agile/Scrum environments. Knowledge of functional safety (ISO 26262) and previous automotive industry experience are considered advantages. Personal Qualities We are looking for a proactive, driven, and solution-oriented professional with a strong interest in automotive cybersecurity. You are a collaborative team player who enjoys sharing knowledge, solving technical challenges, and working across teams. You have excellent communication and documentation skills, a structured approach to your work, and are fluent in English, both written and spoken. We look forward to your application!
