People Operations Specialist – UK & US 9 month FTC

OakNorth · London, United Kingdom

💰 ca. 35 000–42 000 EUR/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)KontraktFjernarbeid · EuropeDirekte fra arbeidsgiverFrist 11.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

HOW YOU'LL WORK This is a hands-on role for someone who takes ownership and keeps things moving. You'll manage your own priorities, follow through on tasks and spot what needs attention — without needing to be told what to do at every step. You'll be comfortable working at pace, using available information and resources to solve problems, and reaching out with a clear question or recommendation when you need support. You'll take pride in getting the details right, checking your own work and flagging risks early. You'll have context, direction and support from your manager and team, alongside the trust to take ownership of your work. We welcome ideas and encourage you to speak up when you see a better way of doing things. As a fast-moving, growing business, there may be times when some flexibility or extended working hours are needed, including to provide cover for our US team. We also recognise the importance of flexibility for our people when they need it. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES EMPLOYEE LIFECYCLE MANAGEMENT - Coordinate end-to-end onboarding for new joiners across the UK & US teams, ensuring a seamless employee experience from offer acceptance through to day one and beyond. - Manage employee lifecycle processes including promotions, transfers, salary changes, probation confirmations, and offboarding activities. - Manage and track probation review processes, ensuring reviews are completed on time, outcomes are accurately recorded, and all required documentation is maintained. - Manage UK & US background screening processes through Zinc, ensuring all checks are completed and appropriately documented. - Partner with IT, leaders, people partners and other stakeholders to ensure all employee changes are coordinated effectively. - Manage and prioritise a varied workload, track outstanding actions and follow through with stakeholders to keep processes moving. - Recognise and escalate unfamiliar, sensitive or higher-risk issues to the appropriate person. PEOPLE OPERATIONS & SYSTEMS - Own the day-to-day administration of employee life cycle activities on our HR systems, ensuring employee information is accurate, complete, and up to date. - Monitor and manage key people processes and workflows through our People systems. EMPLOYEE SUPPORT - Serve as the first point of contact for employee queries across People Operations for our UK & US team. - Respond to day-to-day People administration requests in a timely and professional manner. - Assist employees with benefits enrolment and benefits-related queries. PROCESS IMPROVEMENT - Continuously look for ways to improve operational efficiency, employee experience, and data accuracy. - Leverage AI and automation opportunities to streamline administrative processes and improve service delivery. ABOUT YOU - 1- 2 years of relevant experience in UK or US HR Operations, with strong knowledge of contracts, onboarding, offboarding, BGV processes and employee lifecycle activities - Experience across both geographies is a plus; candidates with solid exposure to one and the ability to learn the other are encouraged to apply. - Exceptional attention to detail and a strong focus on accuracy. - Experience managing employee lifecycle administration from onboarding through to offboarding. - Experience working with modern HR systems. - Strong organisational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment. - Excellent communication skills and a customer-focused approach to employee support. - Comfortable handling confidential information with discretion and professionalism. - Evidence of working independently: organising your workload, following through without reminders, checking your own work and knowing when to ask for guidance. - Interest in leveraging AI and technology to drive operational excellence. - A collaborative team player who enjoys building strong relationships across the business.
