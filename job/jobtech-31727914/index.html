CellaVision is a global leader in digital microscopy, delivering complete solutions that combine advanced instruments, reagents, and software to support healthcare services worldwide. Our vision is clear: To elevate healthcare through the evolution of microscopy - in a sustainable way. We work at the forefront of technology and use advances in programming, chemistry, digital imaging, and machine learning. Joining CellaVision means playing an important role in supporting healthcare through the continued evolution of microscopy. Position: Senior Software Engineer Location: Lund, Sweden Job type: Full Time, Permanent How will you contribute? As a member of an Applications team, you will develop algorithms and software for our analyzers that automatically locate and pre-classify cells. Working together in the Application team, you will specialize in programming and digital image processing, used in our software to automatically detect, and classify cells and other objects in blood and body fluids. You will contribute to our entire development process, from algorithm development, implementation, code reviews, and documentation, to the creation and execution of tests. As a Senior Software Engineer your work will involve: Act as technical lead and advisor for SW development. Develop and maintain Windows desktop applications for lab personnel, using C++, C# and WPF. Develop and maintain backend services for coordinating access to databases and connecting labs, using C++ and SQL. Develop algorithms within digital imaging using C++ and Python. Develop and maintain existing products within Product care. Perform troubleshooting and testing as part of the quality work. Conduct investigations and research tasks. Review designs, reports, and source code contributions from colleagues. Participate in cross-functional demos and forums as team representative. Mentor more junior colleagues. What will you bring? M.Sc. in Engineering within an appropriate field. Extensive experience in programming skills in C++ or C#. Experience in machine learning is a bonus. Very good ability to understand the overall architecture and design of large software systems. Very good ability to take initiatives and come up with new proposals for working methods as well a strong sense of responsibility. Very good at evaluating and solving problems. Very good English communication skills, verbal and written. Experience in product development involving electronics, mechanics, and software. What We Offer Inclusive, Innovative Culture: Work in an international, collaborative environment focused on executing big ideas and adding customer value. Work-Life Balance: Flexible hours and reduced working hours to help maintain balance. Job Security: We offer a collective agreement, including comprehensive insurance, occupational pension, parental leave supplements, and more. Health & Wellbeing: We support your health with free access to a gym, wellness allowance, lunch benefits, sponsored running competitions, and discounts on gym memberships. Modern Workplace: Enjoy a vibrant, collaborative office environment that encourages teamwork and innovation. Team Spirit: Join us for weekly breakfasts and fun team-building activities. Ready to Join Us? This is a full-time position based at our CellaVision HQ in Lund, Sweden—a modern office with great infrastructure and commuting options. We’re looking for someone to start as soon as possible. Apply Now! Follow the application form and attach your resume by November 30, at the latest. We’re excited to hear from you!
