Product Visual Merchandiser Best Practices

H & M Hennes & Mauritz GBC AB · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 420 000–510 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Kontraktvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 29.11.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Job Description We’re looking for a Product Visual Merchandiser – Best Practices to join us in a temporary 12-month assignment, starting as soon as possible. In this role, you will support the development and coordination of global Visual Merchandising best practices for our physical stores. You’ll help translate product, customer and visual direction into clear, scalable and actionable Visual Merchandising principles and tools, creating consistency across customer groups while allowing for relevant market adaptation. You’ll work closely with the Global Head of Visual Product and key Visual Merchandising stakeholders, connecting Product Visual direction with Visual Commercial execution. Key responsibilities Develop, consolidate and simplify global Visual Merchandising best practices for physical stores. Translate product and visual direction into clear, scalable Visual Merchandising principles, guidance and tools. Coordinate best-practice development across Product Visual and key Visual Merchandising stakeholders. Connect Product Visual and Visual Commercial Operations, using insights and feedback to continuously improve ways of working. Bring a Product Visual and Visual Merchandising perspective into new physical-store developments and tools. Support alignment with the global Visual Merchandising framework and overall customer experience. Translate strategies and commercial insights into clear, actionable ways of working for markets and stores. Skills & experience To be successful in this role, we believe you have: 3–5 years of experience within store Visual Merchandising. Experience working within a regional, sales market or global organisation. A strong understanding of visual processes and in-store execution. Proven experience in store visual presentation and curation. An understanding of how to curate assortments based on buying, customer and commercial insights. Strong process and workflow management skills. Excellent planning and co-ordination skills, with the ability to bring structure across different stakeholders and workstreams. The ability to translate strategies and complex direction into clear and summarised actions. A commercial and customer-insight-driven mindset. A structured and organised approach to your work. Additional information This is a temporary full-time position for 12 months, with a start date as soon as possible.
