Lead Proposal Manager, UK & Ireland Your Impact As a Lead Proposal Manager, you will lead Axon’s most complex and highest-impact RFP and tender responses, proposals, and other customer-facing selling documents across the UK and Ireland. You’ll be a member of a global team that supports Axon sales worldwide and will serve as the senior proposal execution lead for the region. You will own proposal development from early procurement engagement through submission and evaluation-period follow-up, translating established win strategy into a controlled response plan and ensuring the final submission is compliant, responsive, scorable, customer-focused, and on time. Your strengths in judgment under ambiguity, cross-functional leadership, technical and proposal writing, and compliance discipline will be central to success. Focused primarily on UK and Ireland public-sector opportunities, you will work closely with Sales, Bid Strategy, Solutions Engineering, Product, Legal, Security, Pricing, Finance, Services, and executive stakeholders. You will personally lead the hardest pursuits while also strengthening regional proposal readiness, reusable standards, coaching, and execution across bids owned by other Proposal Managers. This is an office-based position in the United Kingdom. What You’ll Do Location: hybrid schedule of Monday working remotely, and Tue-Fri in our London office Reports to: Director, Global Proposals Direct Reports: N/A At Axon, the Lead Proposal Manager is the senior owner of proposal execution for the region’s most complex pursuits. You will engage early in the procurement process, establish how the response will be run, make execution and compliance decisions within approved boundaries, and carry the proposal through submission and evaluation-period deliverables. This is a comprehensive individual-contributor role requiring expertise in the following areas: • Proposal Planning & Execution: Translate established win strategy into an executable response architecture, including workstreams, assignments, proof collection, review cadence, decision gates, and submission-readiness criteria. Build pre-bid readiness for anticipated strategic procurements with Sales and Bid Strategy. Drive resolution of product exceptions, compliance issues, and proposal commitments through the appropriate approval paths, while partnering with Sales, Bid Strategy, Pricing, and leadership on commercial, account-strategy, and bid decisions. • Team Leadership: Lead large cross-functional teams across Sales, Solutions Engineering, Product, Legal, Security, Pricing, Finance, Services, Proposal Team members, and executive stakeholders. Drive decisions, close gaps, enforce deadlines, adjudicate conflicting review input, and coach Proposal Managers and Specialists to improve execution judgment, compliance discipline, and independent ownership. • Project & Compliance Management: Own proposal governance, milestones, compliance traceability, mandatory requirements, forms, schedules, portals, review cycles, final packaging, and submission readiness.
