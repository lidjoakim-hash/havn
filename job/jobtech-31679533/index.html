Senior CAE Infrastructure & HPC Specialist

Alfa Laval Technologies AB · Lund, Sweden

💰 ca. 510 000–650 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 17.11.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

About the job In this role, you will help ensure that the systems and computing environments supporting Alfa Laval's engineering work are available, secure, reliable and easy to use. Supporting approximately 300 CAE users globally, you will work with the Specialist CAE, IT Infrastructure, PLM and CAD teams to maintain and improve the technical environment used by our engineering organization. The role combines user support, infrastructure administration and continuous improvement, including contributing to Alfa Laval's Red Hat Enterprise Linux-based cluster with Slurm as the workload manager. You will handle both day-to-day support and longer-term improvements, and may occasionally need to provide support outside regular office hours when urgent system incidents or critical planned activities require it. Key responsibilities Provide technical support to CAE users Investigate user needs and help identify effective technical solutions Manage the installation, configuration, maintenance and troubleshooting of CAE-related applications. Support and maintain Linux-based computing environments Contribute to the administration of high-performance computing resources Help ensure systems are secure, stable and up to date Create and maintain scripts to automate tasks and improve efficiency Analyze recurring problems and recommend improvements that reduce incidents and improve user experience. Collaborate with users, IT teams and other technical specialists This position is based in either Lund or Flemingsberg and reports to the Manager Operations Applications & Infrastructure. Who are you You are a curious and solution-oriented professional who enjoys learning and understanding the technical challenges behind users' needs. You bring structure to problem solving, remain service-minded when supporting others and communicate clearly with both users and colleagues. You work collaboratively, share knowledge and contribute to a positive and effective technical environment. What you know You bring extensive experience supporting technical users and maintaining infrastructure in environments where system availability, reliability and performance are important. To succeed in this role, you have: At least 10 years of professional experience in similar systems support, infrastructure engineering, Linux administration or technical operations role. Excellent technical knowledge of Linux, including Red Hat Enterprise Linux. Experience creating and maintaining scripts in one or more of the following: Linux shell PowerShell Windows batch/CMD Python Experience installing, configuring, maintaining and troubleshooting software applications and operating systems. An interest in understanding technical challenges from the user's perspective and translating user needs into effective technical solutions. A university level Engineering degree It is an advantage if you have experience with Software licence management. Active Directory. Out-of-band management. InfiniBand. High-performance computing environments. Slurm or other workload-management systems.
