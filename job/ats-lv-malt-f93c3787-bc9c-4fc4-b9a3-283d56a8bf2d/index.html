Explore your future career  🔭 Join Malt’s Cash Management department to strengthen and scale our day-to-day financial operations. In this role, you will sit at the core of our financial engine, ensuring the seamless, accurate, and timely execution of all financing transactions across the platform. You will serve as a key guardian of our financial health by maintaining rigorous risk management standards, conducting essential fraud checks, and overseeing credit limit compliance. Operating in a fast-paced environment where efficiency is paramount, you will also play an active part in optimizing our workflows by identifying operational bottlenecks and implementing concrete process improvements every week. Key responsibilities ✨ • Oversee and execute end-to-end financing transactions, including eligibility assessments, financing requests, and payment allocations. • Perform critical risk management and compliance tasks, such as conducting fraud checks, monitoring credit limits, and handling buyback procedures. • Identify and troubleshoot operational bottlenecks quickly with a strong, customer-first problem-solving mindset. • Propose and roll out concrete solutions to streamline day-to-day workflows and increase processing efficiency. • Build and maintain clear operational documentation while tracking and reporting on core performance KPIs. About you 🧑‍🚀 • Currently enrolled in the final year of a Master’s degree (Master 2, Business School, or University) in Finance, Business Administration, or a related field, seeking an end-of-study internship. • A first internship or work experience in credit, financing operations, or corporate finance is a strong asset. • High rigor and meticulous attention to detail with an uncompromising standard for data and transactional accuracy. • Strong analytical and problem-solving mindset, curious and eager to dissect complex processes. • Professional working proficiency in English, paired with a solid command of Google Sheets or Excel. • Strong interpersonal and communication skills, comfortable collaborating with cross-functional stakeholders. • Familiarity with tools such as Jira, Looker, or Notion is a distinct plus. How to join the mission? 🚀 • First call with Ru-Syl, our Talent Acquisition Manager to better understand your background, aspirations, and answer any questions you may have (30 minutes). • Case study & Interview with our Head of Cash Management (Interim) to evaluate your practical legal skills, experience, and role specifics. • Final interview with Chief Financial Officer to discuss Malt’s strategic vision, team culture, and long-term goals.
