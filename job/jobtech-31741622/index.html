The Opportunity Are you passionate about power systems protection and eager to contribute to the energy transition? We are now looking for an experienced HVDC Protection Project Engineer to join our global HVDC team in Ludvika. In this role, you will play a key part in designing, implementing, and testing advanced protection systems that enable reliable and efficient transmission of electricity across the world. You will collaborate with multidisciplinary teams, customers, and suppliers to deliver innovative HVDC solutions that support grid stability and sustainable energy development. Teamwork is at the heart of how we work, and we believe that strong collaboration, communication, and a willingness to support and learn from others are just as important as technical expertise. We therefore place great value on your personal qualities, your ability to build strong relationships, and your motivation to contribute to the team’s success. This role is based in Ludvika, Sweden. How You'll Make an Impact Team leadership and alignment: Guide team members, coordinate activities, and keep the team’s technical direction and deliverables aligned with customer expectations. Customer understanding: Interpret customer specifications, identify key technical and functional requirements, and translate them into suitable technical solutions. Technical leadership: Apply a strong understanding of our products to develop and adapt solutions that meet customer needs and project requirements. Drive the specification, design, and engineering of HVDC protection systems for global projects. Perform protection studies, including fault calculations, relay coordination, and stability analyses for HVDC converter stations and transmission systems. Develop and review protection philosophies, functional specifications, logic diagrams, and test procedures. Configure and validate protection solutions to support reliable system performance. Participate in Factory System Tests and commissioning activities. Collaborate with customers and cross-functional teams to solve technical challenges and support successful project delivery. Share knowledge, contribute to continuous improvement, and support colleagues in their development. Your Background Degree in Electrical Engineering, Power Systems, or related fields. Experience in protection engineering, ideally within HV or DC environment, FACTS, or power transmission. Understanding of HVDC technology, converter topologies (VSC and/or LCC), and protection principles is an advantage. Knowledge of high-voltage substation protection and control systems, including design, configuration, testing, or commissioning. Experience working with Intelligent Electronic Devices (IEDs), including configuration, testing, or troubleshooting. Familiarity with power system analysis tools such as PSCAD, PSS®E, or DIgSILENT PowerFactory. Experience working with Azure DevOps and applying Agile and Scrum methodologies Knowledge of relevant standards and protocols, such as IEC 61850 and IEC 60255. A collaborative and solution-oriented approach, with the ability to work effectively across disciplines and cultures. Fluent in English, both written and spoken.
