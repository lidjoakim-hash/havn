With a proven track record of successful partnerships with top companies, we have rapidly grown our presence in the USA, Europe, and Sweden as a dependable and trusted resource within the IT industry.About the service Assignment Description We are looking for a Senior Mechanical Engineer to strengthen the Interior Group within Cab Development. The role focuses on developing interior trim components and surface materials for next-generation vehicle cabs. You will lead the development of wall and ceiling panel solutions and manage specifications for materials such as textiles, vinyl and leather. You will collaborate with Production, Purchasing, Styling and Engineering to ensure solutions meet technical, quality, styling, manufacturing and cost requirements. What You Will Work On Lead the development of interior wall and ceiling panel solutions. Manage specifications for interior surface materials, including textiles, vinyl and leather. Create and modify designs using CATIA V5. Collaborate with Styling on material appearance and surface requirements. Balance styling expectations with engineering and manufacturing constraints. Ensure components meet project, quality and cost requirements. Work closely with cross-functional development teams. What You Bring Bachelor's or Master's degree in Mechanical Engineering. Minimum 4 years of engineering or design experience. Strong hands-on experience with CATIA V5. Experience working with Styling Surfaces (A-surfaces). Automotive product development experience. Ability to balance technical requirements, styling, production, quality and cost. Strong communication, collaboration and stakeholder management skills. Independent, flexible and solution-oriented working approach. Meritorious Experience with ENOVIA. Previous experience in the automotive industry with the same customer. Knowledge of automotive interior materials, including textiles, vinyl and leather. Understanding of ergonomics and styling-related product development.
