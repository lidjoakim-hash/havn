SUMMARY Dust is looking for a Product Marketer to shape how we bring a fast-moving, technical product to market and build the engine behind our product launches. You will lead product launches and storytelling at Dust, turning a constant stream of product innovation into clear positioning, compelling narratives, and launches that help customers understand what’s new, why it matters, and what they can do with it. This is a hands-on role. You will work closely with product and engineering to understand what we’re building, find the story within it, and bring that story to life across messaging, web, video, social, customer communications, and sales enablement. You’ll own the work from strategy through execution, bringing in designers, contractors, and other partners where they can add specialist expertise. It’s a rare opportunity to help define the product narrative of a category-defining AI company while the product, the market, and the way people work with AI are all evolving quickly.
