Born in Sweden, trusted by the world, and here’s why we stand out: • We are trusted by over 500million active users every month across 190+ countries • We identify over 15 billion calls daily, helping users avoid spam and scams • We are powered by a team of 400+ employees from 45+ nationalities We always look for people who take initiative, own their work, and keep raising the bar. The role: We in the Legal team manage all groups' legal issues globally with a focus on data privacy, tech, and contractual matters. We actively work with stakeholders in the group to support growth and ensure compliance with applicable data privacy and other relevant legislation. As a Senior Legal Counsel, you will be part of our global legal team, where you’ll work closely with our commercial and product teams to draft and negotiate commercial agreements, and ensure and develop data security, data privacy, and data protection in all aspects of our products. You will also assist with general legal matters, including vendor and partner contracts, within the company. What you’ll do: • Act as a legal advisor to business, product, and engineering teams, balancing legal risk with business objectives • Draft, review, and negotiate a broad range of commercial agreements, including customer, vendor, licensing, partnership, and data protection contracts • Advise on product and technology matters including data use, AI/ML, user-generated content, platform terms, and privacy-by-design requirements • Support corporate and strategic initiatives such as partnerships and market expansion, while improving legal operations and internal processes What you bring in: • Strong academic credentials and a law degree • Specialized and have a minimum of 5+ years of legal experience, ideally working with commercial contracts in the technology/telecom field • Knowledge of commercial law, with an emphasis on IT/tech/telecom-related matters • A solution-oriented and proactive approach, excellent communication, and organizational skills • Great communication skills in English and Swedish • Desire to work in a fast-moving environment It would be great if you also have: • Experience from the US market • An interest in tech/telecom or experience working in a tech/telecom company • Swedish or US law degree What we offer: We support growth through learning resources, leadership programs, mentoring, and real hands-on work.
