At Catawiki, tax is more than compliance. As we continue to grow internationally, tax plays an increasingly important role in improving our buyer and seller experience, enabling business decisions, managing risk and supporting sustainable growth. We're looking for a Tax Analyst who combines strong technical tax expertise with a pragmatic business mindset. This role is ideal for someone who enjoys both hands-on tax work and strategic involvement. You'll work closely with Finance, Legal, Product and Commercial teams to embed tax into business decision-making, while supporting robust compliance, effective risk management and the continued development of a scalable, technology-enabled tax function. You won't just manage tax obligations—you'll help embed tax into business decision-making and shape how the function supports Catawiki's continued growth. Whether you're looking to broaden your experience beyond a specialist advisory role or deepen your expertise within an international business environment, this role offers exposure across all major tax disciplines while working closely with business stakeholders. Tax Risk Management & Business Partnering • Identify, assess and manage tax risks and opportunities across the organisation; • Design and maintain a robust tax control framework; • Partner with Finance, Legal, Product and Commercial teams on business initiatives; • Support international expansion projects and new business models; • Contribute to the development and execution of Catawiki's tax strategy. Tax Technology & Process Improvement • Drive automation and digitisation of tax processes; • Support implementation and optimisation of tax technology solutions; • Improve data quality, controls and reporting capabilities; • Leverage analytics and AI tools to enhance tax insights and efficiency; • Design standardised and scalable tax processes that support growth.
