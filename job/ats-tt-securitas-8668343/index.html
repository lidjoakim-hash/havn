Purpose Securitas Technology, part of Securitas, is a world-leading provider of integrated security solutions that protect, connect, and optimize organizations of all types and sizes. We're proud to serve as a trusted advisor and integrator, helping organizations across the globe implement smarter, innovative and seamless security solutions. Our 13,000+ colleagues in 40 countries are focused daily on our purpose to help make your world a safer place and our commitment to deliver an unparalleled client experience. At Securitas Technology, we believe in celebrating our differences and creating a supportive environment where all voices are welcomed, appreciated and represented. Here, our colleagues are empowered to think big, fail fast and push the boundaries of innovation. As a purpose-led organization, we aspire to make a difference every day, and we’re looking for the best and brightest to join our team. We are seeking a detail-oriented and proactive BIM Technician to join our design team. In this role, you will be responsible for producing high-quality technical drawings and BIM models, with a strong focus on electrical systems and infrastructure. This position offers the opportunity to work on complex, large-scale projects, including data centres and high-performance facilities. You’ll work closely with engineers, project managers, and installation teams to deliver accurate, coordinated, and standards-compliant BIM outputs from concept through to construction and as-built phases. Major Responsibilities • Develop and maintain detailed 2D and 3D models using Revit (MEP focus) and AutoCAD. • Interpret mark-ups and design changes to update drawings accordingly. • Generate and maintain drawings and models for tender, construction, and installation phases. • Translate hand sketches and engineering concepts into production-level drawings. • Support compliance with QA/QC standards, BIM execution plans, and documentation requirements. • Collaborate with cross-functional teams including engineering, sales, and site installation crews. • Produce as-built documentation by working closely with installation engineers. • Coordinate with external stakeholders and assist in clash detection, drawing review, and issue resolution. • Take ownership of specific project design elements, ensuring timely delivery to project milestones. • Ensure that all outputs meet internal and client design standards. Personal Attributes • Excellent attention to detail and drawing accuracy. • Ability to work independently and take initiative in solving design challenges. • Strong team player who thrives in a collaborative and fast-paced environment. • Effective time management and ability to deliver to deadlines. • Fluent English with clear written and verbal communication skills. Technical Skills • Proficiency in Autodesk Revit (preferably MEP) and AutoCAD. • Strong working knowledge of Microsoft Office Suite (Excel, Word, Outlook). • Experience with BIM 360, Navisworks, or similar model coordination platforms (advantageous). • Familiarity with Bluebeam Revu for markup and drawing review (a plus). • Solid understanding of BIM workflows and drawing standards (e.g., ISO 19650 desirable). Key Qualities • Minimum of 3 years’ experience in a design environment. • Previous work in the construction industry is required; experience in data centre construction is a strong advantage. • Ability to read and interpret technical drawings, schematics, and specifications. • Background in electrical design, security systems, or infrastructure design preferred Why Join Us? • Be part of technically challenging and innovative projects. • Work in a supportive and experienced team committed to quality and continuous improvement. • Opportunity to grow within a BIM-specialist team How to Apply If you are driven by the challenge of protecting global enterprises from evolving security threats and have the skills and experience we seek, we would love to hear from
