Trade Republic is hiring an Intern for the Executive Office, for quarters Q1/27 and Q2/27. This position is based in Berlin, Germany. Please note that, depending on strategic priorities and personnel demand at the time of your process, you will be embedded within the Executive Office's Corporate Development or CEO Office team. THE BEST WORK OF YOUR CAREER Trade Republic is the largest savings platform in Europe - we operate in 18 countries, serving 10 million customers who trusted us with over 150B in assets. But we’re striving for more. We have a bold mission to empower everyone to build wealth with easy, safe, and free access to financial systems. You will have the opportunity to grow your career by collaborating with a team of outstanding talents and state of the art technology to build a lasting, positive future for millions. WHAT YOU’LL BE DOING Think ahead: In the Executive Office, we take strategic initiatives from zero to one as part of our mission to build a world where everybody can participate in economic growth. We manage projects end-to-end and collaboratively work with all departments across Trade Republic. Every day, we strive to move Trade Republic forward step by step, by constantly challenging the status quo. • Play a vital role in driving and executing projects and initiatives to rapidly scale our business across Europe and expand our product offering • Assume responsibility on high-impact end-to-end strategic projects (zero-to-one from thesis building to operation) • Work closely with the management team across different functions to evaluate and drive internal and external business development opportunities • Conduct (market) analysis to support crucial management decisions WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR • Currently pursuing or already completed a bachelor’s or master’s degree at a top-tier university with excellent grades • First professional experience in management consulting, investment banking, venture capital or business development • Ability to break down complex issues, reduce them to the essential factors and communicate them effectively • Excellent German and English skills • At least three months availability WHY YOU SHOULD APPLY NOW Our culture rewards ownership, excellence, and high energy. We care deeply about outcomes and hold each other accountable - we’re here to win and fix one of the largest challenges Europeans face - closing the pension gap and democratising wealth. If this gets you fired up, reach out! We believe it’s our team’s varied identities and backgrounds that make us sharper and stronger. We’re committed to creating an environment where everyone feels respected and has equal opportunity to thrive in their careers.
