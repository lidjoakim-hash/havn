Join us in making a difference Ready to take your HR business partnering to the next level? As part of Talent & Business Relations, you’ll join the team that connects Talents & Culture (TC) with the business, turning people strategy into action and business needs into smart, practical HR solutions. You’ll work hand in hand with the Performance & Recognition and Talent Development teams, backed by a strong Talent Support team, with one joint goal: driving organizational performance. As the trusted people partner for your business area, you’ll position and shape TC procedures that work in practice. You’ll support leads in successfully leading their teams and meeting their people responsibilities with confidence. At trivago, we are cultivating a Caring Performance Environment where care, performance, and learning reinforce each other so our people and business thrives. Sound like an environment for you? Apply today! How you’ll make an impact: • Advise leads in your assigned departments on complex people topics, including leadership, performance management, compensation, and staffing. • Guide your leads and teams through change processes, such as organizational restructurings, with strategic advice. • Support leads in forming high performing teams through partnering with our recruiting and talent development specialists, advising hiring managers on decisions, succession planning, and development initiatives via data-backed recommendations. • Strengthen our Caring Performance culture by coordinating and challenging calibrations and the performance framework around our job architecture. • Balance lead topics and individual cases, zooming out for strategy and zooming in for day-to-day operations. What you'll need to thrive: • Solid experience as an HR Business Partner (minimum 5 years), with advanced knowledge of German labor law for individual and collective cases. • A strong track record of aligning HR strategy with business goals on talent and organizational topics, building relationships with leads that are trusting enough to challenge and push back when needed. • Strong communication skills and the credibility to represent TC across the organization, with fluency in German (C1) and English (our company language). • A genuine learning mindset: you approach challenges with curiosity, seek out feedback, and continuously look for ways to develop and grow. • A performance mindset: you set ambitious goals, adapt to shifting demands, stay calm under pressure, and have the determination and resilience to follow through, even when things are ambiguous. • An open and curious mindset toward AI tools and automation: you actively explore how AI can enhance your work and are excited to grow alongside evolving technologies. What you can look forward to: As you grow, so do we. This is why at trivago, we prioritize your development, offer personalized coaching through Nilo, and provide workshops, educational meetups, conferences, free online learning courses, and access to a fully-equipped campus library.
