Test Engineer, Lund

Axis Communications Aktiebolag · Lund, Sweden

Are you passionate about application testing? Do you enjoy testing applications from a customer perspective, especially applications that act as clients in a larger system? This could be your opportunity to work in the ADA (Axis Device Assistant) area at Axis, as part of Device Management Test. Who is your future team? Device Management Test is a team of test engineers working with our service platform and its associated applications. In your day-to-day work, you'll be part of an agile, cross-functional team. We aim to give each team end-to-end ownership of the software it develops, tests, delivers, and monitors. You'll also collaborate with test engineers in surrounding teams to align our testing strategies and methods. What will you do? Axis is developing advanced cloud services and applications that, together with our cameras, provide customers with complete and secure surveillance systems. As a test developer focused on the application side, you'll work with user-centered testing of the system's web applications. You'll collaborate with developers and test features early in the development process. Our work includes development and system testing, combining in-depth technical knowledge with a focus on usability, security, and the customer perspective. We also share responsibility for operating our production system and work with a DevOps mindset. We work in an agile way. You'll be part of a small team with developers, planning and delivering features together and making sure they are tested. Your main responsibilities will include: Developing and executing tests, including test automation, within an agile team Carrying out user-centered testing based on use cases Developing monitoring and analysis capabilities for our production system Analyzing results from automated system tests Taking part in troubleshooting and fixing issues Developing scripts, automated test cases, and tools to support CI/CD (continuous integration and deployment) Who are you? You enjoy programming and care about testing and quality. You have a degree in engineering, computer science, information systems, or a similar field. You have a few years of experience in software programming and testing, preferably in an agile development and testing environment. You're comfortable working with computers and understand how networks work. It's a plus if you have: An interest in programming and relevant knowledge from your studies or personal projects Experience testing software, applications, web services, or APIs Knowledge of network administration and TCP/IP Experience with Axis devices What Axis has to offer We are a world leader in network video, where cutting-edge technology meets global impact. Here, you'll contribute to meaningful projects that shape the future of security and surveillance - developing solutions used worldwide. As a fast-growing company, we offer exciting career opportunities. You'll grow professionally through continuous learning, supported by a collaborative team that values creativity, innovation, and work-life balance. Your well-being matters to us. We offer a range of benefits, including a company bonus, Friday cake, wellness allowance, health insurance - and even your own Axis bicycle. To learn more about Axis, our innovative products, solutions, and vibrant company culture, explore: Axis Career Hub Life at Axis blog Engineering at Axis blog Innovation at Axis Ready to Act? Axis is a company realizing the benefits of a diverse workforce. We know that diversity in groups creates a better working environment and promotes creativity, something that is fundamental for our success. We welcome all applications.
