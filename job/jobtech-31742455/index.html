We are looking for a Retail Sales Academy & Learning Journey Design Partner for a global company in Gothenburg (Mölndal). Start is December 16th, 5 months limited contract to begin with. This role is 100% onsite in Gothenburg (Mölndal). Assignment description Retail Sales Academy & Learning Journey Design Partner ﻿ Background: Our client has established a Retail Sales Job Architecture and Skills Building Framework across its Personal Care and Consumer Tissue business areas. The framework provides a common capability foundation across four Sales Profiles: Commercial Management, Account Management, Field Sales and Sales Enablement. ﻿ The framework connects sales roles with functional and leadership skills, future capability requirements, onboarding, and ongoing development. The architecture, skills priorities, and strategic direction have already been defined. The objective of this assignment is not to redesign those foundations, but to translate them into a practical, scalable Retail Sales Academy with role- and skill-based learning journeys delivered through the company's Learning Experience Platform (LXP). ﻿ The LXP brings learning resources together in one place and enables employees to discover relevant learning opportunities, career paths, and development experiences aligned to their needs, interests, and goals. ﻿ The organization is seeking support to establish a practical and scalable Retail Sales Academy. The Academy will support approximately 1,500 employees across more than 20 markets. The focus is on implementing the existing framework and creating effective learning journeys rather than redefining capability models or long-term strategy. Estimated project duration: 4–6 months ﻿ Overview and Need English will serve as the master language for the Academy. However, the solution must be designed for successful deployment across Europe, North America, and Latin America, ensuring that language does not become a barrier to understanding, adoption, or application. As a significant proportion of users are Spanish-speaking, it is important to accommodate local language requirements, particularly as many sales activities are conducted in local languages. A key principle is to use and adapt existing learning content wherever possible before creating new content. Learning journeys should provide meaningful progression and connect learning with practical application, manager coaching, and peer learning rather than simply aggregating courses. ﻿ Responsibilities - Define the Academy structure, learner navigation, and curriculum, including governance, ownership, and maintenance. - Review existing learning content and map suitable assets to priority roles, skills, and proficiency levels. - Design coherent learning journeys with clear learning outcomes, logical sequencing, practical application activities, and reinforcement mechanisms. - Identify priority content gaps and selectively adapt or develop content where existing resources are insufficient. - Define a scalable language and localization strategy using English as the master language and local-language content where needed. - Prepare learning-path specifications, taxonomy structures, and metadata requirements for implementation in the Learning Experience Platform. - Deliver documentation, editable assets, and knowledge-transfer materials to support long-term ownership and maintenance by the organization. The consultant will work independently while collaborating closely with business stakeholders, SMEs, and learning teams. ﻿ Required Experience The ideal consultant has strong experience in corporate academy design, curriculum architecture, and role- or skill-based learning journeys, with the ability to translate business needs into scalable learning solutions.
