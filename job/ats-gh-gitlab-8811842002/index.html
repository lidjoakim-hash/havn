An overview of this role As a Senior Contracts Manager, Commercial (EMEA), you’ll help keep GitLab’s commercial contracting moving by managing a varied pipeline of customer agreements, from established contracting work to more involved and complex negotiations. You’ll work closely with sales and internal teams to keep deals on track while protecting GitLab’s interests. You’ll be part of the team that helps GitLab do business every day, bringing pace, precision, and good judgment to a broad range of commercial legal work. In this hands-on role, you’ll keep the day-to-day contracting work moving while also contributing to various strategic enterprise deals across the region. You’ll report to the Director of Legal and work with a collaborative team that values clear advice, pragmatism, and a willingness to to roll up your sleeves. You’ll also help improve the playbooks, tools, and processes that make commercial contracting more consistent and scalable.
