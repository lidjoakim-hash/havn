A Snapshot of Your Day Step into the role of Chief Engineer – Electrical System with focus on Project Delivery Excellence and become the driving force behind our electrical system solutions. You’ll lead the way in ensuring technical excellence for both customer and development projects, acting as one of our go-to expert for engineers and project teams. Join us to champion quality, standardization, and innovation, while building strong relationships with suppliers and internal partners. If you’re passionate about shaping the future of electrical systems and eager to make a real impact, this is your opportunity to shine. How You’ll Make an Impact Support project teams by sharing your technical expertise and guiding engineers in the field. Act as a senior reviewer for both standard and project-specific technical documents. Represent the Center of Excellence in design reviews, technical meetings, and decision-making forums. Lead updates to standard schedules, purchasing structures, and contribute to NCC follow-up activities. Drive improvements in processes, tools, and technical standards to ensure optimal, cost-effective designs. What You Bring Several years of hands-on experience in order engineering and electrical systems. In-depth knowledge of gas turbines, ideally within the Medium Gas Turbine segment. Expertise in I/O, vibration monitoring, and speed monitoring systems. Strong skills in interpreting technical documents, system descriptions, and design documentation. Excellent communication and collaboration abilities to support and inspire project teams. About the Team You’ll join a dedicated team committed to delivering technical excellence and innovative solutions in electrical systems. The team works closely with project delivery, suppliers, and internal functions such as purchasing to ensure every solution meets the highest standards. As a collaborative and supportive group, we value open communication, continuous improvement, and a shared passion for quality and efficiency. Our Gas Services division offers Low-emission power generation through service and decarbonization. Zero or low emission power generation and all gas turbines under one roof, steam turbines and generators. Decarbonization opportunities through service offerings, modernization, and digitalization of the fleet. We can offer you employment benefits such as: reduction of working hours, advance vacation, health care allowance and an eventual possibility to a flexible working place. Who is Siemens Energy? At Siemens Energy, we are more than just an energy technology company. With ~100,000 dedicated employees in more than 90 countries, we develop the energy systems of the future, ensuring that the growing energy demand of the global community is met reliably and sustainably. The technologies created in our research departments and factories drive the energy transition and provide the base for one sixth of the world's electricity generation.  Our global team is committed to making sustainable, reliable, and affordable energy a reality by pushing the boundaries of what is possible. We uphold a 150-year legacy of innovation that encourages our search for people who will support our focus on decarbonization, new technologies, and energy transformation.  Find out how you can make a difference at Siemens Energy: https://www.siemens-energy.com/employeevideo  Our Commitment to Diversity Lucky for us, we are not all the same. Through diversity, we generate power. We run on inclusion and our combined creative energy is fueled by over 130 nationalities. Siemens Energy celebrates character – no matter what ethnic background, gender, age, religion, identity, or disability. We energize society, all of society, and we do not discriminate based on our differences.
