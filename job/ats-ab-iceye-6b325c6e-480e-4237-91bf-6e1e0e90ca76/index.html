ROLE HIGHLIGHTS - Title: Engineering Satellite Build Lead - Location: Valencia, Spain - Department: Space Systems Testing Team, Engineering - Reports to: Testing Team Lead - Employment type: Permanent - Workplace model: Hybrid per company policy (up to 2 days/week remote); in practice, this hands-on, technical-managerial role requires being onsite at our Valencia office most of the time - Employment is subject to applicable security screening WHY THIS ROLE MATTERS You own the end-to-end build, integration, testing and delivery of the developmental satellites, starting with early preparation and alignment with designers, procurement and operations teams, through the Qualification Model (QM) and Flight Model (FM). You plan the AIT timeline, lead the build team, and keep build documentation, configuration tracking and lessons learned flowing back to designers, so every future unit is faster and easier to build. This position sits within the Testing Team. It is important to note that this is developmental, prototype satellite work rather than repeat-production integration: requirements are not yet frozen, designs evolve frequently, and the Lead is expected to resolve novel problems rather than execute a fixed procedure. WHO WE ARE ICEYE delivers space-based intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance (ISR) capabilities to governments and allied nations, including sovereign and turnkey ISR missions built on ICEYE's world-leading synthetic aperture radar (SAR) satellite technology, alongside access to data from the world's largest SAR satellite constellation. These capabilities let partners detect and respond to critical changes anywhere on Earth, day or night and in any weather, with ultra high-resolution imagery and high-frequency revisits. As a trusted partner for defence, intelligence, security and maritime domain awareness, ICEYE's near real-time data creates a tactical advantage for mission-critical operations. Founded and headquartered in Finland, ICEYE operates globally with over 1000 employees across Europe, North America, the Middle East and Asia-Pacific. YOUR DAY-TO-DAY RESPONSIBILITIES Build, integration and delivery ownership (EC, Engineering Configuration) - Own end-to-end build, integration, testing and delivery of the R&D satellite system, across QM and FM units - Plan all AIT activities and the AIT timeline for R&D build - Manage the build team and actively participate in build execution - Own build documentation, including build records, configuration tracking (parts traceability) and high-level build procedures - Document lessons learned and give structured feedback to designers to improve build efficiency and testability QM and FM build and system-level testing - Plan and own the QM system build and its system qualification activities - Own system-level integration and testing of FM satellites, verifying functional performance, capturing any required modifications, and maintaining close traceability to support knowledge transfer to the production line - Define system-level test blocks and flows with technical owners, and plan, schedule and coordinate execution of functional tests, SLC, Mock Ops and other required system-level test activities (FM) - Across both QM and FM: accountable for test execution readiness and progress, but do not define test cases, test scenarios or test acceptance criteria (owned by Test Engineering / relevant technical owners) Scope boundaries - Does not own unit-level testing or qualification (owned by project leads / technical owners) - Does not own documentation updates resulting from future design changes (owned by designers); this role's build documentation, including Production documentation, reflects as-built execution only - Does not define system functional requirements or system test definitions, including test cases, scenarios or acceptance criteria (owned by systems engineers / test engineers) WHAT WE'RE LOOKIN
