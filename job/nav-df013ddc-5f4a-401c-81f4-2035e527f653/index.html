Site Selection Manager Want to help us find and develop the next locations for data centers? At Lefdal Mine Data Centers, we're looking for a Site Selection Manager to join our team and support our continued growth. In this role, you'll be involved from the early stages of identifying potential locations through to evaluating their suitability and preparing them for investment decisions. You'll look at everything from power availability and fiber connectivity to land conditions, infrastructure, costs and local regulations, and lead the end-to-end site selection process for new data center facilities. You'll work closely with colleagues across engineering, construction, finance and other areas of the business, as well as external partners and local authorities. You can work from one of our offices in Norway or remotely from within Europe. What will you be working on? Your main responsibility will be to identify and evaluate potential sites for new data center developments and help bring the most promising opportunities forward. Some of your key tasks will be to: • Identify and assess potential locations for new data center developments, both in existing and new markets. • Evaluate power availability, fiber connectivity, infrastructure, land suitability and opportunities for future expansion. • Carry out market research and compare potential sites based on technical, commercial and regulatory requirements. • Coordinate technical and environmental assessments, including ground conditions, natural hazards and construction feasibility. • Work with engineering and construction teams to assess site requirements, development costs and timelines. • Collaborate with finance and legal teams on business cases, investment assessments, agreements and property-related matters. • Build relationships with landowners, utility providers, local authorities and other relevant partners. • Identify potential risks and ensure that relevant requirements and regulations are considered throughout the process. Who are we looking for? We're looking for someone who enjoys combining technical understanding with commercial thinking, and who is comfortable taking responsibility for projects from an early stage. We believe you'll be a good fit if you have: • A relevant degree in engineering, real estate, business or a related field. • At least 3 years of experience in site selection, real estate development or similar work. • A good understanding of the infrastructure requirements involved in data center development, particularly power, cooling and connectivity. • Experience with market assessments, feasibility studies, cost evaluations or risk assessments. • The ability to manage several projects and work with different stakeholders across locations. • Good communication and negotiation skills, and the ability to work independently. Experience from large-scale data center developments or hyperscale projects is a strong advantage, along with knowledge of energy markets, utility procurement, permitting processes and environmental regulations. You don't necessarily need to come from the data center industry, but we're looking for someone with a sharp eye for opportunities, sound commercial judgement and the ability to recognise what makes a site worth pursuing. You understand the technical and commercial factors that matter, know how to assess risks and opportunities, and have the confidence to make well-founded recommendations and drive projects forward. Why join us? At Lefdal Mine Data Centers, you'll become part of a growing company operating in an international industry. You'll have the opportunity to work on new development projects, contribute to important investment decisions and work alongside colleagues with different technical and commercial backgrounds. We offer: • Competitive salary and benefits. • Flexible working arrangements, including the possibility to work remotely within the EU. • Opportunities for professional development and growth. • An international working environment with experienced and supportive colleagues. • The opportunity to take part in developing new data center locations and supporting our future growth. Interested in joining us? We'd love to hear from you! Apply today and tell us a little about yourself, your relevant experience and what makes you a good fit for this role.
