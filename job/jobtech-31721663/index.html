ICE Point Enterprises AB Location: Finland Work Schedule: Rotational shifts Start Date: As soon as possible Employment Type: Full-time Job Description ICE Point Enterprises AB is looking for an experienced and dedicated HSE Advisor to join our team on a large-scale construction project in Finland. The role involves supporting and monitoring health, safety and environmental procedures on site, ensuring that all employees and subcontractors follow applicable safety regulations, project requirements and company policies. The HSE Advisor will work closely with site management, supervisors, subcontractors and the client's HSE team to maintain a safe working environment throughout the project. Key Responsibilities Monitor and ensure compliance with site HSE requirements and safety procedures. Conduct regular site inspections and safety walks. Identify hazards and assess workplace risks. Support the implementation of risk assessments and method statements (RAMS). Conduct toolbox talks and safety briefings. Investigate incidents, near misses and unsafe conditions. Follow up on corrective and preventive actions. Ensure employees and subcontractors comply with PPE requirements. Monitor employee training, certifications and site safety inductions. Maintain HSE documentation, inspection reports and safety records. Promote a positive safety culture across the project. Report HSE performance and concerns to site management. Requirements Previous experience as an HSE Advisor, HSE Officer or similar role within construction or industrial projects. Knowledge of construction site health and safety procedures. Experience with risk assessments, method statements and incident reporting. Good understanding of PPE requirements and workplace safety. Relevant HSE qualifications or certifications. Good English communication skills, both written and spoken. Ability to work independently and take responsibility. Strong communication and problem-solving skills. Experience from data centre or large-scale construction projects is an advantage. What We Offer Full-time employment on an international construction project. Rotational work schedule. Accommodation fully provided and paid for by the company. Flights to and from Finland covered by the company. Necessary travel expenses covered. Competitive salary based on experience. Opportunity to work with an experienced international project team. How to Apply Interested candidates are welcome to submit their CV to faktura@icepoint.se Please include relevant HSE experience, qualifications and availability.
