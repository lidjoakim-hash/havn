ABOUT TIDE At Tide, we help SMEs save time and money in the running of their businesses by not only offering business accounts and related banking services, but also a comprehensive set of highly usable and connected administrative solutions, from invoicing to accounting. Tide is transforming the small business banking market and now supports over 2 million members globally across the UK, India, Germany and France. Using advanced technology, all solutions are designed with SMEs in mind. With quick onboarding, low fees and innovative features, we thrive on making data driven decisions to serve our mission: to help SMEs save time and money so they can get back to doing what they love. Tide facts: • Tide is available for UK, Indian, German and French SMEs • Over 2 million members globally and rapidly growing • Over $300 million raised in funding • 3,000 Tideans globally • Recognised with Great Place to Work certification three years in a row, and among India’s Top 50 Best Workplaces in Banking, Financial Services, and Insurance in 2026 • We have offices in Central London, with a member support and technology centre in Sofia, Bulgaria, technology centres in Serbia, Romania, Lithuania and Hyderabad and offices in Gurugram, New Delhi, Berlin, Paris and Luxembourg WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR As a CRM Manager, you’ll play an important role in delivering Tide’s CRM strategy in Germany, helping shape how we engage with our SME audience from onboarding through to retention. You’ll work within our established UK/Europe Lifecycle team to deliver relevant, timely communications that keep members engaged with Tide’s range of products and services. We’re looking for someone who is data-literate, organised and hands-on, with experience planning, building and optimising CRM programmes across the member lifecycle. You’ll be comfortable working with stakeholders across the business and using campaign performance and customer insights to make recommendations and improve results. You’ll be equally happy developing campaign plans, building journeys and automations, analysing performance, and working with stakeholders to turn insights into action.
