Your next chapter starts here! 🪐   At Malt we believe that Ambition is the Way, so all lists of missions and responsibilities are non-exhaustive. Explore your future career 🔭 As a Talent Acquisition Specialist, you will work closely with our Talent Acquisition Manager and the People team. You will support the day-to-dayrecruitment activities for our CentralFunctions (Finance, Operations, HR, Marketing). Your main mission will be to assist the Manager in their daily tasks, ensuring a seamless candidate experience and maintaining high operational standards. You will actively contribute to the accuracy, quality, and reliability of our recruitment pipeline in a fast-paced international environment. Key responsibilities ✨ • Coordinate the recruitment pipeline by managing interview schedules and ensuring seamless communication with candidates. • Source, assess, and hunt diverse talent pools: Master the art of screening resumes to identify top profiles beyond job titles, and use a "salesy", persuasive approach to engage passive candidates through LinkedIn and creative outreach. • Deliver an exceptional candidate experience, ensuring every applicant feels valued and informed. • Optimize employer branding initiatives across social media and local activations to enhance our visibility. • Analyze recruitment metrics to monitor the health and efficiency of our early-career pipeline. About you 🧑‍🚀 • Educational background ideally in Human Resources, Business, or Sales, and looking for an end-study internship. • Job experience with a first internship or project experience in a fast-paced environment is a plus. • Languages & level on a native level in French and professional fluency in English. • Tools & processes include an appetite for learning ATS tools (like Lever or Greenhouse) and a natural ease with LinkedIn. • Communication skills characterized by high energy, empathy, and the ability to pitch Malt’s vision convincingly. How to join the mission? 🚀 • First call with Ru-Syl (our Talent Acquisition Specialist), to better understand your background, aspirations and answer your questions (45 minutes) • Interview with your manager, Claudia, to discuss your experience and the role in more detail (45-60 minutes) • Final interview with Elise, the head of Talent Acquisition of Malt • We will be ready to welcome you in our Parisian offices in January 2027!
