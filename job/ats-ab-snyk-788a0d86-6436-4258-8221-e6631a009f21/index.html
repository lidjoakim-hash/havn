Job Summary Millions of developers now build software faster than ever, and that speed creates security risk at a scale most teams are still learning to absorb. As a Technical Success Manager (TSM) on Snyk's 1:Many team, you will help a broad base of smaller customers get real value from the Snyk platform without owning a fixed set of accounts. Instead of managing named accounts one at a time, you will lead bookable, hands-on deep dive sessions on topics like onboarding, enablement, and product configuration, so that customers can embed security into their development workflows and reach their DevSecOps goals quickly. About the Team You will join the Technical Success team inside Snyk's Customer Experience organization, on the 1:Many team that supports our smaller customers through a pooled model. Rather than a dedicated TSM per account, customers book expert-led sessions from a shared team, so every customer gets access to deep technical guidance at a scale that one-to-one coverage cannot reach.
