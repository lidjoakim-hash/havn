Senior Industrialization Engineer

Retyre AS · Oslo, Norway

Join the future of Sustainable Manufacturing at reTyre! At reTyre, you’re not just joining a company — you’re joining a groundbreaking industrial journey together with a great team of innovators. We’ve built the world’s first sustainable tire factory, right in the heart of Ski, 20 min outside of Oslo. We are now looking for a skilled Senior Industrialization Engineer to join the Manufacturing team and drive our mission forward. Our factory uses a revolutionary production method that makes it possible to replace traditional rubber in pneumatic tires with new, sustainable materials. Our team is internationally diverse, young, energetic, and united by a passion for innovation, sustainability, and growth. Every day, we challenge the conventional and push the boundaries of what manufacturing can be — for the benefit of people and the planet. The company language is English, and the position demands strong written and spoken English skills. The role This role is key in bridging the gap between R&D and production, ensuring that new products and processes are efficiently industrialized following proof-of-concept validation. The Senior Industrialization Engineer will work closely with R&D, Process Engineering, and Quality Engineering teams to design and implement robust, cost-effective manufacturing solutions. What You’ll Do • Establish the most efficient way to produce products and processes after proof-of-concept validation. • Collect and analyze data for data driven improvements and decitions. • Design custom machines or tooling according to our production method. • Work closely with R&D, Process Engineering, and Quality Engineering to ensure smooth industrialization. • Actively participate in CAPEX planning and procurement processes for new equipment and tooling, including close contact with suppliers. • Contribute to PFMEA and Control Plan development to ensure quality is built into the process. • Identify opportunities for process optimization and cost reduction while maintaining product integrity. Who We’re Looking For We are looking for someone who is motivated and enthusiastic about the opportunity to shape industrialization processes from the ground up, bringing valuable experience and insights gained from successfully navigating multiple industrialization and development cycles in previous projects. You are experienced in a Manufacturing/Production and feel confident entering this role with your existing skillsets and are still hungry for further growth and development. You find it motivating to be a part of a Company's journey going from startup to scaleup, that offers a different kind of environment than other typical traditional manufacturing companies offer. Experience matters — but so does attitude and passion. Qualifications • Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering, Industrial Engineering, or related field. • Minimum 5 years of experience in hands on industrialization management or process engineering within manufacturing or automotive sectors. • Great advantage with Lean Six Sigma certification. • Strong understanding of PFMEA, Control Plans, and lean manufacturing principles. • Experience with CAPEX planning and supplier management. • Excellent problem-solving and project management skills. • Strong English communication skills, both written and spoken. • Ability to thrive in a start-up environment with hands-on involvement. • Advantage but not a requirement with experience from either bicycle tire development, injection moulding or RF welding. Personal Qualities • Proactive and solution-oriented. • Motivated by innovation and fast-changing environments. • Flexible and comfortable working in a modern, small-team environment with real influence. • Precise, organized, structured and good work ethics. • Good communication skills. • Independent and self-driven. • A positive team player who contributes to a great work culture.
