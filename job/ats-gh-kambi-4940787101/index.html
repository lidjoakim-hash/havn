About Kambi Kambi Group plc is a leading B2B provider of premium sports betting services to licensed gaming operators. Our services provide an end-to-end solution for operators wanting to launch a standalone Sportsbook or bolster their existing offering with an innovative sports betting product. From front-end user interface to customer intelligence, risk management and odds compiling, all built on our in-house developed software, we strive to deliver the ultimate service and solution to our partners. Our vision is to create the world’s leading sports betting experiences, together with our partners. Do you want to join a growing team responsible for the layer between hardware and applications? Kambi are in the middle of a DevOps transformation and are building a sustainable organisation for the future. For our Engineering Operations department, we are searching an experienced Linux Infrastructure Engineer with automation skills. We are looking for people who are passionate to learning modern technologies, thrives when collaborating with others, have an agile mindset and are solution oriented. Who are we? Engineering Operations supports the infrastructure of the organisation and the success of our partners. We ensure a stable environment twenty-four seven,year-round and we like open source in all flavours. We are dedicated to agile operations, believe dev-ops and automation are great ways of achieving that, whilst using agile methodologies to support our efforts along the road. We thrive in collaborative, ever-changing environment where we are using the collective resources of the team to meet objectives and find the best solution together. The role In this role, you’ll build and maintain on-prem infrastructure with a wide variety of technologies around the globe. You’ll do this by delivering reliable, scalable, and universally available systems. Technologies we are working with today include Ubuntu, Debian, infrastructure services (DNS, DHCP, NTP), globally hosted servers, virtualization (KVM), storage (Ceph), monitoring tools (Prometheus, Grafana, Zabbix), automation (Puppet, Ansible, Terraform, GitHub Actions - reusable workflows) and private cloud environments, alongside our on-prem infrastructure. Who are you? We’re looking for a solution-oriented team player with a solid background in Linux. It is also beneficial if you have experience with infrastructure automation, basic understanding of networking (such as dynamic routing, data centre switching, site-to-site VPNs) and a “security first" approach. An agile mindset is crucial for us, and additionally experience working with Ubuntu, Debian and Puppet profiles would be a great fit within this team. You will like us if you: • Think code & automation is the right way to evolve infrastructure. • Driven and motivated to find the best solution to the problem at hand. • Find technically complex systems thrilling. • Detail orientated. • Enjoy home labbing or tinkering with infrastructure in your own time. • Enjoy working in a self-organizing, self-managing team. • Thinks business value delivered is what defines great operations. • Like to work within a large global environment. Sound like your next challenge? If you're excited by Linux, automation, and building reliable infrastructure for a global platform, we'd love to meet you. Apply now and become part of a team that's building the future of our platform. If you'd like to showcase your work, feel free to include links to relevant GitHub repositories, infrastructure automation projects, or a brief description of your home lab setup. We'd love to see what you've built.
