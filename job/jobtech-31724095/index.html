Engineering Lead Developer Experience

Nordic Investin Group Aktiebolag · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 540 000–650 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 10.2.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Nordic Investin is recruiting an Engineering Lead Developer Experience on behalf of one of our partner companies. You want technical choices, delivery and production behaviour to reinforce each other, even when the constraints are difficult. The partner is strengthening its capability in engineering leadership focused on faster feedback and safer delivery. You will connect developer portals, build systems and CI pipelines while ensuring that platform metrics and coaching receive appropriate discipline. The aim is practical progress that colleagues can use, leaders can evaluate and the organisation can sustain. The environment rewards early questions, visible trade-offs and engineering discipline. Security and operability belong in the design. How you will work You will work with product, architecture, security, operations and domain colleagues. The partner values short feedback loops, evidence from real systems and direct communication. This role calls for visible leadership and disciplined decision making. You are expected to make trade-offs visible, recognise when a decision needs wider expertise and move work forward without creating unnecessary process. What you will do Own the practical direction for engineering leadership focused on faster feedback and safer delivery. Translate goals and constraints into a clear approach for developer portals and build systems. Establish useful standards, routines or decision artefacts around CI pipelines. Coordinate stakeholders and dependencies affecting platform metrics. Use evidence and measurable outcomes to improve coaching. Strengthen knowledge sharing and sustainable ownership through technical strategy. What you will bring Relevant experience delivering engineering leadership focused on faster feedback and safer delivery in a demanding environment. Strong working knowledge of developer portals and build systems. Practical experience applying CI pipelines and platform metrics. Ability to assess priorities, risks and trade-offs involving coaching. Evidence of clear stakeholder communication and dependable end-to-end ownership. Professional English and the judgement to work with sensitive or business-critical information. Experience that would add value Experience from multi-team software organisations. Exposure to technical strategy in a cross-functional or multi-team environment. Swedish language skills or experience working across Nordic organisations. A background that can succeed here Your background may be product engineering, platform work, enterprise technology or consulting. What matters is technical depth connected to user and business consequences. Direct experience of engineering leadership focused on faster feedback and safer delivery is valuable, while adjacent experience counts when it shows transferable judgement. What makes the opportunity interesting The role offers meaningful ownership and the opportunity to influence both the solution and the decisions around it. The focus on engineering leadership focused on faster feedback and safer delivery gives the opportunity a clear mission and room to influence priorities and success measures. The exact partner, employment model, compensation, start date and working arrangement will be explained openly during the process. Nordic Investin will make sure you understand the opportunity before asking you to make a decision. The recruitment conversation Nordic Investin will focus on decisions you have made: the context, alternatives, result and what you would change today. You will also have room to ask direct questions and test whether the role, mandate and partner environment are right for you. How to apply Apply with your CV or LinkedIn profile and a short note about a relevant system, platform or technical change you helped improve. Nordic Investin welcomes different routes into the profession and assesses evidence, judgement and potential alongside formal titles.
