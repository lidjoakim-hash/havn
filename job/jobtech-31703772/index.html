Scania is undergoing a transformation from being a leading supplier of trucks, buses, and engines to providing complete and sustainable transport solutions. Together with TRATON and our sister brands MAN, Volkswagen Truck & Bus, and International, we are working to shape the future of mobility with innovative and environmentally conscious solutions. Our values – customer first, respect, team spirit, responsibility, and elimination of waste – are at the heart of everything we do. Together, we are at the forefront of creating a sustainable future. Role Summary Are you a structured and proactive person who enjoys coordinating technical deliveries, managing priorities, and bringing people together? Do you have project management experience and a good understanding of embedded software development? Join our System and Software group within Scania Special Vehicles as Delivery Manager - System and Software and help us deliver customized vehicle solutions for demanding customers. We are looking for someone with a project management mindset, strong organizational skills, and the ability to turn complex activities into clear plans and actions. Job Responsibilities As Delivery Manager, you will coordinate our development activities, ensuring clear priorities, effective planning, and reliable deliveries. Your main responsibilities will be to: Own the overview of the group’s deliveries, ensuring clear priorities and follow-up. Develop and maintain the group’s roadmap, including milestones, dependencies, and delivery commitments. Coordinate and follow up assignments with external engineering partners. Secure sprint deliveries and facilitate effective, repeatable planning routines. Act as the link between the group and prioritized projects, aligning expectations and commitments. Support resource and capacity planning, including decisions on starting new projects. Identify and mitigate delivery risks. Drive process improvements and challenge existing ways of working. Align priorities with stakeholders and coordinate development needs with TRATON. You will work closely with the group manager and engineers, supporting the team in meeting its commitments. Who You Are You are a team player who values cooperation, contributes to a positive team spirit, and communicates clearly. With good leadership skills, you bring people together, build trust, and drive progress without formal authority. We believe you have: A bachelor’s or master’s degree in engineering, Mechatronics or equivalent experience. Experience in project management, technical coordination, or development planning. A good understanding of embedded software and system development. Experience with agile development methods and sprint planning. Strong communication, cooperation, and leadership skills. A structured, pragmatic, and solution-oriented approach. Experience from Traton/Scania’s development organization, processes, and systems is highly valued. Above all, we value ownership, initiative, team spirit, and the ability to achieve results through collaboration. This Is Us At Scania Special Vehicles, we develop customized solutions for defence and civilian applications, adapting Scania’s vehicles to specific customer needs. Our System and Software group works with a broad range of technical solutions, managing several projects in parallel with different priorities and timelines. We collaborate closely with other development teams, suppliers, and the TRATON Group, focusing on robust, production-ready solutions. We are now looking for a Delivery Manager to strengthen our planning, coordination, and delivery performance.
