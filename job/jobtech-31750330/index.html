Join us as a Senior Reliability Engineer and play a key role in ensuring the long-term performance and reliability of critical manufacturing assets. The position is part of the local Maintenance & Reliability organisation in Uppsala and works cross-functionally with Engineering, Production, Quality Assurance and other key stakeholders. As a Senior Reliability Engineer, you will act as a key technical specialist on both operational and strategic levels, driving the development of a proactive, data-driven reliability and maintenance culture. You will also collaborate closely with Galderma's global reliability organisation to ensure alignment with corporate standards, best practices and long-term strategic objectives. We're looking for someone who combines strong technical expertise with a proactive mindset, takes ownership of complex challenges and is motivated by turning analysis into action. Make your mark. Grow with us. Your mission As a Senior Reliability Engineer, you will be responsible for ensuring the reliability, availability and long-term performance of manufacturing systems and equipment across our operations. Using data, engineering expertise and structured problem solving, you will identify improvement opportunities, assess equipment and asset lifecycles, and support decisions on maintenance strategies, upgrades and future investments. In this role, you will provide subject matter expertise in reliability engineering and asset performance management, supporting both day-to-day operations and long-term strategic initiatives. Through collaboration with local and global stakeholders, you will help shape maintenance practices, improve asset reliability and contribute to sustainable business performance. This is a senior role that requires a high degree of ownership and initiative. You are comfortable working independently, building strong relationships across functions and driving improvements from idea through to implementation. You will join a team of experienced engineers who play an important role in supporting operational excellence and the continued development of our Uppsala site. Key Responsibilities · Drive reliability engineering initiatives across manufacturing operations, systems and equipment. · Lead and facilitate reliability assessments using methodologies such as RCA, RCM, FMEA and FMECA. · Analyse equipment performance, identify failure patterns and implement measurable reliability improvements. · Develop and optimise preventive maintenance strategies to improve asset availability and reduce operational risk. · Support asset lifecycle management and contribute to investment decisions through data-driven recommendations. · Monitor and improve maintenance performance indicators (KPIs) and reliability metrics. · Collaborate with stakeholders across Engineering, Production, Automation, QA and Facilities to drive continuous improvement and operational excellence. · Collaborate with Galderma's global reliability organisation to ensure alignment with company standards and strategic objectives. · Promote a proactive, reliability-focused culture through coaching, knowledge sharing and continuous improvement initiatives. · Lead root cause investigations and drive corrective and preventive actions to improve operational performance Your profile You are an experienced engineer with a background in reliability engineering, maintenance engineering or asset management within an industrial environment. You are comfortable working with complex technical challenges and understand how to translate data into practical actions and long-term improvements. Equally important, you take ownership, build trust with stakeholders and thrive in an environment where initiative and collaboration are valued.
