We are looking for a Demand Planner who combines strong analytical skills with a collaborative and business-oriented mindset. You will turn data, market insights and commercial input into reliable demand plans that help us improve availability, forecasting and supply chain performance. Does that sound like you? About the job As Demand Planner you will play a key role in our business planning process, creating and continuously improving a robust 24-month demand plan for your area. You will work at the intersection of Sales, Customer Service and Supply Chain, combining statistical forecasting with commercial and market insights to balance customer demand, product availability and operational efficiency. Please note: We apply continuous selection, so the position may be filled before the final application date. We therefore encourage you to submit your application as soon as possible. In your future role as Global Demand Planner you will Develop, analyze and maintain a rolling 24-month demand forecast, combining statistical forecasting, historical demand, commercial input and market insights. Partner closely with Sales Managers, Customer Service and Supply Chain teams to align assumptions, manage exceptions and ensure anticipated market developments and commercial strategies are reflected in the demand plan. Monitor forecast performance and demand management KPIs, identify forecast errors and trends, and translate your analysis into corrective actions and continuous improvements. Perform forecast segmentation and maintain appropriate statistical forecasting models in Arkieva, ensuring products and new product/plant combinations are assigned relevant forecasting approaches. Support and facilitate Global Demand Reviews and contribute to Supply Management and Demand Supply Reviews, including inventory strategies, new product introductions and product phase-outs. Drive improvements in planning processes, tools and ways of working and contribute to Supply Chain and demand management projects. We believe you bring A Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Supply Chain, Business or a related discipline OR equivalent relevant experience. Solid understanding of supply chain and business planning processes, combined with strong analytical capabilities. Demonstrated ability to work with large and complex datasets, including data validation, trend analysis and forecasting. Strong proficiency in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel, and confidence working with digital planning and data environments. Very good communication skills in English, with the ability to collaborate effectively across functions and organizational levels. A structured and results-oriented approach, with the ability to prioritize, take initiative and move work forward with limited direction. Great if you have Experience with SAP or another ERP system, Business Warehouse or another data warehouse, and Arkieva or comparable advanced planning software. Experience with statistical forecasting, demand planning or Integrated Business Planning processes. APICS CPIM or CSCP certification. We believe you are You enjoy combining analytical thinking with collaboration and know how to turn complex information into clear conclusions and actions. You build productive relationships across functions and communicate confidently with stakeholders at different levels of the organization. You take ownership of your work, set priorities and proactively move topics forward. At the same time, you are curious about how processes can be improved and comfortable challenging existing ways of working when data points to a better solution. We offer you This is an opportunity to take an important role in Nouryon’s regional planning process and work in a collaborative, international environment.
