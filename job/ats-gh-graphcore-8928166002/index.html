Regional Data Centre & Lab Infrastructure Operations Manager

Graphcore · Bristol, UK, United Kingdom

💰 ca. 44 000–53 000 GBP/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)HeltidDirekte fra arbeidsgiverFrist 17.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

About the job Deliver the power, cooling and capacity that enable Graphcore’s engineers to bring up and test AI systems. Based in Bristol, you’ll lead regional lab and data centre infrastructure delivery and own operational readiness. As Graphcore’s client-side representative, you’ll turn agreed engineering requirements into practical delivery plans and improvements. Your work will keep existing environments effective while creating capacity for testing and experimentation. You’ll identify constraints before they affect engineering work and improve reliability for high-density AI workloads. You’ll guide builds, expansions and upgrades from site assessment through design, installation, commissioning and handover. You’ll manage approved budgets, supplier commitments, schedules and delivery risks. As an individual contributor, you’ll use independent technical judgement to balance regional projects with day-to-day operations. Experience with liquid cooling and pressurised infrastructure is particularly relevant to this work. The team and culture You’ll agree regional priorities with Systems Engineering, hardware engineering, IT, facilities, health and safety, and finance. You’ll coordinate internal teams and suppliers, making readiness, capacity constraints, costs and risks clear. You’ll resolve competing priorities and escalate changes that need additional investment or wider decisions. Working with counterparts in other regions, you’ll share improvements and apply consistent infrastructure standards. What we’re looking for • Experience leading client-side infrastructure projects in data centres, labs or mission-critical environments, including hyperscale or high-performance computing. • Strong understanding of data centre power distribution, cooling, rack infrastructure, connectivity and building requirements. • Experience guiding projects through design, procurement, construction, commissioning and operational handover. • Ability to interpret engineering drawings and technical specifications, and assess supplier proposals against agreed requirements. • Project management skills covering budgets, schedules, dependencies and risks, alongside infrastructure capacity planning and operational readiness. • Clear communication across technical and non-technical teams, with experience coordinating suppliers and resolving competing priorities. While we have outlined a set of requirements, we value transferable skills and diverse experiences. We also welcome engineers returning to the profession after a career break, including through returnship routes. Benefits • Flexible working: Balance your work and personal life with greater flexibility • Generous leave: Take time to rest, recharge and enjoy life outside of work • Retirement planning support: Up to 5% matched pension • Phantom equity: Share in Graphcore’s success • Workplace experience: Enjoy thoughtfully designed office spaces for collaboration, with free food and an on-site barista to support your day • Peace of mind protection: Income protection and life assurance to provide financial security for you and your loved ones
