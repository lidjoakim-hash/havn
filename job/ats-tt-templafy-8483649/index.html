About us: Founded in Copenhagen, Denmark, in 2014, Templafy’s success is built by our 60+ employee nationalities found at offices around the world. We believe that when people feel valued, heard, and empowered, they perform at their best—creating a win for all. Our unique product and dedication to innovation and excellence have raised over $200 million in funding from top investors like Insight Venture Partners, Seed Capital, Dawn Capital, Damgaard Company and Golub Capital. Templafy is seeking a Brand & Motion Designer to join our marketing team and contribute to creating visual content across various touchpoints. We are looking for someone who is passionate about communicating clearly through design and has experience with animation. You will be working closely with our creative team and the rest of our marketing team to craft compelling campaigns, ensure brand consistency across all media, and optimize creative assets for use within the organization. This role is full-time and is based out of our Copenhagen Office. Key Responsibilities  • Design, update and format a wide variety of content including guides, flyers, digital ads, website updates, SoMe, sales collateral, event materials etc. • Design and animate motion graphics for use across a variety of touchpoints such as website, ads, product explainers etc. • Create content and concepts for different media channels such as: blog, advertising, campaigns, and website. • Create compelling visuals for website, sales material, product 1-pagers, guides, digital ads etc.   • Ensure consistent brand standards across all materials by creating and following set guidelines and templates. • Deliver files for print and web according to system specifications. Required Qualifications:  • Knowledge of Adobe Creative Suite (Illustrator, InDesign, After Effects, Adobe Express, Photoshop etc.), Figma, PowerPoint, and other AI assisted design tools. • Experience with layouts, illustration and motion graphics.  • An online portfolio showcasing relevant work experience. • Strong attention to detail and accuracy. • Strong understanding of designing for different media types, both digital and print. • The ability to work independently and collaboratively. • Strong time management skills and the ability to prioritize workload as required. • Experience with data visualization/infographics, motion graphics, and animation.
