Northern Europe Sales Manager

BTR Personnel services AB · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 450 000–550 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 16.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

The role The Northern Europe Sales Manager will build the company’s business in Sweden first, then expand into neighbouring markets (Norway, Denmark, Finland and the Baltics). The role is mainly focused on the international restaurant brands the company already serves across Europe, and on winning new brands in the region. You will be the company’s main commercial contact in Northern Europe and will work closely with the European head office operations team in Italy. Key responsibilities Build and execute the sales plan for Sweden, then for the wider Northern Europe region Manage and grow relationships with existing restaurant brand clients and their franchisees in the region Identify, pitch and win new QSR and hospitality accounts Lead projects from brief to installation, coordinating with head office operations, production and logistics in Italy Prepare quotations, proposals and pricing within company guidelines Manage the pipeline, forecasts and reporting in the company’s CRM and ERP systems Represent the company at client meetings, site visits and industry events Report to company directors on market performance, opportunities and risks Requirements At least 5 years' experience in B2B sales or account management, ideally with international QSR or hospitality brands Proven experience managing restaurant brand accounts at European level Strong knowledge of commercial furniture, fit-out or restaurant equipment products Experience with CRM, ERP and quoting software used in project-based sales Fluent English (written and spoken) Fluent Italian (written and spoken), needed for daily coordination with the European head office operations team Able to travel regularly within the Nordic and Baltic region and to head office in Italy Self-starter able to build a new market with limited local support
