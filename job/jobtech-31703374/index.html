We Are Hiring – Multi-Skilled Beauty Therapist About Our Company Tobo Beauty AB is a growing beauty salon offering a wide range of beauty and wellness treatments. Our services include nail care, manicures, pedicures, massage, head spa, and other beauty treatments. As our business continues to expand, we are looking for a dedicated, versatile, and motivated Beauty Therapist to join our team. Job Responsibilities We are seeking a candidate with experience in multiple areas of the beauty industry who can work flexibly across different treatments and responsibilities. Your duties may include: Manicures, pedicures, and professional nail care. Nail strengthening and nail extensions using gel and other nail products. Massage and body treatments. Head spa and scalp care treatments. Hair washing and scalp massage. Other beauty and personal care treatments. We particularly value candidates with a broad range of practical skills who can perform several types of beauty treatments. You do not need to be an expert in every area, but you should have solid practical experience and a willingness to learn and develop additional skills. Qualifications and Requirements We are looking for someone who: Has professional training or documented work experience in the beauty industry. Has experience in one or more areas, such as nail care, massage, pedicures, head spa, or scalp treatments. Has a genuine passion for beauty, health, and wellness. Demonstrates excellent customer service and communication skills. Is dedicated, detail-oriented, responsible, and flexible. Can work both independently and as part of a team. Is willing and able to learn new treatments and develop additional professional skills. Previous experience in multiple beauty treatments will be considered a strong advantage. Personal Qualities We are looking for a positive, motivated, and versatile individual who enjoys a varied working environment. You should be service-minded, professional, and comfortable working in a salon where quality, customer satisfaction, and teamwork are highly valued. We appreciate employees who are eager to develop their professional skills and contribute to a flexible, supportive, and efficient team. Employment Conditions Position: Multi-Skilled Beauty Therapist Employment Type: Permanent employment (subject to agreement). Working Hours: Full-time. Work Schedule: As agreed. Salary: Fixed monthly, weekly, or hourly salary, depending on the employment agreement. Workplace: Tobo Beauty AB, Stockholm, Sweden. How to Apply Are you a versatile beauty professional with a passion for beauty, wellness, and excellent customer service? We would love to hear from you! Please send your CV and a brief introduction describing your professional experience, qualifications, and practical skills to: Email: tobobeauty@gmail.com We look forward to receiving your application and welcoming a new member to our team!
