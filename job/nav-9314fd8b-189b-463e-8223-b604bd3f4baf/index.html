Job Purpose:  For our global SCU team within Controls & Automation we are looking for a motivated engineering professional to join our diverse team of highly competent engineers. In Controls & Automation we execute subsea control system project deliveries, including design, engineering, fabrication, and system integration. The Subsea Control Unit (SCU) is a topside located unit that provides operator control and monitoring of subsea wells and manifolds. Job Description: • Be the responsible engineer for the Subsea Control Unit (SCU) in delivery projects • Take charge of the SCU detailed engineering during project execution, including preparation of SCU software functionality specifications, SCU cabinet hardware specifications, and other technical documentation • Be the technical point of contact towards the SCU 3rd party supplier, ensuring delivery in accordance with our design input • Witness the SCU Factory Acceptance Test (FAT) at the SCU 3rd party supplier's facility to verify SCU functionality and HMI graphics • Support the Extended Factory Acceptance Test (EFAT) for the SCU part executed at TechnipFMC's facility • Collaborate closely with the global multidisciplinary project team • Participate in technical reviews and workshops • Some travel will be required You are meant for this job if: • Hold a Master’s or Bachelor’s degree, or other relevant education, within Control Systems, Automation Engineering, Information Technology, Electrical Engineering, Systems Engineering, or a related field • Preferably have 2-5 years of work experience within Control System Engineering • Can handle a 3rd party SCU supplier • Can support the Controls Lead Engineer in interactions with the Client • Have high level of autonomy in task execution • Are analytical and have excellent attention to details • Are solution oriented and structured in your approach to complex challenges • Are proactive and continuously learn from every situation • Have relation building abilities and work well in a team-oriented environment • Have excellent command of English, both written and spoken Your future at TechnipFMC: • Your journey starts with our onboarding program • As an international company, we provide you with great opportunities for internal growth • You will have the opportunity to attend development activities • We have benefits like company cabins, in-house gym, and sports teams   • You will be secured by good insurance and retirements plans  • Collaborative and innovative atmosphere that promotes teamwork.
