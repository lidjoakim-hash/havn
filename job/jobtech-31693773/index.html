We provide industry solutions to our customer through our dedicated development center in Bangalore (India) and Stockholm (Sweden). ob Description The TPM – Electrical Buildings is responsible for the technical coordination, planning, design follow-up, construction support, and handover of all electrical buildings within the project. The role ensures that all electrical buildings are delivered in compliance with project requirements, safety regulations, schedule, budget, quality expectations, and applicable standards. The TPM acts as the primary technical interface between Electrical, Civil, Structural, Mechanical, HVAC, Fire Protection, Telecom, Security, PMC, Engineering Contractors, Construction, and Commissioning teams. Main Responsibilities Technical Coordination Own and coordinate all technical activities related to Electrical Buildings. Ensure interfaces between Electrical, Civil, Structural, Mechanical, HVAC, Fire Protection and Telecom disciplines are properly managed. Coordinate design reviews and technical decisions affecting electrical buildings. Follow up building layouts, equipment arrangements, constructability, maintainability, and access requirements. Ensure alignment between engineering, procurement, construction, and commissioning activities. Engineering Management Review engineering deliverables and ensure design maturity. Monitor progress of design activities performed by consultants and suppliers. Identify and resolve design clashes and interface issues. Ensure compliance with applicable project requirements, standards, and regulations. Support technical evaluations and design optimization initiatives. Construction & Site Activities Support construction planning and sequencing. Coordinate building readiness for electrical installation activities. Follow progress of contractors and suppliers. Participate in site inspections, technical walkdowns, and quality reviews. Support resolution of site technical issues and engineering clarifications. Stakeholder Management Act as technical focal point for PMC, engineering and contractors, consultants, and suppliers. Coordinate technical alignment meetings and follow up actions. Support decision-making and issue resolution across disciplines. Communicate status, risks, and technical concerns to project management and stakeholders. Commissioning & Handover Ensure buildings are ready for energization and commissioning. Support punch-list management and close-out activities. Coordinate handover documentation and turnover packages. Support commissioning teams during testing, energization, and start-up phases.
