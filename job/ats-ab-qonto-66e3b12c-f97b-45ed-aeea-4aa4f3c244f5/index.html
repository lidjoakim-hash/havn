MISSION Help keep Qonto’s Bonne Nouvelle HQ running smoothly for our Paris teams. Working side by side with our Hospitality Manager, you’ll handle day-to-day workplace operations and support Agathe on workplace projects. Expect about 70% daily operations and 30% project assistance, with real responsibility and guidance along the way. Start date: As soon as possible 📍 Paris-based, fully on-site. Remote is not available for this role. ➡️ WHAT YOU’LL DO - Keep the site running: Work in tandem with Lindsay, our Hospitality Manager. Do office rounds, talk to Qontoers, and track issues until they’re resolved. - Follow up our service providers: Check cleaning and security tasks, share feedback, and escalate to Agathe when needed. - Support event logistics: Help with practical setup and make sure cleaning and security teams have the right information before and after each event. - Keep operations organized: Update Notion documentation, create purchase orders, follow up invoices, and support basic budget tracking. - Learn through project support: Assist Agathe on workplace project coordination tasks alongside your core operational work. ➡️ WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR - You communicate clearly in French and English: You ask questions early, flag issues, and adapt your updates to colleagues and providers. - You’re organized and reliable: You follow through, care about details, and can mix on-site tasks with admin follow-up. - You have a service mindset: You enjoy helping people and making a workplace welcoming. Experience in hospitality, customer service, or office operations is a plus. - You’re hands-on and adaptable: You like being on-site, work well in a team, and stay calm when priorities change. - You’re ready to learn: You’re comfortable with shared digital tools (Notion, Slack, Google Workspace) and eager to grow in operations and project coordination. No specific academic background required. ➡️ WHAT WE CAN OFFER YOU - Practical Workplace experience: See how a large headquarters runs, from provider coordination to operational administration. - Real responsibility, with support: Own agreed operational tasks while learning from Agathe and working closely with Lindsay. - Exposure across teams: Work with many Qontoers and external partners, building your communication, service, and coordination skills. ➡️ YOUR FUTURE MANAGER You’ll report to Agathe Voulfow https://www.linkedin.com/in/agathevoulfow/, ou
