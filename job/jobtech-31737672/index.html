Are you passionate about creating meaningful customer experiences and making sure they deliver value for our customers and Axis? We're looking for a Customer Visit Manager to help shape the future of customer visits at Axis headquarters in Lund. Customer visits are a powerful way to build advocacy, strengthen relationships and create business opportunities - and you'll help make every visit count. Join our team Sales Enablement and Customer Services is part of Global Sales and Marketing. We help customers, partners and colleagues succeed in their work with Axis. You'll join a team that values collaboration and customer focus, working closely with your customer visit colleagues and colleagues across our regions and HQ. Together, you'll make customer visits more purposeful and valuable: understanding what each visit should achieve and creating an experience that meets customer needs while supporting Axis's business goals. What will you do as Customer Visit Manager? Working together with your customer visit colleagues and stakeholders across Axis, you'll help create personalized, memorable and professionally enriching visits that support customer needs and shared business goals. You'll contribute to the strategic development of the visit process, balancing exceptional customer experiences with efficient ways of working. You'll help coordinate visits from planning through delivery, including master scheduling and adapting when plans change. While customers are at HQ, you'll help ensure their visit runs smoothly by bringing together the right people and resources. By tailoring each experience to customers' priorities, you'll help strengthen relationships and create opportunities for both our customers and Axis. Your responsibilities include: Contributing to the strategic development of the customer visit process at our headquarters in Lund. Supporting the planning and coordination of customer visits, including master scheduling. Collaborating with your customer visit colleagues, account managers, subject matter experts and colleagues across the global sales and marketing organization to create tailored, high-quality experiences. Welcoming and supporting customers during their time at HQ, helping ensure their visit runs smoothly and delivers a positive, engaging experience. Contributing to the ongoing development of the visitor experience and future initiatives. Who are we looking for? You're service-minded and collaborative, with the ability to connect customer needs to business objectives. You enjoy bringing people together, creating clarity and improving how things work. You're comfortable taking initiative and influencing across teams, even when you don't have formal authority. We'd also like you to be: Commercially minded, with strong business acumen and the ability to connect customer needs and visit objectives to business value. Strategic in your thinking, with strong planning and organizational skills. Curious and skilled at understanding customer needs and context. An agile problem-solver who can balance consistency with regional flexibility. Confident communicating and collaborating in international, multicultural environments. Comfortable building relationships across functions and levels. A team player who enjoys creating results together. What we offer A career in a fast-growing, global and constantly developing IT company that takes pride in delivering world-class products, services and solutions to our customers. A strong company culture based on openness, collaboration and innovation, where your personality and contribution are welcome and valued. Excellent opportunities to develop in an organization that supports professional and individual growth. A role where your efforts can have a real impact on the overall success of Axis.
