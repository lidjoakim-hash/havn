HI, WE’RE BACK MARKET. We’re here to help make tech reliable, affordable, and better than new. We're a global marketplace for refurbished devices, helping lower our collective environmental impact by providing trustworthy, affordable tech with 92% less carbon emissions than new. Yep, you read that right. Turns out refurbished tech is way better for the planet than new. In fact, With every device purchased on Back Market, our positive impact on the planet grows. From our Customer Care representatives to our software engineer, every individual at Back Market cuts the planet — and consumers — a break. Our mission is simple: to do more with what we already have. Are you ready to join us? We are looking for a passionate and motivated Product Manager Apprentice to join our Customer Journey Team as part of the Checkout Squad.   This apprenticeship offers a unique opportunity to improve our shopping funnel on web and app, ensuring it’s as seamless and delightful an experience as possible. You will do so by helping to run AB tests on the user interface to uncover the most impactful features.   You will work closely with cross-functional teams, including other Product Managers in the customer teams, Operations, Engineering, Content and Design, to drive impactful product initiatives. YOUR MISSION (IF YOU ACCEPT IT): 🥇 - Support Product Development: Participate across the product lifecycle: solution ideation, prioritization, development, and launch. - Handle Delivery Management: Write detailed specifications and user stories, oversee the implementation of features with IT engineers and perform quality assurance (QA). - Autonomously run AB tests to optimize the conversion rate from product page to checkout. Scope the test, analyse and interpret the results to uncover the best solutions. - Collaborate with Teams: Work closely with PMs, designers and marketing to challenge and prioritize feature requests, ensuring that our features are adopted seamlessly and add value to users (customers, sellers). - Monitor performances: Ensure each feature’s performance can be measured, help estimate the impact and follow up the results post-launch.
