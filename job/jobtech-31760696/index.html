Join a global team at our customer, delivering cutting-edge HVDC Control & Protection software. Here, collaboration is key: you will work in unified project teams designed to simplify knowledge sharing and accelerate your professional growth. About the role As a Software Project Engineer, you will join a global team specializing in HVDC Control & Protection Software. You will play a key role in modernization projects, collaborating across IT/OT, hardware, and operations. This role focuses on delivering high-quality software solutions while fostering a strong culture of mutual learning. You are offeredYou will be provided with the tools and mentorship needed to drive your own professional development, alongside opportunities for international travel and working in a highly collaborative, supportive team environment. Work tasks You will design, integrate, and commission software functions for HVDC modernization projects, collaborating closely with international clients and cross-functional teams. Participate in all phases of HVDC modernization project delivery, from design to commissioning. Design, test, integrate, and verify software functions for safe power transmission. Travel internationally on short-term assignments for commissioning and customer meetings. Collaborate closely with customers and provide them with system training. Plan and organize daily engineering activities with a strong sense of ownership. Solve complex technical challenges in cooperation with cross-functional team members. We are looking for Degree in Electrical Engineering, Software Engineering, or a related technical field Professional proficiency in English, both written and spoken Basic knowledge or interest in software development and programming Basic knowledge or interest in digital control theory, signal processing, and filtering It is meritorious if you have Skills in Swedish, both written and spoken Experience within HVDC or the electrical power industry, such as AC substation maintenance To succeed in the role, your personal skills are: Social Orderly Responsible Intellectually curious Our recruitment process This recruitment process is handled by Academic Work and it is our client’s wish that all questions regarding the position is directed to Academic Work. Our selection process is continuous and the advert may close before the recruitment process is completed if we have moved forward to the next phase.
