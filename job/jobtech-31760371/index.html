Experienced Completions Engineer Coordinator required to support construction completion, mechanical completion, pre-commissioning, commissioning and handover across a multi-disciplinary EPCM project transitioning green energy technology into steel manufacturing. The role provides hands-on field coordination and verification, including Inspection Test Record (ITR) management, punch list close-out, system readiness checks and turnover documentation. Working closely with Construction, Quality, Engineering, Commissioning, Operations, vendors, EPCs and subcontractors, the role ensures equipment and systems are safely installed, tested, documented and ready for controlled handover. Key Responsibilities Field Completion & Inspection Perform field walkdowns and verify mechanical, piping, electrical, instrumentation, controls and utility installations against approved drawings, specifications, vendor requirements and project standards. Coordinate and witness construction completion and pre-commissioning inspections across assigned systems, subsystems and work fronts. Verify inspection readiness, including access, permits, isolations, procedures, calibrated test equipment, materials and vendor support. Identify installation defects, incomplete work, and drawing discrepancies, and coordinate resolution with Engineering, Construction, Quality, and contractors. Mechanical Completion & ITRs Execute, review and verify ITRs, check sheets and supporting evidence for construction, mechanical completion and pre-commissioning activities. Ensure ITRs are correctly allocated to equipment tags, systems, disciplines and work packs within the completions management system. Verify test data, signatures, dates, attachments, red-line mark-ups, punch references and acceptance/witness approvals. Support preparation and verification of mechanical completion certificates and system readiness packs. Pre-Commissioning, Commissioning & Start-Up Support cleaning, flushing, pressure/leak testing, drying, reinstatement, loop checks, functional testing, energisation readiness and equipment run testing. Confirm prerequisites, permits, isolations, test packs, procedures, tools, consumables and vendor support are available before testing. Record test results, equipment status, deviations and actions, ensuring commissioning records are correctly uploaded and linked. Support dynamic commissioning and start-up, including troubleshooting, reinstatement, retesting and technical issue close-out. Punch List Management Raise clear, evidence-based punch items for incomplete, defective or non-compliant work. Categorise, prioritise and assign punch items to the correct tag, system, drawing, ITR or work pack. Coordinate punch close-out with EPCs, subcontractors, vendors, and discipline teams, and verify remedial work before closure. Escalate overdue, high-risk or commissioning-critical items and recommend recovery actions. Systemisation, Turnover & Handover Apply approved system/subsystem boundaries, turnover priorities and commissioning sequences. Support development and field validation of system boundary drawings, tag lists, Work Execution Flows (WEFs), turnover packages and completion dossiers. Participate in mechanical completion, pre-commissioning and Ready for Commissioning walkdowns. Confirm required certificates, test reports, red-line drawings, vendor documentation, punch status and preservation records are complete before turnover. Completions Systems & Documentation Maintain accurate completions data covering tag status, ITR progress, punch items, certificates, test records and handover documentation. Monitor ITR status, inspection backlog, punch trends, documentation gaps and readiness constraints. Ensure records are accurate, traceable, audit-ready and compliant with project quality and document-control requirements. Compile completion dossiers, turnover packages and final handover records.
