WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR Solutions Operations is the internal capability layer behind Braze's Solutions Consulting organization. We build the tooling, systems, and telemetry that let the field move faster — so that what works in one region reaches the others while the deal is still live, and no Solutions Consultant rebuilds something that already exists. This role exists because the work has changed. Technical Solutions Engineering was built to produce demo assets on request — custom in-app messages, Liquid, web forms, browser scripts. AI-assisted development has compressed most of that, and the field now builds a good deal of it themselves. The opportunity is no longer producing artifacts on request; it's owning the systems that make the whole organization faster, better informed, and ultimately more effective. This is an operations role with real technical depth: instead of building demo tooling, you'll build business systems, and you'll use data from across the global SolCon org to decide what's worth building at all. You'll be based in EMEA deliberately. A core part of this role is having a point of view that spans regions — seeing how an AMER enterprise team operates versus an EMEA one, understanding why one pocket of the org wins and another doesn't, and turning that into something reusable rather than leaving it buried in a single deal or function.
