Process Engineer (Team leader)

Retyre AS · Oslo, Norway

Join the future of Sustainable Manufacturing at reTyre! At reTyre, you’re not just joining a company — you’re joining a groundbreaking industrial journey together with a great team of innovators. We’ve built the world’s first sustainable tire factory, right in the heart of Ski, 20 min outside of Oslo. We are now looking for a skilled and hands-on Process Engineer with Management skills to join the Manufacturing team and drive our mission forward. Our factory uses a revolutionary production method that makes it possible to replace traditional rubber in pneumatic tires with new, sustainable materials. Our team is internationally diverse, young, energetic, and united by a passion for innovation, sustainability, and growth. Every day, we challenge the conventional and push the boundaries of what manufacturing can be — for the benefit of people and the planet.   The company language is English, and the position demands strong written and spoken English skills. The role This role is essential for ensuring that our production processes and machines run efficiently and reliably. The Process Engineer will work daily on the shop floor to test and improve processes, troubleshoot issues, and create accurate documentation and Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs). The role will also function as the Team leader for a shift of Operators in Production (up to 3 people). This position will be working on a rotating 3-shift schedule. Its an advantage if you have experience from the bicycle tire industry. What You’ll Do You will play a key role in improving our production line, including: • Test and validate production processes and machine settings. • Continuously improve process efficiency and stability. • Diagnose and resolve process and machine-related issues promptly. • Create and maintain accurate process documentation and SOPs. Actively work on machines to implement improvements and ensure optimal performance. • Work closely with Manufacturing Engineers, Quality Engineers, and R&D teams to support industrialization and quality initiatives. • Be the responsible leader on the shift. Who We’re Looking For Someone who is motivated and enthusiastic for the opportunity to have a  hands-on role with direct impact on process performance. You are experienced in a Manufacturing/Production and feel confident entering this role with your existing skillsets and are still hungry for further growth and development. You find it motivating to be a part of a Company's journey going from startup to scaleup, that offers a different kind of environment than other typical traditional manufacturing companies offer. Experience matters — but so does attitude and passion. Qualifications • Technical diploma or degree in Mechatronics, Mechanical Engineering, or related field. • Strong hands-on experience with production machinery and process optimization. • Excellent troubleshooting and problem-solving skills. • Ability to work independently and in a team-oriented environment. • Familiarity with lean manufacturing principles is a plus. • Proactive and adaptable in a start-up environment. • Strong English communication skills, both written and spoken. • Decition making abilities. Team coordination and management experience. Personal Qualities • Proactive and solution-oriented. • Motivated by innovation and fast-changing environments. • Flexible and comfortable working in a modern, small-team environment with real influence. • Precise, organized, structured and good work ethics. • Good communication skills. • Independent and self-driven. • A positive team player who contributes to a great work culture.
