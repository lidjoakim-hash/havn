About The Role We are hiring a Recruiting Sourcer to join our EMEA Talent team, based in London. This is an in-office role. You will own the top of the funnel for hiring across the region, partnering closely with recruiters and hiring managers to find, engage and convert exceptional people who aren't looking for us yet. You will be expected to operate with urgency, creativity and high judgment. You will not simply run searches. You will build our outbound engine: mapping markets, mining our networks, showing up where great people are and turning cold prospects into engaged candidates. The ideal person is a curious, resourceful hunter who measures success by pipeline quality, not volume, and knows how to create momentum in ambiguous, fast-moving markets.
