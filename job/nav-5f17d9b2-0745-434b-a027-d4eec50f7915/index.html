Are you ready to be at the forefront of digital transformation, working with leading-edge technology? We need a curious mind that thrives on complexities. If your heart says &#34;Yes!&#34;, then CLEVR has the perfect role for you. We're on the lookout for a passionate Product Lifecycle Management engineer! Hva vi ser etter What you bring to the table: • A degree (M.Sc./M.Eng.) in a relevant field (Mechanical engineering, robotics, product development, etc. is preferred), with a strong mechanical or IT affinity. • Being familiar with CAD software,PowerShell,.XML, .NET, JSON & JavaScript is a plus; • Simple and effective communication skills, with curiosity and zest for learning new things fast; • Fluency in Norwegian and good in English both written and spoken. This employment may require security clearance by the Norwegian National Security Authority. For more information visit www.nsm.no. Arbeidsoppgaver You will be working in an agile team with some of the best in the business: we have been awarded as &#34;Smart Expert Partners&#34; by our partner and customer, Siemens. You will be collaborating, interacting, innovating, and creating solutions across different projects. Besides the great responsibility and flexibility that the job offers, you will also have the opportunity to keep on sharpening your skills if you feel like joining a training or workshop. In this role, you will work to understand the customer's challenges, needs, and requirements, helping them find effective solutions. You will contribute to customizing and supporting the implementation of Teamcenter to meet each customer's unique needs while staying up to date with the latest developments in PLM. (PLM for manufacturing) Your role will include: • Gaining understanding of customer challenges, needs, and requirements. • Leading and supporting Teamcenter configuration activities, ensuring that the system is aligned with each customer's business processes and workflows. • Working as a consultant on a broad range of projects across the oil and gas, maritime, green energy, and retail sectors. • Collaborating with cross-functional teams to innovate, create solutions, and produce technical documentation, while proactively supporting colleagues. • Partnering with other PLM Engineers and Project Managers to efficiently solve problems using tools such as Teamcenter and NX. • Customizing, supporting, and implementing software upgrades tailored to each customer's specific needs, while staying up to date with the latest developments in PLM. • Participating in Teamcenter implementation projects, from requirement gathering and system setup to testing and deployment.
