ABOUT AIRWALLEX Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own. We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.   HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT You’ll own the commercial operating backbone for Airwallex’s Germany and DACH business, turning performance data, territory planning, sales tooling and inbound optimisation into scalable revenue operations. You’ll partner closely with commercial leaders as their local strategy partner, providing weekly performance and forecasting support, running country QBRs and driving improvements across the business. You’ll lead go-to-market and expansion initiatives across EMEA, including product-market-fit assessment, product localisation, competitor analysis, win-rate analysis and vertical experimentation. You’ll align stakeholders across Sales, Marketing, Product, Operations, Compliance and Legal, while using AI-driven tools and automation to improve commercial workflows and reporting. You’ll work from our Berlin office three days a week. WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR Essentials - 5+ years of experience, ideally combining top-tier strategy consulting and/or high-growth startup experience with meaningful time focused on commercial strategy or revenue operations. - Strong analytical skills, including data modelling and experience working with data reporting tools. - Experience owning processes end to end, building scalable systems and tooling, and maintaining high standards of data quality and hygiene. - A track record of driving tangible outcomes with a bias for action and a high level of personal accountability. - Strong strategic thinking and the ability to simplify and communicate complex problems clearly. - Excellent written, verbal and presentation skills. - Demonstrated ability to speak, read, write and understand German and English at a native or near-native level. - Experience influencing without authority and building trust with senior stakeholders across functions and cultures. Preferred - Experience in a high-growth fintech, financial services or technology environment. - Experience with complex, cross-functional commercial operations and scaling go-to-market processes. - An outstanding record of academic achievement. - Experience using AI-driven tools or automation to improve commercial workflows and reporting. - Experience leading product-market-fit assessment, product localisation, competitor analysis, win-rate analysis or vertical experimentation. YOU'LL THRIVE HERE IF - You're comfortable owning the roadmap yourself. - You like defining your own space rather than staying in one narrow lane. - You're comfortable making decisions with incomplete information and moving quickly anyway. - You connect dots across functions and hold the big picture without losing grip on the details. - You're low-ego.
