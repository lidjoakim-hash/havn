Learning Experience Consultant for an International Transformation Project

Adecco Sweden Aktiebolag · Göteborg, Sweden

💰 ca. 410 000–480 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 17.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

About the role We are looking for an experienced Retail Sales Academy & Learning Journey Design Partner to support the development of a practical and scalable Retail Sales Academy for a global organization within the consumer goods sector. The assignment is focused on transforming an existing Retail Sales Job Architecture and Skills Building Framework into structured, role-based and skill-based learning journeys that are ready for implementation in a modern Learning Experience Platform (LXP). The Academy will support approximately 1,500 employees across more than 20 markets and cover the areas of Commercial Management, Account Management, Field Sales and Sales Enablement. You will work independently with significant ownership of the project while collaborating closely with stakeholders across multiple functions and markets. In your role as Retail Sales Academy & Learning Journey Design Partner, your main responsibilities will include: Defining the Academy structure, learner navigation and curriculum architecture. Reviewing and evaluating existing learning content and mapping relevant materials to roles, skills and proficiency levels. Designing engaging, role-based learning journeys with clear learning outcomes, progression and practical application. Identifying capability gaps and recommending where existing content should be adapted or supplemented. Developing a scalable language and localization approach that balances English master content with local-language learning assets. Preparing learning-path specifications, metadata, taxonomy structures and implementation documentation. Supporting knowledge transfer and handover to ensure long-term ownership and maintenance of the Academy. Collaborating with subject-matter experts, managers and other stakeholders to validate learning needs and proposed solutions. This position is a consulting assignment with an expected duration of approximately 4 months, starting as soon as possible. The role is based at the company´s office in Mölndal. About you To succeed in this role, you have extensive experience designing corporate academies, curriculum frameworks and role-based learning journeys. You are comfortable translating business requirements, competency frameworks and capability needs into practical and scalable learning solutions that create measurable business value. You have likely led similar projects before and can quickly familiarize yourself with existing structures, identify stakeholder needs and drive progress independently. You are confident working across multiple functions and markets, building relationships and engaging the right people to gather insights and move initiatives forward. You have a structured and pragmatic approach, with a strong belief in leveraging existing learning resources before developing new content. You understand how learning journeys should connect formal learning, practical application, manager support and peer learning to drive real capability development. Important qualifications for the role: Proven experience designing corporate academies, curricula and learning journey frameworks. Experience translating competency models, skills frameworks and proficiency levels into learning solutions. Experience reviewing, curating and evaluating large learning content libraries. Experience supporting learning programs across multiple countries and languages. Experience creating implementation-ready documentation, governance models and handover material. Fluent in English, both written and spoken. Previous hands-on experience with Cornerstone and Learning Experience Platforms (LXP) is considered an advantage.
