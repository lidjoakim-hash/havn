Now we’re building the delivery of (almost) everything and you’ll find us in over 500 cities in 30 countries around the world. In 2022 we joined forces with DoorDash and together we keep on dreaming big and expanding across the globe. Working at Wolt isn’t always easy, but it’s definitely exciting. So, if you’re a self-starter with drive and entrepreneurial spirit, this could be the ride of your life. About the Role The role you’re reading about is part of Wolt Market, our own grocery stores that deliver outstanding convenience and world-leading customer service. Wolt Market is a fantastic concept: customers can order groceries and have them delivered at lightning speed! We are looking for Grocery Associates to join our fantastic team at Wolt Market Trondheim. If you are 16 or older, you are warmly welcome to apply! In this role, you will be at the core of our daily operations, making sure that all orders coming in from customers are picked and ready to be collected by our courier partners. Our store is open 7 days a week, which means you must be able to work weekends and at different times during Wolt Market’s opening and delivery hours. If you are a team player, enjoy working independently, and have a good sense of humor, you might be the person we’re looking for! What you’ll do • Receive orders through our app • Pick and pack groceries and hand them to courier partners • Receive food and supplies delivered by our suppliers • Manage our internal systems, which contain accurate information about nutrients and allergens • Keep the store tidy and clean Our expectations • You are proactive and a fast learner with the ability to multitask • Flexibility to work evening shifts, including weekends and public holidays. Our Wolt Market is open 07:00-00:00. Your working hours will end by 21:00 at the latest. • You are located in the Trondheim area and have the right to work in Norway • You are fluent in English. What you get by joining us • Wolt credits cashback benefit • Employee assistance program • Wolt-wide training programs Next steps The store address is Fjordgata 56-58, 7010 Trondheim.
