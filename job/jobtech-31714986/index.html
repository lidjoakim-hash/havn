Join Galderma and Help Shape the Future of Pharmaceutical Manufacturing At Galderma, we are committed to advancing dermatology through science, innovation, and operational excellence. As we continue to invest in our manufacturing capabilities and digital transformation journey, we are looking for an Automation Engineer to join our growing team in Uppsala. This is a unique opportunity to be at the forefront of pharmaceutical automation. You will work with advanced technologies in a highly invested and transformation-driven manufacturing environment, where innovation is encouraged and continuous improvement is part of the everyday culture. The Role As an Automation Engineer at Galderma, you will play a key role in driving the reliability, performance, and continuous development of our manufacturing automation systems. Working closely with Production, Engineering, Quality, and other cross-functional teams, you will support and enhance critical systems including PLCs, HMIs, SCADA platforms, MES integrations, and related infrastructure. In this role, you will troubleshoot complex technical challenges, implement system upgrades, and contribute to the successful delivery of automation and capital investment projects. You will help ensure that our manufacturing operations remain GMP-compliant while identifying opportunities to improve efficiency, reliability, and digital capabilities across the site. As Galderma continues its growth journey, you will have the opportunity to influence the future of manufacturing by introducing modern automation solutions and supporting the development of a more connected, data-driven, and smart production environment.
