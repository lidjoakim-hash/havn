Veritaz is a leading IT staffing solutions provider in Sweden, committed to advancing individual careers and aiding employers in ensuring the perfect talent fit. With a proven track record of successful partnerships with top companies, we have rapidly grown our presence in the USA, Europe, and Sweden as a dependable and trusted resource within the IT industry. Job Overview Assignment Description We are looking for an experienced Electrical Development Engineer to support the development of customized bus chassis and customer-specific vehicle adaptations. You will design and develop electrical systems and cable harnesses, create electrical schematics and 3D routing solutions, and ensure technical solutions are ready for production. You will take ownership of assigned projects from initial concept through to production. You will work in a cross-functional engineering team, collaborating with electrical and mechanical engineers, production teams, suppliers and colleagues across different locations. What You Will Work On Develop electrical systems and cable harnesses for customized bus chassis. Design electrical schematics and 3D cable routing solutions. Develop and adapt technical solutions based on customer requirements. Take ownership of projects from concept and design through to production. Ensure electrical designs meet production requirements. Collaborate with engineering teams, production and suppliers. Identify improvements and challenge existing technical solutions. What You Bring University degree in Electrical Engineering, Mechatronics or equivalent. At least 8 years of relevant experience in a similar role. R&D experience in the automotive or heavy vehicle industry. Practical experience in electrical development and cable harness design. Experience with electrical schematics and electrical system design. Experience with Saber and/or CATIA. Good verbal and written English communication skills. Meritorious Specialization in electrical systems, automotive engineering or cable harness design. Experience with both low-voltage and high-voltage systems. Experience taking technical solutions from concept through design to production. Experience working in cross-functional engineering teams. Swedish language skills.
