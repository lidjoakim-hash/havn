SLSQ427R384 At Databricks, we help public institutions use data and AI to improve services, increase productivity, and get more value from public data and technology investment. That means putting trusted, relevant insight closer to the people making operational, policy and service decisions, and building services that can evolve as missions, technology and delivery models change, rather than becoming tomorrow's legacy. Databricks is looking for an Account Executive to own and grow some of our most strategic Public Sector customers in the UK government delivering against their core missions. You will inherit an established portfolio of strategic government accounts, with a clear mandate to protect and expand that base while building new relationships across departments and agencies we do not yet serve. This is a succession role: you will step into relationships and momentum that already exist, building trust quickly with current stakeholders while developing your own view of where Data and AI can help solve the sector's most important challenges. You will translate national and departmental priorities into measurable customer outcomes and sustainable growth. Working closely with government stakeholders, internal teams, cloud providers, and strategic partners, you will deepen consumption in existing accounts and extend Databricks' footprint. The Pressures Shaping This Work Government's hardest missions increasingly require insight across institutional boundaries, without requiring those institutions, their accountability, or their data to be centralised. The opportunity isn't one giant government data system; it's enabling institutions to connect, govern and use data and insight across boundaries while retaining control of their own data, responsibilities and decisions. You'll be selling into that reality. The Impact You Will Have • Take ownership of an existing portfolio of strategic government accounts, maintaining continuity with current stakeholders and expanding usage of the Databricks Intelligence Platform. • Build and execute a go-to-market plan for the territory, prioritising the departments, agencies, use cases, and partners most likely to create sustainable growth. • Identify and pursue net-new opportunities across departments and agencies not yet engaged with Databricks, building pipeline alongside your existing portfolio. • Develop a clear view of national and departmental priorities and translate them into compelling Data and AI value propositions, executive conversations, business cases, and qualified opportunities. • Use data and evidence to continuously refine your territory strategy, testing assumptions about where demand exists and which use cases resonate, and changing course when the evidence tells you to. • Map and engage the full range of buying centres within each account, building champions, guiding opportunities forward, and compressing decision cycles. • Run a consumption-led sales motion, using demand plans to prioritise the highest-value use cases in each account and grow usage over time rather than closing one-off deals. • Manage a portfolio that spans quick wins and longer, complex public-sector sales cycles simultaneously. • Build and defend a strong renewal and expansion motion within existing accounts as a foundation for new growth. • Orchestrate cross-functional and partner resources, including professional services, training, systems integrators, and hyperscaler co-sell teams, to maximise account impact. • Represent Databricks credibly with senior government stakeholders, acting as a trusted advisor who puts customer outcomes first.
