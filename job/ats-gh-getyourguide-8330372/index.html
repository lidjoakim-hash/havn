Team mission Business Operations & Strategy exists to drive GetYourGuide's growth by connecting the right experiences with the right travelers — at scale. We work across the full value chain, from supplier partnerships to traveler experience, ensuring that our marketplace remains the world's most trusted and comprehensive destination for unforgettable activities The Categories team takes a category-specific focus on the experiences that matter most to travelers. Our mission is to own and grow a portfolio of Entrance Ticket categories — spanning both established and emerging segments — with a singular vision: to curate the world's most exceptional customer experience and inventory at the most visited attractions in the world, such as Disneyland, the Eiffel Tower, the Louvre Museum, and Broadway. These locations behave differently from other destinations and require customised, cross-functional strategies to unlock their full potential. Your mission • Own the strategy, roadmap, and performance of one or more categories, setting clear goals, milestones, and success metrics. • Build an in-depth understanding of the industry and craft our value proposition to suppliers and travelers. • Assess and prioritise sub-categories and opportunities based on market research, traveler demand, and financial opportunity. • Develop and execute the go-to-market approach for new and emerging segments, from launch to scale. • Partner closely with Product, Marketing, Sales, and other teams to ensure seamless and successful launches. • Run data analyses to identify and act on growth opportunities, and share findings with senior stakeholders (Directors, VPs, and C-level executives). • Report progress regularly to the Head of Categories and key stakeholders, including the Executive team, driving buy-in and resource allocation. • Contribute to team playbooks and ways of working, and share learnings across categories.
