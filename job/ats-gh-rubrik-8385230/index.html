Job Summary Rubrik’s Product Line Sales organization drives focused growth across Rubrik’s specialized workloads. The Cloud Product Line Sales team owns the cloud data protection and cyber recovery business across AWS, Azure, Google Cloud and OCI, including cloud-native workloads, DevOps platforms (GitHub, Azure DevOps), unstructured data, and Rubrik Cloud Vault. The team partners closely with Core Account Executives, Sales Engineers, Customer Success, channel partners and cloud alliances to bring deep product and technical expertise into every cloud opportunity, from first conversation through technical validation to close. We’re hiring a Cloud Product Line Sales Specialist to own and accelerate Rubrik’s cloud business within a defined set of Core districts. This is a highly technical, quota-carrying overlay role. You will reach personas that Core often doesn’t: Head of Cloud, Platform Engineering Leader, VP of Cloud Infrastructure, and Cloud Cyber Manager.
