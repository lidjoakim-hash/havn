Software Engineer – Managed Service Location: Portugal | 100% Remote | No Travel Required Build software that powers scalable cloud services. At SingleStore, we're building technology that helps businesses manage and access their data with speed, flexibility and reliability. We're looking for a Software Engineer who enjoys building reliable software, solving technical challenges and continuously learning. As part of our Managed Service team, you'll contribute to the backend services that power our cloud-native data platform, helping customers connect to their databases while supporting cluster scalability, performance and reliability. You'll work on meaningful engineering challenges, collaborate across teams and have the autonomy to take ownership of your projects. What You'll Do • Design, develop, test and improve backend services for our cloud-native platform. • Contribute to cluster management, scalability and performance optimization. • Build and enhance services that support customer connectivity to hosted databases. • Investigate technical issues and develop practical, reliable solutions. • Collaborate with engineering and product teams to deliver high-quality features. • Take ownership of your work while continuously expanding your technical knowledge. What We're Looking For • Around 2–4 years of experience in software engineering, with a focus on backend development. • Hands-on experience with at least one of the following programming languages: Go, Python or Rust. • Basic knowledge of Kubernetes and containerized environments. • Familiarity with cloud services and cloud-based applications. • Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to work independently. • A collaborative mindset, curiosity and a genuine interest in learning and building software. Nice to Have • Experience with CI/CD pipelines, such as GitHub Actions or GitLab CI/CD. • Exposure to distributed systems, SaaS platforms or cloud infrastructure. • Knowledge of data management, database connectivity or cluster optimization. • Experience working in a product-focused technology environment. Why Join Us? At SingleStore, you'll be part of a product-driven engineering environment where curiosity, collaboration and continuous learning matter. You'll have the opportunity to work on real technical challenges, contribute to a scalable cloud platform and develop your expertise alongside experienced engineers. We value people who ask questions, take initiative and enjoy building things. You don't need to know everything from day one. What matters is having strong foundations, a willingness to learn and the motivation to build great products. Ready to Build With Us? If you're based in Portugal and excited about backend engineering, cloud technologies and building products that make an impact, we'd love to hear from you. Apply and grow with SingleStore.
