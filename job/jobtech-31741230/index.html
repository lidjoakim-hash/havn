Role Summary This role is a part of TRATON Group R&D, located in Södertälje, Sweden Join us at TRATON Group R&D and become part of a team that is shaping the way of handling new vehicle related legislation. We are responsible for overviewing and handling new legislation, coordinating the Technical Lobbyist network and making valid legislation available in the VW database Getex and also in the product structure OAS. We are part of the Domain Product Compliance within Modular Solutions. As a member of the Regulatory Affairs section, you will play a key role in handling new vehicle legislation from a global perspective. Working across areas such as Active Safety & ADAS, Digital Product Compliance, Electrification and Circularity, you will coordinate legislation activities across regions and collaborate with stakeholders from R&D, Public Affairs, Product Planning and external organizations. The role combines leading Regulatory Clusters, evaluating the impact of future legislation on products, supporting technical lobbying activities, and participating in international industry working groups related to vehicle legislation and compliance. You will be active in some suitable external working groups preferably in the field of Active Safety. Most of our external working group participation is in the global vehicle industry association OICA. This is also very much depending on your back ground and experience. Other working group participation could be discussed depending on your background. Job Responsibilities · Lead (Pilot) the Regulatory Cluster working group for Active Safety & ADAS and provide input to the legal roadmap within these areas. · Coordinate legislation-related activities with colleagues across Europe, Brazil, the US and China, including technical lobbyists, type approval engineers and subject matter experts. · Drive process development and ensure Regulatory Affairs responsibilities are integrated into TRATON One PDP and Regulatory Affairs processes. · Summarize Technical Lobbying Criticality and communicate relevant topics to TRATON Public Affairs when needed. · Participate in external technical lobbying working groups, particularly within OICA GEVA and ACEA, focusing on automated, autonomous and connected vehicle regulations, CSMS/SUMS and related legislative developments. · Monitor Active Safety and ADAS related regulations across Regulatory Clusters and working groups. · Stay informed about company strategy, technology developments and legislative trends to proactively support stakeholders. Who You Are We believe you have: · Experience in regulatory affairs topics and participation in external technical lobbying forums. · Experience working within a product development process, with strong understanding of requirements management and Type Approval. · Ability to transform improvement ideas into practical implementation. · Experience evaluating and interpreting vehicle-related legislation. · A relevant Engineering degree or equivalent practical experience. · A structured way of working combined with high integrity and professionalism. · Strong written and spoken English communication skills.
