Elastic, the Search AI Company, enables everyone to find the answers they need in real time, using all their data, at scale — unleashing the potential of businesses and people. The Elastic Search AI Platform, used by more than 50% of the Fortune 500, brings together the precision of search and the intelligence of AI to enable everyone to accelerate the results that matter. By taking advantage of all structured and unstructured data — securing and protecting private information more effectively — Elastic’s complete, cloud-based solutions for search, security, and observability help organizations deliver on the promise of AI. What is The Role As a 3X global award-winning partner of AWS with deep integrations across AWS services and a growing ecosystem of connectors, Elastic is positioned as the context and retrieval layer of choice for enterprise AI on AWS. EMEA represents approximately one-third of Elastic's global revenue, spanning nine sub-regions, ~200 sellers, and a rapidly growing AWS Marketplace footprint. The AWS relationship in EMEA is both Elastic's greatest strategic growth lever and its most complex partnership to execute — where collaboration and competition coexist across cloud, security, and AI. We are seeking a driven, regionally expert AWS Alliance Director for EMEA to lead execution of the joint Elastic–AWS go-to-market strategy across the region. This is a high-visibility individual contributor leadership role that demands executive presence, deep AWS ecosystem knowledge — ideally from time spent inside AWS — and the operational discipline to turn strategy into revenue. You will own Marketplace growth, co-sell engine activation, new logo acquisition, channel development, and geo expansion across EMEA, directly partnering with AWS EMEA leadership and coordinating a broad internal stakeholder map.
