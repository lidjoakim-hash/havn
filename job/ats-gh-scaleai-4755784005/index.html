Head of EMEA Policy

Scale AI · London, UK, United Kingdom

At Scale, we believe that AI will dramatically improve the world, and our mission is to accelerate the development of AI. As Scale continues to grow its Government Relations and Policy Insights function, we are looking for a Head of EMEA Policy to support our engagement with governments and third parties across the EMEA region. The successful candidate will have deep experience engaging European and/or Middle Eastern policymakers, a history of driving policy and regulatory outcomes, and the ability to connect business objectives to policy wins. You will: • Develop and execute Scale's EMEA policy strategy, in close coordination with Global Public Sector, Comms, Marketing, and other cross-functional partners. • Foster Scale’s relationships with governments across the EMEA region. • Partner with Scale’s Global Public Sector business to facilitate market entry and expansion in priority markets. • Represent Scale with trade associations, standards bodies, and industry coalitions in-region and drive consensus on Scale's priority positions. • Develop relationships and partner with key regional third-party stakeholders, such as think tanks and academic institutions. • Track and advise the business and company leadership on policy and regulatory developments that could impact Scale’s business. • Collaborate effectively with cross-functional stakeholders including Business Units, Communications, Marketing, and others. Ideally you'd have: • Significant experience working in, or closely with government, and a proven track record of shaping policy and building relationships with senior officials. • Deep experience engaging governments in the EMEA region. • Expertise on the political, geopolitical, and regulatory environments within the UK, EU, NATO, and major European economies. • Familiarity with tech policy debates within the EMEA region. • A history of working with third parties such as trade associations and think tanks. • Excellent written, verbal, and public speaking skills. • A history of working in a fast-paced environment and successfully managing multiple works streams simultaneously. • Ability to work cross-functionally with a high degree of autonomy. Nice to haves: • History working within a tech company or start-up environment. • Experience working in/exposure to defense or national security. • Proficiency in multiple languages spoken in the EMEA region. PLEASE NOTE: Our policy requires a 90-day waiting period before reconsidering candidates for the same role. This allows us to ensure a fair and thorough evaluation of all applicants.
