Company description: Epiroc is a leading productivity partner for the mining, infrastructure and natural resources industries. Withcutting-edge technology, Epiroc develops and produces innovative drill rigs, rock excavation and constructionequipment, and provides world-class service and consumables. The company was founded in Stockholm,Sweden, and has passionate people supporting and collaborating with customers in more than 150 countries.Learn more at www.epirocgroup.com. Job description:   Do you want to help shape the next generation of automation solutions for the mining industry? As a Product Owner within Surface Automation, you will play a key role in translating product strategy and customer needs into clear priorities and tangible results together with our development teams. Join Our Team You will become part of Surface Software Engineering, where we develop advanced automation solutions for Epiroc’s surface products. Here, we work across teams, functions, and countries, bringing together diverse perspectives to create solutions that deliver real value to our customers.   Your Mission As a Product Owner, you will be responsible for translating the agreed roadmap, Epics, and objectives into clear team goals and a prioritized backlog. You will represent customer and business value throughout the development process and help the team stay focused on what creates the greatest impact. In this role, you will:  Own and continuously prioritize the team’s backlog based on customer and business value. Translate roadmaps, Epics, and objectives into clear priorities, scope, and team goals.  Balance new functionality with quality, technical debt, maintenance, security, and risk reduction.  Follow development progress and deliveries together with the team, ensuring up-to-date forecasts for Epics and features.  Validate outcomes, gather feedback early, and use insights to adjust priorities.  Coordinate priorities, dependencies, and releases together with other Product Owners and relevant stakeholders. Your ProfileWe are looking for someone who thrives at the intersection of customer, business, and technology. You create structure and clarity even when circumstances change, and you are comfortable making decisions and balancing competing priorities. At the same time, you are a good listener who builds trust through close collaboration with development teams, Product Management, and other stakeholders. To succeed in this role, we believe you have:  Experience working as a Product Owner, in agile product development, or in a similar role within software, automation, or complex technical systems.  The ability to understand customer and business needs and translate them into clear objectives and priorities for development teams.  Strong communication skills and the ability to build alignment, prioritize among competing demands, and drive initiatives to decisions-making. Fluency in English is required, both written and verbally    You do not need to be the person defining the technical solution yourself. You will work closely with developers, architects, and other technical experts who contribute feasibility assessments, estimates, risk analyses, and solution decisions. Your strength lies in creating clarity around needs, value, and priorities. Experience with industrial automation, autonomous solutions, mining operations, or other safety-critical and complex products is considered an advantage. Location and TravelThis position is based in Örebro, Sweden. Travel may be required when needed.   In addition to a culture characterized by growth and development combined with a healthy work-life balance, there are a few things that make us especially proud to work at Epiroc: Global career opportunities.
