Artificial Intelligence for Libraries, Archives, and Museums (AI4LAM) is an international research and development community with more than 1,300 members worldwide. The community is dedicated to advancing the use of artificial intelligence (AI) in, for, and by cultural heritage institutions such as libraries, archives, and museums. AI4LAM works to promote the development and implementation of responsible and trustworthy AI solutions that support cultural development and societal growth toward a better 2030. The National Library plays a significant role in supporting and running this initiative. Would you like to help strengthen the administrative, operational and communication foundations of an international community working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, libraries, archives and museums? AI4LAM is seeking a structured and reliable Administrative Operations Manager to support its Secretary General who works from Slovenia with governance, software, projects and member community contact. The role combines day-to-day administration with responsibility for communication channels, digital platforms and coordinating across an international organisation. The role will be primarily based in Norway, but some international travel will be required.
