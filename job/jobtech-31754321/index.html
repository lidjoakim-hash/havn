Since 2008, Solidum Sverige AB / Barnakademin has helped families across Sweden find reliable, caring babysitters. We offer creative childcare in Swedish and specialize in bilingual babysitting, helping children develop their language skills naturally through play and everyday activities. About the Position We are looking for a caring, responsible, and enthusiastic Polish-speaking babysitter for a family in Sundbyberg, with two children aged 3 and 6. The family needs regular help 2 afternoons per week, from 15:00 to 18:00 (6 hours per week). The role involves picking up the children from preschool/school, bringing them home safely, and spending the afternoon together until their parents return. The family would like their children to maintain and develop their Polish language skills, so we are looking for someone who enjoys speaking Polish with children and making the language a natural part of everyday activities. Your responsibilities will include: Picking up the children from preschool/school and accompanying them home safely Organizing fun, creative, and age-appropriate activities Playing, reading, singing, and communicating with the children in Polish Helping with simple afternoon routines and snacks Creating a warm, safe, and positive environment for the children Who We're Looking For We would love to hear from you if you: Speak Polish at a native or fluent level Communicate confidently in English or Swedish Have previous experience caring for children, professionally or privately Are responsible, reliable, patient, and genuinely enjoy spending time with children Enjoy creative play, outdoor activities, and engaging children in learning Are available 2 weekdays per week, from 15:00 to 18:00 Can commit to the position until at least summer 2027 This position is particularly suitable for university students or anyone looking for a rewarding part-time job alongside their studies or other commitments. What We Offer A regular part-time position with a family in Sundbyberg A supportive and friendly working environment Ongoing guidance and support from our central office A welcoming community of fellow babysitters Written references at the end of your employment Opportunities for additional babysitting assignments with other families Valuable experience working with children in a bilingual environment Available Only One Afternoon per Week? We still encourage you to apply! Even if you cannot commit to both afternoons, we may have other families looking for Polish-speaking babysitters whose schedules match your availability. Apply Today! If you love working with children and would enjoy helping them grow up with the Polish language, we would be happy to hear from you! Please submit your application in English or Swedish, including a brief description of your childcare experience, language skills, and availability. We look forward to welcoming you to Barnakademin!
