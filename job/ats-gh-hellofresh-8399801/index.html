The role HelloFresh is going AI-first, and we need Staff-level strategists to set the direction for that shift across the UX organization. As a Staff UX Strategist & Researcher, you’ll take on the most ambiguous, highest-stakes questions across the customer experience. You’ll decide which problems are worth solving, set the research and strategy standards others follow, and turn uncertainty into decisions that hold up at the executive level. You’ll challenge leadership assumptions before investment is committed, and you’ll leave the organization better at making decisions, not just better informed. You’ll bring a deep point of view on how AI changes research and strategy, and you’ll use it as a genuine partner across the product design lifecycle. Strategy and research are your deepest strengths, and you have enough breadth to set quality bars across interface design, service design, and content. You know when to raise the bar, when to delegate, and when to bring in a specialist. We’re looking for people who make the people around them better as much as they make HelloFresh better. If you don’t tick every box but have the judgment and track record for this level, we’d love to hear from you. Above all, we are looking for people who will make HelloFresh better. We believe there are many different ways of developing skills and we love diverse experiences! So even if you don’t “tick all the boxes” but think you’d thrive in this role, we would really like to learn more about you.
