You’ll make things happen fast. The Infrastructure Engineering group (IE) at ZoomInfo is responsible for the innovation, scaling, and automation that powers our global platforms. We are seeking a FinOps Engineer to join the centralized FinOps team of our enterprise-scale, multi-cloud organization. In this role, you will play a crucial part in optimizing our core GCP and AWS spend, as well as our global suite of third-party tech stacks by driving financial accountability and implementing best practices. Getting hands-on with our infrastructure, you will identify, build, and automate cost-saving opportunities. You will work directly with engineering teams to optimize cloud architectures, implement robust tagging frameworks, and deploy the automated guardrails needed to keep our environments highly efficient What You'll do: • Drive cloud and AI cost optimization across GCP and AWS by identifying, quantifying, and implementing savings opportunities spanning compute, storage, networking, and data services.
