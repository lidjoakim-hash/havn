Part-Time Crew Members Wanted – 50–75% Taco Bell is growing, and we are looking for new Crew Members to join our team! Important: This role includes late weekend closing shifts until approximately 01:30, so you must be able to reliably work these hours and have a suitable way of getting to and from work. Live Más with Taco Bell! We're looking for people who enjoy serving customers, working as part of a team and want to develop their skills in a fast-paced restaurant environment. Whether you're looking to build your first work experience or take the next step in your career, Taco Bell offers the opportunity to learn, grow and progress as we continue expanding across Sweden. We offer: Part-time employment at 50–75% Opportunities to learn, grow and develop A fun, fast-paced team environment Recognition for great performance Opportunities for career progression as Taco Bell continues to grow in Sweden Your responsibilities: As a Team Member, you will help us deliver a great experience to every guest. Your responsibilities will include: Welcoming and serving our guests Taking orders, processing payments and answering customer questions Preparing food according to Taco Bell standards Maintaining high standards of food quality and presentation Following food safety, hygiene and health & safety procedures Keeping the restaurant, equipment and workstations clean and organised Receiving, unloading and stocking products when required Working together with the team to deliver fast, friendly and efficient service Supporting the restaurant during both busy and quieter periods Completing opening and closing duties when required What we're looking for: The ideal candidate is positive, reliable and enjoys working with people in a fast-paced environment. Must be at least 17 years old Friendly, positive and service-minded Reliable and punctual A strong team player Good communication and teamwork skills Comfortable working in a fast-paced environment Willing to learn and take feedback Strong work ethic Committed to delivering great customer service Available to work late weekend closing shifts until approximately 01:30 Previous restaurant experience is an advantage, but not essential Willing to complete all required training Join Taco Bell!
