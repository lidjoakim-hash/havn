About Ledger We’re a team of experts pushing the limits of what’s possible, united by our common goal to unlock true freedom through digital ownership, making technology accessible for all. We believe in a world where users, creators and enterprises manage their value with ownership and freedom. Our curiosity drives us to innovate, empowering individuals on a global scale. We believe change is constant and our team moves forward as one, with a culture of problem-solving where every employee is empowered and supported to challenge tradition and create solutions. Our mission is simple: to make self-custody accessible and give people the keys to their own financial futures. If you want to make a true impact, we want you to join us at Ledger. At Ledger, we’re proud to be the global platform for digital assets and Web3, with over 20% of the world’s crypto assets secured through our Ledger devices. With our headquarters in Paris, and offices in Vierzon, Grenoble, Montpellier, London, Portland, Geneva, Zurich and Central Singapore, we have a team of around 600 professionals developing a variety of products and services to enable individuals and companies to securely buy, store, swap, grow and manage crypto assets – including the Ledger hardware wallets line with more than 7.5 millions units already sold in 200 countries. The team: With over 8 million signers sold worldwide, Ledger Wallet is where Ledger users manage and grow their assets. Transactional Services (buy, sell, swap, trading and stablecoin products) is its main source of service revenue. Money Flows connects users' crypto to the fiat world in both directions: on/off-ramp, cash-to-stablecoin, Card, and the growing P2P and Payments use cases. Users buy crypto with fiat or sell it back to their bank, move cash into stablecoins, and spend crypto with a card, all through specialist partners. It is the entry and exit door of the Ledger Wallet economy and a key lever to diversify Transactional Services revenue. The role reports to the Senior Director, Transactional Services. What you’ll be doing: You own and grow Money Flows end to end - revenue, economics, partner ecosystem and the quality of every fiat journey in Ledger Wallet - with a General Manager mindset over a business run mostly through external partners and cross-functional teams. The ambition is to grow revenue 10x in two to three years, through a strategy and roadmap you define and turn into quantified milestones. Key responsibilities include: - Business Line Ownership & Revenue Delivery: Being ultimately accountable for Money Flows revenue, volume and take rate across buy, sell, cash-to-stablecoin, Card, P2P and Payments. Building and defending the forecast, querying the data yourself, and separating market effects from the levers the team controls. - Monetization & Pricing: Owning take rate by line, partner and payment method. Turning volume into direct or indirect revenue (zero-fee partners, cash-to-stablecoin flows), and keeping pricing/product competitive against exchanges, neobanks and other wallets. - Partner Management & Business Development: Identifying, signing, managing and negotiating with the ramp, payment, stablecoin and card-issuing partner bench - on pricing, geographic and payment-method coverage, conversion, KYC friction and operational quality.
