Future Talent - Service Engineer

Aktiebolaget Tetra Pak · Lund, Sweden

💰 ca. 510 000–620 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 13.12.2026

At Tetra Pak we commit to making food safe and available, everywhere; and we protect what's good – protecting food, protecting people, and protecting the planet. By doing so we touch millions of people's lives every day. And we need people like you to make it happen. We empower you to reach your potential with opportunities to make an impact to be proud of – for food, people and the planet. Job Summary Start your career development and become one of our next Future Talents. We are looking for curious and motivated recent graduates to join our Future Talent Programme in September 2027! This is a permanent position based in Lund, Sweden, but you will work in a global arena. As part of our 18-month Technical Track, you will join a professional and dedicated team in the role of Future Talent – Service Engineer and follow a personalised development plan designed around your role, strengths and learning needs. Please note that this advertisement is for three open positions. From day one, you will work on meaningful technical responsibilities and contribute as a member of the team. Through hands-on experience, structured learning and professional relationships, you will develop your engineering capabilities and gain a broader understanding of our business. You will be trusted to share your ideas, explore different ways of solving problems and learn from experienced colleagues. You will also collaborate across functions and, where relevant to the role, across locations and countries. Visit our Future Talent website to learn more about the programme. Make an impact to be proud of – for food, people and the planet. What you will do You will work in the operations of Technical Support Excellence focusing on support on Filling machines or Processing Equipment. Majority of your job activities will be at our customers' plants, following Tetra Pak processes and procedures, this also gives you the responsibility to be an ambassador for Tetra Pak. As a Future Talent – Service Engineer, you will: Analyse and troubleshoot mechanical and automation problems Support systematic root cause analysis to identify underlying causes and enable lasting solutions Perform maintenance activities on our equipment Execute installation of relevant rebuilding kits Document and complete all relevant actions into a technical assistance report Build strong working relationships with customers, local service teams and colleagues across Tetra Pak Participate in commissioning activities at customer site We believe you have Recently completed, or are expected to complete before the programme starts, a technical degree covering both the electrical and the mechanical area (graduated 2026-2027) Experience and skills in automation will be an advantage Driver's license Open to travel approximately 150 days/year. Good command in written and spoken English We are looking for a self-motivated person with a high degree of initiative. You will work autonomously in complex situations, having the ability to make your own decisions and to solve problems under time and customer pressure. You have good collaboration and communication skills, with an openness to working with people from different cultures, backgrounds and areas of expertise. We appreciate if you demonstrate any other genuine technical interests, and high level of passion in operative roles and technical hands-on activities. We Offer You A permanent position from the start of the programme. An 18-month personalised development journey connected to a real technical role. Meaningful responsibilities and hands-on experience from day one. Structured learning opportunities to strengthen your technical and professional capabilities. Support from your people leader, programme network and experienced colleagues. Exposure to a global organisation and collaboration with colleagues from different backgrounds, functions and locations. Competitive compensation and benefits in line with local market
