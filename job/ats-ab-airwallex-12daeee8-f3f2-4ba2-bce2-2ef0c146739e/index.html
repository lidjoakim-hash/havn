ABOUT AIRWALLEX Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own. We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. You’ll engage prospects through email, phone and social channels, understand their needs, identify high-value opportunities and progress qualified leads to meetings with Account Executives (AEs). You’ll work closely with AEs, Marketing and colleagues across our European offices in London, Amsterdam, Tel Aviv and Vilnius to improve outreach, strengthen handovers and scale best practices as we build the BDR function in Spain.
