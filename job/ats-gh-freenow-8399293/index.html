Location: Come and join us in Barcelona! · Hybrid (3 days in office) Freenow by Lyft is seeking a highly organized, efficient and energetic Recruiting Coordinator for a full-time position to help us grow our world-class teams across Europe. Based in Barcelona, the role will support the Recruiters with the objective of successfully coordinating high-volume and fast-paced interview activity on the Recruitment team. We are looking for someone to help us work cross-functionally with hiring teams to be the first impression of Lyft for candidates. We are looking for someone eager and excited to grow their career within Talent Recruiting. YOUR DAILY ADVENTURES WILL INCLUDE: • Manage the end-to-end candidate experience with a focus on attention to detail, by ensuring interviews are scheduled quickly and accurately. • Maintain data accuracy in the Applicant Tracking System (ATS) and assist with data clean-up efforts, ensuring adherence to established processes and timelines for effective candidate communication, while demonstrating strong time management skills. • Communicate professionally with candidates and coworkers, maintaining a high level of confidentiality at all times. • Work closely with Recruiters, Interviewers, and Hiring Managers to enhance existing processes and programs, with a strong emphasis on collaboration and partner management, while fostering creativity in talent acquisition strategies. • Coordinate candidate travel and handle all expenses (if applicable). • Monitor all candidates’ interview experiences and act as the primary point of contact. • Enhance the interviewer training program by actively inviting interviewers in training to live interview shadowing sessions, utilizing attention to detail. • Build diverse and inclusive interview panels and support continuous growth of interviewer teams. • Champion our values, and our philosophy to potential candidates. TO BE SUCCESSFUL IN THIS ROLE: Experience: • At least 1 year of experience in a fast-paced environment, juggling multiple responsibilities, ideally within Human Resources, People Operations, or Recruiting. • Proficient in Google Suite. • A minimum of 1 year of client or customer service experience, preferably in a corporate environment. • Strong verbal and written communication skills in English. • Proven ability to multi-task and adapt quickly to change. Preferred Qualifications: • Exceptionally organized and strong time-management skills are a must. • Self-motivated, maintaining meticulous attention to detail, even with routine tasks. • Familiarity with Greenhouse and GoodTime is a plus. • Familiarity with Slack. Desired Attributes: • Self-directed, ability to work autonomously. • Quick learner with the ability to ramp up rapidly. • Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail. • Must be able to work independently as well as part of a collaborative team. • High level of personal ownership, confidentiality, and flexibility. BENEFITS & PERKS IN A NUTSHELL: • Flexible working arrangements • LinkedIn Learning • Sabbatical & special leave policies • WeRoad partnership • Birthday, 24th + 31st December off • Short term EU work policy • Mobility Credit • Health Insurance • Employee assistance program Plus more local benefits depending on your work location!
