Do you thrive in the ambiguity and possibility of early stage product design? We're looking for a bilingual Product Designer to help build intuitive, AI-driven experiences for government and enterprise clients across the Middle East. At Scale AI, we're building the future of AI through high-quality data and world-class design. Our products power critical decisions for businesses and governments worldwide. You will: • Lead 0-1 Design: Own the entire design process — from raw concept to final implementation — working directly with PMs and engineers to ship impactful products quickly • Develop Concepts: Extract key insights from PM and client feedback and translate them into compelling concepts • Manage Expectations: Respond effectively to multiple stakeholders and align them around a clear design direction • Communicate and Prototype Visually: Deliver polished verbal communication and visual storytelling using AI tools like Cursor and Claude Design to bring concepts to life • Design with Data: Make design decisions informed by research, data, and user insights You're a great fit if you: • Are fluent in Arabic and English (a hard requirement for this role) • Excel at navigating ambiguity in early stage products • Have a mix of startup and established company experience; at least one role at a well-respected company with a strong product development culture • Have experience in B2B products for government or enterprise clients • Want your work to shape the role of AI in modern business applications • Have prior work experience in the GCC (Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, UAE) • Are based in the GCC or open to relocating • Have hands-on experience with AI-powered design platforms such as Cursor and Claude • Have a degree in HCI, Computer Science, or Graphic Design • Have a strong portfolio demonstrating polish, professional presentation, and clear visual storytelling to broad audiences Nice to haves: • Experience at notable AI companies • Technical background For those applying based in Qatar: Residency and employment in Qatar requires certain permissions (visa and permits) issued by the Qatari authorities.
