As AI Transformation Manager, you partner across Sandvik Rock Processing to identify opportunities, simplify ways of working and turn ideas into solutions that create lasting business value. About your job Working at the intersection of business, IT and data, you drive transformation from the initial business challenge through implementation and sustainable adoption. A key part of your scope is strengthening our data foundation, ensuring data is well managed, trusted and ready to enable AI and analytics. You also lead Data Analytics and act as a strategic and operational partner to the CIO / VP IT, Business Area Rock Processing. Job responsibilities Partner with business leader, functions and process owners to identify, frame and prioritize improvement opportunities. Drive business and process development using AI, automation and data, taking them from idea and pilot into sustainable operation. Own and drive the Rock Processing data strategy, strengthening data management, governance, ownership and quality. Lead Data Analytics by setting direction and priorities, developing competence and creating a culture of collaboration and accountability. Build and prioritize a portfolio of transformation opportunities, establish business cases and track measurable value. Lead change and capability building while ensuring responsible use of AI and data in collaboration with IT, Legal and Information Security. Location and flexibility The preferred location for this position is Malmö, with hybrid working possibilities. Your profile You combine strong business acumen with a solid understanding of AI, GenAI, automation and data. With extensive experience in transformation and improvement work, you know how to move from a business challenge to implemented change and measurable results. Your background includes Several years of experience in business development, process improvement, operational excellence or digital transformation, with a proven track record of delivering change. Experience working with senior stakeholders in a complex, international organization and leading people, ideally managers or specialist teams. Good knowledge of data strategy, data management, governance and quality, together with an understanding of what it takes to make data AI-ready. Know-how in Lean, Six Sigma, process mapping and change management A university degree in business, engineering, industrial management or equivalent experience. Excellent skills in English, while knowledge of Swedish is a plus. It’s an advantage if you have experience from industrial, manufacturing or B2B experience, as well as a management consulting or advisory background. Curious and pragmatic, you build trust through listening and collaboration while being comfortable challenging established ways of working. A structured and analytical approach helps you simplify complexity and set priorities, while your hands-on mindset keeps the focus on implementation and real business impact. The team You lead the Data Analytics team. Collaboration spans Rock Processing management and business leaders, function heads and process owners, the IT organization, Group IT and data platform teams, as well as Legal, Information Security, HR and external technology, data and consulting partners.
