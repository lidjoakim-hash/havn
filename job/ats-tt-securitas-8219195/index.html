Technical Security Design Consultant • Location: Remote – Ireland / UK • Travel: Frequent job site visits required. Minimal overnight travel required. • Reporting Line: Technical Design Lead Purpose As a Technical Security Design Consultant, you will support the successful delivery of technical security design projects across the EMEA region. Working closely with internal teams, clients and project stakeholders, you will help develop security solutions, maintain design standards, manage project documentation and contribute to the delivery of high-quality consultancy services. This role plays an important part in ensuring security systems are designed effectively, project milestones are achieved, and client requirements are delivered in line with established standards, regulations and design criteria. Major Responsibilities • Collaborate with internal and external stakeholders to support the successful delivery of technical security design projects across the EMEA region. • Coordinate drawing revisions, project updates and design-related activities throughout the project lifecycle. • Facilitate and support project design handovers to ensure smooth transition between project phases. • Maintain design documentation and document control processes in line with design management requirements. • Compile project metrics and tracking information to support measurable design delivery and reporting. • Drive and coordinate design workgroups, managing design-related matters and supporting collaborative decision-making. • Ensure compliance with established physical security baselines, standards and design criteria. • Deliver or support security consultancy services in accordance with client requirements, regulations and industry standards. • Prepare and present high-quality technical reports, technology studies, cost analyses and tender information to support procurement activities. • Lead and facilitate client-facing meetings and workshops, ensuring clear outcomes, documented actions and effective follow-up. • Assess security risks and recommend appropriate mitigation measures using recognised risk assessment methodologies. • Communicate technical, strategic and design information clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders through reports, submissions and presentations. • Manage client expectations by providing regular updates and ensuring requirements are understood and addressed throughout project delivery. • Protect confidential client information with the highest levels of professionalism and discretion. • Suport project delivery against agreed milestones while managing multiple concurrent projects. Education • Third-level education in Electronics is highly desirable. Experience & Knowledge • 3-5 years’ experience working with IP video and access-controlled enterprise security systems. • Experience in security systems design and telecommunications. • Essential experience using CAD and Bluebeam, together with an understanding of BIM. • Experience with platforms including Lenel, Genetec, AMAG, Software House, Milestone and Avigilon. • Knowledge of EN and BS standards relating to security design and installation. • Understanding of physical security measures including gates, fencing and barriers. • Strong understanding of door ironmongery. • Experience within the data centre sector would be advantageous.
