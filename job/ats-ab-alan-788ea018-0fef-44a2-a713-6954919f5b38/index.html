You'll own these vertical B2B brand campaigns for France, plus a portfolio of other brand projects. You'll work closely with the Brand Lead, Product Marketing, Growth, segment owners and Sales. This is an individual-contributor role at C1-D level. WHAT YOU'LL DO - Vertical campaigns: Define the audience, message, proof points and channels for each priority vertical. Choose priorities and objectives with segment owners, Growth and Product Marketing. Run each campaign from brief to post-mortem, and build reusable toolkits (messaging, assets, case studies) for other teams. - Product and offer launches: Support the launch of new product features (e.g. Mo, Alan Précision) and new offers (e.g. Alan retirees) in France. Turn product positioning into launch plans and assets. - Partnerships and events: Run vertical-specific events, sponsorships, ambassador and creator partnerships that build credibility with each audience. - Local markets: Give feedback on local briefs and plans. Spot French initiatives that could scale to other markets and capture them in playbooks. - Measurement: Set KPIs for every project (reach in the target, qualified leads, pipeline influenced, brand perception).
