Supply Chain Sales Support Manager

Seco Tools Aktiebolag · Fagersta, Sweden

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Do you enjoy creating clarity at the intersection of customers, Sales and Supply Chain? We’re looking for a Supply Chain Sales Support Manager to lead our global function and develop a proactive, structured and data-driven service that colleagues and customers can rely on. At Seco, collaboration, responsibility, curiosity and continuous improvement guide how we work and grow. Here, you get to: Lead a global function at the heart of customer order flow. Shape how Sales and Supply Chain work together through clear governance, service levels, KPIs and digital tools. Join a collaborative environment where curiosity and continuous learning are valued.   About the job  You lead and develop the global Supply Chain Sales Support function, creating a standardized, customer-focused and efficient interface between Sales and our end-to-end supply chain. With a clear focus on governance and proactive support, you ensure that customer needs, order exceptions and supply constraints are translated into priorities and coordinated action. The function is in the middle of a shift: building on a strong foundation in case handling towards an end-to-end control and improvement capability. You lead that change, and you lead it through people. Developing your team’s skills, ownership and confidence is as much a part of the job as the processes and tools. Job responsibilities Lead, coach and develop the Supply Chain Sales Support team: set clear roles and expectations, build capability in customer communication, supply chain understanding, problem-solving and case management, and grow people into broader ownership. Lead the development towards proactive exception management, building on the team’s case-handling expertise, and make new ways of working stick in daily operations. Establish and maintain global governance across the order-to-delivery flow, including ownership boundaries, decision rights and escalation paths. Act as the primary supply chain interface for Sales and Customer Service regarding prioritized orders, supply exceptions and special customer requirements. Develop the service model, including case categories, service levels, prioritization principles, escalation protocols and KPIs. Use case data and performance insights to identify root causes and drive structural improvements with the responsible process owners. Drive digitalization of case management, workflows, reporting and knowledge management, reducing repetitive demand through automation and self-service. Location and flexibility The position is flexible is preferably based in Fagersta, Sweden, but another Seco location can be considered for the right candidate. We offer a hybrid working model that combines time at the office with remote work. Your profile   You have solid experience in supply chain, logistics, planning, order management or customer service operations. Having worked at the intersection of a commercial organization and operational supply chain functions, you’re comfortable creating structure across complex, cross-functional environments and using data to support decisions. You communicate fluently in English; Swedish is considered an advantage. Your background includes: Experience establishing processes, ownership models, escalation paths or service frameworks across multiple functions. Knowledge of customer-order exceptions, escalations and prioritization, preferably within a case-based way of working. The ability to identify recurring operational issues, uncover root causes and turn insights into structural improvements. Experience using CRM or case-management systems, ERP data, KPIs and reporting. People leadership experience and a university degree in Supply Chain Management, Logistics, Business Administration, Industrial Engineering or equivalent experience.
