DC Team Leader Facilities

H & M Hennes & Mauritz GBC AB · Eskilstuna, Sweden

💰 ca. 290 000–350 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Kontraktvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 18.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

WHAT YOU'LL DO As a DC Team Leader Facilities, you manage DC facility operations and maintenance to ensure efficiency, safety and compliance. You coordinate maintenance activities, manage supplier relationships, and drive continuous improvement initiatives to support operational continuity and business goals. You will: Lead and coordinate the Facilities function, ensuring efficient maintenance and functionality of indoor and outdoor environments. Own the performance and availability of facility technical systems. Plan and follow up on preventive and corrective maintenance activities Manage supplier and contractor relationships. Ensure compliance with safety, legal, and operational requirements. Collaborate closely with Operations, Health & Safety, Automation, and external partners to secure a safe and productive working environment. Drive sustainability initiatives and support the Distribution Center's environmental targets and KPIs. Perform hands-on maintenance, troubleshooting, repair, installation, and assembly work across the Distribution Center, while coordinating specialized maintenance activities with external service providers when required. WHO YOU'LL WORK WITH You will be the primary point of contact and subject matter expert for Facilities within the Distribution Center. As DC Team Leader Facilities, you report directly to the DC Security & Facilities Manager. Working cross-functionally across the organization, you will coordinate activities with internal stakeholders and external service providers to ensure a safe, compliant, and efficient workplace. Your key partners will include: DC Management & Operations Teams System & Automation Teams Facility Service Providers and Contractors Property Owners and External Vendors WHO YOU ARE We are looking for people with... Experience within facility management, technical maintenance, property operations, industrial services, or similar technical environments. Strong knowledge of preventive maintenance, facility systems, and operational workflows. The ability to plan, prioritize, and follow up on multiple activities while maintaining a high level of structure and quality. Strong analytical skills with the ability to identify root causes, manage risks, and drive continuous improvement initiatives. Experience coordinating stakeholders, suppliers, and contractors across multiple activities and priorities. Good understanding of health and safety regulations, compliance requirements, and risk management. Experience working with budgets, cost control, and service agreements is considered an advantage. Good communication skills in both Swedish and English. Experience performing practical maintenance, repair, troubleshooting, or installation work within facilities, property management, logistics, industrial, or technical environments. Good technical understanding and the ability to independently diagnose and resolve facility-related issues, while being comfortable working hands-on with tools and equipment. And people who are... Hands-on, practical, and solution-oriented, with a genuine interest in maintenance, repairs, troubleshooting, and continuously improving the workplace environment. Positive, adaptable, and motivated by change with a strong "can-do" attitude. Curious and proactive, with the ability to identify improvement opportunities while balancing operational needs and long-term business goals. Responsible, structured, and committed to delivering results while maintaining high safety and quality standards. Comfortable balancing administrative responsibilities with practical maintenance work in a dynamic operational environment. ADDITIONAL INFORMATION This is a full-time position, starting with a probationary period of 6 months. The location is based in Eskilstuna and part of the Logistic Region Europe.
