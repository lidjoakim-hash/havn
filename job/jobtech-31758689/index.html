Software Developer within estimations

Knightec Group Compliance and Management AB · Göteborg, Sweden

💰 ca. 540 000–650 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 15.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Why this job is for you Are you passionate about software development and automotive technology? Do you enjoy solving complex engineering challenges and developing innovative software solutions? We are looking for a Software Developer with an interest in vehicle dynamics and embedded software development to join our team. As a consultant, you will have the opportunity to contribute to exciting assignments within the automotive industry, working closely with customers and experienced engineering teams. You will work with advanced software development in a dynamic environment, contributing to the development of software solutions that support various vehicle functions and enhance vehicle performance, safety and driving experience. This is an opportunity to combine your technical expertise with the chance to influence future automotive technologies while continuously developing your skills. In our Compliance & Management business area, we enable a smooth transition towards digital, sustainable, and secure solutions through our expertise in quality, safety, and leadership – while also helping to optimize production efficiency to strengthen profitability Your main tasks will be: Develop and maintain software functionality related to vehicle dynamics, including calculations of longitudinal and lateral velocity, yaw rate and accelerations. Develop and maintain software within a continuous integration framework, delivering software updates to production vehicles. Design and evaluate algorithms, including proof-of-concept investigations for new vehicle functionality. Develop software solutions in collaboration with cross-functional engineering teams. Test and verify software in both test bench and vehicle environments. Contribute to continuous improvements in software development methods, tools and processes. Your qualifications: We believe you have a strong technical interest and a background in software development, control engineering or a related engineering discipline. We would also like you to have: Good knowledge of control engineering, software development, signal processing and/or sensor fusion. Knowledge and experience in areas related to vehicle dynamics. An understanding of application software development and its associated environment. Experience with MATLAB/Simulink, CANalyzer and rapid prototyping. Familiarity with C programming. Experience with tools such as GIT, Vector DaVinci, CANoe, dSPACE and SystemWeaver (SE-Tool). An understanding of systems development principles. As a person, you are curious, solution-oriented and enjoy working both independently and as part of a team. You have good communication skills and appreciate collaborating with customers and colleagues to find the best technical solutions. Practical information This is a permanent position with a six-month probationary period, based at our office in Lindholmsallen 2 Gothenburg. Business travel to our clients may be required. The start date is as soon as possible or as agreed upon. Please submit your application as soon as possible, but no later than 2026-12-15. If you have any questions about the position, feel free to contact Oliver Rydberg, Talent Acquisition Specialist. Please note that, due to GDPR, we only accept applications via our career page. We look forward to hearing from you!
