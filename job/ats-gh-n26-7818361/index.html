About the opportunity We are seeking an experienced and visionary Engineering Manager to lead one of our Data Platform teams, currently undergoing a fundamental transformation from a traditional, service-oriented model into a product-oriented platform team focused on automation, scalability, integration, and developer enablement. As Engineering Manager, you will play a pivotal role in accelerating this transition, guiding the team through a challenging but high-potential journey. You will be responsible for shaping and executing long-term technical and organizational strategies that enable scalable ownership models across the company, allowing teams to safely and autonomously leverage platform capabilities. Your leadership will be key to evolving a team with strong operational foundations into a high-impact, autonomous group of engineers who deliver reliable and compliant platform solutions, empower development teams, and contribute to the broader platform ecosystem. You will have end to end ownership of the team’s scope, including strategy, execution, and organizational design.This role is an opportunity to shape the future of our Data Platform, defining how teams across the company build, operate, and scale data and AI-driven products in a compliant and reliable way. In this role, you will: • Define and drive the strategic evolution of the team towards building a modern, scalable data platform that enables advanced data and AI use cases by default, without requiring bespoke solutions or direct platform team involvement • Enable development teams to safely and autonomously leverage data and platform capabilities through robust tooling, guardrails, and well-defined interfaces. • Establish a long-term roadmap that aligns data platform capabilities with emerging AI use cases, product needs, and regulatory constraints. • Build and grow a high-performing, cross-functional team capable of delivering reliable, scalable, and compliant platform solutions • Shape the team’s culture by promoting initiative, critical thinking, technical leadership, and shared responsibility across the platform. • Coach and develop engineers to strengthen their skills across data, infrastructure, and platform engineering, while raising the bar on ownership and delivery. • Take a proactive role in performance management, ensuring the team continuously evolves towards autonomy, maturity, and impact. • Act as a key liaison between Engineering, Product, Security, and other stakeholders to represent the team’s roadmap and priorities.
