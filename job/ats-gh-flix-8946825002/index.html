About You • You are a student in Business, Transport, Law, or a related field. • Possess availability for a full-time internship starting May 2026. • Have native level Portuguese and fluent English are a must. • Have excellent communication and relationship building skills. • Possess strong attention to detail and confidence preparing documents, reports, and research. • Have built solid Excel skills and proficiency with document creation tools. • Are proactive, self driven and with a collaborative, team-oriented approach.
