Geometry, Tolerance & Clamping Specialist

Incluso AB · Göteborg, Sweden

We are looking for a Geometry, Tolerance & Clamping Specialist for a global automotive company in Gothenburg. Start is ASAP, 1,5 months limited contract to begin with. This role is 100% onsite in Gothenburg (Torslanda). Assignment Overview The assignment is within Manufacturing Engineering supports the industrialization of advanced High Voltage Battery Assembly systems for Battery Electric Vehicles. The work is performed in a cross-functional engineering environment covering manufacturing processes, automated production equipment, product-to-process interfaces, quality assurance and supplier collaboration. The consultant will secure dimensional robustness across product, tooling and manufacturing interfaces by defining datum, tolerance, locating, clamping and measurement strategies for High Voltage Battery Assembly. The objective is consistent dimensional quality and stable assembly conditions in automated production. Key Responsibilities Own geometry-assurance activities for assigned battery assembly scope. Define and challenge datum systems, locating principles, clamp sequence and fixture concepts. Perform or coordinate tolerance stack-ups, variation analysis and dimensional risk assessments. Translate product characteristics into measurable manufacturing CTQs and fixture requirements. Review product, process and equipment concepts for dimensional feasibility and robustness. Coordinate with product engineering, metrology, tooling, joining and equipment suppliers. Define measurement, correlation, capability and buy-off strategies. Lead dimensional problem solving, root-cause analysis and corrective-action verification. Required Competencies GD&T and datum-system expertise - 5+ years - Critical Tolerance stack-up, variation analysis and geometry assurance - 5+ years - Critical Fixture locating, clamping and dimensional buy-off - 3+ years - Critical Precision high-volume manufacturing geometry - 3+ years - High Measurement-data and capability analysis - 3+ years - High Preferred Competencies High Voltage Battery Assembly geometry experience. RD&T, 3DCS, CETOL, VSA or equivalent variation-analysis tools. CMM, scanning, vision or inline metrology. Joining-process effects, thermal distortion and compliant assembly. Supplier fixture correlation and automotive production launch. Education Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in a relevant engineering discipline, or equivalent demonstrated professional experience relevant to the assignment. Experience Required Senior-level industrialization experience in automotive, battery manufacturing, electrification or comparable high-volume automated production. The consultant should be able to work independently, own technical deliverables and collaborate effectively with engineering teams and equipment suppliers. Deliverables Geometry-assurance plan and dimensional risk register. Datum, locating and clamping strategy. Tolerance-stack and variation-analysis recommendations. Fixture and equipment geometry requirements. Measurement, correlation and capability plan. Dimensional acceptance and production-readiness evidence. This role requires fluency in English. This is a full-time consultant position in Gothenburg through Incluso. Start is ASAP, 1,5 months limited contract to begin with. This role is 100% onsite in Gothenburg (Torslanda). Please submit your CV as soon as possible since we will review the applications on an on-going basis. For more information about this role, please contact Marianne Nilsson recruiter at Incluso.
