Tata Technologies Nordics AB · Göteborg, Sweden

At Tata Technologies, we make product development dreams a reality by designing, engineering, and delivering the products of tomorrow for the world's leading manufacturers. We are currently looking for a System Designer – Door Control Functions to join an innovative vehicle systems team focused on developing locking, door, window, and access control functions for next-generation vehicle platforms. About the Assignment As a System Designer, you will play a key role in developing, defining, and verifying robust system solutions and customer-oriented functionality related to door control systems. You will work with both existing and future vehicle architectures, ensuring system designs are scalable, safe, and aligned with overall vehicle requirements. The role combines systemization, requirements engineering, system modeling, safety analysis, and supplier collaboration within a highly cross-functional environment. Main Responsibilities As a System Designer, you will: Define and develop system functions and behaviors related to: Door Control Systems Locking Functions Window Control Functions Alarm and Access Features Develop, decompose, and manage system requirements across multiple abstraction levels. Create and maintain system models and technical documentation. Define technical solutions in alignment with system architecture guidelines. Contribute to safety-related system development according to ISO 26262. Participate in technical discussions with suppliers and engineering stakeholders. Support system verification and validation activities. Collaborate closely with software, hardware, function, and architecture teams. Support the development of future vehicle user experiences through robust system design. Required Qualifications Minimum 4+ years of experience in: System Design Function Development System Engineering System Modeling Strong experience in: Requirement Engineering Requirement Decomposition Requirement Traceability Experience working with safety-critical systems. Practical knowledge of ISO 26262 Functional Safety. Experience using: Elektra CarWeaver Experience collaborating with suppliers and managing technical alignments. M.Sc. in: Systems Engineering Control & Mechatronics Computer Engineering Engineering Physics Or equivalent experience Fluent in English, both written and spoken. Meritorious Qualifications Hardware design knowledge. Experience with functional design methodologies. Understanding of user behavior, customer journeys, and use-case-driven development. Experience within automotive electrical architectures and vehicle systems development. Knowledge of vehicle access, door module, locking, or closure systems. If you are passionate about bringing innovation to the projects you work on, then we would love to hear from you. Tata Technologies: Engineering a better world.
