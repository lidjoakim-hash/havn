Mechanical Design Engineer - Power Systems

Husqvarna AB · Jönköping, Sweden

We’re one of the world’s oldest start-ups — and we’re just getting started. At Husqvarna Group, innovation is in our DNA. With over 330 years of heritage and a passion for pioneering technology, we design and deliver world class products and solutions for forest, park, garden and construction care. We develop next-generation handheld products where performance, usability, and environmental responsibility are key. At the heart of these products is the powertrain—and that’s where you come in. Join our Base Engine team in Power Systems We are now looking for a Mechanical Design Engineer to join the Base Engine group within Power Systems, part of Handheld R&D. Power Systems is responsible for developing the powertrains in our products. In this team we focus on petrol-powered handheld products, developing engines and engine components for our 2-stroke engines, including cylinder, piston, crankshaft and bearings. Base Engine is a team of around 11 design engineers, some with specialist areas, who work together to develop the next generation of handheld products for forestry and urban environments. We work with the full life cycle of a component, from the first idea to product care. About the role: As a Design Engineer you take responsibility for your own complex components and drive them forward. You will work with both new product development and product care: Develop and design components for new products, for example a new chainsaw, and make sure they fit in the whole product and meet new product requirements Create 2D and 3D drawings and manage design changes, including tooling changes Carry out root cause analyses to understand why failures occur in complex systems Participate in regular sync meetings with your platform lead, for example to discuss changes of supplier for a component Work closely with neighbouring design engineers, test and verification, manufacturing and suppliers In our different product value streams you work together with a platform lead and look at the product as a whole, not only at the component level. This is a cross-functional role with many internal contact points, and you will need to manage several tasks in parallel. You will have support from experienced colleagues, and we encourage you to ask for help when you need it. About you: You’re proactive, curious and take great ownership of your components. You enjoy digging into technical details and complex systems. You are structured and know how to plan your work, keep track of timelines and drive your own tasks forward, while being a good team player who is not afraid to ask for support. You communicate clearly and are comfortable making decisions and recommending next steps. Your skills and background: Degree in Mechanical Engineering At least 3 years of professional experience in mechanical engineering or product development Experience in mechanical design and in creating 2D and 3D drawings Experience with CAD tools such as CATIA V5 and PLM (e.g. Teamcenter) Ability to plan, structure and prioritize your own work Fluent in English and Swedish Meriting: knowledge of combustion engine technology, especially 2-stroke engines Meriting: experience with casting, metal machining, geometry assurance or cost management Location This position is placed in Huskvarna How we work We believe the best ideas happen when we're connected. We spend most of our time together in the office, creating space for collaboration, creativity, and fast decision-making. This is consistent across all our global offices and helps us maintain a strong culture, support learning and development, and ensure everyone has access to the people and resources they need to thrive. Your Application Apply as soon as possible. We are screening on an ongoing basis and will fill this position as soon as we have found the right person.
