What is The Role In this role, you will be a key member of the Beacon team. The team own the infrastructure layer of AlertZero, which is the Agentic Security Operations platform. We also oversee other components like Graph Visualization and Automatic Migration. You'll have the chance to lead the development of key platform features that support security workflows, collaborating closely with product managers and fellow engineers. You'll have a hand in shaping the architecture that will serve as the foundation for future projects, making your impact felt throughout the organization. What You Will Be Doing • Develop high-quality software solutions for the AlertZero platform while implementing and maintaining essential security features within the infrastructure. Write unit and integration tests to ensure the reliability and performance of the code. • Conduct thorough code reviews to ensure all code adheres to established coding standards. Provide constructive feedback to peers, improving overall code quality. Collaborate with team members to address any issues discovered during reviews and suggest automations that enhance the developer experience and streamline deployments. • Design scalable and secure system architectures for the AlertZero platform (or other features). Create detailed technical specifications and documentation for system components. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure the system design meets business requirements. • Take full ownership of features. This includes managing everything from design to deployment. You will also provide production support. You will drive technical decisions. After release, monitor system health and make improvements based on real-world usage and incidents. What You Bring • 5+ years in software development • Deep TypeScript and JavaScript skills, especially with React and Node.js. • Solid system design skills — you can architect scalable, secure, distributed systems and reason about the tradeoffs involved. • A proven track record of using AI to accelerate development, debug complex systems, and optimize code, while still owning the final outcomes. • The ability to collaborate across functions and teams and seamlessly transition between different projects, codebases, or teams based on business priorities. • You can work autonomously, drive decisions and results in a distributed team by leveraging asynchronous, direct, and transparent communication. • Great communication skills.
