Job Purpose: Contributes independently to setting and maintaining high quality standards and ensures that client demands and expectations are met. Focuses on preventing mistakes and defects throughout manufacturing, from design transfer to delivery and Life of Field. Conducts work in accordance with &#34;Produce/Test Assembly&#34; for REMS Manufacturing and the &#34;Execute Quality&#34;, &#34;Monitor Quality&#34; and &#34;Improve Quality&#34; processes in GPBMS. Job Description: Independently performs and follows up quality assurance activities within an assigned Manufacturing product or process scope.
