Location: Remote | EU Department: ISR Analytics Reports to: VP of Analytics Employment Type: Consultant (4 months; potential to extend) Why this role matters As a SAR Image Annotation Consultant at ICEYE, you will support our mission to deliver actionable intelligence from space by providing high-quality and precise annotation of Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) imagery. Your work directly contributes to the accuracy and reliability of our data products, empowering timely decisions for defense, intelligence, natural disaster response, and commercial applications. Please note, while this while position will start as a short-term contract of 4 months, if ICEYE decides to move into a full-time, permanent contract after this period, this opportunity would be located in either our Finland headquarters, or our office in Berlin, Germany. Who We Are ICEYE delivers space-based intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance (ISR) capabilities to governments and allied nations. Our SAR technology and constellation provide day/night and all-weather Earth Observation, supporting diverse use-cases from security and maritime monitoring to insurance and disaster response. Our global team of 900+ people is united by a vision to improve life on Earth by being the global source of truth in Earth Observation. Your Day-to-Day Responsibilities - Accurately annotate SAR imagery using ICEYE’s internal tools and guidelines - Collaborate with product, research, and engineering teams to ensure annotation requirements are met - Maintain high standards of data quality, traceability, and documentation - Communicate effectively to flag uncertainty, changes in data quality, and edge cases - Support the continual improvement of annotation processes and tooling - Help develop training materials and best practices for other annotators What We’re Looking For Must Haves: - Experience in SAR image interpretation, remote sensing, or geospatial analysis - Meticulous attention to detail and data quality - Ability to follow complex annotation guidelines and maintain consistency - Strong communication and documentation skills - Ability to deliver in a fast-paced, changing environment Nice to Haves: - Previous experience with annotation tools or large geospatial datasets - Familiarity with Python or basic scripting for data manipulation - Understanding of SAR imaging principles and applications - Experience working in international, interdisciplinary teams Working At ICEYE Join a fast-growing, diverse team with a passion for making a global impact. You’ll have real ownership of your work and opportunities for professional growth. Our Values: - Make the impossible possible - Be curious and go deep - See the big picture - Drive effective teamwork - Act as one team - Have fun and celebrate the wins Our Commitment to Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion We welcome applicants from all backgrounds and are committed to fair, inclusive hiring.
