SET A NEW PULSE FOR HEALTHCARE! We're looking for a Localization Operations Manager to audit and redesign how we conduct translation and localization end to end; from content creation, through engineering delivery, to linguistic quality review. You will challenge our current tooling and AI/MT workflows, content ownership and governance models, integrate AI software development best practices, and leave behind a better process and toolset for multiple language markets, not just one. WHAT YOU'LL DO - Audit the current end-to-end localization and translation workflow for our three main markets, from source content definition through engineering execution to linguistic QA, defining and implementing improvements for an updated localization process.  - Evaluate and evolve our use of machine translation and AI-assisted translation tooling, including glossary ownership and governance, content classification, and where and how human review fits in. - Define a vendor and tooling strategy for translation and linguistic quality a cross our language markets; updating our existing tool stack as needed.  - Design and roll-out evolved processes in partnership with engineering, design, product, medical, and local go-to-market teams - Hand off a durable process and toolset by the end of the engagement.   WHO YOU ARE Before you read on: if you don't have the exact profile described below, but you feel this job description matches your skill set, we still encourage you to apply. You'll be a great fit if you: - Prior experience as a Technical Program Manager, Product Manager, or Localization Operations Manager, with hands-on experience designing and implementing localization/internationalization tooling and MT/AI-assisted translation workflows. - Strong proficiency in AI-native workflows as they relate to software development and content management. Can build tooling solutions, agents, and technical solutions where needed.  - Experience with multi-market, multi-language products at scale. Italian, French or German localization experience is a plus. - Track record of shipping quality, speed, or cost improvements to a localization or translation operation. - Strong stakeholder management across design, engineering, and content teams. - Comfortable working independently on a fixed-term, outcome-driven engagement. - Professional working proficiency in English; fluency in Italian is a plus.  OUR INTERVIEW PROCESS - Hiring Manager Interview (1 hour) - Case Study & Restitution - Behavioral Interview (1h30) - At least one reference check We want your experience to be clear, respectful, and transparent. Learn more about our hiring process on our candidate experience page https://careers.doctolib.com/tech-doctolib/getting-hired/.
