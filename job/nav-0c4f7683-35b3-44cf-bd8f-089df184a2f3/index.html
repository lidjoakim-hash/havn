Join our team of Tour Guides in Bergen!

Norwegian Guide Service · Bergen, Norway

Make Bergen your everyday workplace. Meet guests from around the world, share local stories, and join a diverse international team. Gain valuable professional experience while enjoying the culture and social life of a vibrant city, with mountains and nature on your doorstep. Hva vi ser etter Who We Are Looking For We are seeking curious, service-minded, and reliable individuals who enjoy connecting with people and working in a dynamic environment. Requirements: • Fluency in English and at least one of the following languages: German, Spanish, Italian, French, Norwegian, Dutch, Chinese, Greek or Polish. Proficiency in two or more of these languages in addition to English is considered a strong advantage. • If you are not a Norwegian citizen, you must be an EU/EEA citizen or already have a valid Norwegian work permit. • Interest in Norwegian history, culture, and nature. • Guide Certification (or willingness to obtain one). Not Certified Yet? As a part of our quality guarantee to our clients, our guides must hold at minimum a Guide Certification ensuring high professional standards. Several certification options are available in Norway. We work together with and recommend the Guide Academy Certification Programme , which includes an engaging online course (completed remotely) and an intensive on-site training session in Bergen starting April the 12th 2027. No prior guiding experience is required, only passion and enthusiasm! We sponsor the certification fee for candidates who commit to working a full season exclusively with Norwegian Guide Service. Arbeidsoppgaver What do Tour Guides with NGS do? As a Tour Guide, you will lead informative and engaging tours for cruise ships and private guests visiting Bergen. You will help guests discover the city's rich history, culture, and landmarks while ensuring their comfort, safety, and an unforgettable experience. Vi tilbyr What We Offer • **An international job in the heart of Bergen -**Build experience in tourism while living and working in a world leading city with culture, social opportunities, mountains and nature all within easy reach. Bergen was designated by Oxford Economics in 2026 as Europe's best city for quality of life (World's second best). • **A seasonal job that can fit around the rest of your life -**Most assignments take place between May and mid-October, with additional guiding opportunities throughout the year. • **Guaranteed minimum seasonal income -**More predictability while still retaining the variety and flexibility that comes with guiding. • **Professional training and certification sponsorship -**We'll help you gain professional qualifications and can cover your Guide Certification when you commit to a full season with us. • **Fair working conditions -**We focus on creating a sustainable, professional and positive working environment for our guides. We provide full paid training on company specific procedures, tour logistics, and guest service standards. • **More than Bergen -**Want to explore further? We also offer assignments elsewhere in Western Norway, with higher pay for tours starting outside Bergen and paid travel time. • **Opportunities to grow -**Guiding can be a first step into a wider career in tourism, hospitality and destination development. Support with finding accommodation Relocating to Bergen? We can help you get started, including access to rooms in shared apartments through a housing provider, subject to availability and at your own expense. We also provide guidance if you prefer to arrange your own accommodation. How to Apply Start your journey with us and make summer 2027 a season to remember! Visit our website at &#x1f449; https://norwegianguideservice.no/recruitment/ and submit your application! Application deadline: 28.02.2027. Applications are reviewed on an ongoing basis and positions are filled as suitable candidates are identified. As the number of positions is limited, we encourage candidates to apply as early as possible.
