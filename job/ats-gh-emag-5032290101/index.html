IT Support Team Leader (Cybersecurity & IT Infrastructure Team) The IT Support Team Lead coordinates the IT support team, improves work processes, and is responsible for the administrative infrastructure (inventory, KPIs, and reporting), while also being involved in company-wide projects with significant business impact. What you’ll have to do: Processes & Automation • Develop and improve the team's work processes, including automations that reduce repetitive tasks. • Optimize the support workflow (intake, routing, escalation, closure). • Build effective collaborative relationships with other departments. • Ensure the asset inventory process (laptops, monitors, etc.) and the accuracy of data in the inventory management tool. Team Development • Identify the growth potential of team members and build development plans for each of them. • Propose and organize training sessions and knowledge-sharing activities for the support team, based on identified development needs and newly introduced technologies or processes. KPIs & Monitoring • Define KPIs together with the direct manager. • Monitor the task dashboard and the KPI list on a daily basis. • Review tickets and support responses weekly to ensure service quality and consistency. • Identify, based on observed situations and/or together with the team, the most effective ways to address and resolve tasks and tickets. • Create the reports and tools required for monitoring and review them periodically according to team and business needs. Administrative Area • Coordinate the team's administrative activities (reporting, inventory records, process documentation). Operations & Projects • Review requests received through support channels and provide assistance according to their criticality and complexity. • Ensure that established objectives and projects are delivered by each team member in line with agreed priorities and standards. • Actively contribute to projects with significant company-wide impact (e.g. BitLocker implementation, mailbox migrations). What makes you a good fit: • Minimum 4-5 years of experience in IT Support / Service Desk, including at least 1-2 years in a similar role such as Team Lead, Senior Support, or informal team coordination. • Experience managing ticket flows and SLAs in a high-volume support environment (preferably e-commerce or complex organizations). • Experience defining and monitoring support KPIs. • Participation in large-scale IT projects (e.g. security rollouts, email migrations). • Higher education degree, preferably in IT or related fields (or equivalent through relevant experience). • JIRA / JIRA Service Management: dashboards, filters, reports, worklogs. • End-user administration: Windows, macOS, Microsoft 365 / Exchange, Active Directory / Entra ID. • Endpoint security: knowledge of BitLocker, MDM solutions, and device policies. • Automation and scripting (PowerShell or similar) is an advantage. • Asset inventory and fixed asset management tools. • Reporting and data analysis (Excel / BI). • ITIL certifications are an advantage. • Good level of English, written and spoken.
