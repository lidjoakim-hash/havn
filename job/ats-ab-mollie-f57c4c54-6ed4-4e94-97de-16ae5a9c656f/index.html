Your impact You'll own the post-sales integration phase for Mollie's largest and most complex deals, taking full responsibility from contract signature through go-live and the handover that follows. That means acting as the central point of contact for the merchant and every internal Mollie team involved, setting out the activities, owners, and timelines needed to get each implementation live. You'll coordinate closely with sales, sales engineering, onboarding, compliance, product, and other teams to resolve issues as they come up, using your technical understanding to assess merchant problems, work through documentation, and route issues to the right place. You'll keep merchants, digital agencies, and internal stakeholders informed with regular updates, and you'll monitor progress against the agreed go-live date so you can act early when timelines are at risk. This is a newly formed function that's now expanding into new markets. That means you'll have genuine influence over how the role and its processes take shape, working closely with a flat, collaborative team where good practice is shared regardless of hierarchy.
