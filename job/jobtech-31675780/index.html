Job Description Key Responsibilities • Lead and coordinate cross-functional teams, projects, and development initiatives. • Manage multiple projects and activities in parallel, ensuring effective planning, prioritization, and execution. • Ensure project deliveries meet agreed requirements regarding budget, timelines, quality, and installation. • Support and coordinate local activities related to the global NOVA transformation programme. • Manage changes and prioritize initiatives based on business needs and operational requirements. • Monitor and follow up on project activities, risks, dependencies, milestones, and results. • Coach, support, and develop team members throughout the transformation journey. • Contribute to creating a sense of purpose, understanding, and manageability (MBH) within project teams and working groups. • Represent the business in relevant meetings and forums, promoting collaboration across organizational boundaries. • Work closely with local and global stakeholders to support alignment and successful project implementation. Education • University degree in Engineering, IT, Business/Economics, or a related field, or equivalent relevant professional experience. Must-Have Skills & Experience Project Management & Coordination • Experience in project management and/or leading projects or initiatives within production, engineering, digitalization, IT, or business transformation. • Experience leading and coordinating cross-functional teams or projects. • Ability to manage multiple activities and priorities simultaneously. • Strong planning, organizational, and coordination skills. • Ability to monitor project progress, identify risks and dependencies, and follow up on deliverables. • Ability to work both strategically and operationally, balancing overall objectives with day-to-day execution. Change Management & Business Development • Experience relevant to change management, operational development, or transformation initiatives. • Strong interest in leadership, organizational change, and continuous improvement. • Ability to support teams and stakeholders through organizational or operational changes. • Ability to translate business needs into structured activities and practical implementation. Stakeholder Management & Communication • Experience collaborating with different functions and stakeholder groups. • Ability to build relationships, create engagement, and facilitate cross-functional collaboration. • Strong communication and interpersonal skills. • Fluent Swedish and English, both written and spoken. Relevant Additional Experience The following experience would be particularly relevant to the assignment: • Experience working in manufacturing or production environments. • Experience with production-related digitalization, automation, or IT transformation. • Familiarity with MES, ERP, or other production-related business systems. • Experience coordinating activities within larger transformation programmes involving local and global stakeholders.
