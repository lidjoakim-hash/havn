Health & Safety Coordinator

SSC Space AB · Kiruna, Sweden

Welcome to SSC Space (formerly Swedish Space Corporation), the perfect workplace for those passionate about technology in general and space in particular. With space organizations, research institutes, institutional and commercial actors as customers, we offer specialist expertise in satellite communications, spacecraft consulting services, rocket and balloon systems, and much more. SSC Space also has one of the world’s largest global networks of ground stations for satellite communications. We own and operate the Esrange Space Center in Kiruna, northern Sweden, with the aim of launching satellites in the near future. Want to help create a safer workplace where people can do their best work? As Health & Safety Coordinator at Esrange Space Center, you will help managers turn work environment requirements into clear routines and practical improvements. You will work closely with teams across the site to identify risks, strengthen safe working practices and support compliance with Swedish work environment regulations. This is a hands-on role combining coordination, analysis and training in a technical environment, where your focus will be occupational health and safety. JOB REQUIREMENTS A relevant university degree in engineering, risk management, occupational health and safety, or equivalent knowledge gained through work experience. At least five years of relevant health and safety experience. In-depth knowledge of Swedish work environment regulations and experience applying them in practice. Experience working at industrial or technical sites in Sweden where operations involve significant health and safety risks. Fluency in Swedish and English. A category B driving license valid within the European Economic Area. You communicate clearly and can explain requirements in a way that helps people understand what they need to do and why. You are comfortable addressing risks, challenging unsafe practices and following up on agreed actions. You bring sound judgement, integrity and an analytical approach, together with the ability to build effective working relationships across different teams. JOB RESPONSIBILITIES Support managers in systematic work environment management, including risk assessments, safety rounds, allocation of work environment tasks and an annual work environment plan. Develop and improve health and safety processes in line with SSC Space’s global management system. Provide guidance on Swedish work environment regulations and coordinate health and safety inspections and audits with relevant authorities. Support incident investigations and root cause analysis, and follow up on actions to prevent recurrence. Establish routines for tracking required training and certificates for high-risk work, such as working at height. Coordinate chemical inventories, safety data sheets and chemical risk assessments together with the organization. Develop and deliver health and safety training and communication for managers and employees. Monitor health and safety indicators, support the Site Manager with performance reporting and contribute to improvements and certification preparations. You will collaborate with site management, line managers, safety representatives, HR, the Range Safety Office, the Environmental Manager, the Group HSE Officer and specialists responsible for flammable goods and explosives.
