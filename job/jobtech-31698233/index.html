ALTEN is looking to recruit a Senior SW Engineer / CICD Engineer for an on-site role in Gothenburg. You will contribute to modernizing and scaling embedded software delivery platforms, focusing on CI/CD, DevOps, software integration, and delivery automation. Your work will enable reliable, repeatable, and efficient software releases, replacing manual processes with automated, continuously tested workflows. What do we offer We offer exciting and varied tasks, a great working environment, and inspiring colleagues in a dynamic and international company. You will get the opportunity to work with leading technologies and develop your skills in a challenging and supportive environment. At ALTEN, we believe in growing together, and we offer you the chance to shape your own career path. About the role As a Senior SW Engineer / CICD Engineer, you will play a key role in operating, modernizing, and scaling embedded software delivery platforms. You will work in teams focused on CI/CD, DevOps, software integration, and delivery automation, building capabilities that enable efficient and reliable software releases. Your mission will involve replacing manual delivery and integration activities with an automated, continuously tested software factory. This includes artifact and configuration management, simulation workflows, system integration, release management, and dataset lifecycle automation. You will also develop new internal tools and services based on modern CI/CD principles, with a strong focus on quality, traceability, and developer experience. You will collaborate closely with a global community of engineers, including release teams, calibration engineers, simulation and data engineers, test engineers, and product owners. Your main responsibilities Operate and support enterprise Linux (RHEL or similar) and business-critical solutions Work with Jenkins, Git, Artifactory, and other CI/CD tools Operate and support workloads across on-premises, Microsoft Azure, and Kubernetes environments Monitor and troubleshoot systems using Prometheus, exporters, Grafana, and other observability tools Support upgrades, patching, maintenance windows, and operational changes Collaborate with engineering teams, stakeholders, and service providers to resolve issues and improve platform reliability Create and maintain runbooks, operational procedures, and architecture documentation Continuously improve automation, standardization, security, and operational processes Act as a technical expert and advisor, guiding colleagues and stakeholders Your profile You are a curious and driven engineer with a passion for understanding complex systems and improving operational efficiency.
