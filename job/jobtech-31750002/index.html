CNC Engineer – GROB / Mazak

Metallfabriken Ljunghäll Aktiebolag · Vimmerby, Sweden

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

We are looking for an experienced and hands-on CNC Engineer with strong technical troubleshooting skills and practical experience working with GROB and/or Mazak CNC machines. In this role, you will work directly with our CNC equipment at our Södra Vi facility, troubleshooting complex technical problems involving control systems, electrical systems and mechanical components. You do not need experience with both GROB and Mazak. Strong experience with either GROB or Mazak CNC machines is required. An important part of the role is also knowledge transfer and competence development. You will use your CNC expertise to support and train our shift-based Mechanics, Electricians and Automation Electricians, helping us build stronger technical capabilities within the organization. We are looking for someone who understands CNC machines in depth and wants to find the root cause of technical problems, not simply get the machine running again. Your main responsibilities Troubleshooting and fault finding on GROB and/or Mazak CNC machines Troubleshooting of CNC control systems Mechanical troubleshooting, repairs and adjustments Electrical troubleshooting and fault finding Diagnosing machine alarms and technical failures Reading and understanding electrical and mechanical drawings Troubleshooting sensors, drives, motors and machine components Supporting preventive and corrective maintenance Identifying and eliminating root causes of recurring machine problems Working closely with Automation, Maintenance, Engineering and Production to improve machine reliability and availability Training and developing our shift-based Mechanics, Electricians and Automation Electricians in CNC troubleshooting, diagnostics and systematic problem solving Sharing technical knowledge and building stronger in-house competence around GROB and Mazak CNC equipment Supporting the development of structured troubleshooting methods and technical standards Your background We are looking for someone with: Practical experience working with GROB or Mazak CNC machines Strong CNC machine troubleshooting experience Good understanding of CNC control systems Strong mechanical knowledge and troubleshooting skills Experience with electrical troubleshooting Ability to read electrical and mechanical drawings Good general PC and IT skills Experience from an industrial production environment A systematic and practical approach to problem solving The ability and willingness to train, support and develop other technicians Experience with one of the two machine brands is sufficient. Experience with both GROB and Mazak is an advantage. It is an advantage if you also have experience with Advanced diagnostics and parameter settings in CNC control systems Servo drives, motors, sensors and measurement systems Spindle, axis and positioning problems Machine geometry and alignment Preventive and predictive maintenance Automation and equipment connected to CNC machining lines CNC machine optimization and continuous improvement Developing technical training or troubleshooting standards Who are you? You are a hands-on engineer who enjoys solving difficult technical problems and working close to the equipment. When a machine stops, you want to understand why it stopped, not just reset the alarm and restart it. You work systematically, take responsibility and enjoy collaborating with Production, Maintenance, Engineering and Automation. You also enjoy sharing your knowledge with others. You can explain technical problems, diagnostics and troubleshooting methods in a practical and understandable way. You are positive, curious and open to new digital tools, diagnostic methods and technologies that can make troubleshooting faster and more effective.
