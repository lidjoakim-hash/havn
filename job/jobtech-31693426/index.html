Warm, creative nanny for a family in Vasastan Stockholm’s International Nanny Agency is looking for a warm, creative and professional nanny for a long-term role with a family in Vasastan. You might be an experienced nanny, or you might have worked in a preschool and want to use those skills in a home setting. Either way, we’d love to hear from you. About the role Location: Vasastan, Stockholm Children: two young children, aged 3 and 1 Hours: 2 afternoons a week, 16:00–19:00, with occasional late evenings and some weekends Days: Wednesday is required. The second day is ideally Thursday, but Tuesday also works. Start: February, with the option of a slower onboarding process to begin asap Duration: long term A typical afternoon Pick-up from preschool Outdoor play in parks and playgrounds, or play indoors Crafts and creative activities Simple, healthy meals made with whole foods Who we’re looking for Warm: you’re caring and patient, and children feel safe and happy with you Creative: you like coming up with playful activities, crafts and adventures, indoors and out Professional: you’re reliable, punctual and trustworthy, and you take your role seriously Experienced: you’ve worked with young children, either as a nanny or in a preschool You speak fluent Swedish You’re available on Wednesdays and want to stay with the family long term Good to know No pets in the home No allergies How to apply Send your CV and a few lines about yourself and your experience with children to info@stockholm-nanny.se, with “Vasastan – nanny” in the subject line. We review applications as they come in, so we encourage you to apply soon.
