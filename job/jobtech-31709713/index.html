About Premo Premo is owned by Dagens Nyheter (Bonnier News) and Svenska Dagbladet (Schibsted). Our business concept is: We ensure the spread of free speech and meet the demands of the resident's need for daily deliveries through co-distribution of newspapers and packages throughout Stockholm. Our vision is: We make life easier through superior deliveries. Every morning we share about 200,000 newspapers, magazines, packages and other products. Premo reaches about one million households and 2 million inhabitants in the Stockholm region every day of the week. Premo has about 1,100 employees, and most of our employees are distributors. We are proud that Premo is a multicultural company with about 50 different nationalities - we notice daily how people with different backgrounds make our company even better. Inclusion and equal treatment are obvious to us!   About the work You will work with the distribution of newspapers, magazines and packages within the Stockholm region.   To help you distribute you use a walking trolley, an electric bicycle, a moped car or a company car.   We are proud that Premo is a multicultural company with about 50 different nationalities - we notice daily how people with different backgrounds make our company even better. Inclusion and equal treatment are obvious to us! We can offer you: An independent and physically active work at night and early in the morning Work of around 40-60% and usually 4-5 hours per night Secure terms of employment – we have collective agreement A future opportunity to grow within the company since we care about talented employees!
