Company description: Ericsson AB Job description: Join our Team About us Ericsson has around 85,000 employees globally. Every day, people across the company use our products to find their next role, keep learning, bring in new talent and develop their teams. Some are enterprise and software-as-a-service platforms, some we build ourselves, and AI is changing what all of them can do. Our ambition is for employees to get things done without friction, and for leaders to make better decisions with the help of AI. We are building a trusted coworker, and forming product management as a discipline in our global People function. We want people to build it with us.  About the role For us, product management is about solving the right problems, for the right users, to achieve outcomes that matter. You will lead a set of products across Talent Acquisition, Talent and Development, Total Rewards, HR Operations and People Analytics. You will work alongside the People community, user experience and technical leads. You bring a perspective grounded in discovery, data and research, and you help the business see new opportunities, especially the ones AI opens up. You prioritise based on outcomes and value, so our time goes where it matters most to the people we serve. You will report to the Head of Product Management, People Digital Solutions, AI and Analytics, and be one of two Product Managers. Our way of working is still evolving, and you will help shape it.  What you will do •    Shape the strategy, vision and roadmap for your products. Balance short-term needs with longer-term bets, bringing together business priorities, People strategy, user needs and feasibility. •    Build a deep understanding of the people who use our products. Spend real time with employees and leaders, learn where they experience friction, and test ideas with them before we invest in building. •    Lead with AI. Find the problems where AI can make a real difference for people, and make sure what we build is valuable to them and viable for Ericsson. •    Take your products from idea to launch and beyond, and check they achieve what we set out to do. Keep the portfolio clear and coherent, and help decide what to invest in, reuse or retire. •    Lead your product team on the what and the why. Work closely with engineers, who own how we build, so everyone shares the same understanding of the problem, users and desired outcome.  Who you are You are as curious about technology as you are about people. You follow where AI is heading, are energised by what it makes possible, and care about how it can improve people's work. You use AI in your own work every day and know its limits. You turn unclear problems into clear next steps. You lead through influence, and you build trust by listening, being open about trade-offs and doing what you said you would. You hold your ground when it matters, and you change your mind when the evidence says so.  What you bring •    Experience as a Product Manager, with products that people use and value. •    Strong discovery skills, from user interviews to prototyping. •    Experience setting outcomes and measuring them with quantitative and qualitative data. •    Experience with AI-powered products. You can see which user problems AI can solve, and whether results are reliable enough. •    The ability to discuss feasibility with engineers and priorities with senior leaders. •    Agency. You take initiative, move things forward unprompted, and care whether what we build is used and creates value.  Sounds like you? We value how you think and how you learn as much as the experience you bring. If this role speaks to you, we would love to hear from you.
