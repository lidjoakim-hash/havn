We are looking for an Expert IT Analyst with core skills in data modelling and development. Nordea is a place where traditions meet tomorrow. Together, we impact millions of people’s daily lives by ensuring they can access our solutions anytime, anywhere, while safeguarding their personal data and wealth. Join us in making an impact on the banking industry. About our team Meet the Core Finance Technology team. Our role is to manage financial accounting and manage group procurement and third-party suppliers and contracts. Our team is also managing analysis and performance of Nordea business with its customers. The team is represented by people across the Nordics and Poland. Act as an Expert IT analyst: Lead solution design and development activities across teams. Capability to lead technical design discussions and solution decisions. Drive technical decisions and architecture discussions. Coordinate development planning, estimates, and delivery execution. Mentor developers and establish engineering best practices. Ensure code quality, review standards, and technical governance. Manage technical dependencies and support release planning. Who you are This is the right role for you if you a senior technical resource with strong expertise in Data Modelling, Snowflake Data Cloud, Snowflake Cortex AI, and modern Data Platform technologies. The individual should be capable of leading development activities, driving solution architecture, mentoring team members, coordinating technical delivery, and supporting data-driven modernization initiatives within the BRPPM ART. Your background and skills include: Strong expertise in Data Modelling (Conceptual, Logical, and Physical). Extensive experience with Snowflake Data Cloud architecture and implementation. Hands-on experience with Snowflake Cortex AI capabilities, AI-powered analytics, and data intelligence solutions. Experience with data engineering, transformation, and advanced analytics. Strong SQL, query optimization, and data warehouse design skills. Experience with DPT/Data Platform Technologies, data pipelines, data governance, and modern cloud data platforms. Data mapping, source-to-target design, and data quality management. Strong stakeholder management skills It would be ideal if you also have good experience in: Data Vault 2.0 Data Warehousing Data Products Gen AI Expert in SQL Data Architecture SAFe/agile environment Jira and DevOps delivery management.
