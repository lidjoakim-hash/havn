You’ll lead a high-performing team, work closely with SEO, Product, Analytics, and Marketing, and turn commercial insights into measurable results. As the affiliate landscape evolves, you’ll shape how the channel grows and stays competitive in the years ahead. YOUR DAY-TO-DAY - Own the affiliate channel’s growth and profitability. Drive revenue, customer acquisition, and growth while making decisions about where to invest and where to pull back. - Get more out of existing partnerships. Strengthen relationships with leading publishers, comparison websites, agencies, B2B partners and creators. - Find the next growth opportunities. Explore new markets, affiliate segments, content formats, and partnership models. Fast experiments are expected.Learn from failures, and scale what works. - Think beyond traditional affiliates. Build an affiliate strategy that supports Hostinger’s expanding product ecosystem and long-term ambitions. The landscape has changed, AI is taking over - how the activities within the channel should be different? - Lead a high-performing team. WHAT WILL HELP YOU SUCCEED - Proven experience scaling a significant affiliate business, ideally in B2C SaaS, hosting, VPN, or another competitive digital industry. - Deep understanding of the affiliate ecosystem, including major publishers, review platforms, content creators, and affiliate networks. - Strong commercial and negotiation skills.
