The opportunity This is a broad business and channel leadership role with influence over the future of Lindex’s digital commerce. You build an empowered Business Team and balance current sales and delivery with continued development of customer experience, omnichannel growth, operating model and digital capabilities. You play a key part in shaping a more customer- and market-led international retail business and helping accelerate Lindex’s future growth journey. About the role We are looking for an experienced e-commerce leader to accelerate profitable digital and omnichannel growth. As Business Team Director E-com, you own the digital sales channel and its P&L, spanning site, app, digital trading and merchandising, customer experience, fulfilment, returns and customer service. You connect commercial performance with digital product, technology, data and AI-enabled development to strengthen both today’s business and the next generation of commerce capabilities. You lead an empowered Business Team together with a Digital Co-Lead, combining business ownership, digital development and continuous improvement. The team is accountable for running, optimising and developing its area, balancing short-term performance with long-term growth and capability development. The Business Team is supported by Business & Process Developers, analytics capabilities and other functional expertise. The role reports to the Chief Commercial Officer and is part of the Commercial Management team. Key responsibilities Own and develop the e-commerce channel, with responsibility for P&L, profitable growth, customer value and commercial performance. Develop and scale digital customer journeys across discovery, purchase, fulfilment, returns and customer service. Drive digital trading, merchandising, conversion, personalisation, customer acquisition, loyalty and retention. Lead omnichannel business development and integrate physical and digital customer experiences. Translate customer, market and behavioural insights into measurable growth opportunities and business results. Drive operational excellence within fulfilment, e-commerce operations and customer service. Prioritise the e-commerce roadmap together with digital product, technology, data and operational leaders. Evaluate and develop next-generation commerce capabilities, including AI-enabled customer experiences and new digital business opportunities. Translate Lindex strategy into a clear business plan, priorities, resource allocation and measurable results. Lead the Business Team through the Run, Optimise and Develop agenda together with the Digital Co-Lead and supporting functions. Connect commercial priorities with digital development, ensuring that investments and roadmaps create clear customer and business value. Lead direct reports and functional specialists across reporting lines, developing leaders, capabilities and succession readiness. Follow up performance, identify deviations and reprioritise when market conditions or business needs change. About you You are a commercially grounded e-commerce leader who understands how trading, customer experience, operations, technology and data combine to create profitable growth.
