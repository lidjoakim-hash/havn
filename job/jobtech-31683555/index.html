We are looking for a licensed veterinarian with a few years of professional experience who wants to become part of our team and work broadly within small animal practice. With us, you will have the opportunity to use and further develop your expertise across several areas, from outpatient care and inpatient wards to emergency medicine, surgery, and on-call work. About AniCura Kumla Animal Hospital AniCura Kumla Animal Hospital provides highly qualified medical, emergency, and surgical veterinary care in a dynamic emergency setting and a welcoming environment. AniCura Kumla Animal Hospital is part of Area North, which includes animal hospitals and clinics across fourteen locations. The hospital consists of the Emergency Department, Inpatient Care Ward, Surgery Department, Outpatient Clinic, Rehabilitation Department, as well as support functions including Telephone Services and Reception. My name is Jens Berisson, and I work as the Hospital Director here at AniCura Kumla Animal Hospital. We are now looking to strengthen our fantastic team and are seeking a licensed veterinarian who is passionate about animal healthcare and wants to continue developing the hospital together with us. About the Role This role is ideal for someone who appreciates that no two days are the same. Your primary focus will be tailored to your experience and interests, but we would like you to be comfortable working across different areas of the hospital. Examples of responsibilities include: Outpatient consultations and medical workups. Care of hospitalized patients in the inpatient ward. Emergency patients and emergency medicine. Surgery and perioperative care. On-call and evening shifts. More advanced cases based on your competence and experience. Who Are You? We believe you are a veterinarian who: Is a licensed veterinarian with several years of clinical experience. Is confident in your professional role and able to work independently. Has experience from multiple areas of small animal practice, such as outpatient care, inpatient care, emergency medicine, and/or surgery. Enjoys a varied caseload and is motivated to broaden your clinical expertise. Is confident in making clinical decisions and prioritizing when required. Has a genuine interest in high-quality patient care and professional communication with pet owners. Is a team player who enjoys collaborating with skilled and dedicated colleagues. Is curious and wants to continue developing professionally, whether by deepening expertise in a specific area or maintaining a broad clinical role. Is fluent in English, both spoken and written. Experience in emergency medicine, surgery, or a specialist qualification in canine and feline medicine is considered an advantage. Why Choose AniCura Kumla Animal Hospital? At AniCura Kumla Animal Hospital, you will become part of a dedicated and highly competent team where collaboration, job satisfaction, and patient focus are at the heart of everything we do. We are a well-equipped animal hospital with extensive expertise in areas such as surgery, anaesthesia, emergency care, and advanced diagnostics. This provides excellent opportunities for both variety and continued professional development. What many of our employees appreciate most is the strong sense of community. Here, we support one another, share knowledge, and work together to provide the best possible care for our patients. About AniCura Sweden Since 2018, AniCura Sweden has been part of Mars Veterinary Health, a global network that offers outstanding opportunities for knowledge sharing, innovation, and professional development. With more than 450 clinics and animal hospitals across Europe, AniCura is one of the leading providers of advanced veterinary care. In Sweden, we are one of the largest veterinary care providers, with operations spanning the entire country and a strong local presence.
