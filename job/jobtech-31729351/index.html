The Education Office (EdO) is one of SSE’s Professional Services units and consists of around 58 colleagues. As a central part of the SSE journey, we support and develop key academic services for students and stakeholders across our BSc, MSc, and PhD programs. Our work spans assessment, program management, scholarships, international collaborations, academic support, central course and exam coordination, as well as degrees and certificates. We offer you A diverse role at one of the top business schools in Europe with ambitious students and staff in a dynamic, academic environment. This role is ideal for a highly organized, people-oriented person who enjoys complex processes. The role We are looking for Admissions coordinator for our BSc programs. The successful candidate will belong to a team of Admissions & Program management at Education Office, reporting to Group Manager of that team. Your tasks include, but are not limited to: Coordinating application and admissions process for the BSc programs, ensuring an efficient, accurate and applicant-focused process. Assessing applications, including eligibility, qualifications and supporting documentation, and participating actively in the selection of candidates. Guiding prospective students and applicants regarding SSE, the BSc programs, admissions requirements and the application process. Collaborating with faculty, and other internal and external stakeholders involved in admissions. Contributing to recruitment and conversion activities for prospective and admitted students, including information sessions, events and other applicant-facing activities. Supporting the Program Manager for the BSc in Business & Economics with diverse program-related tasks and activities, in accordance with the agreed division of responsibilities and based on the needs of the program. Contributing to the continuous development of admissions processes, systems and ways of working, including the use of digital tools and AI where relevant. Working in a dynamic organization where priorities, processes and responsibilities may evolve. You should therefore be comfortable adapting to change, taking on new tasks and contributing to developing new ways of working. Occasional travel and participation in events outside regular office activities may occur. Your profile As Admissions Coordinator, you will be coordinating admissions process and processing applications and enquiries, often within fixed deadlines. You therefore need to be structured, detail-oriented and comfortable taking responsibility for processes from start to finish. You are proactive, flexible and able to prioritize while maintaining a high level of accuracy and service. At the same time, admissions process is highly collaborative, and you enjoy working closely with colleagues, faculty and other stakeholders. The position involves contact with prospective students and applicants. You therefore have strong interpersonal and communication skills and can explain sometimes complex admissions requirements in a clear, professional and service-minded way. You are also comfortable handling challenging cases and communicating decisions consistently and respectfully. You are comfortable interpreting admissions requirements, assessing applicants and documentation against established criteria, and applying rules and procedures consistently. Qualifications: An academic education Experience of, or a strong understanding of, admissions processes within Swedish higher education. Experience of working with Swedish and international high school qualifications Swedish and English, both spoken and written. An interest in how generative AI and digital tools can support efficient and high-quality admissions processes. Meritorious: Previous experience working with admissions in Swedish higher education, particularly BSc admissions.
