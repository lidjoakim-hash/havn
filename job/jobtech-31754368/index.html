Data Analyst

Sandvik Aktiebolag · Sweden

💰 ca. 450 000–550 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 4.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Turn product data into decisions that improve customer value and product performance. By defining the metrics that matter and connecting product, customer, and commercial signals, you help uncover where the biggest opportunities and challenges are and turn evidence into action. The Cloud Product Organization brings together the capabilities needed to build and grow our cloud products. With clear responsibilities and close connections across the wider TFS portfolio, we work together to create customer value. About the job You build the evidence base for a growth-focused product organization, defining the metrics that matter and making product performance easy to understand. Your analysis supports discovery, prioritization, and decisions across the product portfolio, while helping identify how data can create direct commercial value for customers in and around the products they use. You report to the Head of Cloud Product. Job responsibilities Own product analytics and define, track and develop the metrics that matter. Analyze product usage and user behavior, translating findings into concrete recommendations. Support product discovery and prioritization with evidence and actionable insights. Develop and maintain dashboards, analyses, and recurring insights that make product performance easy to understand. Combine product usage, customer and commercial signals to identify opportunities, risks, and trends. Turn gathered data into customer-facing insights and opportunities that create commercial value in and around our products, while improving adoption and product performance. Location & flexibility This position offers flexibility to work remotely or from one of our entities in Sweden, Germany, the US, or India. Your profile You bring a analytical background and know how to move from complex data to conclusions that non-technical stakeholders can use. Experience in data analytics, product analytics, business intelligence, or a similar analytical field gives you the foundation to understand product behavior and turn it into practical recommendations. Your background includes: Hands-on experience analyzing product usage and user behavior, including defining and working with KPIs, product metrics and measurement frameworks. Strong analytical skills and experience working with large or complex datasets. Experience with analytical and visualization tools such as Power BI. The ability to communicate analytical findings clearly to Product Managers, commercial teams and leadership. Preferably, experience in digital, SaaS or cloud products and working knowledge of SQL or similar data-querying approaches. Experience in a product-led or growth-focused organization is an advantage. It’s also beneficial if you understand SaaS/product metrics, or have supported product discovery, prioritization and experimentation with data. Curiosity shapes how you work. You investigate why customers behave as they do, while keeping the focus on what the evidence means for customers, the product and the business. Collaborative yet independent, you communicate complex findings clearly, work pragmatically and aren’t afraid to challenge assumptions constructively when the data points in a different direction. The team You work closely with Product Managers and the Head of Cloud Product, as well as Data Services, which provides the underlying data layer. Customer Success and Sales are also important partners, bringing customer and revenue signals that help build a more complete picture of product performance. Our culture Our role is clear – through every action, every day, we make the shift and advance the world through engineering. We believe in an inclusive, equal and open-minded culture, and we nurture our diversities to form a solid foundation for achieving great results.  Add fair and rewarding benefits, as well as many different career options.  Are you intrigued?
