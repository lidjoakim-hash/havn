Who We Are Verkada is transforming how organizations protect their people and places with an integrated, privacy-sensitive AI-powered platform that includes solutions for video security, access control, air quality sensors, alarms, intercoms, and visitor management. We’ve got serious momentum in the market: more than 30,000 customers (including 100+ of the Fortune 500), a $5.8B valuation, more than $1 billion in annualized bookings, and backing from CapitalG, Sequoia Capital, General Catalyst, Felicis Ventures, Next47 and more. Physical AI is one of the most consequential technology shifts of our time, and Verkada is at the center of it. You can look at all kinds of communities to see our platform’s impact in the world. It's the retailer that uses our agentic AI to deter theft before it happens. The warehouse that uses AI-powered alerts to make sure its team is protected on the floor with proper PPE. The school that’s alerted to a threat in real-time and triggers a lockdown in seconds, not minutes. We’re rapidly scaling this impact: today, more than 2 million Verkada devices are deployed across 170+ countries. About the Role Our Marketing team brings together marketing operations, engineering, data, and AI to make complex go-to-market work easier to manage. We create reliable systems that help marketers work more effectively, move with greater speed, and deliver measurable business results. As a Senior Marketing Operations Manager, you will lead the campaign operations foundation that supports demand generation while helping shape how AI changes the way our Marketing team works. You will use automation, agents, connected data, and accessible technical solutions to reduce repetitive work, improve execution, and create new capabilities across Marketing. This is a collaborative, hands-on role for someone who enjoys supporting critical marketing systems, working through evolving business needs, and developing practical solutions. Success will be measured by faster execution, improved pipeline outcomes, higher-quality data, and sustained adoption of the systems you help create. We are committed to a thriving in-office culture. This role requires that you be on-site at our office in London five days a week.
