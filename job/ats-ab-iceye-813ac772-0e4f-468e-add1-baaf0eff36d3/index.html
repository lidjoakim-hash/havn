SUPO, where required) - We are unable to support relocation for this role. Why this role matters: As a Stock Coordinator Intern, you’ll keep ICEYE’s materials accurate and ready for production. Every component that ends up in a satellite is stored, counted and moved by our team, so your work directly supports our production and our missions. You will support our daily stock management, help us keep stock quality high and take part in continuous improvement projects. Along the way, you will also be introduced to our inbound and outbound processes, giving you a broader view of ICEYE logistics operations and the chance to learn and support them as a whole. Our team will train you on our systems and procedures, and you will receive daily support while gradually taking more ownership. Who We Are ICEYE is the world leader in sovereign intelligence from space. We deliver persistent monitoring capabilities to detect and respond to changes in any location on Earth. ICEYE owns the world's largest and most advanced SAR (synthetic aperture radar) satellite constellation. To our customers we provide intelligence with unmatched quality, latency and revisit times, in any weather, day or night. To governments who choose to operate their own constellation we provide this proven capability as a sovereign system. ICEYE-built constellations serve customers in defence and intelligence, environmental monitoring, insurance and emergency management. We enable fast decisions that contribute to a safer future. Founded and headquartered in Finland, ICEYE operates globally with over 1000 employees across Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.   Your day-to-day responsibilities   - Support daily stock management by receiving, storing and picking materials correctly - Help keep stock quality high and counts accurate, so the system always matches what is on the shelf - Raise issues early and clearly - Learn our inbound and outbound processes and support them as part of ICEYE logistics operations - Take part in continuous improvement projects and, over the internship, start suggesting and driving improvements What we’re looking for No previous experience is required. If you are precise, service minded and curious about how material flows in a space company, we will train you and help you get started. Must haves: - Current studies or recent graduation in logistics, supply chain, engineering or a related field - Good IT skills, including Excel and/or Google Sheets, and a willingness to learn new systems - Readiness for hands-on work, such as moving light to medium items, printing labels and organising shelves - High attention to detail - Fluent spoken and written English Nice to haves: - Fluent Finnish - Experience working in a warehouse, logistics or goods receiving environment, including familiarity with inbound and outbound processes - Experience with an enterprise resource planning (ERP) or warehouse management system (WMS), such as NetSuite or SAP - Experience with simple material handling equipment, such as pallet jacks and cargo dollies Application Process - Outline the stages of this role, including task stages.
