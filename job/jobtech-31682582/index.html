Network Architect – Cisco & Aruba

Cyber Instincts AB · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 510 000–650 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 2.2.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

About the assignment We are looking for an experienced network and security architect to join a large-scale programme at one of our clients. You will be part of an international network team with members in Sweden and abroad, and play a key role in shaping design solutions in a fast-paced project. The assignment starts in as soon as possible and runs until the end of May 2027 with possibility of extension. You will work about half of your time onsite in Stockholm and the rest remotely. What you will do Develop network and security solutions together with the team Present and anchor design proposals in dialogue with the client Produce design documentation such as HLD, LLD and ADD, as well as delivery instructions Ensure that solutions meet both internal and client requirements Follow up that implementation is carried out according to design and timeline Who we think you are You have several years of experience as a network architect or in a similar senior design role You have in-depth knowledge of Cisco and Aruba You have experience with EVPN/VXLAN in data centre or campus environments You have a solid understanding of network security: NGFW, WAF and IDS/IPS You are used to documenting solutions in a structured and clear way You are fluent in both Swedish and English, spoken and written Why CyberInstincts? With us, you become part of a specialist cybersecurity team working on exciting assignments with large clients. You can choose to work as an employee or as an independent consultant, and we support you throughout the assignment. Apply Send your CV and briefly describe a network project where you were responsible for the design. We review applications continuously, so feel free to apply today.
