Requirements Engineer

A Snapshot of Your Day In this role, you will be responsible for maintaining and publishing machine, spare parts, and partner data in Energy Shop, ensuring high data quality and timely availability for users worldwide. You will act as the primary point of contact for Energy Shop-related questions, supporting internal stakeholders across different functions and helping them get the most value from the platform. Your day will also involve supporting the testing, validation, and implementation of new features and enhancements, while monitoring and reporting on key performance indicators to drive continuous improvement.   How you’ll make a difference Extract, validate, and manage data from source systems (including SAP). Drive Energy Shop improvements by gathering requirements, prioritizing feedback, and collaborating with development teams. Identify and resolve data quality issues, manage backlogs, and coordinate corrective actions. Improve data quality, process efficiency, automation, and AI-supported workflows. Collaborate across functions to improve data flows, standardize processes, and strengthen data governance. What you bring Experience with spare parts catalogs, product information systems, or e-commerce platforms. Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Information Systems, or equivalent experience. Strong knowledge of master data management, data governance, and data quality principles. Experience with SAP and managing large datasets. Experience with requirements gathering, process improvement, or digital platform development is an advantage. Proficiency in Excel; Power BI or similar analytical tools are a plus. Knowledge of rotating equipment, gas turbines, or industrial service operations is beneficial. Strong analytical, problem-solving, communication, and stakeholder management skills. Able to work independently, take ownership, and collaborate effectively in a global environment. Fluent in English.     Who is Siemens Energy?
