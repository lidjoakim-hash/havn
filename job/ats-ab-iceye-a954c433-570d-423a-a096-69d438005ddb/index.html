SUPO, where required) WHY THIS ROLE MATTERS: As a Customs Specialist, you’ll keep ICEYE’s goods moving compliantly across the world. You'll drive the customs compliance and documentation that keeps ICEYE's satellites moving smoothly between Finland, the EU and our sites worldwide. That means classifying goods correctly, applying the right customs procedures, and making sure every shipment clears without unnecessary delay or cost. You'll work closely with the Freight Forwarding Manager to plan how goods travel by road, air and sea, and use your knowledge of trade agreements and preferential origin rules to reduce duty and landed cost wherever possible. WHO WE ARE ICEYE is the world leader in sovereign intelligence from space. We deliver persistent monitoring capabilities to detect and respond to changes in any location on Earth. ICEYE owns the world's largest and most advanced SAR (synthetic aperture radar) satellite constellation. To our customers we provide intelligence with unmatched quality, latency and revisit times, in any weather, day or night. To governments who choose to operate their own constellation we provide this proven capability as a sovereign system. ICEYE-built constellations serve customers in defence and intelligence, environmental monitoring, insurance and emergency management. We enable fast decisions that contribute to a safer future. Founded and headquartered in Finland, ICEYE operates globally with over 1000 employees across Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.   YOUR DAY-TO-DAY RESPONSIBILITIES   - Own day-to-day customs clearance across the EU: release for circulation, outward/inward processing, transit (T1, T2, TIR), customs warehousing, bonded storage and temporary admission. - Maintain HS/Taric classification and customs valuation for both supplier-sourced and in-house manufactured goods. - Spot risks in international shipments early and resolve them before they reach destinations. - Understand and apply Incoterms with confidence, allocating cost, risk and responsibility correctly across every lane. - Prepare for and take part in customs audits, and help strengthen the internal audit and control programme. - Run Binding Tariff Information (BTI) applications regularly and keep the resulting rulings current. - Write clear SOPs that colleagues can follow without you having to explain them. - Pursue free trade agreement benefits (preferential origin, EUR.1, statements on origin, REX registration) and maintain the supplier declaration chain. - Track and report the impact of your work: duty avoided, duty recovered and landed cost reduced. - Contribute to Freight Forwarding strategy across road, air and sea, flagging where routing, mode or documentation choices affect customs treatment or cost. - Recognise dangerous goods shipments (IATA DGR, ADR, IMDG) and check the required documentation. - Support material flow between ICEYE's Espoo site and our sites worldwide, reconciling physical goods against ERP, WMS and TMS records.
