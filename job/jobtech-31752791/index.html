Format Developer (Retail)

H & M Hennes & Mauritz GBC AB · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 540 000–650 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 19.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Are you passionate about the future of physical retail and turning strategy into tangible customer experiences? Do you understand what makes a store format commercially relevant, operationally strong and scalable across markets? We are looking for a Format Developer to join our Retail Strategy team within Commercial at H&M. In this role, you will work at the intersection of strategy and execution, helping shape how our stores evolve and ensuring that strategic direction translates into relevant, concrete and commercially viable store formats. WHAT YOU’LL DO As a Format Developer, you will: Translate overarching retail strategies into tangible store formats and concrete format developments. Lead and coordinate the creation of new store formats and specific developments to increase relevance and performance in our store portfolio. Partner with teams within and beyond retail to ensure cohesive end-to-end execution and define the project pipeline across initiatives together with relevant stakeholders. Develop clear briefs and guiding principles that enable different functions and markets to move from direction to execution. Monitor store and format performance, identify opportunities and capture learnings from implementation. Support global rollouts through clear documentation, playbooks, guidelines and rollout principles. Contribute hands-on throughout projects, ensuring that strategic intent is translated into solutions that work in the reality of our stores. WHO YOU’LL WORK WITH This is a broad and highly collaborative role. You will work across Commercial, global functions and Sales Markets, connecting perspectives across areas such as customer experience, assortment, store operations, interior and technology. You will act as a bridge between strategic development and operational execution, bringing different stakeholders together around a clear direction for our physical retail experience. The role reports to the Head of Retail Strategy. WHO YOU ARE We are looking for people with… A background as a senior retail professional, with a strong understanding of how physical stores operate and how to generate commercial impact and create great customer experiences. Experience driving complex, cross-functional projects or initiatives from direction through implementation. A strong understanding of store concepts, formats and the different components that shape the physical retail experience. Strong stakeholder management skills and the ability to align multiple functions and perspectives. The ability to translate broad strategic direction into concrete briefs, principles and actions. A commercial and customer-oriented mindset, combined with an understanding of operational realities. The ability to work independently, navigate ambiguity and create structure in complex topics. Strong communication and collaboration skills. Experience from fashion retail or another large, complex retail organization is highly relevant. Experience from H&M or a similar global retail environment is an advantage given the cross-functional nature and complexity of the role. And people who are… Curious, pragmatic and outcome-oriented. Comfortable working across organizational boundaries and challenging, influencing and bringing people with you. Able to connect the right perspectives, challenge assumptions and move initiatives forward, without needing to own every area yourself. WHO WE ARE H&M Group is a global company of strong fashion brands and ventures. Our goal is to prove that there is no compromise between exceptional design, affordable prices, and sustainable solutions. We want to liberate fashion for the many, and our customers are at the heart of every decision we make. We are made up of thousands of passionate and talented colleagues united by our shared culture and values. Together, we want to use our power, our scale, and our knowledge to push the fashion industry towards a more inclusive and sustainable future.
