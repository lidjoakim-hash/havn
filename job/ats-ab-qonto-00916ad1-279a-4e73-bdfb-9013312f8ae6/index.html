Lead Sales Inbound - News Business - Central Europe

Qonto · Barcelona, Spain, Spain

Join us as Lead Central Europe – New Business Sales to build, coach and scale the inbound sales team covering Central Europe, primarily Germany and the Netherlands. You’ll report to the Head of New Business – Sales and manage a team of inbound sales representatives. You’ll also be the point of contact for the Central Europe Managing Director on the Sales Acquisition channel.   ➡️ WHAT YOU’LL DO - Grow, develop and manage your team: Run structured 1:1s, team meetings and regular feedback to build sales skills and keep activity and quality metrics consistent across Central Europe. - Build the team: Recruit and onboard new salespeople as the team grows to support growth targets, with hiring validated by the Head of New Business – Sales. - Shape the sales process: Define and refine how the team runs discovery, demo and closing in each market. Adapt established frameworks when market dynamics or team performance shift. - Manage with data: Use Salesforce reporting to track team metrics, diagnose performance gaps in conversion and pipeline health, and decide what to coach on. - Work with the teams around you: Partner with Product, Onboarding and Sales Operations to improve sales materials and the customer journey. Feed ground-level input on lead quality and go-to-market back to Partnerships, Marketing and the Central Europe Managing Director.   ➡️ WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR - B2B SaaS sales experience: You know the full sales cycle, you’ve sold on value and met or exceeded quota, and you can show others how. - Management, coaching and team development: You have at least a first experience managing people. You’ve coached salespeople, supported new hires and helped build a results-oriented culture through regular, structured feedback. - Salesforce and sales analytics: You’re advanced in Salesforce and comfortable with Outreach and Aircall. You read pipeline and conversion data to find the cause of a gap, then design the fix. - Process discipline: You turn strategy into repeatable processes that a team can execute every day. - Product and market knowledge: You learn the product, pricing and positioning for Central Europe in depth and can teach them to your team.   ➡️ WHAT WE CAN OFFER YOU - Real ownership of a team: You decide team composition, how the team executes its sales cycles in each market and the performance standards it works to. - Influence beyond your own targets: Your input on lead quality and go-to-market helps shape how acquisition grows across Central Europe, not only whether the team hits this quarter’s numbers. - A direct line to the Central Europe Managing Director: You’re their point of contact on the Sales Acquisition channel. - A seat between sales and Growth: You work with Partnerships, Marketing and Operations on demand generation and lead routing, and with Product, Onboarding and Sales Operations on materials and customer journey. - Established frameworks to build on: Proven approaches for pipeline management, coaching cadences and conversion optimization, which you adapt to your markets. The stack is Salesforce, Outreach and Aircall. [DIVERSITY] At Qonto, we understand that true diversity isn’t just about ticking boxes on a hiring checklist.
