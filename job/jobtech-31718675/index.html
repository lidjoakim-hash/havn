Want to influence the bigger picture? At Saab, you can realise amazing innovations that keep people and societies safe. Together, we can truly make an impact - and your part matters. Your role We are looking for a Head of Product Management for our new Product Unit Command, Control & Autonomy. The new product unit within Business Unit Naval Combat Systems, develops the next generation of Naval Command, Control & Autonomy systems, built on a modern software stack and serving as the foundation for Saab's future naval capabilities. The product unit is driving the transition towards software-defined, modular and upgradeable products that support advanced naval platforms while enabling future autonomous capabilities and Multi-Domain Operations. As Head of Product Management, you will lead and develop a team of Product Managers responsible for key parts of the product portfolio. Your mission is to define the overall product vision, strategy and portfolio direction, ensuring alignment between customer needs, business priorities and technology evolution. Working closely with Development, Marketing & Sales and customers, you will establish the frameworks, priorities and strategic direction that enable Product Managers to successfully develop and evolve their respective product areas. As a member of the product unit Management Team, you will help shape the future of software-defined naval capabilities, ensuring that our Command, Control & Autonomy portfolio remains at the forefront of technology, customer value and operational effectiveness. At the same time, you will play a key role in building a world-class Product Management capability within the product unit. Key responsibilities: Lead, coach and develop a high-performing Product Management organization. Own the overall product vision, strategy and portfolio roadmap. Drive portfolio prioritization, investment decisions and lifecycle management. Ensure alignment between customer value, business goals and technology strategy. Support Product Managers in defining and executing product roadmaps. Identify future customers and market needs and translate them into strategic product initiatives. Drive the transition towards software-defined, modular and AI-enabled products. Build a culture of accountability, collaboration and continuous improvement. Work closely with Development, Marketing & Sales and customers. Contribute to the strategic development of product unit Command, Control & Autonomy. Your profile To be successful in this role, you are an authentic and modern leader who believes that empowered teams, clear direction and strong collaboration are key to building successful products. You are passionate about developing people and creating an environment where Product Managers can take ownership, grow and succeed. At the same time, you provide the strategic direction and portfolio perspective needed to align product investments with customer needs and business objectives. You lead through vision, values and objectives rather than detailed control, and you are comfortable navigating a complex stakeholder landscape while building trust across the organization. We believe you have: Proven experience in Product Management, Product Strategy or Business Development. The ability to lead through influence, create alignment across stakeholders and drive strategic product decisions. Experience developing product strategies and product roadmaps in a technology-intensive environment. A strong track record of driving growth, innovation and customer value. Experience from software-intensive and high-tech environments. An understanding of modern software engineering practices, AI-enabled development and digital transformation. Strong stakeholder management and communication skills. The ability to create clarity, alignment and direction across a complex product portfolio.
