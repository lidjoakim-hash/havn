Veritaz is a leading IT staffing solutions provider in Sweden, committed to advancing individual careers and aiding employers in ensuring the perfect talent fit. With a proven track record of successful partnerships with top companies, we have rapidly grown our presence in the USA, Europe, and Sweden as a dependable and trusted resource within the IT industry. Job Overview Role Summary Our client is seeking an experienced and technically driven Frontend Engineer for a consulting assignment within a fast-growing, international product company. The assignment focuses on developing and scaling a modern digital platform, with particular emphasis on frontend architecture, performance, accessibility, and technical excellence. The role is suited to an engineer with strong hands-on expertise in React and TypeScript, experience building complex web applications, and the ability to take ownership of critical parts of a product's frontend architecture. A central part of the mission is to build high-quality, scalable, and responsive user interfaces while ensuring excellent performance, maintainability, and accessibility. The engineer will work closely with product, design, and backend teams in a fast-paced, product-oriented environment. Key Responsibilities Lead the design, development, and deployment of features across the client's web platform. Design, develop, and maintain scalable frontend architecture using React and TypeScript. Take ownership of key areas of the codebase, ensuring high standards of code quality, maintainability, and scalability. Build modern, intuitive, and responsive user interfaces with a strong focus on user experience. Collaborate closely with product and design teams to translate requirements into robust technical solutions. Contribute to the development and maintenance of design systems, frontend architecture, and integrations. Optimize application performance and ensure excellent Core Web Vitals across complex user interfaces. Ensure web accessibility in accordance with WCAG standards, including keyboard navigation and screen reader support. Work with backend developers and contribute across the technology stack when required. Technical Stack & Requirements Frontend: Strong hands-on experience with React and TypeScript. Backend: Experience with Node.js and the ability to contribute to backend development when required. Frontend architecture: Strong understanding of application architecture, state management, data flows, and scalable frontend solutions. Performance optimization: Practical experience optimizing frontend performance and working with Core Web Vitals, including LCP, INP, and CLS. Web accessibility: Good knowledge of WCAG, keyboard navigation, and screen reader compatibility. Complex applications: Experience building and optimizing large-scale, data-intensive, or complex user interfaces. Cloud & deployment: Understanding of cloud environments, web application deployment, and production operations. Engineering quality: Experience writing secure, maintainable, and scalable code and contributing to technical architecture decisions. Qualifications & Experience The consultant will have a strong background in frontend engineering and proven experience developing and delivering modern web products. Deep practical expertise in React and TypeScript is essential, together with a solid understanding of frontend architecture, performance optimization, and accessibility. Experience working in product-driven technology companies, international engineering environments, or organizations with large-scale and complex digital platforms is highly advantageous.
