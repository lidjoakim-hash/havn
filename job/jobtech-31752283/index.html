På ABB hjälper vi industrier att bli mer resurseffektiva och renare – och varje person här bidrar till resultaten. Du kommer att få möjlighet att leda, få stöd att växa och kunna vara stolt över den påverkan vi skapar tillsammans. Gå med oss och hjälp till att driva det som driver världen. Denna position rapporterar till: Chapter Lead __ Our R&D engineers and scientists develop breakthrough technologies and products that change the way the world works and industries do business. We constantly expand beyond convention, while retaining our focus on delivering solid returns for our customers and shareholders We are now looking for several R&D Engineers with a mechanical focus to join one of our agile development teams Your role and responsibilities In this role, you will have the opportunity to contribute to ABB’s advancement with technology, product, and system development. Each day, you will identify and implement innovative solutions to relevant problems. You will also showcase your abilities by providing ideas and by being able to work both while managing your own tasks and as a part of a Research & Development (R&D) team. You will be reporting to the R&D Electromechanical Chapter Leader and joining the Load Control Agile Unit based in Västerås, Sweden. This unit operates within ABB Electrification/Smart Power division, dedicated to developing and taking care of our portfolio within low voltage products such as contactors and soft starters. The work model for the role is: Onsite You will be mainly accountable for: Working as an independent and reliable contributor in R&D projects Performing various tasks in the assigned area related to electromechanical design, from building concepts, gathering requirements to designing, implementing, integrating, testing, technology transfer or maintenance, and operations. Understanding and applying applicable processes, standards, guidelines, and requirements, including architectural and design constraints. Managing the achievement of assigned targets (quality, time, scope, cost) and raising and addressing risks. Our team dynamics You will be part of an agile development team, working closely with a Product Owner. The Product Owner sets the direction and goals – the team decides how to accomplish them. This role is not task‑driven in the traditional sense. Instead, you will work in a problem‑solving engineering environment. Qualifications for the role Relevant experience in product development or maintenance is an advantage. We are recruiting across experience levels, from recent graduates to engineers with 5+ years of experience. A Masters degree in mechanics, mechatronics, engineering physics or equivalent. Basic knowledge in CAD software and PLM tools is meritorious. Fluency in English is a requirement, Swedish is a plus. You thrive in situations where you can work freely and do not rely on having every task spelled out ahead of time. You are structured, communicative, and able to define both what needs to be done and what you need from others in order to achieve results. You enjoy working in a collaborative environment while maintaining a high level of initiative. More about us ABB Smart Power provides energy distribution solutions for data centers, industrial and manufacturing plants, critical infrastructure and commercial buildings. The Division’s technical teams work closely with industry partners, delivering advanced solutions that support rapid growth, energy transition, and sustainability objectives. The Division’s portfolio includes industrial circuit breakers, low-voltage systems, motor starting applications, and safety devices like switches and relays. Its Power Protection unit supports the world’s largest data center companies with advanced energy-efficient UPS solutions. The Division’s ABB Ability™ Energy Manager provides a scalable, easy-to-use platform that helps organizations save energy and reduce CO2 emissions.
