WHY FACULTY? We established Faculty in 2014 because we thought that AI would be the most important technology of our time. Since then, we’ve worked with over 350 global customers to transform their performance through human-centric AI. You can read about our real-world impact here https://faculty.ai/impact. We don’t chase hype cycles. We innovate, build and deploy responsible AI which moves the needle - and we know a thing or two about doing it well. We bring an unparalleled depth of technical, product and delivery expertise to our clients who span government, finance, retail, energy, life sciences and defence. Our business, and reputation, is growing fast and we’re always on the lookout for individuals who share our intellectual curiosity and desire to build a positive legacy through technology. AI is an epoch-defining technology, join a company where you’ll be empowered to envision its most powerful applications, and to make them happen. The Internal Communications Lead will build the machine and communications culture that keep Faculty's leadership and teams genuinely connected as we scale - carrying vision, strategy and our worldview on AI to every team undiluted, and surfacing the stories from across the business worth telling This role has two core mandates: 1. Building the internal communications machine - you will design and own how leadership and our teams stay connected day to day - whether live or written, scheduled or ad hoc, and whatever formats a genuinely global, always-on business needs. You will build the architecture - cadence, ownership, structure - so the right message reaches the right people at the right moment, and noise becomes context rather than clutter. 2. Culture, Voice & Connection at Scale - you will be the person who makes sure leadership's vision, strategy and worldview on AI actually reach our teams everywhere, honest and crisp, without being diluted, delayed, or lost in translation as we grow. Leadership owns their voice and their point of view - that stays theirs. Your job is building the stage it's delivered from: sharp, timely, and sensitive to how a message lands differently across cultures and geographies. A team quietly solved a hard client problem. Someone's work never got the credit it deserved. A pattern of small wins nobody's connected into a story yet. You go looking for this, building the instincts and the relationships across the business to find the stories worth telling. - How we organise ourselves to communicate effectively - The architecture and judgment behind all of it so our people get a clear read of what matters without being buried under what doesn't.
