About Wolt At Wolt, we create technology that brings joy, simplicity and earnings to the neighborhoods of the world. In 2014 we started with delivery of restaurant food. Now we’re building the delivery of (almost) everything and you’ll find us in over 500 cities in 30 countries around the world. In 2022 we joined forces with DoorDash and together we keep on dreaming big and expanding across the globe. Working at Wolt isn’t always easy, but it’s definitely exciting. Here you’ll learn more, build more, and ship more than in most other companies. You’ll be challenged a lot, but also have a lot of fun on the way. So, if you’re a self-starter with drive and entrepreneurial spirit, this could be the ride of your life. About the Team Our Infrastructure teams build the foundation that engineers at Wolt, Deliveroo, and DoorDash build on: the compute, cloud, and networking capabilities that enable Engineering teams to develop, deploy, and run services at global scale. The EU Compute team has a specific focus on provisioning, scaling, and the efficient operation of GPU infrastructure for AI and ML workloads. This is an emerging space with plenty of greenfield opportunities. We're looking for a Staff Engineer to be a hands-on technical leader for this infrastructure work that crosses team and brand boundaries. You'll set direction for the platform, turn complex problems into deliverable plans, write and review production code, and help other engineers make sound decisions. It's an exciting time to join: we're building a global platform and the choices made now will shape how engineers across the company ship for years. What you’ll be doing You'll own some of the hardest infrastructure problems in the company and make the platform easier to operate and safer to build on. We’re determined to build great platform products and user experiences, and you’ll be pivotal in making this reality. Day-to-day in this role you’ll: • Set technical direction for shared AWS and Kubernetes infrastructure, balancing reliability, security, developer experience, and cost. • Lead substantial initiatives from design through rollout and operation, coordinating dependencies and decisions across Wolt, Deliveroo and DoorDash, focusing on workloads targeting GPUs. • Evolve Kubernetes clusters, cloud networking, identity, and infrastructure provisioning to support growing and varied workloads. • Build reusable Terraform patterns and self-service tooling that help teams provision infrastructure safely and consistently. • Improve deployment and operational practices, including GitOps, observability, capacity planning, incident response and recovery. • Use production signals and developer feedback to find platform friction, choose where to invest and measure whether changes actually help. • Assist with supporting the platform, with deep focus on GPU infrastructure, as well as broad focus on compute in general. • Gracefully sunset legacy infrastructure and tooling, and create strategies to reduce toil. • Mentor engineers through design reviews, implementation and incidents, raising the quality of infrastructure work across teams. Our humble expectations • Deep experience building and operating Kubernetes and AWS infrastructure in production. Familiarity with GPU workloads and orchestration is a plus. • Strong software engineering skills: you use Go, Python or a similar language to build maintainable tools and automation. • Experience with infrastructure as code (we use Terraform) and with designing systems that other engineering teams depend on. • A track record of leading complex technical work across teams, including architectural decisions, delivery, and operational follow-through.
