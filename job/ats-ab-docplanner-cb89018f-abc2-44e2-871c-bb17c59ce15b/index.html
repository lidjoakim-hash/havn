Job Description At Docplanner, we run the #1 patient–doctor marketplace in most of the markets where we operate, connecting over 100 million patients every month with the doctors and clinics who care for them. Our mission is simple: to help people live longer, healthier lives. Doctors and clinics pay to be on our marketplace because it delivers something they can measure – patients, bookings, and real return on their investment. The Visibility team exists to guarantee that outcome. When a doctor or clinic buys our Marketplace product, this team makes sure they are set up well, get found by the right patients, fill their calendars, and clearly see the ROI that keeps them with us for years. As Senior Product Manager for Visibility, you will own the product and business logic that decides how doctors are ranked and surfaced across the marketplace, together with the paid visibility products (First Class, Media360) that let them grow further.
