Cloud Engineer

Knowit Aktiebolag (publ) · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 490 000–640 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 31.1.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Are you a Cloud Engineer who enjoys connecting cloud strategy with hands-on delivery? Join a growing team where you can shape cloud solutions and strengthen the link between Cloud and R&D. You will become part of a growing cloud team of around eight colleagues, including experienced specialists, architects, and developers with expertise across different cloud platforms. Together, you will work on cloud solutions involving major platforms such as AWS and Microsoft Azure. Acting as a bridge between Cloud and R&D activities, you will contribute across the full technical landscape, from cloud architecture and holistic planning to hands-on cloud infrastructure implementation. You will also have the opportunity to influence how we develop our cloud business going forward. The role is based at our office in Kista, Stockholm. Since remote collaboration generally works well for this type of work, we also welcome applicants from other parts of Sweden. To succeed in the role, we believe you have: At least three to four years of professional experience working with cloud technologies Experience with cloud delivery across multiple projects and one or more major cloud platforms The ability to demonstrate your cloud expertise through a certification from one of the major cloud providers Experience in both cloud architecture and hands-on cloud infrastructure implementation Fluency in Swedish, both spoken and written Strong communication skills and an interest in working both holistically and hands-on We place great value on personal qualities, communication, and social skills. These strengths help us build flexible teams where colleagues work well together and can rely on support from their peers. What will you get with us? You will work in a collaborative environment with challenging assignments, positive leadership, and experienced colleagues who share their knowledge. The pace is high, but so are the opportunities to develop through innovative projects and established skills development models. Knowit’s culture is grounded in openness, honesty, consensus, and respect for each individual. Sustainability is an important part of how we work, and you will join a team that wants to make a meaningful difference for both our clients and the company. Application We review applications on an ongoing basis, so please apply as soon as possible. We look forward to hearing from you. About Knowit Work with us at Knowit Connectivity, where we develop technically advanced systems within embedded and connected solutions. With us, you will work on complex products alongside experienced engineers on assignments at the forefront of technology. Knowit is a Nordic consultancy that helps companies, public sector organizations, and businesses succeed in digital transformation. With expertise in software development, AI, cybersecurity, cloud services, digital customer experiences, and management consulting, we create solutions that contribute to a more sustainable and human-centered society. We are approximately 3,700 employees working across our business areas – Solutions, Experience, Connectivity, Insight, and Products – primarily in the Nordics, as well as in Poland, Serbia, and Germany. We don’t wait for the future. We build it.
