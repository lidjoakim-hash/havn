Your wellbeing, our mission. Join a company shaping a healthier world. GET TO KNOW US At Wellhub we're revolutionizing workplace wellness. Our platform connects employees worldwide to the best partners for fitness, mindfulness, therapy, nutrition, and sleep—all in one simple subscription. Headquartered in NYC with team members in Europe, North America and South America, we’re on a mission to make every company a wellness company. We believe work should be fulfilling, inspiring, and balanced. Here, you’ll find a team that values wellbeing, collaboration, and different perspectives, where passion and creativity push boundaries to create real impact. Your contributions will help shape a healthier, more balanced world for you and millions of people globally. Join us in redefining the future of wellbeing! THE OPPORTUNITY We are hiring a SMB Account Executive to our Client Sales team in Italy! Please note that this role is only open to candidates in Italy. As an SMB Account Executive, you will play a pivotal role in accelerating the expansion of our team and business across Italy. You will be responsible for autonomously managing a robust pipeline of inbound and outbound prospects within the SMB segment (up to 500 employees), driving high-velocity sales cycles, and bringing the mission of Wellhub to organizations throughout the country. YOUR IMPACT • Lead and execute end-to-end sales cycles autonomously, qualifying and converting inbound opportunities and outbound Sales Development outreach into active deals. • Manage and accelerate high-volume pipeline activities across phone, video meetings, email, and social selling to swiftly secure demos and advance prospective clients. • Drive complex deals forward through effective multi-threading, overcoming objections, and executing seamless closings within the SMB market (up to 500 employees). • Consistently achieve and exceed quarterly sales quotas while maintaining high operational discipline and revenue growth. • Leverage performance metrics and data alongside your manager and team to continuously refine sales strategy, conversion rates, and pipeline health. • Thrive and adapt within a fast-evolving, high-velocity environment, pairing a competitive drive to win with strong collaboration and team spirit. • Live the mission: inspire and empower others by genuinely caring for your own wellbeing and your colleagues. Bring wellbeing to the forefront of work, and create a supportive environment where everyone feels comfortable taking care of themselves, taking time off, and finding work-life wellness. WHO YOU ARE • A seasoned sales closer with at least 3+ years of full-cycle sales experience, demonstrating a consistent track record of driving and closing revenue. • A proven high-velocity seller with prior experience in SaaS solutions and/or selling directly to HR stakeholders (experience within high-velocity environments or the fitness/wellness industry is strongly preferred). • An exceptional communicator and active listener who connects effortlessly with prospects and internal partners through charismatic, high-energy engagement. • An ambitious self-starter who operates with tenacity, optimism, and an intrinsic drive to hit and exceed ambitious targets. • An adaptable and coachable professional who embraces change, seeks constructive feedback, and balances individual excellence with collaborative team success. • A mission-aligned advocate who brings genuine enthusiasm for helping people build healthier, happier, and more balanced lives.
