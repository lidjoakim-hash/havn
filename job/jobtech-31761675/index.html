Job Description – Administrative Assistant We are looking for a responsible, organised, and detail-oriented Administrative Assistant to support the company's daily administrative and operational activities within the taxi industry. Key Responsibilities: Vehicle Administration: Coordinate and monitor vehicle maintenance, servicing, repairs, inspections, insurance, and registration. Driver Onboarding: Manage driver registration, onboarding procedures, employment documentation, and driver records. Driver Management: Monitor driver documentation, driving licences, taxi driver licences, and other required qualifications. Payroll Administration: Collect, verify, and maintain driver working hours, completed trips, salary records, and payroll information. Fleet Management: Monitor vehicle availability, maintenance schedules, vehicle assignments, and operational status. Operational Support: Assist with daily operations, driver scheduling, vehicle allocation, and administrative coordination. Document Management: Maintain contracts, invoices, receipts, reports, and other company documents. Customer and Driver Support: Handle administrative enquiries and provide support to drivers and customers when required. Supplier Coordination: Communicate with workshops, insurance companies, service providers, and other business partners. Reporting: Prepare and maintain reports related to drivers, vehicles, operational costs, and company activities. Compliance: Ensure that driver and vehicle documentation is up to date and that administrative procedures follow applicable regulations. Required Skills and Qualifications: Good organisational and administrative skills. Strong attention to detail and accuracy. Good communication and coordination skills. Ability to work independently and manage multiple tasks. Basic computer skills, including Microsoft Office and administrative systems. Good English communication skills. Swedish is an advantage. Previous experience in administration, transport, logistics, or fleet management is an advantage.
