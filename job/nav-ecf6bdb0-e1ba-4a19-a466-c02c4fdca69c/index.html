What you will be working on As a Project Planner, you will play an important role in the planning and monitoring of our delivery projects. Working closely with the Project Manager and the project team, you will help establish structure, maintain an overview, and drive progress - at both a high-level and a detailed level. Your key responsibilities will include: • developing progress schedules in alignment with the project team, suppliers and customer • ensuring a clear overview throughout the project’s various phases – from requirement and design to procurement, integration, verification and delivery • identify conflicts and risks within the project derived from the schedule • monitoring/tracking progress throughout the various phases of the project • assisting the project manager with coordination, reporting and follow-up activities We are looking for someone with: •  a bachelor’s degree or higher in project management, engineering, or another relevant field (relevant experience as a project planner may compensate for formal education) • experience with system-based projects and a solid understanding of the project lifecycle—from design and analysis to procurement, fabrication, testing, and delivery • strong communication skills in both Norwegian and English (written and spoken) • proficiency in MS Office (particularly Excel and PowerPoint) and MS Project • strong digital affinity and being open minded to new solutions including new tools and usage of artificial intelligence Personal attributes We are looking for a planner who does more than just update the schedule - someone who actively uses it to drive the project forward. You will likely thrive and succeed in this role if you: are communicative both in person and via online communication lines • possess strong collaborative skills and the ability to build relationships across different disciplines • are proactive, solution-oriented, and positive, with a clear &#34;can-do&#34; attitude • are structured and analytical, while maintaining a holistic view of the project • find motivation in both complex tasks and more repetitive, routine work What we offer With us, you get more than just a job; you get a daily life filled with professional challenges, a sense of achievement, and a community of colleagues who truly cheer each other on. We take pride in our work environment, where experience, expertise, and a positive attitude go hand in hand. You will tackle exciting and varied tasks, with opportunities for both professional and personal growth within a large, international company that invests in its people. We offer competitive compensation, robust pension and insurance schemes. LOCATION: Kongsberg or Bremen   • Attention: Candidates have to be eligible for a Norwegian or German security clearance!
