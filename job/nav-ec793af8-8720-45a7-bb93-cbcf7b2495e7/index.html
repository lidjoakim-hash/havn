About the project Currently, when aluminum powder is used in 3D printing, it degrades and develops a crusty outer layer, making it difficult to reuse. Instead of throwing it away or using harsh chemicals and extreme energy to clean it, the European AlproCam project uses a physical approach that allows to restoring old, used aluminum powder back to perfect, brand-new quality. The goal is to eliminate the need for toxic chemicals, save energy, and open the door to fully recycling aluminum and other metals in modern manufacturing. The ALPROCAM project aims at making additive manufacturing products more sustainable, cheaper and environmentally friendly.  The PhD position will focus on modelling the thermal expansion and cracking of powder used for the 3D printing. Crystal plasticity and continuum plasticity models in finite element software will be adapted and used.  The work will include collaboration with European partners and a researcher at NTNU who will perform material characterization and testing.  Duties of the position • Complete doctoral education until obtaining a doctorate • Carry out research of good quality within the framework described above • Academic publications and popular science dissemination • Participate in international activities such as conferences and/or research stays at foreign educational institutions Required selection criteria • Strong background with experience with numerical/mathematical modelling and simulations of continuum and preferable also crystal plasticity using finite element software. • You must have a Master's degree in Materials Science and Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Physics or equivalent.
