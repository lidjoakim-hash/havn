Job Overview Assignment Description We are looking for an experienced System Architect for an electrification development project focused on electric drivetrains, embedded systems and system integration. You will define and develop the system architecture, ensuring alignment between requirements, design, implementation and integration throughout the development lifecycle. What You Will Work On Define and maintain the overall system architecture. Translate system requirements into technical solutions and interfaces. Drive architecture activities across hardware, software and system integration teams. Identify and manage technical risks and dependencies. Support integration, verification and validation activities. Ensure architectural decisions and technical solutions align with project objectives and timelines. Collaborate with project management, engineering teams and external suppliers. What You Bring Several years of experience in system architecture and systems engineering. Experience from electrification projects. Good knowledge of embedded systems and system integration. Experience working in both hardware and software development environments. Ability to drive technical decisions and align different stakeholders. Structured and analytical working approach. Meritorious Experience with electric drivetrains, battery systems or power electronics. Experience from the automotive industry or advanced product development. Knowledge of Functional Safety (ISO 26262). Experience with MBSE, SysML or similar architecture methods. Experience with ECU development, verification and system testing. Previous experience as Lead Engineer, Technical Lead or Chief Engineer.
