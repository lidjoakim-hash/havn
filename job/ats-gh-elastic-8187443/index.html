What is The Role This role will architect the learning journey for our International (EMEA & APJ) Sales Development organization. Your mission is simple but critical: shorten ramp time for new hires and enhance the skills of existing SDRs. You will expand on established programs that have transformed performance levels for the SDR organization & take them to the next level. Standardize the SDR Enablement Rhythm and drive readiness through high-impact onboarding, "ever-boarding," programs. What You Will Be Doing • Deliver a best-in-class onboarding experience that takes new SDRs from "Day 1" to "First Meeting Booked" with speed and confidence. • Facilitate interactive workshops and create engaging training materials and resources. • Develop targeted training that focuses on pipeline generation skills with a heavy focus on territory planning, prospecting, messaging and tool adoption. Facilitate ongoing learning sessions to keep SDRs updated on industry best practices and ensure their continuous growth. • Analyze performance metrics to pinpoint areas needing improvement. Evaluate training programs' effectiveness using performance data. Offer insights and recommendations to boost SDR productivity. • Establish clear certification criteria and standards for SDRs and resources to assist SDRs in preparing for their certifications effectively. • Collaborate closely with Sales Leadership to ensure training programs align with sales goals. Facilitate joint sessions to identify skill gaps and address training needs effectively. • Organize all SDR assets & learning to ensure they are easily accessible and up-to-date. • Competency-Based Training: Transform SDR skills through continuous "ever-boarding" and formal certifications that ensure proficiency in pipeline generation skills, messaging, and MEDDPICC qualification. • Gather continuous feedback to improve enablement strategies. Regularly evaluate and refine the onboarding and ever-boarding programs. • Measure training content consumption and sentiment. • Maintain reporting on enablement effectiveness and impact on SDR ramp and conversion metrics. What You Bring • 3+ years with sales development or related roles • A successful track record in prospecting or full-cycle sales. You understand the grit and strategy required to build a pipeline from scratch using the modern sales stack including AI-driven sales tools, Salesforce, Outreach, ZoomInfo, Lusha, Sumble, Seismic, and LinkedIn Sales Navigator. • A passion for mentoring and developing others, with a talent for identifying skill gaps and deploying the right remediation to close them. • Prior experience has equipped you with the ability to train via story telling and use excellent interactive, facilitation and presentation skills, with the ability to simplify complex concepts. • Ability to analyze sales performance metrics and data • Proven track record of shortening onboarding ramp time and enhancing sales skills for SaaS sales teams. • Experience in designing and implementing effective training programs • Ability ability to operate multiple global programs contiguously while keeping leadership informed of status, progress, & impact.
