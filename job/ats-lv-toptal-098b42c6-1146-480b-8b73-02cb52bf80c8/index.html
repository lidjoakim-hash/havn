About Toptal Toptal is a global network of top talent in business, design, and technology that enables companies to scale their teams, on-demand. With $200+ million in annual revenue and team members based around the globe, Toptal is the world’s largest fully remote workforce. We take the best elements of virtual teams and combine them with a support structure that encourages innovation, social interaction, and fun. We see no borders, move at a fast pace, and are never afraid to break the mold. Job Summary: As the General Manager for Communication, Media, Entertainment, & Technology (CMET) at Toptal, you will be the visionary architect of our growth strategy in this dynamic industry. We’re seeking a hands-on leader with an entrepreneurial spirit who can inspire and scale a world-class team to drive exponential growth in our enterprise customer base. In this high-impact role, you will collaborate with Senior Leadership to unlock transformative market opportunities and identify the highest-potential avenues for value creation. Your strategic insight and foresight will be pivotal in shaping the future of Toptal’s presence in the CMET landscape. You will lead from the front, rolling up your sleeves to work directly with your sales team in forging and nurturing relationships with Fortune 500 companies. Your ability to strategically target and close deals will be integral to our success, positioning Toptal as a leader in the industry. If you are a strategic thinker with a passion for driving growth, a knack for crafting compelling strategies, and the ability to execute at the highest level, we want to hear from you. This is a remote position. Resumes and communication must be submitted in English.
