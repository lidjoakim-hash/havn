Stockholm | Full-time | Onsite, five days a week THE ROLE Help bring Legora closer to our clients around the world. From Stockholm in 2023 to 18 offices from Tokyo to San Francisco today, we’re expanding rapidly. As Operations Specialist, Market Expansion, you’ll drive the cross-functional projects that get our local teams ready on time and set each market up to succeed. You’ll work across Legal, Finance, People, Growth, Marketing and Product, bringing stakeholders together around clear plans and keeping multiple launches moving. You’ll manage dependencies, resolve blockers and push for decisions when priorities compete. You’ll also own the Market Entry Playbook, turning lessons from each launch into better ways of working. To succeed you need to be curious, build trust quickly and enjoy making complicated projects happen together with a cross functional team. You can juggle competing priorities without losing sight of the details, and you’re comfortable holding stakeholders accountable while keeping relationships strong. Based in Stockholm, you’ll report to Laura Haidinger, who leads Market Expansion, and work closely with regional leaders and the Go-to-Market Strategy and Operations team. WHAT YOU’LL DO - Run the operating cadence for every market entry. Keep a live status for each market, make sure every owner reports on schedule, and raise decisions before they turn into blockers. - Make hard judgement calls to make work go forward, reassign, re-sequence or escalate early, and bring a recommendation when you do. - Make sure external vendors move with the same urgency and speed we do. When a vendor's timeline puts a launch at risk, push for pace and escalate early. - Make sure everyone working on a launch has the right tools in place to do their work in the best and most efficient way. Set up the tracking for each new market, keep it current, and flag waste so it gets fixed. - Own the Market Entry Playbook. Spot where reality differs from it, agree the change and update it, so the next market runs better than the last. Keep Notion and reporting accurate. - Take on strategic streams: market and competitive research, parts of business cases, and designing how we respond when a market hits an operational problem. - Ask "can an agent do this?" before adding a manual step.   WHAT YOU BRING - Customer satisfaction is second nature to you.
