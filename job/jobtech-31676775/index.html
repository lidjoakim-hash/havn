Electrical Lead – Gävle

OnePartnerGroup Södra Norrland AB · Gävle, Sweden

Are you an experienced electrical professional with strong leadership skills and a background in large-scale construction projects? Do you thrive in a role where you take ownership, coordinate complex electrical installations and ensure projects are delivered safely, on time and to the highest quality standards? Then this could be your next opportunity! OnePartnerGroup is now looking for an experienced Electrical Lead for a consulting assignment with an international company operating within the data centre industry. The company delivers large-scale data centre projects across Europe and is recognised for its expertise in the design, construction and commissioning of these facilities. In this role, you will play a key part in leading and coordinating all electrical works on a major project site in Gävle. About the role As Electrical Lead, you will be responsible for leading all electrical activities on site, ensuring that work is carried out safely, according to the project schedule, within budget and in line with the required quality standards. You will oversee daily electrical site operations and work closely with Project Managers, Planners, subcontractors, consultants and design teams. You will also ensure that technical coordination, quality assurance and safety procedures are maintained throughout the project. Your ability to lead teams, manage subcontractors and proactively resolve technical and coordination challenges will be essential to the successful delivery of the project. Your main responsibilities will include: Leading and managing all electrical activities throughout the project lifecycle. Overseeing and coordinating day-to-day electrical site operations with the wider project team. Ensuring QA/QC processes are implemented and maintained in accordance with project specifications and standards. Coordinating project schedules with Planners, Project Managers, subcontractors, consultants and design teams. Managing electrical subcontractor performance, including progress tracking, manpower planning and coordination meetings. Ensuring all subcontractor documentation, including health and safety documentation, is maintained and up to date. Chairing and attending site meetings, engineering reviews, planning meetings and handover meetings. Liaising with the design team to ensure construction information and technical submissions align with programme requirements. Reviewing drawings and technical submittals and ensuring effective coordination of electrical systems. Driving health and safety standards and promoting a strong safety culture across all electrical activities. Supporting commercial management through variation tracking, cost control and participation in weekly commercial meetings. Preparing and issuing progress reports to senior management and key stakeholders. Overseeing electrical snagging activities and ensuring timely close-out. Proactively identifying, managing and resolving technical, design and coordination issues. Who are we looking for? We are looking for an experienced electrical professional with a strong background in construction and proven leadership experience. You are confident taking responsibility for complex technical activities and know how to coordinate teams and subcontractors to achieve project objectives. You are structured, organised and proactive, with the ability to manage several priorities in a demanding project environment. Your communication and leadership skills allow you to build strong working relationships with different stakeholders, while your technical knowledge enables you to identify challenges and drive solutions forward. To succeed in this role, we believe you have: A bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Building Services Engineering or a similar discipline. At least 7 years of experience within the electrical construction sector, including a strong leadership background. Proven experience managing electrical systems on large-scale construction projects.
