Erithy, previously known as Be Better Contractors, specializes in the design and construction of sustainable subsea infrastructure globally. We deliver consultancy services within the oil and gas industry, high voltage cables, biogas and hydrogen, as well as civil engineering services and project administration. Created in 2017, Erithy has grown into a consultancy team made up of various profiles from Europe, South America and the Middle East, working on projects across Norway. We are always on the lookout for new talented individuals to join our team, and we hope that you will be our new member! We are now looking for a SURF Umbilical Specialist for one of our customers based in Oslo.
