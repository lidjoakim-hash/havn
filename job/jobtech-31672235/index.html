Ready to Take the Next Step in Your Hospitality Career? Do you have experience in housekeeping and a passion for leading people? Are you looking for a workplace where your leadership skills are valued, your development is supported, and your efforts truly make a difference? At OMNILS GROUP AB, we're not just cleaning hotel rooms, we're creating exceptional guest experiences. We are now looking for an energetic and motivated Housekeeping Team Leader to join our growing team in Gothenburg. If you thrive in a fast-paced environment, enjoy inspiring others, and want to be part of a company with ambitious growth plans, we'd love to hear from you! Why Join OMNILS GROUP? OMNILS GROUP is one of Sweden's growing hospitality service providers, specializing in housekeeping and facility services for hotels and Real Estates Approximately 200,000 hotel rooms are served annually Around 200 employees across Sweden ISO-certified in 14001& 9001 Collective agreement for secure employment conditions in Visita, Almega. Pension and insurance according to collective agreements We believe our success comes from our people. That's why we invest in our employees' growth, well-being, and future. Our Core Values Commitment Happiness Respect Your Opportunity As a Housekeeping Team Leader, you will play a key role in ensuring operational excellence while inspiring and developing your team. This is more than a supervisory role. It's an opportunity to build leadership experience, influence daily operations, and contribute to the success of some of Sweden's leading hotels. You will act as the bridge between hotel management, housekeeping staff, and OMNILS leadership while helping maintain exceptional quality and guest satisfaction. What You'll Do: Lead, coach, and inspire your housekeeping team Plan daily operations and allocate responsibilities efficiently Ensure exceptional cleaning quality and service standards Conduct quality inspections and provide feedback Support employee development and training Collaborate closely with hotel management and reception teams Monitor schedules, attendance, and reporting Manage housekeeping supplies and inventory Step into operations when needed and lead by example Create a positive, inclusive, and high-performing work environment Who You Are: We're looking for someone who is positive, reliable, flexible and motivated by helping others succeed. Requirements: At least 1-2 years of housekeeping experience within hotels Previous experience leading or supervising a team Strong communication and people skills Excellent organization and problem-solving abilities Detail-oriented with a passion for quality Comfortable working in a fast-paced environment Proficient in Swedish and/or English Driving license is an advantage Valid work permit in Sweden or Swedish citizenship What We Offer Career Growth We believe in promoting from within. High-performing Team Leaders can develop into Supervisors, Operations Coordinators, Quality Controllers, and other leadership positions as the company grows.
