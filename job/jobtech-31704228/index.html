Veritaz is a leading IT staffing solutions provider in Sweden, committed to advancing individual careers and aiding employers in ensuring the perfect talent fit. With a proven track record of successful partnerships with top companies, we have rapidly grown our presence in the USA, Europe, and Sweden as a dependable and trusted resource within the IT industry. Job Overview Assignment Description We are looking for an experienced ERP Solution Architect to support a large-scale ERP procurement and transformation programme. The organisation is preparing to procure and implement a new ERP platform across its business. The assignment involves two phases: Selection, focusing on evaluating potential ERP solutions, and Discovery, where the selected solution will be validated through a Proof of Concept (POC). As an independent technical expert representing the organisation, you will support the procurement team, evaluate ERP platforms, coordinate technical stakeholders and ensure that the selected solution meets architectural requirements and supports long-term maintainability. The ERP solution will cover financial processes as well as operational processes related to installation projects, service orders and product sales. What You Will Work On Provide independent ERP and solution architecture expertise throughout the procurement process. Evaluate modern ERP platforms from technical and architectural perspectives. Define evaluation criteria and requirements covering architecture, integrations, master data and system management. Coordinate technical resources and IT functions across different workstreams. Support process alignment with standard ERP functionality. Act as the primary technical contact for the implementation partner's solution architect. Define architecture principles and strategies together with the internal IT architect. Prepare technical evaluation scenarios and demonstration scripts for the Selection phase. Define and assess technical POC scenarios during the Discovery phase. Document target architecture and integration requirements. Support data migration and infrastructure planning ahead of implementation. Evaluate POC results, identify gaps and recommend solutions through integrations, process adjustments or system configuration. What You Bring Extensive experience as an ERP Architect or Solution Architect in large organisations. Broad experience with modern cloud-based ERP platforms, such as SAP S/4HANA, Oracle NetSuite/Fusion, Microsoft Dynamics 365 and IFS. Experience representing a customer or procurement organisation in large ERP transformation programmes. Strong understanding of ERP architecture, integrations, master data and enterprise IT environments. Ability to work independently and represent business and technical interests. Strong communication and stakeholder management skills. Meritorious Experience with ERP assignments in project-based and service-delivery organisations. Experience with procurement models involving solution validation and Proof of Concept (POC).
