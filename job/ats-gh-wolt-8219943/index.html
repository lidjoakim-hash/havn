About Wolt At Wolt, we create technology that brings joy, simplicity and earnings to the neighborhoods of the world. In 2014 we started with delivery of restaurant food. Now we’re building the delivery of (almost) everything and you’ll find us in over 500 cities in 30 countries around the world. In 2022 we joined forces with DoorDash and together we keep on dreaming big and expanding across the globe. Working at Wolt isn’t always easy, but it’s definitely exciting. Here you’ll learn more, build more, and ship more than in most other companies. You’ll be challenged a lot, but also have a lot of fun on the way. So, if you’re a self-starter with drive and entrepreneurial spirit, this could be the ride of your life. At Wolt, keeping courier supply healthy across every city we operate in is core to a great experience for both customers and couriers. As we've scaled to dozens of countries and hundreds of cities, planning & managing supply has grown from a market-by-market exercise into a global challenge: we need a system that plans well everywhere at once, from our largest metros to our smallest cities. That's where this role comes in. As Senior Specialist, Global Market Planning, you'll help refine the models and tools at the heart of how we plan courier supply globally. You'll make sure the right supply levers are pulled in the right markets, and you'll help us learn which levers actually work. It's a role at the crossroads of data, tooling, and operations. It's for someone who is as comfortable designing a test or building a model as they are working out why a specific market is off plan and what to do about it. You'll be part of the Global Market Planning team and will partner closely with local operations and courier marketing so that our planning always reflects the reality on the ground. What you'll be doing • Develop the global planning model: Help develop and refine our global supply planning model so it delivers robust coverage across both large and small cities, accounting for local context like holidays, special events, etc. • Allocate investments across markets: Help optimize supply lever allocation, translating region and country budgets into the right mix of levers for each market. • Test and learn in markets: Propose tests and actions within markets, in line with lever allocation, especially where we don't yet know the right answer or don't have enough data to rely on history. You'll work with local teams and analytics to shape the approach and turn what we learn into the next plan. • Build the tooling: Develop tools that ensure the correct supply levers are being used effectively across markets to hit our targets. • Deep dive & solve: Investigate problem areas, identify root causes, and turn your findings into corrective actions and better planning approaches. • Collaborate: Partner with regional supply leads, local operations teams, and central teams (finance, procurement, product, analytics) on plans, performance, and improvements. • Improve how we work: Test and prove new concepts in planning, acquisition, onboarding, and activation, and support the roll-out of global improvements across regions. Our humble expectations • 3+ years of experience in consulting, operations, analytics, or strategic business development in a fast-paced environment. • Strong analytical and modelling skills, with a structured, pragmatic approach to solving complex problems. • Experience designing and interpreting tests or causal analyses, and good judgment about what you can and can't conclude from them in a marketplace setting. • A systems view of supply: you consider trade-offs between levers and their knock-on effects on cost, courier experience, and customer experience. • Comfort making and defending recommendations with incomplete data, and adapting your thinking when the facts change. • Proficiency in SQL, Looker, and Excel/Google Sheets modelling.
