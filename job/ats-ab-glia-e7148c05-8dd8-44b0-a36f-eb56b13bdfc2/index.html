About Glia Glia is the #1 Banking AI platform, empowering community and regional financial institutions to create efficiencies, accelerate loan growth, drive deposits, and deliver experiences that win against megabanks and fintechs. Glia's Banking AI Operating System is a central intelligence layer on top of existing tech stacks, activating an AI workforce of specialized agents that draw from banking data, interaction history, and integrated systems of record. These banking-trained agents automate workflows across voice and digital–from front office to back office–resulting in decreased operational costs and the Universal Banker model. Trusted by 700+ banks and credit unions for its ironclad security and reliability, Glia delivers the industry’s first contractual no-hallucination guarantee. It’s why Glia customers quickly and confidently put Banking AI to work with measurable results from day one. More information about Glia can be found at glia.com http://glia.com. The Mission & The Work This role offers an exciting opportunity to join the Quantum team. You will design and scale the core intelligence services that power both real-time customer interactions and operational foresight. Our team’s core mission is bringing the frontier AI capabilities to our customers. Including, but not limited to, it involves connecting customer knowledge bases to an easy-to-use AI chat window, or allowing our customers to build banking automations with AI. Ultimately, your work will bridge advanced intelligence with human strategy, empowering human agents to leverage AI and work smarter. Working in self-reliant product teams, you'll contribute to diverse projects throughout the entire development lifecycle. This role is perfect for those passionate about owning the entire solution and working throughout the stack - back-end, front-end, infrastructure, and more. What We’re Looking for We are looking for a high-autonomy, impact-driven Software Engineer who thrives on technical complexity and system-wide variety. While our core needs involve Elixir, React, or Terraform, we value a strong technical foundation and problem-solving mindset over strict language loyalty. You should be prepared to work across different stacks. In this role, you’ll shift from executing basic tasks to owning features, joining technical discussions, and keeping our codebase high-quality. We generally look for the following qualifications: - Experience: 2-3 years of professional software development experience , with a track record of shipping production-grade code. In exceptional cases, engineers with a track record of independent building and shipping of products are also considered. - System Design & Initiative: You comfortably take features from start to finish. You design, build, and maintain reliable, highly available systems, ensuring your code solves the actual problem and integrates cleanly into our architecture without adding unnecessary complexity. - Technical Foundation: Strong mastery of strictly-typed/compiled languages, design patterns, and containerized cloud infrastructure (AWS). Familiarity with event-driven architecture. - Engineering Excellence & Code Quality: You write clean, test-driven code (TDD, CI/CD) and proactively spot areas for improvement without waiting for tasks. You effectively leverage AI tools (e.g., Claude Code) to accelerate boilerplate and testing, while maintaining strict accountability for correctness. - Proactive Growth: A self-starter who independently identifies areas for improvement, actively seeks feedback, and constantly learns. No problem if you miss a few of these qualifications! If you find it interesting to learn them, do still apply. Our Tech Stack - Coding languages: Elixir, Ruby, Javascript (React) - Current Persistence Solutions: DynamoDB, AWS RDS for PostgreSQL, S3 - Infrastructure: AWS, Terraform - Monitoring: DataDog - CI/CD: Jenkins - AI: Claude Code, Gemini - Other: AWS
