Manufacturing Operations Summer Intern 2027

Graphcore · Bristol, UK, United Kingdom

💰 ca. 19 000–26 000 GBP/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)PraksisDirekte fra arbeidsgiverFrist 2.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Location:  Bristol   Duration: 12 weeks Salary: £2500 per month Start date: June 2027 Applications deadline: Midnight 29th November We encourage you to apply early, as we may close applications before the advertised deadline. The Internship Programme at Graphcore Explore a career at Graphcore through a 12-week summer internship, gaining hands-on experience on real projects. You’ll bring fresh ideas, put your learning into practice and help shape what we’re creating. Along the way, you’ll tackle challenging problems with a team who welcome your questions and support you as you build your skills and confidence. What you’ll be doing Put your engineering foundations to work exploring how advanced AI hardware moves from design into manufacture. As a Manufacturing Operations Intern in Manufacturing Operations Team, you’ll contribute to a defined project or set of tasks supporting silicon packaging or printed circuit board assembly production in Taiwan. Depending on your project, your tasks could include analysing performance data, helping investigate hardware or software issues, or supporting quality planning and process checks. You could also help review component information or bills of materials. Your contribution will help the team understand manufacturing performance and identify improvements to quality, cost or delivery. Your team and support You’ll join Manufacturing Operations in Bristol, working alongside Product Test, Quality and Supply Chain. Colleagues across the UK, Taiwan and India collaborate with design teams and development partners to solve manufacturing challenges. Experienced engineers and operations specialists will guide you through your tasks and help you understand how data and customer requirements inform decisions. You can ask for help as you investigate a problem, share your findings and use feedback to improve your work. Decisions are shaped by evidence, engineering judgement and open discussion. As an intern, you’ll be encouraged to ask questions, speak up and try out ideas early, with support from experienced colleagues as you learn. How you’ll learn and grow Over the 12 weeks, you’ll explore how engineering decisions affect a finished product and its journey through a global supply chain. Working with manufacturing data and practical investigations will help you connect your studies with real production challenges. You’ll practise comparing approaches, organising your work and explaining your findings, gaining a clearer sense of where a career in manufacturing engineering could take you. Your internship is a chance to explore a future at Graphcore. Depending on your performance and the roles available, there may be an opportunity to join one of our graduate programmes once you’ve graduated. What we're looking for • Currently studying towards a degree in Mechanical or Electrical Engineering, Physics or a related field • A basic understanding of semiconductor or electronics design and manufacturing principles, gained through study, projects or practical work • Some experience using programming or technical tools such as Python, Linux, Bash or Git • The ability to analyse data, approach problems methodically and explain your findings clearly Previous industry experience is not required. Tell us what you built or investigated through coursework, laboratory work, personal or team projects or other relevant experience, how you approached it and what you learned. Benefits • Flexible working: Find a balance between your internship and life outside work • 5 days of annual leave: Take time to rest and recharge during your 12-week internship • Life in the office: Get to know your colleagues in spaces designed for collaboration, with free food, a great selection of ice cream and an on-site barista • Learning and development: Build your skills and explore your interests with access to O’Reilly, our online learning platform We welcome people from all backgrou
