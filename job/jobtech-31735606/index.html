The opportunity At Hitachi Energy, our projects are transforming the future of sustainable energy. We are now looking for a Project Documentation Specialist to play a key role in ensuring high-quality documentation management throughout the project lifecycle. In this position, you will support project teams by overseeing document governance, maintaining project documentation workspaces, and ensuring compliance with established documentation standards. Your work will help drive efficiency, collaboration, and quality across our global project deliveries. How you’ll make an impact Support the creation, review, approval, publication, and revision of project documentation. Ensure proper retention, archiving, and governance of documentation throughout the project lifecycle. Set up, maintain, and administer project documentation workspaces. Monitor documentation quality and compliance with established processes and standards. Provide guidance and support to documentation owners and key stakeholders. Collaborate with project teams, process owners, and Project Office functions to drive continuous improvement in documentation management. Your background Bachelor’s degree in Administration, Engineering, or a related field. Experience in documentation management, document control, or similar governance and administrative processes is an advantage. Experience working in project-based organizations is considered beneficial. Strong organizational skills with attention to detail and document quality. Ability to collaborate effectively with stakeholders across functions and projects.
