About the Job: LaunchDarkly is the market leader in software feature management, and we’re now defining what comes next: the runtime control layer for AI development. Our platform de-risks releases and enables systems that heal and optimize themselves. With a presence in 25% of the Fortune 500 and an incredible period of growth underway, we’re looking for the right leaders to take us to the next step. As Regional Sales Director, Enterprise UKI & Northern Europe, you’ll lead a high-performing team of experienced Enterprise Account Executives focused on new business and expansion opportunities, building the pipeline, sales discipline, and customer relationships needed to deliver predictable growth. You’ll collaborate cross-functionally to drive regional performance and support complex enterprise sales engagements. This role is ideal for a sales leader who understands the developer tools space, is passionate about solving complex customer challenges, and thrives in a high-growth SaaS environment. This role reports to our VP, EMEA. Responsibilities: • Lead, develop, and manage a team of Enterprise Account Executives across UKI & Northern Europe, driving new logo acquisition and expansion within existing enterprise accounts. • Own quarterly and annual team revenue targets, translating regional strategy into clear territory plans and execution priorities. • Build a strong outbound pipeline generation culture, partnering with Sales Development and Marketing to create and progress qualified opportunities. • Own regional sales forecasting and pipeline inspection, ensuring accurate reporting, clear deal qualification, and proactive management of risks and gaps. • Coach Account Executives through complex sales cycles, including discovery, value articulation, stakeholder engagement, negotiation, and close planning. • Provide executive sponsorship for strategic opportunities and customer relationships, helping the team engage senior technical and business decision-makers. • Partner with senior leadership and Revenue Operations on annual planning, territory assignments, coverage, capacity, and quota and compensation planning. • Work closely with Solutions Engineering, Customer Success, Marketing, Sales Development, Revenue Operations, and Enablement to improve sales execution and deliver a coordinated customer experience. • Recruit, onboard, develop, and retain high-performing sales talent, fostering an inclusive culture of accountability, collaboration, and continuous improvement. Qualifications: • 10+ years of enterprise SaaS software sales experience, including 5+ years in enterprise sales management, or equivalent depth of experience and demonstrated impact. • Proven track record of meeting or exceeding team revenue targets and leading experienced sales teams across complex enterprise sales cycles. • Experience selling into the UK, Ireland, and Northern Europe, and adapting territory and account strategies to regional customer needs. • Demonstrated ability to build pipeline, deliver accurate forecasts, and coach teams to improve deal qualification, progression, and conversion. • Experience building and developing sales teams, including hiring, onboarding, performance management, and retention. • Experience selling to technical buyers and engaging senior engineering, technology, and business stakeholders. • Familiarity with MEDDPICC and Command of the Message, or comparable sales methodologies, and the ability to apply them consistently through coaching and deal inspection. • Exceptional management, interpersonal, written, and presentation skills, with strong cross-functional collaboration and business judgment. • Ability to thrive in a fast-paced, high-growth, and rapidly changing environment. • Understanding of how software delivery platforms improve engineering productivity, release velocity, and delivery confidence.
