Job Purpose: The Senior Installation Engineer has responsibility and accountability for the readiness, preparation and  execution of allocated installation engineering work package(s), ensuring on time delivery and to budget through engineering performance monitoring, whilst maintaining safety and quality standards. They shall be allocated within an execution stream (VSO/Non-VSO) to support installation engineering deliverables or initiatives for studies, tenders & project execution in accordance with TechnipFMC standard procedures and processes. Job Description: Senior Installation Engineer (SIE) shall be allocated to project execution, tender or studies, acting as Execution Lead within the Operations Package to deliver technical robust installation engineering solutions for the assigned vessel stream or workscope. Lead Offshore Engineer role ensuring work is executed in accordance with approved installation procedures. Project team representation on the vessel or appointed worksite coordinating project documentation (project briefings, dive/task plans, MOC, Lift Plans, etc) to support the Offshore Management Team. Primary offshore technical interface with client.   Office based with hybrid flexibility. Offshore rotations and site assignments shall be based on project and departmental requirements. Domestic and International Travel as required. You are meant for this job if: • Bachelor’s of Science (BSc) degree or equivalent in relevant engineering discipline • Minimum 5 years of experience in offshore installation engineering • Multidisciplinary project experience • Mastery level of proficiency in installation of at least 2 or more Sub-system & Product Operational level of proficiency in installation of at least 4 or more Sub-system & Product • Lead by example • Strong organizational and problem solving skills • Strong ability to work in a team environment • Fluent in English – clear verbal, written communication • Strong technical foundation in Subsea Umbilical’s, Risers, and Flowline (SURF), Subsea Production System (SPS), sub-systems and vessel operations • Proficient in Microsoft Office Tools (e.g. Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Projects) • Ability to manage allocated work package(s) independently • Experienced in risk assessment, mitigation management and management of change  Your future at TechnipFMC: • Your journey starts with our onboarding program • As an international company, we provide you with great opportunities for internal growth • You will have the opportunity to attend development activities • We have benefits like company cabins, in-house gym, and sports teams   • You will be secured by good insurance and retirements plans  • Collaborative and innovative atmosphere that promotes teamwork.
