Digital Project Manager

Husqvarna AB · Jönköping, Sweden

💰 ca. 450 000–550 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 4.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

We’re one of the world’s oldest start-ups — and we’re just getting started. At Husqvarna Group, innovation is in our DNA. With over 330 years of heritage and a passion for pioneering technology, we design and deliver world class products and solutions for forest, park, garden and construction care. From robotic lawn mowers to cutting-edge chainsaws and sustainable battery systems, we’re shaping the future - and we want you to be part of it. As a Digital Project Manager, you will join a team that delivers digital solutions across multiple customer touchpoints using agile, value-driven development in a close collaboration with different stakeholders. By bridging business stakeholders, customer experience, and cross-functional teams, this role reduces fragmentation, increases alignment, and accelerates value delivery. It provides a single point of accountability for initiatives from discovery to optimization, ensuring that learning, prioritization, and execution are guided by customer insight, data, and strategic intent. You will report to Katja Eriksson, Optimization & Governance Manager. About the role: In this role, you will lead digital initiatives end-to-end, from early idea exploration and discovery through launch and continuous optimization. You will coordinate and collaborate closely with customer experience (CX) teams, development, marketing, and external stakeholders to ensure seamless execution. A key part of your work will be to ensure that all initiatives are grounded in data, insights, and real customer needs. Transparency is central to how we work - you will create clear visibility into progress, decisions, and priorities for all stakeholders. Documentation is also an essential part of the role, ensuring that learning, requirements, and processes are captured and shared in a structured and accessible way. You will follow up on goals, create transparency, and support prioritization through agile ways of working. Acting as the primary point of contact for stakeholders, you will keep the overall picture together and ensure alignment across the organization and external parties. About you: You’re proactive, pragmatic, and collaborative - prestige‑less, curious about the digital landscape, and comfortable driving change across diverse stakeholders. You ask for help when needed to move the work forward. Your skills and background: Relevant education in UX or digital marketing, or equivalent professional experience Several years of experience in agile project management Strong stakeholder management skills Digital savvy, with hands-on experience using data and insights to guide initiatives and decisions Ability to work with high transparency and maintain clear, structured documentation throughout the entire project life cycle. Experience from agency/consultancy is considered a plus but not a requirement Fluent in Swedish and English, both spoken and written Location This position will be based in Huskvarna, Sweden. We believe in a dynamic, energized workplace and day-to-day collaboration, and we meet in the office at least 4 days a week (80%). Your application: Please submit your application in English as soon as possible as we will review applications on an ongoing basis. We do not accept applications via e-mail due to GDPR regulations. For questions about the role, reach out to hiring manager Katja Eriksson, at katja.eriksson@husqvarnagroup.com. For questions about the recruitment process, reach out to Talent Acquisition Partner Nina Hallin at nina.hallin@husqvarnagroup.com. We look forward to hearing from you!
