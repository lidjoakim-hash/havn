Engineering Group Lead, Mobile

Asana · Warsaw, Poland

💰 ca. 110 000–142 000 PLN/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)HeltidDirekte fra arbeidsgiverFrist 2.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Asana's Mobile Group builds the apps that keep millions of users connected to their work wherever they are. As the Engineering Group Lead for the Mobile Group in Warsaw, you will lead a team of 4 Engineering Managers and approximately 35 engineers who are responsible for Asana's iOS, Android, and Desktop apps. You will own the technical direction and execution of the Mobile Group and the growth of leaders on your team. You will also support Warsaw site strategy and operations as a member of senior leadership in Asana’s Warsaw office. This role is based in our Warsaw office with an office-centric hybrid schedule. The standard in-office days are Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday. Most Asanas have the option to work from home on Wednesdays. Working from home on Fridays depends on the type of work you do, and your recruiter can share more about the in-office requirements. We offer a Contract of Employment (UoP) for our employees in Poland. What you'll achieve • Lead, coach, and develop a team of Engineering Managers and their teams of approximately 35 engineers, owning hiring, performance, and career growth across the Mobile Group. • Drive the delivery of high-quality mobile experiences across multiple products, holding accountability for code quality and customer experiences that ship to production. • Develop, in partnership with Product and Design leaders, a multi-product mobile strategy built on top of shared platform investments. • Own relationships across the organization, managing up and out to advocate for the Mobile Group, removing blockers, and creating clarity in a fast-moving environment where many workstreams run in parallel. • Serve as a senior leader at the Warsaw site by flexing into operational, cultural, and cross-functional support roles as needed while helping grow other leaders in Warsaw. About you • You have several years of experience managing people managers and being accountable for a sizable engineering organization (30+ engineers). • You have recent, hands-on experience shipping mobile applications. • You thrive in high-complexity environments and can balance competing priorities gracefully. You have a bias for ownership: you chase things down, build relationships across teams, and create forward motion. • You are a fast and curious learner. The Mobile Group touches many different problem spaces and team contexts; you need to absorb new information quickly and apply it. • You have a low ego. You are as comfortable diving into technical and product details as you are presenting strategy to executive leadership. • You demonstrate curiosity about emerging technologies and a willingness to leverage them to enhance your team's productivity and decision-making. At Asana, we're committed to building teams that include a variety of backgrounds, perspectives, and skills, as this is critical to helping us achieve our mission. If you're interested in this role and don't meet every listed requirement, we still encourage you to apply. What we'll offer • Generous, transparent and fair compensation system (base salary and RSUs) • Contract of Employment (and the option of 50% tax deductible costs for author's rights usage in respect of applicable roles) • Health insurance with dental and travel coverage (Lux Med) • Breakfast and lunch catering on the days that you work from the office • Career growth budget • Home office setup budget • Gym/Fitness card (MultiSport Plus) • Mental Health Support through Modern Health (free therapy and coaching) • Group life insurance • MacBooks with all necessary accessories For this role, the estimated base salary range is between 54.000 - 64.000 PLN gross per month (subject to all taxes and necessary deductions). The actual base salary will vary based on various factors, including market and individual qualifications objectively assessed during the interview process. The listed range above is a guideline, and the base salary range for this role may be modified.
