Here’s what this particular role covers. 🛠️ About our DevOps Teams Our team builds and operates the infrastructure, tooling, and services that underpin Wayve’s software development lifecycle. We work closely with engineering teams across the organisation to make building, testing, and releasing software faster, more reliable, and easier to scale. As Wayve’s engineering workloads grow, we evolve the CI/CD architecture and developer experience needed to support them.   🧠 Your day-to-day - Build, maintain, and improve CI/CD pipelines across development, testing, and release workflows. - Operate and scale build agents, runners, artifact storage, and supporting services. - Monitor and troubleshoot build and pipeline failures, identifying root causes and implementing long-term improvements. - Partner with software engineers to remove workflow bottlenecks and shorten build and test feedback times. - Develop tooling and automation that makes our engineering systems easier and more reliable to use.   🧩 What you’ll be working on - Improving build speed, reliability, scalability, and reproducibility. - Designing effective approaches to caching, dependency management, and incremental builds. - Strengthening the resilience, security, and observability of our CI/CD infrastructure. - Creating self-service tooling that enables teams to manage and evolve their own pipelines. - Helping shape the future architecture of Wayve’s build and continuous-delivery platforms.   🙌 You should apply if - You have at least three years of experience in DevOps, build engineering, CI/CD, developer infrastructure, or a related engineering role. - You have hands-on experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines and automation systems. - You have worked with platforms such as GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Jenkins, Buildkite, Azure DevOps, or similar. - You understand build concepts such as caching, dependency management, incremental builds, and reproducible builds. - You are confident working with Linux, Git, and shell scripting. - You can develop tools and automation using Python, Bash, Go, or a similar language. - You enjoy solving complex infrastructure problems and working collaboratively with engineering teams.   🌱 Not ticking every box?
