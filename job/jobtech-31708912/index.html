Veritaz is a leading IT staffing solutions provider in Sweden, committed to advancing individual careers and aiding employers in ensuring the perfect talent fit. With a proven track record of successful partnerships with top companies, we have rapidly grown our presence in the USA, Europe, and Sweden as a dependable and trusted resource within the IT industry. Job Overview Role Summary Our client is seeking a Senior Data Platform Engineer for a technically focused consulting assignment with a growing Swedish technology company in Stockholm. The assignment supports an initiative spanning the company’s data and systems landscape. The initial objective is to conduct a structured inventory and assessment of an existing data tool used across several parts of the organization. This work will establish a clear view of current solutions, data assets, integrations, use cases, and dependencies while identifying opportunities for improvement and further development. The consultant will work closely with the Data Platform team and with technical and business stakeholders. This is a hands-on role suited to an experienced engineer with a strong background in data engineering, data platforms, and cloud infrastructure. Key Responsibilities Conduct a structured inventory and mapping of existing data and system solutions. Document the current data tool, associated data assets, integrations, use cases, and technical dependencies. Analyze data flows and integrations across the organization’s data and systems landscape. Collaborate with the Data Platform team and relevant technical and business stakeholders. Identify improvement opportunities, architectural concerns, and areas of technical debt. Provide a clear technical foundation for the continued improvement and development of the data environment. Technical Stack & Requirements Data Engineering: Strong practical experience analyzing and working with data flows and data-centric solutions. Data Platforms: Substantial experience with platform environments, shared data capabilities, and organizational data ecosystems. Cloud Infrastructure: Hands-on experience working with cloud-based infrastructure supporting data solutions. Systems Integration: Ability to analyze integrations, dependencies, interfaces, and usage patterns across multiple systems. Qualifications & Experience The assignment requires a senior-level consultant with extensive experience in data engineering, data platforms, and cloud infrastructure. The successful candidate should be capable of independently assessing an established technical environment, creating structured documentation, and translating findings into actionable improvement opportunities. Experience collaborating with both engineering teams and business-facing stakeholders is important for the assignment.
