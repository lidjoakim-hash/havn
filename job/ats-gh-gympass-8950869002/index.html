THE OPPORTUNITY We are hiring a HRIS Associate in our People Systems area in Spain! This is a Remote – Spain position, meaning you can work from anywhere within the country. Reporting within People Systems and partnering closely with People Operations, IT, Finance, and Payroll, this is a hands-on systems role helping power Wellhub’s international scale—configuring and maintaining Workday architectures as we expand into new European markets (such as France and Austria), automating employee lifecycle journeys (leaves, allowances, probation, offboarding), and streamlining processes to eliminate administrative friction. YOUR IMPACT • Global Market Expansion & Country Setups: Partner with People Ops, Payroll, and Legal to configure, test, and deploy country-specific Workday foundational setups (e.g., statutory leaves, mandatory compliance fields, local onboarding/probation milestones) supporting Wellhub’s growth across EMEA and the Americas. • Workday Configuration & Module Ownership: Own day-to-day maintenance of 1–2 core Workday modules, business process framework (BPF) updates, calculated fields, condition rules, and security role assignments, becoming a primary go-to resource for the team. • Mass Data Uploads & Data Integrity: Prepare, validate, and execute bulk data loads (EIBs) and conduct systematic audits across worker records, compensation data, and leave balances to keep Workday as our single source of truth. • People Ops Automation & Process Redesign: Identify bottlenecks in recurring manual workflows (such as Jira-based tracking or spreadsheets) and help transition them into structured, automated Workday workflows, self-service features, and clear employee knowledge guides. • Cross-Functional Support & Ticket Management: Manage incoming JIRA service desk requests from HR, Finance, and Payroll, resolving workflow exceptions and providing responsive, customer-centric support across multiple concurrent priorities. • AI-Augmented Operational Efficiency: Utilize existing productivity and enterprise AI tools—including Workday’s AI Agent, Gemini for Workspace, and NotebookLM—to accelerate troubleshooting, validate data quality, and maintain internal documentation. • Live the mission: inspire and empower others by genuinely caring for your own wellbeing and your colleagues. Bring wellbeing to the forefront of work, and create a supportive environment where everyone feels comfortable taking care of themselves, taking time off, and finding work-life wellness. WHO YOU ARE • HRIS & Workday Experience: 1–3 years of hands-on HRIS experience with system configuration/administrative access; hands-on Workday experience (Core HCM, Absence, Time Tracking, Compensation, or Business Process management) is strongly preferred. • Strong Data & Spreadsheet Skills: Solid proficiency in Excel and Google Sheets (pivot tables, lookups, data reconciliation) with strong attention to detail for preparing and auditing mass data uploads (EIBs). • Multitasking & Customer-Service Mindset: Proven ability to manage multiple competing requests from stakeholders across HR, Finance, and Payroll while maintaining clear, empathetic communication.
