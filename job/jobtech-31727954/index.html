Data Center Technician (Mechanical / Cooling) 📍 Location: Linköping 🏢 Company: CBRE 🕒 Employment Type: Full-Time (FTE) 👥 Team Structure: Part of a 3 FTE team 🔧 Scope: OSA Scope - Mechanical/Cooling competence required About the Role As a CBRE Data Center Technician, you will perform preventative maintenance and corrective repairs within a data center environment. This role sits within CBRE's Critical and Technical Services function, responsible for delivering technical maintenance services in mission-critical client environments. What You'll Do Assist with daily site inspections of all mechanical & engineering systems and technical equipment, including servicing and maintenance Ensure the site's environmental conditions provide a safe working environment Respond to Building Management System (BMS) alarms and complete emergency repairs as needed Work with third-party vendors, ensuring compliance with company processes, procedures, and regulations Alert management if subcontractor performance does not meet requirements under the Master Service Agreement Complete planned preventative maintenance (PPM) and reactive maintenance on all equipment Monitor BMS systems and respond to HVAC/cooling-related issues Conduct building walkthroughs and take meter readings from UPS, PDUs, and utilities Troubleshoot and resolve facility and plant faults and defects Follow established processes and procedures, ensuring compliance with safety, regulatory, and quality standards Maintain accurate maintenance records and perform routine quality checks Complete all required training to support successful job performance Work under close supervision, following clearly defined tasks, processes, and responsibilities What You'll Need Technical training in a relevant discipline (apprenticeship or equivalent) with 1-2 years of experience Mechanical / Cooling (HVAC) competence is required Ability to work shift patterns, including weekends and holidays Ability to meet the physical demands of the role Strong communication skills to interact with colleagues, clients, and management Ability to follow structured routines and procedures Basic knowledge of Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, Outlook) Strong organizational skills with an inquisitive mindset Basic math skills (percentages, simple calculations, etc.) Why Join CBRE? Be part of a high-performing team (3 FTE structure) in a critical environment Work with advanced data center and electrical infrastructure Opportunities for career progression and technical development Collaborative and safety-focused workplace 👉 Apply now and take the next step in your datacenter career with CBRE.
