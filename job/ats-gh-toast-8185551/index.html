Toast creates technology to help restaurants and local businesses succeed in a digital world, helping business owners operate, increase sales, engage customers, and keep employees happy. Toast is building the platform that helps operators adapt, take control, and get back to what they do best: running the businesses they love. We've grown into a leading hospitality software company in the U.S., and we're now entering our next phase of growth, one defined by scale, complexity, and a new class of enterprise customers. Toast is looking for a back-end focused Staff Software Engineer to join our Toast Connect team in Dublin. Toast Connect owns how everything outside Toast connects to Toast. We are the team that makes Toast a platform worth building on. Our APIs, webhooks, frameworks, and development tools let partners and customers solve real problems fast, and our infrastructure lets the ecosystem scale safely. As the ecosystem grows, so does our charter: making Toast the source of truth for everything we know about the customer. As a Staff Software Engineer, you'll be the technical lead for major parts of the architectural evolution of our Ecosystem. If you want to build something used every day by thousands of businesses, help those businesses grow and find success, and take on the challenge of scaling a platform, Toast is the place for you.
