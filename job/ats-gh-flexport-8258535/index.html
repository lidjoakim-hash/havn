Hundreds of engineers and thousands of freight operators around the world rely on Flexport's internal systems every single day to keep billions of dollars of freight moving. Keeping those systems — and the people who use them — secure is not a compliance checkbox. It's the core infrastructure for a company operating across 112 countries. The PSI (Platform, Security & Infrastructure) team is looking for a Senior Security Engineer to own the security of Flexport's corporate environment: endpoints, identity systems, internal networks, and the SaaS estate. This is a security engineering role, not an IT support role, and not a role where you write policies and hope for the best. You'll build, automate, and be on the ground when things get real. What You'll Do Endpoint & SaaS security • Own and mature our endpoint security stack (EDR) across macOS and Windows fleets, writing device policy as code — configuration profiles, remediation scripts, and enforcement rules — with staged rollout and rollback built in. • Own security configuration and monitoring for Flexport's critical SaaS applications (Google Workspace, Slack, and similar) through SSPM tooling, catching risky OAuth grants and configuration drift before they become incidents. Access management • Drive Flexport's identity posture: SSO coverage, phishing-resistant MFA and passkey rollout, SCIM lifecycle automation, and least-privilege access across the SaaS and cloud estate. • Lead Flexport's zero-trust journey — making sure the right people have the right access, with the right controls, verified continuously. Detection & response • Build and tune detection rules in our SIEM, turning signal into actionable alerts, not noise. • Lead incident response for security events: triage, contain, remediate, and write the retrospective. Vulnerability & risk management • Run regular assessments of corporate endpoints and prioritize findings by real business risk. • Manage vendor and third-party security reviews, working with procurement and legal to evaluate risk before it lands in our environment. Security enablement & leadership • Partner with IT, Engineering, and People teams to embed security into the way we work without creating friction that drives people to workarounds. • Develop and maintain runbooks, playbooks, and internal documentation that make the whole team more capable, and mentor engineers earlier in their career. • Bring a point of view on how agentic AI tools and browser-based AI assistants change what corporate security needs to watch for.
