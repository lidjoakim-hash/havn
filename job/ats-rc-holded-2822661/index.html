Join the team. Make an impact. At Holded, we believe that daily admin should never stop a great idea from becoming a success. That's why we create intuitive software to empower anyone who dares to start their own business. Long story short: we want to make business simple. In order to create cutting-edge products that meet the needs of the sector, talent is essential. We are looking for passion, creativity, and commitment. In return, we offer the same. The role Data plays a key role in how we sell, grow our partner network and make better decisions across the company. Our Data team works with teams across Holded (Product, Sales, Support, Marketing and Finance). We are now looking for a Sales Data Analyst to join us, supporting our commercial and customer-facing teams: Sales, Revenue Operations, Accounting Offices (asesorías), Solution Partners, Implementations, Customer Support and Sales Enablement. You will be part of a growing data organisation that includes analysts, data engineers and data science. You will work closely with the leaders of these teams from day one, alongside the analyst who supports them today. Together you will split ownership of these teams. You will own the data and the metrics for the teams you support, including how those metrics are defined. You will be a strong partner to these teams: you will guide them on what they should measure, give them analytical support, and find the areas of the business where they can have more impact. To do this, you will use data from our own platform and from HubSpot. As a Sales Data Analyst, your work will include • Own how we measure the performance of your teams, including targets and incentives: the metrics and dashboards they run on, from definition through to what people actually decide with them • Analyse the full customer lifecycle, from acquisition and conversion to retention, churn and customer health, including the performance of Support and Customer Success • Ensure the quality of HubSpot data, with good data governance practices • Connect our source-of-truth data and real-time product events with HubSpot, so teams work with complete and reliable data • Build and own tested, documented dbt models for the data in your teams' scope, contributing to a semantic layer used across the company • Answer data questions and turn open commercial questions into analyses that change what the team does next • Work closely with Revenue Operations on tracking and new CRM requirements • Work with our data engineers to make the way we move data to our CRMs more efficient and reliable About you • A degree in a quantitative or technical field, or equivalent practical experience • 4+ years of hands-on experience working with data in a professional setting, ideally with Sales, RevOps or commercial teams • Strong SQL • Hands-on experience with dbt and data modeling • Real experience with CRM data: objects, properties, pipelines and their data quality problems. HubSpot experience is a big plus • Critical thinking and a proactive mindset when working with stakeholders • You can explain complex findings clearly and simply to non-technical people • Fluent in Spanish & English Nice to have • You understand B2B SaaS sales. • Experience with BigQuery, Airflow, Lightdash, Google Tag Manager or Intercom is a plus, but not required • Experience using AI tools such as Claude in your analytics work is a plus • Experience with integrations or reverse ETL between a warehouse and a CRM is a plus • Some experience with Python is a plus Within your first year This is a role with significant growth opportunities, both in terms of scope and impact, and it will be adjusted to the candidate's profile and experience.
