About Anthropic Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems. About the role Join Anthropic as an EMEA-based Customer Success Manager supporting our Beneficial Deployments team's work with nonprofits. You'll be a strategic partner and trusted advisor to mission-driven organizations across EMEA, spanning a range of impact areas. Your focus is driving activation, adoption, and value of Claude across Claude for Enterprise, Claude Code, and the API. That means helping these organizations evolve how they work internally, and guiding them as they evolve how they serve their missions and constituents with AI. You'll work as a pod with your Account Executive and Applied AI counterpart. Within the pod, you'll decide what level of engagement each customer most needs and either personally deliver it or connect the customer to resources that serve their needs. CS-led engagements range from high-touch 1:1 partnerships to one-to-many cohort engagements, with the latitude to design other approaches where they serve customers better. As one of our early Customer Success Managers in EMEA, you'll be a pioneer: helping to build regional processes, establish new customer relationships, and represent Anthropic's brand in a high-growth market. You'll operate with significant autonomy while staying closely aligned with global teams, which calls for someone who thrives in ambiguous environments and works effectively across time zones. The insights you gather from these partnerships will directly inform our research priorities, product development, and strategies for beneficial AI deployment. As the voice of the customer, you'll help shape how we build and deliver AI systems that amplify social good.
