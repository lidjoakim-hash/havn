About the role The Global Mobility Team at HelloFresh is looking for a Senior Global Mobility Manager who will use their expert knowledge to help think and develop Global Mobility within a young, rapidly expanding company, and consider this as a strategically important topic within the People Team. As a successful applicant, you will also always have an open ear for the questions of your stakeholders and our employees, manage to organize and structure your tasks independently, and own and lead internal initiatives and projects. Above all, we are looking for people who will make HelloFresh better. We believe there are many different ways of developing skills and we love diverse experiences! So even if you don’t “tick all the boxes” but think you’d thrive in this role, we would really like to learn more about you. This role is based in Berlin and reports to the Head of Global Mobility and Travel What you’ll do • Strategic Advisory to the Business: Have a business impact by assisting the business to onboard new hires and retain international employees via mobility programs, along with an impact on new hires and employees' lives by taking part in their relocation, immigration, and settling-in journey • Tier 3 Case Management: In your daily work, you will have diverse responsibilities, such as: strategically consulting on complex relocation, immigration, and internal mobility cases, managing escalations and exceptions, and accompanying executive cases of senior leaders. • Project management: you will be responsible for some of our key projects, such as reviewing and improving policies and processes, working on benchmarking, globalization, and constant automation and efficiency. • Continuous improvement of our policies and processes: you will have the opportunity to propose improvements in our policies, processes, SOPs, and working documents, creating a big impact on the affected employee population and on our ways of working • Support our Global Mobility Team as a Subject Matter Expert: As an active member of our Global Mobility team, you will work with our incredible team members and support each other to ensure success in our high-performing team Oversee our SSC Operations: Oversee the work of our team member in Manila on a dotted-line basis, promoting operational excellence and supporting the daily work and growth of our team member • Have a key stake in shaping the future of Global Mobility at HelloFresh by taking a leading part in our team’s journey • Collaborate with different stakeholders within the People Team to ensure best practices, compliance, and a seamless new hire and employee experience What you’ll bring • You are an SME on Global Mobility.
