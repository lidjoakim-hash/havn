ABOUT US At LangChain, our mission is to enable every company to own its intelligence. We believe agents will transform how companies operate, and we're building the foundation to make that future possible. Our open-source harnesses and LangSmith platform give developers and enterprises the infrastructure to run, improve, and govern agents at scale. We've raised $460M to date, with our most recent funding round valuing the company at $3B, backed by leading investors including Sequoia, Benchmark, IVP, and CapitalG. As we continue to expand our platform and tackle some of the hardest challenges in agent engineering, every team member has the opportunity to make a meaningful impact and help shape both our technology and the future of the industry. More than 7,000 organizations rely on LangSmith, from fast-growing startups to Fortune 500 enterprises, including NVIDIA, Bridgewater, LinkedIn, Workday, and Rippling. We're just getting started on making high-performing agents easier to build and manage at scale. ABOUT THE ROLE: We’re hiring a Manager - Sales Development Representatives (SDR) (London) - EMEA to lead and scale our founding EMEA SDR team at LangChain. Our SDRs play a critical role in helping companies adopt LangChain and LangSmith. They engage technical buyers, convert high inbound interest into qualified opportunities, and work closely with our sales and marketing teams to build pipeline. This role will own the performance, coaching, and development of the SDR team, while helping design the systems, processes, and workflows that power their GTM motion. You’ll partner closely with sales, marketing, and and RevOps to continuously improve how we identify and engage the right accounts. WHAT YOU'LL DO: - Lead and coach a team of SDRs responsible for generating qualified pipeline - Own SDR performance including activity metrics, conversion rates, pipeline creation, and attainment - Recruit top SDR talent and grow the team as LangChain continues to scale - Develop SDR talent through coaching, call reviews, and structured feedback - Build repeatable outbound and inbound qualification playbooks - Partner with marketing to convert inbound demand into qualified opportunities - Work with sales leadership and RevOps to align on target accounts, territories, and pipeline goals - Implement systems and workflows that improve SDR productivity and effectiveness - Use data to continuously improve performance, analyzing conversion rates across the funnel HOW TO BE SUCCESSFUL IN THIS ROLE: - Have 5+ years of experience in sales development - Have 1–3+ years managing SDRs or leading a sales development team - Are comfortable working with technical products and developer audiences - Have experience with modern GTM tooling (AI Agents, LinkedIn Sales Nav, Power Dialers, etc.) - Are highly data-driven and comfortable managing performance through metrics - Are passionate about coaching and developing early-career sales talent - Thrive in fast-moving environments where processes are still evolving - Have strong communication and organizational skills COMPENSATION PHILOSOPHY: We offer competitive compensation that includes base salary, variable compensation for relevant roles, meaningful equity, benefits, and perks. We are hiring for this role across a range of scope, and the compensation band reflects that. Your offer depends on what we learn about your impact, craft, leadership, and communication during the interview process. We will tell you early where you’re tracking and talk comp with you before offer stage. Team members in the EU, UK, and APAC receive locally competitive benefits aligned with regional norms and regulations. BENEFITS Benefits include medical, dental, and vision coverage, flexible vacation, a 401(k) plan, meals on in-office days in the US and more.
