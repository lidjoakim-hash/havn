Mentimeter is an engagement company with a simple goal: to make every leader better. Through interaction, insights and recommendations, its platform helps organizations turn participation into better understanding and stronger performance. Founded in Stockholm in 2012, Mentimeter is used by 20 million people each month across 1.3 million organizations. At Mentimeter, we believe better leadership starts with listening and that the strongest results are achieved together. That’s why we foster a culture built on curiosity, collaboration and shared ownership. By joining us, you’ll help leaders turn participation into deeper understanding and stronger performance, advancing our ambition to help more than one billion people listen, learn and work better together. The Role As Design Director for our Scaling Engagement product area, you will own the experience that helps teams and whole organisations get lasting value from Mentimeter, well beyond the moment of the session itself. This is a role for a design leader who sets direction by shaping the work, not only by managing it. You will join a product company at a pivotal moment. Mentimeter has become very good at creating a great engagement in the moment. The hardest and most valuable design problems now sit in what comes after: turning that engagement into results people act on, insight that holds over time, and lasting value across an organisation. That is the problem this area owns, and it is one of the least solved in our space. You will operate in two primary contexts: within your Product Area Leadership, where the work happens alongside the Product Director and Engineering Director, and within the Design Leadership Team, partnering the Chief Design Officer and fellow Design leaders. You will define the experience direction for your product area and make sure design decisions translate into measurable customer and business impact. You will report to the Chief Design Officer. What we are looking for We are looking for a design leader who leads through the work. Someone with the judgment to set a clear experience direction and the hands-on capability to raise the quality bar by shaping, critiquing, and making alongside the team. You have led and managed designers in fast-moving, product-led environments, and you have earned your credibility through the impact and quality of your work. You are as comfortable owning experience strategy for a product area as you are getting into the detail of a flow that isn't good enough yet. You operate as a true peer to product and engineering leaders, and you believe design is at its best when it drives outcomes, not when it decorates them.
