Stories have the power to make the ordinary extraordinary. They help us think bigger, see beyond ourselves, and deepen our connection with others. As one of the world’s leading audiobook and e-book streaming platforms, Storytel brings unlimited listening to millions of users across 25+ markets. Driven by our purpose “Leading the future of storytelling, we move the world through stories”, Storytel Group inspires and entertains people around the world by blending innovation with tradition. We bring stories to life across various formats for everyone to discover. Anytime. Anywhere. Ready to start your first chapter, joining a group of passionate story lovers? We’re looking for a Business Controlling Intern to join the team in Stockholm! About the Role What if your first real finance role gave you both a rock-solid grounding in controlling and a chance to help define what it looks like next? At Storytel, you’ll get a close-up view of how the numbers behind a global streaming business come together. You’ll learn the fundamentals from people who know them inside out, and you’ll help take them further by using AI to make reporting and analysis faster, sharper and less manual. This is a paid part-time internship to run alongside your studies, starting in February 2027. No two weeks look the same, and you’ll get hands-on responsibility from the start. In a nutshell, here’s what you’ll do: • Ask the numbers anything. Help build conversational reporting, where leaders ask questions in plain language and get the right numbers back, and test it with the senior leaders who use it. • Deepen the monthly analysis. AI already drafts the commentary in our monthly report. Help develop the analysis so it explains the drivers behind the results more precisely. • Build AI agents. Identify tasks in our reporting and controls that an agent could take over, then build, test and put them to work. • Learn about forecasting. Take part in the monthly reporting and forecast cycles. We are using machine learning in our forecasts, and if you have the interest and skills, you can help develop it further. • Make our data AI-ready. Build an agent that helps the teams code transactions to the right accounts and dimensions, so the data our AI relies on is right from the start. About the team This is a great opportunity for you who want to gain insights about working in a publicly listed tech company. You'd join the Business Controlling team and report directly to the Head of Business Controlling. You’ll work side by side with our business controllers, who own the monthly report, the forecast and the analysis behind our biggest decisions, across streaming markets and publishing houses.
