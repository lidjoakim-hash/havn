Senior Embedded Software Application Engineer

Click to Hire AB · Sweden

💰 ca. 600 000–720 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Kontraktvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 31.1.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

About the Role We are looking for an experienced and curious AI Software Developer with strong Python programming skills and hands-on experience working with Large Language Models (LLMs). Our client has established an AI platform that can be configured for various applications, primarily focused on improving software development efficiency. You will contribute to developing intelligent solutions and automation pipelines that support and streamline engineering activities. This is an exciting opportunity to work at the intersection of traditional software engineering, machine learning and generative AI, collaborating with different development teams to bring innovative ideas into practice. Key Responsibilities Design, develop and maintain automation pipelines to support and automate engineering tasks. Combine conventional software automation techniques with Large Language Models (LLMs). Develop and implement AI-driven solutions to improve software development efficiency. Work in an agile environment, focusing on delivering and demonstrating practical results. Collaborate with different development teams to configure and adapt the machine learning platform to their specific applications. Support teams in exploring, testing and implementing new AI capabilities. Identify opportunities for improvement and proactively contribute to technical solutions. Required Qualifications Master's degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Software Engineering or a related field. Strong software engineering and software design skills. Excellent programming skills, particularly in Python. Hands-on experience working with Large Language Models (LLMs). Good understanding of machine learning concepts and applications. Excellent verbal and written communication skills in English. Strong collaboration and interpersonal skills, with the ability to build effective working relationships. Preferred Qualifications Familiarity with containerization technologies and cloud platforms. Experience with Kubernetes. Familiarity with DevOps practices and tools. Experience working with automotive software development or system engineering. Personal Attributes We are looking for someone who: Has a curious mindset and enthusiasm for exploring emerging technologies. Is proactive, responsible and takes ownership of assigned tasks. Enjoys collaborating with different teams and stakeholders. Is flexible and comfortable working across different technical application areas. Takes initiative, experiments with new ideas and focuses on finding practical solutions. Has a positive, open-minded approach to challenges and is willing to explore possibilities before dismissing them. What We Offer An opportunity to work on innovative AI and machine learning applications. Exposure to practical applications of Generative AI and LLM technologies. Collaboration with experienced engineering and development teams. An agile and dynamic working environment. The opportunity to contribute to solutions that improve modern software engineering processes. Interested? If you have a strong background in Python development, experience with LLMs and a passion for building practical AI solutions, we would love to hear from you. Please submit your CV along with a brief description of your relevant experience.
