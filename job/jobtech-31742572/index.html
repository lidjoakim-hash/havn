Senior Business Analysts – Trading Compliance & Analytics

Sway Sourcing Sweden Aktiebolag · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 500 000–610 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 15.2.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

We are looking for two Senior Business Analysts for a client undertaking a major transformation of its trade surveillance capabilities. About the role You will support the development of a next-generation surveillance platform used by international teams. Working at the intersection of business strategy and technology, you will take ownership of functional requirements and help guide their delivery from initial analysis through implementation. The role involves translating complex business needs into actionable specifications, coordinating delivery activities, and ensuring data integrations meet relevant exchange requirements and technical standards. Key responsibilities Develop clear functional specifications, user stories, acceptance criteria, and supporting documentation for development, quality assurance, delivery, and surveillance teams. Identify, document, prioritise, and validate data and functional requirements throughout the product lifecycle, balancing business value, exchange requirements, and technical feasibility. Collaborate with surveillance teams across Europe and the US, alongside commercial and engineering stakeholders, to plan delivery, track requirements, resolve blockers, and maintain alignment with product design principles. Work with Risk, Compliance, and Audit teams to review requirements, clarify expectations, and agree on enhancements and issue resolution. Maintain the project backlog and establish governance procedures to support effective delivery. Coordinate the calibration and tuning of surveillance models and detection patterns as they are introduced across asset classes, including equities and fixed income. Support day-to-day surveillance activities, including reviews of business initiatives, market abuse risk assessments, and responses to user queries. Required qualifications A master’s degree in Economics, Finance, Computer Science, or a related discipline, or equivalent practical experience. At least five years of experience as a business analyst or product analyst within capital markets, financial technology, or a regulated trading environment. Strong knowledge of trading data structures, order management and execution workflows, trading protocols, and exchange rules. An understanding of multiple asset classes and instruments, including equities, fixed income, futures, options, and forwards. Demonstrated ability to turn complex business and technical requirements into structured documentation, such as functional specifications, user stories, and data flow diagrams. Experience working in Agile delivery environments with cross-functional engineering and product teams, using tools such as Jira. Strong analytical skills and advanced proficiency in SQL. Preferred qualifications Knowledge of trade surveillance, compliance monitoring, or regulatory frameworks such as MAR, MiFID II, FINRA requirements, and relevant market models or exchange rules. Experience with large-scale data integration pipelines or market data feeds across multiple asset classes. Familiarity with instrument reference data and how different traded products affect surveillance requirements. Experience collaborating with international teams and stakeholders across regions. Practical experience with trade surveillance platforms, including the calibration and tuning of detection models. Extensive experience using Jira for backlog management and sprint planning. Familiarity with data visualisation tools such as Tableau or Power BI. Personal qualities You work independently, take ownership of your responsibilities, and communicate clearly with both business and technical stakeholders.
