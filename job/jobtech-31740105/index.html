Docking and Project Superintendent

Stena Line Scandinavia AB · Göteborg, Sweden

💰 ca. 290 000–350 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 1.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Join our team as a Docking and Project Superintendent, where you'll play a pivotal role in ensuring the smooth planning and execution of dry-docking operations for Stena Line’s vessels!     In this role, you will utilize your maritime expertise to provide guidance to our Fleet Groups, ensuring adherence to docking policies and procedures while prioritizing quality, time efficiency, and cost-effectiveness. You will also coordinate fleet and group projects under the direction of Senior Management, while also ensuring vessel accreditation to meet international safety and pollution prevention standards. If you're driven by excellence and adept at optimizing operations, apply now to be part of our team!     Some of your key responsibilities:    - Manage and coordinate dry-docking processes.   - Review and standardize docking and repair specifications.   - Coordinate refit budgeting and cost analysis with Fleet Group.   - Manage pre-docking planning and reporting.   - Act as Project Manager for technical projects.   - Monitor project risks and key milestones.   - Negotiate terms with ship repair yards.   - Ensure efficient vessel management during refits.   - Maintain vessel standards and appearance.   - Provide expertise to Fleet Group and Company management.   - Assist with warranty claims and post-project evaluations.    - Ensure compliance with contractual and commercial agreements.   - Liaise with departments supporting vessels.   - Travel as required.      What you will experience   As a Stena Line employee in Sweden, we offer you an additional benefits package consisting of travel discounts, health allowance, well-being activities, access to a benefits portal, and much more.     Based in our Gothenburg office, this role follows an office-first approach, allowing you to work closely with a collaborative team of colleagues.     Who are you?   At Stena Line, your personal qualities matter just as much as your skills and expertise. Regardless of the role, being welcoming, caring, and reliable guides you in your everyday work and the challenges you face.     We believe you are a team-player who thrives in a collaborative environment. You share your knowledge and pay attention to the details in everything you do. We also believe you welcome new challenges with an open, and positive attitude.     Qualifications:   - Unlimited Chief Engineer or Master certification. Alternatively, a degree in nautical science-related fields.   - Practical experience either onboard with exposure to onboard management teams or a minimum of 1+ year experience ashore in technical/project roles within a shipping company's operations.   - Keen knowledge of vessel operations, and technical and safety requirements for RoPax-type vessels.    - Dry-dock planning and planned maintenance systems.    - Financial knowledge of budgets and cost control to ensure effective project management whilst maintaining company standards of safety and quality.   - Strong understanding of the key concepts of Health and Safety     - Knowledge of ISM and ISO standards and codes     Interested?    This is a full-time, permanent position based in Gothenburg within our Fleet Operations department. To apply, please register your profile and send in your application in English as soon as possible but no later than 1 December, 2026. We are having an ongoing selection, so do not wait with your application.
