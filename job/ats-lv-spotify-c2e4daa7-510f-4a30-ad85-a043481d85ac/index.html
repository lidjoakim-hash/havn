Backend Engineer - Subscriptions

Spotify · London, Sweden

What You'll Do • Design, build, and deliver backend capabilities that support Spotify's commerce platform, from solution design through to production. • Develop and improve scalable services powering merchandising, order management, payment orchestration, and purchase experiences. • Work with complex, high-traffic systems to improve reliability, performance, and scalability. • Collaborate with engineers and teams across Spotify to deliver solutions that support our listeners and subscription business. • Work with cloud infrastructure, databases, and data pipelines to build and maintain reliable backend systems. • Contribute to technical discussions, engineering workshops, and solution reviews, sharing ideas and learning from your peers. • Help improve engineering practices and contribute to a collaborative environment where knowledge-sharing and continuous improvement are part of everyday work. Who You Are • You have experience developing and scaling backend services using Java, Python, or similar technologies in a cloud environment such as Google Cloud Platform. • You're familiar with scalable database technologies such as PostgreSQL and understand the fundamentals of building reliable backend systems. • You have exposure to data systems and pipelines, or you're interested in developing your expertise in this area. • You enjoy tackling technical challenges, breaking down complex problems, and finding practical solutions. • You communicate clearly and are comfortable sharing ideas, participating in technical discussions, and presenting your work to others. • You're proactive, take ownership of your work, and enjoy learning new technologies and approaches. • You value collaboration, feedback, and working with people from different technical backgrounds to achieve shared goals. • Experience in e-commerce, payments, or financial engineering is valuable, but not essential. Where You'll Be • This role is based in London or Stockholm • We offer you the flexibility to work where you work best!
