Are you passionate about laboratory operations and motivated by creating the right conditions for scientific excellence? Would you like to join an international organization where innovation, collaboration, and continuous improvement are part of everyday work? We are looking for a Laboratory Engineer to join our Analytical Development – Protein Chemistry team within Injectables Development in Uppsala. In this role, you will help ensure that our laboratories operate safely, efficiently, and seamlessly, enabling our scientists to focus on developing the next generation of injectable aesthetic products. This is an exciting opportunity to become part of a growing organization where your contribution will have a direct impact on both day-to-day operations and long-term success. Make your mark. Grow with us. Your team Analytical Development – Protein Chemistry is part of Galderma's Injectables Development organization in Uppsala. The team provides analytical expertise that supports the development of innovative products and technologies, helping to strengthen and expand Galderma's portfolio. You will join a collaborative and highly engaged team where knowledge sharing, scientific curiosity, and mutual support are valued. Together, we work in a dynamic environment where quality, innovation, and patient focus guide everything we do. Your mission As a Laboratory Engineer, you will play a key role in maintaining and developing the laboratory environment that supports our analytical development activities. You will coordinate purchasing activities and support the maintenance, qualification, and lifecycle management of laboratory equipment and shared infrastructure, including refrigerators, freezers, safety cabinets, and other laboratory systems. Working closely with scientists, equipment owners, suppliers, Facilities, Quality, and external service providers, you will help ensure that equipment is available, compliant, and performing as intended. Depending on business needs and your experience, you may also contribute to the analytical testing of proteins and related materials using a range of analytical techniques. To thrive in this role, you enjoy creating structure, coordinating multiple activities, and finding practical solutions in a dynamic laboratory environment. You combine a proactive mindset with strong attention to detail and take pride in supporting scientific teams by ensuring laboratory operations run smoothly and efficiently. Key Responsibilities · Coordinate purchasing and procurement activities for the laboratory. · Support the maintenance, servicing, qualification, and lifecycle management of laboratory equipment and infrastructure. · Collaborate with internal stakeholders and external suppliers to ensure equipment reliability and availability. · Maintain documentation and records in accordance with regulatory and quality requirements. · Support laboratory operations by identifying opportunities to improve processes, workflows, and ways of working. · Contribute to maintaining a safe, compliant, and well-functioning laboratory environment. · Perform analytical testing of proteins and related materials when required. · Ensure all activities are conducted in accordance with applicable regulations, quality standards, and internal procedures. Your profile You are someone who enjoys building relationships, creating structure, and enabling others to do their best work. With a proactive and service-oriented approach, you take ownership of your responsibilities and are motivated by delivering high-quality support in a regulated laboratory environment. You thrive in collaborative settings and communicate effectively with a wide range of stakeholders, from scientists and support functions to suppliers and external service providers.
