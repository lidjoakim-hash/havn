Supply Chain Specialist

Rohlik · Prague, Czech Republic, Czechia

At Rohlik, Supply Chain is about precision, speed and data. Every day we decide what customers will find in stock — and what they won’t. We’re now looking for a Supply Chain Specialist who will own a portfolio of categories and suppliers, ensuring strong product availability while keeping inventory healthy and waste under control.   WHY THIS ROLE IS EXCITING - You’ll have a direct impact on product availability for thousands of customers - You’ll work with real data, forecasts and business decisions - You’ll see the results of your work in both numbers and customer experience - You’ll collaborate across Category, Operations and Finance - You’ll help automate and improve how supply chain processes work - We are an AI-first company — you’ll work in an environment where forecasting, ordering and supply chain decisions are increasingly driven by data, automation and intelligent tools   WHAT YOU’LL DO You’ll take ownership of day-to-day supply chain performance within your scope. Specifically, you will: - Own demand forecasting, replenishment and ordering for assigned categories - Manage supplier operational performance and ensure SLA compliance (OTIF, shrink, ASN accuracy, lead times) - Partner with Category Managers, Fulfillment Operations and Finance to balance availability, cost and inventory health - Support automation and system improvements to reduce manual ordering - Resolve supplier and inventory issues before they impact customers - Track and act on key metrics such as forecast accuracy, shrink and availability   WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR - 1–3 years of experience in supply chain, replenishment, inventory management or similar role - Strong analytical mindset and communication skills - Advanced Excel skills; familiarity with BI tools is a plus - Experience in FMCG, grocery retail or e-commerce is an advantage - Knowledge of ERP or WMS systems is a plus - Fluent Czech/Slovak and advanced English   WHAT’S IN IT FOR YOU - Fair reward - You’ll get a strong fixed salary — no bonus games, no politics. Great work is rewarded clearly and fairly. - Good food every day - You’ll enjoy a free lunch every day and fresh fruit & veggies in the office. Because good food fuels great work. - Life, not just work - You’ll have real flexibility – three days in the office, 2 days Home Office where it suits you. And unlimited paid vacation based on trust. - Personal growth - You’ll grow as fast as Rohlik does. Every challenge is a chance to learn and make an impact. - Real impact - What you do here matters. You’ll see your work changing how people eat and live — from day one. - Recognition among colleagues - Your effort won’t go unnoticed. You can recognise others (and be recognised) through peer credits to use in our internal cafeteria. - Xtra membership - You’ll get free Xtra membership and 10% cashback on everything you buy on Rohlik.cz http://Rohlik.cz. - Sport & wellbeing - You’ll stay active with company sports challenges or a subsidised Multisport card. - A team that sticks together - You’ll work with people who move fast, support each other and celebrate wins together. - If this sounds like you…You’re someone who takes ownership, acts fast and gets things done. Ready to bake something great with us?
