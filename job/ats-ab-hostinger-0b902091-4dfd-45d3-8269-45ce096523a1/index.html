YOUR ROLE AT HOSTINGER As a QA Practice Lead, you'll play a key role in shaping the future of quality assurance at Hostinger. You'll lead the evolution of our QA practices by introducing AI-powered testing approaches, improving automation capabilities, and helping QA engineers transition from traditional test execution to orchestrating and validating AI agents. Working closely with Engineering, Product, AI, and DevOps teams, you'll define the tools, standards, and skills needed to ensure quality across Hostinger's products as we continue to innovate and deliver faster. This is an opportunity to build a new approach to QA, experiment with emerging technologies, and drive meaningful change across our engineering organization. Curious to learn more? Connect with your future lead: Asta Dagienė, Head of Delivery https://www.linkedin.com/in/asta-dagiene/ YOUR DAY-TO-DAY: - Shape and drive Hostinger's QA strategy, introducing AI-powered testing tools, methodologies, and best practices across engineering teams. - Experiment with and implement AI agents for test generation, execution, maintenance, and failure analysis. - Help QA engineers transition toward AI-assisted workflows by developing their skills in agent orchestration and validation. - Establish quality standards and metrics to ensure reliable, efficient, and consistent testing across Hostinger's products. - Collaborate with Engineering, Product, AI, and DevOps teams to integrate modern testing solutions into CI/CD pipelines. - Drive continuous improvement, encourage innovation, and turn new ideas into scalable QA practices. WHAT WILL HELP YOU SUCCEED: - 5+ years in quality engineering spanning strategy and automation, with a track record of driving change across teams and a clear point of view on how agentic workflows will transform QA. - Hands-on experience with test automation frameworks (Playwright, Cypress, Selenium, or similar) and programming languages such as JavaScript/TypeScript and Python. - Solid understanding of testing strategies, including unit, integration, API, and end-to-end testing, as well as test reliability and automation best practices. - Hands-on experience using AI tools and agents (Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot, MCP, or similar) to improve QA workflows, with the ability to demonstrate practical applications, validate AI-generated results, and measure their impact. - Experience with CI/CD pipelines, Git, scripting, and quality metrics. - Strong communication, coaching, and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to influence teams and drive change. - Proactive, curious, and adaptable mindset, with a passion for experimenting with emerging technologies.
