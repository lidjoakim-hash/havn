In this role, you will work closely with engineering teams and project managers across international development environments, providing technical cybersecurity expertise and helping ensure that vehicle systems and components are designed, developed and verified with appropriate security measures. You will contribute to cybersecurity activities across ongoing development projects and future vehicle programmes. The position combines hands-on security engineering with technical guidance, review of cybersecurity deliverables, risk assessment and collaboration across multidisciplinary teams. Key Responsibilities Product Cybersecurity & Risk Assessment Support development projects in conducting Product Cybersecurity Screening (PCS). Perform Threat Analysis and Risk Assessment (TARA). Define, review and manage cybersecurity requirements. Support the development of supplier cybersecurity agreements. Contribute to cybersecurity verification and validation activities. Identify product security risks and support the definition of appropriate mitigation measures. Secure System Design & Technical Guidance Provide technical guidance on secure design principles for vehicle systems and electronic components. Support engineering teams in making informed cybersecurity design decisions. Evaluate security considerations in embedded and connected vehicle systems. Review and approve cybersecurity work products prepared by development teams. Define cybersecurity testing activities and support the implementation of corrective actions. Compliance & Security Verification Support cybersecurity approval and compliance activities related to UN Regulation No. 155 (UN R155). Coordinate penetration testing activities and follow up on findings and remediation actions. Support cybersecurity verification, validation and evidence documentation. Help ensure that product security activities follow applicable cybersecurity processes and requirements. Collaboration & Continuous Improvement Coach and guide development teams on cybersecurity methodologies and best practices. Collaborate with engineers, project managers and other cybersecurity specialists. Contribute across different cybersecurity activities based on team priorities and project requirements. Support improvements to cybersecurity methods, tools and processes where relevant. Required Technical Skills & Experience Product Cybersecurity Professional experience in a Product Cybersecurity role. Hands-on experience performing Threat Analysis and Risk Assessment (TARA). Experience defining, documenting and managing cybersecurity requirements. Understanding of cybersecurity activities throughout the product development lifecycle. Experience reviewing cybersecurity deliverables and supporting security verification and validation. Automotive & Embedded Systems Broad technical understanding of embedded and connected systems. Familiarity with automotive communication technologies, including: CAN Ethernet Bluetooth Vehicle connectivity solutions Technical Security Domains Experience or practical knowledge in relevant security areas, including: Cryptography.
