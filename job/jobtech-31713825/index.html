QA Engineer - Video Management Systems

Axis Communications Aktiebolag · Lund, Sweden

Are you a test engineer with great interest in technology and products? Are you curious about software solutions, including network and devices? Now you have the opportunity to work as team test engineer in the product area Video Management Systems (VMS) at Axis HQ in Lund. Who is your future team? You will work at ACS Pro Test, which is responsible for testing Axis' proprietary VMS Axis Camera Station Pro. This Windows product is an important part of Axis' end-to-end solutions for video surveillance. You can find more information here: https://www.axis.com/products/axis-camera-station-pro What you'll do here as a QA Engineer for Axis Camera Station Pro? The development and testing of Axis Camera Station Pro is divided into several agile teams including both developers and testers. The team testing role we are about to fill is for a team that focuses on APIs and communication protocols. Some of the responsibilities are: * Plan and execute short term manual test needs based on new functionality together with the team * Develop automated tests for the technical area * Participate in release testing for the complete solution * Lead the work to set up and maintain good test environments for both automated and manual testing covering the team's needs * Participate in improvement work related to working methods, test strategy, automation, and test environments Who are we looking for? We are looking for you who have experience in software testing and have good knowledge of agile development methods. We believe that you have a master's or bachelor's degree in computer science or equivalent and have programming skills, preferably in Python and/or C#. You have previous experience of testing at different levels, manual and automated testing, functional and non-functional testing. You have experience of working with systems crossing the borders of software, hardware and network. As a person, you are open, curious, structured and have a strong interest in both customer perspective and technology. You cooperate and communicate smoothly with others in both Swedish and English, you take initiative, and you have a good eye for details. What Axis have to offer We are a world leader in network video, where cutting-edge technology meets global impact. Here, you'll contribute to meaningful projects that shape the future of security and surveillance - developing solutions used worldwide. Your well-being matters to us. We offer a range of benefits, including a company bonus, Friday cake, wellness allowance, health insurance - and even your own Axis bicycle.
