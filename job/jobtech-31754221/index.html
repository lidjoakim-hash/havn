Company description: Ericsson AB Job description: Join our Team About this opportunity: We are looking for a Developer to join our organization, contributing to the development of the Uplink Physical Layer (ULL1) software for Ericsson's market-leading 5G Radio Access Network (RAN) products and future 6G solutions. You will be part of a highly skilled organization where development teams work end-to-end across both ULL1 and ULL2, providing a unique opportunity to deepen your technical expertise, collaborate with experienced colleagues, and make a significant impact on the performance and evolution of Ericsson's baseband products.   What you will do: - Develop, maintain, and optimize software for the Uplink Physical Layer (L1) within Ericsson's RAN portfolio.- Design and implement high-performance solutions for real-time embedded systems.- Work with signal processing algorithms and multi-core processor platforms.- Contribute throughout the complete software development lifecycle, from requirements and design to implementation, testing, and maintenance.- Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams to deliver high-quality solutions.- Drive continuous improvement in software quality, performance, and efficiency.- Share knowledge and contribute to a collaborative and innovative engineering culture.- Adapt quickly to changing priorities and support the evolution of next-generation wireless technologies.   The skills you bring: - 5+ years of software development experience.- Strong programming skills and hands-on experience in C.- Knowledge of signal processing.- Experience with real-time embedded systems.- Understanding of multi-core processor programming.- BSc degree in Computer Science, Telecommunications, Electrical Engineering, or a related technical field (MSc preferred), or equivalent experience.- Strong problem-solving and analytical skills.- Quality-focused mindset with a structured approach to development.- Ability to think from a system perspective and understand complex technical environments.- Strong collaboration, communication, and knowledge-sharing skills.- Flexibility, adaptability, proactiveness and willingness to work on multiple tasks when needed.- Proficiency in English.   Preferred Qualifications: - Experience with C++, and/or MATLAB.- Experience within telecommunications, wireless communication, or RAN development environments.- Knowledge of 4G/5G physical layer technologies.- Knowledge of 4G/5G UPC (User Plan Control) technologies.
