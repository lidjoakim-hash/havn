Job Overview Assignment Description We are looking for an experienced Product Owner to work at the intersection of business and technology. What You Will Work On Own and drive the product roadmap and strategy. Manage, prioritise and maintain the product backlog. Balance innovation, maintenance, business value and technical debt. What You Bring Several years of experience in a development and maintenance organisation. Proven ability to balance business value, financial considerations and product priorities. Experience working in agile environments and managing product backlogs. Strong stakeholder management and communication skills. Structured approach, ownership and decision-making ability. Meritorious Experience in telecom or digital customer journeys. Experience working across multiple teams or leading cross-team initiatives. Experience with product metrics and data-driven decision-making.
