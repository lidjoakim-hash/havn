Logistic Specialist International At Nouryon, our global team takes positive action every day, to reach higher collectively and individually. We create innovative and sustainable solutions for our customers to answer society’s needs – today and in the future. We are looking for team members who bring ideas forward, champion others and work together to do better. Does that sound like you? In your future role as Logistics Specialist, you will Be responsible for completion of daily end-to-end international shipment activities for the region. The position will be based at our Nordic Business Center in Gothenburg. You will be part of a skilled team that performs all transportation planning, executing, and monitoring including reporting and export documentation for international shipments. Responsibilities include: Complete and review shipping documentation and coordinate with site logistics to execute shipments and gather necessary documentation. Monitor shipments and resolve shipment exceptions and complications as they arise. Track and monitor logistics provider, customer, and organization logistics KPIs and cost performance. Establish and maintain relationship with internal stakeholders, logistics providers and government agencies. Work daily in transport management systems (TMS) to manage and optimize logistics flows. Support process improvements within supply chain We believe you bring Logistics education and experience within logistics / supply chain. Knowledge in performing ocean logistics activities Fluent in written and spoken English and Swedish. Great if you have Experience with chemical companies Experience with carrier relationship management Good experience of programs specific to logistics (SAP, TMS, WMS) and to general business management (MS Excel, Word) We believe you are A collaborative cross-functional team player, a problem solver and used to work in a fast pace. Demonstrated ability to troubleshoot and quickly develop a solution. You need to have the ability to prioritize your daily tasks and you possess great communication skills. We offer you At Nouryon, we provide a great job and friendly coworkers, plus benefits in addition to salary. Our collective agreement is with IKEM. Plus, we offer unilateral perks, such as bonus, reduced hours, health care allowance, lunch allowance, and more. Have we got your interest? This is a temporary position for 1 year on a full-time basis, reporting to Eva Gennäs av Boije, Senior Logistic Specialist and based in Gothenburg, Gamlestan. Please apply via our online recruitment system. We will not accept applications via e-mail. Once it's with us we will review to see if we have a match between your skills and the role! For more information about our hiring process, visit: nouryon.com/careers/how-we-hire/ As an applicant, you will be subject to a background check before employment.
