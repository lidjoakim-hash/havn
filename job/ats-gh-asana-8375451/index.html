About Us Asana is a leading platform for human + AI collaboration. Millions of teams around the world rely on Asana to achieve their most important goals, faster. Asana has been named to Fortune's Best Workplaces for 7+ years and recognized by Fast Company, Forbes, and Gartner for excellence in workplace culture and innovation. Our teams are focused on helping customers adopt new ways of working that are easier to scale, easier to govern, and more effective for both humans and AI. As we incubate new products and go-to-market motions, we need strong operators who can turn ambiguity into repeatable systems, clear decisions, and measurable progress. The Opportunity We are looking for an experienced Product Operations Program Manager to strengthen the connective tissue between Product, Engineering, Design, and our customer-facing GTM teams. In this role, you will design, scale, and optimize the operational rhythms, data frameworks, and feedback loops that enable our product squads to ship high-impact features faster and with greater predictability. As a mid-level individual contributor with deep cross-functional delivery experience, you will take ownership of major operational programs - ranging from release planning and launch readiness to helping incorporate customer feedback into the product prioritization process. This role is meant for someone who thrives in ambiguity, loves building process frameworks from scratch and knows how to bring structure to cross-functional work without slowing it down. What You’ll Do - Establish Operational Rhythms: Standardize and facilitate key operational rituals across squads, including product planning, roadmap tracking, dependency mapping, and executive reviews. - Scale Customer Feedback Ecosystems: Experience diagnosing and optimizing existing feedback ecosystems (VOC, CAB) to ensure insights translate into product value. - Launch Enablement: Partner closely with PM, PMM, Field Readiness, CX Enablement, and Solutions Engineering to ensure internal teams and external customers are equipped for new releases. - Process Modernization: Identify friction, delivery bottlenecks, and communication gaps across squads; design lightweight, pragmatic frameworks and templates that reduce coordination tax without adding bureaucratic overhead. - Run Cross-functional Strategic initiatives: Lead and oversee broad strategic initiatives (examples - Resource Management, Localization) that span both the product and GTM organizations, resolving competing priorities and removing operational bottlenecks that hinder execution. What We’re Looking For Required - 7+ years of total professional experience, with at least 4+ years directly in Product Operations, Technical Program Management (TPM), or Strategy & BizOps within an Agile software or B2B SaaS environment. - Excellent program management skills with the ability to drive multiple cross-functional workstreams simultaneously and keep stakeholders aligned - Proven ability to influence across functions - engineering managers, product leaders, designers, sales executives, and support leads—without formal authority. - High judgment, strong follow-through, and a bias toward building clear, durable systems rather than reactive one-off fixes - Proven experience building processes, cadences, and reporting in environments where roles, systems, and playbooks are still taking shape. Nice to Haves - Direct experience in a Product Operations role in a high growth environment - Hands-on experience integrating modern AI tooling into operational workflows. You understand where AI genuinely saves team hours versus where human discernment and precision are non-negotiable. - Comfort working with both executive stakeholders and front-line teams, and translating between strategic goals and day-to-day operational realities What We'll Offer Our comprehensive compensation package plays a big part in how we recognize you for the impact you have on our path to achieving our mission.
