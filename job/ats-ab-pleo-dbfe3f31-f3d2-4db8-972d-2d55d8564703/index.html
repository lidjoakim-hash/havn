ABOUT THE ROLE We're looking for a high-performing and dynamic Billing Operations Analyst to join our Finance Team at Pleo. This role is a unique opportunity to actively support the business to reach new milestones having in mind people growth and great team work. If you enjoy working at the intersection of Billing, Accounting and Automation – and want to help drive the future direction of Pleo – we’d love to hear from you. WHAT YOU’LL BE DOING As a Billing Operations Analyst, you will: - Review, approve & process credit note requests - Review contracts & account setup accuracy when the need arises. - Answer internal billing questions in a timely & professional manner, including escalations. - Guide GTM & other teams on billing best practices - Reissue invoices - Create & maintain Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) - Process refunds. - Drive internal projects from concept to completion. - Complete Month-End Close activities as required. What you bring You’ll thrive in this role if you: - Have 1+ year experience in a Finance/Accounting/Operations related field, with basic knowledge on Internal Accounting Standards (IFRS) - Have advanced excel skills including formulas sage and analysing data via pivot tables - SQL knowledge is a nice to have - Can work independently to drive workload and identify areas of opportunity - Have experience troubleshooting and/or answering complex customer queries in a timely and professional manner - Have previous experience with Billing systems and ERP systems (nice to have) - Are proactive and curious in your way of working Curious Who you’ll be working with and reporting to You’ll report to our Senior Billing & Revenue Manager and work closely with teams in Finance, Sales and Engineering . Our team of 3 is highly collaborative and dedicated to providing great support and improvement initiatives across the organisation.
