Head of Enterprise Integrations Do you want to shape how systems, applications and data connect across a global organization? Troax is strengthening its global IT organization and establishing Enterprise Integration as a core capability. We are now looking for a Head of Enterprise Integrations who wants to take a key role in this transformation and build the integration capability that will support Troax’s continued development. The position has a global scope and is primarily based in Jönköping, with the possibility of being based at another Troax office in Sweden. Linus, VP of IT, shares what it’s like to work at Troax "What makes this an exciting time to join Troax is that we are building and developing several important capabilities within IT. There is a real opportunity to come in, take ownership and help shape both the direction and how we work going forward. At the same time, you are close to the business and can see how your work contributes to a global industrial company with a clear purpose and products that create real value for customers. For me, a good working environment is built on clarity, trust and open dialogue. I want people to feel involved, understand the bigger picture and have the freedom to take responsibility and influence how we work. If you enjoy building something new, collaborating across an international organization and seeing tangible progress from your work, Troax offers a very exciting environment.” About the role You will be given the mandate to shape the integration landscape from the ground up, creating the structures, standards and technical foundation needed to connect applications, platforms, data and external partners across the organization. Your mission is to establish a scalable, secure and governed approach to integration across Troax. You will define the vision, strategy and roadmap while building the structures and standards needed for a modern integration landscape. An important part of the role will be to establish and own a common integration platform and create clear principles for how integrations are developed, monitored, documented and managed throughout their lifecycle. You will work across business and IT, collaborating closely with system and process owners, regional IT, external partners and specialists within areas such as Architecture, Data, AI & Automation and Business Applications. Key responsibilities: Define and drive the Enterprise Integration vision, strategy and roadmap. Establish and own a Group-wide integration platform. Establish principles, standards, reusable patterns and governance for integration development. Develop lifecycle management, documentation, monitoring, support and follow-up. Ensure scalable, secure, robust and traceable integrations. Coordinate internal capabilities and external integration and development partners. Contribute to the future sourcing model for integration capabilities. Support larger business transformation and system initiatives across the Group. Meet Elin, Software Developer at Troax. "What I really enjoy about working at Troax is the people and the atmosphere. There is a lot of knowledge and competence here, but at the same time the environment is very open, friendly and down-to-earth. For me, the connection between software development and the physical factory was something that stood out from the beginning. There is a real sense of potential here, with many opportunities to improve, develop and make a difference. And perhaps most importantly, it is simply a workplace where I genuinely enjoy spending time with my colleagues.” Your profile We are looking for a senior profile with experience in establishing or developing integration capabilities.
