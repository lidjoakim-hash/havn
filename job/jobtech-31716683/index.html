With a proven track record of successful partnerships with top companies, we have rapidly grown our presence in the USA, Europe, and Sweden as a dependable and trusted resource within the IT industry. Job Overview Role Summary Our client is seeking an experienced Structural Engineer for an assignment in the nuclear power industry. The role focuses on structural mechanics and advanced structural analysis, covering both static and dynamic behavior. The engineer will assess how metallic and steel structures respond to different loads and loading scenarios, verify compliance with applicable strength requirements, and contribute to the overall assurance of structural integrity. Key Responsibilities Perform static and dynamic structural analyses of safety-critical constructions. Evaluate structural responses to different load cases and loading scenarios. Assess the strength, resistance, and integrity of metallic and steel structures. Develop and apply analytical methods within structural mechanics and strength engineering. Verify that designs and structures satisfy relevant strength and structural integrity requirements. Use finite element analysis tools to model and assess structural behavior. Technical Stack & Requirements ANSYS Mechanical: Extensive experience performing structural and mechanical simulations. LS-DYNA: Experience with dynamic analysis and complex loading scenarios. Finite Element Analysis: Strong practical background in computational structural assessment. Structural mechanics: Advanced knowledge of strength, load response, and structural integrity. Materials and structures: Extensive experience analyzing metallic and steel constructions. Qualifications & Experience A Master of Science or Bachelor of Science degree in engineering with a relevant technical specialization is required. The successful consultant will have approximately 10–20 years of relevant professional experience, a substantial background in structural mechanics and structural analysis, and extensive experience with metallic and steel structures. Previous experience from the nuclear power industry is required.
