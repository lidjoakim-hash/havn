Business Controller – R&D and Product

HMS Industrial Networks AB · Halmstad, Sweden

💰 ca. 450 000–550 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 17.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

HMS stands for Hardware Meets Software and we work to make the world more connected, productive, and sustainable. Our industrial communication products enable millions of machines, such as robots, drives, and air-conditioning systems, to connect with software and information systems – a necessity for meeting future demands for energy efficiency and sustainability. What you’ll do  Would you like to influence the financial success of innovative technology? As Business Controller, you’ll work at the intersection of finance, R&D and product development, helping turn ideas and investments into profitable products. You will be a financial business partner to R&D and Product Offering, supporting the teams in making informed financial and business decisions. You will provide insight into development costs, investments and product profitability, helping the business understand the financial impact of decisions throughout the product lifecycle. Your responsibilities will include: Coordinate budgets and forecasts for R&D and Product Offering, analyzing spending and variances. Follow development projects and investments, including business cases, and financial outcomes after product launch. Analyze product profitability, standard costs and cost variances, identifying opportunities for cost improvements. Maintain pricing data, support annual pricing process and implement approved price changes. Develop reporting and use digital and AI tools to automate routine work and provide better business insight.  Based in Halmstad, you will join INT Business Controlling and report to VP Business Controlling. You will be part of an experienced finance team, working closely with colleagues across the business.  About you  You are analytical, structured and curious about how the business works. You enjoy working closely with stakeholders and turning financial data into clear insights and decisions. We believe you have:  Experience in business controlling, financial analysis or a similar role. A degree in finance, accounting, business administration or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. Strong Excel skills and good communication skills in English.  Experience from a manufacturing environment, as well as familiarity with product or project controlling, cost accounting, Power BI or ERP systems, is an advantage. You do not need experience in every area of the role, we value curiosity, financial judgement and a collaborative approach.  About us  At HMS, we put Heart, Mind & Soul into everything we do. These core values not only make for a fitting abbreviation (HMS), but also describe how we work together and create success. We are an innovative growth company headquartered in Halmstad, Sweden, with approximately 1,100 employees in more than 20 countries. Through our global presence, we combine the opportunities of an international organisation with the close-knit and entrepreneurial spirit that has characterised HMS since the beginning. HMS is an equal opportunity employer. We celebrate diversity and are committed to creating an environment where everyone feels included, respected, and empowered. Visit hms-networks.com and learn more about life at HMS.
