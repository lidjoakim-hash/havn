Social Media Lead

Lovable · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 510 000–640 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)HeltidDirekte fra arbeidsgiverFrist 2.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

TL;DR - We're looking for someone to own how Lovable shows up on social. You'll manage a team of ICs while still spending part of your time doing creative work (copywriting, storyboarding, directing). You must be based in Stockholm or be ready to relocate. You'll turn releases, features, and behind-the-scenes product work into posts people actually want to engage with. And you’ll set the standard your team works to, creating content for an audience of over 1 million engaged users and community members. Why Lovable? Lovable lets anyone and everyone build software with any language. From solopreneurs to Fortune 100 teams, millions of people use Lovable to transform raw ideas into real products - fast. We are at the forefront of a foundational shift in software creation, which means you have an unprecedented opportunity to change the way the digital world works. Lovable-built applications and websites are visited hundreds of millions of times a month, and our enterprise footprint is compounding fast. And we're just getting started. We're a small, talent-dense team building a generation-defining company from Stockholm. We value extreme ownership, high velocity, and low-ego collaboration. We seek out people who care deeply, ship fast, and are eager to make a dent in the world. What we’re looking for - 12+ years of experience in social media with at least 2 years of managing a team - Strategic thinking: You know what content belongs on each channel, and which doesn’t, and you can confidently make that case to stakeholders - Product marketing aptitude: You understand how to turn a changelog or a feature launch into a story that matters to people - Technical communication skills: You can take a powerful feature and write about it in ways that resonate with both developer and general audiences - Visual chops: You can create your own product visuals — screen recordings, GIFs, static graphics — or draft a clear brief for Creative teams to work from - Cross-functional range: You're comfortable working directly with PMM, Product, and Creative, and know how to get what you need from each - Platform-native instincts: You live on X and LinkedIn and understand deeply what each platform actually rewards - A portfolio of product social content you've shipped that you're proud of - Experience using Lovable (genuinely — it'll show) - You’re based in Stockholm — or ready to relocate What you'll do Lead the team - Manage and grow Lovable’s social team - Establish an opinionated social media channel strategy and activate the team to deliver against it - Set the bar for what "good" looks like: give feedback, review work, and help reports do their best work - Split and prioritize the team's workload across launches, always-on content, and community engagement - Build a cadence and format library for product content, so every release has a clear path to social, and your team has a system to work from - Report on and be accountable for team’s metrics Be hands-on - Own social media content related to Lovable's product: launches, features, product education, and the work behind them - Write copy that turns technical concepts into posts a broad audience will follow and want to share - Work with our Creative team on launch videos and polished static assets - Partner with Product Marketing and Product to get early visibility into what's shipping and shape how it's announced - Deepen Lovable's product usage and credibility among both a developer and general audience - Track what resonates and feed those learnings back into how you and your team plan future launches About your application Please submit your application in English. It’s our company language, so you’ll be speaking lots of it if you join. We treat all candidates equally - if you’re interested, please apply through our careers portal.
