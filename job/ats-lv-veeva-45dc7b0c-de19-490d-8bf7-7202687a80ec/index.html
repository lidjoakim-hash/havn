The Role As the Account Partner for Veeva Development Cloud, you will be part of the foundational team representing the Veeva Development Cloud for European Contract Research Organisations (CROs). The Account Partner will be responsible for generating opportunities, winning business, and managing strategic relationships with our CRO customers and prospects. A strong understanding of the life sciences industry and the role of CROs within it, enterprise software sales cycle, and a passion for making market changes with innovative technology are ideal for this role. Candidates must have 3+ years of combined experience across SaaS sales, account management, and/or consulting experience focused on life sciences companies. The candidate must also have a proven track record of account management, servicing CROs, or working for CROs.
