Electronics Engineer

Join ALTEN in Västerås and drive innovation in advanced electronic solutions! As an Electronics Engineer, you will play a key role in designing, developing, and validating cutting-edge hardware solutions. This includes circuit design, PCB layout, simulations, and testing to ensure high performance, reliability, and compliance with industry standards. You will collaborate with cross-functional teams to deliver innovative electronic products. WHAT DO WE OFFER?  As a consultant at ALTEN, you'll have the opportunity to work on challenging and exciting projects with some of our key clients, with:   Technical challenges and development opportunities.  An inspiring community and the chance to contribute to a sustainable future.  Supportive and coaching leadership.  The possibility to work across various industries where our clients operate.  A personal training budget for internal or external courses.  ABOUT THIS ROLE As an Electronics Engineer, your main tasks will include: Designing and developing analog and digital electronic solutions. Creating circuit diagrams and defining PCB layout requirements. Performing simulations, verification, and validation of hardware. Conducting laboratory tests and troubleshooting on prototypes and products. Ensuring EMC-compliant design and adherence to relevant standards. Collaborating with suppliers, production, and development teams for product improvements. Technologies & Tools: Hardware Design: Analog/digital circuits, PCB layout, PCBA, power electronics, MOSFETs, FPGA, Zynq/UltraScale, FPGA redesign/EOL replacement. Simulation & Testing: EMC analysis, climate/vibration testing, functional safety. Tools: Altium Designer, Xilinx/Vivado, ModelSim, VHDL. YOUR PROFILE We are looking for someone who is analytical and solution-oriented, with a structured and quality-conscious approach. You are independent yet a strong team player, driven by curiosity and technical challenges. Strong communication skills and experience working in international environments are essential. Qualifications: Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Electronics, Electrical Engineering, Embedded Systems, or equivalent. Experience in hardware development for electronic products. Strong knowledge of analog and digital electronics. Experience in PCB design, schematic drawing, and PCB design processes. Experience in verification, testing, and troubleshooting of electronics. Familiarity with laboratory equipment such as oscilloscopes, power supplies, signal generators, and analysis tools. Experience with simulation tools for electronics is a plus. Knowledge of EMC, product requirements, and certification processes is a plus. Experience with industrial products, power systems, automation, or embedded systems is a plus. Fluent in English. Knowledge of Swedish is beneficial. ABOUT ALTEN  ALTEN is one of Europe’s largest technology and IT consulting companies, with over 57,000 employees across approximately 30 countries. Our engineers carry out complex and highly technical projects throughout the entire product development chain for leading companies in several industries including Automotive, Defense & Security, Energy, Industrial Equipment, Public Sector, and Life Sciences.  In Sweden, we are approximately 1,300 dedicated employees spread across 9 offices in cities ranging from Lund in the south to Skellefteå in the north.   For the fifth year in a row, ALTEN has been named one of Sweden’s most attractive employers by Karriärföretagen for five consecutive years — an award for companies offering unique career and development opportunities.  Welcome to read more about us at alten.se
