Figma is growing our team of passionate creatives and builders on a mission to make design accessible to all. Figma’s platform helps teams bring ideas to life—whether you're brainstorming, creating a prototype, translating designs into code, or iterating with AI. We are looking for a Senior Account Executive, UK Government who will play a critical role in building and scaling Figma’s presence within Government organisations. This is a specialist position focused on developing a new and strategic segment, requiring a highly experienced seller with deep domain expertise, strong executive relationships, and the ability to operate in an early-stage market. This role currently sits within the Enterprise segment and represents a unique opportunity to establish and grow Figma’s Government business from an early foundation. What you’ll do at Figma: • Own and build Figma’s Government (Public Sector) pipeline and book of business, focused on establishing initial traction and long-term growth • Create and execute a GTM strategy for Government accounts, identifying priority agencies and opportunities • Build and nurture relationships with senior stakeholders and decision-makers across Government organisations • Apply discovery-led, value-based selling to align Figma’s platform with complex public sector needs and procurement processes • Develop and execute strategic account plans, driving multi-threaded engagement across departments and functions • Navigate long, complex sales cycles, including compliance, procurement, and regulatory considerations • Partner closely with cross-functional teams to position Figma effectively, drive deal progression and ensure successful adoption and expansion • Lay the foundation for a scalable Government business, contributing to future team build-out as the segment grows We’d love to hear from you if you have: • Significant experience selling into Government / Public Sector accounts • A proven track record of building new business in greenfield or early-stage markets • Experience managing complex enterprise sales cycles (6+ months) with multiple stakeholders • Strong ability to engage and influence executive-level buyers • Consistent performance in pipeline generation and net new business development While it’s not required, it’s an added plus if you also have: • Experience navigating public sector procurement frameworks and compliance processes • Background selling technical or platform-based solutions • Ability to operate independently in a foundational, first-hire environment Demonstrated success in scaling new segments or markets over time At Figma, one of our values is Grow as you go.
