Shield AI is a venture-backed defense-tech company with the mission of protecting service members and civilians with intelligent systems. Its products include Hivemind autonomy software, V-BAT and X-BAT aircraft, and Aechelon simulation and synthetic reality technologies. With offices and facilities across the U.S., Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific, Shield AI’s technology actively supports operations worldwide. For more information, visit www.shield.ai. Follow Shield AI on LinkedIn, X, Instagram, and YouTube.  What you'll do: • Oversee the day-to-day accounting and bookkeeping activities performed by the third-party accounting service provider, ensuring accuracy, completeness, and adherence to established deadlines. • Serve as the primary point of contact between Shield AI’s US Accounting team, local business stakeholders, and the external accounting provider. • Monitor service provider deliverables, identify gaps or delays, and drive timely resolution of accounting issues. • Ensure transactions are properly supported, classified, approved, and recorded in accordance with company policies and applicable accounting requirements. • Maintain organized and complete accounting records and supporting documentation. • Coordinate local month-end and year-end close activities with the third-party accounting provider and the US Accounting team. • Support reconciliation of local statutory financial records to corporate reporting requirements and identify any necessary adjustments. • Ensure compliance with applicable Ukrainian accounting standards, statutory reporting requirements, and local tax regulations. • Monitor changes in Ukrainian accounting, tax, payroll, and regulatory requirements and communicate potential impacts to the US Accounting team. • Implement and maintain accounting procedures and controls consistent with Shield AI’s global accounting policies and internal control framework. • Assist with documenting local accounting processes and controls in preparation for the company’s continued growth and global ERP implementation. Required qualifications: • Bachelor’s degree in Accounting (preferred), Finance, or a related field. • 6 to 10 years of progressive accounting experience, including experience operating in a public company environment • Strong understanding of U.S. GAAP and experience working within a robust internal control environment; familiarity with SOX compliance is a plus. • Excellent time management skills with the ability to independently manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment. • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and decision-making skills. • High level of initiative and self-motivation, with demonstrated communication and collaboration abilities. • Proven experience coordinating with cross-functional teams and external stakeholders. • Ability to manage projects from inception to completion while incorporating feedback from multiple sources. • Ability to multi-task, prioritize competing demands, and meet deadlines consistently. • High proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite.   Our international teammates receive a comprehensive total rewards package aligned to your country office location.
