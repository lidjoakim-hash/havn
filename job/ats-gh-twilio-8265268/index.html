About the job We are seeking a highly skilled Payroll Tax and Compliance Analyst to oversee payroll employment taxes, regulatory adherence, and mobility compliance across APJ and EMEA regions. As a fast-growing tech company, our workforce is highly mobile. You will serve as the subject matter expert on complex tax laws, social security frameworks, and multi-country remote worker regulations. Reporting directly to the Director, Global Payroll, you will suggest risk-mitigation strategies and ensure seamless compliance for a dynamic, global team. Responsibilities In this role, you’ll: • Tax Oversight: Manage all payroll tax compliance, filings, and reconciliations across the APJ and EMEA regions, covering Australia, Singapore, India, Hong Kong, Japan, Sweden, Estonia, Spain, Ireland, UK, Germany, Belgium, France, Poland. • Mobility Tracking: Monitor employee movement across countries and international borders to ensure accurate tax withholding. • Nexus Assessment: Evaluate corporate tax footprint implications resulting from remote worker locations. • Social Security: Ensure correct application of local social security taxes and international totalization agreements. • Regulatory Research: Track changing tax laws, statutory rates, and local compliance requirements in all active jurisdictions. • Agency Management: Act as the primary point of contact for tax authorities, external auditors, and local payroll partners. • Year-End Reporting: Lead year-end tax reporting processes, for all countries in APJ & EMEA and follow local country statutory filings. • Process Optimization: Build scalable internal workflows to automate tax adjustments for mobile employees.
