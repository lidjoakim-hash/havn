This position offers the opportunity to join the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA) Doctoral Network UNCHANGE, collaborate with academic, museums, and industry partners across Europe, and develop expertise within a multidisciplinary research project on cultural heritage science. The expected start date is 1 August 2027, and the Head of unit will be the immediate supervisor for employment-related matters. About the project About the UNCHANGE project   UNCHANGE (UNderstanding change in Cultural HeritAge: the Next Generation of Experts) is a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA) Doctoral Network that brings together leading European universities, research institutions, and industry partners to train the next generation of heritage scientists. The project focuses on understanding, documenting, and monitoring change in cultural heritage using advanced technologies, including imaging, materials analysis, artificial intelligence, and data science. Through an interdisciplinary and international research environment, UNCHANGE equips doctoral candidates with the skills needed to address current and future challenges in cultural heritage conservation and preservation. About the specific Doctoral project Objectives: (1) Investigate novel object representations, such as Gaussians and Ray Fields, in measuring change for CH objects. (2) Investigate novel object representations in capturing and visualizing CH objects. The above objectives reflect current trends in the development of new object representations that are ‘AI-Friendly’, given the pervasiveness of AI methods in all types of processing. Expected results: Determination of the effect of machine learning-friendly object representations in capturing, visualizing and measuring change on CH objects. Particularly, secondments of a few months’ duration are foreseen to NTNU Museum (Trondheim) and Peggy Guggenheim Collection, Venezia. Be prepared for changes to your work duties after employment.
