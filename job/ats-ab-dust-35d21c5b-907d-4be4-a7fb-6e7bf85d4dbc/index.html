SUMMARY We're looking for a Revenue AI Operations builder to help Dust scale toward $100M in ARR. You'll work closely with Sales, CX, and Finance to keep our CRM and revenue processes running smoothly, improve performance visibility, and redesign how work gets done across the customer lifecycle. You'll also build scalable GTM enablement with Dust agents and skills, helping our fast-growing team ramp quickly and stay up to date. This is a high-scope, high-leverage role on a small team, and your work will directly shape how fast we grow. WHAT YOU'LL DO STRUCTURE CRM OPERATIONS - Support GTM teams day to day in resolving operational issues and requests. - Own daily HubSpot administration, including pipelines, properties, workflows, and automations, responding to incoming needs while proactively flagging improvements. - Keep CRM data clean and trusted through deduplication, territory management, and clear attribution logic. PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT - Audit the current Sales funnel, review our KPIs, and implement the tracking and reporting needed to make performance visible. - Support the preparation of weekly, monthly, and quarterly pipeline, forecast, and performance reviews. LAUNCH STRUCTURAL PROCESS REDESIGN - Lead process redesign projects across Pre-sales, including lead attribution, new business, expansion and renewal sales cycles, and pilot tracking. - Improve CX processes such as onboarding and account management. - Partner with Finance on Deal Desk and Quote to Cash processes. SCALE GTM REVENUE ENABLEMENT - Build AI-powered GTM content with Dust agents and skills, creating and maintaining the assets our teams sell with, including sales playbooks, pitch and demo materials, battle cards, and customer stories. Draw on call recordings, product updates, and market signals to keep them relevant. - Design and run onboarding for new GTM hires as the team triples. - Train the team continuously on product, marketing, and process changes, using Dust to scale enablement. BUILD WITH DUST - Be one of Dust's most demanding internal users: put the product to work on real GTM challenges and push beyond today's standard use cases. - Partner directly with Product and Engineering, bringing them concrete feedback from building and using Dust at scale. - Turn what you learn internally into reusable examples that help customers see what's possible with Dust. REQUIREMENTS Every candidate and employee's success is measured against the same 3 dimensions. Aptitude, Attitude and Agency. APTITUDE - 1-2 years in revenue/sales operations, consulting, VC, or a comparable analytical, high-ownership role - Strong CRM fluency, ideally HubSpot, plus automation tools (Zapier, Make, n8n) - Proven project
