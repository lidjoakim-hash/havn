Our mission at ŌURA is to empower every person to own their inner potential. Our award-winning products help our global community gain a deeper knowledge of their readiness, activity, and sleep quality by using their Oura Ring and its connected app. We've helped millions of people understand and improve their health by providing daily insights and practical steps to inspire healthy lifestyles. Empowering the world starts with living our values and empowering our team. As a quickly growing company focused on helping people live healthier and happier lives, we ensure that our team members have what they need to do their best work — both in and out of the office. We are looking for a Tech Lead Manager to run the HealthOS Platform squad day to day. Oura is evolving into a personal health platform. Health Intelligence builds the shared foundation the rest of that platform stands on: the services, mobile surfaces and AI infrastructure behind Oura Advisor, our AI companion, and the building blocks other teams use to bring their own domains into it. You will split your time between hands-on engineering and leading the squad: setting technical direction, turning ambiguous goals into plans the team can deliver, and keeping several streams of work moving at once. People management is open: you can start as a tech lead and grow into line management if that's the path you want. You can come from iOS, Android or cloud backend. What matters more is that you have already led a team or a technical area through complex projects, all the way to launch and the maintenance that follows. We also want someone who builds with AI coding agents every day and wants to boost ways of working. Success here is measured in what members get and how reliably we ship it, not in tickets closed. This is a hybrid role based in Finland, with a preference for candidates who can regularly collaborate with our teams in Helsinki or Oulu.
