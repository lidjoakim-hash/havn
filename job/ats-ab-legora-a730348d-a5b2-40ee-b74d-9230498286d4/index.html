We work alongside the world’s best legal teams, who expect excellence, precision, and speed, and we hold ourselves to the same bar. Our AI-native workspace lets legal professionals move faster, think more clearly, and operate with sharper precision. By analysing thousands of documents in minutes and powering end-to-end workflows, we cut through complexity, teams can focus on what matters: judgment, strategy, and outcomes. 2,100+ customers across 80+ countries trust us, including Cleary Gottlieb, Goodwin, Linklaters, White & Case, Dentons, and Barclays. We’ve scaled to $200M+ in ARR, with teams across Europe, North America and APAC, and continue to expand through acquisitions including Qura, Walter AI, Graceview, Cadastral, and Wexler. What You Will Be Doing - Own team revenue performance and quota attainment, driving accountability, consistent execution, and predictable growth. - Coach and develop team members across the entire customer lifecycle, providing clear, actionable feedback that improves performance and accelerates results. - Lead pipeline reviews, deal inspections, and forecasting processes to improve decision-making, forecast accuracy, and overall GTM effectiveness. - Identify performance gaps and conversion challenges early, implementing targeted actions to improve pipeline health, deal velocity, and win rates. - Establish and maintain high standards for GTM execution, ensuring consistent adoption of messaging, methodologies, processes, and best practices. - Leverage data, analytics, and AI-driven insights to optimise team productivity, strengthen deal strategy, and improve commercial outcomes. - Partner cross-functionally with Marketing, Product, Customer Success, and Revenue Operations to enhance funnel performance, refine GTM strategy, and continuously improve playbooks and ways of working. What Will Help You Thrive - 8+ years of experience in B2B SaaS within sales, business development, customer success, or other customer-facing GTM functions, with experience leading or influencing team performance. - A strong track record in pipeline management, forecasting, deal strategy, and driving revenue growth in fast-paced, high-growth environments. - Proven ability to coach, develop, and elevate performance through direct, constructive, and actionable feedback. - Strong analytical skills with the ability to interpret data, identify root causes, and translate insights into measurable actions and results. - Experience leveraging AI and modern GTM technologies to improve productivity, forecasting accuracy, pipeline health, and team effectiveness. - Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to align cross-functional teams and turn strategy into execution. - A hands-on, high-ownership mindset with a bias for action, adaptability, and a commitment to continuous improvement and learning. - Communicate confidently in both English and German, with English serving as the primary company language and German required for local market engagement and relationship management.
