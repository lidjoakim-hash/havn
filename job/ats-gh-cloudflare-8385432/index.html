About Us At Cloudflare, we are on a mission to help build a better Internet. Today the company runs one of the world’s largest networks that powers millions of websites and other Internet properties for customers ranging from individual bloggers to SMBs to Fortune 500 companies. Cloudflare protects and accelerates any Internet application online without adding hardware, installing software, or changing a line of code. Internet properties powered by Cloudflare all have web traffic routed through its intelligent global network, which gets smarter with every request. As a result, they see significant improvement in performance and a decrease in spam and other attacks. Cloudflare was named to Entrepreneur Magazine’s Top Company Cultures list and ranked among the World’s Most Innovative Companies by Fast Company. At Cloudflare, we’re not looking for people who wait for a polished roadmap; we’re looking for the builders who see the cracks in the Internet that everyone else has simply learned to live with. We value candidates who have the instinct to spot a "normalized" problem and the AI-native curiosity to create a solution using the latest tools. Our culture is built on iteration, leveraging AI to ship faster today to make it better tomorrow, while ensuring that every improvement, no matter how small, is shared across the team to lift everyone up. If you’re the type of person who values curiosity over bureaucracy, and that AI is a partner in solving tough problems to keep the Internet moving forward, you’ll fit right in. Available Locations Spain About the Role We are seeking an experienced and commercially driven Senior Sales Manager to lead and scale our Digital Natives business in Spain. You will lead a team of Account Executives with a mandate weighted toward new business acquisition, balancing aggressive market expansion with the continued success and growth of current customers. You will combine hands-on coaching with strategic market leadership, helping your team build pipeline, execute complex sales cycles, forecast accurately, and articulate Cloudflare’s value to founders, executives, and technical audiences. You will develop and execute our go-to-market strategy for Spain’s Digital Natives segment, recruit and develop exceptional talent, and strengthen Cloudflare’s presence across the Spanish startup and scale-up ecosystem. Your target customers include SaaS platforms, fintech companies, AI-native scale-ups, developer-focused businesses, and other innovative companies born in the cloud. You and your team will help customers understand and prepare for the agentic cloud transition, as AI-native applications, agents, and autonomous workloads change their infrastructure, security, performance, and commercial requirements. In line with Cloudflare’s AI-native culture, you will be expected to use AI actively in your daily workflow and lead its adoption across your team. This includes applying AI to customer and market research, account and territory planning, meeting preparation, pipeline management, and follow-up—using it as a genuine productivity multiplier, not an occasional aid. Responsibilities • Revenue growth: Meet or exceed assigned quarterly and annual team targets, leading the business’s growth through disciplined execution, market coverage, and pipeline creation. • New business: Own a team portfolio weighted heavily toward new business acquisition.. Drive new-logo acquisition while ensuring existing customers realize value, increase adoption, and expand their use of Cloudflare. • Spain go-to-market strategy: Develop and execute a Spain-specific Digital Natives strategy, including market segmentation, territory design, coverage models, account prioritization, and ecosystem engagement. • Team leadership and coaching: Manage, coach, and develop a team of quota-carrying Account Executives, creating a high-performance culture grounded in accountability, urgency, curiosity, inclusion, and customer value. •
