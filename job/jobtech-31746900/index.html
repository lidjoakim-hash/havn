Junior Business System Integrations Engineer

Academic Work Sweden AB · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 510 000–620 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 16.2.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Ready to kick-start your career at the intersection of systems engineering, AI, and commercial business flows? On behalf of our client, a fast-growing, globally active Swedish medtech company, Academic Work is looking for a newly graduated engineer to automate and develop business-critical systems. With close mentorship from senior architects, you will quickly grow into a key role in an international environment based in central Stockholm. Apply today—we review applications on an ongoing basis! About the role Our client is a leading global MedTech company developing clinically researched, objective testing software for ADHD evaluation. Used by thousands of clinicians worldwide, their technology helps healthcare providers make faster, more accurate diagnoses and improve patient outcomes. As a Junior Business System Integrations Engineer, you will join a tight-knit architecture team consisting of four employees. You will receive dedicated coaching and daily support from senior architects. Your main focus will be streamlining and automating internal processes through scripting, API integrations, and system configuration rather than traditional web or app development. Acting as a bridge between technology and business, you will collaborate closely with internal stakeholders in Finance and Commercial, as well as maintain ongoing dialogue with external system vendors. The role is based at our client’s office near Humlegården in Stockholm, following a hybrid work model where you are expected to be on-site at least three days a week. This is initially a 6-month consulting assignment through Academic Work, with great opportunities for long-term employment with our client thereafter You are offered A unique opportunity to build valuable experience within the regulated and expanding global medtech industry. Close mentorship and continuous support from senior architects in a welcoming, low-prestige environment. Hands-on experience working at the forefront of modern AI tools for code generation and system automation. An international workplace collaborating across global markets, including the UK and the US. Work tasks This role focuses on backend development, business system integrations, and process automation rather than frontend or app development. You will work closely with internal teams to ensure data flows securely and efficiently across platforms. Take technical ownership of the administration, configuration, and continuous improvement of NetSuite and Salesforce. Build and maintain integrations between business systems, the data lake, and the AWS platform using REST APIs (JSON payloads) and AWS Lambdas. Automate manual workflows and commercial processes using Python and TypeScript scripts as well as low-code tools. Leverage modern AI coding assistants to accelerate development, focusing on reviewing, understanding, and ensuring the quality of generated code. Write and optimize database queries in SQL, SuiteQL, and SOQL to extract, validate, and reconcile business data. Collaborate closely with Finance and Commercial teams to translate business requirements (from quote-to-order and contracts to usage and billing) into concrete technical solutions. Ensure high data integrity, accurate numerical reconciliation, and strict compliance with data privacy and information security standards. Coordinate and communicate with external system suppliers regarding ongoing integrations and system updates. We are looking for A recently completed academic degree (M.Sc. or B.Sc.) in Computer Science, Information Systems, Industrial Engineering and Management, or a related technical field. A solid foundation in programming (such as Python, Java, or TypeScript) and the ability to quickly grasp system architecture and codebases. A basic understanding of commercial and financial flows, including quote-to-order, contracts, invoicing, and usage-based billing. Fluency in English, both written and spoken, as English is the corporate language
