Salary Range: PLN 260,000 - 352,000 PLN Subject to alignment to the responsibilities and duties of the role About the job Build the simulator that helps engineers understand and optimise collective communication at AI scale. As a Senior Software Engineer in the Collectives Simulator team, you will design and develop a large-scale simulator for new AI hardware. Your work will help engineers analyse network parameters and develop communication algorithms for high bandwidth and low latency. You will lead development across design, implementation, testing and documentation. You will also work with other engineering teams to develop features, troubleshoot complex issues and integrate new hardware with the wider AI ecosystem. The role combines large-scale software engineering with custom hardware and communication systems. You will shape technical direction, mentor junior engineers and help build the software that enables efficient communication at very high scale. The team and culture We value face-to-face interaction and make time to meet in the office frequently. The team works in two-week sprints, with three stand-ups each week, fortnightly sprint planning and a weekly team meeting. Work is planned with input from the Technical Product Manager, covering both longer-term priorities and upcoming sprints. Engineers typically work in small groups on larger tasks or components, coordinating the work among themselves. The team has a high level of technical expertise, with opportunities to attend thematic conferences and team outings roughly once a quarter. What we're looking for • Extensive professional software development experience using C++ • Strong Python and C programming skills • Experience developing efficient concurrent applications using multithreading and inter-process communication • Strong debugging and problem-solving skills for complex software systems • Experience with unit testing frameworks such as Boost.Test or Google Test • Proficiency with software build systems such as CMake, Make or Ninja While we have outlined a set of requirements, we value transferable skills and diverse experiences. If you meet most of our essential criteria, we encourage you to submit an application and showcase how your background makes you a strong candidate.
