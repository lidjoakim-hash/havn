So, as we shape a more resourceful, less wasteful world, we build our careers too. About the job Ready to take on challenging mechanical engineering work with a clear purpose? With us you will contribute to global priorities such as energy efficiency, clean water, and Alfa Laval's net-zero 2030 ambition. Let's shape a better tomorrow together! We design and supply process solutions within various industries, mainly water treatment. As a Mechanical Engineer and FEM/FEA specialist, you will design solutions in 3D with detailed engineering combined with standard components. Our designs contain pipes, pumps, decanters, evaporators, membranes, heat exchangers etc. Your work involves verification and optimization of mechanical and process equipment, by identifying critical stress concentrations, deformations, and failure modes etc. In this role, you will perform simulations as well as traditional hand calculations to ensure our products meet functional, safety, and regulatory requirements. During project execution you will be working in global project teams with Project Managers, Process Specialists, Automation, Sourcing, and Workshops. Key tasks and responsibilities: Design steel structures, skid solutions and custom designs in Autodesk Inventor FEM/FEA analysis on mechanical designs using ANSYS/Nastran including: Static structural, Buckling, Hydrostatic and internal pressure, Vibrations and fatigue, Thermal and Pipe support. Hand calculations Prepare clear technical reports and design recommendations Review boundary conditions and results from simulations made by others Optimize calculation methods, templates, engineering standards, and best practices DFM in collaboration with workshop and FAT/SAT Review 3D designs and 2D drawings Ensure compliance with Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 and CE marking Apply relevant standards like ASCE/SEI 7-22 and EN1993-1-1 Planned travels, mostly within Europe, for inspections and commissioning is part of the job - approximately 20 - 25 days per year. You will be part of an innovative mechanical team of ten who are continuously implementing smarter ways of working. You report to Jesper Bergstrøm, a people-oriented leader who is passionate about his team thriving in their roles and look forward to going to work. At Alfa Laval, we embrace a hybrid work model, allowing you to work from home when it aligns with your tasks. Who are you You are a results-driven, high-energy professional who maintains focus and a positive attitude while pursuing ambitious goals. You take initiative, make calculated decisions with confidence, and consistently turn plans into action. You build strong networks across and beyond the organization, using your relational skills to influence, collaborate, and create impact. Proactive, timely, and quality-minded, you work effectively with others and help drive collective success. What you know We are looking for you with solid skills and experience in FEM/FEA analysis with skid- and steel structures solutions. The role involves a lot of collaboration and while you can independently, we believe you enjoy collaborating across disciplines and are able to communicate complex technical topics clearly.
