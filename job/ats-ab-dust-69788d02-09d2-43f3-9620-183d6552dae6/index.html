THE ROLE This is Dust's first PM hire. You'll work closely with the product team, turning raw GTM signal, company priorities, and user feedback into well-formed problems the team can act on. WHAT YOU WILL CHANGE: - A lot of product signal doesn't reach the team in an actionable shape today. You'll create and run the process that connects signal to shaping to prioritization. - Implementing company strategies across functions requires ownership and orchestration. You'll translate company priorities into an intelligible, trackable set of product, marketing, and operations initiatives. - You'll enrich prioritized projects with distilled business and user context and market perspective so that the people building them make better decisions. Our designers and engineers stay close to product decisions from conception to delivery. You're here to make their context richer and their priorities sharper. You enable distributed ownership, you don't replace it. WHAT YOU'LL DO - Qualify and synthesize feedback: Sit at the intersection of GTM, users, and product. Turn high-volume, unstructured input into a small set of well-framed problems worth solving. - Shape initiatives: Work with engineers and designers to translate validated problems into initiatives ready for Stack Rank — with clear objectives, KPIs, and scope. - Drive outcome tracking: Own reporting on whether what shipped actually moved the needle. Feed that back into the next round of decisions. - Improve internal coordination: Connect Marketing, Comms, GTM, and Product — reduce the friction that slows things down when multiple functions need to move together. - Own outcomes, not outputs: For cross-functional initiatives, you'll own the goal — objectives, KPIs, orchestration, reporting. - Own artefacts in the Dust platform (agents, skills, data, etc..) to continuously rewire and augment the above for yourself and the team WHAT YOU CAN EXPECT TEAM Dust is a growing team of about a 100 people. Most of the product and engineering team is based in Paris, with close collaboration across our teams in New York, San Francisco, and London. THE INTERVIEW PROCESS The process is designed to understand how you think, collaborate, and operate in an ambiguous, technical environment: - Recruiter conversation: motivation, background, logistics, and fit for an early-stage environment. - Hiring DRI conversation: discussion with a senior member of the product team. - Product exercise: collaboration exercise based on a real company priority. - Founder conversation: discussion with one of our founders on product judgment, attitude, and ability to operate with distributed authority. - Onsite: working sessions with members of Engineering, Design, and GTM.
