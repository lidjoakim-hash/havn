PRODUCTION TEAM LEAD, SATELLITE ELECTRONICS SUBSYSTEMS Role highlights - Title: Production Team Lead, Satellite Subsystems (Electronics, Battery and Software Integration) - Department: Global Manufacturing Organisation - Location: Espoo, Finland - Reports to: Head of Subassemblies - Employment type: Permanent - Workplace model: On-site - Employment is subject to applicable security screening (including SUPO, where required) LEAD TODAY'S PRODUCTION. ENGINEER TOMORROW'S SYSTEMS. Before launch, ICEYE satellites rely on complex electronics, battery packs, and flight software. Today, these critical workflows depend heavily on manual assembly and verification. To support our rapid growth, we are seeking a Production Team Lead to modernize, scale, and own this end-to-end area, enabling exponential output while ensuring quality, cost efficiency, and operational simplicity. Leading teams across electronics, batteries, software, and testing, you will utilize dedicated capital resources to build an automated, data-driven production environment. WHAT YOU WILL OWN - Daily Operations: Lead manufacturing across electronics assembly, battery fabrication, software loading, integration, and functional testing while upholding safety, quality, cost, and throughput standards. - Production Architecture: Standardize workflows, takt times, line layouts, and kitting to deliver predictable, repeatable cycle times. - New Product Introduction (NPI): Scale new subsystem designs and next-gen satellite platforms from R&D to high-rate production, ensuring tooling, test, and workforce readiness. - Automation & Digital Manufacturing: Champion automated functional testing, scripted software deployment, unit-level data logging, and full electronic revision traceability. - Hardware-Software Integration: Optimize workflows spanning assembly, functional testing, firmware flashing, and systemic validation to ensure flawless alignment between software releases and hardware revisions. - DFM/DFT & Continuous Improvement: Participate in design reviews, translating floor-level yield and test metrics into continuous design and manufacturing enhancements. - Risk & Quality Controls: Embed best practices for battery handling, state-of-charge management, ESD protection, FOD control, and cleanroom disciplines directly into daily operations. - Leadership & Development: Recruit, train, certify (J-STD-001, IPC-A-610, ESD), and mentor a team of 10–15 technicians using visual management and Lean frameworks. WHAT YOU BRING Required Qualifications - 5+ years of assembly and testing experience in electronics, battery systems, avionics, rf within high-reliability sectors (aerospace, defense, EV, or robotics). - Proven leadership experience managing and coaching 10–15 technicians in fast-paced production environments. - Strong practical electronics expertise: interpreting schematics/drawings, operating diagnostic equipment, and isolating root causes. - Software integration experience: firmware flashing, version control, configuration management, automated log parsing, and test script execution (e.g., Python). - Foundational knowledge of battery safety and handling protocols, including state-of-charge management. - Applied mastery of Lean manufacturing principles (standardized work, takt time, root-cause analysis) with a track record of driving operational improvements. - Strong analytical problem-solving skills and professional English communication abilities. Technical Domains Managed You will lead and collaborate with experts across PCB design, embedded software, battery electrochemistry, RF testing, and environmental testing. Specialized background in any of these areas is a plus.
