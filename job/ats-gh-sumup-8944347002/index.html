SumUp is already live in 38 markets and still growing. Our Global Expansion Team takes SumUp's payment products into new countries, with Mexico and Canada among our most recent launches and more markets like New Zealand and Argentina on the way. As a QA Engineer in our Berlin payments team, you'll build the automated and manual tests that decide when a new card reader can go live in a market, across frontend, backend and mobile flows, and you'll also help complete L3 certification (the final round of testing that acquiring banks and card networks require). It's hands-on engineering work with real launch impact, because your tests help new markets go live and recently launched ones stay stable, so small businesses there can take card payments with confidence. What you'll do • Build and maintain frontend and backend test automation, with mobile where relevant, and add targeted regression tests to continuous integration (CI) workflows so market specific checks run consistently after code changes • Test SumUp products across markets, with a mix of automated and manual testing, functional, integration and regression checks, plus edge cases and recovery scenarios. • Spot ways to improve how we test, explain why they matter and make a convincing case for the changes with the biggest payoff • Trace failures through complex payment flows using logs and transaction traces, then raise clear, prioritised defects and work with engineers and external partners until they're fixed • Own the testing of end-to-end communication between a payment terminal, software applications, and an acquiring network. with acquiring banks and card networks, and keep the team informed on certification status, quality risks and release readiness so launch decisions rest on evidence • Work closely in collaboration with engineers, product and market-launch teams, as well as acquirers, card schemes and certification labs. What you'll bring • Programming or scripting skill to build and maintain automated tests for frontend web and backend APIs, and comfort reading code in languages such as Golang, Kotlin, Java or others on our Tech Radar. • Experience testing software both manually and with automation, including turning requirements into test scenarios and deciding what to automate first based on customer, launch and technical risk. • Ability to analyse logs, transaction traces and test results, and to write defect reports that include reproduction steps, expected versus actual behaviour, impact and evidence. • Comfort working with Git and Jira (or similar tools) • Experience with SQL and test-data setup, contract testing, mocking or service virtualisation. • An engineering mindset: the ability to spot what can be improved, explain the reasoning and persuade others with clear evidence • A curious, ownership minded approach: seeking out context when things are unclear and using AI tools where they help. Nice to have • Experience with Card payments, payment terminals, acquirers, card schemes or certification labs. • Market launches, device integrations, external technical partners or business-to-business communication. Payments experience is helpful but not required. We'll support you in learning card payments, devices and the certification process.
