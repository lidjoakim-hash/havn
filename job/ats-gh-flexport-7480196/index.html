We're looking for a Corporate Paralegal to own corporate housekeeping and entity governance for Flexport's subsidiaries across EMEA. You'll keep our existing entities in good standing across multiple jurisdictions, and you'll be the go-to person as we stand up new entities in new countries. This role sits within the Global Legal Team and works closely with in-house counsel, Finance, Tax, and People teams, as well as local counsel and registered agents across the region. What you'll do • Own day-to-day corporate housekeeping and governance for Flexport's EMEA entities: statutory registers, minute books, organizational charts, and corporate records across multiple jurisdictions • Prepare and maintain board and shareholder resolutions, consents, minutes, and other governance documentation for subsidiary boards • Manage statutory and regulatory filings (annual returns, changes of director/officer, registered address changes, share allotments, UBO/beneficial ownership filings, etc.) with local registries and authorities across EMEA • Support the formation, licensing, and onboarding of new legal entities as Flexport expands into new EMEA countries, including coordinating with local counsel, registered agents, banks, and tax advisors • Maintain Flexport's entity management system as the single source of truth for corporate structure, ownership, and governance records • Coordinate KYC, UBO, and due diligence requests from banks, customers, and partners across the corporate group • Support entity rationalization projects, including dissolutions, mergers, re-domiciliations, and intercompany restructurings • Draft and administer corporate policies, powers of attorney, and officer/director appointment and resignation documentation • Liaise with Finance, Tax, People, and Compliance teams to keep entity data, licenses, and registrations current as local requirements change • Support commercial and governance matters more broadly, including contract administration and NDA management, as needed • Budget and outside counsel management • Identify
