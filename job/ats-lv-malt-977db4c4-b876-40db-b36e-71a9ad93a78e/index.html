Your next chapter starts here! 🪐 Explore your future career  🔭 As our Senior Engineering Manager - Payment, your mission is to own the payment strategy for the product engineering team. You are taking care of the performance, productivity, and innovation in this scope. You are a hands-on manager; you are close to the topics your teams are handling, iterate on your scope with your team and challenge them to always optimize user experience and deliver value. Key responsibilities ✨ • Lead and inspire a high-performing team You’ll manage 1 product squad (6 to 10 engineers), creating the conditions for them to thrive: clear direction, strong rituals, and a collaborative culture. You foster alignment through regular team moments, stand-ups, retros, and workshops and care deeply about team morale and engagement. • Co-build product and technical strategy You’ll help craft the vision and strategy for the Payment & Cash Advance domain, ensuring your teams are fully aligned on goals and the path to get there.
