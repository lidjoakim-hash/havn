About us GoCardless, a Mollie company, is a global leader in bank payments. Over 100,000 businesses, from start-ups to household names, use GoCardless to collect, manage and send bank payments through Direct Debit, real-time payments and open banking. With US$130bn+ processed annually across 30+ countries, we handle recurring and one-off payments without the chasing, stress, or expensive fees. Our end-to-end payment platform also features AI-powered solutions to improve payment success and reduce fraud, alongside connections to over 350 platforms businesses use everyday. We are headquartered in the UK, with teams and operations spanning North America, Europe and Asia-Pacific. For more information, please visit www.gocardless.com and follow us on LinkedIn @GoCardless. Mollie is the leading payments and financial services partner for business, rooted in Europe, with global reach. Platform Engineering at GoCardless The Platform Engineering team is a unified, globally distributed team, currently located in London, Riga and Lisbon. We work with all other engineering teams to empower them to build, release, run and scale their products, with a strong focus on both strategic project delivery and operational excellence. Our work is a balance of: • Project delivery: building new platform components, from initial design to deployment. • Operational support: keeping our systems healthy and stable through on-call rotations and incident management. • Business as usual: continuous maintenance, improvements, and support for audits and compliance. The role We’re looking for a Senior SRE with deep PostgreSQL expertise to own the reliability, performance and scalability of our database estate. Our databases sit at the heart of a payments platform, so slowdowns and outages directly affect our customers. You’ll work across our self-managed Stolon clusters and our GCP Cloud SQL fleet. Joining Mollie gives us the opportunity to consolidate, modularise and rethink how we run production databases, and you’ll help us do that while keeping everything reliable.
