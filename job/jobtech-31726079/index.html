Are you an experienced HIL Engineer who enjoys working close to hardware, software, and test automation? Do you thrive in a hands-on environment where you build, configure, and improve complex test systems used for advanced vehicle development? Then this could be your next opportunity. ALTEN is now looking for a Senior Embedded HIL Engineer for an assignment towards one of our clients within the automotive industry. In this role, you will work with the development, maintenance, and adaptation of Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) environments, supporting multiple projects and contributing to the verification of next-generation vehicle systems. About the role As a Senior Embedded HIL Engineer, you will be responsible for designing, building, and maintaining HIL test environments used for verification and validation of embedded systems. The role combines hands-on work with hardware integration, wiring, and troubleshooting together with software development, automation, and tool improvements. You will work closely with developers, test engineers, and other stakeholders to ensure robust and scalable HIL solutions. The role also includes supporting test automation efforts, improving reusable tooling, and contributing to the development of efficient verification environments. Main responsibilities Design, build, and maintain HIL test rigs and hardware setups Configure, develop, and maintain Vector CANoe environments Perform wiring, pinout verification, and hardware integration activities Troubleshoot hardware, software, and connectivity issues within HIL environments Develop and maintain reusable tools and scripts for test environments Support the automation of test cases and execution workflows Collaborate with developers and test engineers to improve verification processes Support and train users of HIL rigs and test environments Contribute to the development of scalable solutions supporting multiple projects Your profile You are a proactive and solution-oriented engineer with a strong technical curiosity and a passion for embedded systems and test environments. You enjoy working across hardware and software domains and take ownership of technical challenges from start to finish. We see that you have: B.Sc. or M.Sc. in Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechatronics, or equivalent Several years of experience working with HIL development and embedded systems Experience with Vector CANoe Experience with Git and modern software development practices Fluent in English, both written and spoken Meritorious: Experience with Git and modern software development practices Experience with DDS and RTI Connext Experience working in Linux development environments Experience with C++ or Python Experience with CARLA simulation environments We warmly welcome your application already today! About ALTEN ALTEN is one of Europe’s largest technology and engineering consulting companies, with more than 57,000 employees in over 30 countries. In Sweden, we are a leading engineering consultancy within automotive, telecom, defense, energy, and industry. At ALTEN, we believe in growing together with our employees. Through exciting assignments, technical development, and a strong company culture, we create opportunities for our engineers to develop both professionally and personally.
