WHY IS THIS ROLE IMPORTANT FOR PENNYLANE? Pennylane is building Pennylane Financial Services (PFS), a newly created regulated subsidiary applying for ACPR authorisations as a payment institution and a financing company. From day one of licensing, PFS must be able to operate in full regulatory compliance - not only through policies, but through reliable execution: clear ownership, predictable reporting routines, auditable evidence, and effective coordination across Operations, Finance/Accounting and Compliance & Risk. This role is critical to organise and run that regulatory function, with the ambition to progressively consolidate regulatory reporting ownership within PFS. HOW WILL YOU CONTRIBUTE TO THE PROJECT? You will help build and operate the regulatory office of Pennylane Financial Services, working across Compliance & Risk, Finance, Legal, Operations and Technology. Your initial priority will be to turn PFS’s regulatory requirements into a reliable operating framework: a complete obligations inventory, clear ownership, an integrated regulatory calendar, and effective controls over regulatory reporting and evidence. In practice, you will: - maintain the authoritative inventory of PFS’s regulatory obligations; - coordinate the regulatory-reporting portfolio and directly produce selected returns; - establish clear responsibilities, reporting controls and escalation paths across contributing functions; - support supervisory responses and commitment tracking from day one; - coordinate regulatory change with Compliance, Legal and the relevant subject-matter owners; and - support regulatory governance through clear reporting, decision tracking and follow-through.
