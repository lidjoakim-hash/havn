The Opportunity We’re expanding our Strategic Accounts post-sale team and are looking for a Customer Value Partner, Strategic Accounts to own a handful of our most complex, high-impact enterprise partnerships. This is a senior individual contributor role and the post-sale counterpart to our Strategic Account Executives. Your focus is to ensure customers experience Multiverse as a seamless, proactive partner; turning commitments into outcomes, building long-term trust with senior stakeholders, and creating the conditions for sustainable account growth. Once here, you will: - Own the end-to-end customer journey for key strategic accounts, from launch through long-term value realisation. - Build deep, multi-threaded relationships across customer stakeholders (HR, executive sponsors, business leaders, department partners, line managers) and understand internal dynamics to engage the right people at the right time. - Partner with executive sponsors to broaden engagement across senior leaders and the teams responsible for execution, ensuring learning outcomes translate into measurable business impact. - Create and run joint success plans and discovery workshops with customers (outcomes, milestones, governance, success metrics), and hold a clear operating rhythm to drive progress. - Lead Executive Business Reviews / Quarterly Business Reviews that demonstrate value realised, celebrate shared wins, and course-correct where needed. - Translate delivery outcomes into compelling proof: share learner projects and impact stories with relevant leaders (e.g., department directors) to spark new use cases and strategic business reviews. - Partner closely with a dedicated onboarding/implementation counterpart to ensure a smooth launch and strong first 90 days, then maintain strategic oversight as programmes run. - Collaborate with Sales and Business Value Consultants to strengthen the value narrative and ensure the customer has a clear, evidence-backed view of value over time. - Act as a trusted internal voice of the customer: align Delivery, Product and Support to remove barriers, raise quality, and improve the overall customer experience. - Re-engage the Strategic Account Executive once a commercial opportunity to expand or renew is identified. Note, as a Strategic CVP you do not ‘own’ the commercial expansion outcome.
