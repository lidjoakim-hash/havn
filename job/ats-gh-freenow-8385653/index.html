Command Center Lead - Autonomous Vehicle

FREENOW · London, England, United Kingdom, United Kingdom

Location: London! Freenow by Lyft empowers smarter mobility decisions helping people to move freely and cities to thrive. We are now hiring an AV Command Center Lead to run the day-to-day nerve center of our Autonomous Vehicles (AV) operations. While our fleet teams manage vehicles and partners on the ground, the Command Center is where we watch, support, and safeguard every autonomous ride and provide support in real time. This hands-on operational role sits within a new autonomous vehicle business unit at the forefront of mobility innovation, reporting to the City Fleet Manager and working closely with the Senior Fleet Manager, Europe, AV technology partners, and Freenow by Lyft Operations. Be ready to work in a multinational, diverse, highly motivated and collaborative team of passionate professionals who strive for excellence and like to have fun. Are you ready for your next ride? YOUR DAILY ADVENTURES WILL INCLUDE: • You will define and document the Command Center’s operating processes and create the evidence needed to demonstrate regulatory compliance. • You will act as the first point of contact for in-ride events, supporting customers and coordinating with AV partners’ remote operations teams to resolve rider issues, stuck vehicles, and service disruptions quickly and safely. • As the Command Center scales toward extended and eventually 24/7 coverage, you will help onboard, train, and manage Command Center operators. • You will execute the incident response playbook: triage, severity classification, and escalation to the City Fleet Manager, partners, or emergency services as required and contribute to post-incident reviews with clear, accurate event timelines. • You will serve as the Command Center's operational point of contact for law enforcement and emergency services during live incidents, coordinating vehicle access, sharing required information, and supporting first responders on scene remotely. You will also support the City Fleet Manager in regulatory interactions, providing accurate operational records, incident logs, and data requested by local authorities. • You will own shift-level operations of the Command Center: daily readiness checks, shift handover documentation, alert queue management, and adherence to response-time SLAs agreed with partners. • You will coordinate with remote operations, ground operations and depot teams to dispatch on-the-ground support when remote resolution isn't possible vehicle recovery triggers and cleaning or inspection needs flagged from live monitoring. • You will maintain daily and weekly operational reporting, covering uptime, interventions, response times, customer-impacting events and flag recurring patterns and process gaps to the City Fleet Manager. TO BE SUCCESSFUL IN THIS ROLE: • At least 4 years of experience in real-time operations e.g., dispatch, network/operations control centers, taxi or ride-hailing or delivery live-ops, logistics control towers, or emergency response coordination. • Experienced in leading teams in a live operations environment; responsible for onboarding, training, and managing Command Center operators and the overall command center operations to ensure consistent service delivery. • Demonstrated ability to work structured escalation processes, manage alert queues, and make fast, sound decisions in time-critical situations. • Familiarity with autonomous vehicles, remote operations, or safety-critical monitoring environments is a plus. • Comfortable working across monitoring dashboards, ticketing systems, and communication tools; able to read operational data and turn observations into clear reports. • Clear, precise communicator with ability to give clear situation updates to fleet leadership and partners under pressure. • Comfortable communicating professionally with police, fire, and emergency medical services in live situations, and maintaining the documentation standards that regulatory interactions demand.
