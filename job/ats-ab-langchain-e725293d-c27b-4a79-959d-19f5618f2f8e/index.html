ABOUT US At LangChain, our mission is to enable every company to own its intelligence. We believe agents will transform how companies operate, and we're building the foundation to make that future possible. Our open-source harnesses and LangSmith platform give developers and enterprises the infrastructure to run, improve, and govern agents at scale. We've raised $460M to date, with our most recent funding round valuing the company at $3B, backed by leading investors including Sequoia, Benchmark, IVP, and CapitalG. As we continue to expand our platform and tackle some of the hardest challenges in agent engineering, every team member has the opportunity to make a meaningful impact and help shape both our technology and the future of the industry. More than 7,000 organizations rely on LangSmith, from fast-growing startups to Fortune 500 enterprises, including NVIDIA, Bridgewater, LinkedIn, Workday, and Rippling. We're just getting started on making high-performing agents easier to build and manage at scale. About the Team The Infrastructure team builds and maintains the systems that power LangChain’s developer platform, including LangGraph Cloud and LangSmith. The team focuses on reliability, scalability, and developer productivity across the stack, working closely with backend, frontend, and platform engineers to ensure services are deployed, tested, and operated with confidence. About the Role We’re hiring a Software Engineer to join the Infrastructure team and work across our LangGraph Cloud/Platform, LangSmith products, as well as internal systems. You’ll work closely with Infrastructure, Backend, and Frontend teams to ship with confidence across Kubernetes-based services, APIs, and UI flows. You’ll also help pioneer quality practices specific to LLM applications, such as prompt regression testing and evaluation suites. The team also owns our Sandbox product which enables you to improve scale and reliability of that offering as it powers many internal tools such as our software factory and CI/CD pipelines. Location: In person 5 days/week in San Francisco, CA or New York, NY. Open to remote for exceptional candidates. What You'll Do - Build internal tools and automation that reduce operational toil and help engineers build, test, and ship faster - Improve how we deliver software to SaaS and self-hosted customers, partnering with product teams to make releases faster and safer - Build and scale customer-facing infrastructure products, including our sandboxes, with a focus on performance, reliability, and developer experience - Scale our bring-your-own-cloud (BYOC) offering to improve customer experience and time to value - Establish practical engineering standards for testing, reliability, and safe deployments without slowing teams down - Evolve our observability stack so we can detect and diagnose problems as our infrastructure scales Example projects you might own - Scaling our Internal Software factory to handle 100x more scale - Chaos testing framework to identify ares of improvement for our platform - Redefine testing philosophy in the era of agentic coding - Improve our sandbox product’s performance and scale What You'll Bring - 3+ years of experience as a software engineer or infrastructure engineer - Strong hand-on experience with API testing, integration testing, and unit testing. Including common frameworks like pytest - Experience working with CI/CD systems (GitHub Actions preferred) and improving pipeline performance and reliability - Comfort defining quality standards, writing test plans, and driving cross-team execution - Understanding of technical systems that span backend frameworks and cloud infrastructure Nice to Have - Experience with load and performance testing tools such as k6 - Familiarity with observability tooling such as Datadog or OpenTelemetry - Experience testing services running on Kubernetes and containerized environments - Basic infrastructure
