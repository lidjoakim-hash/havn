About the role We are looking for a Project Manager to join our Trust and Support Operations team. Trust and Support Operations brings together the operational capabilities that help Catawiki deliver safe, reliable and high-quality marketplace experiences. The team works across Trust & Safety, Customer Service, Shipping, Product, Technology, Payments and other operational functions to solve complex problems that cannot be addressed effectively by one team alone. In this role, you will identify systemic operational problems, redesign end-to-end journeys and build scalable operating capabilities that improve user experience, quality, risk management, efficiency, cost and commercial performance. Your initial portfolio will include strengthening Trust & Safety operating processes following the evolution of our detection and investigation capabilities. You will work closely with Trust & Safety Operations, Product, Engineering, Data Science, Legal and Customer Service to improve how risks are detected, reviewed, escalated and resolved. In the longer term, you will also support operations excellence initiatives across other departments within Trust and Support Operations. This role reports to the Director Trust and Support Operations and has no direct reports. What you’ll do [Immediate] Strengthen Trust & Safety operating processes As an initial priority, you will support the continued development of Catawiki’s Trust & Safety operating capabilities.
