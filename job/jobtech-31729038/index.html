Junior Developer: Data Center Automation

Company description: Ericsson AB Job description: Join our Team About this opportunity: Are you curious about technology and excited to turn ideas into solutions that make a real difference? We are looking for a junior Developer who is eager to grow, learn, and help us bring more automation into our datacenter environments. You will join a team to develop smarter and more efficient workflows across our global datacenter sites, while ensuring our solutions align with Ericsson's security directives and frameworks. The team works together with operations and lifecycle management of server HW in the datacenter as well as creating automated workflows for other teams within our global datacenter operations. You will be part of our journey to make our datacenter environments smarter through automation. Working together with experienced colleagues, you will help automate repetitive tasks, improve workflows, and develop solutions that make our labs more efficient and reliable. You will also work hands-on in the datacenter, managing different server environments. It's a role where you can learn, experiment, take initiative, and grow your technical skills while contributing to real R&D challenges.   What you will do: Develop and maintain automation solutions for lab workflows using Python and/or Java. Build and support full-stack applications with Angular or React on the frontend and backend, using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript/TypeScript. Work with relational databases such as MySQL or PostgreSQL, writing and optimizing SQL queries. Use containers and orchestration tools such as Docker and Kubernetes and use GenAI agents to improve the development cycle. Apply application security fundamentals, such as secure coding and handling of sensitive data. Take part in the full SDLC, from requirements, design, and development to testing, deployment, and maintenance, using an agile way of working.   The skills you bring: A technical educational background within IT,  system administration, software development, computer science or a related field A strong interest in technology and a willingness to learn new things A curious, proactive, and solution-oriented mindset The confidence to take initiative and explore new ideas An interest in collaborating with others and contributing to a team A service-minded approach Good knowledge in English, both verbally and in writing
