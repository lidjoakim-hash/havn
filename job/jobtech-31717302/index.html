Loving and Experienced DAYTIME Nanny Wanted for a Wonderful Little Boy

Nanndis AB · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 130–160 SEK/time (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 11.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Ref. 4298Ö We are looking for a warm, caring and experienced daytime nanny to become an important part of a lovely family in Östermalm. Two nannies will share the position, one covering daytime and one nighttime, working alternate weeks. The family hopes to find someone with a genuine love for children who can become like an extra mum to their little boy, providing love, security and stability. The mother has a permanent back injury that limits her ability to lift and physically assist her son. Having a nanny available whenever he is with his mother is therefore essential. The role involves supporting both the child and the family in everyday life. About the Little Boy He is a happy, affectionate, confident and energetic 4.5-year-old who loves tennis, football and cars. He enjoys playing, exploring and spending time with an engaged adult. He attends nursery approximately 09:00–15:00 and is in good health. English is his main language, and the family would ideally like an English-speaking nanny to help maintain and develop his English. Day Nanny – Responsibilities The role combines childcare with light household support. While he is at nursery, approximately 2–5 hours will generally be spent on household tasks. The nanny should remain available in case he is unwell, nursery is closed or plans change. Responsibilities include: Morning routines, breakfast and preparing meals and snacks. Nursery drop-off and collection. Playing, engaging and supporting his interests and activities. Preparing healthy meals for the family. Laundry, ironing and making beds. Keeping the child's areas tidy and cleaning up after him. Wiping kitchen surfaces and general light household tasks. Administering medication when required. The family particularly appreciates someone who enjoys cooking and takes initiative in the kitchen. A weekly cleaner is employed. Working Schedule & Flexibility The position involves 12-hour shifts every other week, averaging approximately 167 hours per month. Reliability and consistency are essential. Occasional cover for the other nanny may be required in case of sickness. Both nannies are expected to work as a supportive team. International Travel The family travels internationally throughout the year, sometimes for 1–2 weeks or longer, and the nanny must be happy to accompany them. During trips, extended working hours may be required, with time off in lieu afterwards. Both nannies may occasionally cover daytime and nighttime duties. The family also has a second home abroad. A driving licence is not essential but would be an advantage. The Ideal Candidate We are looking for someone who is: Warm, loving, empathetic and passionate about children. Experienced with young children. Energetic, playful, calm and patient. Emotionally intelligent and confident setting loving boundaries. Reliable, responsible and trustworthy. Flexible, proactive and helpful. Comfortable working independently and as part of a team. Happy to travel internationally. English- or Swedish-speaking. Comfortable administering medication when required. Enjoys cooking and preparing family meals. A Special Long-Term Opportunity This is an opportunity to become a valued and trusted part of a warm family, building a close and lasting relationship with their little boy. The family is looking for someone who genuinely cares about his happiness and development and brings warmth, stability and positive energy into his everyday life. If you are an experienced, caring and dependable nanny who would love to become part of this family, we would love to hear from you! Location: Östermalm, Stockholm Start date: November 2026 – January 2027 (TBC) Schedule: Every other week Working hours: 08:00–20:00 (±1 hour) Child: One boy, 4.5 years old Languages: English preferred Position: Long-term, full-time employment Intervjuer sker löpande under ansökningsperioden.
