Company description: Epiroc is a leading productivity partner for the mining, infrastructure and natural resources industries. Withcutting-edge technology, Epiroc develops and produces innovative drill rigs, rock excavation and constructionequipment, and provides world-class service and consumables. The company was founded in Stockholm,Sweden, and has passionate people supporting and collaborating with customers in more than 150 countries.Learn more at www.epirocgroup.com. Job description:  Do you want to help us speed up the shift towards a more sustainable mining and construction industry? By doing your thesis in collaboration with Epiroc you will get the chance to be a part of this journey.    You will work together with experienced and helpful colleagues in an atmosphere characterized by creativity, innovation, and workplace diversity. In your specific project you have the chance to be a part of a challenging and educational experience while growing both professionally and personally.     Available Thesis Topics   ⚡ Electrification How can future mining and construction sites transition to electric operations? Explore solutions for charging, energy supply, and infrastructure that enable a safe and efficient shift from fossil-fuel-powered equipment to electrified machines. 🤖 Automation UX - Human & Autonomous Systems How will people collaborate with autonomous systems in the future? Explore user experiences and interface concepts that create trust, control, and effective collaboration between operators and automated solutions. 🎮 Automation UX - Remote Operations How can we create a stronger sense of presence when operating equipment remotely? Investigate new ways of designing user interfaces that support productivity, situational awareness, and decision-making from a distance. ⛏️ Autonomous Mine - Zero Humans What would a fully autonomous mine look like? Analyze the technologies, processes, and infrastructure needed to enable mining operations with little or no human presence underground. 🌱 Sustainability & Circular Solutions How can Epiroc contribute to a more sustainable future? Explore products, services, and business opportunities that support recycling, resource efficiency, and circular material flows. 🏗️ Future Infrastructure What new business opportunities could shape Epiroc's future? Identify emerging needs within infrastructure, construction, urban development, and recycling, and propose innovative products, services, or business models. 🚀 Space Mining How could mining be performed beyond Earth? Explore concepts for mineral exploration, extraction, and maintenance in extreme environments where human presence is limited or impossible. 💡 Open Topic Have an idea of your own? Propose a thesis topic related to mining, infrastructure, sustainability, or future technologies, and develop it together with our team into a project that creates value for the industry of tomorrow.     Mission: The deliverables shall be a presentation explaining the thoughts behind the proposed solutions, the work process and a scaled model. The measurement will be done by the mentors appointed for the project. The exact task for the project will be set by you and your appointed mentors.   Qualification: To be successful in this project you need to have an open mind for different possible solutions. You shall also Dare-To-Think-New. Challenge established conventions in the mining industry.   Education background:  Master Thesis student in Industrial Design   Level of thesis project: Master Thesis.     Functional area: Industrial Design Recruiting manager: Bengt Parkedal, Head of Industrial Design Phone: +46(0) 10 755 16 32, 0720-832 632 Email: bengt.parkedal@epiroc.com Other contacts: Anders Sandström, Experienced Designer.   Number of students in the project: One or two students/project.   How to apply: Does this sound interesting, and do you feel this is a match?   Log in to the recruitm
