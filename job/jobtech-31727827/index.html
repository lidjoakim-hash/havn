Research Engineer

Hydro Extrusion Sweden AB · Finspång, Sweden

💰 ca. 510 000–620 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 31.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Role Purpose Drive the optimization of metallurgical processes across the aluminium extrusion value chain. Ensure consistent product quality and strengthen operational excellence through expertise in alloys, heat treatment, and process performance. Collaborate with research, operations, commercial teams, customers, institutes, and universities to solve technical challenges and develop advanced aluminium extrusion solutions. What you will be doing Provide metallurgical expertise for aluminium extrusion operations, including casting, homogenization, extrusion, quenching, stretching, and aging Develop, evaluate, and qualify new alloys, process technologies, and extrusion products Develop and optimize alloy performance for mechanical and functional properties, extrusion productivity, and sustainability Collaborate with production teams to establish process standards and best practices Support customer projects with technical expertise and metallurgical guidance Support root cause analysis and problem-solving for quality issues related to alloy selection, mechanical properties, and geometrical stability Conduct metallurgical investigations, laboratory testing, and failure analyses Prepare technical reports, specifications, and customer documentation Ensure work is performed in compliance with the Hydro Quality system, HSE regulations, policies, and standardized operating procedures Perform other tasks assigned by the responsible manager that support the operation of the unit and comply with applicable laws and company rules Comply with area-specific customer requirements What will make you successful? PhD or Master’s degree in Physical Metallurgy, Materials Science, or a related technical discipline Strong knowledge of aluminium alloys Knowledge of heat treatment, precipitation hardening, recrystallization, grain structure control, and mechanical property development Familiarity with metallography, microscopy, hardness testing, tensile testing, quality systems, ISO standards, and customer qualification requirements is preferred Experience in aluminium extrusion, aluminium processing, or a metallurgical manufacturing environment Practical experience with metallurgical testing and analysis techniques Experience with automotive, building systems, industrial, electrical, or renewable energy applications is preferred Knowledge of adjacent fields such as corrosion, extrusion simulation, or process modelling is an advantage Familiarity with data analytics tools, including Power BI and Python, is an asset Fluent English and Swedish both written and spoken Curious, data-driven, and motivated to solve complex technical challenges Strong analytical and problem-solving capabilities Ability to combine deep metallurgical knowledge with practical manufacturing understanding Excellent communication, collaboration, and stakeholder management skills Able to work effectively with research, operational, commercial, customer, and academic partners Focused on measurable improvements in product performance, quality, operational efficiency, and customer success What we offer you A high-impact role in advanced manufacturing projects Opportunities to work with cutting-edge technologies International collaboration and exposure across sites A dynamic and hands-on technical environment Autonomy and responsibility in driving process improvements Travel opportunities and involvement in on-site operations A strong culture of quality, safety, and continuous learning
