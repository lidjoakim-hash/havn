Together, we’re delivering the data infrastructure layer that helps organizations move, transform, and trust their data — from the moment data moves, through every transformation, to the context teams and AI systems rely on. It’s an exciting time to join us: we’re creating a company with the scale, talent, and technology to help more organizations put their data to work with greater speed, confidence, and impact. ​ During this transition period, you may see references to both Fivetran and dbt Labs throughout our recruiting process as we integrate our teams, systems, and career sites. About the Role We’re looking for a Staff Engineering Operations Partner to own how Engineering organises, maintains, and uses its internal knowledge. You’ll join our Engineering Operations partner team and lead Engineering knowledge management in Notion as your primary program. You’ll define what good looks like: a clear information architecture, useful content types and templates, accountable content owners, and documentation that engineers can find and trust. You’ll assess the current state, set priorities, lead the improvements, and establish practices that keep the knowledge base useful as Engineering grows and changes. This is a hands-on role. You’ll combine knowledge management expertise with technical fluency, using AI and automation to improve documentation workflows and reduce manual maintenance. You’ll work closely with engineers, process owners, other EngOps partners, and technical enablement to make standards part of everyday practice. You will own the knowledge management framework and its effectiveness, while partnering with subject matter experts who are accountable for technical accuracy. You’ll also serve as the Engineering Operations Partner for our Engineering Strategy & Operations (ES&O) global group, supporting planning, execution, operational health, and process improvement. Engineering knowledge management will be your dedicated program alongside this partnership. This is a full-time position based in Novi Sad, Serbia.
