The opportunity We are a team of 15+ professionals located across multiple sites, delivering Automation & Communication solutions for HVDC projects worldwide. Our scope includes Automation Systems, Communication Networks, RTU solutions, SCADA, and Cybersecurity, supporting projects throughout their lifecycle. Our team brings diverse technical and project backgrounds, enabling us to adapt to the dynamic project environment while ensuring compliance with contractual requirements and customer expectations. We are responsible for defining work breakdown structures, planning deliverables, and driving successful project execution. We are looking for a person with strong leadership and interpersonal skills who can confidently navigate technical discussions with customers, coordinate cross-functional stakeholders, and drive suppliers to deliver high-quality solutions within agreed timelines. The ideal candidate combines technical understanding with the ability to build relationships, influence decisions, manage priorities, and ensure successful project outcomes in a fast-paced international environment. How you'll make an impact Be proactive and solution oriented. Drive technical solutions within Automation and Networking for HVDC projects. Support the design, implementation, and integration of automation systems and communication networks. Work closely with customers, suppliers, and internal engineering teams to ensure successful project delivery. Collaborate across multiple disciplines to achieve high-quality technical solutions. Contribute to the development and verification of communication architectures and network infrastructure. Collaborate with internal and external stakeholders throughout the project lifecycle. undefined Your background Bachelor's or Master's degree in Engineering or a related technical field.Knowledge of industrial communication protocols and networking technologies. Experience with automation systems, industrial networks, or communication infrastructure. Experience with SCADA, RTU, control systems, or networked automation solutions is considered an advantage. Understanding of Ethernet-based communication, network architecture, and system integration is beneficial. Strong communication and collaboration skills with the ability to work in international project environments.
