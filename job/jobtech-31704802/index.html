Every day, we get opportunities to make a positive impact - on our colleagues, partners, customers, and society. Together, we're pioneering the solutions of the future and unlocking the full potential of precious resources. Trusted to act on initiative, we challenge conventional thinking to develop world-leading technologies that inspire progress in vital areas, including energy, food, water, and shipping. As we push forward, the innovative, open spirit that fuels our 140-year-old start-up culture and rapid growth also drives our personal growth. So, as we shape a more resourceful, less wasteful world, we build our careers too. About the job Ready to take on challenging mechanical engineering work with a clear purpose? With us you will contribute to global priorities such as energy efficiency, clean water, and Alfa Laval's net-zero 2030 ambition. Let's shape a better tomorrow together! We design and supply process solutions within various industries, mainly water treatment. As a Mechanical Engineer you will create solutions through selection of standard components and detailed engineering combined. Our designs contain pipes, pumps, decanters, evaporators, membranes, heat exchangers etc., and they are delivered as equipment packages or skid solutions. Work involves both new development and standardization of solutions to bring down lead time on customer projects. During project execution you will be working in global project teams with Project Manager, Process Specialists, Automation, Sourcing, Workshops and customers. Key tasks and responsibilities: Use P&ID + mass balance to design process solutions in Autodesk Inventor Selection of standard components matching process needs 3D pipe routing using Inventor Piping/Sovelia Detailed Engineering of skid solutions, including fabrication documentation DFM in collaboration with workshop FAT, piping line walk and SAT Standardization of 3D design templates using skeleton and iLogic Review 3D designs and 2D drawings Review technical specifications from customers and develop technical deviation lists. Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 and CE marking Agile project execution using MS DevOps Ensure that the quality management system is followed Planned travels, mostly within Europe, for inspections and commissioning are part of the job - approximately 20 - 25 days per year. You will be part of an innovative mechanical team of ten who are continuously implementing smarter ways of working. You report to Jesper Bergstrøm, a people-oriented leader who is passionate about his team thriving in their roles and look forward to going to work! At Alfa Laval, we embrace a hybrid work model, allowing you to work from home when it aligns with your tasks. Joining Alfa Laval means being part of a flat, open, and inclusive culture that values collaboration and empowers employees. The company supports continuous learning and career development, fostering a strong growth mindset. As an established yet nimble global organization with 300+ locations and a long heritage of innovation, Alfa Laval offers both stability and autonomy often found in smaller firms. You will also gain meaningful cross-cultural exposure by working with international teams on impactful projects. Who are you You are a results-driven, high-energy professional who maintains focus and a positive attitude while pursuing ambitious goals. You take initiative, make calculated decisions with confidence, and consistently turn plans into action. You build strong networks across and beyond the organization, using your relational skills to influence, collaborate, and create impact. Proactive, timely, and quality-minded, you work effectively with others and help drive collective success. What you know We are looking for you with solid skills and experience in project execution within the wastewater, fluid handling, or a related processing industry.
