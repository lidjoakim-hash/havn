SLSQ128R13 Do you want to help solve the world's toughest problems with big data and AI? This is what we do every day at Databricks. We are looking for a Strategic Account Executive to join our Travel, Transport, Hospitality & Logistics (TTLH) team in the UK, to make the most of the phenomenal market opportunity that exists for Databricks within the UK rail and public transport sector. Your mission will be to grow one of our most strategic customers in the sector. It is a dedicated, single-account role and is already one of the top three spending customers in our TTLH vertical. Experience selling to the transport or public sector is preferred, as is experience in running large, complex accounts with multiple stakeholders. This is a pivotal moment to step into a mission-critical role within one of the UK’s most vital infrastructure sectors. As the transport landscape undergoes its most significant modernisation in a generation, our key enterprise customers are bringing complex, multi-operator environments together into unified, state-of-the-art platforms. Reporting to the Director, Enterprise for TTLH, as a Strategic Account Executive at Databricks you will bring an informed and compelling point of view on the Big Data, Advanced Analytics and AI space. That view will guide your strategy and, together with our teams and partners, allow you to provide exceptional value to our customer. customer's big data and AI transformation goals through a combination of strategic partnerships, well-scoped professional services, training and targeted executive engagement • You will position Databricks to coexist with and complement the hyperscaler ecosystem, building a joint "better together" strategy • You will orchestrate a wide range of Global Solution Integrators (GSIs) and delivery partners towards shared outcomes • You will develop an understanding of technical product details and roadmap to build trust with executives and business and technical champions What we look for: •
