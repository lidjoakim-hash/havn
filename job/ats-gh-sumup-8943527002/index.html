The team Our Barcelona office management team keeps a growing hub of over 400 SumUppers running smoothly every single day. As the office scales past 500 people by the end of the year, you'll join at a pivotal moment, becoming a key point of contact for everyone who walks through the door, from new starters to visiting SumUppers, clients and vendors. This is a hands-on role where you'll shape what a great day at the office feels like for the whole Barcelona community.
