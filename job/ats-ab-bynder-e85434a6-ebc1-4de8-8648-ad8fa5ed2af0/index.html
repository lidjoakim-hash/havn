ABOUT THE ROLE: The Bynder Legal Team is seeking a Legal Intern to join our Amsterdam office. This is a broad, hands-on role that offers exposure to legal operations, commercial contract management, and the wider administrative work of an in-house legal team. You will collaborate with various departments at Bynder, especially Business Applications, Finance, Sales, and Customer Success You would be part of our unique Bynder Love culture where we encourage each other to share our different points of view in a safe and welcoming environment and we support one another as we grow together. WHAT YOU WILL DO: Contract Management and Administrative Support - Play an active role in a legal operations project to enhance the quality, structure, and efficiency of our contract repository. - Audit, process and maintain legacy and new business customer contracts in Salesforce and ensure records are accurate, complete, and up to date. - Oversee our ticketing systems. - Provide day-to-day administrative support to the Bynder Legal Team. Legal Support - Conduct legal research on a variety of topics. - Review basic commercial agreements, such as Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs). - Support the team with ad hoc legal requests and daily legal operations.   WHAT YOU HAVE: - You need to be available for a full-time or part-time internship for at least 3 months starting October 2026, with the possibility of an extension. - You are currently enrolled in, or will be enrolled in, a Dutch Master's program in Law throughout your internship. - You have a strong understanding of legal terminology and are confident working with contracts, with a proactive and genuine commitment to learning and professional development. - One or more prior legal internships, with demonstrable experience supporting contract management, legal operations, and administrative processes. Experience in contract management is a strong advantage. - Fluency in English is required. - You are highly organised, detail-oriented, and consistently ensure that nothing is overlooked. - You’re motivated, proactive and independent. - You are already based in the Netherlands. NICE TO HAVE - Hands-on experience with tools such as Salesforce and Jira; is a strong plus. - A genuine interest in legal AI tools, ideally backed by some practical experience using them. - Knowledge of Dutch, French and/or German WHY YOU'LL LOVE BYNDER!
