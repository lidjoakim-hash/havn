Business Development Manager

BUILD WITH US Since 2004, we've had one clear goal: to make payments and money management effortless for every business in Europe. Today, more than 250,000 companies use Mollie to get paid, manage money and grow – with products designed to be simple, scalable, and dependable. With 950+ Mollies across 14+ locations, we care deeply about autonomy and craft. So we work in small teams, with real ownership, and we trust you to make the right decisions. We're building for the long term, so we provide the tools you need, processes you can rely on, and a balanced work environment to help you do work you're proud of. Sound like your kind of place? Let's build together. YOUR IMPACT This is a full cycle outbound sales role focused on the Italian market, where you own everything from prospecting to onboarding. You'll be targeting mid-market to enterprise merchants, primarily in e-commerce, fashion, pharmaceuticals, and electronics, managing deals that range from three to nine months in duration and cover companies transacting over €500K monthly. The goal is clear: bring meaningful payment volume onto Mollie's platform and help build our presence in one of Europe's most opportunity-rich markets. Italy is a market where merchants are often price-driven and deeply tied to their existing payment providers. That means your job is not just to sell but to educate. You'll position Mollie as the smarter choice by demonstrating real value, navigating pricing objections with confidence, and building relationships with decision makers at the C-level. The merchants who work with you will leave with a better understanding of payments and a platform that genuinely improves how they operate. You'll be part of a fast-growing team based in Milan, working in a sales culture that rewards structure, ownership, and ambition. With fewer but larger competitors than in other European markets, Italy offers the chance to build meaningful, lasting merchant relationships while contributing to a pipeline that targets €10M or more in annual transaction volume. WHAT YOU'LL SHIP - A consistent outbound pipeline built through 35 to 50 cold calls per week, targeted prospecting, and disciplined data-driven activity planning that keeps deals moving at every stage - Follow-up with inbound marketing and partnership leads, using our suite of sales tools, and attend in-person events to increase sourced leads - Sign mid-market and enterprise merchant contracts, managing the full deal lifecycle from first contact through negotiation, pricing, contract handling, and live transaction - A balanced portfolio of deals that mixes shorter-term closable opportunities with longer-term complex negotiations, ensuring steady revenue contribution alongside pipeline growth - Smooth merchant onboarding outcomes, coordinated with sales engineers and customer success managers to make sure every merchant goes live and stays live - C-level relationships with e-commerce managers, CEOs and leadership executives built on trust, payment expertise, and a clear demonstration of Mollie's value over incumbent solutions - Accurate sales forecasting and pipeline reporting that gives the team full visibility into deal health and expected outcomes WHAT YOU'LL BRING - Around 5 years of experience in commercial roles, with 3 years of full cycle ownership. - Extensive experience running inbound and outbound sales cycles end to end, with a track record of cold outreach that converts and a structured approach to pipeline management - Familiarity with value-based sales methodologies such as MEDDIC, or similar frameworks, and the ability to apply them in complex, multi-stakeholder deals - Experience selling customized or competitive products where the challenge is displacing an existing solution rather than creating new demand, including handling negotiations and contract discussions - Confidence in technical sales conversations, including working alongside sales engineers
