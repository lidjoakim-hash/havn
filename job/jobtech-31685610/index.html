Are you curious about what it’s like to work at Hemfrid? Come and meet us at our recruitment event! We are looking for new colleagues who want to work with home cleaning in Lidingö and the surrounding areas. This is a great opportunity to meet us, learn more about the job and have a short interview with our recruitment team. At the recruitment event, you will have the opportunity to: Meet the Hemfrid team Learn more about working as a Home Cleaner Find out what we offer our employees Ask us your questions directly Have a short interview with our recruitment team What does Hemfrid offer?
