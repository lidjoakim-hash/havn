DIRECTOR, GROUP ACCOUNTING & REPORTING Role highlights: - Director, Group Accounting & Reporting - Location: Espoo, Finland - Department: Corporate Controlling & Accounting - Reports to: VP, Global Corporate Controller - Employment type: Permanent - Workplace model: Hybrid - Employment is subject to applicable security screening (incl. SUPO, where required) Why this role matters: ICEYE is growing fast: new entities, new contracts, new currencies and new people every quarter. The way we close, consolidate and report is being designed now, and this role decides how it will work for the next stage of the company. You will lead Group Accounting & Reporting and report to the VP, Global Corporate Controller. If you want to build a group reporting function and not only run one, and you have the experience to handle a complex group structure, this is the role for you. About ICEYE ICEYE is the world leader in sovereign intelligence from space. We deliver persistent monitoring capabilities to detect and respond to changes in any location on Earth. ICEYE owns the world’s largest and most advanced SAR (synthetic aperture radar) satellite constellation. To our customers we provide intelligence with unmatched quality, latency and revisit times, in any weather, day or night. To governments who choose to operate their own constellation we provide this proven capability as a sovereign system. ICEYE-built constellations serve customers in defence and intelligence, environmental monitoring, insurance and emergency management. We enable fast decisions that contribute to a safer future. Founded and headquartered in Finland, ICEYE operates globally with over 1000 employees across Europe, North America, the Middle East and Asia-Pacific. What you’ll build and lead - A group close that is fast, repeatable and calm, monthly, quarterly and annually, and that scales with the company. - Group consolidation, intercompany accounting and eliminations across a growing structure of entities and currencies. - Group financial statements under IFRS, and the reporting that the CFO, Executive Team, Board and investors rely on. - Statutory reporting across our global entity footprint, reconciled to group figures. - The processes, standards and documentation that do not exist yet, built in the right order. - Our GL team and our outsourced accounting partner, with clear standards and clear ownership. - A team where people learn, grow and speak up: you coach, delegate and set the example. - The main relationship with our external auditors on group matters. WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR Must haves: - Deep experience with complex group structures: consolidation across many legal entities, currencies and jurisdictions, with intercompany flows and a structure that changes through new entities, acquisitions or restructurings. - A track record of owning a group close and IFRS reporting end to end [and US GAAP, if needed], and of building or substantially improving it, not only running something mature. - Comfort starting from a blank page: you can see what is missing, decide what to do first and bring people along. - Leadership that coaches: you build trust, give clear direction and develop people. Coaching matters as much as technical depth. - Clear, confident communication with senior stakeholders, including the CFO, Board and investors. Nice to haves: - Experience in a scale-up or high-growth company. - Experience in complex, contract-driven industries, for example revenue recognised over time. - Experience with ERP and consolidation tools, and with close automation.
