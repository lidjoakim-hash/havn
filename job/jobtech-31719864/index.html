We are looking for one to two professors of Statistics for permanent positions at the School of Business. Subject area The subject area for this position is Statistics. Background Örebro University is seeking one to two professors of Statistics to strengthen and expand our research. Research in the Department of Statistics is mainly focused on Bayesian Statistics with applications in Economics and Finance, as well as Survey Methodology. Formally, and based on a close collaboration, the Departments of Statistics and Economics constitutes a joint research environment with cross-disciplinary research groups in wellbeing and health economics; entrepreneurship, globalization and institutions; macro- and financial econometrics (MAFE) and statistics (stat@oru). In addition, the department benefits from a close collaboration with Statistics Sweden. We are looking for candidates whose expertise can, in particular, strengthen the MAFE and stat@oru groups, and who can also take on a leadership role. Education in the Department of Statistics spans the bachelor’s, master’s and doctoral levels. In addition to the Bachelor’s Programme in Statistics, courses are offered in, among others, the Programme for Business and Economics. At the master’s level, a two-year study programme in statistics is offered which has been awarded the ‘European Master in Official Statistics’ (EMOS) label by Eurostat. The department also offers courses in the Master’s Programme in Economics and Econometrics, and the Master’s Programme in Finance. In addition to Statistics and Economics, Örebro University School of Business hosts the departments of Business Administration and Informatics. The Business School is accredited by AACSB, the largest accreditation body and network for business schools globally – an achievement accomplished only by five per cent of the world’s 16,000 business schools. Duties and responsibilities As a rule, a professor at Örebro University spends the bulk of their working hours on research. They are also expected to work actively to bring in external research grants to the subject, take overall responsibility for its research activities, and monitor developments within the subject field internationally. A professor is expected to be involved at all three levels of higher education and to communicate and collaborate around their own and the department’s research activities. A professor is moreover expected, within the scope of their employment, to make themselves available for executive and administrative assignments at the university. The professorships are aimed at strengthening the research in Statistics. We particularly welcome candidates whose research focuses on Bayesian Statistics, Econometrics, Time Series Analysis and/or Survey Statistics. We are seeking individuals with the vision and potential to drive the development in the subject. As two professors are being recruited, the aim is for the appointments to have complementary research profiles and, taken together, to strengthen the breadth of Statistics at Örebro University across the areas listed above.
