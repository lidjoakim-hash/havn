People Partner - Sales Team description Our EU Sales organisation is growing across Direct and Field Sales, and you'll be the dedicated People Partner for our Sales teams in the Northern Europe (i.e. UK and Germany), covering everything from onboarding and performance to leadership support and retention. Many of the teams and leaders are new, so you'll have real influence over how people partnership works in a Sales environment that keeps evolving, and your work will directly affect how well we keep and grow great Sales talent. You'll work closely with Sales leaders and alongside Regional People Partners, People Operations, Talent Acquisition, Compensation and Benefits, and Legal, so you'll have strong backing while you own your markets. What you'll do • Partner with Sales leaders in the UK and Germany on team structure, role design and hiring needs, and work with Talent Acquisition to prioritise and sequence hiring • Streamline onboarding and offboarding so new employees and consultants can be set up within a day as the team grows • Adapt performance and talent development processes to the needs of Direct and Field Sales, and coach leaders on performance conversations, retention and managing exits fairly in each country • Work with Compensation and Legal teams to benchmark pay and improve employee and consultant contracts • Track people data such as attrition, hiring, promotion and spans of control, and raise issues to leadership with clear, constructive improvement plans • Participate in other People Team projects What you'll bring: • Experience as a People Partner or HR Business Partner in a growing organisation, with a track record of turning people strategy into practical action. • Experience supporting Sales teams, including both employees and self employed consultants (desirable) • Strong knowledge of talent and performance management, and UK and German employment law. • Ability to think about the big picture while staying hands on with the details, with solid change management practice. • Fluency in English, with clear, direct and transparent communication, strong coaching skills and the ability to build trust quickly. Why you should join SumUp • 🌎 Opportunity to work with a truly global, multicultural team from our central London Bridge Location , wrapped in historic charm and modern flair. This involves an office-first setup • 🌈 Commitment to Diversity and Inclusion: be part of a workplace that values and promotes diversity, fostering an inclusive environment where everyone's perspectives are respected and embraced • 🚀 Enrolment onto our Virtual Stock Option programme: you will own a stake in SumUp’s future success • 🏖️ Generous time off: enjoy 28 days of paid leave, plus bank holidays and special leaves • 📚 A dedicated annual L&D budget for attending conferences and/or advancing your career through further education • 🏥 Health matters: private health insurance, including optical and dental • 🚗 Life made easier: salary-sacrifice commuter benefits via Gogeta • 💼 Financial security: retirement scheme (SumUp matches 7% when you contribute 5%) • 🛡 Peace of mind: life insurance from MetLife for 2× your salary • 🌴 Break4me: 1-month sabbatical after 3 years of service • 🔗 Referral Bonus: earn additional rewards by referring talented individuals to join the SumUp team About SumUp Be empowered to do more that matters.
