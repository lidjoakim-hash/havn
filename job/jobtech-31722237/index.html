Senior Project Manager – Communication & Data Links

MultiMind Holding AB · Linköping, Sweden

💰 ca. 500 000–610 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 12.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

We are looking for an experienced Senior Project Manager to lead projects within Communication & Data Links. In this role, you will work with complex hardware and software systems, lead cross-functional teams and coordinate activities from concept development to system integration and verification. Your responsibilities: Lead projects and coordinate resources. Drive requirements gathering and system design. Manage equipment procurement and suppliers. Oversee system implementation, testing and troubleshooting in flight simulators and during flight trials. Support and verify system functionality. Travel internationally when required. What we’re looking for: A relevant technical degree, such as Engineering Physics, Computer Science or similar. Proven project management experience. Experience leading cross-functional teams. Experience with system engineering and integration of complex hardware and software systems. Good communication skills in both Swedish and English. Additional information: Senior-level position. Travel may be required. Interested? If this sounds like the right opportunity for you, please get in touch and share your CV. We look forward to hearing from you!
