The role in one line Being a GRC Engineer at incident.io http://incident.io means turning compliance from spreadsheets and screenshots into something that just runs, so “can you prove it?” gets answered before anyone asks. What that looks like day to day As a GRC Engineer at incident.io http://incident.io, you own how we prove we’re doing the right thing: our compliance programme, our customer security questionnaires, and the evidence behind both. One day you’re building an AI workflow that drafts questionnaire answers, the next you’re pushing our ISO 27001 work forward, the next you’re telling the product team what makes GRC painful. Our GRC programme is young, and Slack is our system of record. You’ll make it scale, using AI-powered tooling and workflows to take the toil out of audits, evidence collection and customer questions. Right now, that means: - ISO 27001. It’s in progress, and you’ll help raise the bar and get us over the line. - ISO 42001. We build AI products, so we want to manage AI responsibly. In your first two quarters or so, you’ll run a gap assessment against the standard. - Dogfooding. We use incident.io http://incident.io for our own security work, and you’ll drive product improvements that make GRC less painful for our customers too. You’ll work closely with the wider security team, engineering, legal and product. Who you are - You’ve run or contributed to a real compliance programme, ideally ISO 27001, and you know what it takes to get through an audit. - You’d rather automate evidence collection than chase people for screenshots. - You write code (Python, Go or similar) or build workflows, and you’re excited about using AI and agents to remove toil. - You can answer customer security questionnaires quickly, accurately and in plain English. - You can explain a control to an engineer and a risk to an executive, and both come away knowing what to do next. - You care about the product, and you’ll tell us what’s missing from our own GRC tooling. - You’re comfortable with high autonomy. Nobody’s going to hand you a detailed spec, you’re trusted to figure out what needs doing and just do it. - Bonus points for ISO 42001 or AI governance experience, SOC 2, GDPR, or time in a fast-growing SaaS company.
