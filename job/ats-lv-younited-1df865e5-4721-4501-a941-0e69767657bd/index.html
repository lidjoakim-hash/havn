Key Responsibilities • Channel execution and optimisation across SEA, SEO, CRO, and GEO, approximately 50% of the role. • Manage the day-to-day execution and optimisation of Google Ads campaigns, including bids, keywords, ads, audiences, and budget pacing, within established account structures and budget frameworks. • Monitor performance daily, identify anomalies, and act on them quickly. • Execute SEO, CRO, and GEO actions with agencies and the central marketing team. • Support the planning, implementation, and analysis of A/B tests on landing pages, and help track their impact on conversion. • Report weekly on channel KPIs and explain what moved and why. • Monitor affiliate and other acquisition channels, identify optimisation opportunities, and share recommendations with the relevant channel owners. • Contribute to cross-team marketing projects with Analytics, Product, Legal, and central and Iberian marketing teams. • Ensure that campaign execution follows established tracking, reporting, and governance standards. What we’re looking for • 1 to 3 years of experience in digital marketing, performance marketing, an agency, or a similar analytical role. • Hands-on familiarity with Google Ads. Certification is a plus. • Comfortable working with core KPIs such as CPC, CTR, CPL, CPA, conversion rate, and ROAS. • An analytical mindset, with confidence using numbers, Excel or Google Sheets, and dashboards. • Execution-driven, organised, detail-oriented, and able to get things done. • Curious and eager to learn, with strong motivation to grow in the role. • Spanish and English are required. Portuguese is helpful but not required • Nice to have: • Exposure to SEO tools such as GA4, Search Console, Semrush, or similar platforms. • Basic knowledge of SQL or BigQuery. • Experience in fintech, lending, or another regulated sector. • Experience working in an international or multi-market environment. What We Offer • A fast-growing, international, and entrepreneurial environment where your impact will be visible from day one. • A clear learning path in performance marketing, with meaningful responsibility and support from central and local teams. • Flexible working arrangements, including up to 10 remote working days per month. • A collaborative, supportive, and people-centric culture where teamwork and innovation are highly valued.
