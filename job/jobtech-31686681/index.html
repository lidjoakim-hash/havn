DevOps Engineer

Explipro Group AB · Göteborg, Sweden

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

About the Role We are seeking an SRE / DevOps Engineer to join an agile team developing and operating business-critical connected vehicle services. You will bridge development and operations to maximize uptime, automate workflows, and maintain robust monitoring for millions of connected vehicles worldwide. What You Will Do Observability: Maintain and optimize monitoring, logging, and alerting (Datadog, CloudWatch, Kibana) for rapid troubleshooting and high availability. Automation & IaC: Streamline operations and deployments using scripting (Python/Bash) and Infrastructure as Code (CloudFormation, CDK, or Terraform). Operations: Run and support enterprise Linux environments and core AWS infrastructure; participate in the team's on-call rotation. Collaboration & Security: Partner with engineers and product owners in a "you build it, you run it" culture while applying cloud security best practices (IAM, encryption). Qualifications & Experience Core Requirements: AWS & Cloud Infrastructure: Solid hands-on experience designing and operating solutions in AWS, including Infrastructure as Code (Terraform or CloudFormation). Automation & Scripting: Strong skills in operational automation using languages such as Python, Bash, or Java. Observability & Reliability: Proven experience setting up distributed metrics, logging, and alerts (e.g. Datadog, CloudWatch, or Kibana). Linux & Operations: Confident working in enterprise Linux environments with a solid grasp of basic networking (TCP/IP). Ways of Working: Good understanding of modern DevOps culture, incident management, and agile delivery. Communication: Professional proficiency in English, both written and spoken. Meriting: Experience with Serverless architectures (AWS Lambda, API Gateway) or message brokers (MQTT, Kafka). Familiarity with container platforms (Docker, Kubernetes) or backend development in Java. Prior experience from the automotive, telematics, or IoT domain. Who You Are An analytical problem solver focused on automation, root-cause resolution, and continuous improvement. A communicative team player who takes ownership of production systems and bridges technical depth with business needs. We are looking for consultants who are passionate about modern cloud technology, eager to tackle challenging client assignments, and drive technical excellence. Join us to work alongside leading technology companies and explore cutting-edge solutions in a supportive and innovative network. We look forward to hearing from you!
