The role: As a RevOps Analyst intern in the PRO business unit, your job is first to simplify and automate. Every manual task is a target: you don't run a process you can kill or automate. You will: Simplify and automate sales operations, so the run produces itself and the team spends its time on what matters. Build with AI (skills, agents, workflows) instead of adding headcount or tickets. Equip the Sales team to build their own AI tools. You will work directly with Adrien, RevOps PRO, who guarantees the vision, the strategy and the RevOps system as a whole. He will coach you day to day: project scoping, review of what you build, and priorities. The goal is for you to own your scope quickly. This is a 6-month internship (an internship agreement with your school is required)! Your Impact at Brevo: • Monthly run, automated: • Make the monthly cycle (performance reviews, forecast, recap) run on its own, and automate the steps that are still manual. • Automate roster changes (joiners, leavers, squad moves) so they flow into every tool: targets, routing and access. • Sales requests, solved at the source: • Handle requests from AEs and managers, and turn each recurring one into an automation, a skill or an FAQ rather than another ticket. • AI enablement for Sales: • Coach AEs and managers to build their own skills, apps and dashboards. • Revenue levers: • Define the lead scoring model and improve qualification; the Forward Deployed Engineer then builds the scoring into our tools. • Scale upsells and upgrades across the existing customer base. What will contribute to your success: Experience and education background: • Engineering school, business school or an atypical path. Your AI practice matters more than your degree. • Side projects where you automated or built something with AI are a strong plus. AI & system skills: • You understand how tools, data and processes fit together, and where they break. No need to code. • You build with AI, beyond using it: you have already created skills, agents or workflows (Claude Code, Cowork or equivalent). Using ChatGPT is not enough. Beyond the technical: • A simplification instinct: faced with a process, your first question is "why does it exist?", not "how do I run it?". • Business acumen: you understand a SaaS sales funnel and what moves revenue. • Clear writing: a short, actionable spec or recommendation.
