Head of Engineering: Financial Data Experience At Adyen, payments and money movement are central to what we do. As the Head of Engineering for Financial Data Experience, you make sure our customers never have to work out where their money went. You build the datasets and experiences that our global merchants, regulators and Adyen Finance rely on to trust their numbers. Each of them receives fully explained financial data in a format they can verify and audit. You do this by defining what a payment, a fee, a payout or a correction looks like to a customer, and what our reconciliation experiences and reports actually show. This role bridges the gap between Adyen's internal ledger complexity and the unified, customer-facing money movement experience. The goal is an automated, self-serve, trustworthy experience. What You Will Do As Head of Engineering, reporting to the SVP of Engineering and working with peers across Payments, Financial Products, Platform, Compliance and Finance, your focus will be: • Own the customer-facing model of money movement for merchants, regulators, and Adyen Finance, ensuring data reflects the merchant's entities and periods rather than mirroring internal systems. • Own how that data is delivered: datasets, reports, files and dashboards, on the same terms for every team that publishes through us. • Create an environment that fosters continuous innovation. • Build a best-in-class team; developing and hiring great people with the right skills to deliver. • Curious, approachable and desire to work in a highly collaborative manner - both internal and external. • Exceptional at prioritization, putting our merchant and business needs ahead of personal ambition. • Accountable for the execution of key projects, ensuring timely delivery, tracking progress, removing blockers, and assisting with planning and prioritization as needed. • Driven by simplicity, standards and reuse. • Stay close enough to the technology to judge a design yourself. • Build, mentor, and manage a high-performing engineering organization, balancing robust scalability with the execution of complex, cross-stream technical projects.
