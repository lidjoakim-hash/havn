Do you want to work with advanced ASIC development at the forefront of technology? Join the team responsible for the ARTPEC SoC - at the heart of Axis products - and help coordinate a complex development project with people across many disciplines. This is an opportunity to contribute to a high-impact technology project while developing your project leadership skills with support from experienced colleagues. We are looking for an Early-Career ASIC Project Manager to strengthen Axis ASIC development in Lund. What is Axis? Axis is a fast-growing and highly innovative company offering intelligent security solutions. Our network video and surveillance solutions help create a safer and smarter world. Our success comes from our skilled people and our culture of openness, collaboration, and innovation. At Axis, we do more than create products - we shape solutions that deliver value to our customers. Who is your future team? The Axis ASIC Department develops custom ASICs that accelerate essential functions in our surveillance cameras. We design, develop, implement, integrate, and verify hardware modules that help strengthen Axis' technology leadership. Our hardware blocks support functions such as image processing, video compression, radar, audio, and computer vision. We also work closely with external vendors who provide third-party IP cores. The ASIC development team brings together colleagues with a range of experience and expertise. We value an open working environment and collaborative, low-ego teamwork. Learn more about ARTPEC: https://newsroom.axis.com/article/artpec-foundation-quality What you do You will join the ASIC project leadership team, which coordinates the internal development of an ASIC at Axis. Working with colleagues across the organization and with external partners, you will help keep the project organized and moving forward. Your tasks will include: Coordinating work across internal and external interfaces, including software, algorithms, electronics, product teams, and partners Tracking project progress, coordinating priorities, and communicating plans and status. Planning and following up on deliveries to and from other projects and external partners, such as third-party IP core providers Helping track and visualize the project budget Supporting technology intake activities within the ASIC Department You will build your project leadership skills while developing your understanding of the technical and commercial aspects of ASIC development. An interest in technology and working with external partners is important. Experience in technical or commercial negotiations is a plus. Who are we looking for? You have a technical background and are curious about ASIC development and the technology behind complex products. You enjoy creating structures, connecting people, and helping a team make progress towards a shared goal. You don't need to have held a formal leadership role. You may have developed relevant skills through your studies, a thesis project, an internship, or your first professional role. What matters is your interest in taking responsibility, supporting collaboration, and growing into project leadership. We believe you have: A master's degree in electrical engineering, computer science, physics, mathematics, or a related field. A bachelor's degree in a relevant field may also be suitable, depending on your experience and overall profile. Some knowledge of ASIC development, or a strong interest in learning about technology. An interest in project planning, coordination, and follow-up. Clear communication skills and the ability to create shared understanding. Familiarity with agile ways of working, or an interest in learning them. Good communication skills in English and Swedish. You bring your own perspective, ask questions, and enjoy working with people across different disciplines. You value collaboration and are motivated to help a team succeed.
