BUILDING THE FUTURE OF OPEN FINANCE Payward - the parent company behind Kraken, NinjaTrader, Breakout, xStocks, Payward Services and CF Benchmarks - has spent the last 15 years building one of the most modern and globally accessible financial infrastructure platforms in the industry, built to advance an open, global financial system.   THE TEAM Founded in 2011, Kraken is one of the world's longest-standing crypto platforms, trusted by over 10 million individuals and institutions across the globe. It offers spot trading, margin, futures, staking, and OTC services, with products built for both individual investors and institutional clients.   We’re looking for a Senior Staff Product Designer to lead design for Krak and help us build a neobank people trust with their everyday money.   This is a high-impact individual contributor role with responsibility for design across the product. You’ll set the direction for the Krak experience, influence product strategy, and stay close to the details through execution. We’re a hardworking team that moves fast and ships with purpose. We’re straight-talking, no-nonsense, and deeply invested in the quality of what we build. We care about craft, we care about our customers, and we care about getting things right. Building a neobank means earning people’s trust in the moments that matter: getting started, moving money, making a payment, or understanding what’s happened when something goes wrong. You’ll help make those experiences clear and reliable, bringing the same care to everyday interactions as you do to the overall product vision. The Opportunity - Own the design direction for Krak, connecting onboarding, accounts, payments, and spending into one coherent experience. - Make everyday banking clear and intuitive, from understanding balances and fees to tracking payments and resolving problems. - Shape product strategy and roadmap decisions with product, engineering, and leadership. - Lead hands-on design and prototyping, setting the standard for interaction design, visual craft, and accessibility. - Work with compliance, risk, and operations to make verification, security, and account recovery straightforward for customers. - Evolve Krak’s design system and align teams around consistent patterns. - Use research and data to improve the product, and mentor designers through critique and collaboration.What You’ll Bring   WHAT YOU BRING - 7+ years in product design, with a strong portfolio of shipped consumer mobile experiences. - Experience operating at Staff level or above, leading design across a product or several connected teams. - Strong interaction and visual design skills, with the ability to work through complex financial journeys and their edge cases. - Advanced prototyping skills and experience building or evolving design systems. - Evidence of influencing product strategy, resolving competing priorities, and guiding work through delivery. - Clear communication, sound design judgment, and experience mentoring other designers. - Experience designing banking, payments, or personal finance products is a strong plus.
