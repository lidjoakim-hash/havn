At Trustpilot, we're on an incredible journey. We're a profitable, high-growth FTSE-250 company with a big vision: to become the universal symbol of trust. We run the world's largest open customer review platform, and while we've come a long way, there's still so much exciting work to do. Come join us at the heart of trust! *This role is based out of our Copenhagen HQ office and requires fluent French, with strong English skills* As an Account Executive for the French Mid-Market, you’ll join our team at the heart of the action, right on our home turf in Copenhagen. You'll be literally at the centre of where the fun happens, with a unique opportunity to drive change and see the direct impact of your work on our business growth. You’ll be responsible for winning new mid-market customers in France and managing the full sales cycle, from identifying and prospecting potential customers through to discovery, demonstration, negotiation and closing. You will join our French Mid-Market team, working closely with both Account Executives and Account Managers. This is an outbound-led role. You will be expected to generate a substantial share of your own pipeline through consistent and targeted outreach. Your prospecting efforts will be complemented by meetings generated by our BDR team and qualified inbound leads. You’ll engage with senior decision-makers, uncover complex business needs and build compelling business cases that demonstrate the commercial value of Trustpilot. We’re not just selling SaaS. You’ll help established French businesses use trust and transparency to strengthen their brands, improve customer acquisition and accelerate growth. What you’ll be doing: • Drive new business across the French Mid-Market, from prospecting to close. • Own and exceed a monthly revenue target through consistent pipeline creation and strong deal execution. • Generate a substantial share of your pipeline through proactive and personalised outbound prospecting across phone, email, LinkedIn and other relevant channels. • Manage opportunities created through your own outreach, BDR-booked meetings and qualified inbound leads. • Identify and engage key decision-makers, including C-level stakeholders, across multiple industries. • Run structured discovery conversations to understand each prospect’s priorities, challenges, commercial objectives and decision-making process. • Build compelling business cases and deliver tailored product demonstrations that clearly connect Trustpilot’s solutions to the prospect’s needs. • Navigate more complex sales processes involving multiple stakeholders, commercial negotiations and higher-value solutions. • Use your initiative and commercial judgement to prioritise opportunities and manage your sales funnel effectively. • Maintain accurate pipeline management and forecasting in Salesforce. • Develop your capabilities for continued growth within Mid-Market and Enterprise SaaS sales. Who you are: • Fluent in French, with strong English skills. • 3 years of experienced in full-cycle B2B sales, including outbound prospecting, qualification, discovery, demonstration, negotiation and closing. • Proven track record in SaaS or Tech sales, with consistent quota achievement. • Comfortable generating your own opportunities and maintaining a high level of outbound activity. • Experienced in selling higher-value solutions to mid-market businesses and managing several stakeholders throughout the sales process. • Confident in engaging with senior and C-level decision-makers and adapting your approach to the French business environment. • Strong discovery and consultative selling skills, with the ability to translate business challenges into clear commercial value. • Able to manage a varied pipeline while prioritising the opportunities most likely to progress and close. • Competitive, resilient and motivated by achieving and exceeding targets. • A proactive self-starter with the ambition to grow a career
