Why Mozilla? Mozilla Corporation is the non-profit-backed technology company that has shaped the internet for the better over the last 25 years. We make pioneering brands like Firefox, the privacy-minded web browser. Now, with more than 225 million people around the world using our products each month, we’re shaping the next 25 years of technology and helping to reclaim an internet built for people, not companies. Our work focuses on diverse areas including AI, social media, security and more. And we’re doing this while never losing our focus on our core mission – to make the internet better for people. The Mozilla Corporation is wholly owned by the non-profit 501(c) Mozilla Foundation. This means we aren’t beholden to any shareholders — only to our mission. Along with thousands of volunteer contributors and collaborators all over the world, Mozillians design, build and distribute open-source software that enables people to enjoy the internet on their terms. About this team and role: The JavaScript team at Mozilla works to make Firefox's industry-leading JavaScript engine SpiderMonkey even more performant. We push the boundaries of what the web is capable of, shaping the future of the JavaScript language through standardization at TC39, while keeping in mind the fundamental principle of “don’t break the web.” As a Staff Software Engineer on the SpiderMonkey team, you'll be a domain expert who drives the engine's runtime performance forward, from the interpreter and JITs to the garbage collector, while also helping maintain and advance the health of a large, mature C++ codebase. You'll typically lead performance projects within the team, planning the work and mitigating the risk yourself, and you'll mentor other engineers as you take on increasingly ambiguous, high-impact problems. What you’ll do: • Lead runtime performance initiatives: Own projects to improve SpiderMonkey's runtime performance, from profiling and identifying bottlenecks to designing and landing optimizations across the interpreter, JITs, and garbage collector. • Provide technical leadership to the team: Mitigate risk for the performance projects you own, planning work that may start out ambiguous and require significant up-front design and prototyping. • Maintain and advance the codebase: Help preserve the correctness and stability of a large, mature C++ codebase while improving its architecture and maintainability as the JavaScript language and the engine continue to evolve. • Track and shape the JavaScript standard: Work with the JavaScript team to keep up with the evolving TC39 standard, and help evaluate proposals where performance or implementation tradeoffs are involved. • Strengthen engine security: Collaborate with the Fuzzing team to identify and close security issues in the JavaScript engine. • Mentor and elevate the team: Mentor other engineers by stewarding some of your existing responsibilities to more junior team members as you take on new, larger initiatives, and help the team build stronger performance-analysis and benchmarking practices. • Communicate clearly: Turn team strategy and goals into concrete action for the projects you lead, and communicate progress, tradeoffs, and technical decisions clearly to your team and to partner teams. • Use AI tools day to day: Incorporate AI-assisted tools into your workflow for debugging, prototyping, code analysis, and development. What you’ll bring: • 5+ years developing C++ applications • Demonstrated expertise in systems programming, including direct experience with one or more of: compilers, garbage collectors, interpreters, and virtual machines • Strong background in performance analysis, profiling, and benchmarking on large and complex software • Experience with general systems architecture (x86, x64, ARM, ARM64, etc.) • A track record of technical leadership: planning ambiguous projects, sequencing project work, and mitigating risk with minimal help • Experience mentoring
