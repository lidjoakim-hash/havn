Balance leadership, labour law, and staffing while driving efficient HR processes for Sellpy Operations. From coaching to strategic site development, lead our local HR team and build a safe workplace. Sellpy’s vision is to empower everyone to live circularly. We see a tremendous financial, social and environmental value in making sure unused items are put to use again. We are looking for colleagues who share this vision, and who are ready to see their work contribute to a sustainable future. HR Manager Interim, at Sellpy We’re looking for a grounded, operational HR professional who thrives in a fast-paced logistics environment and is passionate about driving a modern, proactive People Operations function. In this interim role, you will take full ownership of leading and developing our local HR function at the site in Rosersberg. You will act as a trusted advisor to our Site Manager and local management, ensuring our site meets staffing targets while fostering a safe, empowered, and sustainable work environment. You will dive into labor law - specifically Lager- och e-handelsavtalet - manage union relations, and streamline everything from recruitment to on- and offboarding journeys. Together with the site management, you'll balance day-to-day coaching with continuous improvement projects that boost site performance and scalability. In short, you will • Provide first-line leadership support and coaching to managers across daily employee relations and operational challenges. • Lead, coach, and develop the local HR team at our Rosersberg site. The team consists of one HR Generalist and one Recruiter. • Drive staffing strategies to meet operational targets, balancing direct warehouse recruitment and temporary agency staffing. • Manage labor relations, acting as the primary point of contact for collective bargaining agreements (Lager- och e-handelsavtalet), union collaboration, safety delegates, and occupational health services. • Oversee systematic work environment management (SAM) in close partnership with the Site Manager to ensure a secure and healthy workplace. • Manage authority liaise with relevant agencies and handle work permit applications with Migrationsverket when needed. • Build, optimise, and maintain smooth employee journeys, from onboarding and offboarding to HR system administration and payroll preparations. We use • Modern HRM and ATS platforms for end-to-end People Operations and recruitment tracking • Google Workspace and collaborative communication tools Requirements • A Bachelor’s degree in Human Resources, Labor Relations, Behavioral science, or equivalent practical experience. • Proven experience in an HR Manager role (2+ years) or HRBP role (3+ years) within logistics, e-commerce, manufacturing, or a fast-paced operational setting. • Solid working knowledge of Swedish labor law (arbetsrätt) and practical experience with Swedish collective agreements, preferably Lager- och e-handelsavtalet. • Experience leading or mentoring HR team members and collaborating constructively with trade unions and safety representatives. • Strong organisational skills with the ability to balance hands-on operational duties and structured process improvements. • Pragmatic, business-oriented mindset with high personal integrity and communication skills. • Fluency in both Swedish and English, written and spoken. We’d be impressed if you have • Experience managing temporary staffing agencies and high-volume blue-collar recruitment. • Worked in a fast-growing, product-focused startup or scale-up environment. • Prior experience handling work permit applications with Migrationsverket.
