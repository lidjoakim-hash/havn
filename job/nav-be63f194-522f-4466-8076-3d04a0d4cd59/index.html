Sales Operations Specialist

Clevr As · Sandvika, Norway

Lønn ikke oppgittHeltidvia NAVFrist 30.1.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

As a Sales Operations Specialist, you are an important link between sales, services, customers, finance and our partner Siemens. You’ll join an experienced colleague and help out with tasks that keep our sales operations up and running, while Clevr grows! Hva vi ser etter What you bring to the table • A bachelor/master degree in business administration, economics, marketing economics or a related field • 1&#43; years of experience in relevant field • A detail-oriented and structured approach to work, with the ability to manage multiple tasks simultaneously. • A good understanding of numbers and finance processes such as invoicing and purchasing • A genuine curiosity about our customers, our partners and the values and ambitions that shape our organization. • You enjoy working with a diverse range of people, from customers and Siemens to colleagues across different departments. • Experience with Salesforce or other CRM systems is a plus, but not required • Fluency in Norwegian and good English skills, both written and spoken Arbeidsoppgaver What makes this role unique In this role, you see the whole picture, from the first quote to the final invoice. You'll work closely with Siemens, one of the world's leading technology companies, and become the person sales, services and finance rely on to keep everything on track. You'll be trained by an experienced colleague, take on more responsibility as you grow, and help shape how our sales operations work as CLEVR continues to grow. Your role will include • Register new customers and run credit checks • Process orders, licence transfers and demo licences with Siemens • Handle renewals, cancellations and changes to support agreements and subscriptions • Send and review service proposals • Check that service opportunities and agreements are complete and followed up until signed • Be the go-to person for questions about prices, licences and terms • Support project managers and new employees with access to our systems • Keeping Salesforce data accurate and contributing to the continuous improvement of our sales processes Vi tilbyr What we offer you • Competitive salary. • 30 vacation days. (5 extra paid days) • Learning and development: value creation days, books, and access to the Udemy platform • Excellent insurance plans that cover, among other things, treatments with physiotherapists, chiropractors, and other health services. • Exciting projects and clients where you'll get plenty of responsibility! • Fun social events from our CLEVR Social Club • Annual CLEVRistic party • Your devices: laptop, mobile phone, and accessories
