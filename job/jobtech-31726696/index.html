We are looking for an Electrical & Electronics Interface Engineer who thrives where hardware, software, and electrical systems come together. You bring a curious mindset, enjoy solving cross-functional challenges, and are eager to contribute to technical decisions that shape future products. With us, you will work with innovation and future-oriented large-scale laundry machines! WHAT YOU’LL BE DOING We want to focus on future product features 3–5 years ahead, which is why we have chosen to expand the team with an additional colleague. In this role, you will have the opportunity to influence technical decisions at an early stage, help shape the products of the future and most importantly work with innovation rather than purely maintenance and sustainment tasks. Your responsibilities will include: Act as a technical bridge between electrical design and electronics. Contribute to long-term technical strategy and future product features. Understand and contribute to PCB development, including both hardware and software aspects. Maintain technical documentation and ensure that design data, drawings, specifications, and revisions are correctly managed and released according to established processes in PDM systems. Participate in the development of specifications for electronic control boards. Ensure seamless interaction between electrical systems, electronics, and control systems. Take part in testing, analysis, and further development of solutions. WHAT’S NEEDED FOR YOU TO THRIVE We believe you are relatively early in your career but already demonstrate a strong technical drive and ability to take ownership. You enjoy working where hardware, software, and electrical systems meet, and you are motivated by solving complex technical challenges together with experienced colleagues. Qualification: A few years of experience working at the interface between electrical systems, electronics, software, or embedded systems Solid understanding of PCB development, control boards, and control systems Comfortable working across both hardware and software domains, with the ability to understand how the two interact in a complete product An eagerness to learn and grow technically, combined with a senior mindset and the confidence to challenge solutions and contribute to technical discussions Strong communication skills and the ability to collaborate across multiple engineering disciplines Knowledge of EMC, electrical safety, and relevant standards is considered a merit Analytical, communicative, and solution-oriented mindset Master’s degree in ex. Electrical Engineering /Electronics/Embedded Systems, Mechatronics/Engineering Physics/Mechanical Engineering. You will be part of a team with high technical seniority and strong team spirit. The team is international, and therefore English is the primary working language. Knowledge of Swedish is considered a merit.
