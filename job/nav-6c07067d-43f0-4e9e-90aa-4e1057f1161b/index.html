We are currently looking for skilled and passionate On-Call Chefs (Casual/Freelance) to join our culinary team ahead of an upcoming busy season, supporting both our Færd restaurant and our bustling Banquet & Events kitchen. Skills & qualifications needed  • Solid kitchen experience - preferably from high-volume hotels, top-tier restaurants, or premium banquet operations. • Passion for culinary craftsmanship and working with quality seasonal ingredients. • Ability to thrive in a fast-paced environment while keeping a calm overview and high precision. • Strong team spirit, a positive &#34;can-do&#34; attitude, and great communication skills. • Flexibility to take on shifts (days, evenings, and weekends) during busy peaks. You identify with the following You thrive in a high pressure and dynamic environment  ensuring a consistently high level of productivity and operational standards paying particular attention to details. You are a true team player, approachable and have a can-do attitude. Responsibilities • Prepare high-quality mise en place and dishes for à la carte service or banquet buffets and plated banquets. • Maintain consistency and strict standards in taste, presentation, and craftsmanship. • Ensure smooth, efficient execution during busy services and event rollouts. • Adhere to all kitchen hygiene, food safety, and HACCP standards. • Collaborate closely with the kitchen team and front of house to provide a seamless guest journey. What we can offer (amongst other things) • 25% discount on food & beverages at all our restaurants & bars • Pay according to applicable tariff • Staff discounts and friends & family rates at our 200&#43; hotels • Sweet deals on experiences, travels, retail and lots more! • The opportunity to grow and develop in an inspiring environment • Access to Farris Bad's award winning spa Are we the perfect match? This position is an on-call hourly contract, reporting to the Head Chef of the respective kitchen section (Banquet or À la Carte). If you see yourself in the description above, we’d love to hear from you!  We look forward to reviewing your application.  Interviews will be held throughout the application period.  We hire attitude ….and train skills
