Workplace descriptionThe projects will be performed within the Cell, Tissue and Organ Engineering group led by Dr. Paul Bourgine. Bone is a complex Organ offering structural and mechanical support of our body but also consisting in our principal hematopoietic center. The formation of Bone involves distinct stages, including cartilage, vasculature, bone and bone marrow establishment. The Bourgine lab aims at compiling human-specific knowledge on these processes, toward the development of innovative Regenerative Therapies. Within the research group, we are committed to fostering a positive work environment characterized by respect and consideration in our relationships with one another. We continuously strive to create conditions that promote job satisfaction, professional development, and inclusion for all employees. We are now seeking a Postdoctoral Researcher for a full-time fixed-term position, with a start date of 1st of February 2027 or by agreement. What we offerLund University is a government agency, which means you will receive special benefits, generous annual leave, and a favorable occupational pension. Read more on the university’s website about working at Lund University: Work with us. Duties and responsibilitiesThe Postdoctoral Researcher will work with the following tasks: Development of novel culture systems for tissue engineering. Isolation and differentiation of primary human cells. Identification of cellular and molecular events throughout stages of endochondral ossification. Other responsibilities and work tasks may also be relevant. Eligibility requirementsTo be eligible for a postdoctoral position, you must have a PhD or a foreign degree deemed equivalent to a PhD. This requirement must be fulfilled at the time of the employment decision, and proof must be submitted by that date. Exceptions can be made in cases such as parental leave, illness, clinical duties, union commitments, or similar. Assessment criteriaAt Lund University, postdoctoral positions are intended to provide opportunities for academic qualification. The position primarily allows for research merit, but also includes limited higher education teaching. Teaching may be included in the role. The main assessment criterion is scientific expertise in the subject area. Required qualificationsThe position requires: PhD in Biology or Bioengineering Documented postdoctoral experiences in different laboratories At least three publications as first author in a peer-reviewed journal  Documented experience in vascular biology and engineering of vessels Robust experience in 3D culture applied to ossification Strong laboratory and analytical skills, with the ability to work both independently and within an interdisciplinary team. Robust experience in culture and differentiation of human primary cells in 2D and 3D Experience in microfluidics and microscopy Experience in prototyping with AutoCAD Good knowledge of English (written and spoken). Merits include: Experience with human mesenchymal cell culture and differentiation Background in biomaterials science and/or polymer synthesis. Experience with 3D printing/additive manufacturing and associated software Experience as executive member of scientific societies  Terms of employment This is a fixed-term, full-time position for two years, starting on 2027-02-01 or as agreed.Employment at Lund University takes place in Sweden, and you are therefore expected to carry out your work on site in Lund. The appointment is limited to a minimum of two years and a maximum of three years, in accordance with the collective agreement on fixed-term employment as a postdoc (Arbetsgivarverket). A postdoctoral position is intended as an initial step in a research career and an opportunity to deepen and broaden research expertise.
