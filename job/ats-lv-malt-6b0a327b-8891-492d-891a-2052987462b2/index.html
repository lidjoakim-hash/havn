Explore your future career  🔭 We're looking for an AI & Automation Engineer Intern to join Malt's Data Platform team for 6 months starting January 2027. You will work directly with Léo Martin, our Lead AI Deployment Engineer, as part of Malt's AI transformation. You will contribute to three major production workstreams: Malt Mind (our internal AI-agent platform), n8n & Automation (extending our workflow automation platform), and AI Academy Builders (enabling internal AI Builders to ship their own automations autonomously). Key responsibilities ✨ • Support the execution of Malt's AI internal roadmap for H1 2027; contribute to building our internal Agentic Stack, n8n workflows integrating Salesforce, Zendesk, BigQuery, and Slack. • Build adoption dashboards to track agent usage and iterate on Knowledge Base segmentation to reduce hallucinations. • Contribute to designing and building an evaluations for our internal AI agents (accuracy, hallucination rate, knowledge base coverage). • Implement reliability patterns on the automation platform and document architecture patterns with guidance from the team. • Support the AI Academy program to support our AI Champions & Builders to drive transformation in their department: create training materials, workflow templates, and best-practice guides. • Co-facilitate Buildathons and track AI Academy Builder programme KPIs. About you 🧑‍🚀 • High-level education ideally in Computer Science, Data, Product Management, or a related field (BAC+4/5). • You like building things, not just talking about them: comfortable writing small scripts, wiring n8n/Make/Zapier workflows, and prompting LLMs. • You follow AI developments (LLMs, agents, RAG, evals) and have hands-on experience with at least one framework: OpenAI API, LangChain, Dust, Mistral, or similar. • Comfortable with SQL and data; you measure the impact of what you ship and know a KPI without a baseline is just a number. • Professional working proficiency in English. How to join the mission? 🚀 • A first call with Ru-Syl, ou Talent Acquisition Specialist, to understand your background, your motivations, and to answer any questions you may have about the role or the company. • A second interview with our AI Deployment Engineer to discuss technical topics, problem-solving approach, and ways of working. • Final interview will be a short take-home exercise to present in person.
