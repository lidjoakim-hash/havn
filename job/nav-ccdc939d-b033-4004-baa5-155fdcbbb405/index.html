Experienced Waiters – Ristorante Porto Sociale

RISTORANTE PORTO SOCIALE · Oslo, Norway

Waiters Wanted – Join the New Porto Sociale in Oslo! Be part of an exciting new chapter in Italian hospitality! Porto Sociale, an Italian restaurant in the heart of Oslo, is preparing for an exciting relaunch. With a major renovation, a refreshed concept, and ambitious plans for the future, we aim to create one of Oslo's most exciting Italian dining destinations. Our vision is to combine authentic Italian cuisine, exceptional wines, and warm, personal service in an elegant yet welcoming atmosphere.   We are now looking for passionate and service-minded waiters who want to help us create memorable dining experiences and become part of our new team. Your responsibilities • Provide professional, attentive, and personalized service to our guests. • Welcome guests, present menus, and take food and beverage orders. • Recommend dishes, wines, and other beverages based on guests' preferences. • Ensure smooth and efficient service throughout the dining experience. • Work closely with the kitchen, bar, and management team. • Maintain high standards of presentation, cleanliness, and organization. • Contribute to a positive working environment and an exceptional guest experience. Who are we looking for? • Previous experience as a waiter, preferably from quality restaurants, fine dining, or premium hospitality. • A genuine passion for hospitality and creating memorable guest experiences. • Good knowledge of food and beverages, particularly wine. • An interest in Italian cuisine, wines, and culture. • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills. • A professional, positive, and outgoing personality. • Ability to remain organized and deliver excellent service during busy periods. • Strong teamwork skills and a willingness to learn. • Good English communication skills. Norwegian or Italian is an advantage. • Flexibility to work evenings and weekends. We value personality, attitude, and genuine enthusiasm for hospitality just as much as experience. What we offer • The opportunity to be part of an exciting restaurant relaunch in central Oslo. • A newly renovated restaurant with ambitious plans for the future. • A professional and welcoming working environment. • The chance to work with authentic Italian cuisine and quality wines. • Training and opportunities to develop your knowledge of Italian food, wine, and hospitality. • Opportunities for professional growth and increased responsibility. • Competitive salary based on experience and qualifications. • The chance to help shape the service culture of the new Porto Sociale. Interested? Are you passionate about hospitality, enjoy working with people, and want to be part of something exciting? We would love to hear from you!   Send us your CV and a short introduction about yourself, your experience, and why you would like to join Porto Sociale.   Join us and help create an Italian dining experience that guests will remember!
