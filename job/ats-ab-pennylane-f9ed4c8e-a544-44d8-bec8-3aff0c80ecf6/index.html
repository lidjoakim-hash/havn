YOUR IMPACT Every great employee experience starts with great IT. At Pennylane, our mission is to make technology simple, seamless and secure, so our teams can focus on building the future of finance. As an IT Support Administrator, you'll be one of the main points of contact for our employees. You'll help them stay productive, improve the way we work and contribute to building an IT environment that scales with our growth. WHAT YOU'LL DO Deliver an outstanding IT experience Whether it's resolving technical issues, answering questions or helping employees get the most out of our tools, you'll make sure every Pennylaner has a smooth IT experience. Employee onboarding & offboarding You'll make sure every employee has everything they need before day one. From day one to the last day, you'll make sure every employee has everything they need to work successfully. You'll prepare laptops, provision accounts, manage permissions and introduce new joiners to our IT environment and security best practices. You'll also handle offboarding and manage IT operations for freelancers and external partners. Keep our IT environment secure Security is everyone's responsibility. You'll play an important role in making security part of everyone's daily habits. You'll help monitor compliance with our internal IT policies, manage software access, review permissions and contribute to maintaining a secure environment for the whole company. Manage our internal tools You'll administer our SaaS ecosystem by creating accounts, managing permissions and ensuring employees have the right access at the right time. You'll also help improve how we manage and document our internal tools. Share knowledge You'll contribute to building a strong IT culture across the company. That means writing clear documentation, improving our knowledge base, delivering onboarding sessions and helping employees adopt best practices around IT and security. WHO ARE YOU?
