Development Manager, Creative Studios

H & M Hennes & Mauritz GBC AB · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 450 000–550 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 17.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Job Description At H&M Creative Studios, we develop and deliver H&M’s customer-facing brand and content experiences globally. Bringing together strategists, creatives, designers, creators, producers and specialists, we are responsible for H&M’s global campaigns, editorial storytelling, creator marketing, organic social channels, e-commerce content, brand design and physical brand experiences. Together, we shape how millions of customers discover, experience and engage with H&M every day. WHAT YOU’LL DO As a Development Manager, Creative Production, you’ll play a key role in transforming and evolving H&M Creative Studios by leading strategic initiatives that improve workflows, processes, technology, and operational excellence. You’ll work at the intersection of creativity, business, and technology, helping the organisation scale efficiently while enabling creative teams to deliver exceptional content. You’ll lead cross-functional initiatives from concept through implementation, ensuring solutions are practical, measurable, and aligned with business objectives. From AI adoption and workflow optimisation to automation and production process development, you’ll help shape the future of creative production at H&M. Independently lead cross-functional development initiatives across H&M Creative Studios, including process optimisation, workflow improvements, technology implementation, AI adoption, and facility development. Design scalable production processes and workflows that support efficient and future-focused creative operations. Translate business needs into system requirements, automation opportunities, and technology solutions. Build business cases, define KPIs, and establish success metrics to measure the impact of initiatives. Drive implementation of new tools, technologies, and ways of working that improve efficiency, quality, and scalability. Partner closely with Development Managers and stakeholders across H&M Creative Studios to align priorities and secure successful delivery. Identify opportunities for continuous improvement across creative production, operational processes, and resource utilisation. Support change management efforts and help teams adopt new solutions, technologies, and workflows. WHO YOU’LL WORK WITH You’ll collaborate with Creative Production teams, Creative Strategy & Execution teams Technology Partners, Product Owners, Development Managers and a wide range of creative and operational stakeholders across H&M Creative Studios. Together, you’ll drive innovation and build the foundations for future-ready creative production capabilities. WHO YOU ARE We are looking for people with… Proven experience leading cross-functional transformation, operational excellence, or process improvement initiatives. Strong understanding of creative production environments and the workflows that support content creation at scale. Experience with business development, automation, workflow optimisation, or MarTech-related initiatives. Strong technology and AI fluency, with an understanding of how emerging tools can create value in creative production. Experience analysing business needs and translating them into scalable systems, processes, and technology solutions. Strong project and stakeholder management experience across complex organisations. Experience building business cases, defining KPIs, and measuring the success of implementation programs. And people who are… Strategic and forward-thinking, with a passion for innovation and continuous improvement. Analytical and structured, able to turn complexity into clear and actionable solutions. Collaborative and effective at building relationships across creative, operational, and technical teams. Curious about emerging technologies and excited by the opportunities AI can bring to creative production. Strong communicators who can influence stakeholders and drive alignment across functions. Resilient and adaptable, with the ability to lead through change and ambiguity.
