We are looking for an Executive Recruiter to identify, engage, and hire exceptional leadership talent across Datadog. In this role, you will partner closely with senior executives to lead complex, high-impact Senior Director+ searches across our General & Administrative, Sales, and Technical Solutions organizations in North America and EMEA. You will operate as a trusted advisor throughout the search process – developing search strategies, building market intelligence, assessing executive talent, and creating a highly personalized candidate experience. This role requires strong judgment, discretion, executive presence, and the ability to translate evolving business needs into a clear and actionable talent strategy. At Datadog, we place value in our office culture—the relationships and collaboration it builds and the creativity it brings to the table. We operate as a hybrid workplace to ensure our Datadogs can create a work-life harmony that best fits them. What You’ll Do: • Lead end-to-end executive searches for senior leadership roles, from intake and search strategy through assessment, offer, and close • Partner directly with executives and senior leaders to define leadership profiles, calibrate requirements, and serve as a trusted advisor on talent strategy • Develop comprehensive market maps and talent landscapes, using research and market intelligence to identify high-caliber passive executive talent • Build and cultivate relationships with senior executives through highly targeted outreach, thoughtful engagement, and long-term pipeline development • Assess executive candidates against business needs, leadership capabilities, organizational context, and long-term potential, providing clear recommendations to senior stakeholders • Deliver a high-touch and confidential candidate experience while continuously evolving our executive search strategy, assessment practices, and approach to the market Who You Are: • 8+ years of recruiting experience, including ownership of executive or senior leadership searches • Skilled in executive search methodologies, including talent mapping, market research, targeted sourcing, executive assessment, and closing • Experience partnering directly with senior executives, with the credibility and executive presence to influence, challenge assumptions, and drive alignment • Strong at assessing senior talent beyond resume qualifications, with sound judgment around leadership capabilities, organizational fit, and career motivation • An exceptional communicator who can synthesize market intelligence, candidate insights, and search progress into clear recommendations for senior stakeholders • Comfortable navigating ambiguity, managing sensitive and confidential searches, and balancing multiple complex searches while maintaining a highly personalized candidate experience Datadog values people from all walks of life. We understand not everyone will meet all the above qualifications on day one. That's okay. If you’re passionate about technology and want to grow your skills, we encourage you to apply. Benefits and Growth: • Generous and competitive benefits package • New hire stock equity (RSUs) and employee stock purchase plan • Continuous career development and pathing opportunities • Product training to develop an in-depth understanding of our product and space • Best in breed onboarding • Internal mentor and buddy program cross-departmentally • Friendly and inclusive workplace culture Benefits and Growth listed above may vary based on the country of your employment and the nature of your employment with Datadog.
