About the team The Consumer Bank squad sits at the heart of one of SumUp's most exciting growth bets — scaling SumUp Pay, our consumer fintech product, to millions of active users across Europe. The team owns end-to-end UX and product design across the full user lifecycle, from onboarding and activation through to engagement features like Spaces, Open Banking insights, and budget tracking. This is a high-craft, high-autonomy environment where design isn't just execution — it's central to defining the problems worth solving, and your work will directly shape adoption, retention, and the future of SumUp's consumer offering.
