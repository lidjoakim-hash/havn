Software Engineer - SAR Processing Platform Role highlights: Job title: Software Engineer Location: Espoo, Finland Department: SAR Processing Platform Reports to: Team Lead SAR Processing Platform Employment type: Permanent Workplace model: Hybrid - 3 days in the office Employment is subject to applicable security screening (incl. SUPO, where required) Why this role matters: As a Software Engineer, you’ll help build and run the platform that every ICEYE image passes through before a customer sees it. The SAR Processing Platform validates raw radar data, decides when and where to process it, runs the processing workflows, catalogs the products and gates their delivery. You’ll join a small team that owns this orchestration layer end to end, covering how incoming data is validated, scheduled and processed, how products are cataloged and delivered, and the tools operators use to run it. The platform runs in the cloud and on on-premises clusters at customer sites, and you’ll work daily with SAR processing engineers, other engineering teams across the platform, DevOps, product and delivery. Who We Are ICEYE is the world leader in sovereign intelligence from space. We deliver persistent monitoring capabilities to detect and respond to changes in any location on Earth. ICEYE owns the world’s largest and most advanced SAR (synthetic aperture radar) satellite constellation. To our customers we provide intelligence with unmatched quality, latency and revisit times, in any weather, day or night. To governments who choose to operate their own constellation we provide this proven capability as a sovereign system. ICEYE-built constellations serve customers in defence and intelligence, environmental monitoring, insurance and emergency management. We enable fast decisions that contribute to a safer future. Founded and headquartered in Finland, ICEYE operates globally with over 1000 employees across Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. Your day-to-day responsibilities - Build and maintain the backend services that schedule, process and catalog satellite data - Write and tune the processing workflows: resource sizing, reliability and integration of new processor versions - Keep production healthy: debug failed or stuck jobs from logs, metrics and the database, and turn recurring incidents into fixes or tooling - Ship through automated, version-controlled deployments to test, staging, production and on-prem environments, and roll out new processor versions and products with the SAR processing engineers using gradual rollouts and quality gates - Write tests, documentation and runbooks, and review teammates’ code What we’re looking for Must haves: - 2 to 5 years of backend software engineering experience, with solid Go and working Python, and willingness to read and occasionally change Rust - Hands-on Kubernetes and Docker as a developer: you have deployed, debugged and sized workloads yourself, not only used a cluster someone else ran - PostgreSQL (schema design, migrations, query debugging) and AWS fundamentals (S3, IAM, EKS or equivalent) - Experience running software in production, including incident debugging and postmortems - Pragmatic use of AI/LLM tools in daily development, fluent English, and comfort asking and answering questions in a distributed team Nice to haves: - Argo Workflows, Airflow or similar; NATS or Kafka; any Rust experience - Geospatial or remote-sensing data (GeoTIFF, COG, STAC, GDAL), or any large-file scientific data pipeline - Helm, Flux, Terraform; Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry Application Process 1.
