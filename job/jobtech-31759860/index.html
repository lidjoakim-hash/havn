Senior Product Security Vulnerability Coordinator

Hitachi Energy Sweden AB · Västerås, Sweden

💰 ca. 450 000–540 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 18.2.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

The opportunity Lead the identification, assessment, and mitigation of security vulnerabilities across the product portfolio. You will coordinate timely responses and vulnerability disclosures, connect engineering, product, compliance, and security teams, and help ensure products meet relevant standards and regulatory requirements. This role provides broad visibility across global teams and direct influence on product security, customer trust, and business risk decisions. You will also improve vulnerability processes, reporting, awareness, and remediation effectiveness as the threat and regulatory landscape evolves. How you’ll make an impact Lead product vulnerability management from identification and triage through remediation, verification, and closure. Act as the primary coordination point for internal and external product vulnerability disclosures and coordinate communication among stakeholders and security teams. Maintain accurate vulnerability records, ownership, evidence, timelines, and status reporting to support timely resolution and management visibility. Work with engineering, product, and compliance teams to implement security controls and verify that remediation is effective. Monitor emerging threats and vulnerability trends, recommend proactive measures, and support incident response, root cause analysis, and post-incident reviews. Communicate business impact to senior management and educate product and engineering teams on secure practices and vulnerability awareness. Your background Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Security, or a related field. Minimum five years of experience in product security, vulnerability management, or cyber risk coordination. Strong knowledge of vulnerability disclosure processes, security frameworks, and regulatory requirements, including the EU Cyber Resilience Act, ISO/IEC 29147, and ISO/IEC 30111. Proficiency with vulnerability assessment tools, secure development practices, remediation verification, and incident response. Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with proven experience coordinating across global technical and business teams. CISSP, CISM, or a GIAC certification is highly desirable.
