Scania is undergoing a transformation from being a leading supplier of trucks, buses, and engines to providing complete and sustainable transport solutions. Together with TRATON and our sister brands MAN, Volkswagen Truck & Bus, and International, we are working to shape the future of mobility with innovative and environmentally conscious solutions. Our values – customer first, respect, team spirit, responsibility, and elimination of waste – are at the heart of everything we do. Together, we are at the forefront of creating a sustainable future. Role Summary As a Certification Engineer within Scania BID, you will work mainly with your own certification areas as an expert within Internal Combustion Engines (ICE) and Electrified (e-power) solutions for Power Solution (Power Generation, Industrial and Marine) applications. You will support designers and other teams enabling compliant and certified products, including improving internal processes, coaching teams, and performing inspections and certification activities. The team works together with the designers to make sure they understand the regulatory requirements and have a robust plan to successfully meet them. Job Responsibilities As a certification engineer within Homologation you will handle certification related questions within Internal Combustion Engines and Electrified solutions for Power Solution applications and contributing to the team deliveries. Your responsibilities will include: • Planning and performing certification inspections, tests and audits with technical service and authorities. • Coaching and inspiring the involved teams from e.g. design, project managers with details regarding our products necessary for type approvals. • Monitor the legal requirements changes for your legal areas, working through Scania’s distributors and participate in lobbying activities. • Improve the processes and the collaboration with others. • Follow up deviations and implementations. • Supporting the markets with details regarding our product necessary for the national type approvals. Who You Are We are looking for a curious person inspired by challenging objectives. An engineer with drive and preferably experience with certification, Internal Combustion Engines (ICE) and/or emissions. Working in a multicultural team, to be fluent in English – both written and spoken is a prerequisite. You are: Enthusiastic, have a can-do mentality combined with a structured way of working and prioritize among long term and short term tasks. Flexible and adapt actions and behaviour depending on pre-requisites, while still maintaining a high integrity. A team player capable of working and delivering results together with others as well as on your own, and you take the initiative to solve complex tasks. Have a talent for cross-functional work and are good at developing and maintaining a broad network within the company. This Is Us The department of Product Compliance consists of two groups, Homologation, ECH and National Regulations, ECR working closely together with compliance of Scania products. The work is performed both internally at Scania BID as well as with our colleagues within TRATON group. We are organized in smaller teams for closer cooperation, valuing team spirit within our entire network, offering support and guidance. We succeed, fail and learn as a team! Scania Offers We offer a dynamic and engaging workplace where collaboration, innovation, and continuous improvement are part of everyday life. You will be part of a strong team environment that encourages knowledge sharing and close cooperation across functions. With a structured development plan and a wide range of training opportunities, Scania supports your professional growth both locally and internationally. Benefits include access to our health center in Gröndal or a wellness allowance, result bonus, flexible working hours, and company car leasing.
