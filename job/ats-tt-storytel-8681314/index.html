Stories have the power to make the ordinary extraordinary. They help us think bigger, see beyond ourselves, and deepen our connection with others. As one of the world's leading audiobook and e-book streaming platforms, Storytel brings unlimited listening to millions of users across 25+ markets. Driven by our purpose "Leading the future of storytelling, we move the world through stories", Storytel Group inspires and entertains people around the world by blending innovation with tradition. We bring stories to life across various formats for everyone to discover. Anytime. Anywhere. Ready for your next chapter? We're looking for a Social & Creator Marketer to join the team in Amsterdam! About the role In the Netherlands and Flanders, people discover stories on social media and through the creators they follow. At Storytel, we put organic social and creator-led content at the heart of how we grow. This is a broad role that goes beyond social media alone, covering organic social, influencer marketing and user-generated content (UGC), with Instagram and TikTok as our first priorities. What you'll do: • Plan and run our organic social strategy and day-to-day channels for the Netherlands and Flanders • Be the face of our content: step in front of the camera for formats like our street interviews (vox pops) • Brief and approve content made by external agencies, content creators and publishers • Lead our influencer marketing: select, brief and manage our influencers and ambassadors, and manage the yearly budget • Select and brief creators for user-generated content (UGC) • Track what works and use it to sharpen the next round of content About the team You'll be part of the marketing team in Amsterdam, reporting to the Country Manager. You'll work closely every day with the Marketing Manager and the Content Manager, and with the agencies, creators and publishers who help us bring stories to life. About you To be successful in this role, we believe that you have: • At least a couple of years of experience in social media, creator marketing and content creation, with strong hands-on knowledge of Instagram and TikTok • A natural, confident presence on camera and a good feel for what works in short-form video • Experience selecting, briefing and managing influencers, creators and agencies, including UGC, and keeping budgets in check • Professional language skills in Dutch and English, and a knowledge about the Dutch and Flemish audience • A team-oriented mindset that thrives in an organization where you work together with others • A personality that fits our team values: open, enthusiastic and helpful, with a sociable and collaborative spirit Nice to have: video editing skills, and a portfolio or your own channels we can look at. To thrive with us, you enjoy being in a dynamic environment where things move fast. You're accountable and take ownership of your work, while being brave enough to challenge the status quo and dare to write the next chapter. We always play to win as a team, so you're collaborative and know great stories are never told alone. What we offer you • Team get-togethers every quarter • Hybrid work arrangement with a minimum of two office days a week • Office lunches • A company fitness scheme • A mobile phone allowance • A Storytel subscription • A beautiful office with a view over the IJ • 20 days per year of working from anywhere • A small team of 10 colleagues who are open, enthusiastic, helpful and sociable, with a healthy work-life balance Join a world of stories At Storytel, we're a team of creative story lovers who thrive on collaboration and new ideas. Our workplace is friendly, dynamic, and full of opportunities to experiment and make an impact. We believe in trust, flat hierarchies, and empowering you to grow with us.
