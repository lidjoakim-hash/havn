Multilingual (da/no/de/pl/fr/it) Customer Service / Sales Support for an exciting company Would you like to work with strong brands and interesting products? Would you like a broad and varied role in a friendly customer service team? Are you also driven, positive and multilingual? If so, you might be just the person we’re looking for! Our client is a successful company with international operations that is now looking for a dedicated Customer Service / Sales Support who is fluent in English and Danish, Norwegian, German, Polish, French, or Italian. You’ll be part of a great customer service team where you’ll professionally help customers find the right solution, primarily via email and phone. You’ll work in customer service and consultative sales, guiding customers towards the right solutions in a sales-oriented manner. You’ll also handle after-sales matters and provide some technical support. The role requires good communication skills and a solution-oriented approach. This is an on-site position (not remote). Key responsibilities: Assist customers with advice and problem-solving Carry out consultative sales Handle after-sales matters Contribute to high-quality customer support and an optimal customer experience Your profile: We are looking for someone with experience in sales, sales support or customer service. We would like you to have technical skills and a problem-solving aptitude, combined with strong communication skills. You are a confident computer user and feel comfortable in a digital working environment. It is essential that you have excellent written communication skills with fluency in English and Danish, Norwegian, German, Polish, French, or Italian. Knowledge of Swedish is an advantage. As a person, you are curious, down-to-earth and solution oriented. You enjoy taking responsibility, working independently and are committed to providing excellent service with a customer-focused approach. About the Position: This is a permanent position starting as soon as possible. The role is based in northwestern Skåne. We review applications on an ongoing basis, so we encourage you to submit your application as soon as possible via the application link. We look forward to your application!
