Company Description Welcome to a world, where your ideas lead to something big. Welcome to Bosch! Bosch Thermoteknik AB in Tranås develops, manufactures, and sells heat pumps for the European market. We are approximately 650 employees and form part of the large Bosch Group, which consists of approximately 417,000 employees worldwide. We work with sustainable simplification of everyday life where the focus is on the environment, innovation, and development. In Tranås there is a production unit and a competence and development center. The heat pumps we manufacture draw energy from different sources - the mountain, the water, or the air. We work to constantly be at the forefront and contribute to sustainable energy and a better environment with the goal of creating a simplified everyday life for our customers. In short, we create technology that is "Invented for life". About us We support managers and executives in their environmental and working environment responsibilities. We comply with requirements according to laws and certifications as well as directives within the company group and communicate continuously internally. Job Description Your contribution to something big Our department drives the company's sustainability work, and your internship work will contribute to the implementation of local activities and initiatives in this area. We also need to strengthen our team with an intern who is interested in environmental technology, has a basic understanding of ISO 14001 or similar standards. The internship includes, among other things, environmental technology, waste management, chemical substances, reporting and project management. We want you to have experience in problem solving, collecting and analyzing data, contributing to the development of training materials and work instructions. Duration of the internship: 6 months, preferably starting February 2027 Location: on-site in our plant in Tranås, Sweden Qualifications What distinguishes you You are an organizer, happy to take on projects and organize events in the area of sustainability. Our company language is English, but we work on production projects and it would be beneficial if you could also communicate in Swedish, so a basic knowledge of Swedish is an advantage. Ongoing studies in Industrial Engineering and Management, Sustainability Management, Safety & Environmental Engineering, or a related field. Knowledge and experience of Microsoft Office 365 with a special emphasis on Word Excel and PowerPoint. Good communication skills, both verbal and written. Problem solving skills. Please note: Only applications from students that are currently studying are accepted.
