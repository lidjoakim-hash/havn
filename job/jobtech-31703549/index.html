Teacher Librarian, Bladins International School of Malmö

At Bladins International School of Malmö (BISM), we are looking for an enthusiastic Teacher Librarian to join our Primary Years Programme (PYP) and Middle Years Programme (MYP) in January 2027. If you are passionate about inspiring a love of reading, developing information literacy and supporting learning in an international school, we would love to hear from you. About Bladins International School of Malmö BISM is an IB Continuum School in central Malmö, offering the Primary Years Programme (PYP) and Middle Years Programme (MYP) to approximately 360 students from diverse nationalities and cultures. As part of Bladins Foundation, a non-profit educational organisation, we provide a supportive, internationally minded learning environment where students grow into knowledgeable, caring and responsible global citizens. The Role As Teacher Librarian, you will promote reading, inquiry-based learning and students' research, information literacy and critical thinking skills across PYP and MYP. BISM has two dedicated libraries, one for PYP and one for MYP. You will develop these as welcoming and inclusive learning spaces and work closely with teachers to support learning across the curriculum. Key responsibilities include: Developing and managing both school libraries and their print and digital collections Promoting reading for pleasure through library lessons, initiatives and events Teaching information literacy, research skills, digital literacy and critical thinking Collaborating with teachers to support PYP units of inquiry and MYP subjects, with awareness of the curriculum across MYP Supporting students' Approaches to Learning (ATL) skills, including ethical use of information, referencing and academic integrity Ensuring resources reflect the school's international, multilingual community and diverse learning needs Building positive relationships with students, colleagues and families and engaging with the wider BISM community Contributing to school development and a welcoming library environment Who We Are Looking For We are looking for a qualified, proactive Teacher Librarian with a passion for literacy and student learning. You are: Qualified in library and information science, preferably with school library experience Experienced in supporting children's and young people's reading, research and information literacy Knowledgeable about children's and young adult literature and able to inspire readers of different ages Skilled in inquiry-based learning and collaboration with classroom and subject teachers Confident using digital library systems, educational technology and online resources Inclusive, organised and able to manage library activities independently A positive communicator and collaborative member of an international team Committed to professional development Experience with the International Baccalaureate (IB), particularly PYP and/or MYP, is an advantage. Understanding the IB philosophy, Learner Profile and Approaches to Learning (ATL) is highly valued. As English is the school's working language, professional spoken and written English is required. Why Join BISM? At BISM, you will join a welcoming international community committed to students, colleagues and learning. We offer supportive colleagues, professional development, an IB learning environment and the opportunity to make a meaningful difference to students' development. Employment Information Position: Teacher Librarian Employment type: Permanent employment, with a six-month probationary period Working hours: Part-time (60%) Start date: January 2027 Application Applications are reviewed on an ongoing basis. Please apply by 30 November 2026. For more information, contact: Kumari Nevill, Primary School Principal Kumari.nevill@bladins.se We look forward to welcoming a colleague who shares our commitment to high-quality education, collaboration and student success.
