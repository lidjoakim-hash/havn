BEREDSKAPSLYFTET IDEELL FÖREN · Stockholm, Sweden

Connecting Jobs, run by the non-profit organization Beredskapslyftet, supports newcomers in taking their first steps into the Swedish labour market – while at the same time helping employers access new talent. We are now looking for family assistant. In this role, you will support one of the company’s owner with everyday practical tasks. The role is varied and hands-on, ranging from errands and transport to dog walks. Location: Stockholm and Lidingö area Who we are looking for: Family Assistant Responsibilities: Doing shopping, picking up and dropping off goods, and running daily errands Driving and handling transport as needed Walking the dogs when needed Helping with simple practical tasks and keeping things in order Requirements: You have a very good command of English, conversational Swedish is preferred You can understand instructions and communicate clearly in Swedish or English You have a B driving licence valid in Sweden and feel confident driving You love dogs and feel comfortable handling them. You are physically able to take longer dog walks in all kinds of weather You are energetic, quick and proactive, and you enjoy an active working day You are punctual, careful and responsible You are reliable and service-minded What the employer offers: Full-time job (100%) with the possibility of long-term employment Standard working hours, Monday–Friday, daytime Start date to be discussed Salary and benefits to be discussed during the interview Important: This position is part of a project that is co-funded by the European Union through the Asylum, Migration and Integration Fund (AMIF). The project aims to support third-country nationals in their integration into the Swedish labor market. The participants hold residence permits in Sweden but do not have Swedish citizenship or EU/EEA citizenship.
