- Senior Software Engineer (Telemetry) - Fleet Automation, Engineering - Espoo, Finland - Permanent - Hybrid - Employment is subject to applicable security screening (incl. SUPO) The Mission of the Senior Software Engineer (Telemetry) Satellite telemetry does not forgive gaps. Build the systems that make sure there are none. ICEYE's SAR constellation generates a continuous stream of data that fleet operations depend on to function. As a Senior Software Engineer on the Fleet Automation team, you will be a key contributor to the infrastructure that makes that data usable: the pipelines, parsers, and time-series stores that translate raw satellite signals into operational state in near real-time. The scope is expanding, the ownership is real, and the engineering problems are ones you will not find anywhere else. Who We Are ICEYE delivers space-based intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance (ISR) capabilities to governments and allied nations. This includes sovereign and turnkey ISR missions leveraging ICEYE's world-leading synthetic aperture radar (SAR) satellite technology, as well as access to data from the world's largest SAR satellite constellation. These capabilities enable partners to detect and respond to critical changes anywhere on Earth with unprecedented speed and accuracy, day or night and in any weather, supported by ultra high-resolution imagery and high-frequency revisits. As a trusted partner for defense, intelligence, security, and maritime domain awareness, ICEYE's near real-time data creates a tactical advantage for mission-critical operations. Designed for dual use, the platform also serves civil protection and commercial users for natural-catastrophe intelligence, insurance, maritime monitoring (including oil-spill detection), and finance, contributing to global security and community resilience. ICEYE operates internationally with offices in Finland, Poland, Spain, Japan, the UAE, Greece, and the US. The company has more than 900 employees, inspired by the shared vision of improving life on Earth by becoming the global source of truth in Earth Observation. Expected Outcomes and Requirements Outcomes: - Telemetry pipelines ingest, transform, and store satellite data from across the fleet reliably and in near real-time, with minimal manual intervention - Telemetry data stores are performant and well-maintained, including schema design, query optimisation, capacity planning, and zero-downtime production migrations - Anomaly detection and automated recovery systems reduce operational load on the mission operations team - TTC gateway telemetry integration is delivered as a production-grade component ready for customer deployment - Engineering quality across the team is actively raised through code reviews, testing standards, and support of less experienced engineers Role-specific Competences - Strong Python proficiency in production backend systems; experience with Go or Rust is a plus - Proven experience building and operating telemetry systems, data pipelines, and distributed systems in production - Strong working knowledge of PostgreSQL and at least one time-series database such as InfluxDB or Prometheus - Hands-on experience with Kubernetes-native environments, Docker, and infrastructure-as-code tools such as Terraform and Helm - Actively uses AI and LLM tooling as part of their engineering workflow - AWS or equivalent cloud platform experience is a plus Key Competences In addition to the requirements above, we evaluate candidates on these key qualities throughout the hiring process: Intellectual Firepower: Rapidly comprehends, structures and synthesizes complex information, draws accurate conclusions, and communicates them with clarity. Passion and Work Ethic: Brings sustained motivation, resilience, and high personal standards to every challenge.
