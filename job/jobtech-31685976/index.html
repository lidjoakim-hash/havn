Senior Android Developer

Volvo Business Services Aktiebolag · Eskilstuna, Sweden

💰 ca. 560 000–770 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 29.11.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Transport is at the core of modern society. Imagine using your expertise to shape sustainable transport and infrastructure solutions for the future. If you seek to make a difference on a global scale, working with next-gen technologies and the sharpest collaborative teams, then we could be a perfect match. About the Role As an Android Developer at the Operator Assistance department, you will be a part of a collaborative, enthusiastic team, developing intelligent safety systems that enable our operators to drive our machines safely in high-risk environments. In our team we create innovative solutions where hardware, embedded software and deep learning come together to create our SmartView app. SmartView is our intelligent obstacle detection system that gives operators a 360° view of the machine’s surroundings. (More information on what you will work on: https://www.volvoce.com/sv-se/services/volvo-smart-view/ ) We work in an agile environment where the team collaborates to set direction, solve technical challenges, and continuously improve our solutions. In your role as an Android Developer, you will develop intuitive operator interfaces using Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, integrate live camera feeds and machine data, and help bring AI-powered perception and safety alerts into our SmartView app. You will also troubleshoot and optimize the software to ensure reliable performance in demanding machine operating conditions. If you are excited about making real difference in people’s lives and safety on your day-to-day work and seeing the impact of your innovative engineering, this job is for you! Who are you? You enjoy solving real-world problems through software development and are curious about how technology can make machines safer and smarter. You value collaboration, share ideas openly, and take ownership of your work. You enjoy working in cross-functional teams, learning from others, and contributing to solutions in an international environment. You communicate effectively in English and are comfortable taking initiative, asking questions, and exploring new approaches to technical challenges. Technical skills: We are looking for someone with experience in Android development, particularly Kotlin and embedded systems. Experience with the following technologies is valuable: Embedded Android development using Kotlin and C++ Android camera frameworks such as Camera2 or CameraX, or video streaming CAN bus systems and machine data integration CI/CD experience with pipelines Working knowledge of the general mobile landscape, architectures, trends, and emerging technologies. Edge AI frameworks knowledge, such as TensorFlow Lite or ONNX, particularly for vision or audio applications are an advantage Knowledge in Sensor Fusion and Control Theory is an advantage What’s in it for you? This role is in our Eskilstuna site with our modern facilities, and you will be a part of our Technology Center, where you will have access to our exciting machines, have the opportunity to test what you develop and collaborate with all our stakeholders. At our Technology Center in Eskilstuna, you'll have opportunities to develop your technical expertise, explore new technologies, and grow your career within a large international organization. In addition to technology and career opportunities, we offer a solid package of compensation and benefits, including flexible time, internal growth opportunities and compensation benefits such as Occupational pension – ITP (ITP1 & ITP2), profit sharing and company bonus. We value our employees’ well-being, work-life balance and having fun at work. Ready for the next move? If you are curious to explore how we put our words into actions, follow us on LinkedIn and volvogroup.com. Last day to apply: 29th of November 2026 Location: Eskilstuna, Technology Center Headquarters, On-site For questions about the position, please contact: Ceren Robinson, Head of Operator Assistance (ceren.robinson@volvo.com) or Olof Person, Talen
