Who We Are At Trustly, we are building a smarter, faster, and more secure financial future by revolutionising the world of payments. As a global leader in Open Banking Payments, we are establishing Pay by Bank as the new standard at checkout, giving millions of consumers and merchants worldwide unparalleled freedom, speed, and ease. Our ambition is to build the world's most disruptive payment network and redefine what the payment experience should feel like. Trustly is a global team of innovators, collaborators, and doers. If you are driven by a strong sense of purpose and thrive in a dynamic, entrepreneurial, high-growth environment, join us and be part of a team that is transforming the way the world pays. About the Role Trustly is looking for a passionate and experienced compliance leader to join our Financial Crimes Prevention team as Swedish Deputy MLRO. Reporting to the Global MLRO, with a dotted reporting line to the EU Deputy MLRO, you will take ownership of our Anti-Money Laundering (AML) programme in Sweden, from strategic oversight to hands-on execution. You will be appointed as Trustly's central function responsible (centralt funktionsansvarig) for its Swedish-regulated payment institution under 6 kap. 2 § of the Swedish Act (2017:630) on measures against money laundering and terrorist financing and Finansinspektionen's regulations (FFFS 2017:11), with direct reporting to the board of directors and managing director in that capacity. You will be the company's subject matter expert on AML, sanctions, and Know Your Customer / Know Your Business (KYC/B) matters in Sweden, advising business and product teams, engaging directly with Finansinspektionen and the Swedish Financial Intelligence Unit (Finanspolisen), and building a scalable and robust compliance framework that keeps pace with both a fast-changing regulatory landscape and a fast-growing business.
