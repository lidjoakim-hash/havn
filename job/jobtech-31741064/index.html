I truly felt like part of the team and look forward to returning. To also be part of the program and meet the other students is fun. It is not a summer job in isolation – I’m part of something bigger.”   - Student from Power Up at Volvo Group 24-25  Job description:  Who are you?  Are you a first-year Master Student at Chalmers? GREAT! That means you have the unique possibility to join us within our initiative Power Up at Volvo Group. This student program is a fantastic opportunity to get to know Volvo Group and at the same time be part of developing the future of transport solutions together with us.  What is in it for you?  This program will provide you with a combination of experiences and insights to Volvo Group – both in a home organization and with the program and fellow Power Up-students. In the home organization you will be able to have summer work and master thesis. You will also have a skilled professional as mentor. As a program we will have sessions throughout the program for the full team of Power Up students. These joint sessions make it possible to share even more of the wide variety of opportunities and insights at Volvo Group. This is a unique opportunity to get real life industrial experience alongside with your studies. You will be able to see what the skills you are learning at Chalmers are used for in the Volvo Group. At the same time, you will get valuable insights into our company and the challenges we are facing when it comes to creating sustainable transport solutions on a global scale. You will experience how it is to work with cutting edge technology and customer offers on a global scale together with us at Volvo Group.      Sounds interesting? Keep on reading!  The program will start during the spring semester of 2027 and continue all the way to graduation in June 2028. For each position, a selection is made based on the master programs that are prioritized by the home organization. This ensures that your education is relevant to the home organization that you will work together with.  The program consists of four different elements: (1) program sessions and study visits, (2) individual mentorship with a Volvo team member, (3) your contribution to our deliveries by a summer work position and, (4) Master Thesis in collaboration with your designated home organization.  Who are we?  Take the opportunity to learn about how to develop and create transport solutions together with us. Everything from second life of batteries or internal parts in our transmissions, the logistics of supply chain to the cyber security of our plants.
