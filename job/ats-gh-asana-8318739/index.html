Product Manager

Overview Own the agency experience for Asana Client Management, our new product for teams that deliver client work. Agencies depend on two groups: the account, project, and creative teams who deliver the work, and the clients who review and approve it. You will own how both groups experience Asana, from the agency team's workspace to the branded portal where clients follow updates, approve assets, and raise requests. This is an early-stage product that has achieved product market fit and continues to evolve with a fast-moving roadmap. You will define what comes next with agency customers, take new bets from 0 to 1, and scale what works. You will work directly with Engineering, Design, Experience Research, and Data Science, and with Marketing, Sales, and Customer Success to ship and grow the product. Location: Warsaw, Poland What you'll achieve • Define and deliver a roadmap that makes agencies more profitable and their client relationships stronger. • Take new initiatives from 0 to 1, then scale them once they show product-market fit. • Design for both sides of the agency relationship: the delivery team that runs the work and the client who reviews it. Resolve the tradeoffs between what each side needs. • Synthesize input from agency customers, clients, user research, data science, and business partners to decide where to invest next, and shift quickly when an area stops paying off. • Partner with Design, Engineering, Marketing, Sales, and Customer Success to ship a cohesive journey from first trial to client activation. About you • B2B SaaS experience: 5+ years as a Product Manager, with B2B SaaS as the core of that experience. • 0 to 1 builder: You have taken a product or major new capability from idea to launch, and can describe how you validated demand, scoped the first version, and decided what to scale. • Customer-centric: You spend time directly with customers to understand their challenges, and you synthesize research into clear problems to solve. • Communication and collaboration: You explain complex concepts clearly, write precise product specs, and pair effectively with design, engineering, and business teams. • Systems thinker: You simplify complex workflows into solutions customers can adopt without a training session. • Strategic: You connect customer needs to a roadmap, and you can say what you will not build. • Get stuff done: You break projects into MVPs and ship iteratively with high velocity. • Growth mindset: You welcome feedback, stay curious, and share product management best practices with the team. Nice to have • Experience designing for a two-sided marketplace or a two-sided user experience, where two groups with different needs share one product and the product has to work for both. • Experience with agencies, professional services, or other client-service businesses. • Experience building client-facing or external-user surfaces, such as portals, guest access, or approval workflows. What we'll offer • Generous, transparent and fair compensation system (base salary and RSUs) • Contract of Employment (and the option of 50% tax deductible costs for author’s rights usage in respect of applicable roles) • Health insurance with dental and travel coverage (Lux Med) • Breakfast and lunch catering on the days that you work from the office • Vacation allowance • Career growth budget • Home office setup budget • Gym/Fitness card • Fertility healthcare and family-forming support with Carrot • Mental Health Support in Modern Health • Group life insurance • MacBooks with all necessary accessories The estimated base salary range for this role is 29,000 - 37,250 PLN gross per month (subject to all taxes and necessary deductions).
