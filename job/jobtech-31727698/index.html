Veritaz is a leading IT staffing solutions provider in Sweden, committed to advancing individual careers and aiding employers in ensuring the perfect talent fit. With a proven track record of successful partnerships with top companies, we have rapidly grown our presence in the USA, Europe, and Sweden as a dependable and trusted resource within the IT industry. Job Overview Role Summary Our client is seeking a Full-Stack Developer for an agile development team responsible for developing and continuously improving an e-commerce platform, with a strong focus on customer experience and digital customer journeys. The assignment spans both frontend and backend engineering, with ownership of development tasks from initial needs analysis and solution proposals through testing and production-ready delivery. The role involves building new functionality, improving existing code, and maintaining integrations with other systems. The developer will work closely with both the engineering team and business stakeholders, clarify evolving requirements, understand cross-system dependencies, and make informed technical trade-offs. The development environment follows an AI-first approach in which GitHub Copilot and other AI-assisted development tools are incorporated into day-to-day engineering work. Key Responsibilities Design, develop, test, and deliver functionality across the frontend and backend of the e-commerce platform. Develop new customer-facing features and improve digital customer journeys and the overall user experience. Maintain, refactor, and enhance existing application code and system integrations. Take development tasks from business need and solution proposal through implementation, automated testing, and completed delivery. Analyze dependencies involving adjacent applications and external systems. Clarify requirements that are incomplete or evolving in collaboration with the team and business stakeholders. Evaluate technical alternatives, make appropriate engineering trade-offs, and identify when priorities require further discussion. Contribute to team decisions concerning software architecture, technology selection, and implementation approaches. Develop and improve automated tests and support reliable delivery through CI/CD practices. Use GitHub Copilot and other AI-assisted engineering tools as an integrated part of the development workflow. Technical Stack & Requirements Backend: .NET and Node.js. Frontend: React and TypeScript. Data and caching: Redis. Cloud platform: Microsoft Azure. Engineering practices: CI/CD, automated testing, code improvement, and system integration development. AI-assisted development: GitHub Copilot and related AI-supported development tools. Qualifications & Experience The successful consultant should have practical full-stack development experience and be comfortable moving between frontend and backend tasks. The role requires the ability to understand distributed system dependencies, work with integrations, translate business needs into technical solutions, and deliver tested functionality. Experience contributing to architecture and technology decisions within an agile team is important. Familiarity with AI-assisted development workflows is also expected.
