At VARD, we create innovative and sustainable solutions for the global maritime industry. Combining future-oriented engineering excellence, cutting-edge technology, and strong teamwork, we design and build some of the world’s most advanced vessels for a wide range of market segments, including offshore energy, naval, fisheries, icebreaking and research.  At the same time, we believe our people are our greatest strength.  We need people with a wide range of skills, professional backgrounds, and experience. Engineers, designers, skilled workers, project managers, finance professionals, procurement specialists and many others all work together towards a common goal. Different perspectives and areas of expertise are essential to delivering high-quality vessels.  To be able to build the ships of tomorrow today, we foster an inclusive and supportive environment where talents can grow, ideas are valued, and individuals are empowered to make an impact.   Vard Interiors is a leading supplier of turnkey interior, insulation, piping, and HVAC-R solutions for the maritime industry. As part of the VARD value chain, we work closely with shipowners, designers, and shipyards to create functional, sustainable, and high-quality onboard environments. With a strong focus on innovation, craftsmanship, and customer value, we help bring some of the industry’s most advanced vessels to life. We are currently looking for a Supervisor Piping to join our team. In this role, you will have the opportunity to apply your expertise, collaborate with skilled colleagues, and contribute to exciting projects across the VARD value chain. Ready to make an impact at VARD? Duties and responsibilities • Plan and coordinate piping installation and commissioning activities. • Coordinate personnel and subcontractors to meet project milestones. • Monitor progress, quality and performance throughout the project. • Prepare areas and systems for inspections and follow up remarks and non-conformities. • Ensure necessary materials and documentation are available for production. • Identify and resolve challenges that may impact progress, quality or cost. • Report installation and commissioning progress to relevant stakeholders. Qualifications • Vocational education, or relevant experience that can compensate for formal education. • Experience and good technical understanding of piping systems, installation and commissioning. • Ability to understand technical, construction and functional drawings. • Basic knowledge of classification rules, including DNV and Bureau Veritas. • Good computer skills, including Microsoft Word and Excel. • Ability to work with 3D drawing viewers. • Good English skills, both written and spoken. • Knowledge of safe working methods and practices.   VARD works systematically for inclusion and diversity and encourage everyone to apply, even if your competence does not match 100% the above.  This employment may require a background check, or security clearance by the Norwegian National Security Authority. Personal qualities • Good leadership, coordination and communication skills. • Self-driven and proactive in solving operational challenges. • Structured, reliable and responsible. • Able to work effectively as part of a team. • Practical and solution-oriented approach. • Comfortable coordinating people and activities in a dynamic production environment • Identify yourself with the VARD values Craftmanship, Salesmanship and Fellowship.
