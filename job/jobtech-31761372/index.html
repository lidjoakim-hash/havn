Information Security Engineer

MultiMind Holding AB · Järfälla, Sweden

Are you passionate about cybersecurity and advanced technology? We are looking for a Cyber Security Engineer to work with complex security systems within the naval and defence industry in Lund. Key Responsibilities Identify, analyze, and verify information security requirements. Support IT Architects and Systems Engineers throughout the product development lifecycle. Develop and review IT security architectures for complex systems and distributed environments. Evaluate and review technical security solutions. Conduct security analyses, including vulnerability assessments, threat analysis, and risk assessments. Analyze vulnerabilities and support mitigation planning throughout the system lifecycle. Develop and maintain security documentation, instructions, policies, and technical descriptions. Translate security requirements and analysis into practical technical solutions. Required Qualifications Degree in IT, Engineering, Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent professional experience. Experience or knowledge within Cybersecurity, Information Security, or IT Security. Excellent communication skills in both Swedish and English, written and spoken. Strong collaboration and stakeholder communication skills. Ability to work in a cross-functional team environment. Meriting Experience Information security frameworks and standards such as ISO 27001, IEC 62443, or NIST. Vulnerability assessments, threat analysis, and risk management. IT security architecture and secure system design. Translating security requirements into technical solutions and system designs. Reviewing and assessing technical security solutions. Product development environments involving complex integrated systems. Systems engineering methods and practices. Assignment Details: Working hours: 40 hours/week Work model: Fully on-site Languages: Swedish and English Are you interested in this opportunity, or do you know someone who could be a good fit?
