About Ledger We’re a team of experts pushing the limits of what’s possible, united by our common goal to unlock true freedom through digital ownership, making technology accessible for all. We believe in a world where users, creators and enterprises manage their value with ownership and freedom. Our curiosity drives us to innovate, empowering individuals on a global scale. We believe change is constant and our team moves forward as one, with a culture of problem-solving where every employee is empowered and supported to challenge tradition and create solutions. Our mission is simple: to make self-custody accessible and give people the keys to their own financial futures. If you want to make a true impact, we want you to join us at Ledger. At Ledger, we’re proud to be the global platform for digital assets and Web3, with over 20% of the world’s crypto assets secured through our Ledger devices. With our headquarters in Paris, and offices in Vierzon, Grenoble, Montpellier, London, Portland, Geneva, Zurich and Central Singapore, we have a team of around 600 professionals developing a variety of products and services to enable individuals and companies to securely buy, store, swap, grow and manage crypto assets – including the Ledger hardware wallets line with more than 7.5 millions units already sold in 200 countries. The team The Consolidation team, part of Finance (Accounting & Consolidation), prepares the Group’s IFRS consolidated financial statements across multiple international legal entities. The team is moving its consolidation EPM tool from SAP BFC (ViaReport C&R) to Amelkis EPM, with a go-live targeted for Q1 2027, while preparing the 2026 annual closing. The intern supports both work streams and reports to the Consolidation Director. What you’ll be doing: Annual consolidation closing (2026) - Support the preparation of the Group’s IFRS consolidated financial statements across multiple legal entities - Assist with intercompany reconciliation and eliminations - Process consolidation adjustments and review reporting packages submitted by subsidiaries - Liaise with subsidiary finance teams to collect, control and consolidate reporting packages - Support ad hoc analyses requested during the annual audit EPM tool migration (SAP BFC to Amelkis EPM) - Contribute to data migration, configuration and testing workshops for the new Amelkis consolidation/EPM tool - Participate in the “double run” phase to ensure data accuracy between the legacy and new systems - Help document consolidation processes and procedures throughout the tool transition What we’re looking for: - Master’s-level student (Bac+4/5) in Accounting, Finance or Audit (DSCG, Master CCA, business or engineering school), looking for a gap year (césure) or an end-of-studies internship - First experience with, or strong interest in, consolidation and IFRS (previous internship, audit firm exposure or dedicated coursework) - Comfortable working with large data sets and advanced Excel functions - Curiosity for finance systems and tools (EPM, SAP BFC, Amelkis EPM) is a strong plus - Fluent in English and French (the team reports bilingually) - Rigorous and structured What the internship offers - Hands-on exposure to IFRS consolidation in a fast-growing, international tech group - Active involvement in a live EPM tool migration (SAP BFC to Amelkis EPM), a skill highly valued in the finance job market - Close mentoring from the Consolidation Director and the wider accounting team For regionally specific benefits, your Talent Acquisition contact will be able to provide you with more information.
