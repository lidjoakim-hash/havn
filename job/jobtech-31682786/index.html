Job Description Drive the Future of Automotive Learning Are you an experienced automotive technician with a passion for technical research and content creation? Join our team and help develop engaging training solutions that support automotive professionals worldwide. Key Responsibilities Research, create, and maintain technical training content, presentations, guides, videos, webinars, and workshop exercises. Design and validate practical workshop tasks using vehicles, diagnostic tools, oscilloscopes, and other test equipment. Collaborate with Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) and clients to develop accurate, engaging, and up-to-date learning materials. Ensure content meets quality, compliance, and client standards. Attend technical meetings, product launches, and Train-the-Trainer events. Monitor project progress, report results to stakeholders, and support KPI delivery. Build strong relationships with clients, SMEs, and internal teams. Identify opportunities to improve processes, teamwork, communication, and training outcomes. What We're Looking For Automotive engineering or technical workshop background. Strong hands-on mechanical, electrical, and diagnostic experience. Experience creating technical content, training materials, or learning solutions. Excellent technical writing, troubleshooting, and problem-solving skills. Strong communication, presentation, planning, and organisational abilities. Experience working with cross-functional teams and stakeholders. Preferred Industry-recognised automotive accreditation. Experience in Learning & Development or instructional design. Knowledge of LMS platforms, digital learning tools, and webinar delivery. Additional Information Why Join Us? Work on cutting-edge automotive technologies. Collaborate with industry-leading experts and global automotive brands. Blend technical workshop experience with content creation and innovation. Develop learning solutions that make a real impact on automotive professionals worldwide. Enjoy a flexible hybrid working model and supportive team culture.
