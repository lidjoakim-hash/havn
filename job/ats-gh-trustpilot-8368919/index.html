At Trustpilot, we're on an incredible journey. We're a profitable, high-growth FTSE-250 company with a big vision: to become the universal symbol of trust. We run the world's largest open customer review platform, and while we've come a long way, there's still so much exciting work to do. Come join us at the heart of trust! Our growing sales organisation is compensated through commission, and our Global Sales Planning & Compensation team structures those plans, sets sales targets, and ensures every salesperson worldwide is paid accurately and on time. Acting as the company’s behavioural economists, we partner with commercial leadership to analyse how incentives drive revenue growth, which gives us an inside view across the entire sales journey, from revenue planning to performance and pay. We are looking for a Student Assistant to join our Global Sales Planning & Compensation team in Copenhagen, reporting to the Sales Compensation Manager. This is a hands-on, part-time role with hours planned flexibly around your studies and exam periods. From day one, you will take ownership of real processes that directly impact how colleagues are paid and how leadership tracks performance, working across Sales, Finance, and HR in Denmark, the UK, and the US. To thrive in this role, you bring strong analytical instincts, a sharp eye for detail, and genuine curiosity about how incentives shape commercial behaviour. Experience with Salesforce, Forma, or similar business tools is a plus, but not required. What you’ll be doing: • Support the monthly and quarterly commission cycle to ensure accurate, on-time global payouts. • Investigate and resolve commission questions from sales colleagues, explaining data outcomes clearly. • Upload and reconcile sales targets across our systems and help prepare and launch commission plans. • Run recurring reports and build leadership presentation packs that turn numbers into actionable insights. • Coordinate administration for our sales recognition programmes. • Help develop our commission system (Forma), build controls, and document repeatable standard operating procedures while working across Forma, Salesforce, and Sage. • Collaborate with colleagues in the UK and US across Commercial, Revenue Operations, FP&A, Payroll, Accounting, and HR. Who you are: • In the final year of a Bachelor's degree, or early in a Master's, in finance, economics, or a related field. • Skilled in Excel or Google Sheets (lookups, pivot tables, and clean, structured models) with a strong interest in numbers and data. • Analytical, detail-oriented and proactive, you catch anomalies in data and spot ways to improve processes. • Commercially curious and eager to learn how sales compensation and incentives drive revenue in a global tech company. • A clear, concise communicator who handles confidential pay and performance data with care. • Collaborative, fluent in English, and able to work regularly from our Copenhagen office. What’s in it for you: • Learn how sales compensation works in a global technology company, from plan design to payout, and how commercial leaders use incentives to influence behaviour and reach revenue goals. • Hands-on experience in a function that connects Sales, Finance and HR. • Ownership of real processes that directly affect how our sales colleagues are paid. • Flexible hours that fit around your studies and exam periods. • A strong foundation for a career in finance, commercial operations or reward. • Rich learning and development opportunities are supported through the Trustpilot Academy and Blinkist • Central office with a laid-back vibe and constant buzz of different languages being spoken everywhere you go It’s complete with a coffee bar, canteen, and table tennis and has a wide variety of refreshments available - you can opt-in for breakfast and lunch at an affordable price • Regular opportunities to connect and get to know your fellow Trusties, including company-wide celebrat
