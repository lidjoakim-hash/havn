🚀 We’re Hiring: Senior Expert – System Verification Engineer | EV Charging Company:** Hykmann Global Business Solutions AB Role: Senior Expert Charging Verification Engineer Domain: Automotive & Electromobility Engagement: IT Consulting Assignment Are you a senior automotive verification specialist with extensive experience in electric vehicle charging systems? Do you have the technical expertise to lead complex charging verification activities and guide engineering teams? Hykmann Global Business Solutions AB is looking for a Senior Expert Charging Verification Engineer with deep knowledge of EV charging technologies, communication protocols, vehicle testing, and system verification. About the Role You will take independent technical responsibility for charging verification activities across electric vehicle systems. The role involves planning, leading, and executing verification in vehicle, laboratory, and Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) environments. You will work with advanced charging technologies and protocols, ensuring interoperability, functionality, reliability, and compliance with relevant industry standards. You will also provide technical leadership, support fault investigations, and guide other engineers throughout verification activities. Key Responsibilities - Lead and coordinate complex EV charging verification and testing activities. Plan and execute charging verification across vehicle, laboratory, and HIL environments. Perform hands-on testing and verification of charging systems and functionalities. Analyse CAN communication, measurement data, and diagnostic information. Use CANalyzer, CANoe, and other relevant measurement and diagnostic tools. Investigate charging-related issues through systematic fault tracing and root-cause analysis. Verify charging communication and interoperability across different charging standards and protocols. Identify technical risks, analyse test results, and recommend corrective actions. Take independent technical ownership of verification activities. Provide technical guidance and support to other engineers. Ensure verification activities meet quality, reliability, and project requirements. Mandatory Qualifications - Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, or a related field. 12+ years of experience in the automotive industry. 6+ years of experience in EV charging and charging verification. 2+ years of technical leadership or team leadership experience. Strong hands-on experience in vehicle, laboratory, and HIL verification. Practical experience with CANalyzer, CANoe, CAN analysis, measurement, and diagnostic tools. Extensive experience in fault tracing and root-cause analysis. Strong technical knowledge and practical experience with the following charging standards and protocols: CCS DIN SPEC 70121 ISO 15118 CHAdeMO GB/T NACS Proven hands-on experience in charging verification and testing. Ability to independently lead verification activities and guide other engineers. Nice to Have - Functional Safety knowledge and experience with ISO 26262. Experience in automotive cybersecurity. Experience with charging interoperability testing. Knowledge of PCAP and network analysis. Experience troubleshooting charging communication issues. Category C truck driving licence. Who Should Apply? We are looking for a technically strong and independent engineering professional who combines deep EV charging expertise with practical verification experience and leadership capabilities. You should be confident in handling complex technical challenges, leading verification activities, performing detailed fault investigations, and supporting other engineers in delivering reliable charging solutions.
