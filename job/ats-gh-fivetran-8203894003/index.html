From Fivetran’s founding until now, our mission has remained the same: to make access to data as simple and reliable as electricity. With Fivetran, customer data arrives in their warehouses, canonical and ready to query, with no engineering or maintenance required. We’re proud that more organizations continue to leverage our technology every day to become truly data-driven. About Us Fivetran and dbt Labs are bringing together two industry-leading companies with a shared mission: helping organizations unlock the full value of their data. Together, we’re delivering the data infrastructure layer that helps organizations move, transform, and trust their data — from the moment data moves, through every transformation, to the context teams and AI systems rely on. Fivetran helps organizations automate data movement across the systems, clouds, engines, and tools they rely on. dbt Labs pioneered analytics engineering, helping teams transform data into reliable, governed insights. Together, we support thousands of organizations as they build a trusted foundation for analytics, AI, and better business decisions. As we bring our teams and technology together, we’re building on the strengths of both companies while continuing to deliver the products and experiences our customers know and trust. It’s an exciting time to join us: we’re creating a company with the scale, talent, and technology to help more organizations put their data to work with greater speed, confidence, and impact. During this transition period, you may see references to both Fivetran and dbt Labs throughout our recruiting process as we integrate our teams, systems, and career sites. About the Role Fivetran, now combined with dbt Labs, is scaling its reseller channel across Europe, the Middle East, and Africa. As the EMEA Resell Partner Development Manager, you will own the growth of our reseller, VAR, and distribution ecosystem across the region, spanning UK&I, DACH, France, Benelux, the Nordics, Southern Europe, and the Middle East and Africa. You will manage and expand relationships with existing resell partners, recruit and onboard new strategic partners, and drive partner-sourced and partner-fulfilled revenue. Your focus will be enablement, joint pipeline generation, and establishing Fivetran as the leading platform for modern data integration and transformation. We are seeking a highly motivated, experienced channel leader with a proven track record of building and scaling resell and VAR partnerships across multiple EMEA markets, including pan-European solution providers, regional specialists, distributors, cloud marketplaces, and public sector frameworks. Key qualifications include: Demonstrated experience in channel sales, reseller programs, and GTM execution within the software or SaaS industry across EMEA Established relationships with pan-European and regional solution providers (for example SoftwareOne, Softcat, Computacenter, Bechtle, Insight, Atea, Bytes) and familiarity with how their sales organizations, incentives, and procurement processes operate Experience working with distributors such as Ingram Micro, TD SYNNEX, Arrow, or ALSO, and an understanding of two-tier channel models Working knowledge of cloud marketplace transactions, including AWS Channel Partner Private Offers (CPPO), Microsoft Azure Multiparty Private Offers, and Google Cloud Marketplace Understanding of EMEA public sector procurement, including frameworks such as UK G-Cloud and Crown Commercial Service and EU tender processes, is a strong plus Familiarity with EMEA data regulation and sovereignty considerations, including GDPR, and how they shape customer buying decisions Deep understanding of the data integration and cloud ecosystem, with the ability to articulate Fivetran’s value proposition Strong business development and negotiation skills to identify, onboard, and scale strategic reseller relationships Proven ability to collaborate cross-functionally with regional sales leade
