Engineering Product Manager Are you passionate about engineering excellence and ready to shape the future of DTH (down the hole) and PARD (Percussion Assisted Rotary Drilling) hammer technology? Join us in a strategically important global role where your technical expertise will influence product design, manufacturing, testing, and the continuous development of Epiroc’s hammer portfolio. Join our team As Engineering Product Manager for DTH and PARD Hammer Technology, you will join a global R&D environment focused on developing reliable, high-performing products for the mining and construction industries. Reporting to the Global R&D Manager, you will work closely with engineering teams, Product Companies, manufacturing, laboratories, and other key stakeholders. You will represent the depth and breadth of Epiroc’s technical knowledge within hammer technology and help ensure that our product assortment remains competitive, technically advanced, and aligned with changing market needs and Epiroc’s strategic direction. Your mission Your mission is to strengthen Epiroc’s technical leadership in DTH and PARD hammer technology, ensuring robust design standards, reliable products, and an optimized product portfolio. In this role, you will: Develop and maintain Epiroc’s hammer design system, including design rules, calculation methods, simulation tools, and product design data. Drive technical resolution of major products, performance, reliability, and manufacturing challenges. Establish and continuously improve laboratory testing standards and procedures. Optimize the DTH and PARD product assortment from a technical, market, and IP perspective. Strengthening collaboration across R&D, manufacturing, Product Companies, and capital equipment teams, while leading regular DTH pulse meetings. Your profile You are a technically credible and collaborative engineering professional who can move confidently between detailed product design questions and broader portfolio considerations. You enjoy creating structures, establishing common engineering practices, and bringing people together to solve complex technical challenges. We believe you bring: A university degree in mechanical engineering, mining engineering, product development, or another relevant technical discipline. Strong knowledge of DTH, PARD, drilling, rock excavation, or comparable mechanical product technology. Experience in product development, engineering design, simulation, testing, manufacturing support, or technical product management. You communicate clearly across functions and geographical boundaries and can turn complex engineering knowledge into practical standards, decisions, and improvements. Experience working with international R&D or manufacturing teams is an advantage, as is knowledge of intellectual property and product portfolio management. Location and other This position is located in Fagersta, Sweden. The role involves collaboration with teams and Product Companies in multiple locations. Occasional international travel may therefore be required. Application and contact information We encourage you to submit your application as soon as possible, and no later than November 21st, 2026. Applications will be reviewed continuously. We are committed to a thorough, fair, and inclusive recruitment process. The process may include interviews, reference checks, assessments, identity checks, and drug and alcohol screening. For questions about the position, please contact: Hiring Manager: Mike White, Rock Drilling Tools division Epiroc Drilling Tools LLC, michael.white@epiroc.com We have already made our choices regarding advertising channels and consulting providers and kindly request not to be contacted regarding such services.
