WE ARE AN APPLIED AI LAB BUILDING END-TO-END SOFTWARE AGENTS. We're the makers of Devin, the first AI software engineer. Our team is extremely talent-dense. Among our founding team, we have world-class competitive programmers, former founders, and leaders from companies at the cutting edge of AI including Scale AI, Palantir, Cursor, Waymo, Tesla, Lunchclub, Modal, Google DeepMind, and Nuro. Building Devin is just the first step—our hardest challenges still lie ahead. If you’re excited to solve some of the world’s biggest problems and build AI that can reason on real-world tasks, apply to join us. ABOUT THE ROLE As a Recruiting Coordinator supporting EMEA, you're the reason candidates leave every interaction feeling genuinely taken care of—and the person our GTM and Customer Engineering teams and leadership count on when they need something to just work. Part calendar guru, part host, part quiet fixer of whatever's about to go sideways, you make sure hiring never becomes the bottleneck. The people you help bring on now will shape what Cognition's EMEA team looks like for years. This role fits you if you get a kick out of solving logistics puzzles fast, don't mind things changing under you week to week, and actually enjoy the chaos of meeting new people all day. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES Talent Coordination for EMEA - Run point on scheduling and interview logistics for the EMEA team, juggling calendars, time zones, and last-minute changes so nobody's ever caught off guard - Greet and host candidates onsite, reading the room and adjusting so each person feels comfortable, whether they're a first-time interviewer or a seasoned researcher - Keep everyone in the loop. Candidates, interviewers, hiring managers with updates that are clear, timely, and never robotic - Work shoulder-to-shoulder with recruiters and hiring managers, flagging issues before they become problems and adjusting plans on the fly - Own our ATS data (Ashby) so the pipeline stays clean and nothing slips through Operational Excellence - Poke at how the EMEA team hires and runs meetings, and fix what's clunky before anyone has to ask - Pull together numbers on hiring pace, time to hire, and team growth for leadership - Help pull off team events, offsites, and research show-and-tells - Spot the cracks in the process early and build something more scalable before they turn into real problems YOU MAY BE A FIT IF - You've spent 2+ years doing recruiting coordination, recruiting ops, or talent ops somewhere fast and technical - You're the person who double-checks a calendar invite's time zone before it goes out, every time - A clunky process bugs you enough that you just go fix it—nobody has to ask - You genuinely care whether the interview went well for everyone involved: the candidate, the hiring manager, the interviewer squeezing this in between meetings - You can hold a dozen moving pieces at once without losing your cool - People trust you to follow through, every time, no reminders needed - You like people, all kinds of people, and it shows - You know how to keep sensitive information sensitive - Bonus points if you've supported a technical or research team before, or already know your way around Ashby - You're based in London, or ready to be, and able to work in person 5 days per week.
