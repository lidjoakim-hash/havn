Senior Business Analyst for an assignment in central Stockholm

Invici AB · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 500 000–610 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 30.11.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

We are looking for an experienced Senior Business Analyst to join an international transformation programme in Trade Surveillance, capital markets and compliance. You will play a key role in developing a global surveillance platform, with a focus on requirements, data integration and SQL-based analysis. Ditt anställningserbjudande This is an opportunity to combine your expertise in finance and technology in a complex and rewarding consulting assignment. You will work closely with international teams across Risk, Compliance and IT, with significant responsibility and the opportunity to influence the future of trade surveillance. Beskrivning av tjänsten • Analyse and document business requirements, functional specifications, user stories and acceptance criteria. • Define and quality-assure requirements for trading data, data flows and system integrations. • Perform SQL-based data analysis and validation. • Manage the backlog, priorities and agile delivery planning in JIRA. • Collaborate with international technology, risk and compliance teams. • Support the implementation, testing and calibration of surveillance models across different asset classes. What you should know • Global impact: Work with a leading trade surveillance platform. • Technical development: Take part in a major transformation programme. • International environment: Collaborate with specialists in Europe and the US. • Significant responsibility: Influence requirements, system development and delivery. The assignment starts in early December and will run for one year. Location: Central Sthlm Vem är du? You are analytical, self-motivated and communicative, with solid experience in business analysis within capital markets or financial technology. You are used to translating complex business needs into clear technical requirements and thrive in international, agile environments. Erfarenhet och profil • A master’s degree in economics, finance, IT or a related field, or equivalent experience. • At least five years’ experience as a Business Analyst or Product Analyst in capital markets, FinTech or regulated trading. • Strong knowledge of trading data, order flows, exchange rules and financial instruments, including equities, fixed income and derivatives. • Experience writing requirements specifications and user stories, and documenting data flows. • Experience working in agile environments and with JIRA. • Advanced SQL skills and strong analytical ability. Preferred qualifications • Knowledge of Trade Surveillance, Market Abuse, MAR, MiFID II or FINRA. • Experience with market data, data integration and financial reference data. • Experience with NMS, NTS, Actimize, Trading Hub or Scila, as well as system calibration. • Experience working with global teams and with Tableau or Power BI.
