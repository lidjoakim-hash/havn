Mälardalen University (MDU) is the youngest university in Sweden. In line with our vision, to be a progressive and collaborative University where we shape a sustainable future together, we wish to make a difference.     Do you want to be involved and contribute to our development?     Together, we can create a sustainable future through knowledge and innovation. We believe that knowledge and new perspectives are best attained and reached together in collaboration with others – our colleagues, students, the private and public sectors, both nationally and internationally.     The Faculty of Philosophy, conducts education and research in the humanities, social sciences and education, mathematics and natural sciences, as well as the arts. Our research and education are carried out in close dialogue with the wider community and contribute to critical knowledge development, intellectual growth, and a deeper understanding of complex societal issues. Education is offered at all levels and is grounded in scientific or artistic foundations and characterized by academic breadth. Together, we contribute to a sustainable, open, and knowledge-based society.  The Faculty comprises three departments, employing approximately 420 members of staff in total. Through our collaborative platforms, we bring together academia, the public sector, and industry to jointly advance knowledge development and address societal challenges.     The Department of Social Science and Humanities provides education in social sciences, behavioural sciences, and language and communication. Research focuses on societal issues such as democracy, migration, sustainable development and communication, and is conducted in close collaboration with the surrounding society. Employment information Employment: Permanent employment which begins with possible trial employment   Period of employment/Start date: 2027-08-01   Scope: Part-time 75 %   Number of positions: 1   Closing date for application: 2026-12-02   Campus location: Västerås   Department/Unit: Social Sciences and Humanities For information and a detailed description of basic eligibility requirements and assessment criteria, that apply to a position of a senior lecturer, please consult the Appointment Regulations for Mälardalen University https://www.mdu.se/en/malardalen-university/about-mdu/work-with-us/job-opportunities Position description As a senior lecturer at MDU, your main tasks are teaching. Teaching also includes supervision, examination, course evaluation and course development. At MDU, research and education are closely intertwined and research forms the basis of our programmes. Collaboration is also an integral part of our research and education mission and as a senior lecturer you will actively contribute to the university's development work. The tasks include: teaching and examination in literature and literature didactics on the undergraduate and graduate levels, within the primary and subject teacher programmes as well as in other contexts teaching and examination in English proficiency courses course coordination and other administrative tasks supervision and examination of degree projects participation in pedagogical, scientific and collegial development research and contributions to the department's research environments general administration and competence development as well as participating in the daily activities, in the work of the subject and the department additional tasks, such as work placement visits in the teacher programmes or teaching in research education, may be included Qualification requirements We are looking for a person with documented teaching skills and a degree of Doctor, or equivalent academic competence. To be qualified for appointment as a senior lecturer one must have demonstrated the academic skills and teaching abilities required for the appointment. Teaching skills and academic competence shall be well documented in such a way that their quality can also be assessed.
