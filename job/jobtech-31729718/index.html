Chief Product Owner

Company description: Epiroc is a leading productivity partner for the mining, infrastructure and natural resources industries. Withcutting-edge technology, Epiroc develops and produces innovative drill rigs, rock excavation and constructionequipment, and provides world-class service and consumables. The company was founded in Stockholm,Sweden, and has passionate people supporting and collaborating with customers in more than 150 countries.Learn more at www.epirocgroup.com. Job description: Are you passionate about shaping the direction of complex products and bringing people, technology and customer needs together? As Chief Product Owner, you will have the opportunity to influence the future of our Platform organization, creating a shared direction across teams and helping ensure that hardware and software evolve together as one connected product ecosystem. This is a strategic role for someone who enjoys seeing the bigger picture, building alignment and turning complexity into clear priorities. Your mission As Chief Product Owner, you will shape the overarching product vision and roadmap across multiple product areas and development teams. Working closely with Product Owners, Product Line Owners and cross-functional teams, you will translate business objectives and customer needs into clear priorities, major features and epics. An important part of your mission will be to develop our approach to Agile at scale, creating common ways of working and collaborative planning through forums such as Big Room Planning. Within Platform, you will also bring hardware and software development closer together, helping teams navigate dependencies and align priorities and deliveries. By connecting engineering, product management, marketing, sales and senior management, you will create clarity around where we are going, why it matters and how we succeed together.   Profile description:Your profile You have a strong background in product ownership, product management or strategic product leadership, preferably from a complex technical or industrial environment. You are comfortable working across several teams or product areas and understand how customer needs and business priorities can be translated into a clear product strategy and roadmap. Experience with Agile ways of working at scale, cross-functional planning and managing dependencies between teams will help you succeed in the role. Experience with Big Room Planning or similar approaches, as well as working with products that combine hardware and software, is valuable but does not need to come from one specific industry. Equally important is your ability to listen, bring different perspectives together and create alignment in a collaborative way. You communicate with clarity, approach complexity with curiosity and structure, and are comfortable making thoughtful decisions while keeping customer value and shared goals in focus.   Location The role is based in Örebro, Sweden. We work hybrid, with at least three days a week in the office.   Application and contacts Please send your application, including CV and personal letter, by creating an account in our recruitment system as soon as possible but no later than 27th of November. Applications will be reviewed on a rolling basis, and the position may be filled before last application date. Please note that, due to the current regulations, we will only consider applications received through our system and not via email or social media.    Öppen för alla Vi fokuserar på din kompetens, inte dina övriga förutsättningar.
