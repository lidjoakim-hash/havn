Head of FX Sales Stockholm

Are you passionate about customers, sales, financial markets and leadership? Do you want to lead a highly skilled FX Sales team and play a key role in shaping the future of Swedbank’s FX business? Join us in our mission to help clients grow throughout their lifecycle through proactive advice, strong customer relationships and sustainable business development.Your responsibilities: • Lead a business-driven team focused on profitability, customer value and sustainable growth through proactive client engagement and strong internal collaboration. • Strengthen client relationships through a strong risk advisory and solution-oriented approach. • Develop our FX Services to be the preferred choice for small and mid-sized corporates, as well as selected large corporate clients. • Ensure to be at the forefront of managing customers’ currency flows linked to international payments. • Attract and retain high performing employees who are committed to the One corporate business idea of scaling business and achieving excellence in daily banking support and service. • Maintain a high and healthy profitability and a sound risk level in own portfolio. • Assess and improve the performance of your team members. • Ensure the continuous development of your team and continue to build a team with high level of team spirit and engagement. • Align your work and the team’s contribution to the bank’s direction and strategy. What is needed in this role: • Proven leadership experience with a track record of building and developing high-performing teams. Extensive experience in Foreign Exchange (FX), including FX sales and advisory services for corporate clients. Proven ability to drive sales growth, business performance and sustainable results. Strong relationship management skills, with experience engaging senior decision-makers and key stakeholders. Excellent communication and presentation skills in both Swedish and English. Bachelor's or Master's degree in Finance, Economics, Business Administration, or equivalent relevant experience. Ability to lead in line with Swedbank's values: Open, Simple and Caring. With us, you can experience: Personal and professional growth through self-leadership and continuous development. Meaningful work that positively impacts our workplace, our customers, and society. An open and collaborative culture that encourages cross-functional teamwork and provides networking opportunities. A supportive and inclusive environment that promotes a balanced and sustainable work-life, with flexible working conditions when suitable for the role. Benefits such as our share based reward program Eken, company pension plan, employee offer for banking products, health insurance. This is our team... We are a dedicated and successful team with many experienced professionals. We work with a proactive sales responsibility towards corporate and institutional clients across both Swedbank and the Savings Banks, where we offer products based on the customers' needs. Our work is very much characterized by an analytical way of working to help customers make informed decisions. Internally, we collaborate closely with Client Executives and other product specialists, including Trade Finance and Cash Management, to grow the FX business and deliver the best possible client offering. Together, we work to maximize customer value every day through close collaboration, strong client engagement and a shared commitment to excellence. In our team we value teamwork and we expect you to be a collaborative and engaged team player! As leaders, we trust our people and delegate responsibility, we strive to build a team with an open, positive and transparent environment, where everyone can grow and have the possibility to learn from each other." Cornelia Reifeldt, your future manager.
