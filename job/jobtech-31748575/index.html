Nordic Product Specialist at PostNord

TNG Group AB · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 560 000–680 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 18.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Make Nordic parcel logistics work better for customers and operations. Turn complex product needs into practical improvements. PostNord is developing its Nordic parcel portfolio to create customer value, support profitable growth and harmonise services across markets. As a Nordic Product Specialist, you will play an important role in making these improvements work in practice within your assigned product area. You will combine your understanding of logistics and operational parcel flows with product expertise to improve processes, systems and services across the Nordics.The position offers the opportunity to collaborate with colleagues across functions and Nordic markets, turning complex requirements into clear specifications, reliable data and practical improvements. Purpose, Offer and Benefits The position offers a flexible hybrid workplace in a collaborative environment where colleagues support each other in reaching shared goals. Independent working methods are encouraged, alongside opportunities for professional development and career growth. Benefits include: The opportunity to work in a Nordic and flexible environment Good insurance and occupational pension conditions Wellness allowance and access to office gym and swimming pool, depending on office location Employee benefits through PostNord´s employee foundation, PostNord Plus Employment according to a collective agreement Your Responsibilities As a Nordic Product Specialist, you will turn complex requirements into clear specifications, reliable product data and practical implementation support. Using your product expertise and understanding of logistics operations, you will help improve products, streamline launches and simplify everyday work. The role will focus on either C2C/Recommerce or International & Customs, including international parcel flows, import and export. Responsibilities will be adapted to the assigned product area, customer journeys and operational needs. Your main responsibilities include: Build expertise in product logic, services, terms, customer needs and logistics processes. Support Product Managers with insights for roadmaps, lifecycle decisions, portfolio optimisation and product improvements. Maintain clear product specifications, rules, terms input, operational requirements and documentation. Translate operational needs into system requirements in close collaboration with IT and Operations. Ensure accurate, consistent product data across systems and channels, and make product knowledge accessible to stakeholders. Support launches, changes, migrations and phase-outs through readiness checks, implementation follow-up, issue resolution and hypercare. Coordinate implementation across Operations, IT, Customer Service, Commercial, Legal, CX, Pricing and Nordic markets. Share product knowledge through guidance, internal communication and change support. Capture and escalate local needs, implementation issues and post-launch feedback to support Nordic product development and prioritisation. The Bigger Picture The position is part of PostNord´s Nordic product organisation, where operational understanding and collaboration across different functions are essential to improving parcel services. The Nordic Product Specialist reports to the relevant Manager Solutions Area and works closely with Product Managers and colleagues across functions and Nordic markets. The role provides insight into how logistics processes, systems and customer needs connect across the Nordic network. The position can be based at one of PostNord´s main offices in Solna, Helsinki, Oslo or Copenhagen. A hybrid working model applies, with three days per week at the office. Some travel within the Nordics is expected, particularly when building relationships and gaining a deeper understanding of operations.
