And beyond. ⭐ DATA ASSOCIATE ⭐ The position is a six-month end-of-studies internship in Paris, with a flexible start date. Data Associates join a product or business crew and work alongside a senior member of the Data team, taking on a real share of their projects. It is the same work as the rest of the team, building insights and data-powered products that help our members stay healthy, at a pace and scope designed for someone starting out. Our team is full stack: each member sits in a crew next to engineers, product builders and designers and leads projects from A to Z, covering analytics, engineering and science. We mainly use SQL, Python, dbt, Airflow and Metabase, and Claude Code and AI agents are part of our daily workflow. Want to know more? Read our vision for the Data team https://medium.com/alan/vision-for-a-data-team-2eae845b8052 (old one, but still relevant) and how our Data team is re-inventing its craft with AI https://medium.com/alan/re-inventing-our-craft-how-alans-data-team-is-shaping-its-future-with-ai-e8d73d095ece. This is our first generation of Data Associates, and we built the program with one goal: you join us full-time at the end of it. That decision is made at your 5-month review. ⭐ DETAILED MISSIONS ⭐ What you work on depends on the crew you join, something that is decided once you start your internship, but your six months will mix: - Answering real business or product questions: framing the problem, digging into the data and turning what you find into a recommendation your crew acts on. - Building tools people use: a dbt model, a dashboard a team uses every week, a tool powered by AI. You would ship them end to end, with your senior counterpart supporting you on key steps - Growing with the Data community: joining our main rituals (retros, insights sharing sessions, on-call shifts), and later helping us recruit the next generation of Data Associates You won’t be alone: from day one, you will benefit from a senior Alaner from the Data team to work with at the office and a coach from the Data team as well. ⭐ IS IT YOU WE'RE LOOKING FOR? ⭐ Please note that for this internship, you need to: - Be based in Paris, France - Work from our Valmy office at least 4 days a week - Be in the final year of your studies (engineering or business, with a strong quantitative track), with an internship agreement from your school You would be a great fit if most of this sounds like you: - You want a career in Data, across the whole spectrum. If what you want above everything else is to build ML models, a research internship is probably a better fit for you - You have used SQL and Python, at school, in an internship or on a side project.
