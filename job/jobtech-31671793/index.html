Job description:Develop and test next-generation high voltage solutionsWould you like to contribute to the development of high voltage technology that supports the green transition? As a High Voltage Engineer at NKT’s Technology Consulting in Västerås, you will work with electrical testing, innovation and customer-focused development projects in an international environment. This is an opportunity to contribute to advanced energy solutions while working with modern laboratories and colleagues across multiple disciplines. Are you interested in turning technical challenges into practical solutions? Design and perform advanced high voltage testingAs a High Voltage Engineer, you will support research, development and testing activities related to high voltage technologies. The role involves planning and carrying out experiments, analysing results and collaborating with internal and external stakeholders. Working closely with technical specialists and project teams, you will contribute to both innovation projects and customer assignments while ensuring compliance with safety requirements and international standards.    Your responsibility will be to: •    Design and perform high voltage experiments and measurement methods•    Carry out testing, analysis and technical reporting activities•    Support customer projects and type testing according to international standards•    Perform risk assessments and follow electrical and laboratory safety procedures•    Contribute to improvements of laboratory methods, equipment and workflows The position is based in Västerås at NKT’s Technology Consulting and reports within the HV Technology Department. You will work closely with technical experts, product specialists, IT and procurement teams, with regular interaction with customers.  Profile description:Collaborative engineer with a structured approachYou enjoy working systematically and approaching technical challenges in a practical and organised way.
