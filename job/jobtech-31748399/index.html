Nordic Operations Analytics Specialist: join PostNord as an Operations Analyst in Solna and turn data into better decisions. Help shape the future of Nordic parcel operations. As an Operations Analyst at PostNord, with the role title Nordic Operations Analytics Specialist, you connect operational performance with customer, commercial and financial outcomes. If your background is as a Business Analyst, Logistics Analyst or Supply Chain Analyst, this is an opportunity to build analytical tools, uncover improvement opportunities and turn insights into measurable business impact. Based in Stockholm, Solna or another Nordic head office, you work across markets, influence portfolio decisions and help build analytical capabilities that make a difference. Purpose, Offer and Benefits Make an impact across the Nordic parcel portfolio, work closely with teams across markets and build analytical capabilities that influence real business decisions. We offer you a flexible hybrid workplace with a prestige-free environment where we as a team help each other to reach our goals. We encourage self-sufficient leadership for development and career opportunities. In addition to the benefits mentioned above, we offer: Work in a Nordic and flexible environment Good insurance and occupational pension conditions Wellness allowance & access to office gym and swimming pool Employee benefits through PostNord´s own employee foundation PostNord Plus Employment according to a collective agreement. Your Responsibilities You connect operational performance with customer, commercial and financial outcomes to guide portfolio decisions and improvements. Your responsibilities include: Build dashboards, KPIs and scalable analytical tools with IT, turning business needs into better decision support. Analyse volumes, flows, lead times, service quality and product usage to uncover root causes, risks and improvement opportunities. Strengthen business cases and portfolio priorities with operational assumptions, impact assessments, sensitivity analysis and quantified improvement potential. Provide analytical input and follow-up for product changes, launches and migrations across Nordic markets. Define and govern common product master data structures and drive data quality improvements, while Product Specialists own product-specific data accuracy, maintenance and setup. Turn findings into actionable recommendations with Product, Operations, Finance, CX, Commercial, Pricing, Digital and country teams to improve efficiency, customer experience and scalability. The Bigger Picture Nordic Parcel Product Management develops PostNord’s Nordic parcel portfolio. You report to Manager Solutions Area B2C, with an analytical mandate across all parcel product areas. The role can be based at PostNord’s offices in Stockholm/Solna, Helsinki, Oslo or Copenhagen. The hybrid working model includes three days per week at the office. Our Expectations You combine analytical depth with business understanding. You challenge assumptions with facts, make complex findings clear and work across functions to turn insight into action. Taking ownership of analytical quality and building tools and ways of working that make a difference comes naturally to you. Your background includes experience in analytics, business control, operations development or improvement, product performance or BI/reporting, with strong skills in large-data-set and root-cause analysis. An understanding of operations, product performance, flows and service quality in logistics or another relevant industry is essential. Connecting these with customer, commercial, digital and financial outcomes is an advantage. Experience building dashboards, KPIs and analytical models, and developing scalable tools with IT, is also required. You understand master data structures, reporting dimensions and data quality improvement, and bring stakeholder management and cross-functional collaboration skills in a Nordic or matrix environment.
