Machine Learning & Software Engineer – Edge AI & Autonomous Systems Company: Aeronis AB Location: Gothenburg, Sweden Employment: Full-time Expected start date: 1 January 2027 About Aeronis Aeronis AB is a Swedish deep-tech company developing edge intelligence solutions for space, airborne and ground-based systems. Our technologies combine artificial intelligence, machine learning, distributed computing and sensor data processing to support autonomous operations and real-time decision-making. We work across space, defence, critical infrastructure, energy and industrial applications, collaborating with research institutions, industry partners and innovation programmes in Sweden and internationally. About the Role We are looking for a motivated Machine Learning & Software Engineer to join our growing technical team in Gothenburg. The successful candidate will contribute to the development, integration and validation of AI-enabled software solutions for distributed and autonomous systems. This is a hands-on technical role involving software development, machine learning, sensor data processing, experimentation and system integration. Key Responsibilities Develop, implement and test software components using Python and other relevant programming technologies. Design, train and evaluate machine learning and AI models for real-time applications. Work with sensor data, telemetry, anomaly detection and intelligent data processing. Support the development of distributed AI and autonomous systems. Contribute to multi-agent intelligence, robotics and sensor-fusion applications. Integrate AI models with edge computing platforms and embedded or distributed systems. Perform software testing, validation, performance evaluation and technical documentation. Collaborate with engineers, researchers and project partners on R&D activities and demonstrators. Support technical investigations, prototypes and the preparation of project deliverables. Required Qualifications Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence or a related discipline. Practical experience in software development, preferably using Python. Knowledge of machine learning principles, algorithms and model evaluation. Familiarity with AI/ML frameworks such as PyTorch, TensorFlow or Scikit-learn. Understanding of software integration, data processing and testing. Good analytical, problem-solving and communication skills. Ability to work independently and collaboratively within a multidisciplinary team. Good command of English, written and spoken. Desirable Experience Experience with distributed systems, edge computing or embedded AI. Knowledge of reinforcement learning or multi-agent systems. Experience in robotics, autonomous systems or intelligent sensor processing. Familiarity with SQL, databases and telemetry data. Knowledge of cybersecurity, resilient systems or safety-critical applications. Experience working on research projects, technical prototypes or industry-funded R&D programmes. What We Offer Opportunity to work on innovative technologies across space, AI and autonomous systems. Hands-on involvement in research, software development and product commercialisation. Collaboration with an international engineering team and research partners. A dynamic startup environment with opportunities for professional growth. The opportunity to contribute to the development of new technologies from concept to demonstration. Application Please submit your CV and a short introduction highlighting your relevant software development, machine learning and technical project experience. Contact: info@aeronissolutions.com Website: www.aeronissolutions.com
