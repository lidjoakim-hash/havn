Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years. ABOUT THE TEAM The Test and Evaluation team at Anduril works across the entire spectrum of products and business lines, as well as all flight operations and test range management. Our team conducts full system level development testing, new production acceptance testing, sub-component qualification testing and much more. In short, if it involves test, we support it. If you are interested in working in an extremely innovative and fast paced environment, where your work directly makes an impact and difference in the products that are fielded this a fantastic opportunity. ABOUT THE JOB As a Test & Evaluation Engineer you will have a strong passion for planning, executing and reporting of flight test and evaluation activities on uncrewed fixed-wing and multi-rotor aircraft. You will work alongside a team of engineers and operators across the UK and will support test operations of our Group 2/3 surrogate UAS under the guidance of senior team members. WHAT YOU'LL DO • Under general supervision, you will support test planning, execution, and reporting. • Collect and perform initial test data analysis for the system under test. • Support software and integration testing of UAS systems, in both simulated and field environments. • Collaborate with engineering teams to interpret test data and identify system behaviors, escalating opportunities for product enhancements and process improvements to senior engineers. • Assist with the use of test ranges and coordination with external agencies to facilitate testing activities. • Support test site management duties. • Work in a safe manner in accordance with established operating procedures and practices. REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS: • Bachelor's degree in an applicable field of engineering, or equivalent experience. • Working knowledge of COTS and bespoke UAS technologies. • Quick learner with the capacity to accurately implement new concepts and effectively manage project tasks. • Exposure to designing and executing testing protocols. • Comfortable with data analysis and working in a dynamic, team-oriented environment. • Good communication skills, both written and verbal. • Availability to travel for testing-related activities on an as needed basis. • Flexibility to work additional hours as needed. • Ability to immediately obtain and maintain a UK security clearance. PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS • Some experience with UAS design or operational deployment. • Working knowledge of UAV testing methodologies and data analysis. • Hold UK General VLOS Certificate. • Remote pilot experience on VTOL UAS. • Ability to travel up to 50% of the time. • Familiarity with programming languages such as Go, Java, C++, Python, JavaScript, etc The salary range for this role is an estimate based on a wide range of compensation factors, inclusive of base salary only.
