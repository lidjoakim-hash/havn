Product Owner, Finance

Help shape the future of finance at Sandvik Coromant. We’re looking for a Product Owner who turns strategy into smart, scalable solutions and drives digital transformation across our global finance organization. Taking full ownership of our Finance application product, you create measurable business value, support our SAP transformation journey and lead the development and implementation of AI-enabled solutions going forward. With us, you get to: Shape how finance evolves through digitalization, automation and AI. how finance works through digitalization, automation and AI. Join an empowered, high-trust environment that challenges established ways of working. Collaborate globally and contribute to the future of manufacturing. About the job As Product Owner, you set the vision, strategy and roadmap for the Finance application product, translating strategic priorities into solutions that deliver measurable value. Working in a global and agile setup, you connect business needs, technology and emerging AI capabilities while supporting our strategic capability plans and SAP transformation. Job responsibilities Own and continuously develop the Finance application product across business control, finance control and related processes. Define and communicate the product vision, strategy and roadmap. Drive the development and implementation of AI-enabled solutions that strengthen finance processes and business value. Identify opportunities, prioritize development and guide agile teams based on business needs. Collaborate with business areas, Platform teams and stakeholders to align priorities and strategic capability plans. Ensure solutions are valuable, viable, feasible and usable, from initial opportunity through continuous improvement. Location and flexibility The position is based in Sandviken or Solna, with a hybrid setup that combines office and remote work. Your profile You bring solid experience from the finance domain together with a strong understanding of agile ways of working and product development. With the ability to connect business needs, technology and emerging opportunities, you turn strategic direction into practical action and sustainable solutions. A university degree or equivalent experience is expected, ideally within business or IT. Your background also includes Experience leading changes and building trust across teams and functions. Knowledge of SAP S/4HANA, SAP Clean Core or cloud-based products is an advantage. An interest in exploring how AI can strengthen finance processes and create business value. Collaboration comes naturally to you, and you build strong relationships across functions and cultures. Clear communication, active listening and a structured approach help you create alignment and move work forward. Curious and adaptable, you’re comfortable challenging established approaches and navigating a fast-paced environment. Our culture Innovation is in our DNA and we’re proud to shape the future of manufacturing in close collaboration with our customers and partners. We believe in an inclusive, equal and open-minded culture, and we nurture our diversities to form a solid foundation for achieving great results.
