Engineering Team Manager

Hitachi Energy Sweden AB · Västerås, Sweden

💰 ca. 450 000–550 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 6.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

The opportunity  Do you want to lead and develop engineers working with SCADA and Control & Protection solutions for substation automation? Hitachi Energy’s Grid Automation business is looking for an Engineering Team Manager to lead a local team of more than 8 engineers. You will coach and develop your team, plan resources and support high-quality project delivery in collaboration with local and global colleagues. This is an opportunity to combine people leadership with continuous improvement, helping both your team and the engineering organization develop. This role is based in Västerås, Sweden. How you’ll make an impact Lead and develop a team of more than 8 engineers through clear objectives, coaching and support. Take responsibility for competence development across SCADA and Control & Protection engineering. Drive continuous improvement of engineering processes to strengthen quality and productivity. Enable successful project delivery by ensuring the right competencies, resources and priorities are in place. Work closely with project managers, technical leads and engineering teams locally and globally to solve resource and delivery challenges Responsible for center budget planning and monitor costs throughout the year. Create a safe, inclusive and collaborative working environment while ensuring compliance with local labor law, work environment law and company policies. Demonstrate Hitachi Energy’s Leadership Pillars: live our vision, work together, deliver promises and develop people. Your background A university degree in electrical engineering or an equivalent qualification. Experience in the area of Substation Automation is required. You are a flexible leader with good communication skills and hold a strong customer focus. You have a genuine interest in coaching and developing people and fostering strong collaboration across teams and functions, locally and globally. Previous leadership experience as Team Manager, Team Leader or Project Manager would be considered as advantage. Experience in leading change and supporting teams through organizational or process improvements is an advantage. The role requires fluent Swedish and English both in speaking and writing to be able to interact with both local and global stakeholders.
