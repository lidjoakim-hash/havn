Swedish-speaking nurses in Finland – your next chapter starts in Jönköping County, Sweden A rewarding career. A welcoming team. A place to feel at home. Are you a registered nurse or specialist nurse based in Finland who speaks Swedish? Bring your skills, experience and ideas to Region Jönköpings län in southern Sweden. Here, you can take the next step in your career while building a life with room for the things you love. Whether you are looking for a new challenge, want to develop within your speciality or are simply curious about working in Sweden, we would love to hear from you. Find your place with us With three hospitals in Värnamo, Eksjö and Jönköping, as well as primary care centres across the county, we offer a variety of settings in which to build your nursing career. Discover the team and workplace that suit your interests and ambitions. We welcome registered nurses and specialist nurses within: Intensive care Anaesthetic nursing Operating theatre nursing Psychiatric nursing Midwives Radiographers Medical, surgical and orthopaedic care We are also interested in hearing from specialist doctors. Your expertise matters. So do you. Good care starts with people who feel supported, involved and able to grow. With us, you will work alongside skilled, welcoming colleagues and approachable managers. You can look forward to: An introduction tailored to you, taking your experience and individual needs into account. Opportunities to develop, deepen your knowledge and grow in your professional role. Close teamwork, with colleagues who share their knowledge and support one another. The chance to contribute, bringing your ideas and experience into the development of care. A new chapter in Sweden, with a familiar language Moving to another country is a big decision. Being able to use Swedish from the start gives you a valuable foundation for getting to know your colleagues, connecting with patients and finding your place in a new community. You must be able to communicate confidently in Swedish, both spoken and written, to work with us. This is essential for patient communication, clinical documentation and everyday teamwork. Make room for life beyond work Imagine finishing your shift and heading out for a walk by the lake, meeting friends in town or spending the evening with your family. Jönköping County brings together lively towns, smaller communities, forests and lakes, giving you options for how and where you want to live. Choose the energy of Jönköping, the historic surroundings of Eksjö or the welcoming atmosphere of Värnamo. Whether you are moving on your own, with a partner or with children, there is plenty to discover and enjoy. Our vision, “A good life in an attractive region”, reflects what we want to create for our patients, our communities and our colleagues. Let’s talk about your future Tell us about your nursing background, your interests and what would make a move from Finland worthwhile for you. Together, we can explore opportunities that match your experience and ambitions. Bring your expertise. Find your team. Build your next chapter with Region Jönköpings län.
