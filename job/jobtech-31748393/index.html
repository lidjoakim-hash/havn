As Nordic Product Commercial Lead/T&C at PostNord, you turn product changes into commercial reality across the Nordics. Turn Nordic product transformation into commercial reality. As Nordic Product Commercial Lead/T&C at PostNord, you create commercial product clarity and coordinate market readiness and implementation across markets. You connect product decisions and legal requirements with clear customer terms, commercial offers and practical go-to-market material. You will influence how product changes reach customers, strengthen Nordic collaboration and develop agents and digital tools that make product knowledge easier to access and apply. Based in Stockholm, Helsinki, Oslo or Copenhagen, you join Nordic Parcel Product Management, where customer value, profitable growth and Nordic harmonisation are central. Purpose, Offer and Benefits See your contribution take shape in launches, migrations and everyday commercial execution. You will join a Nordic team, combining clear ownership with broad cross-functional collaboration and great development opportunities. In addition to fair employment conditions according to collective agreements, we offer: We offer you a flexible hybrid workplace with a prestige-free environment where we as a team help each other to reach our goals. We encourage self-sufficient leadership for development and career opportunities. In addition to the benefits mentioned above, we offer: Work in a Nordic and flexible environment Good insurance and occupational pension conditions Wellness allowance & access to office gym and swimming pool Employee benefits through PostNord´s own employee foundation PostNord Plus Employment according to a collective agreement. Your Responsibilities You will ensure that parcel product changes are translated into clear, accurate and aligned terms, customer-facing material and commercial implementation plans. Nordic terms and conditions: Develop, maintain and coordinate Nordic terms and conditions and the process for parcel-product-related changes. Ensure structured planning, stakeholder input, documentation and implementation follow-up, in close collaboration with each country’s price and terms process. Harmonised product content and communication: Ensure clear, consistent product rules, service descriptions, limitations and commercial conditions across Nordic markets, aligned with legal, product and value proposition decisions. Translate these into customer-facing descriptions, FAQs, sales support and launch material. Develop internal communication plans and content across agreed channels. Governance and continuous improvement: Maintain governance and documentation for commercial product content and terms. Identify and resolve inconsistencies, unclear product logic, customer friction and documentation gaps. Use feedback from customers, markets, Sales and Customer Service to improve clarity and commercial product material. Commercial planning and market readiness: Coordinate commercial planning and activation for selected projects, product adjustments, launches and migrations, covering stakeholder alignment, market implementation and follow-up. Agents and digital support tools: Develop agents and digital tools that improve efficiency, quality and consistency in product, T&C and customer-facing work, helping commercial teams understand and apply product information and customer value. You work closely with Product Managers, Pricing Leads, Value Proposition Leads, Legal, Sales, Customer Service, Digital and country organisations. The Bigger Picture Nordic Parcel Product Management owns and develops the Nordic parcel portfolio across markets. Here, transformation means turning complex customer and operational needs into clear portfolio choices and effective delivery. The role reports to the Manager Value Proposition & CX.
