Veritaz is a leading IT staffing solutions provider in Sweden, committed to advancing individual careers and aiding employers in ensuring the perfect talent fit. With a proven track record of successful partnerships with top companies, we have rapidly grown our presence in the USA, Europe, and Sweden as a dependable and trusted resource within the IT industry. Job Overview Role Summary Our client is seeking a Software Engineer specializing in Java and Kotlin to join a cross-functional team working across system integrations, operations, data analytics, and front-end development. The assignment focuses on building and maintaining scalable, microservice-based solutions while using AI-assisted development tools and low-code platforms to deliver targeted business solutions efficiently. You will collaborate closely with domain experts to translate business requirements into robust technical solutions. You will also contribute to DevSecOps practices, technical governance, and security in a fast-moving environment where adaptability and contributions beyond your primary area of expertise are valued. Key Responsibilities Design, develop, and maintain scalable backend services using Java or Kotlin. Build and evolve solutions based on modern microservice architectures. Work with cross-functional colleagues across integrations, operations, data analytics, and front-end development. Collaborate with domain experts to understand business needs and convert them into effective technical solutions. Use AI-assisted development capabilities and low-code platforms to accelerate the delivery of targeted business solutions. Contribute to DevSecOps practices, governance, and security throughout the development lifecycle. Adapt to changing priorities and support technical areas beyond the core backend engineering scope when required. Share technical knowledge and contribute to the continued development of the engineering team. Technical Stack & Requirements Programming languages: Java and Kotlin. Backend framework: Spring Boot or comparable backend technologies. Architecture: Modern backend architecture and scalable microservice-based systems. Delivery practices: DevSecOps, technical governance, and security-conscious software development. Development platforms: Low-code platforms and AI-assisted development tools. Engineering context: Integrations, operations, data analytics, and collaboration with front-end development functions. Qualifications & Experience The successful consultant has 3–5 years of professional experience working with Java or Kotlin and Spring Boot or similar technologies. A strong understanding of modern backend architecture is required, together with practical experience using low-code platforms, AI-assisted development, or both. Experience collaborating in a fast-paced, cross-functional engineering environment is important. The role also suits an engineer who is motivated to broaden their technical expertise, share knowledge, and contribute outside their immediate specialization.
