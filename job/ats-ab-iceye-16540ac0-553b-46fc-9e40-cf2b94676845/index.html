Role highlights: - Supply Chain Specialist (Ground Station & ISR) - Location: Espoo, Finland - Department: Global Supply Chain - Reports to: Category Manager, Ground Station and ISR - Employment type: Permanent - Workplace model: Hybrid - Employment is subject to applicable security screening (incl. SUPO, where required) Why this role matters: This role is critical to ensuring ICEYE can reliably deliver complex Ground Station and ISR solutions to our Mission customers. You will orchestrate and manage the daily execution and supply of hardware and materials, software, services, and external capacity for Ground Station & ISR programs from the supply chain and sourcing side. By working in the supply base and with suppliers for hardware, software, and related services, this role directly impacts delivery schedules and mission success. It also ensures we can adapt quickly to customer-specific requirements and maintain readiness and resilience across the Ground Station and ISR programs. Who We Are ICEYE is the world leader in sovereign intelligence from space. We deliver persistent monitoring capabilities to detect and respond to changes in any location on Earth. ICEYE owns the world's largest and most advanced SAR (synthetic aperture radar) satellite constellation. To our customers we provide intelligence with unmatched quality, latency and revisit times, in any weather, day or night. To governments who choose to operate their own constellation we provide this proven capability as a sovereign system. ICEYE-built constellations serve customers in defence and intelligence, environmental monitoring, insurance and emergency management. We enable fast decisions that contribute to a safer future. Founded and headquartered in Finland, ICEYE operates globally with over 1000 employees across Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. Your Day-to-day Responsibilities - Own end-to-end sourcing and supply and availability of hardware, software, and services for Ground Station and ISR programs - Translate engineering and program requirements into sourcing strategies, RFQs and supplier collaboration and relationship management practices and targets - Assess new and existing suppliers for technical capability, quality, and risks, and ensure the most optimal supplier portfolio to support the ICEYE strategy and growth - Negotiate contracts, pricing, and long-term agreements to optimize total cost of ownership and material availability. - Track supplier delivery performance, quality, and responsiveness against agreed KPI’s for delivery, quality, and service targets - Partner closely with engineering, Mission programs, quality and other relevant stakeholders at ICEYE to align on the technical specifications and requirements for sourcing decisions - Support scaling of the supply base for increasing production volumes and customer programs - Ensure accurate execution and tracking in ERP and related systems, including costs and status. - Ensure compliance with contractual, regulatory, and program requirements from the supply chain and sourcing perspective - Continuously improve execution processes for availability, reliability, responsiveness, and cost optimization Requirements What we're looking for Must haves: - 3+ years of experience in supply chain, strategic sourcing, or category management, including working and collaborating with global suppliers ideally in aerospace, automotive, electronics, or medical industry. - Bachelor’s degree in Supply Chain Management, Engineering, Business, or a related field - Ability to work independently with technically complex products, including interpreting BOMs, specifications, interfaces, hardware, software and services. -
