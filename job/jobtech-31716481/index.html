Software Developer Who are we? Amaris Consulting is an independent technology consulting firm providing guidance and solutions to businesses. With more than 1,000 clients across the globe, we have been rolling out solutions in major projects for over a decade – this is made possible by an international team of 7,600 people spread across 5 continents and more than 60 countries. Our solutions focus on four different Business Lines: Information System & Digital, Telecom, Life Sciences and Engineering. We’re focused on building and nurturing a top community where all our team members can achieve their full potential. Amaris Consulting is your steppingstone to cross rivers of change, meet challenges and achieve all your projects with success. Job description Join Amaris Consulting as a Software Developer in Gothenburg and contribute to innovative digital projects within the automotive sector. You will join a product-oriented environment focused on designing, developing, and improving scalable digital solutions used across international markets. Depending on the project context, the role may involve frontend or backend development, integrations, cloud, platform, or application development. Your missions Design, develop, and maintain digital applications, services, or platforms according to project needs. Contribute to frontend or backend development activities depending on the project context. Build and improve APIs, user interfaces, data flows, or system integrations. Support the development of scalable, reliable, and maintainable software solutions. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand needs and deliver high-quality solutions. Contribute to testing, code quality, documentation, and continuous improvement practices. Support deployment, monitoring, troubleshooting, and performance improvement activities. Participate in technical discussions and contribute to solution design and delivery standards. Your profile Experience in software development, with a focus on frontend or backend development. Experience with programming languages or frameworks such as Java, Golang, JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Spring Boot, or similar technologies. Experience with software architecture concepts such as APIs, microservices, distributed systems, event-driven architecture, or system integrations. Experience with development tools or environments such as CI/CD pipelines, container platforms, cloud services, monitoring tools, version control, or deployment automation. Experience with databases or data storage technologies such as SQL, NoSQL, or similar solutions. Experience in automotive, e-commerce, digital services, insurance, transactional platforms, or large-scale product organizations is a plus. Degree in computer science, software development, information technology, or a related field is a plus. English fluent. Swedish is a plus. Strong communication skills, adaptability, and the ability to work effectively in cross-functional teams. A structured and solution-oriented mindset, with the ability to learn new technologies and contribute in evolving project environments.
