Video/Audio Streaming Specialist – Stockholm

Sway Sourcing Sweden Aktiebolag · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 390 000–480 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 18.2.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

🎬 About the Assignment We are looking for a senior Video/Audio Streaming Specialist with deep expertise in video streaming and mobile playback. You will play a key role in improving streaming capabilities and playback experiences, with a particular focus on iOS. Alongside hands-on technical work, you will coach iOS and Android engineers and document your findings to ensure lasting knowledge transfer. Your background may be in either iOS or Android development; deep streaming expertise is the priority. 🛠️ Key Responsibilities Review the existing playback architecture, including AVFoundation, buffering and error handling. Diagnose and resolve critical playback issues in production. Design and improve offline downloads, DRM implementation and background playback strategies. Enhance player experiences, playback queues and session flows. Measure playback quality using Quality of Experience (QoE) metrics. Coach engineers, communicate technical recommendations and document findings to support long-term knowledge transfer. 🎯 Required Skills and Experience Deep knowledge of streaming protocols, particularly HLS; familiarity with DASH and CMAF is desirable. Strong experience with AVFoundation, AVKit and audio session events. Experience with DRM and content protection, including FairPlay Streaming and key delivery. Expertise in offline playback, download management and license renewal strategies. Proven ability to debug playback failures in production using analytical tools. Understanding of QoE metrics and how to implement playback quality measurements. Strong Swift and modern iOS development skills, with familiarity with SwiftUI and reactive programming patterns. Ability to work independently, collaborate across teams and provide clear technical recommendations. Interest in Chromecast integration. ⭐ Nice to Have Experience with CarPlay or Apple Watch media features. Background in audio-focused applications such as podcasts or audiobooks. Knowledge of backend and CDN processes, including transcoding and access management. Experience with large-scale consumer applications and subscription models. Experience with Android ExoPlayer/Media3. Familiarity with Swedish or Nordic media services. 📍 Assignment Details Location: Stockholm, Sweden Start: As soon as possible, with flexibility Duration: 6–9 months; final duration to be confirmed Work model: Hybrid in Stockholm Language: Professional proficiency in English Seniority: Senior We look forward to receiving your application!
