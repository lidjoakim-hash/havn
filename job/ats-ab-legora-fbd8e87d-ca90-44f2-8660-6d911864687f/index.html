ABOUT THE ROLE The way legal work gets done is changing, and Engagement is at the centre of that transformation. We're looking for an Engagement Manager to own adoption across a portfolio of mid-market corporate clients and help their in-house legal teams fundamentally change the way they work. This is a role for someone who has led change inside complex organisations and knows that real transformation is designed and managed. You will own the adoption and long-term success of 15 – 20 corporate accounts: building the relationships, defining the outcomes and serving as the trusted adviser who turns Legora into an embedded part of how those teams operate. Mid-market means breadth as well as depth. You will be running adoption programmes across 15 – 20 accounts at once, each with its own stakeholders, timelines and success measures, which makes this a role where strong project management is not a nice-to-have but the thing that determines whether you succeed. You will lean on repeatable playbooks where they work and go bespoke where an account earns it. At the heart of the role is designing each client's adoption playbook and delivering the change programmes that transform how they work. For every account you will build a tailored roadmap, sequence initiatives from initial rollout through function-wide scaled adoption, define measurable outcomes and ROI and evolve the programme as new AI capabilities and use cases emerge. You will be critical as we continue to build a best-in-class adoption programme at Legora, helping clients realise measurable ROI, expand successful use cases and transform the way their teams work.
