Video: https://youtu.be/i4SDcLir6OcPhD-position The Faculty of Medicine, Department of Clinical Dentistry (IKO), is offering a full-time PhD-position for a duration of three (3) years.  The position is linked to the large collaborative HORIZON project coordinated by IKO, titled “BioFACTORy”, and financed by the European Commission. The position is funded by the University of Bergen. About the project/work tasks: The BioFACTORy project addresses the need for improved manufacturing of mesenchymal stromal cell (MSC)-based tissue-engineered medicinal products (TEMPs). Its primary objective is to develop the BioFACTORy suite, a trustworthy, AI-powered, GMP-conform automated cell and tissue engineering platform that standardises the entire MSC production lifecycle. By integrating automation, artificial intelligence, and advanced manufacturing technologies, the platform aims to enable the consistent, scalable, and cost-efficient production of high-quality MSC-based TEMPs, making these advanced therapies more accessible. Specific aims: • To identify the cellular morphological link with their functional attributes • To develop a label-free, non-destructive approach to improve regenerative potential of MSCs • To develop machine learning/artificial intelligence-based models for cellular functional prediction • To develop optimized tissue engineered constructs using AI/ML-assisted cell functional screening  Qualifications and personal qualities: • The applicant must possess a Master&#96;s degree or an equivalent in discipline related to the tasks of the project. • The applicant with educational background in dentistry or dental surgery, regenerative medicine, regenerative dentistry, tissue engineering, and stem cell biology is favoured. • The successful applicant must have documented experience in human primary stem/stromal cell culture, histology and histological analysis as well as molecular biological assessment. • Experience in computational engineering, machine learning and artificial intelligence, statistical modelling for object detection is favoured.  • Experience in 3D bioimaging, Fluorescence-Activated Cell Sorting (FACS) and/or bioinformatics for omics data is desirable  • Applicants with experience in one or more of additive manufacturing, 3D cell culture models, biomaterials will be advantageous • The candidate should demonstrate the ability to work independently and in a structured manner, while also being capable of effective collaboration with others • The applicant must exhibit both the ability and motivation to undertake a PhD project. • Fluency in both oral and written English is required, see documentation requirements for further details. The applicant’s personal suitability for the position is a significant factor in the evaluation. Applicants whose educational qualifications are from countries other than Norway must provide a certified translation of the diploma and transcript of grades to English or a Scandinavian language, if the original documents are not in any of these languages. It is also required that the applicant submit a review from the Norwegian Directorate for Higher Education and Skills (HK-dir) to verify whether the education in question (bachelor and master's degree) is of a scope and level that corresponds to the level of a Norwegian master’s degree. Please see https://hkdir.no/en/ for more information about HK-dir’s general recognition. The review process by HK-dir may take some time. Therefore, please submit your application to HK-dir as soon as you decide to apply for the position. If you do not receive a response within the application deadline, you must include documentation from HK-dir confirming receipt of your application. Please note that the automatic recognition offered by HK-dir insufficient and will not be accepted as basis for admission to the PhD programme. Applicants with education from a Nordic country or those with a medical degree from the EEA area and a license to practice medicine in Norway are exempt from the requirement for HK-dir assessment.
