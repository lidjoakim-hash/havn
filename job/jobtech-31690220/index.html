In English We at Hybrus AB are looking for an experienced IT Adminstrator for one of our clients in Sweden (Stockholm/ Goteburg /Mälmo) who has proven experience to manage work independently. Skills Required : Linux , Windows Backup & Recovery - Dell Datadomain, Commvault. Virtualization: VMware (VCF), Nutanix Automation : vRealize (Aria) Network : Cisco, Arista, NSX-T Security : Palo Alto, Fortinet You will be an important person in the team responsible for management and further development of technical services for our customers. An important part of the work is to ensure the availability of systems and digital services. High level of efficiency towards security, stability and accuracy in the work is needed. You will also have a central role in the delivery and support flow of the IT services. It is important to work and deliver as per documented processes/ routines to ensure SLA requirements are met. Examples of what we work with: • Deliver server services according to set SLAs and be part of the operation and support team • Deploy new Servers • Patch management and upgrading of the server environment • Script development and work with automation tools for efficient working methods • Participate in projects linked to the technology platform • Ensure compliance with the safety requirements that exist • Actively participate in technology improvement work Who are you ? We are looking for a committed person, where good knowledge of of Skills listed, is an advantage. Ideally, you have experience from large operating environments with different operating systems. You are a meticulous and service-oriented person who thrives in a team environment. Your ambition to drive the work towards automation and efficiency is important to us. We are looking for someone with communication skills and the ability to collaborate both internally and externally. A service-oriented approach is essential to thrive in the role. We appreciate your curiosity and look forward to you sharing new ideas and suggestions for improvement. Furthermore, we are looking for a flexible person who is ready to face challenges even outside of normal working days. Your readiness to adapt to varying working hours will be an important asset to our team. We are looking for a candidate who: • Has post-secondary education or equivalent work experience • Has good ability to communicate in English and/or Swedish , both verbally and in writing We look particularly favorably on applications from candidates with the following additional skills: • Experience working with Windows, Unix and Linux server systems • Knowledge of OpenStack, Kubernetes, PowerShell, Tanzu and VMware (VCF) • Scripting skills in Python, Powershell and Bash • Network And Security - Physical, Virtual (Cisco, Arista, NSX, Palo Alto/Fortinet) Application procedure If you feel that the position is right for you, you are most welcome to submit your application to careers@hybrus.se with your CV (in English) and a short work summary.
