Field Safety Engineer, Germany

Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states. The Waymo Safety team works to promote and help to continuously improve the safety of Waymo’s fully autonomous driving technology. Our experts develop safety goals and strategies, and conduct safety engineering analyses to ensure safety is being considered throughout the design and development of our vehicles. The team develops and promotes safety strategies and policies for autonomous vehicles for its work with regulatory authorities, lawmakers, law enforcement and public and non-profit organizations. Our Safety Team also helps advise on compliance with applicable environmental, health, and safety regulations. As Waymo’s first Field Safety Engineer in Germany, you will serve as Waymo’s primary engineering/technical liaison with safety regulators in Germany, working closely with Waymo’s Policy, Legal, and other teams in Germany. You will also work with Waymo’s Field Safety team in the United States to identify and analyze issues that could impact the safety of Waymo’s fleet of autonomous vehicles, with a focus on issues specific to Waymo’s operations in Germany. This role is based in Munich, reporting to Waymo’s Head of Field Safety, Europe based in California. You will: • You will serve as Waymo’s primary liaison on post-deployment safety with automotive and traffic safety regulators in Germany. You will work with Waymo’s Policy, Legal, and other teams to establish and maintain proactive outreach and respond to regulatory requests. • You will work with Waymo’s Field Safety team in the United States to identify and analyze issues that could impact the safety of Waymo’s fleet of autonomous vehicles, bringing your local Germany perspective to help identify and prioritize issues that could impact Waymo’s operations in Germany. • You will act as the lead for Field Safety issues impacting Waymo's operations in Germany. • You have willingness and ability to participate in regular shifts as part of a 24/7 Safety on-call rotation, which is an integral part of Waymo’s incident response capabilities. You have: • Bachelor of Science in Engineering, Computer Science, or a related technical degree. • 10+ years of engineering or other technical experience in automotive or another transportation field. • Experience with Germany’s automotive and/or transportation regulatory framework and familiarity with expectations when working with these regulators. • A collaborative mindset and the ability to work effectively with Policy, Legal, Operations, Engineering and other internal teams in UK, Europe and in the United States. • Strong communication and presentation skills and the ability to establish and maintain professional presence with regulators in Germany. • Ability to work occasionally in the evenings to collaborate with the US-based Field Safety team. We prefer: • M.S. or Ph.D. in engineering or a related technical field. • Experience interacting with automotive and traffic safety regulators. • Familiarity with autonomous vehicle technology. • Experience working in a fast-paced, rapidly-scaling company. • Familiarity with SMS, Safety Case and/or UNECE Automated Driving System regulations.
