About Spiideo Spiideo is a fast growing sports technology company headquartered in Malmö. We provide software services to the world of sports in the areas of performance analysis and automated video production. About the role Help us teach our AI camera to film sports like a skilled human operator. You'll work with our machine learning team to capture, review and label footage so AutoFollow learns how to frame and follow play in 10+ sports. What you'll do Record and review matches across 10+ sports (e.g. football, ice hockey, basketball, handball, rugby, volleyball, floorball, lacrosse, field hockey, baseball) Show how each sport should be followed: where the action is, what to keep in frame, and when to zoom, pan or reframe Annotate and quality-check video to produce training data for AutoFollow Review AI-generated camera output and flag where it gets framing, tracking or game situations wrong Work with engineers to define per-sport rules for "good coverage" Help build annotation guidelines and best practices What we're looking for Strong understanding of how many different sports are played: rules, flow of play, key moments An eye for good framing and anticipating where the play is going Accurate, consistent and detail-oriented, including on repetitive tasks Clear written and spoken English Comfortable working with software tools and giving structured feedback Based in or able to commute to Malmö Nice to have Experience as a camera operator, in sports broadcasting or with video production Coaching, playing or video analysis experience in one or more sports Interest in sports technology Terms 6-month contract, 50% Onsite in Malmö. Remote work isn't possible, the role involves hands-on capture and working closely with the team Pay: 180–220 SEK/hour (depending on experience) Start: ASAP
