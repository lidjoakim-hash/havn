Takeback specialist

Role purpose To support operational excellence by managing technical configurations, driving process improvements, implementing new technologies, and ensuring the long term stability and effectiveness of production-related systems and workflows. Tasks and responsibilities Participate in the international Takeback forum and actively work for European alignment and co-operation. Technical setup Overall responsibility for technical set up, point of escalation for technical issues. Strive to keep technical set up current and up to date. Support and facilitate team technical development and ensuring relevant competence is maintained. Overall service responsibility, to ensure active service SKUs are working in system and within the delivery process and new services are implemented according to process. Process and documentation Facilitate process improvement work within the Takeback team. Maintain process documentation and keeping processes current and up to date. Develop new processes when needed, ensure they are communicated and implemented to team. Ensure proper structure and accessibility for department documentation. Overall responsibility for deparment performance index data. Information Security Read, understand and follow Information Security policies and instructions. Meet the Confidentiality, Integrity and Availability requirements to safeguard Dustin information as well as related information assets. Follow the rules for the ownership and acceptable use to assigned company assets and return them upon termination of employment. Attend to Security awareness education. Report Security incidents or suspected breaches. Support others to demonstrate their leadership as it applies to information security within their areas of responsibility. Promote and support the continual improvement to information security management system. Conduct regular ISMS reviews to ensure its continuing suitability, adequacy and effectiveness. Stakeholders Logistics department Sales department Operations department IT department Procurement department Dustin customers Qualifications and formal requests for the role Master of Science in Informatics, Information Systems, Computer Science, or a related field. Equivalent combination of education and relevant professional experience may be considered.
