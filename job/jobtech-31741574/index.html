What you’ll do As the Project Controls Performance and Reporting Lead for Green Steel, you’ll bring together project information from Planning, Cost, Contracts, the EPCM contractor and other area teams. You’ll analyse this information to show how the project is performing, highlight trends and deviations, and provide clear reporting and recommendations to support decision-making. Coordinate the collection, validation and integration of cost, contract, schedule, progress, quantity and resource information. Analyse performance data to identify trends, variances, risks, opportunities and issues requiring action. Prepare consolidated reports, dashboards and management summaries for the area. Work with project teams and the EPCM contractor to align inputs and resolve gaps or discrepancies in reporting. Follow up significant differences between reported progress and site execution, escalating issues where needed. Support consistent project controls processes and reporting standards within the area. Identify practical improvements to reporting, data quality and analysis, including opportunities to use automation and Power BI. Provide technical guidance on integrated reporting and performance analysis, and support project reviews and assurance activities. What you’ll bring You’re analytical and comfortable working across different project disciplines. You can bring complex project information together, identify what it means for performance, and explain your findings clearly to different stakeholders. Relevant experience in project controls, cost control, contract or commercial controls, or project performance reporting in a complex project environment. Experience consolidating and analysing information across areas such as cost, schedule, progress, contracts, quantities or resources. Experience working with EPCM contractors and multidisciplinary project teams. Strong skills in trend and variance analysis, forecasting, progress measurement, and identifying risks and opportunities. Proficiency in Microsoft Excel, Power BI and relevant project controls systems. Knowledge of project controls governance and practices such as AACE or PMI. Ability to turn complex data into clear reports, insights and recommendations. Professional written and verbal communication skills in English. A relevant degree or equivalent practical experience. A project controls or cost management certification is an advantage.
