Are you passionate about driving growth through digital media? Do you thrive at the intersection of strategy, analytics, and commercial impact? Then this could be the role for you. We are looking for a Digital Media Manager to help shape and develop how Tre works with paid digital media investments with a strong focus on performance marketing and traffic acquisition. This is an exciting opportunity to take on a newly established role with significant influence, combining strategic direction with hands-on execution and a clear impact on business results. About the roleAs Digital Media Manager, you will be responsible for Tre's paid digital media investments and the development of our performance media strategy. You will plan, prioritize, and optimize investments across the digital landscape together with our media agency focusing on channels such as Google, Meta, TikTok, Snapchat, and other relevant platforms. You will work closely with both our Online and Commercial teams and serve as Tre's internal digital media expert. The role also involves close collaboration with external media agency, where you will act as a key point of contact. To succeed in this role, you need to be both strategic and analytical. You are curious about what drives performance, comfortable challenging existing ways of working, and skilled at turning insights into recommendations that create tangible business value. What you'll do Own and develop Tre's digital media strategy, with a focus on traffic acquisition, media investments, and digital performance. Plan, prioritize and evaluate digital campaigns while managing budgets, forecasting, and performance reporting. Translate data and insights into actionable recommendations and drive strategic discussions with media agencies and internal stakeholders. Collaborate across functions to identify new opportunities within digital marketing, AI, and automation. Act as a trusted advisor and subject matter expert within digital media and performance marketing. How hiring manager, Maria, describes her leadership style:  "I believe in a leadership style built on openness, transparency, and accessibility. We encourage dialogue, initiative, and the courage to try new things, while collaboration remains a key factor in our success. You'll join a fast-paced environment where people support one another, share knowledge, and work together towards common goals." We believe you will bring the following: Minimum 5 years of experience in digital media and performance marketing. A strong understanding of the digital media landscape and how different channels, strategies, and buying models work together to drive business results. Hands-on experience within platforms such as Google Ads, Meta, and other relevant advertising platforms. Experience with analytics and marketing measurement, including attribution, digital analytics, or marketing mix modelling. Knowledge of tracking, measurement, and data quality, with an interest in developing data-driven ways of working. Experience managing large budgets and optimizing investments against business objectives. The ability to transform complex data into clear insights, recommendations, and business value. Experience leading projects and collaborating with multiple stakeholders across different functions. Excellent communication skills in English, both written and spoken. Experience working with traditional media and media agencies is highly valued, though not required. Experience within the telecommunications industry is considered an advantage, but it is not a requirement. Who you are You are analytical, commercially minded, and naturally curious. You enjoy diving into data and uncovering insights, while also being able to step back and see the bigger business picture.
