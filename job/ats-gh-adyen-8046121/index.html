Sitting in the Global Commercial Strategy team - at the direct intersection of Product and Commercial - you will act as the strategic bridge advocating for our customers' online checkout, in-store, hardware, shopper experience and loyalty needs across multiple industries - in direct collaboration with your GCS peers who own in-store heavy industries such as Retail, Food & Beverage and Hospitality.
