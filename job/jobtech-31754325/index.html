Technical Partnership Lead

Sandvik Aktiebolag · Sweden

💰 ca. 520 000–640 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 4.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Successful integrations depend on both sound technical understanding and strong external partnerships. As Technical Partnership Lead, you own the integration strategy and how our products connect with customers’ technology environments. You identify where integrations create the greatest business and customer value and drive these opportunities forward, bringing technical requirements, internal teams and external partners together. Here, you get the opportunity to: Own technology partnerships and ecosystem integrations across the full product portfolio. Combine technical understanding, external partner relationships, and integration strategy in one position. Help create a coherent integration roadmap across the portfolio, connecting our products with valuable opportunities across the wider manufacturing ecosystem. The Cloud Product Organization brings together the capabilities needed to build and grow our cloud products. With clear responsibilities and a portfolio-wide perspective, we work together to create customer value through effective partnerships and integrations. About the job You lead technology partnerships and integrations across the TFS product portfolio, from identifying the initial need to launch and ongoing development. With a clear view of customer environments and technical dependencies, you align partners and internal teams around priorities, requirements and workable solutions. Job responsibilities Own technology partnerships and ecosystem integrations end-to-end, including the integration roadmap. Build and manage relationships with external technology partners, aligning requirements and expectations. Identify and scope integration needs and translate them into clear technical requirements. Work with Tech and Engineering as integrations are built and tested. Coordinate with Product Owners to sequence integrations into delivery and support them through launch and ongoing maintenance. Contribute integration expertise to roadmap and technical architecture discussions. Location and flexibility This position offers flexibility to work remotely or from one of our entities in Sweden, Germany, the US, or India. Your profile You combine technical integration experience with strong partner and stakeholder management. A relevant technical education within engineering, IT/software or a related field is required, or equivalent professional experience. Your background includes Experience managing external technology partners and working across Product, Engineering or Tech and external partners. Ability to translate business and product needs into technical integration requirements, supported by an understanding of APIs, system integrations, data exchange and software ecosystems. Experience contributing to or managing an integration or partnership roadmap. Experience from industrial technology, manufacturing software, vending machines, inventory management, or CAM, machine and tooling ecosystems. To succeed, you build credible relationships and communicate comfortably across technical and business perspectives. You’re pragmatic when navigating complex integration challenges, influence progress without relying on direct authority and take ownership from initial need through ongoing integration. A strategic perspective helps you connect individual integrations to the broader technology ecosystem. The team You join a cross-functional, 10-person product team that includes the Head of Cloud Product. Sitting within the Product Portfolio part of the team, you work closely with Product Managers, Product Owners, Tech and external technology partners, acting as an important connection between these groups. Our culture Our role is clear – through every action, every day, we make the shift and advance the world through engineering. We believe in an inclusive, equal and open-minded culture, and we nurture our diversities to form a solid foundation for achieving great results. Add fair and rewarding
