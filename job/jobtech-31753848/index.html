Market Development Specialist

IVL Svenska Miljöinstitutet AB · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 350 000–420 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 17.2.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Are you a commercially driven professional who enjoys building business relationships, identifying market opportunities and turning insights into business growth? EPD International, a wholly owned subsidiary of IVL Swedish Environmental Research Institute, is looking for a Market Development Specialist (parental leave cover) to join our market team and contribute to our continued international growth. We operate the International EPD System, helping businesses communicate their products’ environmental impact through independently verified Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs). As sustainability, digitalization and regulatory developments create new business opportunities, you will combine B2B sales and stakeholder engagement with strategic market analysis and business development in a rapidly evolving industry. About the role As Market Development Specialist, you will play an important role in strengthening our market position, developing commercial relationships and identifying new business opportunities. You will investigate market potential, understand customer needs and industry developments, and build a clear view of where our efforts can have the greatest impact. Working closely with the Market Team, CEO and colleagues across the organisation, you will translate your findings into recommendations and targeted market development initiatives.  This experienced individual contributor role has no direct sales targets or people management responsibilities. You will have substantial autonomy and the opportunity to influence market priorities, combining analytical work with external engagement and hands-on implementation. What you will do  The role combines two equally important areas: Market Analysis & Business Development Conduct market research and competitive analysis to identify trends, opportunities and potential growth areas. Develop business strategies and commercial models for new and existing services. Support the development and alignment of commercial agreements, partnership frameworks and related documentation in collaboration with relevant internal experts. Identify and develop new opportunities for collaboration with strategic partners. Translate market insights into concrete business recommendations and actionable initiatives. Sales & Stakeholder Relations Drive B2B sales activities, from identifying potential customers and partners to developing opportunities and closing deals. Build and maintain strong relationships with key stakeholders, including manufacturers, LCA consultancies, industry organisations and technology providers. Identify customer needs and translate them into commercial opportunities. Develop strategic collaborations that strengthen our market presence and generate long-term business value. Represent EPD International in customer meetings and industry events. What you bring We are looking for a commercially minded and analytical professional who enjoys combining relationship-building with strategic thinking and hands-on execution. We believe you have: At least +3 years professional experience in business development, market analysis or commercial strategy. Proven experience in B2B sales and market development, preferably in an international environment. A demonstrated ability to build relationships, identify opportunities and generate business results. The ability to move from analysis to implementation and take ownership of projects from initial investigation through delivery and follow-up.  Experience working with business models, partnerships or commercial agreements. Excellent communication and negotiation skills in English, both written and spoken. A relevant university degree in business, marketing, economics, engineering or a related field. It is an advantage if you also have: Experience in sustainability, environmental services, construction products or related industries. Knowledge of LCAs, EPDs or environmental product information.
