Amaris Consulting is an independent technology consulting firm providing guidance and solutions to businesses. With more than 1,000 clients across the globe, we have been rolling out solutions in major projects for over a decade – this is made possible by an international team of 7,600 people spread across 5 continents and more than 60 countries. Our solutions focus on four different Business Lines: Information System & Digital, Telecom, Life Sciences and Engineering. We’re focused on building and nurturing a top talent community where all our team members can achieve their full potential. Amaris Consulting is your steppingstone to cross rivers of change, meet challenges and achieve all your projects with success. Job description Join Amaris Consulting as a Full-Stack Engineer in Gothenburg and contribute to a project for one of our clients in the automotive sector. You will work within a digital environment focused on service information products, supporting the full service flow from content creation to workshop usage and customer servicing experience. Your missions Design and develop backend services with Java and Spring Boot. Expose services through REST APIs and GraphQL Federation. Build and operate event-driven services using Kafka. Develop React and TypeScript applications within a microfrontend architecture. Define clear boundaries between independently deployable frontend modules. Work with SQL and NoSQL databases according to project needs. Deploy services to Kubernetes using GitHub Actions and ArgoCD. Monitor, trace, and improve service behavior with Grafana. Your profile Solid experience building backend services with Java and Spring Boot, with a deep understanding of microservices architecture and GraphQL Federation. Hands-on experience with event-driven architecture, including designing and running asynchronous services with Kafka. Experience building frontends with React and TypeScript, ideally within a microfrontend architecture. Experience defining boundaries, integrating independently deployable modules, and keeping the user experience consistent. Experience with both SQL and NoSQL databases, and the judgment to choose the right one for each problem. Strong testing mindset, from well-designed test cases to automated integration tests and quality gates in CI/CD pipelines. Curiosity about AI-assisted engineering, with hands-on experience using generative AI tools or a strong interest in working in a spec-driven way. English fluent. Strong communication skills, with the ability to turn conversations with users and stakeholders into well-defined, prioritized work. Ability to work with uncertainty, deliver in small steps, learn from feedback, and adapt when priorities or insights change. Amaris Consulting is proud to be an equal-opportunity workplace.
