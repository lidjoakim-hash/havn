The role You help the Solutions & Delivery team get new customers live and using the product well, by supporting onboarding projects, building small tools and materials, and learning how implementation really works. WHAT YOU'LL DO - Support onboarding projects: prepare kickoff materials, track tasks and open questions, and keep project plans and status notes up to date. - Support and own intake of Technical Delivery and Solutions Engineer requests. - Help configure and test customer setups (workspaces, permissions, templates, integrations) under the guidance of a Solutions Engineer or Delivery Lead. - Run QA on workflows before go-live and document bugs or gaps clearly for Product and Engineering. - Join customer calls as a note-taker, then write up summaries, decisions and action items within 24 hours. - Build and maintain internal resources: how-to guides, FAQs, demo environments and reusable onboarding checklists. - Pull and tidy usage data to show how newly onboarded customers are adopting the product. - Take on one self-owned project during the internship, such as automating a repetitive delivery task or improving a training asset, and present the result to the team. WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR - Currently studying, or recently graduated, in a field such as law, business, information systems, engineering or similar. We care more about curiosity than about the specific degree. - Clear written and spoken communication in English; additional languages are a plus. - Structured, detail-oriented and comfortable juggling several small tasks at once. - Comfortable with technology: you pick up new software quickly, and basic spreadsheet skills are expected. - Interest in legal tech, AI or professional services is a plus. Light scripting (SQL, Python) or prior customer-facing experience is a bonus, not a requirement. WHAT YOU'LL LEARN - How a customer moves from signed contract to live, active users, and where projects tend to stall. - How to scope and run a technical implementation with non-technical stakeholders. - How Solutions, Customer Success, Product and Sales work together around a customer.
