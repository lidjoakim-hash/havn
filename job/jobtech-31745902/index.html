Would you like to combine advanced data analytics and automation with audit judgement and help improve risk identification and decision-making? The opportunity As a Manager in Audit Analytics & Data Insights, you will lead analytics delivery across audit and assurance engagements. You will combine technical capability with knowledge of business processes, internal controls and audit evidence to identify risks, exceptions and actionable insights in complex datasets. Your key responsibilities Lead the design and delivery of analytics across financial, operational and compliance engagements. Oversee the extraction, transformation and analysis of large datasets using SQL, Databricks and Alteryx. Develop and automate scalable, reusable workflows that improve audit efficiency, consistency and coverage. Lead the development of dashboards and visualizations in Power BI or comparable tools. Review data quality, reconciliations, trend analysis, exception reporting, testing and documentation. Manage analytics workstreams, timelines and resources, and coach Senior Associates and junior colleagues. Skills and attributes for success Qualifications Approximately 4–8 years of relevant experience in audit analytics, data analytics, business intelligence or a related field, including experience coordinating teams or workstreams. Experience from audit or from designing, delivering and reviewing analytics for audit or assurance, with an understanding of audit methodology, risks, controls and how analytics can generate audit evidence and insights. Strong hands-on SQL skills, including complex queries, joins, data transformation and validation of large datasets. Experience with data visualization, preferably Power BI, and the ability to explain technical findings to different stakeholders. Experience with audit automation, process mining and AI-enabled tools to enhance audit quality, efficiency and risk identification. Strong project management, quality review and documentation skills. Meritorious Experience with Alteryx, Databricks, Python, SAP or Oracle. Knowledge of Purchase-to-Pay, Order-to-Cash or continuous monitoring. A relevant qualification or certification in audit, accounting, information systems or analytics. As a person you are Clear and structured, with a strong focus on quality. Comfortable working with both technical and non-technical stakeholders. Interested in developing others through feedback and knowledge sharing.
