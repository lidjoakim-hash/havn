Test Kitchen Assistant (All Genders)

HelloFresh · Verden, Niedersachsen, Germany, Germany

Your role • Factor_, a HelloFresh brand, is looking for a Test Kitchen Assistant (f/m/d/x) at our Verden location. You will be responsible for supporting our R&D Team in developing our popular ready-to-eat meals. Your area of responsibility • Primarily work in our test kitchen, within the production environment. Occasionally working across locations in the production facility. • Accurate weighing and portioning of ingredients • Organize ingredient samples and test kitchen refrigerators • Clean dishes and test kitchen equipment as needed • Ensuring product and food quality according to company standards • Keeping your workstation and all equipment clean, sanitized, and disinfected • Proper disposal or recycling of waste and packaging according to our guidelines and training • Adhering to all occupational safety and hygiene regulations • Taking on additional tasks as instructed by your supervisor Your profile • Previous experience in food production is an advantage but not required • A certificate according to § 43 of the German Infection Protection Act (IfSG) – formerly known as a health certificate – is desirable but not mandatory. If you don’t have it, we’ll organize and cover the costs • Good verbal and written skills in German and English • Physically able to work while standing and walking for most of your shift (up to 8 hours or more), including in changing temperature zones between 4°C and 26°C • Capable of lifting products weighing up to 23 kg, and performing tasks that involve bending, twisting, and kneeling • Able to work independently and in a team; reliable, punctual, and committed • Careful and conscientious handling of food • Strong quality awareness and a basic understanding of food quality characteristics, with attention to detail when inspecting products What we offer you • Growth & Development: Internal training opportunities plus a yearly Learning & Development budget • Food & Lifestyle: Discounts on Factor_-boxes • Compensation & Vacation: Competitive salary and 30 days of vacation • Health & Well-being: Access to well-being initiatives, with discounted fitness offers currently being rolled out • Future Benefits: We’re working on introducing additional perks, including mobility support and a company pension scheme, to give our team even more flexibility and security Are you up for a challenge? Apply now with your complete application documents, also without a photo and without stating specific personal characteristics such as nationality or gender if you choose to do so. German or English language certificates and documents regarding residence and work permits are welcome. We look forward to hearing from you! #JD1008
