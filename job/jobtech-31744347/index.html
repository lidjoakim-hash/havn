Want to influence the bigger picture? At Saab, you can realise amazing innovations that keep people and societies safe. Together, we can truly make an impact - and your part matters. Your role Do you want to develop security at one of Sweden's most high-tech companies? Security is prioritized, on top management's agenda and crucial for competitiveness, profitability and trust on a challenging global market. Saab Naval Security Department is looking for a Head of Security Operations. As Head of Security Operations, you are a manager with personnel responsibility, and report directly to Head of Security at the business area (BA) Naval. You will work in an international environment where advanced security threats are part of everyday business alongside complex business environments and projects with high business impact. In the role as Head of Security Operations, you are highly operational but should also feel a tactical responsibility to build and strengthen a general security culture within the BA. You will develop security practices to meet both external and internal security protection requirements. You will work closely with the entire Security Department, but in particular with Business Unit (BU) Security, Supply Chain Security and Security Office at Saab Naval. Your main tasks will be: Coordinating security through all the BU Security and be the contact person for all security issues Leading security protection planning according to all the BU contracts that require security protection Leading and ensuring regulatory compliance, such as continuously reviewing and evaluating applicable laws, regulations, requirements and agreements for the BU related to security protection Being the bridge between the Security Officers in your organization and Head of Security in security-related matters You will liaise and collaborate extensively internally and externally. Business Area Naval provides the most efficient platforms on and under the sea, both for the military and civilian domain. The portfolio covers submarines, surface ships, combat boats, under- surface vessels, torpedoes and more. Within Saab Security, we have anchored a Saab wide ambition to become world leading in security risk management and to be referred to by other security professionals as the company having the best practices in place. Your profile Required skills: Documented experience from leading teams, personnel responsibility Documented experience in Swedish Security Protection Act Good communication skills in Swedish and English, verbal and written Several years of experience from working with security related legislation and legal frameworks Desired skills: Several years of experience of working for government authorities or in a global company, including an understanding of security related issues in the business process Leadership/management experience Experience from and an ability to build and maintain networks and good relations inside as well as outside your own organization You are a true team player, and as such you focus on team results. Personal qualities will be of the outmost importance, and it is paramount that you are trustworthy with strong ethics and personal integrity. You understand the need for balanced security in a business-driven environment. As an Information Security Officer at Saab Kockums, you are likely to get in contact with classified information. This position requires that you pass a security vetting based on the current regulations around/of security protection. For positions requiring security clearance additional obligations on citizenship may apply. What you will be a part of Explore a wealth of possibilities. Take on challenges, create smart inventions, and grow beyond. This is a place for curious minds, brave pioneers, and everyone in between.
