Biomedical Scientist (Biomedicinsk analytiker)

Neko Health AB · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 450 000–550 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 16.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Role Purpose We're looking for three junior Biomedical Scientists who is comfortable working differently, embraces innovation, and takes pride in delivering accurate results at exceptional pace. Neko Health members come through our Stockholm clinics for whole-body preventive health screenings, and our laboratories sit at the centre of the patient journey. They are busy, modern spaces, and the service continues to develop as we scale. This is a hands-on, autonomous role, based on site in one of our central Stockholm laboratories. As a Biomedical Scientist, you will operate our analysers and support the quality systems that allow us to return results within 30 minutes of sample receipt. If you're a Biomedical Scientist looking to start your career in an innovation-led environment, we would love to hear from you! What You'll Deliver in the First 6 - 12 Months Initialise the analysers each day and ready them for sample processing. Perform testing on our analysers, validating patient results and escalating clinically anomalous or unexpected findings. Carry out daily calibration, internal quality control, and routine maintenance of analysers, troubleshooting and escalating technical issues promptly. Contribute to service improvement work, including analyser verification, reagent evaluation, and software testing. Minimum Requirements Recently graduated as a Biomedical Scientist, or at the beginning of your career. You have a registered license as a Biomedical Scientist issued by Socialstyrelsen. Able to work efficiently under time pressure, suited to a low-volume, fast-turnaround service model. You are organized, patient, and thrive in a fast-paced environment. Preferred One year of experience in a clinical chemistry lab. Experience in operating an integrated clinical chemistry analyser. Experience with laboratory information management systems, and digital result reporting. Interviews are ongoing, so don’t hesitate to send in your application today!
