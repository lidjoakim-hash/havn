THE ROLE This role is at the heart of building and running the HR platform that powers Legora's global People operations. You'll join at a pivotal moment for the company, bringing hands-on expertise to help us get the foundations right as we scale. You'll work closely with external partners and internal teams to design, configure, test and release a platform that supports our growing global workforce. You'll make sure it stays reliable, secure and continuously improving as the business grows. This is a hands-on role for someone who has built and run an enterprise HRIS and knows what it takes to make a platform work in practice, not just on paper. You'll combine technical expertise with an understanding of People processes, working closely with stakeholders to turn business requirements into effective system solutions. What you'll own - Platform design and build. Work with our external partners on solution design, configuration, testing and release. Bring practical experience to challenge design decisions and ensure we're building a platform that will work for Legora long term. - Workstream delivery. Own defined sub-streams of our HRIS implementation from requirements to release. Run discovery with stakeholders, document requirements and design decisions, and manage your own plan, risks and dependencies - Platform configuration & administration. Configure, maintain and optimize our HRIS, including business processes, organisational structures, security, approvals and employee lifecycle transactions. - Requirements gathering. Run workshops with People, Finance, IT and business stakeholders to understand how processes work today and how they need to work at scale. - Translate People requirements into effective system configurations and workflows. Partner with functional experts to simplify processes, standardise where possible and build solutions that scale across our global footprint. - Data integrity. Support data mapping, validation and reconciliation across our People systems. Establish system controls and quality checks, working with People Operations and other data owners to maintain accurate, reliable employee data. - Testing & release management. Own functional testing for system changes, coordinate user acceptance testing and support ongoing releases. Assess downstream impacts, maintain documentation and ensure changes are deployed safely. - Platform support & optimisation. Troubleshoot issues, investigate root causes and support users.
