We want people who experiment without waiting for permission, push AI beyond the obvious, know when to trust it, and when to question it. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------   Help Qonto’s Central Functions FP&A team close the books and tell the story behind the numbers. Working side by side with our FP&A managers, Pinar and Anna, you’ll support the monthly close, prepare reporting for business reviews, and dig into ad hoc analyses. You’ll get real responsibility and guidance along the way. Contract: Apprenticeship, at least 1 year Start date: January 2027 Location: Paris-based, hybrid Rhythm: 4 days at Qonto / 1 day at school, or 3 days / 2 days ➡️ What you’ll do - Support the monthly close: Help Pinar and Anna prepare the month-end close, mostly in the first week of the month. - Prepare reporting for business reviews: Build reports and analyses that give our business partners timely insights. - Investigate the numbers: Check data, spot variances and explain what you find in clear words. - Take on ad hoc analyses: Contribute to KPI analyses, documentation and new needs as the business changes. - Experiment with AI: Try it out in your day-to-day work and share what helps. ➡️ What we’re looking for - You’re analytical and rigorous: You structure an analysis, double-check your numbers and care about the details. - You’re comfortable with spreadsheets: Excel or Google Sheets is your tool for organizing, calculating and checking data. - You communicate clearly in English: You ask questions early and adapt your explanations to finance and non-finance colleagues. French is a plus, not a requirement. - You’re curious and collaborative: You ask for context, flag blockers and enjoy learning how a finance team supports the business. - You’re studying finance, business or a related field: Preferably in a Master’s programme. Experience in accounting or management control is a bonus, not a must. ➡️ What we can offer you - The full monthly FP&A cycle: See how closing, reporting and business reviews fit together. - Real responsibility, with support: Own recurring tasks while learning from Pinar, Anna and Emmanuel. - Exposure across teams: Work with several business partners and build your communication and business-partnering skills. - Room to grow: Your scope can evolve with the business. ➡️ Your future manager You’ll report to Emmanuel Le Feuvre https://www.linkedin.com/in/emlefeuvre, FP&A Lead, Strategic Finance, who will also be your mentor.
