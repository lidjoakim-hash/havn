Senior Quantity Surveyor, Sweden

Collen Aktiebolag · Gävle, Sweden

💰 ca. 310 000–360 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 11.2.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Key Requirements: Degree in Quantity Surveying or Construction Cost Management. B.Sc. Construction Economics / Quantity Surveying with 4-5 years’ experience at Senior QS level A proven track record in delivering complex projects on time and within budget Chartered MSCSI / MRICS or embarking on the APC process Time management and communication skills. Computer literacy and digital systems proficiency. Microsoft Office, Cubit / CostX, COINS Reporting and administrative skills. Excellent verbal and written communication skills in English As a Senior Quantity Surveyor, you will play a key role in the successful commercial delivery of projects, providing commercial leadership across cost management, contractual administration, and commercial governance. Through effective cost control, value management, procurement strategy, and commercial risk management, you will help drive strong commercial performance throughout the project lifecycle. Working closely with project teams, clients, subcontractors, and other key stakeholders, you will support commercial decision-making across tendering, contract administration, commercial analysis, and project cost planning. Through collaboration and effective commercial management, you will contribute to positive commercial outcomes and the successful delivery of projects. Key Responsibilities: Reporting to the Commercial Director, your key responsibilities may vary depending on the nature and stage of the project being worked on, but can include: Tendering & Contract Administration Prepare and manage tender documentation, bills of quantities, tender pricing information and contract-related commercial documentation. Analyse subcontractor quotations and support commercially sound appointment decisions. Cost Planning & Commercial Management Manage cost control, value management and commercial performance across assigned projects. Support valuations, payment assessments, final accounts and retention management. Procurement & Subcontractor Management Advise on procurement strategy and support the development and management of project procurement schedules. Manage subcontractor packages, including tendering, appointments, payments, claims and final accounts. Commercial Risk & Change Management Identify and assess commercial risks and support the development of appropriate mitigation measures. Identify and manage variations, contractual claims, delays and disruption in accordance with contract requirements. Commercial Reporting Prepare and support commercial reporting, including cost value reconciliation, cash flow, material and subcontractor cost updates. Contract Management Support the administration of main contracts and subcontract agreements, ensuring contractual requirements and procedures are appropriately followed. Team Support Mentor and support junior and project Quantity Surveyors. Key Skills: Cost management, value management, and commercial risk management skills. Procurement strategy and commercial planning skills. Contract administration and contractual claims management skills. Tendering, estimating, and commercial analysis skills. Subcontractor management, valuation, and payment administration skills. Commercial reporting and analytical skills. Feasibility assessment and commercial advisory skills.
