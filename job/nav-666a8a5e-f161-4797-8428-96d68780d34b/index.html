Marketing Manager Nordics

Monil AS · Oslo, Norway

Lønn ikke oppgittHeltidvia NAVFrist 20.5.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Turn customer insight into growth across the Nordics Monil builds virtual fencing technology that makes it easier for farmers to manage livestock on pasture. Our collars and app give farmers a real-time view of their animals and more flexibility in how they graze. Founded in Norway in 2022 by engineers from Teledyne FLIR, Silicon Labs and Cognite, Today, thousands of farmers around the world use Monil to manage their livestock on pasture. The opportunity The Nordics are Monil’s home market and a key region for growth. As our Marketing Manager Nordics, you’ll own the regional marketing strategy, budget and lead the work to generate demand and support sales growth across the region. You’ll focus on Norway and Sweden today and help shape how we enter other Nordic markets as we grow. You’ll get close to farmers to understand what drives their buying decisions, where they hesitate and what helps them get started. You’ll use those insights to build demand, generate qualified leads for Sales and drive purchases through our online store. This is a hands-on role. You’ll set the plan, decide where to invest and lead campaigns from brief to launch. You’ll work closely with our Nordic Sales and Farmer Success teams, central specialists in performance marketing, content, web, product marketing, brand and PR, and external agencies and partners. You’ll bring the right people into each project and own the priorities and results. What you’ll be responsible for Regional strategy and performance • Build regional marketing plans, set targets and forecasts, and own the campaign calendar and budget. • Track and report results, test new approaches and shift investment toward the channels and activities that deliver the strongest return. Customer insight and messaging • Bring together insights from farmers, Sales, market research and competitors to identify buying drivers, barriers to adoption and opportunities for growth. • Turn those insights into locally relevant messaging, pricing and promotion recommendations, and clear briefs for specialists and agencies. Campaigns, launches and visibility • Lead regional campaigns, product launches, trade shows, demo days, webinars, partnerships and events from planning through delivery. • Select and work with PR agencies to earn coverage in relevant media. • Brief specialists and agencies, review creative work and approve materials for local markets. Website, online store and lead generation • Set regional priorities for website localisation, customer journeys, conversion improvements, newsletters and CRM communications. • Work with the web team to improve how customers buy through the online store and how prospective customers connect with Sales. • Analyse performance by channel and across the funnel, and work with Sales to understand which leads convert and where to improve. What you’ll bring • Experience owning a marketing strategy, budget and measurable business results for a market or region. We’re ideally looking for around 5–7 years of relevant experience. • Hands-on experience across campaigns, paid and digital channels, PR, trade shows and events, websites, CRM and email marketing. • Examples of how you’ve turned customer and market insight into clear priorities, messaging and campaigns. • Strong commercial and analytical judgement. You can connect marketing decisions to lead quality, conversion and return on investment. • Experience taking campaigns, launches or trade shows from planning through delivery. • The ability to bring specialists and agencies together around a clear brief and keep work moving without direct reporting lines. • A good eye for concepts, copy and design. You give clear feedback and are comfortable writing copy yourself when needed. • Fluency in Norwegian or Swedish, with a good understanding of the other language and professional proficiency in English. • Relevant education in marketing, economics or business development, or equivalent practical experience. • Willingness to travel for trade shows, demo days and customer visits in rural areas. You’re curious about how farmers make decisions, comfortable making trade-offs and motivated by seeing the results of your work. It’s a plus if you have • Experience marketing to farmers, working in agriculture or agtech, or introducing customers to a new product category. • Experience marketing hardware and subscriptions through both e-commerce and sales-led channels. • A bachelor’s degree or equivalent in economics or engineering. How we work We keep things simple, move quickly and learn by testing. You’ll have room to challenge how we do things, try new ideas and adjust priorities based on what you learn. You’ll work closely with colleagues across Marketing, Sales, Farmer Success, Product and Finance. What we offer • Ownership of the strategy, budget and results in a key growth region for Monil. • A direct link between your work and the leads, sales and growth it generates. • The freedom to choose where to focus, with the opportunity to shape how Monil grows across the Nordics. • An ambitious team that makes decisions quickly and builds technology to make livestock farming easier, more efficient and more sustainable. Interested? We’d love to hear from you. Please upload your CV and academic transcripts when you apply. We’d also like to hear about a marketing plan, campaign or launch where you owned the strategy, budget and results.
