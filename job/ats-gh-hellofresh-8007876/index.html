Creative Strategy Lead, New Social Growth Platforms (all genders)

HelloFresh · Berlin, Berlin, Germany, Germany

The role Meta is our core social channel, but the next leg of growth depends on what's around it. The 2026 ambition is clear: scale TikTok into our #2 social channel on incrementality-steered investment, and prove Pinterest, Snap, and other emerging platforms on the same evidence bar. This role owns that diversification agenda end-to-end. You're the in-house expert on TikTok — its algorithm, Spark Ads, TikTok Creative Exchange, and the 2–3 day creative fatigue window that punishes slow teams. You also drive structured pilots on Pinterest and Snap: technical setup, account architecture, creative strategy tailored to each platform, and the incrementality tests that decide whether we scale or shut down. You don't run media for these channels in BAU mode — you build the playbook, prove the channel works, then hand a mature operation over to the broader team Above all, we are looking for people who will make HelloFresh better. We believe there are many different ways of developing skills and we love diverse experiences! So even if you don’t “tick all the boxes” but think you’d thrive in this role, we would really like to learn more about you. What you’ll do • TikTok scale-up. Set TikTok as a native-first growth engine. Own account architecture, bidding, audience strategy, and the creative cadence the platform demands. Partner with TikTok directly — joint steering committee, beta access, TTCX program, Managed Services — to unlock funding and capability gaps. • TikTok creative strategy. The single biggest lever on TikTok is platform-native creative. Define the briefing approach with the Creative Strategist and Influencer team, scale Spark Ads and creator partnerships, and build the iteration cadence the 2–3 day fatigue window requires. Push the team away from "repurposed Reels" toward TikTok-first concepts. • Pinterest and Snap pilots. Run structured launch playbooks for each: signal infrastructure (CAPI, pixel, EMQ), account setup, creative strategy fit-for-platform, market selection, and a clean incrementality test (CLS or geo) before we commit a real budget. Same rigor for Reddit if/when it earns its slot. • Incrementality and effectiveness. Design and run the conversion lift / geo experiments that determine which channels stay, scale, or sunset. Calibrate MMM with each result. • Channel-by-channel measurement. Own the data foundation across emerging channels — overlap analysis with Meta, audience cannibalization, true incremental CAC. Build the case (or the kill memo) with evidence, not platform-reported numbers. • Future-channel scouting. Stay ahead on what's next — Reddit, ChatGPT/Copilot Ads, retail media, emerging short-video platforms. Maintain a prioritized backlog and a clear bar for what earns a pilot slot. • Handover and scaling. Once a channel is proven and operationalized, transition BAU execution to the broader paid social team with documented playbooks, while staying accountable for the next bet. What you’ll bring • 5+ years in paid social or performance marketing, with deep hands-on TikTok Ads Manager fluency at a high-spend D2C, subscription, or e-commerce business. You've personally scaled a TikTok account from low single-digit % of spend into a meaningful contributor. • Working knowledge of Pinterest Ads Manager and Snap Ads Manager — or a credible track record of taking a new platform from zero to scale, fast. • Strong incrementality and experimentation muscle — you can design a CLS or geo test, defend the methodology, and read the result without spinning it. • Sharp creative instinct for short-form video and platform-native content. You know why a TikTok hook is different from a Reels hook, and can brief and review against that. • Comfortable building from scratch — pixel setup, account architecture, partner relationships, internal stakeholder buy-in. This role has more zero-to-one work than steady-state. What we offer Compensation & Financial Wellbeing • You’ll receive a competitive salary
