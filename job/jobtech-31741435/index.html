Learning Design Consultant – Retail Sales Academy

JobBusters Aktiebolag · Mölndal, Sweden

💰 ca. 410 000–480 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 15.2.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Your New Role  We’re looking for an experienced learning design consultant to help our client build a global Retail Sales Academy. You’ll translate an established sales job architecture and skills framework into role- and skill-based learning journeys for four profiles: Commercial Management, Account Management, Field Sales and Sales Enablement. The Academy will be delivered through the client’s Learning Experience Platform (LXP) and will support onboarding and ongoing development for approximately 1,500 employees across more than 20 markets. You’ll start by identifying which existing resources can be reused or adapted. You’ll then build learning paths that give employees a clear progression, opportunities to practice in real sales situations, and support from managers and peers. English will be the master language, with local-language content where it helps employees apply what they learn. Spanish-language needs will be a particular focus as you design for rollout across Europe, North America and Latin America. What you’ll do Define the Academy’s structure, curriculum and learner navigation, including who will own and maintain it after launch. Review internal and third-party learning content and map relevant resources to priority roles, skills and proficiency levels. Design learning journeys with clear outcomes, a logical sequence, hands-on activities, manager coaching and peer learning. Identify priority content gaps and adapt or develop materials where existing resources fall short. Define a scalable localization approach, including how translated and adapted content will be quality-checked and maintained. Prepare learning path specifications, taxonomy and metadata requirements for implementation in the LXP. Deliver editable assets, documentation and knowledge transfer so the client’s teams can manage and update the Academy after handover. Company Presentation Our client is a leading global hygiene and health company operating in around 150 countries under several strong brands. They work to break barriers to well-being, combining innovation with a strong focus on sustainability, diversity, and inclusion. Through their efforts, they create value for customers, consumers, employees, and society at large. Good to Know Scope of employment: Full-time, 100% Type of contract: Fixed-term employment as a consultant via JobBusters. Start date: As soon as possible. End date: 2027-04-15, with the possibility of extension In your application: Please ensure that your CV clearly demonstrates how you meet the qualifications required by the client. To succeed in this role, you will need:  Strong experience designing corporate academies, structuring curricula and building role- or skill-based learning journeys aligned with business capabilities and proficiency levels. Experience assessing large third-party learning libraries and selecting content based on quality, relevance and practical application. You know how to reuse and adapt existing resources and create new content where it fills a clear gap. Experience designing learning for sales or commercial teams, with practical activities, manager coaching, peer learning and follow-up that help employees apply new skills on the job. Experience with Cornerstone or a comparable enterprise learning platform and the ability to prepare learning paths, taxonomy and metadata for implementation. Experience supporting learning initiatives across multiple markets and languages, including quality assurance and maintenance of localized content. Ability to lead the design work independently and collaborate closely with business stakeholders, subject-matter experts and learning teams. Experience delivering implementation-ready specifications, editable assets and clear handover documentation so internal teams can maintain and develop the Academy over time.
