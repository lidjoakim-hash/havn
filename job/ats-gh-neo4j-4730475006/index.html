Intelligence that works. Results that matter. Built to work everywhere and integrate with everything across every cloud for dynamic, personalized, and autonomous AI systems. Our Vision: At Neo4j, we have always strived to help the world make sense of data. Director, GraphAware Engineering The role We are looking for a Director of Engineering to lead Hume engineering: about 25 engineers in five product-aligned teams, plus the DevOps team, working across Europe. Hume is a graph-powered intelligence analysis platform. Analysts in law enforcement, customs, financial crime and national security use it to find the connections that matter in their cases. You will report to the Senior Director for GraphAware Products, who leads both product and engineering. You will work as a peer of the Director of Product Management. You have two core responsibilities, and they matter equally. The first is the people: grow the engineering team in size, skills and knowledge, by hiring well and by coaching engineers into stronger engineers, tech leads and future leaders. The second is the system that lets those teams decide autonomously and deliver well: clear team ownership, sound decisions, predictable releases, secure practices and effective AI-assisted development. The two reinforce each other: a good system helps people grow faster, and people who grow keep improving the system. Engineering should not depend on a few individuals to resolve every architecture, release or staffing question, and that includes you. This is a leadership role. You will not write production code day to day. You need enough technical depth to earn senior engineers' respect, ask the right questions in a design review and diagnose a production incident. Then you hand the decision back to the people who own it. What you will own • Teams and people. Lead, coach and grow the team leads and tech leads. Build management and senior technical career paths, hiring within the team, and plan succession so no area depends on one person. • Delivery. Own engineering's organisation-wide commitments. Put in place planning, checkpoints and tracking that the teams run themselves, so risks and dependencies surface early. • Release quality. Treat everything we ship as the product: installation packages, Helm charts, Docker Compose, configuration and documentation, tested on every deployment environment we support, including on-premises and restricted environments. Own the definition of done, code-review discipline and vulnerability mitigation within agreed targets. • Secure engineering. Work with our Security & Compliance colleagues, who run the ISO 27001 programme, and make sure engineering practice meets it. • Technical ownership. Be accountable for Hume's overall technical excellence: architecture, code quality, performance and security. Challenge and shape the major technical decisions together with the tech leads, and set how decisions are made, challenged and recorded.
