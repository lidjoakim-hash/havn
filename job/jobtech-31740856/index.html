Global Incident Manager

Sandvik Aktiebolag · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 450 000–550 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 1.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

When critical IT incidents occur, clear coordination and decisive action make all the difference. As Global Incident Manager, you lead the response to major and high-impact incidents, bringing the right people together to restore services quickly and minimize business impact. You join Service Management and work within the Service Stability area alongside Global Incident Managers and colleagues focused on Incident, Problem and Event Management. About the job You’re the central coordination point during major and critical IT incidents, creating structure, driving progress and ensuring clear communication, escalation and decision support. Your scope covers global Incident Management operations, including major incidents, escalation support and Cybersecurity Incident Management (CSIM) when assigned. Your focus is operational incident leadership, while using hands-on experience and lessons learned to continuously improve our ways of working. Job responsibilities Lead and coordinate the end-to-end response to P1, major and other high-impact incidents. Establish effective command and control, with clear responsibilities, action tracking and decision points. Bring together resolver teams, service providers, technical specialists and business stakeholders to drive incidents toward resolution. Remove blockers and escalate when service restoration, business continuity, security or regulatory targets are at risk. Provide timely and clear communication to operational teams, business stakeholders and leadership. Facilitate post-incident reviews, follow up lessons learned and improvement actions, monitor recurring issues and contribute to improvements in processes, tools and capabilities. Take part in on-call duties and respond to incidents outside normal business hours, according to the agreed operational model. Location The position is based at the World Trade Center in Stockholm or Mossvägen in Sandviken. Your profile You’ve built solid experience in Incident Management or a similar operational IT field and know how to coordinate major incidents in complex environments. Working across multiple stakeholders comes naturally to you, and you combine operational IT knowledge with a structured approach to service management. Your background includes: Experience with IT service-management processes and tools in a global or multi-stakeholder environment. A relevant university degree or equivalent practical experience in IT operations, service management or a related field. ITIL Foundation or an equivalent service-management certification. Professional proficiency in written and spoken English. Experience in process improvement, governance, cybersecurity incident response or crisis management is beneficial. Advanced certification within ITIL, Incident Management, security, process management or crisis management, as well as additional relevant languages, are also considered an advantage. Being calm and decisive under pressure, you create clarity when situations are complex and moving quickly. You communicate clearly with technical, business and leadership audiences, take ownership and persistently drive incidents toward service restoration. You’re comfortable influencing others without formal authority while turning incidents and deviations into opportunities for learning.
