Founded by the team behind PyTorch and backed by AMD, Atreides, Benchmark Capital, Index Ventures, Lightspeed, NVIDIA, Sequoia Capital, and TCV, Fireworks powers production AI with hundreds of state-of-the-art open models across text, image, embedding, audio, and multimodal workloads. THE ROLE We're looking for a hands-on Sales Enablement Manager to help build GTM Enablement as Fireworks scales its go-to-market motion. The role sits in the Revenue Operations organization and reports to the Head of GTM Enablement. This is a builder's seat with a real delivery mandate. Upon arrival, you'll take immediate ownership of two flagship initiatives that define how Fireworks captures and serves its GTM knowledge: organizing our knowledge as a data product and industrializing our internal GTM Tutor App. But you won't live only in content and tooling. You're also expected to deliver traditional enablement, facilitating live and virtual sessions, supporting role-based ramp, and running cross-functional GTM programs that keep the field ahead of a fast-moving product. To do it well, you'll pair the fundamentals of great enablement (facilitation, content development, and program management) with genuine AI fluency, building the tools you teach rather than just briefing them. You'll own processes end to end and drive outcomes across Product, Marketing, and Sales without waiting to be told how. WHAT YOU'LL OWN 1. Complete a full audit of our existing knowledge and content, then define and roll out the taxonomy, standards, and governance for how we create, maintain, and retire content going forward, turning a scattered corpus into a trusted, queryable source of truth that stays healthy and current as it grows. 2.
