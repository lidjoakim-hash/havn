Veritaz is a leading IT staffing solutions provider in Sweden, committed to advancing individual careers and aiding employers in ensuring the perfect talent fit. With a proven track record of successful partnerships with top companies, we have rapidly grown our presence in the USA, Europe, and Sweden as a dependable and trusted resource within the IT industry. Job Overview Role Summary Our client is seeking a Senior Electronics Engineer for a consulting assignment in Hillerstorp. The consultant will join an R&D organization developing electronics solutions for innovative physical products. The assignment will reinforce an existing development team during a transition period and requires an experienced, self-directed engineer who can quickly become productive and contribute deep expertise in electronics design and product development. Key Responsibilities Develop and design electronics and hardware solutions for embedded systems and physical products. Participate throughout the product development lifecycle, from initial design and prototype development to testing, verification, and production adaptation. Create electronic schematics, select suitable components, and perform PCB design. Design new electronic systems and further develop existing solutions. Build and evaluate prototypes through structured testing and verification activities. Troubleshoot electronic systems, resolve technical issues, and conduct root cause analyses. Address EMC considerations, electronics compliance requirements, and applicable technical standards. Collaborate with the existing R&D team and provide hands-on engineering support during the transition period. Technical Stack & Requirements Electronics design: Practical experience developing electronic circuits and hardware solutions. PCB development: Proficiency in schematic capture, component selection, and PCB design. Embedded hardware: Experience developing electronics for embedded systems and integrated physical products. Verification: Hands-on capability in prototyping, hardware testing, and design verification. Diagnostics: Strong experience troubleshooting electronics and performing root cause analysis. EMC: Knowledge of electromagnetic compatibility considerations, electronics compliance, and relevant standards. Production readiness: Experience adapting electronic designs for production. Qualifications & Experience The assignment requires a senior electronics engineering profile with substantial practical experience in electronics design and product development. The successful consultant should be able to work independently, rapidly understand an established development environment, and contribute across multiple engineering phases. Relevant experience should include electronic system design, schematic development, component selection, PCB design, prototype creation, testing, verification, troubleshooting, and production adaptation. Familiarity with EMC requirements, electronics compliance, and applicable technical standards is also required.
