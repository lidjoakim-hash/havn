IT Service Desk Technician

Schibsted · Oslo, Norway

Lønn ikke oppgittHeltidvia NAVFrist 9.1.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Location: Oslo, Norway About the role At Schibsted, reliable IT services are essential to helping our employees do their best work. We are looking for an IT Service Desk Technician to join our Service Desk team in Oslo, providing high-quality technical support to employees across our organisation, primarily in Norway and Sweden. Our Service Desk is more than a traditional first-line support function. We resolve approximately 80% of all incoming IT requests and incidents within the team, which means you will have the opportunity to work with a wide range of technical challenges, take ownership of issues and develop your troubleshooting skills. You will work in an international environment where customer satisfaction, technical expertise and continuous improvement are key priorities. Our IT operations follow IT Service Management (ITSM) principles and ITIL practices, with a strong focus on efficient problem-solving, knowledge sharing and improving the employee IT experience. Your responsibilities As an IT Service Desk Technician, your main responsibilities will include: • Deliver high-quality IT support: Provide professional, friendly and solution-oriented technical support to employees through phone, email and other support channels. • Troubleshoot and resolve technical issues: Investigate and resolve incidents involving computers, operating systems, applications, user accounts, access and other workplace technologies. • Own incidents and service requests: Register, prioritise, investigate, document and follow up on cases in our ITSM platform (ServiceNow/Halo), taking responsibility for driving issues towards resolution. • Ensure a great user experience: Communicate clearly with users, provide regular updates and manage expectations throughout the support process. • Drive efficient resolution and knowledge sharing: Contribute to our high resolution rate by developing technical knowledge, documenting solutions and supporting a shift-left approach. • Collaborate and continuously improve: Work closely with colleagues and other IT teams to resolve complex issues and improve processes related to Incident, Problem, Change and Knowledge Management. • Manage IT equipment and suppliers: Support ordering, inventory management and distribution of IT equipment and accessories, including coordination with external suppliers. What we're looking for We are looking for someone who enjoys solving technical challenges, takes ownership of problems and genuinely likes helping people. You are curious about technology, comfortable investigating unfamiliar issues and motivated by finding solutions rather than simply passing problems on to someone else. You enjoy working in an environment where priorities can change quickly and where teamwork, knowledge sharing and customer satisfaction are essential. Skills and knowledge To succeed in this role, we believe you have: • A good understanding of computers, operating systems, software and common workplace IT technologies. • The ability to troubleshoot technical issues in a structured and logical way. • Familiarity with, or an interest in learning, technologies such as Windows, macOS, Microsoft 365, Google Workspace and Microsoft Entra ID. • An understanding of the importance of documenting incidents, solutions and technical information. • The ability to communicate technical information clearly to users with different levels of IT knowledge. • Professional proficiency in Norwegian or Swedish, as well as English, both written and spoken. Nice to have The following qualifications are an advantage, but not essential: • Previous experience working in an IT Service Desk, IT Support or similar technical support role. • Around two years of relevant technical experience or education. • Experience working with ITSM platforms such as ServiceNow or Halo. • Knowledge of ITIL principles and IT Service Management processes. • Relevant IT certifications or technical training. • Experience with IT equipment management, inventory control or supplier coordination. • Experience with AI tools and technologies, particularly using AI to improve IT support, automate tasks, enhance troubleshooting or streamline workflows. We value practical skills, curiosity and a willingness to learn. You do not need to meet every preferred qualification to be considered for the role. Who you are We believe the following qualities will help you succeed: • Customer oriented: You enjoy helping others, understand their needs and take pride in delivering excellent service. • Problem-solving: You approach technical challenges with curiosity, persistence and a structured mindset, taking responsibility for finding solutions. • Communication: You explain technical issues clearly, listen actively and keep users informed throughout the support process. • Adaptable: You are comfortable handling changing priorities, learning new technologies and working in a dynamic IT environment. • Collaborative: You enjoy sharing knowledge, supporting colleagues and contributing to a positive and supportive team environment. Why join us? You will join a Service Desk team in Oslo where technical problem-solving, collaboration and high-quality service go hand in hand. Unlike traditional first-line support roles, our team handles and resolves the majority of incoming IT issues, giving you the opportunity to work with a broad range of technologies and technical challenges. You will support colleagues across Norway and Sweden, work with modern workplace technologies and contribute to continuously improving how IT services are delivered. We believe great IT support is about more than fixing technical problems. It is about taking ownership, building trust and making people's working lives easier.
