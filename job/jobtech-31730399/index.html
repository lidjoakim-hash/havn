Company description: Ericsson AB Job description: Join our Team About this opportunity: Are you passionate about driving innovation and working on groundbreaking 5G and 6G mobile communication solutions? Do you thrive in a flexible working culture, where new insights are championed, and you are encouraged to develop new skills? Then join Ericsson and our team in Stockholm. We are looking for a Senior FPGA Developer within Ericsson Silicon organization. You will work with dedicated engineers passionate about developing world-class Radio and RAN compute products. You will play a key role in FPGA team - developing and integrating high-performance FPGA solutions.   What you will do Design and implement FPGA-based Radio and RAN Compute solutions.  Develop and optimize FPGA designs for performance, power, and cost efficiency.  Take technical leadership responsibility for FPGA top-level design, including design planning, technical decisions, integration, and coordination of development activities.  Guide FPGA developers, knowledge sharing, and continuous improvement within the team.  Drive technical alignment across FPGA, hardware, software, verification, and system teams Collaborate with hardware and software engineers to integrate FPGA solutions into larger systems.  Perform FPGA verification, testing, troubleshooting, and debugging to ensure functionality and reliability.  Apply industry-standard tools, methodologies, and quality practices for FPGA development and implementation.  Research and stay current with advancements in FPGA technology and relevant academic and industry trends in AI and machine learning.  Document design specifications, technical decisions, test procedures, and results for future reference.  Participate in design reviews and contribute innovative ideas to improve FPGA solutions.    The skills you bring: Bachelor’s or Master’s degree, or equivalent experience, in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field.  8+ years of experience in FPGA development, with demonstrated experience in technical leadership, design coordination, or leading complex FPGA development activities.  Strong technical leadership skills with the ability to make design decisions, manage dependencies, and align teams. Strong communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to mentor colleagues and contribute to the technical development of the team.  Solid knowledge of FPGA technology, design environments, development flows, and design methodologies.  Expertise in FPGA design tools such as Vivado, Quartus, or equivalent tools.  Solid command of hardware description languages, including Verilog, SystemVerilog, or VHDL.  Good knowledge of UVM methodology and modern verification techniques.  Familiarity with modern FPGA device families, implementation tools, and RTL simulation.  Experience with communication protocols and interfaces such as DDRx, AXI4, PCIe, Ethernet, SPI, I2C, and embedded processing.  Experience with scripting languages such as Python, Tcl, or shell scripting.  Familiarity with radio base station hardware architecture and digital signal processing.  Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills.  A track record of successful cross-team and cross-site collaboration.
