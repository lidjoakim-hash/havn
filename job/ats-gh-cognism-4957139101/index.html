WHO ARE WE Cognism is the leading provider of European B2B data and sales intelligence. Ambitious businesses of every size use our platform to discover, connect, and engage with qualified decision-makers faster and close more deals. Headquartered in London with global offices, Cognism’s contact data and contextual signals are trusted by thousands of revenue teams to eliminate the guesswork from prospecting. About the Role As a Senior Software Engineer within the Integrations & Enrich domain, you will build the capabilities that connect Cognism with customers' technology ecosystems. Your work will focus on CRM integrations, enrichment workflows, synchronization services and customer-facing data experiences. You will help enterprise customers seamlessly move trusted Cognism data into platforms such as Salesforce, HubSpot and other business applications. What Success Looks Like • Reliable customer integrations with measurable improvements in stability and performance • High-quality enrichment services supporting enterprise customers • Production
