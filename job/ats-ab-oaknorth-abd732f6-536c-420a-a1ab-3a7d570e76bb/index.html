ABOUT US At OakNorth, we're on a mission to empower ambitious businesses and the communities they serve. Since 2015, we've lent over $21 billion across the UK and US, helped create more than 58,000 new homes and 36,000 new jobs, and supported hundreds of thousands of personal savers — all while driving economic growth in the markets we serve. ABOUT THE TEAM Our Real Estate Finance team sits at the heart of OakNorth's lending business, backing the UK's most ambitious property entrepreneurs and developers. The team originates and executes high-quality real estate transactions end-to-end — from senior debt to stretched senior and mezzanine structures — across a wide range of asset classes. We work closely, move quickly, and hold ourselves to a high standard on both credit quality and client experience. ABOUT THE ROLE As a Real Estate Director, you will be at the forefront of OakNorth's mission to back the UK's most ambitious entrepreneurs and growing businesses. You will originate, structure, and own high-quality deals end-to-end — from first conversation to successful close — with a sharp focus on credit excellence and delivering a standout client experience. Working closely with senior leadership, you will have the autonomy to shape your own portfolio and take an entrepreneurial approach to every opportunity. WHAT YOU'LL DO - Originate, structure, and execute real estate transactions that meet OakNorth's lending requirements, including senior, stretched senior, and mezzanine facilities - Manage and develop existing customer relationships while identifying new opportunities across SMEs, KBIs, advisors, and private equity firms - Analyse financial accounts, spread financial models, and prepare high-quality credit memorandums — including forecasted cash flows and sensitivity analysis to assess debt capacity and repayment - Present credit cases to sanctioning authority, ensure transactions are documented in accordance with credit-approved terms, and maintain close customer relationships with timely reporting to Credit - Meet team and individual targets (profit, credit quality, new customers, retention, and customer satisfaction) while driving innovation in the product proposition to improve client experience YOU'LL BE A GREAT FIT IF YOU HAVE… - In-depth understanding of the London real estate market and a strong, active network across relevant counterparties - Strong credit, financial analysis, and modelling skills — including the ability to write structured, high-quality credit papers and present cases to a credit committee - Experience originating, structuring, and executing senior, stretched senior, and mezzanine real estate transactions, with strong legal documentation skills and a degree in Finance, Commerce, or equivalent Nice to Have - Experience working within a bank or regulated lending environment, with exposure to credit policy and governance frameworks - Existing relationships with private equity sponsors, property advisors, or intermediaries active in the UK real estate market WHAT WE OFFER -
