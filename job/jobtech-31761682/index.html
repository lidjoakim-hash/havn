Veritaz is a leading IT staffing solutions provider in Sweden, committed to advancing individual careers and aiding employers in ensuring the perfect talent fit. With a proven track record of successful partnerships with top companies, we have rapidly grown our presence in the USA, Europe, and Sweden as a dependable and trusted resource within the IT industry. Job Overview Assignment Description We are looking for an experienced PLM consultant to take ownership of a use case or PLM area within a large PLM transformation programme. You will drive the assigned area from business requirements and process definition through development, testing, validation and implementation. Acting as the link between business stakeholders, Subject Matter Experts (SMEs), Solution Architects and the PLM solution, you will ensure business needs are clearly understood and translated into effective solutions. The role is primarily business- and process-oriented, with a focus on PLM, Teamcenter, requirements management, Jira, User Stories and UAT. What You Will Work On Lead the collection and documentation of business requirements. Manage User Stories and Functional Requirements in Jira. Own and prioritize the backlog for assigned use cases or components. Translate programme strategy into clear sprint objectives. Coordinate cross-functional SMEs, Solution Architects and business representatives. Identify gaps between current business processes and the future PLM solution. Define future processes and solutions together with business stakeholders. Support testing, functional validation and UAT. Represent the assigned area in design reviews and coordination meetings. Identify data migration requirements and needs. Support business readiness through Show & Tell sessions, sandbox testing and training materials. Ensure delivered solutions meet business needs and provide business value. What You Bring Experience in PLM, preferably Siemens Teamcenter. Experience defining and managing User Stories, Functional Requirements and business processes. Proven ability to coordinate cross-functional stakeholders across Engineering, Operations and Quality. Experience with testing, functional validation and UAT. Experience from engineering, industrial or manufacturing environments. Strong knowledge of Jira and Confluence. Understanding of Digital Thread and product lifecycle information flows. Ability to translate complex technical topics into clear business requirements and decisions. Strong communication and workshop facilitation skills. Ability to independently represent business stakeholders and take ownership from requirements through implementation. Meritorious Experience with PLM transformation programmes. Experience with Teamcenter implementation. Experience with data migration and business readiness activities. Experience supporting user training, UAT and solution adoption. Experience working across engineering and manufacturing functions.
