About the position We invite applicants for a full-time position as a PhD research fellow at the Department of Psychology, Faculty of Social Sciences, University of Oslo. The position is associated with the Personality unit at the Section for Health, Developmental, and Personality Psychology (HUP). The PhD will focus on development of personality and depression across the lifespan, and will be connected to The Depression Research Initiative (DRIVE) at the Department of Psychology.  The appointment is a fulltime position for a period of three years. The employment period can be up to 4 years given addition of teaching and administrative duties, depending on the competence of the successful applicant and the resources and needs of the Department of Psychology. Start date: Spring 2027. More about the position The PhD candidate will be part of the Personality group and focus on the relationship between personality, depression, and other mental health conditions. Personality is important for the development and course of mental health problems, and may affect how environmental risk factors affect mental health, as well as the consequences of mental health problems for social and academic functioning. The PhD project will be part of The Depression Research Initiative (DRIVE), made up by five work packages https://www.sv.uio.no/psi/english/research/groups/drive/index.html.  The key objective of DRIVE is to encompass several core aspects of depression research, so they collectively can address questions that cannot be managed by a single work package. The current position will be related to the work package focusing on development of depression. The PhD project will answer some of the following overarching research questions, with a focus on depression:  • How do characteristics of the individual and the environment predict personality development? • What are the associations between personality traits and mental health outcomes, including depression, personal relationships, and work and academic functioning? • What are the associations between characteristics of parents and children regarding personality and mental health, including depression? Your main tasks will be • Contributing to developing more specific research questions within the project. • Complete a PhD thesis in Psychology. • Publish research papers in international scientific journals. • Provide scientific presentations to an international audience. • Participate in the PhD program at the Department of Psychology.
