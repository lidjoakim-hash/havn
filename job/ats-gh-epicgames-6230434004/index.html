LEGAL What We Do Epic's multi-specialized team of attorneys and legal professionals partner across our organization to provide legal solutions as well as consult and inform on the products we build and games we develop. What You'll Do Epic's regulatory lawyers advise the company on laws and regulations worldwide. We partner with Public Policy to monitor regulatory developments and support advocacy on regulatory design; with Product Counsel on new products and their regulatory requirements; and with Compliance on the design and operation of our regulatory compliance program. We also help manage the company's response to regulatory inquiries, investigations and RFIS. In this role, you will • Advise Product Counsel, Compliance and Legal leadership on global laws and regulations, with the focus of this role being on online safety frameworks such as the UK Online Safety Act and EU Digital Services Act • Monitor and analyse draft legislation and regulatory proposals, supporting Public Policy's advocacy efforts • Advise Product Counsel on new products and features and their regulatory requirements • Support Compliance in the operation of our regulatory compliance program and compliance strategies • Help manage the company's response to regulatory oversight, such as supervisory meetings and RFIs What we're looking for • Qualified attorney with 5+ years experience working in regulatory compliance, either within a regulatory body, in-house or at a law firm • Experience with online safety regulation in the UK and/or EU • Knowledge of the tech industry; familiarity with video games is an advantage • Ability/flexibility to work US Eastern time hours • Comfort working with multiple stakeholders, navigating complex issues, and leading high-impact projects • Sound judgement and decision-making in ambiguous situations where clear guidance may be limited or non-existent • Proactive, independent and reliable, with the ability to deliver under tight timeframes in a fast-paced environment • Excellent interpersonal skills and a collaborative approach EPIC JOB + EPIC BENEFITS = EPIC LIFE We pay 100% for benefits except for PMI (for dependents).
