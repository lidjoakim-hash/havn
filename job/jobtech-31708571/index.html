The opportunity At Hitachi Energy, we are advancing a sustainable energy future by delivering innovative technologies that make power systems more resilient, efficient, and sustainable. As a global leader in High Voltage Direct Current (HVDC) technology, we enable the transmission of renewable energy across countries and continents, supporting the transition toward a carbon-neutral future. We are now looking for an R&D Senior Engineer to join our MACH R&D organization within HVDC. In this role, you will contribute to the development of one of the world's most advanced control and protection platforms for power transmission systems. For more than 60 years, Hitachi Energy has been pioneering HVDC technology. At the heart of every HVDC system is MACH™, our industry-leading control and protection platform, designed to provide reliable operation around the clock for decades. As part of our global R&D organization, you will work closely with colleagues in Sweden and India to develop next-generation embedded solutions for HVDC systems. In this senior role, you will serve as a key technical contributor, driving complex technical solutions, influencing architectural decisions, mentoring colleagues, and contributing to the continued evolution of the MACH™ platform. How you'll make an impact Develop and maintain embedded software solutions for the MACH™ control and protection platform used in HVDC systems worldwide. Take technical ownership of functionality throughout the product lifecycle, from concept and design to testing, verification, and release. Contribute to software architecture decisions and drive technical improvements that support long-term product performance and sustainability. Collaborate closely with software, hardware, FPGA, system, and verification engineers in cross-functional R&D teams. Support system integration, troubleshooting, and optimization of real-time embedded applications. Participate in technology investigations and contribute to future product and platform development. Share knowledge, mentor colleagues, and help strengthen technical excellence across the organization. Collaborate with global stakeholders while living Hitachi Energy's values of safety and integrity. Your background Bachelor's or Master's degree in Embedded Systems, Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related technical field. Several years of experience in embedded software development within complex industrial, real-time, or mission-critical systems. Strong programming skills in C/C++ and solid experience with embedded systems architecture and real-time operating systems. Experience working with Git, Azure DevOps, Visual Studio, and modern software development practices. Good understanding of software development processes, testing methodologies, verification, and quality assurance. Experience in electronics design, communication protocols, and system-level troubleshooting. Knowledge of scripting languages such as Python is considered an advantage. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, combined with excellent communication abilities and professional fluency in English.
