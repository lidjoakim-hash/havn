Investment Manager

Senior Material (Europe) AB · Eskilstuna, Sweden

💰 ca. 450 000–550 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Kontraktvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 1.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

We are looking for an experienced Fundraising Manager to join our European organisation and lead our investment and financing activities across Europe. This is a senior, hands-on role for a professional with proven experience in equity fundraising, investment execution and European capital markets. The successful candidate will combine strong financial and commercial expertise with an established investor network, excellent negotiation skills and the ability to independently manage complex cross-border transactions. The Role Reporting directly to the CEO, you will take ownership of the company's European investment and financing activities, with a particular focus on local equity fundraising, investor engagement, industrial project investment and transaction execution. You will work closely with senior management, headquarters, European investors and external advisers to develop financing opportunities, execute investment transactions and establish a structured investment and financing framework in Europe. Equity Fundraising & Financing Lead European equity fundraising activities, from investor identification and engagement to negotiation and transaction closing. Develop and maintain relationships with Nordic and European private equity funds, venture capital firms, industrial investors and other strategic capital partners. Identify suitable financing opportunities and develop fundraising strategies aligned with the company's business objectives. Lead the preparation of investor presentations, financing proposals, transaction structures and related documentation. Negotiate financing terms and coordinate transaction execution within approved financing plans and valuation frameworks. Work closely with the CEO, headquarters and external advisers to ensure successful financing outcomes. Investment Execution & Project Management Oversee investment activities relating to European industrial and strategic development projects. Lead investment evaluations, due diligence, transaction structuring, negotiations and closing processes. Coordinate financial, legal, commercial and technical assessments with relevant internal and external stakeholders. Assess investment opportunities, financial implications, commercial feasibility and potential risks. Support investment decision-making through structured analysis, recommendations and management presentations. Monitor post-investment performance, identify potential risks and coordinate appropriate risk management measures. Investor Relations & Capital Market Development Establish and expand the company's network of European financial institutions, investment funds and industrial investors. Maintain professional relationships with existing and potential investors, financing partners and external advisers. Monitor European capital-market developments, investment trends and financing opportunities. Represent the company in discussions and negotiations with European investors, financial institutions and strategic partners. Investment & Financing Management Establish and continuously improve the company's local investment and financing processes in Europe. Support the development and implementation of European investment and financing strategies. Build and coordinate local investment and financing resources, including internal teams and external professional advisers. Ensure investment and financing activities comply with applicable EU and Swedish regulations and internal governance requirements. Identify, monitor and escalate material investment, financing and compliance risks to senior management. Your Background We are looking for someone with: A bachelor's degree or higher in finance, economics, business administration or a related field. At least 4 years of experience in primary-market investment and financing, including at least 3 years of hands-on experience in European or Nordic markets.
