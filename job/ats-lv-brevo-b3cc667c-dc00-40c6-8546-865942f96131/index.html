From emails and SMS to WhatsApp, Chat, and Marketing Automation, our tools are intuitive, powerful, and built to scale with every ambition. As Senior Product Manager in our Pro Business Unit, you'll own the mid-market product line end to end. You'll lead a "pod" - a small, cross-functional team of a PM or designer, and 2-3 full-stack engineers - through the whole cycle: identifying the problem, shaping the solution, building it, shipping it, and measuring the result. You're the single point of accountability for that team. The role blends product management, product design, and engineering judgment, all supported by AI tools. So we welcome candidates from a PM, design, or engineering background, as long as you're comfortable working across all three. You'll report into the Product Engineering director within the BU, alongside other pods led by senior PMs, designers, or engineering managers. Your impact at Brevo: • Gather client signals and usage data and translate them into clearly defined problems, then shape the pitch — appetite, solution, risks and no-gos — and defend it at the betting table. • Act as the pod's sole design reference: frame the user experience, make interaction and interface decisions, and create prototypes in line with our Naos design system leveraging Claude or our in-house prototyping tool. • Run the build cycle against the pitch, using lightweight written status artefacts rather than recurring status meetings, and adjust scope rather than extend timelines. • Agree on one or two measurable outcomes before the build begins, then deliver a clear post-launch verdict: continue, adjust, or discontinue. • Own the launch: run the beta programme, equip PMM for go-to-market, and get Sales, CSM and Support ready ahead of release. • Be the pod's single point of contact for the wider BU — surfacing risks early, coordinating with peer Pod Leaders, and applying AI tools by default across discovery, prototyping and analysis. Who you are: • You have a relevant background as a PM or Product Designer, or Lead Engineer — no single track is preferred. • You can own an experience end-to-end: framing user problems, making interface/interaction decisions, and producing or validating prototypes (Figma and design-system literacy required). • You have a go-getter, get-things-done mentality: you take initiative, push through ambiguity, and drive things to completion rather than waiting to be told. • You can write and defend a complete pitch: problem, appetite, sketch, rabbit holes, no-gos and success metrics. • You have a track record of shipping against a committed appetite using lightweight, async cadence artefacts (e.g. hill charts) rather than heavyweight Scrum rituals. • You're comfortable pulling and interpreting usage data (Amplitude, funnel, retention) and running discovery interviews to turn signals into problem statements. • You're tech-credible: comfortable reading AI-generated code and partnering with a Lead Engineer on trade-offs — and you integrate AI tools by default into you
