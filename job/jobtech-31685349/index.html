Data Analyst

JobBusters Aktiebolag · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 450 000–550 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 2.2.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Your New Role We are looking for a Data Analytics Manager to join a European CRM & Outbound team in Stockholm. In this role, you will play a key part in strengthening data-driven decision-making by tracking, analyzing and optimizing CRM campaigns, consumer growth and product registrations across European markets. You will develop standardized performance reports and tracking frameworks, analyze campaign engagement, conversions and ROI, and translate data into actionable insights and recommendations. You will also collaborate with data teams to improve dashboards, enhance data accessibility and support experimentation initiatives. Additionally, you will help establish a centralized CRM data repository and enable local markets to make better use of available data through documentation and training. Company Presentation Our client is a global leader in home appliances, operating in markets worldwide. With a strong focus on innovation, sustainability and consumer experience, the company continuously works to develop smarter solutions and strengthen its digital capabilities. Good to Know Scope of employment: Full-time, 100% Type of contract: Fixed-term employment as a consultant via JobBusters Working model: Hybrid Salary: According to agreement Start date: 2026-11-01 End date: 2027-01-31, with the possibility of extension To succeed in this role, you will need: A bachelor's degree in Marketing, Business Analytics, Data Science or a related field. Proven experience in CRM analytics, marketing data analysis or campaign performance tracking. Experience working with GA4, SQL and Python. Knowledge of data visualization tools such as Power BI, Tableau or Looker. Experience with CRM platforms and data governance. Strong analytical skills and the ability to turn complex data into actionable business insights. Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with experience working across international and cross-functional teams. A proactive, structured and detail-oriented approach. What we Offer Secure employment with a collective bargaining agreement, insurance coverage and occupational pension. Wellness allowance and employee discounts via Benifex. Flex pension and access to pension advisory services. Additional compensation during parental leave. Occupational health services. A dedicated Consultant Manager and opportunities to develop your career. We look forward to receiving your application We review applications on an ongoing basis. As recruitment processes can move quickly, the position may be filled before the advertised deadline, so we encourage you to apply as soon as possible. You do not need to submit a cover letter. Instead, please respond to the screening questions included in the application process.
