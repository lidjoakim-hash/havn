About Wolt At Wolt, we create technology that brings joy, simplicity and earnings to the neighborhoods of the world. In 2014 we started with delivery of restaurant food. Now we’re building the delivery of (almost) everything and you’ll find us in over 500 cities in 30 countries around the world. In 2022 we joined forces with DoorDash and together we keep on dreaming big and expanding across the globe. Working at Wolt isn’t always easy, but it’s definitely exciting. Here you’ll learn more, build more, and ship more than in most other companies. You’ll be challenged a lot, but also have a lot of fun on the way. So, if you’re a self-starter with drive and entrepreneurial spirit, this could be the ride of your life. About the Team The DoorDash Global Sourcing team supports DoorDash, Deliveroo, and Wolt across more than 40 markets within the Global Procurement, Sourcing, Travel and Supply Chain organisation. We partner with the business to select the right suppliers, negotiate strong commercial agreements, and deliver value through savings, business enablement, and risk reduction. Our team covers Technology, Corporate Services, and Marketing. This role sits in Technology and owns merchant hardware sourcing for Deliveroo and Wolt: the POS terminals, tablets, printers, chargers, and handheld scanners that merchants and riders rely on every day. We are building more consistent sourcing practices across brands and improving our tools and ways of working, including the use of AI. This role offers real ownership from day one and the chance to shape how the category is run, in a growing global business. About the Role As a Sourcing Specialist II, you will own sourcing for merchant hardware across Deliveroo and Wolt. The category spans several product lines, each with its own vendors and buying mechanics: POS hardware, centrally kitted tablets and printers, and handheld scanners. You will partner with the business to understand requirements, manage vendor relationships, negotiate commercial terms, and keep supply moving, balancing cost, quality, risk, and speed. Parts of this category are well established; others are still taking shape, and a few don't have a defined process yet. You will be expected to collaborate with the global peers to improve ways of working, problem solve and help evolve the category. This is an individual contributor role with real latitude to shape how the category is run, not just execute an existing playbook.
