Commercial Counsel We are looking for a driven and enthusiastic Commercial Counsel based in Amsterdam to join the global Deal Desk within the Commercial Legal team at Adyen. You will be responsible for negotiating complex contract negotiations with our vendors and commercial partners. You will also be responsible for drafting comprehensive negotiation playbooks, training internal teams on how to navigate and negotiate vendor and partnership relationships, advising on contract interpretation, and other general commercial matters. This is a role for a counsel who thrives in a fast-moving, complex environment, who is commercially-minded, and who can leverage their sophisticated legal expertise and strong communication skills into tangible, scalable value that accelerates Adyen’s global growth. What you will do: • Provide strategic, business-oriented legal advice across procurement, partnerships, product, and regulatory teams globally, including on our contracting principles, commercial projects, and legal and regulatory compliance within our vendor and partner contracts. • Draft, review, and negotiate a wide variety of complex, multi-jurisdictional commercial agreements. • Proactively problem solve for emerging legal and operational risks, providing risk-based decisions on complex legal issues and proposing improvements to mitigate future exposure. • Advising on early-stage disputes, managing initial phases of legal conflicts, and providing counsel on complex legal interpretations. • Drive legal automation efforts and process improvements to help scale our team and bring greater efficiency to the negotiation process. • Work autonomously in a cross-functional manner and build strong relationships with colleagues, leaders, and stakeholders across the company. • Participate in training our colleagues across the globe. Who you are: • Around 6+ years of legal experience, in house or at a law firm including strong commercial contracting and/or other transactional experience with a solid understanding of contract law principles. • You are a clear communicator who ensures your stakeholders understand the purpose and reasoning behind decisions. • You have solid people skills and work well with a variety of internal and external stakeholders from varying disciplines and cultures. • You are curious, ask questions, and take ownership over your learning and professional growth. • You are self-motivated, pragmatic, reliable, adaptable and detail-oriented. • You have experience educating others on complex ideas, communicating them clearly, and providing additional training when necessary. • You have the ability to structure and prioritize your work in a high volume, fast-paced environment and build and drive processes to completion.
