We are proud to contribute to the development of the region and to creating a brighter, greener future.About Nova IndustriNova Industri AB is now part of SMS group Nordic, with SMS group GmbH in Germany as our parent company. We are a leading service company specialising in industrial production and mechanical engineering, with operations in Boden and Kalix.For more than 20 years, we have delivered high-quality solutions in turning, milling, welding and steel construction. Our customers are primarily industrial companies in Norrbotten, and our strong local presence is an important part of our success.About the roleAs a manual welder with us, you will be responsible for carrying out qualified welding work on various materials and in different dimensions. You will work daytime shifts, both in the workshop and out in the field.This is an independent role, making it important that you can read and work from technical drawings, welding procedure specifications (WPS) and work instructions. You will also be responsible for ensuring that the work is carried out in accordance with applicable quality requirements.Your main responsibilities will include:    Manual welding using the MMA (111), MAG (135, 136, 138) and TIG (141) processes.    Interpreting technical drawings, welding procedures and work instructions.    Performing welding work in various positions, such as PA, PB and PF.    Carrying out visual self-inspections of completed welds.    Contributing to a safe and clean working environment.We believe that you have    A vocational upper-secondary education in welding or equivalent qualifications.    At least two years of documented experience in manual welding in an industrial environment.    As our working environment is international, it is an advantage if you understand and can communicate in English.    An understanding of and experience in applying occupational health and safety regulations, as well as experience working with personal protective equipment.It is an advantage if you have    A valid welding certificate in accordance with SS-EN ISO 9606-1 for steel, or another relevant standard depending on the material.    Experience in stainless steel and aluminium welding.    Hot Work certification.    SSG Lift and Fall Protection training.    A valid driving licence and an overhead crane licence.As a person, you are    Meticulous, with a strong focus on safety and quality.    Self-motivated, able to take initiative and solve problems that arise in your work.    Cooperative and flexible in your approach.Submit your application today. Interviews will be conducted continuously.We look forward to receiving your application in English.
