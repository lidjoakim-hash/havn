Permanent nursing positions in Jondal, Norway

KLETOR Sverige AB · Sweden

On behalf of Ullensvang Municipality, we are recruiting nurses for permanent positions in municipal healthcare services in Jondal, Western Norway. You will work across both nursing home and home care services, giving you the opportunity to use a broad range of your nursing skills. You will follow patients over time, make independent clinical assessments and work closely with colleagues and other healthcare professionals. Jondal is a small community in the Hardanger region on Norway’s west coast, around 1 hour and 45 minutes from Bergen by car and ferry. Here, you will live by the Hardangerfjord, surrounded by mountains, with Folgefonna, one of Norway’s largest glaciers, just 19 kilometres away. Moving to another country is a big step, and we will support you throughout the process. Dignus Medical provides free Norwegian language training, assistance with Norwegian authorisation and practical support with relocation and settling into your new community. About the position As a nurse in Jondal, you will work with patients with different healthcare and care needs, both in a nursing home and in their own homes. The role gives you a high degree of professional responsibility and the opportunity to follow patients over time, while working closely with colleagues and other healthcare professionals. Your responsibilities will include: Providing nursing care and following up patients Clinical observation, assessment and documentation Medication management and treatment Providing care to patients in nursing homes and in their own homes Making independent clinical assessments Working closely with colleagues and other healthcare professionals Communicating with relatives and other parts of the healthcare service Who are we looking for? We are looking for qualified nurses who are confident in their professional role, comfortable working independently and motivated to build a new life and career in Norway. You: Hold a valid nursing licence from an EU/EEA country Hold citizenship from an EU/EEA country Speak English fluently and are highly motivated to learn Norwegian Are willing to complete our free Norwegian language programme before starting work Hold a valid driving licence Work well both independently and as part of a team Preferably have experience from community healthcare, nursing homes or home care services We help you prepare for working in Norway You do not need to speak Norwegian when you apply. If you are selected for the position, Dignus Medical will guide you through the entire process of preparing for your move to Norway. We provide: Free online Norwegian language training with a private teacher Language training up to the required B2 level, including Norwegian for healthcare professionals Assistance with your application for Norwegian nursing authorisation Guidance with the documentation and administrative processes required before starting work Help with finding accommodation and practical aspects of relocating to Norway Personal follow-up before, during and after your move The language training and authorisation process take place before you start working, so you have time to prepare both professionally and practically for your new life in Norway. What you can expect in Jondal A permanent position in the Norwegian municipal healthcare system Varied work across nursing home and home care services The opportunity to use a broad range of your nursing skills Independent professional responsibility Close cooperation with colleagues and other healthcare professionals The opportunity to follow patients over time Support with housing, relocation and practical arrangements Life between the fjord, mountains and glacier Jondal is located in Hardanger on Norway’s west coast, around 1 hour and 45 minutes from Bergen by car and ferry. It is a small community of around 400 people, situated directly by the Hardangerfjord and surrounded by mountains. Folgefonna, one of Norway’s largest glaciers, is only 19 kilometres away.
