Line Planner

Hitachi Energy Sweden AB · Ludvika, Sweden

The opportunity  We are looking for a Line Planner for Control & Protection, Automation and Cybersecurity at HVDC to act as the center of expertise for planning and scheduling within the Line Planning organization. In this role, you will ensure that best execution practices are applied in tenders and projects and will actively contribute to planning, monitoring, and reporting within your group. You will also provide operational support to the project planners and project managers within HVDC Control & Protection, Automation, and Cybersecurity teams on an ad-hoc basis. This is a role with many interfaces and interesting work tasks within engineering and supply chain planning. This role requires operating with strict deadlines while conducting scenario-based analysis as a core part of daily responsibilities. This role is based in Ludvika, Sweden. How you’ll make an impact The Line Planner is responsible for overseeing availability of resources on a macro level to ensure timely delivery of C&P scope within projects. Support line and project managers in planning methodology and understanding delivery targets linked to their scope. The Line Planner develops specific expertise in planning and execution of the line organizations scope in EPC HVDC deliveries. Establish accurate scheduling methods, best execution practices, and ensure that this is reflected in type schedules applied in project and tenders. Compiles information, analyses and makes recommendations in formats that assist in effective management and decision making. Work with line managers to align material and space requirements and availability as per the scope of projects and tenders. Assist project managers in tenders and projects startup phase to ensure that best planning and execution practices are applied. Identify and mitigate bottlenecks and risks operational continuity from material and space aspect. Collaboration with various stakeholders involved in the process. Your background You have a degree in Supply Chain, Engineering, or related field. Knowledge and 5+ years of experience from engineering/ resource planning, preferably with Energy sector. You are structured, flexible, and have some qualifications in project management. You have skills in planning and controlling a time schedule and have prior experience using Primavera P6 or MS Project. You have genuine technical interest and are always open to developing your skills within other fields. Knowledge of how to use Microsoft's Office suite of software, especially Microsoft Excel, Word and PowerPoint. Fluent in written / spoken English, Swedish language skills would be an advantage. You are a dynamic and social person who enjoys working with people from various business areas and parts of the world.
