This role also includes reporting, day to day account management, coordinating and successfully launching all local operations needs, as well as analysing market performance and proactively proposing strategies and action plans for business growth, thus requiring extensive collaboration with central teams (e.g., Central Operations, Product, Marketplace Performance, Business Analytics). YOUR DAILY ADVENTURES WILL INCLUDE: • Business Development: fully understand the taxi industry in Greece and Freenow’s products to develop win-win relationships with taxi stakeholders for ongoing success. Support contract negotiations effectively. Continuous monitoring of performance. Identify Product improvements and align with relevant stakeholders to deliver them • SaaS Sales: Engage and build relationships with Taxi Dispatchers (Radiotaxis), demonstrating the value of our product solutions, develop and maintain a robust sales pipeline, ensuring consistent growth. • Regional Expansion: drive expansion initiatives in new regions via dispatchers partnerships • Strategic project management: Coordinate and successfully launch all local Operations projects, fostering strong relationships with key stakeholders within the company for ongoing success • Data-Driven Optimisation: Analyse market performance, detect areas of improvement to drive success and proactively propose new projects & action plans for business growth • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner with Product, Marketplace Performance, Business Analytics, and other relevant teams to drive operational excellence and enhance business development • Execute and help define the strategy for Operations in Greece, together with the General Manager and the Business Development Manager.
