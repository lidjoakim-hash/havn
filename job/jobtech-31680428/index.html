Create the technical solutions of the future with us!   In this role, you will have the opportunity to help develop innovative technical solutions based on Building Management Systems (BMS) for both residential projects and commercial properties in Sweden. You will shape the future of Foresight, working with industry‑leading technology in a dynamic and future‑oriented environment. With us, you will be part of a skilled and dedicated team that works together to create value for our customers through smart and customized solutions. As a System Architect with a focus on BMS, you will play a key role in designing and implementing cost‑effective and sustainable system architecture. You will work strategically and operationally to develop technical solutions that meet both business needs and future requirements. The role of System Architect C&SP Focus   As a System Architect within Foresight, you will be responsible for designing and implementing complex BMS system architecture that meet business needs. You have a deep understanding of system design, integration and architecture principles. Key responsibilities as a System Architect Design and implement system architectures that are in line with business objectives and technology strategy Focus on specializing in BMS (Building Management System) solutions within Foresight for our Nordic customers Collaborate with cross‑functional teams to gather and analyze requirements Develop system specifications and architecture designs Evaluate and recommend technologies, tools and frameworks to support system architecture Ensure scalability, reliability and security of the system architecture Perform technical validations together with the Tender Engineer Perform analysis and optimization of system performance Perform technical validation together with the Tender Engineer What will make you successful?   We are looking for someone with excellent technical knowledge of property automation and BMS. You have at least 5 years' experience in a similar role. You need to be able to express yourself fluently in Swedish and have a good knowledge of English. To be successful in this role, you need to be customer‑oriented, structured and able to handle many different tasks simultaneously. You are self‑motivated, communicative and solution‑focused. We will place great emphasis on your personal suitability and are looking for someone with high energy levels, strong drive and commitment to your work and our joint development.
