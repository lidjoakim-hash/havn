Data Engineer

E.ON Sverige Aktiebolag · Malmö, Sweden

About us Within Data, AI & Automation at E.ON, we bring together data engineering, automation and AI to create smarter ways of working and turn data into solutions that create real value across the business. As part of our BizDevOps organization, our Data Engineers and Automation Engineers work closely with product teams and the business. We are an evolving team with plenty of room to share ideas, learn from each other and help shape how we work going forward. Your role As a Data Engineer, you will turn data into reliable, reusable and high-quality data products that enable better decisions and digital solutions across E.ON. You will work hands-on with our modern data platforms and collaborate closely with engineers, architects, product owners and colleagues across the business. You will be part of an agile product team where you plan and deliver together, while also belonging to our wider Data, AI & Automation community. In this role, you will: build and develop scalable data pipelines and data products using Databricks, Python and SQL work with data transformation, integration and event-driven solutions ensure high standards for data quality, security, testing and maintainability contribute to technical design and continuously improve our engineering practices collaborate across teams and domains to make data accessible, trusted and valuable Your experience We are looking for someone with experience working as a Data Engineer, including hands-on experience with Databricks and modern data platforms. You have experience working in, or with, larger organizations and understand the complexity that comes with large-scale data environments. Experience with technologies such as dbt, CI/CD or Apache Kafka is a plus, as is experience with data mesh, data governance or agile ways of working. You are curious, engaged and comfortable taking ownership of your work. You take initiative, ask questions and enjoy exploring better ways of working when you see opportunities to improve. Collaboration comes naturally to you, and you enjoy sharing knowledge, listening to different perspectives and moving things forward together with others. You are also adaptable and comfortable with change, with a willingness to learn and explore new possibilities as technologies and needs evolve. Fluent English is required, and Swedish is an advantage. What is it like working with us? You will join a collaborative team of 13 colleagues, including Data Engineers and Automation Engineers, where Data, AI & Automation come together. We are shaping the team together, sharing knowledge, helping one another and exploring how our different areas of expertise can create even more value. Your closest manager, Almir, believes in trust, responsibility and inclusion. He gives people room to take ownership, welcomes new ideas and perspectives and encourages you to challenge how things are done when you see a better way forward.  We warmly welcome your application and invite you to join us on our journey towards a more sustainable society!
