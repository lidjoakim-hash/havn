Join Axon and be a Force for Good. At Axon, we’re on a mission to Protect Life. We’re explorers, pursuing society’s most critical safety and justice issues with our ecosystem of devices and cloud software. Like our products, we work better together. Your Impact As an Embedded Technical Account Manager at Dedrone, an Axon company, you will be the technical face of Dedrone inside one of the most strategically important Counter-UAS programs in Europe. You will be the primary in-country technical point of contact for the customer, fostering a long-term strategic partnership and ensuring Dedrone’s solutions deliver against the operational mission of protecting people, public safety operations, and critical infrastructure from the drone threat. You will provide technical expertise, day-to-day operational ownership, and customer advocacy to ensure stakeholders achieve their mission objectives using DedroneTracker.AI, RF sensors, radar, and EO/IR camera systems. Collaborating with cross-functional teams across Dedrone PSO, Sales Engineering, Product, and R&D, you will surface valuable insights into customer needs and challenges, influencing internal product roadmaps and projects. Wearing multiple hats — from consultant to product expert to hands-on field engineer — you will help the customer optimise their daily workflows, keep their installed base healthy, and adopt new capabilities as they are released. Communicate customer feedback across Dedrone / Axon teams and collaborate to drive product improvements. • Provide hands-on field support across the deployment lifecycle: hardware reception and unpacking, sensor commissioning, mast and tripod setup, network connectivity validation, C2 platform configuration, and acceptance testing. Maintain knowledge of the customer’s technical landscape, including infrastructure, networking, security policies, and procurement processes. • Monitor support tickets, provide tier-2 technical troubleshooting — sensor → network → server → UI — and escalate to L3 / R&D when necessary.
