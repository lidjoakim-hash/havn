Job Scope Why does the role exist? High level purpose of function. The key responsibilities of manager quality control are to plan, manage and coordinate the activities of the Cell Analysis lab at Lyten Labs, including the chemical, mechanical and failure analysis teams. Purpose is to secure reliable, traceable pipeline and on-time quality control for incoming material, production samples and to support material development with analytical capability and instrumentation expertise. Lead the development of laboratories into one standardized and scalable organization. Key Responsibilities Key responsibilities include (but are not limited to) Lead by example and safety-first principle Lead the quality control team members Plan, organize and review organization, competences, development and training of the lab Plan and align the resources and the demand across team members Ensure the lab works according to the scope of internal laboratory and the analytical scope and ownership matrix Ensure methods, SOP’s, risk assessments and work instructions are developed, implemented and reviewed according to the company rules and guidelines Ensure the laboratory utilities/equipment are maintained, calibrated and safe to use, including MSA and method validation Plan and implement new methods, equipment and lab capabilities Manage product and process deviations, NCM’s and escalations towards stakeholders Define follow up and report lab KPI’s Manage improvements, the implementation and maintain 5S in the Cell Analysis lab Represent the lab in internal, customer and supplier audits Plan and follow up the lab budget and investments Attest time reports on time and ensure to comply with local and companywide processes Accountability/Authorization Budget responsibility: Yes Direct reports: Yes Work Environment responsibility: Yes Authorized to decide on investments according to role and policy Requirements
