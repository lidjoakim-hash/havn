Do you thrive at the intersection of software architecture, problem-solving and hands-on collaboration? Join us in shaping digital solutions that support manufacturing on the workshop floor, while taking the architectural lead in building smart and future-ready systems. About your job As Solution Architect for MES Production Information & Labeling, you define and evolve the architecture that brings together the information needed to run manufacturing equipment. Working at both an architectural and technical level, you help transform future needs into scalable, user-friendly solutions. Job responsibilities Design and evolve end-to-end system architectures. Define integration strategies, technical standards and architecture across the software stack. Participate in the MES architecture forum and establish shared development principles with fellow architects. Shape modern solutions using C#, Angular, OAuth 2.0, Kubernetes, pipelines and DevOps practices. Guide development teams through design reviews, code reviews and technical coaching. Collaborate with stakeholders to translate future needs into sustainable solutions. Location This position is based in Sandviken or Gimo in Sweden and offers a hybrid work setup, with the possibility to work remotely a couple of days per week. You also spend time close to the workshop environment, where many key interactions take place, and occasional travel may occur. Your profile You’re an experienced software architect with a strong background in software development. Combining architectural thinking with hands-on technical expertise, you build clean, flexible and scalable solutions while supporting development teams in moving from ideas to implementation. Your background includes Solid experience in software architecture and modern distributed systems. Strong full-stack development skills and experience guiding development teams. Knowledge of DevOps and technologies such as C#, Angular, OAuth 2.0, Kubernetes and pipelines. A relevant technical degree. Strong communication skills in English. Curious and collaborative, you combine structure with creativity and enjoy solving complex technical challenges. You communicate clearly, take the lead when needed and help teams move in the same direction. Our culture Innovation is in our DNA and we’re proud to shape the future of manufacturing in close collaboration with our customers and partners. We believe in an inclusive, equal and open-minded culture, and we nurture our diversities to form a solid foundation for achieving great results.
