About the position as Director Biostatistician As Director Biostatistician, you will be the strategic statistical lead for clinical development programs, responsible for statistical rigor, consistency and quality across all studies within each program. Partnering closely with cross-functional leaders, you will help shape development strategy and guide study designs, as well as recommend the methodologies, estimands and endpoints that best support program objectives. The role also includes overseeing statistical deliverables from internal and external partners, providing clear direction for outsourced work and acting as the escalation point for complex statistical issues. In addition, you will contribute to integrated database planning and, where applicable, the use of external and real-world data, while anticipating data needs for interim analyses, adaptive trials and regulatory interactions. The analyses you oversee will underpin regulatory strategy, publications, safety evaluations and internal decision-making. When engaging with health authorities, you will act as the statistical representative, preparing responses to their questions and attending regulatory meetings in compliance with ICH guidelines and best practices in clinical statistics. Within cross-functional teams, safety review committees and governance meetings, you will translate complex statistical concepts into clear, actionable insights for non-statisticians. Key responsibilities include: Provide strategic input into clinical development plans, target product profiles, and overall program strategy. Advise on quantitative decision making, including go/no go criteria, dose selection, and adaptive design strategies. Collaborate with Clinical Development to design scientifically sound and efficient clinical trials across phases (First-in-Human through registration) Provide oversight and review of statistical sections of protocols, SAPs, and mock TLFs for individual studies. Oversee statistical and programming activities conducted by CROs and external vendors across all studies in the program.
