UAS Pilot

Krush Tech · Oslo, Norway

We are looking for a qualified military veteran with verified combat and instructional experience in Unmanned Aerial Systems (UAS) and/or Unmanned Ground Vehicles (UGVs) to join us in Oslo as a UAS Pilot. If you hold internationally recognised UAV operator certifications, have completed professional instructor training with a certified training provider, and are capable of delivering personnel training in accordance with NATO and EU requirements and modern military standards, don't hesitate to reach out to me. In this role, you will: • Conduct classroom instruction, simulator sessions, and supervised flight training covering UAS operation, mission planning, system capabilities, and emergency procedures, with UGV instruction where qualified. • Prepare lesson plans, training materials, and practical exercises aligned with the centre’s approved syllabus, applicable EU and national aviation requirements, and relevant NATO and military training standards. • Prepare training missions, perform risk assessments and pre-flight checks, confirm airspace and operating permissions, and supervise activities in accordance with established safety procedures. • Evaluate theoretical knowledge and practical performance, provide structured feedback and remedial instruction, and maintain accurate attendance, assessment, and qualification records. • Conduct serviceability checks, identify and report defects, coordinate maintenance with technical personnel, and ensure training aircraft, ground-control systems, simulators, and associated equipment are ready for use. • Work with instructors, technical staff, and international partners to deliver consistent training; contribute to exercises and post-training reviews; and support emergency preparedness and first-aid response within the scope of certification. You have: • Military service experience in UAV units, reconnaissance, artillery, Special Operations Forces (SOF), robotic systems, or related fields. • At least 5 years of hands-on experience operating UAVs and/or UGVs. • Proven experience as an instructor, trainer, or operator training lead. • Valid UAV operator certificates recognised in the European Union (EASA Open Category A1/A3 and A2, Specific Category, or equivalent national certifications). • English proficiency at C1 level or above. • Knowledge of risk management procedures, flight safety, mission planning, and operations in an international environment. • First aid certification at FPOS Level 3, FREC 3, or an equivalent international standard (TCCC, TECC, Combat Lifesaver). • Completion of an instructor training course delivered by a Recognised Training Provider / Awarding Organisation or another officially recognised training provider. • Ability to deliver theoretical and practical training sessions, assess trainees, and prepare training documentation. What We Offer • An opportunity to join a growing technology company at an exciting stage of development • Meaningful responsibility and direct impact on products and processes • A collaborative and international working environment • Professional development and career growth opportunities • Competitive compensation and benefits package
