As the Sales Specialist for NAS Cloud Direct, you own the number for this product in your territory. You'll be the go-to expert in the field, with the tools, enablement and One Rubrik selling motion to take your career, and your earnings, as far as your results allow. You'll work alongside Core Account Executives, Sales Engineers, Sales Development, Channel Development and our partners, and you'll be the consolidated voice of the field and customer back to Product Management, Product Marketing and PnP. Specialists, so the whole team learns faster Experience You'll Need • 5+ years of field technology sales experience, ideally selling data protection, storage, cloud infrastructure or NAS-related
