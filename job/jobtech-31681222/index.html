. Mips gives you the confidence to follow your passion. Whether you are training for fitness, riding to work, heading out on the mountain or playing team sports, our mission is leading the world to safer helmets. Mips specializes in helmet-based safety and is a world-leader in this area. Based on an ingredient brand business-model, Mips’ safety system is sold to the global helmet industry. The technology is based on over 25 years of research and development together with the Royal Institute of Technology and the Karolinska Institute, both located in Stockholm, Sweden. Mips’ headquarter with 84 employees engaged in research and development, sales, marketing and administration is in Stockholm, where its product and technology test facility also is located. The Mips share is listed on Nasdaq Stockholm, Large Cap.&nbsp; For more information: www.mipscorp.com 2025, Mips announced its acquisition of KOROYD, strengthening the company’s world-leading helmet safety technology portfolio with complementary technology. Recognizing the potential synergies between the two brands and their respective technologies, Mips and KOROYD will continue to operate separately under their respective leadership teams.&nbsp; www.koroyd.com As Mips grows across brands, markets and channels, digital is becoming a central driver of commercial value. We are now looking for a Digital Lead to own our digital agenda and turn it into one clear strategy, one operating model and a well-directed investment portfolio. The roleYou set the direction for how digital creates commercial value across brands, markets and channels, you decide where the digital investment goes, and you lead the team and partners who deliver it. You are part of the marketing leadership team, with a group-level mandate across both Mips and Koroyd. Your mission is to build a unified digital strategy that bridges the gap between B2B ingredient branding and B2C consumer pull. What you will do  Set the digital strategy and roadmap. Define the multi-year strategy, anchored in company strategy and the commercial plan, and translate business objectives into priorities, targets and a prioritized portfolio of initiatives. Own the digital ecosystem for Mips and Koroyd. Paid media, website, social media, SEO, ambassador and influencer marketing, and e-commerce campaigns. Set targets and KPIs per channel and decide on reallocation across the mix, while each brand keeps its own distinct presence and tone of voice. Own the digital investment. Be responsible for the total digital investment across media, platforms, technology, agencies and resources. Set business cases and return requirements, propose the annual budget and own in-year reallocation, with a clear cost-efficiency and ROI focus. Lead data, marketing technology and AI. Own the marketing technology stack and the data, measurement and attribution model behind all reporting and optimization. Set the AI agenda across search and generative engine optimization, AI-assisted shopping and product discovery across our digital ecosystem, and AI-driven content and campaign production. Set governance and manage risk. Establish decision rights, standards and quality gates, and own digital risk and compliance together with Legal, including privacy, consent, accessibility and platform policy. Build the team and the operating model. Lead a team of three digital specialists (analytics and performance, website and social media), design one digital operating model for the group, secure the right competence in the digital team and build digital capability across the wider business.
