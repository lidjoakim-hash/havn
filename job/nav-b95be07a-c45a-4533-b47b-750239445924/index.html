Are you our new Mechanical Designer?

Join us in shaping the next generation of maritime technology. We are looking for a Mechanical Designer to strengthen our hardware organization and develop robust, functional and manufacturable solutions for maritime applications. In this role, you will turn requirements and ideas into mechanical designs, contributing from early concepts through detailed design, prototyping and production follow-up. We are looking for someone who combines practical mechanical understanding with an interest in product development. If you enjoy 3D modelling, solving design challenges and working with materials and manufacturing processes—and preferably have an interest in maritime applications—we would love to hear from you. As a Mechanical Designer, you will develop mechanical parts, assemblies, enclosures and mounting solutions as part of a multidisciplinary team. You will collaborate with hardware engineers, product management, suppliers and production to ensure that design meets requirements for functionality, quality, assembly and serviceability throughout the product lifecycle. If you enjoy combining creative design with structured engineering and hands-on problem-solving, this role could be a great fit for you.   Key accountabilities • Develop concepts, 3D CAD models and detailed mechanical designs for parts and assemblies. • Prepare and maintain production drawings, bills of materials and technical documentation, including tolerances and material specifications. • Select materials, surface treatments and manufacturing methods, considering corrosion resistance, cost, quality and environmental requirements. • Collaborate with suppliers and production to optimise designs for manufacturing, assembly and serviceability. • Participate in design reviews, prototype builds and verification testing, following up findings and design improvements. • Support existing products through mechanical design updates, engineering changes and resolution of production and field issues.   This employment may require a pre-employment check procedure in accordance with company security standards, role or site requirements. Please note that this might affect the offer process or employment. This employment requires security clearance by the Norwegian National Security Authority. The successful candidate may start work with limited accesses whilst the clearance is being processed. Please note that this might affect the offer process or employment. Qualifications and experience • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree, or technical college qualification, in Mechanical Engineering, Mechanical Design, Product Development or a related field. We encourage 2026 graduates to apply. • Proficiency in 3D CAD modelling and preparation of technical drawings, gained through studies, projects or professional experience. • Knowledge of dimensioning, geometrical tolerancing (GD&T), tolerance analysis and design for manufacturing and assembly. • Good English communication, written and verbal. • Understanding materials, surface treatments and manufacturing processes such as sheet metal fabrication and machining. • Familiarity with product data or lifecycle management systems (PLM), bills of materials and revision control is an advantage. • Experience with maritime or industrial equipment, prototyping, mechanical testing or structural analysis is an advantage. At Kongsberg Maritime, we believe that diversity fuels innovation. We encourage you to apply, even if you do not meet every requirement. Your unique perspective is valuable to us. Your skills, attitude and perspective could be exactly what we are looking for!   You are able to • act on own initiative, make things happen and accept responsibility for the results. • demonstrate specialist knowledge and expertise in own area. • communicate in a clear, precise, and structured way. • co-operate well with others, share knowledge, experience, information, and support others in the pursuit of team goals.   What we can offer you • An exciting and important position working for the world-leading supplier of maritime solutions. • Professional development and career opportunities. • Competitive terms including attractive insurance policies and pension schemes. • We offer flexible working hours and the option to work remote or from home office. • Social welfare club with social and recreational activities.   Work location Ålesund, Kongsberg or Horten.
