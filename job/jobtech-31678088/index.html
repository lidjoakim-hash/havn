WHAT THE ROLE OFFERS Lead end-to-end delivery of Engineering Information Management and Document Control solutions across complex enterprise environments Architect scalable and high-performing OpenText ECM system landscapes aligned with client requirements Design and implement integrations with enterprise platforms such as SAP, Microsoft 365, SharePoint, and engineering systems (e.g., CAD, CAE, GIS, Primavera) Configure and manage cloud-based proof-of-concept environments to support workshops and project validation phases Act as a trusted advisor to clients, translating business needs into technical solutions and influencing product improvements Contribute to innovation by enhancing Professional Services methodologies and best practices Collaborate within a global, cross-functional team in a dynamic hybrid work environment WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology or a related field (or equivalent practical experience) Proven experience (6+ years) in enterprise software implementation and solution delivery Hands-on expertise (3+ years) with ECM platforms such as OpenText Content Server, Extended ECM, or similar solutions Strong understanding of system architecture, integration patterns, and enterprise application landscapes Experience or familiarity with web technologies (HTML, XML, JavaScript) Knowledge of programming or scripting languages (e.g., C, C++, C#, OScript, or web services) is beneficial Strong communication, problem-solving, and stakeholder management skills ONE LAST THING: OpenText is more than just a corporation, it's a global community where trust is foundational, the bar is raised, and outcomes are owned. In this role, you’ll work on cutting-edge industry solutions that power the world’s most complex engineering and manufacturing projects.
