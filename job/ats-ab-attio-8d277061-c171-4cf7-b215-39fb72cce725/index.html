Attio is the CRM for agentic revenue. Designed for the most ambitious go-to-market teams, it gives companies the power to understand every customer, automate at scale, and build their go-to-market motion exactly as they need. We've raised $116M from some of the world's best investors: GV (Google Ventures), Redpoint, Balderton, Point Nine, and 01A. We hire builders who thrive on complex technical challenges, hold themselves to a high bar, and genuinely care about delighting the people who use what they build. The team here brings sharp judgement, real craft, and the drive to do exceptional work. We're obsessed about the details and energized by the frontier. If you want to do the best work of your career, this is the right place. Purpose of the role and role description As we scale into 2027 and beyond, we're adding a Senior Recruiter in London to extend our reach into the world's most competitive hiring markets. In this role, you'll own full-cycle recruiting across a range of functions, with a particular focus on building Attio's presence and network amongst GTM talent, specifically Sales, Success, Support and Marketing. You'll also play a role in connecting top talent to opportunities across Attio's global team, but primarily London and New York. You'll work closely with GTM Executives, our Hiring Managers and the Talent team, partnering on structured, calibrated hiring processes that consistently raise the bar. This is a recruiting role for someone who recognises the impact of great hiring on company success and who see’s this as an opportunity to perfect the craft of calibration, engagement and network building to become one of the best in their field in this market. Key candidate attributes - Skills - Full cycle skills for briefings, partnering, calibration and networking - Role launch design and sourcing strategy execution across multiple channels - Candidate pipeline management and data hygiene - Experience - High-growth AI, B2B SaaS or tech startup recruitment experience either in agency or in house - Proven track record hiring across IC and manager level hiring. Bonus for exec - Deep familiarity with talent networks [GTM] and building teams across London and NYC. - Behaviours - Resilient - able to maintain momentum in a market where the bar is genuinely high, and sees that bar as an opportunity rather than a blocker - Resourceful - finds creative paths to candidates, doesn't rely on inbound - Proactive - surfaces ideas, spots gaps, doesn't wait to be directed - High agency - treats every open role like their own problem to solve - Facilitator, not the hero - understands that great recruiting is hiring manager-led; their job is to calibrate, enable and accelerate - Evangelism - deep belief in what Attio is building Hiring process and hiring panel Stage 1 - Introduction - Motivations, market background, understanding of high-bar recruiting environments. Stage 2 — Track Record - Deep dive into recruiting track record. Roles closed, hiring bar held, sourcing methods, and how they have operated in high-growth and competitive US markets. Stage 3 — Panel Interview - Working with Hiring managers, commercial judgement, market understanding, existing networks, working style, Talent team. Stage 4 — Executive Interview - Mission alignment, trajectory, and final calibration on the type of talent hire Attio needs Compensation & Benefits - Equity in an early-stage tech company on an incredible trajectory - 25 days holiday plus local public holidays - Apple hardware - Private medical insurance through AXA - Pension contribution through Hargreaves Lansdown - Enhanced family leave - Team off-site in fun places! (We've been to Barcelona, Lisbon, Malta, and Split so far)
