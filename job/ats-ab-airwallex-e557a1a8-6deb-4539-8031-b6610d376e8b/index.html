ABOUT AIRWALLEX Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own. We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.   HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT As Sales Development Manager, you’ll lead two outbound SDR teams covering Payment Acceptance and Spend across French SMB and Mid-Market accounts. You’ll build the operating rhythm, coaching culture and processes that create higher-quality pipeline across a competitive French market. - Lead, coach and develop the Payment Acceptance and Spend SDR teams across SMB and Mid-Market. - Build effective territories, account lists, sequences andoutbound plays by segment, persona and use case, while setting clear standards for activity, qualification, meeting preparation, follow-up and handover. - Coach reps through regular 1:1s, call reviews, role plays, product training and individual development plans, and make outbound calls yourself to lead by example. - Use activity, meeting, pipeline, conversion and win-rate data to improve execution, meeting quality, AE acceptance and visibility on SDR-sourced deals. - Partner closely with Sales Managers, Account Executives, Marketing, RevOps and Enablement to improve ICP definition, messaging, tooling, routing, training and pipeline feedback. - Document and maintain practical playbooks covering outbound execution, product knowledge, qualification and competitive messaging. You’ll work from our Paris office three days a week. WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR Essentials - 4+ years of B2B sales experience, including meaningful experience managing or leading SDR or BDR teams. - Experience running outbound motions across SMB and Mid-Market accounts. - Strong personal cold-calling and multi-channel prospecting experience. - A track record of coaching reps and improving individual and team performance. - Experience building or standardising territories, sequences, qualification processes or sales playbooks. - Confidence discussing activity volume, meetings, pipeline, conversion and win rate. - Strong communication and stakeholder-management skills, with the ability to work with SDRs, AEs, Sales Managers, Marketing and RevOps. - Fluent French and professional English. - A hands-on, data-driven and high-ownership approach to management. Preferred - Experience in a high-volume but non-transactional environment, ideally in SaaS, fintech, payments, banking, FX, ERP, procurement or spend management.
