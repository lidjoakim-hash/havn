CAE Engineer

Sandvik Aktiebolag · Svedala, Sweden

Join our Technology Development team in Svedala and help develop simulation models that improve the performance of our crushing solutions. In this position, you combine analytical work with hands-on verification and testing, while building a detailed understanding of our machine systems and their functionality. About your job As a CAE Engineer, you design, implement and use simulation models to support the development and optimization of our machine systems. Your work ranges from physical modelling and analysis to practical model verification, testing and measurement activities, with close connections to product development. Job responsibilities Design, implement and use simulation models across different physical domains. Develop functionality that supports the design, optimization and improvement of machine systems. Verify and validate simulation models through testing and measurement activities. Develop algorithms and code related to simulation and modelling. Collaborate with design engineers on product improvements and new concepts. Contribute to developing our CAE methods, tools and ways of working. Location and flexibility This position is based in Svedala, where Division Crushing Solutions has its global development center for crushers. Your profile We’re looking for someone with a strong technical and analytical foundation and an interest in physical modelling and simulation. Experience in developing and validating simulation models enables you to move confidently between theoretical analysis and practical verification. Your background includes A Master of Science or higher degree in physics, mathematics or mechanical engineering. Experience developing simulation models in different physical domains. Experience verifying and validating simulation models. Experience in algorithm development and coding. It’s beneficial if you have experience in testing, as well as experience leading projects or teams. You enjoy working with new technology and approaching complex challenges in a structured, analytical and pragmatic way. Collaboration comes naturally to you, and your communication skills help you work effectively across functions, cultures and with both internal and external stakeholders. Just as importantly, you contribute to a positive team spirit. The team You join a small and agile Technology Development team, giving you the opportunity to influence methods, tools and ways of working within CAE. Cross-functional collaboration is a natural part of your day, connecting you with colleagues and stakeholders across our organization. Our culture Sandvik is so much more than an engineering group in the manufacturing industry. We’re a global company with many opportunities for personal and professional development – and great benefits. We’ve been an employer to trust for 160 years, and our reward philosophy states that remuneration and other employment conditions should align with local market practice, be competitive, and reflect responsibility and performance. We have a clear objective of being a leader within digital solutions in our industry and we focus on growing our digital customer offering. To get there, we work towards an even more diverse and inclusive organization – because we’re sure that differences improve Sandvik's business and creates a culture of innovation.
