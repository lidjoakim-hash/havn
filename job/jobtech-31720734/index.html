Denna position rapporterar till: R&D Unit Lead __ Your Role and Responsibilities At ABB, we are dedicated to enabling a more sustainable and resource-efficient future through innovative electrification solutions. We are now looking for a Product Certification Specialist to join our team. Whether you already have experience within product certification or want to develop your expertise in this area, this role offers a unique opportunity to work closely with product development, standards, and compliance in a global environment. In this role, you will play an important part in ensuring that our products meet relevant national and international standards and regulatory requirements. You will work closely with colleagues across R&D, Product Management, Quality, and other functions to support successful product launches and maintain compliance throughout the product lifecycle. Your responsibilities will include: Leading and coordinating certification activities for new low voltage products, ensuring compliance with applicable standards and regulations. Collaborating with cross-functional teams to collect, review, and maintain the technical documentation required for certification submissions. Working closely with certification bodies, testing laboratories, and regulatory authorities to obtain approvals and support market access. Monitoring certification requirements and regulatory developments in target markets and assessing their impact on new and existing products. Managing renewals and updates of existing certifications, ensuring continued compliance throughout the product lifecycle. Supporting internal stakeholders with certification-related expertise and contributing to continuous improvements of certification processes and ways of working. Living ABB's core values of safety and integrity by taking responsibility for your actions while caring for your colleagues and the business. Qualifications for the Role We believe you are an organized and proactive team player who enjoys collaborating with different stakeholders and driving tasks forward. You have a strong interest in technical compliance, standards, and product development. To be successful in this role, you likely have: A degree in Electrical Engineering or a related technical field. Experience working in an R&D, product development, testing, quality, or similar technical environment. An interest in or experience of product certification, standards, compliance, or regulatory requirements. The ability to structure and manage multiple activities while meeting deadlines. Strong communication skills and the ability to collaborate with stakeholders in an international environment. Fluency in English, both written and spoken. Swedish is considered an advantage. Willingness to travel occasionally when required. A strong awareness of Health, Safety and Environment (HSE), with a commitment to creating a safe workplace. It is considered a merit if you have: Previous experience with product certification. Knowledge of international standards and certification processes related to low voltage products or electrical equipment. Experience working with certification bodies, testing laboratories, or regulatory authorities. What’s in it for you? We empower you to take initiative, challenge ideas, and lead with confidence. You’ll grow through meaningful work, continuous learning, and support that’s tailored to your goals. Every idea you share and every action you take contributes to something bigger.
