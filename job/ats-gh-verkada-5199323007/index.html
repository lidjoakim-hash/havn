About the Role The Regional Sales Manager (DACH) is responsible for hiring, managing and mentoring a team of Mid-Market level talent to meet and exceed their sales revenue goals. The Mid-Market team focuses on accounts with full-time employee counts of up to 2,000 employees within the DACH region. Our Account Executives are responsible for maintaining high activity standards; daily prospecting, pipeline growth, prospect qualification, and delivering assigned monthly and quarterly sales revenue targets.
