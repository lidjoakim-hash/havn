Asana · Dublin, Ireland

As a Senior Solutions Architect inside the Solutions Center of Excellence, you will serve as a strategic, technical partner in our most complex enterprise sales cycles. You are a recognized subject matter expert who empowers global teams and customers to achieve their mission by showcasing "the art of the possible" through deep technical expertise in Asana’s API, AI, and platform ecosystem. You will manage large, complex technical projects with our strategic customers, influencing both internal product roadmaps and external customer technology frameworks. This role is a transformation from one-off technical fixes to building the architectural foundation that orchestrates work at scale. This role is based in our San Francisco or Dublin office with an office-centric hybrid schedule. The standard in-office days are Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday. Most Asanas have the option to work from home on Wednesdays. Working from home on Fridays depends on the type of work you do and the teams with which you partner. If you're interviewing for this role, your recruiter will share more about the in-office requirements.
