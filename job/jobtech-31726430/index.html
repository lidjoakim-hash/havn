Imaging Engineer, Lund

Axis Communications Aktiebolag · Lund, Sweden

We are looking for a dedicated Imaging Engineer to strengthen our team! In this role you will further develop the imaging platform for our production systems but also support industrialization and R&D to turn new products and ideas into mass production. Does this sound interesting? Keep reading and welcome with your application! Who is your future team? You will join the Imaging team, a specialized group of four experienced engineers focused on developing the imaging parts needed within our production systems to produce Axis cameras with high image quality. These production systems play a critical role in supporting high-volume manufacturing operations at our global facilities, where millions of products are produced and tested annually. The Imaging team is part of a larger division of about 60+ engineers working with Production Engineering and Technology (PET), and together with our colleagues within SW, electronics and mechanical engineering we develop and maintain production systems globally. What you'll do here as Imaging Engineer As an Imaging Engineer, you'll contribute to the design, development and maintenance of imaging production tests that enable the testing of Axis products in our production systems. You will also be a part of our development projects where we use innovative imaging and optical solutions to design efficient and reliable production systems. Typical tasks include handling topics related to optics, collimators and other imaging equipment needed to produce Axis' products. You will also play an important role in designing, developing, and maintaining software frameworks used for verification and calibration of our production systems. This includes collaborating with cross-functional teams to ensure seamless production processes. Key responsibilities include: Designing and maintaining both SW and HW for the imaging tests framework to support production processes. Developing and maintaining software frameworks to facilitate machine monitoring, verification, calibration and production testing using C# and Python Providing technical support to R&D product development initiatives Analyzing production data to drive system improvements and innovation Contributing to strategic planning for future imaging test development Our team leverages a range of technologies, including: Hardware like different types of optics and collimators Programming languages: C#, Python, C++ Tools: Jira, Confluence, QlikView, Visual Studio, GitHub Copilot Version control systems: Git, SVN, Gerrit Continuous integration platforms: TeamCity Agile methodologies Who are we looking for/Who are you? We think you are a dedicated engineer with skills in imaging, optics and software development who is curious, responsible, organized and methodical. You enjoy problem solving and working with both hardware and software. Ability to communicate is important as the role requires collaboration with other teams and departments. The work includes a lot of freedom and independence, which requires that you are proactive and take initiative. We'd love to hear that you have: Experience within optics, collimators or other imaging equipment A huge interest in software development and optics Finding solutions to complex technical problems Interest in the complexity of production and creating efficient production systems for our factories around the world University degree preferably within computer science, engineering, physics, electrical engineering or equivalent At least a couple of years of experience from roles in a development organization Good communication skills in English and Swedish, both in speech and writing The position is full time. Travelling is not required, but there will probably be possibilities to visit our factories around the world.
