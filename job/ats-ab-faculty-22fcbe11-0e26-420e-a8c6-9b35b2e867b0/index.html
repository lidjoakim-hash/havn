WHY FACULTY? We established Faculty in 2014 because we thought that AI would be the most important technology of our time. Since then, we’ve worked with over 350 global customers to transform their performance through human-centric AI. You can read about our real-world impact here https://faculty.ai/impact. We don’t chase hype cycles. We innovate, build and deploy responsible AI which moves the needle - and we know a thing or two about doing it well. We bring an unparalleled depth of technical, product and delivery expertise to our clients who span government, finance, retail, energy, life sciences and defence. Our business, and reputation, is growing fast and we’re always on the lookout for individuals who share our intellectual curiosity and desire to build a positive legacy through technology. AI is an epoch-defining technology, join a company where you’ll be empowered to envision its most powerful applications, and to make them happen.   ABOUT THE TEAM   Our National Security and AI Safety business unit is dedicated to advancing the responsible development and deployment of AI in support of national security and global stability. From strengthening mission-critical capabilities across national security and intelligence, to working with frontier labs to provide robust AI safety red teaming and evaluation, we work at the frontier of high-stakes, high-impact missions. We understand that powerful AI systems bring both transformative opportunities and complex risks and we are proud to partner with Government and the biggest tech organisations in the world to ensure AI is not just transformative but is also secure, trustworthy and safe for all. Because of the nature of the work we do with our Government clients, you may need to be eligible for UK Developed Vetting (DV) and willing to work on site with our clients from time to time.     ABOUT THE ROLE   As a Frontend Engineer, you'll be instrumental in visualising insights generated by our data scientists for our clients. Working closely with cross-functional delivery teams, especially within the Government & Public Services Business Unit, you will help solve unique challenges using AI and Machine Learning.   We're looking for an entrepreneurial technologist to drive industry best practices and standards within our evolving frontend guild and bring Faculty's machine learning work to life.     WHAT YOU'LL BE DOING:   - Collaborating closely with data scientists, data engineers, and design specialists to build client-facing products - Prioritisation, planning, coordination, and communication of frontend features to internal and external stakeholders - Setting up and maintaining the development and deployment infrastructure for your dedicated frontend team - Maintaining and enforcing the technical best practices and standards across Faculty's entire frontend capacity - Creating and maintaining reusable component libraries for use in projects company-wide - Providing training, mentoring, and onboarding support for new frontend engineers - Acting as the go-to authority for frontend software engineering within your team     WHO WE'RE LOOKING FOR:   - You have demonstrated experience in building scalable, maintainable, and well-tested web applications using React - You are highly proficient in foundational web technologies, including ES6 JavaScript, HTML, and CSS3 - You have hands-on experience writing TypeScript or other typed languages, contributing to robust codebases - You are experienced in data visualisation techniques and implementations, such as d3, to bring data to life - You have exposure to deployment into the Cloud with platforms such as AWS, GCP, or Azure - You possess a strong focus on technical excellence, eager to set standards and mentor others to build a collaborative, high-performing culture   OUR INTERVIEW PROCESS   1. Talent Team Screen (30 minutes) 2. Pair Programming Interview (90 minutes)
