Choose the Lifestyle That Suits You Working arrangements vary depending on the project. Moving to Greece Is Easier Than You Think You don't have to organize your entire relocation on your own. Who Are We Looking For? We are looking for candidates who: • Speak and write fluent Norwegian • • Have good English communication skills • • Are service-minded and enjoy helping others • • Are comfortable using computers and digital systems • • Are solution-oriented and eager to learn • • Are open to living and working in Greece Extensive previous work experience is not required for many of these positions. Motivation, communication skills, and a positive attitude can be just as important.
