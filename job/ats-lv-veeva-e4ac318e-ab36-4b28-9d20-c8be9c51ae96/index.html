As one of the fastest-growing SaaS companies in history, we surpassed $3B in revenue in our last fiscal year with extensive growth potential ahead.   At the heart of Veeva are our values: Do the Right Thing, Customer Success, Employee Success, and Speed. We're not just any public company – we made history in 2021 by becoming a public benefit corporation (PBC), legally bound to balancing the interests of customers, employees, society, and investors.   As a Work Anywhere company, we support your flexibility to work from home or in the office, so you can thrive in your ideal environment.   Join us in transforming the life sciences industry, committed to making a positive impact on its customers, employees, and communities. The Role As a Customer Success Manager, you will be the strategic bridge between Veeva and leading global life sciences organizations, empowering them to maximize the value of Veeva’s industry-defining Pharmacovigilance (PV) suite.   In this role, you won’t just be a member of the account team - you will be a trusted advisor and PV expert. You’ll partner directly with customers’ executive stakeholders and PV leaders to understand their vision and operational challenges, while helping them to unlock the full potential of their investments. You’ll be an active member of a cross functional team to champion the voice of the customer, proactively drive product enablement to fit customers’ goals/needs, and turn complex PV challenges into streamlined success stories. What You'll Do • Support 4-6 large, global customers post-implementation with Veeva Pharmacovigilance applications • Lead with curiosity, act as a trusted advisor and own the relationship with customers’ business decision makers and application owners • Liaise with Veeva Strategy, Sales, Product and/or Services’ stakeholders to address customer needs • Through proactive engagement, provide strategic guidance to optimize use of Veeva applications and help create a customer roadmap based on their business goals and needs • Facilitate customer-to-customer or customer-to-Veeva connections for collaboration opportunities and industry learning Requirements • 7+ years of experience in Pharmacovigilance • Experience working with or in large global pharmaceutical companies • Customer-facing consulting experience • Experience managing relationships with key decision makers and application owners • Ability to travel up to 25% and reside in Europe Nice to Have • 7+ years of working in Pharmacovigilance software, or consulting • SaaS/cloud experience in the management of pharmacovigilance applications • Experience working with Veeva Vault Interviewing with Veeva We value your time and believe in a transparent hiring process.
