Production Operative Derby, UK Rate of Pay: £12.71/hour + 20% Night Shift Uplift About The Team: This role is responsible for assisting in the waste sortation process to allow continuous flow of our production of our meal kits, whilst ensuring that the Health & Safety and Food Safety standards are met. What You Will Be Doing: • Ensuring that Food Safety and Health & Safety standards are met and sustainably maintained. You are required to cooperate with HelloFresh in all health and safety matters. You are responsible for ensuring your own health, safety and welfare in the activities you undertake. You must report incidents immediately and actively raise health and safety-related concerns to your Line Manager. • Undertaking basic problem solving activities and escalating to the Production Waste Team Leader where necessary. • Ensuring compliance with Standard Operating Procedures and Instructions. • Maintaining accuracy within waste sortation by following guidelines to ensure waste is disposed of correctly • Identification of correct pallet locations and staging areas for waste products by reviewing potential changes to working area to maximise accuracy & efficiency • Operate machinery and equipment related to the waste process, such as pallet wrap machines, conveyor systems and compactors. Maintain cleanliness and organisation within the warehouse, including workstations, storage areas, and equipment. • Ensuring waste disposal equipment is available in all areas of the warehouse at all times and returned to correct location ready for reuse.
