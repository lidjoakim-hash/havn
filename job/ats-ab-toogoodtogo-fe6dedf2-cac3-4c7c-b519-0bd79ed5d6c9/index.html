Too Good To Go is on a mission to inspire and empower everyone to fight food waste together. Our app connects people with surplus food from stores, restaurants and brands, so good food gets eaten instead of wasted. Your Mission: As B2C Marketing Specialist (f/m/d) for Spain, you will take ownership of end-to-end consumer marketing across owned, paid and organic channels. Your primary objective is to acquire, activate, and retain Spanish users, growing brand love and driving consumer demand across the country. Working as part of our South Europe Marketing Cluster, you will balance global brand strategy with local cultural nuances. You will orchestrate compelling campaigns across Social Media, CRM (email, push, in-app) and Content, while continuously tracking data to optimize performance. This role is a hybrid of creative storytelling, cross-functional execution, and data-driven optimization in a fast-paced scale-up environment. Your Role: Reporting to the Senior B2C Marketing Manager for Italy, Spain and Portugal, you will: - Plan, build, and deploy end-to-end CRM workflows (push notifications, email marketing, in-app messaging) for local B2C and co-marketing (B2B2C) initiatives. Track performance across open rates, conversions, and retention, providing actionable reporting to optimize consumer LTV. - Coordinate and execute local consumer marketing campaigns tapped into seasonal moments, collaborating closely with cross-functional teams (Creative, Partner Marketing, Paid Marketing, PR) and the South Europe Cluster. - Translate, adapt, and write high-converting native Spanish copy across all consumer touchpoints (CRM, app UI, landing pages, and social channels), ensuring local cultural resonance and brand consistency. - Steer the creative engine for Paid User Acquisition by scouting trending content online, outsourcing production to UGC creators and localizing/translating global creative assets. Track performance signals to identify winning hooks and scale what works. - Own the local social media editorial calendar and community interactions. Translate and localize global assets while developing original, locally driven content to leverage cultural insights, address consumer barriers and showcase key partners. Requirements - Professional business fluency in Spanish and English (written & spoken) for seamless translation, copywriting, and collaboration with other teams. Portuguese is nice to have. - Degree in Marketing, Communications, Business, or a related field. - Marketing experience in B2C/B2B2C environments, ideally within a tech scale-up, app/marketplace, FMCG, or consumer brand. - Hands-on experience driving CRM & lifecycle marketing (push notifications, email, in-app messaging). - Proven track record managing organic social media and paid content delivery, including scouting UGC creators, briefing influencers, and adapting creative assets to local trends and consumer insights. - Exceptional copywriting, storytelling, and localization skills in Spanish, with sharp attention to brand tone of voice and cultural nuance. - Strong cross-functional and project management capabilities with a data-informed approach to tracking campaign performance and optimizing metrics. - Self-starter with high accountability, a proactive roll-up-your-sleeves attitude, and a genuine passion for Too Good To Go's mission to fight waste.
