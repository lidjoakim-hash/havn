About the position A permanent position as Senior engineer (position code 1181) is available at The Department of Physics, University of Oslo. Starting date as soon as possible.  Place of work is Department of Physics at Blindern, Oslo. Job description and work tasks The Electronics section has seven academic positions (two full-time and five part-time) and two technical positions. The research activity is currently largely related to projects in medical technology, particularly those using methods based on impedance spectroscopy. The successful candidate may also provide technical support for new research activities in electronic components and neuromorphic electronics, especially related to electrical characterization, measurement setups, and laboratory infrastructure.  Together with the Department of Informatics, the section is responsible for a study program in electronics, informatics, and technology. The academic staff are therefore responsible for several courses in electronics and sensor technology and also contribute to teaching basic physics courses. The section’s staff are also involved in the supervision of students at both master’s and doctoral level.  The engineering position is intended to support both the research and teaching activities in which the section is involved.  The tasks connected to teaching will primarily involve training and assisting master’s and PhD students in the use of specialized measuring instruments and software.  Part of the position also involves organizing the section’s student laboratories. This includes testing of hardware and software, as well as other typical engineering tasks related to the day to-day operation of the laboratories. What skills are important in this role? Qualification requirements: The minimum educational requirement is a master’s degree (or equivalent) in electronics/electrical engineering. In addition, the candidate must have experience with:  • Instrumentation, measurement systems, and laboratory work  • Impedance spectroscopy and interpretation of bioimpedance data  • Programming in Python and MATLAB, or equivalent  • Finite element modelling, e.g. COMSOL Multiphysics  • Good oral and written English skills The following qualifications are strongly desirable and will be emphasized in the evaluation process:  • Research experience, preferably demonstrated through a PhD degree  • Use of various sensors for monitoring food quality, such as colorimetry, pH measurements, and NIR spectrometry  • Electrical characterization of semiconductor devices or other electronic components, preferably including low-current and/or pulsed measurements  • Automation and integration of laboratory instruments and measurement systems  • Technical supervision of students What are we looking for in you? Personal skills: • A service minded attitude  • Ability to work independently  • Ability to work precisely in a structured and solution-oriented manner and swiftly adapts to new tasks  • Good communication and collaboration skills  • Positive attitude and the ability to handle hectic periods  • Have a safety-oriented attitude Employment in the position is based on a comprehensive assessment of all qualification requirements applicable to the position, including personal skills. We can offer you • A pleasant and stimulating work environment • Good welfare schemes. • Opportunity of up to 1.5 hours a week of exercise during working hours.  A workplace with good development and career opportunities. • Membership in the Statens Pensjonskasse, which is one of Norway's best pension schemes with beneficial mortgages and good insurance schemes. • Oslo’s family-friendly surroundings with their rich opportunities for culture and outdoor activities • Salary in position as Senior Engineer, position code 1181 in salary range NOK 700 000 – 775 000, depending on competence and experience.
