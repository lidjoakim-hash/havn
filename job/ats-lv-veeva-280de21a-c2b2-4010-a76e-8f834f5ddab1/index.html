Veeva Systems is a mission-driven organization and pioneer in industry cloud, helping life sciences companies bring therapies to patients faster. As one of the fastest-growing SaaS companies in history, we surpassed $3B in revenue in our last fiscal year with extensive growth potential ahead.   At the heart of Veeva are our values: Do the Right Thing, Customer Success, Employee Success, and Speed. We're not just any public company – we made history in 2021 by becoming a public benefit corporation (PBC), legally bound to balancing the interests of customers, employees, society, and investors.   As a Work Anywhere company, we support your flexibility to work from home or in the office, so you can thrive in your ideal environment.   Join us in transforming the life sciences industry, committed to making a positive impact on its customers, employees, and communities. The Role Veeva Link Key People provides deep KOL data to improve engagement with scientific, digital, and clinical leaders. Your mission as a Senior Lead is to be the operational backbone of our curation efforts. You will guide and lead a remote team of 50+ freelancers who do rule-based web research. You will design the production processes of tomorrow, driving the technological improvements that elevate our efficiency and data quality. To thrive in your role, you are a "can-do" leader who loves to solve problems and communicate solutions. Whether you are speaking with a curator, an analyst, or senior leadership, you know how to align teams and drive results. You take full ownership of your team's performance, viewing their success as your own. You are someone who thrives in fast-paced, multi-cultural, and remote-first environments. If you are passionate about data and operations, eager to grow your data and leadership skills, and ready to make a tangible impact in the Life Sciences industry, this is your platform to build something remarkable. What You'll Do • Lead a remote team of 50+ freelancers • Implement impactful quality and efficiency projects • Build a dynamic, proactive, and high-performing team • Own your team’s KPIs and its performance • Collaborate with engineering teams on process automation • Escalate all risks and incidents early and unambiguously Requirements • 3+ years of experience in a fast-paced environment • Proven knowledge of business process design, management, and optimization • Previous experience mentoring junior team members, leading projects, or managing teams • Excellent written and verbal English skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively in a distributed, international environment Nice to Have • Connection with life sciences (education or professional experience) Interviewing with Veeva We value your time and believe in a transparent hiring process.
