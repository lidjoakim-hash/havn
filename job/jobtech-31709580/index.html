Logent Bemanning AB · Stockholm, Sweden

Logent provides contract logistics, transport management, customs services and related solutions across the Nordics and Europe. Sustainability is integral to our strategy and operations, creating long-term value for customers, employees, society and the environment. The Role: We are looking for a business-oriented Sustainability Manager to lead and develop Logent’s Group-wide sustainability agenda. You will drive strategy, compliance, ESG reporting and continuous improvement, working with management, operations, HR, Finance, Procurement and customers to embed sustainability in decisions and daily operations. As the Group’s sustainability expert, you will translate requirements into practical actions and measurable environmental, social and governance outcomes. Key Responsibilities: Sustainability Strategy & Governance: · Develop and implement the sustainability strategy and roadmap; monitor progress, risks and deviations across the Group. · Develop and maintain sustainability policies, guidelines and procedures, and support their implementation. · Translate objectives into business-unit action plans and KPIs. · Advise executive management and the Board on sustainability. · Coordinate governance processes and quarterly performance reviews. ESG Reporting & Compliance: · Lead sustainability reporting under CSRD, ESRS and other applicable regulations. · Manage double materiality assessments, ESG data collection and internal controls. · Coordinate external assurance and auditor reviews. · Monitor sustainability legislation and ensure ongoing compliance. · Assess sustainability risks and opportunities and integrate them into business planning. Climate & Environmental Management: · Drive emissions reduction programmes and SBTi target implementation. · Oversee Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions reporting and improvements. · Support energy efficiency, waste management and resource optimisation. · Work with operations to develop sustainable logistics solutions and customer offerings. Social & Employee Sustainability: · Support health and safety, employee engagement, diversity and inclusion initiatives. · Partner with HR on sustainability training and awareness. · Promote a strong sustainability culture across the organisation. Supply Chain & Customer Engagement: · Identify business opportunities through sustainable logistics solutions. · Support customer development, sustainability requests, questionnaires and tenders. · Drive supplier sustainability assessments and responsible sourcing. Data, Performance & Continuous Improvement: · Develop sustainability KPIs, dashboards and performance reports. · Ensure high-quality sustainability data across business units. · Identify improvements and support sustainability project delivery. Qualifications: · Degree in Sustainability, Environmental Science, Business Administration, Engineering or a related field. · 3+ years’ experience in sustainability, ESG, environmental management or a related discipline. · Strong knowledge of CSRD, ESRS, EU Taxonomy, GHG Protocol and relevant ESG frameworks. · Experience in change management and turning complex requirements into practical business actions. · Experience in sustainability reporting and ESG data management. · Experience working across functions in a multi-site organisation. · Excellent communication and project management skills. · Fluent English, written and spoken. Preferred Qualifications: · Experience in logistics, transport, supply chain or industry. · Experience with the Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi). · Knowledge of ISO 14001, ISO 45001 and sustainability assurance. · Experience managing sustainability audits and customer assessments. Personal Qualities: · Strategic and analytical, with a hands-on approach. · Structured, proactive and results-driven. · Strong stakeholder management skills and ability to influence change. · Committed to sustainability and business value creation.
