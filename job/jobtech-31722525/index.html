Digital Twin Coordinator

Hitachi Energy Sweden AB · Västerås, Sweden

The opportunity Join our HVDC Engineering Digital Twin team and help drive the successful delivery of IdentiQ across HVDC projects and tenders. As a Digital Twin Coordinator, you will coordinate the end to end Digital Twin delivery within projects, ensuring that scope, requirements, engineering inputs, dependencies, schedule, cost, risks and stakeholders are aligned for successful execution. You will work at the intersection of engineering, project execution and digital technology, collaborating closely with Digital Twin Engineers, Technical Solution Owners, Product Management and engineering functions across HVDC. You will also work directly with project teams and customers, helping translate requirements into a structured and executable Digital Twin delivery. This is an opportunity for someone who enjoys bringing structure to complex engineering projects, coordinating across organizations and contributing directly to the continued digital transformation of HVDC. How you’ll make an impact undefined Coordinate end to end Digital Twin delivery across HVDC tenders and projects, acting as the main coordination point between project stakeholders and the Digital Twin team. Capture, review and coordinate customer and project requirements, ensuring scope, deviations and dependencies are identified and addressed with the appropriate stakeholders. Plan and follow up Digital Twin activities, including budget, resource needs, schedule, risks, issues, documentation and overall project progress. Coordinate across Digital Twin Engineers, Technical Solution Owners, Product Management and other engineering functions to ensure prerequisites and dependencies are addressed for successful delivery. Present IdentiQ to project teams and customers during tender and project execution, supporting workshops, onboarding and training activities. Drive continuous improvement by capturing lessons learned, improving delivery processes and providing structured feedback on product and solution improvements. Your background undefined Bachelor's or Master's degree in Engineering, Technology, Project Management or a related technical field, or equivalent relevant experience. Experience in engineering projects, technical project coordination or project management, preferably within power systems, energy, industrial automation or digital solutions. Strong ability to coordinate cross functional teams, manage multiple stakeholders and translate technical and project requirements into clear actions and deliverables. Good understanding of project execution, including planning, budgeting, cost estimation, requirements management, risk management and progress follow up. Strong communication and presentation skills, with the confidence to work with engineering teams, project management and customers. Fluent English is required. A structured, collaborative and proactive mindset with the ability to take ownership, drive topics forward and bring clarity to technically complex projects.
