ABOUT THE ROLE We're looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to join our Bank Infrastructure team at Pleo. This team builds and maintains the payment rails that power Pleo's money movement, integrating with banks and payment partners to handle inbound and outbound bank transfers reliably and at scale. You'll be working on some of the most critical systems at Pleo: the infrastructure that guarantees that each financial transaction, whether incoming, outgoing, or internal, is processed and settled with absolute accuracy, on time, and with the resilience to recover gracefully when things go wrong. If you're excited by the challenge of building highly reliable financial systems and want to work in a culture that values transparency, collaboration, and engineering excellence, this role is for you. WHAT YOU’LL BE DOING As a Senior Backend Engineer on Bank Infrastructure, you will: - Design, build, and maintain integrations with banks and payment partners across multiple payment schemes (SEPA, Faster Payments, SWIFT, and others). - Own the reliability and correctness of inbound and outbound payment flows, including reconciliation, settlement, and exception handling. - Architect systems that are resilient to partner outages, network failures, and edge cases inherent in financial messaging. - Lead initiatives to improve system observability, fault tolerance, and operational excellence across payment rails. - Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including compliance, finance operations, and product to evolve Pleo's payment capabilities. - Participate in code reviews, post-mortems, and provide mentorship to other engineers. - Proactively address technical debt and guide the team through technical challenges and migrations. WHAT YOU BRING You'll thrive in this role if you have: - Hands-on experience building payment integrations, bank APIs, payment scheme integrations, or financial messaging formats (ISO 20022, SWIFT, SEPA, Faster Payments, or similar). - Strong understanding of payment flows end-to-end: payment initiation, routing, reconciliation, settlement, and exception/failure handling. - Experience designing and operating highly available, resilient distributed systems with robust error recovery and observability. - Expertise in server-side development, ideally Kotlin or Java, with strong experience in microservices and distributed systems. - Proficiency with relational databases (PostgreSQL), cloud environments (AWS, Kubernetes), and observability tooling (e.g. Grafana). - Awareness of payment compliance and regulatory considerations (e.g. AML, sanctions screening, PSD2) is a plus. - Understanding of safeguarding of customer funds and the impact of money movement pipelines. - Strong problem-solving skills and experience owning production payment
