About us Traditional banking has never really given you control over your financial life. Satispay exists to change that: to empower people through finance. That means making financial services easy and accessible for over 6.5M users. We started with payments, then benefits, investing, cards... and we’re not done. Not even close. We follow the same logic every time: find a real problem, solve it properly, then look for the next one. The destination? To build the most loved financial platform in the world. If you own outcomes rather than tasks, stay close to what users need, and refuse to wait for someone else to fix what's broken — you'll find people here who work exactly in the same way. And together, you'll build something worth being proud of. What you’ll be doing As our HR Operations Specialist, you'll optimize the entire employee lifecycle and ensure HR compliance and operational efficiency across the organization. Here's what that looks like in practice: - End-to-End HR & Payroll Administration - Own Italian payroll preparation and execution in coordination with outsourced providers to guarantee accuracy, compliance, and timeliness, while supporting international payroll operations (e.g., Spain, Luxembourg) as needed. - Employee Lifecycle Management - Oversee and optimize processes from onboarding to offboarding, including drafting contracts, coordinating promotions, managing organizational changes, and handling terminations in line with legal requirements. - HR Administrative Operations & Employee Support - Manage the HR inquiry inbox and AI-powered ticketing system to resolve employee requests, update and communicate company policies, and maintain administrative documentation. - Cross-Functional Collaboration - Partner with HRBPs and other departments to co-create solutions and roll out cross-functional HR programs. - HRIS Data Integrity & Implementation - Maintain the quality and accuracy of employee records (hires, terminations, leaves) and support the evaluation, customization, and integration of new HRIS solutions with other business platforms. - HR Data Analysis & Reporting - Collect and analyze HR metrics, building dashboards and KPIs to share actionable insights with senior leadership. Who we're looking for This role needs a detail-oriented problem-solver who enjoys teamwork, thrives in fast-paced environments, and takes real ownership to get things done. - HR Administration & Payroll Expertise - 4+ years of hands-on experience in personnel administration and end-to-end Italian payroll management; prior exposure to international payroll coordination is a strong plus. - Italian Labour Law & CCNL Knowledge - Solid expertise in Italian labour regulations and collective agreements (e.g., CCNL Terziario / Commercio, CCNL Metalmeccanica Industria), with proven experience drafting internal HR policies, guidelines, and contractual templates (a background in a Labour Consulting Firm / Studio di Consulenza del Lavoro is a strong plus). - Tech Savviness & AI Mindset - Comfort working with modern ticketing tools and HRIS platforms, paired with an open approach to using AI-driven tools (such as chatbots). - Communication Skills - Fluent in both written and spoken English, able to explain complex administrative topics clearly across all levels of the organization. - Precision & Adaptability - High attention to detail in administrative execution, combined with the agility to manage ad-hoc special projects and shifting priorities. - Data-Driven Approach - Comfort working with numbers and building straightforward HR dashboards to support data-informed decisions.
