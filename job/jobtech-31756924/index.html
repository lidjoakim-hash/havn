Quality Coordinator

Web Manuals Sweden AB · Malmö, Sweden

At Web Manuals, we’re redefining aviation with our state-of-the-art SaaS solutions. Our mission? To revolutionize how aviation professionals work while reaching new heights of innovation and service. As a bootstrapped company, we chart our own course, breaking barriers and setting our sights on the stars and beyond. With offices in Malmö (HQ), San Diego, New York, Singapore, Sydney and Barcelona, we are proud to offer an environment where you can soar, both professionally and personally, and explore a world of opportunities within an international team. Now, we are on the hunt for a Quality Coordinator to join our team in Malmö! Take the leap and join Web Manuals on our journey to revolutionize aviation – apply today! About the role As Quality Coordinator at Web Manuals, you will work with our quality management system and help build a growth-enabled business based on continuous improvement. You will also play a leading role in our planned ISO 14001 implementation, helping us extend our management system into environmental management. Beyond quality, you will govern the full lifecycle of our supplier contracts, from evaluations and audits to negotiations and keeping our contract repository up to date. You will be the go-to person in the organization for supplier contract matters. You will report to our General Counsel and work closely with our Information Security Specialist in the Legal & Compliance Team. Together you will manage audits and compliance reporting, and you will support our ISO/IEC 27001 and SOC 2 audits. You’ll also support our Commercial and Customer Success teams in answering RFP questionnaires where applicable. You will be the main contact for our certification body, customers and suppliers on quality matters, so clear communication and a collaborative approach will be key. Core responsibilities include, but are not limited to: Ensuring compliance with ISO 9001. Leading our planned ISO 14001 implementation, from gap analysis to certification. Managing the Deviation Report Process, including following up on analysis and corrective and preventive actions. Planning and conducting internal audits and management reviews, and managing customer and supplier audits. Driving continuous improvement of our Management System, and publishing revisions of the Management System Manual. Preparing monthly compliance reports, and contributing to our Annual Compliance Report. Managing supplier contracts throughout their lifecycle, and keeping our Supplier CLM up to date. Holding recurring quality meetings and training to build awareness of quality, the environment and supplier governance across the company. What we’re looking for: Post-secondary degree within a relevant field. Previous experience with quality management or a similar role. Working knowledge of ISO 9001 and the principles of management systems. To thrive in this role, we believe that you have: A structured, detail-oriented approach, and you’re comfortable maintaining controlled documents. The ability to engage colleagues across the company in quality and continuous improvement. Clear and professional communication skills in English, both spoken and written, as English is our corporate language. Ideally but not required, you also have: Experience from the SaaS and/or aviation industries. Experience from auditing or contract management. Experience from work or studies in an international environment. A curiosity about environmental management and sustainability. Other information Start date: January/February Location: Malmö Work extent: Full-time, permanent As part of our recruitment process, we use the Predictive Index (PI) assessment to ensure a great fit between your skills and our company culture.
