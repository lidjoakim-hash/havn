Assignment description ﻿ We are looking for an experienced Automation Engineer to join exciting industrial and process automation projects. In this role, you will be responsible for programming, testing, commissioning, and optimizing automation solutions tailored to various production processes and customer requirements. ﻿ You will work closely with process engineers and other technical specialists to ensure seamless integration and full functionality of the delivered solutions. The role combines technical expertise with a strong understanding of process automation, enabling you to contribute throughout the project lifecycle. ﻿ As an Automation Engineer, you are familiar with our standards, processes, and our Plant Master, and you play a key role in delivering high-quality and sustainable automation solutions. Depending on project needs, work may be performed at our customer sites, or supplier locations. ﻿ Key Responsibilities Develop and program automation solutions for industrial processes Perform testing, commissioning, and troubleshooting activities Collaborate with process engineers to ensure optimal system performance Work according to company standards and best practices Support customers and project teams throughout implementation and start-up phases ﻿ This is an excellent opportunity for a proactive and solution-oriented engineer who enjoys working in dynamic project environments and contributing to innovative automation solutions. ﻿ This role requires fluency in English.
