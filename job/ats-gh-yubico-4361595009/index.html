We are publicly traded on Nasdaq Stockholm; our global footprint includes headquarters in Stockholm, Sweden, Silicon Valley, California; and Singapore. Why Join Our Mission? In everything we do, we live by our core values: • Battle Friction: We constantly look for new ways to simplify our products, our work, and the user experience. • Inspire Higher: We think big, champion the open internet, and strive to do the right thing even when it is difficult. • One Team: We are open, responsive, and collaborative, overcoming challenges and celebrating our successes together. Yubico is seeking the next new team member for our Firmware team. We're looking for someone with a passion for security to join us in our work to develop new features for our products. Your role will be to implement established cryptographic protocols, as well as to take part in creating the protocols of tomorrow. As part of our team you'll be working on our core products and the tools and libraries which make them useful. Use your passion, insights, and technical skills to help us create a trusted Internet. Tasks & Responsibilities: • Develop, design and test new features for the firmware of Yubico's products, such as the YubiKey and the YubiHSM. • Write libraries and utilities to expose new features to higher level applications. • Develop integrations with existing systems to enable them to take advantage of our products.
