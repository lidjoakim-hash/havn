Risk & Compliance Officer – Resilience & Security AB Tetra Pak Site Management safeguards the licence to operate and creates the conditions for Tetra Pak’s businesses in Sweden at all sites to operate safely, sustainably, efficiently and resiliently by providing local governance, compliance, infrastructure and cross-company coordination, as well as managing and coordinating relationships with relevant external authorities within these areas. Our team’s purpose is to enable decisions that protect, strengthen and create value for Tetra Pak. We connect people, priorities and decisions, simplify governance, strengthen compliance and contribute to business resilience. This is a permanent position based in Lund, Sweden. What you will do You will work across regulatory compliance, preparedness and security, driving assignments from assessment to implementation and follow-up. Depending on business priorities and developments in the external environment, you will: Independently monitor and assess regulatory developments, determine implications for Swedish operations together with legal and subject-matter experts, and recommend prioritised actions to management Lead implementation of relevant security and preparedness requirements, translating them into clear responsibilities, plans and controls, and following up on progress and gaps Drive the development and continuous improvement of crisis management frameworks, plans and exercises Lead and coordinate initiatives within business and civil preparedness, including Tetra Pak's role in the Swedish food system Represent Tetra Pak in selected external networks, standardisation activities and public-private partnerships Build relationships with authorities, industry organisations, academia and other relevant stakeholders Lead risk, compliance and preparedness workstreams in cross-functional projects, ensuring requirements become operational solutions with clear business ownership Provide senior management with risk assessments and decision material, including options, trade-offs and recommendations Challenge unnecessary complexity and support pragmatic, business-oriented governance A particular focus: Preparedness and Food Resilience An important and growing part of the role is to lead the development and coordination of Tetra Pak’s contribution to food resilience and civil preparedness in Sweden, translating strategic priorities into an actionable roadmap together with relevant business owners. This work connects business perspectives with government, industry, academia and wider society, and helps shape Tetra Pak’s role, priorities and milestones within food resilience initiatives.
