Build with us Businesses deserve better from finance. Less friction, more freedom. Since 2004, Mollie has been on a mission to make payments and money management effortless for every business in Europe. Today, more than 250,000 companies trust our all-in-one platform to get paid, manage money and grow on their terms. Simple, scalable and built with real businesses in mind. We're one of Europe's fastest-growing fintechs, with 900+ Mollies across 12+ locations. Our people make this growth possible. We move fast, build with purpose and care deeply about our customers. Whether you're solving problems, building market-leading products or exploring how AI can make work smarter, you'll have the freedom to create and the trust to deliver. If you're ready to grow, shape the future of fintech and join an ambitious, high-performing team, this is the place for you. Your impact As a Digital Agency Partner Manager based in Paris, you will play a key role in expanding Mollie’s commercial footprint across France. You will join our Regional Partnerships Southern Europe team, collaborating daily with local sales, marketing, and sales engineering colleagues in our 20–25 person French hub. This is a commercially driven, high-visibility role focused on a "one-to-many" growth approach. By building and nurturing relationships with e-commerce agencies, platform integrators, and funded startup communities, you will turn trusted advisors into a scalable engine for merchant acquisition and retention across France. What you’ll ship - Build and Scale the Agency Ecosystem: Identify, engage, and onboard new e-commerce agency partners, platform integrators, and startup communities across France aligned with Mollie’s commercial goals. - Drive Merchant Activation: Own a quarterly quota of merchant activations generated through your partner portfolio, executing shared activation plans and self-serve programs. - Enable Partner Success: Facilitate knowledge sharing, best practices, and co-marketing initiatives to empower agencies to champion Mollie’s payment solutions. - Optimize Through Data: Monitor agency performance through regular check-ins and data analysis to refine partnership strategies and maximize mutual value. - Represent Mollie in the Market: Meet with agencies in person, attend monthly industry networking events in Paris, and travel across French regions roughly once a month to strengthen local ties. - Collaborate Cross-Functionally: Support the broader local partnerships team with targeted outbound efforts and partner qualification, working closely with Sales, Marketing, Product, and Legal. What you’ll bring - 2–3 years of experience in Account Management, Partnerships Management, Sales, Program Management, or Digital Marketing within a tech environment. - Ecosystem & E-Commerce Knowledge: Solid familiarity with the e-commerce landscape, ERP solutions, or the French tech and startup community. - Indirect Sales Mindset: Comfort with "selling through others" and building long-term, trust-based relationships that drive commercial outcomes. - Analytical & Problem-Solving Skills: A numbers-driven approach to tracking partner performance, paired with strong communication skills and the ability to manage up effectively. - International & Remote Readiness: Fluency in French and strong English skills (roughly 80% of internal communication is in English), plus comfort working with a remotely based manager and traveling occasionally to our Amsterdam HQ. - Background (Nice to have): Experience at a CMS platform (such as WordPress/WooCommerce), French fintech or tech scale-up, ERP provider, or major e-commerce retailer/marketplace. Grow your way At Mollie, growth is personal. We believe everyone should have the chance to develop their skills, explore new challenges and shape their career on their own terms.
