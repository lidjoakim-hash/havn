Senior Partner Marketing Manager, EMEA Come join the team that’s building our partners’ growth engine. Right now, we are executing a critical strategic transition from a legacy direct-led approach to one made up of high-velocity, specialized ecosystem partners. As the Senior Regional Partner Marketing Manager, you are not here to distribute brochures, manage a portal graveyard, or chase passive deal registrations. You are an Ecosystem Growth Architect. Based in London and reporting directly to the Director of Global Partner Marketing (US-based), your mission is to treat co-marketing as an economic multiplier, laser-targeting premium MSSPs, security VARs, and GSIs across our priority regional territories while showing them how to become a high-margin services business using Fastly. What You'll Do: • Drive Pipeline Through/With Partners: Drive incremental, high-intent pipeline coverage across targeted EMEA market segments to directly address regional pipeline deficits, working closely with both Channel Account Managers and Territory Sales. • Activate the Partner Demand-gen Engine: Act as the regional demand-generation subject matter expert, architecting and launching end-to-end partner marketing campaigns that activate the ecosystem co-marketing flywheel. • Market the Services Revenue Multiplier: Demonstrate to partners how Fastly can serve as the engine for their own high-margin services business through GTM value proposition and high-impact joint marketing engagements. • Localize & Scale Channel Programs: Master our partner program framework to seamlessly adapt global marketing initiatives for the unique nuances of the EMEA market, while also originating net-new regional programs with the blueprint to scale globally. • Act as a High-Agency Owner: Bring a proactive, builder-marketer mindset to this foundational role, with the autonomy to shape our EMEA partner marketing framework and adapt strategies as our regional footprint grows. What We're Looking For Basic Qualifications: • 8+ years of B2B technology marketing experience, with a minimum of 4 years dedicated specifically to channel, distributor, or partner ecosystem marketing within the SaaS, cloud infrastructure, or cyber-security sectors. • Proven experience collaborating with specialized two-tier distribution frameworks or premier security VARs and GSIs across EMEA. • Demonstrated success building and executing co-marketing frameworks that drive measurable enterprise pipeline growth. • Strong written and verbal communication skills; based in London with a track record of successfully working across global, distributed cross-functional teams. • You have an interest in independently and proactively running multiple projects in a dynamic environment using superior project management skills • You have knowledge of specific regional considerations in EMEA, including knowledge about sub-regional differences and nuances, and can supply strategic input across both global and regional teams • Fluency in a second European language (particularly French) is highly advantageous. Work Location(s) & Travel Requirements This position is based out of Fastly’s London, UK office.
