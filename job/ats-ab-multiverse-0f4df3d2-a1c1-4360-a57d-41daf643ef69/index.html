Join Multiverse and power our mission to equip the workforce to win in the AI era. INTERNATIONAL LEGAL COUNSEL (UK & GERMANY) *German and England & Wales qualification to practice law required. The Role This is a newly created position within Multiverse’s Legal team, reporting directly to the General Counsel. As we expand our international footprint, this role is vital in establishing our in-house legal capability across both the UK and German markets. You will serve as the primary legal partner for our German operations—including StackFuel GmbH and future acquisitions—while providing commercial legal support to the UK business. We are looking for a collaborative professional who enjoys working across cultures and is excited to contribute to a growing, impact-driven organisation. What You’ll Do - German Operations: Act as the lead legal advisor for German group entities, providing guidance on commercial, employment, and corporate matters. - Contract Management: Lead the German commercial contracting lifecycle (B2B, B2C, and B2G), drafting and negotiating agreements that help the business scale safely. - Regulatory Guidance: Navigate the German regulatory landscape, including vocational training regulations (AZAV), providing practical advice to support business growth. - Strategic Integration: Support post-acquisition workstreams, including contract migrations, governance alignment, and compliance harmonisation for European M&A transactions. - Employment Law: Advise on German employment matters, including works council engagement and equity schemes. - External Partnerships: Manage relationships with external German counsel to ensure high-quality, cost-effective support. - UK & Cross-Border Support: Draft and negotiate UK commercial contracts (tech, data, and partnerships) and act as a strategic bridge between UK and German business functions. What We Are Looking For We recognise that excellence comes from a variety of backgrounds. If you meet the core requirements below and are passionate about the role, we encourage you to apply. Core Requirements: - Legal Qualification: Qualified to practice law in Germany. - Jurisdictional Experience: A strong understanding of the German legal market and experience working within the UK legal environment (e.g., in-house, international firm, or UK-based role). - Language Proficiency: Fluency in both German and English (written and spoken), with the ability to negotiate and draft legal documents in both languages. - Commercial Law Foundation: Experience managing a variety of commercial contracts and regulatory matters. Great to Have (but not required): - Experience in M&A transactions or post-closing integration. - Background in German employment law (works councils or incentive plans). - Prior experience in an in-house environment, particularly within the tech or edtech sectors. - Familiarity with UK commercial legal frameworks. Skills & Attributes: - Practical Judgement: The ability to provide clear, actionable advice to business
