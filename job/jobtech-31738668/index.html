🚀 We’re Hiring: Administrator – Prequalification & Ancillary Services Monitoring Company: Hykmann Global Business Solutions AB Role: Administrator – Prequalification and Follow-up Seniority: Intermediate – Competence Level 2 Location: Sundbyberg, Sweden Work Model: Hybrid – up to 50% remote Assignment Period: 15 February 2027 – 15 February 2029 Extension Option: Up to 2 additional years (1 + 1 year), potentially until 15 February 2031 Workload: Full-time (100%) Application Deadline: 23 November 2026, 23:30 CET Security Requirement: Security clearance and background/register check are required. Application Language: Swedish About the Role Hykmann Global Business Solutions AB is looking for an experienced Administrator specialising in prequalification processes and follow-up of ancillary service deliveries. In this role, you will support administrative processes related to the prequalification of frequency regulation reserves and the monitoring of ancillary service deliveries. You will work closely with internal teams, service providers and other stakeholders to ensure that administrative activities, documentation, registrations and deadlines are handled accurately and efficiently. The role also involves supporting the development of administrative processes in response to new regulatory requirements, market roles and changes to existing operational procedures. Key Responsibilities Support the unit manager with planning, scheduling and meeting coordination. Coordinate meeting agendas, prepare meeting documentation and take minutes. Manage and coordinate administrative email communication with service providers, including test requests and follow-up of ancillary service deliveries. Review incoming prequalification applications to ensure that the required documentation has been submitted. Coordinate case registration and request case numbers for prequalification matters in the official records system. Perform administrative activities related to prequalification and follow-up processes for ancillary service deliveries. Support the improvement and development of administrative processes and routines. Handle administrative tasks related to the implementation of Article 182 of the System Operation Guideline (SO GL) in the prequalification process. Register and document information in a dedicated administration system and monitor important deadlines. Administratively prepare prequalification cases for the handling and disclosure of public records. Support administrative activities related to the introduction of new market roles, including BRP/BSP. Undertake other related administrative tasks as required. Required Profile We are looking for a structured and dependable administrator who can manage several parallel activities and work independently while collaborating effectively with colleagues and external stakeholders. The ideal candidate should demonstrate: Professional experience in administration, coordination or process support. Strong organisational and planning skills. Experience coordinating meetings, agendas, minutes and documentation. Ability to review documents and ensure that submissions are complete and accurate. Experience handling case registrations, records and administrative follow-up. Good written communication and professional email-handling skills. Ability to monitor deadlines and manage multiple ongoing administrative matters. A proactive approach to improving administrative routines and processes. High attention to detail, reliability and a service-oriented mindset. Meritorious Experience Experience in one or more of the following areas is an advantage: Prequalification or qualification processes. Energy markets, electricity systems or frequency regulation. Ancillary services and monitoring of service deliveries. Regulatory or public-sector administration. Handling official records and public documentation. Work involving SO GL regulatory requirements. Familiarity with BRP/BSP market roles. Administration syste
