About Flexport: At Flexport, we believe global trade can move the human race forward. That’s why it’s our mission to make global commerce so easy there will be more of it. We’re shaping the future of a $10T industry with solutions powered by innovative technology and exceptional people. Today, companies of all sizes—from emerging brands to Fortune 500s—use Flexport technology to move more than $19B of merchandise across 112 countries a year. The recent global supply chain crisis has put Flexport center stage as we continue to play a pivotal role in how goods move around the world. We are proud to have the support of the best investors in the game who believe in our mission, solutions and people. Ready to tackle global challenges that impact business, society, and the environment? Come join us. Why Climate Matters More Than Ever Supply chain leaders are being asked to cut costs and carbon at the same time. Yet logistics and sustainability teams often work with different data, tools, and priorities. Meanwhile, regulations and industry standards are raising expectations for how companies measure and manage their emissions. Most climate tools stop at reporting: a dashboard or a year-end PDF, produced long after a shipment has moved. They rarely reach the people deciding how to route goods, when to ship, or how much inventory to hold. The Climate team at Flexport is building emissions intelligence directly into the platform where supply chain decisions are made. We help customers weigh cost, speed, and emissions together—and turn that understanding into action. The Opportunity Flexport already runs the shipments, holds the data, and provides the platform where customers manage their supply chains. That puts us in a strong position to connect climate insights with the operational decisions that drive emissions. You will be the primary engineer for Climate, working independently of our existing engineering teams and partnering closely with the Climate Manager, your main day-to-day business stakeholder. Think of it as a small startup within Flexport: direct access to the business, a broad scope, and substantial ownership. Together with the Climate Manager, you will identify the most valuable problems to solve, shape the product direction, and turn ideas into reliable software. This role calls for someone who enjoys both deciding what to build and building it. Things We ❤️ • Turning complex shipment and emissions data into clear, actionable choices. • Working directly with stakeholders and users to understand what would make their lives better. • Building simple, reliable products and learning from how people use them. • Finding opportunities where lower costs and lower emissions go together. • Taking an idea from an ambiguous problem to a useful product in production. You Will • Work closely with Climate Programs Manager to understand customer needs, challenge assumptions, and translate business goals into a practical product roadmap. • Own delivery from problem discovery and technical design through implementation, launch, operation, and iteration. • Build emissions intelligence into everyday supply chain decisions, including routing, transport mode, shipment timing, and inventory planning. • Help customers identify opportunities to reduce emissions using Flexport’s shipment data. • Make lower-emission choices easier to understand and act on within existing workflows. • Develop products that help customers understand the wider environmental footprint of their supply chains, beyond freight. • Deliver useful improvements incrementally, balancing speed, scope, maintainability, and data accuracy. • Coordinate with other engineering teams when your work depends on shared systems, integrations, or expertise. • Measure whether what you ship solves the underlying problem, and use that evidence to decide what comes next.
