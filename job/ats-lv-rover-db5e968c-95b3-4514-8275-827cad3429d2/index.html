Meet the Team: As the Backend Platform and Support team, we focus on building and managing the internal platforms and core technology that drive the Rover application. Through these efforts, we empower our engineering teams to deliver a robust, stable, and reliable experience for both owners and sitters. We are looking for a developer who is passionate about datastores and invested in improving the reliability and scalability of our databases!   This is a hybrid position that has the minimum expectation to work out of our Barcelona Office in the Poblenou neighborhood two days per week on Mondays and Thursdays.
