Elastic, the Search AI Company, enables everyone to find the answers they need in real time, using all their data, at scale — unleashing the potential of businesses and people. The Elastic Search AI Platform, used by more than 50% of the Fortune 500, brings together the precision of search and the intelligence of AI to enable everyone to accelerate the results that matter. By taking advantage of all structured and unstructured data — securing and protecting private information more effectively — Elastic’s complete, cloud-based solutions for search, security, and observability help organizations deliver on the promise of AI. What Is The RoleElastic, the Search AI company, is looking for a Regional Vice President to drive net-new revenue and expansion, working across a mix of Enterprise and high end Mid Marker Accounts (80% greenfield - 20% customers) across the Benelux. You’ll be based in the Netherlands, to turn a transactional Benelux enterprise business into a transformational, services-led one. What You Will Be Doing • Own the enterprise strategy, pipeline and forecast for the Benelux, and the targets that come with it. • Lead and coach the enterprise account executives, and move them from transactional to value-based selling. • Build and run account plans for named greenfield targets, with an executive engagement plan per account. • Get access at senior levels personally and sponsor Elastic's executive relationships in key accounts. • Work with Professional Services, solutions architects, partners and system integrators to shape and deliver outcomes. • Work with marketing on executive events and programmes that create enterprise demand. • Raise the quality of forecasting, deal reviews and account planning, using MEDDPICC or an equivalent. • Recruit, develop and, where needed, make hard people decisions. The mission: land, go high, go wide • Land with a concrete use case tied to a business objective and a strategic IT or AI initiative. • Deliver the outcome with Elastic Professional Services, so the customer succeeds through its transformation. • Go high in the management chain, building executive sponsorship at CIO, CTO, CDO and CISO level. • Go wide until search, observability and security meet in one strategic relationship. What You Bring • A track record of landing and expanding strategic enterprise accounts in the Benelux, with a personal network at senior IT and business level. • Experience selling outcomes tied to business strategy and AI, not features or licences. • Experience selling a platform across several buying centres, ideally with a services-led motion. • Experience leading sales teams and changing how they sell. • Experience working with partners and system integrators as co-sellers. • Credibility with technical and executive audiences, and fluent Dutch and English. Bonus Points • Knowledge of search, observability or security. • Experience at a challenger vendor that built enterprise presence against established competitors. • Experience with Dutch financial services, high tech or public sector.
