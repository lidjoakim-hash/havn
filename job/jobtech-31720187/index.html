Hello Software Developer! Inter IKEA Group connects IKEA franchisees with range development and suppliers and ensures alignment of the overall IKEA strategic direction. Every day, we strive to develop, produce, and sell products and services that are affordable and accessible to everyone. This role is positioned in Retail Concept Operations, Digital Engineering and Technology Platform Management. You will belong to the Commercial Intelligence team, reporting to the Technology Manager. In Retail Concept Operations, as part of Retail Concept, we protect, develop, and enable the IKEA Concept across markets and franchisees. We turn the "what" of the IKEA Concept into the "how" of everyday retail operations, making it easy to use, scale, and trust across the value chain. We take pride in working in progressive ways of working, with empowered teams and strong digital competence. We focus on simple, practical solutions that strengthen both short- and long-term low cost, while supporting consistency and quality in how the IKEA Concept comes to life. Together, we build ways of working where teams take ownership, collaborate closely, and continuously improve, so we can create meaningful value for IKEA, our franchisees, co-workers, and the many people. Digital Engineering and Technology Platform Management is where we build, run and continuously improve the digital products and platforms that power the IKEA franchise offer. We bring together software engineering, platform engineering and quality engineering competence to create optimal preconditions for the business and people who design, develop and manage IKEA's offer across a connected and optimised value chain — all to deliver growth and enable low cost. We are fully integrated into the development process, providing competence, tools, solutions, processes and ways of working across many different areas. We work in cross-functional, agile teams that value ownership, collaboration and continuous improvement, and we're never done exploring better tools, technologies and practices to build a stronger digital foundation for IKEA's digital products and business. Our team is based in Malmö, Sweden. We believe that your presence with the team and working the majority of the time in the workplace is important. About the role As a Software Developer, you'll play an important part in the team and bring valuable IKEA knowledge into a new setting. In the coming years, we will continue to build the digital backbone that connects our range, our data and our customer experience — and semantic technologies are at the heart of how we make that connection meaningful. Your primary focus will be continuing to grow our knowledge graph and data-driven capabilities. We hope that you are curious about ontologies, data modelling and building software that helps machines and people understand IKEA's world a little better. This is an entry-level software engineering role, assigned to a technology domain or digital product. You'll work on one or several product components — spanning front-end, back-end, mobile, data modelling, data ingestion pipelines, or embedded systems depending on your area. You'll be part of a team that values quality, reuse, and learning from each other. You'll plan your own work, keep the backlog up to date, and contribute both to day-to-day delivery and to continuously improving how the team works. The impact of our work continues to resonate in homes and hearts across the world. On a typical working day, you will: Develop and maintain software solutions within your assigned technology domain, platform, or product area. Deliver tested, maintainable, and deployable code that supports business outcomes while adhering to architectural principles, security requirements, and engineering best practices.
