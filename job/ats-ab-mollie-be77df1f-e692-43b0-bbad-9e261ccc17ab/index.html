Your impact As a Sales Engineer at Mollie, you sit at the intersection of technology and business, supporting our sales team through the pre-sales phase with solutioning, demos, and technical presentations. This role sits within a newly formed professional services structure, separate from the commercial organisation. That means real autonomy in how you work, dedicated leadership focused on craft and efficiency, and a direct line to product teams. You will own the full customer lifecycle from initial discovery through to deal closure, going deep on complex API integrations and custom solutions rather than off-the-shelf implementations. You are joining at a pivotal moment. Mollie is growing fast, moving upmarket into more complex, longer deal cycles, and building out Sales Engineering as a true centre of excellence. The work you do here will directly shape how we win in one of our expansion markets. What you'll ship - Custom technical demos and solution pitches tailored to specific customer problems, built using tools like Postman and AI to bring Mollie's API capabilities to life in a compelling and relevant way -
