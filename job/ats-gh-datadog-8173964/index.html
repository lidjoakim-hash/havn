The Team Our GTM Enablement team plays a critical role in helping Datadog's Sales, SDR, and Customer Success teams succeed by equipping them with the right skills, knowledge, and tools at the right time. We partner closely with teams across Sales, Customer Success, Product Marketing, Curriculum, and Operations to ensure enablement programs translate into real performance in the field. If you like adding value and seeing the direct impact of your work through the people you lead, and you thrive in dynamic environments where you can roll up your sleeves and get things done, this role is for you. The Opportunity Datadog is investing further in EMEA, and we're looking for a Manager, Field Enablement to lead our team of Field Enablement Managers supporting Sales, SDR, and Customer Success across the region — a frontline people leadership role where you'll manage, coach, and grow your team while helping execute the enablement strategy and quality bar for EMEA. You'll partner closely with EMEA Sales, SDR, and Customer Success leadership, as well as Enablement Curriculum, Program Management, and Operations teams, to translate regional priorities into a coordinated enablement plan across markets and segments — keeping your team's programs tightly aligned with our global Field Enablement standards. This role is ideal for someone who has excelled in field/sales enablement and leadership, cares as much about developing enablement practitioners as developing sellers and CSMs, and wants to help build the enablement leadership layer for one of Datadog's fastest-growing regions. What You'll Do • Spearhead the Field Sales Enablement team’s transition from facilitators/trainers to data-driven business partners and coaches who are able to proactively identify GTM knowledge/performance gaps and deliver targeted enablement solutions that drive behavior change • Manage, coach, and develop your team of Field Enablement Managers — regular 1:1s, goal-setting, performance feedback, and career growth plans calibrated against the Field Enablement career path. • Own workforce planning for the EMEA team, including recruiting, interviewing, and onboarding new Field Enablement Managers as headcount and market coverage scale. • Set the enablement strategy and priorities for EMEA in partnership with Sales, SDR, and Customer Success leadership, translating regional business goals into a coordinated plan across your team. • Establish and maintain a consistent quality bar for delivery — facilitation standards, coaching rigor, program effectiveness, and AI fluency — through practice sessions, calibration exercises, and delivery reviews. • Serve as the strategic partner and escalation point for EMEA Sales, SDR, and CS leaders, coaching your team to turn field listening into insight that shapes enablement priorities and regional recommendations. • Keep EMEA aligned with the global Field Enablement organization: partner with the Director and other regional leaders on standards and certifications, represent EMEA in global forums, and report team impact and outcomes to stakeholders.
