In this role, you will take responsibility for establishing and maintaining effective documentation processes, coordinating deliverables and ensuring that project documentation meets contractual requirements, quality standards and agreed deadlines. You will act as the central point of contact for documentation-related matters, working closely with project teams, customers and suppliers. The position requires strong organisational and coordination skills, attention to detail and the ability to manage multiple documentation activities throughout the entire project lifecycle. Key Responsibilities Project Documentation Management Establish, implement and maintain project documentation processes, procedures and routines. Coordinate documentation deliverables throughout the project lifecycle. Ensure that project documentation complies with contractual requirements, quality standards and agreed deadlines. Maintain structured and up-to-date project documentation. Manage document registers, document lists and tracking records. Monitor documentation progress and identify delays or potential delivery risks. Document Control & Coordination Act as the main contact for documentation-related matters towards project teams, customers and suppliers. Coordinate document preparation, submission, review and approval processes. Follow up on outstanding documentation and ensure that required deliverables are completed on time. Review documentation requirements and identify gaps, dependencies and potential risks. Coordinate document reviews and approval workflows. Support cost estimations related to documentation requirements and activities. Reporting & Project Follow-up Prepare regular documentation progress reports. Monitor documentation status against project plans and contractual milestones. Communicate progress, issues and risks to relevant stakeholders. Ensure proper coordination between technical teams, suppliers and project management. Project Closure & Handover Compile and organise final project documentation. Ensure that all required documents are complete, reviewed and approved. Coordinate structured documentation handover at project completion. Ensure that documentation is properly organised and accessible for future reference. Process Improvement Identify opportunities to improve documentation workflows and working methods. Contribute to standardisation of document management processes. Support effective use of document management systems and databases. Promote consistency, quality and traceability across project documentation activities. Required Qualifications & Experience Professional Experience At least 3 years of relevant professional experience in project documentation, document control or project coordination. Experience working in technical, engineering or complex project environments. Demonstrated organisational, coordination and project management capabilities. Experience coordinating documentation activities involving multiple stakeholders. Document Management & IT Skills Good general computer skills and confidence working in digital environments.
