Who we are We simplify wealth creation. Founded in 2014 in Vienna, Austria by Eric Demuth, Paul Klanschek and Christian Trummer, we’re here to help people trust themselves enough to build their financial freedom — for now and the future. Our user-friendly, trade-everything platform empowers both first-time investors and seasoned experts to invest in the cryptocurrencies, crypto indices, stocks*, precious metals and commodities* they want — with any sized budget, 24/7. Our global team works across different cultures and time zones, bringing our products to more than 7 million customers, making us one of Europe’s safest and most secure platforms that powers modern investing. Headquartered in Austria but operating across Europe, our products are built by fast-moving, talented, “roll-up-your-sleeves-and-make-it-happen” kind of people. It’s these diverse perspectives and innovative minds operating as ONE TEAM that keep Bitpanda at the cutting edge of our industry. So if you’re someone who thinks big, moves fast and wants to make an impact right from day one, then get ready to join our industry-changing team. Let’s go! Your mission At Bitpanda, resilience is key to keeping our business running, even when things go wrong. As a Resilience Manager, you'll help strengthen our Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery capabilities across the organization. You'll work closely with operational teams, drive improvements, and make sure we're prepared to respond to disruptions and recover effectively. This is a hands-on role where you'll take ownership, connect the dots across teams, and turn plans into practical solutions. What you’ll do • Drive and continuously improve the Business Continuity Management (BCM) framework, maintaining reliable data, documentation, and reporting. • Work hands-on with cross-functional teams to develop, maintain, and test Business Continuity Plans (BCPs) and support Disaster Recovery planning. • Coordinate Business Impact Assessments (BIAs) to help teams identify critical processes, dependencies, and recovery requirements. • Actively participate in crisis management activities, simulations, and exercises to improve organizational readiness. • Collaborate with stakeholders, including risk management and auditors, to address requirements and drive resilience improvements. Who you are • You have 4+ years of experience in Business Continuity Management, operational resilience, IT operations, risk management, or a related field. • You're a hands-on problem solver who takes ownership, challenge existing approaches when needed, and drives topics forward independently. • You understand how business processes, technology, people, and external dependencies fit together, and are comfortable working with different teams and stakeholders. • You have an analytical, data-driven mindset and enjoy bringing structure to complex topics. • Experience with BCM, Disaster Recovery, crisis management, or regulatory frameworks such as DORA is a strong plus. What’s in it for you • Flexibility to work where you thrive – Enjoy the freedom of our Hybrid working model, combining onsite collaboration and remote work, with an additional 25 days per year to work from a city or country of your choice. • Reward for your impact – Receive a competitive total compensation package aligned with Bitpanda’s pay-for-impact policy, including participation in our stock option plan. • Support for your mental wellbeing – Access confidential coaching, counselling, and mental health resources whenever you need them through OpenUP. • Time to recharge – On top of your standard annual leave, prioritise your wellbeing in 2027 with 3 additional days of paid time off to rest, reset, and recharge, available once you’ve been with us for 6 months. • Continuous learning and growth – Grow your skills and stay ahead in your career with unlimited access to Udemy’s library of online courses at your own pace. • Exclusive perks and rewards – Enjoy
