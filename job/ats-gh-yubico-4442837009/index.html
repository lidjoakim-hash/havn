About the role Yubico is looking for an Operations Associate to join our Stockholm Operations team. In this role, you will support the day-to-day execution of our fulfilment, inventory and operational activities, helping ensure that customer orders are prepared accurately, efficiently and to the expected quality standard. This is a hands-on role where attention to detail, reliability and consistency are important. You will work closely with the Operations team across inventory handling, quality inspection, order fulfilment and related operational activities.You will report to Operations Supervisor or Manager. The role is based at our Stockholm site and requires a 5 day/ week full day on-site presence. Tasks and Responsibilities Fulfilment & Operations • Perform operational activities related to order fulfilment, including preparation, inspection and handling of outgoing products. • Support daily production and fulfilment requirements to ensure customer orders are completed accurately and on time. • Perform repetitive operational tasks with a high level of precision, consistency and attention to detail. • Support other areas of Operations when required to meet daily workload and production requirements. Inventory Management • Receive, count, inspect and record incoming inventory. • Maintain accurate inventory usage and support alignment between physical inventory and system records. • Support inventory-control activities and report discrepancies or issues when identified. • Handle products and materials according to defined operational and security requirements.
