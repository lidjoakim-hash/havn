CRM Manager- Consumer Team description SumUp's Consumer Bank is building the next generation of an everyday financial product. The Consumer CRM team turns every new sign-up into an active, engaged customer by designing the journeys that guide people from onboarding through to their first transaction and beyond. As the business enters a major growth phase in 2027, you'll take ownership of this activation funnel, build the lifecycle comms that bring each new launch to life, and have a direct hand in lifting one of our most important conversion metrics. What you'll do • Build and launch event driven activation journeys that guide new customers through sign up, KYC, first fund and first transaction, across push, email, in app, WhatsApp and SMS • Write clear, on-brand campaign copy for every channel, from push notifications to email, supported by AI agents built for the job • Build audience segments, track daily performance and run A/B tests on copy, timing and channel • Partner with Product and GTM teams across six markets to ensure every new launch has its lifecycle communications ready for launch and tailored to each market.
