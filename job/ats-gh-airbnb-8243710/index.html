Airbnb was born in 2007 when two hosts welcomed three guests to their San Francisco home, and has since grown to over 5 million hosts who have welcomed over 2 billion guest arrivals in almost every country across the globe. Every day, hosts offer unique stays and experiences that make it possible for guests to connect with communities in a more authentic way. Every year, millions of guests and Hosts across EMEA and LATAM reach out to us at moments that matter, a booking that fell through, a stay that didn't go to plan, a question that needs a clear answer in their own language. How we show up in those moments decides whether they come back. We're looking for a senior operations leader to own that outcome. You'll lead a network of outsourced BPO partners delivering customer support across one of Airbnb's most complex regions, and you'll be accountable for the quality our community actually experiences, measured above all by NPS. This is a performance leadership role, not an administrative one. You'll build the frameworks, insight loops and performance culture that genuinely move partner performance, and you'll lead a team of Partner Operations Managers who make that happen across every language, market and site.
