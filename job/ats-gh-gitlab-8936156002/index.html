An overview of this role As a Strategic Account Executive, you’ll own a portfolio of strategic and large enterprise customers and prospects in Spain, helping them realize the full value of GitLab’s AI-powered DevSecOps platform. You’ll solve real business problems for senior stakeholders by breaking into net new enterprise accounts and expanding adoption within existing customers, using a consultative sales approach to connect their priorities with GitLab’s DevSecOps and AI capabilities. You’ll lead full-cycle, complex deals, collaborate closely with pre- and post-sales teams and strategic channel partners, and act as the voice of your customers by sharing their feedback and product ideas through GitLab’s public issue tracker. In your first year, you’ll be expected to build and execute focused account plans, establish a healthy, qualified pipeline, and drive successful evaluations and rollouts that set the foundation for long-term growth across your territory.
