JOIN THE TEAM BUILDING AN AI-FIRST COMPANY. Hostinger serves more than 5.5 million clients across 150 countries, but we're still excited about what we're creating next. AI is changing how we build products, support customers, work together, and solve problems. It helps us move faster, spend less time on repetitive work, and focus on bigger ideas. If you want your work to influence both what we build and how we build it, we'd like to meet you. Our culture: Guided by 10 company principles https://www.hostinger.com/principles.   YOUR ROLE AT HOSTINGER Join Hostinger as a Social Media Manager and take ownership of how we show up across Instagram, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, and Facebook. You'll shape and execute our social media strategy, bringing Hostinger's products, people, and stories to life through engaging, creative content. This is a role for someone who combines strategic thinking with hands-on creativity. From developing content ideas and writing scripts to creating short-form videos, experimenting with new formats, and analyzing performance, you'll own the process from idea to execution. We're looking for someone who is comfortable taking initiative, thrives in an evolving environment, and knows how to turn great content into measurable impact. Curious to learn more? Meet your future Team Lead: Gabrielė Račaitytė-Krasauskė, Global PR Team Lead https://www.linkedin.com/in/gabriele-racaityte/ YOUR DAY-TO-DAY - Build and own Hostinger’s social media strategy across Instagram, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, and Facebook, aligned with brand awareness and company positioning goals. - Plan and run a platform-native content calendar that brings Hostinger’s products, people, and stories to life. - Lead short-form video content, including Reels and TikToks, owning concepts and scripts, coordinating freelance videographers and editors for production, and shooting simple content in-house when speed matters. - Write clear, on-brand copy for posts, captions, and video scripts, adapting tone and format to each platform. - Translate product updates, company news, and industry insights into accessible, educational content that resonates with our audiences. - Partner closely with key stakeholders to source and amplify stories: PR & Communications (company news and media moments), Product and Product Marketing (launches and updates), Brand & Design (visual identity and assets), People & Employer Branding (culture and team stories), and Leadership (executive presence on social). - Monitor performance using reach, engagement, and audience insights to continuously improve content and distribution. - Experiment with new formats, trends, and creative approaches to grow Hostinger’s visibility. - Establish scalable workflows and processes, owning social media initiatives from idea to execution. WHAT WILL HELP YOU SUCCEED - 5+ years of experience in social media management, content strategy, or brand communications, ideally managing social media for an international brand. - Proven track record of developing and executing social media strategies that drive measurable growth in reach, engagement, and brand awareness. - Strong understanding of Instagram, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, and Facebook, including platform-specific content formats, audience behavior, and best practices. - Hands-on experience creating engaging social media content, including short-form video production, filming, and storytelling. - Ability to translate complex product information and business topics into clear, creative, and engaging content. - A strategic yet hands-on mindset, with the ability to independently turn ideas into action and measure their impact. - Comfortable working in ambiguity, building processes from scratch, and proactively identifying opportunities without waiting for direction. - Curiosity about AI and emerging social media trends, with a willingness to experiment with new tools and approaches. - Excellent written
