ABOUT TIDE At Tide, we help SMEs save time and money in the running of their businesses by not only offering business accounts and related banking services, but also a comprehensive set of highly usable and connected administrative solutions, from invoicing to accounting. Tide is transforming the small business banking market and now supports over 2 million members globally across the UK, India, Germany and France. Using advanced technology, all solutions are designed with SMEs in mind. With quick onboarding, low fees and innovative features, we thrive on making data driven decisions to serve our mission: to help SMEs save time and money so they can get back to doing what they love. You will own Membership Plans and the wider Subscriptions platform across multiple markets, shaping how we structure, price, and deliver value to our members as Tide scales. This role is central to defining the future of our subscription offering, ensuring it is flexible, competitive, and aligned to member needs. Working closely with cross-functional teams, you will optimise the end-to-end subscription experience. This includes defining and refining membership tiers, benefits, and pricing models, while ensuring seamless lifecycle management—from sign-up and upgrades through to renewals, downgrades, and cancellations. You will collaborate closely with Billing, Payments, Marketing, Data, Finance, Engineering, and Design to deliver a frictionless, transparent, and engaging subscription journey.
