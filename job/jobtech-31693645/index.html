Project allocation will depend on your experience, technical strengths, and areas of expertise. Work with AWS-based data and analytics environments. Contribute to initiatives involving Advanced Analytics, Analytical AI, Generative AI, and agentic solutions. Identify appropriate tools, techniques, and approaches to address different analytical challenges. Collaborate with Data Scientists and other technical specialists across project teams. Engage directly with business stakeholders to understand requirements and identify opportunities for data-driven improvements. Communicate analytical findings and technical solutions clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences. Contribute to operational practices and the continuous improvement of data science solutions. Required Qualifications Strong professional experience in Data Science or a related technical discipline. Excellent programming skills in Python and PySpark. Practical experience operationalising Data Science solutions in Databricks. Experience working with AWS cloud environments. Broad and up-to-date knowledge of Data Science methods, techniques, and tools. Strong analytical, exploratory, and problem-solving capabilities. Excellent communication and collaboration skills. Ability to work effectively with multidisciplinary teams and business stakeholders.
