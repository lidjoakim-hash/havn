Team mission The Care team at GetYourGuide exists to make every interaction with our platform - for travellers, suppliers, and partners - feel effortless, trusted, and human. We sit at the intersection of customer experience, operational excellence, and business strategy, and we play a critical role in ensuring that the people who power our marketplace can thrive on it. From how we design our support channels to how we measure and improve satisfaction, Care is a strategic function that directly shapes GetYourGuide's reputation and growth. Within Care, the Care Experience & Strategy team drives the thinking and direction behind how we support our stakeholders at scale. We translate company strategy into Care roadmaps, lead cross-functional initiatives that improve the experience of travellers, suppliers, and partners, and ensure that data and insight - not assumption - guide every decision we make. As Senior Strategy Lead for Partner Support & Success, you will be a senior individual contributor, owning the strategic agenda for how Care serves and grows our supplier and partner relationships, with direct impact on stakeholder satisfaction and GMV.
