Political Economic Assistant

Amerikanska Ambassaden · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 310 000–380 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 29.11.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

The Political Economic Assistant plays a key role in supporting the U.S. Embassy’s Political Economic Section by monitoring and analyzing political developments in Sweden, with a focus on domestic politics, migration and integration, counterterrorism and human rights. Responsibilities include conducting independent research, preparing analytical reports and briefings, and providing policy recommendations. The position also involves building and maintaining contacts with Swedish government officials, Parliament, civil society, and other key stakeholders, as well as coordinating official visits and supporting high-level meetings and events. The Political Economic Assistant contributes to advancing U.S. interests in Sweden and strengthening U.S.-Swedish relations. Education Requirements: A University degree in political science, Economics, Political Economics (economic policy), International Relations. Experience: At least 2 years of experience in political science, international affairs, economic policy or public policy with a strong emphasis on research and analysis. Language: English: Fluent speaking/reading/writing, may include the ability to translate. Swedish: Fluent speaking/reading/writing, may include the ability to translate.
