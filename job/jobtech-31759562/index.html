Software Developer to Westermo

Westermo Network Technologies AB · Västerås, Sweden

Are you a an experienced C-programmer? Are you a Linux enthusiast? Join the Westermo Software Team as a Software developer! Westermo provides networking solutions for some of the most demanding applications imaginable, from onboard trains to the bottom of the ocean floor. To deliver that kind of reliability and level of security, we build our own operating system WeOS on one of the most battle hardened technologies in existence; the Linux kernel. We are now looking for an experienced developer to join our team and help us level up! Are you our new WeOS Developer? As a Software developer for WeOS, you will work in teams in order to develop and maintain our software. As many others, we work in an agile way and have created our own version of agile work. We like the phrase "sharing is caring", and to us that means helping each other out, both asking for and offering help on a daily basis. We strive to use Open Source components whenever possible and to act as responsible members of those communities by contributing back the changes and improvements we make. Besides the work with development of WeOS, we expect you to contribute to improvements in our ways of working. If you like to develop new solutions, solve problems and develop for the future - then Westermo is the place for you to grow. We work in a flat organization with small teams and are focusing on develop the most important deliverables first, with the right quality. You get to work with a really competent and caring team. There's a strong sense of "we", and we like to have fun together at work! So, who are we looking for? We are looking for someone who's a team player, likes to learn and teach others what you already know. Someone who is creative and curious about technology. You are well experienced in C programming in a Linux environment, especially in embedded systems, and you know a bit or two about computer networks. Requirements: Strong C programming skills Strong Linux knowledge and hands-on experience Knowledge and interest in Embedded Systems Good understanding of computer networks and networking principles Fluent in English, and at least moderate in Swedish Meritorious: Experience with secure software development lifecycle (SSDLC) practices Experience using AI-assisted tools in software development Experience developing automated tests in Python Experience working with Git In return, we offer: You will be working at the forefront of technology in a friendly, open culture where we you get the opportunity to learn from skilled colleagues every day. A mature and caring leadership in a flat organization. Through our collective bargain agreement you will be covered in terms of insurance, pension and other benefits. We like to have fun together and we regularly enjoy sports and other social activities as a team. We believe that magic happens when people meet. We are a learning organization and we truly love to try new things, think outside the box and be innovative together! Basic information: Location: Kopparlunden, Västerås Job type: Full time Employment contract: Permanent Don’t miss out on this opportunity – apply today. The very robust and secure communication networks built with our products provide a high value for our customers. We are focused on leveraging growth opportunities in our market and are devoted to delivering the world's best industrial networks.
