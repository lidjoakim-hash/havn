We are seeking an experienced Package Buyer

Folk AS · Lysaker, Norway

💰 ca. 510 000–610 000 kr/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Kontraktvia NAVFrist 23.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

We are looking for an experienced Package Buyer for a long-term assignment with one of our clients.   Accountabilities: • Be part of the project procurement team from FEED and EPC phases through project delivery. • Establish and execute sourcing strategies for technically complex procurement packages in line with the overall project procurement strategy. • Ensure procurement activities and deliveries meet requirements related to cost, HSSE, quality and schedule. • Prepare and manage RFQs, clarifications, commercial evaluations, negotiations and contract awards. • Follow up suppliers throughout execution, including variations, claims, progress, documentation and commercial matters. • Cooperate closely with suppliers to identify commercially and technically sound solutions. • Work closely with engineering, legal and compliance, construction, HSSEQ and other relevant project functions. • Identify and manage commercial, contractual, schedule and cost risks related to assigned packages. • Ensure effective contract administration and close-out of procurement packages. Skills required: • Strong sourcing and contract management experience within technically complex procurement packages. • Experience preparing RFQs and managing clarifications, negotiations, contract administration, variations and claims. • Experience working in an EPC project environment. • Ability to work closely with engineering/PRE functions and understand how technical decisions affect schedule, cost and risk. • Solid contractual understanding and experience reviewing and negotiating commercial and contractual documentation. • Experience with change management and project reporting. • Broad understanding of EPC project execution, including technical, commercial and planning aspects. • Knowledge of SAP Procurement/S4H. • Experience with Ariba Contract Management and VIM is an advantage. • Strong negotiation and stakeholder management skills. • Ability to work independently and drive assigned procurement packages forward. Qualifications: • Minimum 5 years of relevant experience within procurement and sourcing. • Preferably a technical educational background due to the technical nature of the procurement packages. • Relevant technical, commercial or administrative education and/or experience within package procurement may also be considered. • Experience from project-based industries and technically complex procurement environments. • Good digital competence, including MS Office and relevant procurement systems. • Good English communication skills, both written and verbal. Personal Skills: • Diligent, structured and able to maintain momentum in a busy project environment. • Flexible and comfortable handling changing priorities. • Determined and able to drive discussions and progress within assigned packages. • Strong communication skills and confident when working with internal and external stakeholders. • Commercially minded and comfortable in negotiations. • Independent, proactive and solution-oriented. • Able to balance technical, commercial and contractual considerations.   Please upload diploma and transcripts with your application. Folk can offer: • Good long-term opportunities with our clients • Individual and adapted follow-up while on assignment • Good career development opportunities in an interesting and innovative sector • Competitive terms and conditions • Social events and pleasant tokens of appreciation throughout the year We see possibilities in your competence!   In Folk, we work in accordance with our values: ethical, personal, enthusiastic and flexible.
