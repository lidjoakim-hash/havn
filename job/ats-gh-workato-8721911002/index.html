About Workato Workato is the leading Control and Execution Platform for Enterprise AI — the neutral platform enterprises trust to put AI to work across their business. Workato unifies data, applications, and processes into a single platform so AI can reliably orchestrate business processes in production at enterprise scale. Built on more than a decade of running mission-critical processes for over half the Fortune 500 — including Nasdaq, Amazon, Cisco, Vodafone, Atlassian, and Lucid Motors — Workato turns over 14,000 enterprise systems AI needs to act on into one governed execution layer. We are driven by innovation and looking for team players who want to actively build our company. But, we also believe in balancing productivity with self-care. This is a high-ownership role for someone who thrives in ambiguity: you turn unclear requirements into clear execution plans, make confident decisions with incomplete information, and keep both the team and leadership aligned. The team is fully remote and international - you'll build the processes and culture that make it work at pace. About the Team The ChatBots and AI team's mission is to deliver AI agents to end users. We own the runtime and integration layer - conversation storage, API design, streaming, and integrations with external interfaces like Slack and other applications. The team is made up of Senior and Staff engineers working in Ruby/Rails and Go on a modern stack: PostgreSQL, Kafka, Redis, Kubernetes, and AWS. You'll collaborate with dedicated QA, SRE, and UX/UI teams, as well as engineering teams across the broader organization. Workato has a strong cross-functional culture - your team won't work in isolation.
