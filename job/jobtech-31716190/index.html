Part-Time Delivery Driver – One Day per Week Omami – Asian Street Food | Gothenburg, Sweden Location: Gothenburg (Göteborg), Sweden Employment Type: Part-Time (1 Day per Week) Company: Omami Group AB Start Date: As soon as possible / By agreement About Omami Omami is a growing Asian Street Food restaurant chain in Sweden, committed to delivering fresh, high-quality food and exceptional guest experiences. As we continue to expand, we are looking for a reliable, responsible, and punctual Part-Time Delivery Driver to support our restaurant operations and logistics. Job Description We are seeking a motivated driver to manage our weekly deliveries between Gothenburg and our restaurant locations. The ideal candidate is organised, dependable, and comfortable driving between cities while ensuring timely and safe deliveries. Weekly Driving Route: Gothenburg → Ulricehamn → Brämhult → Varberg → Gothenburg Working Schedule: One day per week, with working hours agreed upon based on delivery requirements. Key Responsibilities Collect food supplies, packaging materials, and other restaurant essentials from the designated pickup location. Deliver supplies safely and on time to Omami restaurant locations along the assigned route. Load and unload goods carefully and efficiently. Check delivery quantities and ensure accurate handovers. Maintain proper handling and hygiene standards when transporting food products. Communicate with restaurant managers and the operations team regarding deliveries. Follow Swedish road safety regulations and company procedures. Maintain cleanliness and report any issues with the delivery vehicle. Requirements Valid Swedish or EU Category B driving licence. Good driving record and responsible driving habits. Punctual, reliable, and able to work independently. Physically capable of loading and unloading delivery items. Good communication skills in English or Swedish. Familiarity with roads in Västra Götaland and Halland is an advantage. Previous delivery or logistics experience is preferred but not mandatory. What We Offer Flexible part-time employment – only one day per week. A consistent weekly delivery route. A friendly and supportive working environment. Opportunity to be part of a growing restaurant chain in Sweden. Competitive hourly compensation based on experience and agreement. How to Apply Interested candidates are welcome to submit a brief introduction, relevant driving experience, and contact details.
