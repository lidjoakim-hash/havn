Role Summary This role is a part of TRATON Group R&D, located in Södertälje, Sweden Help shape the future of vehicle visibility and mechatronics at TRATON Growing demands for safety, comfort and functionality are driving the development of tomorrow’s commercial vehicles. As new technologies enable more advanced functions, vehicle systems become increasingly complex, placing higher demands on the capabilities, performance and integration of our lighting and mechatronic components. TGREEVV Visibility Components and Mechatronics develops components for TRATON’s heavy commercial vehicles. Our scope includes exterior lighting such as headlamps, fog lamps and roof lamps. Interior lighting such as reading lights and ambient lighting. And mechatronic components such as sensors, washer systems, actuators and sound systems. We are looking for a Development Engineer who is curious about technology and wants to help develop reliable, high-quality products for future TRATON vehicles. Job Responsibilities You will take technical responsibility for components throughout their development and product lifecycle. Working with colleagues across TRATON and with external suppliers, you will help turn requirements into robust solutions, from early development through testing, industrialisation and production follow-up. Your work will combine technical problem solving, collaboration and practical evaluation. This may include reviewing designs and requirements, building or assessing prototypes, and planning or carrying out tests in laboratories, vehicles or at test facilities. In this role, you will: Define, review and follow up technical requirements for your components. Coordinate development activities with suppliers and internal stakeholders. Plan and follow up verification and validation, including laboratory, vehicle and field testing. Analyse test results, resolve technical issues and drive corrective actions. Work cross-functionally with areas such as project management, system engineering, styling, purchasing, production and aftermarket. Follow up component quality and support the product after launch. Identify opportunities to improve products, processes and ways of working. Who You Are You enjoy taking ownership and driving development forward. As a component owner, you are the central point of contact for your components bringing together colleagues across different functions and external suppliers. You enjoy being at the centre of communication, initiating discussions, aligning expectations and following up agreed actions. You are curious about technology and structured in your work. You build strong working relationships, actively seek the information you need and involve the right people to solve problems and move development forward. We also believe you have: A university degree or equivalent qualification in electrical engineering, mechatronics or mechanical engineering. Experience in product development; experience from the automotive industry is an advantage. An interest in vehicle technology and the development of components and systems. Good communication skills and the ability to collaborate across teams and organisations. Strong written and spoken English. Swedish is an advantage. Competence in automotive lighting is a merit.
