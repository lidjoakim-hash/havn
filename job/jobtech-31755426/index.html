Mechanical Engineer to Mycronic

Academic Work Sweden AB · Stockholm, Sweden

Mycronic is a global high-tech company whose innovative solutions have been advancing electronics technology for over 50 years. Today we continue to grow and serve customers in an expanding variety of industries. What we do impacts the future of technology, and in turn, the way we live our lives tomorrow. We’re looking for people to join our talented, motivated and friendly team on the cutting-edge. Here, in an open, collaborative and flexible environment, you’ll work alongside leading industry experts and take on challenging projects that bring tomorrow’s electronics to life. Welcome to Mycronic! About the role Mycronic has experienced strong growth in recent years, both organically and through acquisitions. With headquarters in Sweden, Mycronic is a global organization with subsidiaries, agents, and distributors in over 50 countries. In this role, you will be part of Mycronic's PCB Assembly Solutions division (PA), a leading provider of equipment and solutions for surface mount technology (SMT). Our solutions enable electronics manufacturers to adapt production quickly while maintaining high efficiency, ranging from individual systems for specific process steps to complete production lines and advanced software for assembly process management and connected factories.  In this role, you will be part of a multidisciplinary engineering group comprising mechanical, electronics, and mechatronics engineers. You will also collaborate closely with colleagues across different functions, including architects, service, project management, product management, production, and management. With the majority of Mycronic’s R&D operations and production facilities located on-site in Kista, you will have the opportunity to work in close proximity to both the products you develop and the people involved in bringing them to life. Work tasks You will take part in the entire development chain, from requirements specification to design and implementation, while working with a diverse range of materials and technologies, including welded and machined steel structures, large and small aluminum and plastic components, high-precision parts, and units for highly accurate motion control. You will work with a range of products within the PA Division, including for example Mycronic Jet Printers, MYTower systems and Pick-and-Place machines. You will, for example: Work with the design of mechanical components and functions. Participate in multiple parallel projects, covering new product development, maintenance, improvements to existing solutions, and product redesign. Participate in defining requirements and requirements reviews. Perform verification and documentation to ensure that designs meet specified requirements. Contribute to the development and improvement of existing ways of working and solutions by identifying opportunities for improvement and suggesting new solutions, features and accessories. We are looking for This role is ideal for a curious and collaborative Mechanical Design Engineer with a genuine passion for technology and engineering. You enjoy exploring new ideas, challenging existing solutions and finding better ways of working. You are eager to develop your skills, share your knowledge and learn from the experience and perspectives of others. You enjoy working hands-on with mechanical design, turning ideas into practical solutions and contributing to continuous improvements. You are a team player who values open communication, enjoys collaborating across functions and is motivated by solving technical challenges together. Furthermore, you have: A bachelor’s or master’s degree in mechanics, mechatronics, or a related field. At least some years of professional experience in mechanical engineering, covering the entire development life cycle. Good knowledge in CAD, Mycronic uses SolidWorks.
