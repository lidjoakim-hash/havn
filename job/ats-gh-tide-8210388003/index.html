ABOUT TIDE At Tide, we help SMEs save time and money in the running of their businesses by not only offering business accounts and related banking services, but also a comprehensive set of highly usable and connected administrative solutions, from invoicing to accounting. Tide is transforming the small business banking market and now supports over 2 million members globally across the UK, Germany and France. Using advanced technology, all solutions are designed with SMEs in mind. With quick onboarding, low fees and innovative features, we thrive on making data driven decisions to serve our mission: to help SMEs save time and money so they can get back to doing what they love. Tide facts: • Tide is available for UK, German and French SMEs • Over 2 million members globally and rapidly growing • Over $300 million raised in funding • 3,000 Tideans globally • We have offices in Central London, with a member support and technology centre in Sofia, Bulgaria, technology centres in Serbia, Romania, Lithuania and Hyderabad and offices in Gurugram, New Delhi, Berlin, Paris and Luxembourg ABOUT THE TEAM The Ledger team owns the core ledger. It's the system of record behind every account, balance and payment at Tide. Payments, cards and lending all sit on top of it. If it's slow or wrong, members can't pay people, and that hits their business directly. The team works on this one problem. ABOUT THE ROLE Tide is growing into new markets and new products, and the ledger has to scale with that. We're rewriting it and migrating to the new version. The goals are better performance for members and a foundation that keeps up as volumes and products grow. You'll help design it and deliver it. This is a hands-on role in one team, working on one problem. You'll do architecture, system design, coding and mentoring. You won't have direct reports, but you'll own the technical direction of the ledger. You'll partner closely with the team's Engineering Manager and Product Manager, and lead by example. We're also changing how we build and test. The team uses AI and agentic tools to deliver working software. We're applying the same approach to functional and performance testing, so we can deliver this work in a fraction of the time it would have taken before. You'll help shape how that works.
