Company description: Part of Volvo Group, Volvo Autonomous Solutions accelerates the development, commercialization and sales of autonomous transport solutions, focusing on defined segments for the on- and off-road space. The combination of strong tech expertise and skilled customer solutions creates innovative transport offers never seen before. We are constantly pushing our own skills and ability to drive change in a traditional industry to meet a growing customer demand. We are now looking for innovative, committed individuals to join us in our endeavor to create customer solutions that enhance safety, flexibility and productivity. Job description:You will own and be accountable for the end-to-end Virtual Driver offroad stack, ensuring strong performance, robustness, requirements fulfillment, and scalability, including responsibility for system validation results and product maturity metrics.   Alongside this, the role involves continuously evolving the full system architecture of the Virtual Driver stack, covering interfaces and their impact on surrounding systems, while providing clear technical and architectural leadership across the organization. Close collaboration and alignment are expected with Virtual Driver teams, external domain engineering teams within V.A.S., and development partners both inside and outside the Volvo Group, across areas such as perception, planning, control, AI, integration, and platform layers. A key part of the role is defining and leading the execution of the Virtual Driver technology strategy, as well as driving cross-functional technology initiatives and broader architectural efforts. This includes proactively identifying and managing technical risks, trade-offs, and dependencies to ensure robust and sustainable solutions over time. Maintaining high engineering standards is essential, with a strong focus on coding practices, system design, and validation approaches, while also contributing to the continuous improvement of software development and validation processes, including HIL/SiL and DevOps setups and practices.    As a Lead Engineer, you act with ownership and end-to-end accountability, align teams around shared goals, and support engineers through coaching and mentoring. You also handle difficult conversations with clarity, focusing on outcomes and progress.   You will be an integral part of the Virtual Leadership team, made up of People Managers and Technical Leads with diverse backgrounds. Do you dream big? We do too, and we are excited to grow together. In this role, you bring hands-on experience in automotive software engineering, preferably within the truck or construction equipment industry, along with a proven track record of delivering autonomous driving, off-road automation, or ADAS features into production and industrialized environments. You have a solid understanding of simulation, cloud platforms, and data pipelines, enabling you to operate effectively across modern software ecosystems.   You have experience in system integration and large-scale validation of complex systems, ensuring robustness and readiness for deployment. Familiarity with agile development methodologies and DevOps practices allows you to thrive in fast-paced, collaborative environments while maintaining a strong focus on quality and delivery.   Beyond your technical expertise, you are proactive, structured, and solution-oriented, with a strong sense of ownership and accountability. You are comfortable navigating complexity, making informed decisions under constraints, and communicating clearly with diverse stakeholders. With a collaborative mindset, curiosity to learn, and the ability to connect deep technical knowledge with a system-level perspective, you contribute effectively in a cross-functional and innovative environment.
