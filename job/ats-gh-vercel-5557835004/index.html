About Vercel: Vercel is the agentic infrastructure company, freeing people and agents to ship what's next. For more than a decade we've helped builders move from idea to production with speed, security, and exceptional developer experience. Now we're scaling our products for both agents and people to ship and run software, built in the open and trusted by OpenAI, PayPal, Ramp, Supreme, and millions of developers worldwide. About the role As a Sales Development Representative, you’ll start sales cycles at some of the largest companies in the world. You’ll build qualified pipeline through cold outbound and book meetings for Enterprise Account Executives. You’ll work with Marketing, Sales Engineering, and Customer Success to learn Vercel’s product, positioning, and messaging. Success means a steady flow of qualified meetings with large enterprise accounts. This is a hybrid role with 3 in-office days per week in London. What you'll do • Identify and qualify future customers in your assigned geography and Enterprise market segment • Write tailored messages and cold call qualified prospects • Set and book qualified meetings for Enterprise Account Executives • Target some of the largest companies and logos alongside Enterprise Account Executives What you need • 6+ months of experience as an outbound SDR or in a sales-adjacent role • Prospecting experience: researched potential leads and crafted outreach to them • Built pipeline through outbound prospecting • Cold called and sent tailored outbound messages to prospects Bonus if you • Vercel, Next.js, or any other technical background or understanding • Prospected into large org structures, such as Fortune 500 companies, and navigated multiple stakeholders to reach decision makers • Salesforce, Outreach, HubSpot, LinkedIn Sales Navigator Benefits: • Competitive compensation package, including equity. • Inclusive Healthcare Package. • Learn and Grow - we provide mentorship and send you to events that help you build your network and skills. • Flexible Time Off. • We will provide you the gear you need to do your role, and a WFH budget for you to outfit your space as needed.
