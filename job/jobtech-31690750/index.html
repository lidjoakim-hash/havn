Long-term positions for nurses in beautiful Western Norway

KLETOR Sverige AB · Sweden

Would you like to combine a meaningful job with proximity to stunning nature? We are recruiting registered nurses for permanent full-time positions within municipal healthcare services in Western Norway. The positions are connected to nursing homes and home-based care services, where you will have the opportunity to work closely with patients and contribute to safe, high-quality healthcare services. The workplace is beautifully located between fjords, high mountains and spectacular scenery. The area offers excellent opportunities for outdoor activities throughout the year, while still providing easy access to a larger town within approximately 40 minutes by car. Here, you will find a working environment that offers responsibility, professional challenges and good opportunities to develop your experience within municipal healthcare. You will become part of a workplace characterised by cooperation, quality and professional commitment. Your responsibilities Clinical nursing in home care, nursing homes or assisted living facilities Observation, assessment, medication management and accurate documentation Follow-up of patients with diverse medical conditions and functional levels Collaboration with relatives, coordinating units and external partners Interdisciplinary cooperation with doctors, therapists and other health professionals Contributing to service development, improvement work and quality assurance We are looking for nurses who: Hold a valid nursing authorization from an EU/EEA country Previous experience in home care services or nursing homes is preferred Be proficient with basic computer skills, necessary for modern healthcare environments Speak and write English fluently Motivated to learn Norwegian Hold a bachelor’s degree in nursing (BSN) Hold a category B driving licence Personal qualities: Responsible, structured and solution oriented Positive attitude and good teamwork skills Flexible and comfortable with varied tasks Motivated to contribute to a small but highly competent interdisciplinary team We offer: A full-time permanent position in the Norwegian public healthcare system Competitive salary and working conditions A supportive work environment that values cooperation and professional confidence Opportunities for further development as a nurse Free online Norwegian language course with a private teacher Relocation assistance and help with housing Personal follow-up throughout the recruitment and relocation process Ready to apply? Send us your application and we will evaluate candidates on an ongoing basis. Feel free to reach out for more information. We look forward to hearing from you!
