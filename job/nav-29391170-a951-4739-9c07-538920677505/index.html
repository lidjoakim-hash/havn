Are you looking for an exciting full-time job where you can grow and learn new things? Then we have the perfect opportunity for you! We are looking for several enthusiastic full-time employees , preferably experienced waiters/waitresses, to join our wonderful team in the heart of Tromsø. Who are we looking for? We are looking for someone who: • Is over 18 years old (due to serving alcohol). • Learns quickly and enjoys new challenges. • Thrives in a fast-paced environment where the guest is always the main focus. • Speaks Norwegian and English fluently. • Is not afraid to step in and help when needed. • Is passionate about providing guests with a great experience. • Has an interest in food and wine and is curious to learn more. • Is flexible and able to work evenings, weekends, and holidays. Personal qualities we value: • Service-minded with a big smile. • Cheerful, positive, and sociable. • Responsible and reliable. • Comfortable working both independently and as part of a team. • Organized, tidy, and detail-oriented in your work. It's a plus if you: • Have knowledge of Asian cuisine. • Have experience from the service industry (experienced waiters/waitresses are preferred). Training will be provided, but prior experience is highly valued. The most important thing is having the right attitude and a strong commitment to quality service. Why work with us? At Suvi, you'll be part of an inclusive work environment with friendly colleagues, varied workdays, and opportunities to develop both professionally and personally. We are proud of our exciting menu featuring sushi, Vietnamese dishes, and other flavors from Asian cuisine. Interested? Send us an email to suvi.tromso&#64;gmail.com, telling us a bit about yourself and why you would like to work with us. Applications are reviewed on an ongoing basis, so don't wait too long! NB: Please note that accommodation is not provided, and unfortunately we are not able to assist with housing.
