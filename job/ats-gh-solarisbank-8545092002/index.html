Solaris is Europe's leading embedded finance platform. Solaris’ full German banking license and proprietary modular B2B tech stack empowers its partners – from SMEs to large, multinational, non-financial companies – to offer compliant, customer-centric banking services, providing seamless experiences to customers across all industries. Founded in 2016, Solaris pioneered the Banking-as-a-Service market with an unparalleled combination of tech and banking. Solaris is headquartered in Berlin and employs 300 people in Europe. Your Role Manual Underwriting Operations • Define and refine the structure of complex credit transactions. • Assess and approve credit transactions, ensuring full compliance with the OHB (Operational Handbook) and external regulatory frameworks. • Continuously perform, optimize, and maintain the credit approval process to enhance efficiency and accuracy. • Establish and maintain manual credit file management systems. • Conduct regular reviews and updates of credit files to reflect current risk profiles. Automatic Underwriting & Logic • Assist in setting and refining the technical criteria for the Decision Engine. • Align the conceptual consistency of underwriting principles across both manual and automated channels. Credit Portfolio Steering • Prepare regular qualitative portfolio reviews accompanied by action proposals to maintain credit risk exposure within established risk appetite levels. • Execute ad-hoc assessments as required to mitigate emerging risks. • Monitor partnership team activities specifically regarding dunning, workout, and collection actions to ensure alignment with risk standards. Strategic Projects & Compliance • Take responsibility for closing audit findings within the respective focus areas. • Lead training initiatives for CRM and Solaris staff on core underwriting principles. • Drive project-related tasks, including the development and launch of new loan products.
