Mission before ego. Everyone contributes. No one coasts. If you’re driven by impact, pace, and raising the bar. This is the place. The role As a Knowledge Lawyer, you’ll sit at the intersection of legal expertise and knowledge management: capturing, curating and transferring Legora’s Legal Engineers’ know-how so that it is findable, reusable and current. You’ll put that know-how to work in two places: inside the team, through onboarding and internal training, and with our clients, through the Certified Legal Engineer Program (CLEP), where we train clients’ power users to become certified Legal Engineers in their own organisations. You’ll combine deep legal knowledge, a strong process mindset and a genuine passion for enabling others. What you’ll do - Capture and curate knowledge from client work and internal experts, turning valuable expertise into structured, reusable assets. - Co-run the Certified Legal Engineer Program: develop and deliver sessions, build and maintain programme content, and support on-site and virtual cohorts. - Design and deliver internal training and onboarding for Legal Engineers, and run regular knowledge-sharing sessions across the team. - Identify knowledge gaps and surface where new content or expertise is required. - Manage and continuously improve the knowledge platform so it stays intuitive, well structured and actually used. - Drive change management to build a culture of knowledge sharing across the team. What you bring - 4+ years in law: at least one year in a knowledge lawyer or professional support lawyer role, plus at least three years in practice. Familiarity with European legislation is required. - Experience across both law firm and in-house settings is highly valued, as is exposure to multiple practice areas. - Experience with knowledge management platforms; involvement in evaluating or selecting one is a plus. - Technologically fluent and comfortable with new tools and platforms, including generative AI. - Excellent written and verbal communication, with the ability to convey complex ideas clearly and concisely, including in front of a room. - Analytical and structured, with a talent for bringing clarity and order to complex or ambiguous areas. - Outcome-oriented, with a disciplined approach to measuring impact and iterating on evidence and feedback. - Intellectually curious, with a genuine interest in diverse practice areas, industries and geographies. - Collaborative and relationship-driven, with the interpersonal skills to build trust and influence across teams. Why join now - A new function with real autonomy, working directly with the Legal Engineering leadership team and the CLEP lead. - Your work shows up in every new joiner’s first
