Job ID: 5898 Senior Risk Expertfor for Risk Materiality (permanent position) Would you like to play a key role in strengthening how Nordea identifies, assesses and explains ESG risks across our risk profile? We are looking for a senior risk expert who can take ownership of an important risk materiality process, connecting ESG risk drivers, transmission channels, heatmaps and expert judgement into clear and decision-useful input for risk management and governance. About our team Meet the Group Credit Risk team. Our role is to ensure that Nordea has a strong, well-documented and forward-looking understanding of material risks. This includes how ESG risk drivers may affect Nordea’s customers, industries, geographies, value chains and risk profile over different time horizons. As the Senior Risk Expert for Risk Materiality, you’ll play a valuable role in owning and further developing the process for ESG risk identification and materiality assessment. You will ensure that ESG risks are properly identified in Nordea’s Common Risk Taxonomy and materiality assessment, identify and document transmission channels, develop and challenge ESG heatmaps, and ensure that outputs are clear, robust and useful for risk areas, governance forums and senior decision-makers. The role requires a strong combination of risk management expertise, ESG risk understanding, analytical judgement, stakeholder management and documentation discipline. You will work closely with subject matter experts and risk colleagues across Nordea to ensure that ESG risks are identified and assessed in a consistent, transparent and well-governed way. Collaboration. Ownership. Passion. Courage. These are the values that guide us in how we work and how we make decisions – and that we imagine you share with us. Main responsibilities in this role: Own and develop the risk identification process for ESG risks, including ESG in risk taxonomy and materiality assessment, transmission channel framework and heatmap methodology. Translate complex ESG risk drivers into clear risk narratives, explaining how environmental, social and governance factors may transmit into Nordea’s risk taxonomy. Coordinate and challenge expert input across risk areas and stakeholders, facilitating alignment on relevant ESG risks, time horizons, industries, geographies, assumptions and methodological choices. Secure high-quality documentation and governance, including methodology updates, annual reviews, expert judgements, deviations, assumptions and outputs that can be used in materiality assessment and risk governance. Prepare decision material for Senior Stakeholders incl. Credit Risk Sub-Committee. Who you are This is the right role for you if you: Have strong understanding of ESG risk drivers — including climate-related, nature-related, social and governance factors — and can explain how these may affect a bank’s customers, portfolios, value chains and overall risk profile. Can own and drive a structured risk process across units, ensuring clear planning, stakeholder involvement, timely delivery, strong documentation and continuous improvement. Have strong analytical skills and sound judgement, including the ability to work with heatmaps, scoring logic, vulnerability assessments, time horizons, data limitations and expert-based assumptions. Are able to translate complex methodology into clear communication, including narratives, executive summaries and decision-useful material for risk areas, governance forums and senior stakeholders. Have excellent collaboration and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to facilitate discussions, challenge constructively and build alignment across different levels of the organisation and across the value chain. Bring high integrity, ownership and a solution-oriented mindset, with the courage to challenge assumptions and the drive to ensure that the process remains robust, transparent and fit for purpose.
