Spend season 2027 with us! FOOD TRUCK TEAM MEMBERS Flåm, Eidfjord & Norheimsund, Norway Good food. New faces. A team to be part of. And the Norwegian fjords just outside. Looking for a season somewhere different? Come and join our food truck teams in Flåm, Eidfjord or Norheimsund. You'll prepare food, meet guests and share busy days with the people working beside you. Already worked in hospitality? We'd love to hear about it. Taking your first steps? You're welcome too - everyone receives training. Bring a willingness to learn, a friendly approach and the energy to get involved. Your part in the team • Get to know our food and prepare dishes using the recipes and training we provide. • Give guests a warm welcome and friendly, attentive service. • Help orders reach the right guests smoothly, even when the pace picks up. • Keep your workstation, equipment and service area clean, tidy and ready to use. • Follow food hygiene and safety procedures. • Share the work, help your colleagues and follow your Manager or Site Coordinator's guidance. You'll feel at home in this role if... • You enjoy meeting people and want guests to leave with a smile. • You're reliable, ready to learn and happy to support your colleagues. • You can stay organised and work efficiently during a busy service. • You speak English at B2-C1 level. • You hold a valid category B driving licence - this is required for the role. • Hospitality experience is welcome, but it isn't essential. We'll train you. What's waiting for you • Full-time seasonal work with hourly pay and several contract periods to choose from. • Training to help you settle into your role and get to know our food. • The chance to meet new colleagues and work together in Norway's fjord region. • Help finding accommodation for your season in Norway. • A staff meal on each working day. Join our team and spend the season surrounded by Norway's breathtaking fjords! With food trucks in Flåm and other beautiful locations, we're all about great food, friendly faces, and making people feel welcome. We'd love to have you on board and share an unforgettable season together! Send your CV and recomendation: job&#64;grpnorway.com
