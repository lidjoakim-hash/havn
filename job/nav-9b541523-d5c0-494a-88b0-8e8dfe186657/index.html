Location: Stavanger, Norway Project: Offshore Oil & Gas – Modification and Maintenance (M&M) Start Date: December 2026 / January 2027 Duration: Until 31 December 2027 Workload: 100% About the Role Ready to engineer reliable HVAC solutions for offshore oil and gas projects? We are seeking an experienced HVAC Engineer to join an offshore modification and maintenance project in Stavanger. You will contribute to technical design, quality assurance, and multidisciplinary coordination, ensuring deliverables meet project requirements and applicable standards. Key Responsibilities • Prepare and quality-assure HVAC engineering deliverables. • Perform capacity calculations and technical assessments of HVAC systems. • Coordinate HVAC interfaces with clients, suppliers, and other engineering disciplines. • Contribute to installation planning, testing, and design reviews. • Ensure compliance with project specifications, regulations, and technical standards. • Review 3D models, technical drawings, and supplier documentation. • Recommend design improvements and cost-effective solutions. • Maintain engineering documentation and ensure design changes are incorporated. • Ensure deliverables meet project quality requirements and agreed deadlines. • Support engineering planning, progress monitoring, and reporting. Qualifications • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Mechanical Engineering, HVAC, or a related field. • Minimum 3 years of relevant HVAC design experience. • Experience with offshore modification projects, preferably in the North Sea oil and gas industry. • Good understanding of relevant HVAC standards and applicable regulations. • Strong communication and collaboration skills. • Valid offshore safety course or relevant offshore certification, as required for the assignment. • Preferred Technical Skills • Familiarity with MIPS, Navisworks, and/or E3D. • Experience with 2D/3D drawings and modelling. • Experience with technical documentation and preparation of installation packages. • Strong IT skills and familiarity with relevant engineering software. Why Join This Assignment? Join a technically challenging offshore project, collaborate with experienced professionals, and contribute to practical HVAC solutions for the oil and gas industry.
