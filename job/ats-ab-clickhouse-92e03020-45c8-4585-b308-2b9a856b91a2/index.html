Director of Product Management, Security

ClickHouse · The Netherlands, Netherlands

We're looking for a Director of Product Management to own our security product strategy end to end -   the vision, the roadmap, build vs buy decisions. You will set the multi-year investment strategy for our security offerings, go deep with engineering on database security, networking, and identity management. You will be the subject matter expert for other PMs navigating security-adjacent decisions.. Responsibilities - Own the product vision, strategy, and multi-year roadmap for our security offerings, and make the case for it - internally with engineering and leadership, and externally with customers and the market. - Go deep on database security, networking, and identity and access management (IAM) - enough to argue the details with engineering, not just approve what they propose. - Partner closely with engineering, design, sales, and marketing to ship products that are actually secure, not just marketed that way. - Translate complex security requirements into specifications and user stories that engineering can understand. - Own backlog prioritization and the trade-offs that come with it, weighing business value, customer risk, and engineering cost. - Drive go-to-market for security launches - positioning, sales enablement, documentation. - Monitor how security features perform in the real world and use that data, not just instinct, to decide what to build next. - Be the security subject-matter expert other teams come to, and provide guidance and coaching to PMs working on security-adjacent problems elsewhere in the org. - Represent our security posture and roadmap externally - with top customers, prospects, and analysts when the conversation needs someone who actually understands the tradeoffs. Qualifications - 10+ years of product management experience in B2B SaaS, with a significant and sustained focus on security products or features, including experience setting security strategy at an organizational level. - Real, hands-on experience with database security principles and best practices. - Strong understanding of networking concepts and their security implications. - In-depth knowledge of identity and access management (IAM). - Security certifications (e.g., CISSP, CISM, CompTIA Security+) preferred. - Solid program management skills: planning, execution, and risk management across multiple concurrent workstreams. - A track record of representing product externally - with customers, analysts, or at industry events, and translating deep technical security topics for non-technical audiences. - Experience guiding or informally mentoring other PMs, even without a formal reporting relationship. - Strong communication and presentation skills, and the judgment to know when to escalate versus when to just make the call. - Comfortable in a fast-paced, collaborative environment where priorities shift and you're expected to bring order to it, not just follow along. PERKS - Flexible work environment - ClickHouse is a globally distributed company and remote-friendly. We currently operate in over 25 countries. - Healthcare - Employer contributions towards your healthcare. - Equity in the company - Every new team member who joins our company receives stock options. - Time off - Flexible time off in the US, generous entitlement in other countries. - A USD$500 Home office setup if you’re a remote employee. - Global Gatherings – We believe in the power of in-person connection and offer opportunities to engage with colleagues at company-wide offsites.
