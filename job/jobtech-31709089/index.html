Product Development Engineer Turn Innovation into Life-Changing Medical Technology About the Role Smartwise Sweden AB is looking for an experienced Product Development Engineer to develop minimally invasive medical devices, with a focus on catheter-based endovascular technologies. You will work across the product lifecycle—from new development and product improvements to new configurations—partnering with R&D, Quality, Regulatory, suppliers and manufacturers to deliver safe, robust and manufacturable solutions. This is a hands-on role with ownership, complex problem-solving and a clear impact on next-generation medical technology. What You’ll DoDevelop Products Participate and lead product development, design changes, improvements and new variants. Develop and assess technical solutions, prototypes and product configurations. Implement engineering changes with safety, performance and manufacturability in focus. Maintain specifications, drawings, bills of materials (BOMs) and design documentation. Support design reviews, design control and risk management in line with ISO 14971. Test and Solve Plan and execute engineering testing, design verification and validation. Create test methods, protocols and reports. Troubleshoot product issues, lead root-cause analysis and support CAPA. Drive Delivery Maintain design documentation and medical device technical files. Work with suppliers and manufacturing partners on components, processes and improvements. Support design transfer and implementation in manufacturing. Coordinate engineering activities in line with project plans, quality procedures and regulatory requirements. What You BringMust have Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Mechanical Engineering, Biomedical Engineering or a related field. 5+ years in medical device product development or design engineering. Experience with product modifications, engineering change management and design control. Hands-on experience in testing, verification, validation and technical documentation. Knowledge of ISO 13485, ISO 14971 and relevant medical device regulations. Experience working across functions and with suppliers and manufacturing partners. Strong analytical, problem-solving and organizational skills. Ability to manage engineering activities independently and communicate effectively in English and Swedish. We believe you are established in Sweden and live in the Stockholm/Uppsala region. Nice to Have Additional experience with minimally invasive devices, particularly catheter-based endovascular systems, would be valuable. We also welcome experience in technical investigations, root-cause analysis and CAPA, as well as working with contract manufacturers and external test laboratories. Knowledge of design for manufacturing and assembly (DFM/DFA) is an advantage. Who You Are You are structured, practical and detail-oriented, with an analytical and solution-focused mindset. Proactive and accountable, you manage priorities confidently and collaborate effectively in multidisciplinary R&D teams. Above all, you are motivated by medical innovation and the opportunity to improve patient care. Why Join Us? You will join a team where your curiosity, practical mindset, and attention to quality can make a real difference. Together, we develop solutions that support meaningful progress in medical device innovation. We offer you to be part of an innovative journey pioneering new treatment paradigms based on world-class science, a workplace where everyone feels valued, respected, and integral to the company’s success, a transparent culture where vital information is shared across the organization and competitive compensation & benefits. Ready to Make an Impact? Apply today and take the next step with Smartwise. Applications are reviewed continuously, and we aim to fill the position as soon as possible.
