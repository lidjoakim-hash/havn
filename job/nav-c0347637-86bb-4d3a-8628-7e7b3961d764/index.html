Professor/Associate Professor in Educational Psychology

University of Oslo · Oslo, Norway

About the position The Department of Education at the University of Oslo invites applications for a position in Educational Psychology. We seek applicants with a strong research interest in counselling practices targeting schools, Early Childhood Education and Care (ECEC) settings, and/or Educational Psychological Counselling settings.  Educational systems in Norway and internationally face growing challenges concerning inclusion, diversity, and equity. Addressing these challenges requires stronger collaboration across the education system, including early childhood education and care (ECEC), schools, and support services. It also calls for closer links between research and practice, to improve educational quality and learning outcomes. We are seeking applicants with a research portfolio in these areas.  The position is suited for candidates who wish to advance research on counselling, implementation processes, and/or organizational development. We particularly encourage applicants whose research focuses on inclusion in educational communities. Relevant experience includes experimental interventions and longitudinal studies involving families or educational settings and examining the conditions that support learning, development, well-being, and engagement.  We welcome diverse scholarly profiles but particularly value innovative, methodologically rigorous research. Expertise in quantitative methods will be emphasised, while experience integrating qualitative approaches, such as observation of collaboration and interaction, is an advantage. Experience leading large-scale projects, managing complex project structures or coordinating interdisciplinary teams will be valued. The position involves 47 % teaching, 47 % research, and 6 % administrative tasks. More about the position The successful candidate is expected to strengthen research in educational psychology and contribute to the Faculty of Educational Sciences’ research group, Development, Equity and Literacy in Learning Contexts (DEAL).  The successful candidate is also expected to actively participate in the collective development of our education programs and teach at the bachelor- and master-level programs in Educational Psychology. The candidates’ scientific production in the past 5 years will be emphasized in the assessment. It is expected that the successful candidate publishes internationally and seeks external funding.  Work tasks and responsibilities The appointee must be able to lead and initiate research, supervise doctoral degree students, participate in teaching and examination work at all levels, and undertake administrative tasks, in accordance with applicable provisions.  More specifically the appointee must: • Contribute to the development of research and study programs in Educational Psychology • Contribute to the research group DEAL  • Participate in national and international research networks  • Plan, teach, and supervise in the bachelor and master level courses in Educational Psychology  • Disseminate research  • Apply for external funding  • Conduct administrative tasks  Qualifications For Associate Professor: In this role you must have: • Doctoral degree within Education Psychology or competence at an equivalent level documented through scientific work of the same scope and quality  • Documented basic university pedagogical competence. Applicants who do not have documented basic university pedagogical competence must acquire this within two years from appointment • Good communication skills both written and oral in English and Norwegian or another Scandinavian language at level B2 are a prerequisite for performing all the tasks associated with the position. Applicants who do not master a Scandinavian language at the time of appointment are given a deadline of three years to learn Norwegian • Publications in high ranked international journals within educational psychology It is an advantage if you have: • strong competence and experience in research methods and experience with quantitative methodology related to longitudinal and/or experimental designs  • experience in obtaining external funds • experience in leading large-scale research projects • documentation of qualifications in the field of academic management and administration For Professor: In this role you must have: • Doctoral degree or competence at an equivalent level and a track record that documents efforts and results over time within the field of Educational Psychology. • Substantial academic production and significant contributions to research at the highest level, in accordance with international or national standards within the field.  • Success in obtaining grants for major research applications and experience in leading and completing research projects. The research should be of high quality, demonstrating both breadth and depth within the research field and showing the ability to address new issues. • Research production should reflect an independent research profile, but also the ability to initiate and conduct high-level research in collaboration with other researchers and research institutions.  • Strong competence and experience in research methods. Experience with quantitative methodology related to longitudinal and/or experimental designs is an advantage. • Documented basic university pedagogical competence  • Broad experience in the development and implementation of teaching, supervision and collegial work with high pedagogical quality, as well as a willingness and ability for continuous professional development as a teacher and supervisor. • Good qualifications in the field of academic leadership and experience with management of research projects or research groups. • Good communication skills both written and oral in English and Norwegian or another Scandinavian language at level B2 are a prerequisite for performing all the tasks associated with the position.
