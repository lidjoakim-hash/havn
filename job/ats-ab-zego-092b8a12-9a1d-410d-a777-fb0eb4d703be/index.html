THE TEAM Zego’s Claims department is expanding, and we’re on the hunt for a highly skilled Technical Claims Manager to elevate our standards of technical excellence. In this pivotal role, as a Technical Claims Manager, you’ll drive excellence, quality, and innovation. You will lead the performance of our Quality Assurance Framework, ensuring best-in-class auditing of both our internal claims team and external providers. Your expertise will ensure that every aspect of our claims handling, from technical precision to customer experience, meets and exceeds industry-leading standards. We’re looking for an analytical and detail-oriented claims expert with a strong technical background in motor claims. You must have hands-on experience in handling and/or auditing motor claims. If you're ready to make an impact, drive innovation, and set new standards in claims excellence, we’d love to hear from you! 🚀 WHAT YOU WILL BE DOING - Lead continuous improvements of our Quality Assurance Framework, ensuring technical excellence across all claims processes. - Deal with complex indemnity referrals applying all relevant legislation and delivering swift resolutions for our customers. - Develop and refine best-practice documentation, keeping our teams equipped with cutting-edge claims strategies. - Train, mentor, and develop our claims team, fostering a culture of continuous learning and technical excellence. - Monitor, analyse, and report on claims initiatives, identifying opportunities to optimise efficiency and drive performance improvements. - Collaborate cross-functionally with experts across Fraud, Data, Product Management, Engineering, Actuarial, Reserving, and Sales to enhance our claims strategy. - Represent Zego in supplier and client meetings, building strong external relationships and ensuring alignment with our quality and service expectations. - Shape the future of claims processing, influencing system development, enhancing automation capabilities, and driving innovation. - Champion claims data quality and completeness, working closely with our Data and Product teams to ensure data is accurate, accessible, and actionable across all internal systems.
