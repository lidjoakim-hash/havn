Experienced Waiter / Waitress – Winter Season in Tromsø, Norway

NYT TROMSØ · Tromsø, Norway

Lønn ikke oppgittSesongvia NAVFrist 6.1.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Are you an experienced waiter or waitress looking for an exciting opportunity in Northern Norway this winter?   NYT is looking for an experienced and service-minded waiter/waitress to join their team for the winter season, starting as soon as possible and until the end of March 2027. Main responsibilities • Deliver excellent and professional customer service. • Take orders and serve food and beverages. • Provide guests with menu and beverage recommendations. • Ensure a welcoming and memorable dining experience. • Maintain high standards of cleanliness and service. • Work closely with the kitchen and front-of-house team. • Contribute to an efficient service during busy periods. Qualifications • Previous experience as a waiter/waitress in a restaurant is required. • Good knowledge of food and beverages. • Strong communication and interpersonal skills. • Fluent English, both spoken and understood. • Scandinavian language skills are an advantage. • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced environment. • Flexibility to work evenings, weekends and public holidays. • Valid right to work in Norway. Who are you? You are passionate about hospitality, professional, reliable and genuinely interested in creating memorable guest experiences. You enjoy working in an international environment, take initiative and remain calm under pressure. What we offer • Seasonal employment in one of Norway's most exciting winter destinations. • A dynamic and international working environment. • The opportunity to work alongside experienced hospitality professionals. • Valuable professional experience during Tromsø's busy winter season. • A unique opportunity to experience Arctic Norway and the Northern Lights. Applications will be reviewed on an ongoing basis, and suitable candidates will be contacted for an interview.   Are you ready for an exciting winter season in Northern Norway? We look forward to receiving your application!
