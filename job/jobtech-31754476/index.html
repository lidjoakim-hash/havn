Vehicle Test Driver

WANVE Sweden AB · Stockholm, Sweden

Company: WANVE Sweden AB Location: Stockholm, Sweden Job Description We are looking for motivated and responsible Vehicle Test Drivers to join our team in Sweden. In this role, you will participate in vehicle testing activities, including test driving, vehicle performance evaluation, and road testing under various driving conditions. You will work closely with engineering teams to support automotive testing projects in Sweden and other European countries. Key Responsibilities Conduct vehicle test driving according to predefined test plans and project requirements. Perform road testing under different driving conditions and environments. Monitor vehicle behaviour, performance, and functionality during testing. Record test observations and report any abnormalities or technical issues. Support engineers in vehicle testing and validation activities. Perform basic vehicle inspections before and after testing. Follow all traffic regulations, safety requirements, and testing procedures. Participate in vehicle testing projects across Europe when required. Requirements Excellent English communication skills, both spoken and written. Valid BE, C, or CE driving license (at least one of these categories is required). Strong interest in vehicle testing and willingness to work in automotive testing projects. Responsible driving behaviour and strong awareness of road safety. Ability to follow technical instructions and testing procedures. Willingness and ability to travel to other European countries according to project needs. Business trips typically last no longer than one month per assignment. Good communication and teamwork skills. Flexible, reliable, and able to work independently. Preferred Qualifications Previous experience in vehicle testing, professional driving, or the automotive industry. Experience driving different types of vehicles, including passenger cars, commercial vehicles, or heavy-duty vehicles. Basic knowledge of vehicle systems and automotive technology. Experience working in international environments. Personal Qualities We are looking for someone who is: Safety-conscious, responsible, and detail-oriented. Enthusiastic about vehicles and driving. Flexible and willing to adapt to different testing environments. Patient and capable of following structured testing procedures. Comfortable working both independently and as part of a team. How to Apply Please submit your CV and a brief introduction highlighting your driving experience, driving license categories, and relevant qualifications. We look forward to receiving your application!
