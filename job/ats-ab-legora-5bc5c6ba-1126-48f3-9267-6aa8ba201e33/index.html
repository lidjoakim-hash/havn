About the Role The way legal work gets done is changing, and Engagement is at the centre of that transformation. We're looking for an experienced Engagement Manager to own Legora's global relationship with one of the Big 4, one of our most significant partnerships, and to fundamentally change the way that firm works with Legora. This is a role for someone who has led change inside large, complex organisations and knows that real transformation is designed and managed. You will own the adoption and long-term success of this client globally: building the relationships, defining the outcomes and serving as the trusted adviser and orchestrator who turns Legora into an embedded part of how their teams operate. Global means global. You will set the strategy for how the firm adopts Legora worldwide, and Engagement Managers in other regions will run their local programmes against the direction and decisions you set. Day to day, you will be the point of contact for the firm's UK and Ireland practice from our London office, and you will travel regularly to other member firms in the network to build those relationships and land the programme on the ground. Expect to be travelling around 25% of the time. At the heart of the role is designing the client's adoption playbook and delivering a multi-year change-management programme that transforms how they work. You will build the roadmap, sequence initiatives from initial rollout through firm-wide scaled adoption, define measurable outcomes and ROI and evolve the programme as new AI capabilities and use cases emerge. You will be critical as we continue to build a best-in-class adoption programme at Legora, helping the client realise measurable ROI, expand successful use cases and transform the way their teams work.
