AI-Driven HR Operations Specialist

Tacton Systems AB · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 520 000–640 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 14.2.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

About the role Imagine HR where efficient, trusted processes and AI go hand in hand: smooth onboarding, people data everyone can trust, and reliable AI answers to everyday questions about how we work. As our AI-Driven HR Operations Specialist, you make that happen. You design, document, and automate our core HR processes, and you put AI to work wherever it does the job better, so managers and employees across Tacton spend less time on admin and more time on what matters. This is the role for someone who thinks in processes, rules, and data flows, gets a real buzz from what AI can do, and wants to turn that into everyday impact. You'll report directly to Eva Lessing, our CHRO, and team up with the HR team, our AI Automation Specialist, IT, and Security and Compliance to bring your ideas to life. What you will do • Build the HR automation and AI roadmap, prioritizing by business value, process readiness, data sensitivity, and effort, and choosing between automation, AI, or leaving a process as it is. Then build and run the solutions that make HR work smarter for everyone at Tacton. • Run, document and continuously improve our core HR processes: onboarding and offboarding, goal setting, performance review, and our engagement survey. • Own our HRIS (Workday) and Performance system (Leapsome), and set up, maintain, and reconfigure integrations with other systems such as our ATS (Teamtailor), LMS (Docebo), and Microsoft Entra ID. • Set up dashboards and self-service reporting so managers and HR get reliable people data whenever they need it. • Make sure every solution meets our security and privacy requirements, including ISO 27001 and SOC 2. • Measure results in time saved, error rates, and adoption, and enable HR colleagues and managers so the changes stick. What we are looking for • 5+ years in HR, ideally in HR operations, HR systems, or HR technology, with hands-on experience designing and automating HR processes and a solid understanding of people data. • A genuine curiosity and passion for what AI can do, backed by hands-on experience with AI tools such as Claude (our main AI tool at Tacton), ChatGPT, or Microsoft Copilot, including AI and automation solutions you have built and maintained, such as workflows and assistants, with measurable results. • Strong skills in documenting processes so others can follow them. • A self-driven way of working, comfort with ambiguity, and a collaborative style. You bring HR, IT, Security, and Compliance colleagues along and can explain trade-offs to senior stakeholders. • Good judgment with confidential employee data. • Fluent English. Swedish and Workday experience are a plus. Your first six months • Map how our HR processes and integrations work today. • Agree a roadmap with priorities and baseline measures. • Get at least two improvements live and measured. • Introduce a documentation standard that the HR team uses. Why join us • You build it from the ground up. This is a brand-new role with no playbook to inherit. The processes, standards, and AI solutions you create become how HR works at Tacton. • Your expertise matters from day one. Our HR team already uses AI and is eager to do more, but we need an expert to lead the way. You will raise the bar for all of us, and because the need is big, your work will be seen and truly appreciated. • Your impact reaches the whole company. Every process you simplify saves time and increases quality for employees and managers in every country we operate in. • You have room to move fast. You report to our CHRO, who sees her job as removing obstacles for you. You join an engaged HR team of six in Stockholm, Chicago, and Karlsruhe that loves trying new things. • You grow with a company on the move. Tacton develops every day and is expanding from a single product into a full platform, so the role grows as you grow.
