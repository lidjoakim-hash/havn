Employer: WAHEGURU AB Organisation Number: 559081-8919 Location: Trelleborg, Skåne, Sweden Industry: Restaurant and Food Services Position: Experienced Indian Cuisine Chef (Indisk specialkock) Employment: Full-Time, Permanent Working Hours: Full-time, including evenings and weekends according to schedule Starting Date: As agreed About WAHEGURU AB WAHEGURU AB is an established Swedish restaurant company specialising in restaurant and food service operations. As part of our plans to develop and expand our restaurant operations, we are seeking an experienced and professionally skilled Indian Cuisine Chef who can contribute specialist culinary knowledge, maintain high food quality standards, and support the continued development of our kitchen. We are particularly interested in candidates with extensive practical experience in authentic North Indian and Punjabi cuisine, traditional tandoor cooking, Indian curry preparation, and professional restaurant kitchen operations. Main Responsibilities The successful candidate will be responsible for: Independently preparing and cooking authentic Indian dishes according to traditional recipes and restaurant quality standards. Managing daily kitchen preparation, including curry bases, marinades, sauces, spice blends, and food preparation. Preparing traditional North Indian and Punjabi dishes, including Butter Chicken, Chicken Tikka Masala, Lamb Karahi, Rogan Josh, Korma, Jalfrezi, and other regional specialities. Operating and managing traditional tandoor ovens for preparing Tandoori Chicken, Chicken Tikka, Seekh Kebab, Lamb Tikka, and freshly baked Indian breads. Preparing different varieties of naan, roti, paratha, and other traditional Indian breads. Preparing authentic biryani, rice dishes, lentil preparations, vegetarian curries, and Indian side dishes. Ensuring consistency in flavour, texture, presentation, cooking methods, and portion sizes. Developing and improving menu items while maintaining authentic Indian culinary traditions. Managing ingredient preparation, stock rotation, food storage, and minimising kitchen waste. Maintaining kitchen hygiene, food safety, temperature controls, and allergen procedures in accordance with Swedish food safety regulations. Coordinating kitchen operations during busy lunch and dinner services. Supporting and training other kitchen employees in Indian cooking techniques when required. Assisting restaurant management with menu planning, food costing, and kitchen efficiency. Required Professional Expertise 1. Indian Curry and Gravy Preparation Candidates must have advanced practical knowledge of traditional Indian curry preparation, including the preparation of onion-tomato gravy bases, makhani sauces, cream-based curries, slow-cooked meat dishes, and vegetarian specialities. 2. Traditional Tandoor Cooking Professional experience operating a traditional clay tandoor oven is required, including meat marination, temperature control, tandoori grilling, kebab preparation, and baking Indian breads. 3. Indian Spices and Traditional Cooking Techniques Candidates must demonstrate strong knowledge of Indian spices, including cumin, coriander, turmeric, cardamom, cloves, fenugreek, garam masala, and traditional spice-roasting and tempering techniques. The chef must be capable of preparing spice blends and marinades from scratch rather than relying exclusively on ready-made commercial sauces. 4. North Indian and Punjabi Cuisine Practical experience preparing Punjabi and North Indian restaurant dishes is particularly important. Candidates should understand traditional preparation methods and be able to maintain authentic flavours across different menu items. 5. Professional Kitchen Management Candidates must be able to organise kitchen preparation independently, maintain food quality during high-volume service, manage ingredients efficiently, and follow professional food hygiene procedures. Qualifications and Experience Minimum five years
