Head to getyourguide.careers to take the first step. Team mission The Financial Reporting team is part of Group Reporting within GetYourGuide's Finance function. We own the financial statements - the core output that tells GetYourGuide's financial story - as well as the accounting standards and policies that underpin how the company reports. We partner closely with other finance teams and across the wider business to ensure that the output is accurate, complete, and of high quality. As GetYourGuide continues to grow and evolve, our team plays a critical role in building the technical expertise and reporting frameworks that will support the company's next chapter. As Financial Reporting Manager, you will own key parts of our financial reporting cycle and serve as a trusted technical accounting authority across the business. You'll bridge accounting policy and business operations - not just applying standards, but actively proposing solutions, advising on reporting implications, and advocating for approaches that work for both the business and its stakeholders.
