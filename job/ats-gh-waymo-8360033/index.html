Facilities Market Specialist, London

Waymo · London, England, United Kingdom, United Kingdom

Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states. Our cars may be self-driving, but Waymo’s workplace services and facilities are steered by the GPS team. The GPS team manages a portfolio that includes oversight of executing multiple teams, strategic business planning with regional site leads, and developing programs to support Waymonaut’s environment and productivity. The team is responsible for the delivery of large-scale operations and portfolio-wide programs ensuring and facilitating the successful delivery of a consistent service program in an extremely fast-paced environment with a high degree of ambiguity. In this role, you will report to the Global Head of Facilities and Occupational Safety. You will: • Provide Tactical Site Oversight: Maintain a highly-visible presence across Waymo's critical operations sites in the assigned market to ensure facilities run smoothly 24/7/365, and the physical environment remains safe, functional, compliant, and mission-ready. • Ensure Service Delivery & Critical Uptime: Drive end-to-end facilities operations service delivery across the assigned market, ensuring high standards of execution that meet critical uptime SLAs. • Manage Budget & Resource Optimization: Oversee and optimize the local facilities budget, operational resources, and space utilization within the assigned market, ensuring that daily maintenance and space utilization adjustments do not disrupt business-critical operations. • Serve as the Local Stakeholder POC: Act as the primary local facilities liaison and build strong partnerships with local market teams (including Fleet Operations, Security, and EHS) and site leads to resolve daily operational friction and align facilities with business needs. • Govern Onsite Vendors & Service Contracts: Provide direct, "on-the-ground" governance and oversight of the contracted Integrated Facilities Management (IFM) service provider, monitoring performance against SLAs and scorecards to immediately correct service gaps. • Support Process & Operational Reporting: Implement and manage local facility dashboards and reporting workflows to analyze site performance data, measure key success metrics, and support high-volume site operations and space utilization. You have: • BA/BS degree in a related field such as Facilities Management, Business Management, or Engineering (or equivalent practical experience). • 5+ years of account management, outsourcing management, or facilities operations experience with a track record of managing multi-site portfolios. • Demonstrated experience managing complex budgets, service delivery contracts, and third-party vendor performance. • Deep understanding of technological advances, change management, organizational leadership, and compliance requirements, and how each supports/drives workplace and facilities strategies. • Excellent verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to present in formal settings and adapt communication to articulate program goals to a wide audience in a simple, straightforward way. • Ability to work in a hybrid schedule in the assigned market location and travel between multiple local site locations within the market area. We prefer: • Certification or Industry Designation in Project Management, Engineering, or Facilities Services (e.g., CFM, FMP, CPM, PMP). •
