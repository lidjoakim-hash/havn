Member of Technical Staff (Enterprise Security Engineer)

Perplexity · San Francisco, United Kingdom

ABOUT THE ROLE Perplexity is growing fast, and every new employee, laptop, SaaS app and integration expands what we have to protect. We want enterprise security that's strong by default, mostly automated, and invisible to people doing their jobs. We're looking for an Enterprise Security Engineer to own the security of how Perplexity works internally: our identities, devices, SaaS applications and corporate access. You'll sit on the Security Engineering team and work closely with IT, building controls and automation together so the secure path is also the easy one. RESPONSIBILITIES - Own identity security: SSO and MFA policy, conditional access and device trust, privileged and admin access, service accounts, and break-glass procedures. - Harden our SaaS stack. Manage OAuth app and integration risk, admin roles, sharing and retention settings, and audit logging across our core collaboration and productivity tools. - Define and enforce endpoint security baselines with IT, across EDR, device management, disk encryption, OS and browser policy, and software allowlisting. - Build access governance that scales: just-in-time and request-based access, regular access reviews, and joiner/mover/leaver automation that removes access promptly and reliably. - Write detections for corporate threats such as account takeover, OAuth abuse, data exfiltration, insider risk and malicious extensions, and respond when they fire. - Automate everything you can. Write code and infrastructure-as-code to configure systems, remediate drift, and replace manual tickets with self-service workflows. - Secure corporate network access and email, including zero-trust remote access, phishing protection and domain security. - Partner with IT, People, Legal and GRC on new tools and vendors, security reviews, and evidence for compliance programs. QUALIFICATIONS - 4+ years in security engineering, with significant time on corporate or enterprise security, identity, or endpoint security. - Hands-on depth with at least one major identity provider (such as Okta or Entra ID), including SSO, SCIM, MFA and conditional access policy. - Experience securing SaaS environments such as Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, Slack, and developer tools, including OAuth and third-party app risk. - Experience with endpoint security and device management across macOS (and ideally Windows and Linux), including EDR, MDM and configuration baselines. - Proficiency in at least one programming language (such as Python or Go) and comfort building automation against vendor APIs. Experience with infrastructure-as-code is a plus. - Experience writing detections and investigating incidents with a SIEM or log platform. - Good judgment about trade-offs between security and productivity, and the ability to roll out controls without slowing the company down. - Bonus: experience at a fast-growing company where you built enterprise security from an early stage. - Bonus: experience securing AI tools and agents in the workplace, such as agent access to SaaS data, browser agents, and AI app governance.
