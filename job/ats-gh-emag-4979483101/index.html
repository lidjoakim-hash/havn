What you’ll have to do: Strategy & Prioritization • Define the vision and strategic direction for delivery capabilities (courier integrations, delivery allocation and processing infrastructure, tracking and notification flows, etc.) and track measurable product objectives aligned with the company's strategy; • Identify opportunities to use AI and AI agents across the transportation and delivery area - for example, improving courier allocation (cost and time optimization), sharpening delivery time estimate accuracy, or dynamically balancing delivery capacity; • Identify business opportunities based on data, market research, and feedback from across the organization; • Prioritize initiatives based on estimated impact (customer experience, revenue, saving) vs. implementation cost, and contribute to business case creation, including risks and alternatives; • Recommend resource allocation toward the highest-impact initiatives, in collaboration with the organization's leadership; • Adapt strategy and priorities to market-specific needs across Romania, Hungary, and Bulgaria, accounting for regulatory and operational differences between markets. Data & Validation • Analyze data to validate strategic hypotheses (testing, metric definition, impact modeling) and monitor product metrics (adoption, performance, or satisfaction); • Identify trends and early signals that should influence the product roadmap, including through competitive analysis and monitoring of emerging technologies.
