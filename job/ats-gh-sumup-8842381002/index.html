Senior GL Accountant Sofia, office-first | Full-time About the team Our Finance team in Sofia is part of SumUp’s global Finance organisation. We help keep financial information accurate, consistent and delivered on time, enabling stakeholders across the business to make informed decisions. By strengthening financial processes, governance and internal controls, we support SumUp’s responsible growth across markets. Working in an international and collaborative environment, you’ll have the opportunity to contribute to the financial foundations of a global fintech company and help make the way we work more efficient and effective. 📍 Explore our Sofia office What you'll do • Process and post vendor invoices and credit notes across several legal entities, keeping accuracy and deadlines on track • Prepare and post monthly accruals and support month-end close activities • Reconcile vendor balances, resolve discrepancies and manage day-to-day vendor communication • Prepare weekly payment proposals and complete weekly bank postings • Support the rollout of a new procurement system (ZIP), including testing and helping stabilise new workflows • Identify process inefficiencies and contribute ideas for automation and standardisation within the team You'll be great for this role if… • Strong knowledge of core accounting principles, including debit and credit reconciliation, accruals and deferrals • Experience processing prepayments and payments in a fast-paced, high-volume environment • Familiarity with SAP or a similar ERP system used for procure to pay processes • Comfort working with Excel, including pivot tables, VLOOKUP and data tables • Excellent English communication skills, both written and verbal • You’re curious about AI and automation, and use - or are keen to use - AI tools responsibly to streamline repetitive tasks, improve accuracy, and make P2P processes more efficient.
