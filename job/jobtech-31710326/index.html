Headsource söker en Strategic Purchaser till stort växande bolag i framkant! Department: Group Function Job Category: Administration: Procurement Job Title: Strategic Purchaser (STR) Level 2 As Associate Procurement Product Leader, you will step into a dynamic role at the intersection of Procurement, technology and business, helping turn business needs into coordinated Procurement action. You will collaborate closely across the Procurement organization and with key stakeholders in Project Management and Production, building strong relationships and ensuring effective coordination across functions. You will help translate product delivery needs into clear Procurement activities, make sure that information reaches the relevant procurement and project resource, monitor progress, risks, milestones and commitments within your assigned scope. Your main focus will be on turning needs into action, connecting the right people and driving activities forward through analysis, coordination and structured follow-up. The role gives you a broad understanding of how Procurement contributes throughout the product and project lifecycle and provides an excellent opportunity to develop your skills in procurement, supply chain and project management. You will work independently with defined assignments and gradually take on broader scope and responsibility as your experience grows. Your responsibilities will include Coordinate and secure procurement activities are executed within assigned products or projects, working closely with relevant Procurement teams and stakeholders,primarily within Production and Project Management. Drive Procurement actions forward, follow milestones, risks and commitments, and ensure agreed activities are completed on time. Support project and product forums with relevant procurement information, address issues and turn agreed priorities and decisions into concrete actions and follow-up. Work closely with Buyers, Strategic Purchasers, Supplier Quality and other Procurement functions to coordinate and progress activities across functional boundaries. Identify and follow up supply chain risks, opportunities and upcoming business needs, supporting actions to address them. Maintain clear communication and action visibility between Procurement and relevant project and production stakeholders. Contribute to continuous improvement of Procurement processes, tools and ways of working, including practical implementation of improvements. Build a strong understanding of our products, suppliers, business requirements and Procurement organization. Your profile We believe you are at the beginning of your professional career and motivated by working in a role that combines business, technology, supply chain and project execution. You are structured, analytical and curious, with an ability to coordinate different activities and keep track of details while understanding the bigger picture. You enjoy working with others and are comfortable building relationships across different functions. Skills: For this position, we are looking for someone with: A relevant university degree, preferably a Master's degree in Engineering, Industrial Engineering and Management, Supply Chain Management, Business Administration or similar. Up to a few years of relevant professional experience. Experience may come from internships, graduate assignments or roles within procurement, supply chain, project management, operations or a technical environment. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills. A structured way of working and the ability to coordinate several activities in parallel. Good communication and collaboration skills. An interest in developing within Procurement, supply chain and project-oriented ways of working. Good communication skills in English and Swedish Previous experience from procurement, supply chain, project management or a manufacturing/technology environment is an advantage.
