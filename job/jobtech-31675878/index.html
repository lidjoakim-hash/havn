About the job Valjochslapp is currently looking for detail-oriented and motivated delivery drivers for evening shifts in the northern Stockholm area. As a proud partner to Airmee, we deliver parcels directly to customers' doorsteps with a strong focus on quality, punctuality, and excellent service. This is a part-time position, making it perfect to combine with studies or other daytime commitments. Working hours are scheduled for evenings, typically between 16:00 and 23:00. You will pick up the van from Vällingby to start your shift at our client's warehouse in Järfälla, load your vehicle, and follow an optimized, pre-planned route via the delivery app. Your responsibilities: Loading parcels into the delivery van at the terminal. Safe and time-efficient distribution of parcels to residential addresses. Interacting politely with customers and managing delivery statuses in the app. Taking care of the company vehicle and keeping it clean. Requirements: Valid Swedish or EU B-category driver's license (Mandatory). Your driver's license must be at least 6 months old (Mandatory). Experience or comfort driving automatic transmission vans in urban environments. Good communication skills in English or Swedish to correspond with customers and support. Reliable, punctual, and physically fit for a fast-paced environment. What we offer: Modern, automatic delivery vans provided by the company for the shifts. Fixed evening schedules with a supportive team. Onboarding and practical training in the Airmee system. Type of employment: Part-time (Evenings) Location: Stockholm (Vällingby / Järfälla) Salary: Hourly rate Öppen för alla Vi fokuserar på din kompetens, inte dina övriga förutsättningar.
