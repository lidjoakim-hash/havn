Transport is at the core of modern society. Imagine using your expertise to shape sustainable transport and infrastructure solutions for the future. If you seek to make a difference on a global scale, working with next-gen technologies and the sharpest collaborative teams, then we could be a perfect match. What you will do Are you ready to lead the future of software integration? Join Volvo Group Buses as a Senior System Design Engineer and play a pivotal role in shaping our development processes and delivering cutting-edge solutions to our customers. The role would involve building and maintaining HIL (Hardware In the Loop) rigs ensuring functionality, performance, and automation of test procedures. It is a position that requires a collaborative person that has strong communication and teamwork skills. Key Responsibilities for the role: • Test Rig Development: Designing, developing, building and maintaining test rigs, for automotive components and systems. • System Integration: Integrating hardware and software components into the test rig environment. • Test Automation: Developing and implementing automated test procedures to improve efficiency and reduce manual testing. • Requirements Analysis: Working with system requirements to define test cases and ensure proper test coverage. • Performance Optimization: Analyzing test results and optimizing the performance of the test rigs. • Documentation: Creating and maintaining technical documentation for the test rigs. • Collaboration: Working closely with other engineers and teams, including software developers, system verification engineers, and other stakeholders. Required Skills and Experience: • Expertise: Experience with MAZE HIL systems is highly desirable. • Automotive Engineering: A strong understanding of automotive systems, including electrical and electronic systems, is crucial. • Test Method Development: Experience in developing and implementing test methods and procedures. • Programming/Scripting: Proficiency in programming languages such as Python, or C++. • Problem-Solving: Strong analytical and problem-solving skills. • Communication: Excellent verbal and written communication skills, including the ability to collaborate effectively in a team environment.
