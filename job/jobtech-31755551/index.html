PhD student in pavement engineering / AI based analysis

Statens Väg- och Transportforskningsinst · Linköping, Sweden

VTI, the Swedish National Road and Transport Research Institute, is looking for a PhD student in pavement engineering with an emphasis on AI based automated data back-analysis method for pavement conditions and performance predictions. The position will be based in Linköping or Stockholm, Sweden About the position The goal of this doctoral project is to increase our knowledge and create new competencies regarding deterioration and condition development of flexible roads at the network level by utilizing artificial intelligence and large datasets. Collected data from rolling road surface testing methods (non-destructive testing) as well as climate data and registered road construction data will be analysed and used to provide reliable information about the structural condition of the pavement structure. Your assignment will be to develop methods to interpret rolling road surface testing data together with climate and pavement layer data to predict the pavement condition. The aim is to use analysis results to predict the mechanical performance of different layers in the road structure as a function of time. You will work in collaboration with the division’s professor of Road and Railway Engineering. The Swedish Transport Administration is funding the project for four years. Your work will use information from rolling road surface measurements from Sweden and the Nordic region. Although you will devote yourself mainly to your doctoral education, you will also work to some extent with other research, field and laboratory testing, and the dissemination of research results to various actors and stakeholders. Your qualifications You have an MSc or equivalent in engineering or sciences, e.g. within Civil and Environmental Engineering, Mechanical Engineering or other relevant fields including computer or computational sciences. You have an interest in data analysis and modelling, as well as laboratory and experimental work. Experience in the field of pavement/structural/geotechnical engineering, programming and in computational modelling is beneficial. The job duties require a solid foundation in the relevant theory and methodology, and you must be able to compile, analyse and present material independently. The work will entail numerous contacts with various organisations within the transport field, cooperative partners and other researchers. You must consequently possess both good organisational and cooperative skills, as well as strong skills in written and spoken English. A good knowledge of Swedish is advantageous, but not a requirement. We will place major emphasis on your personal characteristics, analytical abilities, and ability to take personal initiative and work independently. Duration of employment The position is fixed term, with increments of a maximum of one year at a time and will be reassessed on an ongoing basis each year. The position is predicated upon your acceptance into the postgraduate programme at KTH. As a PhD student, you will be employed by VTI while simultaneously being enrolled in the doctoral programme in Civil and Architectural Engineering at the Division of Highway and Railway Engineering, KTH Royal Institute of Technology, Stockholm, Sweden. About VTI The Swedish National Road and Transport Research Institute (VTI) is an independent and internationally prominent research institute in the transport sector with some 250 employees and an annual turnover of roughly MSEK 300. Our principal task is to conduct commissioned research and development related to infrastructure, traffic and transport. We are dedicated to the continuous development of knowledge pertaining to the transport sector, and in this way contribute actively to the attainment of the goals of Swedish transport policy. More information Additional information about the position is available from Research Director Björn Kalman or Professor Sigurdur Erlingsson, +46 (0)13 204123 as well docent Denis Jelagin, +46 (0)8 790 8698.
