ABOUT US At OakNorth, we’re on a mission to empower ambitious businesses and the communities they serve. Since 2015, we’ve lent over $21 billion across the UK and US, helped create more than 58,000 new homes and 36,000 new jobs, and supported hundreds of thousands of personal savers, all while driving economic growth in the markets we serve. ABOUT THE TEAM We manage a portfolio of complex, high-value real estate exposures where performance has deteriorated or the original business plan is no longer achievable. Our job is to protect and maximise the Bank’s recovery, taking ownership from the earliest signs of distress through to restructuring, consensual resolution or, where necessary, enforcement. We work across a broad range of real estate assets, sponsors and capital structures. Our approach is commercially minded, analytically rigorous and decisive. We engage directly with borrowers, sponsors, investors, advisers and legal counsel, and expect our people to take control, challenge conventional thinking and deliver the best achievable outcome for the Bank. ABOUT THE ROLE We are looking for an exceptional real estate restructuring professional to join as a Director. You will own a portfolio of complex and often contentious distressed real estate exposures, leading recovery strategy from initial diagnosis through to final resolution. This takes more than technical credit or restructuring knowledge. You will combine strong commercial judgement, deep real estate expertise, financial and analytical capability, and the confidence to lead difficult negotiations and decide under pressure. You will understand the property and the business, identify what has gone wrong, establish realistic recovery options and take control. That may mean resetting the business plan, restructuring the debt, securing additional equity or capital, negotiating a consensual exit, or pursuing formal enforcement where it offers the best recovery. You will present clear, evidence-based recommendations to senior management and Credit Committee, and work closely with Portfolio Management, Credit and Legal. This is a hands-on role for someone who has personally led complex restructurings and can show a track record of successful outcomes. You will report to a Senior Director, Workout & Restructuring. WHAT YOU’LL DO Own and deliver recovery strategies - Take end-to-end ownership of a portfolio of complex distressed real estate exposures, from initial diagnosis through to resolution. - Develop and recommend commercially grounded recovery strategies across the full range of options: business-plan restructuring, asset repositioning, debt restructuring, new capital, sponsor solutions, consensual sale, refinance, enforcement and insolvency. - Understand the asset, borrower, sponsor, capital structure and realistic exit routes. Challenge assumptions and identify the true risks to recovery. - Continuously assess alternative strategies, balancing recovery value, risk, timing, execution certainty and cost. - Act decisively to protect the Bank’s position, preserve asset value and maintain leverage over borrowers and other stakeholders. Lead complex restructurings and negotiations - Lead negotiations with borrowers, sponsors, shareholders, equity investors, mezzanine lenders, co-lenders and other stakeholders. - Structure and execute consensual restructurings, including debt amendments, maturity extensions, capitalisation, repayment arrangements, equity injections, new money and business plan changes. - Challenge sponsors and borrowers, hold firm on the Bank’s position and use negotiation and legal leverage effectively. - Recognise when consensual solutions will not deliver the best outcome and recommend enforcement or insolvency decisively. - Appoint and manage external advisers, including solicitors, valuers, receivers, insolvency practitioners and asset managers.
