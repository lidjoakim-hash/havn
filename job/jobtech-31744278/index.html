Company description: At NKT in Karlskrona, we develop and manufacture high voltage power cables that enable the transition to renewable energy. Here, you will be part of an international engineering centre with advanced high voltage test halls, modern cable production and the cable laying vessel NKT Victoria. As Connectors, we collaborate to develop innovative technology that connects a greener and more sustainable world. NKT is headquartered in Denmark and operates in more than 30 countries.NKT - We connect a greener world. www.NKT.com. Job description:At NKT, we are engaged in innovative projects that drive the green transition. Our complex cable installations, both offshore and onshore, utilize the latest technology to ensure quality and efficiency. Managing the entire process from production to testing requires careful planning and coordination. We’re now looking for a communicative and motivated IT Service Manager to manage business-critical IT services and digital projects. You’ll play a key role in supporting our global operations and developing scalable IT solutions that meet evolving business needs. Take responsibility for business-critical IT services and lead projectsAs a Service Manager, you will manage the full lifecycle of assigned IT services and ensure reliable, secure and cost-effective delivery. Working closely with business stakeholders, IT teams and external vendors, you will translate business requirements into effective solutions and support continuous improvement. Your main responsibilities will include: •    Ensure stable IT services across on-premises and cloud environments.•    Manage and coordinate changes, releases and digital projects.•    Build solid stakeholder and vendor relationships.•    Manage service improvements while ensuring IT governance and security. You will report to Sr Manager Digital Solutions. The location for this position is Karlskrona. Communicative IT Service Manager with digitalization experienceTo perform well in this role, you have solid knowledge of digitalization and the ability to analyze business needs and translate them into IT solutions. You are structured, cost-conscious and strategic, with good communication and collaboration skills. Knowledge of IT infrastructure is an advantage. Your CV should also include: •    Relevant education in IT or equivalent experience•    Professional experience in delivering and managing IT services and systems•    Familiarity with agile methods and service management frameworks such as DevOps and ITIL•    Experience managing external vendors•    Fluency in spoken and written English. Swedish language skills are meritorious.  Join a skilled team in an international environmentWe believe that a diverse organization enables sustainable performance, and that an inclusive and welcoming culture makes for a better workplace. We are committed to a diverse organization and a culture where people from different backgrounds and perspectives can thrive and be inspired to perform at their best. You will have exciting opportunities to develop your career and enjoy attractive benefits. Are you interested in a career at a growing company while contributing to the green transition? Then we would like to talk to you. Even if you don’t meet all the requirements – tell us. The opportunity to grow and contribute to our future is important, where your involvement and potential matter greatly.   “Working as a Service Manager in NKT gives you the opportunity to see how many different parts of the whole NKT business works. You are not working towards one business area or function, but many. As a Service Manager you cannot know all the details, but you get a good understanding in general. Focus is always on understanding the business needs and translating them into requirements that will end up in tangible solutions.
