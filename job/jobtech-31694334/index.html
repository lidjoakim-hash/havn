Siemens Energy AB · Finspång, Sweden

A Snapshot of Your Day Your day as a manager in engineering will always focus on the continuous development of your team but it will also entail cross-functional discussions regarding our processes and tools. Your team will work with leading technical information deliveries in multiple order projects together, and you will be there to guide and support them. As a part of the management team in customer order engineering, you will join a team committed to creating the best delivery to our customers. We work together to implement the strategies of our future. How You’ll Make an Impact Develop the team of sub project managers with coaching and training Personnel and budget responsibility for your team, securing that the correct competence is in place to execute all planned activities Fostering psychological safety and a good working environment for people to thrive and develop at work Work strategically with efficiency and quality of technical information deliveries Being a team player with focus on cooperation and building strong partnerships with your interfaces, securing smooth order execution and efficient customer solutions What You Bring As a leader you build trust and collaboration around yourself and in your team. You enjoy working with people and are happy when people around you are successful You are strategic, with genuine interest of leadership, people development and great communications skills. Previous leadership experience is an advantage You have the ability to build networks & communicate in all directions. You value different experiences and cultures and work with inclusion to ensure you get the benefits from a diverse team. A good understanding of the delivery process for Medium Gas Turbines is an advantage as a lot of the work circles around the order projects. You take pride in solving challenges together, focusing on solutions and how to reach them together You are interested in continuous change and development. About the Team Your team is responsible for leading deliveries of technical information products and consists of sub project managers located in Sweden & India. It is a team of about 25 individuals, both employees and partners. The team has a good mix of experience and tenure, ranging from newly employed colleagues to people with more than 20 years of experience. The team fosters a culture of collaboration and support, working together to solve issues and support personal development. You will work together with a team lead who supports the group in the project-related tasks and guides them in their way of working. Tog You report to the head of technical information and is a part of the technical information leadership team. We work together to find the best solutions and value everyone’s input. You will join a team where we have fun in our daily work but also come together to solve tough problems and questions. Our Gas Services division offers Low-emission power generation through service and decarbonization. Zero or low emission power generation and all gas turbines under one roof, steam turbines and generators. Decarbonization opportunities through service offerings, modernization, and digitalization of the fleet. We can offer you employment benefits such as: reduction of working hours, advance vacation, health care allowance and an eventual possibility to a flexible working place. Who is Siemens Energy? At Siemens Energy, we are more than just an energy technology company. With ~100,000 dedicated employees in more than 90 countries, we develop the energy systems of the future, ensuring that the growing energy demand of the global community is met reliably and sustainably. The technologies created in our research departments and factories drive the energy transition and provide the base for one sixth of the world's electricity generation.  Our global team is committed to making sustainable, reliable, and affordable energy a reality by pushing the boundaries of what is possible.
