Production Team Member - Premium Salmon Processing (InnovaMar, Frøya)

SalMar As, Avd Slakteri- og Bearbeiding · Sweden

💰 ca. 400 000–480 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 31.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

SalMar is one of the world's leading salmon producers - and the most sustainable food producer on the planet (Global 100, Davos 2026!). With operations across Norway, sales offices in Asia, and a team of over 3,345 people, we turn innovation, passion, and pure Norwegian seawater into world-class salmon. Want to help shape the future of sustainable seafood? Dive in with us! Head office is located at InnovaMar on Frøya in Trøndelag. Why Join SalMar? Become part of a diverse team representing over 40 nationalities. At SalMar, we're more than a company - we're a community built on innovation, collaboration, and excellence. Here, you'll work in a modern facility with access to an on-site gym and cafeteria serving good, nutritious meals, while being part of a global leader in sustainable aquaculture. If you're a dedicated team player who values efficiency and strives for outstanding results, this is your chance to shine. Your Role As a Production Team Member at InnovaMar, you'll be at the core of our operations, contributing to the harvesting and processing of premium salmon. Your tasks will include manual cutting, packing, sorting, and preparation. Work shifts alternate between day and evening, offering flexibility and balance. What We're Looking For Experience in salmon or food production is an advantage, but not required. Good communication skills in English or Norwegian. Strong team spirit and collaboration skills. Ability to provide a valid background check from the Norwegian Civil Aviation Authorities - Clean Criminal records(we assist with info). VALID EU PASSPORT OR NORWEGIAN PERMANENT RESIDENCY/ WORK PERMIT (mandatory). Driving license is essential - living on Frøya requires personal responsibility for transportation. For permanent relocation, you should have the opportunity to purchase a car. Personal Qualities Reliable and punctual. Strong work ethic. Flexible and adaptable. Responsible and positive attitude. What We Offer Competitive salary and benefits. A supportive and inclusive work environment. Comprehensive pension and insurance agreements. Opportunities for long-term employment and growth. Access to an on-site gym and a cafeteria serving good, nutritious meals. Join a workplace that values your well-being, growth, and success. Ready to make an impact in sustainable seafood production? Apply now and join SalMar at InnovaMar, Frøya - where your work makes a difference. We value diversity and inclusion and encourage all qualified candidates to apply, regardless of gender, age, religion, ethnicity, or other individual differences.
