Product Manager/Product Owner

EPAM Systems (Nordic) AB · Göteborg, Sweden

We are looking for a Product Manager/Product Owner to join our team in a hybrid working model in Goteborg and help our clients and internal teams transform business needs into well-defined technology and AI solutions while leading their delivery. You will work closely with stakeholders to understand how the business operates, shape requirements that teams can build and adopt and guide cross-functional teams through agile delivery. As a consultant, you will combine analytical rigor and structured delivery management with an advisory mindset, helping stakeholders prioritize, make informed decisions and realize measurable value. You will take ownership of one or more products or workstreams, working with client stakeholders, architects, engineers and specialists. Assignments may vary in size, industry and delivery model, requiring flexibility to support diverse priorities across client engagements and internal initiatives. Responsibilities Work with business stakeholders and users to understand goals, current processes, pain points, and constraints, and translate them into clear problem statements, product scope, and measurable success criteria Facilitate discovery activities such as interviews, workshops, and process walkthroughs, and map current and target business processes and capabilities Define, prioritize, and maintain requirements as epics, user stories, and testable acceptance criteria, covering business rules, data, integrations, edge cases, and non-functional needs Set up and run agile delivery, adapting Scrum, Kanban, or hybrid approaches to the product, and refine the backlog, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives Plan and track scope, schedule, budget, resources, and dependencies, and keep delivery transparent through regular reporting and clear status updates Identify and manage risks, issues, and dependencies early, and escalate decisions beyond the product's scope in a timely way Act as a main point of contact for client and internal stakeholders, facilitate workshops, steering meetings, and reviews, and build agreement across teams Coordinate cross-functional, often distributed teams, including vendors and partners, creating a collaborative and accountable working environment Identify where AI can support business processes, and manage AI and data initiatives from discovery and proof of concept through production adoption, working with AI specialists on feasibility, data readiness, evaluation, and responsible use Use AI tools responsibly to support analysis and product work, such as summarizing input, drafting requirements, planning, and reporting, while following client and company data-handling rules Requirements Proven experience in
