Are you an analytical and structured finance professional with experience in cash flow forecasting? In this role, you will take ownership of cash flow planning in large-scale projects, working closely with stakeholders across multiple functions to ensure accurate forecasts and support sound financial decision-making. About the position Perido are looking for a Treasury analyst for our client in Stockholm. In this role, you will play a key part in ensuring accurate and reliable cash flow forecasts across large-scale projects. You will work closely with stakeholders across finance, procurement, contracts and project delivery, gathering and analysing financial information to support effective cash flow planning and decision-making. Your daily tasks As a Treasury Analyst, you will be responsible for coordinating and managing the project cash flow forecasting process from start to finish. You will gather information from multiple project teams and workstreams, analyse payment schedules and contractual terms, and translate these into reliable cash flow forecasts. The role involves maintaining and developing forecasting models, conducting scenario analyses and following up on payment milestones and financial commitments. You will collaborate with stakeholders across different functions, ensuring that relevant information is collected, validated and documented. This includes understanding project payment cycles, such as payment certifications, invoice processing and advance payments, as well as identifying potential discrepancies or risks that may affect cash flow projections. Your characteristics We are looking for someone who is structured, analytical and confident in communicating with stakeholders at all levels. You have the ability to build relationships across different functions and obtain the information needed to produce accurate forecasts, even when working with incomplete or unstructured data. You are proactive, pragmatic and comfortable taking ownership of your responsibilities in a fast-paced and complex environment. Furthermore, you have strong organisational skills and can manage several parallel workstreams and deadlines while maintaining a high level of accuracy. Does this sound like an exciting opportunity? We look forward to reading your application! Please submit your application for this role in English. Qualifications: At least a B.Sc. in Accounting, Finance or Business 3–5 years of relevant experience in cash flow forecasting within large construction projects and/or process manufacturing industries Demonstrated experience in cash forecasting, payment scheduling, or financial planning within a project environment Proven track record of working across multiple workstreams or packages simultaneously, coordinating inputs from diverse stakeholders Strong command of Excel for model building and scenario analysis Ability to interpret contract structures and understand how payment terms translate into cash timing Solid understanding of large project payment cycle: certification, invoice processing, and advance payment mechanics Meriting: Prior exposure to large-scale infrastructure, EPCM, or complex construction projects strongly preferred Experience with project management tools (ACC) is an advantage Experience from cash management or other treasury collaborations Contract type and hours Full-time consulting assignment until 2028-01-18.
