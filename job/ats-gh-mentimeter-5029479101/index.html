Mentimeter is an engagement company with a simple goal: to make every leader better. Through interaction, insights and recommendations, its platform helps organizations turn participation into better understanding and stronger performance. Founded in Stockholm in 2012, Mentimeter is used by 20 million people each month across 1.3 million organizations. At Mentimeter, we believe better leadership starts with listening and that the strongest results are achieved together. That’s why we foster a culture built on curiosity, collaboration and shared ownership. By joining us, you’ll help leaders turn participation into deeper understanding and stronger performance, advancing our ambition to help more than one billion people listen, learn and work better together. The Role As Brand Director, you'll lead Mentimeter's brand team and the creative work that helps people understand, recognize and choose us. You'll set an ambitious creative direction, develop the team, and build the systems that help great work reach further. Our mission is to make every leader better. We have a clear brand direction grounded in that mission, and your role is to develop how we express it across audiences, channels and markets. You'll own our Brand Platform and Creative Platform, building on the strategy in place to create ideas and storytelling that people connect with and remember. Brand sits within Design at Mentimeter. We see our product and brand as one experience, and you’ll work closely with product managers, designers, researchers, analytics and the design system team to bring that to life. Our brand designers, copywriters and producers work as an internal agency, supporting teams across the business through a retainer model that serves both of our go-to-market motions: product-led growth (PLG) and Enterprise. You'll lead that agency end to end: setting priorities, directing the work and developing how it gets made. That includes building AI-enabled tools and ways of working that help colleagues across Mentimeter produce on-brand material themselves, freeing the creative team to focus where their expertise makes the biggest difference. You'll report to our Chief Design Officer, join the Design Leadership Team, and work closely with our CDO and CEO on the direction of the brand. What we are looking for You're a creative leader with depth in your craft and the ability to make the people around you better. You can turn a strategic direction into a strong creative idea, explain why it works, and help a team develop it into work that delivers. We're particularly interested in a copywriting background. You enjoy working with language, ideas and storytelling, and can move comfortably between shaping a campaign concept, working through the details of a headline and coaching someone else through their approach. You'll join a team with expertise across brand design, copywriting and production, bringing your own craft and creative direction to help develop both the work and the people behind it. You also think about what makes good work possible beyond your own involvement. You've built tools, standards or processes that others can use, and you're actively exploring how AI can improve creative production. We'd like to hear what you've tried to build, where it worked, where it fell short and how your thinking changed as a result. Experience across both agency and in-house or product environments would be especially valuable. You understand how to serve different stakeholders, prioritise competing needs and keep developing a brand over time.
