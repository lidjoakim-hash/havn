Toast creates technology to help restaurants and local businesses succeed in a digital world, helping business owners operate, increase sales, engage customers, and keep employees happy. ABOUT THE ROLE At Toast, we’re building the future of the restaurant industry—and our Global Tax team is building the foundation that makes it all possible. The International Tax Manager is a highly visible, hands-on role responsible for supporting Toast's non-U.S. tax matters across our growing international footprint. The role will have a primary focus on foreign corporate income tax compliance and reporting, while also providing support across local statutory matters, tax audits, and other international tax requirements. Working closely with the U.S.-based Corporate Tax team, local-based business partners and external advisors, this role will help coordinate international tax compliance, support quarterly and annual tax reporting, monitor evolving local tax requirements, and provide tax support for business expansion and other cross-border initiatives. WHAT YOU’LL DO International Income Tax & Compliance • Coordinate and review corporate income tax compliance for Toast's non-U.S. entities, working with accounting teams and external advisors. • Support the international components of the quarterly and annual income tax provision, including current and deferred tax calculations and related reporting. • Monitor international income tax developments and assess potential impacts to Toast's foreign operations. • Support local tax audits, inquiries, assessments, and statutory tax requirements across international jurisdictions. • Coordinate with the Corporate Tax team on transfer pricing, intercompany transactions, and other cross-border tax matters. • Lead international R&D tax credit and incentive activities in partnership with the Corporate Tax team, business stakeholders, and external advisors. • Oversee ongoing compliance with IDA grant commitments, including review of the required tracking, reporting, and coordination with relevant business stakeholders. International Business Support • Support tax analysis related to new-country entry, changes to Toast's international operating model, and other business expansion initiatives. • Partner with Legal, Finance, Accounting, and other stakeholders to identify tax requirements associated with new business activities and cross-border transactions. • Coordinate with external advisors to obtain jurisdiction-specific technical guidance where appropriate. Process Improvement & Governance • Identify opportunities to simplify and standardize international indirect tax processes and documentation. • Help improve the consistency and quality of tax data, workpapers, documentation, and control across non-U.S. entities • Participate in automation, tax technology, and process improvement initiatives across the Global Indirect Tax function. WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR Required • 6+ years of progressive corporate tax experience or international tax experience, ideally with a combination of public accounting and in-house experience • Chartered Accountant, CPA, CTA, or other equivalent professional qualification • Strong knowledge of corporate income tax compliance and reporting, with experience supporting multinational companies • Solid understanding of accounting principles, financial statements, and the interaction between accounting and tax reporting • Experience coordinating with external tax advisors and managing multiple jurisdictional compliance requirements • Strong written and verbal communication skills with the ability to collaborate across functions, cultures, and geographies • Demonstrated ability to operate independently, exercise sound judgment, manage multiple priorities, and drive work to completion • Willingness to work flexible hours with global business • Must be able to multitask, work efficiently under tight deadlines and proactively track and report progress
