About the role Adyen's commercial partners are involved in a large share of our new business and a significant portion of the volume we process today. The next stage of growth depends on how well we connect partners to the rest of our commercial engine and on how deliberately we place new bets with them. As Strategic Initiatives & Growth Lead, you will own programs that turn partner relationships into measurable commercial outcomes. You will run initiatives that bring new products to market through partners, align Partnerships with Account Management, BDRs and Sales around shared revenue goals, and incubate the next set of high-impact commercial plays with key partners and segments. This is a cross-functional role with no direct reporting line over the teams you work with. You will lead through clarity, data and the strength of your plans.
