PROPERTY COORDINATOR - PANORAMA VIEW HOUSE Flåm, Norway A welcoming room, a thoughtful reply and the little details that make a stay special - if that sounds like your kind of work, come and join us. We're looking for an experienced Property Coordinator to care for Panorama View House in Flåm. You'll help guests enjoy their stay while spending your own season in Norway's fjord region. This is a practical role with independence: you'll organise cleaning and property preparation, look after reservations and help guests when they need you. It suits someone who enjoys taking ownership and seeing the results of a job done well. You'll report to the CEO and work closely with HR. A part-time colleague will help one day a week, and we hope this season will be the start of working together again next year. Your part in the team • Help each stay run smoothly through reservations and messages on Booking.com and Airbnb. • Clean and prepare the property between stays, making it ready and welcoming for arriving guests. • Keep the property clean, tidy and well presented throughout the season. • Be a friendly point of contact when guests need help. • Order supplies and collect or receive deliveries. • Help keep staff accommodation ready for use and check its condition after employees move out. • Plan your day around bookings, arrivals and departures. • Coordinate your part-time colleague's tasks and prepare the schedule with HR around their availability. • Share weekly updates with the CEO, raise important matters promptly and report staffaccommodation issues to HR. You'll feel at home in this role if... • You have experience in accommodation operations, housekeeping and guest service. • You know your way around Booking.com and Airbnb. • You enjoy working independently and notice the details that make a property feel cared for. • You're organised, practical and comfortable finding solutions to everyday problems. • You communicate with guests in a friendly, professional way. • You speak English at B2-C1 level and hold a valid category B driving licence. • You're comfortable using a computer and online booking platforms. What's waiting for you • A full-time, hourly-paid role from late May to early September. • The prospect of returning for the next season. • Independence in your daily work, direct contact with the CEO and support from HR. • Help finding accommodation for your season in Norway. • A staff meal on each working day. Join our team and spend the season surrounded by Norway's breathtaking fjords! With food trucks in Flåm and other beautiful locations, we're all about great food, friendly faces, and making people feel welcome. We'd love to have you on board and share an unforgettable season together! Send your CV and recomendation: job&#64;grpnorway.com
