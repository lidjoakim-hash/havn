TL;DR - We’re seeking exceptional Fullstack Product Engineers to help build and scale Lovable’s Integrations Platform. You will work across our entire stack to connect millions of applications and AI agents to third-party services, LLM providers, and each other while building product experiences that drive adoption across our user base. WHY LOVABLE? Lovable is the software creation platform that gives people the power to act on the problems closest to them. For decades, turning an idea into software required so much capital, technical fluency, and time that many ideas never came to life. Lovable is the counterargument: a platform for all people with ideas, ambition, and problems worth solving. From solopreneurs to small business owners to teams at companies like Adidas and Zendesk, people have built over 60 million projects on Lovable since its launch in November 2024. And we’re just getting started. We’re building a generational company from Stockholm, with growing teams in London, Boston, New York, and San Francisco. Our team is small, talent-dense, and moving quickly, with a culture rooted in extreme ownership, high velocity, and low-ego collaboration. We look for people who care deeply, ship fast, and are eager to make a dent in the world. Lovable is one of TIME’s 100 Most Influential Companies and has been recognized on the Forbes AI 50 and CNBC Disruptor 50, reflecting our momentum as one of Europe’s fastest-growing AI companies and one of the most ambitious places to build in this next era of software. WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR - 5+ years of experience building and shipping full-stack software products - Strong product intuition, with a focus on feature adoption, developer ergonomics, and user satisfaction - Hands-on experience with modern frontend frameworks, design systems, and product analytics - Commitment to service reliability and building high-quality user experiences - Ability to move comfortably between user-facing product work and the services and infrastructure that power it Nice to have: - Experience integrating third-party APIs, OAuth flows, and AI/LLM providers - Experience building agentic workflows and tool-calling interfaces - Background in user growth, driving feature adoption, or building developer platforms WHAT YOU’LL DO - In one sentence: Build the connectivity layer that gives millions of Lovable apps and AI agents access to the services and capabilities they need. - Build integrations with third-party services and leading AI model providers - Create user-facing product experiences that make integrations intuitive to discover, configure, and use - Drive integration usage and adoption by iterating based on user feedback and product data - Identify new opportunities and expand support for third-party services across the Lovable ecosystem - Empower AI agents with dynamic integration capabilities and inter-app connectivity - Build and evolve the backend services and infrastructure that power integrations at scale - Ensure our services run reliably while continuously improving the experience and adoption across our user base - Collaborate closely with product, design, and experienced engineers to take features from idea to production OUR TECH STACK We're building with tools that both humans and AI love: - Frontend: React - Backend: Golang and Rust - Cloud: Cloudflare, GCP, AWS, Many LLM providers - DevOps & Tooling: Github Actions, Grafana, OTEL, infra-as-code (Terraform) And always on the lookout for what's next!
