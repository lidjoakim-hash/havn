Job Description Background & Objective In heat pump sound power declarations under ISO 3741 (direct method) and EN 12102-1, spatial averaging uncertainty of sound pressure is the dominating contributor to overall measurement reproducibility. In Bosch’s reverberation test chamber (chamber volume approximately 250 cubic meters), low modal density below the Schroeder frequency (around 255 Hz) and discrete compressor tones sometimes cause high spatial variance across stationary microphones in low-to-mid frequency region (50 – 1500 Hz). The primary objective of this Master thesis is to minimize spatial averaging uncertainty (in aforementioned frequency range) in said chamber by investigating and optimizing two complementary physical levers: Active Diffusion (w. a rotating diffuser): Maximizing dynamic mode-mixing while preventing aerodynamic blade / structural self-noise and cyclic averaging errors. Low-Frequency Modal Absorption: Increasing modal bandwidth to smooth out spatial variance while respecting ISO 3741 reverberation limits (T60 at least > what-standard-requires seconds) Scope of Work Diffuser Self-Noise vs. Speed Envelope: Map chamber background noise versus vane rotational speed (0 to 10 RPM, tip speed up to 2.0 m/s) to determine the safe operational limit where aerodynamic wind noise OR structural noise does not corrupt heat pump measurements. Active Diffusion & Integration Time: Quantify spatial variance reduction for pure tones and compressor harmonics; validate the rule of averaging time to integer multiples of full vane revolutions. Microphone Array Optimization: Determine the minimum number of stationary microphones (such as 6, 12, or higher) and required spacing (minimum distance greater than half wavelength) needed to reliably achieve Grade 1 precision accuracy. Low-Frequency Absorption Assessment: Develop and validate targeted absorption and damping treatments to the chamber to increase the modal overlap at frequencies<315 Hz, while ensuring the chamber absorption satisfies ISO 3741 criteria. Key Deliverables Diffuser Operating Envelope & Self-Noise Guideline: Recommendations on vane RPM limits, tip speed thresholds, revolution-locked integration rules and self-noise-reducing measures. Optimized Microphone Array Setup/Configuration: Quantified trade-off between active diffusion, absorption, and the required microphone count. Low-Frequency Absorption Evaluation Report: Assessment of modal damping at f<315 Hz, on spatial standard deviation and chamber compliance. Master’s Thesis Report & Defense Presentation: Academic thesis report and an internal presentation of practical laboratory recommendations.
