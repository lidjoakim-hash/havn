*Job Title: Senior Network Architect* *Location:* Stockholm, Sweden *Work Arrangement:* Hybrid – 50% remote *Assignment Period:* 15 November 2026 – 31 May 2027 *Application Deadline:* 6 November 2026 *Job Description* We are looking for an experienced Senior Network Architect to join a network and security architecture team for an exciting consulting assignment in Stockholm. The selected candidate will collaborate with network teams across different locations, contribute to technical solution design, prepare architecture proposals, coordinate with stakeholders, and ensure successful implementation within project timelines. *Key Responsibilities* - Design and develop network and network security architecture solutions. - Prepare technical solution designs and architecture documentation. - Develop High-Level Design (HLD), Low-Level Design (LLD), and Architecture Design Documents (ADD). - Work with network and security technologies. - Ensure solutions meet technical, security, and business requirements. - Collaborate with technical teams and stakeholders throughout implementation. *Required Skills and Experience* - Strong experience in Network Architecture and Security Architecture. - Hands-on expertise in Cisco and Aruba technologies. - Good knowledge of EVPN/VXLAN. - Experience with Next-Generation Firewalls (NGFW). - Knowledge of Web Application Firewalls (WAF) and IDS/IPS. - Experience preparing HLD, LLD, ADD, and technical implementation documentation. - Strong solution design and communication skills. *Language Requirements* - English – Proficient - Swedish – Proficient *How to Apply* Interested candidates are requested to share their updated CV at: careers@i-raysolutions.com Please mention *Senior Network Architect* in the email subject line.
