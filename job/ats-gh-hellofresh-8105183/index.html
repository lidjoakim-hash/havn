Join our R&D Tech Hub in Warsaw We’re building a new R&D Tech Hub in Poland and are looking for people who want to make a real impact from day one. The role We are looking for an experienced People Technology Senior Integrations Specialist to join our HelloFresh People Technology team. This role represents the senior individual contributor level within the Integrations and Engineering discipline and is designed for specialists who can independently deliver complex technical solutions across our People Technology landscape. Today, the role has a strong focus on Workday integrations and connected enterprise systems. Over time, the scope will increasingly extend across system-agnostic integrations, workflow automation, software and low-code development, and AI-enabled solutions. You will take end-to-end responsibility for designing, building, operating, and supporting reliable, secure, and scalable solutions across global environments, using the most appropriate technology for the problem rather than being limited to a single platform. The role combines deep hands-on engineering with mature operational ownership. You will work across Workday, APIs, middleware and emerging engineering capabilities, and will contribute to automation, intelligent workflows and AI-enabled solutions where these create practical value. You will act as a senior point of expertise for incidents and changes, applying judgement to diagnose root causes, manage risk and drive permanent fixes. While you will not define the overall engineering architecture or platform strategy, you will contribute actively to technical standards, patterns, quality improvements and the evolution of the discipline. Above all, we are looking for people who will make HelloFresh better. We believe there are many different ways of developing skills and we love diverse experiences! So even if you don’t “tick all the boxes” but think you’d thrive in this role, we would really like to learn more about you.
