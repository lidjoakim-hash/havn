The role As a Systems Engineer for C-UAS System-of-Systems, you own the high-level architecture of a distributed air defence capability. You define the top-level architecture (L0 and L1), partitioning the capability into network, sensor, and effector subsystems and the interfaces between them. The architecture is open and hardware agnostic: any qualified node can join the network. It is controlled centrally but is designed to keep operating when nodes are lost or contested, so that no single point of failure breaks the chain. This is the layer that enables engage-on-remote and launch-on-remote, where the effector need not sit with the sensor that cued it. This is a role for someone who thinks at the level of the whole system. Your decisions on architecture, networking, and interfaces determine whether a distributed capability performs as one coherent whole under real operational conditions.
