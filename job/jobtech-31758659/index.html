Job ID: 4069 Senior/Lead Quantitative Risk Analyst, Wholesale Model Validation - IRB (permanent position) We are looking for a credible and highly experienced Wholesale IRB model risk professional who combines deep technical expertise, regulatory experience and strong leadership skills gained within SSM-supervised banks. About our team Meet the Wholesale Model Validation team. Our role is to lead complex IRB validation assignments and regulatory interactions, provide sound independent judgement on significant modelling issues, and help develop the capabilities of colleagues through coaching, constructive challenge and knowledge sharing. Collaboration. Ownership. Passion. Courage. These are the values that guide us in how we work and how we make decisions – and that we imagine you share with us. What you’ll be doing The role will focus on Wholesale IRB model validation, with particular emphasis on corporate, institutional and other low-default portfolios. Main responsibilities in this role: Providing senior technical leadership: Apply deep Wholesale IRB expertise to complex methodological, quantitative and regulatory questions, providing authoritative technical challenge and driving robust, independent validation conclusions. Leading complex Wholesale IRB validations: Take ownership of significant validation assignments across Wholesale portfolios, defining the validation strategy and approach, directing complex quantitative analysis and ensuring high-quality conclusions and deliverables. Addressing Wholesale-specific modelling challenges: Provide expert assessment of low-default portfolios, internal rating systems, risk differentiation, calibration, representativeness, margin of conservatism, expert judgement and the treatment of limited default data. Providing expertise across IRB risk parameters: Critically assess PD, LGD and CCF/EAD models and methodologies, including model design, risk drivers, rating philosophy, parameter estimation and calibration, downturn considerations, underlying data, model performance and implementation. Assessing rating system performance: Provide senior-level challenge on discriminatory power, calibration, stability, representativeness, overrides and expert judgement, including the appropriateness and effectiveness of quantitative and qualitative components of Wholesale rating systems. Driving regulatory engagement: Represent Model Validation credibly and confidently in interactions with supervisors, providing technical leadership through ECB/JST reviews, on-site inspections and other supervisory engagements relating to Wholesale IRB models. Coaching and developing colleagues: Provide technical guidance, coaching and constructive challenge to other quantitative specialists, supporting their professional development and raising IRB validation capabilities across the team. Managing delivery: Plan, prioritise and coordinate complex validation activities, effectively managing deliverables, dependencies and competing priorities. Building trusted relationships: Communicate complex validation conclusions clearly and constructively to senior management, model owners, developers and other stakeholders, while safeguarding the independence of the validation function. Strengthening the function: Shape and continuously improve Wholesale IRB validation methodologies, standards, analytical tools and ways of working, while promoting knowledge sharing and consistent validation practices. Who you are Your background and skills include: 8+ years of relevant Wholesale Credit IRB modelling and/or model validation experience, including significant experience gained within SSM-supervised banks. Deep expertise in Wholesale IRB modelling and validation, with strong technical knowledge of PD, LGD and CCF/EAD methodologies.
