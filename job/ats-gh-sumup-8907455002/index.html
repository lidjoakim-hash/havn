Location: Luxembourg (field based, remote within Luxembourg) Team description SumUp's New Markets field sales team is scaling fast, already supporting a network of more than 300 independent sales representatives who bring SumUp's card readers and payment tools directly to small businesses across Europe. As a Territory Manager based in Luxembourg, you'll take ownership of your own territory from day one, recruiting, training and leading a team of sales representatives who turn new merchants into long term SumUp customers. It's an early stage, high visibility role: you'll help shape how SumUp grows in a brand new market, with real autonomy and close support from experienced Sales, Business Operations and Revenue Operations colleagues. 🚦 What you'll do • Build, coach and lead a team of 8 to 12 field sales representatives • Grow merchant activity and transaction volume across the Luxembourg territory • Identify the merchants with the strongest growth potential and prioritise outreach • Track team performance closely, stepping in with coaching or talent decisions when needed • Partner with Sales, Business Operations and Revenue Operations colleagues to shape territory strategy 🙌 You'll be great for this role if… • Experience selling digital payment or fintech products directly to small businesses. • Strong track record leading and coaching field sales teams, including freelance or contracted representatives. • Comfort managing a sales territory independently, from planning through to day to day execution. • Fluency in French and English, with the communication skills to motivate a team and work well with stakeholders across the business. • A competitive, team first mindset paired with a genuine drive to get results. 🎉 Why you should join SumUp 🌎 Opportunity to work with a truly global, multicultural team from our Paris location.
