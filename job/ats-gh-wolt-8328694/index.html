About Wolt At Wolt, we create technology that brings joy, simplicity and earnings to the neighborhoods of the world. In 2014 we started with delivery of restaurant food. Now we’re building the delivery of (almost) everything and you’ll find us in over 500 cities in 30 countries around the world. In 2022 we joined forces with DoorDash and together we keep on dreaming big and expanding across the globe. Working at Wolt isn’t always easy, but it’s definitely exciting. Here you’ll learn more, build more, and ship more than in most other companies. You’ll be challenged a lot, but also have a lot of fun on the way. So, if you’re a self-starter with drive and entrepreneurial spirit, this could be the ride of your life. About the role Join us in our mission to transform the way people shop and eat, where impact, innovation, and growth drives everything we do. Our People team are the architects of a great workplace where brilliant minds come together to do their best work. From discovering diverse talent and nurturing future leaders, to creating spaces that spark collaboration and crafting rewards that celebrate success - we're passionate about building a culture where everyone can thrive. We are bringing our recruitment teams together into one EMEA organisation aligned to the business departments we support. We are looking for a Senior Recruitment Manager to lead recruitment for Marketplace for Wolt and Deliveroo, the B2B space and SevenRooms, building a team that combines strong business partnerships with consistent hiring delivery across both brands. Based in Helsinki, you will lead a geographically distributed team of recruitment managers and recruiters across EMEA. You will set the strategy for your portfolio, develop the leaders and specialists in your team, and work with senior business, Finance and People leaders to translate hiring demand into a clear delivery plan. This role combines leadership through managers with direct leadership of selected individual contributors. You will bring together different markets and business needs, make informed choices about capacity and priorities, and help the team establish effective ways of working in the combined organisation. What you will be doing: Lead through managers. Coach and develop recruitment managers and individual contributors, set clear expectations and build a team with strong judgement, accountability and room to grow. Develop leadership capability and succession within your portfolio. Own the recruitment strategy for your portfolio. Translate business plans for Marketplace, B2B and SevenRooms into short- and longer-term recruitment priorities across Deliveroo and Wolt. Balance department requirements with country, language and role-level needs. Advise senior business leaders. Build trusted partnerships with the leaders of the areas you support. Bring an informed perspective on talent availability, hiring priorities and trade-offs, and challenge plans constructively when demand, resources or market conditions do not align. Plan capacity against demand. Partner with Finance and People colleagues to forecast hiring, maintain the position and capacity plan, and allocate recruitment resources using productivity, hiring complexity and business priorities. Surface delivery risks early and recommend practical responses. Deliver measurable hiring outcomes. Hold your teams accountable for agreed hiring goals, conversion, time to fill, offer acceptance and candidate experience. Use data and talent-market insights to diagnose issues, improve delivery and explain progress to senior stakeholders. Build consistent hiring practices. Work with Talent Acquisition colleagues and recruiting operations to improve sourcing, assessment and interview practices. Support effective adoption of shared tools and standards while retaining the local expertise needed to hire well across EMEA. Lead the team through change.
