You will be reactive to customer questions on adoption and business value, enabling champions to articulate Okta vs. the competition. • You will demonstrate mastery of core Okta products and strategically leverage features and best practices to consistently drive measurable business results. You will diplomatically tackle escalations and provide comfort by listening to and understanding customer needs. • You will demonstrate mastery in planning and executing programmatic engagement strategies across your entire portfolio. This involves highly effective time management and proactive account health monitoring. You will adeptly identify, diagnose, and independently resolve a broad range of standard customer issues. • You will consistently drive high customer satisfaction and retention by ensuring customers achieve measurable business outcomes. You will be proactive in identifying and acting on standard expansion opportunities by effectively demonstrating and articulating Okta's value proposition.
