The Publishing team in EMEA provides many direct-to-player publishing functions, such as content operations, marketing communications, strategy development, competitive experience, events, and full-funnel strategy. The team also establishes and maintains internal services such as platforms, vendor management, project management, RFPs, and working with player insights. The team is dedicated to providing platforms for authentic local interactions and channel experiences for players and Rioters, both online and offline. The team also ensures that our pan-EMEA content will reach players through the right channel, collaborating closely with EMEA and country-based publishing teams. Reporting to the Regional Community Lead, EMEA, you will be based in Berlin (Germany). As Social Media Strategist II, EMEA, you will work on League of Legends, collaborating with the EMEA Community, Brand, Esports, Local Publishing Teams, Global Publishing, and partners to deliver impactful campaigns, content, and initiatives across all our products and social channels, including LEC. You will be responsible for driving the ongoing success and sustainable strategy for our regional and LEC socials, and positioning Riot for success in the years ahead, working closely with our partner agencies. You will work closely with the Regional Community Lead, EMEA, to create campaigns that resonate with our players and ensure these campaigns deliver on their objectives. You will also bring a high level of social media expertise, supporting local publishing teams in their channel efforts to target different audiences and contributing from a social operations perspective. This is a 12 month contract role starting in January 2027 and based onsite in Berlin on a hybrid basis.
