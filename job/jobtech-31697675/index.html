What does the Shinkansen high speed railway in Japan, Burj Khalifa in UAE, NASA space probes and offshore wind turbines have in common? They are prime examples of constructions with constant vibrations causing stress on the applications which, if not secured properly, jeopardizes safety. Most people don’t devote a thought to bolts loosening – but we do. Every minute. Every hour. All day. All Year. Our promise to build connections that last goes beyond manufacturing the best bolting technologies to expert support and pioneering digital solutions. Do you have a strong background and passion for Marketing Communications and Digital Media? Then you might be the person we are looking for! Take on the role as Marketing Specialist. For a fixed-term period, due to parental leave, from February 1st to September 30th 2027, we are looking for a Marketing Specialist based in Malmö, who will be part of our international team. From Malmö, you will be part of the EMEA marketing team and report to our EMEA Marketing Manager in Germany. The role as Marketing Specialist As our Marketing Specialist your mission will be to lead marketing initiatives for the Business Unit Nord-Lock across Sweden, Denmark, Norway, Finland, Italy, France and Spain. You will drive our success in the regions by planning and executing digital marketing initiatives, events and exhibitions. This pivotal role involves close collaboration with sales representatives and marketing colleagues, meaning thriving in a collaborative-focused environment is crucial. Your tasks • Strategic and operational implementation of marketing activities across the Nordic and South Europe countries • With your ideas, creativity, and flair for B2B marketing, you will support our communication on all print and online channels • Planning and implementation of online campaigns (e-mail marketing, LinkedIn Advertisement) • Preparation, realization and follow-up of our participation at exhibitions and other events • Administrative marketing activities such as coordination of giveaways and other sales-related materials Your profile You have experience in B2B marketing and in collaborating with different stakeholders. As a person you are motivated to rock exciting projects independently and as part of a team. You are passionate about marketing, you are well organized and you agree with us that driving campaigns to success is a lot of fun! Requirements Education in Marketing Communications / Digital Media / Eventmanagement, or relevant Previous experience in project management Knowledge of CRM systems (MS Dynamics), desirable experience with online and social media Fluent in both spoken and written English language. Swedish is desirable Very good MS-Office and basic knowledge of InDesign, Photoshop & Illustrator are an advantage What we offer Dynamic workplace and flexible working hours A great opportunity to gain valuable experience as part of an international team Genuinely great coworkers who want to see the company and each other succeed Modern and comfortable workplace in the center of Malmö How to apply Please apply with your CV and cover letter as soon as possible as we are reviewing candidates continuously. Latest date of application is 29th of November. For more information about the position, please contact Anne Henning, EMEA Marketing Manager: anne.hennig@nord-lock.com. About Nord-Lock Group Nord-Lock Group is a global leader in bolting and engineering solutions. Our people are experts in the lifecycle of secure bolted joints, innovators at the forefront of digital solutions and committed to doing business with respect for people and planet. Our brands are all at the forefront of their respective technologies: Nord-Lock® wedge-locking washers, Superbolt® mechanical tensioners, Boltight® hydraulic tensioners, Expander® System pivot pins, and Energy Bolting safety-critical fasteners.
