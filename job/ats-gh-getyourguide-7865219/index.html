Role Overview As a Senior Product Manager in our Discovery organization, you will be an integral part of a cross-functional team, working closely with other product managers, engineers, UX designers, researchers, and data professionals. Your role involves creating and executing a vision for a product area, constantly learning from our customers, and iterating to improve the user experience. You will also collaborate with our UX team, composed of product designers, researchers, and writers. We are looking for a Senior Product Manager who can envision GetYourGuide beyond its current state, champion our users' voices, and execute a compelling vision. Successful PMs at GetYourGuide bring a growth mindset and drive business and customer impact by identifying opportunities, getting buy-in from your stakeholders, and executing with urgency. What You'll Do • Develop and implement a comprehensive strategy for your product area, set clear, measurable goals, and take full responsibility for outcomes. Drive sustained customer impact through a long-term (12 months+) roadmap, identifying areas for non-incremental impact. • Articulate and connect your product area’s strategy to the company’s mission. Break down the strategy into actionable steps, regularly update stakeholders on progress, and create excitement for your long-term vision. Build alignment across cross-functional teams, partner teams, and senior stakeholders. • Drive coherence across multiple discovery entry points (e.g., browse, landing pages, inspiration modules), balancing short-term wins with a durable information architecture and scalable content strategy. • Synthesize customer feedback, research insights, and business metrics to identify and prioritize the most meaningful traveler problems. Define success measures and iterate based on evidence to ensure we deliver real customer value. Make strategic decisions on sequencing and prioritizing product work to maximize impact and learning. • Build and execute an experimentation strategy—developing a robust hypothesis pipeline, defining guardrails and a learning agenda, and ensuring high-quality measurement. Use experiment outcomes to derive actionable insights, inform roadmap decisions, and align cross-team priorities and trade-offs. • Drive end-to-end delivery, ensuring high-quality execution from discovery through launch and iteration, and enabling effective collaboration across engineering, design, research, and data. • Build strong relationships and alignment across partner teams that influence discovery quality (e.g., Search, Recommendations, Brand/Content, Commercial). Set clear expectations, align on shared goals and trade-offs, and drive cross-functional execution against your roadmap. • Represent the traveler perspective at all levels, communicate decisions with clarity and transparency with your team, peers, and executive leadership. Coach PMs and cross-functional partners to raise the bar on product thinking and execution and foster a culture of continuous improvement.
