Ready to tackle global challenges that impact business, society, and the environment? Come join us. Senior Applied AI Engineer, Autonomous Supply Chains About Flexport Look around you. Almost everything in the room crossed an ocean to get there. Moving one container from a factory to a warehouse takes 10 to 15 companies: truckers, customs brokers, port terminals, ocean carriers, insurers, lenders and warehouses, each with its own systems, paperwork and rules. A freight forwarder runs that whole journey, so the brand that makes the goods doesn't have to. Most forwarders still run it on email, spreadsheets and phone calls. We call it email freight forwarding. Flexport runs it on one platform, where AI agents do the routine work and experts step in where judgment matters. We've moved $175B of goods for more than 13,000 brands across 135 countries. Our agents handle 21 million tasks a year, and up to 80% of customs work is automated. Global logistics is a $13 trillion industry, and even the largest forwarder earns less than 3% of global logistics outsourcing revenue. People-powered operations don't scale: to grow, you hire. Software broke that ceiling for taxis with Uber, and for payments with Adyen and Stripe. Freight is next. Amsterdam is one of Flexport's six engineering hubs, alongside San Francisco, Seattle, Chicago, Shanghai and Shenzhen. We own four domains end to end: Customs Technology, Autonomous Supply Chains (Applied AI), Platform and Security. See what we build at amsterdam-engineering.flexport.com. The team: Autonomous Supply Chains Logistics is famously hard to automate, because the happy path rarely happens. One customer won't take deliveries on a Tuesday. Another wants invoices only on Fridays, with some lines merged and others renamed. Until recently, people handled all of that by hand. Now operators use Forge, the agentic platform we build, to automate their own work: catching anomalies, routing exceptions, escalating stuck containers and adjusting invoices to each customer. Forge remembers every customer's rules and learns from every workflow run, so it gets smarter every day. Autonomous Supply Chains is our Applied AI team: small, senior and reporting to the VP of Engineering. Self-driving cars are rated from level 0 to level 5. Most of the freight industry still runs at level 0: people emailing spreadsheets. Our goal is level 5 for freight, with Forge as the best agentic platform there is, handling every complexity, every calamity and every odd preference without skipping a beat. Stack: TypeScript, Rust, Next.js, Temporal, Postgres and Snowflake, with models from Anthropic, OpenAI and Google. The role As a Staff Applied AI Engineer on Autonomous Supply Chains, you'll set the direction for the agents that run real logistics work. You'll find the highest-leverage problems in our operations, decide what's worth building, design the solution and ship it to operators moving freight across 135 countries. Nobody hands you a spec, and nobody needs to. What you'll do • Set the technical direction for Forge: how its agents plan, remember, use tools and hand off to people. • Design and ship end-to-end AI agents that handle real logistics work, such as customs compliance, document processing, invoice validation
