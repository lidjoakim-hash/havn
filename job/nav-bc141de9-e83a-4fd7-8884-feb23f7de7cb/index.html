Postdoctoral position in Behavioral and Experimental Economics

NHH Norwegian School of Economics · Bergen, Norway

About the position NHH in Bergen, Norway, invites applications for a postdoctoral position in behavioral and experimental economics at the Department of Economics, as part of the FAIR Markets research project funded by the European Research Council (Project 101267064). The duration of the position is three years. The postdoctoral position offers a unique opportunity to join an ambitious research environment in behavioral and experimental economics and to become an integral part of the FAIR – Centre for Experimental Research on Fairness, Inequality and Rationality and the Department of Economics (FAIR) at the Department of Economics. The successful candidate will join an ambitious new research agenda on the moral foundations of markets and work closely with Bertil Tungodden and other researchers at FAIR and The Choice Lab on projects combining experimental methods, large-scale surveys, and rich administrative data to study how people evaluate the fairness and legitimacy of market transactions. The position provides substantial scope for intellectual contribution and for developing independent research within the broader FAIR Markets agenda. We are looking for a highly ambitious and collaborative researcher with a strong background in behavioral and experimental economics and a particular interest in questions related to fairness, markets, and public policy. The successful candidate should have excellent analytical and technical skills. The ideal candidate will combine methodological rigor with creativity and a strong motivation to contribute to high-quality research aimed at publication in leading international journals. The department seeks ambitious candidates early in their careers. Applicants should hold, or be close to completing, a PhD degree in a business-oriented field such as economics or a closely related field from a recognised business school or university. NHH offers • Competitive terms depending on the level of competence/length of service. • An academically stimulating working environment. • A favorable membership in the Norwegian Public Service Pension Fund (SPK) and good welfare benefits such as parental leave. • Research funds for travel and conference participation, and a bonus system for publication in top international journals (up to NOK 200 000). Note that public health care, public schooling for children, and most other public services are free in Norway. The city of Bergen has a mild, coastal climate and offers rich opportunities for culture and outdoor activities – as well as a family-friendly environment (explore visitnorway.com). General information The appointee must comply with the guidelines that apply to the position at any time. NHH emphasises diversity and encourages qualified candidates to apply for the position regardless of gender, age, disability and cultural background. In the assessment of applicants, international experience and qualifications will be emphasised. For the sake of gender balance at NHH, female applicants will be given preference in the event of equivalent qualifications among candidates. Applicants are requested to present a research statement with their application. The application should contain details of the applicant's scientific and pedagogical education and work experience. The application package, including a CV, certificates with grades, a maximum of 5 published or unpublished research papers, a complete list of published papers and papers in the pipeline, and any other relevant documentation, should be submitted through the Jobbnorge online portal. To open the application, please click 'Apply for this job'. For English version, go to the menu at the top right side. Two or three reference letters (in PDF) must be submitted by the referees directly to NHH at hr-references&#64;nhh.no, by latest 22 December 2026. As part of the recruitment process, a background check may be conducted with the candidates' consent. In accordance with §25 (2) of the Freedom of Information Act, information about applicants may be made public even if they have requested not to have their names entered on the list of applicants. Applicants will be notified if their request is not granted. Enquiries about the position may be directed to: • Professor Bertil Tungodden, by email: bertil.tungodden&#64;nhh.no • Administrative coordinator Mette Soltvedt, by email: mette.soltvedt&#64;nhh.no The deadline for applications is 22 December 2026. This date also applies to the submission of publications/written work.
