Senior Product Designer – 50%, Remote Sweden🚀 About the Assignment We are looking for an experienced and hands-on Product Designer to join an exciting assignment within the retail and e-commerce sector. In this role, you will create exceptional digital customer experiences by transforming ideas, business requirements, and customer needs into intuitive, visually compelling designs. You will collaborate closely with Product, Engineering, and business stakeholders to develop and refine digital products. The ideal candidate combines strong UX thinking with excellent UI craftsmanship and enjoys taking ownership of the entire design process, from early concepts to polished, high-fidelity solutions. This is an opportunity to contribute to customer-facing digital products in a fast-paced, collaborative, and innovative environment. 💻 Key Responsibilities Translate concepts, requirements, and customer challenges into high-fidelity UX/UI designs and interactive prototypes using Figma. Collaborate closely with engineers to refine designs, address technical constraints, and support implementation. Participate in and facilitate workshops, discovery sessions, and design discussions. Create intuitive user experiences with strong visual design and attention to detail. Work with established design systems and identify opportunities to improve existing patterns and components. Stay informed about native iOS and Android features, platform capabilities, and emerging design patterns. Use tools such as Figma, Miro, Microsoft Teams, and AI/Copilot-supported workflows. Communicate design decisions clearly and collaborate effectively with stakeholders. Rapidly develop, test, and iterate on design concepts based on feedback and business needs. Contribute to continuous improvements in customer experience and digital product development. 🎯 Requirements Strong professional experience in Product Design, UX/UI Design, or a similar role. Excellent UX and UI design skills, with demonstrated experience creating polished, high-fidelity designs and prototypes. Advanced proficiency in Figma. Strong understanding of native iOS and Android design patterns, features, and platform capabilities. Experience working with design systems and reusable components. Proven ability to collaborate directly with developers and adapt designs to technical requirements. Ability to work independently and manage design activities in evolving or ambiguous environments. Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills. Full professional proficiency in English, both written and spoken. A hands-on approach to design, with a strong focus on delivering practical, high-quality solutions. ⭐ Nice to Have Experience within retail, fashion, e-commerce, or other customer-facing digital products. Experience designing mobile applications for iOS and Android. Familiarity with Miro, Microsoft Teams, and AI-assisted design workflows. Understanding of customer journeys, conversion optimization, and commercial business objectives. 🌟 Who You Are You are a creative, proactive, and solution-oriented designer who enjoys turning complex challenges into outstanding digital experiences. You combine strategic UX thinking with exceptional attention to visual detail and thrive in environments where collaboration, curiosity, and rapid iteration are essential. You are comfortable challenging assumptions, exploring new opportunities, and taking ideas from concept to implementation. Above all, you enjoy designing and creating solutions yourself, rather than focusing primarily on managing other designers. 📍 Assignment Information Location: Remote within Sweden Start date: December 1, 2026 - November 30, 2027 Duration: Approximately 12 months Workload: 50% On-site attendance: Workshops approximately once per quarter Sway Sourcing är en innovativ rekryteringspartner som specialiserar sig på att matcha rätt talang med rätt företag – snabbt och effektivt
