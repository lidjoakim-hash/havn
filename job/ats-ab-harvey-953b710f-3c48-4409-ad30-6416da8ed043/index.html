ROLE OVERVIEW Harvey is building a best-in-class go-to-market engine, and we're looking for a GTM Systems Administrator to join the GTM Systems team and help scale the systems that power both our pipeline and our customer experience. You'll take on a variety of projects across the full GTM lifecycle, from how we source, enrich, and route leads at the top of the funnel to how we support and engage customers through Zendesk and our Customer Success platforms. This is not a traditional RevOps or Salesforce admin role: we expect this person to work AI-first, using AI tools and agents to build, automate, and maintain systems that would traditionally require engineering support or manual admin work. Reporting to the Head of GTM Systems, you'll partner closely with Sales, Marketing, Support, and Customer Success to translate business needs into scalable, reliable systems that directly drive revenue and retention outcomes. WHAT YOU'LL DO - Work across a variety of projects on the GTM Systems team, with some sprints focused on top-of-funnel growth and others on support and customer success tooling, flexing to where the business needs you most. - Design and improve top-of-funnel systems including lead sourcing, enrichment, scoring, and routing, building enrichment workflows in Clay and across data providers (ZoomInfo, Cognism, Apollo, Clearbit, etc.) to power inbound and outbound pipeline. - Configure and optimize Zendesk as Harvey's primary support platform (ticket routing, triggers, automations, macros, SLA policies, agent workspace) and support adjacent CS platforms like Catalyst. - Contribute to Salesforce configuration across the funnel, including lead routing, scoring, lifecycle stages, and automation (Flows, validation rules, custom objects), and how data flows across Salesforce, marketing automation, support, and enrichment tools. - Use AI as a default working method: build and maintain AI agents, automations, and workflows (AI triage, copilots, agent-based enrichment, LLM-powered data quality checks) to eliminate manual admin work and extend what the systems team can ship without engineering support. - Integrate GTM and CS tools with Harvey's broader ecosystem (CRM, product data, internal tools, communication platforms) to ensure a unified view of the customer, and build reporting and dashboards that provide visibility into pipeline conversion, support performance, and customer health. - Maintain high data quality and compliance through deduplication, normalization, enrichment governance, and consent/opt-out handling, and document system architecture, workflows, and processes so everything scales as the company grows.
