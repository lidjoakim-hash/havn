Finance Manager Sweden

Kongsberg Automotive AB · Mullsjö, Sweden

About the position Kongsberg Automotive is looking for an experienced and commercially minded Finance Manager Sweden to join our management team in Mullsjö. The position reports to the Plant Manager / Managing Director Sweden. In this role, you will combine financial leadership with strategic business partnering. Working closely with plant management and operational leaders, you will drive business performance, improve profitability and support strategic decision-making. You will lead the local finance organization, with full people management responsibility, while ensuring strong financial governance and reporting. Beyond traditional financial management, your focus will be on turning financial insights into actions that create measurable business value. We are looking for a person who can drive the transformation to use of more AI in the accounting and finance processes. Responsibilities As Finance Manager Sweden, you will act as a trusted business partner to the Plant Manager / Managing Director and the local management team. Your main responsibilities will include: Business partnering: Support and challenge business leaders through financial insights and proactive decision support. Business performance: Lead financial reviews, monitor key financial and operational KPIs, and identify improvement opportunities. Profitability and planning: Drive cost optimization, productivity improvements, budgeting, forecasting and investment evaluations. Financial governance: Ensure accurate reporting, compliance with Group requirements, Swedish regulations and internal controls. People leadership: Lead and develop the local finance team, with full responsibility for people management, performance and competence development. Requirements We are looking for a business-oriented finance professional with strong analytical skills and the ability to influence business performance. Bachelor's or Master's degree in Finance, Business Administration, Economics or equivalent. At least 7 years of experience in Finance, Controlling or Business Partnering, preferably within manufacturing. Proven experience in financial planning, budgeting, forecasting and investment analysis. Experience in leading and developing people, preferably within a finance organization. Strong business acumen, strategic thinking and a hands-on approach. Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to challenge and influence constructively. Experience from an international organization and knowledge of ERP and business intelligence tools are advantages. Motivation and drive for the use of AI and digitalization in general for further development and improvements of the accounting and finance processes Fluency in Swedish and English. What we offer At Kongsberg Automotive, we believe Finance should create value and enable better business decisions. We offer an influential position within the local management team, where you can directly impact business performance and strategic development. You will have the opportunity to lead and develop the finance organization, drive improvements and contribute to the continued development of our Swedish operations in an international manufacturing environment. Application / Contact Are you ready to combine your financial expertise with business leadership and make a real impact? We look forward to receiving your application. Apply today and become a key business partner in driving Kongsberg Automotive's continued success. Please register your CV/resume and cover letter. The applications will be handled on an ongoing basis and the position may be filled before the application deadline date.
