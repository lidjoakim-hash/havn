Field Sales Manager Tampere, Finland Team description SumUp's field sales team in Tampere works directly with local shops, cafés and service businesses, helping them find payment solutions that fit how they actually operate. As Field Sales Manager, you'll build this team from scratch and shape how SumUp grows across one of its key Finnish territories, leading from the field alongside your team rather than from behind a desk. It's a role for someone who wants genuine ownership over how a team and a market take shape, and who's driven by turning strong groundwork into measurable results. What you'll do • Lead, coach and develop a team of field sales representatives across the territory • Work alongside the team in the field, joining client meetings and supporting the sales process • Own the team's sales targets, performance and overall results • Spot new business opportunities and adapt the sales strategy to the local market • Build and grow the team by partnering with Recruiting and Sales Coaching You'll be great for this role if… • Experience managing and developing a sales team, with a hands-on, close-to-the-ground leadership style • Strong knowledge of field sales and what it takes to win new business through active prospecting • Ability to lead by example, building trust by working alongside the team rather than from a distance • Comfort using sales data and results to guide decisions and sharpen performance • Fluency in Finnish & English, along with ease working independently in a fast-moving environment 🎉 Why you should join SumUp • 🌍 Opportunity to work with SumUppers globally on large-scale fintech products used by millions of businesses worldwide, from our Stockholm office • 🌈 Commitment to Diversity and Inclusion: be part of a workplace that values and promotes diversity, fostering an inclusive environment where everyone's perspectives are respected and embraced • 🏖 Generous time off: • 🌴 Break4me: 1-month sabbatical after 3 years of service • 🔗 Referral Bonus: earn additional rewards by referring talented individuals to join the SumUp team 🤔 About SumUp Be empowered to do more that matters.
