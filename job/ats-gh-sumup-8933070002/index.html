💼 Inbound Sales Representative (On-Site – Paris) Want to grow your sales career in one of the world's leading fintech scale-ups? Join the inbound sales team in Paris, turning warm leads into happy customers and helping small businesses across France succeed with SumUp. 🏢 Team description The inbound sales team is the first real conversation a small business owner has with SumUp, turning curiosity into confidence in getting paid. With over 4 million businesses already using SumUp worldwide, the team's role is to make sure every French merchant who reaches out gets a clear, honest answer about how SumUp can help them run their business better. It's a role built for someone who wants to learn the fundamentals of sales fast, in a team that measures success by how many small businesses it genuinely helps get started. 🎯 What you'll do • Engage with inbound leads generated by the marketing team, qualifying, consulting and converting them into customers • Connect with small and medium-sized businesses by phone and email, building trust and understanding their needs • Deliver product demos and explain clearly how SumUp solves real business challenges • Manage the sales pipeline in Salesforce, keeping follow-ups timely and lead conversion smooth • Report regularly on sales performance and stay aligned with team goals and growth targets 🙌 What we’re looking for • Experience in a phone-based or customer-facing role, such as sales, support or retail • Comfort speaking with people persuasively and warmly, with a solution-focused approach • Curiosity about fintech, small business and how things work behind the scenes • Ability to thrive in a fast-paced environment where learning and adapting are part of the job • Fluency in French and English, with a proactive, team-first attitude 🌟 Why you should join SumUp 💸 Opportunity to work with a truly global, multicultural team from our Paris location. This involves an office-first setup 🌈 Commitment to Diversity and Inclusion: be part of a workplace that values and promotes diversity, fostering an inclusive environment where everyone's perspectives are respected and embraced 🚀 Enrolment onto our Virtual Stock Option programme: you will own a stake in SumUp's future success 📚 A dedicated annual L&D budget of €2000 for your individual development, which can be used to attend conferences and/or advance your career through further education 💼 Financial security: corporate pension contributions that support your long-term future 🏥 Healthcare support: we cover 50% of the monthly cost of your health insurance for your Mutuelle and Prévoyance 🚌 Extra allowances: we offer restaurant tickets (Swile) and cover transportation costs About SumUp Small business owners are at the heart of all we do, so we create powerful, easy-to-use financial solutions to help them run their businesses. With a founder’s mentality and a 'team-first’ attitude, our diverse teams across Europe, South America and the United States work together to ensure that the small business owners we partner with can be successful doing what they love.
