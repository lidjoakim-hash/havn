Job Description You'll run Docplanner's paid search activities for patients across multiple markets, including Brazil, Mexico, Colombia, Chile, Italy, Spain and Poland. You'll manage a large-scale Google Ads setup with 100+ accounts, tens of thousands of campaigns and millions of keywords, working with our in-house ads engine and AI agents. Your responsibilities: - Manage and optimise Google Ads campaigns for doctor and clinic profiles across all markets, including campaign structure, bids, budgets, keywords, negatives, ads and ad scheduling. - Execute large-scale changes using Google Ads Editor, bulk uploads and scripts. - Work with AI agents and automation scripts: review AI-driven changes, handle edge cases and improve automated scripts. - Handle requests from country teams, including new activations, budget changes, campaign pauses and custom ads. - Monitor daily pacing, cost per booking and campaign health, and act on alerts when needed. - Ensure conversion tracking is accurate in collaboration with data and web teams. - Manage healthcare advertising policies, certifications and ad disapprovals. - Report on performance using SQL queries and dashboards. Qualifications: - 6+ years of hands-on Google Ads experience, including managing large, multi-account setups across several markets. - Expert knowledge of Google Ads Editor and bulk campaign management. - Strong experience building Google Ads scripts and/or working with the Google Ads API, including reading data, modifying scripts and debugging automation. - SQL skills, with the ability to independently query a data warehouse (Redshift or similar). - Experience with healthcare advertising policies and certifications. - Comfortable working with automation and AI tools on a daily basis. - Fluent English. Nice to have: - Knowledge of Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Polish, Turkish or German. - Experience building and scaling large Google Ads accounts. - Experience with YouTube, Performance Max or Demand Gen. - Background in a marketplace, health tech or subscription-based business.
