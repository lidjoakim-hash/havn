Our Mission ⚖️ At Doctrine, we're building Europe's leading legal AI platform. Our belief? Law shouldn't be slowed down by repetitive tasks. It should be augmented by a specialised AI, designed to understand, analyse and produce legal reasoning at the highest standard. Our ambition? To help legal professionals work with greater reliability, efficiency and peace of mind, through a single, comprehensive and secure platform. Today, 27,000 legal professionals across five countries (France, Italy, Germany, Spain and Luxembourg) trust us to do just that. Our growth is guided by a clear approach: a global vision, grounded in a deep understanding of local specificities. Behind this ambition, a team of around 250 people, united around one goal: supporting lawyers and legal professionals at every stage of their work, from research to drafting. And we're just getting started. The context We’re looking for a senior/fullstack engineer to help us build the first legal intelligence platform. You’ll join the “Scribe” team with a clear mission: revolutionize legal drafting (Drafting) in France, Italy, and Germany. We deploy cutting-edge solutions (LLMs) directly where lawyers and legal professionals work. Your roadmap (3–6 months): Web App: Build a multi-document drafting tool and a dedicated chatbot. Integrations: Launch add-ins to assist daily legal drafting. AI & Translation: Enhance our automatic PDF translation features. Our web tech stack is based on NodeJS, NestJS, React & NextJS.   You can find full details about our stack on GitHub https://github.com/DoctrineLegal/#our-tech-stack !   Note: Previous professional experience in law is not required, but a willingness to learn and develop expertise in understanding legal documents is important 🙂 Missions 🛠 • Work closely with product designers and product managers to design the best possible user experiences. • Build reusable components used across all our product teams. • Develop products at the intersection of Web technologies and Machine Learning. • Help disseminate our internal best practices and actively contribute to our Engineering strategy. Ideal Profil 👀 • Strong command of TypeScript (Node and React). • Knowledge in Machine Learning is a plus • Strong focus on quality: best practices, testing, CI/CD, reliability and availability. • Comfortable working in an agile environment, with a strong product and UX mindset. • Enjoys sharing knowledge, collaborating, and giving constructive feedback. • Proactive and able to partner with all teams, acting as a driver of positive change.
