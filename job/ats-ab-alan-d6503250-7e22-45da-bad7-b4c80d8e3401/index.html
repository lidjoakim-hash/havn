And beyond. ⭐️ THE INSURANCE TEAM ⭐️ Being part of Alan's insurance community is a great opportunity to have a significant impact on people's health and wellbeing in our multiple countries of operation. - We are at the heart of Alan's mission, bringing to the team our technical leadership and expertise on healthcare and death & disability insurance. - We work hand in hand with our data scientists, designers, engineers and product managers to build the most differentiating products and provide a delightful experience at a fair price. - We build and manage offers for large companies and public institutions with our sales and account management teams. - We make sure our portfolio is closely monitored and risks are effectively managed. - We also manage our capital, steer our reinsurance strategy and maintain a trusting relationship with our insurance regulators. ⭐️ THE INSURANCE EXPERT ROLE IN BELGIUM ⭐️ Being an Insurance Expert at Alan in Belgium means being part of a small and talented team currently working on delivering the best experience to our members. Recent missions include: - Contribute to new product design and pricing strategies. - Support Sales with insurance expertise to help sign large customers in Belgium. - Monitor the portfolio and improve the depth and accuracy of reporting. - Document and structure insurance knowledge to up-level the Belgium team. - Join the insurance on-call rotation to support internal teams with day-to-day questions. - Work with Engineering on tools that create tailored insurance offers at scale. - Being part of an insurance community at the global level, with an impact on our organisation, hiring and training across multiple countries. ⭐️ IS IT YOU WE'RE LOOKING FOR? ⭐️ You may be a great fit for the role if: - You combine a scientific background (actuarial sciences, statistics, mathematics or data science) with a first experience or strong interest in insurance (product, pricing, regulatory or analytics) - You have strong analytical and strategic thinking skills, with the ability to translate complex data, business and legal questions into actionable outputs - You have a builder mindset: you continuously identify opportunities to improve and scale processes and systems - You are willing to deeply change the insurance and healthcare industry and challenge the status quo - You communicate in a clear, structured and to-the-point way in English (written and spoken) For this role, we are looking to hire within the B1-C0 level range. 🎯 IMPORTANT NOTE: WE HIRE PEOPLE, NOT ROLES.
