🚀 We’re Hiring: Senior Test Engineer / Verification Engineer – Electromobility Company: Hykmann Global Business Solutions AB Role: Test Engineer Specialist Domain: Automotive & Electromobility Engagement: IT Consulting Assignment Are you an experienced Test Engineer passionate about automotive technology, electric vehicles, and safety-critical systems? Hykmann Global Business Solutions AB is looking for a skilled Test Engineer / Verification Engineer to contribute to the development and verification of next-generation electric vehicle systems. About the Role You will be responsible for verifying the functionality, performance, quality, and reliability of complete electromobility systems in both real-world and simulated environments. Working closely with engineering teams and cross-functional stakeholders, you will translate functional requirements into test cases, execute verification activities, analyse results, and identify potential issues that could affect product quality or project delivery. Key Responsibilities Plan and execute system and functional verification of complete electromobility systems. Perform testing in both physical and simulated environments. Break down functional requirements and use cases into detailed test cases. Maintain and update test cases throughout the development lifecycle. Conduct functional testing and regression testing. Analyse verification results and prepare comprehensive test reports. Perform troubleshooting across different test objects and system configurations. Identify, document, and report software and system defects. Communicate test results, findings, and potential risks to relevant stakeholders. Identify and highlight risks that could impact delivery quality, project schedules, or system reliability. Collaborate with engineers and cross-functional teams to ensure successful verification and validation. Required Qualifications Several years of professional experience in verification, validation, and testing of safety-critical systems. Experience within the automotive industry. Good understanding of automotive software development processes. Proven experience in system verification, functional testing, and test execution. Hands-on experience with Vector tools is mandatory. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills. Excellent communication and teamwork abilities. Nice to Have Experience with engineering tools such as Protom and Kola. Category C driving licence. Who Should Apply? We are looking for a proactive and detail-oriented test professional who understands the importance of quality and reliability in safety-critical automotive systems. You should be comfortable working with multidisciplinary engineering teams, investigating technical issues, analysing test outcomes, and taking ownership of verification activities. How to Apply Are you ready to contribute to the development of next-generation electric vehicles? Submit your updated CV through the Hykmann careers portal: https://www.hykmann.com/careers Please highlight your experience in: Automotive verification and validation. Safety-critical system testing. Electromobility or electric vehicle systems. Vector tools and other relevant engineering tools.
