Based in our Dublin, Ireland office and reporting directly to the Director of Global Security & Safety, the Global Health & Safety Manager will own the design, execution, governance, and continuous enhancement of Okta’s global health, safety, and occupational wellness framework. While anchored in Dublin, this is a broad, truly global leadership role. Because Okta operates dynamic, distributed workplaces across multiple international jurisdictions, the Global Health & Safety Manager will scale operations by directing an extensive network of specialized third-party vendors, global EHS consultants, and local practitioners. In addition to overarching workplace safety, this leader serves as the global program owner for Okta’s Workers’ Compensation and Occupational Injury/Illness Claims Management.
