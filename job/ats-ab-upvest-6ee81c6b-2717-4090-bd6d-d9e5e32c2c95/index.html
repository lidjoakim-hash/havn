Senior SME Trading infrastructure (f/m/d)

At Upvest, we are on a mission to make investing as easy as spending money. Upvest empowers businesses to offer a wide range of investment products and the best experience in the field of capital market investment and retirement planning. Upvest’s Investment API is easy to integrate so that fintechs and financial institutions can save resources and fully focus on their core business. We are proud to partner with Europe’s leading Fintechs and financial institutions such as DKB, Revolut, N26 and Raisin. Founded in 2017 by Martin Kassing, Upvest now brings together over 270 talented professionals from more than 70 nationalities. Upvest is backed by €280M in total funding from world-class investors, including BlackRock, Tencent, Sapphire Ventures, and Bessemer Venture Partners, Earlybird, Notion Capital, and Motive. Our latest €105M funding round in March 2026 - led by Sapphire and Tencent - serves as a massive catalyst for our growth, allowing us to offer premier investment experience. ABOUT THE ROLE: As Upvest continues to scale, we are looking for an exceptional SME Trading Infrastructure. You will work closely with Götz Röhr http://linkedin.com/in/g%C3%B6tz-r%C3%B6hr-2274781a4/, Pim Hannay https://www.linkedin.com/in/pim-h-a3506a64/ and the Brokerage Product & Engineering Leadership team. In addition you will need to build strong relations with the Operations, Banking and Commercial teams, driving topics critical to Upvest's success. What you can expect from this role: - Impact and influence, laying the foundations on how one of Europe's leading infrastructure companies builds their trading infrastructure. - Product-centricity throughout the organisation, with no struggle for relevance that product people may encounter elsewhere. - Autonomy and ownership. You run your topics and are trusted to drive them to results. - Being part of a group of experts, all having their own areas of expertise but working strongly together. - Continuous learning across investments, API products, and modern product operations. WHAT YOU’LL DO: Develop upvest's future trading infrastructure You will drive the strategy to evolve and scale our trading infrastructure in line with our company strategy. You will own and support the decisions that shape it: which execution venues to connect in which markets, which assets and order types to support next, how to initiate post-trade, where to expand and, just as importantly, where not to. You will weigh client needs, market structure, cost, and regulatory requirements to place the right bets at the right time. The goal: a trading infrastructure that fits our clients' needs and growing expectations perfectly, built through fast, confident decisions and a focus on outcomes. Accelerate our trading initiatives You will drive the initiatives that matter to Upvest’s success: big trading bets, new venues and CCP connectivities, and the products and procedures our clients need to trade across Europe. You bring an up-to-date understanding of the European trading landscape (exchanges, MTFs and Systematic Internalisers, CCPs and their connectivity, and how it all links into clearing, settlement and custody) and use it to assess market places, their propositions, and where they are heading. You will develop our long-term trading infrastructure strategy and guide its execution: shaping trading and migration strategies for clients, running RfPs for potential partners where needed, and enabling the Product organisation to deliver the right results quickly. WHAT YOU BRING: - 4+ years in investment banking, product management, trading operations, at a financial institution or SaaS-company with the focus on trading infrastructure. - Deep knowledge and understanding of market infrastructures, exchanges, CCPs and others, the connectivity options and current developments - Proven ability to also understand client expectations and connect this to possibilities given by external trading infrastru
