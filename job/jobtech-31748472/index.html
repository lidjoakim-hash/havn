Watch our anniversary film (https://vimeo.com/1114843079?share=copy) and join us on our journey! Are you an experienced Midwife looking for a new career opportunity in Sweden? We are currently recruiting Midwives to join the maternity and women's healthcare department at a modern regional hospital in central Sweden. This is an opportunity to work in a healthcare environment where patient safety, teamwork, and professional development are highly valued. You will become part of a dedicated team providing high-quality care for women throughout pregnancy, childbirth, and the postnatal period. About the role As a Midwife, you will work at a well-established regional hospital together with obstetricians, pediatricians, nurses, and other healthcare professionals. Your responsibilities may include: Providing care during pregnancy, childbirth, and the postnatal period Monitoring the health and wellbeing of both mother and newborn Supporting women and families throughout the maternity journey Providing patient education and counselling Collaborating closely with multidisciplinary healthcare teams Contributing to safe, evidence-based, and patient-centered care Participating in the continuous development of maternity and women's healthcare services The department is committed to delivering high-quality care while supporting professional growth and continuous learning. Requirements Hold a valid Midwife license issued in an EU/EEA country Minimum one year of documented experience as a Midwife Good English language skills Motivation to learn Swedish Motivation to relocate and work in Sweden on a long-term basis Meritorious Experience in labour and delivery care Experience working in a hospital-based maternity department Knowledge of Swedish or another Scandinavian language What the workplace offers Permanent employment Structured introduction and onboarding Modern facilities and equipment A supportive and experienced multidisciplinary team Opportunities for professional development and continuing education A strong focus on patient safety and quality of care A positive work environment with a healthy work-life balance Why central Sweden?
