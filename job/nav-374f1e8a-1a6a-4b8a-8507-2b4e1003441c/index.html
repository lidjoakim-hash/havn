kta naval systems is a joint venture owned by KONGSBERG, TKMS and TKMS ATLAS ELEKTRONIK. As the exclusive supplier and Design Authority, we are responsible for the complete combat systems delivered to TKMS submarines. As our operations continue to grow, we are strengthening our team and are currently seeking system engineers to drive the successful design and development of the ORCCA combat system and help shape the future of submarine operations.
