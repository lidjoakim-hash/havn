Building Our DACH Presence in Frankfurt While our DACH BDR team is currently operating out of our Paris and London hubs, we are now establishing a local BDR presence directly in Frankfurt. This move allows our team to be closer to our local customers, while working hand-in-hand on the ground with our Account Executives and Sales Leadership. As our very first BDR based in Frankfurt, you will play a key role in launching this local hub, while benefiting from the existing structure, playbooks, and support of our wider DACH team.
