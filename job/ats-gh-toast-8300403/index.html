Toast creates technology to help restaurants and local businesses succeed in a digital world, helping business owners operate, increase sales, engage customers, and keep employees happy. We are hiring a Staff Software Engineer to join Toast Reporting Experience (TREX) in Dublin. TREX is building the Intelligence Platform: a governed data layer that brings Toast's restaurant intelligence to prospects, operators, Toast IQ and AI agents. As a Staff Software Engineer, you will be the technical lead for the Intelligence Platform. You will establish its architecture on Toast's data lake and make the key design calls across data processing, privacy, publishing and serving. You will work closely with Data Platform, Marketing, Product, Legal and Data Science, do deep hands-on work, and grow the engineers on your team. You will take the platform from its first version, a regular publication of aggregated benchmark data, to fresher data, self-serve access for Marketing, and eventually real-time access for AI agents. Success looks like new data products shipping on the platform quickly and safely. The Intelligence Platform will source data from multiple sources: OpenSearch, Data Lake, Snowflake. Toast's data lake is built on AWS: Apache Iceberg tables on S3 and Apache Spark for data processing. TREX's backend services are primarily Kotlin and Java, use Apache Pulsar for messaging, and serve data to other teams through APIs, including federated GraphQL.
