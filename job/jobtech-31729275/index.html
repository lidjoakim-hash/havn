Consultant Routing/Switching Malmö or Gothenburg The position is a permanent employment opportunity with our client. The Company is a long-term partner of Cisco. They are now hiring consultants to cover the customer demand of support with their Networking (Routing & Switching) portfolio They are is a re-seller of IT, networking, SaaS, and cybersecurity solutions. On top of this offering, they also offer expertise in the form of project-based consultants that are experts in their field. They are now looking for Network consultants that can act as experts that help implement, run, and fine-tune the installations out in customer environments. What we're looking for Experience: University degree (preferred but not mandatory) 5+ years of experience in Routing/Switching and Networks within Cisco (CCNP Wireless, CCNP R&S (Routing&Switching), CCIE R&S , CCNP Security, are examples of relevant certifications) Experience working with Cisco Networks is mandatory. Bonus if they have experience in Cisco Wireless Conversational Swedish as a minimum. Preferably fluent/native Swedish Fluent English Personality type that likes to work with other people and don’t mind sitting in the customer’s environment Drive and proactiveness in their work Ability to work without supervision What you'll do Examples of work assignments: Be an expert in anything related to Networks and Cisco Act as an advisor and expert within the field of architecture and design of Cisco solutions Administrate workshops and presentations together with customers Installation, configuration and development of IT security environments Experience in designing and architecting Cisco environments Experience in infrastructure security (firewalls etc.) Experience in SD-WAN Interviews are conducted on an ongoing basis, so please submit your application as soon as possible. You will be employed directly by the company. You will receive information about which company the advertisement refers to when you are in contact with the recruiter. Bumbli Group is therefore not the employer but the recruitment agency. Welcome to submit your application!
