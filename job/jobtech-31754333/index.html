Join our team as an ASIC SoC System Architect! Are you passionate about advanced ASIC development and eager to work at the forefront of technology? Do you thrive in a collaborative environment where your contributions truly matter? We are looking for candidates to expand our dynamic ASIC SoC System Architect team in Lund to help shape the future of our ARTPEC SoC chips. Learn more; https://newsroom.axis.com/article/artpec-foundation-quality Your future team The ASIC System team is at the heart of developing the next generations of ARTPEC SoC chips. We are responsible for the SoC architecture and the ASIC implementation phase, playing a crucial role in both ongoing projects and future innovations. Our team consists of Project Managers, ASIC SoC System Architects and Back-End Engineers. We work closely with our ASIC vendor, internal RTL design and verification teams, and key stakeholders to ensure our SoC designs meet the highest standards. We are a highly skilled and motivated team with a mix of junior and senior members. Our strength lies in our individual expertise and our collaborative, open working environment. The team designs and implements the SoCs used in Axis products including security features that give our products a high level of trust in IoT ecosystems and enables interoperability with major IoT security standards. For this we are now looking for candidates that want to take the step to become ASIC SoC System Architects to strengthen our team. What you will do as an ASIC SoC System Architect As an ASIC SoC System architect, you will play a pivotal role throughout the ASIC project lifecycle. You will specify system-level requirements, define architecture, evaluate and select third-party IPs, and support RTL design and verification teams. You will also cooperate with our ASIC vendor during the implementation phase and work closely with internal hardware and software teams. In this role you will be involved in and/or drive activities within competence areas such as: SoC Architecture Low-power architecture Package and PI/SI External interfaces Interconnect Use-Cases and overall system design. Security Performance CPU Memory systems Qualification Prototype testing Requirements IP evaluations Thermal Technology evaluations Etc. Who are you? We are seeking highly motivated engineers with a background within ASIC development such as: ASIC Design ASIC Verification ASIC Backend ASIC System Architecture Most importantly, you want to grow within the field ASIC System Architecture. We welcome both junior and senior engineers who bring qualities such as: Master's degree in electrical engineering, computer science, physics, mathematics or similar 5+ years of ASIC Development experience Genuine interest in some of the competence areas above Self-driving and with leadership capabilities Documentation skills Presentation and communication skills in English As a person, we believe you are dedicated, analytical, systematic, and enjoy sharing your knowledge and ideas with the team. Teamwork is crucial at Axis, so it's important that you contribute to a positive team spirit and a creative work environment. You are person with a strong personal drive and an ability to work both unsupervised and as a part of a team. You can create and maintain the needed network for your task, and you can also manage technical leadership. What Axis Has to Offer We are a world leader in network video, where cutting-edge technology meets global impact. Here, you'll contribute to meaningful projects that shape the future of security and surveillance - developing solutions used worldwide. As a fast-growing company, we offer exciting career opportunities. You'll grow professionally through continuous learning, supported by a collaborative team that values creativity, innovation, and work-life balance.
