The Role Veeva is looking for an experienced Sales Leader to drive sales across our largest, strategic accounts for Veeva Quality Cloud- the leading technology foundation for Life Sciences manufacturing. As a Sales Leader for Veeva Quality Cloud, you will lead a team of Account Partners responsible for establishing and building influence with VP-level stakeholders in Quality Assurance and Quality Control at leading Life Sciences companies. This is an opportunity to build long-term strategic roadmaps with key customers as well as to identify and develop new accounts. This is a critical position responsible for hiring, managing, and mentoring a team of APs. Creativity, energy, and the ability to hire, manage and support a diverse team are critical to your success.
