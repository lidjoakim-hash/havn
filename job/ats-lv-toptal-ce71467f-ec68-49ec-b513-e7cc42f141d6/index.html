Job Summary: As General Manager, Industrial Products & Services (IPS), reporting directly to the EVP, Industries, you will own the strategy, growth, and profitability of Toptal’s business across Automotive, Manufacturing, Agriculture, Oil & Gas, Chemical, and Logistics clients, with full P&L and budget authority. You will identify where we can win, decide where to invest, and build the team and client relationships to deliver sustained growth. Your mandate covers winning new enterprise clients, expanding existing relationships, and increasing the value clients receive from Toptal’s capabilities. You will bring a clear perspective on the challenges facing potential clients and where Toptal can help. Working with our Services leaders and subject matter experts, you will translate those challenges into viable sales proposals across our AI solutions, managed services, management consultancy, and talent offerings. You will choose which markets, accounts, and opportunities to pursue, allocate resources accordingly, and give your team a focused plan. You will lead from the front: personally leading executive client conversations, shaping proposals, working through difficult commercial negotiations, and helping close critical pursuits. Your team should see the selling standard you expect in your own work. You will understand what is driving performance, where growth is constrained, and what needs to change. You will build on established enterprise relationships to expand the problems clients trust Toptal to solve through our services, AI-based solutions, and talent-based capabilities. You will have the authority to build the team and invest in profitable growth. This is a remote position. Applicants must be authorized to work in the US at the time of hire. Resumes and communication must be submitted in English. Responsibilities: The following information is intended to describe the general nature and level of work being performed. It is not intended to be an exhaustive list of all duties, responsibilities, or required skills. Set and execute the industry strategy. Identify the markets, client problems, and offerings with the strongest growth potential. Build an IPS business plan with clear priorities, financial objectives, and investment decisions. Test your assumptions with clients and adjust the plan as evidence changes. Own the P&L and budget. Be accountable for revenue, profitability, and spending. Set and manage the budget, allocate resources, and evaluate returns on investments in people and growth initiatives. Understand how pricing, delivery economics, retention, and portfolio mix affect results, and act when performance diverges from the plan. Win new enterprise clients. Lead your team in pursuing priority accounts. Develop a credible reason for each client to engage with Toptal, build relationships with decision-makers, and personally advance important opportunities through discovery, solution development, negotiation, and close. Expand and retain client relationships. Work with Client Partners to understand client priorities, broaden relationships across buying centers, and develop opportunities across Toptal’s portfolio. Stay close to important engagements, resolve concerns with the responsible teams, and turn successful outcomes into further business. Make account planning actionable. Establish clear priorities and ownership. Expect your team to explain why an account matters, whom they need to engage, what opportunity they are pursuing, and how they will create progress. Ensure priority accounts receive sustained attention. Bring the right expertise into the sale. Work with Services leaders, solution architects, and specialists to diagnose needs and develop credible proposals across services, AI-based solutions, and talent. Coordinate with supporting functions to resolve obstacles and keep opportunities moving toward a decision. Maintain a credible forecast.
