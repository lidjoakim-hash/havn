WHY THIS ROLE EXISTS At Pennylane, Procurement drives our growth by ensuring compliant and efficient purchasing, managing costs and risks, and enhancing the internal experience. As we scale and expand internationally, the number of suppliers, contracts, and renewals is increasing. We are seeking a Procurement professional to strengthen our execution and strategic sourcing, with a focus on IT purchasing (software and hardware).
