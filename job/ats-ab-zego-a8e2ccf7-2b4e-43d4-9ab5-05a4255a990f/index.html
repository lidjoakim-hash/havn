Purpose of the role First Notification of Loss is the first thing a customer experiences after an accident, and it sets the tone for everything that follows. In 18 months, every Zego customer reporting a loss will get an accurate, empathetic first contact and be routed to the right next step first time. You are the reason claims start clean, so the rest of the journey runs smoothly. What you will be doing - Handle First Notification of Loss queries by phone, email and live chat with empathy, accuracy and efficiency. - Capture every relevant claim detail correctly at first contact, so nothing has to be chased again later. - Triage customers and route them to the right next step in their claims journey. - Leave customers feeling heard, supported and informed at a genuinely difficult moment. - Keep records in our claims management systems accurate, protecting data quality at every stage. - Work with the wider Claims team and supply chain partners so claims progress smoothly from the very first touchpoint. What you will need to be successful - Call centre experience. You have worked in a call centre and in a fast paced environment. Insurance experience is not required. - Empathy under pressure. You communicate with clarity and warmth, especially when customers are in distress. - An eye for detail. You get the facts right and build rapport quickly over the phone. - Prioritisation at volume. You can multitask, set priorities and manage your time in a high volume contact centre. - Shift availability. 37.5 hours a week, five days out of seven, on shifts between 8am and 6pm Monday to Sunday. That normally means one weekend a month. - You work AI-first. You will use AI daily here, and we mean daily. You do not need to arrive an expert, but you do need to arrive curious, experiment fast, and take ownership of getting good quickly. People who wait to be trained will find this uncomfortable.
