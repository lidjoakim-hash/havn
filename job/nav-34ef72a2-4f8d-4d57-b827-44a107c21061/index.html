Senior Integration Engineer

Monil AS · Oslo, Norway

Lønn ikke oppgittHeltidvia NAVFrist 19.5.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Monil is building technology that helps farmers manage their animals through virtual fencing. As we grow, our systems need to support more customers, more activity, and increasingly complex operations. We are looking for a Senior Integration Engineer to take ownership of the integration lifecycle at Monil - from technology decisions and design to implementation and operations. You will connect our business systems with each other and with our product platform, helping data flow reliably and processes run with less manual work. Joining Monil means becoming part of a fast-growing company where the integrations you build will play a key role in enabling our next stage of growth. You will have the opportunity to shape how our systems work together, make technology decisions, and see your work make a difference across the business. If you enjoy building, taking ownership, and growing alongside a company with big ambitions, this is an exciting time to come on board. At Monil, growing the company is a team sport - we roll up our sleeves, turn ideas into reality, and see the impact of what we build. Your role You will shape and develop Monil’s integration capabilities, making practical technology and architecture decisions while delivering solutions that address real business needs. Working closely with Business Technology colleagues, product engineering teams, business stakeholders, and external partners, you will own integrations throughout their lifecycle. This includes improving existing solutions, building new ones, and evolving or retiring them as our needs change. What you will do Make technology and design decisions • Own integrations and microservices throughout their lifecycle - from technology choices and design to development and operations. • Connect our business systems and product platform, automating processes and reducing manual work. • Build secure, reliable solutions with reusable components, automated testing, and effective monitoring. • Collaborate across teams to understand needs, troubleshoot issues, and continuously improve solutions. What you bring • Experience designing, building, and operating integrations, APIs, or microservices in production. • Python skills • Experience using Claude to support integration development, with careful review and validation of generated code. • Proficiency with Git, automated testing, and deployment practices. • Experience with event-driven architectures, cloud services, and infrastructure as code. • Sound technical judgment and the ability to communicate clearly with both technical and non-technical colleagues. You do not need experience with every technology we use - we value strong engineering fundamentals, curiosity, and the ability to learn. How you work You enjoy making technical decisions and turning them into working solutions. You take ownership, keep things practical, and balance delivery speed with reliability. You collaborate openly, share knowledge, and adapt as Monil grows. What success looks like • Business systems and the product platform exchange data securely and reliably. • Integrations have clear ownership throughout their lifecycle. • New integrations are easier and faster to deliver through reusable components and practical standards. • Business processes require fewer manual steps and handovers. • Problems are detected early and are straightforward to diagnose and resolve. • Solutions remain understandable and maintainable as Monil grows.
