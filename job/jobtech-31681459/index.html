Tax Technology Consultant | Tax Technology & Operations | Stockholm

Ernst & Young Aktiebolag · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 410 000–480 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 29.11.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Do you want to help businesses turn complex tax rules into practical technology solutions? Join EY's Nordic Tax Technology and Operations team in Stockholm and work where indirect tax, finance and technology meet. The opportunity Digital reporting requirements are changing how companies manage VAT and e-invoicing. In this role, you will help clients understand what new requirements mean for their data, processes and systems, and support them from early analysis through implementation. You will collaborate with tax specialists, finance teams, IT architects, developers and technology providers across the Nordics and our wider international network. This role offers the opportunity to build specialist expertise in a fast-developing area while contributing to major client projects and the continued growth of our Nordic offering. Your key responsibilities Your responsibilities will be adapted to your experience and may include: Lead client workshops to understand current processes, identify needs and define requirements. Translate indirect tax and e-invoicing regulations into clear process and system requirements. Design practical solutions and documentation that connect tax, finance and technology. Work with client teams and delivery specialists to implement and test solutions, including SAP-based e-invoicing solutions. Coordinate stakeholders and contribute insights on developments such as ViDA and real-time reporting. Skills and attributes for success You enjoy making complex topics easier to understand and can connect business needs with technology. You are comfortable working with different stakeholders and take a structured, collaborative approach to delivery. Qualifications A university degree in taxation, business, finance, information systems, computer science or a related field. Relevant experience in indirect tax, tax technology, finance transformation, ERP implementation or a comparable advisory environment. The ability to analyze complex requirements and turn them into clear processes, documentation or technology solutions. Strong communication skills in English and the ability to work effectively with both business and technology stakeholders. Knowledge of SAP, particularly SAP Document and Reporting Compliance (SAP DRC) Meritorious Experience with VAT or GST, e-invoicing, digital reporting or tax compliance transformation. Tax technology platforms such as Avalara, Vertex or Thomson Reuters. Familiarity with Peppol, country-specific e-invoicing frameworks, ViDA or real-time reporting requirements. Experience with requirements gathering, solution design, user acceptance testing, agile delivery or project coordination. Experience working with multinational organisations, cross-border teams or large-scale transformation projects. As a person you are Curious and motivated to learn in a fast-changing specialist area. Analytical and solution-oriented, with an eye for both detail and the bigger picture. Collaborative, clear and confident when working across tax, finance and technology teams. What we offer you Continuous learning: Develop the mindset and skills to navigate whatever comes next. Success as defined by you: Tools and flexibility to make a meaningful impact, your way. Transformative leadership: Insights, coaching, and confidence to be the leader the world needs. Diverse and inclusive culture: Be embraced for who you are and empowered to use your voice. Are you ready to shape your future with confidence? Apply today. Please submit your application no later than 29th of November 2026. Selection and interviews will be conducted on an ongoing basis.
