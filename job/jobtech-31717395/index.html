Night Nanny – Östermalm, Stockholm

Nanndis AB · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 130–160 SEK/time (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 11.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Ref. 4297Ö We are looking for a warm, caring and experienced night nanny to become an important part of a lovely family in Östermalm. Two nannies will share the position, one covering daytime and one nighttime, working alternate weeks. The family hopes to find someone with a genuine passion for children who can become like an extra mum to their little boy, providing love, security and stability. The mother has an injury that limits her ability to lift and physically assist her son. A nanny must therefore always be present and available when he is with her, providing essential practical support. About the Little Boy He is a happy, affectionate, confident and energetic 4.5-year-old who loves tennis, football and cars. He enjoys playing, exploring and spending time with an engaged adult. He attends nursery approximately 09:00–15:00 and is in good health. English is his main language, and the family would ideally like an English-speaking nanny to help maintain and develop his English. Night Nanny – Responsibilities He usually goes to bed around 20:00 and sleeps until 05:30–06:30, occasionally waking briefly. Responsibilities include: Being available and monitoring his wellbeing throughout the night. Comforting and assisting him if he wakes. Preparing breakfast, meals and snacks. Completing morning routines and getting him ready for nursery. Nursery drop-off and collection. Playing and spending quality time together. Supporting his interests and activities. Packing his nursery bag. Making beds and keeping his areas tidy. Child-related laundry and ironing when needed. Light household tasks, including tidying up and wiping kitchen surfaces. Administering medication when required. The nanny sleeps in a separate area with the child, away from the mother. Working Schedule & Flexibility The position involves 12-hour shifts every other week, averaging approximately 167 hours per month. Reliability and consistency are essential. Occasional cover for the other nanny may be required in case of sickness. Both nannies are expected to work as a supportive team. International Travel The family travels internationally throughout the year, sometimes for 1–2 weeks or longer, and the nanny must be happy to accompany them. During trips, extended working hours may be required, with time off in lieu afterwards. Both nannies may occasionally cover daytime and nighttime duties. The family also has a second home abroad. A driving licence is not essential but would be an advantage. The Ideal Candidate We are looking for someone who is: Warm, loving, empathetic and genuinely passionate about children. Experienced with young children. Energetic, playful, calm and patient. Emotionally intelligent and confident setting loving boundaries. Reliable, responsible and trustworthy. Flexible, proactive and helpful. Comfortable working independently and as part of a team. Happy to travel internationally. English- or Swedish-speaking. Comfortable administering medication when required. A Special Long-Term Opportunity This is an opportunity to become a valued and trusted part of a warm family, building a close and lasting relationship with their little boy. We are looking for someone who will genuinely care about his happiness and development and bring warmth, stability and positive energy into his everyday life. If you are an experienced, caring and dependable nanny who would love to become part of this family, we would love to hear from you! Intervjuer sker löpande under ansökningsperioden. Om Nordic Light Nannies: Vi söker nu en ny medarbetare som vill ha nanny som sitt yrke. Vi arbetar mot familjer som ställer höga krav på kvalitet när de väljer en nanny. De flesta av våra tjänster är på minst halvtid och vi tycker det är väldigt viktigt att även nannyn trivs bra ihop med familjen. Därför tar vi dina önskemål på stort allvar när vi presenterar familjer för dig. Vi har Sveriges bästa villkor för nannies med bland annat sjuklön och semesterersättning. Lönerna
