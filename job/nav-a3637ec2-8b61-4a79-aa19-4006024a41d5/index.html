Carpenters - Bergen - Exciting Projects & Career Opportunities Skuteviken Personell AS continues to grow and has several exciting projects in Bergen and the surrounding area. We are now looking for independent and skilled carpenters to strengthen our team. In addition to traditional carpentry work, we also need candidates with experience in interior system installations. You do not need to be a fully qualified carpenter - practical skills and solid experience are equally valuable. Typical work tasks include: • Building exterior walls and wooden house structures • Installation of walls, ceilings, doors, and windows • Interior system installations (walls, ceilings, office partitions) • Renovation and finishing work • Reading and working from technical drawings We offer: • Competitive salary: NOK 275 - 307 per hour (depending on experience) • Permanent full-time employment • Varied projects - both traditional carpentry and/or system carpentry • Coverage of daily travel costs (if more than 7.5 km from home to workplace) • Free professional tools and work clothes • Housing in company apartments if needed • Supportive and service-oriented administration • Career development opportunities for the right candidates Requirements: • Minimum 2 years' experience in carpentry or interior system installations • Ability to read and understand technical drawings • Knowledge of Scandinavian building methods is an advantage • English or Scandinavian language skills (professional competence is most important) Personal qualities: • High work ethic • Reliable and punctual • Independent and solution-oriented Interested? Please send your CV and application as soon as possible. Applications are reviewed on an ongoing basis.
