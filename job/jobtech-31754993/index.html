🚀 We’re Hiring: Senior Expert – Electrical & Electronics Hardware | ECU Global Component Owner Company: Hykmann Global Business Solutions AB Role: Senior Expert – Hardware Electrical & Electronics Engineer Specialisation: Electronic Control Units (ECUs) | Electrical Hardware Development | PCB Design | Component Lifecycle Management | Supplier Management Seniority: Senior Expert – typically 12+ years of relevant experience About the Role Hykmann Global Business Solutions AB is seeking an experienced Senior Expert in Electrical & Electronics Hardware Engineering to take ownership of the development, delivery and lifecycle management of a family of Electronic Control Units (ECUs). In this role, you will act as a Global Component Owner (GCO), ensuring that assigned electronic and mechanical components meet technical and quality requirements and are correctly integrated into their intended application environments. Key Responsibilities Global Component Ownership & Lifecycle Management Take responsibility for the delivery and lifecycle management of a family of ECUs and associated electronic and mechanical components. Ensure ECUs meet defined technical, quality and performance requirements. Verify that components are correctly installed and integrated into their target environments. Manage complex components or a large portfolio of less complex components. Coordinate component development, qualification, delivery and lifecycle activities. Secure cross-functional alignment and business commitment for component-related decisions. Electrical & Electronics Hardware Development Develop, evaluate and document electrical and electronic components. Document engineering activities, including relevant technical analyses and simulations. Define and communicate technical requirements and specifications to suppliers. Evaluate new components and recommend suitable component selections. Provide expertise in electrical hardware development and electronic component design. Support PCB design and related hardware development activities. Supplier & Stakeholder Management Define technical requirements and expectations for suppliers. Coordinate supplier activities and distribution of technical work. Manage operational supplier relationships within the assigned component area. Evaluate supplier capabilities and recommend suitable suppliers. Lead technical discussions, reviews and delivery follow-ups. Build effective collaboration between suppliers, engineering teams and business stakeholders. Product Strategy & Roadmap Develop and execute product strategy roadmaps for assigned component areas. Align component lifecycle plans with product and business objectives. Identify opportunities for component improvements, standardisation and future development. Monitor relevant technology developments and market trends. Contribute to long-term technical direction and strategic component decisions. Technical Leadership & Knowledge Development Act as a recognised subject-matter expert in electrical and electronics engineering. Provide technical guidance to engineering teams. Mentor colleagues and contribute to engineering capability development. Build and maintain internal and external technical networks. Communicate complex technical decisions and technology opportunities clearly. Required Technical Competence Electrical & Electronics Engineering More than 10 years of relevant experience in electrical and electronics engineering. Extensive knowledge of electronic hardware and electrical component development. Experience with Electronic Control Units (ECUs) and related technologies. Understanding of component development, qualification, integration and lifecycle management. Ability to define, evaluate and document technical component requirements. Component & Supplier Ownership Experience managing technical component portfolios. Defining technical requirements and specifications for suppliers. Evaluating supplier proposals and assessing component suitability.
