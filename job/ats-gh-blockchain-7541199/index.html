Blockchain is connecting the world to the future of finance. As the most trusted and fastest-growing global crypto company, it helps millions of people worldwide safely access cryptocurrency. Since its inception in 2011, Blockchain has earned the trust of over 90 million wallet holders and more than 40 million verified users, facilitating over $1 trillion in crypto transactions. We are looking for a Head of Business Intelligence to lead our BI function and help scale reporting and data-driven decision-making across Blockchain.com. This role is based in either London or Paris and sits at the intersection of analytics, finance, product, and business operations. The Head of BI is a senior people leader responsible for building and managing a team of data analysts who own company-wide business intelligence and reporting. Your team will deliver high-quality dashboards, recurring reporting, executive insights, and self-service analytics capabilities that empower teams across Finance, Accounting, Product, Growth, Trading, Fraud, and Operations. A key priority for this role will be partnering closely with our Finance and Accounting teams to support the company's evolving financial reporting, governance, and public company reporting requirements. You will help establish reliable, consistent, and auditable reporting frameworks, ensuring that business-critical metrics are accurate, well-defined, and aligned with the needs of senior leadership and external reporting stakeholders. This role requires strong leadership, stakeholder management, financial and operational acumen, and a passion for building scalable reporting systems and a strong analytics culture across the organization.
