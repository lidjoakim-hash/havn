The Senior Finance Systems Developer role will proactively support the AI-driven development, implementation, and maintenance of Carta’s enterprise finance systems with a primary focus on NetSuite, Zuora, and related peripheral systems. This role will also collaborate with US counterparts to support the Middle East and APAC regions.
