SUPO, where required) WHY THIS ROLE MATTERS:   The SAR Training Specialist within ICEYE’s Data line of business will support the development, customization, and global delivery of technical training programs to customers across the world. This role enables customers, particularly in the Defence and Intelligence (D&I) sector, to effectively understand and apply ICEYE’s SAR capabilities, products, and services to support mission-critical objectives.   WHO WE ARE ICEYE is the world leader in sovereign intelligence from space. We deliver persistent monitoring capabilities to detect and respond to changes in any location on Earth.   ICEYE owns the world's largest and most advanced SAR (synthetic aperture radar) satellite constellation. To our customers we provide intelligence with unmatched quality, latency and revisit times, in any weather, day or night. To governments who choose to operate their own constellation we provide this proven capability as a sovereign system.   ICEYE-built constellations serve customers in defence and intelligence, environmental monitoring, insurance and emergency management. We enable fast decisions that contribute to a safer future.   Founded and headquartered in Finland, ICEYE operates globally with over 1000 employees across Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. YOUR DAY-TO-DAY RESPONSIBILITIES   - Develop, maintain and tailor training programs covering remote sensing principles, SAR fundamentals, and the practical application of ICEYE’s technology across operational use cases. - Deliver both in-person and virtual training sessions to a global customer base, adapting materials and delivery approaches to suit varying levels of technical proficiency and requirements. - Align training content with evolving product features and customer needs, ensuring materials reflect the latest advancements in ICEYE’s portfolio of offerings. - Support customer adoption and operational readiness through hands-on instruction, live demonstrations, and scenario-based learning that emphasizes real-world use. - Collaborate with internal teams, including Product and Engineering, to ensure training materials are accurate, current, and aligned with broader customer engagement strategies - Represent ICEYE at customer workshops, briefings, and industry events, acting as a trusted technical educator and advocate for the effective use of SAR data   WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR Must haves: - Experience in technical training and curriculum development - Strong communication and presentation skills, with the ability to explain complex topics to technical and non-technical audiences. - Experience of D&I sector use of satellite imagery/SAR e.g. IMINT analyst - Proficiency with satellite imagery/SAR processing tools and GIS platforms desirable - Willingness to travel internationally regularly - Ability to work effectively across diverse cultural and operational environments - prior experience engaging with international customers is strongly preferred - Bachelor's degree (or higher level) or equivalent in Remote Sensing, Geography, Engineering or a related technical field.   APPLICATION PROCESS -
