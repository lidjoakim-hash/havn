Job description Are you in the early stages of your career and passionate about public health, digital networks, and communication? We are currently seeking a Junior Expert for an exciting consultancy assignment at the European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control (ECDC) in Stockholm. In this role, you will be employed as a consultant through us, starting in January 2027. As a Junior Expert, you will work directly with the Lighthouse team. You will be asked to engage with multidisciplinary public health professionals, researchers, and practitioners. The ideal candidate has some experience with digital communities, understands how online platforms function, and grasps the relevance of behavioural and social factors in community discussions. Practical Information & How to Apply Start date: Mid-January 2027. Duration: Initial order of 3 months, with a strong possibility of extension up to 6 months. Employment: You will be employed as a consultant via us for the duration of the assignment. Deadline to apply: December 25th, 2026. Your CV and letter of motivation must be combined and submitted as one single document (do not upload two separate files). Responsibilities Your Tasks and ResponsibilitiesEngagement & Platform Management: Support the planning and delivery of community activities. Facilitate and initiate discussions, peer exchanges, and other collaborative activities. Onboard new members and support management to administer the user base. Report on community participation and platform usage, collect feedback, and provide first-line support to members on the use of the platform. Communication & Project Management: Coordinate email communication to the Community, including newsletters and announcements. Competences and Experience (Required) University degree or relevant professional experience in public health, health communication, behavioural sciences, knowledge management, or a related field. Some exposure to professional expert- or knowledge networks. (Experience with an online network would be a strong asset). Experience in community engagement, facilitation of groups, or stakeholder engagement. Excellent English communication skills (spoken and written). Project coordination experience, along with strong analytical and organisational skills. Experience using Microsoft 365, and preferably digital content or community-management tools.
