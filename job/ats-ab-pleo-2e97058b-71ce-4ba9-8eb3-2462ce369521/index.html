ABOUT PLEO Messy spend management is tricky business. And tedious processes are a lose-lose situation for all involved, not just finance. At Pleo, we're changing that. We build spend solutions that make managing money seamless, empowering, and surprisingly effective for finance teams and employees alike - with a vision to help all businesses ‘go beyond’. The word ‘Pleo’ actually means ‘more than you’d expect’, and living by that mantra has been the secret to our success over the last 10 years. Now, we’re at a pivotal moment in our journey; every move we make has a direct impact on our 40,000+ customers, our business, and our collective success. We need people who take pride in uncovering customer needs, who turn complex problems into simple solutions, challenge the way things are done (respectfully), and always aim high. With great ambitions driving us forward, we can’t say we’ve got this whole thing figured out. And frankly, that’s half the fun! What we can say is that we’re a driven, progressive, and, importantly, a kind bunch of 850+ people from over 100 nationalities, all committed to delivering the future of business spending, together. About the role We’re looking for a Sales Operations Business Partner to sit between our sales leadership and the systems, data, and processes that run the revenue engine. You’ll help the sales team understand where deals stand, where revenue is coming from, and where it’s at risk. You’ll own how we track pipeline, report on performance, and build the dashboards leaders rely on to make decisions. This is a role for someone who thinks in business outcomes first and uses data and tools as the means to get there. What you’ll do - Own pipeline management processes, including stage definitions, forecasting cadence, and hygiene standards, and work with sales managers to keep the pipeline accurate. - Build and maintain regular sales reporting covering bookings, pipeline coverage, conversion rates, win/loss, and rep and team performance. - Design, build, and maintain dashboards that give leadership and frontline managers a clear view of performance against targets. - Act as a business partner to sales leaders: understand their priorities, translate them into operational requirements, and bring data-backed recommendations to planning and business reviews. - Support forecasting and quarterly business reviews by preparing the data, challenging assumptions, and flagging risks early. - Own day-to-day administration and configuration of the CRM (e.g. Salesforce or HubSpot), including fields, workflows, user access, and reporting structure, in partnership with the RevOps or systems team. - Drive adoption of CRM and sales processes by working with managers and reps, and by making the right behaviour the easy one. - Support territory, quota, and compensation-related analysis as needed, in partnership with Finance. - Identify process gaps and inefficiencies across the sales funnel and lead improvements, documenting changes and training the team on them. - Work cross-functionally with Marketing, Finance, Customer Success, and Product Ops to keep data consistent across systems.
