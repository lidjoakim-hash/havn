About Staffbase We inspire people to achieve great things together. Our mission is to help organizations unlock the power of inspirational communication with the first AI-native Employee Experience Platform. Our industry-leading and award-winning agentic AI communications channels - intranet, employee app and email solutions - create engaging experiences that connect and empower employees. Headquartered in Chemnitz, Germany and New York City, with offices in Berlin, London, Sydney, Tokyo, Prague, and Minneapolis–St. Paul, our diverse team of 550+ employees supports 1,500+ customers—reaching over 14 million employees—in transforming their employee experience. We are proud to be a Unicorn company—privately valued at over $1 billion—demonstrating strong growth, innovation, and lasting impact in our industry. Together, we’re shaping the future of workplace communication. This isn't a general sales role. You'll own a demanding pipeline of mid-market deals, positioning our mission-critical platform as a must-have for HR, Communications, and IT leadership. If you're energized by juggling multiple deals at once, creating urgency, and relentlessly pushing toward five-to-six-figure ARR, we want to talk to you. This role can be filled remote within Germany, or in one of our Germany based office locations (Chemnitz, Berlin, Munich, Cologne, Leipzig, Dresden). What you’ll be doing • Managing complex sales processes with multiple external and internal stakeholders and leveraging all available resources efficiently • Full circle sales responsibility, managing end-to-end process from first contact to final contract closing • Acquisition of new customers, focusing on companies with 1,000+ employees • Pitching the Staffbase vision to decision makers in Communication, HR and IT teams • Strategic account and territory planning by industry and ICP, incorporating key insights (challenges, etc.) • Close collaboration with marketing, customer success, renewal and product teams to optimize sales strategy • Representing Staffbase as an enthusiastic brand ambassador • Contributing your own ideas and strategies to further develop our company culture, values, and vision What you need to be successful • At least 3 years of experience in a B2B sales role – ideally in software/SaaS sales • Experience managing and owning complex sales projects with multiple internal and external stakeholders • Strong closing abilities and a track record of consistently meeting or exceeding targets • Previous collaboration with communication departments, IT, HR, and C-level decision-makers • Advanced pitching skills, with the ability to engage and influence senior stakeholders • High emotional intelligence and strong ability to identify and understand customer needs • Excellent communication and negotiation skills • Fluent German (C2) and business-proficient English skills • Fluency in a third language such as French, Spanish or Italian is a plus • Passion, humor and enthusiasm • Experience in a scale-up environment is a plus What you'll get • Competitive Compensation -
