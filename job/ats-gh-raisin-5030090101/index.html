Raisin is the world’s leading platform for savings and investment products. Founded in 2012, the FinTech connects consumers with banks in the EU, the UK and the US. This gives consumers better interest rates and banks a diversified form of refinancing. Our vision is to offer savings and investments without barriers and thus open up the global 160 trillion euro market. Raisin currently employs more than 800 people from over 75 countries worldwide. Today, the platform holds over 80 billion euros in assets from more than one million investors which have accrued over 5 billion euros in returns. Team The Business Development team at Raisin is constantly on the hunt for new partnership opportunities with banks across Europe. Our team, comprising Regional Sales Directors and Sales Operations, works collaboratively to identify and analyze high-value potential clients, optimize the sales pipeline, and seamlessly bring financial partners live onto Raisin’s platforms. As a Junior Sales Operations Manager, you will serve as the engine of the sales organization: supporting deal execution, automating workflows, and assisting in the deployment and adoption of cutting-edge AI tools to continuously empower our team and scale our regional growth. Your Responsibilities As a Junior Sales Operations Manager, you will play a key role in empowering our Business Development team to build and scale partner bank relationships across Europe. Sitting at the intersection of sales, analytics, and strategy, you will help streamline our sales processes, maintain CRM hygiene, generate insights to optimize partner acquisition, and ensure smooth operational execution as prospective banking partners progress from initial contact to going live on the Raisin platform. Sales Process & Pipeline Management: • Support Regional Sales Directors by maintaining and optimizing the sales pipeline within the CRM . • Ensure accurate tracking of deal stages, bank onboardings, and key milestones from initial outreach through contract signing to technical launch. • Monitor and report on core sales KPIs, conversion metrics, and regional deal progress. AI Implementation & Workflow Automation: • Assist in identifying opportunities across the sales lifecycle to leverage AI and automation tools (e.g., AI call debriefs, automated email drafting, predictive lead scoring, automated CRM entry). • Build, test, and roll out automated workflows to streamline administrative tasks for Regional Sales Directors and improve deal velocity. • Train and support the sales team on adopting our AI tools and best practices, driving continuous team enablement and efficiency gains. Data Analysis & Reporting: • Collect, structure, and analyze commercial and operational data to evaluate potential bank partnership opportunities. • Prepare regular reporting dashboards, pipeline performance reviews, and market/competitor analyses for sales leaders and management. Cross-Functional Operations & Enablement: • Coordinate with internal teams including Legal, Compliance, Product, and Integration Engineering to streamline the onboarding process for new bank partners. • Help create, maintain, and update sales collateral, onboarding checklists, training materials, and process documentation. • Assist in managing sales tools, ensuring seamless integrations and optimal usage across the sales team.
