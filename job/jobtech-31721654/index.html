​ Location: MalmöDepartment: Do It Yourself & E-commerce, Business ApplicationsHiring Manager: Marcus Brattström The roleAs Product Owner for E-commerce at Verisure, you will help shape and develop our e-commerce platform together with an experienced, distributed development team. You will connect business needs with technical delivery, create clear priorities and support collaboration between developers, architects, business stakeholders and other technology teams. The role also includes system ownership of Adobe Commerce/Magento, with responsibility for balancing new development with platform maintenance, security, quality and stability. You will join an international environment where collaboration, openness and learning from different perspectives are central to how we work. What you will be up toOwn and prioritise the team’s backlog based on business needs and team capacity Translate business needs into clear requirements and realistic delivery plans Coordinate the day-to-day work of a distributed development team Facilitate planning, refinement, follow-ups and retrospectives Create alignment with stakeholders around priorities, timelines and dependencies Act as system owner for Adobe Commerce/Magento Ensure maintenance, security, incidents and technical improvements are included in planning Collaborate closely with the AEM team, architects and other dependent teams Good fit if you haveA couple of years of relevant experience as a Product Owner or in a similar digital product or technical delivery role Experience owning and prioritising a software or digital product backlog Experience working closely with distributed development teams The ability to turn business needs into clear requirements and priorities Enough technical understanding to discuss integrations, APIs, releases, security and dependencies with technical colleagues Strong communication and stakeholder collaboration skills A structured, thoughtful and pragmatic approach Nice to have / technologies and areas we work withE-commerce and digital customer journeys Adobe Commerce/Magento Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) APIs and enterprise integrations System or application ownership Technical project management Platform lifecycle, security and incident management Jira, Azure DevOps, Scrum or Kanban Retail, DTC or subscription businesses You do not need experience in every area. We value curiosity, relevant experience and a willingness to learn. Should I apply?You may enjoy this role if several of these resonate with you: You enjoy bringing clarity and structure to complex topics You like connecting people across business and technology You listen to different perspectives and help turn them into shared priorities You enjoy working closely with development teams and technical specialists You are comfortable balancing customer needs with technical quality and long-term platform health You value collaboration, knowledge sharing and learning from others FAQDo I need Adobe Commerce/Magento experience?No. It is valuable, but experience with another complex digital or e-commerce platform can be equally relevant. Do I need to be a developer?No. You should be comfortable discussing integrations, APIs, releases, dependencies and technical considerations, but you will work alongside developers and architects who provide deep technical expertise. Is this a strategic Product Manager role?Not primarily. The role is close to the development team and combines product ownership, delivery coordination and system ownership. What is the main challenge?Finding the right balance between new functionality, stakeholder needs, security, maintenance and the long-term health of the platform. Bottom line If you enjoy connecting people, business and technology, this is an opportunity to take broad ownership of an enterprise e-commerce platform while working closely with an experienced international team.
