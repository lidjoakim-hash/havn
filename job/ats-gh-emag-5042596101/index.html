Join us, grow faster! AI Platform Engineer (InsightOps, DevEx & Cloud Enablement Team) InsightOps is the team that measures and governs how 800+ engineers at eMAG build software. We own the FinOps and engineering-insights layer in Backstage: cloud and AI costs across AWS, GCP, Tencent and the LLM providers, developer productivity data (Jira, GitLab, GetDX, worklogs), Jira administration and automation, and the seat and license governance for AI tools (Cursor, Claude Code and the rest of the AI coding stack). In short, we make sure managers have clear, reliable data on what their teams use and what it costs. We're looking for an AI Platform Engineer to own the AI insights and governance layer: from AI cost attribution and budget controls, to adoption and productivity metrics, seat and API key lifecycle automation, and AI agents that turn our engineering data into answers. You’ll work at the intersection of FinOps, developer analytics and platform engineering. What you’ll have to do: • Owning AI cost governance: attributing OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini and AI-tool spend to teams and products through provider admin APIs, key mapping and the AI Costs plugin in Backstage; • Building the AI usage data pipeline (provider billing exports, gateway telemetry, Cursor and Claude admin APIs) into Backstage, Tableau and Elasticsearch, reconciled monthly against invoices; • Automating the seat and API key lifecycle for AI tools: provisioning, migrations, inactive-seat reclaim and budget alerts, with audit trails for compliance; • Measuring AI adoption and impact across the SDLC (GetDX, Jira, GitLab, worklogs) and surfacing it as role-aware views for managers and executives; • Building AI-powered insights over our engineering data: agents and MCP servers on Jira, GitLab, Backstage and cost data, ticket smart routing, spend anomaly and infrastructure drift detection; • Productionizing agentic workflows for reporting, Jira automation and SDLC audit evidence, with evaluation, guardrails and cost controls; • Partnering with the AI tooling and security teams on AI policy enforcement and data classification; • Measuring and documenting what you build: cost saved, adoption rate, data freshness and correctness. What makes you a good fit: • 3+ years of backend, data or platform engineering experience (Python or TypeScript/Node.js), comfortable with SQL and data pipelines; • Hands-on experience with LLM APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini), including usage and billing endpoints — building integrations, not just prompting; • Agentic workflow design (tool-use, multi-step reasoning, orchestration) and MCP (Model Context Protocol) server development; • Familiarity with Docker/Kubernetes and CI/CD (GitLab CI preferred); • Understanding of FinOps: cost allocation, showback and chargeback, budgets, anomaly detection; AWS, GCP or Tencent cost tooling is a plus; • Experience with LLM observability and cost instrumentation (Langfuse or similar) and reporting tools (Tableau, Kibana or similar); • Nice to have: Backstage plugin development (React/TypeScript), Jira REST API and automation, developer-productivity analytics; • You think in systems and data, not features, and care whether a number is right before it reaches a dashboard; • You me
