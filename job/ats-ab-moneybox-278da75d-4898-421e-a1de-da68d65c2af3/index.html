About Moneybox At Moneybox, our mission is to give everyone the means to get more out of life. We're guided by our belief that wealth isn't about the money, it's about the means to more - more freedom, opportunities, possibilities, and peace of mind. It's a hands-on role at the centre of the People Team: you'll make sure every employee has a smooth, accurate and compliant experience at each stage of their time with us. As a regulated business, getting the details right matters. You'll own our SM&CR administration and background screening, keep our people data accurate across our HR systems, and run payroll administration each month. We also want someone who sees a manual process and asks how it could be automated.
