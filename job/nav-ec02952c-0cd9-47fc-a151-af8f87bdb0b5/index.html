Do you want to lead a skilled accounting team, build trusted customer relationships, and help shape Staria’s growth in Norway? Are you energized by a role that brings together people leadership, customer satisfaction, and service quality? We are looking for a proactive, growth-minded Service Manager to lead our Norwegian accounting team and take ownership of key customer relationships. This is your opportunity to make a visible impact – shaping how we lead our team, serve our customers, and grow our operations in Norway. Journey that awaits you As Service Manager, you will lead a skilled team of seven Accounting Advisors and own a portfolio of ambitious growth companies. As the key link between your team, our customers and Staria’s delivery teams, you will build trusted partnerships and shape scalable services that help both our accounting team and customers succeed. This is a unique opportunity to shape Staria’s future in Norway. Rather than stepping into a fully defined setup, you will influence how we lead our people, collaborate with customers, and develop our operations, creating the structures, routines, and ways of working needed for sustainable growth. You will take ownership of both team performance and customer delivery, ensuring high-quality services and long-term customer value. By creating clarity, anticipating risks, and turning decisions into action, you will keep priorities, responsibilities, and deadlines on track. You will also work closely with Sales to help accelerate Staria’s growth in Norway. While there is no individual sales target, you will act as a trusted subject-matter expert, uncovering customer needs, identifying opportunities to expand partnerships, and ensuring our solutions create value for both customers and Staria. In practice, you will: • Lead and develop your team, fostering performance, collaboration, wellbeing, and professional growth. • As the team expands, you will be responsible for recruiting and onboarding new team members. • Have direct customer contact and build strong customer relationships through regular, proactive communication and clear expectation management. • Oversee service delivery, deadlines, risks, and commitments, translating customer needs and feedback into clear actions for the delivery team. • Guide escalations to smooth resolutions while using customer insights, team feedback, and recurring patterns to drive measurable improvements in delivery, continuity, and ways of working. • Oversee customer profitability, contracts, pricing, scope, and invoicing together with the Service Director. • Work together with sales and contribute with your expertise to find additional service opportunities, proposals, renewals, contract changes. Your everyday at Staria We are seeking candidates based in the Oslo or Stavanger area. Staria’s hybrid work model offers the best of both worlds: a modern office in the heart of Oslo, combined with the flexibility to work remotely. To ensure smooth collaboration with your team and stakeholders, we hope you’re able to spend a few days per week at the office on average. Our salary range for this role is 75.000-95.000NOK/month, depending on your background and scope of the role. Keys to success in this role For this position, we are looking for candidates who are located primarily in Oslo. We believe that the right person for the role is an experienced and commercially minded leader who combines strong leadership and accounting knowledge with a genuine customer focus and interest. You thrive in a fast-moving environment, take ownership, and create clarity when priorities shift. To succeed in this position, we believe that you also have: • Solid experience in the field of financial administration and a deep understanding of the accounting processes of medium-sized and large companies. Experience in an accounting office environment is a big advantage. • Experience in customer-facing tasks, managing customer relationships, and monitoring profitability. • Proven leadership experience and the ability to develop, motivate, and support accounting professionals. • Enthusiasm to grow and develop with Staria.
