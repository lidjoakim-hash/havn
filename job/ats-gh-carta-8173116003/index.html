Carta Lux S.à r.l. is a CSSF-licensed Specialised PFS providing fund administration, transfer agency, domiciliation, and related services to alternative investment funds in Luxembourg. As we grow our fund client base, our clients require a dedicated, CSSF-approved Responsable du Contrôle at the fund level. This is a newly created role sitting within our team in Luxembourg. This role carries a dual reporting line: operationally to the Authorised Manager responsible for the operations, and functionally to the RR. You will be formally appointed as RC for a portfolio of Carta-administered investment funds, carrying full statutory responsibility for each fund's AML/CFT framework.
