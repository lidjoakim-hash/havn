Senior Paralegal, Commercial Contracts Why We Have This Role Qualtrics’ Legal team supports a fast-growing book of commercial agreements across EMEA, and our Dublin office is central to that work. This role exists to keep contracts moving: reviewing agreements against our playbook, managing the CLM workflow end to end, and clearing the queue so our lawyers can focus on the deals that need them most. It’s built as a growth seat. The person who takes it will develop the judgment and process ownership that leads into a Contracts Manager role. As our volume grows, we need someone who can bring consistency to routine contracting while recognising when a deal needs a closer legal look. You’ll help make the contracting experience clearer and more predictable for our internal partners and customers, without losing sight of the risks we need to manage. How You’ll Find Success You’ll do well here if you’re precise under pressure, comfortable owning a queue of live matters at once, and curious about the “why” behind a playbook position rather than just applying it by rote. You ask good questions, flag risk early instead of guessing, and build trust with the lawyers you support by being consistent and organised. You’re motivated by growth: you want this role to be a launchpad, not a landing spot. You keep stakeholders informed, set clear expectations, and bring practical next steps when a matter gets stuck. Over time, you’ll use feedback and experience to handle more matters independently and spot opportunities to improve how the team works.
