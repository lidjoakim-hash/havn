Chefs Wanted - Porto Sociale - Italian Restaurant

RISTORANTE PORTO SOCIALE · Oslo, Norway

💰 ca. 400 000–480 000 kr/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia NAVFrist 23.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Join an exciting new chapter in Italian dining! Porto Sociale, an Italian restaurant in the heart of Oslo, is undergoing a major transformation. With a refreshed concept, upgraded premises, and ambitious plans, we are preparing to create an exceptional Italian dining experience.   We are now looking for passionate and skilled chefs who want to become part of our new kitchen team. Our vision is to combine authentic Italian traditions with high-quality ingredients, excellent craftsmanship, and a modern approach to hospitality. From handmade pasta and outstanding pizza to carefully prepared seafood, meat, and regional Italian specialties, we want to showcase the very best of Italian cuisine.   This is an exciting opportunity to join a restaurant during its relaunch and help shape its future. Your responsibilities • Prepare and present high-quality dishes according to the restaurant's standards. • Work across different kitchen sections, including pasta, meat, fish, and hot kitchen. • Ensure consistency, quality, and attention to detail in every dish. • Maintain excellent food safety, hygiene, and kitchen organization. • Collaborate closely with the Head Chef and the rest of the kitchen team. • Contribute ideas and creativity to menu development and daily operations. • Help create a positive, professional, and efficient working environment. Who are we looking for? • Previous experience as a chef in a professional restaurant kitchen. • A genuine passion for Italian cuisine and quality ingredients. • Experience with Italian cooking, fine dining, or premium restaurants is an advantage. • Experience with Italian pizza is a big advantage. • Good knowledge of cooking techniques, preparation, and presentation. • Ability to work efficiently under pressure while maintaining high standards. • A positive attitude, strong teamwork skills, and willingness to learn. • Good communication skills in English. Norwegian or Italian is a plus. • Flexibility and motivation to contribute across different kitchen stations. We welcome applications from chefs with different specializations and experience levels, particularly those with strong skills in Italian cuisine, seafood, meat, grilling, or pasta. What we offer • The opportunity to be part of an exciting restaurant relaunch in central Oslo. • A modernized restaurant with ambitious plans for the future. • The chance to work with authentic Italian cuisine and quality ingredients. • An opportunity to contribute to the development of a new kitchen team. • A professional and supportive working environment. • Opportunities for professional development and career growth. • Competitive salary based on qualifications and experience. Interested? Are you passionate about cooking and ready for a new challenge in Oslo? We would love to hear from you!   Send us your CV and a short introduction about yourself. Tell us about your experience, culinary interests, and why you would like to join Porto Sociale.   Come help us create the next chapter of Porto Sociale!
