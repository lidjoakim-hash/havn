Our mission at ŌURA is to empower every person to own their inner potential. Our award-winning products help our global community gain a deeper knowledge of their readiness, activity, and sleep quality by using their Oura Ring and its connected app. We've helped millions of people understand and improve their health by providing daily insights and practical steps to inspire healthy lifestyles. Empowering the world starts with living our values and empowering our team. As a quickly growing company focused on helping people live healthier and happier lives, we ensure that our team members have what they need to do their best work — both in and out of the office. Oura is building a Global Trade Program organized around three connected pillars: Risk, Controls & Governance; Trade Value Optimization; and Market Expansion & Enablement. As the Global Trade Compliance Manager, you will own the Market Expansion & Enablement pillar. You will make sure new products and new markets have trade readiness built into launch timelines, rather than discovered as a blocker after commitments have already been made. This is a ground-floor opportunity to build a program, not inherit one. What you will do: Trade readiness for launches • Own trade readiness for new product introductions (NPI) and new market entries, running classification/origin pre-assessments and market-entry regulatory scans before launch commitments are made • Build and administer repeatable trade-readiness checklists and review cadences so trade input reaches product and go-to-market decisions early, not after shipping dates are locked in • Serve as Trade's voice in launch decision forums, giving product and regional expansion teams a clear, defensible trade-readiness signal for go/no-go decisions Cross-pillar coordination • Act as the connective tissue between the Risk, Controls & Governance pillar and the Trade Value Optimization pillar, so risk clearances and landed-cost/duty inputs reach launch decisions with enough lead time to act on • Establish the handoff mechanisms this program needs today: routing new SKU, market-entry, or channel-expansion signals to the risk register, and requesting risk clearance before a duty-savings position is claimed • Give the Trade Value Optimization lead advance visibility into upcoming launches so FTA eligibility, preferential program qualification, and first-sale opportunities can be evaluated before sourcing and pricing are finalized Program building • Design and stand up the intake, documentation, and escalation processes for the pillar • Build and maintain a Trade Request Intake process so landed-cost and risk-clearance requests are logged and tracked rather than handled ad hoc • Report on pillar KPIs, including the
