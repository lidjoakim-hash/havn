Development Engineer within R&D to Olink

Academic Work Sweden AB · Uppsala, Sweden

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Would you like to work hands-on with laboratory development and contribute to the next generation of Life Science products? At Olink, you will have the opportunity to be part of developing innovative protein biomarker panels that help researchers worldwide better understand complex diseases and advance the field of precision medicine. As a Development Engineer, you will combine practical laboratory work with method development, experimental evaluation, and the optimization of advanced workflows. We are looking for someone with experience in laboratory work within areas such as molecular biology, biotechnology, or assay development, who enjoys working hands-on in a laboratory environment and is motivated by turning scientific concepts into products that can make a real difference to human health. We look forward to receiving your application! About the role Olink – Part of Thermo Fisher Scientific has experienced tremendous growth in recent years and has quickly made a global impact. Its groundbreaking products are used by leading research institutes and pharmaceutical companies to drive the next major breakthroughs in medicine. With headquarters and service laboratories in both Uppsala and Boston, Olink is a truly global Life Science company. Its technology is developed and used to provide new insights into complex diseases such as cancer, cardiovascular diseases, and neurological disorders – research that has the potential to genuinely change people's lives. Olink is now looking for a Development Engineer to join its R&D team, Reveal Development, which is responsible for developing a specific product line within low-plex solutions. The team focuses on developing and evaluating new protein panels, assessing their performance, and optimizing the laboratory processes used in product development. In this role, you will contribute to hands-on development activities, conducting experiments and laboratory testing to help ensure that new panels and methods meet established requirements. The role is primarily hands-on and laboratory-based. Your responsibilities will include conducting experiments, performing pipetting, operating laboratory instruments, and working with automated processes. An important part of the role will also involve evaluating and documenting test results and identifying opportunities to improve existing methods and workflows. You will work according to project plans and maintain close communication with project leads regarding the planning, prioritization, and execution of different tests. You will join a team of approximately 10 people, consisting of Development Engineers and Scientists. The team has a relatively new structure and is still evolving, but already offers a positive working environment where colleagues support one another and share knowledge. You will work closely with your team members while also collaborating with other functions across the organization. Your onboarding will include practical training, a structured onboarding checklist, and a dedicated mentor to support you during your initial period. This is a consultant assignment, meaning you will be employed by Academic Work and work on-site at Olink. The assignment will initially run for six months, with the possibility of an extension. The planned start date is February or March 2027. The role is primarily based on-site in the laboratory in Uppsala, with some flexibility to work remotely when the tasks allow.
