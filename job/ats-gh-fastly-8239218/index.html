Fastly helps people stay better connected with the things they love. Fastly’s edge cloud platform enables customers to create great digital experiences quickly, securely, and reliably by processing, serving, and securing our customers’ applications as close to their end-users as possible — at the edge of the Internet. The platform is designed to take advantage of the modern internet, to be programmable, and to support agile software development. Fastly’s customers include many of the world’s most prominent companies, including GitHub, Yelp, Paramount, and JetBlue. We're building a more trustworthy Internet. Come join us. The Role You own and grow partner-attached bookings and ecosystem growth across France. Reporting to the Director of Partner Development - EMEA, you will recruit top-tier regional partners (MSPs, MSSPs, VARs, SIs), and execute a partner-first go-to-market motion, building long-term, high-value partnerships that drive new business. If France grows and our partners succeed, you win. Core Technology Focus CDN & Edge Compute | Next-Gen WAF | DDoS & Bot Mitigation | Edge Observability The Role • Recruit: Map the tech ecosystem in France to identify, pitch, and sign top-tier security, cloud, and edge partners. • Scale: Lead Joint Business Planning (JBP) with key partners to design sales initiatives, marketing activities, programs, and offerings that generate new opportunities with and through partners. • Activate & Enable: Partner with enablement, AEs, and marketing to train partner technical teams, deploy playbooks, and accelerate deal velocity. • Execution: Own regional partner forecasting, deal registration, and co-selling operations across France. What We Look For • Outcome Ownership: Self-directed execution. You identify market gaps in France and fill them without needing a task list. • Commercial & Technical Judgment: Comfortable driving complex negotiations and holding credible architectural conversations around cloud and security. • French Market Depth: Native fluency in French, business English, and an established network across French IT, cloud, and security channels. • Proven Track Record: Demonstrated success structuring and running partnerships and alliances and scaling partner-attached bookings.
