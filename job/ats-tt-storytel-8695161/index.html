Business Analytics Intern

Stories have the power to make the ordinary extraordinary. They help us think bigger, see beyond ourselves, and deepen our connection with others. As one of the world’s leading audiobook and e-book streaming platforms, Storytel brings unlimited listening to millions of users across 25+ markets. Driven by our purpose “Leading the future of storytelling, we move the world through stories”, Storytel Group inspires and entertains people around the world by blending innovation with tradition. We bring stories to life across various formats for everyone to discover. Anytime. Anywhere. Are we part of your next story? Apply for our Business Analytics Internship to join our team in Stockholm. This is a part-time position with an estimated start in early spring 2027. About the Role As a Business Analytics Intern, you’ll work with real data from a global streaming business and help answer questions that matter: Why do subscribers stay? What question should we be asking that nobody has asked yet? Where should we grow next? This is a paid part-time internship, alongside your studies, starting in February 2027. No two weeks look the same, and you’ll get to work hands-on from the start. In a nutshell, here’s what you’ll do: • Use AI, mainly Claude, to turn data into insights faster. • Help shape the future of AI analytics at Storytel. • Dig into subscriber and marketing data to find what drives growth. • Build dashboards and reports that colleagues actually use. • Support analyses behind real business decisions. About the team You’ll be part of a friendly, curious team where your ideas are heard from day one, and you’ll learn how data turns into decisions at a company operating in 25+ markets. You’ll get unique, hands-on mentorship from our VP Business and Data, who is leading how Storytel uses data with AI to make better decisions. About You You don’t need a perfect CV or years of experience. We care more about how you think than what you’ve already done. We believe you’re: • Curious, fast-learning and not afraid to ask questions. • Someone who enjoys numbers, patterns and finding the story in the data. • Hands-on and a self-starter who likes to get things done. • Down-to-earth and a good team player. • A clear communicator who can explain findings in a simple way. Your background: • Currently enrolled as a student at a university or higher education institution (any field with an analytical flavour, such as business, economics, statistics, engineering or similar). • Available around 40% (two days a week) during spring 2027. • Good communication skills in English. • Comfortable with Excel or Google Sheets. Experience with data or BI tools is a plus, but definitely not a must. We’ll teach you. • Ideally, you're comfortable working in Claude and other AI tools. You’ll use them a lot in this role. What we offer you • A part-time job with flexibility that fits around your studies. • Unique mentorship from our VP Business and Data. • A seat at the table, with real data and real responsibility. • Learning from skilled colleagues in an international, fast-moving environment. Join a world of stories At Storytel, we’re a team of creative story lovers who thrive on collaboration and new ideas. Our workplace is friendly, dynamic, and full of opportunities to experiment and make an impact. We believe in trust, flat hierarchies, and empowering you to grow with us. Does this sound like your next story? Simply fill out the application form in English and share your CV or LinkedIn profile. Answer a few questions, and you’re all set.
