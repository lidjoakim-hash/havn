Some roles can’t be filled with a standard search. Talent Discovery is the part of MongoDB’s Talent Acquisition team that finds and builds relationships with the people we want to hire. As a Talent Sourcer on the Talent Discovery team, you’ll work on our hardest-to-fill, niche, and business-critical roles, and build talent pipelines for the roles we hire for again and again. This work is deep research and real conversations: you’ll dig into adjacent markets and unconventional talent pools, find people who aren’t looking, and earn their interest in MongoDB. You’ll partner with recruiters and hiring managers to understand what a search really needs, build a targeted plan for finding it, and hand off strong, well-documented slates and pipelines as candidates move into formal interviews. We are looking to speak to candidates who are based in Dublin or Cork for our hybrid working model. In this role, you will • Own and drive the sourcing plan. Turn hiring needs into a clear search strategy, align stakeholders on the target profile, and lead the approach from there • Map talent markets. Use tools like Gem, SeekOut, LinkedIn Recruiter, GitHub, and X-ray search to research scarce talent pools and track talent movement, including competitors, adjacent markets, and emerging companies and geographies. • Reach out to candidates with intent. Write personal, informed outreach that reflects each person’s background and what would motivate them, not keyword matching. • Build long-term relationships. Engage and nurture passive candidates over time, so MongoDB is top of mind when the right role opens. • Qualify beyond the resume. Run conversations that surface what motivates each candidate, and keep them informed and interested as searches progress. • Hand off with full context. Present qualified slates and pipelines to recruiters and hiring managers, including each candidate’s background, motivations, fit, and history. • Share what you learn. Bring market insight back to the recruiting team, such as where talent is and what competitors are doing. • Keep the CRM/ATS clean. Keep search progress, pipeline health, and relationship history visible to the whole team. You are someone who has • 2 years of experience in sourcing, research, or recruiting, ideally including niche or hard-to-fill searches • Sharp research instincts and the confidence to take an unclear hiring need and shape it into a clear search strategy • A track record of engaging passive candidates and sustaining those relationships over time • Working knowledge of sourcing and market-intelligence tools (e.g., Gem, SeekOut, LinkedIn Recruiter, GitHub, X-ray search, or equivalents) • Experience using AI in sourcing and research, and good judgment about where it helps and where human judgment has to lead • Excellent written and verbal communication, and the ability to build trust with someone who isn’t looking for a job • Strong attention to data hygiene and follow-through • A collaborative mindset: you’ll work closely with recruiters and hiring managers, and you want to see your work turn into hires About MongoDB MongoDB is built for change, empowering our customers and our people to innovate at the speed of the market. We have redefined the data platform for the AI era, enabling builders to create, transform, and disrupt industries with software. MongoDB’s unified data platform, the most widely available, globally distributed data platform on the market, helps organizations modernize legacy workloads, embrace innovation, and unleash AI. Our cloud-native platform, MongoDB Atlas, is the only globally distributed, multi-cloud data platform and is available across AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure.
