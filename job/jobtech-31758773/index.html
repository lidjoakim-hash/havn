Job Description WHAT YOU’LL DO As an Art Director, Beauty, you’ll be responsible for shaping inspiring and commercially relevant beauty content across Editorial, Category Inspiration, and Lookbook productions. Combining strong creative vision with deep beauty expertise, you’ll lead concept development from idea through execution, ensuring every asset reflects the brand’s visual identity and resonates with customers. You’ll inspire creative teams, drive excellence on set, and elevate storytelling across every touchpoint. Develop creative concepts and visual ideas for Editorial content, Category Inspiration, and Lookbook imagery. Define and communicate creative direction across beauty productions to deliver engaging and impactful content. Provide input on casting and create briefs for lighting, hair, and make-up to support the desired visual expression. Lead and inspire creative teams on set, ensuring alignment with concept, quality standards, and brand direction. Guide final image selection, editing decisions, and retouching feedback to ensure a polished and elevated result. Stay informed on emerging trends within beauty, fashion, lifestyle, art, and visual culture to continuously evolve creative expression. WHO YOU’LL WORK WITH You’ll collaborate closely with Creative Lead, Stylists, Photographers, Hair & Make-up Artists, Producers, Retouchers, Content Creators, and other creative specialists. Together, you'll develop customer-facing content that strengthens the brand, drives engagement, and positions beauty at the forefront of the customer experience. WHO YOU ARE We are looking for people with… Proven experience as an Art Director within beauty, fashion, editorial, e-commerce, or brand-focused creative environments. A strong portfolio showcasing beauty-focused creative concepts, editorial storytelling, and visual excellence. Deep understanding of the beauty industry, including products, trends, aesthetics, and customer expectations. Strong visual storytelling capabilities with the ability to create inspiring, customer-centric creative concepts. Experience leading creative productions and directing multidisciplinary teams on set. Expertise in image selection, post-production processes, and delivering high-quality creative assets. A strong understanding of brand identity and how to translate strategy into compelling visual communication. And people who are… Passionate about beauty, creativity, and visual culture. Confident leader who can inspire teams and make clear creative decisions. Highly collaborative and skilled at building strong relationships across creative disciplines. Curious and trend-driven, with a constant desire to explore new ideas and opportunities. Detail-oriented while maintaining a strong commercial and customer-focused perspective. Adaptable and comfortable working in a fast-paced, evolving creative environment. WHO WE ARE At H&M Creative Studios, we develop and deliver H&M’s customer-facing brand and content experiences globally. Bringing together strategists, creatives, designers, creators, producers and specialists, we are responsible for H&M’s global campaigns, editorial storytelling, creator marketing, organic social channels, e-commerce content, brand design and physical brand experiences. Together, we shape how millions of customers discover, experience and engage with H&M every day WHY YOU’LL LOVE WORKING HERE At H&M Group, we are proud to be a vibrant and welcoming company. We offer our employees attractive benefits with extensive development opportunities around the globe. Join a creative environment where your vision directly influences how customers experience beauty and fashion. Work alongside talented creatives and industry experts across photography, styling, production, and content creation. Access opportunities to develop your leadership and creative expertise within a global organisation. Be part of a culture that values creativity, collaboration, innovation, and continuous lear
