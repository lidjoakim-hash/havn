The role of the Senior Projects Controls Engineer is to perform the tasks relating to the technical aspects of projects that have been agreed with the Project Manager at the outset. The Senior Projects Controls Engineer will provide on an ongoing basis, technical assistance to the site team on all controls and monitoring systems and ensure the installation is completed in compliance with the intended design, drawings and specifications.
