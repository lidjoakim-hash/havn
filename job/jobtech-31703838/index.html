Denna position rapporterar till: R&D Team Lead __ Your Role and responsibilities This role offers a fantastic opportunity to gain a central part in shaping future experiences of ABB’s industrial products and solutions. The position is based at ABB Corporate Research Center in Västerås, where cutting-edge research meets real industrial impact. You will belong to a highly motivated, international research team while collaborating with researchers, technology and product managers and industrial partners. ABB offers an inspiring work environment with excellent opportunities for professional growth and long-term career development. How can we design industrial technologies that help users make better decisions, work effectively, and navigate increasingly complex systems? We are looking for an innovative User Experience Researcher who combines deep human understanding with strategic thinking. You will design novel concepts and ensure that emerging solutions are not only technically advanced, but also trusted and valuable for the people who use them. Where appropriate, you will use AI-assisted tools to support research, design and validation activities.
