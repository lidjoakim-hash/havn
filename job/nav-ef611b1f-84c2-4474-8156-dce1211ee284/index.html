Construction Worker – Data Centre Projects

Fortica · Bjerkvik, Norway

💰 ca. 700 000–900 000 kr/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia NAVFrist 3.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Real projects, real impact. Join our site team. Fortica builds and finishes data centres across Norway. A data centre is a large building full of servers that run the internet, the cloud and the apps we all use every day. We are looking for construction workers who want to be part of building them. What you will do • Install and assemble building parts, such as ceilings, sandwich panels and flashing • Do finishing works and prepare the site • Follow drawings and instructions from your supervisor • Help other trades and subcontractors on site • Move materials, load and unload deliveries • Keep the work area clean, tidy and safe • Take part in toolbox talks (short safety meetings) and safety training Who you are • Experience from construction or fit-out projects. Data centre or technical projects are a plus. • You can read simple construction drawings • You know how to use tools safely and how to work with PPE (personal protective equipment such as a helmet, safety shoes and gloves) • Good English, spoken and written • Driving licence class B • Physically fit and comfortable with manual work • Flexible and mobile: willing to travel and work on projects in Norway and abroad • Reliable, on time and safety-minded • Happy to learn and to take direction New to data centres? No problem. If you have solid construction experience, we will teach you the rest. What we offer • 3:1 rotation (3 weeks on, 1 week off) with flights, accommodation and meals covered • We help you get your HMS card (the Norwegian ID card for everyone working on a construction site) if you do not have one • Stable work on large, modern projects • A safe workplace with fair terms and a written employment contract • Part of CTS Group, a large international construction group You report to: Site Supervisor / Site Manager Locations: Narvik, Kristiansand and Fyresdal, Norway
