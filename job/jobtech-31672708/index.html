Help Modernize Our Azure Footprint and Enable Teams to Work Smarter Are you passionate about automation, infrastructure as code, and helping teams move from manual processes to modern cloudnative practices? We’re looking for a Infrastructure Specialist to join our Azure platform team. In this role, you’ll help convert legacy infrastructure into automated, standardized offerings—and you’ll spend focused periods embedded inside product teams, helping them adopt Terraform, CI/CD automation, and platform guardrails. If you enjoy learning, collaborating, and solving real technical problems that remove friction for engineering teams, this role is for you. Your Mission You’ll work alongside Azure engineers to modernize older Azure environments, migrate manual setups to Terraform, and help product teams adopt platform standards. By embedding into teams, you’ll accelerate their transition away from adhoc infrastructure, guiding them toward automated, secure, and repeatable patterns. Your work will make our Azure landscape cleaner, more governed, and easier for teams to build on. What You’ll Do Support the modernization of legacy Azure resources into automated, Terraformbased implementations. Work directly inside product teams for short periods, helping them adopt modules, pipelines, and landing zone standards. Assist in cleaning up old infrastructure: tagging, RBAC, policy alignment, subscription hygiene, and drift reduction. Contribute to Terraform modules, documentation, and examples under guidance from senior engineers. Help implement and troubleshoot CI/CD workflows using GitHub Actions. Participate in creating automated guardrails that keep environments consistent and compliant. Collaborate with network security, virtualization, and AVD specialists when product teams need integrated platform capabilities. What You Bring Foundational understanding of Azure services and cloud concepts. Experience with Terraform or other IaC tools. Experience with structuring work in Jira / Confluence or equivalent tools. Interest in CI/CD automation (GitHub Actions experience is a plus). Basic scripting skills in PowerShell, Bash, Python, or .NET. Curiosity about cloud governance, landing zones, and platform engineering practices. A desire to learn while owning smaller modernization tasks independently. Enjoy collaborating with product teams and making their lives easier. Why This Role Matters This role is ideal for someone early in their cloud/platform career who wants: Hands on experience with real Azure modernization work Exposure to platform engineering patterns The opportunity to embed into product teams and understand their challenges To help create a secure, governed, and automated Azure environment used across the entire company Please note that this role is based in Valencia or Gothenburg.
