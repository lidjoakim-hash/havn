Job Summary As the Marketing Manager, Europe, you will be responsible for designing and executing the programs that build pipeline, strengthen brand presence, and accelerate revenue across the region. You will own end-to-end regional marketing strategy with a heavy emphasis on field events, account-based marketing (ABM), and pipeline generating campaigns that directly support the European sales team. You will operate as a collaborative partner to specialized marketers across Andela's global team — including growth, content, design, PR, and client marketing— bringing their capabilities to bear in the European market context. This role reports into the Senior Director of Marketing GTM Strategy and is based in the UK within a commutable distance to London. Exceptional Leadership As an Andelan, you'll serve as a role model for the rest of the company — you do not need to manage people to be a leader at Andela! Key Responsibilities Regional Event Strategy & Execution - Own the end-to-end planning and execution of European field events — including owned events, sponsored industry conferences, roundtables, and executive dinners — aligned to pipeline and revenue goals. - Build and maintain a European events calendar, managing logistics, vendor relationships, budget, and post-event follow-up to ensure measurable ROI. - Partner with the sales team to ensure event audiences reflect target account lists, and that every event ties to a clear pipeline outcome. - Track and report on event performance including attendee quality, influenced pipeline, and cost-per-opportunity. Account-Based Marketing (ABM) - Partner with the Growth Marketing team to design and execute ABM programs targeting high-priority European accounts in collaboration with sales leadership. - Use account intelligence to both inform campaigns and support the regional sellers. - Measure ABM effectiveness at the account level, reporting on account engagement, pipeline created, and influenced revenue.
