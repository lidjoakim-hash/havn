Manager, Tissue Field Service

Valmet Aktiebolag · Karlstad, Sweden

Are you ready to lead a world-class Tissue Field Service organization? We are looking for a highly motivated and experienced Manager, Tissue Field Service to lead our Tissue Field Services organization. In this key leadership role, you will be responsible for managing a team of Field Service Engineers, Specialists, and Technicians supporting our Tissue customers mainly throughout EMEA region but also worldwide. You will have direct responsibility for field personnel based in Karlstad (Sweden) as well as other strategic Tissue Service locations across the EMEA region. You will ensure safe, efficient, and high-quality service delivery while driving operational excellence, employee development, customer satisfaction, and business growth. Working closely with Sales, Project Management, Performance Centers, Product Management, and regional Operations teams, you will play a critical role in strengthening Valmet’s position as the preferred service partner for Tissue producers. Primary Location: Karlstad (Sweden) or another major Valmet location in EMEA. Your responsibilities In your daily work, you will: Lead, coach, and develop a team of Field Service Engineers, Specialists, and Technicians located in Karlstad and other key EMEA service hubs. Drive a strong safety culture and ensure compliance with Valmet's safety standards, policies, and customer site requirements. Manage resource planning, workforce allocation, and competence development to ensure efficient execution of field service activities. Ensure successful delivery of maintenance services, shutdowns, audits, inspections, troubleshooting assignments, machine upgrades, commissioning activities, and technical improvement projects. Support recruitment, onboarding, training, and career development of field service personnel. Define and monitor team performance through operational KPIs, utilization, customer satisfaction, profitability, safety, and employee engagement metrics. Collaborate with Sales organizations to identify service opportunities, support customer development activities, and contribute to business growth targets. Support quotation activities by providing technical expertise, resource estimations, and execution strategies for service projects. Ensure effective coordination between Field Services, Project Teams, Performance Centers, Engineering, and Product Organizations. Drive standardization and continuous improvement of service processes, methods, tools, and best practices. Act as an escalation point for critical customer issues and ensure timely resolution of customer concerns. Foster a culture of collaboration, accountability, innovation, and customer focus throughout the organization. Support the development and execution of strategic initiatives related to service growth, digitalization, remote services, and operational excellence. Expectations for you We expect you to bring: A degree in Engineering or equivalent technical experience. Several years of experience in industrial field services, maintenance, tissue production, project management, or related service operations. Proven experience in people leadership, team development, and performance management. Good knowledge of Tissue production technologies, maintenance practices, and customer operations. Experience managing multicultural teams and working within an international business environment. Strong business acumen and understanding of service profitability, resource management, and customer value creation. Excellent organizational, communication, and stakeholder management skills. Ability to prioritize activities and make decisions in dynamic and fast-paced environments. Experience with ERP, resource planning, and service management systems. Fluency in English; additional languages are considered an asset. Limited travel within the EMEA region could be needed, primarily team leadership and key operational activities.
