Our Story Our story goes back more than 150 years. From our early success in Sweden and Denmark, we have grown into the world's leading specialist producer of plant-based oils, employing more than 4,000 people across the globe. We work with our customers to make the products people love even better. The oils we produce help make chocolate extra creamy and put the crunch into a croissant. Beyond food, we work across industries to create products that are higher quality, healthier, and better for the planet. Everything we do is about Making Better Happen™. AAK Location This position is based in Karlshamn, Sweden, with commercial responsibility for Ukraine, Moldova, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, UzbekisHetan, Kyrgyzstan, and other selected CIS and Eastern European markets. You will work closely with customers across the region while collaborating with colleagues in Sales, Customer Innovation, Marketing, Product Management, Customer Service, Supply Chain, Quality, and Finance to deliver sustainable growth and customer value. About the Role We are looking for an ambitious and commercially driven Sales Manager to develop AAK's business across Ukraine, CIS, and Eastern Europe. You will be responsible for driving profitable growth, developing strategic customer relationships, identifying new business opportunities, and executing regional Go-to-Market plans aligned with AAK's strategy. Working closely with customers and internal stakeholders, you will support the successful commercialization of AAK's solutions while ensuring a high level of customer satisfaction and collaboration across the value chain. The role combines business development, key account management, market intelligence, and cross-functional collaboration, with a strong focus on customer co-development and long-term partnerships. Responsibilities Commercial Growth Drive sustainable and profitable growth across Ukraine, CIS, and Eastern Europe. Develop and execute regional Go-to-Market plans aligned with AAK's commercial strategy and business objectives. Identify and develop new business opportunities and market segments. Develop and implement customer growth plans to strengthen existing partnerships and expand business opportunities. Manage commercial negotiations, pricing, contracts, and profitability in line with business objectives. Deliver sales targets, forecasts, and pipeline development plans for the region. Customer Development Build and maintain strong relationships with key customers, distributors, and industry stakeholders. Collaborate closely with Customer Innovation, Customer Service, Supply Chain, Quality, Product Management, Marketing, and Finance to ensure successful customer deliveries and project execution.
