About the job We are looking for a Legal Services Manager to join our Center of Excellence function within our larger Legal Operations Team. In this role, you will be responsible for reviewing and vetting high-volume, repeatable work across multiple facets of the Legal Team and serving as the initial legal point of contact. You will also support ad-hoc operational activities to make the Legal Team more efficient. Responsibilities In this role, you’ll be responsible for the following: Legal Support Requests & Commercial Contracts • Serve as a first point of contact for GTM and Telecom Carrier Relations colleagues and provide education and process guidance on commercial contracts and other legal inquiries • Review, vet, and provide timely responses to a high volume of incoming legal requests, including general legal questions, agreement negotiation requests, and other legal support needs, while ensuring adherence to internal processes and policies • Independently review and negotiate NDAs, SOWs, assignment and assumption agreements, transition services agreements, service orders, and other miscellaneous legal documents on Twilio's template and counterparty's template Legal Processes & Operations • Support law firm eBilling matter creation and configuration, and resolve escalations related to law firm invoices • Be an operational expert on all processes that touch the Center of Excellence (e.g., Twilio's GTM-facing and telecom carrier-facing contracting processes, law firm billing-related processes) • Contribute your knowledge and learnings from novel situations to the Center of Excellence Playbook • Support a range of ad-hoc operational projects and high-volume tasks across multiple legal functions Qualifications Twilio values diverse experiences from all kinds of industries, and we encourage everyone who meets the required qualifications to apply.
