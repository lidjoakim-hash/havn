ABOUT PLEO Messy spend management is tricky business. And tedious processes are a lose-lose situation for all involved, not just finance. At Pleo, we're changing that. We build spend solutions that make managing money seamless, empowering, and surprisingly effective for finance teams and employees alike - with a vision to help all businesses ‘go beyond’. The word ‘Pleo’ actually means ‘more than you’d expect’, and living by that mantra has been the secret to our success over the last 10 years. Now, we’re at a pivotal moment in our journey; every move we make has a direct impact on our 40,000+ customers, our business, and our collective success. We need people who take pride in uncovering customer needs, who turn complex problems into simple solutions, challenge the way things are done (respectfully), and always aim high. With great ambitions driving us forward, we can’t say we’ve got this whole thing figured out. And frankly, that’s half the fun! What we can say is that we’re a driven, progressive, and, importantly, a kind bunch of 850+ people from over 100 nationalities, all committed to delivering the future of business spending, together. ABOUT THE ROLE We're looking for a Talent Acquisition Partner to join our Talent Acquisition team at Pleo. In this role, you'll manage high-volume, mid-to-senior hiring across the organisation, partnering with teams across Tech, Commercial, Finance, Legal, Product and other functions. You'll own multiple searches at once and play a key role in building strong, proactive talent pipelines that keep our hiring moving. You'll bring structure, pace and consistency to the recruitment process, using repeatable and scalable approaches while still creating a thoughtful experience for every candidate. If you're someone who loves being organised, thrives when managing multiple priorities, enjoys proactive sourcing and gets satisfaction from turning complex hiring needs into well-run recruitment processes, we'd love to meet you. WHO YOU’LL BE WORKING WITH AND REPORTING TO You’ll report to our Head of Talent Acquisition and work closely with hiring managers and stakeholders across Pleo, including senior leaders and functional teams across various business areas. You'll be part of a collaborative team that values high-quality delivery, strong partnership and continuous improvement. You'll work closely with the wider People team and cross-functional partners to make sure our hiring processes are efficient, consistent and deliver a great experience for candidates and hiring teams. WHAT YOU’LL BE DOING As a Talent Acquisition Partner, you will: - Own full-cycle recruitment across a high volume of mid-to-senior roles, managing multiple requisitions simultaneously across different functions. - Build proactive talent pipelines through sourcing, market mapping, talent research, referrals and personalised outreach - rather than relying on inbound applications alone. - Develop and use repeatable sourcing and recruitment processes that enable us to hire efficiently and consistently at scale. - Partner closely with hiring managers to understand requirements, agree hiring plans and keep searches moving from briefing through to offer. - Manage a busy recruitment workload with excellent organisation, prioritisation and attention to detail, ensuring no candidate or stakeholder falls through the cracks. - Create engaging, personalised outreach that gets the attention of passive candidates and builds interest in Pleo. - Use recruitment data and dashboards to track pipelines, identify bottlenecks and understand where searches need more focus. - Maintain accurate and up-to-date recruitment data and processes in tools such as Ashby, LinkedIn Recruiter, Notion and Google Sheets. - Use emerging AI and recruitment technology, including tools such as Claude, to improve research, sourcing, workflows and overall recruitment effectiveness. - Support effective and consistent technical hiring
