Technical Responsible Automation and OT Systems Finishing

Aktiebolaget Tetra Pak · Lund, Sweden

💰 ca. 520 000–640 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 4.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Are you passionate about automation engineering and curious about complex converting machine technologies? As part of Tetra Pak Development & Technology organisation, you will be responsible for developing, maintain and implement world class converting solutions, enabling Tetra Pak to deliver competitive packaging solutions. We are currently looking for a Technical Responsible within Automation Finishing, where finishing represents the last process step in production of packaging material. In this position you will lead and supervise specific high complex Automation design activities, related to D&T Converting solutions equipment’s. You will act as mentor and coach for other Automation Engineers, give support and guidance to other colleagues and suppliers in the area as well as participating in strategy and roadmap work. This is a permanent position based in Lund, Sweden. What you will do As our Technical Responsible, you will have the technical responsibility for the automation and OT solutions in Finishing, including Life Cycle Management. You will plan, conduct and supervise assignments, review progress and evaluate results. You will belong to a network of Technical Responsible Automation and OT systems (TRA/TRO) from other equipment areas. In addition, you will ensure stable integration, operation, support, development and continuous improvement of Operational Technology (OT) systems within the Finishing area. You will participate in portfolio analysis together with System Engineers and Architects and act as the bridge between equipment teams and IT/OT development teams. Other responsibilities: Drive the development, support, and continuous improvement of Automation and OT solutions. Act as the interface between equipment teams, IT/OT development teams, System Engineers, and Architects. Monitor system performance, manage incidents, and ensure reliable operation and availability. Lead and prioritize lifecycle management, upgrades, and continuous improvement initiatives. Ensure data flow integrity and data quality across connected systems. Provide technical support to factories and share knowledge through mentoring and coaching. Develop and maintain technical documentation in compliance with company standards and legal requirements. Plan, estimate, and coordinate automation projects and technical activities. Conduct and follow up technical reviews with internal stakeholders, factories, and suppliers. Collaborate with Technical Responsible across other equipment areas to align standards and best practices. Who you are We believe that you have a solid background with more than 4 years of relevant work experience in automation. You have extensive knowledge and understanding of Automation solutions generally with Siemens’s systems. Experience in Finishing and/or knowledge within Motion systems is considered an advantage and you have an understanding of Industrial IT and Operational Technology. You have personal drive with a clear focus on delivering results with quality. You also understand how to lead and motivate people, having the ability to adjust your leadership to different situations. As Tetra Pak for sure is a global company, you communicate fluently in English, both written and spoken. We offer you Variety of exciting challenges with ample opportunities for development and training in a truly global landscape Culture that pioneers a spirit of innovation where our engineering experts drives visible results Equal opportunity employment experience that values diversity and inclusion Market competitive compensation and benefits with flexible working arrangements
