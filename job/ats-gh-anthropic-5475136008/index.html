About Anthropic Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems. About the role: Anthropic is seeking an International Infrastructure Accounting professional to join our Infrastructure Accounting team. Based in Dublin and reporting to the Head of International Infrastructure Accounting, you will execute day-to-day accounting for the leased and owned infrastructure supporting Anthropic's expansion outside the United States, i.e. the data centers, network infrastructure, and compute capacity underpinning our international growth. As we continue our rapid growth, you will help ensure the operational scalability and financial accuracy of the core functions that enable Anthropic's research and commercial success. This is an opportunity to be at the heart of one of the fastest-scaling companies in the industry, and to shape how Anthropic's growing international infrastructure portfolio is accounted for and understood across the business. You will own end-to-end accounting for Anthropic's international infrastructure portfolio, with an initial focus on leases, working closely with cross-functional partners across Data Center Operations, Real Estate, Finance, and Legal. You'll apply deep technical accounting expertise under ASC 842 for the US GAAP books, and carry those same arrangements through to the IFRS 16 or local GAAP books. You will partner closely with our central team to ensure global policies, processes, and controls are applied consistently as each new market comes online. Responsibilities: • Lead end-to-end accounting for Anthropic's international infrastructure portfolio — leased and owned, with an initial focus on leases — including monthly close, reconciliations, roll-forwards, and flux analysis • Apply technical accounting expertise to complex infrastructure arrangements under ASC 842 (lease classification, measurement, modifications, embedded leases, and reassessments) and ASC 360 (capitalization, useful lives, impairment, and disposals) • Prepare the US GAAP to IFRS 16 and local GAAP adjustments for each international entity, and partner with our International Statutory Reporting team and external providers so the statutory accounts are accurate and filed on time • Partner with Real Estate, Legal, and Finance teams during contract negotiation and execution to ensure accounting implications are understood before commitments are made • Serve as a trusted finance partner to non-finance stakeholders — explaining the financial statement impact of their decisions in clear, simple terms they can act on • Apply global infrastructure accounting policies, processes, and controls consistently as each new market comes online • Help stand up the accounting foundations for each new market, i.e. entity and chart of accounts mapping, functional currency, intercompany flows, and lease system onboarding • Collaborate cross-functionally to improve the quality of upstream data pipelines from operational and contract systems, particularly around contract terms, asset receipt, deployment, and in-service dates • Collaborate with and mentor accounting professionals across the Infrastructure Accounting team • Serve as a key point of contact with external auditors and local statutory auditors on international infrastructure matters • Drive process improvements and automation, including leveraging AI tooling, to enhance the efficiency and accuracy of financial reporting Minimum Requirements: • Have 10+ years of progressive experience in accounting, with operational experience at technology or infrastructure-heavy companies • Are a qualified accountant (ACA, ACCA, CPA, or equivalent) with a bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or equivalen
