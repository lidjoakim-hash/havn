THE ROLE We are hiring a Founding Customer Success Lead/Manager for the UK market. This is a high-ownership role for someone who wants to build a market and a function, not simply manage a book of business or an expansion. You will work with some of our most strategic customers, helping them move from initial experimentation to broad, measurable adoption of AI across their organizations. You will own the full customer journey, from executive alignment and implementation through long-term adoption, value realization, renewal, and expansion. You will build trusted relationships with C-level and VP-level leaders, help customers navigate the organizational change that comes with adopting AI, and turn successful customer outcomes into a repeatable Customer Success motion from our London HQ. As our first senior Customer Success leader based at the London HQ, you will help define how Dust serves customers from the office. You will create playbooks, establish operating rhythms, shape our approach to executive engagement, and feed what you learn back into the wider Customer Success, Sales, Product, and Deployment teams. Dust is an AI-native company, and that changes what Customer Success can look like. You will help customers understand how AI can reshape their work while using AI to make your own practice more effective, scalable, and precise.
