We connect people, data, and processes in one intuitive platform, with AI agents that work alongside every team, from Finance to HR, to build, adapt, and align strategic plans in real time. Today we’re a growing team of 700+ across Paris, London, New York, Toronto, San Francisco, and Austin, and we’ve been named a Visionary in the Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Financial Planning Software two years running. Our Engineering team Our Engineering team is responsible for developing our SaaS platform and building a comprehensive and user-friendly product. Pigment engineers participate in the entire application development lifecycle, focusing on design, coding, and keeping the production platform up and running. They can be specialized, but there is no strict separation between the infrastructure, backend, and the frontend. We value user-centricity and pragmatism: we choose the most relevant tools for the problem we have to solve, understanding the strengths and constraints of each technology. Our engineering culture also values curiosity, humility, trust, ownership and team spirit. 👉 Curious to see what we’re building? Check out our Tech Blog! As a Software Engineer Intern, you will work on meaningful projects that directly impact our product, collaborating with experienced engineers and contributing to challenges at scale. During your internship, you will have the opportunity to work on one of the following projects: Project overview in the Data Ingestion team: The Data Ingestion team is in charge of how customer data gets into Pigment, notably through our library of connectors to external systems. Upgrade, migrate and improve our connector library Upgrade and migrate existing connectors to newer versions and approaches. Improve the reliability, performance, and consistency of the connector library. Contribute to shared tooling and documentation to make new connectors faster to build. Project overview in the Modeler team: The Modeler team builds the AI-powered tools that help customers create and evolve planning models in Pigment. Importing a model from another planning tool with the Modeler Agent  Explore whether recent progress in LLMs and the Modeler Agent makes it possible to recreate a model from another planning tool in Pigment. Design an automated approach to translate its structure, dimensions, and formulas into Pigment blocks and valid formulas. Define tests to validate that the imported model behaves as expected.
