The role We're hiring a Design Systems Engineer to enable high-quality, lightning-fast implementation to support Legora’s rapid growth. You'll join our growing design team and work alongside our existing Design Systems Engineer to build the foundations that keep our UI consistent, accessible, and fast to ship. It's hands-on work, day to day with designers, engineers, and PMs in our Stockholm office. AI will reshape how frontend gets built. The right system, paired with the right tooling, will enable builders to move at lightning speed. You'll be the person making that real. What you'll do - Build and maintain our React component library and token architecture - Translate Figma designs into fast, reusable, accessible components - Wire design and code together with Code Connect, Dev Mode, our MCP server, variables, and component properties - Reduce drift between Figma and production through automation and tooling - Standardize interaction patterns and UI architecture across the product - Remove bottlenecks in design-to-code workflows and speed up delivery What you bring - Strong experience with React, TypeScript, and modern frontend tooling - Deep knowledge of design systems, tokens, and component architecture - Hands-on experience with Figma's developer surface: Code Connect, Dev Mode, MCP, variables, REST API, plugins, component properties - Sharp eye for detail and design precision - A strong POV on what makes for an excellent product experience - You lead by building.
