Pinterest is looking for an Integrated Marketing Specialist to help shape and deliver business marketing programs for the Netherlands. You’ll partner with the UKNL Business Marketing Lead, local sales teams and global cross-functional partners to turn global priorities into locally relevant programs that strengthen advertiser and agency awareness and consideration. What you’ll do: • Partner with the UKNL Business Marketing Lead and local sales partners to develop the Netherlands marketing plan, aligned to global priority tentpoles, local calendar moments and relevant industry opportunities. • Lead priority business marketing programs from objectives and KPIs through project management, execution, measurement, learnings and reporting. • Deliver effective campaigns across business marketing channels, including owned and industry events, communications, partnerships, content and customer success stories. • Collaborate with global teams to adapt programs and messaging for the Netherlands market while maintaining alignment with broader business priorities. • Build foundational relationships with local sales partners and use their market perspective to inform marketing activity. • Work closely with sales, product, measurement and marketing partners to coordinate priorities and deliver cohesive programs. • Use AI to accelerate planning, analysis and iteration, while applying judgment and verification to ensure accuracy, quality and responsible use. What we’re looking for: • Experience independently managing marketing workstreams and collaborating with cross-functional partners in a fast-paced environment. • Experience delivering business marketing campaigns across channels such as events, communications, industry partnerships and content. • Strong project and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to organize priorities, timelines and dependencies across teams. • Strong analytical skills and a data-informed approach to measurement, reporting and recommendations. • Genuine interest in B2B marketing and the advertising industry. • Demonstrated ability to use AI to improve the speed and quality of relevant marketing outputs. • Strong track record of critically evaluating and verifying AI-assisted work, while protecting sensitive information and remaining accountable for final decisions and deliverables. • Bachelor’s degree in marketing, a related field or equivalent experience.
