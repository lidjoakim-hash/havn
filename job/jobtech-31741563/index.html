Are you a specialist in medical oncology looking for a role that combines professional development, multidisciplinary collaboration, and access to modern cancer treatments? We are recruiting on behalf of a well-established oncology department in Sweden that provides comprehensive cancer care across a broad spectrum of diagnoses. You will join an experienced and supportive team where collaboration, clinical excellence and continuous development are highly valued. This is an opportunity to work in a specialty that is constantly evolving, with access to modern therapies and the possibility to contribute to the ongoing development of cancer care while maintaining a healthy work-life balance. About the role As a specialist in oncology, you will be involved in: Outpatient consultations and follow-up of oncology patients. Systemic cancer treatments and individualized treatment planning. Participation in multidisciplinary tumour conferences (MDTs). Inpatient care as part of a rotating ward responsibility. Collaboration with radiation oncology, palliative care and other specialties. Supervision and mentoring of resident doctors. Contributing to the continued development of clinical pathways and oncology services. Depending on your experience and professional interests, there are opportunities to further develop specific areas of expertise within the department. Why this opportunity? Collaborative and supportive environment:  Join an experienced oncology team known for its strong collegial atmosphere, close cooperation and willingness to support one another. Modern oncology practice: Work in a department that continuously follows developments in cancer treatment and offers access to innovative therapies and evidence-based care. Professional development: Benefit from ongoing opportunities for continuing medical education, subspecialization and participation in developing multidisciplinary tumour teams. Varied clinical work: Combine outpatient care, inpatient responsibilities, multidisciplinary collaboration and specialised oncology treatments within a broad clinical practice. Long-term career opportunities: Become part of a stable department with low staff turnover and a strong commitment to quality improvement and professional growth. Life in central Sweden The position is located in a scenic region of central Sweden, offering an excellent quality of life with forests, lakes and coastline within easy reach. The area combines a welcoming community atmosphere with modern infrastructure, short commuting times and access to outdoor activities throughout the year. Families appreciate the safe environment, excellent schools and generous work-life balance, while larger cities and international travel connections remain easily accessible. Requirements EU-recognised specialist certification in Medical Oncology. Solid clinical experience as a specialist in oncology. Ability to work independently while contributing positively to a multidisciplinary team. Interest in teaching, supervision and professional development. Strong communication skills in English. Motivation to learn Swedish through our language programme. Why Choose Dignus Medical?
