Celonis is the trusted platform to industrialize Enterprise AI. At our core is the Celonis Context Model — which combines process data, business knowledge, and intelligence into a living digital twin of the enterprise that AI can actually understand, turning AI's operational blind spots into operational clarity. World's leading companies trust Celonis and its global ecosystem of partners to make their AI agents, people, systems, and processes work together, achieving transformational outcomes. We believe there's a massive opportunity to unlock global productivity and sustainability by giving AI the context to understand how every business process really works. Join our mission to make processes work for people, companies, and the planet. The Team: The Celonis Legal team is an energetic and proactive global group of experts in their field, focused on identifying, avoiding, mitigating and solving challenges within the Celonis business. We sit at the heart of the Celonis organization and are proud to be seen as valued business partners across all aspects of the business. The Celonis Legal team is led by the Chief Legal & Trust Officer, with deputy and assistant GCs leading our global GTM & Product, Corporate, Employment and Ethics & Compliance practices. The Senior Counsel, DACH Public Sector will sit within the GTM practice, reporting to our Legal Director, EMEA Commercial. The Role: The Senior Counsel, DACH Public Sector position will be a new and important role in the company’s Legal group, responsible for supporting Celonis’ fast-growing Public Sector Go-to-Market (GTM) efforts in Germany, Austria and Switzerland, as well as providing additional legal support as requested. As Celonis’ home market, DACH is a critically important region and Celonis is committed to working with and supporting many of the region’s largest public sector organisations. The primary focus of the role is negotiating key commercial customer transactions in DACH with both state and federal organisations, working alongside senior Celonis stakeholders. A successful candidate will be expected to provide pragmatic, creative, collaborative and solution-oriented legal support. As a self-starting, experienced communicator and problem-solver, the Senior Counsel, DACH Public Sector must be able to prioritize effectively and drive issues to resolution within a team environment focused on growth, execution and process improvement.
