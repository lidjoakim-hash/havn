Role highlights: - Location: Espoo, Finland - Department: Production Finland - Reports to: Power & Electronics Team Lead - Employment type: Permanent - Workplace model: Onsite - Employment is subject to applicable security screening (incl. SUPO, where required) Why this role matters: The Power & Electronics team builds and tests three unit families for every GEN4.3 satellite: the avionics systems, the RF units (transmitters, receivers, X-band synthesizer, combiners and dividers) and the battery. We are moving from low-volume builds to standard, repeatable production. We need a senior technician who builds to flight standards without supervision, keeps the work area ready, solves build and test problems together with engineering, and coaches the team. You report to the Power & Electronics Team Lead and work daily with AIT Engineering, Quality, Supply Chain and the satellite integration line. Who We Are ICEYE is the world leader in sovereign intelligence from space. We deliver persistent monitoring capabilities to detect and respond to changes in any location on Earth. ICEYE owns the world's largest and most advanced SAR (synthetic aperture radar) satellite constellation. To our customers we provide intelligence with unmatched quality, latency and revisit times, in any weather, day or night. To governments who choose to operate their own constellation we provide this proven capability as a sovereign system. ICEYE-built constellations serve customers in defence and intelligence, environmental monitoring, insurance and emergency management. We enable fast decisions that contribute to a safer future. Founded and headquartered in Finland, ICEYE operates globally with over 1000 employees across Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.   Your day-to-day responsibilities - Build and test avionics, RF and battery units to control work instructions, to flight standard. - Run bench tests: power-up, continuity and isolation checks, functional tests with unit test setups, and RF measurements where needed. - Build and test battery units safely, following lithium-ion handling rules. - Keep your production area ready: tools, fixtures, test equipment, kits, work instructions and workstation setup. - Be the first point of contact for build and test problems. Lead fault-finding with AIT Engineering and Quality, and close non-conformances through root cause and corrective action. - Coach and cross-train technicians in soldering, connectors and coax, ESD and FOD rules, and test execution. - Redline and improve work instructions, ncs and checklists, and take changes through the approval process. - Support new unit variants into production: first-article builds and lessons learned. - Remove bottlenecks, repeat defects and rework together with the team, using Lean and 6S. - Keep full traceability: work orders, Jira tickets, as-built records, labels and photos. - Support New Product Introduction (NPI) activities and manage workflow across the team. - Coordinate closely with different cross-functional teams and operational areas. - Serve as the primary point of contact for technical issues within the team. What we’re looking for Must haves: - 5+ years of hands-on electronics or electro-mechanical assembly, integration and test. - Background in a quality-critical environment: space, aerospace, defence, medical devices, telecom or automotive electronics. - Soldering and rework to IPC Class 3 (IPC-A-610 / J-STD-001) or ECSS standard; certified, or able to certify during probation. - Confident with bench test equipment: multimeter, power supplies, electronic load, oscilloscope. - Able to work from schematics, wiring diagrams, assembly drawings and test procedures. - Experience with connectors, coax assemblies and harness termination. - Strong ESD, FOD and cleanroom discipline. - A track record of owning a work area or technical problem and seeing it through. -
