Who we are Helsing is a defence AI company. Our mission is to protect our democracies. We aim to achieve technological leadership, so that open societies can continue to make sovereign decisions and control their ethical standards. As democracies, we believe we have a special responsibility to be thoughtful about the development and deployment of powerful technologies like AI. We take this responsibility seriously. We are an ambitious and committed team of engineers, AI specialists and customer-facing programme managers. We are looking for mission-driven people to join our European teams – and apply their skills to solve the most complex and impactful problems. We embrace an open and transparent culture that welcomes healthy debates on the use of technology in defence, its benefits, and its ethical implications. The role As a Specialist Export Control, you will hold a critical position in bridging the gap between regulatory compliance and operational execution. You will act as a high-agency professional, taking full ownership of our daily export control operations—including licensing, releases, screening, and the customs interface. Beyond day-to-day throughput, your work will directly enable our growth by driving the projects that make these operations scalable, such as system rollouts, process automation, and building licensing architectures for new programs and sites. This role requires a hands-on specialist who thrives on operational excellence, complementing our ITAR/EAR regulatory function by owning our process architecture. You will work directly with our Executive Export Control Officer (Ausfuhrverantwortlicher) and design the vital controls they rely on, while formal personal liability remains at the management level. The day-to-day • Operations — Driving Daily Execution • End-to-End BAFA Licensing: Manage individual, global, and general licenses (AGG) for exports, transfers, and tech data, including conditions and deadlines. • Transaction Releases: Clear shipments, services, and software releases (list checks, end-use/end-user verification, catch-all assessments). • Sanctions Screening: Execute and document EU, UN, OFAC, and UK list screenings, resolving hits and false positives. • ERP Master Data: Ensure export control classifications (AL-No., ECCN) in the ERP match the reality of our products. • Customs Interface: Align with logistics and brokers on ATLAS filings, proofs of origin, and Carnets. • Advisory & Documentation: Guide internal stakeholders (Sales, Engineering, Procurement) and maintain an audit-proof record of all compliance decisions. • Projects — Scaling for Tomorrow • Program Export Concepts: Build licensing architectures for new programs, consortia, and sites right from the proposal phase. • System Rollouts & Automation: Drive the implementation and optimization of export control tools (e.g., SAP GTS, AEB, Descartes) to automate workflows. • ICP Evolution: Upgrade internal processes and control mechanisms to meet BAFA ICP requirements. • Audits & Metrics: Lead internal checks, support external/AEO audits, and establish hard KPIs (lead times, hit rates) over gut feeling. • Team Enablement: Design and roll out practical, target-group-specific compliance trainings.
