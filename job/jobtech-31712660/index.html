Financial Controller needed!

Perido AB · Göteborg, Sweden

Are you an analytical and business-oriented Financial Controller who enjoys working with financial reporting, analysis and budgeting? In this role, you will take responsibility for assigned departments, combining financial controlling with business partnering and supporting the business through accurate reporting and forecasting. About the position Perido are looking for a Financial Controller for our client, an exciting and forward-thinking company in the automotive industry. The position is based in Gothenburg. Your daily tasks In this role, you will be responsible for financial controlling and business partnering for assigned departments, including monthly financial reporting, analysis, budgeting and forecasting. You will also be responsible for accruals, capitalization and providing financial documentation for audits. Key responsibilities: Act as the responsible controller and business partner for assigned departments Be responsible for monthly financial reporting and analysis to group functions, including FP&A and Accounting, as well as to the business team Manage the accrual and capitalization process, including hourly rate calculations, and ensure timely delivery of input to Group Accounting during month-end closing Support the business in the budget and forecast process Provide supporting documentation for internal and external audits related to assigned control functions Perform ad hoc reporting and analysis Act as the finance approver for assigned departments Your characteristics To succeed in this role, you are analytical and business-oriented, with a strong ability to understand and interpret financial information. You communicate clearly and effectively, adapting your communication to both finance professionals and other stakeholders. You are also structured and independent, with the ability to plan, coordinate and prioritize your work while remaining flexible and handling ad hoc requests. Furthermore, you enjoy collaborating with others and feel comfortable working both independently and as part of diverse teams. Does this sound like an exciting opportunity? We look forward to reading your application! Please submit your application for this role in English. Qualifications: 5+ years’ experience in financial controlling Strong business acumen and strong analytical skills Good at interpret and communicate financial information in a clear and concise way Good in excel and relevant ERP and business analytics tools Being able to plan and coordinate work independently as well as jointly in diversified teams Good at prioritize and be able to handle ad hoc requests Good at communicate financial information to both finance and non-finance Contract type and hours Full-time consulting assignment for 6 months.
