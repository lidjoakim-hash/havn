At Flix, we offer a dynamic work environment with competitive pay, strong growth opportunities, and a tech-driven approach to making travel more accessible, sustainable, and affordable. You will join Flix's People & Organization Digital Transformation team to optimize how our global HR processes and technology work together. As a hands-on Senior/Expert HR Tech Business Analyst (m/f/d), you will bridge HR operations and our internal Workday technology team, combining strong HR domain expertise with practical Workday knowledge to simplify processes, improve data quality and enable greater self-service across our global organization. You will support day-to-day Workday tech requests, lead process gap analysis and redesign, and translate HR requirements into feasible solutions in close partnership with our Workday tech team. The role reports to the Head of Digital Transformation and works closely with People Operations, People Partners, process owners, our internal Workday technology team, FlixTech and other stakeholders globally. This role is based in Berlin (hybrid model). Capture which data needs to flow between Workday and surrounding systems, when and why, to remove manual work for HR. You will combine hands-on Workday expertise with business process transformation, helping HR teams move faster, reduce manual work and make better use of our technology. You'll play a key role in shaping how HR processes are optimized, automated and adopted across Flix's global organization.
