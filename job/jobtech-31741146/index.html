Company Description Vattenfall is a leading European energy company with 12 million customers and approximately 21,000 employees. We are convinced that there is a way to build a future in which everyone can choose fossil-free ways to transport, produce, and live. Job Description Are you a student looking to apply your knowledge to real-world solutions that support the energy transition? With us, you'll have the opportunity to complete your internship close to the business, working alongside experienced specialists and tackling challenges where your ideas and perspectives can make a real difference. About the internship As an intern, you will join the Energy Transfer & Environment group within Vattenfall R&D Laboratories, working alongside specialists in fluid mechanics and Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD). Our work includes numerical flow modelling and experimental measurements for energy applications, including hydropower and nuclear power. During the internship, you will work with CFD on a defined fluid-mechanics problem relevant to the group’s ongoing activities. Proposed tasks include preparing simulation models and computational meshes, setting up and running simulations, evaluating flow behaviour, and comparing results with available experimental data or reference cases, where applicable. The specific application and simulation tools will be agreed with the supervisor. The aim of the internship is to develop practical skills in CFD and contribute to the understanding of an energy-related flow problem. You will document modelling assumptions, assess the quality and limitations of the results, and present your findings in a technical report and a final presentation to the group. Duration: Five months, full-time.This internship is for two students.  Qualifications We are looking for students who are currently enrolled in ongoing studies and are eager to put their knowledge into practice through an internship at Vattenfall. You are also someone who identifies with our principles: Active, Open, Positive, and Safety. In addition, we expect you to meet the following requirements: Education: Ongoing university studies in Mechanical Engineering, Engineering Physics, Energy Engineering or a related discipline, preferably with coursework in fluid mechanics, numerical methods and CFD. Previous experience with a CFD solver and programming or data analysis is an advantage. Language skills: Good command of English, both spoken and written, for technical discussions, documentation and presentations. Availability: Able to undertake a five-month internship as part of your ongoing studies. Additional Information Start of assignment: By agreement. Location: Solna.  Application – a document with your CV. Application deadline: 2026-11-30. Contact person and proposed supervisor at Vattenfall: Eric Lillberg, Senior Principal R&D Engineer, Fluid Mechanics/CFD, eric.lillberg@vattenfall.com, +46 70 537 29 94.
