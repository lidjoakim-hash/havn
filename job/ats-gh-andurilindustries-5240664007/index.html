Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years. ABOUT THE TEAM  The Voyager team in Anduril Industries is an engineering and design division with a vast experience in developing innovative computing and communications solutions for the network edge. The team specializes in integrating enterprise networking capabilities from global IT leaders with purpose-built hardware and software platforms designed to meet market demands for the most stringent of environmental requirements. Our team is a small electrical and electronic hardware team which works on a wide variety of design projects. These projects focus on the creation of powered chassis which are used to power our Voyager Module range. The work includes mix of power, analogue and digital circuit design. The team is responsible for the design from initial concept though to full rate manufacturing. ABOUT THE JOB We are looking for an Electrical Technician to join our growing team in Dublin. In this role, you will be responsible for supporting design Engineers to create and develop various electronic products including powersupplies, UPS, networking and communication equipment. This will require a mix of design, testing and analysis skill. If you are someone who is self-driven and can work on your own or as part of a team, then this role is for you. WHAT YOU’LL DO • Builds, assembles, and modifies electrical and electronic circuits or test equipment. • Tests and debugs PCB Assemblies and final product assemblies. • Maintains bills of materials (BOMs) and updates component lists. • Creates cables, cable drawings and prototypes • Manages lab equipment e.g. bench power supplies, electronic loads. • Works with manufacturing and engineering teams to resolve assembly or production problems • Analyse data, build models, and share insights to refine our solutions. • Swiftly troubleshoot and resolve technical challenges. REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS • Diploma or equivalent in electrical/electronic engineering technology or a related field. • Knowledge of electronics, circuit analysis, and industry standards. • Good soldering skills (IPC qualification would be beneficial) • Proficiency in tools like OrCAD, LTSpice. • 3 - 5 years of experience working in an R&D environment, Contract Manufacturing or RMA • An ability and willingness to work in a team.
