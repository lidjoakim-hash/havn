Product Specialist - Top Hammer

Epiroc Rock Drills Aktiebolag · Fagersta, Sweden

💰 ca. 560 000–680 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 20.11.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Do you want to join a global leader and make a real difference in the energy transition? As a Product Specialist in our Rock Drilling Tools division, you will play a vital role in supporting continuous product development and ensuring technical excellence for our top hammer solutions across the globe. This is an opportunity to work in a dynamic, international environment where your expertise will directly influence product performance and customer satisfaction—and help shape the future of the mining and construction industry. Join us as we accelerate the transformation! Join our team At Epiroc, it all starts with people. People like you. You will be part of a collaborative and ambitious team committed to innovation and customer success. Our mission is to deliver world-class drilling solutions and ensure our customers achieve the highest productivity and safety standards. In this role, you will report to the Global Product Support Manager in the Top Hammer Solutions Business Line and work closely with R&D, Product Management, Customer Centers, and end users. Your mission As Product Specialist, you will be hands-on in field activities acting as a trusted technical partner. You will be supporting product portfolio development and strengthen technical competence across the organization. Key responsibilities include: Providing technical support for internal teams and second-line support for customers and sales channels Participating in field tests and proof-of-concept activities Maintaining and developing a product performance database, collecting and analyzing performance data Delivering technical training and creating training materials Collaborating on new product releases and marketing materials Frequent global travel to customer sites for field support and training Your profile To thrive in this role, you should have a strong technical background in drilling, mining, or construction equipment, combined with excellent communication skills and a proactive, customer-focused mindset. You enjoy collaborating and are comfortable working in a fast-paced, international environment. Qualifications: Technical, marketing, or business education with relevant industry knowledge. Minimum 5 years of experience in technical or application support within relevant field. Skilled in data analysis and reporting, with proficiency in MS Office. Excellent communication skills and fluency in English (written and spoken). Willingness to travel globally and ability to work independently while collaborating across teams. Knowledge of Epiroc drilling equipment and operating procedures for drill rigs is an advantage. With us, you will have the chance to grow professionally and personally while contributing to the success of an industry leader. Join us as we accelerate the transformation! Location and other This position is located in Fagersta, Sweden. Travel is required by approximately 70-80 days per year. Application and contact information We encourage you to submit your application as soon as possible, but no later than November 20, 2026. We are committed to a thorough recruitment process, including interviews, reference checks and assessments. To ensure a safe working environment, we conduct identity checks and drug and alcohol screening. Our process is designed to be fair and inclusive; you can expect transparent communication and a balanced evaluation of your skills and experience.
