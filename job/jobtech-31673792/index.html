Data Governance Manager

Läkare Utan Gränser · Stockholm, Sweden

We have come a long way in our digital transformation, and we are now looking for a Data Governance Manager, who will ensure data governance practice and strengthen our ability to manage data as a strategic asset within MSF Sweden. In this role, you will lead and coordinate data governance initiatives across the organization. You will work closely with stakeholders from different departments to establish clear ownership of data, improve data quality, support compliance requirements, and ensure that data is managed consistently and responsibly throughout its lifecycle. The role You will facilitate collaboration between business and technical teams, helping define standards, policies, and processes that enable trusted and well-governed data. You will also contribute to the continued development of our data management capabilities, including data cataloguing, data stewardship, and master data management. The role also involve implementing measures to ensure data security, confidentiality and regulatory compliance. Further responsibilities: Drive the implementation and continuous improvement of data governance policies, standards, and processes across MSF Sweden. Facilitate collaboration between departments to establish common definitions, ownership, and accountability for key data domains. Lead data stewardship initiatives and support stakeholders in managing data quality and lifecycle activities. Support the development and adoption of data cataloguing and master data management practices. Monitor and improve data quality through governance frameworks, controls, and reporting. Ensure that data management practices comply with organizational policies and applicable regulations. Participate in security and compliance initiatives related to information and data management. Act as an internal advisor and advocate for data governance best practices. System owner: Responsible for the data management platform, data storage solutions, compliance tools and other tools in the data management systems portfolio. We are looking for you with At least 3 years of experience working with data governance,data management, master data management, data quality or similar roles. Experience in implementing or supporting data governance frameworks, policies, and processes. Ability to engage and collaborate with stakeholders across different functions and levels of the organization. Bachelor’s degree or higher in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field. Strong technical understanding and experience with data management tools and technologies such as SQL, ETL processes, data warehouses, and data integration tools. Excellent analytical skills and experience using data analysis tools. Ability to communicate complex technical concepts clearly and understandably to non-technical stakeholders. Strong problem-solving skills and experience in addressing data quality issues. Knowledge of relevant data protection and data security laws and regulations, such as GDPR. Fluency in English, written and spoken You need to be driven, take initiative and be confident as one of the few experts within the area of the organization in Sweden. You will need to act as a role model to others and be comfortable with inspiring and teaching others within this area. It’s valuable if you have Experience of implementing data management frameworks and architectures, data lifecycle management and data quality management practice Relevant certifications in data management or data protection Strong commitment to humanitarian principles and values Experience of working in humanitarian or charity organizations As part of this recruitment process we do a background check on the final candidate due to access to sensitive data. Employment terms and benefits Type of contract: Permanent (6 months probation period) Entry date: ASAP Goal oriented working time (37,5 hours/week is full time) Salary: 45
