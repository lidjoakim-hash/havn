What you'll do Right now, we are looking for a Quantity Surveyor/Change Management for one of our project areas. In this role, you will report to the Project Controls Manager and sit within the specific area. The team supports the project within its assigned area through cost, schedule and performance control. Specific to this role, the day-to-day tasks will include the following, but as we are a growing company with very little siloing between teams, other tasks might be included as well. Review and validate estimates, quantities and cost calculations for assigned packages. Assess costs against design information, project scope and contractual requirements. Review contractor change orders and variations, including quantities, cost build-ups and supporting justifications. Challenge submissions where evidence or alignment with scope is unclear, and provide documented findings and recommendations. Assess the cost impact of project changes and contribute to change control. Coordinate with Project Controls, Contracts, Engineering and package teams to resolve information gaps and document cost assessments. Support project reviews and follow up on agreed actions. Placement: Boden Employment type: Fixed-term employment (12–24 months) What you'll bring You’re curious and believe that real progress happens when we work together. You’re not afraid to challenge old ways of thinking, and you treat others with respect while doing it. At Stegra, we value people who take initiative, build trust and help create a workplace where everyone can do their best. Specific to this role, we would like to see that you can tick off a fair amount of the boxes below: Experience in quantity surveying, estimating, cost assurance, or change and variation management on complex industrial or construction projects. Experience reviewing large or complex packages and coordinating assessments across multidisciplinary teams. Strong understanding of quantities, estimates, cost build-ups, design information and contractual scope. Ability to identify gaps or risks, challenge cost submissions with evidence, and make clear recommendations. Ability to maintain clear and traceable assessments and supporting documentation. An analytical, detail-oriented and proactive approach, with the confidence to raise concerns constructively.
