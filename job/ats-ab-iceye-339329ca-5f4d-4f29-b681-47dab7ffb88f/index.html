ROLE HIGHLIGHTS: - Security Officer - Location: Lisbon, Portugal - Department: Security - Reports to: CEO of ICEYE Portugal - Employment type: Permanent - Workplace model: On-site (our office is located at Av. Defensores de Chaves 4, 1000-117 Lisboa) - Employment is subject to applicable security screening (incl. SUPO, where required) WHY THIS ROLE MATTERS:   ICEYE Portugal turns sovereign intelligence from space into an operational capability built in Lisbon. This role keeps that capability secure. As ICEYE Portugal's named Local Security Officer, you are the formal interface to GNS / ANS and the CEO's trusted partner on every clearance, audit and facility question, accountable for our people, our facility and our information meeting ICEYE's global security standards and Portugal's national and NATO sovereignty requirements. It is a hybrid role, modelled on how ICEYE runs its other international sites, and it sits at the centre of everything ICEYE Portugal does.   WHO WE ARE ICEYE is the world leader in sovereign intelligence from space. We deliver persistent monitoring capabilities to detect and respond to changes in any location on Earth.   ICEYE owns the world's largest and most advanced SAR (synthetic aperture radar) satellite constellation. To our customers we provide intelligence with unmatched quality, latency and revisit times, in any weather, day or night. To governments who choose to operate their own constellation we provide this proven capability as a sovereign system.   ICEYE-built constellations serve customers in defence and intelligence, environmental monitoring, insurance and emergency management. We enable fast decisions that contribute to a safer future.   Founded and headquartered in Finland, ICEYE operates globally with over 1000 employees across Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. YOUR DAY-TO-DAY RESPONSIBILITIES   Facility and physical security - Own ICEYE Portugal's facility security programme: physical access control, secure-room build-out, visitor management, alarms, CCTV and key management. - Drive the office's NATO Restricted readiness audit with GNS and prepare for higher classifications as the customer mission evolves. - Run the secure handling of classified material in line with Portuguese national rules and ICEYE policy. - Partner with the CEO, Head of People & Operations, and the local IT Site Engineer on the Class A office fit-out, specifically on secure-area readiness for clearance-restricted workflows. Personnel security and clearances - Coordinate national security clearance applications (GNS / ANS) for the CEO, FDEs and other cleared roles, and maintain the cleared-personnel register (levels, expirations, briefings and debriefings). - Run security on-boarding and off-boarding for every employee and visitor: NDAs, security awareness, classified-handling training and access provisioning. Information and cyber security - Partner with ICEYE Oy Security leadership and HQ IT to translate the global cyber baseline (endpoint, MDM, network, identity) into local controls. - Lead local incident-response coordination: classified leaks, lost devices, social engineering and suspicious foreign-contact reporting. Vendor and supply-chain security - Vet and onboard local vendors against ICEYE security standards (cleaning, IT, real estate, broker, legal, accounting). - Run periodic security audits on key suppliers. Programme and customer security interface - Be the named security interface to the Portuguese Air Force, GNS / ANS, NATO and the Portuguese Space Agency on ICEYE Portugal matters. - Support the FDEs on customer-site security questions during the SAR / SIP deployment, maintain the local security policy library, run the annual security training programme, and contribute to ICEYE's global security community of practice.   WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR Must haves: - Formal Facility Security Officer (FSO) or Security
