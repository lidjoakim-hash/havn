We are looking for a dynamic Dermatologist who wants to redefine skincare services of the future. Working with us will allow you to provide both private face-to-face and digital consultations to patients in local, regional and even national areas. You will play a key role in building our dermatology services from the ground up, collaborating with top medical professionals to ensure the highest standards of care. We're shaping the future of dermatology. Come join us! About the position As a key member of the skindoc team in the UK, you will have a significant influence in shaping our dermatology services and can participate in various non-clinical aspects, such as governance, training, development, and service design. About the skindoc clinic (Fulham) • Prime Location: Centrally located, just a 3-minute walk from Fulham Broadway. • Modern Design: Featuring a Scandinavian look-and-feel with innovative digital features. • Team Culture: Six consultation rooms with a mix of dermatologists and other specialists, along with a kitchen and team-room on site for R&R An ideal candidate will: • Be a Consultant Dermatologist with at least 6 months of post-CCT experience (you must be on the GMC Specialist Register). • Have a passion for patient-centred care with excellent communication skills. • Be experienced and comfortable working in telemedicine. • Have strong IT skills and be open to learning new systems. • Enjoy working in a vibrant, innovative environment. • Have additional skills such as minor dermatological surgeries (highly desirable). Core responsibilities include: • Providing top-notch dermatological care to all our patients, both in-person and digitally. • Working flexibly with support from management and the broader team. • Delivering outstanding care in line with our CQC score. • Contributing to a culture of learning and personal accountability. • Being an integral part of our quality assurance program. What we offer As a Dermatologist at skindoc, you'll enjoy a competitive per-consultation rate, job security, and a comprehensive administrative package designed to support your clinical activity. We also support private medical insurance (PMI) and both evening and weekend out-of-hours working. This is your chance to be part of an innovative company at the beginning of its UK journey, helping to shape the future of dermatology care for our patients. We encourage personal development and celebrate diversity, without discrimination based on race, religion, colour, national origin, gender, sexual orientation, age, or marital status. About skindoc skindoc is a leading provider of dermatology services, committed to creating the skincare solutions of the future. Our vision is to combine digital and in-person services to provide the best possible care. Launched in 2020, our goal has always been to become a leading provider of dermatology services across the UK. Our values Success at skindoc aligns with our core values: • Patient-Centred Care: We prioritize empathy, respect, and a passion for high-quality, accessible skincare. • Collaboration: We believe in teamwork, cultivating talent, and combining expertise to achieve outstanding results. • Integrity: We uphold professional quality and ethical standards in every decision. • Innovation: We strive to stay ahead with innovative thinking and the courage to implement new solutions.
