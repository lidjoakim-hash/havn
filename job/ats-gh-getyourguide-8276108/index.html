Team mission As Director of Product, Growth, you will be the most senior product leader for our Growth organization, joining the Chief Product Officer's staff. Your mandate is to build each product in our growth and marketing portfolio in the right direction, in close alignment with our business goals — while always delivering real value back to our users, not just building to what marketing needs. Marketing channels that have historically been siloed are converging, and you will drive the scale, leverage, and consistently excellent user experience that convergence makes possible. This is one of the largest and most consequential product scopes at GetYourGuide: it sits atop the single largest line item on our P&L outside of revenue itself, spanning Landing Pages, Paid Search Technology, Strategic Partnerships, Lifecycle Marketing, and our Creative Platform (which includes our Marketing Technology Platform and Creator tooling). You will likely own or co-own strategy for a broader cluster as well. Equally important, you will build a great product management organization — hiring, developing, and growing PMs across the growth, data, UX, and platform archetypes that a world-class marketing product org needs. You will lead a team of several direct reports (a mix of product leads and managers) across a wider organization, partnering closely with senior stakeholders on the business side. This is a role with clear, measurable success criteria and a genuine path toward broader scope and impact as our growth investment continues to compound.
