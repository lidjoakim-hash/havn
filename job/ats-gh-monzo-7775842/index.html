🚀 We’re on a mission to make money work for everyone. We’re waving goodbye to the complicated and confusing ways of traditional banking. After starting as a prepaid card, our product offering has grown a lot in the last 10 years in the UK. As well as personal and business bank accounts, we offer joint accounts, accounts for 16-17 year olds, a free kids account and credit cards in the UK, with more exciting things to come beyond. Our UK customers can also save, invest and combine their pensions with us. With our hot coral cards and get-paid-early feature, combined with financial education on social media and our award winning customer service, we have a long history of creating magical moments for our customers! We’re not about selling products - we want to solve problems and change lives through Monzo ❤️ 📍 London / UK | Salary dependent on experience + Stock Options + Benefits Flex is one of Monzo’s fastest-growing and most commercially significant products. Four years ago, we reinvented the credit card experience—building something simpler, more transparent, and genuinely useful. Today, over a million customers use Flex. We’re looking for a Product Director to lead Flex into its next phase of growth. This is a high-impact, deeply commercial role at the intersection of product and credit. You’ll define how we scale borrowing responsibly, expand into new segments, and continue building products customers genuinely love. You’ll work closely with Engineering, Credit, Data, Design, and senior leadership to identify new opportunities, optimise performance across complex segments, and deliver at pace.
