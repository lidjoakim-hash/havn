About the Center for wellbeing, welfare and happiness and research area Wellbeing research examines how people experience and evaluate their lives, what enables them to thrive, and how individuals, organizations and societies can contribute to better lives. The field brings together perspectives from psychology, economics and behavioural science to understand different dimensions of wellbeing, including happiness, meaning in life and psychological richness. At the Center for Wellbeing, Welfare and Happiness (CWWH), we connect these questions to the development of knowledge that can inform policy and practice. A central methodological challenge is how to capture the richness of people’s experiences through reliable and valid measures. Advances in natural language processing and large language models offer opportunities to complement traditional rating scales with systematic analyses of people’s own descriptions of their lives, emotions and experiences. This research combines wellbeing science, psychometrics and computational methods to investigate what language-based assessments can reveal about wellbeing, how they relate to established measures, and how they can capture differences between people and changes over time. The position will contribute to developing and validating these approaches, with relevance to the center’s research in Sweden and across countries. The role The postdoc will develop an independent research line on the measurement of wellbeing beyond traditional rating scales, with a focus on natural language (open-ended responses, text and speech) analyzed with computational methods, including large language models. The postdoc will contribute to the center's work on wellbeing in Sweden and across nations, co-author with center researchers, and connect CWWH to international collaborators and funding opportunities. The role also includes active participation in the center's outreach, such as media, public lectures and events.  Your profile The applicants must hold a PhD by time of employment and to be eligible, applicants must have obtained their doctoral degree within the three years preceding the date of employment. This period may be extended only where special circumstances apply. Professional proficiency in English (written and spoken) is a requirement. We expect the candidate to relocate to Stockholm.  Qualifications PhD in business administration, psychology or a related field. Candidates with a set dissertation date may also apply.
