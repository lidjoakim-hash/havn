About the Opportunity We are seeking a motivated intern to support the Executive Office and Corporate Governance Team at N26 for 6 months. The Executive Office plays a key role in coordinating cross-functional work between C-level executives, ensuring efficiency in strategic planning and execution. As an Intern, Corporate Governance Team, you will gain hands-on experience in administrative support, project coordination, stakeholder management, and executive operations, making this an excellent opportunity to develop critical business and organizational skills in a fast-paced environment. In this role you will: • Administrative & Financial Support: Assist in processing expense reports, purchase orders, and managing incoming correspondence for the Executive Office. • Travel & Logistics Coordination: Support travel arrangements, including research and bookings for flights, hotels, and team activities. • Meeting & Event Preparation: Prepare meeting materials, presentations, and agendas for high-level executive sessions. • Project Coordination: Collaborate with cross-functional teams to track deadlines, follow up on deliverables, and support Executive Office initiatives. • Stakeholder & Guest Management: Act as a liaison between the Executive Office, internal teams, and external visitors to facilitate seamless communication and hospitality. • Ad-Hoc Support: Assist with special projects and additional tasks as required by the Executive Office team. What You Need to Be Successful: • Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in business, finance, law, economics, management, politics, or a related field. • Excellent communication skills in English (and German beneficial). • Highly organised, detail-oriented, and adaptable to a dynamic work environment. • Proficiency in Google Workspace (Docs, Sheets, Slides) is an advantage. • A proactive, problem-solving mindset with a strong sense of ownership. • Ability to maintain confidentiality and discretion in handling sensitive information. • Based in Berlin or willing to relocate for the duration of the internship. What’s in it for you: • Accelerate your career growth by joining one of Europe’s most talked about disruptors • Employee benefits include discounts to fitness & wellness memberships, language courses, public transportation and more. • A Premium N26 bank account. As well as subscriptions for friends and family members. • Varying vacation days depending on your location of work and duration of your internship. • A high degree of autonomy and access to cutting edge technologies - all while working with a friendly team of peers of diverse nationalities, life experiences and backgrounds. Who we are N26 has reimagined banking for today’s digital world. Technology and design empower everything we do and it’s how we are building the global banking platform the world loves to use. We've eliminated physical branches, paperwork, and hidden fees for an elegant digital experience and supreme savings. Giving people the power to live and bank their way is what gets us out of bed in the morning and inspires the work that we do. We are headquartered in Berlin with offices in multiple cities across Europe, including Vienna and Barcelona, and a 1,500-strong team of more than 80 nationalities.
