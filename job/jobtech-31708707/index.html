Title: Regional Sales Director, Nordics & Baltics Location: Stockholm, Sweden (Remote) Experience: Director Level Job Function: Direct Sales Employment Type: Full-time Travel Requirements: up to 30% Industry: Computer Network & Security Job Summary The Regional Sales Director will own enterprise growth across Scandinavia and the Baltic states as a senior individual contributor. Developing new business and expansion with autonomy, specialist support and connection to Keyfactor’s European headquarters in Stockholm. Responsibilities Builds and executes enterprise account plans, prioritizing whitespace, use cases and partner access. Creates qualified pipeline through prospecting, executive outreach, events and partner activity. Develops multiple champions, engages economic buyers and maps each customer’s decision process. Connects technical needs to business outcomes through value-based discovery and clear storytelling. Leads complex opportunities through evaluation, tenders, procurement, negotiation and close. Works with Sales Engineering, SDR, Marketing, Channel and delivery teams to advance opportunities. Maintains disciplined qualification, accurate Salesforce records and reliable, evidence-based forecasts. Meets or exceeds quota, protects deal value and develops lasting customer and partner relationships. Minimum Qualifications, Education, and Skills Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Technology, or equivalent practical experience. Repeated success in complex enterprise software, infrastructure or cybersecurity sales, supported by real deal examples and quota attainment. Experience developing enterprise accounts in Nordic or Baltic markets, with knowledge of local buying processes and partner ecosystems. Multi-threading, champion development, value-based selling and clear storytelling with C-level and technical decision-makers. Proven negotiating skills, sound commercial judgment and a commitment to customer outcomes. High self-motivation, organization and attention to detail; resilience, composure and follow-through under pressure. Practical use of MEDDPICC or comparable methods, Salesforce and disciplined qualification and forecasting. Ability to align specialists and partners through influence, share knowledge and take ownership of complex deal outcomes. Curiosity and learning agility; PKI, CLM or cryptography knowledge is advantageous. Sales Engineering provides technical depth. Professional fluency in Swedish and English; other regional languages are advantageous. Right to work in Sweden. Compensation Salary will be commensurate with experience.
