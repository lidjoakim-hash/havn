The position As a Senior Salesforce Analyst at Job&Talent, you’ll shape how our Sales and Operations teams use Salesforce across 10 markets. You’ll work directly with country managers and the Head of Sales to identify where Sales Cloud can improve commercial processes and decide which projects to prioritize. You’ll lead those projects from discovery through rollout, stay hands-on with configuration, and keep our Salesforce org reliable, scalable, and easy to maintain. What you'll do: • Partner with country managers and the Head of Sales to understand commercial priorities, assess requests for new tools, and recommend how Salesforce can best support sales processes across markets. • Lead regional and global Salesforce projects, including integrations with internal systems and third-party tools. Define scope and timelines, coordinate delivery with vendors, technical teams, and business users, and keep data consistent across systems. • Own day-to-day Sales Cloud administration across countries and business units, including platform configuration, user management, security, and permissions. • Design and build scalable workflows using Salesforce objects, flows, validation rules, and approval processes. Take a declarative-first approach to reducing manual work and making Salesforce easier to use. • Own CRM data quality and governance. Build reports and dashboards that give Sales, Operations, and leadership a reliable view of the pipeline and commercial performance. • Provide people management and technical guidance within the Salesforce team. What you'll bring: • 3+ years of hands-on experience as a Salesforce Administrator or Consultant with Developer or/and Analyst knowledge. • Strong declarative configuration skills and sound judgment about when to use standard Salesforce capabilities and when to involve a developer. • Experience leading projects with business stakeholders, from gathering requirements and recommending an approach through to rollout and adoption. • Understanding of sales processes and the ability to clearly explain technical trade-offs to commercial leaders. • Hands-on experience with Salesforce Sales or Commercial Cloud. • Familiarity with Apex/LWC and the ability to read and discuss existing code. • Fluent in English & Spanish.
