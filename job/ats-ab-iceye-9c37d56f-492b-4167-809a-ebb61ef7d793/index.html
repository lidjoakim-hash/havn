Role highlights: - Senior Smart Manufacturing Engineer, Electronics - Location: Espoo, Finland - Department: Global Manufacturing Organization, Smart Manufacturing Engineering - Reports to: Team Lead, Smart Manufacturing Engineering - Employment type: Permanent - Workplace model: Hybrid - Employment is subject to applicable security screening (incl. SUPO, where required) Why this role matters: As a Senior Smart Manufacturing Engineer, Electronics, you’ll own the test systems that every ICEYE satellite passes through before it reaches orbit. You’ll design and standardize the production and integration test equipment used across our manufacturing sites, find the bottlenecks that would otherwise cap how fast we can scale, and set the technical standard for the engineers around you. This is a hands-on design role with organization-wide influence: what you build and document becomes how we manufacture. Who We Are ICEYE is the world leader in sovereign intelligence from space. We deliver persistent monitoring capabilities to detect and respond to changes in any location on Earth. ICEYE owns the world's largest and most advanced SAR (synthetic aperture radar) satellite constellation. To our customers we provide intelligence with unmatched quality, latency and revisit times, in any weather, day or night. To governments who choose to operate their own constellation we provide this proven capability as a sovereign system. ICEYE-built constellations serve customers in defence and intelligence, environmental monitoring, insurance and emergency management. We enable fast decisions that contribute to a safer future. Founded and headquartered in Finland, ICEYE operates globally with over 1000 employees across Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. Your day-to-day responsibilities   - Design, build and own the production and integration test systems used on the line, including the electrical ground support equipment. - Drive standardization of test equipment across products and sites, so designs and interfaces are reused rather than rebuilt each time. - Define test strategies: coverage, test limits, guard bands, failure modes and diagnostics. - Produce and maintain the design documentation other engineers execute from, including schematics, test requirements, procedures and interface definitions. - Use production and test data to find yield issues, bottlenecks and systemic weaknesses, then implement the fixes before they limit the ramp. - Review designs from other teams and set the requirements they must meet to integrate cleanly into our production workflow. - Mentor junior and mid-level engineers through design reviews, feedback and knowledge sharing. What we’re looking for Must haves: - Several years of electronics design and test system development experience, ideally on high-reliability or high-complexity products (space, defence, telecom, automotive, industrial or similar). - Strong analog and digital electronics design, including high-speed digital work (signal integrity, impedance control, power integrity) and PCBA design for manufacturability and testability. - A track record of designing, implementing and maintaining automated test systems or electrical ground support equipment, taken from concept through to stable production. - Hands-on RF and mixed-signal test and characterization, including S-parameters, matching networks and filters, with the instruments that go with them (spectrum analyzers, vector network analyzers, signal generators). - Test automation in Python and embedded development in C/C++ on common microcontrollers such as STM32, TI or MSP430. - Experience mentoring engineers and running design reviews, with documentation quality to match. Nice to haves: - Altium Designer or KiCad, and RF simulation in CST or AWR. - Lean or Six Sigma background, and quality methods such as FMEA, 8D or fault tree analysis applied to production
