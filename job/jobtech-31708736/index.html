Title: Senior RFP & Bid Manager, EMEA Location: Stockholm, Sweden; Aachen, Germany; Barcelona or Madrid, Spain; United Kingdom Experience: Senior Job Function: Revenue Operations Employment Type: Full-Time Industry: Computer and Network Security About the position The Senior RFP & Bid Manager, EMEA is responsible for establishing, managing, and continuously improving the RFP, RFI, and tender response management framework across Keyfactor’s EMEA sales organization. Operating as a specialized individual contributor, the role owns the operational processes, governance standards, tools, and knowledge management practices required to support effective and consistent bid responses. This position can be based out of our offices in following locations and we have a hybrid work model: Stockholm (Sweden), Barcelona or Madrid (Spain), Aachen, Germany or United Kingdom. Responsibilities Own and continuously improve the EMEA RFP and bid management operating framework, establishing standardized intake procedures, response workflows, submission controls, and governance practices to ensure consistent and effective execution across the region. Coordinate the end-to-end execution of complex RFP, RFI, and tender responses, working closely with Sales, Solutions Engineering, Legal, Compliance, and other relevant functions to obtain required inputs, manage timelines and dependencies, and ensure timely, accurate, and complete submissions. Develop and continuously improve bid management methodologies and standards, identifying operational inefficiencies, evaluating improvement opportunities, and implementing solutions to enhance response quality, turnaround time, and cross-functional effectiveness. Apply specialized knowledge of public and private tender procedures, assessing procedural requirements, identifying submission risks, and providing guidance to internal stakeholders on appropriate response processes while escalating matters requiring legal, compliance, or commercial decisions to the relevant functions. Establish and govern a centralized RFP content and knowledge management framework, including the administration of Loopio and approved supporting tools, ensuring content accuracy, consistency, currency, and appropriate validation by relevant subject-matter experts. Facilitate effective cross-functional collaboration throughout the bid response process, coordinating contributions from Sales, Solutions Engineering, Product Management, Legal, Compliance, Finance, and Professional Services, and ensuring that dependencies, responsibilities, and escalation requirements are clearly communicated. Exercise independent professional judgment in managing RFP response activities, determining when approved content can be used, identifying requirements for additional specialist input, and escalating complex or sensitive matters to the appropriate accountable stakeholders. Coordinate the review and validation of technical, security, service-related, and contractual information included in bid responses, ensuring that product capabilities, roadmap statements, security representations, implementation commitments, and service deliverables are reviewed and approved by the relevant authorized functions prior to submission. Serve as the primary operational point of contact for customer procurement representatives and external bid management partners, coordinating procedural clarifications, submission requirements, joint-bid activities, and response logistics while involving Sales and other relevant stakeholders in substantive customer discussions. Develop and maintain RFP management performance reporting, monitoring submission volumes, turnaround times, response quality, content effectiveness, and relevant bid outcomes, analyzing operational trends, and recommending process improvements to Revenue Operations and Sales leadership.
