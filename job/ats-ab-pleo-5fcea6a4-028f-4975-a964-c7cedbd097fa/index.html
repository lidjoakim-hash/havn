ABOUT PLEO Messy spend management is tricky business. And tedious processes are a lose-lose situation for all involved, not just finance. At Pleo, we're changing that. We build spend solutions that make managing money seamless, empowering, and surprisingly effective for finance teams and employees alike - with a vision to help all businesses ‘go beyond’. The word ‘Pleo’ actually means ‘more than you’d expect’, and living by that mantra has been the secret to our success over the last 10 years. Now, we’re at a pivotal moment in our journey; every move we make has a direct impact on our 40,000+ customers, our business, and our collective success. We need people who take pride in uncovering customer needs, who turn complex problems into simple solutions, challenge the way things are done (respectfully), and always aim high. With great ambitions driving us forward, we can’t say we’ve got this whole thing figured out. And frankly, that’s half the fun! What we can say is that we’re a driven, progressive, and, importantly, a kind bunch of 850+ people from over 100 nationalities, all committed to delivering the future of business spending, together. About the role We're looking for an Account Executive to join our Commercial Nordics Sales Team at Pleo. In this role, you'll help drive the entire sales cycle, from prospecting to closing deals and be part of a collaborative and successful sales unit as we scale Pleo's expense management solution. If you're excited about converting new business and are passionate about SaaS outbound sales this is the opportunity for you! Who you’ll be working with and reporting to You'll report to our Sales Manager and work closely with teams in SDRs, Customer Success and Marketing. Our team is highly collaborative and dedicated to achieving revenue goals. You'll also have the chance to partner with teams across product to ensure success What you’ll be doing As an Account Executive, you will: - Own the entire sales cycle - from outbound to qualification, product demos and deal conversion. - Achieve new business revenue targets - focusing on building new relationships. - Collaborate closely with the Customer Success team - to ensure the customers in your portfolio successfully adopt with Pleo. - Become an expert in expense management. - Give outstanding product presentations - acing those demos to get deals closed. This role is a good fit for you if: - You’re comfortable working in a fast-paced, high-growth environment and enjoy taking ownership. - You’re able to collaborate effectively across teams and stakeholders. - You’re proactive, pragmatic, and motivated by making a tangible impact.
