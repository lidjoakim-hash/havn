About Wolt At Wolt, we create technology that brings joy, simplicity and earnings to the neighborhoods of the world. In 2014 we started with delivery of restaurant food. Now we’re building the delivery of (almost) everything and you’ll find us in over 500 cities in 30 countries around the world. In 2022 we joined forces with DoorDash and together we keep on dreaming big and expanding across the globe. Working at Wolt isn’t always easy, but it’s definitely exciting. Here you’ll learn more, build more, and ship more than in most other companies. You’ll be challenged a lot, but also have a lot of fun on the way. So, if you’re a self-starter with drive and entrepreneurial spirit, this could be the ride of your life. About The Team At Wolt, we create technology that brings joy, simplicity and earnings to neighbourhoods. Our People team supports employees and managers throughout their time with us. As an HR Operations Specialist, you’ll provide reliable and responsive HR support across our Romanian entities, covering employee administration, payroll coordination, employee relations and local compliance. What you’ll be doing You’ll manage assigned HR processes and cases, working closely with internal partners and external providers. You’ll also help maintain compliant, accurate records and improve the way we support our people. Day-to-day in this role you’ll: • Respond to Romania-specific and escalated HR enquiries, guiding employees and managers on employment terms, policies, leave, benefits and payroll. • Coordinate onboarding and approved exits, prepare employment documents and maintain personnel records and updates across Workday, payroll and REGES-ONLINE. • Validate payroll inputs, investigate discrepancies and administer leave and benefits with payroll teams and providers. • Manage assigned employee relations cases, including grievances and disciplinary matters, and support performance processes in collaboration with People Partners and Legal. • Maintain accurate HR data and reports, coordinate service providers and help improve local HR processes and guidance. Our humble expectations You’ll bring practical HR experience and sound judgement to support employees and managers across Romania. • At least 3 years of experience in HR operations, personnel administration or an HR generalist role, including practical experience with Romanian employment processes. • Strong knowledge of Romanian employment legislation, payroll processes and statutory reporting requirements. • Confidence using HR systems and Excel, including working with data and preparing reports. • Strong organisation, attention to detail and the ability to prioritise, solve problems and handle confidential information with discretion. • Excellent Romanian and English communication skills, with the ability to work with employees, managers and other stakeholders. What we offer Our benefits in Romania include: • Meal vouchers for eligible working days. • A private medical subscription with Regina Maria. • Group life insurance to provide financial support to your beneficiaries. • A wellbeing benefit to support an active and healthy lifestyle. • Access to Bookster for books and learning resources.
