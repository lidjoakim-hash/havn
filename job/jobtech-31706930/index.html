We want you to work on assignments that you are passionate about, grow professionally, and maintain a healthy work–life balance.  About the role As an Embedded C Developer, you will play a key role in developing the transmission control software for future electric propulsion solutions. You will work on the full software lifecycle of the transmission control unit (TCU), from specifying and designing the architecture to implementation, verification, integration and testing in C. You will also develop test frameworks. We can offer you challenges that are both fun and exciting. You will work on software that brings the driveline to life. This includes gear shift and clutch control functions, actuator control for solenoids, valves and electric motors, and sensor handling for speed, position, pressure and temperature. The software platform covers MCU low-level drivers, device drivers for all connected peripherals, and CAN and Ethernet communication stacks. It also covers RTOS configuration, the diagnostic event manager and ECU power management. On top of this, you will help improve how we work, our development tools and our continuous integration framework. We are looking for you… …who wants to grow as a professional and brings an open mind and a proactive approach. You have strong drive and creativity, and you come up with ideas and solutions all the way from system design to verification. You understand how software and mechanics work together in a transmission and enjoy solving problems where timing, control and hardware meet. You like working in a team but are just as comfortable working on your own. Requirements: B.Sc./M.Sc. in Embedded Software, Electronics, Mechatronics, Physics or equivalent education. At least five years of experience in embedded software development and real-time systems programming. Strong skills in embedded C for safety-critical real-time systems. Experience developing software for transmission, driveline or propulsion systems, for example TCU, gearbox, clutch or e-axle control. Skills in embedded software architecture, design and verification. Skilled in Python and Git. Fluent in English, both spoken and written. It is a merit if you have experience with: Shift strategies, torque control, clutch control or control engineering in general. ISO 26262 (functional safety)  AUTOSAR. Tools such as the Lauterbach TRACE32 debugger, oscilloscopes, CANoe/CANalyzer and Robot Framework. Electronics and actuator control (PWM, current control, solenoids). Software development for Volvo Powertrain or another transmission supplier, which will be highly valued. You are open-minded, proactive and structured, and you have the drive to take the lead.
