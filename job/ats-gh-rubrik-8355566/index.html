About Team & About Role Rubrik’s Channel team is how we bring cyber resilience to the organisations across the UK and Ireland that people rely on every day, including hospitals, banks, government bodies and utilities. Identity is now the front door attackers go after, and as Business Development Manager, Identity, you’ll build the partner ecosystem that helps UKI customers keep Active Directory, Entra ID and Okta trusted, protected and recoverable. This is an individual contributor role with real autonomy over which partners you invest in, how you plan with them, and how fast you help them grow a new identity-led market. What You’ll Do • Build joint business plans with the top, Managed UKI resellers and identity specialists that matter most, so partners have a clear path to growing their Rubrik identity business and Rubrik has a clear view of where it will come from. • Drive Partner Initiated Deals and net-new pipeline by working with partner sales teams to identify, qualify and close identity resilience opportunities alongside Rubrik Account Executives. • Open doors with IAM, IGA and PAM-focused partners and integrators, positioning Rubrik as the recovery layer behind their customers’ identity investments. • Lead enablement for partner sales and SE teams, so they can position the identity risk story, demo Rubrik for AD, Entra ID and Okta, and explain why identity recovery needs its own plan. • Build joint solutions and co-selling motions with adjacent identity vendors and the Microsoft and Okta ecosystems, so customers see one coordinated answer to identity attacks. • Run account mapping and joint planning sessions between Rubrik field teams and partners, uncovering identity-driven opportunities in existing accounts and new logos. • Identify, onboard and grow new Identity specialist partners that fill gaps in identity expertise, vertical or regional coverage. • Work with Channel and Field Marketing to host partner and customer events and campaigns on identity threats and recovery, putting Rubrik in front of CISOs and identity leaders. • Report on partner performance, pipeline and forecast to Channel and Sales leadership, giving the business a clear read on identity growth in UKI. Experience You’ll Need • Strong track record in channel development, channel sales or partner management at a technology vendor, ideally in identity, IAM or cybersecurity, working with resellers and distributors. • Good working knowledge of identity technology, including directory services (Active Directory, Entra ID or Okta), IAM, IGA or PAM, and how identity attacks unfold. • Established relationships with UKI partners and distributors, especially those with identity or security practices. • Experience identifying, initiating and closing deals with partners in close coordination with direct sales teams. • Experience running quarterly and annual planning for partner sales activity and reporting on pipeline and forecast. • Confidence presenting to larger audiences and hosting events with partners and end customers. • A collaborative approach to working across Sales, Systems Engineering, Marketing, Alliances and Sales Operations. Preferred Qualifications • Experience at an identity vendor (for example IAM, IGA, PAM or identity threat detection) or a partner with a strong identity practice. • Experience building joint solutions or co-selling with adjacent security and infrastructure vendors, including Microsoft or Okta. • Familiarity with backup, recovery or cyber resilience, and the case for protecting identity systems as a recovery priority. • A background running partner enablement or certification programmes. • Experience acquiring and onboarding new partners.
