Hej Software Engineer! Do you want to furnish the future to make room for everyone? With us, you can feel at home at work while sharing that feeling with millions of people around the world. We encourage you to stand up for yourself and others, creating an environment where everyone feels welcome. We invite you to open new doors, embrace mistakes, and grow from them. As our Software Engineer, you’ll have many opportunities to try things you’ve never done before. We are looking for a highly skilled Software Engineer to join our Digital Product Area: “Product Engineering”, a part of Digital Engineering at Inter IKEA, Range. The Product Engineering Area is dedicated to ensuring an attractive, consistent, and efficient way of working for product development and our suppliers. Our goal is to establish a strong foundation for IKEA's product information, enabling its seamless use across the entire value chain. We are growing our team in Malmö, Sweden, and are looking for someone with strong communication and collaboration skills who can work effectively with colleagues across locations and seamlessly collaborate with our offshore team. Your new job We’re looking for a Software Engineer who wants to solve complex, real-world challenges and building digital products that make everyday work simpler and more effective. You will help shape the next generation of engineering platforms that enable effective collaboration between IKEA and suppliers around the globe. Through our modernized Supplier Collaboration Platform and Standard Product Lifecycle Management (PLM) initiatives, you will develop scalable solutions that simplify how product information is shared, managed, and governed across the value chain. You will report to a Software Engineering Manager. The impact of your work will touch homes and hearts around the world. On a typical day, you will: Help shape and evolve the modernized Supplier Collaboration Platform, enabling seamless collaboration between IKEA engineers and suppliers across the globe. Design, build, and maintain modern web applications, APIs, and cloud-native services using technologies such as React, .NET10 and Azure. Contribute to the Standard Product Lifecycle Management (PLM) initiative, helping create scalable, secure, and user-centric solutions that support the future of product development. Work across the full product lifecycle, from discovery and solution design to implementation, deployment, and continuous improvement. Collaborate closely with engineers, architects, product owners, and business stakeholders to solve complex challenges and deliver business value. Influence technical direction and contribute to architecture, design decisions, and engineering best practices. Foster a culture of collaboration, openness, continuous learning, and knowledge sharing. Bring curiosity and fresh ideas that improve both our digital products and the way we work. Ensure high-quality, maintainable software through code reviews, automated testing, observability, and modern software engineering practices. What we’d like you to bring to the table This role as a Software Engineer requires you to: Have strong hands-on experience in .NET 10 backend development, combined with modern frontend development using React, with 8+ years of professional software engineering experience. Be proficient in designing, building, and operating scalable web applications, APIs, and cloud-native solutions. Have solid experience working with Microsoft Azure and modern cloud architectures. Demonstrate strong problem-solving skills and the ability to translate business needs into robust technical solutions. Have good interpersonal skills: active listening, coaching and facilitation.
