WHY BOX NEEDS YOU Box's Growth & Demand Marketing team is at the heart of how we connect with the industries and accounts that matter most. As Manager, Industry Campaigns & ABM for EMEA, you will lead the design and execution of high-impact, vertically-focused campaigns and account-based marketing programmes that drive pipeline and deepen engagement with our most strategic target accounts across the region. This is a high-visibility role that sits at the intersection of strategy and execution. You will work closely with demand generation, sales, and customer success teams to bring campaigns to life across EMEA's core markets, localising messaging, activating intent signals, and measuring what matters. If you thrive in a fast-moving environment and are passionate about building programmes that generate real business outcomes, this is the role for you.
