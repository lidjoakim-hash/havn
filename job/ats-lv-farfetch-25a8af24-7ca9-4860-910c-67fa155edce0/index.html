Luxclusif is a technology-driven global B2B platform founded in 2013 and part of Farfetch since 2021. Dedicated to redefining the luxury paradigm by offering premium pre-owned fashion to a global audience, Luxclusive has pioneered the global luxury resale market, with advanced data-driven solutions to facilitate the buying and selling of high-end pre-owned items. With operations spread across the globe and strategic warehouses in key locations, ensure a seamless supply of luxury goods, ensuring item curation, quality control, operations, logistics to technology integrations and sales, delivering an unmatched resale experience for brands, retailers, and end-customers.    PORTO Our office is near Porto, in the north of Portugal, and is located in a vibrant business hub. It offers a dynamic and welcoming environment where our employees can connect and network with a large community of tech professionals.    THE ROLE We are looking for a New Business Account Manager to help grow Luxclusif’s client portfolio and improve how we launch new partnerships. This is an individual-contributor role covering the full new business lifecycle: identifying prospects, conducting outreach, qualifying opportunities, driving commercial negotiations, defining client operating models and coordinating onboarding through launch. You will also help strengthen the team’s ways of working by creating clear processes, documentation, guidelines and tools. WHAT YOU’LL DO • Research and identify prospective clients, markets and partnership opportunities. • Conduct targeted outreach and build a strong pipeline of qualified leads. • Lead discovery conversations and assess opportunities based on commercial potential, strategic fit and operational feasibility. • Prepare proposals and business cases, and drive commercial negotiations through to agreement. • Translate client needs into clear, scalable and commercially sustainable operating models. • Coordinate new client onboarding across Commercial, Operations, Logistics, Technology, Product, Finance and Legal. • Track milestones, risks, decisions and actions through testing and launch. • Create playbooks, templates, guidelines and processes to improve how the New Business team operates. • Develop new business models in response to emerging market opportunities. WHO YOU ARE • At least 2 years of experience in B2B new business, business development, partnerships or client solutions. • Experienced in prospecting, outreach, qualification, pipeline management and commercial negotiations. • Able to combine commercial thinking with strong operational execution. • Comfortable leading complex cross-functional work without formal authority. • Structured, analytical, proactive and hands-on. • A clear and confident communicator in English. • Experience in luxury, fashion, resale, marketplaces or e-commerce is a plus. • German or Arabic is a plus.
