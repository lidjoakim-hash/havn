The Rubrik Engineering team is comprised of people who produce extraordinary results. Our engineers are driven to build efficient, reliable, and cost-effective products. As part of the Rubrik Engineering team, you will work closely with product managers, designers, and other engineers to define the next generation of products for Rubrik. At Rubrik, nothing will stop you from thinking big. We are looking for individuals who are comfortable with ambiguity and excited by the prospect of a challenge. Job Overview The Platform Solutions Architect is a key sales specialist who brings deep technical expertise to the broader sales organisation. You will work with customers & prospects to educate how Rubrik protects and manages data across the enterprise estate, from virtualisation, physical servers, NAS, and databases to cloud, SaaS, and identity. You will own the technical side of the sale from first conversation to validated decision. That includes running proof of concept, producing technical documentation, diagrams and guiding complex, multi-stakeholder discussions. You will also bring field insight back to Product and Engineering, test features before release, and help enable the wider sales team. Your technical credibility and influence with customers will drive results now and shape the product over time. Build consensus among them and handle objections and competitive pressure. • Own the technical win. Agree POC scope and success criteria with the customer, plan and deliver the POC, resolve blockers, and present results that support the business case. • Assist with technical implementation and validation of the product. • Design resilient backup, recovery, and cyber resilience architectures across hybrid and multi-cloud environments.
