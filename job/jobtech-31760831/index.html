A Snapshot of Your Day  Ready to make a real impact? As a Customer Contract Manager (CCM), you will drive our service contracts and be the trusted partner our customers rely on. You will leverage your strong commitment and understanding of customer needs to ensure high satisfaction levels. In this role, you will be part of the project management office for Long Term Program & Overhauls, responsible for delivering exceptional services to our gas turbine customers in the Power Generation and Oil & Gas industries, within Middle East and Americas. Regular visits to customers and collaboration with regional Siemens Energy colleagues will be essential, making international travel a key aspect of your role. How You’ll Make an Impact You will take full responsibility for your service contracts, ensuring their successful execution and delivery. Lead a cross-functional project team, collaborating with colleagues from engineering, commercial project management, overhaul project management, and more. Build trust and maintain transparent communication as the primary contact for your customers. Develop long-term relationships by understanding your customers’ needs, decision-making processes, and key stakeholders, while also fostering strong connections with Siemens Energy regional colleagues. Identify customer needs, promote our service products, and uncover opportunities for additional sales. Structure and plan contract activities on time, aligning with customer requirements. Travel to customer sites to negotiate and follow up on contracts alongside local representatives. What You Bring Previous experience in international business, such as sales or project management. Strong negotiation skills, with confidence in communication at various levels and the ability to deliver under pressure. A collaborative spirit and the ability to work effectively with diverse teams and cultures. A proactive, can-do attitude that drives results. A strong commitment to making a difference every day. Understanding of advanced risk and opportunity management to create win-win situations. About the Team Diverse team: We are a team with different ages, backgrounds and experiences, and we value the different perspectives this brings. Different roles, one goal: Our team brings together different roles and competencies, such as contract management and overhaul project management. Together we deliver on our commitments to our customers. Learning from each other: Experienced colleagues and newer team members share knowledge, and we grow by learning from one another. Collaboration and inclusion: We work closely together across cultures and regions with a strong team spirit. Everyone's voice counts. Who is Siemens Energy? At Siemens Energy, we are more than just an energy technology company. With ~100,000 dedicated employees in more than 90 countries, we develop the energy systems of the future, ensuring that the growing energy demand of the global community is met reliably and sustainably. The technologies created in our research departments and factories drive the energy transition and provide the base for one sixth of the world's electricity generation.  Our global team is committed to making sustainable, reliable, and affordable energy a reality by pushing the boundaries of what is possible.
