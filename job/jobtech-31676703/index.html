Logistics Manager – Gävle

OnePartnerGroup Södra Norrland AB · Gävle, Sweden

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Are you an experienced logistics professional with a background in large-scale construction projects? Do you enjoy taking ownership, coordinating complex operations and ensuring that everything runs smoothly on site? Then this could be your next opportunity! OnePartnerGroup is now looking for an experienced Logistics Manager for a consulting assignment with an international company operating within the data centre industry. The company delivers large-scale data centre projects across Europe and is recognised for its expertise in the design, construction and commissioning of these facilities. In this role, you will play a key part in ensuring efficient, safe and well-coordinated logistics operations at a major project site in Gävle. About the role As Logistics Manager, you will be responsible for managing and coordinating all logistics activities on site. You will ensure that deliveries, material handling, site access, traffic management and storage are planned and executed efficiently, in line with the project schedule and applicable health and safety requirements. You will work closely with Project Managers, Package Managers, subcontractors, suppliers and other project stakeholders. Your ability to plan ahead, identify potential challenges and find practical solutions will be essential to keeping the project running smoothly. Your main responsibilities will include: Leading and managing all site logistics activities throughout the project lifecycle. Developing and implementing site logistics plans based on project requirements. Coordinating deliveries, material handling and site movements to ensure efficient flows and minimal disruption. Managing site access, traffic management and security in accordance with health and safety standards. Coordinating logistics activities with Project Managers, Package Managers, subcontractors and suppliers. Monitoring storage areas and ensuring materials are stored safely and efficiently. Managing logistics subcontractors, including performance follow-up and coordination meetings. Ensuring that logistics documentation, permits and safety records are maintained and up to date. Participating in site meetings, planning meetings and logistics coordination meetings. Identifying and mitigating logistical risks, including congestion, delays and site constraints. Preparing logistics reports and providing updates to the project team and senior management. Proactively identifying and resolving logistical and coordination challenges on site. Who are we looking for? We are looking for someone with solid experience in construction or engineering logistics who is comfortable taking responsibility for complex site operations. You are structured, proactive and solution-oriented, with strong planning and coordination skills. You enjoy working in a fast-paced project environment and are confident communicating with different stakeholders. You have a strong safety mindset and the ability to manage several priorities simultaneously without losing sight of the bigger picture. To succeed in this role, we believe you have: A bachelor's degree in Logistics, Supply Chain Management, Construction Management or a similar discipline. At least 5–7 years of experience in a logistics role within construction, engineering or a related industry. Extensive experience managing site logistics on large-scale construction projects. Good knowledge of traffic management, material handling and site coordination. A strong understanding of health and safety requirements related to site logistics. Excellent organisational, planning and prioritisation skills. Strong communication skills and experience coordinating multiple stakeholders. The ability to manage several priorities in a demanding project environment. Experience from data centre, industrial or other mission-critical projects is considered an advantage.
