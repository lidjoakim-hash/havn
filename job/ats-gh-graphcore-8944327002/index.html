About the job Set the direction for Graphcore’s ML software, with accountability for organisational leadership, investment priorities and delivery across platform generations. As Senior Director of ML Software in Bristol, you’ll report to the VP of Software. Your organisation delivers the machine learning software and analysis tools that enable AI models to run on Graphcore’s platform. You’ll translate business objectives into multi year roadmaps, aligning resources, investment and delivery commitments. You’ll own outcomes from early platform bring-up through production release and support. Your remit spans machine learning frameworks, kernel libraries, hardware simulation, networking software, profiling and debugging tools. You’ll lead through senior engineering leaders, shape organisational structure and develop successors. Beyond your function, you’ll shape wider software priorities, influence executive decisions and sponsor initiatives across the organisation. The team and culture You’ll partner with silicon, hardware, architecture, system software, product and operations leaders to align roadmaps and resolve integration risks. With architecture leaders, you’ll challenge design assumptions across frameworks, simulation, collectives and tooling. Your leadership team will own achievable, adequately resourced plans, with clear expectations for milestones, quality and outcomes. You’ll resolve risks promptly, make portfolio trade-offs and escalate decisions requiring executive alignment. Delivery and quality measures will guide investment in continuous integration, automated testing, hardware-in-the-loop validation, regression coverage and release governance. You’ll turn incidents into lasting improvements, with reliability, security and testability treated as engineering outcomes. What we’re looking for • Organisational leadership: You’ve led sizeable system software organisations through managers and senior technical leaders, accountable for complex, multi-team hardware and software programmes. You set clear expectations and hold leaders accountable for delivery, quality and business outcomes. • Building leadership capacity: You’ve scaled engineering organisations, shaped structures and workforce plans, and led performance management and organisational change. You coach senior leaders, develop successors and build the capabilities needed to meet changing product and business priorities. • Strategy and investment: You’ve owned multi-year roadmaps and delivery portfolios, aligning investment, capacity and commitments with business objectives. You make portfolio-level trade-offs under resource constraints, resolve critical technical dependencies and manage risks across competing programmes. • Engineering quality and governance: You’ve set organisation-wide quality and release standards, supported by hardware-based automation, regression coverage and robust validation. You use delivery and quality measures, incident learning and risk governance to drive improvements across teams. • Executive influence: You’ve aligned senior stakeholders across organisational boundaries, resolved complex decisions and communicated strategic risks and investment choices. You translate technical trade-offs into implications for products, customers, operational resilience and business outcomes, contributing beyond your immediate function. • Strategic and technical judgement: You balance delivery commitments and business outcomes with reliability, security, maintainability and long-term platform needs. Experience with machine learning software, hardware-software interfaces, AI accelerators, GPUs or high-performance computing would be valuable. While we have outlined a set of requirements, we value transferable skills and diverse experiences.
