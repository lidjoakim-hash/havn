The Sr. Manager, Event Operations, leads the operating model behind MongoDB’s global Strategic Events portfolio. Reporting to the Director of Global Strategic Events, this leader owns the processes, platforms, planning cadence, and measurement framework that turn portfolio strategy into consistent, high-quality execution at scale, and leads the team that runs them. This role is for an event operations leader who has run events hands-on and is ready to design how a global events function operates: how work is prioritized and resourced, how technology and AI are applied, and how event impact is measured and reported to senior leadership. You will build the operating model that allows Strategic Events to grow its reach and impact without a proportional increase in resources. We are looking to speak to candidates who are based in Dublin for our hybrid working model. What You’ll Do • Lead and develop the Strategic Events Operations team. Set priorities, define roles and performance standards, coach and develop team members, and hold a consistent bar for quality and execution across every program in the portfolio • Design and own the event operating model. Define the intake, workflows, governance, service levels by event tier, and operating rhythms that the Strategic Events team and its partners run on. Build the playbooks and standards that make execution repeatable across regions and event types • Translate strategy into execution. Partner with the Director of Global Strategic Events to turn portfolio strategy into team standards, event execution plans, and resourcing models • Own the events technology and data ecosystem. Be accountable for the project management tools (e.g., Wrike, JIRA), event and registration platforms (e.g., Cvent, Luma), and reporting systems the team relies on. Decide what to standardize, integrate, automate, or retire • Build and govern agentic workflows in production. Identify where AI agents and automation can take on work across planning, registration, reporting, and stakeholder communications, and design, deploy, and maintain those workflows at scale. Define where agents can act autonomously and where human review or approval is required, measure their impact on speed, quality, and team capacity, and lead the team in operating and evolving them • Plan capacity and manage the global events calendar. Run capacity planning, balance workload against company priorities, and surface resourcing risk early so it never becomes a delivery problem • Drive measurement and reporting. Partner with Marketing Ops to define the metrics, lead-flow tracking, and post-event reporting used across first- and third-party events, and synthesize results into clear recommendations for senior stakeholders • Act as the operations partner to Sales, Marketing, and Partner teams. Work through competing priorities, budget trade-offs, and process gaps directly, and keep stakeholders aligned on how Strategic Events works and what to expect • Continuously improve how the work gets done. Redesign roles, consolidate tools, and rebuild processes so the team can take on more with the resources in place.
