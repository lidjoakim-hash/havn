PRODUCT DESIGN DIRECTOR - PATIENT (X/F/M) THE OPPORTUNITY Doctolib is a mission-driven healthcare technology company, founded in Paris in 2013. Today, nearly 3,000 people work across Europe to transform the way healthcare is delivered and experienced. More than 500,000 healthcare professionals use Doctolib’s solutions, and the patient app is used by approximately 90 million people across Europe. For patients, Doctolib began as a simple way to find a healthcare professional and book an appointment. We are now building something much more ambitious: an AI-powered Health Companion that helps people navigate care, understand their health and take the right next step throughout their health journey. This means moving beyond a primarily transactional product towards an experience that supports patients before, during and after care, through access to care, health information, patient data, messaging, recommendations, care plans and AI-powered assistance. We are looking for a Product Design Director to help define what this next generation of patient experience should look and feel like. WHAT YOU WILL BUILD AND LEAD As Product Design Director, Patient, you will own the design vision and quality bar for Doctolib’s patient experience. You will report to our VP Design and work closely with the Product, Engineering, Medical, Data and Business leadership teams.  Your scope will cover the full patient journey, including: - access to care, search and booking; - patient messaging and teleconsultation; - the Patient Health Record and patient context; - the app homepage and daily health experience; - health content and prevention; - recommendations, reminders and care plans; - new patient services and subscription experiences; - conversational and AI-powered experiences. The current team includes six Senior Product Designers and one Senior Content Designer. You will lead, coach and develop this team while shaping the organisation and operating model needed to support the next phase of the Patient product. YOUR MANDATE You will: - Define and drive the design vision for the Patient stream, from a high-traffic booking experience to a trusted Health Companion used throughout the health journey. - Bridge two equally important challenges: continuing to improve the core access-to-care experience while creating new reasons for patients to return beyond booking. - Raise the quality bar for product design, interaction design and content across the patient experience. - Stay close enough to the product and the craft to make high-quality design decisions, not only set direction from a distance. - Shape AI-native experiences that are clear, useful and trustworthy, including recommendations, conversational interfaces and next-best actions. - Establish strong design principles for transparency, explainability, uncertainty, user control and safe escalation in a healthcare context. - Build a high-performing design organisation, including hiring, coaching, talent development and second-line leadership. - Create effective ways of working across Design, Product, Engineering, Medical, Data and Business. - Help scale a coherent experience across countries, languages and different healthcare contexts. - Connect design decisions to user behaviour, product outcomes and business impact. WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE In your first year, you will have: - established a clear and compelling design vision for the Patient stream; - raised the quality bar across both core access-to-care journeys and newer Health Companion experiences; - helped define a coherent interaction model for AI-powered patient experiences; - strengthened collaboration between Design, Product, Engineering and Medical; - developed a clear operating model and growth plan for the Patient Design organisation; - helped Doctolib increase the value and relevance of the patient experience beyond appointment booking.
