ABOUT US Founded in 2019 and part of Y Combinator's 2020 cohort, Photoroom https://www.photoroom.com is the leading visual solution for e-commerce. We've raised Series B funding and reached 300+ million users worldwide, processing over 5 billion images annually and serving both small businesses and major enterprises like Amazon, DoorDash, and Decathlon through our mobile app, web platform, and API. We're a team of 100+ passionate builders giving e-commerce businesses superpowers to create visuals that help them grow, making the hardest parts of selling online disappear. We focus on craft, innovation, and collaboration to create exceptional impact for e-commerce businesses worldwide. ROLE SUMMARY We are hiring a VP of Sales to build Photoroom's global sales-led business into one of the company's major growth engines. Sales-led revenue is still early, at roughly $5-6M today. The foundations are real: strong enterprise customers, product-market fit in marketplaces, a growing pipeline, a large existing user base, and a team of 14. The opportunity now is to take that from early traction to a repeatable global engine, with the ambition to build towards $100M in sales-led revenue over the next few years. This is not a role for someone looking to apply a mature SaaS playbook to an already-defined market. You will need to decide where we play, sharpen the ICP, help shape pricing, build the sales operating system, raise the bar on the team, and stay close enough to customers to personally help win the deals that matter. One of Photoroom's biggest advantages is our existing product adoption. A core part of the job is figuring out how to turn that into enterprise growth: identifying organisations already using Photoroom, reaching the right buyers, landing strategic accounts and expanding across seats, usage, workflows and API consumption. You will work closely with our CEO and leadership team, and partner deeply with Product, and Marketing.
