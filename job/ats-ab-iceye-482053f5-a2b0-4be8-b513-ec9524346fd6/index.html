Working alongside a global IT team, you will manage local and global requests and route complex escalations to the appropriate team members.   WHO WE ARE ICEYE is the world leader in sovereign intelligence from space. We deliver persistent monitoring capabilities to detect and respond to changes in any location on Earth.   ICEYE owns the world's largest and most advanced SAR (synthetic aperture radar) satellite constellation. To our customers we provide intelligence with unmatched quality, latency and revisit times, in any weather, day or night. To governments who choose to operate their own constellation we provide this proven capability as a sovereign system.   ICEYE-built constellations serve customers in defence and intelligence, environmental monitoring, insurance and emergency management. YOUR DAY-TO-DAY RESPONSIBILITIES - Provide responsive, friendly first-line desktop and hands-on IT support for local and global staff across macOS, Windows, and Linux workstations. - Manage local mobile phone subscriptions. - Set up, deploy, and physically maintain laptops, docking stations, monitors, printers, and meeting room AV equipment. - Support basic day-to-day hardware maintenance, workstation setups, and physical network patching on the manufacturing floor. - Assist with user onboarding and offboarding by imaging laptops and provisioning standard accounts using checklists. - Triage, resolve, and document local support tickets within Jira Service Management, escalating complex network or infrastructure issues to senior specialists. - Maintain accurate local hardware inventory, track asset movement, and help keep site IT documentation up to date.       WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR Must haves: - 2+ years of practical IT support, desktop support, or service desk experience (or a completed vocational qualification / degree in IT or a related field). - Basic understanding of core networking concepts (TCP/IP, DHCP, DNS, Wi-Fi connectivity, and physical cable patching). - Working familiarity with cloud productivity suites such as Google Workspace or Microsoft 365. - Clear verbal and written English and German communication skills with a customer-focused, helpful attitude.   Nice to haves: - Initial exposure to ticketing platforms like Jira Service Management. - Basic understanding of identity and access concepts (such as Okta or Active Directory). -
