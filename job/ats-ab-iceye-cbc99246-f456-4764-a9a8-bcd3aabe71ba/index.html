SUPO, where required) WHY THIS ROLE MATTERS:   ICEYE now employs over 1,000 people and is valued at more than €10 billion, following a Series F funding round in June 2026. We’re hiring an External Communications Specialist to expand our media relations and external communications impact as we continue to scale. You will cover corporate news, business line announcements, and support country-level communications, working across the global business and directly with the Corporate Leadership Team (CLT). ICEYE moves fast. We need someone proactive who thrives operating at a fast pace without dropping quality: every release, Q&A, pitch and briefing is accurate, clear and on time. Rather than wait for a brief, you spot the next opportunity and act on it.   WHO WE ARE ICEYE is the world leader in sovereign intelligence from space. We deliver persistent monitoring capabilities to detect and respond to changes in any location on Earth. ICEYE owns the world's largest and most advanced SAR (synthetic aperture radar) satellite constellation. To our customers we provide intelligence with unmatched quality, latency and revisit times, in any weather, day or night. To governments who choose to operate their own constellation we provide this proven capability as a sovereign system. ICEYE-built constellations serve customers in defence and intelligence, environmental monitoring, insurance and emergency management. We enable fast decisions that contribute to a safer future. Founded and headquartered in Finland, ICEYE operates globally with over 1000 employees across Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. YOUR DAY-TO-DAY RESPONSIBILITIES - Plan and deliver external communications for corporate news, the business lines (Missions, Data and Solutions) and support country communications teams. - Run media relations day to day: pitch stories, handle inbound requests and build relationships with financial, space, defence, business, insurance and climate journalists. - Prepare and brief spokespeople, including members of the CLT. - Write and edit press releases, statements, articles, Q&As and briefing notes. - Manage ICEYE’s owned channels, including the newsroom and website. - Direct ICEYE’s global PR agencies: set the brief, review the output, hold the deadline. - Judge when ICEYE has something useful to add to breaking news, such as floods and wildfires, and respond quickly when it does. - Bring new ideas for stories, formats and channels. - Work closely with Marketing, Social, Internal Communications, Legal and Product. - Track coverage, report results with numbers and recommend next steps. - Help build the processes and tools our communications team needs as ICEYE scales.   WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR Must haves: - 5+ years in PR or communications, at an agency or in-house at an international company. - A record of national and international coverage. - Strong writing and editing skills, with clean, accurate copy produced at speed. - Rigorous attention to detail. Facts, figures and names are right every time. - Experience preparing and advising senior spokespeople. - Experience coordinating agencies and stakeholders across functions and countries. - Fluent English, written and spoken. Nice to haves: - Knowledge of Earth observation, SAR or the space industry. - Experience in defence, government, insurance or natural catastrophe communications. - Background in a technology or data company. - A degree in communications, journalism, PR or a related field. - Additional languages.
