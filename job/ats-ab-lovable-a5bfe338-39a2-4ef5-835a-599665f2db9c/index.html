TL;DR: We're looking for someone who has run a US GAAP close, sat across the table from Big 4 auditors, and wants to build a continuous close where AI does the routine work and people do the thinking. You'll own our month-end close and consolidation process across the US, Sweden and the UK, reporting to our Accounting Controller. You'll build a close designed for scale, with the structure and controls a much bigger company will need built in from the start rather than bolted on later. The role Most accounting teams take the close they have today and add AI to it. We'd rather design it from scratch, assuming agents and automation are available, and keep people for review, judgment and the hard calls. Over time we want the close to run continuously rather than as a scramble at month-end. That only works if the structure underneath is solid. Every automated reconciliation still needs an owner, a review and evidence an auditor can rely on. Every journal entry an agent prepares still needs the right controls around it. You'll be the person who holds both ideas at once: pushing hard on automation while making sure what we build would hold up under SOX. You'll report to our Accounting Controller, and work alongside a small team. This is a hands-on lead role. You'll own the close, do a lot of the work yourself and help build a legacy with no formal direct reports today. What you’ll do - Own our month-end close and consolidation process across our US, Swedish and UK entities under US GAAP. - Run the close calendar so it lands on time every month, and shorten it as automation takes over routine work. - Partner with the Controller on the move toward a continuous close. Build or direct automated reconciliations, flux analysis and journal entry workflows, and decide where human review actually adds value. - Design controls, review steps and evidence trails into those workflows from the start, so they're audit-ready without a separate clean-up exercise. - Support the group external audit for your areas: PBC requests, walkthroughs, reconciliations and support for audit adjustments. - Assist with the preparation and support of statutory audits in the UK and Sweden. - Keep close documentation and policies current and clear enough that a new team member or an auditor can follow them. - Work with Order-to-Cash, Financial Systems, Data and FP&A so the GL, subledgers and source systems tie out and stay that way. - Coach the accountants on the team and raise the bar on quality and ownership.
