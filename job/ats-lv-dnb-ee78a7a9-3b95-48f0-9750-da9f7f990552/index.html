We are looking for an experienced enterprise sales professional who understands both the strategic value of AI and the critical role that trusted data plays in delivering successful business outcomes. The successful candidate will help enterprise clients unlock value through Dun & Bradstreet's Sales & Marketing Solutions, Master Data, and AI-enabled data offerings, creating measurable business impact while accelerating D&B's growth in Finland.   The Role As a Senior Solution Sales Manager, AI & Trusted Data Solutions, you will work with some of Finland's largest enterprises, helping them improve customer acquisition, sales effectiveness, data governance, AI readiness, and business decision-making. You will act as a trusted advisor to senior business and technology leaders, translating complex AI and data concepts into tangible business value. Working closely with Relationship Managers, Client Success, Product teams, and strategic partners, you will identify opportunities, develop compelling value propositions, and execute growth strategies across existing and new accounts. This role requires a strong understanding of enterprise AI initiatives, master data challenges, revenue generation processes, and how trusted third-party data can improve AI outcomes and business performance.
