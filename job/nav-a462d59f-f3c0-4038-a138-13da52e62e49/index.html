Tractor and Farm Machinery Operator

Holsfjord Produkter DA is looking for an experienced and reliable tractor driver for the 2027 season. We are a vegetable farm in Sylling, Lier, approximately 40 km west of Oslo. We grow iceberg lettuce, Chinese cabbage, cauliflower, and kale. The job Your main tasks will be tractor and machinery operation, including soil preparation, planting, and cultivation. Other duties include: · Moving and handling irrigation equipment · Daily checks and basic maintenance of machinery · Transporting vegetables from the fields to cooling and storage facilities · Loading and unloading produce and equipment · Helping with planting, harvesting, and other physical farm work when needed What we are looking for · Experience operating tractors and agricultural machinery · A valid driving licence: category T, C, or BE · Ability to speak and understand English · Someone who works independently, takes care of equipment, and is willing to help with different tasks What we offer · Seasonal employment from April to September 2027 · NOK 190--215 per hour before tax, depending on experience and qualifications · Guaranteed 40 hours per week, with an expected average of around 45 hours depending on the season, weather, and workload · Employment conditions in accordance with Norwegian labour laws, including overtime compensation, holiday pay, pension, and employee insurance · Accommodation available on the farm with a private room; rental price to be confirmed · A friendly and supportive working environment · Opportunity to return for future seasons --- we hope to find someone who will work with us for many years How to apply Send a short introduction and a few details about your experience to holsfjordprodukterda&#64;gmail.com. Please include which tractors and implements you have used and when you are available to work. Please include a reference from a previous employer, if available. You are welcome to attach a CV. Application deadline: 1 December 2026 For questions, contact: John Rasmus Swang | Farm Manager holsfjordprodukterda&#64;gmail.com We look forward to hearing from you!
