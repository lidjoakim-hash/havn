Company Description Tidal was founded for artists by artists as the next innovative streaming platform to bring value back to the music industry. We empower artists with the products, resources, services, and content required to take control of their careers and connect more deeply with fans. Available in over 60+ countries, Tidal continues to help artists break down economic barriers to continue creating what's next in culture. Tidal is part of Block, Inc. (NYSE: SQ), a global technology company with a focus on financial services. The Role Tidal is a small, nimble design team building at the intersection of music, technology, and culture—shipping across uploads, streaming, AI-powered creative tools, and financial services that connect artists directly with their audiences in 60+ countries. We're looking for a Creative Director to help set the vision and quality bar across Tidal's product and brand ecosystem. This is a rare opportunity to be a foundational creative voice on a small team where your work will have outsized impact—shaping not just pixels but the future of how artists build careers. You'll drive complex initiatives from concept to ship, influence cross-functional strategy, and directly elevate the craft of everyone around you. This is an individual contributor role. You'll lead project teams and set the creative standard. You Will • Originate brand platforms and campaign-level ideas, and own them from concept through 360 activation • Reposition, reshape the brand and bring a tapped in culture and keep the platform coherent across brand and product surfaces • Hold the idea intact from first cut to final placement • Shape the brief alongside an embedded creative-strategy team • Design and when needed direct designers, writers, and production partners on the work • Work with marketing, product, and partnerships leadership to get campaigns made You Have • A portfolio of work you led as a creative director: brand platforms, repositionings, campaigns, activations • An art direction background and the visual judgment to lead the idea in any medium • A track record setting creative vision and quality standards for a brand, while staying hands-on with the work • Credibility with cross-functional leadership as a peer • A history of catching cultural shifts early and putting them in the work • Experience carrying brand thinking across multiple products or business lines • Experience working in agencies, in-house, or both How to Apply Please submit your application along with a link to your portfolio. If your portfolio is password-protected, include the password in your application.
