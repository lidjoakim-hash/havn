At Catawiki, every day brings the extraordinary! Whether it’s Daniel Ricciardo’s Formula 1 Car, a Woolly Mammoth’s Skeleton, Lady Gaga's Jumpsuit or Usain Bolt’s running shoe, we encounter exceptional objects every day. We’re a one-of-a-kind marketplace for buying and selling special objects. Each week, more than 100,000 unique items are auctioned, all carefully curated by our passionate in-house experts. Having sold over 25 million unique objects, our mission is to become the world’s most popular destination for special objects. As a growing, diverse and sustainable scale-up, we proudly live by three core values. If these values resonate with you, we’d love to explore how you can join us. • Taking ownership and driving impact • Being open to change and feedback • Being passionate about our mission and our customers. About the role As a Facilities Intern, you will be able to gain working experience in the complete field of Facility / Office management as major touchpoints for the employee experience. You will have an opportunity to support the team with promoting an engaging, safe and efficient workplace environment. You will help manage and monitor day to day building services and operations. In addition to this, you will have the opportunity to work closely with colleagues from other departments across the business by supporting event planning and onboarding of new joiners. This is a great role for learning about the different teams and functions of a corporate office and discovering where your interests lie. This role is ideal for someone still discovering the field of work they want to pursue. What you’ll do Office management hands-on responsibilities: • Supporting the office management team in all day-to-day issues to ensure the employees and visitors feel welcome and safe • Ordering office supplies and responsible for stock inventory • Preparing regional and international shipments with relevant courier service providers • Checking invoices for accuracy and accounting • Involved in the update of the internal office management manual • Help maintain office cleanliness and organization, including stock management & replenishment. Employee experience improvements: • Prepare, analyze, interpret, and report on office data such as attendance, attendance patterns, etc and recommend future data-driven decisions based on behavioural data and also the intended HR policies • Support the Learning and Development department including onboarding of employees to Catawiki • Review the existing ‘employee goodies’ stock and propose improvements, simplification of items, find the right providers for branded employee swag Community Events • Manage Catawiki events and liaise with a diverse range of suppliers to optimise the experience • Think outside of the ‘facilities’ box on how to engage with the remote employees (>50% of our workforce) Collaborate with internal and external stakeholders to identify opportunities and improvements of the workplace environment What you’ll bring • Available to start immediately • You are enrolled at an educational institution in the Netherlands for the entire duration of the internship, pursuing a degree in human resources, office management, hospitality, tourism or another related field • Motivated to gain experience and understanding about the basic functions of office management in a business environment • Ability to lift and move objects up to 15 kg (33 lbs) frequently. • Ability to be physically active for extended periods. • Excellent social communication and interpersonal skills • Familiarity with Google software such as Google Docs, Sheets, Gmail, etc. • Willingness to help and support others and a growth mindset • Hands on mentality, not reluctant to do simple tasks • Fluent in English, Dutch proficiency is a plus • In this position you will be required to be physically on site in the office for 4 days a week (note that the days might vary, depending on the team
