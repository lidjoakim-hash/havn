Field Assistant

Starship Technologies · Jyvaskyla, Finland, Finland

💰 ca. 27 000–33 000 EUR/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)HeltidDirekte fra arbeidsgiverFrist 20.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Starship Technologies is revolutionizing deliveries with autonomous robots. These robots are designed to deliver food, groceries, and packages across neighborhoods in minutes. Starship has now completed millions of autonomous deliveries to date, traveled millions of miles, and is currently doing more than 140k road crossings each day. Our contribution to society includes reducing congestion, and pollution, providing zero-emissions deliveries, increasing the quality of life for residents, empowering seniors and disabled people, and enabling affordable delivery for local businesses. We are now looking for a Field Assistant at our hub in Jyväskylä. You will join our global operations - Finland team and play an essential part in our mission to provide the world with affordable and environment-friendly deliveries. This is a hybrid role that will involve re-deploying robots to our partner’s locations across Finland as well as supporting operational and maintenance activities in the Jyväskylä hub. The role will also contain learning technical aspects of the robot and carrying out maintenance work. You will know everything there is to know about how our Starship robots operate and will be able to deploy/pick up Robots from our fleet! This is a full-time position on a 6-month contract, with shift work scheduled Monday to Saturday. Please note that you will be hired through a staffing agency that we have a partnership with. Although you will be formally employed by the agency, you will be fully integrated into our company culture, working closely with our team and collaborating with our employees on a daily basis. Your main responsibilities are: - Deploying Robots using a company van in the Jyväskylä region, and remote cities across Finland. - Supporting the maintenance activities in the Jyväskylä hub and upgrades on robots and infrastructure. - Supporting operational tasks and field rescues of robots. - Maintaining hub-based warehouse and regular inventories. - Cleaning the robots and supporting ad-hoc tasks at the hub. - Carrying out routine maintenance tasks and audits checklists at stores. What’s in it for you? • Opportunity to work on industry-leading self-driving delivery robot technology on a uniquely large scale, creating history and making things happen • You can immerse yourself in the world of technology and innovation • You will have a chance to work with people with a strong inner burning and motivation. We are self-aware and seek feedback and improvement. We don’t want to wait until we are told what needs to be done but take it as a matter of pride to figure out solutions for topics in various teams and areas • You’ll have endless opportunities to learn from our inspirational, talented team members across the globe What we are looking for: • A valid driving licence for manual transmission vehicles (mandatory requirement) • A passion for robots and innovative technologies and a willingness to learn about our operations • Good self-discipline & time management • Ability and willingness to travel • Fluency in English • Eligibility to work in Finland Beneficial skills and experience: • Previous work experience in logistics or operations-related roles • Previous work experience as a technician • Previous experience driving a van • Finnish language skills Want to learn more about our robots and our people? Get in touch and let’s have a chat!
