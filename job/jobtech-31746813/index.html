The selected candidate will be employed and hosted through their current ABB location or the nearest suitable ABB office About the Role As Global Nuclear Standards Manager, you will be a part of ABB’s Nuclear Competence Cluster, and play a key role in building the quality, standards and assurance framework required for ABB to scale as a global nuclear supplier. The role will act as a global point of expertise for nuclear quality and standards, helping ABB establish a consistent approach across businesses and markets while ensuring that local requirements are appropriately addressed. Own and continuously develop ABB’s global nuclear quality management system, standards framework and assurance model. Lead ABB’s roadmap toward ISO 19443 readiness and ensure alignment with applicable nuclear quality and regulatory requirements. Translate nuclear standards, regulations, industry and customer requirements into practical processes for engineering, procurement, manufacturing and project execution. Establish and maintain governance for nuclear audits, corrective actions, supplier qualification, management review and continuous improvement. Monitor evolving international and market-specific nuclear requirements and assess their impact on ABB’s operations and offering. Provide expert guidance to ABB businesses and local Quality teams on nuclear standards, compliance and quality requirements. Support customer audits, assessments and qualification activities including direct engagement with customers, representing ABB on nuclear quality and standards topics. Drive nuclear safety culture and best-practice sharing across ABB’s nuclear organization. Collaborate across Quality, Engineering, Supply Chain, Operations and business teams to ensure consistent implementation of nuclear requirements globally. You will be mainly accountable for: Influencing and consulting with senior management across ABB global organizational units/functions to innovatively shape the design and development of your functional area, to improve the organization’s overall performance. Driving resolution of customer cases to completion, following robust root cause analysis, and implementing effective preventive and corrective actions.
