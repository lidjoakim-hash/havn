Account Executive, DACH, Commercial (Existing Business) About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies, from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups, use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team The Commercial Grower team works with the most ambitious and fastest growing companies in the Commercial segment. Our Commercial segment includes businesses typically processing $20M–$50M in annual payment volume. Stripe’s payments and financial infrastructure unlocks new revenue streams and growth opportunities for these businesses. The Commercial Grower team supports our users to increase their domestic presence whilst expanding into new territories and unlocking new revenue streams. This is a role that can be based out of our Dublin or Berlin office or Germany remote. What you’ll do As an Account Executive (Existing Business), you will deepen and expand partnerships between Stripe and the most innovative and fastest-growing companies in EMEA by helping them to understand how Stripe’s online commerce infrastructure can make payments a competitive advantage for their businesses. Responsibilities • Directly manage and nurture a named account list and develop territory and account plans for winning and expanding business with the Commercial segment. • Own the renewal and retention strategy for your territory, proactively identifying and mitigating churn risk. • Build and maintain key relationships with C-level executives, while orchestrating the right executive touch points as well. • Work with company leaders from multiple functions (e.g., Engineering, Product, and Finance) to lead complex product workshops and financial analyses • Orchestrate cross-functional teams including Solutions Architects, Customer Success, and Product Specialists to deliver technical value and drive activation • Contribute to shaping our Commercial strategy and building repeatable processes and scaled engagement models. • Develop strategies for growing wallet share, driving multi-product adoption, and defending/expanding existing revenue for your named account list. • Lead and contribute to team projects to develop and refine our sales process • Engage with internal Product and Engineering teams to help drive product and DACH market strategy. Who you are You are a top class salesperson and relationship manager capable of engaging in business and technical conversations at multiple levels of the organization, including the CTO, COO, and CFO. You are used to building value in competitive situations and enjoy working on products that require deep product understanding, combined with technical knowledge. A positive attitude and tireless work ethic when encountering blockers to success, whether external or internal. You are naturally curious and analytical and enjoy digging into business models and helping customers quantify their investment decisions.
