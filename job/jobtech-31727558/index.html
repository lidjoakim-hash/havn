Global Office Manager HVDC Lead Engineers Plants

Hitachi Energy Sweden AB · Ludvika, Sweden

The HVDC Lead Engineers Plants team is looking for a manager to lead, support and develop the individuals and the team. The team is part of engineering at HVDC, with a leading technical role and overall solution responsibility for our plants, civil and interface design in projects and tenders on a global scale. Are you ready to join Hitachi Energy for a career with many possibilities, in a place where you can be yourself? As a manager, you will report to the Global manager and become a member of the management team. The team you will be responsible for consists of 15-20 people mainly based in Sweden but also in other countries. You will lead and ensure that the team members support the tenders and projects and feel engaged and follow a good culture. With great collaboration and diverse backgrounds, we are excited to welcome you to join us! The role requires frequent presence at our office, with some flexibility of hybrid working. The HVDC Lead Engineers Plants team is a group with technical leaders supporting our engineering teams and leading them and the projects forward. We have big interfaces with many different functions and stakeholders. We’re part of the design phases and supporting site and partners. I hope that you share the passion for people and our interest in technology and support the culture of transparency about the design solution and its impact on projects for a greener future. Are you ready to join us leading this great team? - Kamilla Sommarsjö, Global Head Lead Engineers Plants HVDC. How you’ll make an impact Building and developing the team and the individuals, as well as providing support and good leadership. Encourage collaboration with receiving organizations and global teams. Providing insight and managing priorities for the team while ensuring performance and quality goals are met during tender/project phases. Ensure lean design execution on exciting projects worldwide. Planning for project staffing and adjusting it according to business needs, as well as planning for both short-term and more strategic long-term changes in the team structure. Work with the management team to develop, improve, and implement strategic people management initiatives and ensure processes are in place to capture and share best practices and operational experiences. Your Background Experience as a people manager or other relevant leadership experience with personnel responsibility, such as team lead. Interested in technology and people with strong communication and decision-making skills. Be a genuine team player who enjoys collaboration with peers and partners. Previous experience working with similar team or experience within HVDC, FACTS, or Substations is beneficial. Hold at least a Bachelor of Science degree in a relevant technology field.
