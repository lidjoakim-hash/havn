Experienced carpenter for new-build and renovation projects

TN Bygg Järfälla AB · Järfälla, Sweden

TN Bygg Järfälla AB is a family-owned construction company working on new-build and renovation projects in the Stockholm area. To strengthen our team, we are now looking for two experienced carpenters. As a carpenter with us, you will work on a variety of projects, both independently and with colleagues in larger teams. You will work from drawings and be responsible for the quality of your work from start to finish. Duties The role includes: Framing and fit-out work, such as external infill walls, stud and plasterboard partitions, suspended ceilings and boxing-in Installing windows, balcony doors, and internal and external doors Complete installation of kitchens and bathroom units Adapting and fitting cabinets, wardrobes and built-in storage to drawings Fitting skirting boards, architraves and mouldings About you We are looking for a confident and experienced carpenter. You do not need to be an expert in every area, but you have a solid foundation and take care to deliver high-quality work. Requirements: At least five years of documented experience as a carpenter in new-build and/or renovation Able to read drawings and work from them independently Good physical fitness, as the work is physically demanding Good spoken and written English or Swedish Right to work in Sweden Desirable: Trade certificate (yrkesbevis) or equivalent Driving licence (category B) Certificates and training relevant to the tasks above We place great emphasis on attention to detail and a responsible approach to work. What we offer You will join a well-run family business with skilled colleagues. As part of our team, you will get: Varied projects in new-build and renovation, on well-organised sites Opportunities for professional development A workplace where good work is recognised and appreciated Permanent full-time employment, starting with a probationary period Pay based on experience, with terms under the Swedish collective agreement for the construction sector (Byggavtalet) How to apply Send your CV and a short cover letter to info@tnbyggjarfalla.se, with "Carpenter" and your name in the subject line. In your letter, briefly describe the types of projects you have worked on in recent years and which of the tasks above you have most experience in. Applications are reviewed on an ongoing basis, and the positions may be filled before the closing date. First interviews are held by video call. If you have any questions, you are welcome to contact us at info@tnbyggjarfalla.se.
