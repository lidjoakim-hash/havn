The word ‘Pleo’ actually means ‘more than you’d expect’, and living by that mantra has been the secret to our success over the last 10 years. Now, we’re at a pivotal moment in our journey; every move we make has a direct impact on our 40,000+ customers, our business, and our collective success. Step into the world of Customer Onboarding, a place where seamless onboarding experiences are crafted to bring delight to customers and businesses alike.
