About the role: We're looking for a Digital Onboarding Solutions Specialist to join our Digital Onboarding Solutions team at Pleo. In this role, you'll help customers who have got stuck while onboarding themselves onto Pleo, unblock them, and make sure they get to a successful first spend. You'll also help us find and fix the friction that causes these blockers in the first place. If you enjoy solving real problems for real people, and you care about making onboarding work better for everyone, then this is the opportunity for you! Who you'll be working with and reporting to You'll report to our Manager, Onboarding and work closely with the Digital Onboarding Solutions Manager. Our team is highly collaborative and dedicated to getting every new customer to a smooth, successful start on Pleo. You'll also have the chance to partner with teams across Product, Growth, Sales, Support and RevOps to remove friction for our customers. What you'll be doing As a Digital Onboarding Solutions Specialist, you will: - Monitor escalation channels, handle live chats during coverage hours, diagnose blockers, and agree next steps with customers. - Pick up Freshdesk tickets raised outside live chat hours, reply by email the next business morning, and offer a call when needed. - Schedule and run calls with customers who are blocked during self-onboarding and have escalated via chat or email. - Coordinate internally on specialist escalation consultations and take ownership of seeing each case through to resolution. - Record the stage, blocker and outcome for every account, and run 1–2 week progress checks to keep customers moving. - Work closely with the Digital Onboarding Solutions Manager to improve our processes by flagging inefficiencies and proposing fixes. - Run proactive outreach on stalled accounts before they churn or give up. What you bring You'll thrive in this role if you have: - Experience in customer support, customer success, onboarding or another role where you help people get unstuck quickly. - A clear, structured approach to diagnosing problems: you ask the right questions, find the real blocker, and agree a next step. - Confidence running calls with customers, including those who are frustrated or short on time. • Strong attention to detail and the discipline to record stage, blocker and outcome consistently. - A continuous improvement mindset, and the willingness to raise inefficiencies and help fix them with others. - Comfort working across different channels (live chat, email, calls) and managing your own queue. - Working hours are 9am–5pm.
