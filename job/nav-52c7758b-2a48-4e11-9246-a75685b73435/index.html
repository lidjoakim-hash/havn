December–April | Staff accommodation on site | Opportunity to continue in Lofoten Resort Team Member – Skibotn Sky Lodge As a Hotel Team Member at Skibotn Sky Lodge, you will help create a welcoming and comfortable stay for our guests. Hva vi ser etter Qualifications and Personal Qualities We are looking for a friendly, reliable and flexible team member who enjoys taking care of guests and working in a small team. • Experience in hospitality, restaurant service or housekeeping is an advantage, but training will be provided. • Good spoken English; Norwegian or another Scandinavian language is an advantage. • A positive attitude and a commitment to making guests feel welcome. • Good attention to detail, especially when cleaning and preparing the igloos. • The ability to work independently, take initiative and support colleagues. • A practical approach and willingness to handle varied tasks. • The ability to stay organised and provide good service during busy periods. • Willingness to work shifts, including mornings, evenings and weekends. Above all, we value your attitude, willingness to learn and pride in a job well done. Arbeidsoppgaver Hotel Team Member -- Skibotn Sky Lodge As a Hotel Team Member at Skibotn Sky Lodge, you will help create a welcoming and comfortable stay for our guests. This is a varied, hands-on role combining restaurant service, housekeeping and general resort duties. Your main responsibilities will include: • Setting up and serving breakfast and dinner. • Welcoming guests, answering questions and providing friendly, attentive service. • Clearing tables, washing dishes and keeping the restaurant clean and ready for service. • Cleaning and preparing the igloos, including changing bed linen, cleaning bathrooms and replenishing guest supplies. • Keeping common areas and staff work areas clean and tidy. • Assisting with laundry and other practical tasks throughout the resort. • Following hygiene and safety routines and reporting maintenance needs. • Supporting colleagues and helping wherever needed in the daily operation. We are looking for someone who is reliable, flexible and enjoys working as part of a small team. You take pride in your work, notice the details and understand that a warm welcome and a clean, comfortable igloo can make all the difference to our guests. Vi tilbyr We Offer • An exciting workplace in beautiful Skibotn • A varied working day with international guests and groups • Competitive salary based on experience and qualifications • A professional and friendly working environment • Flexible working hours according to operational needs • We can assist with staff accommodation on site A winter in Skibotn --- and perhaps a summer in Lofoten We arrange staff accommodation on site, where our seasonal team will live throughout the winter. For the right candidates, there may also be an opportunity to continue into the summer season at our sister hotel, Live Lofoten in Stamsund. A winter in Skibotn could be the beginning of a longer journey with us.
