Want to influence the bigger picture? At Saab, you can realise amazing innovations that keep people and societies safe. Together, we can truly make an impact - and your part matters. Your Role As Head of BU Procurement, you will serve as Procurement's senior strategic representative towards one of Saab Surveillance's Business Units. Acting as a trusted partner to Business Unit leadership and Product Owners, you will ensure Procurement plays an active role in business planning, governance, and decision-making while providing an independent supply chain and commercial perspective. You will translate business strategies, customer commitments, product roadmaps, and future needs into Procurement priorities and contribute to strategic discussions around industrial footprint, make-or-buy decisions, outsourcing, and long-term supply chain development. You will also ensure market developments, sourcing opportunities, risks, and commercial considerations are taken into account. A key part of the role is leading and developing a team of Procurement Product Leaders and driving effective collaboration across products, projects, and business opportunities. Working closely with Category Management, Strategic and Operative Purchasing, and other Procurement functions, you will ensure alignment, prioritization, and successful execution across the organization. This is a unique opportunity to strengthen Procurement's position as a strategic business partner and influence how supply chains, sourcing strategies, and Procurement capabilities contribute to future business success. As our Business Units go through a period of unprecedented growth, Procurement's ability to ensure our supply chain and suppliers can respond will be critical to meet future growth plans. As Head of Business Unit Procurement you will be instrumental in translating business needs into stable supply chains, working together with the Procurement teams and our Suppliers. You will join a collaborative and business-focused Procurement organization where your leadership will have a direct impact on both customer success and long-term growth. Key Responsibilities: Act as Procurement's senior strategic representative towards the assigned Business Unit. Translate business strategies, growth plans, customer commitments, and product requirements into Procurement priorities and action plans. Provide a long-term supply chain perspective to strengthen competitiveness, resilience, and delivery capability. Identify and communicate supply chain risks and opportunities while promoting initiatives that improve competitiveness, resilience, and sustainability. Lead, develop, and support a team of Procurement Product Leaders. As a member of Surveillance Procurement leadership team, contribute to the overall leadership and development of the Procurement function Foster a collaborative, high-performing culture and consistent ways of working across products, projects, and programs. Support strategic decision-making and ensure alignment between Procurement capabilities and future business requirements. Your Profile You are a strategic and commercially minded leader who enjoys working closely with senior stakeholders and influencing decisions that shape future business success. You understand complex business needs and can translate them into clear Procurement priorities while creating alignment across organizational boundaries. You build trust, challenge constructively, and balance strategic thinking with a strong understanding of operational realities. As a leader, you create direction, develop people, and establish the conditions for both team and business success. We believe you have: Significant experience within Procurement, Supply Chain Management, Category Management, Project Management, or a related field.
