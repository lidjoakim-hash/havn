Solutions Engineering, Staff: Key Responsibilities Use a customer-centric approach to work dynamically across prototyping hardware product lines and/or business units, developing comprehensive documentations that address end-to-end product needs. Work with international hardware R&D Teams to understand the product deeply with customer perspectives Partner with Account Executives (AEs) to engage with customers and industry partners, gather requirements, and propose effective solutions. Provide strategic design services to enable and accelerate customer adoption and improve Quality of Results (QOR). Drive, prototype, and design new methodologies and solutions across Synopsys hardware products. Analyze gathered data, assess technology viability, and present findings and recommendations to R&D. Provide insights and findings to R&D teams to support future product enhancements and feature development. Leverage technical knowledge of multiple Synopsys prototyping hardware products and tools to define and create differentiated solutions that meet customer requirements and specifications. Role requirements: Academic qualifications Bachelor’s degree in electrical/Electronic Engineering. Professional Experience Minimum of 5 years experience in hardware documentation, good understanding of how hardware functions and software flows that impact over all prototyping solutions Specific Skills Required Working expertise with Oxygen, FrameMaker, HAPS hardware, Hardware Assisted Verification solutions, Scripting, HTML/Web publishing, Perforce & Git repository management, good experience in Jira/Confluence management
