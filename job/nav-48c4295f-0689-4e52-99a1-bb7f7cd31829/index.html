Logistics Supervisor – Data Centre Projects

Fortica · Fyresdal, Norway

💰 ca. 450 000–530 000 kr/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia NAVFrist 4.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

The right material, in the right place, at the right time. Fortica builds and finishes data centres across Norway and the Nordics. As Logistics Supervisor you make sure materials, equipment and plant reach the site at the right time, in the right place and in good condition, safely and without holding up the work. This includes heavy and sensitive data centre equipment such as generators, transformers, switchgear, UPS units, chillers and server racks. What you will do • Run the delivery schedule and the booking system for delivery slots, in line with the 2–6 week look-ahead plan • Plan oversized deliveries (routes, permits and escorts) and track long-lead equipment (items that take months to deliver) • Manage laydown areas (storage zones on site), unloading zones, site roads and the site store • Receive deliveries: check quantity, condition and paperwork, and report any damage • Coordinate cranes, forklifts and telehandlers, and take part in lift planning and risk assessments • Coordinate daily with subcontractors, suppliers and hauliers • Make sure safety (HSE) rules are followed and logistics areas are kept clean and tidy • Send a weekly logistics report and check logistics costs before invoices are approved • Lead a small team of delivery coordinators, storekeepers, plant operators and banksmen (signallers who guide lifts and vehicles) Who you are • Hands-on experience with heavy lifting and site logistics on large construction projects • Experience leading a logistics or warehouse team • Structured, and good at planning ahead • Comfortable with Excel or similar planning tools • Good English, spoken and written • Driving licence class B • Lifting or forklift certificates (for example truckførerbevis) are a plus What makes you a good fit You plan ahead and keep control when there is a lot of time pressure. You stand firm on safety and stop unsafe lifts or deliveries. You communicate clearly and solve conflicts between subcontractors before they grow. What we offer • 3:1 rotation (3 weeks on, 1 week off) with flights, accommodation and meals covered • 4 % pension and health insurance • We help you get your HMS card (the Norwegian ID card for everyone working on a construction site) if you do not have one • Real responsibility on large, modern projects • A safe workplace with fair terms and a written employment contract • Part of CTS Group, one of Europe's leading data centre design and build groups You report to: Site Manager / Project Manager Location: Fyresdal, Norway
