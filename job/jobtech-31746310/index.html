About the Client Our client is a leading global home appliance company headquartered in Stockholm, Sweden. With over 100 years of history and operations in around 120 markets, the company has a strong focus on innovation, sustainability, and digital transformation. Overview We are seeking a highly autonomous, product-focused Senior Data Engineer to help accelerate the evolution of our modern, AI-ready data platform. This role is for engineers who leverage modern tooling, AI-assisted development, and strong engineering judgment to deliver outcomes quickly and independently. We are not looking for individuals who simply execute tickets. We are looking for engineers who can take ownership of ambiguous problems, challenge existing assumptions, and drive meaningful improvements across the platform. Our Technology Stack Candidates should be comfortable operating within a modern Azure-based data platform, including: Azure Data Lake Storage (ADLS) Azure Data Factory (ADF) Apache Airflow Databricks dbt Cloud Git-based development workflows CI/CD practices Data quality and observability tooling What We´re Looking For Product & Customer Focus Strong customer-centric mindset with the ability to understand business needs and translate them into scalable data products. Experience partnering with business stakeholders, product managers, analysts, and engineers. Ability to balance technical excellence with business outcomes. Comfort working in ambiguous environments and defining solutions rather than waiting for detailed requirements. Agentic Engineering Mindset We are specifically looking for engineers who embrace AI-enabled software development. Candidates should: Actively leverage modern AI development tools and coding assistants to increase personal productivity and accelerate delivery. Be comfortable adopting emerging engineering workflows and AI harnesses (e.g., OpenCode, Copilot, Claude Code, Cursor, or similar tools). Demonstrate a mindset focused on outcomes, iteration, and continuous improvement. Use AI as a force multiplier while maintaining strong engineering judgment and accountability. Technical Excellence Deep experience building and operating data platforms using Databricks, dbt Cloud, Airflow, ADF, and Azure Data Lake. Strong understanding of modern data architecture patterns. Experience designing scalable ELT pipelines and data products. Hands-on experience with data quality frameworks, testing strategies, observability, lineage, and governance. Understanding of what is required to build and operate an AI-ready data platform. What Success Looks Like The ideal candidate: Requires minimal direction and oversight. Can take a loosely defined problem and rapidly deliver incremental value. Moves from idea to implementation in hours or days, not weeks. Consistently improves systems, processes, and developer experience. Makes pragmatic, high-quality decisions when trade-offs are required. Takes end-to-end ownership from discovery through implementation and operationalization. Leaves every area of ownership better than they found it. We Are Not Looking For Engineers who primarily execute predefined tasks. Candidates who require detailed specifications before they can begin delivering value. Classified as InternalIndividuals whose experience centers solely on building pipelines without demonstrating ownership, product thinking, or business impact. Engineers who are hesitant to adopt AI-assisted development workflows. Ideal Candidate Summary We´re looking for a high-leverage engineer who combines: Strong Databricks and modern data stack experience. Product thinking and customer obsession. Agentic Engineering practices and AI-enabled delivery. End-to-end ownership. Excellent judgment and decision-making. Interested? If this sounds like the right opportunity for you, please get in touch and share your CV in English. We look forward to hearing from you!
