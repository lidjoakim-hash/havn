Company description: Founded in 1927, the Volvo Group is committed to driving prosperity and shaping the future landscape through sustainable transport, mobility, and infrastructure solutions.  Job description:   At Enterprise Cybersecurity, we are strengthening our Compliance & Audit function. To achieve this, we are establishing a common cybersecurity control framework that brings together regulatory, contractual, industry-standard and internal requirements into one clear, testable and measurable baseline. We are now looking for two experienced cybersecurity professionals to help define, build and operate this capability.   Your responsibilities will include: Establishing and maintaining a common cybersecurity control framework covering requirements from regulations, industry standards, customer commitments and internal policies, including frameworks such as NIS2, ISO/IEC 27001 and the Cyber Resilience Act. Managing the requirement lifecycle by analyzing new and updated requirements, normalizing them and determining how they should be reflected in the control framework. Designing and maintaining clear, testable controls, including control objectives, ownership, applicability criteria and testing requirements. Collaborating closely with the Standards & Procedures Office to ensure a clear separation between what must be achieved (controls) and how it is implemented (standards and procedures). Designing assurance methodologies for evaluating control effectiveness, including testing approaches, effectiveness ratings and evidence models. Creating a reporting structure that enables visibility into compliance status, control effectiveness, gaps and exposure across the organization. Driving the use of tooling and automation to support control management, requirement mapping, testing and evidence collection. Supporting audits, assessments and regulatory reviews by providing a consistent and reliable control framework. Who are you?   We believe you have At least 5 years of experience in cybersecurity governance, compliance, IT audit, risk management or assurance. Strong experience working with cybersecurity regulations and frameworks such as NIS2, Cyber Resilience Act, ISO/IEC 27001/27002, NIST CSF or similar. Experience harmonizing requirements from multiple sources into a single control framework or baseline. Experience defining, maintaining or assessing cybersecurity controls. Experience working with Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) platforms and supporting processes. Strong communication and stakeholder management skills. Fluent English, spoken and written. A Bachelor's or Master's degree in Information Security, Computer Science, Information Systems or a related field, or equivalent professional experience.   Valuable additional experience Certifications such as CISA, CISM, CISSP, CRISC or ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Auditor/Lead Implementer. Experience working in large, complex and globally distributed organizations. Experience supporting regulatory audits or compliance initiatives.   Ready for the next move?
