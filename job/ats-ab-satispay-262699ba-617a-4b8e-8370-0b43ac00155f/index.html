Data Analyst - Growth & Marketing About us Traditional banking has never really given you control over your financial life. Satispay exists to change that: to empower people through finance. That means making financial services easy and accessible for over 6.5M users. We started with payments, then benefits, investing, cards... and we’re not done. Not even close. We follow the same logic every time: find a real problem, solve it properly, then look for the next one. The destination? To build the most loved financial platform in the world. If you own outcomes rather than tasks, stay close to what users need, and refuse to wait for someone else to fix what's broken — you'll find people here who work exactly in the same way. And together, you'll build something worth being proud of. What you'll be doing As our Data Analyst, you'll be a key member of our Growth & Marketing (G&M) team, putting data and intelligence first to drive real business impact, from user acquisition and engagement to retention and monetisation. Here's what that looks like in practice: - Drive Decision-Making Through Data - Analyse complex datasets to identify patterns impacting the consumer funnel (Acquisition, Engagement, Retention, Monetisation). Partner with Marketing Managers and other stakeholders to deliver in-depth analysis across the full product lifecycle to maximise ROI. - Partner with Key Stakeholders - Act as a strategic partner for Marketing, Finance, Operations, and Product, bridging the gap between technical data and business goals and translating complex analysis into clear, executive-level recommendations. - Build High-Impact Visualisation Tools - Define key metrics and develop dashboards and reports that communicate analytical insights effectively, empowering stakeholder self-sufficiency. - Contribute to Data-Mesh - Actively work on the development of the federated data layer by designing robust data models and building pipelines that integrate into the company's data strategy, ensuring scalable and governed data ownership. - Promote Agentic Analytics - Contribute to the enrichment and adoption of AI-powered agentic workflows for data transformation and exploration, accelerating analytical capabilities and enabling self-service for key stakeholders. - Drive Testing Initiatives - Own the end-to-end testing pipeline, from opportunity identification to experiment design and recommendation delivery, operating effectively in a fast-paced environment.
