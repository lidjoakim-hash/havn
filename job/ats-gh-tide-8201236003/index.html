ABOUT TIDE At Tide, we help SMEs save time and money in the running of their businesses by not only offering business accounts and related banking services, but also a comprehensive set of highly usable and connected administrative solutions, from invoicing to accounting. Tide is transforming the small business banking market and now supports over 2 million members globally across the UK, Germany and France. Using advanced technology, all solutions are designed with SMEs in mind. With quick onboarding, low fees and innovative features, we thrive on making data driven decisions to serve our mission: to help SMEs save time and money so they can get back to doing what they love. Tide facts: • Tide is available for UK, German and French SMEs • Over 2 million members globally and rapidly growing • Over $300 million raised in funding • 3,000 Tideans globally • We have offices in Central London, with a member support and technology centre in Sofia, Bulgaria, technology centres in Serbia, Romania, Lithuania and Hyderabad and offices in Gurugram, New Delhi, Berlin, Paris and Luxembourg ABOUT THE TEAM: Tide is one app with many products for small businesses: business accounts, payments, credit, insurance, invoicing, accounting and more. Over 2 million members across the UK, India, Germany and France use it to run their businesses. Our Product Design team designs every part of that experience. Designers work inside cross-functional teams alongside Product Managers, Engineers, UX Writers and Researchers, and we encourage designers to move between teams so they keep learning and growing. We’re at an exciting point. Under our new design structure, we’re raising the bar on craft, making the whole app feel like one connected experience, and building a stronger design community. We’re also building a senior individual contributor track in design for the first time. This role is one of the first, so you’ll help define what design looks like at Tide. ABOUT THE ROLE: As a Senior Staff Product Designer, you’ll work on our most complex and high-visibility problems. You’ll report directly to the Head of Design and work across teams rather than sitting in one, moving to wherever the biggest design challenge is. Projects include continuing to evolve our AI solutions, helping shape the next phase of our app architecture and taking lead on new 0 to 1 initiatives. You will also help actively raise the quality bar across the entire design organization. We’re open to designers operating at Senior-Staff or Principal level. Titles vary across companies, so what matters most is the strength of your craft, the quality of your thinking, and the impact you’ve had. As Senior Staff Product Designer, you will: • Lead multiple projects end to end. Go from early discovery and concepts to prototyping, testing and handover to engineering, working hands-on in Figma. • Shape the experience strategy. Partner with Product and Engineering leaders to turn messy, ambiguous problems into a clear design vision, priorities and roadmap. • Connect the app. Work with design leads across Tide to create shared patterns and foundations, as Tide app continues to evolve across 55+ products. • Raise the craft bar. Set the example for quality, mentor other designers, and introduce better ways of working. • Pioneer AI. Shape how AI shows up in our product, and how we use AI in our own design process, from ideas to code. • Measure and iterate. Ship, learn from real member behaviour, and improve. You’d rather launch, learn and refine than wait for perfection.
