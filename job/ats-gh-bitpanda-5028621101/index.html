Founded in 2014 in Vienna, Austria by Eric Demuth, Paul Klanschek and Christian Trummer, we’re here to help people trust themselves enough to build their financial freedom — for now and the future. Our user-friendly, trade-everything platform empowers both first-time investors and seasoned experts to invest in the cryptocurrencies, crypto indices, stocks*, precious metals and commodities* they want — with any sized budget, 24/7. Our global team works across different cultures and time zones, bringing our products to more than 7 million customers, making us one of Europe’s safest and most secure platforms that powers modern investing. Headquartered in Austria but operating across Europe, our products are built by fast-moving, talented, “roll-up-your-sleeves-and-make-it-happen” kind of people. Your mission Markets is the engine behind everything our clients trade, across crypto and traditional assets. As a Markets Operations Associate, you will work directly with the Head of Markets and our Trading Operations & Asset Lifecycle teams. You will help keep the business running smoothly, measured and ready to scale. It’s an entry-level role with real responsibility from day one. You’ll see how a front-to-back trading business works, learn from experienced Markets professionals, and make a visible difference to how we operate and perform. What you’ll do • Keep trading running: monitor daily processes and help resolve issues before they reach clients. • Run the asset lifecycle: take crypto and securities listings and delistings from request to go-live. • Keep Markets on track: own the weekly and monthly reporting and coordinate across teams. • Remove bottlenecks: work with Trading, Product, Engineering and Compliance to unblock initiatives. • Make it scale: spot manual work worth automating and improve our processes, including with AI tools.
