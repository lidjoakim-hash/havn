At HelloFresh, the goal is to change the way people eat forever by delivering high-quality food across 18 countries and 3 continents. As a global food solutions group and the world's leading meal kit provider, the portfolio includes HelloFresh, Green Chef, EveryPlate, Chefs Plate, Factor_, and Youfoodz. Engaging with HelloFresh in Warsaw means collaborating with HelloTech—a global technology division of over 1,000 people powering an end-to-end food ecosystem. From digital personalization and forecasting to supply chain optimization and food waste reduction, technology drives every step of this direct-to-consumer model. About the Role: What's in the Box HelloTech operates in autonomous, cross-functional alliances, each holding accountability for a specific product domain end to end. In this role you will join an assigned squad, with the flexibility to transition between teams and projects as technical objectives and business priorities evolve. Several technical domains are actively engaging providers. Projects will be aligned based on individual strengths and expertise during the process. Key technical domains include: • Deliveries & Orders / Plans: Core backend systems turning a subscription into correct physical deliveries for millions of customers. • Active Journey: Maintaining customer lifecycle stability: features covering habits, subscription management, ordering, and graceful error recovery at scale. • Menu Personalization & Forecasting: Utilizing data and applied science workflows to tailor user interfaces and predict regional consumer demand. • Operations & Supply Chain (SCM): Procurement, inventory, labeling, and production systems solving physical-world challenges with software. Across all domains, the technical profile consists of highly available, event-driven distributed systems serving global architectures. This role does not include people management responsibilities. What You’ll Do: The Recipe At HelloFresh we are moving away from a model where software developers just execute tickets toward one where product engineers are trusted to own customer problems. Your specialization is your anchor, not your boundary. • Operate what you build. You instrument, monitor, and improve your systems in production. Shipping is the beginning of the learning cycle, not the end.
