Discover more about life at DeepL onLinkedIn https://www.linkedin.com/company/deepl/,Instagram https://www.instagram.com/deepl_official/, and our Blog https://www.deepl.com/en/blog.   MEET THE TEAM BEHIND THIS JOURNEY Are you ready to shape the financial architecture powering hyper-growth Language AI? We are seeking a Senior Revenue Accountant to step into our revenue finance team of approximately 3 people and take the wheel on our revenue recognition strategy and modern systems. In this individual contributor role, you will bring deep technical accounting mastery to a high-tempo Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) environment, tackling complex enterprise contracts and massive transaction data. You will work closely alongside Deal Desk (our sales contract structuring team), Sales Operations, Legal, Product, and the broader finance team. Together, we ensure seamless IFRS 15 compliance while continually reimagining and optimizing our global revenue engine.   YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES Revenue Recognition - Ensure accurate and timely recognition of revenue in full compliance with IFRS 15. - Review and analyze contracts and add-ons to determine appropriate revenue recognition treatment. - Handle complex revenue scenarios across our SaaS and high-volume data environment. Financial Reporting - Prepare and review monthly, quarterly, and annual closing adjustments and reconciliations, ensuring accuracy and completeness. - Assist in the preparation of financial statements and disclosures related to revenue. - Ensure accounts are closed in a timely manner with robust documentation. Data Analytics & Automation - Develop and maintain SQL queries to extract data from various infrastructure (e.g., Salesforce, Databricks) for monthly revenue closing and reporting. - Automate large-scale reconciliations between source systems and the general ledger, identifying and resolving discrepancies in real-time. Process Improvement - Identify opportunities to streamline efficiency and enhance revenue recognition processes within complex contracts. - Implement best practices to elevate overall financial efficiency and precision. - Drive full automation and optimization across revenue workflows. Compliance and Audit - Ensure strict compliance with all relevant accounting standards and regulatory requirements. - Coor
