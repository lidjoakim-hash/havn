About the Team & Role Rubrik’s Product Line Sales organisation drives focused growth across Rubrik’s specialised workloads, including our unstructured data protection and security portfolio. This team partners closely with core Account Executives, Sales Engineers and Customer Success to bring deep product and technical expertise into every unstructured data opportunity, from first conversation through technical validation to close. We’re hiring a NAS Cloud Direct Product Line Sales Specialist to own and accelerate Rubrik’s unstructured data protection business across the UK. This is a highly technical, quota-carrying overlay role: you’ll be the subject-matter expert who can go deep on NAS storage architectures, file-generating applications, backup and recovery mechanics, and the security behind protecting these fast-growing data sets, and use that fluency to win deals against competing point solutions. What You’ll Do • Own the NAS Cloud Direct number: partner with core Account Executives to identify, qualify and close unstructured data protection and security opportunities across net-new and existing accounts in the UK. • Lead with technical credibility: articulate Rubrik’s NAS Cloud Direct architecture, security posture, and the gaps in storage-native snapshot protection in a way that resonates with storage and infrastructure teams, security and compliance teams, and executive economic buyers. • Drive technical discovery and validation: run or co-lead discovery calls, demos and proof-of-concept engagements alongside Sales Engineers. • Win competitive deals: build and defend positioning against competing solutions and “do nothing”, translating technical differentiation into business value. • Build territory and account plans: develop a coverage strategy in partnership with core sales, SE and channel teams to maximise NAS Cloud Direct attach and expansion within the install base. • Co-sell with distributors, resellers and VARs to build repeatable, partner-led momentum across the region. • Champion and evangelise the product inside and outside Rubrik, training the field on positioning, objection handling and competitive differentiation so more teams can sell it with confidence. • Bring market and customer feedback to Product Management and Product Marketing, shaping what we build next and how we take it to market, and share what’s working with fellow Product Line Specialists. • Forecast with discipline: maintain accurate pipeline and forecast data in Salesforce and participate in regular business reviews. • Stay current: maintain deep knowledge of Rubrik’s NAS Cloud Direct roadmap, storage ecosystems and the competitive landscape. Experience You’ll Need • Storage sales experience: 3+ years of quota-carrying sales experience selling storage infrastructure (file preferred) or related data protection solutions, with a deep understanding of these technologies. • Genuine technical depth: comfortable discussing NAS architectures, SMB and NFS protocols, snapshots and replication, file-generating applications, and ransomware and compliance requirements for unstructured data without relying solely on a Sales Engineer. • Proven technical selling motion: a track record of running or co-leading technical discovery, sizing and proof-of-concept engagements for storage or data protection solutions, not just handing off to a technical resource. • Consultative approach: comfortable navigating complex, multi-stakeholder sales cycles across storage and infrastructure teams, security and executive buyers in mid-market and enterprise accounts. • Overlay/specialist experience: prior success working in a platform-selling model alongside core Account Executives, Sales Engineers and Customer Success. • Track record: consistent history of quota attainment and strong pipeline generation. • Language: professional fluency in English, written and spoken, to engage technical and executive buyers across the UK. • Education: bachelor’s deg
