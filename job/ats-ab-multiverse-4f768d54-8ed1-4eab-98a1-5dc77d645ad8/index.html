ABOUT THE ROLE We’re looking for a proactive, organised, and people-focused Workplace Manager to lead the day-to-day operations of our London HQ and help create an exceptional employee experience. This is a hands-on role that combines workplace operations, employee experience, office management, and continuous improvement. You’ll ensure the office runs smoothly, efficiently, and compliantly while helping to shape a workplace culture where people feel supported, connected, and able to do their best work. Working closely with the Workplace Operations Coordinator and cross-functional teams including People, IT, Finance, and Executive Assistants, you’ll oversee workplace systems, vendor management, office projects, events, health & safety, and operational processes. You’ll also play a key role in improving how we work by introducing smarter systems, automation, and AI-driven efficiencies across workplace operations. This role is ideal for someone who enjoys balancing operational excellence with hospitality and employee experience in a fast-paced, scaling environment.
