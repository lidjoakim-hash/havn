ABOUT THE ROLE We’re looking for a proactive, organised, and people-focused Workplace Manager to lead the day-to-day operations of our London HQ and help create an exceptional employee experience. This is a hands-on role that combines workplace operations, employee experience, office management, and continuous improvement. You’ll ensure the office runs smoothly, efficiently, and compliantly while helping to shape a workplace culture where people feel supported, connected, and able to do their best work. Working closely with the Workplace Operations Coordinator and cross-functional teams including People, IT, Finance, and Executive Assistants, you’ll oversee workplace systems, vendor management, office projects, events, health & safety, and operational processes. You’ll also play a key role in improving how we work by introducing smarter systems, automation, and AI-driven efficiencies across workplace operations. This role is ideal for someone who enjoys balancing operational excellence with hospitality and employee experience in a fast-paced, scaling environment. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES WORKPLACE OPERATIONS & OFFICE MANAGEMENT - Lead the day-to-day running of the London office, ensuring the workplace operates efficiently, professionally, and proactively at all times - Partner with the Workplace Operations Coordinator to oversee front-of-house operations, meeting rooms, catering, hospitality, office standards, and employee support - Manage and resolve operational issues quickly and effectively, ensuring strong ownership of ticketing workflows, response times, contractor responses. - Develop and continuously improve workplace systems, processes, documentation, and operational playbooks - Manage relationships with vendors, contractors and service providers, alongside oversight of budgets, invoices, and operational spend - Support with planning and execution of company events, including meetings, off-sites, socials, volunteering initiatives, and cultural programmes, ensuring a high-quality employee and stakeholder experience - Provide reception cover during absences, acting as a professional and welcoming first point of contact - Support upcoming office move activities including planning, communications, coordination, design, and execution - Manage key workplace platforms including desk booking, ticketing, access control, learning systems, digital signage. - Identify opportunities to improve efficiency and employee experience through smarter systems, AI tools, and workflow automation - Use data, reporting, and employee feedback to drive continuous improvement across workplace operations HEALTH, SAFETY & COMPLIANCE - Own day-to-day workplace health & safety processes and compliance requirements for the London office - Conduct workplace audits, DSE asse
