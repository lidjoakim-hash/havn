By combining frontier agentic AI, an enterprise-grade platform, and deep domain expertise, we’re reshaping how critical knowledge work gets done for decades to come. This is a rare chance to help build a generational company at a true inflection point. We have strong product-market fit and world-class investor support. At Harvey, the future of professional services is being written today — and we’re just getting started. ROLE OVERVIEW Harvey's law school program has reached 50+ US schools in year one. Now we are taking it global, and we need the right person to lead that charge expanding in the EMEA, and APAC markets. As International Law School Manager, EMEA you will own the full lifecycle of Harvey's academic partnerships across your markets — signing new schools, building faculty and dean relationships, running trainings, launching ambassador programs, and driving the curriculum and on-campus embeds that make Harvey helpful to each institution with whom we work. You will start by taking over account management of existing EMEA partner schools and then look to sign and expand our presence across the region. You report directly to the Head of Legal Partnerships, Law Schools (New York). WHAT YOU'LL DO - School Partnerships: Lead outreach, relationship development, and onboarding for EMEA law schools — from flagship institutions to smaller schools. - Market Expansion: Build the playbook for Harvey’s Academic presence in each new EMEA/International market together with Law School Lead; execute new international launches throughout the year. - Cross-functional: Partner with Harvey's GTM commercial team, legal engineering, and marketing on in-market events, firm-to-school pipeline, and co-activation opportunities. - Account Management: Serve as the primary point of contact for every school in your portfolio — owning relationships with faculty, library staff, student organizations, and administrators across each partner institution. - Training & Enablement: Run all product training, demos, workspace setup, and activation events for your portfolio — including semester-start all-hands pushes, large-group faculty workshops, and 1:1 curriculum advisory sessions. Draw on the global Content Specialist and CS Lead for materials and operational support. - Ambassador Programs: Launch and own the EMEA student ambassador and faculty champion networks, adapting the US/UK model for European legal education norms. - Curriculum Advisory: Advise faculty and curriculum committees on how to integrate Harvey into courses, clinics, and legal writing programs — doing the hands-on work that makes academic embeds real. WHAT YOU HAVE Ideal candidates for the Law School Manager, EMEA role at Harvey can demonstrate comfort and experience with the following: - Law degree preferred — deep understanding of how EMEA law schools are structured and how faculty, students, and administrators operate - Strong experience in legal practice, legal tech, legal innovation, or academic programs — experience in a client-facing or externally-facing role is a strong differentiator - Proven ability to manage a portfolio of relationships in a high-growth or start
