You will find more information about working at NTNU and the application process here.    Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v&#61;_KHQjc4ndas&t&#61;41sAbout the position The Trondheim Academy of Fine Art (KIT), Faculty of Architecture and Design, invites applications for a fixed-term, three-year PhD position in Artistic Research. The PhD will be part of the focus area Digital Environments within Artistic Research. This PhD position explores, through artistic and practice-based research, the role and societal impact of AI and other emerging technologies, including how digital technologies reshape the relationships between knowledge production, creative practice, accountability, and social and ecological transformation. The position contributes to artistic research, critical inquiry, and experimentation across AI, sensing, data analysis and visualisation, and investigative practices, addressing key societal challenges at the intersection of art, architecture, design, media, and technology. Embedded in KIT’s strategic focus on the role of art in addressing societal change, the position is connected to ongoing research initiatives, digital infrastructure, and externally funded projects. It will strengthen KIT’s research profile in digitalisation and societal transformation while supporting future national and international research collaborations and funding opportunities. As a PhD Candidate, you will work towards a doctorate while gaining valuable research experience and qualifications for a career in higher education, research, and related sectors. Your immediate leader will be the Head of Department. Duties of the position You will develop an independent artistic and practice-based research project within the overall scope of the position. The project should engage with at least one of the first two areas below and at least one of the last two, without being limited to them: 1. Investigative practices, including open-source, scientific, data-driven and participatory approaches. 2. Sensing, analysis, modelling, visualisation and communication of disparate data and digital environments, through approaches including critical spatial practice and data science. 3. Technology in the public interest, including accountability and community-centred approaches to emerging technologies. 4. The role of artistic research in the development and application of emerging technologies, including new forms of knowledge production and collaboration between artistic, scientific and technological research.  The successful candidate will contribute to and make use of the research infrastructure of the Artistic Research Sensing and Visualisation Lab (ARSV-Lab), which develops technological resources and knowledge in multi-perspective sensing and data visualisation. You will have opportunities to participate in relevant existing externally funded research projects and networks, including the ERC project Sensing Ecocide and the COST Action Artistic Intelligence, and to contribute to the wider research environment and research culture of the department. You will participate in the Norwegian Artistic Research School (NARS) as part of their obligatory training component, alongside other candidates in Norwegian PhD programmes in Artistic Research. - Complete doctoral education until obtaining a doctorate - Carry out research of good quality within the framework described above - Academic publications and popular science dissemination - Participate in international activities such as conferences and/or research stays at foreign educational institutions - Other career-promoting work Be prepared for changes to your work duties after employment. Required selection criteria • You must meet the requirements for admission to the Doctoral Programme for Artistic Research PHKUNST, see Section 6-1 of the PhD regulations for more information.  • You must have a relevant Master's degree in art, architecture, design or a closely related field, or equivalent qualifications. Your degree must include a major independent project (minimum 30 ECTS) equivalent to a master’s thesis. • Demonstrated experience or interest in artistic, practice-based or interdisciplinary approaches to digital technologies, and digital environments. • Demonstrated ability to pursue independent, mentored research and work collaboratively with interdisciplinary research team. • Track record in artistic research and/or relevant professional practice at an international level. • Strong command of both written and spoken English is a prerequisite.  The appointment is to be made in accordance with NTNUs guidelines for recruitment positions for general criteria for the position. Personal characteristics • Excellent communications and teamwork skills  • Work independently  • Work in a structured way, set goals and make plans to achieve them  • Present and discuss your research with other professionals  • Get involved and contribute constructively with feedback  Emphasis will be placed on personal qualities.
