The role For our growing North America IT function, we are looking for a Team Lead, IT Operations to own the technology experience across all US fulfillment centres. Spanning locations from Phoenix to Newark, this is the most operationally embedded senior IT leadership role in the North America portfolio; directly accountable for the infrastructure, endpoints, and field technology that keep HelloFresh's US fulfillment network running. You will translate global IT strategy into consistent, high-performance execution at every US site; driving standardization, being a business partner for key initiatives, and ensuring the endpoint and device estate that underpins daily fulfillment operations is resilient and well-managed. As the lead you will operate as the trusted senior IT interface for USA Operations leadership, be an active contributor to Global IT strategy, and a HelloFresh DNA ambassador across the Field IT team. Above all, we are looking for people who will make HelloFresh better. We believe there are many different ways of developing skills and we love diverse experiences! So even if you don’t “tick all the boxes” but think you’d thrive in this role, we would really like to learn more about you. What you’ll do • Own the Fulfillment Centre IT portfolio roadmap, setting direction across service delivery, tooling, and team capability across all US Fulfillment Centres. Make trade-off decisions quickly, communicate them clearly to operations leadership, and reduce ambiguity for your team. • Be present in the field. Regular travel across US sites is expected. You assess what is happening on the ground, make decisions in the room, and hold the standard. When a site is struggling, you go there. • Lead the Fulfillment Centre IT team dispersed across US locations, balancing performance management with the freedom to operate. Coach engineers into IT leaders. Recognize contributions and manage conflicts before they become problems. • Act as the senior IT interface for USA Operations leadership, building trusted relationships with site directors and operations VPs. Communicate proactively, push back when an ask is unreasonable, and deliver credible timelines. Operations culture in Fulfillment Centres is demanding and fast-paced, and you are practiced enough in that environment to hold your position without it becoming adversarial. • Lead major cross-functional projects and initiatives: You’ll be a key partner for standardization across all Fulfillment Centres. Including coordinating IT coverage and readiness during changeover windows, and technology refreshes, and rollouts in collaboration with the production technology and network infrastructure teams. • Own the full endpoint and device estate for field operations: MDM (Jamf/Intune), Zscaler deployment, and endpoint compliance across all locations. Use Jira Service Management data to hold the team accountable for SLA performance.
