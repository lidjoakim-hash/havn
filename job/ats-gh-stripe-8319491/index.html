Millions of companies, from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups, use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. About the team The UK Financial Services sales team works with some of the largest and most important financial institutions in the UK. We help our users modernise their infrastructure, create better digital experiences for their customers, launch new products, and develop new sources of revenue. As the banking expert within the UK Financial Services team, you will target net new enterprise business from a named account list comprising some of the UK’s largest Tier 1 banks. If you’re motivated, smart, persistent, and a great teammate, we want to hear from you! You will also help strengthen our understanding of the banking market and shape how Stripe positions its products and capabilities with major banks.
