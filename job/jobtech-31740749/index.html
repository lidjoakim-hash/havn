Bidding Assistant

Clevry Sweden AB · Stockholm, Sweden

About the company The company will be presented at the first interview with the responsible recruiter. About the role As a Mid-level Bidding Assistant, you will support the full bidding lifecycle. You will help interpret customer requirements, develop high-quality bid documents and coordinate resources across teams. Working closely with project teams and stakeholders, you will make sure every bid is competitive, compliant and delivered on time. Your responsibilities Bidding strategy and document development Support the project team in interpreting Requests for Proposal (RFPs) and identifying key customer requirements Help define and implement bidding strategies, contribute to solution development, and identify issues and drive them to resolution Develop bidding documents that reflect customer requirements, strategies, solutions and decisions Own the quality of the documents and make sure they are submitted on time Bidding process management Support the organisation of key control points throughout the bidding process, including quality control of bidding strategies and high-level solutions, document reviews and authorise-to-bid (ATB) decisions Make sure bidding processes and standards are followed consistently Coordinate project resources, develop bidding plans, and manage the members and tasks of the bidding team Post-bid follow-up Support and track post-bid clarification activities Archive digital bidding documentation in a structured and accurate way What we are looking for Must-haves ICT background: Solid knowledge of at least one of the following domains: wireless, core network, datacom, access network, IT or data storage. Experience from the telecom or ICT industry is important for this role. Good knowledge of CRM processes and a strong understanding of bidding processes and regulations Experience in project coordination or project management, including planning, time and task management, and coordinating people and resources Bachelor’s degree or higher, or equivalent experience Professional proficiency in English Merits Previous experience in bid or proposal management within telecom or ICT Fluency in Chinese Location: Solna, Stockholm Start: As soon as possible Scope: Full-time, 1-year consulting assignment with a strong possibility of extension
