Culinary Intern (f/m/x)

HelloFresh · Amsterdam, North Holland, Netherlands, Netherlands

💰 ca. 23 000–32 000 EUR/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)PraksisDirekte fra arbeidsgiverFrist 4.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

The role: What's in the Box We are looking for a Culinary Intern for our ambitious, fast-growing team in Amsterdam from March 2027 for 6 months. During your internship, you will be supporting the Recipe Team that is responsible for all recipes in the HelloFresh Box. You will be at the center of the dynamic team, connecting all product team members, including recipe developers, dietitians, nutritionists, menu planner and recipe editors. This is a unique opportunity to learn and gain experience in a growing business that is leading the way in the food industry. What you’ll do: The Recipe • Supporting the recipe developers in the kitchen, by preparing the cooking and tasting sessions and keeping the kitchen organized and clean. • You occasionally assist with (re)cooking recipes when needed. • You make sure the test kitchen is stocked with the needed groceries. • Assisting in ongoing projects, ranging from marketing initiatives to culinary inspiration sessions. • Carry out deep dives and research into recipe successes and potential improvements. • You support the team on operational tasks, like ensuring our recipes are 100% accurate. What you’ll bring: The Ingredients • You are currently enrolled in a relevant program. • You speak fluent English and Dutch is a plus. • Enthusiasm for cooking and recipe improvement and a strong motivation for HelloFresh are essential! • You’re proactive, ambitious, like to be creative and make things happen • You are full of ideas and can act as a sparring partner for the recipe developers Above all, we are looking for people who will make HelloFresh better. There are many different ways of developing skills, and we love diverse experiences! So even if you don’t “tick all the boxes” but think you’d thrive in this role, we would really like to learn more about you. Are you the missing ingredient? If this sounds like a tasty opportunity, we’d be excited to hear from you. We aim to review your profile and respond within 5 business days. This is a paid internship designed to offer hands-on experience in a fast-paced, international environment. As an intern, you will have structured guidance to support your learning and development. We aim to create an environment where you can build practical skills, gain exposure to how HelloFresh operates, and learn through doing. The internship is suitable for students or recent graduates whose educational requirements allow them to complete an internship during the proposed period. During the application process, we will ask required questions regarding internship eligibility and university requirements (such as duration, documentation, or approval criteria). Please ensure you are able to provide this information as part of your application. What we offer: The Toppings Compensation & Financial Wellbeing • Enjoy 20% off HelloFresh and 40% off Factor_ subscriptions. • Access the "Benefits at Work" platform for discounts on brands like Nike, Bol.com, and IKEA. • You'll receive a HelloFresh laptop. Health & Wellbeing • Get discounts on ClassPass and gym memberships via Bedrijfsfitness Nederland, • Free bootcamp classes Flexibility & Work-Life • Benefit from a flexible hybrid work setup with a €2.45 daily remote work allowance. • You’ll get 10 days of leave, based on 40 hours per week • Full reimbursement for 2nd-class public transport to the office, and €0.23 per km for car/bicycle travel. Career & Development • Access internal/external opportunities aligned with our Job Framework. Culture & Environment • Enjoy fresh homemade lunches in the office (€3.00/day), weekly vrij-mi-bo (Friday drinks), quarterly strategy sessions, and team events. Are you up for the challenge?
