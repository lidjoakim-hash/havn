Senior scientific project coordinator

NORCE Research AS · Bergen, Norway

Lønn ikke oppgittHeltidvia NAVFrist 25.12.2026

About the position The research group DARWIN (Data, AI, and Robotics) at the NORCE Technology and digital systems division is looking for a senior scientist in a permanent 100% position with the main role of initiating and coordinating large national and EU research proposals for the research group and scientifically coordinating them as well as designing novel concepts and solutions for AI and robot deployments. The group has strong technological professional expertise in data science, AI, computer vision, machine learning, robotics, mechatronics, cybernetics, computer engineering, visual sensing, visualization, advanced maintenance and smart instrumentation. The place of work and employment can be any NORCE location. Travelling is expected. Work tasks The main tasks of the position will be to drive new large national and EU research possibilities, initiate and coordinate scientific proposal writing and applications, carry out the scientific coordination and management of projects within AI and robotics. Additional tasks include carrying out technological research and development projects in collaboration with industrial partners and research organization in Norway and in Europe, as well as collaborative projects with other groups and divisions within NORCE. Required skills, expertise and experience • Scientific background: minimum master’s degree in mechanical electronics, computer science and/or mechatronics or a related scientific area, with extensive knowledge in robotics, AI and perception systems. • Documented experience of at least 10 years in national and EU research funding programs with a track record on initiating scientific large national and EU project applications, writing scientific national and EU project proposals and coordinating national and EU projects is particularly relevant to the position. • Industrial experience is an advantage • Being and having a certified project management with IPMA, PMP or similar • Proficiency in English. Knowledge of the Norwegian language is an advantage. • Good team working skills while being proactive. • Excellent communication and organizational skills. We offer • A good and professionally challenging working environment • Exciting interdisciplinary projects together with national and international partners • A nationwide network of researchers in NORCE • Exciting, varied and flexible workday • Interdisciplinary work in teams with experts in other fields • Great opportunities for professional and personal development in a new and expansive competence environment • A social and creative work environment where you have a large influence on your own workday • Good pension and insurance schemes, good welfare schemes and competitive wages How to apply Please upload your application by pressing the application button. Your application should be written in English and must include the following: • CV • A cover letter with a description of your professional qualifications and motivation to apply for the position • Names and contact information of two references • Copy of diplomas, transcripts and relevant certificates from your higher education Application deadline: 3 January 2027 Further information about the position can be obtained by contacting research leader Nabil Belbachir, tel. 401 08 137, or nabe&#64;norceresearch.no.
