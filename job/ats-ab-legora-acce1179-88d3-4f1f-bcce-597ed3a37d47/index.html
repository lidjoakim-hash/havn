The Role We're looking for a Senior Brand Designer focused on Product Marketing! You'll own the visual output that makes our product tangible and compelling, from key visuals and decks to product shoots and videos in the brand studio. You'll join the Brand team and work closely with the Product Marketing team, Copywriter, Web Designer and other Brand Designers, translating product stories into work that looks and feels unmistakably Legora. A Project Manager across web and product will work side-by-side with you to keep things moving. This is a hands-on execution and craft role. You're not just executing briefs, you bring a strong point of view on what will make product marketing stand out, and you take ownership of the work from concept to final output. The Responsibilities Key product visuals. Design the visual assets that carry our product marketing: campaign visuals, feature launches, social and web assets, with a consistently high bar for craft. Product shoots and videos. Own product-related photo and video shoots end-to-end, whether shot in the brand studio or on location in market. Work closely with the Project Manager and Product Marketing to plan and resource shoots. Decks and sales enablement. Own the design of decks and sales enablement materials used across product marketing and growth, turning dense content into visually clear, well-structured, on-brand output. Agency & partner network. Bring a strong point of view and network of photographers, videographers and other creative partners. Source external agencies and freelancers to elevate output where needed. Cross-functional collaboration. Work closely with Product Marketing, Copywriters, Web Designer and the Project Manager to ensure visual and written narratives land together and execution stays on track. Partner with Brand Designers to keep output consistent with the wider brand system. Creative quality bar. Push the craft and originality of product marketing output, without sacrificing speed. Know when work meets the bar, when it needs another round, and when good enough is genuinely enough to keep momentum. AI-enabled workflows. Use AI tools to speed up ideation, iteration and production, while keeping a sharp eye on quality and taste.
