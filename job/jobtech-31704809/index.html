We look forward to receiving your application!   Are you interested in the potential of citizens' assemblies and other democratic innovations to drive climate transformation? Come join us! Work assignments  The Environmental Change division (tema Miljöförändring) at the Department of Thematic Studies is looking to hire a two-year postdoctoral researcher specializing in democratic innovations. You will be part of the Formas-funded research project Democratic Innovations for Climate Transformation (2027-2029), which entails a systematic and interdisciplinary knowledge synthesis of how climate citizens’ assemblies and related democratic processes can contribute to system transformation. The project explores what the existing research literature tells us about the political impact of climate assemblies, how they might contribute to broader systemic transformations, and what this means for Swedish climate governance. As postdoc on the project, you will take a leading role in carrying out the systematic literature review across academic databases and grey literature, following established standards for searching, screening, and quality assessment. You will contribute centrally to the comparative synthesis of findings across studies, and to identifying evidence gaps in how deliberative processes connect to system-level transformation. You will also help design and facilitate stakeholder dialogues with Swedish municipalities and practitioners, which ensures that the resulting knowledge is robust and genuinely useful for policy and practice. You will work in close collaboration with the project’s principal investigator and a research team from LiU and Stockholm Resilience Centre. There is considerable room to help shape the project’s methodological choices and analytical framework, and to co-author academic outputs arising from the review and synthesis work. As postdoc, you will principally carry out research. A certain amount of teaching may be part of your duties, up to a maximum of 20% of working hours. Qualifications To be qualified to take employment as postdoc, you must have been awarded a doctoral degree or have a foreign degree that is deemed to be equivalent to a doctoral degree. This degree must have been awarded at the latest by the point at which LiU makes its decision to employ you. Your doctoral degree should preferably have been awarded no more than three years ago. If there are special reasons, a degree awarded more than three years ago may be accepted. Special reasons include sick leave, parental leave, trade union positions of trust, service in the defense, or other similar circumstances, as well as service or assignments relevant to the subject area. We are looking for someone with a doctoral degree in social sciences, humanities, or related fields, and a demonstrated familiarity with the research area of democratic innovations – for example, deliberative democracy, citizens’ assemblies, participatory governance, or environmental/climate governance. Experience carrying out systematic literature reviews and others forms of qualitative content analysis is considered a merit. So is prior experience with stakeholder involvement and co-production through workshops and similar formats. You are curious, rigorous, and comfortable moving between the fine-grained work of systematic screening and coding and the bigger-picture task of synthesizing what it all means. You enjoy working collaboratively, taking initiative, and are able to communicate complex research to non-academic audiences, such as policymakers and practitioners.
