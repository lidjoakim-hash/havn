About Anthropic Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems. About the role As a Marketing Events Manager, International at Anthropic, you will own the execution of events across our international markets that drive pipeline, deepen customer relationships, and build Anthropic's brand and share of mind in markets where we're growing fast. You'll deliver a portfolio ranging from global flagship programs adapted for local markets to brand activations, executive dinners, roundtables, customer summits, and third-party events. This role sits on the Marketing Events Team, reports to the Head of International Events, and partners closely with International Sales, regional Field Marketing, Communications, Policy, Product Marketing, and Creative. You'll bring hands-on operational excellence and a strong eye for brand storytelling, turning plans into experiences that resonate with diverse audiences, from developers to enterprise executives to policymakers. As part of a small team building Anthropic's international events program, you'll help create the systems, processes, and playbooks that let it scale across regions.
