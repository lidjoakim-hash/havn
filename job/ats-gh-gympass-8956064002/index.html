THE OPPORTUNITY We are hiring a Business Development Representative (BDR) to join our Revenue team in Vienna! This is a hybrid role, requiring a minimum of two days per week in our Vienna office. Wellhub is officially expanding into Austria and Switzerland, and you will join a high-impact team at the forefront of this new venture! As one of the first BDRs covering the region, you will play a key role in building our presence from the ground up—generating qualified pipeline, engaging HR leaders and key decision-makers across corporate accounts, and contributing to both the development of our local sales playbook and our go-to-market approach. This is a rare opportunity to take on real ownership from day one, introduce a global brand to new markets, and help shape our long-term commercial success in Austria and Switzerland. YOUR IMPACT Reporting to the Sales Dev Team Leader, you will: Reporting to the Business Development Manager for New Markets, you will: • Identify and map prospective corporate clients across Austria, engaging HR stakeholders, People leaders, and executive decision-makers from scratch. • Lead outbound activity across cold calling, tailored emailing, and LinkedIn to generate and qualify new opportunities. • Position Wellhub clearly for companies discovering corporate wellbeing solutions for the first time or looking to invest further in their current offer. • Share prospect insights, common objections, and local business nuances to shape our sales strategy. • Represent Wellhub at industry events and HR forums, building your network and our visibility in Austria. • Live the mission: inspire and empower others by genuinely caring for your own wellbeing and your colleagues.
