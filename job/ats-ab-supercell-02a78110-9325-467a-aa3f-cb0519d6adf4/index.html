Hammer Jam 2026 - Builder Swarm [https://media.ffycdn.net/eu/supercell/Lz4vdC5EMVmFoukCV6yo.png?width=2400] THE CLASH OF CLANS TECH & QUALITY TEAM KEEPS THE GAME FAST, STABLE AND EASY TO BUILD ON. WE FOCUS ON PERFORMANCE, PRODUCTIVITY, DAY-TO-DAY WORKFLOWS, TECHNICAL HEALTH, QA AND PRODUCTION QUALITY, AND TECH DEBT AND FUTURE READINESS. EVERY CELL IN THE GAME DEPENDS ON THIS WORK. WE'RE ADDING A TECHNICAL PRODUCER TO HELP THE TEAM DELIVER ON THESE GOALS AND MAKE COLLABORATION WORK, INSIDE THE TEAM AND WITH EVERY CELL IT SUPPORTS OR COLLABORATES WITHIN SUPERCELL. We're looking for a Technical Producer to help the Tech & Quality team run well and to be the main connection between the team and the rest of Clash of Clans or other teams in Supercell. You'll work closely with the head of tech & Quality, tech team leads, engineers, release manager and QA, understand what they're working on and why, and make sure the right people are talking to each other at the right time. That covers feature cells, live ops, art, design, analytics, and central Supercell teams. This role runs on influence, seeing the big picture and creating a vision, not authority. You'll empower the team with clarity, focus, and an inspiring big picture, giving engineers the autonomy to build effectively. The right person understands engineering well enough to earn the team's trust, is an organised operator by nature, and makes planning and coordination feel light rather than bureaucratic. A background in software development or QA is a strong plus - Experience shipping and running live games or live services: release cycles, client and server updates, and incident handling - An inspiring coordinator who can influence and get people moving across disciplines without positional authority - Clear communication, written and spoken, for both technical and non-technical audiences - Operational discipline: you can manage many moving parts and dependencies without losing track of any - A light approach to the process. You know when structure helps and when it gets in the way WOULD BE NICE IF YOU ALSO HAVE - Experience with mobile games, large live codebase
