Backend Developer

Dashy Studios AB · Stockholm, Sweden

Why Dashy? We believe in creating meaningful and memorable digital experiences that leave lasting impressions. As a consultant at Dashy, you will get the opportunity to work with some of the most inspiring digital entertainment companies in the world, within fields such as gaming, media streaming and other immersive experiences. We strive to work with clients that build products and services that they, and we, can be truly proud of. At Dashy our people are always in the center - together we build and grow the company with happiness as our guiding principle. We enable growth and personal development, with collaboration and knowledge-sharing as an integral part in everything we do. As a Backend Developer at Dashy: Dive deep into the world of digital entertainment, designing, developing and iterating on systems and features. Collaborate with a vibrant cross-functional team, including developers, designers, producers, product managers, and more. Support other colleagues in their growth and with their challenges. Help build and grow Dashy. We're looking for: 3-7 years of experience as a Backend Developer. Strong experience with backend services using languages such as Java/Spring, Golang, Python, C#/.NET, or JavaScript/TypeScript (NestJS, Express). Solid understanding of database systems, data modeling, and query optimization. Proficient in designing and implementing RESTful APIs and/or RPC APIs. Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud), including serverless architectures, IAM, and cloud databases. Familiarity with containerization (Docker, Kubernetes) and microservices architecture. Knowledge of common engineering practices such as CI/CD pipelines, version control (Git), and automated testing. Experience in AI-enhanced workflows (e.g. Claude, Co-Pilot, ChatGPT etc). Strong problem-solving skills and ability to work in a collaborative team environment. A degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or equivalent experience. Passion for continuous learning and mentoring others. Genuine interest in games, music, and interactive media. What we offer: Working with some of the most inspiring tech companies with a global footprint. Office in central Stockholm with hybrid setup. Structured mentorship program, tailored for each individual. Competitive salary, occupational pension and wellness allowance. Yearly company summits. Office perks to make your workday smoother. Strong group of passionate individuals, team activities and collaborative environment. Nurture your passion for technology and entertainment, and turn it into a fulfilling career with us at Dashy Studios!
