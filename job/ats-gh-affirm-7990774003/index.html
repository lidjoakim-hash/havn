At Affirm, we exist for the moments that matter—giving people a clear, predictable way to pay over time, with no hidden fees, no surprises, and no tradeoffs on what matters most. About the Product team The Product team listens to our customers’ needs and translates those insights into a clear product vision. The team works closely with design, engineering, and analytics to build scalable financial products that empower people to manage their money with confidence. We rely on empathy, curiosity, and business sense to prioritize features and improve experiences. Our role is to lead with clarity, support cross-functional execution, and champion the user in every decision. The role We’re looking for a Product Designer to join us in supporting our post-purchase experience and international expansion. Design serves a vital role at Affirm: from building narratives through data to creating products that help people make important financial decisions. As a designer, you are responsible for crafting experiences that build trust, confidence and deliver on our principles of honest, transparent financial products. We’re at a critical inflection point in Affirm’s journey, with millions of people engaging with our products and new opportunities to expand how they use Affirm every day. We’re also growing globally, reaching new customers in new geographies every day. This Product Designer will help shape our post-purchase experiences, as well as building the next new geographies that Affirm will expand into. You can demonstrate a breadth of design experience leveraging your ability to create delightful experiences that simplify our customers’ payment management experience through Affirm as well as contributing to our wider international expansion strategy. You’ll partner well with design leadership, design research, and content design. You’ll work end-to-end, from contributing towards Design and Product vision to delivering class leading design. You are able to work closely with Product and Engineering to tailor unique customer experiences for our merchants & consumers while ensuring cohesion to the core Affirm experience - championing our customers needs no matter where their journey starts. What You'll Do • Lead end-to-end design for key initiatives within our UK and international experiences, as well as our post purchase experiences globally, across App and Web.
