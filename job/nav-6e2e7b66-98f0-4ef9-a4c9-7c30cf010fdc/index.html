About the role Novanet is a specialised technology consultancy with deep expertise in .NET, architecture and advisory services. You will work with experienced colleagues and recognised Norwegian customers on complex technology challenges. What you will do • Develop modern backend and full-stack solutions with .NET, C# and React. • Advise customers on architecture, technology choices and delivery. • Work with Azure, DevOps pipelines and modern engineering practices. • Take responsibility in customer-facing teams and contribute to knowledge sharing. Who we are looking for • At least three to four years of professional development experience and strong .NET and C# skills. • Experience with Azure, DevOps and modern JavaScript frameworks. • Commercial understanding and confidence in customer dialogue. • Motivation to learn Norwegian over time. What we offer • An experienced .NET environment, employee ownership and dedicated development budgets. • Five weeks of vacation, 6% pension and strong insurance coverage. • Total compensation typically around 1–1.2 MNOK. • Hybrid working with normally one to two office days per week.
