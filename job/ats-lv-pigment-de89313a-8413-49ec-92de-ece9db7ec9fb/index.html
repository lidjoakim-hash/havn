If you're driven by innovation and ready to make an impact at scale, we’d love to hear from you. We’re looking for a Tech Talent Acquisition to join our People team at Pigment! You’ll be part of a close-knit team of 3 dedicated to Talent Acquisition and a manager, focusing on our R&D department (Engineering, Product) and IT & Security. Together, we aim to build an outstanding candidate journey and attract the brightest talents to help Pigment thrive. What you'll do • Headhunt top talent for our Engineering, Product, IT, and Security teams by understanding needs, sourcing proactively, and reaching out to great profiles. • Assess candidates through screening applications and the all hiring process until the onboarding for the roles you own. • Deliver an excellent candidate experience, making every interaction meaningful and aligned with our values. • Partner with hiring managers / Interviewers, coaching them on best practices and sharing feedback to make hiring smoother and sharper. • Own projects that shape our TA strategy: Candidate experience improvements (communication flows, feedback loops, transparency), Interview calibration and training with hiring managers, Employer branding initiatives to strengthen our visibility in the tech ecosystem, etc. • Track and share insights by monitoring talent acquisition metrics to celebrate successes and spot areas for improvement. Who you are • You already have solid experience in Talent Acquisition within a fast-growing environment, with exposure to Engineering hiring (from Junior to Staff engineer). • You bring an entrepreneurial mindset: you enjoy challenges, move fast, and aren’t afraid to get hands-on. • You have great communication skills: you connect easily with people, build trust with candidates and colleagues, and know how to pitch with impact. • You’re organized and structured, able to lead projects and make the most of productivity tools to juggle priorities. • You’re curious and eager to learn, thriving when given autonomy. • You’re pragmatic and problem-solving oriented, with an analytical eye to back decisions. • You’re fluent in French & English, both spoken and written, as you’ll be working with international candidates daily.
