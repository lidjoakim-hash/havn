Paid Social Media Lead - Marketing Activation

H & M Hennes & Mauritz GBC AB · Stockholm, Sweden

Job Description WHAT YOU’LL DO As Global Paid Social Media Lead, you’ll own and develop the Paid Social channel, driving best-in-class activation across platforms while enabling markets and teams to deliver strong performance and continuous improvement. This is a highly visible role where deep Paid Social expertise goes hand in hand with commercial thinking and the ability to influence at senior level. You’ll move comfortably between platform detail and the bigger business picture, setting direction for the channel and turning performance and insights into recommendations that move the business forward. You’ll combine strategic direction with hands-on expertise, ensuring consistent execution and innovation across our global Paid Social ecosystem. Your key responsibilities include: Own the global Paid Social setup, including channel strategy, activation principles and ways of working. Define, maintain and evolve best practices across formats and platforms such as Meta and TikTok. Ensure consistent application of Paid Social best practices across markets and agency partners. Define and prioritise Test & Learn initiatives, scaling proven approaches globally. Collaborate closely with platform partners to drive innovation and capability development. Align the Paid Social development agenda with cross-channel media initiatives. Work closely with internal teams such as Creative Production and Measurement & Analytics to enable effective Paid Social activation. Translate Paid Social performance and data into clear business implications and recommendations for senior stakeholders. Lead senior stakeholder conversations, building trust, creating buy-in and constructively challenging existing approaches when the data or business opportunity calls for it. Connect channel performance to broader business priorities, identifying where Paid Social can create the greatest impact. Share insights and learnings to drive continuous improvement across markets. WHO YOU’LL WORK WITH You’ll work in a highly collaborative, global environment, partnering with Paid Media Specialists, Creative Production, Measurement & Analytics teams, markets, agencies and external platform partners. As a trusted Paid Social expert, you’ll connect teams and perspectives across the organisation, helping create alignment around how the channel evolves and delivers impact across markets. WHO YOU ARE You’re a Paid Social expert who combines deep channel knowledge with strong commercial judgement and the presence to influence at senior level. You’re comfortable moving between the details of platform performance and the bigger business picture, and you know how to turn complex information into a clear point of view and recommendation. You’re someone who takes ownership, asks the right questions and isn’t afraid to challenge established ways of working when you see an opportunity to improve. You build credibility through expertise, but you also know that driving change in a global organisation requires curiosity, collaboration and the ability to bring people with you. You communicate with clarity and confidence, adapt your message to your audience and are comfortable leading conversations with senior stakeholders. Rather than simply presenting results, you help others understand what they mean for the business, what needs to happen next and why. We are looking for people with: 7-9 years’ experience in Paid Social, digital media or performance marketing roles. A Bachelor’s degree in Business, Marketing, Economics or a related field. Strong expertise in Paid Social platforms and advertising products, such as Meta and TikTok. A structured and analytical mindset, with experience leading Test & Learn initiatives. The ability to translate performance data into actionable improvements and best practices.
