AIT Technician

Isar Aerospace Sweden AB · Kiruna, Sweden

💰 ca. 390 000–470 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 6.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

As an AIT (Assembly, Integration & Test) Technician at our rocket engine test site in Kiruna, you will be responsible for the preparation, assembly, integration, checkout, and troubleshooting of rocket engines, test articles, and associated hardware. Working closely with engineers and test operators, you will ensure test articles are ready for safe and successful test execution while supporting commissioning and test campaigns. Your Role in Our Space Mission: Perform assembly, integration, disassembly, and inspections of rocket engines, test articles, and associated mechanical systems. Prepare and configure test articles for testing, including instrumentation installation, checkouts, and pre-test verification activities. Support commissioning, pressure testing, and hotfire campaigns through execution of operational procedures and test preparations. Troubleshoot and resolve issues related to test articles, engine hardware, and mechanical interfaces. Conduct maintenance, repairs, and modifications of test articles and associated support equipment. Assist in the installation, removal, and transportation of engines and test hardware within the test facility. Maintain accurate records of assembly, inspections, non-conformances, maintenance activities, and test preparations. Ensure compliance with operational procedures and safety requirements during all activities. Proactively identify opportunities to improve processes, tooling, and test article handling methods. Support facility maintenance and infrastructure activities when required by operational priorities. Qualification Checklist At least 3 years of experience in a hands-on mechanical, assembly, or integration role. Experience assembling and troubleshooting complex mechanical systems. Ability to read and interpret engineering drawings, procedures, and assembly instructions. Experience using precision measurement tools, hand tools, and workshop equipment. Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills. Safety-conscious mindset and ability to work in hazardous environments. Effective communication skills and ability to work within a multidisciplinary team. Fluent in English, both spoken and written. Bonus Skills Experience with rocket propulsion systems, aerospace hardware, or development testing. Experience with instrumentation installation and checkout activities. Familiarity with pressure systems, leak testing, and cryogenic operations. Crane, forklift, or rigging certifications. Relevant technical education or vocational training
