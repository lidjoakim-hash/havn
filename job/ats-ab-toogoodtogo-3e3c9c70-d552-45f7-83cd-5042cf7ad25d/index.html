The Mission & The Opportunity Over one third of all food produced globally goes to waste driving 10% of global greenhouse gas emissions. At Too Good To Go, we’ve built the world’s #1 marketplace for fighting food waste, saving over 500 million meals across 20 countries. Now, we are scaling Parcels: our fast-growing D2C e-commerce business that rescues surplus inventory directly from major FMCG manufacturers, curates and repacks it into high-value consumer boxes via our 3PL network, and delivers it straight to customers' doorsteps across Europe and beyond. Your Role: Reporting to the VP of Operations, Parcels and sitting on the Global Parcels Leadership Team, you will be the operational architect and commercial owner behind our international fulfilment engine. Leading a global team of 15+ across 7+ countries, you will focus on three core pillars: building and governing a world-class 3PL partner ecosystem, designing scalable warehouse and repack/kitting processes, and leading a high-performing international logistics organisation. If you’ve scaled a D2C subscription box, meal-kit, or high-velocity grocery e-commerce fulfilment operation through third-party partners and thrive on combining commercial 3PL rigor with hands-on warehouse process design, this is a rare chance to build at global scale with tangible environmental impact. In the role, you will: Build and scale a best-in-class fulfillment network (strategy + execution) - Own and optimise the full Parcels logistics value chain: inbound to warehouse, repack/kitting, inventory management, pick/pack, carrier handoff, last-mile delivery, and returns/claims. - Architect and execute a logistics and fulfillment strategy that supports cost efficiency, service quality, and international growth. - Operate comfortably in ambiguity: create structure fast, make trade-offs, and iterate as we scale. Own and manage the 3PL and carrier ecosystem - Evaluate, select, and onboard 3PLs, fulfillment partners, and data/visibility providers; negotiate commercial and legal terms and ensure strong governance. - Define and implement operational standards with partners: facility/site design, process flows, repack specifications, SOPs, WMS/data integration, ERP migrations and capacity planning. - Lead quarterly and annual business reviews; own partner performance against SLAs and drive corrective action when service or cost drifts. Drive performance through S&OP, planning, and inventory discipline - Be an active driver of the monthly S&OP cadence, connecting and challenging demand forecasts, supply planning, capacity, and operational constraints. - Partner closely with Commercial and Supply Planning to maintain healthy inventory: reduce obsolescence, shrink, and write-offs, and improve inventory turns. - Build dashboards and operating rhythms that create transparency and decision speed across markets. Lead teams and cross-functional execution in a matrix - Lead and develop a global logistics organisation; set clear priorities, empower local leaders, and raise the operational bar across markets. - Work shoulder-to-shoulder with Commercial, Marketing, Product, Finance, Projects/CI, and local Operations teams to execute against business goals. - Lead complex cross-functional initiatives (e.g., carrier transitions, new country launches, peak readiness, system implementation, process changes). What success looks like for this role: - A scalable, repeatable fulfillment model that behaves like a modern e-commerce operation: reliable delivery, strong unit economics, and operational control. - Strong partner performance and accountability: clear SLAs, measurable outcomes, and continuous improvement. - Define and implement service metrics (e.g., on-time delivery, refunds, delivery quality) while driving down cost-to-serve. - Inventory health and planning maturity: fewer write-offs, fewer surprises, faster decision cycles.
