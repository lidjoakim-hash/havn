Before the detail, here's the challenge you'd help us solve. We build the embodied intelligence that moves real vehicles safely, and the ecosystem a billion machines will run on in the future. Very few people in AI can say this. Every role here, whatever the team, plugs into that. Here’s what this particular role covers. 🛠️ About our Network & Infrastructure Teams Our team owns the network and site infrastructure that keeps Wayve connected across offices, garages, labs and test facilities in six countries. We’re a growing team of seven engineers across the UK, US, Japan and Israel, delivering a multi-year network upgrade and supporting new sites as Wayve expands. As Network & Infrastructure Engineering Manager, you’ll lead the team, partnering with a staff-level network engineer who sets architecture and standards, and with our Security, Cloud and Workplace teams.   🧠 Your day-to-day - Lead, hire and develop a distributed team, helping engineers grow into domain owners. - Own the roadmap, delivery priorities and vendor relationships for our network and site infrastructure. - Work with our staff-level network engineer to turn technical direction into reliable delivery. - Stay hands-on for roughly 20% of your week, reviewing designs, setting engineering standards and improving automation. - Take part in the network on-call rotation, own P1 escalations and drive corrective actions after incidents. - Partner with Security, Cloud and Workplace colleagues, with regular travel to our sites in Europe, the US and Japan.⁠   🧩 What you’ll be working on - Delivering an approved, multi-year network replacement across our sites, including hardware standards, vendor coordination and safe cutovers. - Building and operating local and wide area networks, wireless, firewalls, certificate-based 802.1X network access control, monitoring and out-of-band management. - Shaping new sites end to end: network connectivity, audio-visual systems, meeting rooms, desk technology and fit-out IT. - Improving connectivity between our sites and cloud platforms, in partnership with the team that owns those platforms. - Turning manual infrastructure work into repeatable automation and infrastructure as code. - Delivering network security controls and evidence that support customer requirements and security assessments.   🙌 You should apply if - You’ve led a network or infrastructure team across sites and time zones, including hiring, performance management and developing engineers. - You bring deep hands-on enterprise networking experience across local and wide area networks, wireless, firewalls and certificate-based 802.1X. - You’ve owned site infrastructure beyond the network, such as AV, meeting rooms, fit-outs or new-site builds. - You’re comfortable with infrastructure as code and network automation, and can set standards and review your team’s work. - You’ve owned major network incidents and delivered multi-site hardware programmes with vendors and resellers. - You’ve delivered network controls and evidence for TISAX, ISO 27001, SOC 2 or an equivalent security assessment. - You want a people-leadership role that still includes hands-on engineering, on-call responsibilities and regular travel—not a network-only or purely strategic position. Experience with Juniper SRX and Mist, Azure or AWS hybrid connectivity, RADIUS and certificate infrastructure, or automotive supplier-security assessments would be a plus.⁠   🌱 Not ticking every box? That’s totally okay! If you’re passionate about autonomy and keen to learn, we encourage you to apply even if you don’t meet every requirement.   More about Wayve: 🚀 Wayve is building the leading AI platform for autonomous driving. We are pioneering an end to end AI approach that enables vehicles to learn directly from real world experience, developing the ability to adapt, generalise and improve at scale.
