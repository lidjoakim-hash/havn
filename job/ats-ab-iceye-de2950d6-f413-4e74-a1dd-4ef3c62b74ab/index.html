ROLE HIGHLIGHTS - Senior Backend Software Engineer - Location: Espoo, Finland - Department: Engineering - Reports to: Engineering Manager - Employment type: Permanent, full time - Workplace model: Hybrid, 3 days a week in the Espoo office - Employment is subject to applicable security screening (incl. SUPO) WHY THIS ROLE MATTERS Picture an operating system for a satellite mission. A customer tasks a satellite, the system plans the pass, talks to the satellite through our ground stations, downlinks the data, processes it and delivers imagery. One system, and it keeps getting better with every update. We build that system, and we are hiring several senior backend engineers to make it faster, smarter and more reliable. The satellites behind it are real and multiplying: more than 80 in orbit, and we now build about one a week. Every new satellite puts more load on the software you will own. Governments can run the whole system on their own infrastructure, so what we ship has to be right and has to keep working without us watching. The roles sit across three areas: - Business Automation: how customers around the world task satellites and receive their data, through customer-facing APIs - Fleet Control and Satellite Communications: plans and optimises what the satellites do next, downlinks data fast and runs the ground station software. Constraint-heavy problems where software meets real hardware - Flight Dynamics: knows precisely where each satellite is, keeps it where it needs to be and helps it steer clear of other objects in space You get real ownership from day one, a direct line to the people who use what you build, and a culture where AI-assisted engineering is part of everyday work. You go through one interview process, and we match you with a team together, based on your background, what interests you most and where we have openings. WHO WE ARE ICEYE is the world leader in sovereign intelligence from space. We deliver persistent monitoring capabilities to detect and respond to changes in any location on Earth. ICEYE owns the world's largest and most advanced SAR (synthetic aperture radar) satellite constellation. ICEYE-built constellations serve customers in defence and intelligence, environmental monitoring, insurance and emergency management. We enable fast decisions that contribute to a safer future. Founded and headquartered in Finland, ICEYE operates globally with over 1000 employees across Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. YOUR DAY-TO-DAY RESPONSIBILITIES - Own production backend services end to end, from design to running reliably in production, and keep improving them - Make and explain design decisions on scale, consistency, concurrency and failure modes for services other teams depend on - Keep production healthy: find root causes that remove whole classes of failure, and build the monitoring and alerting that shows problems early - Ship fast and often, backed by automated testing and CI/CD - Work closely with the people who use what you build, such as operations teams and domain experts, to shape the problem, not just implement the spec - Mentor other engineers and help raise the engineering bar in your team - Use AI-assisted tools in your daily workflow and share what works with the team WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR MUST HAVES - Experience owning a production backend system end to end, with a clear picture of its scope (traffic, users or teams depending on it) - Strong reasoning about how systems fail and scale, backed by design trade-offs you made yourself - Product thinking: you have shaped a problem together with customers or the people who use what you build - Operational experience: incident handling, root cause analysis and observability you helped build - You have taken a hard problem to production fast, scoped it down to what mattered, and can tell us what you left out and why - Recent, hands
