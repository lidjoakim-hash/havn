Before the detail, here's the challenge you'd help us solve. We build the embodied intelligence that moves real vehicles safely, and the ecosystem a billion machines will run on in the future. Very few people in AI can say this. Every role here, whatever the team, plugs into that. Here’s what this particular role covers. 🛠️ ABOUT OUR PRODUCT FEATURE OWNER TEAM Wayve’s Product & Delivery team turns our AI Driver technology into valuable, scalable vehicle capabilities for global OEM partners. We work at the intersection of customer needs, product strategy and engineering delivery—defining what great looks like, aligning teams around a clear outcome and ensuring that Wayve’s technology can be brought to market safely and effectively. As part of this team, you’ll take end-to-end ownership of specific Parking functionalities. You’ll work closely with OEM customers, product and engineering teams to define feature scope, set performance targets and guide delivery from concept through to production. Your work will directly shape how Wayve’s technology performs for customers and drivers as we progress towards higher levels of autonomy. 🧠 Your day-to-day - Act as the accountable product owner for specific Parking features, ensuring they are delivered on time, to the agreed specification and in line with customer and business needs. - Partner closely with OEM customers to understand their requirements, represent their needs internally and align expectations without compromising Wayve’s product strategy. - Work with feature group owners and engineering teams to define feature scope, specifications, performance targets and delivery plans. - Partner with systems, hardware, perception, platform, safety and supplier teams to shape the Parking sensor and compute architecture. - Drive integration and issue resolution across the Parking sensor stack, including ultrasonic sensors (USS) and surround-view cameras (SVCs). - Engage throughout feature development, providing early feedback, participating in testing and supporting evaluation and validation activities. - Identify risks early, manage trade-offs and keep teams aligned as customer priorities, platform constraints and the technology evolve. 🧩 What you’ll be working on: - Owning the end-to-end lifecycle of Parking features, including Park Distance Control, Rear-AEB, Auto-Park Assist, Remote Park Assist and Memory Parking. - Defining and evolving feature specifications in partnership with OEMs, engineering and product teams. - Translating sensor, compute and platform constraints into clear feature scope, performance targets and delivery trade-offs. - Leading integration across the Parking sensor stack, including USS and SVCs, and resolving sensor-specific performance and integration issues. - Harmonising customer requirements across OEM partners to maximise reusable assets, scalability and efficient feature delivery. - Setting clear performance targets and ensuring features meet customer requirements, internal capabilities and agreed delivery specifications. - Supporting testing, evaluation and validation to ensure feature quality and readiness for production. - Maintaining and evolving the strategic feature roadmap as Wayve’s technology advances towards higher levels of autonomy. - Participating in agile planning and delivery rituals, helping teams manage dependencies, risks and priorities effectively. - Ensuring feature delivery aligns with relevant automotive quality, safety and compliance expectations. 🙌 You should apply if: - You have strong experience specifying and delivering Parking features from concept through to production, particularly Park Distance Control, Rear-AEB, Auto-Park Assist, Remote Park Assist or Memory Parking. -
