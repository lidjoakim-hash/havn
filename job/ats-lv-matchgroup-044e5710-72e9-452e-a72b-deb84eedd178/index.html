At Match Group, we're behind some of the world's most recognized dating brands, including Tinder, Hinge, Match, Meetic, OkCupid, Plenty of Fish, and others, helping millions of people create meaningful connections. Our brands operate across different markets, audiences, and growth stages, giving talent the chance to make an impact at both the brand and portfolio level. We combine the scale of a global organization with the pace and ownership of a consumer tech company. About the Role As a Senior Recruiter, you'll lead hiring across multiple brands, functions, and markets, partnering closely with senior leaders as a trusted talent advisor. You'll go beyond individual requisitions understanding business priorities, anticipating talent needs, and helping leaders make sharp hiring decisions. Success requires strong market knowledge, business acumen, sourcing expertise, and the agility to manage multiple brands and stakeholders while holding a high bar for talent. Based in Dublin, Ireland, with a hybrid schedule of three days per week in office. Recruiting Excellence & Partnership • Partner directly with hiring managers, and senior leaders across Match Group brands, shaping hiring strategies around position priorities and long-term talent needs. • Own end-to-end recruiting across multiple functions and brands, from intake through offer and close. • Continuously improve hiring processes for quality, consistency, and efficiency. • Drive interview calibration and structured, competency-based evaluation. • Apply AI-enabled recruiting tools thoughtfully to boost productivity and decision-making. • Help candidates understand both their target brand and the broader opportunity of joining Match Group. Market Intelligence & Talent Mapping • Build talent maps for critical functions and roles, giving stakeholders visibility into competitor organizations and talent pools. • Track emerging skill sets, compensation trends, and competitor hiring activity. • Build sourcing strategies across outreach, referrals, networks, events, and communities. • Deliver ongoing talent intelligence to inform hiring and workforce decisions. Offer Strategy & Stakeholder Management • Guide hiring managers through offer decisions, balancing candidate motivations, business priorities, and internal equity. • Develop competitive offer strategies informed by market benchmarks and candidate insight. • Collaborate closely with HRBPs to align hiring plans with broader talent and organizational strategies.
