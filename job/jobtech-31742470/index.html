Embedded Software Engineer - Radar team Do you want to work at the forefront of technology, where your creativity and ideas directly shape the next generation of intelligent surveillance? As an Embedded Software Engineer in our Radar team, you'll help develop world-leading radar and sensor fusion solutions that make a real difference. This is a permanent, full-time role based on-site at our headquarters in Lund, Sweden. Who is your future team? You'll join a small, supportive team of four dedicated software engineers. We value different perspectives, continuous learning and open collaboration. We work closely with colleagues in electronics, mechanics, software, signal processing and graphics to take innovative products from concept to market. Our recent work includes advanced radar analytics, precise speed measurement and intelligent algorithms that keep improving for our users. We have also developed new ways of combining radar with PTZ (pan-tilt-zoom) cameras, which enables powerful automated tracking. We build everything from standalone radar systems to integrated platforms that solve real-world surveillance challenges. What you'll do here as Embedded Software Engineer As an Embedded Software Engineer, you'll play an essential part in developing our new radar products. You'll work across the whole embedded Linux stack, so you'll see your contributions in everything from low-level drivers to the features our customers use every day. You will: Be part of our product development team, building the next generation of radar products Analyze, design and write software for features in our upcoming products Find and fix issues wherever they occur, from Linux kernel drivers to applications Who are we looking for? You're curious and interested in technology, and you enjoy tackling challenges together with your team. You take initiative and do well in a workplace built on openness, creativity, trust, commitment, playfulness and encouragement. As your closest manager, I will support your personal development, give you freedom and trust, and guide you in your career. - Peter Wahlberg, Engineering Manager We'd love to hear that you have: A couple of years of professional experience developing software in C++, or equivalent experience Experience with Linux Professional proficiency in Swedish and English, written and spoken Bonus points for: Experience with low-level programming on Linux Experience programming FPGAs What Axis has to offer At Axis, you'll have the freedom to explore different technical areas, grow your skills, and shape your own career path. We offer a flexible work environment, opportunities for professional development, and the chance to work with cutting-edge technology in a global company.
