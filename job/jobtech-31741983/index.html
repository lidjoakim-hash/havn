Postdoc in materials informatics and materials digital infrastructure

Linköpings Universitet · Linköping, Sweden

💰 ca. 480 000–580 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 8.12.2026

Work assignments  The primary focus of the position is on developing methods and software for making materials data accessible, interoperable, and reusable, as well as for managing, processing, and analyzing large-scale materials datasets. The research aims to advance data-driven materials science by improving the standardization, exchange, and utilization of research data and computational tools.   The work may include the development of methods for data management and interoperability, standardized data representations, integration of databases and computational workflows, and the application of AI/machine learning methods to materials data. The research may also involve the development and application of computational physics methods, high-throughput simulations, and data-driven approaches to understanding and predicting materials properties.   The position is connected to the EU project MaterialsCommons, which aims to establish a federated European digital infrastructure and a common digital market for materials data and services, connecting databases, computational tools, and other digital resources across institutions and national boundaries. As postdoc, you will principally carry out research. A certain amount of teaching may be part of your duties, up to a maximum of 20% of working hours. Qualifications To be qualified to take employment as postdoc, you must have been awarded a doctoral degree or have a foreign degree that is deemed to be equivalent to a doctoral degree.  This degree must have been awarded at the latest by the point at which LiU makes its decision to employ you. It is considered advantageous if your doctoral degree is no older than three years at application deadline for this job. If there are special reasons for having an older doctoral degree – such as taking statutory leave – then these may be taken into consideration. Your application must include documents that clearly show that you fulfill the following requirements for the position: A doctoral degree in physics, materials science, or equivalent. A course transcript with grades must be included in the application. A record of multiple research publications in theoretical and computational materials science, materials design, and/or materials informatics. Co-authored publications demonstrating experience with data-driven materials research. Extensive, documented programming experience, particularly in Python, demonstrated, for example, through co-authored publications describing the use of Python and links to open-source software to which the applicant has contributed. It is considered a strong merit for the position if your application shows (i) co-authored publications that involve work with materials databases and datasets; (ii) direct technical experience with maintaining materials databases and making research data accessible through them. It is considered a merit for the position if your application shows: (i) course transcripts demonstrating high grades in completed courses of high relevance to the work assignments; (ii) other documented experience demonstrating a high level of understanding and skill in the fields of thermodynamics, statistical mechanics, group theory, crystallography, and crystal symmetry; (iii) experience with AI/ML for materials science. You are expected to be creative, independent, and intellectually curious, and to have a high level of engagement and motivation to contribute to materials science and materials data infrastructure. The workplace The position is at the Materials Design and Informatics Unit within the Division of Theoretical Physics, Department of Physics, Chemistry and Biology (IFM), Linköping University. The unit conducts research in theoretical and computational physics, with a particular emphasis on computational materials science and materials informatics. Our research includes large-scale computational studies of materials, development of software and automated workflows for scientific computations
