Roku is changing how the world watches TV Roku is the #1 TV streaming platform in the U.S., Canada, and Mexico, and we've set our sights on powering every television in the world. Roku pioneered streaming to the TV. Our mission is to be the TV streaming platform that connects the entire TV ecosystem. We connect consumers to the content they love, enable content publishers to build and monetize large audiences, and provide advertisers unique capabilities to engage consumers. From your first day at Roku, you'll make a valuable - and valued - contribution. We're a fast-growing public company where no one is a bystander. We offer you the opportunity to delight millions of TV streamers around the world while gaining meaningful experience across a variety of disciplines. What is the role? Roku's Content Distribution team drives strategic growth by sourcing, negotiating, and closing distribution partnerships with leading streaming video providers and major TV networks. As Director, Content Distribution, UK, you will lead strategy and execution for Roku's content distribution business in the UK. You will directly own high-value, complex negotiations with local public service broadcasters (PSBs), operators, and digital distributors, and guide the long-term roadmap for partner acquisition, renewals, and revenue expansion in the market. This role requires a senior leader with a proven track record of closing large, strategic content deals, deep expertise in streaming and subscription business models, and established C-level relationships across the UK media industry. You will shape how Roku is perceived by the local media community and represent Roku in the UK and EU. How will I use AI at Roku? At Roku, we don’t just use AI, we work with it. AI agents and smart tools help power drafts, analysis, and repetitive workflows, while our people bring direction, judgment, and accountability. We value curious, adaptable builders, who can show how they’ve used AI, agents, or automation to move faster, improve quality, and scale their impact. Strong candidates know how to frame problems, guide AI-assisted work, check the output, and learn quickly. Above all, they bring curiosity, adaptability, and sound judgment.
