Principal Product Manager

Wallapop is a Barcelona based scale-up driven by the purpose to empower people to embrace a more conscious and human way of consumption. We believe in a world where collaborative economy is mainstream. This is what drives us. 💫 Wallapop operates in Spain, Italy and Portugal, offering a catalogue of several hundreds of millions of products and services. Powered by technical innovation and continuous improvement, we bring together the scale & trust of classifieds with the marketplace’s convenience & reach. 🌱 Our mission is to enable a connected trade ecosystem, making 2nd-hand the norm through smart use of technology. The Challenge 🧩 Drive long-term user value and sustainable business growth by shaping and executing the product strategy for some of Wallapop’s most complex, high-impact initiatives. You will leverage data, experimentation, technical collaboration, and deep user empathy to unlock new marketplace opportunities, elevate product craft, and deliver step-function improvements in user experience. What You Will Do 👇 • Product Strategy & Vision: Define and execute a long-term product roadmap for high-impact marketplace initiatives, aligning product vision with company goals to drive meaningful user and business outcomes. • End-to-End Delivery & Collaboration: Lead discovery, definition, and delivery across cross-functional squads (engineering, design, analytics, and business operations) to turn complex challenges into simple, delightful user journeys. • Technical Collaboration: Partner with engineering leadership to ensure product architecture supports long-term scalability, advocating for foundational improvements that support platform stability and experimentation velocity. • Experimentation & Insights: Combine quantitative analytics, experimentation (A/B testing), and direct user research to validate hypotheses and make high-confidence roadmap decisions. • Stakeholder Alignment: Communicate strategic trade-offs, roadmap priorities, and vision clearly across executive leadership and cross-functional teams to build alignment and shared momentum. • Product Craft & Mentorship: Elevate product management standards across the chapter through active coaching, shared best practices, and collaborative knowledge-sharing. What We’re Looking For 🔎 • Proven track record of defining product strategy and delivering measurable business outcomes (e.g., conversion improvement, user retention, revenue growth, or marketplace liquidity). • Ability to collaborate closely with engineering teams on architecture, API integrations, and data flows, effectively balancing immediate delivery with long-term technical health. • Competence in utilizing quantitative data, self-serve analytics, and user research to build structured, evidence-based business cases and product roadmaps. • Ability to navigate ambiguity, prioritize high-leverage opportunities, and advocate for user-centric product decisions in complex organizational environments. • Full professional working proficiency in English, with strong verbal and written communication skills across technical and non-technical audiences. Spanish is a plus. What Would Be A Plus 🚀 • Experience integrating emerging technologies (such as LLMs or automation tools) into consumer products or internal workflows to improve user experience and team velocity. • Prior experience in two-sided marketplaces, e-commerce, or consumer tech platforms Do note that all our jobs are 📍 Barcelona based. We follow a hybrid model where flexibility rules. We commit to a minimum of 6 days per month in the office. Each team self-organizes to decide on cadence and in-person/remote rituals. Wallapop is an equal opportunity employer. We celebrate diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive environment for all employees as we want Wallapop to be a place for everyone. We sponsor visa processes for international candidates when applicable and provide legal & admin support along the process combine
