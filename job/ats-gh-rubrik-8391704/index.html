About Team & About Role Identity is now the front door for attackers, and governments, banks, hospitals and critical infrastructure providers rely on Rubrik to recover fast when systems like Active Directory and Entra ID are compromised. As Regional Director, Identity Sales Specialist, you’ll lead a team of six identity specialists across EMEA, turning a new category-defining product into a repeatable revenue motion in some of the most regulated markets in the world. You’ll report to the RVP, Identity and have real ownership over how the team is built, how it sells and how it grows.
