About Falkor We build industrial intelligence software for heavy-asset sectors. As a KONGSBERG company backed by over 200 years of heritage, Falkor helps the energy, chemicals, utilities and other industrial sectors operate with absolute clarity, confidence, and control. Our AI-enabled platform connects people, systems, and workflows—expanding human capability by turning complex data into real-time, actionable decisions. We help leading organizations improve safety, efficiency and performance while redefining how heavy assets are designed and operated. Help turn marketing into commercial momentum at Falkor Falkor is looking for an experienced and hands-on Demand Generation & Events Manager to join our Marketing team on a temporary basis. You’ll lead our Demand Generation & Events function, manage a team of two, and take ownership of our global demand generation campaigns and events program. Working closely with colleagues across Marketing, Sales/Growth and our regional teams, you’ll help ensure our marketing investments translate into measurable engagement, opportunities and pipeline. This is a role for someone who enjoys combining people leadership, strategy and hands-on execution. You’ll step into an established function with ongoing campaigns, events and plans, while having plenty of opportunity to bring your own experience and ideas. What you’ll do Lead the Demand Generation & Events team • Translate Falkor’s commercial and marketing priorities into a focused demand generation and events plan. • Prioritize resources and investment across campaigns, markets, and events based on expected impact. • Build effective ways of working across Marketing and with our commercial and regional teams. • Combine team leadership with hands-on involvement when needed. Drive demand generation • Plan, execute and optimize integrated demand generation campaigns across paid and organic channels. • Manage paid media activities across channels including LinkedIn and search. • Work closely with Product Marketing, content and commercial teams to develop campaigns for priority audiences, markets and offerings. • Track campaign performance and continuously optimize based on results. • Help strengthen the connection between marketing activity, engagement, opportunities, and pipeline. Lead our events program • Own and coordinate Falkor’s international events program, from prioritization and planning through execution and follow-up. • Work with regional commercial teams to identify and assess event opportunities against clear business objectives. • Manage Falkor’s participation across conferences, exhibitions, sponsorships, speaking opportunities, executive engagements and hosted events. • Coordinate content, speakers, promotion, event pages, partners, vendors and internal stakeholders. • Establish measurable objectives for events and ensure structured follow-up and reporting. • Manage event budgets and help determine where Falkor should invest. Who we’re looking for We believe you'll thrive in this role if you: • Have solid experience in B2B demand generation, campaign management, field marketing and/or events. • Have experience leading and developing a small team. • Understand how marketing activity connects to commercial outcomes and pipeline. • Are data-driven and use performance insights to make decisions and improve results. • Can manage agencies, partners, budgets, and multiple stakeholders effectively. • Are comfortable setting priorities and providing direction while staying close to execution. • Are highly organized and can move between strategic planning and hands-on delivery. • Communicate confidently in English and are comfortable working across teams, markets and seniority levels. • Experience from B2B technology, SaaS, industrial technology, or energy would be an advantage. Why join Falkor? You'll be joining a global technology company helping to redefine how complex industries operate. At Falkor, you'll have the opportunity to: • Work on meaningful challenges with real-world impact • Collaborate with talented colleagues across technology and industry domains • Learn from experts in software, engineering, operations, and digital transformation • Grow your career in a business that is expanding globally • Help shape technology that expands human capability in complex industries We believe diverse perspectives make us stronger and are committed to creating an environment where everyone feels respected, included, and able to do their best work.
