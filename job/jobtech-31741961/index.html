As part of the Volvo Group, we are committed to creating a safer, more sustainable, and highly productive transport industry—guided by our ambitions of being 100% Safe, 100% Fossil-Free, and 100% More Productive.  We are now seeking a Lead Engineer for our Autonomous Driving software stack that we call the Virtual Driver.   We are turning advanced autonomous-driving technology into an industrialized product and are looking for a deeply experienced autonomous-driving engineer to shape, build, and evolve the core software stack behind our autonomous vehicles.  This is a deeply hands-on role: write code, debug complex system behavior, prototype solutions, and review critical implementations alongside engineers across the autonomous-driving stack.  Your mission As Lead Engineer, you will be the engineer behind our Virtual Driver, the autonomous-driving capability at the heart of our transport solution.  You will tackle some of the toughest challenges in autonomous driving like understanding an ever-changing worksite, deciding how to act when the drivable surface itself changes or proving safe operation across countless scenarios. Your work will shape both the Virtual Driver of today and the technology generations that follow.  You will move between hands-on development and system-level thinking. Staying close to the technology gives you the insight to make the architectural decisions and technical trade-offs that shape our autonomous systems and how we develop them.  End-to-end ownership will be central to your role. At this level, we expect you to go beyond your own code and beyond the Virtual Driver itself, working across components and with experts throughout the autonomous system to drive the best overall solution for the customer.  Balancing the introduction of new technology with the robustness required for production will be a key focus. That means ensuring high-quality customer releases today, while looking ahead and setting the technical direction and targets for the future.  Teamwork will be vital to your success. You will connect People and Technical Leaders with Virtual Driver development teams, turn ambitious targets into executable engineering solutions, and drive complex technical challenges through to resolution.   Own the outcome, not the activity. For us, success is an autonomous system that performs to customer needs in the real world.  This is not a people-management role or a pure architect role.
