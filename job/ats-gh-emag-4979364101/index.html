DevOps Engineer (Data & AI, Data Operations Team) Join our Data Operations Team as an DevOps Engineer and play a key role in building a cutting-edge ML Platform on Google Cloud from the ground up—shaping the future of scalable, automated machine learning across the organization. What you’ll have to do: • Managing and optimizing ML workflows in Google Cloud Platform, Cloud Functions, and BigQuery ML; integrating CI/CD pipelines for model training and deployment; monitoring and automating model performance and retraining in Kubernetes-based environments; • Define and manage cloud infrastructure using Infrastructure as Code (IaC) tools such as Terraform; • Configuring and tuning a variety of services in order to increase system stability, performance and security; • Capacity planning and performance tuning for high traffic events such as Black Friday; • Adding observability and alerting to data pipelines and services; • Testing new technologies and industry trends and assessing their fitness for our needs; • Sharing technical expertise and helping data engineers and data scientists implement best practices that maximize performance and security; • Identifying repetitive issues or tasks and focus on automation and implementation of permanent solutions. What makes you a good fit: • Have very good knowledge of Google Cloud Platform, Linux environments, containers and container orchestration solutions (Kubernetes); • Have experience with at least one programming language (Python, Java, Ruby, PHP, etc), Shell scripting and regular expressions; • Have solid understanding of best practices around software development, release engineering and CI/CD; • Have good understanding of networking, operating systems, automation, distributed computing, high availability and load balancing concepts. What makes you stand out: • Prior experience in managing modern ML Platforms and Big Data highly-available systems; • HashiCorp tools - Vault, Consul, Terraform; • Experience with public cloud platforms - AWS, CGP, Azure, etc.; • Knowledge of monitoring tools (Prometheus, Grafana, StatsD, ELK stack, LGTM stack, etc); • Development experience in SQL, proficiency with relational databases and SQL queries; • Active member of the community.
