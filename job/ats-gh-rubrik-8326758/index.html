Rubrik is looking for a Senior Event Manager to lead our biggest customer events across EMEA. You’ll own the experience from first concept to post-event results, including our in-person flagship, Forward EMEA, our global virtual summits, and major tradeshows such as it-sa and Infosec. These events are how we meet customers, partners and prospects face to face, and you’ll be accountable for the pipeline they generate as well as how they feel. The role in brief You’ll sit within the EMEA Field Marketing team, leading the creative vision and delivery of our largest field events. You’ll work closely with sales leaders, our expert speakers, our London-based SDRs, and the global marketing and strategic events teams. The role carries real visibility and ownership, and a clear line from the events you run to revenue across a fast-growing region.
