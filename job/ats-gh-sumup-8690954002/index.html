Senior Marketing Manager - Consumer About the team SumUp is building a consumer financial product with a distribution advantage that almost no other fintech can replicate. More than four million merchants use SumUp, and more than a billion consumers come into contact with our products each year. We now have the opportunity to turn that reach into one of the world's largest consumer financial ecosystems. Our consumer product is already live across six European markets. The next challenge is to grow it from an emerging proposition into a product used by millions of consumers, and this role will lead that journey. This role sits at the heart of that growth. As Senior Marketing Manager (Merchant to Consumer), you'll drive the merchant-to-consumer acquisition engine: the channel that converts SumUp's merchant network and their customers into SumUp personal banking customers. This is a rare chance to build a differentiated acquisition channel from scratch inside a small, ambitious team, competing head-to-head with some of the best-funded neobanks in Europe. What you'll do • Plan and execute merchant-to-consumer acquisition campaigns across six markets — owning messaging, creative strategy, localisation, and performance reporting • Embed consumer sign-up moments into merchant touchpoints, including the super app, card readers, merchant CRM, and merchant website • Drive cross-sell from SumUp Local into the consumer banking app, managing conversion, activation, and incentives for consumers coming through merchant-led channels • Work closely with teams across Hardware, CRM, and other business lines to get consumer acquisition placements shipped through channels you don't directly own • Build and maintain reporting on merchant-to-consumer acquisition performance, using it to inform channel-mix decisions and prove cost-per-customer against direct advertising You'll be great for this role if… • Strong B2C growth or lifecycle marketing experience, ideally in fintech, payments, retail, or a marketplace • Proven track record of planning and executing campaigns across multiple markets, including localisation and creative direction • Experience marketing through partner or owned channels, such as CRM, in-product surfaces, or point-of-sale, rather than relying solely on paid media • Comfort owning acquisition metrics, attribution, and reporting end to end, with a data-driven and commercially minded approach • Ability to influence and coordinate across matrixed teams to get placements and campaigns shipped without direct authority Why you should join SumUp 🌎 Opportunity to work with a truly global, multicultural team from our central London Bridge location, wrapped in historic charm and modern flair.
