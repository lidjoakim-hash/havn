THE ROLE This is a commercial role with a revenue target. You’ll be measured on retention and expansion across your accounts. While you won’t be delivering the technical recommendations and implementation, the work is technical by nature. Our customers are engineers running GPU workloads, inference, and batch jobs in production, and you’ll be a credible partner in those conversations. The profile we’re hiring is a technical account manager. You’ve worked at companies that are deeply technical and consumption in nature. You bring the commercial instincts to grow revenue from an existing base and enough technical depth to know which conversation actually matters. And you can work credibly with everyone from a CxO to a Developer. What the role is - An early seat. You’re helping build the AM function with us, not joining one that already runs. The account plans, expansion motions, and customer cadence this team figures out are what we scale to every AM we hire after you - A technical customer-facing role. You’ll work directly with Developers and ML teams on what they’re building and how Modal fits. You translate and triage the deeper technical work to FDEs and Product when it’s needed - A book of business. You’ll own a set of accounts and be responsible for the revenue that comes from them. Expect to be meeting with customers in your first few days because the best playbooks are built from real experiences What the role is not - A traditional post-sales role. You’re measured on revenue, not health scores - An on-call support function. You’re not fixing bugs or triaging tickets. You’re growing accounts - A technical delivery role. You’re not implementing for the customer. You know where the technical work goes and you pull in the right people - A fully built motion. Much of this is still being built. If you spot gaps in process, tooling, or a playbook, you help build them - A single-lane job. Our priorities go customer → company → team → self.
