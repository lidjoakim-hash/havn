Staff Engineer – Identity and Access Management User Platform

Spotify · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 62 000–81 000 EUR/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)HeltidFjernarbeid · EuropeDirekte fra arbeidsgiverFrist 4.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

What You'll Do • Drive technical direction, guide architectural decisions, and lead complex engineering initiatives to evolve Spotify's identity and access management systems. • Design and evolve distributed systems that support authentication, authorization, and identity-related capabilities across Spotify's platform. • Partner with engineers, product managers, and security specialists to identify opportunities, align priorities, and deliver improvements to foundational platform capabilities. • Improve system reliability, performance, observability, and operational resilience, ensuring our identity services can support Spotify's continued growth. • Mentor and support engineers across User Platform, sharing knowledge, raising engineering standards, and creating an environment where others can grow. • Encourage thoughtful experimentation, continuous improvement, and collaboration while taking ownership of meaningful technical outcomes. Who You Are • You have extensive experience designing, building, and operating large-scale systems across different platforms. • You have a deep understanding of identity and access management concepts, including authentication, authorization, customer identity lifecycle management (e.g., account creation, user state, and account recovery), and secure access patterns. • You have experience influencing architecture and technical direction across multiple engineering teams, even when you don't have direct authority over their work. • You know how to evaluate complex technical trade-offs and make pragmatic decisions that balance security, reliability, performance, and developer experience. • You care about building resilient, observable, and maintainable systems and have experience improving services operating at significant scale. • You communicate complex technical ideas clearly, bring different perspectives into decisions, and help teams move forward together. • You enjoy mentoring engineers, sharing knowledge, and creating an inclusive environment where people can contribute, learn, and do their best work. Where You'll Be • We offer you the flexibility to work where you work best! For this role, you can be within the EMEA region as long as we have a work location (excluding France due to on-call restrictions). • This team operates within the Central European and GMT time zone for collaboration.
