At Swedish Match – An affiliate of PMI, we are championing a smoke-free world from the Nordics across the globe. Here, you get the best of both worlds: the entrepreneurial spirit of a Nordic company with more than 200 years of heritage, and the scale and career opportunities of a global leader. Join our 1,900 colleagues across the Nordics and help us deliver a smoke-free Nordics by 2035. Are you passionate about turning product and process knowledge into measurable improvements? We are looking for a Manager, Product & Process Improvement (PPI) to join the Product Management team in Sweden. As Manager PPI, you will lead the team and its portfolio of improvement initiatives, translating business and 4PM priorities into focused technical activities. In addition, you will lead and continuously develop the 4PM forum and its governance, ensuring clear priorities, effective cross-functional alignment, timely decisions and disciplined follow-up. You will work across functions to strengthen product performance, manufacturing processes, specifications and the robustness of our commercial product platforms. Your role Lead, develop and empower the PPI team. Lead and continuously develop the 4PM forum – cross functional forum for live platforms Manage priorities, resources, budget and delivery across the project portfolio. Turn business and 4PM priorities into clear technical plans and recommendations. Drive cross-functional alignment and effective technical risk management. Ensure robust scientific methods, appropriate test plans & effective technical risk that are applied. Keep the team’s knowledge of products, processes & technologies connected to business needs. Build deeper understanding of product, material and process interactions. Ensure work meets applicable quality, compliance and EHSS requirements. Who are we looking for? University degree in Engineering, Science or a relevant industry-related discipline; an MBA or PhD is an advantage. At least 5+ years of experience in Engineering or a Product or Process function. Strong leadership, communication, collaboration and project-management skills. Full professional proficiency in written and spoken English. Beyond the formal requirements, we are looking for someone who is comfortable working across organizational boundaries, can bring structure to complex technical problems and enjoys developing both people and technical capability This is an opportunity to lead a technical team working directly on the performance and continuous improvement of our commercial product platforms. If you enjoy combining scientific rigor, practical problem-solving and people leadership, this could be a great next step.
