BUILD WITH US Since 2004, we've had one clear goal: to make payments and money management effortless for every business in Europe. Today, more than 250,000 companies use Mollie to get paid, manage money and grow — with products designed to be simple, scalable, and dependable. With 1,000+ Mollies across 14+ locations, we care deeply about autonomy and craft. So we work in small teams, with real ownership, and we trust you to make the right decisions. We're building for the long term, so we provide the tools you need, processes you can rely on, and a balanced work environment to help you do work you're proud of. Sound like your kind of place? Let's build together. YOUR IMPACT We're looking for an experienced Finance Contractor to join our Accounting team for an initial 6–9 month period. You'll help keep the wheels turning on our day to day accounting, supporting the monthly and yearly close process, while also bringing a fresh pair of eyes to how we can do things better. This is a hands-on, broad remit. You'll pitch in wherever the team needs support, from general ledger and reconciliations to month end deliverables, and you'll have the chance to help us tighten up processes and controls along the way. You'll also get involved in team projects as they come up, working alongside the Financial Reporting team, FP&A and other finance colleagues. It's a great opportunity to get exposure to a fast growing, scaling fintech while making a real, visible impact in a short space of time. WHAT YOU'LL SHIP - Support accurate, on time monthly and yearly close, including journal entries, reconciliations and reporting deliverables - Help manage day to day business as usual accounting across the general ledger - Spot opportunities to improve and streamline existing processes, and help put those improvements into practice - Get stuck into team projects and ad hoc pieces of work as they arise, working closely with other members of the finance team - Help prepare schedules and support documentation for external auditors as needed - Work closely with colleagues across Accounting, FP&A and the wider business to keep information flowing smoothly WHAT YOU'LL BRING - A solid grounding in accounting or financial control, with 5+ years of relevant hands-on experience; a degree in accountancy and/or finance is preferred but not a hard requirement - Prior experience in financial services or fintech is a nice to have, though not essential - Comfortable working across the month end close cycle, including journals, reconciliations and reporting - A proactive mindset, with an eye for spotting process improvements and a willingness to get involved wherever the team needs a hand - Good working knowledge of IFRS and/or Dutch GAAP is a plus - Able to work independently, pick things up quickly, and hit the ground running in a fast paced environment - Strong communication skills, comfortable collaborating with colleagues across accounting, FP&A and other finance teams UNLOCK YOUR FULL POTENTIAL AND JOIN US TO ELIMINATE FINANCIAL BUREAUCRACY If you're excited by the idea of building what's next, for yourself and for thousands of businesses across Europe, we'd love to hear from you.
