Working at Mercell At Mercell, we're on a mission to revolutionize public procurement, making it accessible and effortless for everyone involved. Imagine a world where public buyers and visionary suppliers come together seamlessly to make a great deal easier and shape the future. That's what we're all about. With a dynamic culture built on continuous growth, trust, and collaboration, Mercell offers an environment where your talents can thrive. You will be part of an international environment with ambitious and dedicated colleagues who are passionate about what they do and supported to be themselves. Together, let's shape the future and create real value for society, one innovative solution at a time. Your Mission  As the Talent Acquisition Partner, you'll be the driving force behind attracting, assessing and nurturing top talent that fuels Mercell's success. Your mission is to leverage our recruitment strategy, ensuring that we continue building diverse, skilled, and dynamic teams across Mercell in all different functions and locations. This is an opportunity to make a lasting impact on Mercell's growth story and contribute to shaping the future of our organisation with your passion for talent. We see the following qualities as important to thrive in this role: • Proactive and agile with a creative mindset • Strong in stakeholder management including collaboration with senior leadership • Able to take ownership and multitask effectively • Self driven and structured Core Responsibilities   • Full lifecycle recruitment: Lead end-to-end recruitment processes, from identifying talent needs to sourcing, interviewing, and onboarding exceptional individuals. • Review resumes and credentials for appropriateness of skills, experience, and knowledge in relation to position requirements. • Source candidates by utilizing cutting-edge technology, social media and targeted networking. • Develop, regularly update and maintain the recruitment database. • Stakeholder collaboration: Work closely with hiring managers and leadership to understand business needs, ensuring a strategic and collaborative approach to talent acquisition. • Innovative recruitment techniques: Implement creative and effective recruitment methods, staying ahead of industry trends to attract top-tier talent. Required Qualifications   • Proven experience (ideally 3-5 years) in Talent Acquisition or in a similar recruitment role. • Demonstrated success in managing effectively multiple recruitment processes while ensuring the best candidate and hiring manager experience. • Exceptional communication and interpersonal skills. • In-depth knowledge of interviewing, recruitment best practices and industry trends in multiple sectors such as tech, finance, commercial, product. • Strong experience with LinkedIN as hiring tool. • Experience in sourcing various profiles. • Fluent in written and spoken English. Preferred Qualifications   • Experience in competency based interviewing (CBI). • Previous experience in tech or SaaS recruitment is a plus. • Familiarity with HR best practices, technologies and tools as well as AI tools such as Claude. • Employer branding experience. Start date: Approximately by January/February 2027 Duration: Full time (minimum 80%) - Temporary Maternity Cover (around 1 year) Workplace type: Hybrid - working onsite at the office min. 3 days and from home, balancing flexibility with collaboration Location: Oslo, Norway Application Deadline: 06 December 2026. However, do not wait to submit your application - we will read applications and talk to potential candidates as we receive them    What we offer  As a fast growing technology company, we are committed to taking care of our employees through initiatives such as hybrid work to support work-life balance, health and insurance plans (may differ per country/office), pension plans, paid parental leave, social happenings and competitive salary packages.
