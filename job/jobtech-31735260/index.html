E-commerce Specialist – Commercial Platforms

HMS Industrial Networks AB · Halmstad, Sweden

💰 ca. 350 000–420 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 14.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

HMS stands for Hardware Meets Software and we work to make the world more connected, productive, and sustainable. Our industrial communication products enable millions of machines, such as robots, drives, and air-conditioning systems, to connect with software and information systems – a necessity for meeting future demands for energy efficiency and sustainability. About the Role  Would you like to help shape how customers and partners experience HMS online? Join us as our E-commerce Specialist and take an active role in developing our Marketplace and webshops.  As our E-commerce Specialist, you will play an important role in managing and developing HMS’s digital sales channels, including our Marketplace, partner portals and B2C webshops. You will ensure smooth daily operations while identifying opportunities to improve our platforms and customer experience.  Working closely with colleagues across Sales, Marketing, Customer Service, Finance and IT, as well as external suppliers, you will: Manage daily operations: Support our Marketplace and webshops, handling matters related to products, pricing, availability, customer accounts, user access and orders.  Coordinate platform development: Manage support tickets, technical issues and change requests, gather requirements, test new functionality and coordinate improvements with IT teams and external suppliers. Support and improve processes: Work with connected systems such as CRM, ERP/F&O and PIM, support internal users and superusers, and use analytics and reporting to identify opportunities for improvement. You will also contribute to documentation, stakeholder communication and the continuous development of our commercial platforms. This is a temporary position covering parental leave for approximately one year.  Who You Are  You are analytical, service-minded and structured, with a strong sense of ownership and an eye for detail. You enjoy understanding complex processes, identifying solutions and solving problems. You are comfortable working independently while collaborating with different teams across the business. An interest in digital platforms and how technology can improve processes and support business needs is also important to you. We believe you have: A relevant academic degree, preferably in Business Administration, Economics or a related field. Other educational backgrounds may also be relevant. Experience working with order management, finance or e-commerce platforms. A good understanding of the business processes behind successful online sales. Strong analytical skills and the ability to understand and navigate complex systems and processes, particularly within e-commerce. Experience working across business and IT. The ability to gather business requirements and translate them into clear requests. Experience coordinating system issues, changes or improvements with technical teams or external suppliers. What We Offer  As an E-commerce Specialist at HMS Networks, you will have the opportunity to contribute and develop your skills in a fun and international environment. Make a real impact on the digital customer experience and how customers interact with HMS online. Work closely with colleagues across business and technology, with the opportunity to contribute ideas and influence the development of our e-commerce platforms. Keep learning and developing your expertise in a global technology company where digitalization is an important part of the business.  About us HMS Networks make the World more connected, productive and sustainable. Our industrial communication products enable millions of machines such as robots, drives and air-conditioners to get connected to software and systems - a necessity to meet the future demands for energy efficiency and sustainability. We are an innovative growth company based in Halmstad, Sweden with 1100 employees in more than 20 countries. HMS is an equal opportunity employer.
