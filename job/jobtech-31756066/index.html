Project Manager - Customer Implementations Ehra Talent Solutions · Kalmar · Consultant assignment, with openness to permanent employment Do you want to own customer implementations from kickoff to go-live, and be the person who brings customers, consultants and technology partners together? Ehra Talent Solutions is looking for an experienced Project Manager for our Talent business area. We are primarily looking for an independent consultant for an assignment, but we are also open to permanent employment for the right candidate. About us We are a SaaS company dedicated to the employee journey. We provide the tools and knowledge that help our customers attract, hire, and develop their talent. With 60 colleagues across five countries, Sweden, Norway, Latvia, the UK, and the Netherlands, we are united by a single mission: helping our clients create the world’s best workplaces. https://ehratalentsolutions.com/ The role You will lead implementations of our learning and talent management solutions for mid-sized and large organisations in the Nordic private and public sectors. Projects range from 200 to 1,000 hours, and you will typically run 2–5 in parallel. They include new implementations, migrations from other platforms, and expansions with new modules or business units. Your key objective is transparency and predictability, so that customers get what was agreed, on time and within budget. You will Support Sales in presales and scoping by clarifying requirements and estimating hours, resources and timeline, so that what is sold can be delivered Plan, lead and follow up projects covering workshops, configuration, integrations (HR systems, SSO), data migration, testing, training and go-live Lead a cross-functional team of solution consultants, integration specialists and partner resources, as well as the customer’s own project team Manage scope and change requests and keep time reporting and invoicing aligned with the contract Run steering groups and status reporting, and secure a structured handover to Customer Success Contribute to developing our delivery methodology Who you are Must have At least three years’ experience leading customer-facing IT or SaaS implementation projects Experience managing scope, hour-based budgets and change requests with external customers Fluent Swedish and confident English, written and spoken, as you will work with customers and partners in both languages A relevant degree or equivalent experience Strong advantage Experience from LMS, HR or talent management systems Understanding of integrations, SSO and data migration Project management certification such as PRINCE2, PMP, Scrum or SAFe Experience working with external partners in a distributed organisation You are structured and calm across several projects at once. You build trust with customers at both operational and management level, and you lead people without formal line authority. You are comfortable saying “no”, or “yes, as a change request”. What we offer You will have a central role in a growing Nordic company and a collaborative team with real influence on how we deliver. For employees, we offer benefits including pension, wellness allowance, health insurance, extra days off and development opportunities. Practical details Engagement - Consultant assignment or permanent employment Location - Kalmar or Stockholm Travel - Occasional customer visits Start - By agreement Reports to- Business Area Director Process - Includes candidate tests and background checks How to apply Send your application in Swedish or English. In your application, please tell us: Whether you prefer a consultant assignment or employment, or are open to both Your availability and earliest start date We will carry out selection and call for interviews on an ongoing basis, so please send your application as soon as possible.
