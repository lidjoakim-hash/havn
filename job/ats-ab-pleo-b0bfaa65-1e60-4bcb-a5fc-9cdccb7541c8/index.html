ABOUT THE ROLE We're looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to join the Data Serving team that owns the production-grade boundary between Pleo's raw product data and everyone who consumes it, including internal product teams, AI agents, and customers directly accessing their own data. This Senior Backend Engineer role builds and owns that boundary: APIs and the low-latency serving layer underneath it. You will build customer-facing infrastructure where contract stability and latency are non-negotiable, and where the clean abstraction you maintain is what lets every downstream consumer trust the data without ever touching a raw source. WHO YOU’LL BE WORKING WITH AND REPORTING TO You will report to our VP of Data, and work closely with our Staff Data Platform Engineer, Data Engineers, Product Managers and others Engineers across the business. You'll also coordinate closely with the GenAI Platform and Data Infrastructure teams. WHAT YOU’LL BE DOING As a Senior Backend Engineer, you will: - Build and own the Data API boundary: delivering clean, versioned, and performant endpoints for internal dashboards, customer-facing features, and third-party tools (e.g., Fivetran connectors via incremental syncs and cursor pagination). - Design and optimise the read models, indexes, and caching strategies in Postgres (and future OLAP stores) to guarantee low-latency query performance. - Build reliable integrations with Pleo’s Kotlin-based product backend services to ensure the serving layer surfaces the correct data required for a customer facing feature. - Own the reliability and performance of the services you build: monitoring, incident response, and SLOs appropriate to customer-facing infrastructure. - Partner with Data Engineers to define clean contracts between the ingestion/aggregation layer and the serving DB, ensuring read patterns match storage schemas. - Partner with the Authorization team to secure APIs using industry-standard authentication and authorization protocols. - Build a near-real-time data aggregation service to collect and unify data across Pleo services. - Design metadata, lineage, and governance capabilities that make the serving layer explainable, auditable, and trustworthy. - Own access control and permission management services, including row and column-level data access with full audit trails. To put things into context, you can expect to work with the following tech stack: AWS (product side), GCP (data side), Docker, Kubernetes, BigQuery, PostgreSQL dbt, Kafka, Airflow, Python, Kotlin and more!
