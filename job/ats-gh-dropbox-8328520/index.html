Role Description As a Senior Atlassian Cloud Engineer at Dropbox, you will design, build, and evolve the platforms that help our employees collaborate and get support. With a focus on Jira Service Management (JSM), you will own solutions that simplify service delivery, automate manual processes, and improve the employee experience. You will lead the design and implementation of service workflows, portals, integrations, asset management, and AI-powered support experiences. Your work will also strengthen the security, reliability, and governance of our broader Atlassian ecosystem, including Jira Cloud and Confluence. Partnering with Engineering, Corporate IT, Security, and other stakeholders, you will translate complex needs into scalable, maintainable solutions. You will take projects from discovery through delivery and ongoing operations, making technical decisions that balance immediate needs with long-term platform health. Our Engineering Career Framework is viewable by anyone outside the company and describes what’s expected for our engineers at each of our career levels. Check out our blog post on this topic and more here. Responsibilities • Own the design, implementation, and ongoing improvement of Jira Service Management capabilities that make employee support simpler, faster, and more effective. • Build scalable workflows, service portals, queues, service-level agreements (SLAs), and automation across incident, request, problem, and change management. • Develop secure, maintainable integrations between Atlassian Cloud and enterprise systems using APIs, webhooks, and scripting. • Improve employee self-service through Confluence knowledge bases, Rovo, and AI-powered support experiences. • Develop JSM Assets and configuration management capabilities that improve data quality, asset visibility, and service delivery. • Strengthen governance, access controls, security, and operational reliability across Jira Cloud, JSM, and Confluence while reducing configuration complexity and technical debt. • Partner with stakeholders across teams to define requirements, evaluate technical tradeoffs, and deliver projects from discovery through rollout and ongoing support. • Create reusable configuration patterns, documentation, and runbooks, and use operational metrics and employee feedback to guide improvements. On-call work may be necessary occasionally to help address bugs, outages, or other operational issues, with the goal of maintaining a stable and high-quality experience for our customers.
