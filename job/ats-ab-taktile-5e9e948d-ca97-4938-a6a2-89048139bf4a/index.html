ABOUT THE ROLE Taktile empowers financial institutions to transform into truly AI-native organizations. We’re growing rapidly with enterprise customers across banks and insurance companies, and we’re looking for a Backend Engineer to join our Engineering team. Team Optimize turns decision history into better decisions. We build the systems customers use to evaluate, monitor and improve their decision flows and agents. As a Backend Engineer, you'll build the backend systems and APIs behind them. Your work directly moves approval rates, false positive rates, and how confidently customers ship changes. At scale, the systems you build will: - Process millions of decisions per day - Persist and query terabytes of data per day - Execute large-scale experiments over billions of historical decisions - Power agentic features such as natural-language analytics and automated optimization of flows and agents (Taktile AI) What You'll Do - Build and operate backend services in Python / FastAPI on AWS serverless infrastructure. - Contribute to the design of high-scale subsystems operating on historical decision data, and deliver features end-to-end, from implementation and testing to deployment and monitoring - Write well-tested, reliable code and contribute to code reviews - Work with Product and senior engineers to turn business problems into well-scoped technical work - Operate in a high-leverage environment where AI-assisted and agentic coding are embedded into daily workflows, with access to state-of-the-art coding models (e.g., Claude Code) and the autonomy to use them deeply while maintaining strong engineering standards. Requirements - 3+ years building production backend systems, with experience delivering moderately complex projects or features. - Comfort working with large datasets. - Prior industry experience with Python. - Experience with a major cloud provider, AWS preferred. - Good communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work effectively with technical and non-technical stakeholders. - Fluency in English, both written and spoken. - Location: Berlin (hybrid setup). Taktile will not cover relocation services for this role. Ideal, But Not Required - Experience with building AI native applications - Experience with large-scale data technologies such as Apache Iceberg, Athena, Spark, or similar. - Experience with AWS serverless architectures (Lambda, SAM, Step Functions). - Familiarity with system design concepts and architectural trade-offs. - Experience with React and TypeScript. - Exposure to fraud, AML, risk, or financial decisioning domains. What we offer - Work with colleagues that lift you up, challenge you, celebrate you and help you grow. We come from many different backgrounds, but what we have in common is the desire to operate at the very top of our fields. If you are similarly capable, caring, and driven, you'll find yourself at home here. - Make an impact and meaningfully shape an early-stage company. - Learn from experienced mentors and achieve tremendous personal and professional growth. Get to know and leverage our network of leading tech investors and advisors around the globe. - Receive a top-of-market equity and cash compensation package. - Get access to a self-development budget you can use to e.g. attend conferences, buy books or take classes. - Receive a new Apple MacBook Pro, as well as meaningful home office set-up. Our Stance - We're eager to meet talented and driven candidates regardless of whether they tick all the boxes. We're looking for someone who will add to our culture, not just fit within it.
