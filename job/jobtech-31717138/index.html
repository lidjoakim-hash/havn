Digital Designer - Creative Production Enjoy turning creative ideas into polished digital campaigns? At Cherry Online, we're looking for a hands-on Digital Designer who enjoys the craft of design production — creating, adapting and delivering digital marketing assets across our casino and sports betting brands. You'll work with banners, social media graphics, digital ads and campaign materials for different channels and markets. Much of your work will involve bringing existing creative concepts to life, making sure every format, layout and detail is right. You'll join our creative team and work closely with Brand Managers, Creative Directors and fellow designers. We're looking for someone who's confident in Figma and Adobe, enjoys working across several projects and takes pride in delivering great work. There's room for your own creative ideas, but the heart of this role is high-quality, efficient digital production. What you'll be doing You'll be part of our creative team, working closely with Brand Managers, Creative Directors and fellow designers. With several brands and campaigns running at the same time, no two days will be quite the same. Your work will include: Creating banners, digital ads, social media assets, website graphics and other campaign materials. Adapting existing Key Visuals into different formats, channels, languages and markets. Making sure every asset looks right, from layout and typography to spacing and alignment. Working mainly in Figma and Adobe Creative Suite, alongside AI tools that help make production smarter and faster. Creating simple animations and motion assets when needed. Taking ownership of your assignments, from brief to final delivery. Finding practical ways to improve workflows and make everyday production more efficient. Most of your work will start with an existing creative direction, but you'll also have opportunities to develop visuals and contribute your own creative solutions. Who we're looking for You probably have a few years of experience in digital design production, perhaps from a creative or digital agency, production studio or in-house marketing team. You know your tools, understand how a design production workflow operates and feel comfortable taking a brief and running with it. We think you'll be a great fit if you: Have solid experience producing digital campaign assets, particularly banners, adaptations and marketing materials. Are confident using Figma, Photoshop and Illustrator. Have a sharp eye for design and care about the small details that make the difference between something that's okay and something that's really good. Enjoy working at a good pace, managing several assignments without compromising quality. Are independent, organised and comfortable making your own decisions. Have a practical, solution-oriented approach and enjoy figuring things out. Are curious about AI and how new technologies can improve creative production. Like being part of a team where people help each other, share ideas and get things done. Experience with motion design, AI image or video generation, or the iGaming industry is a plus, but not essential. We're more interested in your skills, attitude and the work you've done than the number of years on your CV. Why Cherry Online? You'll join an international company working with established brands in casino and sports betting, with plenty happening across different markets. Our creative team is small enough for you to have a real impact, but busy enough to offer variety and new challenges. You'll work with experienced colleagues, take ownership of your projects and have opportunities to develop your skills along the way. We like people who are curious, down-to-earth and happy to roll up their sleeves. People who enjoy working together, take responsibility and look for solutions rather than getting stuck on problems.
