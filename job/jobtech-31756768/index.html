Product Manager Tools & Accessories

Lyko Operations AB · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 450 000–550 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Heltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 6.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

We have calendars that people count down to, toiletry bags made for the wishlist and tools that make everyday routines a little bit better. Here, you’re the one who makes them happen. 💭 Ever heard of Make up Store, Rebecca Stella, Waterclouds or Estelle & Thild? They’re part of our ever-growing family of own brands. Now we’re looking for a Product Manager with experience in both product development and purchasing to own one of our most trend-driven categories: tools and accessories. From spotting the next big thing to holding the finished product in your hand, it’s all yours. What you’ll do Fast trends, lots of launches and never a dull week. Over the year you’ll juggle lots of projects, big and small, often at the same time. You plan the assortment, negotiate with our suppliers in Asia and around the world and make sure every product lands with the right quality, cost and margin, right on time. Together with our design resources, you turn trends into products that feel spot on. → Take products all the way from brief to shelf: calendars, toiletry bags, tools, lovables and more→ Build the assortment and the strategy behind it, with trends as your compass→ Negotiate price and terms with our suppliers in Asia and other international markets, and push back when it matters→ Keep lots of projects moving at once, without losing track of a single detail→ Brief and steer design and packaging with our design resources, so every product shines in store and online→ Team up with product managers across every category, Brand Managers, Stores and Supply Your category touches every other category in the team, so you’ll never be short of people to work with. Who are you? You spot a trend before it hits the feed. You love the pace, you love the details, and you’re not shy at the negotiating table. A beauty background? Not needed. Fashion, accessories or gifts work just as well, as long as trends have been calling the shots. What you bring: 3+ years in both product development and purchasing of trend-driven consumer products, in fashion, accessories, beauty or similar Strong product knowledge and a gut feeling for what’s next Experience planning an assortment and thinking strategically about it The confidence to negotiate and challenge suppliers on price and terms The ability to project manage yourself and keep every detail in check, even when the to-do list is long Great communication skills and energy from working with lots of people Fluent English, written and spoken. Swedish is a plus, not a must Bonus points if you’ve worked directly with suppliers in Asia. Everything Lyko-specific, we’ll teach you on the job. At Lyko Own Brands we have a lot of fun and get to be creative, but it takes focus and a high pace to keep it all on track. So we’re looking for someone who loves owning both the big picture and the small stuff. Don’t tick every box? Apply anyway! Who you are and what you can do matters more to us than your CV. The practical stuff This is a permanent position with a six-month probationary period, starting as soon as possible or by agreement. You’ll be based at our office on Sveavägen in Stockholm and report to Elisabet Kernell, Head of Product & Sourcing Own Brands. We believe great, inclusive cultures are built by people actually meeting and working together, so we’ve landed on what we call “Business First”: at least three days a week at the office, the other two up to you, as long as it works with the needs of the business. A pretty good balance, if you ask us. Using AI? Totally fine! Feel free to use AI to polish your wording, we do it too sometimes! But it’s you we want to get to know, not your chatbot. So keep the content your own, with your experiences and thoughts. If you move forward, we’ll dive deeper into your answers later in the process. And just to be clear: a person always makes the call on who moves forward 😊 Apply without CV or cover letter If this sounds exciting and you’re eager to apply, go ahe
