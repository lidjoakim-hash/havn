About the role: We are looking for a Backend Software Engineer with a strong security background to join our product development team based in Paris, which focuses on building innovative features to enrich the security protection for both our B2B and B2C users. The goal of the team is to research and build product capabilities that improve user protection, strengthen identity and access controls, reduce security risks, and raise the overall security posture of the Dashlane experience. In this role, you will design, build, and operate backend services that power security-sensitive features, working in close partnership with product, design, infrastructure, and the Security organization. This role is a strong fit for an engineer who has already built security-relevant product features in a SaaS environment, understands application security fundamentals, and can contribute pragmatically to secure, scalable backend systems. Location: You will be based in Paris, with English as your working language. At Dashlane, we embrace a hybrid culture that combines the best of both worlds: the creativity and energy of in-person collaboration with the flexibility of remote work. Our model is designed to strengthen team connections while supporting individual productivity and work-life balance. To maximize collaboration, we come together in the office on Mondays, Tuesdays, and Thursdays, while Wednesdays and Fridays offer more flexibility for focused work.
