Asana is seeking an experienced IT Support Specialist for our growing office in Dublin, Ireland. In this role you will provide critical holistic support of the Dublin office in addition to supporting Asanas globally through ticketing and Slack. You are someone who asks questions, is confident in not knowing everything, and would rather collaborate with others than go it alone. You are crisp and detail oriented, but able to work outside of the boundaries when necessary. On top of your proven history of success, you have a passion for technology and, like us, you value hard work and fun in equal measure.
