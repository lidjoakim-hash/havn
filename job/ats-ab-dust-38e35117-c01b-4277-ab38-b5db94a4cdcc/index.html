SUMMARY We're looking for a commercially minded lawyer to work hands-on with GTM, shaping, negotiating and closing customer deals. You'll own the legal workstream on commercial opportunities from early deal discussions through signature, while bringing privacy, regulatory, security and AI considerations into the work early. You'll also help turn recurring deal questions into playbooks and processes that let Dust move faster. WHAT YOU'LL DO GTM partnership and deal execution - Work directly with Sales and GTM on customer and partner opportunities, from deal shaping through negotiation and signature. - Develop negotiation strategies, propose workable alternatives and help resolve customer objections. - Explain risk in commercial terms and help teams move deals forward, escalating material decisions to the General Counsel. - Act as a legal engineer to help develop and refine our daily use of AI agents across the business. Commercial contracting - Draft, review and negotiate SaaS agreements, MSAs, order forms, DPAs, security terms and procurement documents. - Work through terms involving pricing and usage commitments, liability, indemnities, data use, security, audit rights and service commitments. - Support vendor, technology-provider and strategic partnership agreements. Privacy, regulation and product considerations - Integrate privacy, data protection, AI regulatory, security and other relevant considerations into commercial deal strategy. - Work with Product, Security, Privacy and other teams to assess proposed customer commitments and identify practical solutions. Playbooks and legal operations - Turn recurring GTM questions and negotiation patterns into templates, fallback language, playbooks and training. - Use Dust and other appropriate tools to make legal work more efficient, while applying sound judgment to confidential and sensitive matters.
