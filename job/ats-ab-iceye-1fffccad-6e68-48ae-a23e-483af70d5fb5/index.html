SUPO, where required) Why this role matters: We are looking for a Senior Mechanical & Thermal Production Technician to strengthen our satellite production operations as we scale toward standardized, repeatable, high-volume manufacturing. In this hands-on role, you will independently execute complex assembly, subsystem integration, and thermal verification testing following detailed work instructions and AIT procedures. You will act as a technical anchor for your assigned production area, ensuring workstation readiness and driving frontline troubleshooting alongside Specialists and AIT Engineering. We seek a proactive technical leader who takes full ownership of build activities, models high standards of quality and ESD compliance, and coaches technicians to elevate team capabilities and process stability. Who We Are ICEYE is the world leader in sovereign intelligence from space. We deliver persistent monitoring capabilities to detect and respond to changes in any location on Earth. ICEYE owns the world's largest and most advanced SAR (synthetic aperture radar) satellite constellation. To our customers we provide intelligence with unmatched quality, latency and revisit times, in any weather, day or night. To governments who choose to operate their own constellation we provide this proven capability as a sovereign system. ICEYE-built constellations serve customers in defence and intelligence, environmental monitoring, insurance and emergency management. We enable fast decisions that contribute to a safer future. Founded and headquartered in Finland, ICEYE operates globally with over 1000 employees across Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. Raise capacity or workload concerns early and help address their underlying causes. - Build support for process changes by involving the people doing the work, explaining the reasons, and incorporating their feedback. - Work with engineering and quality teams to resolve production issues and introduce process changes through the appropriate review and approval steps.
