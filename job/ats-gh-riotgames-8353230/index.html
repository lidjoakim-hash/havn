Production Coordinator - League of Legends (12 Month Contract)

Riot Games · Dublin, Ireland, Ireland

💰 ca. 54 000–63 000 EUR/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)KontraktDirekte fra arbeidsgiverFrist 5.12.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

As a Production Coordinator on League of Legends’ Purchase Point theme, you will help Riot bring quality content & events to our players. You will keep the players' needs front of mind as you help implement planned work and organise future work. Your work will directly touch millions of players every day. As part of a distributed development team, you will work with rioters from all crafts across multiple offices, maintaining impeccable communication & collaboration standards. Please note this is a 12 month contract role and will report to the Director, Game Production based in Dublin and will be in office on a hybrid basis Responsibilities • Help a range of teams organise and coordinate their upcoming content, including providing visibility to other teams dependent upon your work • Implement new loot and missions in League of Legends, and add content to its Store system based on specifications provided • Help organise League of Legends content that will be translated into other languages • Track bugs identified by QA, investigate problems, and resolve them within our tools • Help improve our processes for implementation of events Required Qualifications • Extensive knowledge of League of Legends in all its aspects. • Excellent problem-solving, organizational, and analytical skills. • Outstanding communication and presentation skills. • Passion for game development. Desired Qualifications • Understanding or practical experience of application of Agile and Lean development process and practices Applications will close 5pm, Thursday, 12 November, and you can expect to hear from our Talent Acquisition team from Monday, 16 November onwards regarding next steps. For this role, you'll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes the delight of players. We will be looking at your past studies, experience, and your personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players' experiences, this could be your role! At Riot Games, we put players first. That mission drives every decision in our quest to create games and experiences that make it better to be a player. Whether you’re working directly on a new player-facing experience or you’re supporting the company as a whole, everyone at Riot is part of our mission. And just like in our games, we’re better when we work together. Our goal is to create collaborative teams where you are empowered to bring your unique perspective everyday. If that sounds like the kind of place you want to work, we’re looking forward to your application.
