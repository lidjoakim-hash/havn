Welder for collision damage – your next challenge in Västerås! Are you a welder who thrives on challenges? Alstom is now looking for a new colleague who will work with repairs of damaged and crash-damaged vehicles in a dynamic and exciting environment. Your future role You will report to the Production Leader and work together with a dedicated and competent team. The group in the workshop consists of about 12 people and is a mix of both welders/blacksmiths and modification technicians (fitters). You will get good support and support from our colleagues from other functions such as engineering, industrialisation engineering, quality and purchasing. In this role, you will be an important part of the problem-solving process and we are looking for someone who has a passion for finding effective solutions. Your creativity and initiative are crucial to our daily work. The position includes, among other things: Replacing and aligning body parts Welding, gluing and riveting Disassembling and assembling vehicles You will work with both black and stainless steel as well as aluminum. We would like you to have a few years of experience in welding and be able to read drawings. Who are you? We value passion and attitude over experience. We don't expect you to have all the skills, but we believe that the following skills will help you succeed and grow in this role: Relevant education or experience in welding Previous work with welding and repair of vehicles or similar industries Good knowledge of Black MAG (145 2-12mm) and Stainless Steel MAG (145 2-12mm) to ensure high quality and efficiency in the work Knowledge of drawing reading and technical specifications related to welding Worked with welding that has requirements for non-destructive testing, such as Visual Inspection, Penetrant or Magnetic Powder. Be able to communicate freely in Swedish and good knowledge of English Qualifications: Welding certificate Post-Weld Direction Alu MIG (141 2-12mm), TIG (141) and pin welding (131/111) Heat direction/cold direction of bumps We value the following personal qualities in you: Creativity: You have the ability to think outside the box, which helps us find new and innovative solutions to welding-related problems. Solution-oriented: You quickly identify welding challenges and address them effectively, which is important for keeping our projects on track. Collaboration-oriented: Your unpretentious approach lifts collaboration in the team and creates a positive work environment where we achieve common goals. The work is carried out during the day, but you should be open to being able to work shifts. This service requires you to undergo a medical examination, which Alstom will provide. Interviews take place on an ongoing basis, so send in your application today.
