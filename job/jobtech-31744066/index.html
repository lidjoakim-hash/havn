RaySearch develops innovative software solutions to improve cancer care. Over 1200 clinics in more than 54 countries use RaySearch software to improve treatments and quality of life for patients. RaySearch was founded in 2000 and is listed on Nasdaq Stockholm. The headquarters is located in Stockholm, with subsidiaries in the US, Europe, Asia and Australia &amp; New Zealand. Today we are more than 485 employees with a common vision of improving cancer care with innovative software. Our great staff is crucial for our success and we offer a fantastic working environment in modern offices, flexibility and good opportunities for development. We believe in equal opportunities, value diversity and work actively to prevent discrimination. RaySearch Laboratories is looking for an HL7 Integration Engineer to join our Service department. In this role, you will work with healthcare customers to integrate RayCare with hospital information systems and other third-party systems. You will be the main point of contact between the customer and RaySearch for HL7 integration projects, combining technical integration work with customer communication and project coordination. We are flexible regarding the location of this position, as long as you are based in Europe. Priority will be given to candidates based in Sweden, France, Spain, the UK, Belgium, the Netherlands and Germany.  About the job As an HL7 Integration Engineer, you will support customers throughout the integration process, from understanding their technical environment and requirements to specifying, testing, and validating interfaces. The role involves close collaboration with customers as well as technical teams within RaySearch. You will work hands-on with HL7 messaging, troubleshoot integration issues, document solutions, and contribute to the planning and delivery of integration projects. An important part of the role is understanding what the customer needs, identifying what is technically feasible, and translating this into clear specifications, deliverables, and estimates. You will Collaborate with customers and our technical teams to provide integration support for RayCare. Develop integration specifications and workflows for HL7 messaging. Test and validate HL7 interfaces with different hospital information systems. Troubleshoot and resolve integration issues involving third-party systems. Participate in project planning, implementation, and documentation. Gather technical requirements from customers and translate them into clear integration specifications. Contribute to estimates, quotations, and statements of work for integration projects. Stay up to date with relevant standards and technologies within healthcare IT and HL7. Share your knowledge and experience within the Service team. Your profile We are looking for someone with practical experience of healthcare system integrations who is comfortable working directly with customers. To succeed in the role, we believe you have: Practical experience working with HL7 v2 messaging. Knowledge of FHIR and healthcare messaging and data exchange. Basic programming or scripting skills, preferably in JavaScript or a similar language. At least one year of relevant experience with healthcare integrations, either within a hospital IT/integration department or at a medical technology or healthcare software company. Relevant experience may also come from a clinical environment where you have worked with system integrations. Professional proficiency in English. The ability to communicate technical information clearly to both customers and internal stakeholders. It is meritorious if you have: Further experience with FHIR and an understanding of its role in modern healthcare interoperability. Experience with integration engines such as Mirth Connect (NextGen Connect).
