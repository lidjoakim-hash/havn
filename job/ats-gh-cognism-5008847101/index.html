WHO ARE WE Cognism is the leading provider of European B2B data and sales intelligence. Ambitious businesses of every size use our platform to discover, connect, and engage with qualified decision-makers faster and close more deals. Headquartered in London with global offices, Cognism’s contact data and contextual signals are trusted by thousands of revenue teams to eliminate the guesswork from prospecting. OUR WORK MODEL  Hybrid: This is a hybrid role, requiring you to work from our Paris office at least 2 days per week, with flexibility to work remotely on other days.  YOUR ROLE  As an Account Manager, you will be responsible for growing and retaining a portfolio of Mid-Market & Enterprise customers by managing renewals and identifying expansion opportunities. You will proactively engage with decision-makers, lead commercial negotiations, and maximize revenue from your accounts. In this high-growth, fast-paced environment, you’ll play a key role in scaling our Account Management function and driving long-term customer success.  YOUR CHALLENGES & OPPORTUNITIES  • Drive Revenue Growth – Manage a portfolio of Mid-Market & Enterprise customers, ensuring successful renewals, upsells, and cross-sells.  • Expand Customer Relationships – Build strong connections beyond your main point of contact to engage multiple stakeholders, decision-makers, and buying groups within each account.  • Strategic Account Management – Work closely with Customer Success Managers to align on customer health and develop tailored renewal and expansion strategies.  • Lead Sales Processes – Own the full sales cycle for upsells, from identifying opportunities to conducting demos, negotiating pricing, and closing deals.  • Customer Engagement & Advocacy – Leverage customer insights to position Cognism’s product suite effectively, driving adoption and identifying growth potential.  • Retention & Risk Mitigation – Monitor customer satisfaction, proactively addressing risks to ensure high retention rates.  • Optimize Processes – Contribute to the rollout of new account management workflows, ensuring efficiency and scalability in a rapidly growing team.  OUR EXPECTATIONS  • Sales Experience – Proven success in a client-facing role, managing a book of business and exceeding revenue targets.  • Commercial Acumen – Confident in running commercial negotiations and guiding customers toward the best Cognism package for their needs.  • Proactive & Strategic – Ability to identify expansion opportunities, engage new buying groups, and drive customer growth.  • Communication Excellence – Strong ability to build relationships, influence stakeholders, and articulate value propositions clearly.  • Process-Driven – Structured approach to pipeline and account management, with attention to detail and responsiveness.  • Collaborative Mindset – Team player who thrives in a fast-paced environment and works cross-functionally to achieve shared goals.  • Goal-Oriented & Ambitious – Motivated by targets, with a track record of meeting and exceeding sales quotas.  WHY
