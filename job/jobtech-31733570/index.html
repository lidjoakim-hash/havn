Company description: Getinge is a leading global provider of products and systems that contribute to quality enhancement and cost efficiency within healthcare and life sciences. We employ more than 10,000 people, and have operations in more than 40 countries. Saving lives is the greatest job in the world. Everything we do, from architectural planning to the equipment, therapies and solutions aim to secure the safest and most reliable care. As a leading medtech company, we focus every talent and resource towards helping our customers save as many lives as possible. Job description:Job Overview This position leads strategic global Quality compliance, Regulatory & Medical Affairs (QcRM) programs and projects, providing governance, cross-functional coordination, stakeholder engagement, risk management, and performance reporting. The role also supports IT-enabled initiatives by aligning QcRM business needs with Group IT solutions and oversees KPI reporting on quality-system health. This is a fully remote position.   Job Responsibilities and Essential Duties Leads a portfolio of strategic programs and projects in alignment with QcRM objectives, organizational priorities, and applicable quality, regulatory, and compliance requirements. Partners with executive sponsors and business owners to develop business cases, define program scope, objectives, success measures, governance, and decision rights, and prioritize initiatives based on value, risk, urgency, and available capacity. Establishes and facilitates program governance forums, ensuring effective decision-making, accountability, issue resolution, and alignment across sponsors, workstream leaders, and stakeholders. Develops and maintains integrated program plans, roadmaps, schedules, budgets, resource plans, and dependency maps; works with functional leaders to resolve resource, sequencing, and priority conflicts. Directs cross-functional program execution from initiation through closure, coordinating workstreams, deliverables, milestones, interdependencies, and handoffs to achieve agreed outcomes. Identifies, assesses, and manages program risks, issues, assumptions, and changes; coordinates mitigation and recovery plans and escalates matters requiring leadership decisions. Builds strong stakeholder alignment through clear communication, expectation management, and timely, audience-appropriate reports and presentations highlighting progress, performance, risks, financial status, and decisions required. Establishes and monitors program and portfolio KPIs to evaluate delivery, quality, adoption, and benefits against approved plans and to inform corrective actions and continuous improvement. Coordinates organizational change management, stakeholder readiness, communications, training, and transition to operational owners to support successful implementation and sustained adoption. Tracks expected benefits with business owners, leads program closure and post-implementation reviews, and ensures lessons learned are captured and incorporated into future planning and delivery practices. Develops, maintains, and continuously improves standardized program and project management methods, tools, templates, and controls; coaches teams on effective planning, execution, governance, and reporting practices. Serves as a key liaison between QcRM leadership, business stakeholders, and Group IT for technology-enabled initiatives, translating business needs into clear requirements and aligning solution delivery with quality, compliance, and operational objectives. Co-chairs the QcRM IT Business Alignment Meeting (BAM) and supports governance of IT-related project intake, prioritization, approval, delivery oversight, and escalation from initiation through completion.
