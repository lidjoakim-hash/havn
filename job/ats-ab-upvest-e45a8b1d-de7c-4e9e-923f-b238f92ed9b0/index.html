At Upvest, we are on a mission to make investing as easy as spending money. Upvest empowers businesses to offer a wide range of investment products and the best experience in the field of capital market investment and retirement planning. Upvest’s Investment API is easy to integrate so that fintechs and financial institutions can save resources and fully focus on their core business. We are proud to partner with Europe’s leading Fintechs and financial institutions such as DKB, Revolut, N26 and Raisin. Founded in 2017 by Martin Kassing, Upvest now brings together over 270 talented professionals from more than 70 nationalities. Upvest is backed by €280M in total funding from world-class investors, including BlackRock, Tencent, Sapphire Ventures, and Bessemer Venture Partners, Earlybird, Notion Capital, and Motive. Our latest €105M funding round in March 2026 - led by Sapphire and Tencent - serves as a massive catalyst for our growth, allowing us to offer premier investment experience. We are scaling fast and investing heavily in expanding our international footprint. Now, we are looking for an Expansion Lead to help us grow our presence across Europe. ABOUT THE ROLE: As Expansion Lead, you will join our Strategy team, working directly with our Head of Strategy, Tine von Villiez https://www.linkedin.com/in/von-villiez/, to drive Upvest's expansion into new markets and scale our presence in existing ones. You will own the end-to-end process of evaluating, planning and executing market entry - from market sizing and regulatory assessment to go-to-market execution and post-launch optimisation. You will run expansion as a cross-functional programme, working day to day with commercial, product and engineering, legal/compliance and operations, with direct exposure to senior leadership and to clients in each new market. This is a high-impact, highly visible role. WHAT YOU’LL DO: - Own the international expansion strategy: Evaluate and prioritise new markets, then own the business case and roadmap for your countries, grounded in market sizing, competitive dynamics and regulatory feasibility - Lead expansion programmes end-to-end: Own each market entry as a programme, from the first regulatory application through to launch and beyond. You are accountable for the overarching project and you keep the cross-functional work running smoothly by collaborating closely with legal/compliance, product and engineering, and operations every step of the way. - Engage clients and prospects: Work with sales and partnerships to understand B2B client needs in your target market, validate demand, shape the proposition and support the onboarding of launch clients - Build external relationships: Engage with regulators, partners and industry bodies to unlock and de-risk expansion opportunities - Own cross-functional alignment: Align senior leadership and other departments on expansion priorities, timelines and resourcing - Track and report on expansion progress: Develop and own KPIs/OKRs for expansion initiatives and report progress to leadership and the board - Support fundraising and investor narrative: Contribute country and market insights to investor updates and board materials WHAT YOU BRING: - Experience: 4+ years of experience in top-tier consulting, investment banking/private equity, or a high-growth scale-up, with direct exposure to market entry, GTM strategy or international expansion - You have hands-on experience in launching a product or business in a new European market, ideally in fintech, payments or capital markets - You have regulatory & financial literacy with a strong understanding of how regulation shapes market entry for a regulated financial business. You are comfortable engaging directly with regulators and compliance teams - You have a strong track record of leading complex, cross-functional programmes end-to-end, from scoping and sequencing to delivery across multiple stakeholders.
