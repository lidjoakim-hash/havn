The Role Veeva Batch Release helps life sciences companies modernize the decision to release a batch by connecting the quality, manufacturing, laboratory, document, and enterprise resource planning (ERP) information needed for disposition. In this European Strategy role, you will work with customers, partners, and Veeva teams to shape how the market understands and adopts the application. This is a highly customer-facing role for someone who combines regulated-manufacturing knowledge with experience turning complex processes into practical software, services, and field strategies.
