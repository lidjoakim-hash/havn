Job Description | Supply Chain Associate About The Team: The Voyager team in Anduril Industries is an engineering and design division with a vast experience in developing innovative computing and communications solutions for the network edge. The team specializes in integrating enterprise networking capabilities from global IT leaders with purpose-built hardware and software platforms designed to meet market demands for the most stringent of environmental requirements. We are seeking a diligent and detail-oriented Supply Chain Associate to join our team in Dublin. This role is essential to the smooth operation of our production and supply chain processes. You will be the key person responsible for processing production orders and ensuring material and inventory accuracy, providing critical support that underpins our manufacturing success. This position is primarily focused on processing and administrative tasks (approx. 80-100%), making your accuracy and efficiency vital to the team's performance and our transition from SAP to Oracle systems. Key Responsibilities • Manage the end-to-end processing of incoming production orders with a high degree of accuracy. • Verify product details, including serial numbers and finished goods, to ensure order correctness before processing. • Align with Contract Manufacturers (CMs) on production schedules and requirements. • Utilize our ERP system (SAP/Oracle) to build and maintain Bills of Materials (BOMs) and process production builds. • Oversee the timely closing of production orders, ensuring all associated data is complete and accurate. • Collaborate with the stock controller on inventory and material tracking to minimize discrepancies and maintain precise stock levels. • Maintain and update records meticulously within our key systems, including MS Excel, our ERP, and Salesforce. • Previous experience of manufacturing administration (from a similar industry, e.g. electronics manufacturing is highly preferred) Key Requirements • Qualification in Logistics or Supply Chain (min.
