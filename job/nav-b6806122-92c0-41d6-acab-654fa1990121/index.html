Tromsø - Tour Guides March 2027

Nordic Gateway AS · Tromsø, Norway

💰 ca. 400 000–480 000 kr/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Sesongvia NAVFrist 30.1.2027

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

We are looking for guide candidates in Tromsø for the month of March 2027. Join our team of experienced tour guides and help guests experience Northern Norway in winter. As a tour guide, you will be leading excursions in Tromsø and the surrounding area, showing guests both fascinating culture and stunning nature. The right combination of knowledge and enthusiasm will make your tours an unforgettable highlight. Meet new people and have fun at work on specific days during March of 2027. What we require of you - Fluent in German and English. - Living in Tromsø or moving there for reasons other than this job. We do not provide accommodation, and work is only on specific dates. - Friendly appearance and positivity in contact with guests. Prior experience in customer service is an advantage. - Interest in Norwegian nature, culture and history. Prior knowledge is an advantage. - Flexibility and ability to travel on short notice. - Honesty and reliability. What we offer you • Competitive wages, and possibility to start working as early as beginning of March. • Varied assignments, including possible tours outside of Tromsø. • Chance to guide in future seasons, and possibly in other Norwegian ports.
