Berlin, office-first | Working hours: 20h/week during the semester, can be increased during semester break | Duration: minimum 12 months, ideally 24 months About the team The Diversity, Inclusion and Sustainability team oversees SumUp's global sustainability work, including ESG compliance, our 1% for the Planet pledge, corporate social responsibility, and our carbon footprint calculations. You’ll play a central role in helping the team prepare for reporting requirements and improve processes across these working areas. It’s a practical way to learn how sustainability works inside a global fintech, with real projects, support from the team, and regular work with our Finance and Controlling teams. What you'll do • Support preparation for local reporting standards (e.g. EU’s Corporate Social Responsibility Directive) by tracking reporting requirements, facilitating the remediation of open reporting gaps, and helping draft sustainability reports • Analyze the annual greenhouse gas emissions, including data sources, methodology and supporting evidence • Keep an overview of active donation partners, including due diligence status, next actions and documentation, and support budgeting and donation tracking • Support with diversity and inclusion activities such as surveys, data analysis, and vendor management. What you'll bring • Enrolment in a Master's or PhD programme in sustainability, finance, controlling, or similar. • Strong data and analytical skills; close eye for detail when working with data and documentation. • Confidence using spreadsheets and comfort working across different reporting formats. • AI fluency, with hands-on experience using LLMs and building agents to produce high-quality outputs. • Strong communication skills and the ability to coordinate tasks, follow up with colleagues across teams, and keep projects moving towards clear goals. • Quick to learn new tools and systems, with a proactive and practical approach.
