ROLE HIGHLIGHTS: - Junior / Mid-level Thermal Engineer - Location: Espoo, Finland - Department: Spacecraft Hardware - Reports to: Thermal System Engineer Team Lead - Employment type: Permanent - Workplace model: Hybrid / Office - Employment is subject to applicable security screening (incl. SUPO, where required) THE MISSION OF THE JUNIOR / MID-LEVEL THERMAL ENGINEER In this role, you will join ICEYE’s Thermal Engineering team and contribute to the thermal analysis, design, and testing of spacecraft. You will work alongside experienced engineers, build your practical engineering skills, and take on increasing responsibility as you learn. We welcome recent graduates. Previous professional experience is not required: we are looking for sound engineering fundamentals, curiosity, a willingness to learn, and someone who shares the values described below. You will help prepare thermal models and analysis reports, support thermal test activities, and use test data to understand spacecraft thermal performance. There may also be opportunities to improve data processing and engineering workflows through scripting, automation, or AI tools. WHO WE ARE ICEYE delivers space-based intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance (ISR) capabilities to governments and allied nations. This includes sovereign and turnkey ISR missions leveraging ICEYE’s synthetic aperture radar (SAR) satellite technology, as well as access to data from the world’s largest SAR satellite constellation. These capabilities enable partners to detect and respond to critical changes anywhere on Earth with unprecedented speed and accuracy – day or night and in any weather. Designed for dual use, our platform also serves civil protection and commercial users for natural-catastrophe intelligence, insurance, maritime monitoring (including oil-spill detection), and finance, contributing to global security and community resilience. ICEYE operates internationally with offices in Finland, Poland, Spain, Japan, the UAE, Greece, and the US, and has hundreds of employees inspired by a shared vision: improving life on Earth by becoming the global source of truth in Earth Observation. EXPECTED OUTCOMES As you develop in the role, we expect you to: - Contribute to thermal analyses and clearly document assumptions, methods, and results, with guidance and review from experienced engineers. - Support the preparation and execution of thermal tests, and help turn test data into useful engineering findings. - Help compare model predictions with test results and support thermal model correlation. - Produce clear, traceable engineering documentation and gradually take ownership of agreed tasks. ROLE SPECIFIC RESPONSIBILITIES Working with the Thermal Engineering team, you will: - Help build and maintain spacecraft thermal models and run analyses under the guidance of experienced engineers. - Assist with assessing thermal design options and checking performance against requirements. - Support the preparation of thermal vacuum (TVAC), thermal cycling, and thermal balance test procedures and reports. - Help process analysis results and test telemetry, generate plots and tables, and investigate differences between predictions and measurements. - Document analysis cases, assumptions, results, and model correlation work so that findings can be reviewed and traced to source data. - Collaborate with engineers across disciplines and identify opportunities to improve thermal engineering workflows. REQUIREMENTS FOR JUNIOR POSITION You are likely a good fit if you: - Have a bachelor’s or master’s degree in mechanical engineering, Aerospace Engineering, Physics, or a related field, with a foundation in thermodynamics and heat transfer is preferred but not needed it. - Can apply engineering fundamentals to problems, explain your reasoning, and work carefully with technical data. - Are interested in spacecraft thermal engineering and willing
