Skin & spa therapist, part-time

N.A.P. AB · Stockholm, Sweden

💰 ca. 280 000–330 000 SEK/år (estimat · vanlig lønn, ikke bekreftet)Deltidvia ArbetsförmedlingenFrist 15.11.2026

Søk på stillingenÅpne i Havn (lagre, sammenlign, KI-hjelp)

Are you someone who genuinely loves taking care of others? Do you believe skincare is more than just a routine, that it is a way of reconnecting, recharging, and making someone's day better? Then you might be the person we're looking for. NAP is a growing skincare and wellness space, grounded in presence, simplicity, and care. We work from our own New Aesthetic Philosophy. We don't follow fixed routines, we follow clinical thinking, and our therapists adjust every treatment as it happens. No two are quite the same. That also means the work here is broader than facials. People come to us for skin treatments and peels, for facial and full-body massage, for wellness and stress relief, and for lashes and brows. Some come before a wedding, some while they are pregnant, and some simply to sleep for 80 minutes. We're now ready to welcome a new team member who shares our philosophy and values. What you’ll be doing: Performing personalized treatments based on our treatment philosophy, including facials, chemical peels, facial massage, and more Giving body and relaxation massage as part of our wellness treatments Creating a safe, relaxing, and welcoming space for every client Offering honest, thoughtful product recommendations Supporting the daily rhythm at NAP, from prepping the room to creating beautiful client experiences ﻿You are: Trained, or training, in skincare, spa or massage therapy. Certified, newly graduated, or still studying, all three are welcome Passionate about skin health and the power of human touch Caring, positive, and naturally warm in your way of being Someone who brings good energy into the room Curious, grounded, and open to learning and growing with us Already living in Sweden, with the legal right to work here Able to get to our studio in Kungsholmen on the days we agree, since none of this work can be done remotely If you are still studying, this is a part-time place to get real hands on real skin while you finish. The role: Part-time, hourly, on a schedule we agree in advance Occasional weekend work is expected English-speaking Kungsholmen, Stockholm Start date: Flexible Working at NAP means being part of something small, personal, and meaningful. We're not about rush or routines that feel robotic. We're here to make people feel seen, held, and truly taken care of. To apply: Everything happens here: https://join.napsthlm.com We're looking for the right person, not the perfect CV. We'd love to know what draws you to this role, and what makes you feel at home in your work. We hope you understand that we will not be able to respond to everyone who reaches out by DM or email. The form is the only way for us to properly consider you, and there is no other way in. We usually receive hundreds of applications, so please take your time with it. We read every one of them ourselves, and we will do our best to come back to everyone who has thoughtfully filled it in, including the people we will not be taking further. We will start interviewing within the next 2 to 3 weeks. Either way, you will hear from us.
