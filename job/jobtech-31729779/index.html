British American Tobacco Fiedler & Lundgren has an exciting opportunity for a Project Manager, Engineering,in Malmö As a Project Manager within Engineering, you will manage projects and initiatives from concept to implementation, ensuring delivery on time, within budget, and according to quality standards. You will work on a wide range of projects, from machine development and improvement initiatives to sustainability and construction projects. Your key responsibilities will include: Responsible for project management within investment projects in all manufacturing, sustainability, utility and construction technologies. Responsible for CAPEX submission, forecast, budget and capitalization within project. Responsible for allocated OPEX project budget. Driving projects according to IM standard and GSUM workbook Responsible for compliance towards BAT guidelines regarding but not exclusively Strategic asset management and Technical design guide. Contributing to improvements and innovations within the factory for better manufacturing processes and technologies, safer working environment, more sustainable production and lower production costs. Responsible for project following all Swedish regulation, ex, ATEX, Arbetsmiljölagen, CE, building permits. Responsible for compliance towards procurement and legal within projects. Comply with and implement activities in accordance with regulations related to environmental protection, occupational health and safety, fire protection, and internal EHS or Sustainability standards and procedures. What are we looking for? 5+ years of experience in production engineering. Strong technical expertise and a genuine passion for engineering. Documented experience in project management. Excellent interpersonal and stakeholder management skills. Structured, goal-oriented, and capable of managing multiple priorities. Service-minded, adaptable, and solution-focused. Fluent Swedish and English communication skills, both written and verbal. Technical education at university level is considered an asset. What we offer you?
